However, if you use the original images from the dataset, they are downscaled in a different way. After downscaling the width and height are rounded up, which is different from nerfstudio, where it is assumed they are rounded down. You can use ns-process-data to downsample the images or manually modify cameras/cameras.py:799 to round up instead.

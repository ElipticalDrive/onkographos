Adds a native C++17 surround-view foundation targeting Qualcomm Linux, with portable OpenGL ES 3/EGL rendering and a separate NVIDIA CUDA backend. Recorded RGB camera sets are projected onto a configurable bowl and stitched using shared projection mappings; YAML controls camera geometry, lens parameters, vehicle footprint, viewpoint, output and blending.
Includes a CPU reference renderer, strict configuration validation, requirements/design documentation, DMA-BUF capture/display interface contracts, calibration acceptance policy, and a Linux GLES CI workflow.
Validation: local CMake build passed; all three CTest suites passed (core, YAML validation, and GLES/CPU parity across three view/image configurations). Maximum measured channel difference was one 8-bit level in the initial parity case. Synthetic 640x480 GLES render completed and was visually inspected using Mesa llvmpipe.
Draft pending NVIDIA CUDA compilation/device testing and physical Qualcomm integration. Live capture, NV12 conversion, zero-copy buffer import, direct display, persistent frame rendering and targetless online calibration estimator are not implemented yet. The sample calibration is synthetic. No physical-board performance or safety-qualification claim is made.

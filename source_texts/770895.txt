What
The indexer's top-k (kernel_argsort_f32_i32_desc + merge) keeps every 1024-row block's
top 512 and merges them in log2(blocks) dependent passes: at 435k rows that is 9 passes
over 217k survivors, 2-3 ms per call, and the 32-row causal batches of prefill took ~5 ms.
kernel_topk_select_{init,hist,scan,tiecount,compact,finish} (metal/argsort.metal) find
the k-th key exactly with four 8-bit histogram levels over the sortable-int form of the
scores, count the ties at it per chunk, compact the winners in one pass (the rows above
the k-th key, then the first ties by index), and let one threadgroup order them (score
desc, index asc). Taken when a row has at least 4096 visible entries and top_k <= 2048;
the sort stays for everything else. The choice is made per visible width, the same for a
token alone and inside a causal batch — a batch straddling 4096 is cut in two — so a
position's top-k does not depend on how it was submitted; make test-deepseek41-metal
checks exact IDs across frontiers 1024..65535, including rows of all-equal scores.
DS4_METAL_DISABLE_TOPK_SELECT=1 restores the sort.
Where the two differ: scores exactly tied at the k-th boundary. The sort keeps the
members of the tie its comparison network happens to leave in place; the select keeps
the lowest indices, deterministically. The set and its order are identical whenever the
k-th score is not tied.
Measured
M5 Max 128 GiB, macOS 26.5, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash Q2, Metal SSD streaming.

Replaces argsort-plus-merge with exact k-th selection (four 8-bit histogram
levels over sortable-int keys, one compaction pass) when a row has >=4096
visible entries and top_k <= 2048. Upstream measured this on an M5 Max under
SSD streaming: 169 -> 178 t/s prefill of a 10k prompt at 16k context, and
2.4 -> 0.4 ms per top-k call at 435k rows. DS4_METAL_DISABLE_TOPK_SELECT=1
restores the sort.

Scores exactly tied at the k-th boundary now keep the lowest indices
deterministically; the set and its order are unchanged when that score is not
tied. `make test-metal-topk-select` and `make test-deepseek41-metal` both pass
on this box.

I am not able to mark intersection of a circle and ray that is placed on top of a segment. Steps to reproduce:
Draw segment AB (or use one that is already existing)
Draw ray AB
Draw circle with center A and radius less than |AB| (avoid clicking on segment AB to make sure you do NOT already select the intersection point)
Use intersection tool and try to mark intersection -> it does not work
I am trying to use this as part of primitives-only solution for level 10. Probably not the most efficient one but still should work i.e.:
(1) ray BA
(2) ray DC
(3) point E as intersection of (1) and (2)
(4) circle center = E, radius = EC, point F as intersection of this circle and ray BA
(5) circle center = E, radius = ED, point G as intersection of this circle and ray BA
(6) circle center C, radius CG
(7) circle center G, radius GC, point H as intersection of circles (6) and (7)
(8) circle center H, radius HF
(9) intersection of circle (8) and BA solves the task
I am not able to perform the final step due to above described problem.

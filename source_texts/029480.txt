This PR reimplements the procedural cloud raymarching with a mathematically correct, true 3D extruded SDF and a unified sphere tracing loop.
Key Improvements:
Curved Extrusion 3D SDF: We calculate the exact 3D SDF of the 2D Voronoi-based footprint SDF extruded between baseFloor and baseCeiling relative to the curved surface of the Earth. This ensures perfect alignment with the planet's spherical curvature.
Simplified Unified Raymarching Loop: Removed the complex, nested jumping/adaptive loops and replaced them with a single-loop distance-assisted sphere tracer.
Optimizations: Added standard distance-based step scaling to increase minimum step size at large distances, resulting in faster and more robust tracing.
Jitter & Quality: Properly maintained start-up ray jittering and multi-wavelength density tracking.

What
The volumetric clouds looked very repetitive while flying. They read tiling 3D noises at power-of-two periods (the shading frame), and on the flat world at 5 cm the noises tile at these periods:
noise
period
shape
6.55 km
detail
819 m
weather (cover, type)
26.2 km
So the whole cloud field repeated exactly every 26.2 km, and the same cloud shapes came back every 6.55 km under a different cover.
Fix: a smooth domain warp of every noise read (cloud_warp, src/clouds.cpp).
Two sine waves per axis, whole cycles per a 2^23-voxel period (419 km at 5 cm; CloudLayer::warp_period_vox). That keeps the clouds the same from any render origin.
Each wave's amplitude is 0.015 periods. That stretches the clouds by 0.42 at most (~0.2 typical).
Points one shape period apart now read the noises ~1.5–2 km apart; points one weather period apart, 5–9 km apart.
The warp drifts with the wind, so the clouds move as a whole. The wind's offset now wraps at the warp period.
Each period now fits inside the next longer one: weather ≤ warp / 4, shape ≤ weather, detail ≤ shape. Without that, at tiny voxels the frame's longest period would clamp them all to the same value.
The march and the shadow pass skip the weather (and its warp) at points outside the layer.

This cost was measured before the passes skipped the weather outside the layer, so the final cost is at most this.
At full amplitude, the poses measured +0.6 to +1.0 ms. That extra comes from the cover: the warp lands on a different part of the weather there, and the visible cover went from 21% to 29–42%. The noise's statistics are unchanged, so cloud_cover keeps its meaning on average.
Tried and dropped: a warp read from the shape noise itself, at a long period. It broke the repeat, but its fine octaves (gradients ~1.5) sheared the clouds into streaks.
Not covered (named in docs/look.md)
The field still repeats every warp period: at most 2^23 voxels, 419 km at 5 cm. A per-tile hash from the origin's high bits would remove that; it would be a ShadeFrame-wide change.
The light march reuses its start point's warp, as it already reused the weather.
The plain cloud layer (VoxelSky's clouds.png, used without the volumetric clouds) still tiles every cloud_scale_m.

Render origin with the clouds in view: bench.sh --scenario=fly --fly_dist=20000 --still=1 --pitch=10 --validate=64 --audit=1. 38 rebases; shot_diff ok, with 0.22% of pixels differing (limit 1%).
Unit tests:
The periods are powers of two and the warp is ≥ 4 weather periods, including at 1 mm voxels.
Every $ constant is substituted; WARP_AMPLITUDE now goes in at full precision.

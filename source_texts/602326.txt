Problem
There is no Voronoi or cellular distance field node in V1.
Voronoi SDFs produce organic cell-like surface patterns
and are commonly used in procedural geometry for
biological, architectural, and abstract forms. They are
a natural extension of the existing noise infrastructure.
Proposed approach
A voronoiNode that generates a 2D or 3D Voronoi distance
field with parameters for cell scale, jitter, and a
combiner type (F1 for standard cells, F2-F1 for borders/ridges).
The GLSL implementation follows the standard approach from
Inigo Quilez's Voronoi articles.
Files to look at
src/rendering/GLSLEvaluator.js — add voronoiNode
template in _generate2DNode() and _generate3DNode()
src/graph/NodeSpec.js — add voronoiNode spec
src/graph/NodeEvaluator.js — CPU evaluation for
marching squares and STL export
Reference
https://iquilezles.org/articles/voronoilines/

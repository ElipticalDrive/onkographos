What
Makes the factory look more realistic, with more detailed surroundings, machines and factory buildings (feedback from Tien).
Lighting & materials: soft shadows, depth haze on the far scenery, gentle glow, sky ground colour. New shared vertex-coloured materials (paint, steel, glass, water, solar, screen, see-through mesh) plus world-space noise "grain" on grass, asphalt, concrete and floors, so large areas are no longer a single flat colour.
Hall/warehouse/office: a tall back wall (ribbon windows, steel columns, pipes, cable tray, cabinets, painted sign) that hides when the camera looks from the north. Ribbed low walls with bollards, bay and walkway markings, workbench, compressor, forklift, roller door, bulk bags on pallets and a furnished office.
Machines: all 5 models get base frames, hazard stripes, control panels and signal towers. Each also gets type-specific parts: tie bars and heated barrels, a glass cabinet, a sprue picker with conveyor, light curtains, and a 6-axis robot arm.
Trucks, cars, parking, annex racks, sales office, break room, solar canopy and scaffolding are redrawn.
Scenery: silos, chillers, transformer, gate booth with barrier, kerbs, zebra crossing, bus stop and power line. Detailed houses and neighbour factory, a pond with reeds and a jetty, a farm with barn, tractor and wheat. Wooded hills, multi-clump trees, bushes, rocks, grass tufts (MultiMesh) and layered clouds.
Performance: the new MeshBatch helper merges static geometry into a few meshes, where before every box was its own draw call. The sky now refreshes every few game minutes instead of every frame. Web and mobile builds get fewer trees, less grass and a smaller radiance map.
The only overlap with #1 (web/mobile) is scripts/view/main.gd. The SSAO line is left untouched, so a merge should be trivial.
Testing
All runs used Godot 4.7.2.
godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/run_tests.gd: 184 checks, 0 failures.
Smoke test (-- --smoke-test, 30 s at x4): runs with no script errors.
Screenshot tool under xvfb with --rendering-method gl_compatibility (the renderer the web build uses): every shot renders, including day, showcase, wide and night.
The first run caught _build_annex_piles being dropped during the rewrite. It is restored in the second commit.
Not yet checked: Forward+ desktop rendering, and frame rate on a real iPad.

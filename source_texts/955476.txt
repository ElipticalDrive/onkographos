The volumetric lighting system was invisible because the necessary atmospheric physical constants (Rayleigh/Mie scattering, extinction, etc.) were not being bound to the shaders by the AtmosphereManager. Additionally, the coordinate mapping for weather data in the shaders was incorrect, leading to misaligned or zeroed-out weather modulation.
This PR:
Updates AtmosphereManager::BindToShader to set all required atmosphere parameter uniforms.
Fixes the weatherUV calculation in shaders/atmosphere/common.glsl to use the correct terrain anchor and grid scale.
Ensures all shaders pass validation and the project build is stable.

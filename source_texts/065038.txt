Abstract
We introduce recent advances in volumetric displays based on femtosecond laser excitation in terms of graphics scale and color. Centimeter-scale graphics with sufficient brightness for human observation have been achieved. Furthermore, a rendering method involving the generation of light scattering points and controlling the color of illumination, which is promising for color graphics, has been demonstrated.

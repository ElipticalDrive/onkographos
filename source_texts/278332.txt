Result
Integrate PR #97's actual source-H Standard Building and the existing Standard Terrain on the grid generated by pinned VoxCity 1.7.0 public APIs. The opt-in runner reuses the existing 64×64 m ROI and 128×128 m computation domain/32 m halo, freshly validates the complete Consumer chain, samples DEM at actual engine centres, and preserves full source/derived building shapes across boundaries.
Executed on local Windows OUEM venv: 41 real Buildings, 3,746 building columns, 128×128×27 int8 voxels. Classes: 34,802 BUILDING; 46,882 subsurface; 16,384 common Bareland surface; 344,300 air. All grid/ID/contributor/ground and full-array scalar checks PASS. Maximum source roof-to-segment upper-edge difference is 1.413052 m, recorded as voxel discretization/surface-layer error, with no H conversion or offset. The two successful runs have byte-identical grid metadata and all 12 saved arrays.
Method and reusable artifacts
Use public precise Building rasterization and Voxelizer.generate_combined. Stabilize owner IDs against per-building DEM means; reject engine ID merging, footprint changes, missing Buildings or NoData/outside engine-centre samples. Preserve raw DEM separately from engine-required conditioned ground/D_eff, and record D0_raw versus D0_effective and ground +1 layer semantics. A real-engine float32 half-cell boundary test guards the diagnostic rounding oracle.
Ignored local outputs under data/work/real-komae-voxel/run-20261028-04 include all arrays, exact grid/centre coordinates/ROI mask, sparse contributor/segment records, whole footprints with IDs/heights/ground, five exact-affine EPSG:4326 GeoTIFFs, plan/section PNGs, report and RESULTS.md. They provide explicit PR #99 reuse inputs without conflating the engine grid with the older EPSG:6677 north-up grid.
Validation
Final whole pytest: 887 passed, 5 skipped, 259 warnings in 205.07 s. Skips are Windows symlink privilege cases; warnings are Rasterio/Affine deprecations and the existing stat/fstat diagnostic.
New focused suite: 9 passed, 5 warnings, including public-engine sloped/boundary integration, CRS/sampling/orientation, NoData/DEM edge failure, IDs, D0, voxel placement, rounding and saved-array/GeoTIFF XY readback.
Actual input hashes unchanged; fixed VoxCity version/commit and installed source RECORD checks PASS.
Details and reproduction: docs/experiments/real_komae_voxel/RESULTS.md and README.md.
Limits
16 Buildings cross the computation boundary; 4 complete footprints extend beyond DEM bounds. Their whole shapes are retained, while ground/voxels only describe covered computation cells. The 2.5D roof proxy, common Bareland label, source-declared vertical semantics and 32 m halo are not survey/land-cover/solar acceptance. QGIS visual acceptance was not run; saved plots were inspected and GeoTIFF readback checked. No Canopy, Solar, heat evaluation, Standard mutation, profile eligibility, formal acceptance or Phase 5 gate change. No large artifacts committed and no automatic merge.

The matrix has the following properties:
It is tridiagonal in that the only non-zero entries are on the diagonal and adjacent sub-diagonals.
This means that the time taken to solve the equations will be proportional to n, and not n3 ,
as it would be for a general matrix.
It is symmetric positive definite, which means that the Cholesky factorisation that we previously
discussed can be deployed. If we store the diagonals in the vector d[1] . . . d[n−1], the off-diagonal
elements in the vector a[2] . . . a[n − 1], and the rhs vector in b[1] . . . b[n − 1] the whole solution
process can be reduced to:
for i = 2 to n-1 do
a[i] = a[i] / d[i-1];
d[i] = d[i] - a[i] * d[i-1] * a[i];
end do
for i = 2 to n-1 do
b[i] = b[i] - a[i] * b[i-1];
end do
for i = 1 to n-1 do
b[i] = b[i] / d[i];
end do;
for i = n-2 downto 1 by -1
b[i] = b[i] - a[i+1] * b[i+1];
end do
with the solution stored in b[i] where the rhs originally was.
In class I discussed the Thomas algorithm for tridiagonal matrices, which does not use symmetry.
If the diagonal elements are stored in the vector a,
a[i] = (h[i] + h[i + 1])/3.0 for i = 1 . . . n − 1
the subdiagonals in the vector b,
b[i] = h[i]/6.0 for i = 2 . . . n − 1
the superdiagonals in the vector c,
c[i] = h[i + 1]/6.0 for i = 1 . . . n − 2
and the rhs in the vector d
d[i] = rhs[i] for i = 1 . . . n − 1
the solution process consists of a forward elimination step
for i = 2 to n-1 do
factor = b[i] / a[i-1];
a[i] = a[i] - factor * c[i-1];
d[i] = d[i] - factor * d[i-1];
end do
followed by a back substitution step
d[n-1] = d[n-1] / a[n-1];
for i = n-2 downto 1 by -1
d[i] = ( d[i] - c[i] * d[i+1]) / a[i];
end do

Having solved for the parameters defining the two splines, we are able to do the usual manipulations you expect to be able to do with curves.
If you want to generate the curve from (x1 , y1 ) to (x2 , y2 ), then s will range from s1 to s2 .
If you want to evaluate Y given X, for some point (X, Y ) on the curve, you will first have to
find the appropriate s interval, which will be identified by the fact that X lies between xk and
xk+1 , and then solve the equation

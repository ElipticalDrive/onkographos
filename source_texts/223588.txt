v0.7.0
Highlights
Point cloud streaming engine — modular cycle-based pipeline replaces monolithic CopcStreamingLoader
BasemapStore — dedicated store, MapStore decoupled from basemap logic
Changes
Features
Cycle-based point cloud traversal with per-frame pipeline: traversal, budget, loading, eviction
Octree visitor with SSE and lod separation, frustum culling, and priority-based scheduling
Point budget planning with sibling-block reservation and truncation
LRU-based tile eviction
BasemapStore with cached health checks, additive config registration, and reactive setting sync
Refactoring
Decompose CopcStreamingLoader into traversal, budget, loading, eviction, geometry, priority, and render modules
Extract frustum, screen-space error, WGS84, and camera snapshot into geometry module
Extract RenderBufferPool, SSE filter, and frustum filter into render module
Extract BasemapStore from MapStore; reduce MapStore to applyStyleFragment and removeOwnedLayers
Move basemap config loading from registerMapTools to registerBasemaps
Rewrite checkBasemapHealth from Image probe to HEAD fetch
Remove BasemapSync, basemapSettings, initBasemap, and PerfTracker
Chores
Update pointcloud-streaming and store-architecture specs
Remove archived cesium change spec and stale budget and visibility tests
Add basemap preview images

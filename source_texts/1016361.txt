A regular grid of dark dots shows on faces in soft shadow (the owner: "why I see a grid on a face, like black dots in a regular pattern? is it lighting quality?", the mage's face under his hat's brim in the lobby).
Likely cause (read from three.js 0.185's shader, not yet reproduced in the lab): PCFShadowMap (renderer.ts) samples the shadow map 5 times per pixel (a Vogel disk, each tap a hardware 2x2 compare), the disk turned at each pixel by interleaved gradient noise (shadowmap_pars_fragment, getShadow). That noise is made for a temporal resolve; the game has none, so in every soft shadow edge the 5 taps' pattern stays on screen as a fixed screen-space dither: a regular grid of dots. Our penumbra is wide (PENUMBRA 0.12 m in core/quality.ts, shadow.radius several texels at every preset), so the dithered band is wide too; a hat brim's shadow falling across a face shows it most. It is the same at every quality preset (the radius is kept in metres), so it is not "lighting quality".
What to do
A lab measure first: the hero under a brim (or any caster near a lit surface) pictured close, the shadow's penumbra band read for a periodic pattern (the dither's period in screen pixels), with a canary (a plain hard shadow) that must read clean.
Then the fix, weighed against the frame's cost (every lit material samples the shadow): more taps (e.g. 12 to 16 on the Vogel disk), a blur that doesn't dither (a fixed rotated grid of taps, hardware-filtered), or a narrower shadow.radius on the hero's close views. Time it with lab perf and on the phone's set-up (--boot low@518400 --throttle 4), and check Shaders N (+M) stays at +0: a changed chunk must still compile at load.

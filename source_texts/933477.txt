Closes the recorded M2 open failure on the Mandelbulb (RT R3), with bounds unchanged. The coordinator scheduled this on 2026-10-11 as a free double-float experiment.

So the limit is the refinement's precision. sdf_refine now keeps its search point, bracket and midpoint as double-float pairs built with TwoSum. TwoSum uses additions only, so multiply-add contraction cannot affect it, and DXC already builds with -Gis. Every distance query still uses the float32 position o + d * hi. The march, the estimator and the normal are unchanged.

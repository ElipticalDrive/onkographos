What
ADR 035. EnvironmentMap (rendering-domain) is built on the CPU from an equirectangular radiance image (from_equirect) or a procedural Sky (zenith/horizon/ground plus an optional sun disc): six GGX-prefiltered specular levels (level i is width >> i by height >> i, roughness i/5) and nine spherical-harmonic coefficients of diffuse irradiance. Pure math, unit-tested (uniform sky stays uniform at every level and in irradiance, a sun disc spreads with roughness, direction/uv round trip).
Port: FrameRecorder::set_environment(Option<&EnvironmentMap>); the fake and the contract suite cover it. GpuFrameRecorder forwards to SceneRenderer::set_environment, which uploads one Rgba16Float 2D texture with the mip chain (hand-rolled f32 to half, overbright values clamp) and rebuilds the flat lit pipelines' cached bind groups. A 1x1 black texture stands in while none is set.
Shading: with an environment, ambient light is irradiance(n) * diffuse + prefiltered(reflect, roughness) * (f0*A + B) (Karis' analytic split-sum, no LUT), times occlusion. Without one the hemisphere gradient is used exactly as before, so existing scenes are unchanged. The lit uniform grows 416 to 576 bytes; environment bindings are 3/4 (flat colour) and 9/10 (textured).
gltf_viewer lights the model with a sky whose sun matches its directional light (MERIDIAN_ENV=none turns it off). sdk re-exports EnvironmentMap and Sky.
Refactors forced by the arg-count and function-length rules: bind group entries are built with small helpers; create_shadow_bind_group takes the shadow map as a SampledTexture; create_mipped_texture takes a size array.
Not in this PR
An environment image loader (Radiance .hdr) and a cube-map environment; both noted in the ADR. Specular/horizon occlusion is not modelled.
Verified
Lavapipe, gltf_viewer on DamagedHelmet: the visor reflects the sky gradient, metal parts show blurred reflections, no validation errors or panics. Gates green locally: tests for the touched crates, clippy backlog, craftcheck, check-deps, rustdoc, nightly fmt (the full workspace test run is left to CI because this machine's disk is nearly full).

Yes -- run one pregen on your server with Chunksmith (/cs radius <blocks>, then /cs start), it builds the LODs as it goes and stops when it's done, and any friend who joins with Chunksmith plus Distant Horizons or Voxy gets them sent automatically -- just don't install LOD Server Support alongside it, as the two conflict.

Transform Coding for Hardware-accelerated Volume Rendering
This paper presents a novel block-based transform coding scheme designed specifically with real-time volume rendering in mind, such that the decompression is fast without sacrificing compression quality, and develops a new block classification scheme which allows us to preserve perceptually important features in the compression.

What this changes
Refs #207. Adds typed engine half-space clipping, conservative visible-ID masks, set-view-clipping/clear-view-clipping, personal CAS storage and export-view --view UUID. Custom planes, world work area and the constructed view's depth expand to at most 16 effective planes.
Selected-view exports use BCVXFRM3: four length-prefixed JSON sections (definitions, element, grids, presentation), followed by the unchanged engineering snapshot bundle. Presentation carries a resolved camera, exact model/snapshot and personal-file pins, planes and visible UUIDs. Missing view anchors refuse export. Legacy raw/V1/V2 exports remain unchanged.

Review of PR #248 (V15 view clipping) — approved
Reviewed the diff against base feat/v12-named-model-views (13 files, ~+788): engine::view_clipping (ClipPlane/Clipping/visible_elements), personal clip records, set-view-clipping/clear-view-clipping, the BCVXFRM3 presentation export, tests and the design note.
Verdict: approving. No correctness bugs found. Points verified:
Engine admission: ≤8 user planes, unit-normal enforcement on stored settings, bounded points; effective expands work area (6) and the constructed depth band (2) into ≤16 total planes, matching the visible_elements budget; the corner-based AABB rejection is conservative (only proven-outside elements are dropped) with a scale-aware epsilon.
Isolation: clipping is presentation-only — the framed bundle is byte-identical to the legacy export (tested), model inspection and BOM unchanged, and no quantity path reads the mask.
Transport: BCVXFRM3 keeps the four-section layout with the engineering bundle last and untouched; legacy --with-* exports keep their old magic and rules (replies_framed still requires --out -); --view + FILE writes the same frame and reports presentation in the JSON summary (tested).
Personal state: clip records are closed typed JSON with capped planes; depth-enabled requires a constructed view; clear resets planes, work area and depth; delete-view removes the clip record too; CAS reused.
Export of unresolved views is refused with DependencyUnavailable rather than falling back to the stored camera — matches the documented contract.
Nice-to-have (no change required): Clipping::validate unit-normal strictness is effectively a no-op after ClipPlane::new normalization in the read path (read normalizes, then validate compares against the already-normalized record) — harmless, but the two-step could be one normalize-and-admit.

Issue body actions
Under Enhanced graphics the terrain casts no sun shadow: a mountain does not shade the valley at
its foot, a cliff does not shade the lake below it, and the sun's glint on the water shines inside
what should be the cliff's shadow. The ground was taken out of the sun's shadow maps because, under
a 25° sun, Godot's soft directional filter makes a flat sheet shadow itself in bands at the shadow
map's texel pitch (WorldBuilder.IsShadowlessGround; git log --grep=BL-1037 holds the
measurements). The original's world is fullbright and has no terrain shadow either, so this is a
remake-only feature of Enhanced mode; Faithful is untouched.
The cause is in Godot's renderer, not in a setting: every tap of the PCF and PCSS filters compares
one depth per fragment (no receiver-plane bias), and the PCSS blocker-search radius grows with the
fragment's distance from the cascade's light-space near plane, which for a terrain cascade is set
by the tallest caster. No bias reaches that disc without detaching every contact shadow. The design
keeps the terrain out of the shadow maps and bakes its sun occlusion instead, which the static sun
allows.
Design
The sun's orientation is authored once per chapter (SUNLIGHT_ORIENTATION,
docs/formats/weather.md; only C2/MP2 and C2/MP3 author a different pitch in ZONE2) and the
terrain never moves, so the terrain's sun occlusion is baked at world build into a
shadow-height texture over the ocean mask's grid (8 m texels, the world's rect, OceanMask):
per texel, the height of the sun ray grazing the highest terrain upstream along the sun's azimuth,
and the horizontal distance to the blocker that set it. A fragment below that height is in terrain
shadow; the edge softens over blocker distance × tan(sun angle), floored at one texel, so the
penumbra is contact-hardening like the building shadows beside it. The compare is a world height
against a bilinearly filtered height, so there is no texel-depth acne by construction.
Casters: the terrain. The bake rasterises WorldBuilder.IsTerrainMesh meshes to a
max-height field; the water sheets are in it only as floor fill (a sheet never rises above its
shore, so it shades nothing). Buildings, zeppelins and aircraft stay on Godot's cascaded shadow
maps, and the ground stays out of them.
Receivers: every Enhanced world-lit arm (terrain, water sheet, buildings, clutter) and the
ocean shader. Aircraft are unchanged; the original's SHADE_* darkening of the player's
aircraft under a mountain (docs/org/shadows.md) is a separate item the same texture would
answer.
Bake: a max-height raster (the OceanMaskRaster fill, thread-pool row bands), then one
scanline sweep along the sun's azimuth, linear in the texel count. One bake per distinct
orientation among the mission's zones, all at build; a zone apply rebinds the global uniform.
Outside the rect reads lit.
Delivery: a light() on the three arms (Lambert diffuse; a copy of Godot's Schlick-GGX
specular on the water and ocean arms) multiplies the directional light's ATTENUATION by the baked
factor, so Godot's shadow maps compose with it, ambient is untouched, and the omni LIGHT_STATE
lamps and fireball bursts light these surfaces as before. Globals follow the csky_ocean_*
pattern: the texture, its rect, and the live sun angle's tangent.
Switch: follows Shadow Quality (ShadowQualitySetting): Off bakes nothing and shades
nothing, every casting level is on, and the level's angular distance (1 / 0.5 / 0.25 / 0°) is
the edge's sun angle. --terrain-shadows=on|off is the A/B lever; the default is on.
Placement: Effects/TerrainShadow.cs beside OceanMask.cs, the pure compute in its own file
like OceanMaskRaster.cs, a csky_terrain_shadow.gdshaderinc.
Steps
The height raster and the sweep as engine-free compute, with a CSVM.Tests unit: a ramp and
a wall give the exact shadow line and blocker distance.
The bake at world build for every distinct zone orientation, the globals, the rebind on the
zone apply, --terrain-shadows=on|off, and --dump-terrain-shadow= writing the PNG.
light() on the world-lit and water-lit arms (SceneBuilder) and on OceanShader, the
factor on the directional light only.
One --run-tests suite over the built world: a C3 waterline texel under the cliff reads
shaded and an open-sea texel lit, through the published texture.
The complete RunTests.ps1; the Enhanced goldens that move (c1-lake-enhanced and
c1b-ocean-enhanced at least) re-pinned in the landing; the architecture entries, the
docs/cli.md bullets, and one line in docs/org/shadows.md naming this remake-only.
⚠ Traps
Do not put the terrain back into the sun's shadow maps to get this: the banding is Godot's
single-depth filter compare against the PCSS search disc, and no bias, atlas size, filter rung,
sun angle or pitch cured it everywhere (git log --grep=BL-1037).
Multiply ALBEDO instead of writing a light() and the ambient dims too (black shadows under a
low sun), while the sun's glint, which is not albedo-scaled, keeps shining inside the shadow on
water.
The sweep direction is the scene sun's SUNLIGHT_ORIENTATION, never the original's
SHADOW_ANGLES, which is straight down in every shipped mission and drives only the aircraft
ground shadow.
A heightfield casts an overhang or a bridge as a solid column; accepted, since the terrain tiles
are heightfield-shaped and buildings are not in the bake.
Beyond the map edge the repeated border tiles read lit: a border mountain's shadow stops at the
edge. A known limit, at or past the fog ramp on every chapter.
The spyglass disc is lit by a shadowless copy of the sun and shows the baked terrain shadow
through the global, which matches the main view; do not read that as a leak.
Done when
Under Enhanced at any casting Shadow Quality level, the C1 cliff shades its lake and the C3 cliff
shades the waterline below it, with the glint gone inside the shadow and no banding on open
ground or water at the C3 poses --pos=-5200,120,-2250 and --pos=-5200,10,-2250.
Shadow Quality Off shows no terrain shadow and runs no bake; --terrain-shadows=off gives the
present picture at any level.
C2/MP2 and C2/MP3 show the ZONE2 sun's shadows after the zone change, with no hitch.
The unit test, the suite and the complete battery are green, the moved goldens are re-pinned,
and the project owner's look at the controls is recorded in the closing commit.
Researched and drafted with the help of an AI coding agent (Claude Code, Claude Fable 5.1); the
design decisions are the project owner's.

Deviations from the design text, each deliberate:
Casters are the meshes SceneBuilder set casting no Godot shadow (IsShadowlessGround), not every IsTerrainMesh tile: a tile carrying a building wall still casts on Godot's maps and would otherwise cast twice.
The penumbra's growth per metre of blocker distance is tan(angular) × (1 + tan²(elevation)), the slope span of the sun's disc, which is the design's distance × tan(angle) with the vertical factor that turns a horizontal width into a height.
A launch at Shadow Quality Off bakes nothing, so a level raised live shows terrain shadows from the next load only (a ponytail: comment marks it).
Evidence in the closing commit: the C3 cliff pose on and off, the C3 open-water poses --pos=-5200,120,-2250 and --pos=-5200,10,-2250 with no banding and the glint intact, and the bake timings (C3 2048², 178 to 372 ms; C1 1536², 297 ms).

Fixed. Under Enhanced the terrain's sun occlusion is baked at the world build into a shadow-height texture (TerrainShadowRaster: the ground height at 4 m texel centres, then one sweep along the sun's azimuth carrying the grazing line and the blocker distance), once per distinct zone sun, and the lit arms' light() scale the directional light by it, so Godot's shadow maps compose on top and the ambient stays. The ground stays out of the shadow maps, as the trap requires. The switch follows Shadow Quality, --terrain-shadows=on|off is the lever, --terrain-shadow-cell= the texel.
What settled it: the C1 lake and the C3 waterline read shaded in the goldens and in the graphics-terrain-shadow suite (the cliff's waterline texel at sun share under 0.05, the open sea over 0.99, Original back on the lit defaults); the C3 open-water poses --pos=-5200,120,-2250 and --pos=-5200,10,-2250 show no banding and keep the glint at 4 m texels under the aimed sun; a C2 MP2 run forced to zone 2 bakes both zone suns at the build (zone 1 aimed at its dome's sun at -26/166, zone 2 its authored -25/90, since dome_zone2 draws no sun card), so the zone change rebinds and bakes nothing; Shadow Quality Off bakes nothing and --terrain-shadows=off draws the picture without the shade. The owner judged the look at the controls in C1B and C3.
The owner's decisions on the way: the Enhanced light aims at the zone's dome sun or moon card, so the baked shadow and the visible moon agree (Original keeps the authored SUNLIGHT_ORIENTATION); the texel is 4 m, not the 8 m above, at 64 MB and under a second per zone sun over C3; the flat sea tiles stay casters, so a swell trough under a low moon reads shaded, and the ocean shader reads the shade at a point that fades to sea level with the waves; the ocean grid's first band ends at 1400 m so no swell cuts at a band step (+0.11 ms on a sea-filled 1080p frame); a 768-segment grid measured +0.40 ms and was declined; the shadow-map distance stays as it is.
Limits that do not reopen it: a heightfield casts an overhang as a column; the repeated border tiles read lit past the map edge; a Shadow Quality level raised live from Off shows terrain shadows from the next load; the line height is a half float, 0.5 m steps above 512 m; the off lever keeps the aimed light, a look choice independent of the bake.
Verification: the complete battery on the merged tree: 7896 units passed and 6 skipped, 632 engine suites passed and 2 skipped with engine errors clean, 38 goldens regenerated (six Enhanced shots moved: c1-lake, c5-city-night, c1-rocket-hit, c1-cockpit, c1-cockpit-xr-sim, c1b-ocean) and hash-identical on a following check.

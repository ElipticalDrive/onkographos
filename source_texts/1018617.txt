#161 is merged; this is now on main.
Summary
Every lit shader hard-coded its ambient: 0.08 in lit_shadow and the textured PBR shader, and 0.35 as the terrain's shading floor. A world lit more softly or more darkly had no way to say so. A private consumer pack's world, for one, is authored with a 50% ambient.
SceneLight.ambient on the directional light (Light.ambient at runtime) is how much of a surface's colour shows with no direct light, above 0 and up to 1. Scene validation keeps it in (0, 1].
It travels in the scene light payload's lightColor.w, a lane that was unused until now. 0 there means unset.
lit_shadow, including its instanced and skinned forms, and the textured shader use it when it's set and their own 0.08 otherwise.
The terrain feature writes it into the terrain's ambient lane in place of 0.35, so base and layered terrain both follow it.
Unset, every shader keeps its own value, so existing scenes render as before.
The simple instanced, skinned and skinned_textured variants aren't changed. They also hard-code their light direction, so they don't take the scene's light at all.
Tests
Parity, aSceneAmbientSetsTheTexturedShadeOnBothBackends: a mid-grey texture under scene ambient 0.5 reads 93, which is 255 · ((128/255)^2.2 · 0.5)^(1/2.2), on Vulkan and WebGPU. With the default it reads 41 (fix(render): keep textured meshes' shade out of the sun #161's test), so a lost ambient fails.
Vulkan, aSceneAmbientLiftsTheTerrainsShade: terrain's shadowed patch is under 60% of the lit patch by default, and over 75% at ambient 0.8.
Packing: ambient lands in lightColor.w, and 0 when unset.
Scene:
the lighting compiler carries the sun's ambient into the frame, or null;
it survives a save/load round trip;
0, negative, above-1 and NaN values are rejected.
desktopTest passes for passes (121), scene3d (133), scene runtime, parity (16), shader-pack, shader-compiler, Vulkan, WebGPU and the terrain-layers kit.

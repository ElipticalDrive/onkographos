Parent
M1a: Walk the Planet, part of the Vertical-slice spec.
What to build
Extend EditOp to the full M1 tool set: dig and place with sphere, cube and cylinder Brushes from 1 Voxel up to ~4 m, smooth/flatten (flatten against the local tangent plane), placed shapes aligned to the local up at the cursor, snapped to 45°, then voxelised into the global grid, with a world-grid toggle. Server limits per player: ~20 ops/s, ~8 m³/s, radius ≤ 2 m, reach ≤ 8 m. A ghost Brush preview at the cursor.
Read first: Choose M1 Materials & Edit tools, ADR 0003.
Acceptance criteria
Every shape and mode is integer-rasterised and deterministic (in the determinism matrix), with client prediction matching the server through the loopback
Placing a wall on the side of the Planet stands upright relative to the local up (tested at several points)
Server rejects ops over the limits, and the client rolls back
EditOp stays a small fixed-size message (size tested)
Ghost preview shows the exact Voxels that will change
Blocked by
Material table from a data file #42

Landed in PR #110 (merge 2b1d9ff). CI is green across the determinism matrix.
What it does:
Dig, Place, Smooth and Flatten with sphere, cube and cylinder Brushes.
Up is exactly radial at the cursor, with the rotation about it snapped in 45° steps (EditOp is 24 bytes).
All rasterisation is integer-exact; the re-review checked 368M Voxels against exact geometry, with no mismatches.
Limits (the dev's decisions):
20 ops/s, and 8 m³/s of Voxels changed (72k burst).
A per-player CPU budget for Voxels examined.
A round-robin per-tick cap of 2× one maximum Brush.
Dig and Place up to r 2 m; Smooth and Flatten up to r 1 m; reach 8 m.
The client mirrors the budgets, refunds them on rejection, and greys out the ghost.
Ghost: it shows exactly the Voxels that will change, and re-plans only when the op changes.
Plan cost: plan reads Edits, then the Ground, then a brick cache, then the generator. Worst Edit about 4.8 ms cold at maximum reach, otherwise ≤ 1.2 ms. Worst tick about 9.6 ms (two cold maximum Brushes).
Not verified: how the ghost looks on a real GPU. The reach bypass through a client-chosen spawn is #90.

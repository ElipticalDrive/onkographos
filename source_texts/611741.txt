First of a stack of four PRs (this one → viewports → picker tests → CPU work).
What
ImGui text as signed distance fields. A FreeType font loader bakes glyphs as SDFs at a fixed resolution (from the glyph bitmap, which avoids the speckles FreeType's outline-based generator leaves along thin strokes), the renderer handles ImGui's dynamic textures, and gui.slang turns the distance into coverage per pixel. Text stays sharp at any zoom, including the frame graph editor's canvas.
Frame graph node dropdowns work. A popup opened inside a node is placed and hit-tested in the canvas' zoomed space, and suspending the canvas inside a node loses what the node drew. In the editor a combo now draws only its button; the list is drawn after the nodes at screen position, scaled to the zoom.
ASan builds start. new (std::nothrow) was served by ASan's allocator and freed by our header-stripping operator delete; the nothrow forms are defined now.
Testing
Headless Debug runs of every game, in editor and player mode, with validation: clean.
Frame graph combos (LUT, MSAA, cluster grid, occlusion test) exercised by hand.

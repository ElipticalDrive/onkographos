No. Voxelization in vacuum is required to work with the stock game's "occluded" settings for enclosed RCS thrusters, engines, etc. Not voxelizing would result in similar behavior as #67 and there isn't anything I can do to fix this.
Only option is to ignore certain animations, but even then, I can't decide which animations to ignore and which to consider. Under those circumstances, I have to take the most conservative options, which is voxelize everything.

The hero was the only cinematic part of the page, and everything after it felt flat. This PR gives the whole page one shared visual system.
Voxel world (replaces the hero-only PixelField)
One fixed layer for the whole page. A single WebGL layer sits behind every section, and the same voxels form a different 3D shape for each chapter:

Transitions: crossing a chapter boundary bursts the voxels into particles that reassemble into the next shape. Each chapter has its own colour ramp, the shape sits beside the content, and the camera follows the pointer.
Performance:
one instanced draw call, with the morph, burst and intro done in the vertex shader;
chapter boundaries are re-measured as lazy-loaded sections arrive;
slow devices without GPU acceleration fall back to rendering only on scroll;
nothing renders without WebGL;
the world is dimmer on phones.
Reduced motion: shapes swap without the burst and render only on scroll.
Kinetic type and motion
Section headings: titles slide up from a mask, and a giant outlined chapter word drifts sideways behind every heading as you scroll.
Marquee bands: two bands (after About and after Projects) speed up and skew with scroll velocity.
PointerFX: one delegated pointer listener gives cards a cursor spotlight. Cards whose transform framer-motion doesn't control also get a subtle 3D tilt, which is off under reduced motion and on touch screens.
Small details: About stats count up, and a glowing line draws down the experience timeline as you scroll.
Verification
tsc, lint and build are clean.
axe: 0 violations in dark and light.
QA at 1440, 820 and 360 px: no console or network errors, no horizontal page scroll (the tilted marquee bands bleed past the edges by design and are clipped), all anchors resolve, and no errors under reduced motion. The simulation, carousel, flashcards and theme toggle still work.
Screenshots taken of every chapter in dark mode and a selection in light mode, a mid-transition burst, the marquee, the hover spotlight and tilt, and phone views.

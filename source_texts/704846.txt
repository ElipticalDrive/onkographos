Completely new tiling architecture for the zraster software rasteriser supporting:
tri3,tri6,quad4,quad8,quad9 elements
nodal interpolation shading
texture sample shading with a variety of high order filters: cubic (Catmull-Rom, Mitchell-Netravali, BSplin), quintic (BSpline), Lancsoz (Lancsoz3)
Configurable SIMD options for accelerating the raster loop

Why
Lot P4 of the fluids parent: the SHOULD and NICE items. They are delivered one by one, each on the maintainer's decision.
To do
Each item gets its own pull request, and each respects the parent's rules and budgets.
SHOULD
Volumetric smoke (WebGPU), as retained by S3. WebGL2 falls back to flipbooks with the same API.
Underwater view: fog, tint and the waterline when the camera goes under the surface.
SSR reflections in high, WebGPU only.
Caustics from a projected texture generated by our tool.
VAT and flipbook player for pre-computed liquids generated by our own tool. It runs on both renderers at almost no cost.
Wind on foliage, through the wind API frozen in P2.
NICE
FFT ocean (Tessendorf) in WebGPU compute.
Fire spreading on a coarse CPU grid, with flammability per material.
Interactive liquids (WebGPU)
PBF in WGSL compute: at most 32k particles in a bounded volume, a spatial-hash neighbour grid, and 2–4 constraint iterations.
Screen-space fluid rendering at half resolution: a depth splat, a bilateral blur, rebuilt normals, and thickness for refraction and absorption. No marching cubes.
Jolt bodies are kinematic colliders. The force back on a body is aggregated and read asynchronously, 1–2 frames late, and only when the object asks for it.
WebGL2 falls back to VAT, or declares the feature unavailable.
Example: the gallery entry a-fluid-of-particles.
Proof
For each item: its budget respected at the bench, its example, its docs.

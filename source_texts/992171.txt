After #2019 textured the terrain, paths, roads and city ground were the flat, hard-edged shapes left in every outdoor game.
Shared shader: noise and micro-normal GLSL moved into terrain/terrainSurfaceShader.ts, so terrain and ribbons compile the same jgSurface(). terrainSurfaces.ts gains terrainSurfaceClassIndex and terrainSurfaceRoughness.
terrain/surfaceRibbonMaterial.ts (createSurfaceRibbonMaterial, useSurfaceRibbonMaterial, exported from @jgengine/shell/terrain):
The surface is picked by name, and the authored colour is kept.
Soft ground gets a trodden centre, wheel ruts and darker shoulders.
Pavement gets aggregate, patches, a partial crack network and tyre lanes.
Soft edges fade with noise around the authored edge, and the ribbon is built wider so the authored width still reads.
resolvePathSurface takes meta.surface, then path kind (road paves), then dirt.
Adopters:
authored paths (scene/AuthoredScene.tsx)
road() asphalt, curbs, sidewalks and junctions (RoadRibbons.tsx, RoadJunctions.tsx)
city studio ground layers (CityRenderer.tsx)
Core: new road({ surface }) option.
Tiers: low takes the cheap path from terrainQuality.
jgengine-world reference; changes/road-surface-detail.md.
Opt-outs:
one path: meta.surfaceDetail: false, with meta.edgeSoftness (0 = hard) and meta.wear (0–1)
all paths: pathSurfaceDetail={false}
a road: road({ surface: false })
a city: meta.surfaceDetail: false
Verification

core world: 1124 pass.

Perf: same mesh and draw count; materials swap. Soft-edged paths blend over terrain (one transparent mesh per path).
Not covered: Harbor Heat captures timed out (before 4–10× faster headless captures: llvmpipe renderer and --quality draft #2022's faster renderer); some Ember city-ground edges are still straight where the city's ground geometry ends opaque. That belongs in the core city ground builder (follow-up).

Reference for a fast, simple ray-casting algorithm for tiled/voxel worlds - could be relevant anywhere Terasology needs voxel-grid ray traversal (block picking, line-of-sight, lighting, etc.).
Video walkthrough: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NbSee-XM7WA (javidx9, "Super Fast Ray Casting in Tiled Worlds using DDA")
Reference implementation: https://github.com/Victorique-GOSICK/Voxel-3D-DDA/tree/master/Voxel-3D
Source paper (surprisingly readable): A Fast Voxel Traversal Algorithm for Ray Tracing (Amanatides & Woo)

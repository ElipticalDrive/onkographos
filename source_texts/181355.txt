An implicit moving-least-squares immersed boundary method for high ...
Jun 1, 2025In this work, an implicit moving-least-squares immersed boundary method (MLS-IBM) is proposed to accurately enforce the velocity boundary condition on immersed objects. This method effectively eliminates errors induced by the inequality between the interpolation and spreading operations, while preserving the conservation of the force and torque.

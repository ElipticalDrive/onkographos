find_intersections: a surface's self-intersections, or two surfaces'
interpenetration, by the exact orient3d/orient2d triangle-triangle test
over a bucket grid of candidate pairs (detail/tri_pairs), so every hit is
exact. On one surface, triangles sharing corners (bitwise-equal
coordinates) count only when they meet beyond them
(tri_tri_intersect_unshared). Returns the sorted cell pairs, the crossing
segments as a line mesh (tri_tri_segment) and intersections:count per
cell; coplanar overlaps are counted without a segment.
thickness: wall thickness (inward) or clearance (outward, to the same
or a second surface) per facet, by a ray from each cell's centroid along
its normal through a TriBvh. The ray-triangle kernel is factored out of
tri_bvh_ray once (detail::ray_triangle) with a filtered cast beside it;
tri_bvh_ray's behaviour is unchanged.
On every surface: Python, C (mio_find_intersections with an opaque
result, mio_thickness), Fortran, Julia, R, WASM, both CLIs
(find-intersections, thickness), MCP tools and FindIntersections /
Thickness pipeline steps. New option structs are pure ABI-36 additions,
pinned in test_abi_layout.cpp and recorded in doc/abi_reviews.md.
Docs: doc/find_intersections.md, doc/thickness.md, the binding and tool
pages, precondition pointers in repair/shrinkwrap/remesh_volume/
tetrahedralize, the roadmap (3.1.2 and 3.1.4 removed) and its map,
CHANGELOG, README; notebook 47.
Fortran, Julia, R and WASM code is not compiled here (no toolchains).

Summary
Overhauls the DGRP sprite-depth to 3D mesh reconstruction to fix the terraced, blobby, and scattered-triangle artifacts of the original. The shipping path is the per-view reconstruction; volumetric fusion is included behind an off-by-default flag for future work.
Per-view path (active)
DepthConditioner - monotone edge-aware bilateral denoise/de-terrace, isolated invalid z-pixel inpaint, and a validity mask. Removes 8-bit terracing and noise without opening holes.
Island culling - removes scattered fringe triangles after triangulation by dropping small disconnected components, so solid surfaces stay whole while foliage scatter goes away.
Gentler decimation - aggressiveness 3.5->2.5, Quality default ->2, so thin parts (e.g. the mailbox post) keep their shape instead of faceting.
SmoothNormalsAcrossGeoms - welds normals of near-coincident vertices across the 4 overlapping rotation reliefs, fixing the "differently shining" shading patchwork on surfaces seen by multiple rotations (e.g. the trash-can lid).
New reconstruction params (DepthConditioning, DepthFilterStrength, DepthDiscontinuity, Quality) with versioned FSOR serialization; CURRENT_RECONSTRUCT bumped so cached meshes regenerate.
Volumetric fusion (experimental, DGRPRCParams.Fusion, default off)
DGRP3DFusion + MarchingTets - TSDF / depth-space visual-hull fusion of the 4 rotation depth maps into a single watertight mesh via marching tetrahedra (Freudenthal subdivision), with per-triangle best-view texture projection and double-sided rendering. Produces smoother silhouettes than per-view, but has unresolved gaps near object tops; retained behind the flag for a future, instrumented attempt.
Testing
Verified in-game (-3d) on the reference street objects - mailbox, post, trash can, and plant all render cleanly with correct shapes and consistent shading. See docs/MESH_RECONSTRUCTION_PLAN.md for the full design and the fusion post-mortem.

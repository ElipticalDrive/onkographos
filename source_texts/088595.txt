Tensor decomposition
Tensor: A tensor is a multidimensional array. also known as d-way or d-order array. Special tensor: zero-way tensor -> scalar, one-way tensor -> vector, two-way tensor -> matrix.
Tensor decomposition: Factorization of d-way tensor to simpler principle components.

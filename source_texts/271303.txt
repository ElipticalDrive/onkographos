I've chosen the Bitonic sort because it was the best working example I
could find then. All this work is actually a practical part of my M.Sc
final project, so due to time constraints
I preferred to take the best working examples as building blocks for
algorithms. Since I use the same sorting algorithm for all algorithms that
I compare, it won't affect the results of comparison, but may affect the
overall performance.

A large move could give coarse terrain overlaps the finest spacing of an unrelated near tile, creating roughly 67 million samples per swap group and multi-gigabyte temporary work. Sample each overlapping pair at its own finest spacing. Build grids are aligned dyadic checkerboard triangulations, so their difference is affine on the finer triangles and extrema remain at finer-grid vertices. The real 0→50 km selection regression bounds each sampled grid to 65×65 instead of allowing a 4097×4097 matrix.
Seam inpainting also converted an entire sRGB tile/margin to linear colour only to sample a narrow strip. Apply the identical LUT to its four interpolation taps instead. Preserve the interpolation order and exact output bytes, including supplied/absent margins, clamping, saturated pixels, smooth tiles and unchanged pixels.

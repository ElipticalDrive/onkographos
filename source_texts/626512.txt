SDF Rounding
What?
Adds a rounding parameter to Box primitives to round their edges
How?
By subtracting the rounding from the HalfExtent and the returned distance. This results in an SDF being rounded (which doesn't affect Sphere or Line anyways, as they are already perfectly round shapes)
Reviewer Steps
Check that only Boxes will let you access smoothing
Verify that smoothing works
Verify that bricks are correctly allocated
Verify that smoothing isn't influenced by the GPU scale

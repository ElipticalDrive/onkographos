Gaia streams from R2 as one index object plus content-hashed chunk objects into a fixed-slot GPU pool, drawn by the existing GPU cut. SKST v1 keeps loading behind the source row's format switch, so the v1/v2 A/B runs on one renderer.
Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-chunked-star-octree-design.md · Plan: docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-11-chunked-star-octree.md · builds on preps #871 #872 #873.
What changed
Container (src/data/octree/): SKOI index + SKOC chunks, JSON header after the magic, 24 B node rows, columnar records with affine dequantisation. The index header is the chunk manifest; a chunk lists its roots (multi-root packing in Morton order under a 2,048-row bound and the 32,768-record cap).
Bake (tools/octree/, tools/stars/buildStars.ts): brightness stratification L(M) with flux-conserving blobs, capacity-bounded chunking, one bake per catalog; the per-tier gzip search is gone. Gaia: 975 chunks, index 3,561 rows / 344k records / 2.95 MB.
Runtime: both formats decode to one StarOctree; the cut draws own records and blobs over a pool with per-slot live-row counts; subtrees of at most 64 records fold to points; a CPU planner (camera/tier change or every 4th frame while unsettled) requests chunks through createTileStreamSubsystem<StarChunk, SlotPool>; picks name a catalog-global record ordinal and snapshot the slot table at the pick draw.
Deploy: two DATA_FAMILIES rows, one bulk gzip group, fetch-data pulls the chunks an index lists.
v1 symbols take the Skst mark; the v1 reader and TS tree builder stay for the A/B (deletion candidates at feature-done).

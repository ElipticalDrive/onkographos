What changed
Add lightweight procedural shading for asphalt roads and terrain to break up flat, uniform colors.
Keep the effect in a small shader compatible with Godot's Compatibility renderer, with no downloaded assets or added dependencies.
Enable the material pass through a small autoload that waits for the generated world surfaces and applies materials by node name.
Validation
Android export CI must pass before merging.
This is an incremental material improvement, not a claim of photorealistic graphics; later passes can add proper textures, detailed buildings, vegetation, and vehicle assets.

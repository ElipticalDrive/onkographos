Abstract
Every day, a huge figure of medical face images are created by dermatologist and medical imaging center for research, surgical, and acne diagnostics. All of such images are kept for future remark and citation of the patients consequently, such medical images are required to endure the compression process prior to its storage. Acne face images have several parts which are having more information than the supplementary part of face called the region of interest (ROI). The lossless compression approach is essential in archival and communiqué of remedial images and it is found that discrete wavelet transform is used in this rationale. In this paper, a new algorithm is anticipated to choose automatically a part or patch of an image from the whole image which contains more information and is to be sent uncompressed.

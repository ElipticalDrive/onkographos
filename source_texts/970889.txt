D497 listed bloom as still to follow. This settles it from the game's own files. The game was never run.
What the game does (tools/game-bloom.py, read-only; numbers only, nothing from the game in git)
The Rendering/Volume profile's Bloom has threshold 1.5, intensity 50, scatter 0.55, clamp 2, HQ filtering, Gaussian, half resolution, 6 levels, no dirt. BloomSetting turns it on at Medium, High (the default) and Ultra.
HDR is on: the URP asset has m_SupportsHDR 1 (B10G11R11), and the camera has m_HDR 1 with post-processing on. So bloom does run in play.
URP uses the threshold in gamma (GammaToLinearSpace: 2.54 linear) with a soft knee from 1.27. Only a pixel brighter than display 1.11 before the grade (240 of 255 after it) feeds the bloom. The clamp of 2 is exactly where the post chain saturates.
The game uses bloom for its lights: EnvironmentURP's _BLOOM_ENABLED variant raises a lit building's lighting map to 2.
The pixels. The tool runs URP's Gaussian bloom at these values on frames, as an upper bound.
On the probe's 260 day-lit start shots, at most 0.22 % of a frame rises by 2 luma or more (0.12 % by 4). The lift sits round the start's marker and the sunlit tips of crowns. Land and water stay well under the knee.
On 32 of our frames (4 maps; Standard and High) it would lift up to 3.8 % of a frame by 2 luma. All of it sits round places where our colours outrun the game's: the start's near-white model (+80 to +113 in High; the game's marker is straw and gold), High's blueberries (+53) and the ruins' lit panels (+13).
Decision recorded. D497 is amended and docs/progress/3d-view.md updated: bloom is not drawn, and no look gains a screen pass. The next step is to bring those colours to the game's with the pixel judge. Once they match, bloom would lift about a fifth of a percent of a frame.
Falls short. This is not the brief's "never shows". Bloom does act in play, on specks. An eruption's lava (ours, an emission at 3.2) would bloom under the game's values and stays without it. Planning may want either point decided differently, so this PR is labelled hold.
Checks: typecheck, and the doc-reading unit tests (mergedecisions, retired-terms, look-readable).

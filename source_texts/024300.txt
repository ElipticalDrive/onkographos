Does anyone know how to sort this weird effect? The sides of the volume we're trying to render seem artificially hazy. I'm running it on 2014 MacBook Pro, Intel Iris 1536 MB GPU, Yosemite v 10.10.2 (14C1514). I've heard that this is only a problem on machines running OS X, and it doesn't appear on Windows machines.
I've also noticed it in some other places e.g. Leberba

I think we've cornered this behaviour now. All artefacts (hazy sides, concentric rings, and separately visible layers) disappear when we switch from interpolation to nearest neighbour sampling i.e. by adding this line texture.minFilter = THREE.NearestFilter; to our THREE.js code. Incidentally, this drastically slows down the rendering, which we found very surprising. Isn't the shader doing far fewer texture lookups now?
– nrob
May 8 at 14:07
it seems that the artefacts are caused by the mip mapping in the texture lookup. Anyone know why this might be OS dependent? Or how to sort it? we'd like to keep using mip mapping.

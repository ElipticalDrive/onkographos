Closes #62.
Generates free-standing boulders and cliff-edge outcrops as ordinary terrain: they remain fixed, diggable, material-aware, and part of the existing collision and streaming system.
The visual revision replaces repeated interlocking blocks with independently varied fracture faces, chipped shoulders, shallow seams and embedded bases. Stone now combines CC0 mineral diffuse/normal/roughness maps with continuous weathering and smoothly distorted texture coordinates; the former outlined polygon pattern is removed. This also fixes tree material selection beside culled terrain and updates the lake regression to recognize generated water beneath rock overhangs.
Draft: visual acceptance is still pending. Do not merge.

Capture gallery and reproduction instructions. These are unretouched native-app captures, including a closer view of each feature, on an M1 Pro at 10:00 lighting with procedural ground enabled. Real-GPU procedural repetition test passed: rock correlation 0.406, below its 0.5 limit.
Paired shader benchmark completed on Apple M1 Pro/Metal. At 4096×2524, full-screen stone opaque GPU time rose 11.84 → 15.11 ms (+28%); grass/soil rose about 5%/4%. Raw paired results and limitations. This is a documented quality/cost tradeoff, not a gameplay-FPS claim.
The task retains one patch bump, rebased to 0.4.2 atop main's 0.4.1. No GPU buffer layout change. Texture provenance and CC0 terms are included beside the assets.

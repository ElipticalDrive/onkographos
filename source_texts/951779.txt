Second stage of mesh → TSDF (S1), stacked on #81 (S0, the block allocation). tsdf::MeshIntegrator writes a triangle mesh's truncated distance field into a grid's tsdf and weight, in one of two modes. The 2026-09-27 decision records it.
Two modes
Signed: ±distance, signed by the closest triangle's face normal. This is the research codec's rule, meant for a closed, outward-wound mesh. Nothing about the mesh is checked, and no topology is built.
Shell: distance minus a half-thickness (1.5 voxels by default), for any mesh at all. This is the research codec's fallback for non-watertight input, and the unsigned offset field of Xu & Barbič (GI 2014).
Both are the prior engine's MeshToSDF::computeSigned / computeShell. The acceleration structure is not.
Why no topology
The field is the codec's input (block SDF → DCT → drop the high frequencies → quantize → entropy-code), not a reconstruction of the mesh. An earlier revision of this PR signed by angle-weighted pseudonormals, which are correct at sharp features. That cost a host topology pass (a weld, adjacency, fans, pseudonormals), about 9.7 ms of a 22.6 ms signed call, plus a set of refusals the research codec never needed. It is gone.
What Signed gives up, stated on MeshSdfMode::Signed:
Near an edge or corner whose faces meet at under 90°, the lowest-indexed of the tied faces decides the sign. On the test tetrahedron that is 973 of 10 822 observed voxels.
An open mesh grows a skirt out to trunc_dist past its rim. Use Shell for those.
An inside-out mesh comes back inside out.
No voxel measures the whole mesh
The prior engine's signed pass, like the research codec's kernel, was brute force over every triangle. Its shell pass used a dense grid over the bounding box.
Binning: mesh_bin.comp bins triangles per block, as a count dispatch and then a fill dispatch, over S0's own (triangle, candidate block) work items. The count pass records each item's slot and each binned slot's coordinate. The fill replays those slots, so every bin fills exactly, and no compaction is needed.
Integrate: mesh_integrate.comp runs one thread per voxel over its block's bin. The write is split into dispatches of at most kMaxDispatchBinEntries (2^20) bin entries, and a bin past kMaxBinTriangles (2^16) is refused.
Shared decomposition: both halves are shared with allocate_from_triangles: the host count (volume::triangle_candidate_offsets) and the GLSL decode and band test (triangle_candidates.glsl). So the blocks allocated and the blocks binned cannot drift apart.
Missing blocks: a band block that isn't allocated is refused before anything is written.
Ties break on the triangle index, so the same mesh writes the same bytes, and a soup writes the bytes of its indexed mesh.
What stays on the host is two O(n) scans, and no topology:
triangle_candidate_offsets: the per-triangle count and prefix sum, plus the index bounds check.
The per-slot prefix sum after the count pass.
A GPU scan primitive would retire both.
What is written
Every voxel of every band block is overwritten, not blended. Voxels within trunc_dist get weight 1; every other voxel gets tsdf = 0, weight = 0, the codec inverse's fresh zeros, so a mesh-derived grid reads like a decoded frame. color, and blocks outside the band, are untouched.

Tests
recon_tsdf_mesh_integrate checks every voxel of every written block against a brute-force host reference. The reference uses an independent closest point, and the winding number for inside/outside. It checks the sign wherever the faces tied for nearest agree, and the distance everywhere. It covers:
a sharp tetrahedron
a dented cube (concave edges and a concave vertex)
an open quad, whose field runs past its rim
a triangle 0.87 mm across (no size threshold in the closest point)
a metre-square sheet of 320 000 triangles, whose write splits across dispatches
a soup, including negated zeros, which must write the same bytes as its indexed mesh

the move rules

Review fixes
A code review returned 14 findings. 7 are fixed, 4 went with the topology pass, 2 are fixed in part and 1 is documented. DECISIONS.md records each under the 2026-09-27 entry. In short:
A fill/count disagreement could leave a bin short, and the integrate pass would then read an unwritten entry as a triangle index. The fill now replays the count pass's recorded slots.
Nothing bounded a bin's length or a dispatch's size. Both now have named ceilings.
The closest point collapsed onto a corner for triangles under ~1 mm. The absolute 1e-12 guard is now against zero, with the barycentrics clamped.
Four signed-topology findings were fixed, then removed with the pass:

Documented: blocks this writes aren't reported as dirty.
Also changed
refactor(volume): S0's host count and shader decode moved into the shared pieces above. No allocation changes; S0's test passes unchanged.
core: vrClosestPointOnTriangle no longer collapses sub-millimetre triangles onto a corner.

tsdf::MeshIntegrator writes a triangle mesh's truncated distance field
into a grid's tsdf and weight, in one of two modes:

- Signed: +-distance, signed by the angle-weighted pseudonormal of the
  closest feature (Baerentzen & Aanaes), not the nearest face's normal
  the prior engine used, which signs sharp edges wrongly. For a closed,
  manifold, consistently wound mesh. The host welds a soup by exact
  position and refuses non-manifold edges and vertices and flipped
  winding. A voxel nearest an open rim is left unobserved.
- Shell: distance minus a half-thickness, for any mesh. This is the
  unsigned offset field of Xu & Barbic (GI 2014).

Every voxel of every band block is overwritten: weight 1 within
trunc_dist, and the codec inverse's fresh zeros elsewhere. The band must
be allocated by allocate_from_triangles; a missing block is refused
before anything is written. Triangles are binned per block by a count
and a fill dispatch over the allocation's own work items, so no voxel
measures the whole mesh, and ties break on the triangle index so the
same mesh writes the same bytes.

The test checks every written voxel against a brute-force host
reference: an independent closest point, and the winding number for
inside/outside. 14 of 16 planted bugs are caught; the tie-break and one
equivalent mutant are not.

Addresses the fourteen findings of the PR 93 review; DECISIONS.md records
each under the 2026-09-27 entry.

- mesh_bin: the count pass records each work item's slot and each binned
  slot's coordinate, and the fill replays them, so every bin fills exactly
  and the integrate pass never reads an unwritten entry as a triangle
  index. That also drops the fill's repeated probe, the whole-map
  compaction and the host's copy of the counts.
- A bin past MeshIntegrator::kMaxBinTriangles is refused, and the write is
  split into dispatches of at most kMaxDispatchBinEntries bin entries
  (MeshIntegrateStats::dispatches).
- triangle_common: the face-interior guard is against zero, not 1e-12 m^4,
  which collapsed every sub-millimetre triangle onto a corner; the
  barycentrics are clamped onto the triangle.
- Signed topology: a zero-length double normal contributes nothing rather
  than a NaN; zero-area triangles keep their adjacency, so a sliver closing
  a T-junction is not three rim edges; a rim vertex is not a pinch; and a
  closed mesh with negative enclosed volume is refused as inside out.
- The missing-block message counts triangle-block pairs, the O(1) binding
  checks run first, triangle_candidate_offsets refuses a null mesh before
  allocating and takes a null `who`, the bins and item slots are
  device-local, and voxel_hash_map.cpp drops two dead includes.
- The class notes that the blocks it writes are not reported as dirty.

New fixtures for each fix; eight of nine planted reversions fail them.

The mesh-to-TSDF conversion feeds the codec (a block's SDF, DCT, drop the
high frequencies, quantize, entropy-code); it is not a reconstruction of the
mesh to be measured against it. The research codec produced its results with
the closest-face rule and no topology, so Signed now uses that rule too.

Removed: the host topology pass (weld, edge adjacency, fans, angle-weighted
pseudonormals in double), its refusals (non-manifold edges and vertices,
flipped winding, inside-out), MeshIntegrateStats::boundary_edges, the
pseudonormal bindings, and the feature-reporting closest point. Signed now
costs what Shell does: 12.8 ms wall against 22.6 on the 82k-triangle sphere.

What it gives up, stated on MeshSdfMode::Signed: past an edge sharper than
90 degrees the lowest-indexed of the tied faces decides the sign, an open
mesh grows a skirt past its rim, and an inside-out mesh comes back inside
out. The test checks the sign wherever the tied faces agree and the distance
everywhere.

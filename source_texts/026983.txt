Innovation that transforms the everyday
For more than 175 years we have been turning ideas into innovations. The world and people’s lives have changed a lot during these years but technology with purpose remains a main driver for improvement. Today it is our aim to exploit the power of digital transformation for a sustainable future.
An idea alone has little worth. The value of an invention lies in its practical implementation.
Werner von Siemens
Our founder Werner von Siemens still inspires us: Our mission is to create technology with purpose that helps us to cope with future challenges. We support our customers from industry, infrastructure, transport, and healthcare on their journey to digital transformation and decarbonization.

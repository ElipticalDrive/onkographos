var raycaster = new THREE.Raycaster();
raycaster.set(detectOrigin, detectDirection);

var intersects = raycaster.intersectObjects( scene.children, true )};

It appears you are using the incorrect detectDirection. The direction is in relation to the camera's position, not the position of the object in world space. If you want to cast towards the direction the camera is facing, you can simply use camera.getWorldDirection().

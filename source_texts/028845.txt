An approach which is grounded on the traditional Marching Cubes algorithm is proposed for medical three-dimensional reconstruction based on the characteristics of modern medical image. This method,combined with seed-occupying algorithm to expand isosurface,replaces cell edge interpolation by midpoint selection with DMC and orders isopoints directly in predefined cases polygons,which reduces the number of faces and solves the ambiguity problem. The experiment results show that the proposed approach makes a great improvement on topological structure,storage space and processing speed.

Description
Add support for implicit geometry modeling, where shapes are represented as level sets or signed distance fields (SDFs) rather than explicit meshes or boundary representations.
Context
Available in nTop. Implicit modeling enables robust Boolean operations, smooth blending, and resolution-independent geometry — particularly valuable for organic and lattice structures.

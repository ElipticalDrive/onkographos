# DeepFusion-NeRF(DFN)
We present DeepFusion NeRF (DFN), a Transformer-based neural radiance field framework designed for cross-scene 3D reconstruction and novel view synthesis. DFN employs a dual-transformer architecture, inspired by prior multi-stage designs, comprising a View Transformer and a Ray Transformer. To strengthen local feature encoding, we integrate a convolutional gated squeeze–excitation (GSE) module. Additionally, a composite multi-scale rotary positional encoding scheme assigns distinct positional scaling factors to individual attention heads, improving robustness to scale variations.

The full code implementation for our paper "Enhancing Neural Radiance Fields with Geometry-Aware Transformers and Depth Fusion" is currently under embargo pending peer review and acceptance.

We plan to release the complete source code, models, and detailed instructions after the paper is accepted. This repository will be updated accordingly.

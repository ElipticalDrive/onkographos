Summary
Promotes the existing fixed-function material path to an explicit bounded shading-model contract without adding a second raster path. MaterialShadingModel::BlinnPhong preserves historical behavior by default; Lambert keeps diffuse/emissive/environment-diffuse lighting while suppressing direct and environment specular semantics.
Executable behavior
adds trailing MaterialShadingModel::{BlinnPhong,Lambert} state with Blinn-Phong as the compatibility default
bounded MTL illum 1 imports Lambert and illum 2 imports Blinn-Phong; duplicate, malformed, and unsupported illumination models fail deterministically
Lambert does not sample map_Ks or map_Ns, does not emit direct Blinn-Phong highlights, and does not contribute glossy environment reflection
normal/world-position requirements are derived from the material-aware active lighting surface instead of forcing reflection-only state onto Lambert
direct raster, shared range/model preflight, and prepared construction reject unknown shading-model values before framebuffer mutation
model inspection exposes the semantic model; fingerprinting adds a versioned extension only for non-default shading models so historical default fingerprints remain stable
Regression evidence in this branch
default vs explicit Blinn-Phong compatibility
Lambert diffuse behavior with nonzero Ks and suppression of direct specular
strict MTL illum 1/2 mapping and malformed/duplicate/unsupported rejection
file-driven OBJ/MTL propagation into canonical ModelAsset plus inspection/fingerprint distinction
Lambert suppression of environment reflection with direct/prepared byte equivalence
unknown runtime model rejection before color/depth mutation
Scope
This remains the renderer's bounded teaching-space fixed-function model. It does not claim PBR, energy conservation, arbitrary MTL illumination-model conformance, metallic/roughness workflows, BRDF programmability, or GPU/API equivalence. The exact candidate must pass Ubuntu, macOS, and ASan/UBSan CI before integration.

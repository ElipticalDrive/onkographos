The camera was locked in orbit mode — always circling (0,0,0) with lookAt pinned to the sun. Mouse drag should instead rotate the view direction freely, decoupling the camera axis from the sun.
Changes
update_camera() — inverted the computation: now derives lookAt from lookFrom + direction(yaw, pitch) × LOOK_DISTANCE instead of deriving lookFrom from lookAt + spherical offset
Mouse drag — motion() still updates cam_yaw/cam_pitch, but those angles now steer where the camera looks, not where it sits; axis is no longer fixed on origin
Scroll — replaced cam_radius zoom with forward/backward translation of lookFrom along the current view vector
cam_pitch init — flipped from +0.3096 to -0.3096 to correctly represent the downward tilt of the view ray from {0, 800, 2500} toward origin (initial view is preserved)
Refactor — extracted get_direction() helper to eliminate the duplicated direction-vector calculation that appeared in update_camera() and both scroll branches
```
// Before: mouse moved the camera position around a fixed target
cam.lookFrom = lookAt + spherical_offset(yaw, pitch, radius);

// After: mouse rotates where the camera looks; position is independent
cam.lookAt = lookFrom + direction(yaw, pitch) * LOOK_DISTANCE;
```

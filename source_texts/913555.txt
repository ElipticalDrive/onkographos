Branch: frontier-voxel-engine · Tracking: #24 · Architecture: https://github.com/DrewPhi/MicroVoxelProceduralGeneration/blob/frontier-voxel-engine/frontier/ARCHITECTURE.md
Task and acceptance
Phase: Presentation | Dependencies: FV16/FV19/FV23/FV25 | Gate: G4.
Implement a coherent sky/sun/atmosphere and large-range landscape lighting, shadow coverage on heightfield + volume + forest, stable shading across all LODs. Use physically plausible aerial perspective, but no opaque fog used as a missing-geometry mask; optional haze=0 reference view mandatory. Budget realistic shadows (cascades, clipmaps, virtual-shadow-inspired pages or distance shadows where warranted) and invalidate affected pages on local edits. Avoid full-world fine-resolution shadow tracing.
Acceptance: compelling open valley and mountain flight views, correct sunlight/shadow at cave mouths/trees/buildings, no blatant shadow popping at boundaries, controlled GPU budget.
Legacy reference policy
R/B — older main/successor/viewer/src/sky.wgsl, environment.wgsl, gpu.rs may be inspected as a baseline. Do not copy its static horizon shadow map as a final production solution. Inspiration https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/virtual-shadow-maps-in-unreal-engine .
Guardrail: isolated frontier/; preserve original branches and saves; attach test evidence and benchmarks before closing the issue.

Abstract
We discuss wavelet-based application to image and data compression in this chapter. Compression is important and has different ways in which it can be implemented. We discuss the different modes of implementing compression, which includes lossy and lossless compressions. The different algorithms are discussed, and we compare each for better compression ratios including the wavelet-based EZWT, SPIHT, EBCOT, WDR, and ASWDR algorithms and the usefulness of the wavelet-based compression coding schemes.

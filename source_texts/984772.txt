Cylinder portal navigation currently normalizes the transverse direction to test closest approach, and cylinder intersections use general 3D cross products even when the cylinder is exactly aligned with the global z-axis.
Use transverse dot/cross-product comparisons in CylinderNavigationPolicy to avoid normalization and square roots. Add an exact z-axis fast path in CylinderSurface for the quadratic coefficients and longitudinal bounds check. Translated cylinders and rotations about the axis are supported; tilted cylinders retain the general calculation. Both intersection roots and the existing boundary-tolerance behavior are preserved.
Add regression coverage for transformed cylinders, near-axis-parallel rays, tangency, disc blocking, and boundary tolerances, plus a cylinder intersection/navigation microbenchmark.
Validation:
CylinderSurface, NavigationPolicy, NavigationStream and SurfaceIntersection unit suites pass.
Independent algebra comparison covers 1,000,000 random rays and 500,000 near-tangent cases. Exact rotated tangencies remain sensitive to floating-point rounding.
ITk geometry, bin factor 1: 10,000 fixed-seed tracks per mode, four library variants, two reverse-order rounds and ten measured repeats after warmup. All 1.6 million recorded propagations match the corresponding baseline exactly in recorded per-track status, counts, surface-sequence hash, path and endpoint, with no failures.
The curved-track mode uses numerical integration with EigenStepper in a uniform 2 T field. Material interactions and covariance transport are disabled.
Performance on the tested GCC 15 Release build:
Isolated aligned-cylinder intersection pairs: approximately 13% less time.
Isolated cylinder candidate selection: approximately 21–28% less time.
Full numerical propagation at bin factor 1: 36.613 to 35.965 microseconds/track CPU time (1.8% reduction).
Full straight-line propagation: 31.518 to 31.899 microseconds/track CPU time (1.2% regression). Timing variation prevents a robust overall navigation speedup claim; the isolated gains are clearer.
The surface-array filling and JSON serialization fixes are separate from this change.

You can voxelize your tree(or your point cloud) and calculate the volume of voxels that contain points. If you keep voxel resolution small, then it would give you a decent estimate. Better than convex hull at least.
You can think of it as meshing methods with voxelization. Then calculating the volume of that mesh.

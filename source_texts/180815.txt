12.6. Volume Rendering Command
The Volume Rendering feature enables you to visualize field
variables throughout the entire domain by varying the transparency
and color of the plot as a function of the variable value. For example,
you can make realistic images of smoke and analyze how it spreads
and how the smoke affects visibility.
Tip: A gradient background in the viewer can interfere with the interpretation
of volume rendering results. You may find it useful to set the viewer
background to solid white so that the gradients of the solution itself
are easier to see. You can set the viewer background with the Tools > Options > CFD-Post > Viewer > Background: Color Type option.

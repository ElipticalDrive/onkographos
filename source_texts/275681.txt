Implement Wronski/Frostbite-style frustum-aligned volumetric fog that composits over the path-traced radiance and feeds DLSS-RR.
froxel_common.slang: FroxelPush ABI + frustum ray reconstruction, exponential slice distribution, value-noise fog field, HG phase.
lighting.comp.slang: one thread per froxel fills the volume; casts amortized ray-query occlusion rays toward the dominant sun/moon from the shared sky module, producing god rays / light shafts. Submerged cameras get a density boost for underwater murk.
integrate.comp.slang: composite the volume along each view ray against the reversed-Z guide depth; 1:1 copy when disabled.
RtFroxel.java: compute pipeline + TLAS descriptor ring, resize-aware volume/history buffers, gated temporal EMA (no garbage reads on the first frame or after a resize).
RtComposite.java: create RtFroxel, allocate foggedOutput, record the two passes after the trace, and feed the fogged image to DLSS-RR/blit.
CausticaConfig.Rt.Froxel: tunable density, falloff, phase-g, albedo, noise, intensity, temporal blend, tile size, slices, far.
layout_probe + GenerateShaderRecords: probe/codegen FroxelPushData.
No local toolchain was available for a compile validation; verification is by inspection against the existing RtSkyLut/RtOverlayPipelines patterns and the world shader ABI.

Implement Wronski/Frostbite-style frustum-aligned volumetric fog that
composits over the path-traced radiance and feeds DLSS-RR.

- froxel_common.slang: FroxelPush ABI + frustum ray reconstruction,
  exponential slice distribution, value-noise fog field, HG phase.
- lighting.comp.slang: one thread per froxel fills the volume; casts
  amortized ray-query occlusion rays toward the dominant sun/moon from
  the shared sky module, producing god rays / light shafts. Submerged
  cameras get a density boost for underwater murk.
- integrate.comp.slang: composite the volume along each view ray against
  the reversed-Z guide depth; 1:1 copy when disabled.
- RtFroxel.java: compute pipeline + TLAS descriptor ring, resize-aware
  volume/history buffers, gated temporal EMA (no garbage reads on the
  first frame or after a resize).
- RtComposite.java: create RtFroxel, allocate foggedOutput, record the
  two passes after the trace, and feed the fogged image to DLSS-RR/blit.
- CausticaConfig.Rt.Froxel: tunable density, falloff, phase-g, albedo,
  noise, intensity, temporal blend, tile size, slices, far.
- layout_probe + GenerateShaderRecords: probe/codegen FroxelPushData.

No local toolchain was available for a compile validation; verification
is by inspection against the existing RtSkyLut/RtOverlayPipelines
patterns and the world shader ABI.

Terrain above terrain, step 2 (the view), part 2: sky light and sun visibility under roofs, both looks. Spec: docs/progress/3d-view.md, "Part 2".
What: src/render3d/caveLight.ts adopts Erode's light volume on the mesher's column masks (sky walked through roofed air, sun rays marched, 2 bytes a cell) as a 3D texture. The terrain shader (materials.ts caveAt / caveLightOf) reads it half a cell along the face's normal; only a face whose own air is roofed takes it (0.30 sky floor), everywhere else the baked light.ts light stands untouched. One typed hook, ShaderHooks.caveSun (High multiplies in its soft mesh shadow); no string substitutions.
Equivalence: a map without non-plain columns builds no volume. tests/unit/caveLight.test.ts builds one anyway on 7 generated maps (all themes + Any, 96²/128²/256²) and samples every quad through caveSample (the shader's steps): no point takes the volume, so the baked light is exact. Frames dev vs branch (Canyon 128 s7, Standard and High, whole + cliff, real Chrome on the RTX 2070 SUPER): 22–109 px differ by ≤2 levels, all on the moving water (diff-masked); terrain pixel-identical.
Cave maps: floors, walls and ceilings under roofs take their own air's light (bright at mouths, dim inside, sun where rays reach) instead of the fixed overhang shade / the top's sky. From above only hollowed cliff faces change (0.4–1.7% of the frame).
Relight: changed tiles, the sky walk within 8 tiles, the sun only for rays crossing the change; byte-equal to a rebuild (60 random dabs in the test). Only that box is uploaded (BoxUploads, texSubImage3D; checked in Chrome equal to a whole upload, no GL error). Dab of radius 6 at 256², light work, once in Node: cave imports 0.52–0.59 → 0.58–0.72 ms median; generated maps unchanged.
Review fixes (mouths): a cave's light carries on from the open ground at the roof line it opens by (caveMouth): the mouth cell takes that ground's sky, then −26 a cell; High's occlusion is the line's (caveSky hook); the sun starts from the open ground's at the line and fades over 0.6 tile to the cave's own, never above it (Standard marches the ray through the volume until rock or the open sky's baked shadow, caveSunMarch; High uses its soft mesh shadow; caveSunOpen hook). On the review's 28 mouths (flat grey, both looks): step across ±0.05 tile ≤ 4 levels in 54 of 56 (High 8 and 10 at two tall overhangs); b14 (176,63,7) 117→116 Standard, 80→80 High; b18 (119,90,3) sun now 4 cells deep in both looks; any rise inward is a second opening.
Memory: 3.0 MB at 256², 12.6 MB at 512², only on maps with roofed air.
Falls short: water, objects and falls under roofs keep open-sky light (part 4 / item 7); ceilings stay near-black (mortar colour). Per D484 a look reviewer should judge the sheet before merge.
Sheet: docs/sheets/3d-view-light.jpg (generated map and two official cave imports, Standard and High, 1920×1080 dark plus one 2560×1440 light, whole map and a view into a cave; before | after).

Verdict from forces play (owner) under D484: good to merge once CI is green.
What it does: caves, overhangs and their floors take their own light (sky walked through air, sun rays marched) instead of the light of the tile's top, in both looks. On generated maps nothing changes: no volume is built, and the terrain is pixel-identical (tests/unit/caveLight.test.ts).
The rule set while judging: light never steps at a cave's roof line and never rises going inward, unless a second opening lights the cave. Sun enters where it truly reaches.
Five fresh-Opus rounds, each measuring mouths on official cave maps in real Chrome (flat grey, against a ray marched through the rock voxels):
Mouths brighter than open ground.
A hard sun step, and Standard letting no sun in.
Mouths onto a drop lit from the foot of the drop.
False shade bands in Standard and a wrong mouth edge; an edit above a sun path left the march stale.
A step beside side walls (no contact shadow on cave floors).
All are fixed. Round 5 verified the edit case: a Raise above a sun path darkens the cave floor without a reload, and the live volume equals a fresh build byte for byte.
Down mouths' middles the step is ≤1 level. Beside side walls it is now 5–7 of 255 (was 16–42), with the cave side darker. I'm accepting that as continuous.
Follow-up, not this PR: Standard's baked heightfield shadow shades some open ground in front of caves where the true light is lit. It's Standard's shadow model, item 8 in docs/progress/3d-view.md; marching only near caves would just move the step.
Cost: opening a map with caves builds the volume in 0–24 ms; a stroke's relight is 0.6–0.8 ms. 3.0 MB at 256², 12.6 MB at 512².
Sheet: docs/sheets/3d-view-light.jpg.
Note: look-high.spec's "Standard look is the same after High" currently fails on dev itself, from today's merges, not this PR. A fix is being bisected separately.

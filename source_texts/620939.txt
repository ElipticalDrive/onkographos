Landed on branch feat/pose-camera (stacked on fix/layer-visibility → feat/paint-opacity).
Camera.roll was the bearing; it is now bearing, and roll is a real view-axis roll applied as GL JS rotateZ(-roll) before the pitch. The roll style option is parsed and applied by both map constructors. The far plane widens its field of view with the roll as _calculateNearFarZIfNeeded does.
CameraPose { position, altitude_meters, bearing, pitch, roll } with ViewState::set_camera_pose (port of calculateCenterFromCameraLngLatAlt: iterative Mercator scale at the center, zoom from the camera distance) and ViewState::camera_pose (the getCameraAltitude / getCameraLngLat formula). Center elevation is honoured through _distanceToCenterFromAltElevationPitch.
Pitch beyond 90 degrees works once set_max_pitch allows it (test at 120 degrees: finite depth range, camera below the center elevation as GL JS computes it).
Tests: pose round trip at bearing 35 / pitch 55 / roll 12 (position 1e-9 deg, altitude 1e-6 m), center-on-view-ray geometry, roll of 90 degrees moves east onto the vertical axis, camera ground position equals the view-matrix eye under bearing and roll, style roll parsing.
Fixtures: line-overlap (roll 0.78) 0.0226 → PASS 0.0192; text-pitch-scaling/line-half-roll (roll 180) PASS 0.0124; icon-roll-alignment/* PASS; terrain fixtures unchanged. sky/roll* and fog-*-roll still wait on #13.
Follow-up found on the way (separate fix on fix/layer-visibility): layers with layout.visibility: none were still tessellated and drawn; with opacity honoured a hidden transparent fill blanked the visible one (fill-visibility/visible). Hidden layers are now left out of tile requests, draws, the background pass and raster coverings.

Hi Authors,
Thank you for your valuable contribution to the open-source community for Gaussian splatting.
However, I have encountered issues reconstructing a mesh in the DTU dataset (btw, is there a difference between DTU provided in your drive and 2DGS?). The extracted meshes/point cloud (centroid of gaussians) appear sparse and of poor quality, and I’m unable to determine the cause. I have followed your setup and training instructions, except for the custom-mc component, as I don’t require UDF learning. Could you provide guidance or hints to resolve this issue?
btw, I also modified the files:
raster_settings = GaussianRasterizationSettings, antialasing=False, authors seem to discard this param which can not be omitted,
rendered_image, radii, invdepth = rasterizer, the rasterizer returns 3 params, but only 2 handled in your code so I add the inverse depth at last.
Could you confirm whether these modifications are correct? If so, it might be helpful to update the corresponding code.
Best,

Hi. The point cloud we used in DTU is with a little higher quality than the original version, which will better help the SDF field converge to zero-level set in the early stages. I suggest you try using the data we provide.
Regarding the issue with the rasterizer, I notice that 3DGS has updated a version of the rasterizer with depth rendering, but I used the old version of rasterizer(without rendering depth) in my environment, resulting in a mismatch. Your modifications seems correct. Thank you for the reminder. I will update a version of my code.

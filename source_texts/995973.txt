🎯 What: Optimized the core 3D and 2D Perlin/Value noise generation algorithms (Noise.noise and CLOUD_NOISE.valueNoise2D) by aggressively inlining helper functions (fade, lerp, grad, smoothstep, hash2D) and pre-calculating gradient corners.
⚠️ Why: Noise functions run inside extremely hot loops during terrain and cloud generation (e.g. Noise.fractal executes Noise.noise millions of times per frame). Function call overhead and repeated interpolations were causing the scripts/perf.mjs performance benchmark to fail its budget limits.
📊 Impact:
Noise.fractal: Performance improved significantly, reducing the median evaluation time from ~131ms to ~108ms.
CLOUD_NOISE.fbm2D: Reduced median evaluation time from ~98ms down to ~91ms.
Passed pnpm test:perf safely within their respective 110ms/135ms budgets.
All optimizations are completely mathematically equivalent to the original logic.
🔬 Measurement:
Run pnpm test:perf to verify the new timings against the strict budgets. Run pnpm test to verify no regressions in math logic output.

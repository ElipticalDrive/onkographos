Centerline endpoints were not reaching the ends of the aortic surface because auto-detect ran on the raw closed surface representation. Slicer's CreateClosedSurfaceRepresentation pads the label map by one voxel before running marching cubes, producing a watertight mesh with no open boundaries. This PR regenerates the surface from the unpadded label map to preserve open edges, then applies VMTK flow extensions to extend those boundaries before passing the surface to ExtractCenterline.
Changes
setupVMTK(): Defers cleanup of tempRefinedLabel until after flow extensions are applied, and passes it to _applyFlowExtensions(polyData, tempRefinedLabel) before setting the result as InputSurface on the VMTK parameter node
New _applyFlowExtensions(): Three-stage pipeline:
Open surface generation — Runs vtkDiscreteMarchingCubes directly on the unpadded binarized label map, transforms IJK→RAS, then smooths (vtkWindowedSincPolyDataFilter) and cleans (vtkCleanPolyData) the result to produce a surface with open boundary edges where the mask reaches the image border
vtkvmtkBoundaryReferenceSystems — identifies open boundary edges and computes normals/radii
vtkvmtkFlowExtensionsFilter — extends surface at each open boundary along boundary normals (ratio 2×, adaptive length/radius)
Graceful fallback: Returns the original closed surface if vtkvmtkComputationalGeometryPython is unavailable, no open boundaries exist, or the filter fails
```
# In setupVMTK(), after extracting closed surface from segmentation:
if polyData.GetNumberOfPoints() > 0:
    polyData = self._applyFlowExtensions(polyData, tempRefinedLabel)  # ← new
    inputSurfaceModel.SetAndObservePolyData(polyData)
```

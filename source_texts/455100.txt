Summary
```
homogeneous event candidate
  evaluate Le at the sampled wavelengths
  accumulate beta * (T_maj / pdf) * sigma_a * Le / average(r_e)
  continue with the existing absorb / scatter decision
```
This follows the pbrt-v4 wavefront medium-emission estimator. Flat IR accepts nonzero Le for homogeneous media only; unsupported heterogeneous emission continues to fail explicitly.
Evidence
Before: nonzero homogeneous Le was rejected during Flat conversion.
After: homogeneous scattering media with Le flatten successfully; Lescale doubles the stored emission spectrum as expected.

Changes
Fix the half-interval midpoint/stratified sampler: every point represents its full bin width, with no artificial minimum opacity.
Share stable f64 compositing and the MSE VJP between rendering and training; keep tensor outputs/gradient seeds f32. Retain only one ray's weights/transmittance and assemble field inputs directly.
Validate ray shape, logical layout, finite values, interval order and allocation arithmetic. Snapshot admitted datasets into owned row-major storage, and correct zero/large frame-weight sampling.
Keep physical ray directions active when field direction conditioning is disabled. Failed trainer calls preserve sampler RNG; later module updates are explicitly not an all-parameter transaction.
Add real native/Node-WASM fixtures and an independent PyTorch eager CPU/autograd control, plus CI regression coverage and explicit integration units.
Verification
The seven new public integration regressions all fail against the previously admitted main libraries and pass after the repair.
Analytic thin/opaque/constant-density cases, finite-difference VJPs, logical layouts, input rejection and fixed-seed 20-step training are covered.
Twelve source-bound local stages passed, including 134 Rust tests; eight numerical-validator and eleven archive-verifier tests also passed.
Six balanced orders retain all 324 measured / 54 preconditioning records. Rendering max difference is 2.98e-8; one-step parameter-report max difference is 9.10e-13 against the native preflight.
Complete compact evidence, hashes, negative attempts, interval timings and rerun sources are included; raw arrays and frozen workers remain local. Archive verification checks bytes/links/aggregation, not numerical replay.
The external review's bytecode-cache finding was reproduced and fixed. Plain Python and unittest discovery pass without -B; before/after records are retained and the archive verifier still rejects unexpected files.
The largest condition remains faster in PyTorch: about 2x versus native, 4.6x versus WASM. Small-shape entry-cost wins and substantial block spread are preserved, not promoted into a universal speedup.
Scope and Compatibility
Saved parameter shapes are unchanged, but historical checkpoints can render differently under corrected quadrature. Bounds are in ray-parameter units, not implicitly normalized distance. This does not add a public Python/browser NeRF API or route the separate WGSL kernels; GPU parity is not claimed. The old numerically incorrect integrator is not a valid correctness-matched speed baseline. No convergence, image-quality or universal speedup claim is made.
Do not merge until the final evidence, actual hosted CI execution and review are complete.

CloudyView renders 3D cloud condensate fields (from LES, cloud-resolving models, or synthetic cascades) at three levels of physical fidelity: glimpse computes column optical depth for a quick satellite-like view, witness ray-marches the volume on GPU with visually tuned multiple scattering, and behold path-traces it with Mitsuba 3 using Mie scattering phase functions and a physically based sky. The same WGSL ray marcher also powers Soar, a browser-based real-time fly-through.

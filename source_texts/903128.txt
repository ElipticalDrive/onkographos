Hi, thank you for releasing the code and the great research.
While reproducing the results of the paper (arXiv:2406.12286), I found that the SDF preprocessing for the Fusion360GalleryDataset seems to produce inconsistent or incorrect SDF values.
The generated SDF fields do not match the expected behavior described in the paper and sometimes appear mis-scaled or incorrectly signed.
Could you please confirm:
Whether the current SDF preprocessing code matches the one used in the paper?
If there are additional preprocessing steps required (e.g., normalization, mesh cleaning, sampling settings)?
Thanks again for your excellent work.

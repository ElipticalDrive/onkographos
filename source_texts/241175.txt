A 150 mm collectible figure generated entirely from analytic signed
distance fields, no third-party meshes involved.
sdf_lib.py: SDF primitives, smooth booleans, 2D profiles and the
decal operators (emboss/engrave) that make costume relief hug the
surface, plus a spherical projection so face details wrap the skull
instead of sticking out as flat tabs.

lasso), swept cape and display base.

decimation that is rolled back if it breaks the seal, STL export.
render_preview.py: OpenGL-free clay renders used to review the sculpt.

triangles, 56 cm3.

Reference-driven rebuild of the sculpt.

Face (head_lib.py, its own local frame):
- cranium plus a separate facial mass, so the front of the head no longer
  falls away below the eyes and leaves every feature sitting on it as a lump
- landmarks follow the classical thirds; the profile is verified against a
  target with a numeric probe rather than by eye
- eyes are a globe inside a lid shell with an almond aperture extruded along
  the line of sight and tilted down, which is what makes the upper lid
  overhang; a lens built from two spheres is flat and never cuts through
- brows, lid crease and mouth line are surface decals

Figure:
- caught mid-stride, hips and shoulders counter-rotated, head turned
- movie-style plate: sweetheart bustier with gold trim and radiating panels,
  plate skirt, bracers, over-the-knee boots with knee guard, cuff and ribs
- hair rebuilt as seven streaming locks swept back and to her left
- the cape is now a short mantle: a full length one hid the stride entirely
- decals are clipped off the arms, and costume relief is measured against a
  frozen copy of the field so errors cannot stack

Result: watertight single-body mesh, 53.9 x 54.3 x 150.0 mm, 742k triangles.

Summary
Final phase of the voxel-model-v2 spec: rendering now uses the real stored mixel fractions (#531) at tissue boundaries instead of the synthetic blur-derived ones.
rendering.partial_volume_mixel: at mixed voxels, f·S_a + (1−f)·S_b — S_a is the voxel's own rendered value (texture detail survives), S_b the second tissue's slice-mean signal; second-tissue-absent voxels stay pure. Wired at both partial_volume call sites (image path + quantitative-map PVE). pv_sigma=0 still disables PV; sidecar-less regions (Brain, synthetic fallbacks) keep the synthetic path unchanged.
Loaders carry the sidecar through the exact atlas transforms on both surfaces: desktop (build_region_mixel with per-channel straighten, region-mixin L/R flip, app handoff) and browser (web_adapter straighten/flip; worker.js fetches <Region>_mixel.npy, already bundled by feat(data): mixel partial-volume sidecars (voxel model v2, phase A) #531).
Slicing rides _get_phantom_slice(volume=...) for both channels, so crop/zoom/oblique geometry is pixel-identical to the phantom slice.
The orientation guard (the phase's known risk)
Objective, not eyeballed: loaded Spine and Pelvis mixels must have >90% of their mixed voxels on the transformed atlas's 3³ label boundaries — any flip/straighten mismatch scatters them off-boundary and fails hard.
QC
Spine T2 boundary crop: endplates and canal edges soften from hard label steps into thin anatomical gradations; interiors untouched (0.8% mean image change, concentrated at boundaries).
Test plan
TestPartialVolumeMixel (blend math, pure voxels untouched, absent-second-tissue left pure)
Flip-consistency loader tests (Spine + Pelvis + Brain-has-none)
Engine integration: sidecar engages, WM+CSF blend brightens WM on T2, pv_sigma=0 no-op
Full quartet: ruff, mypy, 2,180 + 193 GUI smoke — green

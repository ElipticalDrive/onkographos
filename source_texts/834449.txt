At d3c4737, Neuroglancer import copies raw pixels without converting their dtype. PyReconstruct/modules/backend/view/image_layer.py:389-406 then wraps every Zarr buffer as Format_Grayscale8, including uint16 buffers.
Importing a uniform (1,4,8) uint16 raw volume filled with 0x1200 succeeded. Rendering at native size produced [0,18,0,18,0,18,0,18] on each row instead of a uniform image. The display interprets individual bytes as pixels.
Done when: Zarr rendering handles supported dtypes correctly or rejects them clearly before import, with a uint16 regression test.

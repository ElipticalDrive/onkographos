FROM TOMOGRAPHY TO NEURAL FIELDS: A UNIFIED FRAMEWORK FOR 3D IMAGE RECONSTRUCTION
Book Name: ADVANCED MEDICAL IMAGE PROCESSING AND SECURE AUTHENTICATION OF NIFTI NEUROIMAGING DATA V6B3143
Authors: Anita Tandan, Dr. Vibha Dubey, Vasudha Sharma, Priyanka Chouhan
Keywords: 3D image processing, NeRF, neural rendering, CT imaging, volume rendering, Gaussian Splatting, computer vision, medical imaging, spatial intelligence, digital twins
Area/Stream: MEDICAL SCIENCES (ALLOPATHY & TRADITIONAL) / Other /
Published in: IIP Series
Volume: 6, Month:June,Year:2026
Page No.: 186-197
e-ISBN: 978-93-7020-529-1
DOI: https://www.doi.org/10.58532/nbennur3143C11
Abstract:
Three-dimensional (3D) image processing has emerged as a fundamental pillar of modern computer vision, enabling machines to perceive and reconstruct spatial structure beyond flat image representations. Traditional methods based on geometric modeling and tomographic reconstruction have been widely used in domains such as medical imaging, robotics, and computer graphics. However, these approaches often rely on explicit representations and handcrafted pipelines, limiting their flexibility and scalability.
Recent advancements in neural rendering, particularly Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF), have introduced a paradigm shift by representing 3D scenes as continuous, learnable functions optimized through differentiable rendering. This chapter presents a unified view of 3D image processing and NeRF, connecting classical tomography (e.g., CT imaging) with modern neural scene representations. Furthermore, it discusses recent extensions such as Instant-NGP and 3D Gaussian Splatting, highlighting their computational improvements and real-time capabilities.
Finally, the chapter explores emerging trends in hybrid physics–neural systems, particularly in medical imaging and real-time spatial intelligence, outlining a future where AI systems evolve from image perception to full 3D world understanding.

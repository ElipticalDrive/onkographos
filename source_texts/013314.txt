Roadmap outcome 4, mapped in #136 and profiled in #137, #138, #139 and #140. Vocabulary follows CONTEXT.md. ADR 0001, Keep streamed Voxel Volume residency separate from scene meaning, governs residency. This milestone adds ADR 0002 for detail levels.
Problem Statement
Outcome 3 lets a developer fly through and edit a streamed 16x16 world, but they can only see the neighbourhood that is materialized at full detail:
The view ends at the neighbourhood. The far plane and fog are sized to the edge of the full-detail neighbourhood (7x7 in the demo, 3x3 in qualification). Everything beyond it is missing, so the world looks like a small island floating in fog.
Full detail is the only detail. Neither Render Path can show a volume without its complete 64³ materialization, and full detail for all 256 volumes would exceed every ratified byte cap.
Whole-world Brickmap is too slow. Profile whole-world detail levels and derive selection maxima, byte caps and laptop frame time #137 measured p99 81.5 ms because each ray scanned every volume. Choose a front-to-back scene traversal for whole-world Brickmap #138 and Re-profile whole-world Brickmap and finish detail-level byte caps #139 showed that a front-to-back grid traversal fixes this (worst p99 7.48 ms), but only in a prototype.
Raster installs stall the frame. Re-profile whole-world Brickmap and finish detail-level byte caps #139 showed whole-scene Raster installs stalling the frame thread for 0.5–1.0 s, and 5 of 188 crossings took more than 2.5 s from preparation to installation.
Coarse data has no owner. Nothing produces, caches, versions or invalidates a coarse approximation of a volume. An edit to a far volume would leave any coarse copy stale.
Solution
A developer launches the streamed world and sees all of it, from anywhere. Volumes near the camera render at full detail. Rings beyond them render at 32³, 16³ and 8³, so the world is visible to its edge through fog that never hides the farthest content. Moving the camera changes each volume's level without cracks, flicker at band edges or stale coarse content. Edits to any volume, even one far away and not materialized, show up at that volume's current level. Raster and Brickmap can be switched at any point. Each meets p99 ≤ 10 ms at 1920x1080 on the RTX 3060 Laptop.
The pieces are:
Voxel Volume Detail Levels. The Voxel Frontend produces non-semantic coarse summaries (32³, 16³ and 8³) for each volume, cached per Voxel Volume Content Version. Each coarse voxel takes a direct vote over the full-detail values it covers. Summaries are rebuilt after edits, including edits to volumes that aren't resident.
One Voxel Detail Selection. An independently ordered selection gives every volume exactly one level. Its full-detail part is the Voxel Residency Selection. It drives the Voxel Frontend and both Render Paths with required and installed variants, newest-only convergence and atomic installation, as residency does today.
Policy in the application. A pure function maps the accepted camera to a detail selection. It uses centre hysteresis, Chebyshev bands, and a reset on initialization and on teleports.
Both Render Paths. Raster renders coarse levels as meshes whose skirts close the cracks between levels. Brickmap builds a coarse brickmap for each volume's selected level and traverses the volume grid front to back through a grid-to-volume table. Both prepare and install coarse levels per volume, so a crossing never rebuilds the whole scene.
Evidence. Whole-world qualification runs on the production seam. A selected-detail reference oracle is built independently from source content. A verifier checks the ratified byte caps, coarse revision correctness, zero coverage stalls, laptop p99 frame time, fog retention and detail latency.
Existing workloads keep their exact behaviour: the canonical scene, dense/sparse comparisons, raster/compute switching, the large sparse terrain and outcome 3 streamed qualification.

Implementation Decisions
Glossary and ADR
Add Voxel Volume Detail Level (one level of a volume: a rendering approximation of the same scene-space extent) and Voxel Detail Selection (the level assigned to each volume), with Required and Installed variants defined like residency. Record them through /domain-modeling.
Add ADR 0002: detail summaries belong to the Voxel Frontend, are non-semantic like residency and are cached per Voxel Volume Content Version. Render Paths derive their own data from them, so far volumes never need full materialization.
ADR 0001 still governs. Detail changes never publish a Voxel Scene Revision or a Voxel Change Set. Streamed scenes stay sparse, and streamed dense compute stays a typed rejection.
Detail levels in the Voxel Frontend
Level chain: 64³ (full detail), 32³, 16³, 8³. A volume has one level at a time. Every level spans the volume's scene-space extent, with voxels 2, 4 and 8 times larger, so level boundaries fall only on volume faces.
Downsampling: each coarse voxel votes directly over every full-detail value it covers. Any occupied value makes it solid. Its material is the most frequent occupied Voxel Material, empty values don't vote, and ties go to the lowest Voxel Material identity.
Summary cache: owned by the Voxel Frontend and keyed by scene identity, volume identity, Voxel Volume Content Version and level. Entries are immutable and shared by reference with Render Paths. The 8³ summary of every volume stays resident. 32³ and 16³ summaries are held only while a required or installed detail selection, or a reader, needs them.
Startup sweep: runs after streamed publication and before residency begins. It materializes one volume at a time, derives that volume's resident summaries, and releases the copy.
Non-resident edits: after a value-changing edit, every affected volume is marked dirty at its new content version. Rebuilds use the shared query-only copy slot and don't change full-detail residency. The rules:
Newest-only per volume, with repeated changes coalesced.
Pending work is kept for every dirty volume, and one rebuild runs at a time.
An occupied slot delays admission but never discards the work.
A rebuild completes only for the content version it was admitted for, so a superseded rebuild can't acknowledge newer content.
Resident volumes derive summaries from their materialized copy.
Detail selection API: an immutable Voxel Detail Selection value.
It has an identity, ordered independently of revisions, and assigns one level to every volume in the scene. Its full-detail volumes form a Voxel Residency Selection.
A missing volume, an unknown volume, or a selection over the configured per-level maxima is a typed rejection.
The frontend accepts a Required Voxel Detail Selection and establishes it newest-only. It reports the Installed one and hands consumers the materialized copies and summaries for that selection at a given view.
Limits: the existing residency limits (Derive streamed residency limits from a caller-chosen neighbourhood size #134) extend to per-level maxima. The materialization cap stays 2N+1: N = 49 gives 99 copies in the demo, and N = 9 gives 19 in qualification.
Selection policy in the desktop demo
A pure function of the previous detail selection and the accepted Camera State. It is frozen from Profile whole-world detail levels and derive selection maxima, byte caps and laptop frame time #137:
Centre: per axis, the centre keeps its volume while the camera is within 16 units of it, then snaps to the nominal volume.
Reset: initialization, or an accepted scene-space displacement over 64 units, resets the centre to nominal.
Bands: Chebyshev distance in volumes from the centre. Demo full detail ≤ 3, 32³ ≤ 6, 16³ ≤ 12, 8³ beyond. Qualification ≤ 1 / 2 / 4.
The demo's full-detail band remains the caller-chosen neighbourhood from Derive streamed residency limits from a caller-chosen neighbourhood size #134, and the coarse bands derive from it by doubling reach per level. Parse-time validation rejects configurations whose maxima have no ratified caps in qualification.
The far plane is D + 1, where D is the largest eye-to-corner distance to the finite scene bounds. Fog is smoothstep(0.5·D, 1.6·D) in linear space. Both replace sizing to the neighbourhood edge.
The overlay adds the Required and Installed Voxel Detail Selection identities.
Render Backend and switching
The Render Path interface gains detail selection submission. It supersedes residency submission for streamed scenes, since full detail is part of the detail selection. Unstreamed paths treat every volume as full detail and do no detail work.
The path-neutral stamp reports the Required and Installed Voxel Detail Selection identities. Full convergence requires Required to equal Installed for both revision and detail selection. Handoff mismatch gains a detail selection variant, and handoff completes only when the replacement's installed detail selection and revision equal the presenting path's required ones.
Camera coverage counts any installed level. Because every volume always has its 8³ level, an accepted camera is always covered once the initial selection installs. Coarse fallback satisfies coverage but completes no latency deadline.
Convergence is atomic across every affected selected level. Older complete, revision-correct frames stay visible while preparation runs.
Raster Render Path
Per-volume coarse artifacts are derived from summaries, keyed by content version and level, and reused across selections and unrelated edits.
Preparation and installation are incremental per volume: a crossing prepares only the volumes whose level or content changed, and installation swaps them at a fence-safe boundary without a whole-scene rebuild or a frame-thread stall. Atomicity across the affected set still holds.
Skirts are the crack solution and must qualify. The mesher already closes each volume at its boundary faces (Profile whole-world detail levels and derive selection maxima, byte caps and laptop frame time #137), and the costs ratified in Re-profile whole-world Brickmap and finish detail-level byte caps #139 include this. Skirts must cover mixed levels, volume corners and independently meshed Raster Regions, including overhangs, cavities and material boundaries. Intrusion is limited to one coarse voxel of the coarser neighbouring level. If skirts fail, stop and reopen shaping, with stitched transitions as the fallback.
Raster Region identities stay volume-local.
Compute-ray Render Path
Brickmap builds each volume's coarse brickmap from its selected level's summary.
For single-layer regular grids, the scene words carry a grid-to-volume table: origin, cell size, cell counts, then one volume index per cell (6 + 256 words, 1,048 B). Traversal is a front-to-back DDA over the volume grid with early exit. Other scenes keep the linear scan, and the GPU DDA regression and Semantic Ray agreement must still hold.
Changed volumes are installed incrementally if bundle immutability and pool-base rules allow it. Otherwise the complete bundle is rebuilt, provided the latency gate still holds.
Dense compute with a streamed scene stays a typed rejection.
Byte caps (ratified in #139, fixed)
Structure as in #118: 6 assemblies (3 owners × old/new), one raster worker, and Brickmap construction scratch over every selected volume.

They never assert on private representation structure. Allocation and copy counts are part of the external contract here, because the caps are requirements.

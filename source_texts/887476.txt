Adds decision record D-129 for M2-EX18, the temporal anti-aliasing (TAA) prototype. It changes only that file. The owner rules on it.
The record recommends not shipping TAA for 1.0. The anti-aliasing presets stay as they are: FXAA on Low and MSAA 4x from Medium up.
What the prototype measured, at 960 x 540 against a 64-sample reference:
TAA cut the shimmer of the Creek's far grass by 92% and the water's by 75% against MSAA.
It drew the Creek at 0.55 to 0.75 of the reference's sharpness, even with the camera still. MSAA draws at about 1.
Swaying grass and water smeared two to three times as much as with MSAA. Nothing tells TAA how they move.
With a still camera, thin lines that MSAA holds still crawl with TAA.
MSAA came closer to the reference than TAA in five of the six runs.
GPU cost per frame at 1920 x 1080 on the Mac:
WebGPU: about 0.3 ms in the Creek, and 1 to 2 ms in a light scene.
WebGL2: 7.5 ms in the Creek with MSAA, and 0.75 ms without MSAA. The record explains the gap.
The prototype code stays on the branch proto/m2-ex18-taa and does not merge.
This is a docs-only pull request, so it has no auto-merge.

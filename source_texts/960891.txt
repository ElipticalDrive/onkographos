Spherical maths everything else builds on (03-architecture.md §4.1).
Scope
Direction representation (Vec3 unit vector) ↔ yaw/pitch
Equirectangular pixel ↔ direction (incl. arbitrary resolutions and partial panoramas via GPano cropping fields)
Direction ↔ cube face/UV
Gnomonic (rectilinear) and stereographic (little planet) camera projections
Rotations as quaternions; rotation from direction A to B
Angular distance, spherical caps, great-circle interpolation (slerp), bounding caps of point sets
Property tests (proptest): round trips, seam, both poles; benchmarks for hot paths
Definition of done: 100 % coverage of public functions, documented conventions (axes, handedness, yaw zero direction) in the crate docs.

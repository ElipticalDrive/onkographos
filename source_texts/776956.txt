Objective
Implement a documented discrete parallel-beam forward projector for the initial 2D CT pipeline.
TDD plan
Analytical/simple-object projection tests.
Known geometry and symmetry tests.
Shape, finite-value, and deterministic tests.
Boundary and sampling behavior tests.
Design constraints
Select and document one discretization (e.g. ray-driven or pixel-driven) for this implementation.
Do not claim exact equivalence to a clinical scanner.
Keep geometry and projector separate.
Acceptance criteria
Mathematical discretization is documented.
Tests cover analytic/simple cases and edge conditions.
Output shape and orientation match the ProjectionData contract.
Numerical behavior is compared to an independent reference or analytical result.
No DICOM dependency is introduced.
Backlog ID: M1-02
Depends on: #44 (M1-01), #43 (M0-04)
Workflow: Follow the repository's Red → Green → Refactor TDD process. Add/adjust Catch2 tests before production implementation, preserve existing tests, and keep CI green.

Closes #60.
What it adds:
EditOp is 20 bytes: a mode (dig, place, smooth, flatten), a Brush (sphere, cube or cylinder, from 1 Voxel up to a 2 m radius), a centre, a Material and an alignment (local up or the world grid).
Local up is the centre's direction from the Planet's centre, snapped exactly to the nearest of the 26 cube directions, which are 45° apart. The other axes come from integer cross products.
Rasterisation is integer-only: one function, sim::plan, lists every Voxel that changes. Apply, the server's volume count and the ghost all use it.
Server limits per player: 20 ops/s and 8 m³/s (tick-counted token buckets), reach 8 m and radius 2 m. An op over a limit is rejected, and the client rolls back.
Ghost: the outline of exactly the Voxels that will change, drawn in the overlay pass.
Bound growth: placed matter above the scene's bounding ball grows it, so it isn't clipped (Clip rays to the Planet's bounding sphere #85's note).
Tests:
hand-worked counts for each shape;
three new golden_brushes_* tests in the determinism matrix;
a loopback test with two clients over every mode and shape, with rejects;
Edits 200 m from the spawn, and taking them back;
an upright wall at 8 points on the Planet;
one test per limit;
EditOp's size;
the ghost matching the Edit over all 12 mode and shape pairs;
bound growth.
Controls: 1–4 pick the mode, B the shape, [ and ] the size, - and = the Material, G switches local up and the grid, and a click applies.
Not verified: how the ghost looks on a real GPU. No shader or upload changes, so no bench.

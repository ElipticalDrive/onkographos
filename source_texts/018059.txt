Summary
Follow-up to #185: there is still no voxel structure in kornia-3d. Proposal: a sparse voxel grid plus the standard point-cloud cleaning filters, designed after Open3D and PCL, with the goal of matching their results and beating them on speed and memory.
Proposal
VoxelGrid<T>: a sparse hash keyed by integer (x, y, z), with voxel_size and origin, point <-> key conversion, and 6/18/26-neighbour iteration.
voxel_downsample(cloud, voxel_size, mode) (centroid / first / nearest-to-centre), deterministic output order.
remove_radius_outliers(cloud, radius, min_neighbours) and a voxel neighbour-count variant.
remove_statistical_outliers(cloud, k, std_ratio).
CUDA for the downsample and the neighbour count (sort-by-key hashing).
References
Open3D VoxelGrid, voxel_down_sample, remove_radius_outlier, remove_statistical_outlier.
PCL VoxelGrid, RadiusOutlierRemoval, StatisticalOutlierRemoval, octree::OctreePointCloud.
Acceptance
Parity tests against Open3D outputs on fixed clouds (checked in).
Deterministic results on CPU and CUDA.
Benches vs Open3D (and PCL where available) with numbers per op.

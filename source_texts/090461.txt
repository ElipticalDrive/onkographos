Currently, we can properly render volumes only if camera is located outside of any volume. In order to place camera inside a volume, the media list have to be properly initialized with all the surrounding media before path tracing starts.
The same stands for lights and light tracing, as well as for photon tracing.

Our raymarching code is placed into PathTracer. This means that the initial ray cannot be raymarched, since it is not processed in a PathTracer but directly in sample renderer.
The design of our renderer is clear: primary ray either hits any surface or the environment, and then the corresponding shader is evaluated. However, in case of volume rendering there is a problem here.
Let's say we implemented multiple scattering. And we got a scene where the camera is located inside a volume. Logically, the primary ray can be outscattered even before it is able to reach any surface, i.e. even before the surface shader is evaluated. But that is contradictary! Without evaluating the shader we don't even know if multiple scattering is enabled, not to mention that the parameters of raymarcher are missing.
For single scattering and homogeneous volumes we can integrate the lighting postfactum, in the very beginning of PathTracer::trace, but it is obviously a cheating.

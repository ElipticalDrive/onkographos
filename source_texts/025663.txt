The development of GPU gave a new platform for general purpose computations.First the input cube-dataset was mapped to textures.Then based on the parallelity of graphic hardware,a new algorithm for isosurface extraction from cubes was designed.At last the result isosurface was rendered to output texture.Experiment result of representation for 3D electromagnetic environment based on Cg shader programming language indicates that the method effectively lightens CPU's burden,accelerates isosurface extraction process and can be used in real-time application.

Reworks the site around current research: autonomous mobile robots that perceive with a single 360° camera and a monocular 3D Gaussian Splatting map.
Live hero simulation: a robot with one 360° camera drives a loop, incrementally builds a Gaussian-splat map, marks obstacle footprints, and shows its equirectangular camera view with labelled obstacle detections. Splats are rendered in Canvas 2D by projecting each 3D covariance through the perspective / equirectangular Jacobian (js/splatmap.js). Drag to orbit, pauses when off-screen, static frame for reduced motion.
New Research section: capture → reconstruct → perceive → navigate pipeline with illustrations, plus "why camera-only" highlights.
Hero, about, experience and toolkit copy rewritten around AMR work; new Robotics & 3D vision skill group.
Scroll progress bar, keyword ticker, paper grain texture, Open Graph tags.
Checked at 1440, 820 and 390 px in light and dark themes: no console errors, no horizontal scroll; ~6.6 ms script time per frame.

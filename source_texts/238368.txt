HyperHeight LiDAR data cubes (HHDCs) and lossless-compression results accompanying the paper on the lossless compression of noisy HyperHeight LiDAR forested landscapes data. The tensors are simulated from NEON discrete-return LiDAR of the Smithsonian Environmental Research Center (SERC), Maryland, USA, in two geometries - CASALS (16×32×128) and 1×1 (96×96×256) - and three density categories (min, med, max). The package contains 18 noise-free tensors and 999 noisy tensors generated under a per-voxel Poisson background model Y = S + Poisson(B) over a logarithmic grid of background levels B with five noise realizations each. All tensors are stored as compressed NumPy .npz files (single uint16 array arr_0). Also included are the compression results reported in the paper: 15 figures (PDF) and 6 CSV tables covering compression ratio and coding/decoding time for NPZ, bit packing, unary coding, and unary coding combined with RLE, WNC, and LZMA-style entropy coders.

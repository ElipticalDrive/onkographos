simu.affine writes the simulated image and its ground truth label onto one common grid instead of the grid of the input, which is what a training set needs. It is applied after all anatomical modifications and before the synthesis, so the T1w image is generated from the already resampled label and stays an exact function of the label it ships with.
The affine is fitted to the overall deformation field of the segmentation by least squares rather than taken from res.Affine. res.Affine only initialises the unified segmentation, and when that initial registration fails the nonlinear stage compensates for a part of the error, so the composed field is aligned while its affine part is not. simu.affine.method = 'spm' keeps the old behaviour, and .mask selects the region the fit is optimal for.
The default grid is the one the deepmriprep training scripts use, 336x384x336 voxels of 0.5mm, with the SPM matrix placing voxel (1,1,1) on the nibabel origin (-84,-120,-72)mm of their zero based index 0. A custom grid is given through dim/mat or bb/vx.
Resampling details:
The label is interpolated as the scalar PVE image and the tissue fractions are rebuilt from it. Interpolating the three fractions on their own lets a voxel become a CSF/WM mixture without GM, the one configuration a scalar label cannot represent, which shows up as spurious GM at a ventricle border.
Sinc (-5) is the default and overshoots at the hard edges of a stripped label, measured at -0.33 and +3.30 on a 0/3 step, so the result is clamped back into [0,3], or [0,4] when WMHs are simulated.
Below a label of 1 the fractions sum to less than one and that sum is kept instead of normalized, so the synthesis blends into the bias corrected image as it does at the brain boundary. Normalizing would snap the ramp to full CSF and dilate the brain by half a voxel.
The bias corrected image is carried to the new grid and handed to synthesize_from_segmentation, because its DCT basis is defined over the field of view of the input and cannot be re-evaluated on a rotated grid.
add_bias_field resamples a predefined MNI field onto the output grid, the same guard simulate_atrophy and simulate_WMHs already use.
Outputs gain the BIDS space entity and the sidecar records the fitted affine, the grid and the interpolation under SimulationParameters.AffineRegistration.

Pull request overview
This PR adds an optional affine-registration stage (simu.affine) so the simulated T1w image and its ground-truth label are written onto a shared target grid (defaulting to the deepmriprep training grid), improving dataset consistency for training workflows.
Changes:
Adds simu.affine option parsing and an affine-resampling pipeline that runs after anatomical modifications and before synthesis.
Updates synthesis and bias-field handling to support a precomputed bias-corrected image on the new grid and records affine/grid metadata in the JSON sidecar.
Documents the new feature and updates the parameter reference in README.md.

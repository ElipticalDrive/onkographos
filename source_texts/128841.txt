Adds optional HDR height fog and volumetric direct-light scattering before TAA, bloom and tone mapping. Scene settings and the rendering.fog engine switch both gate the feature.
Depth-bounded rays integrate exponential height density and up to 16 transformed box volumes with additive extinction and Henyey–Greenstein scattering. Directional, point and spot lights share the scene’s attenuation and shadow maps. Volume shadow lookups use hardware PCF at the actual sample point, without surface-normal or depth offsets that can leak light through nearby blockers. This also removes the cost of repeating the raster surface’s 16-tap soft-shadow filter at every march step.
Ray–box intervals preserve thin-volume extinction between march samples. Non-finite colors/albedos are sanitized and invalid volume transforms are rejected. The feature supports perspective, orthographic and infinite-far cameras, resizing, engine switch transitions and the shared HDR post-processing chain.
The Cornell sample now exposes --gi-demo --fog --taa --no-gi, including height fog and a local floor volume. src/Paradise.Rendering.Pbr/FOG.md documents usage, captures, profiling and quality limits.
Validation:
Final full PBR suite: 110 passed, zero failures or skips.
Fifteen focused fog tests passed on the GPU-capable local runner with zero skips: Beer–Lambert attenuation, height falloff, depth clipping, infinite far plane, thin/rotated/overlapping volumes, input validation, all three light types’ shadows, switch transitions, resizing and AA/bloom ordering.
The nearby-blocker regression failed with the surface-biased shadow lookup and passes with the volume lookup.
Core rendering suite: 140 passed on the reconciled branch; no core code changed afterward.
Profiling sample build: zero warnings and errors; 80-frame fog-on/off captures inspected.
Apple M3 Max, 640×480, 48 steps, 60 timed frames: 4.18 ms/frame with fog, 2.68 ms disabled. The previous surface-shadow lookup measured 8.36 ms with fog. These are whole-frame GPU idle-to-idle measurements, not summed per-pass timestamps.
Scope and limits: full-resolution fixed-step single scattering; up to 16 local volumes; height density and lighting sampled at segment midpoints. TAA can accumulate the fogged scene, but fog has no dedicated history or multiple scattering. Blended surfaces use opaque depth rather than separate per-layer fog distances. Wide raster soft-shadow filtering does not apply to the fog pass.

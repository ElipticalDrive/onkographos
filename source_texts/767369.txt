Yikes!
Both look wrong in some way:
In Slicer, the ribcage looks very compressed
In VolView, the ribcage looks good, but the skull is stretched.
If adjacent slices don't have the same inter-slice distance, they should not be combined into a single volume. VTK and ITK don't have means to store/render such data. We should not interpolate/subsample data in a viewer without being very explicit about it.
The discussion in this thread (https://discourse.itk.org/t/usage-of-imageseriesreader-getgdcmseriesfilenames/3806) states "The [itk] series reader just receives a list of images, looks at the distance between the first two along the z direction and assumes that this is the spacing in z for all images."
We need to see if GDCM / ITK has a flag to force splitting or returning false if a series can't form a valid 3D volume without resampling due to inconsistent inter-slice distances. I'll dig a bit.

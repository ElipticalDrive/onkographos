Issue body actions
A 64 km terrain or a scan of 50M points can't be drawn whole, and a terrain plate stops at 4,097 × 4,097 samples. Add chunks@1, a tree of chunks with bounds, a geometric error and a payload, selected for each view by projected error as a pure function of the view, with residency that loads and uploads chunks nearest first within a byte budget and changes only how long a live view settles. Canonical renders, render-back, exports and checks wait for every selected chunk, and a missing one is BLOCKED, never drawn coarser.
Epic: #219 · Design: Chunks streamed by view · Level of detail · Builder plate: terrain
Scope
chunks@1: a tree of chunks {id, bounds, error in metres, payload by hash, ≤ 8 children}, probed once: a child whose error isn't below its parent's is refused with chunks.order, a payload outside its bounds with chunks.bounds, each naming the chunk; the tree's hash covers every payload's
Payloads: meshes in the builder's output shape, height tiles of ≤ 4,097 × 4,097 samples with their cover, and point rows of position, color and size, ≤ 65,536 a chunk; readers: a mesh item, a terrain plate's heights and a 3D mark.point set
Selection, top down: a chunk draws when its error projected at its nearest point is at most lod_px or it has no children, and otherwise its children do, computed on the CPU in f64 as a pure function of the view at t, and never blended
Residency: selected chunks load from the content store, decode in the decode worker and upload nearest first within residency.bytes, each parent kept until its children arrive; a live frame draws the nearest resident ancestors and reports settling
Canonical renders, render-back, exports and checks wait for every selected chunk; a payload missing from the store makes them BLOCKED with chunk_missing, never drawn coarser
A first-party asset job, chunks.build, that splits a mesh, a heightmap or a point file into a tree by hash, with errors from the simplify job's measure
A plate whose heights come from chunks draws and has no collision; physics and navigation keep their own capped shapes
Each chunk's declared error is checked at probe against the error the simplify job's measure finds between the chunk's payload and its children's, and a chunk that declares less is refused with chunks.error, naming the chunk and both errors, so a tree's own word never chooses the detail a canonical render or a check reads
Point files: chunks.build reads PLY, PCD (ASCII, binary and binary compressed), and LAS and LAZ through PDAL, pinned as name@version, and is BLOCKED with one unblock ask without it; positions are computed in f64 and written relative to the tree's origin, the source's coordinates of its zero kept in f64; units come from the file's coordinate system, a US survey foot being 1,200 ÷ 3,937 m; and a PCD file's packed rgb is read as the bits 0x00RRGGBB, never as a number
3D Tiles: the tiles3d import kind (Imports onto existing kinds: sprite sheets, tile maps, MIDI files, layered 3D scene files and molecules, each checked before an item names it #216) walks a tileset for a declared region and error, keeping a tile whose bounds meet the region and whose parent's geometricError is above the error, and writes a chunks@1 tree by hash: glTF and b3dm content as mesh chunks with their base color maps and pnts as point rows, relative to the region's center with +Y up there; each chunk declares the error the probe measures, never the tileset's geometricError, which the receipt keeps; a selection past max_bytes, a tile that refines by ADD, or one with other content fails the job, naming it; and every tile is fetched once, at the job, through the Source block, never while a piece plays
Not in this task: voxel brick payloads (the voxels task); splat payloads; trees a logic item writes; fetching chunks over the network while a piece plays; and collision on chunked terrain
Fields
Level of detail: chunks streamed by view
Asset layout chunks@1: {payload: mesh | height_tile | points | voxel_bricks, origin: [f64, f64, f64], m = [0, 0, 0], root: chunk}, a chunk being {id: u32, bounds: [vec3, vec3], error: f32, m, payload: sha256 | none, children: [chunk] ≤ 8}
chunks: asset ID (on what reads one: a mesh item, a terrain plate's heights, a 3D mark.point set, voxels@1)
residency: {bytes: u32} (session; at most the class's GPU memory share)
Payloads: a mesh in the builder's output shape, with its own base color map by hash where its source has one; a height tile of ≤ 4,097 × 4,097 samples with its cover; point rows of position, color and size, ≤ 65,536 a chunk; voxel bricks (Voxels voxels@1)
Issues: chunks.order {chunk}, chunks.bounds {chunk}, chunks.error {chunk, declared, measured}, chunk_missing {chunk}
It can never: Choose a chunk by load order, an earlier frame or time spent; draw a coarser chunk in a canonical render, an export or a check; fetch a chunk from the network while a piece plays; exceed its bytes
Asset job: imports
tiles3d {tileset: url | asset ID, region: {center: [lat, lon] deg, radius: m}, error: m, max_bytes: u32} → a chunks@1 tree by hash (Level of detail: chunks streamed by view)
Level of detail: levels by error
On the simplify job: levels: [{ratio, max_triangles}] | auto = auto, auto: {growth: f32 = 1.5, min_gain: f32 = 0.25, max_error: f32 = 1}
Probe output: lod_errors: [f32] (each level's object-space error as a fraction of the bounds; index-only levels over one vertex buffer)
lod_px: f32, device px = 1 (on each budget class; a document may only lower it)
It can never: Be chosen by time or by an earlier frame; replace an item a check reads at full detail; emit a level whose error is lower than the level before.
Builder plate: terrain
terrain: {height: field | heightmap {sha256 (16-bit PNG), range: [m, m]}, size: [m, m] ≤ 4,096, spacing: m = 1, tile: m = 64, lod_levels: u8 ≤ 6}
heights: chunks@1 asset ID (in place of height, past the plate's size cap, a chunk tree of height tiles: Level of detail: chunks streamed by view; such a plate draws and has no collision)
cover: [{material, where: field over slope, height and noise, blend: m}] ≤ 4
It can never: Be painted or sculpted; read a heightmap from a URL; exceed 4,097 × 4,097 samples in one plate, or in one height tile of a chunk tree.
Acceptance criteria
Selection is the formula: a three-level fixture tree with errors of 8, 4 and 2 m, seen through a 60° camera at 1080p from five poses, selects at each pose exactly the chunks the formula gives in f64; flying from pose 1 to pose 5 and seeking straight to pose 5 select the same chunk IDs; and a planted selection that keeps the chunks the previous frame selected fails on the flight back to pose 1, naming the chunk
Residency changes only settling: a flyover of the 64 km terrain fixture with residency.bytes at 64 MB and at 512 MB gives equal pixel hashes on one machine at ten sampled times once settled, and the 64 MB run reports settling for longer; and a planted residency that lets a settled frame draw a resident ancestor when the budget is tight fails, naming the time and the chunk
Canonical renders wait: an export of the flyover equals its settled live frames by pixel hash; deleting one selected payload from the store makes the export BLOCKED with chunk_missing naming that chunk, with no frame written; and a planted export that draws the chunk's parent instead fails, naming the chunk
The probe: a tree whose child has an error of 5 m under a parent of 4 m is refused with chunks.order naming the child; and a payload with a vertex 1 m outside its chunk's bounds is refused with chunks.bounds naming the chunk
Points: a 50M-point scan built by chunks.build into chunks of at most 65,536 points draws as a 3D mark.point set, and at the fixture's view the drawn point count equals the count the selection formula gives; and a point chunk of 65,537 rows is refused at probe, naming it
Terrain: a 64 km heightmap at 4 m spacing (16,385 × 16,385 samples) built into height tiles draws through a plate with heights: chunks; at 20 sampled ground points the drawn height equals the heightmap's within each selected tile's error; and a chunked plate named as a physics shape is refused, naming the plate
A tree whose root declares an error of 0.5 m where the probe measures 8 m is refused with chunks.error, naming the root and both errors; and a planted probe that takes the declared errors lets the root alone draw at every pose of the selection fixture and fails, naming the root
A LAS fixture in a projected system in US survey feet, at easting 1,968,500 ft and northing 13,779,500 ft (600,000 m and 4,200,000 m), holding 101 points 0.01 ft apart along x, builds a tree whose points stand within 1 mm of their positions at 1,200 ÷ 3,937 m a foot, relative to the tree's origin, 0.3048006 m end to end; a planted build that reads feet as metres puts them 1 m end to end and fails, naming the unit, and one that writes absolute positions in f32 snaps them to f32's 0.0625 m steps at an easting of 600,000 m and fails, naming a point. A PCD point whose packed rgb holds the bits 0x00FF8000 builds with color (255, 128, 0), and a planted reader that takes the field's float value fails, naming the point
A three-level tileset fixture served locally, with geometricErrors of 64, 16 and 4 m and one level-1 tile outside the region, imports with error: 16 into a tree of the root and its level-1 tiles inside the region, leaving out the 4 m level and the outside tile; a tile that states 1 m where the probe measures 8 m imports declaring at least the measured 8 m, while a planted import that copies geometricError is refused with chunks.error, naming the chunk; a b3dm tile's base color map draws on its chunk; and a tileset that refines by ADD fails the job, naming the tile, with nothing written
Budgets, on the reference Mac in the local preflight, compared with the merge base: selection over 10,000 candidate chunks takes under 0.5 ms per camera change and nothing while the camera is still; uploads stay within the class's upload bytes per frame; and once settled the flyover's p95 frame time, over every frame of each run, stays within the showpiece class
Over 300 frames of the 64 km flyover, no object-ID pixel lacks a terrain ID while its four neighbors carry one, since chunks.build locks each mesh chunk's border and gives each height tile a skirt lowered by its error
A chain declared 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 m whose levels each measure 2 m from their children is refused at the root, naming it, since a declared error must cover its measured error plus its children's largest; and a stub simplifier that reports half its true error is recorded at the measured value
Proof
The selection tables at each pose beside the formula's, both residency runs' settled hashes and settling times, the BLOCKED export's receipt, the probe refusals, and each planted twin's failure.
Oracle: other projects' own tests, pinned by commit, for 4 of 12 criteria: the tests, per criterion in doneright/oracles (DeepBuildAI/deepbuild). A linked test counts only when run against our output (#136). Without a runner the lane is BLOCKED with one unblock ask.

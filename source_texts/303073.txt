Summary
Measured with --bench, volumetrics were about 60% of the render time and caustics about 10%.
Volumetrics: all 12 steps are marched only on rays the camera sees, directly or through water. Indirect bounces evaluate one random step scaled by 12 (same average), using a plain ray instead of a mostly empty 8-wide packet.
Caustics: full sample count on what the camera sees, 8 samples on indirect bounces.
Early exit: a ray rising above the highest block in the world stops, instead of marching through empty air to the top of the grid.

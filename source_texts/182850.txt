Implicit vs. Explicit
Different sources
2
Explicit: {f (u)|u ∈ R }
Image of a function
Easy to enumerate points
Hard to check whether a given point is on the surface
Implicit: {x|f (x) = 0}
Kernel of a function
Hard to enumerate points
Easy to check whether a given point is on the surface

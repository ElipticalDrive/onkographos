These videos show PDE benchmarks as they are used in the PhD thesis "Efficient Massively Space-Time-Parallel Simulations with Adaptive Spectral Deferred Correction". They contain a 2D and 3D version of the Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion equation as well as two versions of the Rayleigh-Benard convection fluid dynamics benchmark with different Rayleigh number and resolution.

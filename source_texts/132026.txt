The VolPack Volume Rendering Library
VolPack is a portable software library for volume rendering. Developed as part of our volume rendering research project, it is based on a new family of fast volume rendering algorithms (see Philippe Lacroute and Marc Levoy , Fast Volume Rendering Using a Shear-Warp Factorization of the Viewing Transformation, Proceedings of SIGGRAPH94).

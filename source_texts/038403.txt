2D-DICOM image slices, like CT and MRI datasets, are widely used in modern diagnosis. Because of less direct-viewing, they make the diagnosis time-consuming and need many experiences of the doctor. The paper researches a 3D medical visualization and analysis system based on VTK, and realizes it on PC by Microsoft Visual C++ 6.0. An improved volume rendering method based on ray-casting is discussed with emphasis on circumscribed sphere. Slice extraction from arbitrary orientation and the interpolating of the virtual slice are also presented. The system can accurately and quickly reconstruct the medical volume data, which can be then controlled by different methods according to the diagnosis requirement. The slice extraction from arbitrary orientation is also possible. The system will be valuable for diagnosis and improves the visualization, accuracy and scientificity.

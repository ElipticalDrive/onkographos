Issue body actions
Process regions where the mesh is a regular lattice (blocks of identical hexes, octree leaves, Voronoi HCP bulk) as structured patches with implicit indexing — neighbours by (i±1, j, k) arithmetic, contiguous coalesced access, shared reconstruction tables — while walls, transitions and irregular cells keep the general unstructured path. Goal: approach block-structured (AMReX-style) throughput in the bulk without giving up body-fitted, high-order walls or the single solver.
Stage 1 — same numerics, faster (applies today: generated cartesian meshes and the bulk of most hex meshes, e.g. Taylor-Green, channel, isotropic turbulence): detect logically-structured blocks after partitioning/renumbering (#277); store their states block-contiguously; specialized kernels for MUSCL and TENO-E with implicit stencils and one shared table set per block (#431); unstructured kernels handle the rest and the block interfaces. Results identical to the unstructured path up to floating-point reassociation (tolerance-checked; see #429 on the bitwise contract). Measure cells/s against the general path on 3D TENO5 TGV and MUSCL.
Stage 2 — octree and Voronoi bulk: the same patch path for octree leaves (with the hanging-face interfaces of the Cartesian-AMR mesh type) and for Voronoi lattice regions (#439).
Stage 3 (separate decision): a dimension-by-dimension scheme inside structured blocks (cheaper per cell, different numerics — accuracy and conservation at block interfaces must be designed and validated with the work-precision study #430).
Related: #430, #431, #439, #277, #242.

Memory: on lattice regions every interior cell's stencil is the same up to translation, so TENO reconstruction coefficients (the ~40-51 KB/cell of #431) and connectivity can be stored once per stencil shape instead of per cell; lattice-dominated meshes would approach state-only memory. Unstructured cells (walls, transitions) still need #431.

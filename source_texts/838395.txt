💡 What:
Optimized MicrotubuleTorus rendering in components/QuantumScene.tsx by replacing individual standard <mesh> elements with THREE.js <instancedMesh>. The instance positions and Z-axis animations are efficiently updated within the useFrame render loop using setMatrixAt and a reusable THREE.Object3D instance.
🎯 Why:
Previously, rendering thousands of individual <mesh> nodes created a high draw call count (~1080 draw calls for two torus rings of 360 and 720 elements), causing severe CPU overhead, WebGL context thrashing, and reduced FPS during real-time animation.
📊 Measured Improvement:
Baseline: ~1080 individual mesh draw calls per frame across the two torus structures.
Optimized: 2 instanced draw calls total (1 instanced mesh per torus structure).
Impact: ~99.8% reduction in draw calls and significantly lower frame render latency and garbage collection overhead.

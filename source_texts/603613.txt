What Sanctuary needs and why
Sanctuary renders a fixed isometric orthographic camera that sits IsoView::distance (1500 world units) back from its focus. Precipitation, wind, ground fog and strikes all centre on the camera position, which under this camera is 1500 units away from anything on screen. Sanctuary cannot adopt PrecipitationPlugin, the wind field, FogPlugin or the cloud march until they can centre on a focus point and work under an orthographic projection.
Current behaviour (main bb50494)
follow_camera (src/plugin.rs:328) feeds ElementsView.position from the camera transform. That position centres the precipitation box (precipitation/mod.rs:355, PRECIPITATION_BOX_M 60), the wind field (wind/mod.rs:338), the ground fog (fog/mod.rs:292, GROUND_FOG_EXTENT) and the strikes (lightning/strikes.rs:195).
The cloud march builds rays from view.world_position (clouds/march.wesl:218-219, composite.wesl:29-30), which is wrong for orthographic rays (all parallel, origins on the view plane).
The bolt width needs Projection::Perspective (lightning/visual.rs:278).
Proposed API
An ElementsFocus component (or ElementsCamera { follow: Entity }) naming the entity or point the camera-relative domains centre on. Defaults to the camera, so perspective users see no change. Orthographic-correct per-pixel ray origins in the cloud march and composite. Bolt pixel size from OrthographicProjection::area.
Edge cases
Focus moves every frame (the iso camera pans); the particle box must wrap, not respawn.
No ElementsCamera at all: ElementsView.position stays the origin (Sanctuary's current use for strikes).
Orthographic scale changes at runtime (zoom).
Acceptance criteria
With an ortho camera at distance 1500 looking at the focus, precipitation particles fill a box around the focus, and a test asserts the box centre is the focus, not the camera.
The same test for the wind field, ground fog and strike centre.
Cloud march ray origins under Projection::Orthographic differ per pixel and share one direction (unit test on the ray builder).
Bolt width is non-zero and finite under an orthographic projection.
Filed from Sanctuary (github.com/Ladvien/sanctuary), crate main bb50494, 0.5.3, Bevy 0.20.0.

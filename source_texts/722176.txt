What to build
The environment becomes a floating-point equirectangular HDR map, importance-sampled by NEE and combined with the BSDF through MIS. Today the environment is an 8-bit PNG cubemap that is never sampled directly: it has neither the intensity nor the direction of real light. ADR 0004 deferred environment importance sampling to exactly this point.
The sampling distribution follows pixel luminance, weighted by the area each row covers on the sphere. ADR 0004 must be updated: the environment joins the NEE set. This issue also fixes the EnvironmentMapping toggle, which is inverted today: the UI shows the colour picker while the shader uses the cubemap.
Acceptance criteria
An .hdr file loads and lights the scene, with shadows consistent with the map's sun
NEE on and off converge to the same mean within 0.5% with an HDR map
With a small-sun map, noise is much lower at equal samples
The environment toggle in the UI matches what the shader uses
Zero validation layer errors
Blocked by
Directional light (sun) in NEE #12

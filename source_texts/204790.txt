ABSTRACT
This paper addresses the problem of image quality improvement in
three-dimensional geometric scenes illuminated by multiple LED
light sources. Since the classical Lambertian model is insufficient to
represent the optical properties of real materials, a composite
reflectance model combining Oren–Nayar for diffuse reflection and
Cook–Torrance BRDF for specular reflection is employed. The
lighting process is formulated using the Rendering Equation
introduced by Kajiya, and its solution is approximated by Monte
Carlo integration. The proposed approach jointly considers scene
geometry, surface material properties, camera configuration, and
multi-light LED placement to achieve photorealistic rendering. The
results demonstrate improved visual realism for real materials and
the feasibility of optimizing lighting configurations in complex
scenes.

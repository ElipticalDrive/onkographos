Summary
Native fixture for the raster-only sun bake: IRCanvasStress --probe-particle-raster registers the stateless particle pass (selecting the legacy primary bake), --probe-ray-casters <count> <distance> <step> stacks GRID boxes on the shadowbox sun ray, --sun-primary-probe reads the floor ray's primary/fallback sun words per cascade and counts each caster's canvas pixels; --probe-hidden-box now also covers the GRID shadowbox and --probe-floor-span reaches 120.
IRPrefab::SunShadow::readPrimaryDepthProbe (engine probe) and a CPU unpackSunDepth twin.
c_bake_sun_shadow_map becomes a thin wrapper over c_bake_sun_shadow_map_body in both dialects; c_bake_sun_shadow_map_second_depth compiles the body with IR_SUN_SECOND_DEPTH (two-slot distinct-depth atomic min into the otherwise empty indexed fallback layer). BAKE_SUN_SHADOW_MAP::retainSecondPrimaryDepth_ selects it lazily (opt-in diagnostic, default path unchanged); ir_sun_shadow_sample reads the fallback layer for raster receivers when sunBasisV.w is set (new secondPrimaryDepth parameter).

Decision published in docs/design/sun-face-index-capacity.md § "Raster-only primary layer: measured decision"; evidence under docs/pr-screenshots/claude/4799-raster-sun-depth-preservation/.

Plan departures
Decision 3's second-retained-depth prototype ships as a compile-time bake variant (c_bake_sun_shadow_map_second_depth over a shared body) rather than a runtime switch: the switch measured +10 % on the production bake row, and the GPU stage timing cost model requires specialization for hot-kernel modes.
The plan's Phase 0 predicted a loss only from a caster beyond the throw; the fixture also showed the nearest-two family failing on a single flat caster, because a flat raster top spans three quantized sun depths per texel, so the three-depth counterexample is the natural case rather than a contrived one.
The fixture flag --probe-hidden-box now also hides the GRID shadowbox and --probe-floor-span clamps to 120 so the cascade-0 placement keeps a receiver under the ray; neither changes a manifest run.

Efficient volumetric ray casting for isosurface rendering
In isosurface ray casting, traversal operations consume the most time. Several techniques have been used for efficient traversal. Hierarchical data structures such as octrees [3], [8] or K-d trees [10] can be used to skip over empty regions by using a macrocell that contains the minimum and maximum value for its children nodes.

💡 What: Caching tile UV coordinates in web/game.js (and synced in android/app/src/main/assets/game.js) using a memoized UV_CACHE Map lookup inside uvFor(tile, corner).
🎯 Why: uvFor was previously executed 6 times per visible voxel face during chunk meshing, repeatedly performing modulus/division operations and allocating temporary 2-element arrays ([u, v]). This produced significant garbage collection pressure and CPU overhead when streaming or meshing chunks.
📊 Impact: Eliminates thousands of heap array allocations and float calculations per chunk mesh pass, making chunk streaming faster and reducing frame stutters from GC during world navigation.
🔬 Measurement: Verified using node -c syntax check and Playwright automated BlockRealmDebug.selfTest() execution on http://localhost:8080.

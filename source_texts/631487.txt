Idea
Frustum culling (#7) removes what is outside the view; nothing removes what is behind something else. Bistro's exterior
is a street canyon: from most viewpoints the majority of submitted triangles are behind a façade.
Build a hierarchical Z-buffer from the depth prepass, then test each submesh's screen-space bounds against the coarsest
covering mip in a compute pass and zero its indirect instance count on a miss. Reproject last frame's HZB so the prepass
can be culled by it without a frame of self-dependency.
Prerequisites
Bistro D3: editor camera clip range and speed for large scenes #7 (AABB bounds and frustum culling)
A cached render list (Bistro E1: AABB bounds and frustum culling #8) and a depth prepass (Bistro E2: draw-loop hot path — cached render list and material sort #58)
Interacts with LOD selection, which wants the same projected screen size
Unscheduled — revisit once #8 and #7 have landed and a measurement says this is what is left.

🎯 What: Modified randomId in src/systems/net_sphere.ts to use rejection sampling instead of raw modulo arithmetic.
⚠️ Risk: If the alphabet length is changed in the future to a value that does not evenly divide 256, the modulo operation would bias the generated characters towards the beginning of the alphabet. In a security or UUID context, this modulo bias reduces the entropy of generated tokens, making them less random and easier to predict.
🛡️ Solution: Replaced the modulo operation with standard rejection sampling. It calculates a safe limit and loops over randomly drawn bytes, rejecting any that fall into the biased upper range, until enough valid bytes are accumulated. This guarantees a perfectly uniform distribution of characters regardless of the alphabet length.

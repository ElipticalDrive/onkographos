add_refinement(z_range, ratio) asks for cells ratio times finer inside a z slab (a subgrid). Only run() on a uniform mesh has a subgridded lane. Every other path solved the unrefined grid and said nothing. On the issue's 12 mm PEC box (1 mm cells, Ez current source, Ez probe, 60 steps), a non-uniform (graded) mesh gave a probe bit-identical to the same box without the refinement (peak 1.079170e6 in both), and preflight passed. On a uniform mesh, forward() also gave a bit-identical probe (1.079171e6 in both). Only run() on the uniform mesh changed the result, moving the peak to 6.611695e4. Every path without a subgrid now refuses the refinement with a message saying why and what to do instead. On a graded mesh, preflight() also reports it as an error before any run. Results from run() on a uniform mesh, and from any model without a refinement, do not change. vmap_material_sweep()'s sequential path calls run() and keeps solving a refinement. With n_steps left to num_periods, it used to hand run() the coarse step count, which the subgridded lane reads as fine steps, so a ratio-2 sweep covered half the requested time (105 steps against run()'s 210). It now covers the full time. Unrefined sweeps on the sequential path keep main's step count, so the traces of an auto-meshed sweep still stack.

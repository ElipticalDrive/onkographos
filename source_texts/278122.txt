Keep the startup camera out of generated terrain
With this new loop, a chunk at (-1, 0, -1) is generated, covering world x/z -32..-1, while the unchanged camera starts at (-5, 10, -5). The terrain generator fills that column up to about y=17 (16 + sin(-0.5)*5 + cos(-0.5)*5), so the default launch places the camera inside opaque terrain instead of outside the scene. Please move the startup camera above/outside the generated terrain or avoid generating the chunk containing it.

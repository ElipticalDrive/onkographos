Introduction
We are the TaoTian Meta Technology Team（淘天Meta技术团队）, dedicated to exploring and implementing 3D/XR technologies. Our research focuses on 3D object reconstruction, 3D human reconstruction / editing / driving, cross-platform 3D rendering engines, on-device AI inference engines（MNN）, and cross-platform XR R&D solutions.

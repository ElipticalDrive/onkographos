Autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) typically communicate with scientists on the surface over an unreliable wireless channel. The challenges of underwater acoustic communication result in very low data throughput. While there are several examples of scientific data, even imagery, being successfully transmitted over high rate acoustic links, channel coding methods with high rates of error-correction are often employed that limit data throughput to tens or a few hundred bits per second. Little research exists into appropriate methods for image and data compression for acoustic links at these very low rates.

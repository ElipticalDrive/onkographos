Under the virtual shadow map design, volumetric fog samples only VSM coarse pages until it has its own page marking. Today's conventional path gives fog views full-resolution, unfitted cascades (the receiver fit is disabled for views that render volumetric fog, SceneRenderer.cpp ViewRendersVolumetricFog), so coarse-page fog shadows would be a quality step down.
Fix: Stage 5 builds the occupied-froxel list from scene depth on the volumetric fog grid, and VSM marks directional and local pages from it before Stage 8, as UE5.7 GeneratePageFlagsFromFroxels / Froxel::FRenderer.
Scheduled: VX-SHADOW-01 slice S11 (after S6, directional consumers), closed by that slice with its evidence.
Design: projects/Oxygen.Engine/design/vortex/lld/virtual-shadow-maps.md §1.2.1 and §5.3; plan: projects/Oxygen.Engine/design/vortex/milestones/VX-SHADOW-01/README.md S11.

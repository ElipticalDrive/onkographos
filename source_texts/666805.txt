This change improves the quality and accuracy of volumetric particle tracking and ensures that emissive particles correctly contribute to both direct surface lighting and ambient spherical harmonic probes.
Key improvements:
Resolution: Doubled the depth resolution of the volumetric froxel grid to 128 slices.
Terrain Lighting: Fixed a bug where volumetric light was added as a flat glow; it is now correctly multiplied by the surface albedo.
Ambient Tint: SH probes now sample the volumetric grid and include a subtle tint (5% intensity) from nearby particles in the ambient bounce.
Injection: Increased the injection kernel size to maintain smooth light distribution with the higher grid resolution.
Optimization: Improved SH probe shader performance by sampling the volumetric grid once per probe origin instead of redundant fetches per ray.

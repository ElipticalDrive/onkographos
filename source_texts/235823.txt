Replace starter stubs with a pbrt-style raytracer:
BSDFs: Lambert, perfect mirror, smooth/rough dielectric (GGX VNDF), rough conductor (Schlick F0), emissive, and a glTF metallic-roughness PbrMaterial with normal-map support.
Lights: point lights, diffuse area lights bound to any primitive with cone-from-reference sampling for spheres.
Integrators: path tracer with NEE + power-heuristic MIS + Russian roulette; Whitted; direct-lighting baseline.
Acceleration: SAH-binned BVH with slab-test AABB and flat stack traversal; Scene falls back to brute force for unbounded primitives.
Geometry: full Sphere intersection with UVs+tangent; Triangle gains per-vertex normals/UVs and tangent-from-edges; new Quad primitive.
Camera: pinhole + optional thin-lens, fixed primary-ray generation.
Renderer: tile-parallel std::thread workers, progressive accumulation, per-pass PNG dump callback, ACES tonemap + sRGB.
Textures: ImageTexture (stb_image), bilinear, sRGB-aware, repeat/clamp.
OBJ loader: tinyobjloader wrapper, UVs+normals passed through to Triangle for textured meshes.
CLI: --scene, --integrator, --sphere, --spp, --w/--h, --max-depth, --threads, --headless, --gui, --pbr-dir, --out.

Add bump-from-height fallback in PbrMaterial
…
fbd0212
```
When a PBR pack ships a height/displacement map but no baked normal map,
derive the perturbed normal at shade time via forward differences on the
heightfield in tangent space. Falls back to a flat normal when neither
map is present. Loader keys: displacement|height|bump|depth.

Normal map still takes precedence when both are present (the normal map
is usually already baked from the same heightfield and applying both
would double-count the surface detail).
```

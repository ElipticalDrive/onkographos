## Highlights

- **Complete NeRF pipeline** -- positional encoding, volume rendering, hierarchical sampling, training, and novel view synthesis
- **Coarse + Fine networks** -- hierarchical rendering with importance sampling for efficient ray marching
- **Two dataset types** -- Blender synthetic (360-degree) and LLFF real-world (forward-facing)
- **Stratified + hierarchical sampling** -- coarse uniform samples + fine inverse CDF samples in high-density regions
- **NDC support** -- Normalized Device Coordinates for forward-facing scenes
- **Novel view rendering** -- test views, 360-degree orbit videos, depth maps
- **PSNR/SSIM metrics** -- quantitative evaluation during training

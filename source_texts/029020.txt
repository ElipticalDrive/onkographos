Animation Collection
The collection contains multiple MP4 animations corresponding to different scientific simulations and visualization configurations.
Each animation is provided as a separate downloadable video file. The filenames identify the underlying simulation codes.
The animations illustrate the use of SpaceConverter for processing scientific simulation data and preparing them for visualization workflows.

New depth page: meshes (winding, formats), point clouds, voxels, SDF vs
occupancy fields, marching cubes, and how a latent-set 3D generator
(Hunyuan3D-2.1 / 3DShape2VecSet) produces a mesh: Fourier-embedded
query points cross-attending a 4,096-token latent set, chunked and
hierarchical volume decoding, then marching cubes on the CPU.

Measured:
- marching cubes on an analytic sphere: 23 ms at 128^3, 156 ms at 256^3
- one 10k-query attention chunk vs 4,096 latents (Hunyuan3D-2.1 VAE
  sizes, MLX 0.32.2, M2 Pro): 44 ms -> 74 s for a dense 257^3 grid
- hierarchical decoding (reference algorithm re-implemented): 0.89M
  queries instead of 17.0M on the sphere (19x fewer)
- skimage winding: positive-inside fields give inward normals (signed
  volume -0.5233 vs +0.5233), which is why Hunyuan3D reverses faces
- the reference's index / grid_size vertex mapping shrinks the sphere
  to r=0.4962 and shifts it by -0.0078 -- keep it for parity

Grounded in the Hunyuan3D-2.1 MLX port (surface extractor, volume
decoders, face flip). Glossary: 6 new terms. Validator passes.

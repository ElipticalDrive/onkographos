Implements the OpenSpec change openspec/changes/solid-angle-disk-and-tube-lights/.
What changes
CylinderLight: a one-sided tube seen from outside now samples only the arc of
wall facing the shading point. It draws the axial position equiangularly along that
wall, which follows its 1/r². CRUST_TUBE_SAMPLING = area | arc |
equiangular, default equiangular.
DiskLight: Guillén et al. 2017's spherical-ellipse sampler, opt-in with
CRUST_DISK_SAMPLING=ellipse, applied within [0.1, 6.22] sr. The default stays
area.
SolidAngleSampler::sample / pdf may now refuse a point (the tube's silhouette).
When that happens, both MIS sides fall back consistently.
Docs: the lighting design record, docs/light_sampling.md §3.13, and the
environment-variable reference.
Measurements (notes.md)
Synthetic sweep. equiangular wins at equal time on all 12 tubes (1.3–20×) and
on usdlux (1.82×). ellipse loses on glossy floors and small disks (0.30–0.67×),
so it stays opt-in.
Hot path. sample_li is −0.76% by callgrind instruction count on veach_mis.
Goldens. All 37 samples are bit-identical with both switches at area.
Real scenes (one seed for ALab):
No-change check. The OpenPBR Playground and the Moana island at defaults, and
ALab with both switches at area, are bit-identical to the merge base 2c7e0c3
at 16 spp with clamp 0.
ALab frame 1004 has 13 cylinders and 1 disk.
As authored, every variant ties area at equal time (0.99–1.01). relMSE is
equal to six figures, because indirect fireflies dominate and the round lights
carry ~2.3% of the crop's direct light.
With only the round lights lit: equiangular 1.21×, arc 1.18×,
disk ellipse 1.10×.
No bias. relMSE falls without a floor from 16 to 64 to 256 spp, and the
crop's mean brightness agrees between variants within the standard error.
Defaults are unchanged by the real-scene check, per design D7.
Notes
The branch is rebased on main, which includes honour-dome-pole-axis. The
measurements were taken before the rebase, against the old merge base.

High-Level Assessment
The following are alternative approaches to this PR:
1. Sample disks through a circumscribed spherical rectangle
➕ Reuses an existing, cheaper solid-angle sampler.
➖ Roughly 21% of samples miss the disk, and the rectangle approach does not directly cover sheared placements.
2. Use rejection-based exact cylinder solid-angle sampling
➕ Accounts for the cosine variation across nearby thick tubes.
➖ Rejection disrupts the fixed-size low-discrepancy sample sets; complexity is hard to justify for typical thin tubes.
Recommendation: Keep the facing-arc, equiangular tube strategy: it preserves the existing sampling structure and wins across the measured tube cases. Keep the exact disk sampler opt-in because its cost and glossy-surface losses do not support making it the default; the cheaper rectangle alternative would still waste samples.

2. Far end-on views of thin tubes sample coarsely ✓ Resolved
Description
```
wall_line/tube_point compute theta_a = atan(-s0/h) and theta_b = atan((L - s0)/h) in f32 and
invert with s0 + h * tan(theta); when h is tiny relative to s0 both angles sit near ±π/2, so
theta_b - theta_a keeps few significant bits and tan amplifies the angle's rounding by
1/cos²θ. A shading point far beyond a thin tube's end and close to its axis line then draws axial
positions on a coarse grid while tube_pdf reports the analytic density, so the reported pdf no
longer matches the density actually sampled, on the new default equiangular path.
```

```
The equiangular angles are atan of -s0/h and (L - s0)/h in f32 (shape.rs 895-896). Near −π/2 the
f32 spacing between representable angles is about 1.2e-7, while the angular span is about

ellipse sampler in the same PR runs in f64 for exactly this kind of cancellation (ellipse.rs header
```

```
## Issue description
The tube's equiangular axial sampler computes `theta_a`, `theta_b`, their difference and `s0 + h*tan(theta)` in f32. When `h << s0` (a thin tube seen nearly end-on from far away), the angles crowd near ±π/2. The sampled positions become coarsely quantised and drift from the analytic pdf that `tube_pdf` reports.

## Fix Focus Areas
- crates/crust-core/src/light/shape.rs[862-874]
- crates/crust-core/src/light/shape.rs[880-898]
- crates/crust-core/src/light/shape.rs[930-940]

## Recommended Fix
Compute `s0`, `h`, the two angles, the angle difference and the inverse `s0 + h*tan(theta)` in f64, as the ellipse sampler does. Alternatively, sample with the cancellation-free form: write the span as `atan2(L·h, h² + s0·(s0−L))`, and sample `s` relative to the near end using the tangent-subtraction identity. Add a unit test with a far, near-axis shading point that checks the sample histogram against `tube_pdf`.
```

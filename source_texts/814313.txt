#120 has merged; this PR now targets master, with master merged in (no conflicts). The silhouette PR (#126) stacks on it. It needs no code from #119 or #120.
The multi-view model predicts every frame's per-joint scales and mesh scale independently, and nothing in its loss ties them together: not the frames of one animal, nor its left and right limbs. In short mouse fine-tunes, the left and right femur scales drifted clearly apart. This PR adds two optional loss terms on the per-joint log scales, both off by default. scale_symmetry pulls paired _L / _R joints to the same scales. scale_consistency pulls each frame's scales toward a running mean of its session, since a session is one animal.
Changes
scale_symmetry (commit 1). _lr_joint_pairs pairs joints whose names differ only in an _L / _R token (femur_L / femur_R, Foot_L_tip / Foot_R_tip). The term is the mean squared difference of the paired per-joint log scales.
scale_consistency (commit 1). _scale_consistency_loss keeps a running mean (EMA, momentum 0.99) of each session's per-joint log scales and, with mesh scaling on, of its log mesh scale, and penalises each frame's squared distance from it.
The means are detached, move only in training mode, and live in the module's __dict__ (state_dict is unchanged). Validation measures against them, or its own batch mean for an unseen session.
Frames with a non-finite scale, or |log| ≥ 10, are left out of the term and the mean, so one diverged prediction cannot poison the mean.
The term and the means are computed in fp32. Under use_mixed_precision an MLP head's scales are fp16, where a 1% update rounds away and the mean stalls (at log scale 0.7 it closed 17% of a 0.05 gap in 500 updates instead of 99.3%). For fp32 inputs (the default transformer-decoder head, also under mixed precision) the term and the means are bit-identical to before.
Session names in the targets (commit 1). SLEAPMultiViewDataset.__getitem__ adds session_name to y_data, since the loss only receives the targets. Merged HDF5s prefix each session with its source file.
Start-up checks (commit 1). Both terms need per-joint scales (scale_trans_beta.mode "separate" without the PCA transformation): a positive weight anywhere in the curriculum without them stops training with a ValueError before the model is built. A positive scale_symmetry on a model without _L / _R pairs stops right after it is built. The joint-limit check's curriculum-max loop becomes _max_curriculum_weight(name), used by all three checks.
Defaults. Both weights are 0.0 in base_weights and in the regressor's fallback weights.
Tests (commits 2, 4 and 5), tests/test_scale_regularisers.py, on stand-in models: the _L / _R pairs; the scale_symmetry term of the full loss (positive for unequal paired scales, 0 for equal ones, and equal to the total when it is the only weight); _scale_consistency_loss (0 for constant scales, no mean moved or created in eval mode, diverged frames left out).
Docs (commit 3, extended in review). Two rows in the configs README's loss table. scale_consistency: start it with a curriculum_stages entry once the scales have settled, at epoch 1 or later in a fine-tune (from epoch 0 it pins the initial checkpoint's mesh scale) and later in a from-scratch run (the combined run used epoch 20; not measured); from the epoch it starts, it is part of the validation loss, so best_model.pth compares epochs with and without it; keep it off for data with several animals per session_name, e.g. replicAnt HDF5s. scale_symmetry needs _L / _R joint names. The typical-range column shows the values of the pilots and runs below: 0.25 for scale_symmetry, 0.05 from a later epoch for scale_consistency.

Evidence

scale_symmetry 0.25 alone brought the femur scales close together, with no gain in 3D error;
scale_consistency 0.05 from epoch 0 gave the largest 3D error: the mesh scale stayed at the initial checkpoint's and the forearms grew to about twice their label length;

Notes (decided in review, 2026-11-03)
Merge order. As stacked: this PR first (master merged in, retargeted to master, merge commit), then Multi-view silhouette loss against a SAM 2 mask sidecar #126 the same way.
The means are per rank and not saved. The replicas stay identical (gradients are all-reduced; confirmed by the 2-GPU smokes above); a resume restarts the means. Kept that way: after a resume the means restart from the resumed model's first batch, and that model has already adapted, so the epoch-0 pinning does not recur. Saving them as buffers would need per-session buffers created as sessions appear, which DDP's buffer broadcast from rank 0 would not match across ranks.
The scale_consistency design. The lagging mean resists any steady change of the scales and holds a state the model falls into: in a 10-epoch run a loss spike left the mesh scale markedly smaller, and the mean held it there. The mesh-scale part weighs as much as all per-joint entries together (3 × 51 = 153 for the mouse). Merged as is (off by default; the README says when to start it). A redesign (a warm-up, the within-batch spread only, or a mesh-scale weight like one joint-axis entry), decided with an A/B on an M103 fine-tune and a from-scratch run, is a planned follow-up issue; the current behaviour stays reachable, since the combined run's resume and the White-only runs use it.
Side tokens. The stick insect and OmniAnt models end joint names in _l / _r (l_1_co_l) and get no pairs; matching the last token would give 24 pairs for each of them (21 for SMPL_STICK). Planned follow-up issue: _lr_joint_pairs also recognises lowercase _l / _r (the names stay, for replicAnt compatibility) and pairs only joints whose rest positions mirror across a model plane within a tolerance, with a clear message otherwise. The OmniAnt models are about symmetric; the stick insect model is not.
One animal per session. replicAnt HDF5s use the batch file's name as session_name, so many synthetic individuals count as one session. The scale_consistency README row now says to keep it off for such data. Skipping samples with ground-truth shape instead would also switch it off for M103, whose rows all carry the Falkner stand-in betas.
session_name in y_data. Every multi-view y_data now carries session_name. Nothing in neuralSMIL, multiview_common or sleap_data iterates over or serialises multi-view y_data (multiview_collate_fn passes the dicts through). Single-view samples carry session_name in x_data only; their y_data is unchanged.
README. Added in review (see Docs): the typical values 0.25 and 0.05 from a later epoch, the best_model.pth sentence, the from-scratch rule and the replicAnt sentence.

Two optional multi-view loss terms on the per-joint log scales ('separate'
scale mode without the PCA transformation), both off by default:

- scale_symmetry: paired _L/_R joints share their per-joint log scales.
- scale_consistency: each frame's per-joint log scales and log mesh scale
  stay at a running per-session mean, since one animal per session has one
  set of proportions. The mean is detached EMA state (momentum 0.99),
  updated in training mode only and not saved with the model. Frames with
  a non-finite or out-of-range scale are left out of the term and the mean.

The dataset adds session_name to each sample's targets for this. At
start-up the trainer stops when either weight is positive anywhere in the
curriculum without per-joint scales, or when scale_symmetry is positive for
a model without _L/_R joint pairs, instead of training without the terms.
The joint-limit fail-fast loop becomes a shared _max_curriculum_weight
helper.

Three tests in the new tests/test_scale_regularisers.py, on the methods
alone with a stand-in model:

  (Foot_L_tip / Foot_R_tip), and leaves unpaired joints out.

- frames with an inf or overflowed scale, or a NaN mesh scale, are left out
  of the term and of the mean, and the next batch stays finite.

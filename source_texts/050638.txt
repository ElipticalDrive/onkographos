These are the processed DTU, BMVS, and MipNeRF360 datasets formatted for use with Instant-NGP (https://github.com/NVlabs/instant-ngp). They are provided solely to help reproduce the results presented in the Radiance Field Loss paper (https://rgl.epfl.ch/publications/Zhang2025Radiance). They are provided solely to help reproduce the results presented in the Radiance Field Loss paper (https://rgl.epfl.ch/publications/Zhang2025Radiance).
Each dataset includes Instant NGP-specific parameters such as `scale` and `aabb_scale` used during training. However, any NeRF-compatible dataset can be used in place of these.

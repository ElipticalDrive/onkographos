What to build
Exact region classification on the sparse Storage Tier costs work proportional to content, and it works for every valid region.
Skip absent space. Sparse-tier classification walks the persistent brick tree and skips absent subtrees as a whole. It no longer looks up each brick the region intersects one at a time. Uniform bricks and subtrees still read no individual voxels.
No full voxel count. Classification doesn't compute or require the region's total voxel count. Any region that is valid for the volume can be classified, including the whole of a volume far too large for dense storage. Operations that really do read every voxel keep their existing size checks.
Unchanged meaning. Results stay exact and match what Voxel Cell Grid enumeration reports for the same clipped cell region, on both Storage Tiers.
Acceptance criteria
With qualification counters, classifying an entirely empty region does work bounded by tree depth, not by the number of bricks the region covers, and examines no individual voxels. A region covering sparse content does work that follows the content, not the region's size.
On an empty volume with 2²² voxels per axis, classifying the whole volume returns Uniform(Empty). After a single edit it returns Mixed. Both run in the existing subprocess with its timeout and memory limit.
Classification of regions spanning absent, uniform and mixed bricks, with non-aligned bounds and partly outside the volume, matches the dense tier and brute-force reads on small scenes.
Classifying a clipped cell region still agrees with enumeration for every enumerated cell on the existing fixtures.
Dense reads of a region too large to read still return their existing contextual error.

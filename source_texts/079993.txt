WebGPURenderer hands WebGPU the projection matrix in three.js's WebGL convention, which maps the view volume to clip-space z from −w to w. WebGPU clips z below 0, so the near half of the view volume is lost.
With an OrthographicCamera (near 0.1, far 10), a plane at view distance 1 or 5 draws nothing, and only one at distance 9 draws (Chromium 149, Apple M1). three.js r186's own WebGPURenderer sets each camera's coordinateSystem to its backend's before rendering (src/renderers/common/Renderer.js), and WebGPUBackend returns WebGPUCoordinateSystem, so its projections map depth to 0..1.
Found while probing for #303, on main at 445b9bf. No existing issue covers the depth range or the projection convention.

Abstract
It is shown that, in the pseudogradient estimation algorithm of image parameters, the convergence rate of estimates depends on the method of pseudogradient calculations through the finite differences. An approach to optimize the step of finite differences on the parameters in the problem of estimating interframe geometrical image distortion is considered.

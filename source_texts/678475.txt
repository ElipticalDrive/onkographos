Voxelization of 8x8x8 cube crashes on VoxelShape::getVoxelIdsForVertices. There's a mismatch between the number of indices in the mesh according to the voxelizer and according to the stream from MGeometryExtractor. I think the proper way to do this would be to store blind int data (voxel IDs) on the MfnMesh on creation - let Maya process it alongside any other vertex attribute. Then use MGeometryExtractor to get the vertexid (semanticName) vertex buffer, and use that to map the blind data to the expanded vertex position stream (from MGeometryExtractor).
edit - adding more to this.
While the idea above is good in practice, the vertex data passed into MFnMesh.create really needs to match what comes out on the other side. Other things are predicated on that data (like the Surface* and Interior* face components used for attribute and shading set transfer). In this case, the root cause was that Maya seems to discard degenerate triangles on mesh creation. By eliminating those ourselves in the CGAL section, first, the vertex data is aligned, and no need for changes to VoxelShape::getVoxelIdsForVertices.

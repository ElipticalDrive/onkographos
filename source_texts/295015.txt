The current LOD system works by selecting every n blocks out of a chunk, and making the selected blocks n times bigger. This simplifies the volume of a chunk, thus reducing the quads (the purpose).
This has 2 big issues:
it doesn't work well with our 62-sized chunks (which become 64 with neighbor blocks), because 62 can only be divided by 2 and 31. Giving us only two (very spaced) LOD levels.
naive block selection leads to seeing blocks which are not exposed to air
For issue 1 we could try 126-sized chunks, using simd for meshing. 126 has a LOT more divisors than 62. This would also improve the padding/data ratio and reduce draw calls if we stay with 1 mesh per chunk.
We could also rework the LOD system to work with any value regardless of the divisibility of chunk size, probably a saner option 😅
For issue 2 we should rework block selection to favor voxels exposed to air.

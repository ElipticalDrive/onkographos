Medical image visualization technologies build 3D models with 2D medical image series to provide useful and precise informa- tion for the doctors. It is one of the crucial technologies in CAS system, Image Guide Surgery and Minimal Invasive Surgery. VTK is an advanced toolkit for the medical 3D visualization. 3D reconstruction has two ways of volume rendering and surface rendering methods. The content of the article introduces the principle and the realization of the method of mapping the surface, and through the MC algorithm of widely used internationally visualization tool kit VTK to realize the surface rendering.and display random sections from next three direc- tion: Axis、Crown、Arrow.

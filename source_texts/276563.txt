Machine-readable dataset to determine the best compression settings for a given goal such as "fastest compression" or "fastest search over compressed data". Graph model (JSON + auto-derived Dot format) along with CSV measurement results of 13 compression tools and 30 tool-configuration-search combinations. Associated publication: Josef Spillner, «Comparison and Model of Compression Techniques for Smart Cloud Log File Handling», CCCI 2020. This dataset also contains a snapshot of the associated software prototypes.

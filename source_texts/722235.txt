Title says it all.
One place to start would be constructing 3D surfaces (e.g. sphere and torus) as a composite of rectangular panels, and placing a light source in the ThreeDScene. Then in the rendering loop, set the brightness of each panel individually with respect to that light source, using the normalized dot product between the distance vector and the normal vector. Then move the light source around and see how the values change.

In order to develop a recommendation for thresholding values if minimal loss of quantitative fidelity is desired. Include discussion of the compression properties at various levels of fidelity loss.
In particular, investigate effects on integration when the lower threshold is <<, ~, and >> the reciprocal of the product of the voxel axis dimensions. Logic at this time is that 0.1/{product} should be an effectively lossless thresholding operation.
Probably will want to revisit this after --zero is implemented (#21), or even wait until it is implemented to do it.
May want to investigate more than one of the following, to see if effects of the thresholding and/or truncation vary in significance across them:
Entire volume
Basins
Density basins
ELF basins
other?
Within, e.g., orbital isosurfaces (integrating, e.g., difference densities between two states)

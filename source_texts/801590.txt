Parent
Epic #64 (ADR 0011 — GPU brick-field display sink).
What to build
AFK. The slice that makes the sink scale: emit two world-fixed coarse occupancy levels above the brick set (the benchmark''s proven config — cells of 8 blocks, then 64 blocks; "any-brick-inside" sets), and port the hierarchical DDA: a ray jumps to the exit of the coarsest EMPTY level covering its position, descending to per-block brick work only where the finest level is occupied. Multi-producer + scattered scenes engage the brick path. This ports ADR 0009''s measured 160→10240 (~64x) scattered-ceiling lift.
World-fixed levels only (a min-mip of the brick set) — camera-centred residency rings are deliberately out of scope (pure eviction policy behind G1''s miss contract, unfiled until measured).
Acceptance criteria
Each coarse level is conservative: its occupied-cell set is a superset of the true occupied cells — a ray never strides past a real surface (parity gate clause b, coarse tier)
Finest-LOD goldens still render pixel-identical (LOD is the only sanctioned approximation)
Multi-producer and scattered scenes source display from the brick path
A scattered-scene perf probe demonstrates the empty-space-skipping lift vs G1''s flat DDA
Full verification gate green on both feature sets
Blocked by
G1: minimal brick raymarch — live display swap for a single ported producer, finest LOD only #67 (G1 minimal brick raymarch)

### Summary

Adds support for translating USD `Material` volume terminals (`outputs:mitsuba:volume` and `outputs:volume`) into Mitsuba 3 interior participating media across both `hdMitsuba` (C++ Hydra render delegate) and `usd_mitsuba` (Python USD-to-Mitsuba translator):

- **Media, Phase Functions & Volume Data Sources:**
  - Registers `volume`, `constvolume`, `gridvolume`, `homogeneous`, `heterogeneous`, `isotropic`, `hg`, `rayleigh`, `blendphase`, `sggx`, and `tabphase` in `SdrMitsubaDiscoveryPlugin`.
  - Translates volume shader networks connected to `outputs:mitsuba:volume` or `outputs:volume` into Mitsuba `Medium` instances attached to shape `interior` properties (`UsdGeom.Mesh`, `GeomSubset`, `PointInstancer` prototypes, and implicit surfaces `UsdGeom.Sphere`, `UsdGeom.Cylinder`, and `UsdGeom.Cube`).
  - Supports loading `.vol` files in `gridvolume` nodes via `ArAsset` -> `mitsuba::MemoryStream` -> `mitsuba::VolumeGrid`.
  - Defaults the surface BSDF to `null` instead of `diffuse` when a material defines an interior volume medium without a surface shader.
- **Authoring (`usd_mitsuba.material_authoring`):**
  - Adds `author_mitsuba_medium` to convert Mitsuba `Medium` dictionaries (including nested `PhaseFunction` and `Volume` plugins) into USD shader networks.

### Current Limitations / Future Work

- **No global volume caching mechanism (`VolumeCache`):** Unlike 2D `Bitmap` textures (which are pre-discovered and cached across materials in `TextureCache`), `.vol` `VolumeGrid` data is currently loaded per `gridvolume` evaluation during material translation (deduplicated only within a single material network via `cache[nodePath]`). Sharing `.vol` grids across multiple distinct `UsdShade.Material` prims or avoiding `.vol` reloads when re-syncing a material (along with in-place parameter updates for `Medium` objects in `UpdateMaterialInPlace`) is left for future work.
- **Interior media only:** Volume terminals map exclusively to a shape's `interior` medium. `exterior` media on shapes and media attached directly to cameras (`Sensor`) are not yet exposed.
- **6-channel `constvolume` for `sggx`:** `sggx` with a 6-channel `.vol` `gridvolume` is supported, whereas authoring a 6-float array `constvolume` is not yet handled.
- **Material-bound media vs. `UsdVol.Volume`:** Participating media are authored via `UsdShade.Material` volume terminals on closed shapes using Mitsuba `.vol` grids; standalone `UsdVol.Volume` / OpenVDB (`.vdb`) geometry prims are not translated.

What
Task 0014: probe volumes, like Unity 6's Adaptive Probe Volumes, kept live by the voxel GI.
Probe Volume component: Global, or a box that follows the object's rotation and scale.
Placement: automatic bricks of 4 × 4 × 4 probes, fine near geometry and coarse in open space.
Inside geometry: probes are moved out (virtual offset) or marked invalid and dilated.
Each probe is an ambient cube plus its sky share, so a new sky colour relights it without a re-bake.
Bake: Generate Lighting bakes the probes after the lightmaps, in slices within the frame budget.
Live: Realtime GI keeps the probes live, with or without a bake.
Shading: per pixel, with normal bias, view bias and APV's normal-based leak reduction.
Receive GI (Lightmaps or Light Probes) on MeshRenderer.
Debug display: probes show.
Task 0015: the GPU viewport go / no-go spike (stress gpu_viewport, ADR 0013). Verdict: no-go.
The bar: two views at ≤ 0.50× the CPU's time.
The GPU's own render plus read-back costs 0.54–0.60× on the RTX 4070 SUPER and 0.79–0.89× on the
RTX 3060 Ti, against a 19.6 ms CPU frame at 1080p.
No viewport code is written.
Stacked on #13; merge #11 → #12 → #13 → this in order.
Review fixes (the 0014 reviewer's findings)
Blocker: an enormous volume overflowed a float-to-int cast in placement (UBSan), and the probe cap
could be broken. Counts are now in double and the cap is strict.
Live probes never showed with Realtime GI on, yet they re-rendered the views. Probe-lit objects
now keep the probes, as in APV. This changes the approved spec (see below).
Double counting with Baked GI off: baked probes are now gated on Baked GI, and probes from another
bake are refused on open.
Stale probes:
A stale ProbeVolume.bin is removed when you re-bake without a volume.
Probes are saved even without lightmaps.
The probe bake ran on the UI thread in one go: it is now stepped, and the bake's mesh copies are
freed.
The Global pad is now 1.5 spacings (probes no longer sit in the floor plane).
Minors:
Indirect Intensity applies to the probes.
Fallbacks for cells whose probes are all behind the surface.
The virtual offset is clamped.
No lightmap charts for probe-lit objects.
The live key covers both views and only the relevant settings.
The volume box is cached, and Probe Volumes are in the views' scene hash.
Test fix: a NaN box built with AABB::add is a valid point box on GCC; the test now sets it
directly.
Needs your sign-off (deviations from approved specs)
0014:
Ambient cube instead of SH L1/L2.
Probes win over per-pixel voxel GI for probe-lit objects.
Live probes restart as a whole, not only near a change.
The debug display draws dots.
All recorded in the spec and ADR 0012.
0015: a no-go means no viewport. The recommended next step is moving voxel GI and probe sampling
(about 12–15 ms each at 1080p) to compute, or making them cheaper on the CPU.

Linux and the sanitizer build: Linux with libraries 7,856 checks, 0 failed. Sanitizer build 7,778, 0 failed, no reports. The sanitizer also caught a NaN cast in the volume box key and a far-probe voxel lookup, both fixed. A pilot-view test was flaky there: it compared the Statistics overlay's frame time.

Repo
Features completed
Core — diffuse BSDF with cosine-weighted sampling, stream compaction, sorting by material, stochastic antialiasing
Refraction (glass with Fresnel), mirror and rough metal (GGX), depth of field, direct lighting (NEE + MIS), Russian roulette, motion blur
Things I'd highlight
NEE + MIS reaches the same quality as BSDF sampling in about 4x less time (RMSE 29.6 -> 12.2 at 32 spp), also checked on Veach's MIS scene.
Russian roulette makes each iteration 17% faster in the open Cornell box and 22% faster in the closed one, with no visible change to the image.
Material sorting is a net loss even though Nsight Compute shows it helps shading: 77% fewer divergent branches and a 31% faster shading kernel, but the sort itself costs much more (31.2 -> 66.9 ms per iteration).
Stream compaction with thrust::partition is slower on this machine. Nsight Systems shows the GPU sitting idle while thrust allocates and frees temporary memory on every call.

Summary
Closes #341.
A light's inputs:diffuse and inputs:specular were ignored with light.unsupported_multiplier. They are now honoured on every UsdLux light type, as the light's LobeGains, applied on both MIS sides:
inputs:specular scales the specular, coat and transmission lobes, and a hair fibre's R term.
inputs:diffuse scales the diffuse, sheen, subsurface and translucent lobes, and a fibre's TT/TRT terms (Hair::r_fraction splits the fibre lobe by term).
NEE connects a gained light share by share (gained_split).
Bounce side: the bounce records the specular share of its sample's value (PrevBounce::specular) and scales the emission it reaches (a hit light, a hidden-light crossing, an escape, a shadow-link twin) by the blended gain. Exact for the beauty.
Volume scattering takes the light whole (no lobe split).
Any finite value is accepted (negative, >1); a non-finite value falls back to 1 with light.non_finite_input. LobeGains::blend is a weighted sum, so even opposite-sign extreme gains stay finite. light.unsupported_multiplier is retired.
The gains live on the light list beside the light, so an MCP edit applied in place renders like a full re-import.
Path guiding trains on the gained radiance by design, as it does on a light's intensity. A guided render is unbiased at each gain but not linear in the pair.
Known gap (documented): bounce-side light reaching a gained light is routed to the LPE lobe AOVs at the blended gain, not lobe by lobe. AOVs still sum to the beauty, but C<RD>L can hold part of a specular-only light's reflection.
Capability
lighting: openspec/specs/lighting/spec.md and design.md updated (behaviour, the bounce-side blend, the guiding exception, the LPE gap). Also docs/architecture.md, site/content/docs/usd/lights.md (new section with a hair-kicker example), site/content/docs/reference/warnings.md, site/content/docs/architecture/limitations.md.
Image impact
Bit-identical
Changed intentionally: only for scenes that author inputs:diffuse / inputs:specular ≠ 1, which previously rendered as if both were 1.
Not applicable
Evidence is in crates/crust-core/tests/lobe_gains.rs rather than a relmse sweep. The gains steer no sampling decision of an unguided render, so the image is exactly linear in the pair. The test pins per pixel, under the light, bsdf and power strategies, that a render at (0.25, 3) equals 0.25 × diffuse-only + 3 × specular-only. Other tests cover:
strategy agreement on the mean;
a metal going black under a diffuse-only light;
import and non-finite handling;
opposite-sign extreme gains blending to finite values;
an in-place edit matching a re-import bitwise;
a unit test of the hair R split.
Removing the bounce-side scaling makes three of them fail.
Scenes without multipliers: cornellbox at 2 spp is bit-identical to the base commit (crust diff relmse 0). scripts/check_images.sh was not run across all scenes.

1. Finite light gains can corrupt pixels ✓ Resolved
Description
```
LobeGains::blend subtracts the diffuse gain from the specular gain in f32, which can overflow
even though both authored values are finite. With opposite-sign gains such as −3e38 and 3e38, a
diffuse-only share then computes 0 × infinity, producing a NaN that passes through light sampling
or bounce emission into the film.
```

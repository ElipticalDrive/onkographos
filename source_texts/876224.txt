Fix
Return Box3d.Invalid for empty face bounds before reading corners, including through face/polygon facades. Ray, closest-point and box-surface queries now skip faces with fewer than three corners rather than reading outside their intervals.
Object-filter ordering, index slices, cutoffs and valid-face payloads are unchanged. This does not change cleanup, topology, ObjectIsInsideBox, hit-info generation or shared kd-tree traversal.
Performance
Forty matching triangle/quad workloads cover direct and flat-tree queries and face bounds with 1/64 faces; all measured calls allocated 0 B. Median paired changes over 14 rounds ranged from −23.7% to +19.7% elapsed/−10.8% to +17.9% CPU. Longer selected repeats ranged from −4.2% to +9.6%/−2.9% to +7.5%, including quad bounds +9.6%/+6.6% and quad ray misses +8.4%/+6.2%.
Identical-binary controls varied −5.6% to +4.3% elapsed/−4.2% to +4.3% CPU. Some costs exceed those controls; other phases conflict, so no speedup or neutrality claim. Formerly throwing inputs are not successful-path baselines. Kept draft for nonempty-query costs and uncertainty.

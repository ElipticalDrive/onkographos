In the real-time rendering field, shadows are crucial visual clues. It can help people better understand the geometric, sizes, and relative positions of objects in 3D space. In this paper, we propose a soft shadow algorithm based on the first-order signed distance field. The basic idea is that the occlusion area projected from the shading point to the spherical light source is approximately the union of multiple arcuate areas. When calculating the occlusion rate, the signed distance field is used to analytically judge whether the shadow ray intersects with the arcuate areas. Combined with the Monte Carlo method, the occlusion rate can be efficiently and accurately estimated. Experiments show that this algorithm can render higher quality soft shadow at a real-time rate.

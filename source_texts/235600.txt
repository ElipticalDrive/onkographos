Build the first real reconstruction path in scanner-core.
Target architecture:
point/depth integration into a metric spatial representation;
sparse TSDF or equivalent explicit geometry suitable for incremental fusion;
live point-cloud/surface preview;
incremental mesh extraction;
bounded memory and latency;
per-region provenance/confidence hooks;
object-local geometry separable from room structural geometry.
Neural/Gaussian representations may be experimental secondary projections but must not replace canonical metric geometry.

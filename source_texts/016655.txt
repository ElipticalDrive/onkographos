Kiln is a WebGPU-native, out-of-core volume renderer for large volumetric datasets. It streams multiscale OME-Zarr (NGFF v0.4/v0.5) and a sharded binary format over HTTP into a bounded GPU residency cache using virtual-texture indirection, and renders them in the browser with compute-shader ray marching (direct volume rendering, maximum intensity projection, isosurface and slice views) at interactive frame rates.

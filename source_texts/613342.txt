Reusable, spec-tested algorithms for games:
Randomness: seedable PCG32, weighted pick, shuffle, dice
Noise: Perlin and simplex (2D/3D), fBm, ridged, domain warp, Worley
Pathfinding: grid A* (4/8-dir, no corner cutting, weighted), graph A* and Dijkstra, flow fields, path smoothing
Grids: Bresenham, line of sight, flood fill, shadowcasting FOV, hex coordinates
Procedural generation: Poisson disk sampling, BSP rooms, cellular automata caves, mazes, L-systems
Spatial queries: quadtree, spatial hash, 3D grid or BVH
Steering and flocking; finite state machine, behavior tree, generic minimax with alpha-beta
Geometry: convex hull, segment intersection, point in polygon, Bezier and Catmull-Rom splines with arc-length following, critically damped springs

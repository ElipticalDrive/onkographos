Research findings
Cost. Affordable only if the sun shadow ray is cast per voxel, not per march step. One ray query per step on a full-screen smoke (~48 steps x 1.64 Mpx at 1440p DLSS Quality) is ~79 M rays, about 10x the rest of the frame's rays. Lighting the 0.5 m grid once per frame in compute (one sun ray per occupied voxel plus a self-shadow march through the grid), then marching the lit grid from the camera, is estimated at 1-2 ms for smoke covering the whole screen and much less for partial coverage. Shipped volumetrics land in the same range (Horizon clouds ~2 ms PS4, Unreal volumetric fog 1 ms PS4 / 3 ms GTX 970, Frostbite 0.8-3 ms PS4). VR: the voxel lighting is shared between eyes, but the march runs per layer and scales with headset resolution (~2.5-5 ms at 90 Hz full-screen), so a fixed half-resolution march may be needed in VR. These are estimates to measure with a prototype.
Upscalers. FSR 3.1 takes a reactive mask (write smoke opacity, clamp ~0.9), a transparency-and-composition mask, or auto-generates reactivity from an opaque-only colour copy. DLSS's only equivalent, BiasCurrentColor, is documented as "should not be used" with the current presets (J/K/L/M); no reactive mask exists for them. So the smoke must be temporally stable on its own: no per-pixel stochastic noise left for the upscaler to resolve, and (to verify) motion vectors overwritten in smoke-dominated pixels with the smoke's own reprojection.
Compositing. A full-screen composite before the forward pass cannot hide tracers/particles behind the smoke: each translucent fragment needs the smoke between the camera and its own depth. Proposed: the smoke pass also writes a froxel grid (Frostbite/Unreal-style fogging of translucency) that the forward pass samples.
Contradictions with the ADRs
ADR-0067 puts bloom/exposure/tonemap before the upscaler; the DLSS guide (3.1) requires DLSS before tone mapping and FSR 3.1 lists tonemap and bloom after the upscaler, with linear HDR input.
ADR-0072 "lit by the sun with a shadow ray": the per-step reading is ~10x the frame's rays; per voxel is cheap.
ADR-0072 "lit by the sky": unoccluded sky makes indoor smoke glow; sample ADR-0067's probe grid instead.
ADR-0072's single composite between lighting and translucency doesn't by itself hide translucents behind the smoke (needs a depth-indexed result).
ADR-0072 "no quality setting": a VR half-res march would be a fixed per-platform choice; the ADR should say so.

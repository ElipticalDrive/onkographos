Implements a real-time grid-based incompressible Navier-Stokes solver (Stam stable-fluids) over the existing voxel grid:
fluid.rs: inflow forcing, viscous diffusion, semi-Lagrangian advection, Gauss-Seidel pressure projection, and no-slip obstacle handling
renderer.rs: thousands of tracer particles advected through the velocity field, colored blue->red by local speed
geometry.rs: native file-picker import of GLB/glTF models (rfd), with auto-fit into the tunnel and surface voxelization into the solid mask
grid.rs: cell<->world coordinate helpers and obstacle clearing
main.rs: wires the sim + viz together with keyboard controls and a HUD (wind speed, viscosity, pause/reset, particle/obstacle/color toggles, model import/clear)
Adds WINDFLOW.md documenting the design, solver, and controls. Forces wayland-sys dlopen on Linux so the build needs no wayland dev package.

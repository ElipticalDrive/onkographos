Summary
Stage 2, based on #143. Merge #143 first, then retarget this PR to feature/gpu.
Match pbrt-v4 CPU's Disk/Sphere intersection orientation rules, including its difference between intersection and light-sampling normals under a handedness-swapping shape transform.
```
Disk / Sphere intersection
  world-space cross product flip:
    reverse_orientation XOR total_transform_swaps_handedness
                        XOR shape_transform_swaps_handedness
  additional flip of flattened vertex normals:
    shape_transform_swaps_handedness
Light sampling
  retains its existing ReverseOrientation-only rule
```
Keep shape-generation handedness separate from the composed instance transform. v4 TransformedPrimitive transforms the existing interaction without reapplying the shape rule.
Use a derived intersection normal kind in the GPU Geometry record (stride 16 -> 32 bytes).
Apply the rule in shading and shared intersection reconstruction. Preserve v4's unflipped quadric normal derivatives by undoing ReverseOrientation already present in flattened vertex normals.
Pass the sampling normal to area-light alpha evaluation instead of the intersection normal.
Triangle/PLY/LoopSubdiv rules, CPU implementations, and light-bound generation remain unchanged. Cylinder behavior is outside this stage.
Local reference: v4 shapes.h, shapes.cpp, interaction.h, and cpu/primitive.cpp.
Evidence
Before: Villa at 100x44, halton 64 spp, path maxdepth 8 had mean Y 0.119303987 on GPU versus 0.090190265 on r4 CPU (+32.28%).
After: GPU mean Y 0.092494463, reducing the difference to +2.55%. All pixels are finite. The same-condition v4 CPU reference is 0.088165373. CPU and pre-change references were reused from the prior investigation.
Disk/Sphere one-sided emission: 20 conditions passed (identity, rotation, positive nonuniform scale, one-axis/two-axis mirrors, each with/without ReverseOrientation). CPU/GPU agree on emitting versus black sides.
Disk dielectric with mirrored ObjectInstance: 4 conditions passed. An initial test caught accidental reapplication of shape handedness at the instance boundary; the final implementation matches CPU.
Existing Triangle area-emission regression: 4 conditions passed.
Mirrored Disk/Sphere minimal scenes also agree with v4 CPU on emitting sides. v4 reference binary provenance is recorded by SHA-256; its source commit is unverified.
Focused Node/Flat/ABI/shader/composer/stage tests: 150 passed. cargo fmt and release build passed. GPU comparisons ran on an RTX 3060 with WebGPU ray query.
Self-review limitations: Independent light-sampling/BSDF contribution checks, two-sided quadric emission, and normal-dependent alpha textures do not yet have dedicated coverage. Sphere triangulation/analytic sampling differences and the remaining Villa difference are not resolved by this PR.
Merge Danger
Door: two-way
Revert this behavior change before reverting its metadata dependency (#143).
Blast Radius: rendering
Disk/Sphere surface normals affect emission, scattering, ray offsets, alpha evaluation, and medium-side selection. Shape handedness adds an instance orientation flag; the Geometry ABI changes on both Rust and WGSL sides. Existing triangle behavior is retained and covered by the emission regression.

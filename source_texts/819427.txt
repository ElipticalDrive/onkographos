What
A new single-file generator in sdf/: an SDF solid editor.
12 primitives: sphere, box, cylinder, cone/frustum, capsule, torus, ellipsoid, n-sided prism, pyramid, octahedron, chain link, half-space cutter. Rounding where it makes sense.
Operations in list order: union, subtract, intersect and smooth versions with a blend radius. Per object: position/rotation (also with a move/rotate gizmo), shell, inflate, texture strength, mirrors, radial copies and linear arrays.
Global geometry: offset, twist, bend, taper, hollowing (wall measured under the relief), bottom/top flat cuts.
Textures: all 12 patterns from relief/ with all their settings. The noise and patterns code is copied unchanged. The pattern is sampled at the projection onto the base surface and displaces along the normal. Depth, carve/emboss/centre, masks by object and by face direction (e.g. keep the bottom flat), fade above the cut, and pattern offset.
Meshing: marching cubes in a web-worker pool, slice by slice with shared vertices and empty-space skipping. A coarse pass runs first when the full preview is slow.
Export: closed binary STL, dropped to Z = 0. Also: undo/redo, scene in the URL, localStorage, save/open .json, and 8 presets.
Checks
A Node test on all primitives, booleans, hollow + cut, and textured + twisted fields: every mesh is watertight (each edge used exactly once in each direction, including across worker slabs). Volumes match the analytic ones within grid error. Orientation is outward.
Headless Chromium from file://: every preset builds with no console errors. Adding objects, changing ops, undo, the gizmo outline and STL download all work. The exported 585k-triangle STL had 0 open edges.

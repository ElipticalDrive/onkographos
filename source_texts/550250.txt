Summary
This PR introduces a novel 3D Facial Coherence Regularizer to the GaussianAvatars codebase, enhancing the stability and surface continuity of photorealistic head avatars under extreme expressions.
Details
Implements a new facial coherence loss that constrains local relative positions between neighboring Gaussians (defined by mesh topology) to maintain spatial consistency during facial deformations.
Supports face-adjacent and edge-adjacent neighbor graphs, with adaptive regularization strength for highly-deformed regions.
Integrates loss initialization and computation into the main training loop and progress/tensorboard reporting.
Adds new training arguments: --use_facial_coherence, --lambda_coherence, --coherence_use_face_neighbors, --coherence_use_edge_neighbors, and disables via --disable_coherence_edge_neighbors, --disable_coherence_face_neighbors.
Provides detailed theory, usage guidance, best practices, and reference documentation in INNOVATION3_USAGE.md and INNOVATION3_SUMMARY.md.
No breaking changes; all previous features remain untouched unless new arguments are enabled.

Adds a print-ready "clear brain shell" export, separate from the viewer's layered STL export (which is unchanged).
What it does
Builds surfaces from the nested regions (brain, WT, TC, NCR) using the existing segmentation and viewer_data._surface (marching-cubes core split out of _mesh, same viewer output).
Taubin smoothing, quadric decimation, pymeshlab repair, removal of tiny loose bits; reports volume before and after smoothing per layer.
manifold3d booleans with CLEARANCE: brain_clear, edema_green, enhancing_yellow, necrotic_red, each clipped to its parent so they nest without overlapping. Raised "L" on the left side.
MODE=solid_clear (multi-material in-place printing only, warns otherwise) or MODE=split_halves (sagittal cut through the tumour-core centroid, 2 magnet pockets per brain half, cut face on the bed).
Checks: walls under 1.2 mm, watertight (also as stored in float32), pairwise overlap, fit in BUILD_VOLUME with a suggested SCALE.
Writes a 3MF with named, coloured parts grouped as one object, per-part STLs, preview PNG and summary table.
Usage
```
python scripts/export_print_model.py --backup --no-network
python scripts/export_print_model.py --backup --mode split_halves --scale 1.3 --no-network
```
All parameters are in PrintConfig at the top of app/services/print_export.py. New dependencies: trimesh, manifold3d, pymeshlab, shapely, rtree, lxml, embreex (pinned in requirements-lock.txt).
Tests: 6 new tests on a synthetic phantom; backend suite 58 passed, repo suite 129 passed.
Known issues on the sample case: SCALE 1.5 doesn't fit 220 x 220 x 250 (use 1.30 or less). Edema has thin film areas where the tumour core reaches its edge. The necrotic core comes out in many small pieces. The 3MF hasn't been opened in PrusaSlicer or Bambu Studio yet.
Research and teaching prototype; model-predicted regions; not for clinical use.

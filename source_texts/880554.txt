Why. A cube larger than one node's memory, or a frame rate one node cannot reach, needs several nodes rendering one view.
What exists today. The backend runs on one SLURM node; scripts/launch_backend_slurm_dask.sh spreads computation (not rendering) over several nodes with Dask.
Scope
Render processes on several nodes of one SLURM job (MPI), each with its part of the bricks.
Parallel image compositing across nodes (IceT, as ParaView uses, or VTK's compositing), the backend on the first node sending the final frame through the push stream.
A launcher option for the number of rendering nodes.
Before building: evaluate reusing ParaView's server, OSPRay's MPI module or NVIDIA IndeX for this layer.
Done when
One view renders across at least two PLEIADI-GPU nodes and reaches the desktop through the tunnel.
Scaling with the number of nodes and the compositing cost are measured.
Notes. Step 3 of the scaling plan (#73); depends on step 2.

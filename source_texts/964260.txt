First stages of MIGRATION_SRESAMPLE.md: the SDF obstacle sampling moves from resampling/ to the sresample library (zulianp/sresample#1). external/sresample points at that PR's branch. Merge it first, then bump the pointer to the merge commit.
Changes
Option SFEM_ENABLE_SRESAMPLE (ON by default): sresample is built as a subproject.
It gets sfem's OpenMP, MPI, CUDA, testing and DEV_MODE settings, and sfem's real_t/geom_t/idx_t instantiation.
#cmakedefine and the installed config are updated to match.
Removed: resampling/{surface,interpolate,quadshell4,trishell3,beam2}. sresample now has their B-spline, surface and nodal sampling and the z-slab gather. One kernel per element serves host and device, so the GPU samples TRISHELL3 and BEAM2 too.
Contact code:
frontend/sfem_Obstacle.hpp (always built) holds the Obstacle interface and obstacle_unreachable, which replaces SFEM_SDF_UNREACHABLE.
SDFObstacle moves to frontend/sdf_contact/, built with sresample.
ContactConditions and NodeObstacleContact use only the interface; its new accumulate and weight calls replace a dynamic_cast and a direct sampler call.
Only the SDF factories, SDF tests and SDF drivers require the option.
On the device the obstacle takes its normal rows as a host array.
Tests: the B-spline tests moved to sresample. sfem_SDFSamplingTest keeps the contact-surface cases.
Drivers: gap_from_sdf and geometry_aware_gap_from_sdf reach sresample through drivers/resampling/sresample_gap_calls.hpp until they become sresample demos.
Checks
macOS, DEV_MODE, all options and Python: ctest 93/93 with SFEM_ENABLE_SRESAMPLE=ON, 84/84 with OFF.
Alps GH200, CUDA, DEV_MODE: build clean, ctest 82/82.
Same results as main (SDFObstacle on generated TRISHELL3, QUADSHELL4 and BEAM2 surfaces, one thread):
every output is bitwise equal, except the QUADSHELL4 lumped weights (at most 2 ulp, and the gap divided by them, relative 1.8e-16);
the cause is where the compiler places fused multiply-adds: with -ffp-contract=off they agree.
bench_MaMAL's MG SDF contact on one GH200 (sphere and multisphere, line search on and off, sizes 1–8, 2 repetitions, against main):
all 40 runs have identical iteration counts and residual histories;
time ratio head/base: geomean 1.000, 1.001 at sizes 6–8.
Open point
Outside the SDF grid, QUADSHELL4 elements report the reverse of their winding normal, while TRISHELL3 elements report it as wound. This is main's behaviour and is kept here.

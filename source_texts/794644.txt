PPLL capture resets shader-written storage before the required API-update synchronization, and residual emissive replay can inherit a previous batch's model matrix. Both correctness defects can occur at constant window dimensions without resource reallocation.
Changes:
Issue buffer-update and texture-update barriers before resetting the atomic counter and clearing the head texture. Preserve the existing shader-access barrier after the resets and the capture-to-resolve barrier.
Issue a buffer-update barrier before optional PPLL counter statistics readback.
Apply each face's model matrix immediately before emissive drawing in all four replay paths: rigged/unrigged, legacy/PBR. The existing rigged-depth replay ordering remains intact.

Compare against PPLL disabled for regressions. For symptom attribution, isolate the barrier and transform changes separately if needed.

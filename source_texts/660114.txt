Tracking issue for M39 — Texture streaming, the promise in docs/prd/texture-streaming.md (APPROVED 2026-11-03, rev 4).
Every streamable 2D DDS becomes sampleable at its floor on first use, gains finer mips only while something on screen or in the ray-traced scene wants them, and loses them again, least recently wanted first, when the texture pool goes over its budget. Floors are always resident, no texture ever samples a non-resident level, and a budget overrun never turns a surface into the missing-texture checker.
Decisions (§10, all decided 2026-11-03, each the recommendation): Q1 reallocate on change (new image per promotion/demotion, shared levels copied on the GPU, old image retired through pending_destroy); Q2 (a) CPU wanted-mip estimate from instance bounds, distance and a per-mesh UV texel density captured at NIF import, (b) BSA promotions re-extract through the stream pool; Q3 floor = the last BA2 DX10 chunk, and the census edge for every other source; Q4 budget = a fraction of the live VK_EXT_memory_budget DEVICE_LOCAL budget minus the ledger's non-texture rows; Q5 two settings (budget override, streaming switch), presets at defaults.
Waves: Wave 1 = BA2 games (FO4, Starfield); Wave 2 = BSA games (Oblivion, FO3, FNV, Skyrim SE).
Phase issues are listed below. Commits reference them with Refs #N and name the requirement IDs they satisfy (Satisfies TXS-UP-003); a phase issue closes only when its PRD §9 exit holds.

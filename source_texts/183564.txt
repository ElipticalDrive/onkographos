Abstract. We find an asymptotic expression of the volume of the intersection of the dimensional sphere with random half spaces. This expression coincides with the one found by E. Gardner ([3]) using replica calculations. We get also the same value for. Our proof is rigorous and based on the cavity method. The required decay of correlations is obtained by means of a geometrical argument which holds for general hamiltonians. c des sciences/Éditions scientifiques et médicales Elsevier SA

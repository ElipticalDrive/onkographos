Summary of Changes
Multi-Contour Cell Decomposition: Implemented DMC cell decomposition in extracting variable dual vertices (1 to 4) per voxel for true 2-manifold multi-sheet resolution.

Deterministic Sort-and-Gather Pipeline: Zero dynamic hash tables; uses 64-bit integer edge keys and Thrust radix sort.

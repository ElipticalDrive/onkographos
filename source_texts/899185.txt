Summary
Direct lighting done properly: traced light records built from the same light
definitions the raster path reads, next-event estimation choosing one light or the
environment per bounce, the power heuristic weighting area lights and the
environment, and shadow rays that accumulate attenuation through transmissive and
alpha-tested surfaces instead of testing simple occlusion.
Parent epic: #129
Acceptance criteria
The light array is a storage buffer unbounded by the raster path's eight-slot
uniform; a forty-light scene renders every light, and the capability descriptor
reports the difference so the UI can state it.
Punctual lights (point, spot, directional) contribute as delta lights with
weight one; rect and disc area lights and the environment are combined with the
power heuristic.
Soft shadows emerge from sampling light extent, including the radius the light
definitions gain for point and spot softness; no shadow maps are involved.
Shadow rays attenuate through transmissive and alpha-tested hits, so tinted
glass tints its shadow.
Light-sampling probability densities match their samplers by histogram test,
and a two-light analytic scene matches its closed-form answer within tolerance.
Verification
cargo test -p solarxy-renderer and a converged comparison of an area-lit scene
against the analytic fixture.

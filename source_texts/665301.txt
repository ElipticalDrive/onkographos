Summary
Lasso: free-form polygon selection — click to place vertices, close by clicking near the first vertex; rubber-band preview between clicks; undo/cancel reverts edge marks on the real volume
HoleRim2D: selects the closed rim of a hole in an axis-aligned coplanar surface; bounded BFS in the face plane; tries multiple axis planes automatically so clicking any face of a rim voxel works
ColumnRim2D: selects the cross-section ring of a bounded column, pillar, or pipe; tries the clicked face's plane first, falls back to the other two axes if that slice is unbounded; optional locked normal axis for explicit control
HoleRim3D: finds the shortest loop of surface voxels encircling a hole on curved/non-planar surfaces via BFS + non-tree-edge cycle detection; per-cycle planarity projection, figure-8 (there-and-back) rejection, and 3D centroid air check to filter false positives
Test plan
Build and run SelectBrushTest — 4 HoleRim3D tests, Lasso square/cancel tests, HoleRim2D/ColumnRim2D tests all pass
Lasso: click 3+ vertices on a flat surface, close polygon, verify interior voxels selected and edge marks reverted
HoleRim2D: click rim of a simple floor hole, verify only rim voxels selected
ColumnRim2D: click top of a tall pillar (bounded), click side (falls back to horizontal cross-section), click large flat floor (unbounded → nothing selected)
HoleRim3D: click rim of a well opening, a hollow tube, a 1-voxel-thin square tube; verify correct ring selected and no false positives on large flat floors

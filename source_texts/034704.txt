This paper presents the basic principles for the visualization of objects which are defined by implicit\nfunctions and CSG trees. The basic principles (Marching cubes, Marching tetrahedra and Marching triangles)\nfor iso-surfaces rendering of such objects are compared. A new fast modification of the Marching triangles\nalgorithm is presented and compared with others algorithms. It is based on the space subdivision\ntechnique that enabled a significant speed-up of the Marching triangles algorithm. The speed-up grows with\nthe grid resolution in which the object is represented. The presented algorithm is convenient for objects with\nlarge smooth and complex surfaces. The method produces a triangular mesh that consists of well-shaped\ntriangles.

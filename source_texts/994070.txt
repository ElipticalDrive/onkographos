G115
voxel.Mesh.RemeshAll did not apply a mesher cvar change in F2.9's bisect. Traced on main:
Streaming running, it worked. It re-meshed every interior and restaled every seam. The finding's seam guess was wrong: the seam registry restales a chunk's seams whatever content version it is passed.
Streaming paused, it did nothing. F2.9 held its scene with vox.Freeze 1 streaming. The paused tick (F3.4's) launched only the chunks a recreated scene proxy lost and ran no seam scheduler, so RemeshAll's dirtied chunks and restaled seams waited for the resume.
Evidence. PlanetTest, 45°N 20°E, a 16:48 sun. A temporary per-job probe cvar moved every chunk and seam mesh 60 uu; shots are from the pawn's view, with the saves set aside before every PIE. Mean absolute difference out of 255 (the run-to-run floor is 0.23–0.40, the probe itself 7.5):

Change
UVoxelChunkManager::RemeshAllLoadedChunks() marks every loaded chunk dirty. While streaming is paused it also enlists the Loaded and Meshing chunks for the paused tick and turns on its seam rebuild. A chunk already queued for a LOD change keeps that request for the resume.
TickPausedRenderRecovery is renamed TickWhilePaused, with the set and flag renamed PausedRemeshChunks and bPausedRemeshActive. It launches the enlisted chunks' meshes and keeps a chunk that is re-queued after its mesh lands. After a paused RemeshAll it also runs TickSeamScheduler (new bPausedSeamRebuild).
voxel.Mesh.RemeshAll calls it and says in its log when streaming is paused.
New test VoxelWorlds.Streaming.RemeshAll.Planet / .Plane (a DC planet and an MC plane, three LOD bands), with streaming running and paused. It checks for a mesh job per loaded chunk, every chunk mesh and seam bucket submitted again, no residency or LOD change, and identical geometry. Without the fix, the paused half runs 0 jobs (verified).
FRecordingMeshRenderer now counts submissions per chunk and per seam owner.
Docs: STREAMING_AND_SEAMS "One tick" and its diagnostics row, and the renamed tick in RENDERING_SYSTEM.
Normal streaming is unchanged.

RemeshAll re-meshed every interior and restaled every seam whenever streaming
ran (a per-job mesher cvar change showed in PIE), but it changed nothing while
streaming was paused (`vox.Freeze 1 streaming`, `voxel.Stream.Enabled 0`), the
usual way to hold a scene for an A/B: the paused tick launched only the chunks
a recreated scene proxy lost (F3.4) and ran no seam scheduler, so the dirtied
chunks and their restaled seams waited for the resume. F2.9's bisect held its
scene that way, which is how G115 was seen.

- UVoxelChunkManager::RemeshAllLoadedChunks: marks every loaded chunk dirty
  (MarkChunkDirty restales its seams whatever the content version); while
  paused it enlists the Loaded and Meshing ones for the paused tick and turns
  on its seam rebuild. A chunk already queued (a LOD change) is not enlisted.
- The paused tick (TickPausedRenderRecovery -> TickWhilePaused; the set and
  flag renamed PausedRemeshChunks, bPausedRemeshActive) launches those chunks'
  meshes, keeps a chunk re-queued after its mesh landed, and runs the seam
  scheduler once a paused RemeshAll asked for it.
- voxel.Mesh.RemeshAll calls it and says when streaming is paused.
- Test Streaming.RemeshAll.Planet / .Plane (DC planet, MC plane, three LOD
  bands): streaming running and paused, a mesh job per loaded chunk, every
  chunk mesh and seam bucket submitted again, no residency or LOD change, the
  geometry as before. Without the fix the paused half runs 0 jobs.
- FRecordingMeshRenderer counts submissions per chunk and seam owner.

Normal streaming is unchanged.

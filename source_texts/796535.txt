Thank you for your interest in our work!
When fine-tuning a checkpoint at iteration 30000 using the command
```
python train.py --splatting_config configs/hierarchical.json -s <path to COLMAP or NeRF Synthetic dataset> --start_checkpoint <checkpoint path> --iterations 35000
```
training will resume at iteration 30001. Since the default value of densify_until_iter is 15000, densification will not be performed.

CV-9 2: the light casts shadows (cascaded shadow maps from a directional sun).
Light → Cast shadows (on at first, effective with Shading). A lit color is multiplied by a + (1 − a) · max(n · l, 0) · v, v the light reaching the point (0–1).
Cast and receive: the ground (colors or materials, beyond the limits too), the course map's terrain and its walls, the Visible walls — as drawn: heights stretched, the terrain at the view's own stride, the course map's walls only at stride 1. Lines/marks, translucent curtains and the sky neither cast nor receive (pictures come in CV-9c).
Cascades: 4 maps of 2048² (DEPTH_COMPONENT32F array, 64 MB; one map in a parallel view), splitting the view's depth from the nearest to the farthest shown ground (boxes of the visible chunks/terrain blocks), splits 0.8 geometric. Each map covers the bounding sphere of its piece of the view; splits and radius are held to steps (2^¼, 2^⅛) and the centre to whole texels, so the maps do not swim as the camera moves or drives; each map reaches toward the sun as far as any caster, so what lies outside the view casts into it.
Look-up: the point moved 1.5 texels along its facet's normal (no acne without a depth offset that would detach shadows at a wall's foot), half a texel of depth bias, 3×3 bilinear compares; each cascade's last tenth blends into the next.
Export PNG: maps as fine against the picture's pixels as the screen's against the screen's, at most 4096² (256 MB for four); the same course, view and settings give the same picture.
Vertex attributes now have fixed locations so the depth programs share the vertex arrays.
Development: the Light paragraph and the Export sentence.
Files: tools/course-viewer and Development only.

multiplies its share: a + (1 − a) · max(n · l, 0) · v. The sun draws
cascaded shadow maps (four of 2048 texels; one in a parallel view) over the

terrain and walls and the visible walls cast and receive, as drawn; points
are read a texel and a half off their facet, 3 by 3 bilinear compares, the
cascades blended over their last tenth. Export PNG draws its maps finer.

the vertex arrays.

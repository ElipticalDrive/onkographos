What's the best approach to generate meshes for chunks?
I use chunks that are 32^3 in size. Each 'block' has a model class that is fed a reference to the six neighboring blocks, once for each block in the chunk. This means to mesh a chunk, 6* (32^3) calls to the chunks 'get block' method are used.

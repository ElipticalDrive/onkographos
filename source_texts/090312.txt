PDF Volume Rendering Using the Fourier Projection-slice Theorem
The Fourier projection-slice theorem states that the inverse transform of a slice extracted from the frequency domain representation of a volume yields a projection of the volume in a direction perpendicular to the slice. This theorem allows the generation of attenuation-only renderings of volume data in O(N2 log N) time for a volume of size N3.

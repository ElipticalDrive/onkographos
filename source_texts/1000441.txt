Follow-up to #734 (native noise), from the fractal_clouds profile: #736 (comment) (items 3 and the per-call table).
Gap: the two top lines of their profile are octave loops that ridged2/fbm2 can't express, so they stay in PostScript at about 6–8 µs per call, against 0.29 µs for native ridged2:
fc-ridged, a Musgrave ridged multifractal. Each octave is weighted by the previous one, with offset/sharpness and gain, the domain rotates about 37° per octave, and the last octave is fractional so detail fades with distance.
fc-eroded, a derivative-damped fBm: each octave is divided by 1 + |accumulated gradient|², built on gnoise2d.
Proposed: two native operators, with names that don't collide with artkit, plus PostScript references in lib/noiseref.ps that are bit-equal (same frozen-algorithm rules as #734: fixed operation order, no transcendentals outside src/fmath):
ridgedmf2 (or an options-dict form of ridged2): x y octaves(real) lacunarity gain offset rotation → n
erodedfbm2: x y octaves(real) lacunarity gain → n
Settle the exact signatures against Musgrave's published ridged multifractal and the widely used derivative-damped ("eroded") fBm formulation. Document them in the psart skill. Keep the existing operators bit-identical.
Done when: tests/noise.rs holds both bit-equal to the references; the per-call cost is under 1 µs at 5 octaves; there's a skill example.

Summary of work (PR #851)
Delivered
src/ops/noise.rs: x y oct gain lac offset wgain angle ridgedmf2 v (Musgrave's ridged multifractal) and x y oct gain lac angle erodedfbm2 v (derivative-damped fBm). The octave walk is frozen in the module header.
lib/noiseref.ps: nrridgedmf2 and nrerodedfbm2, bit-equal PostScript twins.
tests/noise.rs: the bit-equality grid, pinned values, edge cases, errors, and --sweep-seed.
Catalog rows in src/capabilities.rs, a runnable psart skill example, the pscat skill listing, the artkit guide, HANDOFF, and notes/845-ridged-multifractal-eroded-fbm.md.
Decisions
Operand order is oct gain lac first, as in fbm2/ridged2, not the issue's sketch. gain is the amplitude ratio (Musgrave's H gives gain = lac^-H), and his feedback gain is wgain.
Real oct in 0..32: the fractional last octave is scaled by the remainder.
angle is in degrees, applied per octave with lac; the k*SHIFT offset is kept.
Ridged weight follows Musgrave: the weighted signal times wgain, clamped to 0..1. His early exit is dropped.
Eroded fBm is the common Quilez formulation: the gradient is summed unrotated, with no chain rule. fractal_clouds co-rotates its accumulator; the notes record the difference.
One IEEE division per octave in erodedfbm2. It is correctly rounded, and the module header names the exception.
Neither operator normalizes, like the rest of the noise family.
Review record
Plan and implementation were reviewed by the advisor.
Codex round 1 found nothing (see above).
Negative controls: mutating the reference (dropping the clamp, re-associating one addition) failed the bit-equality test.

Textures upload whole, all mips, when their material arrives. Materials themselves are small (a slot and ~100 bytes of parameters) and are fine to add when a renderable enters the scene; textures are what is worth streaming.
Proposal: coarse mips first, finer ones as the GPU reports they are needed: culling (or the shading passes) record per texture the finest mip sampled, read back a frame later; the bridge uploads the missing mips within a budget (FrameUploads), and evicts unused fine mips under pressure. The sampler's min LOD clamps to what is resident, so nothing samples missing data.
Scene side: true-sim/scene#13 (mips stream coarse first).

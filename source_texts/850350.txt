(take this with a grain of salt as I'm not entirely sure myself)
My suspicion is that the SDF values are computed like this rather than directly feeding the section endpoints into the network to enforce a kind of geometric consistency. Essentially, the SDF normals (network gradients wrt coordinate inputs), ray directions, and SDF values at the section endpoints should follow a certain mathematical relationship that is actually used during the upsampling in this line of code:
NeuS/models/renderer.py
Line 144
in
629b9cf
cos_val = (next_sdf - prev_sdf) / (next_z_vals - prev_z_vals + 1e-5)
As you can see, this piece of code goes in the other direction: it uses the difference between the consecutive SDF values to estimate the cosine value (between the ray direction and the SDF normal) instead.

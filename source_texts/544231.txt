Overview
This update establishes the foundation for scalable rendering of large and complex scenes through GPU scene management, geometry streaming, meshlet-based visibility, Forward+ lighting, and virtualized shadow rendering. In parallel, this significantly expands rendering fidelity through physically based atmospheric lighting, ReSTIR direct illumination, ray traced global illumination (RTGI), ray traced reflections, screen space reflection improvements, and advanced denoising techniques.
Beyond visual improvements, this work refactors and consolidates multiple rendering subsystems, removes legacy infrastructure, improves render graph correctness, strengthens Vulkan platform support, and enhances engine scalability for future rendering features. Collectively, these changes represent a major step toward a modern, physically based, GPU-driven renderer capable of supporting high-fidelity real-time experiences across a wide range of content scales and hardware configurations.

Introduced GPU scene driven rendering pipeline.
Added GPU scene material routing and primitive range management.
Uploaded canonical GPU surface material data.
Synchronized extracted scene geometry through subsystem integration.
Preserved primitive ranges and visibility information across rendering stages.

Stabilized GPU scene LOD selection.
Shadow Rendering
Integrated virtual shadow rendering through scene subsystems.
Added GPU-driven local shadow rasterization.
Improved local shadow prioritization and distance fading.
Preserved partial shadow atlas updates.
Stabilized virtual shadow page sampling.
Unified shadow sampling for Forward+ rendering.
Added material-opacity-aware shadow rendering.
Visibility & Occlusion
Corrected Hi-Z occlusion semantics.
Integrated temporal Hi-Z visibility.
Added meshlet occlusion culling.
Removed legacy visibility shader infrastructure.
Lighting & Materials
Added physically based atmospheric scattering.
Improved clustered light assignment.

Implemented Forward+ shading for the GPU scene.
Added layered clear coat material support.

Shared physical surface lighting implementations.

Added analytical and emissive light sampling.
Added finite emitter sampling.
Added hit visibility tracing.

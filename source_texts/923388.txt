What this is
The full new-game setup screen a player walks through before starting a colony: choosing difficulty, starting citizens, a landing zone, and starting supplies, one step at a time.
Why it matters
This is the first thing a new player sees, so a confusing or overloaded setup screen risks losing them before the game even starts.
Current state
Carried over to the new engine's board unchanged.
Design locked: four decisions in a straight line: difficulty, roster, zone, loadout; no storyteller pick.
All setup choices travel together from step to step, so none get lost.
The difficulty preset sets how many starting citizens are offered.
Rerolling a citizen uses a fixed seed, so it can tie into family traits later.
2026-10-24: the previewer shows the landing banner and reveal ring from the start (fog of war, #380).
The difficulty step: list on the left, description and live preview on the right.
Done when
Four decisions (difficulty, roster, zone, loadout) accumulate into one options resource with no modal pause; world commit takes the struct.

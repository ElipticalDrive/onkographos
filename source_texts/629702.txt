Performance
Multi-component updates fall from 4,632–11,112 to 2,472 bytes; five moving components save 8,640 bytes (about 78%). Idle and single-component allocations remain 312 and 2,472 bytes respectively. Multi-component CPU timing medians improve by about 50–82%; single-component medians improve by 1–15%.
Idle timing medians range from about 4% faster to 5% slower, with overlapping ranges. This remains draft for the idle-path timing cost and uncertainty. These CPU measurements do not establish frame rate or GPU throughput.

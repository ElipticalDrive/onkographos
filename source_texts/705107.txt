Roadmap: existing Slice 5 / Milestone 16.
Goal
Add a native high-fidelity splat path initialized from accepted reconstruction rather than treating provider splats as the only splat source.
Scope
Initialize and optimize position, covariance, opacity, and appearance from accepted cameras/points; provide browser viewing and a portable export representation.
Acceptance
Training uses accepted camera/evidence identities.
Optimization failure cannot invalidate the underlying mesh/point reconstruction.
Quality/performance can be compared against dense mesh/point representations.
Portable splat output round-trips through the viewer.
GPU/runtime requirements are explicit.

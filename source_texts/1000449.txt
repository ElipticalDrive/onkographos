Part of the terrain look plan (docs/research/terrain-look.md). Serves N2 and M1.
The continent's shape comes from the built-in Noise: fractal value noise with no rotation between octaves, so its features line up with the grid. Its ridges are one fold of the summed noise (1-|2*fbm-1|) rather than a per-octave ridged multifractal. Every reference starts from warped gradient or ridged noise. FastNoise already offers Ridged fractals and domain warp.
Scope:
move the continent's and the presets' ridges and hills to warped, ridged FastNoise;
add a Pow expression (and any other operator the shapes need), so a pack can shape amplitude by height or slope;
leave Noise's output alone, since saved worlds and digests depend on it (N9).
Done when
tests/presets.rs's sweeps, tests/fastnoise.rs and the continent tests pass;
contact sheets are regenerated and attached;
the changes to digests are noted in the PR.

splatreg registers native Gaussian splats: it finds the rigid (SE(3)) or similarity (Sim(3), with scale) transform that aligns two 3DGS scans, then merges and dedupes them into one splat. It combines a global initializer (super-Fibonacci SO(3) sweep / FPFH+RANSAC / learned correspondences) with a Levenberg-Marquardt refinement over ICP and a closed-form-Jacobian Gaussian-SDF residual derived directly from the target Gaussians. Pure PyTorch; no meshing, no CUDA extension.

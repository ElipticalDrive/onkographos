Here are the pre-trained models (five training rounds) for FERN: a fetal echocardiography network for 2D-to-3D registration, MIDL 2025.  Models are trained on end-diastole standard view frames (4CH, LVOT, RVOT, 3VV), SVoRT architecture with 1 iteration, downsampled resolution grid of 1x1 mm and 94x94 image size.

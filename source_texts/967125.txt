Depends on #35187 for optional per-room influence bounds.
Adds optional DDGI-style visibility weighting to LightProbeGrid to reduce light leaking through walls. Rasterized probe captures store distance moments; shading blends probes with wrapped-normal weights and Chebyshev visibility. Inactive probes are excluded.
visibilityInterpolation selects eight-probe 'trilinear' interpolation (default) or four-probe 'tetrahedral' interpolation to reduce per-pixel costs, with possible directional artifacts. All three examples expose this choice in the GUI.
Based on DDGI, NVIDIA RTXGI, and tetrahedral interpolation on regular grids.
```
const probes = new LightProbeGrid( 5.6, 4.7, 5.6, 6, 6, 6 );
probes.visibility = true;
probes.visibilityInterpolation = 'trilinear'; // Or 'tetrahedral' for four probes.
probes.visibilityGridSize = 8;
probes.bake( renderer, scene, { cubemapSize: 32, near: 0.05, far: 20, bounces: 3 } );
scene.add( probes );
```

Title
Using Mesh Shaders for isosurface extraction
Content partner
University of Waikato
Collection
ResearchCommons@Waikato
Description
This thesis investigates the use of the Mesh Shader graphics pipeline, utilising in particular its ability of parallel and dynamic choice of geometry, to gain computation efficiencies in realising the Marching Cubes isosurface extraction algorithm. The thesis details how the Marching Cubes algorithm was mapped to the Mesh Shader pipeline, to form a baseline implementation.

Follow-on to the P3 general-filters work (part of #125). The initial P3 ships the standard trio — Triangle (bilinear), CatmullRom (bicubic a=-0.5), Lanczos3 — validated against Pillow parity, plus upscale.
The FilterKernel trait is unsealed and the engine is generic over (kernel × coordinate-convention × edge-policy), so the following are purely ADDITIVE — each is a FilterKernel impl plus its own parity suite, no infrastructure change:
Mitchell-Netravali (B=1/3, C=1/3) — the high-quality general cubic.
OpenCV-exact kernels: INTER_CUBIC (Keys a=-0.75) and INTER_LANCZOS4 (a=4), for cv2 parity beyond the box INTER_AREA already matched.
ffmpeg / swscale filter conventions.
zimg filter conventions.
Each is its own small PR. Tracked as enhancements; not blocking the core P3 trio.

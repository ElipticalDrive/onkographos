When the TAA was used, the current code avoided to use the history buffer for the temporal volumetric integration. It used a some kind of simplification.
Instead of it, we use the history buffer to "mix" the volumetric from one frame to another (as it was recommended by https://es.slideshare.net/DICEStudio/physically-based-and-unified-volumetric-rendering-in-frostbite and only used for viewport offline rendering until now).
Regarding performance i didn't see negative effects.

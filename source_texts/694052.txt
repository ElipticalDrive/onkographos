Seed KNN traversal with current root index
The neighbour-search kernel always pushes node 0 as the root (push_node(0, …)). After any insertion that extends the octree bounds, ensure_root_bounds appends a new root node to host_nodes_ and updates root_index_ to the last slot instead of relocating it to index 0. As a consequence, when the tree grows beyond its initial bounds the kernel starts traversing from the former root and never visits the newly created root or its children, so any points inserted outside the original volume are skipped from KNN queries. The traversal should seed from root_index_ (captured into the kernel) instead of assuming the root lives at index 0.

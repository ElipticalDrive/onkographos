Owner
Motivation
Make cloud lighting more physically plausible by attenuating sun light with the transmittance LUT, replacing a fixed ambient term with sampled sky irradiance, adding powder and silver-lining effects, and giving artists control over forward/backward lobes.
Pack new lighting controls into the GPU cloud struct so the renderer can evaluate dual-lobe phase, powder, and silver-lining without extra CPU/GPU bandwidth.
Description
Shader: extended VolumetricCloud in raytrace.comp.wgsl with lighting_params0/lighting_params1, implemented a dual-lobe Henyey–Greenstein phase via cloud_phase_lobe, and added cloud_powder_term and cloud_silver_lining helpers.
Lighting: replaced the single fixed ambient term with sky_ambient_probe(...) (sampling sky_view_lut), used atmosphere transmittance to attenuate sun color for cloud samples, applied the powder and silver-lining modifiers to direct lighting, and blended per-sample aerial-perspective tinting via aerial_perspective_lut.
CPU/GPU packing: extended VolumetricCloud component with forward_anisotropy, backward_anisotropy, lobe_blend, powder_strength, and silver_lining_strength, exposed those in the inspector, and packed them into GpuVolumetricCloud as lighting_params0/lighting_params1 in scene.rs and object.rs.
Integration: applied aerial perspective both to individual cloud samples and to the final integrated cloud color before returning from composite_clouds, and tuned authoring defaults to better noon/sunset/horizon/backlit behavior.

Ran git diff --check with no reported whitespace or diff issues (success).

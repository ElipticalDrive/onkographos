Summary
Cloud shadows multiply direct sunlight by the cloud transmittance and drop the blocked light completely, so terrain, grass and distant trees under a cloud lose their shading and AO reads flat.
This adds a Scattering setting (default 0.4, range 0–1). That fraction of the blocked direct light comes back as a soft, wrapped skylight term facing the cloud deck. It's applied in Lighting.hlsl, RunGrass.hlsl (all three paths) and DistantTree.hlsl.
ShadowSampling::GetWorldShadow gets an overload that also reports how much light the cloud blocked. The existing two-argument form is unchanged for other callers.
Effects 11 still controls Opacity. Scattering stays a user setting outside the "managed by Effects 11" lock.
Cloud Shadows .ini goes from 1-6-0 to 1-7-0 (new setting).

Differences from the fork version
GetCloudScatterLighting returns early when nothing is scattered, and normalizes DirLightDirection + up with a guarded rsqrt. Every caller adds the result to ambient unconditionally, even without Cloud Shadows, so a light pointing straight down would otherwise turn the ambient term into NaN.
Otherwise identical; it applied with a 3-way merge.

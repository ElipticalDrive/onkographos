The 75k -> 2.1M gaussian blowup drives backward, Adam, and sort cost (throughput halves). Taming 3DGS's score-based constructive densification hits an exact budget (4-5x smaller models at competitive quality); gsplat's MCMC config caps at 1M with relocation instead of clone/split (needs 4D re-derivation of split preservation).
Experiment: caps at 1.0M and 1.5M vs the 2M baseline on the 8-cam split; adopt the largest saving that holds held-out PSNR. Expected up to ~2x end-to-end if 1M holds.

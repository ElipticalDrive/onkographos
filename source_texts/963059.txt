Brings back the baked edge ambient occlusion that the greedy box-face mesher had dropped — without giving up the O(boxes) meshing cost.
How
Each exposed face rectangle is tessellated with a 1-cell border (splitCoords → at most a 3×3 patch: corner cells, edge strips, one big interior quad). Per vertex, AO darkens by how many of the four outside-layer cells meeting at that vertex are filled (aoAt), reusing the old [1.0, 0.88, 0.74, 0.5] ramp:
concave edges/corners (a neighbour box rises beside the face) → darkened within ~1 cell, exactly like the old per-cell AO;
flat faces and convex silhouette edges → no occluders → stay bright;
the interior is still a single bright quad, so a 1000³ box is ~54 triangles (6 faces × 9 patches) instead of 6 — still trivial.
Occluder lookups use the existing grid has() index. Transparent (glass) surfaces skip AO, as before.
deno fmt/lint/check/test/build all green. Only render.ts changes.
Worth an eyeball in the browser — the AO strength is the AO4 ramp; say the word if it's too strong/subtle. (It's seam-free because the per-vertex value is symmetric in the 4 surrounding cells, so shared vertices agree.)

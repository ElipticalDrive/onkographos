Thank you for your interest in our MixNeRF. :)
First of all, we perform coarse sampling and fine sampling with a single MLP as same as mip-NeRF.
And the estimated mu is exactly the same as the estimated color.
In other words, we use the notation mu for the estimated colors in our MixNeRF because we leverage the mixture modeling framework and we use the estimated colors as the location parameter mu for our mixture model.
However, it's just the difference in the notation, the mu and color are not the different outputs, they are the same.
Regarding the FlipNeRF code, yes we will but we are polishing our code yet, so please stay tuned for our FlipNeRF. :)
And thank you for your interest in our works again. :)

Sorry for the late reply. :(
We use the MSE Loss only for the original input rays because we conjecture that the quality of rendered pixel values can be degraded if we use the MSE Loss on both of the original input rays and flipped reflection rays, which are not the GT rays but newly generated auxiliary rays.

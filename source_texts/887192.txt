Wanted (the maintainer, 2026-10-30): at the highest zoom the waveforms look jagged and unpleasant — the 1 px polyline through the sample points is drawn aliased, so a gentle slope becomes a staircase between the markers. They should look smooth, without changing the data: every line still goes exactly through every sample point, and sharp drops and spikes (a single sample far from its neighbours) stay exactly as sharp as they are. So no curve fitting or interpolation of the signal — the lines between the points are drawn anti-aliased.
Today: ours draws the waveforms as GDI-like aliased pixel lines on purpose (oscgeom::PixelLines, record entry L9.180), matching the original's GDI Polyline.
To do: anti-aliased segments (egui's feathered strokes, or our own coverage-based line drawing) between the exact sample points, the sample markers unchanged; the same for the diagrams' curves if they look the same; check at 100 % and at 125 / 150 % scaling (Blurry text at fractional display scaling (125 % / 150 %) #5 / Waveforms and pixel lines look broken at fractional display scaling #6) that a line still reads as 1 px. Decide (ask the maintainer) whether it is a setting (e.g. «Сглаживание линий», on by default) or always on. Tests: a spike sample keeps its peak pixel; the polyline passes through every sample's pixel centre.
A deliberate improvement over the original (aliased GDI lines), documented in docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md.
Filed at the maintainer's request (2026-10-30).

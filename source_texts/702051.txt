There are a few ways to do this:
compute vertex normals (using cell-centred finite-differences) and interpolate between them
use a cubic interpolation (which is C1 rather than C0), but this requires a numerical root solve rather than a quick quadratic solve
Both ways will require storing an extra layer of vertices around the edge of the tile (so a level-10 tile would have 1024 cells along one axis but 1027 vertices)

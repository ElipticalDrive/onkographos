Fast ray tracing procedure using space division
with uniform rectangular grid | Electronics Letters
Skip to main content
Electronics Letters
Contents
Abstract
A fast ray tracing procedure using space division is presented with the assumption that the reflection/transmission surfaces exactly coincide with the grid lines. Cleary's algorithm is used for the fast space traversing of a ray. The CPU time for the proposed method is approximately 14% of that of the visibility procedure.

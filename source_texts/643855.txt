Hi Xiaolong,
Thanks for making this great repo for 3D computer vision!
Just would like to mention three of our recent works:
"3D human pose, shape and texture from low-resolution images and videos" (TPAMI 2021, repo: https://github.com/xuxy09/RSC-Net) for robust 3D human reconstruction through contrastive learning;
"3D Human Texture Estimation from a Single Image with Transformers" (ICCV 2021 Oral, repo: https://github.com/xuxy09/Texformer) for self-supervised 3D human reconstruction;
"Geometry-Guided Progressive NeRF for Generalizable and Efficient Neural Human Rendering" (ECCV 2022, repo: https://github.com/sail-sg/GP-Nerf) for efficient and accurate human rendering.
It would be great if these works can be added to your paper list and shared with a wider audience.

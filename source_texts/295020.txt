Summary
Adds Voxel Frontier, a single-file, offline-capable Minecraft-style voxel game rendered with Three.js, plus a homepage door linking to it. It follows the site's existing standalone-game convention (like medevac/).
Play: https://madebymatt.uk/voxel/
Engine systems
Infinite streaming terrain — 16×16×64 chunks generated on demand around the player and freed when out of range. Surface height from 4-octave Simplex noise; underground caves carved by a ridged 3D value-noise field. Includes trees, water at sea level, sand beaches and snow peaks.
Optimized rendering — per-chunk meshing with hidden-face culling and baked per-vertex ambient occlusion. A spawn chunk of 7,032 blocks meshes to ~3,994 triangles (≈4.7% of a naïve cube-per-block build).
AABB physics — player box resolved independently per axis (no wall-sticking), with gravity, jumping, sprinting and step-safe collision against the voxel grid.
DDA raycast editing — break (left click) and place (right click) using the hit block's face normal; won't place inside the player; edits persist across chunk unload/reload.
UI & extras — 9-slot hotbar (keys 1–9 / scroll), live day/night lighting cycle, and mobile touch controls (drag to look + on-screen pad and mine/place/jump buttons).
Files
voxel/index.html — the complete game (HTML/CSS + engine).
voxel/vendor/three.min.js — Three.js r128 vendored locally so the game works with no CDN and no installs, matching the site's offline-first games.
voxel/README.md — play link and an explanation of how the engine works.
site.json — adds a "Voxel Frontier" door to the homepage.
Notes / decisions
Vendored Three.js instead of a CDN. The existing games load zero external scripts and are marketed as "works offline," so a CDN dependency would break that promise.
Skipped real-time shadow maps and dynamic (flowing) water. Both are heavy across a streaming world and would jeopardize the 60 FPS target; AO + day/night directional shading provides the depth, and water is rendered as static transparent blocks. Straightforward to add later.
Testing
Ran a headless smoke test of the pure engine logic (with Three.js loaded in Node) confirming: terrain heights in range, chunk generation + meshing producing geometry with face culling at ~5% of naïve triangle counts, AABB collision correct (no hit above ground, hit when inside it), and trees generating across chunks. Full HTML/JS syntax-checked.

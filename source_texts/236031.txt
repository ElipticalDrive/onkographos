Describe the bug
The NeRF camera-to-world algorithm in backend/ml/nerf/camera.py sends the center ray away from look_at, and transposes its row-stored basis when rotating rays. A camera at [0,0,2] looking at [0,0,0] emits [0,0,1] at the center: its dot product with the viewing direction is -1. A rotated pose at [1,2,3] has center dot -0.4563. Raster coordinates include borders rather than pixel centers, including an off-axis single pixel. Degenerate poses emit NaNs and aliased caller arrays can invalidate the cached basis.
To Reproduce
Create CameraPose(np.array([0.,0.,2.]), np.zeros(3)), then inspect get_ray_directions(60,(3,3))[1,1]. Repeat at [1,2,3], then with position equal to look_at or an up vector parallel to the viewing axis. Inspect the 1x1 ray and mutate the supplied position after construction.
Expected behavior
Build a consistent right-handed camera-to-world basis with camera -Z toward look_at, raster Y directed downward and documented pixel-center intrinsics. Preserve one owned pose generation across origins, directions and matrix results, and reject invalid or unrepresentable geometry explicitly.
Screenshots
Not applicable; direct native camera geometry reproduction.
Environment (please complete the following information):
OS: macOS / native Linux gate planned
App Version: main 938da14

Python 3.12 / NumPy 1.26.4
Additional context
Original #3629/#3663 introduced this camera helper. Existing #17182/#17183 repairs model skip binding and volume integration, changing model.py only; this proposal changes camera geometry exclusively. routes/nerf_routes.py consumes the spiral/orbital poses, but full service rendering remains constrained by the existing model bug and is not claimed here. The original NeRF reference uses -Z and inverted raster Y: https://github.com/bmild/nerf/blob/master/run_nerf_helpers.py . Pixel-center sampling is explicitly documented here, rather than its historical integer-index offset.
Acceptance criteria:
Construct a right-handed orthonormal camera basis and transform camera -Z toward look_at for arbitrary valid poses.
Generate unit rays at documented pixel centers with horizontal FOV and consistent downward raster Y, including rectangular and single-pixel rasters.
Own pose data and return independent ray/matrix arrays so caller mutations cannot desynchronize origins and directions.
Validate finite complete pose vectors, nondegenerate viewing/up axes, FOV/raster parameters and generated camera-path parameters before publishing results.
Add independent geometric projection/orientation and camera-path regressions, full changed-file lint, a focused native Linux gate and convention/migration/limitations documentation.

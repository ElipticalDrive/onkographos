Three finished changes, merged with current main.
Empty-space skipping in the realistic march (bb20bed)
Each 4³ brick gets a 64-bit occupancy mask and a one-byte element id per cell (src/shaders/occupancy.js). They are stored in brickDist after the distances, and one dispatch builds them right after the brick map.
The march loads the mask once per brick. Empty cells read nothing. Other cells read 1 byte instead of 16 bytes of state, except where the state's values are used (the opaque hit, glass, grains, a liquid's own cell).
The shadow map now reads ids only.
About 28% fewer bytes per ray in the lab overview. Known gap: the lab still differs by 14 pixels, a single red voxel at the lava rim, cause unknown.
Mesh-only frames (d3c03b3)
While only three's meshes move (birds) and the camera is still, the volume's scene is captured once at output size. Each frame then restores it and draws the meshes plus post, so the volume and far-field marches don't run.
Any real change (camera, edit, sim, settings, wake) goes back to full frames.
Deep idle still reaches 0 submits.
Physics v2 (4c08959)
Under ?physics=v2, the photon and neutron tools are refused with a toast. v2 has no particle layer yet.

March: per-brick occupancy masks and a compact id volume (K3)

bb20bed
```
The realistic view's march and the sun's shadow map read a cell's element
from a per-brick occupancy record instead of state A: a 64-bit mask of the
cells that hold anything and their ids, a byte each (shaders/occupancy.js,
dubiousconst282's 64-bit-mask brick). An empty cell reads nothing; any
other one byte; state A is read only where its values are used (the crisp
hit, a pane, a grain, a liquid's own cell). In a brick of crisp matter only,
out of liquid, an empty cell is passed on the mask without the cell body,
with the same DDA arithmetic and loop count, so every hit is the one the full
march finds, at the same t.

The record rides in the brickDist buffer after the distances (the volume's
fragment stage is at the 8 storage buffers every device allows), rebuilt by
one kernel over the brick map's own list each derived frame, and over every
brick at a window shift. Pick stays exact on the state (it must not wait for
the brick map).
```

Sep 26, 2025Voxelization Pipeline Overview The scene voxelization process employs a GPU-based pipeline using OpenGL shaders to convert 3D triangle meshes into voxel fragments. This implementation follows the conservative rasterization method described in Chapter 22 of OpenGL Insights.

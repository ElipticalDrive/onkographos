The problem
Volume shading (lgShade), occlusion (lgAO), the light glow and the vignette are drawn as colored layers with stop-opacity. A semi-transparent dark layer over a color washes it toward the shadow color and loses saturation; a semi-transparent light layer washes it toward white. This is the same muddy effect as sh(), but in the lighting pass.
resvg supports mix-blend-mode (checked: a #808080 rect over #808080 gives 64 with multiply and 192 with screen).
What you would like
Shadows (lgShade dark stops, lgAO, cast shadows, vignette): draw with mix-blend-mode: multiply and a shadow color that is a saturated, darker version of the sky/ambient color, instead of a grey at low opacity.
Light (glow, the lit side of lgShade, window light): draw with mix-blend-mode: screen (or soft-light where it looks better).
Groups that receive blended layers need isolation: isolate so the blend does not reach the background.
Where to change
src/Services/LightingService.ts and wherever lgShade / lgAO / castF are used per object.
src/Utils/SanitizeUtil.ts: if custom SVG may use blend modes, allow mix-blend-mode and isolation with a test for each (repo rule).
src/Modules/CritiqueModule.ts: check the critique render still measures the same layers.
Scene that shows it
test/visual/cases/kits/5-illustrated-story-flat/scene.yaml (the dog and the girl's dress) and test/visual/cases/use-cases/1-explainer-reel-01/scene.yaml (the blue sweater).
Done when
Shadows keep the hue of the object they fall on; lit sides do not turn pastel.
pnpm check clean, baselines updated with notes.
Notes
This moves visual baselines on purpose: run pnpm test:visual --update, check test/visual/report/, and say in the PR why each case changed.

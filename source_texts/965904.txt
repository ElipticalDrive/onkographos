There are a few areas on some maps that under raytracing are pitch black but when switching to the opengl render to compare the original lighting seems the intended design was for those areas to have at least a minimum level of visibility. Would it possible to have a setting to add more bounces to the light to ensure it reaches those darker areas via indirect illumination? (I propose a setting instead of just increasing the number in a hardcoded way because I assume this might have a performance impact)

Each bounce adds less and less light, so if two bounces won't light up an area, additional bounces most likely won't either.
(Or in other words: the minuscule gain in visual accuracy probably isn't worth doing additional bounces, at least if additional bounces means shooting more rays.)

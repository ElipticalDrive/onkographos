Raycasting in C : r/gamedev - Reddit
You are supposed to have a collider (bounding circle in the case of ray casting so you can slide along walls) with a volume set accordingly so your camera plane doesn't intersect or pass through solid objects/walls. A camera plane (or frustum in the case of a true 3D) is pretty much a convention.

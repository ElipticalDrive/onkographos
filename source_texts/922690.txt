# Simplex Noise

Simplex Noise implementation offering 1D, 2D, and 3D forms w/ values in the range of 0 to 255. Based on work by Heikki Törmälä (2012) and Stefan Gustavson (2006). Core algorithm designed by Ken Perlin (2001).

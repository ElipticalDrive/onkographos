Description
The realistic road is one asphalt photograph, tiled every 3 m, used as a grain on the gradient tint. Real roads are worn: polished wheel tracks, patched repairs, a dusty edge where tyres rarely go, faded paint. Add that procedurally in the road's existing shader — no new texture, no new draw call — while keeping the road's colour as the information it is.
Parent Epic: #615 · Phase: 2 · Story Points: 3 · Cost: free · Needs an owner ruling first: no
Context (read on main at f9b83dc)
three-renderer.ts §photographicRoadMaterial: the asphalt's luminance over its own mean, clamped to realistic-light.ts §PHOTOGRAPHIC_ROAD_GRAIN (which is terrain.ts §ROAD_SURFACE_GRAIN), multiplies the vertex tint; the normal map is kept; every face is lit as facing up; ROAD_SHEEN 0.25.
⚠️ The road's colour is information (ADR 0026 D-2): terrain.ts tints by signed gradient against MINIMUM_TINT_CONTRAST_RATIO (3). The realistic read-back of the steepest climb against the steepest descent is 3.97 : 1 at today's sheen. Wear darkens and lightens the surface, so it spends that margin, and must be bounded so it cannot spend it all.
terrain.ts builds the road in route distance for its dash grid (Make the road read as a road: edge lines, centre line and a gradient cue #242), and six vertex columns across its width (terrain.ts about line 209). Route distance is what makes a patch stay put rather than crawl; the across-position is what places a wheel track.
The road is one mesh and one draw call in both worlds, and game.browser.spec.ts counts it.
Acceptance Criteria
Wheel tracks: a lighter, smoother band in each lane where tyres run (reduced normal strength and roughness), at a stated position across the lane.
Patches: seeded rectangles of slightly different tone and grain, placed by route distance from seeded.ts so a patch is where it was on every ride and does not move with the rider.
A dusty edge band beside each edge line, and worn paint (the lines' own brightness modulated by a seeded low-frequency pattern).
Every wear term together is bounded so the climb/descent read-back stays ≥ 3.5 : 1 (keeping most of today's 3.97), asserted in the browser gate.
Still one mesh, one draw call, no texture added; if the across-position needs a vertex attribute the corridor lacks, the PR adds one float and says why.
The stylised road is unchanged, byte for byte in every figure the browser gate prints.
Browser gate (shared ?realistic load): the steepest climb against the steepest descent reads ≥ 3.5 : 1 with wear on; a probe across one lane reads a luminance difference between the wheel track and the lane's centre above a stated floor. Control: wear off must read the difference below the floor.
Mutation list per CLAUDE.md §5.
Hardware — owner, on the Pixel Tablet: by eye at speed (validation 0002 Z2's shimmer question again, and Z3's "is the gradient still easy to read"), and a 20-minute pair by Measure the realistic world on main: a triangle counter, per-layer switches and spike 0015's 20-minute procedure #616's Part. The pull request writes Refs to this issue; it is closed by hand when the row is filled.

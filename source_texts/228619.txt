Issue body actions
Real-time light gets close, but a final can afford what a path tracer gives: bounce light, glossy reflections and light through glass, all at once. Render finals with a path tracer of our own on the ray layer, frame by frame and seeded per frame, so a final replays exactly, and hold each frame to the real-time frame you approved.
Epic: #310 · Design: Path-traced finals · Decided · Export · Check input: export kinds
Scope
A render mode on the structures and materials of ray-traced light: paths from the camera with next-event estimation toward every light and the sky, the materials' own lobes as published (subsurface as a random walk, hair as its fiber model, glass through its real thickness with attenuation and each channel at its own IOR under dispersion, the thin film, the specular color, and the specular lobe with its multiple-scattering compensation), volumes by delta tracking, the lens and the shutter sampled per path, up to 8 bounces with Russian roulette after 3
Frames: an export with frames: path_traced renders each frame at its samples (1,024 by default, up to 65,536), each path's random numbers from hash(document seed, frame index, pixel, sample index), so a frame depends on its own index alone; nothing carries between frames and no denoiser reads another frame; with denoise: oidn, a pinned single-frame denoiser on the CPU cleans each frame's traced light from its own linear color, albedo and normals before anything drawn after lighting, so far fewer samples suffice
What an export draws after lighting (ink, effects, bloom, the finish, overlays, 2D writer layers and status colors) draws as in the real-time export, and puffs and sprite particles draw over it by depth
final.match: each final frame against the real-time export frame at its time, each kept item's silhouette from the object-ID pass within an IoU of 0.99 and the mean log luminance within 1/3 EV, failing with the frame, the item and the numbers; render.noise runs on finals, and its fix names a sample count, or denoise: oidn where it's off
Kept by hash: frames hashed on canonical raw buffers and stored as an asset by hash that the export encodes under the export guards (Export guards: no blank or broken frames, verified color, a delivery true to its frames, and a complete streaming profile #157); a second export of the same final renders nothing
A runner job under the GPU lease, sharded by frame range, and BLOCKED without ray queries, naming why; the outside path tracer's render job (Asset jobs for modeling, simulation, render and light: boolean, remesh, fracture, simulate, render and light bakes #128) stays an optional oracle, compared by final.match as a reading, never a gate
A film with a part whose material has a light response is refused with light_response_in_final, naming the part, before any frame renders, since paths take the materials' own lobes; that film's final is its real-time export
A light's receivers and casters hold on every path type in a final: on light-sampled paths and on paths whose BSDF sample reaches the light's emitter alike
The denoiser's albedo through glass and mirrors is stated: at a specular or transmissive hit the albedo it reads is taken from the next non-delta hit, so textures behind glass and in mirrors keep their detail
Fields
Export: path-traced finals
On Export: frames: realtime | path_traced = realtime
path: {samples: u32 = 1024 (≤ 65,536), bounces: u8 = 8, roulette_after: u8 = 3, denoise: none | oidn = none} (oidn: Open Image Denoise, pinned by version and weights hash, run on each frame alone)
match: {iou: f32 = 0.99, exposure: EV = 0.33} (final.match, against the real-time frames)
Receipt adds: final {frame_hashes, samples, denoise: {tool: oidn@version, weights: sha256} | none, match: [{frame, item, iou, ev}]}
It can never: Seed a path by anything but its frame's own index; read another frame; pass a frame final.match failed; count as evidence across machines; run in the live view.
Operator output: light response
On a material operator: light: step function | graph (once per shaded light) and ambient: step function | graph (once per pixel)
light inputs, computed by the core: n, v, l: vec3 (the shading normal, to the eye and to the light, in view space); radiance: vec3 (linear, after the light's falloff, IES distribution, light function and receivers); shadow: f32 (0..1, from shadow maps or rays); light_hash: u32 (a hash of the light's ID); and the operator's own outputs (base_color, roughness, metallic and the rest)
light output: radiance: vec3 (linear, ≥ 0)
ambient inputs: irradiance: vec3 (probes and the environment, after ambient occlusion) and env: vec3 (one environment sample along the reflection at the roughness); output radiance: vec3
Defaults: core.light@1 and core.ambient@1, the core's own shading as step functions
Registry: entries of gpu.output@1 that scene3d fills
Issue: light_response.range {op, t}
It can never: Read a light the policy cut, a shadow map or the light list; change a light's count, flux, shadow or budget; carry light between frames; return a status color; reach a path-traced final
Acceptance criteria
A white furnace (diffuse shapes of albedo 1, a bowl and a corner among them, in a uniform environment of radiance 1) renders every pixel at 1 within 1% at 1,024 samples, which a path cut short or a biased roulette fails, and a Cornell box at 4,096 samples matches a converged CPU path tracer in f64 within 1% on the mean of every 16 × 16 block of pixels
Frames 10 to 20 of a final, rendered twice on one machine, are byte-identical, and frame 15 rendered alone equals frame 15 of the sequence, which a seed taken from the render order fails; frame 15 rendered in batches of 16 and of 64 samples gives one hash, which a planted job that sums per-batch partials fails
final.match fails a final whose camera is planted 2° off, naming the kept item and its IoU, and one planted 1 EV bright, naming the difference, and passes the true final
A planted NaN in one final frame fails frames.finite naming the frame, and a second export of the same final is a cache hit that renders no frame
On a software adapter, or a GPU without ray queries, a final is BLOCKED with the reason, and the outside path tracer's still of the product fixture, where its runner is set up, is compared by final.match as a reading and gates nothing
A final of the combined envelope's scene (Document checks: geometry audit, determinism, frame budget and the film audit #99), with skin, hair, a volume and a physics world in frame, renders within this task's time per frame and memory, as the product fixture does
A film with one part under a cel light response is refused with light_response_in_final, naming the part, before the job writes a frame, and with that response removed the same film renders its final; and a planted final job that traces the part with the default lobe instead writes frames and fails, naming the part
In a white furnace of radiance 1, white metal spheres (base color 1, metallic 1) at roughness 0, 0.5 and 1 render every pixel at 1 within 1% at 1,024 samples, and a planted final that traces the lobe without its multiple-scattering compensation renders the roughness 1 sphere's center at 1 − ln 2 ≈ 0.31 and fails, naming the material
In that furnace, a clear glass sphere of ior 1.5 with no attenuation, one with dispersion 1 and one with iridescence weight 1 each render every pixel at 1 within 1% at 1,024 samples, and a planted final that adds the thin film's reflection without taking it from the refracted path renders the iridescent sphere above 1 and fails
Two glass slabs with attenuation {color [0.5, 0.8, 1], distance 2 cm}, 2 cm and 4 cm thick, seen head-on against a white backdrop, show the 4 cm slab's pixels as [0.5, 0.8, 1] times the 2 cm slab's within 1%; and a 60° prism of ior 1.5 and dispersion 1, framed so that a CPU trace in f64 puts the red and blue images of a thin white line at least 4 px apart, places each channel's image within 1 px of that trace at IORs 1.4875, 1.5 and 1.5125, where a planted final that traces every channel at 1.5 fails, naming the material
With denoise: oidn at 64 samples, frames 10 to 20 of the product fixture's final rendered twice on one machine, each into an empty store, are byte-identical, and frame 15 rendered alone into an empty store traces one frame's paths and equals frame 15 of the sequence; a planted denoiser that also reads the previous frame's color gives frame 15 alone another hash and fails, naming the frame
A lone sphere whose only lobe is diffuse (specular_level 0, diffuse_roughness 0) in a uniform environment of radiance 1 sees only the environment, so its radiance equals its base color everywhere: rendered at base color 0.25, 0.5 and 1 in turn with denoise: oidn at 16 samples, every pixel more than 2 px inside the sphere's silhouette in the object-ID pass is at its base color within 1% in the denoised linear light, before anything drawn after lighting; a planted denoise step that multiplies its output by the albedo input again renders the 0.5 sphere at 0.25 and fails, naming the material
A final with denoise: oidn and one without, at the same samples, are two keys, each receipt naming its denoise; a cached undenoised frame is never returned for a denoised request, and a planted key that leaves denoise out returns it and fails, naming the frame
In the house fixture, a lamp whose receivers are its own room contributes 0 to every pixel outside that room at 1,024 samples, and its casters hold in the final as in raster light; a planted final that filters receivers only on light-sampled paths lets BSDF rays that hit the lamp's emitter light the next room and fails, naming the light
A clear glass slab and a mirror over a checker floor at 16 samples with denoise: oidn keep the checker's edge contrast through and in them within 10% of a 4,096-sample undenoised reference; a planted albedo of 1 at the first delta hit lets the denoiser blur the checker and fails, naming the material
Budgets, on the reference Mac in the local preflight, compared with the merge base: a 1080p final of the product fixture at 1,024 samples renders in under 60 s per frame on the reference Mac under the GPU lease and holds under 4 GB, the same final at 64 samples with denoise: oidn renders in under 10 s per frame, and final.match adds under 0.5 s per frame
Proof
A 10 s product film rendered as a final and in real time, side by side, with final.match's report, the replay hashes and the white-furnace and Cornell-box comparisons.
Oracle: upstream tests, pinned by commit, for 3 of 16 criteria: the tests, per criterion in doneright/oracles (DeepBuildAI/deepbuild). A linked test counts only when run against our output (#136). Without a runner the lane is BLOCKED with one unblock ask.

Hi Dr. Lawlor,
Thank you for sharing AuroraRendererUnity and your work with Dr. Genetti on Interactive Volume Rendering Aurora on the GPU.
I used ideas from your rendering approach for the globe auroras on my photography website, VIDI: https://vidi.yun.earth
In particular, separating the auroral curtain footprint from the altitude-dependent emission profile helped me move beyond a simple glowing ring toward more varied volumetric curtains viewed from space. My implementation adapts those architectural ideas to Cesium/WebGL, computing an animated 2D footprint field before integrating the emission through a planetary shell.
Your source code and paper were very helpful references. I really appreciate you making this work available so others can learn from it and build on it.
Thank you again!

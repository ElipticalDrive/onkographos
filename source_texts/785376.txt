Frequently Asked Questions
How do Scaniverse, on-demand capture and bring-your-own-data differ?
Scaniverse is the web and mobile workflow for teams that capture and manage data themselves. On-demand capture is a full-service option for large or complex sites. Bring your own data connects imagery, scans and telemetry you already have.
Which devices and data sources are supported?
iOS and Android devices, 360° cameras and drones, plus drone imagery, LiDAR scans, satellite imagery and other sensor datasets.
How accurate are captures?
It depends on the capture method, sensors, coverage and processing. On-demand capture uses multi-sensor hardware for georeferenced, survey-grade results on enterprise sites; mobile capture is high-fidelity but varies with conditions.
What can one capture produce?
Gaussian splats (SPZ, PLY) and meshes (GLB, USDZ), localization maps, and georeferenced inputs for understanding and simulation.

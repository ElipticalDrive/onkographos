Render Feature
We implemented volumetric textures as intermediate storage, and used compute shaders and UAVs to raymarch and write efficiently.
First and most important part is performing lighting and shadowing calculations for every volume cell.
In parallel, we estimated and animated the participating media density.
Then using this information stored in volume textures, we performed 2D raymarching through the volume and stored the result in volume slices.
And finally using pixel shaders, applied information on screen for forward or deferred shaded objects.

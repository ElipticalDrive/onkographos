Summary
This PR adds an end-to-end automatic tiling workflow for large Gaussian-splatting datasets. It plans tiles from scene evidence, lets users preview and inspect them in the GUI, validates tile feasibility before expensive training starts, trains a global coarse prior followed by sequential tile workers, and merges owner-cropped exports.
The workflow is available from both the CLI and Training panel.
Partition planning and preview
Compute robust scene bounds from sparse points, camera centres, or both, with configurable outlier trimming.
Generate adaptive tile counts, fixed grids, or target-size grids.
Align partitioning to a 3D OBB/PCA frame, planar yaw/pitch/roll, or dataset axes.
Configure overlap, source evidence, boundary crop padding, feasibility thresholds, coarse-pass settings, visibility-mask settings, exposure locking, crop, and merge from the GUI or CLI.
Generate previews asynchronously so planning does not block the GUI.
Preview tile boxes, select any tile, inspect its exact camera allowlist, and isolate its crop box, associated cameras, and sparse points in the viewer.
Preserve edited preview layouts as tile plans for the batch run.
Ownership and feasibility
Keep training context overlap separate from unique ownership cores.
Export only the final core-owned splats, allowing streaming merge without intentional overlap duplicates.
Apply optional boundary padding only after camera assignment, in the tile-local OBB frame.
Gate the run before the coarse pass using minimum camera and multi-view-support requirements.
Resolve infeasible neighbours through direct rectangular coalescing or rectangular closure: split along neighbour boundaries, merge only aligned rectangular fragments, and retain all remaining fragments explicitly.
Record resolved tile counts, feasibility reasons, provenance, and topology operations in the generated reports.
Coarse-to-tile training
Train a global coarse prior before local tile workers.
Initialize tiles from the coarse model, retaining Gaussian geometry and appearance attributes.
Estimate global SfM-track exposure gains once and apply them consistently to coarse and tile workers.
Optionally lock per-tile learned exposure correction after global calibration.
Generate dense coarse Gaussian depth/opacity visibility masks for selected cameras.
Require dense masks by default; sparse SfM fallback is explicit opt-out recovery behavior.
Record mask coverage, fallback use, unavailable views, and ineligible camera UIDs in tile reports.
Execution and output
Run tiles sequentially in isolated processes and stop on the first failed worker.
Support cancellation during planning, feasibility processing, coarse-mask generation, and tile execution.
Merge compatible binary PLY exports in bounded chunks without loading all tile models into memory.
Validate PLY schemas and lengths and write the merged result atomically.
Preserve coarse output, completed tile exports, manifests, feasibility reports, exposure calibration, and mask artifacts for inspection after failure or cancellation.
Validation
Adds or extends CLI parser and Training-panel regression coverage.
Keeps shipped locale keys and runtime RML translation directives in sync.
Enforces binary I/O discipline for generated mask and camera/exposure artifacts.

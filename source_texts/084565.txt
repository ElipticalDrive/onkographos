Feature Request
Support 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) scene representations and differentiable rendering in jax3d.
Proposed Components
Primitives: A data structure to represent 3D Gaussians (positions, scales, quaternions, opacities, and spherical harmonics).
Projection & Covariance: Math utilities to project 3D Gaussians into 2D camera coordinates and compute 2D covariance matrices.
Differentiable Rasterizer: A JAX-compatible rasterization and alpha-blending pipeline.
Reference
3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Radiance Field Rendering (Kerbl et al., SIGGRAPH 2023)

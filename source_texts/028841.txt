In order to overvome the shortage that when the surface reconstruction method based on Marching Cubes is used,the ambigouous planes may lead to cavities between layers,a surface reconstruction method based on Double Marching Cubes is introduced.Double cubes element is adopted as the basic unit when surface mesh is generated,a 12-dimensional double cubes element index table is established according to the different marked situations of double cubes element's 12 vertexs,three-dimensional surface is generated through searching the 12-dimensional double cubes element index table.For all the wrong connections of ambiguous planes are avoided when the searching table is established,no cavities between layers will appear.No complex calculations are needed,so the surface reconstruction is accelerated to some extent.

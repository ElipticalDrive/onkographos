Part of #69, follows 3D-3. Design: docs/3d-conversion-design.md, phase 3D-4.
Sim-owned heightfield asset with a pinned bilinear sampler (own GDScript, no plugin/physics calls in sim code) and Windows↔Linux bit-exactness tests; Terrain3D (or the HTerrain/clipmap fallback — decide here, after verifying GDExtension behaviour in headless CI) rendering from the same source image; render-side projection of soldiers onto height.
Gameplay effects of height (slope speed, charge, missile range) are explicitly OUT of scope — they change balance and replays and get their own design round.
Exit: elevation renders; sim untouched on flat maps (height fn ≡ 0 replays stay bit-identical).

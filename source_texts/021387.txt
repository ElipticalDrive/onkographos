Problem
• Goal was to determine whether Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) could be used with satellite
imagery to reconstruct a volumetric representation of a scene.
• NeRFs use deep learning to construct a scene representation that can be queried for color
and density at points in space. Novel views may then be synthesized by ray casting through
the volume, querying the network at positions along each camera ray.
• This enables generation of novel viewpoints that may be useful for analysis
Figures from Mildenhall, et al.
2
Approach
• Using Nvidia’s instant-ngp NeRF implementation with an RTX A5000 GPU
• Used Matlab to convert Planet metadata to NeRF format, ImageMagick to convert
images, and Blender to correct for bad attitude.
• From Planet video data (left), reconstructed 3D NeRF (right) from 27 frames
Planet Labs source video
Live capture NeRF reconstruction

Results/Findings
• Successfully generated NeRFs using Shanghai dataset
• Difficulty mapping pointing metadata from Planet to NeRF.
Worked around this using Blender to align video frames.
• Realistic satellite focal lengths appear to cause artifacts (Moiré
– see below).
• Extracted a reasonable NeRF using an artificially short focal
length. Expect better resolution if this can be overcome.

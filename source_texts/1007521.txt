Closes #43. Finishes the frontier's look from #44.
What
AmbientOcclusion: a pass between the depth prepass and the scene pass. The scene pass scales its indirect light by it, and the direct light by as much as direct lends it. The reach is set in scene units and projected per pixel. The blur is sized by the same reach and weighted against the centre's plane. Where the reach projects to under a couple of pixels, the effect fades out. That is what the removed term lacked: it printed its sample rotation into far creases.
DepthOfField: a pass after the volumetric fog and before the bloom. It gathers 40 taps, each weighted by whether its own circle of confusion reaches the centre, so a sharp edge doesn't halo over a blurred background. Focus is read on the GPU at a point on the picture. The band in focus is a share of the focus distance, so the look holds at every zoom.
Each effect is a scene singleton (saved with scenes, shown in the editor), a graphics setting ("Ambient occlusion", "Depth of field") and a RendererCapabilities flag. The frontier turns both on; the duel has neither.
Cost
RTX 3070, 2560×1377, Release. The frontier's average frame over 4 × 240 frames, with each pass turned off through kuki.config.json:
Extra time per frame
Both
~2.1 ms (1.8–2.4)
Ambient occlusion
~1.3 ms (0.8–1.8)
Depth of field
~0.75 ms (0.3–0.95)
Running the occlusion at half resolution would cut it to about a quarter, but it needs an upsample that respects depth.

The frontier's soft, painted look needed two things the engine lacked: shade where things meet,
which settles a prop onto its cell where the probe field is too coarse to, and a shallow focus,
which is much of what makes a map read as a small model on a table. Each is a pass, a scene
singleton, a graphics setting and a `RendererCapabilities` flag, and the duel has neither.

`AmbientOcclusion` runs between the depth prepass and the scene pass, and the scene pass scales its
indirect light by it (and the direct light by as much as `direct` lends it). The screen space term
that was taken out cancelled its sample rotation against a window fixed at four pixels, so a crease
seen from far away printed the rotation into the picture. This one sets its reach in scene units
and projects it per pixel, sizes its blur by the same reach and weights it against the centre's
plane, and fades out where the reach projects to under a couple of pixels. Seen through the debug
view at the frontier's widest zoom it is smooth, with no grain.

`DepthOfField` runs after the volumetric fog and before the bloom. It gathers a disc of forty taps,
each weighted by whether its own circle of confusion reaches the centre, and holds a tap behind the
centre to the centre's circle, so a sharp thing's edge does not halo over a blurred background. The
focus is read on the GPU from the depth at a point on the picture, and the band in focus is a share
of the focus distance, so the look holds at every zoom.

Measured on an RTX 3070 at 2560x1377 in Release, the frontier's average frame over four steps of
240 frames, with each pass turned off through kuki.config.json: both together cost about 2.1 ms
(1.8 to 2.4), the occlusion about 1.3 ms (0.8 to 1.8) and the depth of field about 0.75 ms (0.3 to
0.95). The occlusion is the costlier and is a setting for that reason; at half resolution it would
cost about a quarter, which needs an upsample that respects depth.

The pipeline cache's Clear also resets the stylize and reflection root signatures, which it missed.

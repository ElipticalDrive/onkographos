Closes the 4D Sampled Solid representation decision (the P1 bead blocking the
4D Solid implementation and, behind it, the space-tiling epic's last two items).
The question
accumulateVoxels4 deposits into a rotor-projected, w-sliced 3D grid —
rows 0-2 of a RotorProjection4 give xyz, row 3 gives sRaw, which becomes a
Gaussian slice weight (floor 0) and never a coordinate. w is gone before
voxel-material.ts sees the texture, so 3D Solid's query-space fold cannot be
lifted onto it: it would fold the displayed space instead of the attractor's.
What representation replaces the fold?
Verdict: deposit bounded pre-projection images into the UNCHANGED displayed volume
No new voxel memory, no new buffer shape, no shader work at all. The whole lift
is the voxel worker's plot boundary, taken exactly the way accumulateFlame4
took it one renderer over.
Two rules invert the 3D lift and are now written down so nobody inherits
them: the max-density hierarchy stays enabled (nothing folds, so a straight
visible ray maps to no reflected source segments) and tiling edits restart
the worker instead of recompiling a material.
Selection is per-arm, and the asymmetry is structural. The finite arms keep
the shipped 32-image credit estimator. The lattice keeps the same credit rule
but re-weights its proposal CDF by a per-cell, source-independent
slice-visibility ceiling — free (candidate tests = attempts; 1013 ms against
998 ms), unbiased to 1.6e-4 relative mass, visible-deposit fraction 0.64 to
0.91, and it roughly halves the error. The finite groups' per-image visibility
is source-dependent, so the same idea costs a full orbit enumeration per
acceptance and is refused on measurement.

The selected architecture costs nothing it did not already cost: 27.0 MB
texture and 108.0 MB working set at 192^3 (identical to untiled), a measured
per-ray fetch multiplier of 0.968-1.000 against the untiled baseline's mean
162.64 fetches/ray — below one, because a tiled volume terminates rays
marginally sooner — and a settled rotor/slice rebuild at 0.82x-1.28x today's
untiled restart.
The sheet reproduces exactly on an independent second run (6/6, 339 s).
Refused, with numbers rather than arguments
A raw-4D canonical volume with a query-space 4D fold. It is the only
candidate that keeps a genuine 4D fold and the only one that would have made
rotor and slice edits free — proven pose-independent, byte-identical between a
volume built at one pose and rendered at another, because the raw volume reads
no pose at all. It fails anyway: at N = 64 (the largest resolution inside both
memory caps, exactly at the 64 MiB texture cap with a 256 MB working set) it
holds 22,017 occupied cells of 16,777,216 — 0.13% — so a 3-plane through a grid
that sparse retains 1.43-1.61 of the reference occupancy on the finite arms and
0.30-0.34 on the lattice, at L1 0.739-1.055 everywhere. Depositing and then
slicing integrates the slice exactly from continuous orbit points; voxelizing
in 4D and then slicing loses it. And its w-taps multiply every one of the
seven folded query paths by 24 (finite) or 231 (lattice) against a 2x cap.
Sparse or bricked storage would move the memory number, not either of those.
The post-projection displayed-3D fold, at L1 1.82-1.97 with an occupancy
symmetric difference of 0.83-2.52. It draws a different object, and a different
wrong one at each pose.
Slice-aware selection on the finite arms. Strictly better per attempt,
not better per second above A4 — its F4 row (0.3297 in 2.44 s) loses to
spending the same time on more attempts (0.2953 in 2.41 s) — and it breaks two
frozen bounds: candidate tests reach 3.63x-8.04x attempts and the settled-edit
rebuild reaches 1.84x-3.39x.
Two things this PR does not paper over
The lattice arm misses the sheet's own predeclared detail floor: 19.20
voxels per content diameter at the frozen 10R carrier against a floor of 32,
and against the untiled 4D volume's 205 — the order-of-magnitude loss the
decision predicted, now measured. The cap was not moved to fit. That bar
is the carrier-to-resolution ratio and not the representation: the raw-4D
volume bought 64 voxels per content diameter and lost the object. The carrier
sweep measured 10R/8R/6R/4R at 171/81/33/19 cells and L1
0.0875/0.0873/0.0791/0.0987, so 6R is the first row that clears the floor and
also carries the best error — but resolvePointTilingPlan refuses every
presentation but 8R->10R and that policy is frozen across renderers, so it
stays a presentation decision with its own evidence rather than one settled
by the renderer that wanted a smaller number. Filed as its own decision bead.
A measured correction to the contract's own acceptance figure. Canonical
finite acceptance is 1/order only for a fixture balanced against the
canonical chamber, which is what the point-family sheet used. An authored
system's attractor sits in its own frame, unrelated to the canonical Coxeter
roots: pentatope measured 3.026%/1.955%/0.698% under A4/B4/F4, i.e.
3.63x/7.51x/8.04x the prediction. Nothing downstream breaks — comparisons are
mass-normalized and the credit rule is defined on attempts — but a cost bound
written in terms of 1/order is not a bound, which is exactly what retired
the finite slice-aware arm.
Dimensional parity
This is the 4D half's own decision bead, so there is no unlifted twin: it
exists to close the gap fr-7007.7 disclosed. The 4D Solid capability itself
is still not shipped — this PR selects the architecture, records the
measured matrix and the refusals, and unblocks the implementation bead with the
exact carrier, buffers, weights, bounds, revisions and oracle it must follow.
No src/ behaviour changes here; the material's 4D refusal line stays until
that lands.

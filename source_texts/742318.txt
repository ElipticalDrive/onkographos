sdfgi_enabled adds no light. The Environment sheet names SDFGI as not drawn, and volumetric_fog_gi_inject adds nothing because no GI exists.
Godot behaviour
SDFGI voxelises the scene into cascades of signed distance fields and lights the surfaces from them (servers/rendering/renderer_rd/environment/gi.cpp, servers/rendering/renderer_rd/shaders/environment/sdfgi_*.glsl).
The Environment properties are sdfgi_cascades, sdfgi_min_cell_size, sdfgi_use_occlusion, sdfgi_bounce_feedback, sdfgi_read_sky_light, sdfgi_energy, sdfgi_normal_bias, sdfgi_probe_bias and sdfgi_y_scale (scene/resources/environment.cpp).
Scope
Diffuse indirect light from one cascade of static GeometryInstance3D meshes with gi_mode Static.
Sky light read, sdfgi_energy and sdfgi_use_occlusion.
Define the frame that Godot's capture shows once the cascades have settled.
Wire the consumer (the node or resource that uses it) in the same change, so the feature draws in the viewport.
Add a golden of a fixture that shows the change, and compare it with pnpm ref:godot at several property values, including edge cases.
Update the comparison sheet, the support mark and the node details of every type the change touches.

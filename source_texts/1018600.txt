Problem
#177 step 2c, part 2. #541 read LightData's DirectColor and AmbientColor into the recorded sky sample, but the renderer still lit terrain with the fixed World light. Plan in issuecomment-6715593643; this is step 1.
Approach
WorldSkyLook (gui) carries the sample's direct and ambient colours, 0..255, with a light weight of coverage × light share, the same way it weighs the fog. A sample without recorded light has weight 0.
WorldStreamTransport.Sky gains direct, ambient and lightWeight, quantized like the rest: whole colour steps, weight in thousandths. The existing constructor still means no recorded light.
The camera line gets an optional LIGHT <12 hex> <weight> tail after SKY, written only when the weight is above 0. A LIGHT tail with weight 0 is refused. The longest camera line is now 273 bytes, so WorldStreamControl.MAX_LINE_BYTES goes from 256 to 320. Both ends are the same build, and the existing longest-line test pins it.
The renderer sets u_worldLight from light.withRecorded(recordedSky): diffuse moves toward direct/255 and ambient toward ambient/255 by the weight. The direction stays fixed, because LightData records colours, not a direction (step 3 of the plan). A camera without a sky, or without recorded light, keeps the renderer's own light. No shader change.
Without -Dvimi.world.sky=recorded, no sky is sent, so nothing changes by default.
Validation
Tests:
WorldLightTest.aSkysRecordedLightIsMixedInByItsWeight: no sky or weight 0 returns the same light; weight 1 gives exactly direct/255 and ambient/255; weight 0.5 gives half-way; the direction is unchanged.
WorldStreamControlTest.aSkysRecordedLightRoundTripsAndAZeroWeightLightIsRefused: the LIGHT ffe0c0404060 750 tail round-trips exactly; no tail without light; a zero-weight tail is refused. The longest-line test now includes a full light tail and still fits.
WorldSkyLookTest.carriesTheRecordedLight: colours come from DirectColor/AmbientColor, the weight is coverage × share, an unlit sample gives weight 0, and the light is carried even with no fog.

APPROVE (head 26106e4)
The recorded direct and ambient light travel and get mixed in correctly. Without -Dvimi.world.sky=recorded it changes nothing. The camera line bound is consistent, and the new oracle and the unit tests catch the obvious mutations. Nothing blocking. Notes below.
Findings
Low: rendering-bgfx/.../WorldStreamTransport.java:213. When lightWeight quantizes to 0 (e.g. 0.0004), direct/ambient keep their colours. The writer (WorldStreamControl.java:159) then leaves out the tail, so the decoded Sky has zero colours and isn't equals to the sent one. I checked: encode, then decode, then equals gives false. Rendering is identical because weight 0 keeps the fixed light. Still, the fog fields are canonical in the constructor, so I'd zero the light colours when the rounded weight is 0, like the fog fields. WorldSkyLook already does this for an unlit sample, but not for 0 < lit < 0.0005.
Low (follow-up, not this PR): the horizon doesn't pick up the recorded light. WorldStreamHorizon.java:273-274 writes the unlit normal marker, and fs_model.sc:31 skips lighting for it, so the horizon keeps its baked slope light (about 0.955 on flat ground). Under the fixed light, near terrain and the horizon roughly match (about 0.88 on flat ground). With a dark recorded light at night, near terrain darkens and the horizon ring doesn't. The recorded fog hides most of it, but the nearest horizon tiles will show a brightness step at the full-detail edge. Please add it to the step 3/5 list in the plan comment. Water is also unlit (no normal), as the PR says for step 4. Objects use the separate world-wmo program and don't read u_worldLight, so they keep the fixed light as stated.
Info: WorldStreamControl.java:198-208. The explicit LIGHT keyword check and the zero-weight check are redundant with the canonical re-encode at :452. Disabling either one alone leaves all tests green, because the re-encode still refuses the line. That's fine as defence in depth. I probed the parse separately and every malformed tail was refused (18/18): wrong keyword, uppercase hex, 11/13-digit hex, + in hex, weight 0750/1001/-1/NaN/Infinity/7.5e2/750.0/0, a duplicate tail, a missing weight, an extra token, a double space, and a LIGHT tail without SKY. Old lines (no tail, SKY only) still parse, with weight 0 and null sky respectively.
Info: WorldLight.withRecorded (WorldLight.java:78) mixes 0..255 gamma colours divided by 255 into a gamma-space light. This matches how WorldLight documents the fixed light (albedo × (ambient + diffuse·N·L), gamma space). Full direct plus full ambient can reach 2.0 and saturates in the target. That's consistent with the shader and the 0..4 validator.
Info: thread confinement and stale state are fine. sky() calls requireOwner() and maps null to EXPLORER (weight 0). The owner calls it on every camera (WorldStreamNativeOwner.java:603,641), and the light is derived per frame at BgfxWorldStreamRenderer.java:2024, with nothing cached.
Info: the bound change covers both sides. encode and decode/read all use MAX_LINE_BYTES, and there's no other camera-line limit (the 256 in WorldStreamStdout is the status record). The branch is 3 commits behind main but merges cleanly.
Default path
withRecorded returns this for a null/EXPLORER sky (assertSame in the test). WorldSkyFeed is only built when vimi.world.sky is set. The owner proof's default-path pixels match main exactly: terrainLit=ff9d111a terrainUnlit=ffb4141e, same as the values recorded on #581.

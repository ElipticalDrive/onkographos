Hi, thanks for this amazing repo.
I am trying a demo on WIN10 by installing the latest pre-compiled wheel, and I found a fork about the migration from diff-gaussian-rasterization to gsplat (graphdeco-inria/gaussian-splatting@258123a)
I tried that repo and compared the speed between the gsplat and the vanilla 3DGS. Surprisingly, I found out that GSPLAT is even slower than vanilla 3DGS rasterization based on the same small dataset (around 15 images), at least not faster.
I am new to 3DGS so I appreciate any comments and ideas in this community. Thanks in advance.

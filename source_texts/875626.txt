What is asked
A libm-free Fixed32 gradient or simplex noise beside the value noise, usable in 2D for terrain and climate and in 3D for density fields.
Why it matters: Every report names Perlin or simplex as the base field, value noise shows grid artefacts, and 3D density terrain needs a 3D noise.
What is true today
Value noise with fBm, ridged and warp variants ships and gate P7 folds it.
What is missing
Gradient tables and a skewed simplex lattice in Fixed32.
How we will know it is done
A noise test folds the same 2D and 3D simplex signatures on host and in ring 0, and the recipe can switch its NoiseKind without changing anything else.

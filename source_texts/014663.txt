This research presents a novel approach to improving render time performance for row displacement compressed grids in ray tracing. The authors develop a multi-level hashed grid implementation that combines the benefits of low build time and memory consumption with high render time performance. By employing a finely divided hashed grid with a high density parameter, compressing empty cells using row displacement compression, and skipping empty regions during traversal, the proposed method achieves significantly improved render time performance compared to traditional multi-level array grids

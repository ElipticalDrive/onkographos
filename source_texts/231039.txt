Theme
The deterministic pieces every neural-field application needs and geonnax does not yet have: coordinate-derivative operators (including on the sphere), a Gaussian Fourier-feature encoder, the BayesNF architecture as a reusable core, and a real-data neural-field notebook.
Parent Wave
Wave epic: [EPIC] Wave 6: neural fields across the stack geostack#82 (jejjohnson/geostack)
Motivation
geonnax already has the representation side (SIREN, MultiScaleSIREN, FourierNet, GaborNet, SphericalHarmonicEncoder, SlepianEncoder). What blocks applications is around it: physics losses and geostrophy need derivatives with respect to coordinates (only private jax.jacfwd uses exist today), the most common INR front-end (Tancik Gaussian Fourier features) is missing, and the BayesNF architecture lives only as a pure-prior model inside pyrox-nn, so it cannot be combined with geonnax's sphere encoders. Plan: design page.

Native GPU plan, step 6.1c: render mode now traces against the driver's acceleration structures too, when Settings > System > Graphics > Ray tracing hardware is on. The studio already did this in #407.
What changed
Kernels. There are two new ones: render_trace_rq and render_trace_maps_rq, both RAY_QUERY in render_trace.slang and Vulkan only.
Each ray is cast once against a top level over every item.
A hit's merged triangle comes from a per-item table of geometry starts, which lives in the Order buffer (from offs.z).
Candidates that are not opaque, such as hidden triangles or unused voxel slots, are turned down when a corner repeats in the merged indices.
Shared structures. Render mode uses the same bottom level per mesh as the studio, so each mesh's is built once for both. It builds its own top level over the placed items.
Scene buffers. No chunk trees are copied into render mode's scene, so its scene buffers shrink from 23.9 to 17.2 MB in the parity scene.
Switching. Turning the switch over starts render mode's samples over the other way.
Async safety.
A graphics submission that refits a structure in place now waits for the last compute-queue submission that traced against it (Vk_Cmd.accel_writes and Vk_Accel.compute_read).
This means the studio cannot refit a bottom level while a render band is still tracing against it on the compute queue.
Studio kernel. trace_rq_kernel now makes the studio's kernel, and trace_prepare no longer does. Each caller passes ray_query once its kernel exists.
Parity tolerance. scripts/parity_check.py --tolerance PCT lets that share of a picture's pixels differ.
Exit stats, README and skill. There is a new exit stat, render_rq=. README and sm-render are updated.
Verified (Linux, lavapipe)
Ray queries against the walk (--no-ray-query):
At 4 samples, 0.04–0.065% of pixels differ, by at most 11 levels. These pixels are scattered noise plus a few clusters in highlights and contact shadows: a path that meets a triangle a hair elsewhere bounces another way.
This holds for a half-hidden sphere (the hidden part is correctly cut away), a voxel sphere (at most 1 level of difference) and --async-compute.
There are no validation messages under --gpu-debug.
Parity against the merged base:
Exact for Vulkan --gpu-debug --no-ray-query and for --gpu=sdl.
For Vulkan --gpu-debug and Vulkan --gpu-debug --async-compute, every case matches except the three render-mode cases, which pass with --tolerance 0.2.
Gates: check, test, smoke, maccheck, iphonecheck, wincheck and androidcheck are all green.
Not verified: speed and memory on a real RTX or other hardware ray tracing, and the async wait while a studio pane and a render band run at the same time. Nothing here exercises that wait.

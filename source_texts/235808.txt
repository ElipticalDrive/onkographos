Thanks for the report.
In the current released inference path, raw OpenCV pinhole cameras are not directly supported.
The camera conversion and ray-generation code are intentionally F-theta-only.
the remaining “simple pinhole” conversion is used internally for FOV conditioning and is not a supported input camera model.

I tried waymo data and recalibrated the images to f-theta model, the error was sub-pixel, and also found the result is very blurred and hazy.
I also done another experiment by cropping the image from front-left and front-right cam on Nvidia dataset by about 10 deg of FOV, then found that the depth inference result degenerate a lot. So I more believe that current released model is kind of overfitted to Nvidia's own rig. Migration to other datasets might need fine tuning.

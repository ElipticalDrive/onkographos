Is your feature request related to a problem? Please describe.
This is not related to a problem, beyond the inability to achieve the effect.
Relevant issues
I couldn't find anything for "metaball" or "isosurface"
Describe the solution you'd like
Metaballs are volumes defined by the influences of one or more points. The volume encapsulates all points with sufficient influence.
The volumes can merge together.
Points can have negative influence.
These would be great for cheap approximations of fluids and other wibbly-wobbly objects.
They would also make for a very neat whisper bubble effect: nearby bubbles would stick together, making a single cohesive shell.
Describe alternatives you've considered
I don't see any other way of doing this in Neos.
Additional context
I am uncertain about how performant this would be, so that could be a deal-breaker. It might be necessary for these to be a "baked" effect -- that is, you can't have the shapes constantly updating as you move points around. Whilst less fun, it'd still be very handy.

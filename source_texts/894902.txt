Adds a NumPy TSDF fusion reference for Phase 5, which the coarse TSDF kernel of Phase 4 will be checked against.
slscan/tsdf.py resamples each tracked depth frame into a depth image at its sample grid, averages each voxel's truncated distance along the camera axis, and extracts zero crossings as surface points
track.sweep_bench takes a surface function, so the same pose and artifact checks measure a TSDF surface
slscan sweep-bench --tsdf with --voxel-mm and --truncation-mm
design entry D51, pipeline text, Phase 5 status
On the 25-frame sweep with second-order windows and a sample every 8 px, over three noise seeds, 0.5 mm voxels with 1 mm truncation read the ball bar -10.5 to -5.2 µm and the mat 4.1 to 4.8 µm RMS. Point fusion of the same frames reads +2.4 to +11.4 µm and 15.5 to 17.0 µm RMS. Samples every 16 px are too sparse for the spheres' sides.
Verified: bash verify.do green on every commit with fresh file times.

buildings.js builds every chunk's geometry (footprint triangulation, wall quads, DEM sampling for skirts, vertex colours) on the main thread, one chunk per frame budget. It is fast enough on desktop, but a dense 1 km small-block chunk in Mong Kok or a 4 km big-block chunk over Kowloon can still cost a visible frame, and the 5.2 MB gz inflate + HKBL parse (~180 ms desktop) blocks the UI on load.
Do
move parse + chunk building into a Worker: post the inflated buffer once, request chunks by key, receive transferable Float32Array / Uint8Array / Uint32Array buffers; the DEM sampler needs the current grid (post the elevation array on source change)
keep the main thread to BufferGeometry assembly and mesh insertion
preserve the progressive big-tier build order (near the focus first) and eviction
Acceptance: no frame > 33 ms attributable to chunk building while walking through Mong Kok with ?debug=1 timings; load-to-first-buildings not worse than today.

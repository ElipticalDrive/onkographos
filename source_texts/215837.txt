Crytek Sponza in render_lab, with baked direct light and a camera flying through it. There are two scenes: Sponza (17.4k triangles) and Sponza Lite (8.7k triangles).
```
flowchart LR
  obj[Sponza OBJ, fetched and checksummed] --> dens[densify] --> bake[bake sun + sky per vertex, Embree] --> weld[weld, averaged colour] --> simp[meshoptimizer, colour as an attribute] --> oct[cluster octree] --> gen[sponza_*_mesh_generated.c]
  gen --> r3d[render/r3d_lit: cull, transform, span raster on 2 cores] --> half[184x224, doubled to the panel]
```
Loading
Engine (render/r3d_*):
An octree cull of clusters against the frustum.
A Z-buffered Gouraud span rasterizer in fixed point, with 32-bit edges for on-screen triangles.
The two cores split the rows at a balanced split row.
Rendering runs in the app's update(), overlapped with the previous frame's panel send.
Bake (launcher/tools/r3d, with a pinned meshoptimizer v1.3 submodule): this can be reused for other meshes. Sibenik is next.
Board (diagnostics build, both cores, before present)

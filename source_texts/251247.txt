Concrete problem
F3F-NS-02 requires nested, nonzero signed Euclidean spread and finite Gaussian effects under arbitrary ancestor affine transforms. Current private src/runtime/execution_2d/shadow.rs and support.rs materialize neutral triangle-union meshes; nested zero-spread/zero-blur effects can propagate their exact translated support as triangles, but generic offset/erosion/dilation/blurred support cannot safely be collapsed back into the same finite triangle inventory. The current draft correctly rejects unsupported nested nonzero kernels rather than substituting an AABB or sampled alpha. The outstanding problem is compositional geometric support and coverage, not simply selecting a faster rasterizer.
A distance-field representation is an attractive candidate for reusable shapes, Boolean operations, clipping and signed spread. But a signed field is not automatically an exact Euclidean signed distance function after arbitrary Boolean composition or nonuniform transforms; a raster SDF/MSDF texture is a sampled physical realization, not semantic truth. Furthermore the accepted 3σ separable Gaussian coverage convolution and ordered linear-light paint are not determined by a scalar distance value alone. Investigate these limitations experimentally and mathematically rather than pre-accepting or dismissing an SDF design.
Invariants: criteria candidates MUST preserve
One canonical F1 semantic input: immutable source-neutral 2D composition, exact fill rules, strokes/caps/joins, image destination geometry, already-shaped glyph outlines, nested groups, conjunctive structural clips and arbitrary admitted transforms. Never infer support from source paint color, image texel alpha, glyph atlas/MSDF alpha, group opacity or prior shadow pixels.
Exact support operations in their owning frames: each shadow consumes the same pre-shadow child support C; signed spread means Euclidean disk dilation or erosion with truthful complete erosion; offset and separable 3σ blur operate in the group's immediate-parent logical frame; only afterward does each ancestor affine transform completed support. Blur has a finite axis-aligned square support envelope [-3σ,3σ]² in the owning frame. Group clips intersect the completed child/shadow support; transparent shadows still contribute geometric support to ancestors.
Coverage is distinct from support: reference shadow alpha is the normalized finite Gaussian convolution of the spread-region indicator (identity at σ=0), within separately justified numerical tolerances. No AABB-as-membership, incorrect distance-as-alpha shortcut, unknown-as-zero rule, root-space blur after shear, unproved finite-field truncation, or accidental shadow-of-shadow substitution.
One painter and execution authority: preserve F3E's globally phased correlated 4×4 sample compositor, authored shadow ordering behind child paint, group clip/opacity once, linear-premultiplied source-over, exact RunenGPU work/evidence ownership and cache/device reconstruction. SDF cannot silently replace image texture sampling, gradient color semantics, shaped-font input facts or general compositing.
Bounded admission and safe failure: deterministic work/depth/precision bounds; complete peak-live host+GPU resource, cache, update and in-flight costs; typed path-carrying failure before caller-target mutation; no fabricated coverage, second execution/rendering authority, or dependency on a particular backend.

A. Precise live code boundary
RunenRender ARCHITECTURE.md lines 109–173 fixes the actual law: independent effects consume the same pre-shadow C; Euclidean disk signed spread in immediate-parent coordinates; parent-frame translation; normalized separable finite 3σ Gaussian; neutral support expansion by an axis-aligned square, not disk; clip after union with child support; geometry independent of color/opacity; ancestor affine only after effect. It explicitly permits multiple semantically equivalent private realizations.
shadow.rs group_child_sources derives F1-neutral group-parent triangle unions. Nested spread≠0 or sigma≠0 is typed unsupported; only nested identity kernels propagate actual transformed triangles. mod.rs admit_runs independently enforces this prior to font preparation. This is not GPU-conformance proof for nonzero nested kernels.
support.rs computes signed Euclidean morphology, exposed-union erosion boundaries, geometric area and normalized Gaussian integrals with bounded work. sample_space/shadow.rs currently projects triangles into final sample space and derives disposable R32Float masks; projecting before applying nonzero spread/sigma under ancestor shear would be incorrect, so the nested unsupported gate is necessary.
vector.rs uses finite-tolerance Lyon-class tessellation. Even current triangles are a physical approximation of source paths, not an independent semantic ontology. A replacement must retain curve/fill/stroke semantics and explicit error bounds.
sample_space.rs owns the one 4×4 linear-premultiplied painter/group compositor. A geometry study MUST preserve it rather than building an isolated SDF-based UI renderer.
Existing CPU-work, per-mask size, total retained coverage, tile upload and private scratch budgets are valuable but not an established full peak-live/in-flight proof. Geometry DAG/field caching must not bypass admission.
B. Independently reproducible counterexamples (RR86-F1–F5)
All scalar distances below use negative inside, positive outside, and unit Euclidean coordinates. These are independent analytic/numerical reference cases; they do not call RunenRender or constitute hosted Rust/Vulkan validation.
RR86-F1 — min-union is not necessarily true interior distance (blocking for erosion). Union of unit disks centered (-0.5,0) and (+0.5,0). At (0,0), naïve min of primitive SDFs = -0.5. The nearest exposed union boundary is an intersection point (0,±sqrt(3)/2), hence the true union SDF is -0.8660254038. Erode by disk radius 0.6: naïve field+0.6=+0.1 rejects center; correct SDF+0.6=-0.2660254 retains center. Merely preserving the union's zero set did not preserve its Euclidean erosion.
RR86-F2 — max-intersection is not necessarily true exterior distance (blocking for dilation). Intersect those unit disks. At (0,0.9), naïve max of disk SDFs is +0.0295630141; the nearest lens point is (0,sqrt(3)/2), actual distance +0.0339745962. Dilation radius 0.031 makes naïve threshold include a point that true disk dilation excludes. This applies to clipped neutral support.
RR86-F3 — inverse-affine evaluation preserves sign, not Euclidean metric. A unit disk under affine diag(2,1) becomes ellipse x²/4+y²≤1. At (1.5,0), locally evaluated circle SDF = -0.25, true ellipse SDF = -0.5 (closest ellipse point (2,0)). Erosion radius 0.3 flips naïve membership. A single scalar “transform scale” cannot generally restore the metric under nonuniform scale/shear. Transforming parent-frame effect radii into root frame is unsound.
RR86-F4 — a sampled field can lose a real caster. A one-dimensional cross-section of a vertical strip x∈[0.1,0.2] is between field texel centers x=-0.5,+0.5. Correct SDF at those centers is +0.6 and +0.3. Linear interpolation at x=0.15 reports +0.405 (outside), whereas actual signed distance is -0.05 (inside). More channels/greater resolution can improve finite samples but do not make arbitrary subtexel geometry automatically exact. Offscreen and high-scale support must survive independent of sampled atlas sign.
RR86-F5 — one distance value does not determine Gaussian coverage. Compare a half-plane x≤0 and a radius-one disk centered (-1,0). Both have SDF=0 at the origin. For RunenRender's accepted separable, independently normalized, square cutoff ±3σ at σ=0.5, convolution at origin is 0.5 for the half-plane but approximately 0.39751051 for the disk. The numeric value integrates the disk's true cross-sections with Gaussian CDFs using 65,536-interval composite Simpson quadrature; 16,384 vs 65,536 differs by ~6.36e-9, and an independent SciPy adaptive quadrature gave ~0.3975105124. Important nuance: this disproves a pointwise distance-to-opacity function, NOT convolution over the full field's interior predicate. A field-based integrator could compute correct coverage, but still needs bounded neighborhood work and certified error.
Minimal reproduction (Python 3, standard library, no renderer dependency)
```
from math import erf, exp, hypot, pi, sqrt
u_naive, u_true = hypot(.5, 0) - 1, -sqrt(.75)
assert u_naive + .6 > 0 > u_true + .6
i_naive, i_true = hypot(.5, .9) - 1, .9 - sqrt(.75)
assert i_naive < .031 < i_true
assert (-.25 + .3) > 0 > (-.5 + .3)
assert (.35 * .6 + .65 * .3) > 0 > -.05

sigma = .5
cut = 3 * sigma
norm = erf(3 / sqrt(2))
def axis(a, b):
    if b <= a:
        return 0.
    return (erf(b/(sigma*sqrt(2))) - erf(a/(sigma*sqrt(2)))) / (2*norm)
def fy(y):
    q = sqrt(max(0., 1-y*y))
    a, b = max(-cut, -1-q), min(cut, -1+q)
    gy = exp(-.5*(y/sigma)**2) / (sigma*sqrt(2*pi)*norm)
    return gy*axis(a,b)
def simpson(n):
    h = 2/n
    return h/3*(fy(-1)+fy(1)+sum(
        (4 if i%2 else 2)*fy(-1+i*h) for i in range(1,n)
    ))
coarse, fine = simpson(16384), simpson(65536)
assert abs(coarse-fine) < 2e-7
assert abs(fine - .397510512) < 2e-7
print("union", u_naive, u_true, "intersection", i_naive, i_true)
print("Gaussian halfplane", .5, "tangent disk", fine)
```
Note: the 2-grid agreement is an empirical convergence check not an interval-certified quadrature error bound; a final quality decision still needs independently bounded analytic/error reference.

Gaussian support from neutral geometry is not blurred cached paint alpha; later ancestor transforms act on finished region/support.
This candidate may use analytic SDFs, distance lower bounds, triangle/curve boundary extraction, cached SDF/MSDF textures and GPU shaders as private optimizations where certification permits. F3F-NS-02 requires the actual geometric answer, not simply an attractive field abstraction.

RR86-F6 — Gaussian and ancestor affine do not commute
Let a shadow's immediate-parent neutral caster be the half-plane x≤0, with signed spread=0, offset=0, accepted finite normalized separable Gaussian sigma=0.5 and cutoff at ±3 sigma in that parent frame. Apply an ancestor affine A=diag(2,1). Inspect root-space sample (0.5,0).
Correct parent-first: inverse-map root x=0.5 to parent x=0.25; the truncated Gaussian half-plane CDF yields alpha 0.308019229798.
Incorrect flatten-first: transform geometry to root x≤0, then apply an isotropic Gaussian of the unchanged sigma=0.5 there; alpha 0.157731197967.
Absolute alpha error ≈ 0.150288031831, far above an ordinary small sampling tolerance, despite exact geometric half-plane membership and zero spread. Reproduce with Python standard library:
```
from math import erf, sqrt
sigma = 0.5
normalizer = erf(3 / sqrt(2))
def a(x):
    t = min(1.5, max(-1.5, -x))
    return (erf(t / (sigma*sqrt(2))) - erf(-3/sqrt(2))) / (2*normalizer)
print(a(.5/2), a(.5), a(.5/2)-a(.5))
# 0.30801922979837487  0.15773119796715201  0.15028803183122286
```
For a general affine A, even an untruncated isotropic Gaussian transformed into root coordinates has covariance proportional to A Aᵀ, not a root-isotropic Gaussian; the finite square kernel cutoff also transforms under A into an affine parallelogram, often with correlated axes after shear. Evaluating integrals in the original immediate-parent frame is the simplest correct baseline. The F3F nested-nonzero typed gate must stay until independently proved otherwise.
RR86-C1 — Precise language, avoiding a false rejection
It is mathematically possible to associate a true Euclidean SDF with suitable composed 2D regions, including a clipped nested result. The objection is not existence. It is that cheap pointwise min/max/composition of primitive exact SDFs does not generally remain a true distance oracle to the exposed boundary. Exact re-distancing/boundary extraction may be as expensive as another analytic geometry realization and MUST be measured.
A signed-value field (correct zero set and sign), a certified conservative distance bound, a true Euclidean distance and integrated Gaussian coverage are different capabilities. A single `f64` without an evidence/capability contract is insufficient for F3F erosion/offset certification. RunenSDF's validated numerical semantics is relevant precedent, not RunenRender ownership transfer.
For min/max of correctly signed 1-Lipschitz primitive fields, the result can retain a useful 1-Lipschitz signed-value property and exact Boolean membership. Since it is zero on the composed boundary, `|f(p)|` is at best a conservative lower bound to nearest-boundary distance. This enables safe cell-pruning/certificates, but not both-sided exact signed-radius admission: `|f| ≤ r` leaves ambiguous points requiring certified refinement or actual boundary witnesses. Under affine, track a provable bound (e.g., inverse-map Lipschitz/affine singular values), not a guessed scalar scale.
An SDF texture may be a valuable disposable cache with explicit range, source identity, transform phase, min-feature/error certification and regeneration. A half-texel-radius uncertainty margin could force exact fallback rather than falsely rejecting the RR86-F4 stripe.
“Everything SDF” must be disambiguated: (i) all geometric support is represented by an evaluable implicit region (potentially correct), versus (ii) all image, text, gradient, paint, Gaussian convolution and painter order is replaced by a scalar distance (no longer a meaningful distance-only design unless it reintroduces separate data/operations). RR86-R4 — Algebraic fast paths: precisely when Boolean SDF thresholds are exact
Independent critical review of RR86-R3's comparator; mathematical deduction, not a new production authorization. The zero-error rows in research draft #87 are not accidental for the tested two-region Boolean shapes: set algebra proves why they can be exact even when min/max do not return general true exposed-boundary Euclidean distances.
Let D_r(S) = {x | B_r(x) ∩ S ≠ ∅} be Euclidean disk dilation and E_r(S) = {x | B_r(x) ⊆ S} disk erosion for closed regions and nonnegative r. Then, for arbitrary valid regions A,B:
```
D_r(A ∪ B) = D_r(A) ∪ D_r(B)            EXACT
E_r(A ∩ B) = E_r(A) ∩ E_r(B)            EXACT

E_r(A) ∪ E_r(B) ⊆ E_r(A ∪ B)           STRICT IN GENERAL
D_r(A ∩ B) ⊆ D_r(A) ∩ D_r(B)           STRICT IN GENERAL
```
Proof sketch: dilation tests existence of a witness in the shape; existence distributes over OR (union). Erosion tests that every point in a disk belongs to the shape; universal quantification distributes over AND (intersection). Neither quantifier distributes in the opposite direction.
This directly explains the independently checked #87 cases:
The two-disk union + positive dilation naïve min-field threshold happens to be algebraically exact whenever the primitive disk SDFs are exact, although the interior signed distance of the union remains inexact.
The two-disk intersection + negative erosion naïve max-field threshold is algebraically exact whenever primitive erosions are exact, although the outside signed distance of the intersection remains inexact.
Union + erosion cannot distribute; (0,0) survives true radius-0.6 erosion of the overlapping union but does not survive eroding either disk individually. The [RR86 research] Reproducible CSG distance-field certificates and counterexamples (#86) #87 scan found 247 wrong exclusions.
Intersection + dilation cannot distribute; (0,0.9) lies in both radius-0.031 individually dilated disks but outside dilation of their lens-shaped intersection. The scan found 2 wrong inclusions.
Additional coordinate-frame algebra
For an invertible affine with linear part L, mapping the completed operation preserves set membership if the structuring element is also transformed:
```
L(D_B(S)) = D_{L(B)}(L(S))
L(E_B(S)) = E_{L(B)}(L(S))     (invertible L)
```
But an isotropic Euclidean disk maps to an ellipse under general shear/nonuniform scale; it remains a disk only under a similarity transform (rotation/reflection with uniform scale). Therefore applying an unchanged isotropic parent-frame spread after flattening through an ancestor general affine violates #79. The accepted Gaussian has a finite parent-axis-aligned square kernel; under an ancestor affine that square likewise becomes a parallelogram (see RR86-R2). Rank-deficient mappings require separate capability/precision treatment, not use of the invertible identity.

Before choosing a universal SDF, mesh or analytic DAG implementation, define an operation-specific certification matrix:

This is a stronger design than labeling entire representation families either exact or approximate. It permits provably exact symbolic simplifications where valid and a signed-field certificate/refinement path otherwise, while keeping the accepted F1 semantics as sole authority. It does not establish GPU performance or solve general nested CSG Gaussian integration.

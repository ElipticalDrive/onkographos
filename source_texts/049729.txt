This PR has three things:
option to save meshes locally, and reuse them. To later use these meshes, or faster debugging
option to compute distances following vector fields, instead of straight ray-tracing (crucial for cerebellum, could be useful for other regions)
option to bypass ultralizer and use simple marching cube algo from trimesh to avoid smoothing artifacts
better handling of cerebellum, by doing ph per lobules, and using curved ray-tracing

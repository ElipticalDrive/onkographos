Problem
Three samples record compute work on a QueueKind.GRAPHICS command list:
frustum_culling/main.c3 dispatches the culling compute pipeline and records COMPUTE_SHADER barriers on its graphics list before drawing indirectly.
gpu_driven_draw_sdl/main.c3 dispatches its draw-generation compute pipeline on the graphics list.
pipeline_cache_timing/main.c3 dispatches its compute smoke pipeline and records the compute-to-host barrier on the graphics list.
This works when the selected graphics queue family also supports compute, which is common, but that native capability is not guaranteed by the public graphics queue role. A strict device request may validly select a graphics-only family and a separate compute family. On that topology, compute dispatches and COMPUTE_SHADER stage masks recorded for the graphics family are invalid Vulkan usage.
This is distinct from the cross-family resource-ownership bugs tracked in #20 and #30: even resources with legal sharing cannot make an unsupported pipeline stage or command legal on the recording queue.
Impact
These samples can produce validation errors or undefined behavior on a Vulkan device topology that gpu.c3l otherwise supports. Because most desktop graphics families also expose compute, the assumption is easy to miss during local testing.
Suggested direction
Record every compute dispatch and compute-stage barrier on QueueKind.COMPUTE.
Split compute and graphics command lists/submissions where a sample uses both roles.
Use completion-point waits for compute-to-graphics execution and memory ordering.
Declare each allocation or texture's queue access for every role that actually reaches it.
Keep graphics-only rendering and presentation work on QueueKind.GRAPHICS.
For pipeline_cache_timing, the compute smoke can be a separate compute submission from the graphics smoke rather than relying on a combined graphics list.
Acceptance criteria
frustum_culling, gpu_driven_draw_sdl, and pipeline_cache_timing no longer record compute dispatches or COMPUTE_SHADER barriers on graphics-role command lists.
Cross-role resources declare the required access roles and cross-queue dependencies use completion points.
All three targets build and pass their existing correctness checks.
The paths validate on a device or test topology with a graphics family that lacks compute support and a separate compute family.

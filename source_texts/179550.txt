2D/3D registration based on biplanar X-ray and CT ... - ScienceDirect
Dec 1, 2024This study proposes a novel 2D/3D registration approach utilizing biplanar X-ray images combined with computed tomography (CT) to significantly reduce registration and navigation errors. The method is successfully implemented in a surgical navigation system, enhancing its precision and reliability.

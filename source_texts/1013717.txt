Issue body actions
The depth-only passes (the prepass, the sun cascades and the atlas) read whole 48-byte vertices to use 12 bytes of position. And each instance record is 80 bytes, where a 3×4 matrix and a material index fit in 64.
The change
Split vertices into a position stream and a packed attribute stream, with normal and tangent packed (octahedral or 10:10:10:2). Depth-only pipelines bind positions alone. The skinning shader writes both streams.
Instance records become a 3×4 affine matrix plus the material index. The culler uses sizeof instead of a literal 80.
Done when
The picture is unchanged, apart from a very slight change in packed normals.
The gains are measured on the profiler ship build on the Big Walk map (depth-prepass, cascade-still, atlas-bake, instance-upload). Until then they are estimates: 3–4× fewer vertex bytes in depth passes, worth 5–15% of those passes on vertex-heavy maps (low confidence); 20% less instance upload and fetch.

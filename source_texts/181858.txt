A fast 3D adaptive bilateral filter for ultrasound volume visualization
This work proposed a 3D bilateral adaptive filter for the effective and quick removal of noise in an ultrasound volume data. A fast bilateral filter was implemented by obtaining the PBFIC from the 3D summed area table of a quantized spatial filter.

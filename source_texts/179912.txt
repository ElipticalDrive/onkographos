lmage processing is one of the areas which are known to be very computalional intensive. To achieve interactive response of image processing systems usually dedicated systems or (mini-) supercomputers are necessary. Image processing is also an area in which the application of parallelism is very suitable, because of the large amounts of (similar)data involved. In this particular case, 3-D images (images in 'voxel space') have to be processed interactively. Operations on the voxel space involve 3-D image processing algorithms and visualization of the data from arbitrary viewing angles, and with several options for the type of rendering. This paper describes an alternative for special hardware or supercomputers for a voxel processor, based on a network of INMOS T800 Transputers

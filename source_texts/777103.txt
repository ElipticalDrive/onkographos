Fields and flows (electromagnetism, fluids, phase portraits, the Lorenz attractor) are a staple of explainers and no kind draws one, so give a vector field one formula, drawn as arrows or as stream lines integrated at check that flow in closed form.
Epic: #310 · Design: Vector field vector_field@1 · Constraint · Particles particles@1: space and paths · Films and figures on the canvas
Scope
vector_field@1: a vector at each point from one formula, in 2D or 3D, on a chart's scales or on 3D axes, with vars a control or a channel moves
Arrows on a grid, at most 4,096, sized by a sigmoid, a linear or a constant length and colored by magnitude or an expression through a ramp, evaluated per frame from the fn
Streams integrated by RK4 at a fixed step in pinned f64 at check, and again when a var changes, off the frame thread; each is a sub-path <id>#s<i> that stops where it leaves the grid's box widened by pad or meets a non-finite vector; up to 16,384 points when there are 8 starts or fewer, so a single-start trajectory such as the Lorenz system is one stream
Flow as a trim window in closed form, p = fract(t × speed ÷ time + phase_i), so seeking equals playing
follow_stream, the data module's entry in the core's constraint@1 (Placement by relation: beside, on a frame edge, or aligned with another item #221), carries a dot along a stream from a table built at check and passes the constraint kit; a group's warp flows along the stream table the field builds at check in data layout streams@1 (its fn with its vars' values, its step and each stream's points), read by layout, never by the vector_field@1 kind (Warps: a group's paths and glyphs carried through a formula #223)
The name: a vector field is its own word, apart from a field, which stays an expression over position, normal, index and named attributes
Fields
Vector field vector_field@1
fn: {x, y: expressions over x, y} | {x, y, z: expressions over x, y, z}
vars: {name → path | control | channel}
scales: {x, y: scale ID} | axes: axes3d ID (optional; axes3d@1 is the data module's too, so the field names no other module's kind)
arrows: {grid: {x, y, z: [a, b, step]}, length: {by: sigmoid | linear | constant = sigmoid, max: u = 0.45 × step}, color: {by: magnitude | expression, ramp: [role] ≤ 8, domain: [f64, f64] = [0, 2]}} (optional; at most 4,096 arrows)
streams: {starts: grid {x, y, z, step} | points [vec3] ≤ 4,096, jitter: f64 = 0, seed, step: f64 = 0.05, time: f64 = 3, method: rk4, pad: f64 = 3, max_points: u16 = 512 (≤ 16,384 when starts ≤ 8)} (optional)
flow: {speed: f32 = 1, flash: 0..1 = 0.3, phase: hashed | zero = hashed, warm_up: bool = true} (optional)
Read-only: each stream as <id>#s<i>, and all of them as <id>#streams
It can never: Integrate between frames; keep state; exceed 4,096 starts or arrows; change its step count during a run.
Constraint
kind also takes follow_stream {vector_field: ID, from: vec3, speed: f32 = 1, time_offset: s = 0}, the data module's entry in constraint@1
Acceptance criteria
F = (−y, x): the stream from (1, 0) for time 2π at step 0.05 returns within 1e-4 of its start; a planted Euler integrator ends more than 0.1 away and fails
The arrow of F = (−y, x)/2 at (1, 0) points along +y within 0.1° and has length 0.45 × sigmoid(0.5) within 1e-6; with a 4-role ramp over [0, 2] its color is 75% of the way from role 1 to role 2
A follow_stream dot from (2, 0) in F = (−y, x) sits at (0, 2) within 1e-6 at field time π/2, and seeking to 7.3 s gives the same trim windows and dot position as playing, by hash
On a build without the data module, a document whose constraint is follow_stream is refused with module_missing, naming data, and motion's interface digest equals the full build's; a planted motion that registers follow_stream itself changes that digest and fails, naming the entry
The Lorenz system (σ 10, ρ 28, β 8/3) from (1, 1, 1) at step 0.005 for time 40 gives byte-identical points on two machines, at most 16,384 points
Budgets, on the reference Mac in the local preflight: 1,000 streams of 60 steps integrate in under 30 ms at check
A flow of a unit square along the vector field (−y, x) for time π/2 ends rotated 90° within 0.5 px, at 24 and 60 fps alike
Shown to you: screenshots of the arrow field of F = (-y, x)/2 with its colour ramp, the stream from (1, 0) and the Lorenz attractor, taken by the check run from the canvas's own frames, with a recording of the follow_stream dot, are attached to the PR and shown to you in the session. Whether they look right is one taste call, recorded; without it the run stays INCONCLUSIVE, never PASS, and no criterion above reads a picture to judge it
Proof
A fixture film of a swirl field drawn in and flowing, with a dot carried; the Lorenz trajectory with a riding point; goldens across two machines.
Oracle: upstream tests, pinned by commit, for 4 of 8 criteria: the tests, per criterion in doneright/oracles (DeepBuildAI/deepbuild). A linked test counts only when run against our output (#136). Without a runner the lane is BLOCKED with one unblock ask.

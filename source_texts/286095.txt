# Voxel Terrain Generator

A real-time procedural voxel terrain system built in Unity 6 (`6000.4.0f1`) with deterministic world generation, biome-aware materials, and budgeted chunk streaming.

This project focuses on terrain generation, rendering, and runtime smoothness rather than survival or crafting mechanics.

## Highlights

- deterministic seeded terrain generation with cached terrain column sampling
- seven visually distinct biomes: meadow, wetland, dunes, badlands, foothills, alpine, and crags
- runtime biome-specific material synthesis from shared base materials
- cave carving with warped 3d noise
- prioritized chunk generation, chunk pooling, and budgeted mesh rebuilds to reduce loading stutter
- neighbor-aware chunk meshing to avoid open faces and boundary gaps

- `Mouse` look
- `Space` jump

## Project Layout

- `Assets/Scripts/ProceduralGeneration/VoxelTerrainGenerator.cs` orchestrates terrain sampling, biome blending, cave carving, and runtime material registration
- `Assets/Scripts/ProceduralGeneration/TerrainBiome.cs` defines the biome contract and shared sampling helpers
- `Assets/Scripts/VoxelTerrain/VoxelWorld.cs` manages active chunk data and prioritized generation queues
- `Assets/Scripts/WorldRendering/VoxelTerrainRenderer.cs` streams visible chunks and spreads generation, activation, and mesh work across frames
- `Assets/Scripts/WorldRendering/VoxelChunkRenderer.cs` builds render meshes from voxel data with per-material submeshes

## Setup Notes

- assign the base `Solid0` and `Solid1` materials in the terrain generator inspector
- keep the chunk renderer prefab assigned on the terrain renderer component
- biome surface and filler materials are generated automatically at runtime

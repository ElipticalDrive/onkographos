Why
Turn the renderer from an interactive demo into a creation tool. Keyframe-driven camera flights (fly-through, orbit, ISCO push-in) with frame-by-frame video export, plus 360° panorama / stereo panorama export (a first-person "falling into the hole" view) are high-impact for education and outreach.
What
Keyframe camera path: define waypoints (position + look-at + FOV), interpolate, scrub a timeline, render each frame to disk
360° equirectangular export: render the full sphere of rays for a static viewpoint
Stereo panorama: left/right eye equirect for VR viewing
Reuses the existing orbit controller basis (src/camera.rs); add a "record" mode
Acceptance
Keyframe path editor + timeline scrub
"Export video" renders a path to a frame sequence (ffmpeg → mp4)
360° equirect export for a chosen viewpoint
Stereo panorama export (left/right)
Prerequisites
High-res / stereo frames are expensive — depends on temporal accumulation (Temporal accumulation (TAA-style reprojection) for near-offline quality #2) to make per-frame cost affordable.
References
Camera: src/camera.rs (orbit controller: yaw/pitch/zoom + FOV)

Closes #7.
Adds eight selectable SSR renderers: linear, conservative Hi-Z, adaptive cell traversal and adaptive mip-1 traversal in mirror and stochastic GGX modes. The isolated three-hiz submodule pins fac1b2050ae4cd5a59b890a878e69e616c2782f7 without changing the original core or existing pipelines. Every hierarchy reads the exact SSR scene depth.
Nine diagnostic scenes were captured at 480×360, quality 0.5, seed 1 and 64 completed frames using a physical RTX 3060 Ti and Chrome 148. Matching-linear and pathtrace presets provide PSNR/delta comparisons. All 18 conservative PNG comparisons are exact; repeat captures of all eight mirror-scene variants are byte-identical.
Adaptive mirror loses 0.0378 dB mean pathtrace PSNR and has visible offscreen reflection stair steps. Adaptive GGX loses 0.3315 dB (worst scene: -0.9966 dB). Full-pipeline throughput across 72 sequential 10-second windows is 0.865× linear for adaptive mirror and 0.819× for adaptive GGX. This preset shows no net improvement; the separate 1080p/quality-1 demo benefit is context-specific. Coarse variants, raw timing counts, PNG/delta evidence and limits are included in docs/SSR-HIZ-RESULTS.md and docs/data/.
After deployment, compare all variants at https://three-ssr-fidelity.ben3d.ca/ and run the live view at https://three-ssr-fidelity.ben3d.ca/render/performance.html?scene=diag-offscreen&renderer=three-hiz-mirror-adaptive&run=1 .
Validation: pnpm build, pnpm tsc, pnpm lint (existing warnings), pnpm test --coverage (242 passed, 1 skipped), pnpm site:build, Canvas2D unified UI contracts, 72 hardware captures, 72 completed-frame throughput cases and processing 876 fidelity pairs passed. Audit reports 7 existing advisories (1 moderate, 4 high, 2 critical) in the pathtracer's tooling dependencies. Hardware evidence and all raw results are committed; no software GPU was used.
Merged the concurrent power-lobe integration into this branch and reran combined checks. Both renderer families and their captures/presets are retained; scene definitions and all nine pathtrace reference blobs are unchanged.

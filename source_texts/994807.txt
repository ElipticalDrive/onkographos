Step 7 of the terrain roadmap:
replace the single marching-cubes proof with a fixed 2×2 resident chunk grid
generate the base scalar field from absolute voxel-world coordinates
give each chunk one GPU density-sample halo for border smoothing and gradient normals
keep cell ownership exclusive while sharing deterministic border sample planes
route each sparse stamp only to chunks intersecting its sphere plus halo
transform global stamp coordinates into each affected chunk's local compute space
avoid regenerating chunks whose relevant stamp signature did not change
retain one indirect GPU draw per resident chunk and zero GPU-to-CPU readbacks
expand cursor coordinates and save metadata to the full voxel world
migrate version 4 single-chunk stamps into the centered version 5 volume
preserve transactional imports, global undo/redo, heightfields, objects, and WebGL fallback behavior
This phase keeps all four chunks resident. Camera-driven streaming, per-chunk dirty-region dispatch, LOD transitions, and heightfield-to-voxel stitching remain deferred.

To improve speed and effect of volume rendering,volume data classification and calculation of volume rendering on graphic processing unit(GPU) is studied.Firstly,point-based and pre-integrated classification are applied relatively,it allocated color and opacity for every volume data and color interpolated with opacity-weight in order to improve rendering effect,then volume data are ren-dered with Phong optical model.At the same time software acceleration with look up table and graphic hardware acceleration are imple-mented and compared,the experiment results show that GPU-based acceleration method achieves real-time interactive effect and improves performance of volume rendering.

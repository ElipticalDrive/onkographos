This paper proposes a high-precision and efficient voxelization algorithm for the development of voxelization in the preprocessing stage of industrial simulation. The main idea is to establish an octree data structure to evenly divide the three-dimensional mesh model space into voxel sets. By using the separation axis method and the improved scan line method, the position relationship between voxels and triangles can be directly determined by skipping the calculation of intersection between voxels and triangles. The innovation of this article lies in the use of the separation axis algorithm and the improved scanning line method for voxelization, and the simplification of the algorithm's operations through the octree data structure, thereby reducing the time and spatial complexity of the algorithm.

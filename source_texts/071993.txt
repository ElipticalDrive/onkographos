Volume Rendering Algorithms Bibliography & References
This document gathers the literature references, preprints, and publications related to the volume rendering algorithms implemented in Octant for further review and verification.
0. Direct Volume Rendering (DVR / Volume Raymarching)
Front-to-back optical emission-absorption raymarching with alpha compositing and Beer-Lambert extinction.
Reference: Levoy, M. (1988). "Display of surfaces from volume data", IEEE Computer Graphics and Applications, 8(3), pp. 29–37.
DOI: 10.1109/38.511
Reference: Drebin, R. A., Carpenter, L., & Hanrahan, P. (1988). "Volume Rendering", ACM SIGGRAPH Computer Graphics, 22(4), pp. 65–74.
DOI: 10.1145/378456.378484
1. Isosurface Raymarching with Sub-Voxel Bisection & 3D Gradient Normals
Raymarching until isovalue crossing, refined with 3-step binary search bisection and shaded using 26-neighbor 3D Sobel-Feldman normals.
Reference (Volume Graphics & Root Finding): Engel, K., Hadwiger, M., Kniss, J. M., Rezk-Salama, C., & Weiskopf, D. (2006). "Real-Time Volume Graphics", CRC Press / AK Peters.
DOI: 10.1201/b10624
Reference (Gradient Operator): Sobel, I., & Feldman, G. (1968). "A 3x3 Isotropic Gradient Operator for Image Processing", Stanford Artificial Intelligence Project.
Paper: ResearchGate Link
2. Maximum Intensity Projection (MIP & Attenuated MIP)
Finds maximum scalar along the ray, optionally attenuated by accumulated density for depth perception.
Reference (MIP): Wallis, J. W., Miller, T. R., Lerner, C. A., & Kleerup, E. C. (1989). "Three-dimensional display in nuclear medicine", IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging, 8(4), pp. 297–303.
DOI: 10.1109/42.41482
Reference (Attenuated Falloff): Bruckner, S., & Gröller, M. E. (2007). "Style-Transfer Functions for Illustrative Volume Rendering", Computer Graphics Forum, 26(3), pp. 715–724.
DOI: 10.1111/j.1467-8659.2007.01095.x
3. Minimum Intensity Projection (MinIP)
Tracks the minimum scalar along the ray (useful for airway visualization, low-pressure troughs, and cold cores).
Reference: Remy-Jardin, M., et al. (2003). "Minimum Intensity Projection (MinIP) CT of the Lung", European Radiology, 13(12), pp. 2771–2778.
DOI: 10.1007/s00330-003-1959-1
4. Average / Mean Intensity Projection (Radiographic Column Transmission)
Integrates and averages valid scalar densities through the column to simulate X-ray radiographic transmission.
Foundational Reference: Radon, J. (1917). "Über die Bestimmung von Funktionen durch ihre Integralwerte längs gewisser Mannigfaltigkeiten", Berichte Sächsische Akademie der Wissenschaften, 69, pp. 262–277.
Reprint / Review: 10.1109/TMI.1986.4307775
5. Categorical / Label Segmented Surface
Isosurface rendering for discrete integer/categorical data, computing normals from the binary foreground mask.
Reference: Tikhonova, A., Correa, C. D., & Ma, K.-L. (2010). "Visualization of Multi-Parameter Data with Qualitative Transfer Functions", IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, 16(6), pp. 1525–1532.
DOI: 10.1109/TVCG.2010.180
6. Optical Absorption RGBA & Additive Emission
Radiative transfer models for absorption and emission volumes.
Reference: Max, N. (1995). "Optical Models for Direct Volume Rendering", IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, 1(2), pp. 99–108.
DOI: 10.1109/2945.468400
7. Volume Indexed RGBA
Texture-palette indexed discrete material volume rendering.
Reference: Beyer, J., Hadwiger, M., & Pfister, H. (2015). "State-of-the-Art in GPU-Based Large-Scale Volume Visualization", Computer Graphics Forum, 34(8), pp. 13–37.
DOI: 10.1111/cgf.12735
8. Shaded Contours
Non-photorealistic gradient-based silhouette and contour enhancement for boundary visualization.
Reference: Csébfalvi, B., Mroz, L., Hauser, H., König, A., & Gröller, M. E. (2001). "Fast Visualization of Object Contours by Non-Photorealistic Volume Rendering", Computer Graphics Forum, 20(3), pp. 452–460.
DOI: 10.1111/1467-8659.00538

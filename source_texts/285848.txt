May be there is a terminology problem... The "marching squares" algorithm finds all the contours at the same time (including holes, see en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marching_squares). Given an image what kind of output are you looking for? A general polygon (i.e. not necessarily connected or simply connected) for each of the values that is present in the image?

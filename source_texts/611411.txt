Parent
T006 — Spatial Encoders and Observation Efficiency #16
Motivation
The existing Garden residual 3D encoder demonstrates that pretrained voxel features are useful, but we do not yet have a principled way to choose or scale the architecture. Unconstrained searches over large 3D CNNs would be expensive and could optimize parameter count or FLOPs without improving actual RL inference latency.
EfficientNet suggests a more economical procedure: tune one small, resource-efficient baseline, empirically determine how depth and width should scale together, and derive a small family of Pareto-efficient models. We should borrow that methodology without assuming that 2D MBConv blocks, depthwise 3D convolutions, or image-resolution scaling transfer directly to tiny voxel grids.
Outcome
Define, pretrain, and validate a voxel_resnet_3d family whose members provide measured representation-quality/resource tradeoffs for voxel observations.
Proposed work
Establish the current r2:v1 encoder as the fixed baseline/control.
Correct and version the reconstruction objective, including true positive-class weighting.
Search a compact B0-style residual 3D baseline:
standard versus preactivation residual blocks;
GroupNorm configuration;
stage widths, block counts, and limited downsampling;
average versus average-plus-max pooling;
optional squeeze-and-excitation and coordinate channels.
Tune the training recipe separately from architectural capacity:
AdamW versus RMSProp;
learning rate, weight decay, warmup, and schedule;
BCE, Dice, and focal combinations;
masked-voxel ratio and progressive world difficulty.
Measure depth-only, width-only, and balanced depth/width scaling from the selected baseline.
Keep voxel resolution controlled by the sensor contract; do not scale it merely to enlarge the network.
Compare standard 3D convolutions with any depthwise or fused alternatives using actual batch-1 latency, not FLOPs alone.
Fully pretrain only the best Pareto candidates and expose optional pretrained weights through the RLlib encoder interface.
Evaluation
Do not optimize validation reconstruction loss alone. Record at least:
held-out and out-of-distribution reconstruction IoU;
precision, recall, and sparse/single-voxel sensitivity;
face-occupancy accuracy and auxiliary-feature probes;
frozen-feature downstream RL learning/sample efficiency;
parameter count, observation bytes, peak training memory;
warm batch-1 CPU and GPU policy-path latency.
Evaluate finalists on both procedural primitives and observation patches collected from real environment trajectories. Report at least three seeds for final candidates.
Acceptance criteria
A reproducible B0 architecture/training search is available with pruning and resource limits.
The legacy r2:v1 checkpoint remains a comparable control.
The corrected loss has regression tests and objective/checkpoint versioning.
At least three scaling experiments isolate depth, width, and balanced scaling.
A documented voxel_resnet_3d family is selected from measured quality/latency/memory Pareto results.
At least one pretrained model beats r2:v1 on the agreed representation score without unacceptable batch-1 inference cost.
The selected encoder is validated in an RL training comparison against random initialization and the legacy encoder.
Checkpoints preserve seeds, dataset provenance, dependency versions, metrics, and architecture settings needed for reproduction.
References
EfficientNet: Rethinking Model Scaling for Convolutional Neural Networks — https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/tan19a.html
EfficientNetV2: Smaller Models and Faster Training — https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/tan21a.html

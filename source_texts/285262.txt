Stacked on #176.
Features
The solver collects all surfaces that a ray hits within 1 nm of the closest hit, e.g. the faces of a cemented doublet or a coating on its substrate, as a chain of hits (BeamletOptics.coincident_hits), each with its own object, shape and normal
Preference coincidence_tolerance (default 1e-9 m), BeamletOptics.Config.get_coincidence_tolerance()
Bugfixes
Changes results: a Hint no longer wins over a closer hit, it only orders the tests. Parts moved apart, e.g. the back lens of a doublet or the front prism of a cube beamsplitter, now leave an air gap that the trace sees (5 @test_broken of the test matrix pass)
Performance
Objects whose bounding sphere the ray enters only beyond the closest hit found so far are not tested
The hinted object is tested as a whole, not only the hinted shape, to find the touching parts: doublet and cube beamsplitter alone ~20–40 % slower, among 50 lenses ~0–15 %; a plate beamsplitter ~0–20 %
Doc changes
Docstrings of Intersection, trace_all, trace_one, intersect3d(parts, ray), object!

No skill files affected

Preserve hits when shape metadata is absent
For an extension whose intersect3d returns Intersection(t, n) and leaves the documented optional shape field as nothing, this identity check treats every later hit as a duplicate, so coincident hits from other objects are omitted from the chain. Keep unknown-shape hits distinct, or deduplicate them using a non-null surface key, so custom geometries can use the generic coincident-hit path.

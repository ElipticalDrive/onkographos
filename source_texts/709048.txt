Implements milestone M4b of #1001 (stacked on #2360), formalizing Haitner, Holenstein, Reingold, Vadhan, and Wee, Inaccessible Entropy II: IE Functions and Universal One-Way Hashing, Theory of Computing 16(8):1–55, 2020 ([HHRVW2020], Sections 5.1.1–5.1.3 and Section 5.1.5, Theorem 5.1 STEPS 1–3, Lemmas 5.3–5.5, pp. 26–31) to construct a uniform polynomial-time candidate grid (ShrinkingCollisionGrid) of strictly 1-bit shrinking hash functions containing an index sequence collision-resistant on random inputs from any uniform OneWay function.
Summary of mathematical progress
Complexity/Crypto/Computational/InaccessibleEntropy/Repetition.lean (Section 5.1.1, Lemma 5.3, pp. 26–27):
Defines coordinatewise t-fold repetition repHashFin and repAccessibleSet, proving product fiber decomposition (fiberSet_repHashFin, card_fiberSet_repHashFin, card_repAccessibleSet), additive sample entropy (realSampleEntropy_repHashFin), and additive accessible log-cardinality (logb_card_repAccessibleSet).
Proves independent-coordinate Hoeffding tail bounds (repHashFin_realSampleEntropy_le_prob, repAccessibleSet_logb_card_ge_prob, and their flat BitString (t * inLen₀) transports repEvalWord_realSampleEntropy_le_prob and repBitAccessibleSet_logb_card_ge_prob via PMF.pi_sum_le_mean_sub and PMF.pi_sum_ge_mean_add).
Defines word-level evaluator repEvalWord (computableInPolyTime_repEvalWord) and the dyadic planted-coordinate escape reduction repEscapeInverter (isPPT_repEscapeInverter), proving repEscape_le_dyadicSize_mul_escape (any escape from repBitAccessibleSet occurs with probability at most dyadicSize (t n) times single-coordinate escape).
Complexity/Crypto/Computational/InaccessibleEntropy/EntropyReduction.lean (Sections 5.1.2–5.1.3, Lemmas 5.4 and 5.5, pp. 27–29):
Formalizes two-universal input hashing $F_2^{(j)}(z, h) = (F_1(z), h, \mathrm{zeroPadPrefix};n_1;j;(h(z)))$ (inputHashFin, inputHashEvalWord) and 1-bit shrinking output hashing $F_3^{(j)}(w, h_{\mathrm{out}}) = (h_{\mathrm{out}}, h_{\mathrm{out}}(\mathrm{enc}(F_2^{(j)}(w))))$ (shrunkHashFin, shrunkHashEvalWord), proving shrunkHashOutLen_add_one (shrunkHashOutLen n₁ m₁ + 1 = shrunkHashInLen n₁ m₁ for $1 \le n_1$) and plain polynomial-time evaluation (computableInPolyTime_shrunkHashEvalWord).
Proves inputHashFin_accessible_collision_le (Lemma 5.4, Equation (5.4), p. 28: colliding inside L under inputHashFin f₁ j has probability at most #L / 2^(min j n₁)), card_image_inputHashFin_le (Lemma 5.5, Equation (5.6), p. 29: image size of inputHashFin f₁ j is at most $(\Pr[H_z \le K] + 2^{\min(j, n_1) - K}) \cdot 2^{n_2}$), and shrunkHashFin_false_positive_le (Lemma 5.5, Equation (5.7), p. 29: outer-hash false-positive collision probability is at most $|F_2^{(j)}({0, 1}^{n_2})| / 2^{n_2 - 1}$).
Defines randomCollisionGame (Definition 3.6, p. 13) and the truncation-oblivious uniform strict-PPT escape reduction shrunkHashEscapeInverter (isPPT_shrunkHashEscapeInverter), and proves the combined concrete security bound shrunkHash_randomCollision_le.
Complexity/Crypto/Computational/InaccessibleEntropy/Amplify.lean (Section 5.1.5, Proof of Theorem 5.1, STEPS 1–3, pp. 30–31):
Defines pure-ℕ schedule ampSlack, ampReps, ampGridCount, and fallback-guarded ampRepInLen, ampRepOutLen, ampRepEvalWord, together with the ShrinkingCollisionGrid structure bundling $\kappa(n)$ uniformly polynomial-time 1-bit shrinking candidates sharing widths outLen n + 1 = inLen n and n + 1 ≤ outLen n.
Proves InaccessibleEntropyFamily.exists_shrinkingCollisionGrid: choosing candidate index $q^(n) = \lfloor t(n) a_0(n) / \Delta(n) \rfloor_+ + 3 < \kappa(n)$ places $j^(n) = q^(n) \Delta(n)$ inside $[t a_0 + 2 \Delta, t k_0 - 3 \Delta]$, bounding all four statistical terms by $6 \cdot 2^{-n}$ while the composed reduction (repEscapeInverter $\circ$ shrunkHashEscapeInverter) remains uniform strict-PPT without reading $q^(n)$.
Composes with OneWay.exists_inaccessibleEntropyFamily (feat(crypto): construct inaccessible-entropy family from any one-way function #2360) to obtain OneWay.exists_shrinkingCollisionGrid.
ComplexityTests/Crypto/InaccessibleEntropy.lean:
Adds concrete unconditional checks of repHashFin_two_firstBitProj_gap (entropy doubling at $t = 2$), isPPT_repEscapeInverter, repEscape_le_dyadicSize_mul_escape, shrunkHashOutLen_add_one at $n_1 = 4, m_1 = 2$, isPPT_shrunkHashEscapeInverter, shrunkHash_randomCollision_le, and the ShrinkingCollisionGrid endpoints.
Source-to-Lean mapping and limitations
In [HHRVW2020], Section 5.1.5, STEPS 1–3 (pp. 30–31), the truncation parameter $j(n)$ depends on the (not necessarily efficiently computable) real conditional entropy $k_0(n) = H(Z \mid F_0(Z))$; ShrinkingCollisionGrid makes this explicit by exposing the full uniformly polynomial-time grid of $\kappa(n) = 6 D(n) (n_0(n) + 1) + 3$ candidates $F_3^{(q \cdot \Delta(n))}$ ($0 \le q < \kappa(n)$) together with existence of a candidate sequence goodIdx n < gridCount n secure in randomCollisionGame against all uniform strict-PPT adversaries.
Milestone scope: this PR covers STEPS 1–3 of Theorem 5.1 (M4b of Signatures from one-way functions: Merkle trees to Rompel #1001). Target shifts (Lemma 5.6), chain domain extension (Lemma 5.7, STEP 4), and $\kappa(n)$-way concatenation / tree hashing for full target collision resistance and Rompel's signature theorem remain open in Signatures from one-way functions: Merkle trees to Rompel #1001.

The uniform Lean endpoint does not assume an extra entropy or nonuniform-advice premise. In parallel, relativizing the Holenstein pseudoentropy, three-source, entropy-grid, XOR-combination and reindexing pipeline to polynomially bounded advice families yields the nonuniform construction chain [`NonuniformOneWay.exists_nonuniformPRG`](../../Complexity/Crypto/Computational/OneWayToPRG.lean#L68) (one-bit stretch) and [`NonuniformOneWay.exists_nonuniformPRG_three_mul`](../../Complexity/Crypto/Computational/OneWayToPRG.lean#L77) (length-tripling `3 * n + 1` stretch) from any [`NonuniformOneWay`](../../Complexity/Crypto/Computational/Nonuniform/Basic.lean#L304) function.
For universal one-way hashing infrastructure, [`OneWay.exists_inaccessibleEntropyFamily`](../../Complexity/Crypto/Computational/InaccessibleEntropy/OneWay.lean) constructs an [`InaccessibleEntropyFamily`](../../Complexity/Crypto/Computational/InaccessibleEntropy/Basic.lean) with gap denominator `denom n = 64 * (n + 1)` from any uniform `OneWay` function, proving a weaker constant-threshold adaptation of Haitner–Holenstein–Reingold–Vadhan–Wee, Section 4.2, Theorem 4.5 [hhrvw20][hhrvw20] via the `3`-wise independent Boolean affine hash family [`isThreeWiseIndependentHash_affineHash`](../../Complexity/Probability/BitString/AffineHash/ThreeWise.lean) and prefix-collision seed projection `adjustAffineSeed`: whereas the paper uses threshold `2^(n - i) / n^c` to achieve gap `d log n / n` for every constant `d > 0` under Definition 3.9, the Lean construction fixes one adversary-independent accessible-set family against uniform strict-PPT adversaries, encodes the prefix length in a dyadic `(Nat.log 2 n + 1)`-bit saturating field, and uses constant threshold `2^(n - min r n) / 4` to obtain the smaller inverse-linear gap `1 / (64 * (n + 1))`.

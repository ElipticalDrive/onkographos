Issue body actions
Mesh levels chosen by projected error and blended through complementary masks, the bands and the light cut as pure functions of the view, and a live view that climbs above its class on a machine with headroom while canonical renders never climb.
Epic: #310 · Part of: #78 · Design: The cost model · Meter and degrade ladder
Scope
Orbit and zoom with the canvas's one set of gestures, and levels of detail that open on zoom
Level of detail by error: inside the full and proxy bands, a mesh asset's index-only levels are chosen by projected error against the class's lod_px (1 device px), shadow passes using the light's projection; a band margin blends through an integer level the CPU computes from the canonical view, compared on the GPU with complementary masks keyed by pixel and item ID in the color, depth, shadow and object-ID passes
The ladder's climb: on a machine with headroom the live view rises above its class's base (render scale while moving to 1, then 4 samples while moving, full-resolution effects and shadow maps at twice their size), from coefficients measured on this machine; canonical renders never climb
Fields
Level of detail: levels by error
On the simplify job: levels: [{ratio, max_triangles}] | auto = auto, auto: {growth: f32 = 1.5, min_gain: f32 = 0.25, max_error: f32 = 1}
Probe output: lod_errors: [f32] (each level's object-space error as a fraction of the bounds; index-only levels over one vertex buffer)
lod_px: f32, device px = 1 (on each budget class; a document may only lower it)
It can never: Be chosen by time or by an earlier frame; replace an item a check reads at full detail; emit a level whose error is lower than the level before.
Acceptance criteria
Level of detail and the light cut are pure functions of the view: a camera that crosses a band threshold or the light cut from either side, and a seek to the same time, give the same bands, light weights and pixel hash on one machine, with no session hysteresis and no fade in wall-clock time
The same view always draws the same mesh level, the coarsest whose projected error is at most 1 device px, and a camera that crosses a level's threshold from either side or a seek to the same time gives the same pixel hash on one machine
Inside a band margin the two bands' complementary masks cover every pixel exactly once in the color, depth, shadow and object-ID passes, and the blend level is the same integer on every machine for the same view
With a stub meter that reports half of the showpiece class's frame time free on this machine, a showpiece's live view climbs from render scale 0.5 to 1 after 120 frames of headroom and steps back down within 30 frames once the headroom is gone; a render-back snapshot and an export of the same revision are byte-identical whether or not the live view climbed
A showpiece that stepped down for frame health stays down while its view is unchanged, even with headroom, and climbs again once the camera moves or you raise it
Shown to you: screenshots of a mesh at each level of detail, a band margin blend, and a view climbing the ladder, taken by the check run from the renderer's own output, with a recording of an orbit and zoom across the bands, are attached to the PR and shown to you in the session. Whether they look right is one taste call, recorded; without it the run stays INCONCLUSIVE, never PASS, and no criterion above reads a picture to judge it
Proof
Band, level and light-cut crossings from both sides with equal hashes, the masks covering each pixel once, and the climb under a stub meter.
Oracle: no upstream test checks these criteria; why, per criterion in doneright/oracles (DeepBuildAI/deepbuild).

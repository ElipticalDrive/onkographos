With progress in medical tomography, multiple-layer images have easily been obtained. Various methods to construct three-dimensional surfaces have been proposed. The “surface rendering method,” in which the surface of an object represented by triangles or square patches is shaded with a normal line to each patch and optical interference, is a popular approach. To obtain the surface data from the volume data, “marching-cubes methods” have often been used, and many related papers have been published (e.g., compensation of triangle patches, high-speed operation, separation of cells). However, no method preserves the volume of an object correctly, since each uses linear interpolation. This paper proposes a method to preserve the volume accurately by adjusting the value of voxels (voxel value) before the marching-cubes method is applied. The proposed method uses an algorithm which adds a small value to each insufficient voxel, or subtracts a small value from each excess voxel. The method has been applied to four examples, and realistic three-dimensional images have been produced. © 1998 Scripta Technica, Syst Comp Jpn, 29(13): 28–35, 1998

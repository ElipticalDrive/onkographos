This adds exciting procedural generation features! The --twist <degrees> argument spirals meshes around the Z-axis, while --taper <factor> scales them along the Z-axis, acting as dynamic deformers before voxelization. The --porous <threshold> argument acts during solid voxelization, evaluating 3D Perlin noise to drop voxels, allowing users to create sponge or swiss-cheese-like porous structures.

This commit adds three new creative features to the voxelizer-rs project:

1. Twist: Deforms the mesh by rotating vertices around the Z-axis based on their Z-height.
2. Taper: Deforms the mesh by scaling the X and Y coordinates based on their Z-height.
3. Porous: Drops voxels during generation using a Perlin noise threshold, creating a sponge-like texture.

These features expand the procedural generation capabilities of the tool.

Changes include updates to the CLI args in main.rs, the TransformConfig, the deformer logic in MeshProcessor::from_file, and the porous logic in MeshProcessor::voxelize. Corresponding tests have been added to ensure correct operation.

## 📋 Overview

The Voxelizer converts molecular structures into **multi-channel 3D tensors** suitable for 3D convolutional neural networks (3D-CNNs). Each atom's density is modelled as an isotropic Gaussian spread across neighbouring voxels, producing element-specific channels analogous to RGB channels in computer vision.

This is the companion project to Week 7 Project 1 ("The Digital Skin"), which used the Marching Cubes algorithm to extract surface meshes from scalar fields. Here, the scalar field itself — the Gaussian density grid — becomes the primary output, serving as direct input for AI models.

---

## 🧬 Key Concepts

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Gaussian Density** | Each atom contributes `exp(-r²/2σ²)` to surrounding voxels |
| **Multi-Channel Encoding** | Separate channel per element type (C, N, O, S, H, …) + Total |
| **MRI View** | Scroll through Z-axis slices like a medical MRI scan |
| **Resolution Scaling** | Same molecule at 8³ → 64³ — watch atoms blur at low resolution |
| **Sigma Tuning** | Control Gaussian width: sharp peaks → smooth electron clouds |
| **CNN-Ready Export** | `.npy` and `.h5` files loadable by PyTorch/TensorFlow |

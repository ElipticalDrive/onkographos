(ISMRM 2025) 1-Click Bone Volume Rendering with ZTE MRI
Abstract #3118

1-Click Bone Volume Rendering with ZTE MRI
Michael Carl1, Laura Carretero-Gomez2,3, Sagar Mandava4, and Maggie Fung5
1GE Healthcare, Fallbrook, CA, United States, 2GE Healthcare, Madrid, Spain, 3Medical Image Analysis and Biometry Lab, Rey Juan Carlos Universi, Madrid, Spain, 4GE Healthcare, Atlanta, GA, United States, 5GE Healthcare, New York, NY, United States
Synopsis
Keywords: Bone/Skeletal, Bone
Motivation: Our motivation is to investigate a method to generate CT-like positive bone-contrast images using MR.
Goal(s): Our goal is to facilitate CT-like Volume Rendering of cortical bone using ZTE MR.
Approach: We added additional processing steps to clinical oZTEo bone images, such as DL chemical shift correction, background air signal removal and histogram adjustments.
Results: We show the feasibility of bone imaging using oZTEo with the potential to enhance the clinical utility of MSK MRI by making volume rendering easier/faster leading to VR renderings that more closely resemble the benchmark CT images.
Impact: Our work has the potential to become a one-stop-shop imaging approach with the ability to get co-registered bone and soft-tissue images in a single, radiation-free MR exam.

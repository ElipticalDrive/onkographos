💡 What: Hoisted WebGL program activation (gl.useProgram), texture binding, and global matrix/camera/light/fog uniform uploads into prepareWorldProgram(p, sky, torch, water) executed once per render pass, instead of redundantly inside drawWorldMesh for every chunk mesh.
🎯 Why: Previously, drawWorldMesh issued 16+ WebGL uniform/program driver calls per chunk mesh on every frame. For scenes with 25 loaded chunks, this generated over 800 redundant GL driver calls per frame.
📊 Impact: Reduces per-frame WebGL driver uniform setter calls for world rendering by ~96% (from ~800 calls down to 32 calls per frame).
🔬 Measurement: Verified using BlockRealmDebug.selfTest() and Playwright E2E browser context rendering verification.

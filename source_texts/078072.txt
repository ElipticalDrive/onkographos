Summary
The voxel meshers (blocky, marching cubes, hybrid and the LOD coarse mesh) moved out of
moho_core (ENG-F10) and carry its pre-gate tests with them.
Acceptance criteria
```
Scenario Outline: Every mutant in a voxel module is caught or excluded with a reason
  Given the voxel module group <group>
  When the mutation tool runs over it
  Then every viable mutant is caught by a test
  Or it is excluded in the mutation config with a stated reason

  Examples:
    | group             |
    | meshing and faces |
```
Notes
Split 2026-10-20 along the Examples rows (363 survivors + 12 timeouts in the baseline): this card keeps meshing and faces; grid, chunk, chunk store and modification moved to [ENG-F9-09] Voxel grid and chunk storage fail a test when their behaviour changes #450 (ENG-F9-09); lighting moved to [ENG-F9-10] Voxel lighting fails a test when its behaviour changes #451 (ENG-F9-10). Tech spec
Gate class: domain
Design
Tests, plus a winding fix.
Winding (found while probing; the same bug [ENG-F9-02] Pawn-facing game rules fail a test when their behaviour changes #366 fixed on actor cubes). Blocky blocks'
bottom (−Y) face and the LOD coarse mesh's −Y quads wind clockwise seen from outside,
so the renderer's back-face culling (CCW front) hides them. Visible when looking up at an
overhang or a structure's underside. Fix: reverse the −Y vertex order in
FaceDirection::vertices_and_normal (keeping each vertex's AO with its vertex) and in
the coarse mesh's −Y quad. Marching cubes is already right: all 820 triangles of the 254
non-trivial cells wind outward.
Winding tests, one rule for every mesher: each triangle's (b−a)×(c−a) points the
same way as its vertex normal (blocky, coarse) or away from the solid corners (marching
cubes, exhaustively over cell_triangles(1..=254); [ENG-F9-03] Raycast hits agree with the surface the player sees #367's agreement test ties those to
generate_mesh).
Blocky AO (118 of the 133). A property test over random 3×3×3 neighbourhoods
computes each vertex's expected AO from geometry alone: the face normal n, the two
in-plane steps e1, e2 toward the vertex, occluders at n+e1, n+e2, n+e1+e2, and the
standard rule (both sides → 0.4, else 1 − 0.2·count). Today's mesher passes it (probe:
9,600 vertices, 0 mismatches), and every offset typo in compute_ao's table fails it.
vertex_ao's unused _corner parameter is dropped.
Blocky add_face (10): light and sky values per vertex against a grid with known
light (the / and * survivors are the 0..15 → 0..1 scaling).
Coarse mesh (57): a solid region and a single-block region at stride 2: quad
positions on the 2-block lattice, only exposed faces emitted, normals per face. Plus the
winding test.
Marching cubes (31): process_cell's edge interpolation with non-0/1 densities (vertex
sits at the iso crossing along the edge) and AO from sample_density (0.6 + 0.4·(1−d))
at a known point. Delete create_density_field if it still has no caller.
Out of scope
Smooth-shading normals that disagree with the face on thin features (10 of 764 triangles
in a probe); that's shading, not winding.
Greedy meshing, face culling changes, AO formula changes.
The renderer's cull mode.
Footprint
One part (the winding helper is shared by all mesh tests).

Risks
The −Y fix makes undersides appear where they were invisible; manual check: look up at
an overhang and at a placed structure from below.
Shared rules
Rule for the three voxel cards (#370, #450 ENG-F9-09, #451 ENG-F9-10): a function with no production caller whose only uses sit in that
card's own files is deleted with its tests (confirm with GitNexus impact + rg). One used
by another card's tests (for example MaterialRegistry::set_lighting, which lighting tests
use as setup) is kept and tested instead, so no card edits another's files.
Timeouts: where a mutant makes a loop never see its own writes (the BFS re-queues forever),
it is excluded with "hangs rather than survives", the repo's precedent
(world_blocks). Where the loop can be bounded without changing behaviour, bound it instead.

Deviations recorded while implementing (PR from branch claude/project-thread-s68uqq):
Blocky AO tables replaced by a derivation. compute_ao's 24 hand-written offset triples were a second table that had to list the same corners in the same order as vertices_and_normal; the −Y fix had to reorder both. Each vertex now finds its occluders from its own position and the face normal (same rule, same values; the AO proptest passed against the old tables first).
Two unreachable marching-cubes guards removed instead of excluding their mutants: the cube index 0/255 check (EDGE_TABLE is 0 there) and the |d1−d0| < 1e-5 → t = 0.5 fallback (a crossed edge has one end above 0.5 and one at or below it).
One equivalent-mutant exclusion in .cargo/mutants.toml: replace & with [|^] in MarchingCubes::process_cell (the edge filter only skips edges no triangle uses).
Observation, not changed (out of scope): every marching-cubes vertex sits on the 0.5 isosurface, so smooth-terrain AO is a constant 0.8.

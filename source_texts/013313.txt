Map for roadmap outcome 4. Outcomes 1 (#58), 2 (#98) and 3 (#122) are complete. Outcome 3 is qualified on the RTX 4070 and the RTX 3060 Laptop.
Notes
Outcome: see the entire 16x16 streamed world from anywhere, through per-volume detail levels in both Render Paths. Coarse levels stay revision-correct, every category stays within ratified byte caps, and frames meet p99 ≤ 10 ms at 1920x1080 on the RTX 3060 Laptop.
Sequencing: map → bounded profile/feasibility → to-spec → ADR 0002 and CONTEXT.md terms. If a cap or gate fails, shaping reopens. Neither is loosened quietly; a traversal-structure outcome is the likely remedy.
Evidence policy: frozen qualification with provenance applies only to claims where a regression would be silent or dangerous. Cracks, popping and hysteresis behaviour are accepted through automated tests plus a demo capture. GPU work is qualified on the RTX 3060 Laptop first, then confirmed on the RTX 4070.
Vocabulary: CONTEXT.md. Residency architecture: docs/adr/0001-non-semantic-streamed-volume-residency.md.
Decisions so far
Detail levels
Ownership: the Voxel Frontend produces coarse summaries per volume. They are non-semantic like residency and cached per Voxel Volume Content Version (ADR 0002). Render Paths derive their own data from them, so far volumes never need full materialization.
Level chain: one level per volume at a time: 64³, 32³, 16³, 8³. Every level covers the same scene-space volume extent with proportionally larger voxels, so level boundaries fall only on volume faces.
Downsampling: each coarse voxel votes directly over all full-detail values it covers, independent of how the chain is built. Any occupied value makes it solid. Empty values do not count as materials, and a tie goes to the lowest Voxel Material identity.
Residency: the 8³ level stays resident for all 256 volumes. The 32³ and 16³ levels are streamed through a selection.
Startup: a sweep runs before residency begins and holds one materialized volume at a time.
Edits to non-resident volumes: ADR 0001 permits them without materialization. The volume's summaries are rebuilt through the shared query-only copy slot, without changing its full-detail residency. Newest-only applies per volume: repeated changes coalesce, pending work is retained for every dirty volume, and one rebuild runs at a time. An occupied slot delays admission but never discards the obligation, and a superseded rebuild cannot acknowledge newer content.
Selection and hysteresis
Bands: nominal bands depend only on camera position. Full detail is the existing caller-chosen neighbourhood (Derive streamed residency limits from a caller-chosen neighbourhood size #134); rings beyond it get 32³, 16³ and then 8³.
Hysteresis: the required selection applies ±16-unit hysteresis (a 32-unit deadband) to every band, including the edge of full detail, using the previous selection. The frozen hysteresis = false boundary-churn check is replaced.
Resets: nominal bands apply on initialization and after a teleport, defined as a scene-space camera displacement greater than 64 units between accepted camera states.
Copy caps: superseded by Profile whole-world detail levels and derive selection maxima, byte caps and laptop frame time: centre hysteresis, N = 49 (99 copies) demo / N = 9 (19 copies) qualification.
Byte caps: caps for materialization, summaries and Render Path allocations are re-derived and ratified through profiling.
Terms: Voxel Volume Detail Level (one level of a volume, a rendering approximation of the same extent) and Voxel Detail Selection (the level assigned to each volume), with required and installed variants like residency.
Rendering and correctness
Render Paths: both get detail levels. Raster uses skirts as a proposed crack solution that must qualify: crack coverage across mixed levels, volume corners and independently meshed Raster Regions, including overhangs, cavities and material boundaries, with a defined limit on skirt intrusion. If skirts fail, shaping reopens with stitched transitions as the fallback. Compute uses a coarse Brickmap for each volume's selected level.
Coarse correctness: coarse levels follow the newest content version, including after eviction from full detail and after non-resident edits. A stale coarse level is a correctness failure.
Convergence: atomic across every affected selected level. Older complete, revision-correct frames may stay visible while preparation runs.
Handoff: a Render Path switch completes only when the replacement path has installed the same detail selection at the required revision.
Oracle: Semantic Rays keep their meaning against the logical Voxel Scene View. A separate selected-detail reference computes expected coarse values independently from source content, so a broken summary generator cannot agree with itself. It has a defined allowance for raster skirts.
Profile
Profile whole-world detail levels and derive selection maxima, byte caps and laptop frame time: selection frozen (centre Schmitt trigger, Chebyshev bands 3/6/12 demo and 1/2/4 qualification, proven maxima 49/120/156/87 and 9/16/56/231). Route, windows, scopes and fog/far rule frozen. Per-level per-volume costs measured. Raster whole-world passes (p99 2.68 ms). Brickmap fails (p99 81.5 ms): its per-ray scan over every volume is the cause. Shaping reopened.
Choose a front-to-back scene traversal for whole-world Brickmap: a front-to-back grid DDA over the volume grid with early exit, plus a grid-to-volume table and the linear scan as fallback. In scope. Whole-world steady p99 8.54 ms (was 24.2). The gate still fails on an unattributed 8-frame GPU burst.
Attribute the 8-frame GPU burst after Render Backend initialization: a harness artifact. The burst is a one-time cost after each backend initialization, Brickmap only, and the harness re-initialized per segment. With one long-lived backend switching scenes in place, whole-world DDA p99 is 6.67 ms. Re-profile laps use one long-lived backend.
Re-profile whole-world Brickmap and finish detail-level byte caps: PASS. Grid-to-volume table in the scene words (1,048 B; 1,728/1,728 Semantic Rays agree). With one long-lived backend and Best performance confirmed, worst Brickmap p99 is 7.48 ms over the whole route, 7.54 ms in crossing windows and 8.70 ms in switch windows, with 0 stalls. Per-level configured costs and caps are ratified (Reopen streamed qualification GPU feasibility after Brickmap route freezes the host #118 structure, ×6 assemblies). The demo Raster CPU cap exceeds the fully resident baseline; tightening it is left to spec. For spec: incremental coarse preparation and installation, because whole-scene Raster installs stall the frame thread and 5 of 188 crossings exceed 2.5 s.
Frozen gates
Byte caps: derived from fixed counts times the worst measured per-volume cost, then ratified, as in Profile the streamed multi-volume qualification fixture and derive residency byte caps #117/Reopen streamed qualification GPU feasibility after Brickmap route freezes the host #118. Summaries and Render Path allocations have their own bounds.
Coarse revision correctness: including edits to non-resident volumes.
Zero coverage stalls with the whole world visible.
Frame time: p99 of each frame's max(cpu_frame_ms, gpu_frame_ms) ≤ 10 ms in the demo configuration (7x7 full detail, fog and shading on). Each Render Path qualifies separately, over the whole route and separately over frozen crossing and switch windows. Warmup, route, GPU timestamp scope and CPU scope (frame-thread preparation, submission and waits) are frozen. Maximum frame time is recorded but not gated. Laptop on mains power, Windows "Best performance", discrete GPU forced, V-Sync off; power and thermal context recorded.
Fog: the farthest visible scene content retains at least half of the linear-space difference between its unfogged shaded colour and the fog colour. The far plane and fog distances are sized from the accepted camera position and the finite scene bounds.
Detail latency ≤ 2.5 s: from selection demand until the Presenting Render Path atomically installs the required levels at the required revision. The earliest unresolved demand time is preserved through supersession. Handoffs are measured through the fence-safe boundary using the existing crossing/switch timing rule. Coarse fallback coverage satisfies coverage only and completes neither deadline.
Tests and capture: cracks, popping, hysteresis, thickening of thin surfaces, closed cavities, material boundaries, and a fog-off whole-world view.
Fog
Deferred: crossfade or dithered transitions, geomorphing, screen-space-error selection, a larger world, dynamic volume membership, durable persistence, new traversal structures (64-tree, multi-level Brickmap), DAG, editable SVO, palette compression, streamed Dense storage/compute, and a startup-time gate.

Built
6e2f440, the froxel volume (shaders/fog.slang, crates/forge-render/src/fog.rs): 160 × 90 × 64 to 64 m. Each froxel is sampled at its middle, and a lamp's distance is clamped to half a froxel. Jittered samples shimmered 1.50 → 2.81 % and lit white froxels by lamps; at the middle the shimmer is 0.23 %.
Authored boxes (FogBox): density, height falloff, colour, absorption, priority. They blend by an edge fade in metres per axis, in priority order. A box of no fog cuts a hole.
a1f07ac, the lamps: each lamp's share of the fog (Lamp::fog), read through the frame's lamp columns. The scene also gives lamps that light the fog alone (at most 256).
a9060c8, the scene's fog everywhere (GlobalFog: density, height, falloff, absorption), how much of the sun's and the lamps' light it scatters (light_scales), and its phase from the scene.
The private district's data: 223 fog boxes and the master environment's fog curves over the hour. --no-fog turns it off.
Measured: fog/light 1.21 ms on the district's benchmark path (2560 × 1440, DLSS Quality) with the fog everywhere: every froxel loops its lamps.
Open
Cost: a coarser volume where only the fog everywhere stands, or skip the lamps where extinction is negligible.
Too faint with data-faithful values: the district's boxes read as extinction per metre need about 5× to match the source's frame. A factor is still missing (the lamps' default fog share? emissive surfaces' light into the fog?).
Emissive surfaces (neon) into the fog, and the probes' ambient light in the fog.
The volume ends at 64 m.
Shadows in the fog: only after the patent queries are re-run (D-099's patent record).

The probes' light. Each froxel now scatters the probes' light, every way alike. That light is the sky's and the scene's bounce, glowing surfaces' included. The irradiance is looked up facing up and down, in the froxel's own region (a room's grid inside a room). The mean radiance is taken as (E_up + E_down) / 2π.
Its shares. FogParams::ambient holds two shares: one for the fog everywhere, one for the boxes, which multiplies each box's FogBox::ambient. The lamps' shadows in the fog: D-099's option, built and off by default. 5ee2556, CI green.
Patent search first (a research agent; web only, not legal advice). No live patent found reads on either of the two forms considered. The nearest are:
NVIDIA's light importance caching (US 11,756,254 / US 12,374,024);
the ReSTIR family;
spatiotemporal blue noise;
the shadow denoisers;
Disney's distribution caching (US 9,619,922): its claims couldn't be read.
The rules the fog keeps to stay clear of them are listed in D-099. Two items to read before the single-ray form ever ships: Disney's claim 1 and the granted claims of US 12,374,024.
What was built: the threshold form. At each froxel, the K strongest lamps (at most 4) that each give at least a tenth of the froxel's lamp light get one shadow ray each. It is deterministic, with no random draw. It is switched on with --fog-lamp-shadows N.

The fog's history (D-099's amendment): af40196, CI green.
The owner, on the private district's bar: "Fog is not good, pixelized and not stable".
What changed
Each froxel is lit at a point jittered within it every frame: the R2 sequence, plus a pcg3d offset of each froxel's own.
The result is blended into last frame's volume where the froxel's middle stood then, keeping 0.9 of it. It is reprojected through last frame's view-projection and brought to this frame's exposure.
There is no history on:
the first frame, or after a frame without fog;
a cut: the anti-aliasing's reset, or the camera jumping more than 4 m or turning more than 30° in a frame;
a point outside last frame's volume;
a value that is not finite.
--no-fog-history is the A/B.
fog/light writes the history alone and fog/integrate reads it: no transient image and no read-write buffer for the pass.
Numbers
The look: the lamps' cones lose their 16-pixel blocks, which no longer crawl in motion.
A still camera, with fog against without (pixels changing by more than 2 codes, frames ten apart): the bar at 20 s 0.71 % against 0.97 %, at 15 s 0.25 % against 0.35 %. The fog adds no shimmer.
Cost on the path (2560 × 1440, DLSS Quality, interleaved runs): fog/light 0.43 → 0.46–0.47 ms. The frame stays within the runs' spread.
A measuring note: fog/light's zone reads short. It runs beside the particles' draw, with no barrier between them, and its zone counts only what comes after that draw: 0.47 ms, against 0.91 ms with no particle alive. Older zone figures for it in PROFILE.md are short the same way.
Tried and left out: fog/light on the async compute queue. There it shared the GPU with the lamps' ReSTIR, and the path got slower.
The owner's "the fog pops": in the street it was the steam (#271). At the bar's far end, it was the camera turning a lit stretch of haze into view.
Checks: the gate (1,085 tests); the village's map check (13 of 13 views identical); two read-only reviews.

The fog's cull (D-099's amendment): 53123e7, CI green.
No pixel reads the froxels that lie behind every surface in view, so they are no longer lit.
How it works
fog/tiles finds each froxel column's farthest surface from the depth; where a column shows sky, the distance is infinite.
fog/bound turns that into the last slice to light: two slices past the farthest surface of the column and the two columns round it each way.
fog/apply filters across one column and between two slices.
A history read reaches one slice further, and the jitter can shift it one column further.
The first review found that with only the eight neighbouring columns, a froxel beside a silhouette lost its history every other frame. The wider neighbourhood fixes that.
Skipped froxels are marked with an extinction of −10,000, which fog/integrate treats as empty. A history read that reaches a mark is refused, so a froxel the camera uncovers is lit afresh.
Tried and left out: carrying the skipped froxels' history forward. Where the bar's far end comes out from behind a pillar, its haze stayed dim for several frames: 4.8 % of the frame off by more than 2 codes, up to 65. With the marks it is 0.67 %, up to 29, and looks the same.

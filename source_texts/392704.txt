# octree-raytracer

A CPU ray tracer that renders scenes described in a small declarative scene
language to PNG. It implements Whitted-style recursive ray tracing: primary rays
through a perspective camera, Blinn–Phong-style direct shading from point and
directional lights, hard shadows via shadow rays, and recursive specular
reflection to a configurable bounce depth. Meshes are intersected through an
octree built over their triangles, and rays that escape the scene sample a
cube-map environment.

Skybox — Preetham Atmospheric Sky
Swap the custom aurora shader for three.js built-in Sky.js (Preetham atmosphere model).
Deliverables:
Import Sky.js from three/examples/jsm/objects/Sky.js
Set sun position, turbidity, rayleigh, mie coefficients for a cyberpunk night-time city vibe
Mount as a background/backdrop to the scene
Remove the old aurora shader if still present
Reference: Node already has three.js r185, Sky.js ships built-in at node_modules/three/examples/jsm/objects/Sky.js
Acceptance: Skybox visible on all camera angles, sun glow in correct position, no regression on existing scene objects.

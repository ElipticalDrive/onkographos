sdf.rs:
- extrude_y: lift 2D SDF into 3D along Y axis (circle→cylinder)
- revolve_y: surface of revolution around Y (circle→torus with offset)
- twist_y: helical domain warp before SDF evaluation
- displace: additive displacement operator for bump/wave effects
- repeat_1d: tile SDF infinitely along any axes via modulo fold

color.rs:
- blend_overlay, blend_hard_light, blend_soft_light: Photoshop-compatible blend modes
- adjust_brightness: additive RGB offset, clamped
- adjust_contrast: pivot-at-0.5 linear contrast scale
- adjust_saturation: HSV saturation factor

math.rs:
- Vec3::min_component / max_component
- Mat4::normal_matrix: inverse-transpose of upper-3x3 for correct normal transforms
- basis_from_normal: Duff et al. 2017 branchless TBN frame construction

Tests: 30 new unit tests (728 total)

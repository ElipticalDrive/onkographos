Voxel compaction kept private boundary flags after their scan had finished.
Reuse the already-owned index buffer for mark output, let the original scan
consume it, then recover boundaries from adjacent inclusive-prefix values while
overwriting it with final indices. Private flag requests fall from 4N to four
bytes, saving 4N-4 bytes per nonempty call. Per-pass bindings remain distinct;
owned outputs never enter scratch pools. Stage/layout/scalar-sync/transfer and
public metadata contracts remain; no new CPU work or API/dependency changes.
Validation: 383 native correctness tests, 80,211,910 phased parent/CPU/guard words,
24,036,610 complete parent/CPU values, exact fresh-pool savings, optional profiling,
warm/zero-budget/retained-output checks; Clippy, CPU-only, WASM, fmt and whitespace
pass. Measured sources and executable match final validation hashes.
Two balanced thirty-pair surveys cover twelve sparse/dense/tail cases. Write:
18 favorable / 6 inconclusive / 0 adverse; resident: 2 favorable / 22 inconclusive / 0 adverse; full:
1 favorable / 23 inconclusive / 0 adverse. All 72 rows, adverse/inconclusive results and shared-host limits
are in notes/2026-10-17_reused_gpu_voxel_boundary_storage.md. Timing gains remain
workload-dependent; no application-wide or transfer-inclusive guarantee is inferred.

*** Inserting Update ***
Problem found, this calculation is wrong and should be:
MaxVoxelX = (MapSizeX * 16)-1
MaxVoxelY = (MapSizeX * 16)-1
MaxVoxelZ = (MapSizeX * 24)-1
This is because coordinates that go into CalculateLine are "voxel positions" across the entire map, and are not tile based positions ;)
I believe a tile is 16x16x24, if this is incorrect than please fix and correct it ! :)

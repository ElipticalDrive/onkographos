💡 What
Hoisted global WebGL shader uniforms, program activation (gl.useProgram), and texture unit binding into a new prepareWorldProgram(p, sky, torch, water) function executed once per render pass in renderFrame(). Updated drawWorldMesh(m, p) to only bind mesh attributes and issue gl.drawArrays.
🎯 Why
Previously, drawWorldMesh was setting ~15 uniforms and binding textures redundantly for every chunk mesh drawn per frame. With dozens of loaded chunks, this generated thousands of unnecessary WebGL state calls and uniform queries per frame.
📊 Impact
Eliminates hundreds of redundant WebGL driver uniform calls per frame, reducing JS frame execution time and CPU overhead during 3D scene rendering.
🔬 Measurement
Verified via syntax checks (node -c) and automated Playwright browser self-tests (BlockRealmDebug.selfTest()).

What
Adds backend/services/colmap_io.py, a pure-numpy loader for COLMAP sparse models, plus tests/backend/test_colmap_io.py. Implements T1 of V1_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md per the 2026-06-11 release audit (docs/release-audit-v1.md).
Why
The splat trainer (T3) needs camera poses and sparse points from the model that _run_colmap() leaves in colmap_dir/sparse/0/, without COLMAP Python bindings, torch, or gsplat — CI has none of them. The module depends on the stdlib and numpy only and imports nothing else from backend.
How
Frozen dataclasses ColmapCamera, ColmapImage, ColmapModel.
read_model(sparse_dir: Path) prefers the BIN model (authoritative — model_converter writes TXT alongside it) and falls back to TXT; raises RuntimeError(f"COLMAP sparse model not found in {sparse_dir}") when neither exists.
BIN parsing follows COLMAP's documented binary layout via struct.unpack_from / np.frombuffer: cameras.bin (<Q count, <iiQQ + <d×num_params), images.bin (<idddddddi header, null-terminated name, <Q num_points2D, skip 24×num_points2D bytes), points3D.bin (<Q count, <QdddBBBdQ, skip 8×track_len bytes).
Supports PINHOLE (id 1) and SIMPLE_PINHOLE (id 0); any other camera model raises RuntimeError naming the model.
TXT parsing mirrors the line conventions of _read_colmap_points3d / _store_reprojection_errors in reconstruction.py (skip # comments, two lines per image).
qvec_to_rotmat (wxyz, world->cam) and world_to_cam_matrix (4x4 viewmat).
Tests
All fixtures are built in-test with struct.pack — no binary files committed.
tests/backend/test_colmap_io.py: 8 passed (BIN readers for cameras/images/points3D incl. exact skip-length checks, BIN-over-TXT preference, TXT/BIN equivalence, missing-model error, unsupported-model error for BIN and TXT, quaternion math + viewmat).
Full suite: 199 passed.
ruff check .: clean.
Acceptance: python -c "import backend.services.colmap_io" succeeds and pulls in no torch/gsplat/fastapi/sqlalchemy.

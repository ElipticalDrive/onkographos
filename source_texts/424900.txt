DualContouringOverlap.joint / .joint_contacts, EvaluationOptions.mesh_extraction_overlap
and resolve_dual_contouring_overlap; early dispatch before legacy triangulation.
pretty stays the default.
joint: leaf-native adaptive extraction with shared erosion/onlap contact vertices,
validated before any triangle is emitted (fault-free models).
joint_contacts: joint's contacts plus pretty's fault rule; horizons borrow fault
vertices, with a deterministic fallback region and multi-fault leaf fallback.
Fixed-weight queries: outputs keep an independent copy of their solved weights;
fixed_weight_snapshots checks the live cache fingerprint/payload. The production
drift sampler (query, query_batch) runs on NumPy, Torch CPU/CUDA and KeOps
(stacked block-sparse batches).
Pure steps live in modules/dual_contouring/joint_* (cell complex, branches,
contact relations, lattice, ownership, edge decisions, field queries, triangle
plan); none imports another module. API files orchestrate.
Tests (joint_cell_complex, joint_topology, joint_integration, joint_contacts,
fault_drift_sampler, joint_modules), docs/implementation/joint_extraction.md and
tests/benchmark/model7_backend_benchmark.py.

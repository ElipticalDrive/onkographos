Closes #106
What
The greedy merge phase re-evaluated the semantic identity of a face for every width/height expansion probe — two voxel/shell samples, the transparency rules and biome-color lookups, per probe. Chunk::buildMeshRanged now runs a classification pass that materializes each slice cell's face inputs once into compact per-cell keys, and the greedy expansion evaluates the merge rules on the key fields: no voxel/shell sampling and no biome array lookups left in the hot loops.
Key layout (thread-local planes, reserved once per worker — no per-slice/chunk heap churn; acceptance criterion "no new heap churn"):
```
lo: bits 0..7   type at cell      (the -q side voxel, may be a border)
    bits 8..15  type at cell+q    (the +q side voxel, may be a border)
    bits 16..47 owner color of the cell-side type (biome color of its
                column / constant water tint / 0)
    bit  48     a visible face exists (classified once in phase 1)
    bit  49     face owned by the cell+q voxel (-q)
hi: bits 0..31  owner color of the cell+q type
```
Plus an inert fast path (identical types, or two different non-air opaque types — no face possible from either side) that skips color/classification work for the majority of cells, and a precomputed 256-entry owner-color-kind table replacing the per-cell blockIsFoliage switch. Occupancy metadata (#105) already keeps empty Y slabs out of classification entirely; AO/light stay out of the key and are sampled at final merged quad corners exactly as before.
Why the key carries the voxel PAIR, not the classified face
A pure "same face" equality key — the issue's sketch — changed merge semantics (vertex counts shifted in both directions across the corpus): the old rules evaluate the candidate's voxel pair, not its classification:
a +q face fronts AIR in one cell and a transparent block in the next (stone top over air merges with stone top over water);
a -q face may merge with a candidate whose own first-match classification is the opposite face of a different transparent block (a water underside merging across a cell fronting kelp/leaves/ice).
My first equality-key prototype was +13% slower and non-equivalent; the hash corpus caught it immediately, and the pair-carrying key restored byte-identity and the win. This iteration is documented in docs/benchmarks/issue106/benchmark.txt.
Byte-identity proof
New tests/test_mesh_facekey (--hash-corpus 25 97): 3 seeds × 25 strided chunks with generated 4-neighborhood borders, full greedy and LOD payload hashes + counts, plus one 600-edit noisy chunk per seed → 78/78 lines byte-identical before/after (covers plains/desert, forest foliage, mountains/caves, water/coast geometry across seeds; deterministic on a given build). The same test pins each boundary class with hand-built scenes: block-type splits, transparent pairs (glass|glass, glass|stone, glass|air), water (box, water-over-stone), biome tint boundaries (grass + foliage colors), chunk borders (solid neighbor occludes, transparent doesn't, border voxels never own faces), AO corner sampling, span-edge faces, key-path vs forced-full-range equivalence per scene, and 4-worker concurrent build determinism.

Issue #106: the greedy merge phase re-evaluated the semantic identity of a
face for every width/height expansion probe - two voxel/shell samples, the
transparency rules and biome-color lookups per probe - instead of deciding
once per slice cell.

buildMeshRanged now runs a classification pass that materializes each
cell's face inputs into a compact per-cell key pair (thread-local workspace
reserved once per worker, no per-slice/chunk heap churn):
  lo: type at cell, type at cell+q, owner color of the cell side,
      has-face + side flags (classification done once)
  hi: owner color of the cell+q side
The greedy seed walk reads the precomputed classification and the
width/height probes evaluate the exact merge rules on the key fields, with
no voxel/shell sampling and no biome array lookups left in the hot loops.
An inert fast path (identical types, or two different non-air opaque types)
skips color/classification work for the majority of cells, and owner-color
kinds come from a precomputed 256-entry table.

The key must carry the raw voxel PAIR rather than the cell's classified
face: the old rules merge a +q face fronting air with one fronting a
transparent block, and accept a -q candidate whose own first-match
classification is the opposite face of a different transparent block. A
first equality-key design shifted vertex counts in both directions and was
+13% slower; the hash corpus caught it and the pair key restored
byte-identity. AO/light stay out of the key (sampled at final quad corners).

Byte-identity is proven by tests/test_mesh_facekey --hash-corpus (3 seeds x
25 strided chunks with generated neighbor borders + LOD + edited chunks:
all payload hashes and counts equal before/after). The new test also pins
the merge/non-merge contract with deterministic scenes (block types,
transparent pairs, water, biome tint, chunk borders, AO corners), the
metadata-bounds vs forced-full-range equivalence, and multi-worker
determinism.

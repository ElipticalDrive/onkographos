Issue body actions
Add a MeshInstance3D with a QuadMesh and a spatial shader that covers the whole view from the near plane (POSITION = vec4(VERTEX.xy, 1.0, 1.0) for Godot 4.3+ reversed-Z), reconstructs the world-space ray per pixel from INV_PROJECTION_MATRIX and INV_VIEW_MATRIX, marches a placeholder SDF (a sphere and a ground slab), shades it flat, and writes DEPTH so ordinary meshes intersect correctly.
Details:
render_mode unshaded, cull_disabled, depth_draw_always; large extra_cull_margin so the quad is never frustum-culled.
Marching loop parameters as uniforms: max steps, max distance, step scale, hit epsilon.
Put shared helpers (sdBox, sdBox2, ray setup) in shaders/include/*.gdshaderinc.
Acceptance: a test MeshInstance3D cube placed inside the SDF sphere is correctly occluded and intersected; resizing the window keeps the image correct; the camera from the previous issue flies through it.
Branch: <this issue number>-<slug> in its own worktree, PR against develop, merge commit after rebase.

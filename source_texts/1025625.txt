Stacked on #14.
Shape: cloud_noise is now RG — perlin fbm + worley fbm. cloud_shape() pushes the perlin outline out into worley lobes; detail octave is worley too. Edge softness 0.22 → 0.1 (night/fullmoon presets keep 0.22: moonlit cloud stays thin). Warp 0.6 → 0.35, less smear.
Fields: a broad, low-frequency density swing drifting against the wind, so clouds cluster with clear sky between and grow/fade instead of only sliding.
Storm: billows and fields fade out with cloud_cover (cloud_fair()), so a full storm stays a flat, closed deck.
Lighting: Beer's law over two sunward taps + own thickness (grey bases), powder darkening on rims with the sun behind, Henyey-Greenstein silver lining replacing the old pow-24 rim.
Ground/model shadows follow the new shape via cloud_density.
Cost: sky +2 texture taps (6 → 8), shadows +1 (4 → 5); noise bake ~90 ms once vs ~33 ms.
tools/cloud_shot.tscn: renders the sky from a map spawn (MAPS=, STORM=, OUT=) for tuning.
Checked by render on golem, 2fort, ravine (night), golem at storm 0.55 and 1.0. Test suite: 1 unrelated failure (test_table_matches_declared_arity doesn't know --dynamic-weather).

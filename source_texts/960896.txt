Normalize initial pitch before clamping
The initial pitch is taken directly from transform.eulerAngles.x, which is in 0–360°. If the camera starts at a negative pitch (e.g., -10°), Unity reports that as ~350°, so the next Mathf.Clamp immediately snaps it to maxPitch (60°). This causes an abrupt jump on the first frame whenever the initial X rotation is in the 180–360° range. Consider normalizing the angle to a signed range (e.g., via Mathf.DeltaAngle(0f, angles.x)) before clamping so the initial orientation is preserved.

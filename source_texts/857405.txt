Description
This PR introduces a fully procedural, physically based sky shader using single-scattering raymarching. Instead of using static skyboxes or basic color gradients, the sky is now mathematically rendered in real-time based on the sun's position, simulating how light interacts with a planetary atmosphere.
Key Features & Additions
1. Ray-Sphere Intersection & Occlusion
Implemented mathematical intersection logic to detect where view rays enter and exit the atmosphere.
Added ground sphere intersection to naturally occlude rays that hit the planet surface, separating the sky from the ground.
Added solar occlusion checks to block sunlight from illuminating the atmosphere when the sun is behind the planet (naturally creating nighttime conditions).
2. Atmospheric Raymarching (Transmittance & Optical Depth)
Implemented a nested loop architecture for raymarching.
Primary View Loop: Steps through the atmosphere along the camera's view ray to accumulate light.
Secondary Light Loop: At each view step, casts a ray toward the sun to calculate the optical depth of the air blocking the light.
Applied the Beer-Lambert Law to dynamically calculate light transmittance, which naturally absorbs blue light over long distances to create realistic red/orange sunsets.
3. Dual Scattering Models
Rayleigh Scattering: Simulates gas molecules to render the deep blue sky.
Mie Scattering: Simulates aerosols, dust, and water vapor using the Henyey-Greenstein phase function. This adds realistic white horizon haze and a concentrated, blinding halo effect around the sun.
4. HDR Color Pipeline
Tone Mapping: Added exposure-based tone mapping (1.0 - exp(-color * exposure)) to cleanly compress the high-dynamic-range physical light values into a renderable SDR range without clipping.
Gamma Correction: Applied standard 1.0 / 2.2 gamma correction for accurate monitor display.
Safety clamping to ensure final output remains strictly within the [0.0, 1.0] bounds.
C++ API / Uniform Updates
The fragment shader requires the following new uniforms to be uploaded from the render loop:
cameraPos (vec3) - The world-space position of the camera.
sunDirection (vec3) - The normalized direction pointing toward the sun.
sunIntensity (float) - Multiplier for the sun's brightness.
viewSamples / lightSamples (int) - The resolution of the raymarching loops (e.g., 16 and 8).
Atmosphere Config: groundRadius, atmosphereRadius.
Rayleigh Config: betaR (vec3), Hr (float scale height).
Mie Config: betaM (float), Hm (float scale height), mieG (float phase direction).
Camera Config: exposure (float).
Visual Verification
When testing this PR, you should observe:
A bright blue sky at noon.
A glowing sun halo that smoothly fades into the blue sky.
A whitish, hazy band near the horizon.
Natural red/orange gradients as the sun direction nears the horizon.
A stark dark mask for rays that intersect the ground or when the sun is entirely blocked by the planet.

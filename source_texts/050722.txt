Summary
Append new VoxelType IDs for wood families (logs, leaves, and plank types), vegetation, terrain, Nether blocks, ores, and utilities.
Render grass, ferns, bushes, flowers, and vines as two intersecting, double-sided alpha-cutout planes instead of solid cubes.
Keep vegetation in the existing cutout/depth rendering path and make it non-occluding for light propagation.
Preserve existing voxel IDs 0–14.
Rendering notes
The cross planes reuse the existing foliage atlas tile and cutout material, avoiding changes to atlas dimensions or the water/glass/shader pipelines.
Plant height and vertex tint vary by vegetation type.
This adds the mesh silhouette only; distinct per-plant resource-pack textures, multi-block flower models, and non-solid interaction/collision behavior remain separate follow-up work.
Scope
Changes are limited to src/types/physics.ts and src/rendering/VoxelWorld.ts. The existing opaque cube mesher, water simulation, glass rendering, and shader pipeline are otherwise left intact.

Do not let crossed plants hide adjacent block faces.
When a CROSS_PLANT_TYPES voxel reaches meshing, both cube and water face checks treat it as a full occluder. The crossed planes do not cover the removed face. This can create a localized visual hole. Ordinary terrain generation and block inventory do not currently generate or place these plants broadly, so this is a minor rendering artifact rather than a major gameplay issue.

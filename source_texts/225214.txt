[Milestone C] Axis 3: High-Fidelity Geometry Extraction & Priors
C1. Explicit TSDF → Implicit SDF Extraction
Problem: Marching cubes over TSDF leaves holes in unobserved voxels (weight=0), creates floating artifacts from depth outliers, and fails to guarantee watertight meshes.
Action Plan: Extract level-sets directly from a continuous global Gaussian field.
Option A: Gaussian Opacity Fields (GOF) (extract tetrahedral grid from ray-accumulated opacity fields).
Option B: 2DGS → NeuS/SDF Distillation (fit an implicit SDF MLP to Gaussian geometry; the zero-set guarantees watertightness while the MLP naturally interpolates unobserved holes).
C2. Monocular Geometric Prior Injection
Problem: Textureless surfaces (walls, tabletops) lack photometric gradients, resulting in bumpy/wavy geometry.
Action Plan: Inject normal predictions from monocular estimators (e.g., StableNormal, Omnidata) via a normal-prior loss to stabilize flat, textureless surfaces.

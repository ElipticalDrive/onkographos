Abstract
An algorithm for the reconstruction of three-dimensional images in X‑ray computed tomography with a cone irradiation beam was developed. The algorithm is based on the precise analytical representation of a 3D Radon transform of the projection data. Modeling of the algorithm showed that high-quality tomographic images are obtained at large source irradiation cone angles.

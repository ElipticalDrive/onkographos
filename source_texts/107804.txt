Enhanced shaders/effects/cloud_temporal_reprojection.comp to calculate a luminance-guided rejection factor (lumaRejection) based on relative luminance difference. This rejection factor is combined with spatial density rejection into totalRejection, resetting historyLength and boosting accumulation weight alpha when rapid lighting shifts or flashes occur, allowing volumetric clouds to adapt instantly to flashes of light or sun shifts without ghosting or temporal lag.

3D building models have a complex structure and a large number of triangular meshes, which can put great pressure on computer real-time rendering. Therefore, the model needs to be simplified to reduce the number of meshes without affecting the overall visual effect of the model. The algorithm in this paper is based on triangle folding, which simplifies more but loses model details. In order to make up for the shortcoming of triangle folding, more constraints need to be introduced for error control. Meanwhile, this paper solves the problem of higher complexity of the algorithm due to the introduction of more constraints by calculating the Mahalanobis distance of each constraint factor introduced. The vertices with obvious sharp features are constrained to keep the structure of the model undeformed. Experiments prove that the algorithm in this paper can better preserve the detailed features and the visual effect of the model.

Introduction
In these research hotspots such as virtual reality, digital twin, and metaverse, 3D building models are the basic components in these virtual 3D scenes, but the complex structure and large amount of data in 3D building models lead to high pressure on the rendering efficiency of large-scale 3D scenes.1,2 Model simplification algorithms can reduce the number of meshes contained in the model at a very small visual cost, thus reducing the rendering pressure.3 Among them, the edge folding algorithm is fast and efficient in simplification, while it can keep the topology of the original model well, and has become the most widely used simplification algorithm. The triangle folding algorithm based on the edge folding algorithm can reduce two vertices,4 six edges, and four triangles in one folding operation, which is more efficient in simplification,5 but the triangle folding has the defect of easy loss of local detail features.
Hamann was the first to propose a mesh simplification algorithm based on triangle folding,6 using the curvature of the surface and the degrees of the three angles of the triangles to do certain operations to get the folding error of each triangle, and then folding according to the magnitude of the error to get the simplified model, and the simplified result has poor retention of the detailed features of the model. On this basis, Hasselgren et al. introduced the Laplace coordinates of the folding point and the information of the normal vector of the original triangle to update the position of the folding point, and the folding cost is determined by the spreading degree of the folded triangle together with the volume error of the folded triangle in the region,7 which can retain the detailed features of the model well, but the simplified result has a large error with the original data. In order to improve the error accuracy, Ovreiu et al. combined the point-plane distance and Hausdorff distance and introduced a more accurate quadratic error metric,8 while vertex optimization was performed on the simplified model, and the quality of the obtained model was higher, but too many local detail features would be lost. In the simplification process, Xie et al. improved the mesh quality by constraining the generation of narrow triangles in the simplified mesh through the triangle interior angle judgment method.9 Liu et al. combined visual perception and geometric error to define a triangle folding error metric function to control the simplification depth of the model.10 The mesh model simplification with visual feature preservation was achieved by optimizing the new vertex position with error update. The algorithm can maintain the visual and geometric feature information of the model better, but the complexity of the algorithm is high because both visual and geometric features are considered.
Various current triangle folding algorithms generally consider combinations of two to three constraint factors in terms of retaining detail features, and although they all retain model detail features to some extent, they do not address the fundamental problem of balancing detail features and algorithm complexity. In order to solve the problem of introducing more constraints to better retain the model detail features leading to higher algorithm complexity, this paper improves the triangle folding algorithm based on the Mahalanobis distance11; after calculating the Mahalanobis distance of each constraint factor, only one Mahalanobis distance needs to be introduced as the constraint factor, which greatly reduces the algorithm complexity, and the triangle folding algorithm based on the Mahalanobis distance has a better simplification effect; the algorithm finally also optimizes the vertex position after folding, which better preserves the detailed features of the model.
In addition, the general simplification algorithms applied directly to 3D building models may lead to distortions with high simplification rates because the shapes of buildings are strictly constrained.12,13 Therefore, targeted simplification algorithms are needed to accommodate the special characteristics of 3D buildings. Xiang Hanyu et al. filtered the mesh model to produce a planar structure while retaining clear features while detecting the shape of planar regions, simplifying both planar and non-planar regions constrained by the overall shape and local geometric features, and avoiding the overall structure of the model from being destroyed.14 In addition, Wang et al. divided the vertices of the building model into boundary vertices, hole vertices, and other vertices and simplified the model constrained by the topological dependence of each vertex. The aforementioned methods can preserve the overall structural features of 3D building models well, but the preservation of local detail features needs to be further improved.15
In this paper, triangle vertex types are considered, and based on the topology and association of triangle vertices, the vertex types are classified as ordinary vertices, boundary vertices, and other vertices,16 and based on the vertex types, the triangles are classified as ordinary triangles, boundary triangles, and other triangles, and folded according to the different types. In addition, a rectangular enclosing box excluding the top and bottom is built based on the farthest distance values around the model, and the distance from each point to the farthest value in four directions is calculated, and the distance from the point to the farthest value in the region with distinct features in four directions is used as a constraint. Classifying triangles can keep the overall topology of the model well, and using the enclosing box can keep the areas with distinct visual features on the surface of the model well.
The rest of this paper is organized as follows. In method details, I describe in detail the simplification method of the 3D building model. The experimental results and analysis are presented in results and analysis. Finally, we will present the results in discussion and briefly summarize this paper.

Discussion
A triangle folding algorithm based on Mahalanobis distance and vertex constraint is proposed in this paper, which can well preserve the overall shape and detail features of the 3D building model. First, the type of triangle is determined by judging the type of points, and the constraint is applied according to the type of triangle in the folding process, which well preserves the overall structure of the model. Then, the vertex coordinate values calculated by the QEM algorithm are optimized, and the coordinates are weighted by using the angle of the neighboring triangles of the folded triangle, and the new coordinates obtained can better maintain the topological structure. Finally, calculating the Mahalanobis distance of the introduced constraint factors significantly reduces the algorithmic complexity of the model and improves the simplification speed while preserving the detailed features of the model. The experimental results show that, compared with the original QEM algorithm, the algorithm in this paper is more efficient in simplification and can better preserve the detailed features of the model and effectively avoid the collapse of the model as a whole. However, there are shortcomings in this algorithm. First, the algorithm does not consider the texture and semantic information of the model, which needs to be studied in the future; second, the algorithm needs to continue to improve the overall structure and shape maintenance of the model under the high simplification rate. Next, the goal of the algorithm is to consider the influence of geometric information, texture, and semantic information on simplification, so as to obtain a more practical and useful 3D building model.

Method details
The experiment in this paper is roughly divided into four steps.(1)
judge the vertex type according to the topological relationship between the vertices, build a rectangular enclosing box according to the maximum distance between front and back and the maximum distance between left and right, set a suitable threshold value, the horizontal distance from each point to the enclosing box is less than this threshold value, the point type is judged to be other vertices, and finally judge the triangle type according to the vertex type.
(2)
Combine the angle of the neighboring triangles of the folded triangle to optimize the position of the new coordinates calculated by the QEM algorithm.
(3)
Calculate the values of each constraint factor, followed by their Mahalanobis distances, and then calculate the folding cost of each triangle based on the Mahalanobis distance and the new coordinate values as well as the constraint of triangle type.
(4)
Sort the folding cost from smallest to largest, fold the one with the smallest folding cost each time, and then update the information of the triangles affected by the folding operation. The flow chart of the simplified algorithm in this paper is shown in Figure 8.
Vertex type determination based on geometric features and topological relations
Building models have more planar regions, such as walls, windows, and roofs, etc., with more significant sharp features than general 3D models.19,20 If simplified using the original QEM algorithm can only preserve the local features of the model well, for these planar regions may be distorted and the overall shape may be destroyed.21 Therefore, the simplification results can be further improved by judging the type of vertices and constraining them at the time of simplification.
First check the coordinate origin of the original mesh model, based on the coordinate origin, calculate the maximum value of X positive and negative axes and the maximum value of Z positive and negative axes, then calculate the distance from each point to the maximum value in four directions, and take the distance from the point to the maximum value of the region with distinctive features in four directions as the constraint range, within which, if the adjacent triangles around vertex v appear perpendicular to each other three times or more, it is defined as other vertices, such as the vertex F in Figure 9.
For vertices in other regions the following definitions can be made20,22,23: if all adjacent vertices around a vertex v appear only 2 times in all triangles around vertex v, then v is a normal vertex, as vertex A in Figure 9; if at least one of the adjacent vertices around vertex v appears only once in all triangles around vertex v, then v is a boundary vertex, as vertex B in Figure 9 and C. If the adjacent vertices of vertex v appear three or more times in all triangles around vertex v, then v is the other vertex, such as vertices D and E in Figure 9.Figure 9
Type determination of vertices
With the above definition, the following improvements can be made in the process of edge folding: If all three vertices of the triangle are normal vertices, the new vertex can be selected during folding according to the method introduced in 2.3. If one of the three vertices of the triangle is a boundary vertex, the new vertex can only be selected as a boundary vertex. If both vertices of the folded triangle and the triangle with both of its vertices are boundary vertices or at least one vertex is another vertex, the triangle cannot be folded.

Mahalanobis distances for triangular narrowness, area, volume, Gaussian curvature, and vertex curvature
The Mahalanobis distance is a similarity measure of distance that corrects the problem of inconsistent and correlated scales for each dimension in the Euclidean distance.26 Borrowing from the idea of QEM algorithm using distance as an error measure for simplification effect, this paper uses the Mahalanobis distance as an error measure for the constraint factor, which improves the simplification effect while reducing the complexity of the algorithm.
In order to better retain the detailed features of the model, this paper also considers the Gaussian curvature and vertex curvature of the mesh based on the selected triangle slenderness, local area and volume, where the curvature refers to the approximate curvature, and since the surface of the model is a second-order integrable surface, the surface of the model can be regarded as approximately smooth. For Mesh model, the closer the triangle is to a positive triangle, the higher the mesh quality is. Therefore, the triangle narrowness can prevent too many narrow triangles in the model simplification process and improve the mesh quality. The smaller the area of a local region indicates that the characteristics of the region are more obvious, then the folding cost of the region should be greater and the simplification order is backward. The volume comparison before and after model simplification can be used to better control the simplification error, assigning a smaller folding cost to those with small volume changes and prioritizing folding to prevent simplification with excessive error. Both Gaussian curvature and vertex curvature can quantitatively represent the degree of concavity of the simplified region, so using these two constraints can better preserve the detailed features prominent.
When folding, the shape of the folded triangle should be as close to a positive triangle as possible. Because multiplication is a relatively complex arithmetic operation, it requires more computational resources and time to perform. Folding operation
Based on the calculation result of Equation 15, the triangle with the smallest folding cost is folded sequentially. First, two vertices of this triangle are deleted. The deletion rules are: if one of them is a boundary vertex, the vertex is kept and the other two vertices are deleted; if all of them are normal vertices, the three vertices are arranged in index order, the vertex with the smallest index is kept and the other two vertices are deleted. Then the coordinates and vertex type information of the new vertex are assigned to the retained vertex; then the triangle and the triangles connected to both vertices of the triangle are deleted, and their information is also deleted; then the other triangles affected by the folding operation are updated, including the vertex index of the triangle and the area and volume of each triangle; finally the folding cost of the affected triangles is calculated. Finally, the folding cost of the affected triangles is calculated, and the folding cost of all triangles is reordered, and the above steps are repeated until the end of simplification.

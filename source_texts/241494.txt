```
Add cone_face_sdf_from_frame (pure JAX, jittable) and cone_face_sdf
(convenience wrapper) closing the cone branch of Marschner-style
trim-aware SDF built in #72.  The cone primitive's signed distance
in the (radial, axial) plane is the perpendicular from the slant
line:

  d_s_raw = (r - ref_radius) * cos(semi_angle)
          - axial * sin(semi_angle)

Positive on the outward radial side of the infinite cone surface,
negative inside.  sign_flip is applied for REVERSED faces.

The foot UV is (u, v) with u the longitude around the polar axis
(wrapped to [0, 2*pi]) and v the slant distance axial / cos
(semi_angle).  Degenerate guards:

- on-axis (radial = 0): u undefined, double-where on arctan2;
- cos(semi_angle) == 0 (a degenerate cone OCCT does not produce):
  safe-divide fallback so the VJP stays finite.

16 unit tests across two fixtures.  A full cone built in-test via
BRepPrimAPI_MakeCone(3, 0, 9): off-seam queries match OCCT
BRepExtrema_DistShapeShape within 0.15 on four representative
positions.  A half cone built via BRepPrimAPI_MakeCone(3, 0, 9, pi)
exercises the phantom regime: query (0, -0.5, 3) projects to
foot_u = 3*pi/2 which is outside the trim [0, pi]; untrimmed
primitive says the query is inside the cone body, but the signed
blend flips it positive.

jax.grad is finite at the cone's apex, on the polar axis, and in
the phantom regime; the pure composition JITs end to end.

Co-Authored-By: Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>
```

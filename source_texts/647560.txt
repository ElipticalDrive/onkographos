Problem
Perceived loudness can vary significantly between media files, requiring frequent manual volume changes.
Proposed behavior
Add an optional volume-normalization setting.
Keep normalization disabled by default.
Apply a deterministic normalization strategy in the playback audio path.
Preserve the user's normal device volume and volume-boost settings.
Bypass processing cleanly for unsupported formats or audio paths.
Acceptance criteria
Users can enable and disable normalization from Settings or playback controls.
Disabled normalization is a true bypass and does not alter audio output.
Enabled normalization reduces large perceived loudness differences without restarting playback.
Normalization does not change the device volume value.
Unsupported media continues playing without an error.
Enabled, bypass, transition, and unsupported cases are covered by automated tests.

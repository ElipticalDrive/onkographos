Shaded 3D spring view (SDF ray-marching)
Replaces the plotters wireframe with a GPU-shaded 3D spring rendered by signed-distance-field
ray-marching, with the wireframe kept as an automatic fallback when no GPU adapter is present.
All five families render (compression, conical, extension, torsion, assembly); metallic
material look, rectangular-wire-ready cross-sections, scroll-wheel zoom, ground/flattened-end
rendering, and per-member material hues for future multi-material support.
Architecture: viz/sdf.rs is a pure-Rust SDF mirror (ADR 0008 — no iced/wgpu imports);
viz/shader3d.{rs,wgsl} is the humble iced shader::Program + WGSL ray-marcher, mirrored
function-for-function with shared {{PLACEHOLDER}} constants and gated by a naga parse+validate
test (the no-GPU shader gate). The helix SDF's conservativeness (never over-estimate → march
safety) is closed by proof in the sd_helix doc comment and pinned by property sweeps.

sd_helix reduces the transcendental nearest-point-on-helix problem to a
2D cross-section-plane profile distance at four candidate turn indices
(floor(k_est)-1 ..= +2 — the close-wound neighbor guard), with linear
conical taper and assembly axial offset.

Two deviations from the brief's literal text, each with evidence:

1. Clamping the RAW angle phi = theta + TAU*k to [0, TAU*turns] discards
   theta for out-of-range candidates, creating a phantom start-cap
   candidate at the wrong azimuth that under-runs the true surface
   distance (measured d = -0.167 at a genuine surface point: radius 10,
   pitch 6, turns 8, wire_r 1, phi ~ 0.2526, ring_ang 4.0). Fixed by
   clamping the TURN INDEX k to [0, floor((TAU*turns - theta)/TAU)]
   first, preserving azimuthal alignment; the raw-phi clamp remains only
   as the terminal boundary safety net.

2. The brief's close-wound test asserted only a negative-guard, which is
   provably blind to a shrunk candidate set (min over a subset can only
   increase, never go negative). Strengthened with a conservativeness
   upper bound at off-midpoint gap fractions, where a single-candidate
   reduction OVER-estimates (floor picks the far turn) — the actual
   sphere-tracing failure the guard exists to prevent.

Conservativeness argument documented on the function: the ring-plane
reduction never over-estimates — safe for sphere tracing.

R2 flagged that sd_helix's window anchor k* (the ring-plane objective's
vertex) might diverge from k**, the true vertex of the Jordan-refined
per-winding objective V(k) = min_u f_k(u). Solving f_k(u)'s 2x2 joint
stationarity system proves k* == k** exactly for every geometry: a(k,u)
and b(k,u) depend on (k,u) only through the combined phase TAU*k+u
(dR_turn = TAU*g, pitch = TAU*s), so the azimuth term's stationarity
condition forces u**=0 at the joint optimum, collapsing it onto the same
u=0 slice k* already comes from. No functional change was needed — the
existing closed form already computes k** — so this extracts it into a
named helper and rewrites the doc's "four neighbors still bracket"
hand-wave into the proven statement, backed by an independent numeric
proof test and a broadened 2000-point randomized conservativeness sweep
targeting the interior/high-radial/high-taper regime R2 flagged.

WGSL/WebGPU permits finite-math-only implementations where isNan() and
NaN comparisons may legally constant-fold to false, so the prior NaN
sentinel for taper_small_r's None could silently misdecode tapered vs.
untapered helices once Task 5's shader reads this buffer. Replace it

reliable in both Rust and WGSL.

documented Some(f64::NAN) edge case: it's unreachable through real

than collapsing to None, so a violation of that invariant stays
visibly poisoned instead of silently laundering into a valid

User-reported: the wire appears to kink ~90 deg up/down at the ground
ends. Root cause: the virtual-boundary-winding branch froze the azimuth
penalty at the sub-range endpoint nearest zero while letting the planar
minimizer travel the whole sub-range, so at every segment seam azimuth
the axial residual cancelled at near-zero azimuth cost and the SDF
reported negative values up to a full coil gap outside the wire - an
s*PI-tall vertical phantom band per seam (all three body segments share
one seam azimuth via phase continuity, compounding the effect).

Fix: boundary windings now keep the TIGHTER of two independent lower
bounds - the clamped Jordan-chord quadratic (sign-correct at seams; the
sub-range always sits inside [-PI, PI] where Jordan holds) and the

Surface reconstruction from 3D Gaussian representations remains challenging, as Gaussian parameters are mainly optimized for rendering and often lack geometric consistency, leading to floaters and surface artifacts. We propose Point-GS, a point-cloud-guided, geometry-aware framework for surface reconstruction from 3D Gaussians. Instead of performing mesh extraction as a post-processing step, our method integrates differentiable surface meshing into the Gaussian optimization pipeline, enabling joint optimization of volumetric Gaussians and an explicit surface mesh. We introduce a geometry-aware initialization strategy using point-cloud normals and projected depth priors to improve early-stage geometric alignment. A bidirectional volume-surface consistency constraint is further enforced through depth and normal agreement between Gaussian rendering and mesh rendering. In addition, a curvature-guided adaptive sampling strategy and a point-cloud depth consistency loss are employed to improve efficiency and reconstruction accuracy. Experiments on several benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method produces clean and accurate surface meshes with improved quality-efficiency trade-offs compared to existing approaches.

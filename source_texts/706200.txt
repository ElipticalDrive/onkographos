# Gaussian Interior Editor

**Turn a phone video of a room into a 3D scene where every piece of furniture can be
selected, moved, hidden, recoloured or replaced — in a browser, offline.**

Master's thesis project · International School, Vietnam National University, Hanoi
· Intelligent Systems and Multimedia · Supervisor: Dr HO Tuong Vinh

---

## The problem

3D Gaussian Splatting reconstructs a room beautifully, but the result is **one
undifferentiated block** of a million fuzzy blobs. You can orbit it and zoom into it. You
cannot move the sofa.

Everything useful for interior design — trying another couch, repainting a wall, emptying a
room to see it differently — requires being able to point at *one object*. Making that
possible is what this project is about.

---

## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Reconstruct** | phone video → COLMAP → 3D Gaussian Splatting + Gaussian Grouping |
| **Segment** | per-object files, with a repair pass that fixes the segmentation |
| **Select** | click an object in the browser, without any mesh to hit-test against |
| **Manipulate** | move, rotate, hide, empty a whole room |
| **Recognise** | CLIP labels the selected object, automatically or on demand |
| **Replace** | swap a piece of furniture, correctly placed, oriented and scaled |
| **Recolour** | change a colour without retraining, preserving lighting |
| **Tour** | the camera walks the apartment on its own, through the doors |
| **Talk to it** | a local language model turns a sentence into a verified action plan |

---

## Three contributions, measured

None of the underlying bricks are mine — 3DGS, Gaussian Grouping and CLIP are published
work. **The manipulation layer built on top of them is**, and three parts of it are measured
rather than asserted.

Part of: #116 · Size: M · Depends on: #239
Goal
Cells are picked by a pure Amanatides-Woo DDA over the grid and the drawn levels, however detailed the ground mesh becomes. Tokens, props, walls and lights still raycast, on a dedicated pick layer. The Pick contract and the cells it returns stay the same.
Why
Today a cell comes from raycasting the terrain boxes or from a table plane at y=0. Neither survives this milestone: the boxes are no longer drawn, and raycasting organic ground is expensive. In Node, brute-force raycasting one 205k-triangle ground mesh measured about 10.6 ms per pick, against 0.24 ms for today's 1,728-instance terrain, and picks run on every pointer move. A logical DDA is exact, cheap and testable in Node, and keeps decorative geometry from deciding what a click means.
Current state
src/lib/tabletop/renderer.ts pickAt:
tries tokenLayer.pick, then wallLayer.pick, lighting.pick and propLayer.pick (each intersectObject on its group)
compares terrainLayer.pick(raycaster) (raised boxes, top or side) with raycaster.ray.intersectPlane(tablePlane) and takes the nearer; worldToGrid gives null outside the grid
computes worldToCorner, worldToEdge and edgeDistance from the hit point
onPointerMove times the whole call as 'pick'. After #240 the boxes are an invisible proxy. THREE.Layers is not used.
Approach
world/pick.ts (new, pure, no three.js): pickCell(grid, heightAt, origin, dir, cutLevel = Infinity) returns { cell, point, face: 'top' | 'side' }, or { cell: null, point } with the ray's point on the y = 0 plane (or null if it never meets it). heightAt(cell) returns the drawn floorY from the continued levels (#239), CHASM_DEPTH for void (#243), and min(level, cutLevel) once #281 passes a cut.
Algorithm.
Clip the ray to the grid's box: x and z bounds, y from the chasm depth to the highest top.
Step through cells with tMaxX/tMaxZ and tDeltaX/tDeltaZ.
In each column, if the ray drops below the cell's top before leaving the column: a top hit where it crosses the top if it entered above, otherwise a side hit at the entry point. Side hits return the raised cell, as TerrainLayer.pick does for box sides.
Snapping and the border. Corner, edge and edgeDistance come from the point exactly as now. A ray that meets no cell returns no cell, as worldToGrid does today, but its y = 0 point still feeds worldToCorner and worldToEdge, so walls along the map's edge can still be drawn. tablePlane goes.
Pick layer. PICK_LAYER (1) is enabled (not set) on every token, prop, wall and light-fixture mesh, so they still render; the raycaster tests only that layer. Ground chunks, cliffs, the backdrop, dice and effects stay off it. #291 adds skipping of cut, lowered and dithered hits.
Rounded corners differ from the logical columns by at most about 0.1 cell near grid corners. Picks stay logical by design.
Cost: proportional to the cells crossed, under 0.05 ms per pick on 100x100. No GPU cost; identical on every backend and tier.
To do
Write world/pick.ts and world/pick.spec.ts (server project): straight-down, grazing, side, void, border, off-grid and cut rays.
Equivalence test in Node on GM views (no continuation): for thousands of seeded rays from each fixture's named poses, the DDA returns the same cell and face as an analytic reference of today's picker, written in the test without three.js (a ray against one axis-aligned box per raised cell, from y=0 to floorY, plus the y=0 plane). Void cells are checked by their own rule: a ray into a hole returns the void cell or the owner of the wall it meets.
On fogged-player views, check that picks match the drawn, continued surface.
Wire pickAt to the DDA, keeping the priority order: tokens, walls, lights, props, then cells.
Enable PICK_LAYER on tokens, props, walls and fixtures, and restrict the raycaster to it.
Client test on the monastery and Hollow fixtures: hovering the gallery at level 5, and the ground below it, picks the same cells as before; clicks beyond the grid give cell: null.
Record the 'pick' timing before and after in docs/PERFORMANCE.md, with the DDA timed on its own.
In the milestone's closing PR, after the gates pass, delete TerrainLayer, WorldGround's play-area plane, tablePlane and the ?off=terrain fallback.
Acceptance criteria
The equivalence test passes on every fixture: the same cell and face as before, including on raised ground.
Clicks beyond the grid return no cell, and corners still snap along the border.
The DDA takes under 0.05 ms per pick for the Hollow on the iGPU machine, and the whole 'pick' timing is no higher than before.
Decorative meshes never appear in raycast results (a test counts the objects tested).
Both adventures play: moving, targeting, interacting and building work on raised ground.
Notes
three's Raycaster has no first-hit mode (firstHitOnly is three-mesh-bvh's). three-mesh-bvh stays out until a single pickable mesh passes about 20k triangles; tokens and props are instanced, and per-instance bounding spheres keep them cheap.
The cut level is a parameter here; the cut plane itself is [M72] Add a storey-snapped cut plane with auto mode and lock #281's.
Secrecy: heightAt reads the continued levels, so a pick on an unexplored cell reveals nothing the picture doesn't.

Hi, assume we have the local transformation T, we can always generate two homogeneous planes given a pixel coordinate (x,y). The distance of gaussian center to each plane should be smaller than a cut-off value. Take x plane as an example: we first transform the x plane hx into the local plane through T; then, we can calculate the distance from the Gaussian center (which is origin here) to the hx plane. With this constraint, we can solve the two x planes that exactly intersect the gaussian plane with distance to its center (origin) to be the cut-off value..

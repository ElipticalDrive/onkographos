Parent
#188 (M3 spec), criterion 6. ADR 0020. Decision, items 4 and 17. Carries visual check 3, covering #203 and #204 (bundle rule).
What to build
Every point light in a pixel's Froxel list traces one hard shadow ray toward it, ending at the light, against both TLAS; a ray is skipped only where N·L ≤ 0 or the pixel lies beyond the light's range. No cap: a cap per pixel or per Froxel would make shadows pop (decision, item 4).
This PR carries visual check 3: its images show every affected scene before #203 and after this PR, and the owner judges #203, #204 and this PR at once. A rejection becomes a fix ticket before the exit.
Acceptance criteria
Test at capture: on shadows, shadowed pixels match the analytic shadow of the sphere under its point light outside a one-pixel band.
Goldens regenerated for scenes with point lights; images from before Sun shadow rays and the shadows Reference scene #203 to after this PR; owner's visual OK (Decide).
Shadow-ray time with point lights reported per scene (rays on minus off).
Verification seam
Capture; Goldens; bench per pass. Must not regress: GPU frame total beyond the rays' measured cost. Size: small to medium.

Hand-off from #203 (PRs #259 and #258), for point-light shadow rays:
The ray. shadowed(position, normal, direction, distance) in src/render/shaders/main.wgsl takes any direction and length: for a point light, the unit direction to the light and the distance to it. It composes the origin in the static TLAS's frame itself (camera.tlas_anchor), offsets it along the normal and traces with RAY_FLAG_FORCE_OPAQUE | RAY_FLAG_TERMINATE_ON_FIRST_HIT.
Its inputs. Start from in.centroid_position, not in.camera_relative_position: at an MSAA edge pixel the centre can lie off the triangle, past a concave crease inside the mesh (SciFiHelmet showed specks along its edges). Offset along geometric_normal.
When to trace. Only where the light adds something: N·L > 0 by the shading normal, and within the light's range (decision item 4). A coarse smooth-shaded mesh's terminator then steps along its triangles where they face away from the light; skipping those rays leaked light into cast shadows on SciFiHelmet, so Sun shadow rays and the shadows Reference scene #203 does not (fog line on Foundations and ladder: architecture decisions and milestone order #17).
The oracle. tests/shadows.rs classifies each pixel centre in f64 (sky, sunlit or unlit sphere, sunlit or shadowed ground) and judges pixels outside a one-pixel band and outside the 5.3° band of the sphere's terminator, rays on against rays off. The point light is at (1.5, 2.5, 1.0), 900,000 cd, range 20 m; its shadow on the ground is left of the sphere, the sun's right of it, overlapping in about 20 pixels.
Where it sits. On Froxel light lists in place of four point lights #201's lights: the first directional light (lights[0], branch-free) and each further one in its loop trace their ray in fs_main; the point lights' loop, through the Froxel lists, is where theirs go. The Goldens test compares Froxel lists with brute force with shadow rays off.

Texels now stop sampling once their noise is confidently within a target, and a render ends when every texel has stopped or the cap is reached. The noise estimate is the SNR's own: K independent scrambles whose spread gives each texel's standard error. On by default in profile.json pathTracer.adaptive: threshold 0.02 (relative luminance error, 1/SNR; 0 turns it off), confidence 0.95, minSamples 8.
Test: at the boundary SD(mean) = threshold x mean, (K-1) s^2 / (threshold mean)^2 is chi-squared on K-1 dof (Cochran 1934); its CDF is the p-value against "not yet converged".
Multiplicity: a per-texel 95% bound selects lucky draws when most texels are unconverged (stopped texels' squared error was 5-120x the tolerance on cornell at 16 passes). The Benjamini-Hochberg 1995 step-up runs over all active texels at each checkpoint at a false discovery rate of 1 - confidence. Only statistics at or below the alpha quantile can be rejected, so the CDF is evaluated only for those.
Zero spread is no evidence: replicates that agree exactly (every sample missed a rare path) never stop; stopping them biased stump by -1.1%.
Checkpoints at pass n = K 2^j >= minSamples, where every replicate holds a power-of-two prefix of its own Owen-scrambled Sobol sequence, so a stopped texel keeps full stratification and complete rounds.
Driver idles and headless ends once the list is empty; PassRecord and the HUD report the share still sampling; -bench completes a stage at the cap or an empty list; bench configs record the three keys.
validCounts is exported and re-run after the viewer's -size / -max-samples (an override could previously set a cap that split a replicate round). threshold > 0 needs replicates >= 2.
snrAov reads the shared replicateEstimate; its output is bit-identical.
render_beauty reports the passes actually run.
Roadmap: the Adaptive sampling row is replaced by Foveated sampling: a per-pixel tolerance that grows with distance from a gaze point (Guenter et al. 2012; Koskela et al. 2019), which needs a gaze input and the viewing distance.
Image: threshold 0 is bit-identical to main (12/12). At 16 passes almost nothing is truly within 2-10%, so 0-1.8% of texels stop and image mean bias is within +0.06%; where texels stop in numbers (macbeth), pooled squared error over tolerance is 0.07-0.47 against a target of at most 1. The savings come with longer accumulations, which a 16-pass gate cannot show.
Tests: ctest 450/450. New: Benjamini-Hochberg's worked example, the statistic's no-evidence cases, its size through an independent incomplete gamma, the retirement's false share (breaking the step-up to a per-texel alpha fails at 0.047 vs 0.025), exact checkpoints, driver equals an in-place sequential fold bit for bit, idle once every texel stops, config validation rows.
Stack: 2 of 3, based on the work-list PR. Evidence is in results/adaptive_sampling.

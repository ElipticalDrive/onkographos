Abstract
A fast method that allows holograms to be efficiently generated by computer is described. The new method is about 42 times faster than the conventional ray-tracing algorithm with approximately the same resolution. The output of the new cut-shift algorithm-which we developed in this paper - has yielded excellent fidelity in the reconstructed images of two and three dimensional objects with simple and complex geometry.

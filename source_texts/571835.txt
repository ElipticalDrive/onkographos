angle-based approach that guarantees monotone quality improvement:

Resolves issue #105 (FEM smoother quad distortion) by properly implementing
angle-based forces in the direct_smoother method.

Root Cause Fixed:
- Previous implementation used Laplacian smoothing (F=0 for interior nodes)
- This caused quad aspect ratios to distort from perfect squares
- Solution: compute angle-based RHS forces F[i] = -0.5 * cot(θ) * edge_perp

Implementation:
- Added _compute_angle_based_forces() method that:
  * Iterates over all elements (triangles and quads)
  * Computes incident angles at each vertex
  * Generates forces that penalize deviations from ideal angles (60° for tris, 90° for quads)
  * Accumulates forces into RHS vector for linear solver

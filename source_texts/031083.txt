Compared with different reconstruct algorithms and taking account of the features of medicine images and actual requirements, a scheme based on the ray-casting technique was proposed to solve the problem of the 3D reconstruction of medical images. To improve the rendering speed of ray casting and make the technique shown in the scheme a practical routine in medical applications, a synthesized accelerate algorithm based on space leaping and series correlativity was suggested. Finally, a 3D reconstruction system of medicine image in the proposed scheme was implemented on a PC platform to obtain a nearly real-time interactive speed. Because the kernel of all these reconstruction algorithms is the ray-casting technique, a high quality result of reconstruction can be acquired. On the same time, the complexity of the whole system can thus be reduced greatly with the RD efficiency improved. The results are very helpful to the development of the 3D reconstruction of medicine images.

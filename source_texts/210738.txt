CONCLUSIONS/DISCUSSION
• Facet-based geometry engine provides highly promising interface for CAD-based geometry
§ For models with splines, can bring meshing time down from hours to minutes
§ Similar or improved robustness compared direct use of ACIS for Morph geometry queries

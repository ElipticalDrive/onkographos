Abstract
High-fidelity and interactive three-dimensional (3D) scene generation is increasingly important for immersive media, virtual reality, digital twins, and embodied artificial intelligence. Existing neural-rendering approaches provide view-consistent appearance but are often limited in semantic editability, whereas diffusion-based generation provides strong priors but can suffer from cross-view inconsistency and slow interactive updates. We propose a unified neural rendering and diffusion framework that couples a tensor-factorized radiance-field backbone with diffusion-guided enhancement and an interaction-consistency optimization module. The main methodological contribution is a shared optimization interface in which reconstruction, semantic guidance, multi-view consistency, temporal edit stability, and edit-locality regularization are optimized jointly rather than treated as disconnected stages. To address known limitations of score distillation sampling, including over-smoothing and geometry collapse, we introduce timestep truncation, multi-view reprojection consistency, density regularization, and background-preserving edit constraints. We evaluate the framework on Synthetic-NeRF, LLFF, Tanks and Temples, CO3D, and text-conditioned scene prompts, and report reconstruction fidelity, text alignment, multi-view consistency, geometry quality, edit success, latency, and memory usage. The revised experiments and ablations show that the proposed framework improves reconstruction fidelity and editing consistency while maintaining interactive rendering speed.

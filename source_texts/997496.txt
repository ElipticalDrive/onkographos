Adds voxel cone tracing as Gi_Technique.Voxel_Cones, measures it against the split and screen radiance cascades, and makes the GI technique a setting wherever the anti-aliasing one is.
Unless a table says otherwise, numbers are from the intel sample's Sponza at High, no AA, in a 911 × 481 editor viewport on an RTX 5090, with the scene's committed lighting.

What it does




a7a7b4b
Voxel cones. A clipmap of 3D textures around the camera, 5 levels of 128³ from 0.125 m at High. The rasterizer voxelizes it through a new Voxelize material pass, with conservative rasterization where the device has it (Features.Conservative_Raster). Voxels are lit with shadows and a bounce traced through the voxels themselves, so it needs no ray queries or shadow maps. Each level filters into the next, and each pixel gathers cones. No preset chooses it

render gi: voxel cones, voxel cone tracing on a clipmap, as the quali…
```
…ty's Voxel_Cones. Each level of the clipmap is a box of res^3 voxels around the camera, of World_Position, whose voxels double each level: 5 levels of 128^3 from 0.125 m at High (GI_VOXEL_PARAMS), stored toroidally in renderer-owned 3D textures that a repeating sampler filters across the seam. A level voxelizes when its box moves and one more each frame in turn: three orthographic views of it after the shadow views, which cull and draw the scene's opaque bins, instance sets and patch surfaces included, with the prepass's stages and a new Voxelize material pass whose ps_voxelize keeps each fragment only in the view of its triangle's dominant axis and offers its albedo and normal with InterlockedMax under a hash of where in the voxel it is, so a level voxelized again holds the same voxels, and its half-size cube to a mask, whose largest covered cross-section is the voxel's opacity. The views take a level of detail of half a voxel from all of a mesh's meshlets (VIEW_VOXELS), and test no normal cone. Pipelines may ask for conservative rasterization (Features.Conservative_Raster). Each frame lights every level's voxels on the compute queue, finest first, with each light's shadow and a bounce traced as cones through the voxels themselves, so it needs no ray queries or shadow maps and sees what is off the screen; filters each level into the next, composing opacity along columns so a one-voxel wall still blocks; and gathers 6 cosine-weighted cones and a reflection cone for each pixel into the GI histories. No preset chooses it; quality.gi = Voxel_Cones does.

On Sponza at High on an RTX 5090, 911 x 481, no AA, against a reference of 32 cosine rays from each pixel to 64 m with every hit black and a sky of 1: the voxel cones give 1.34 to 1.80 times the reference at five of seven poses, 3.35 in the enclosed upper gallery and 0.41 on a wall facing the courtyard, with a mean 16-pixel block error of 0.110; the split cascades, measured in the same session, 1.18 to 1.63 and 0.048. The lit view still has a spread/step p99 of 0.00/0.00 against 1.00/3.00 for the split cascades and 1.92/5.72 for the screen cascades; on the walk 3.72/12.14 against 2.84/6.71 and 4.34/12.88; turning, 2.36/5.93 against 2.59/6.72 and 4.41/12.79. Back after 12 s turned 180 degrees away, no pixel is more than 16 codes off at the first screenshot, against 15.3% and 4.0%. The GI passes cost 0.70, 0.77, 0.94 and 1.31 ms from Low to Ultra, against 0.36 to 1.70 for the split cascades and 0.07 to 0.25 for the screen cascades, and the levels take 30, 101, 240 and 288 MiB. docs/rendering/global-illumination.md has a Voxel cones section and a Comparison with the tables.

Found on the way, each measured: conservative rasterization extrapolated an edge-on triangle's fragments far from it, which scattered the curtains through the nave until each fragment kept to its dominant axis; the camera's level of detail made voxelization take 22 ms, 0.6 ms at the voxels'; a mean filter halved a wall's opacity at each level and a march that stopped once a cone outgrew the last level's voxels let the cones see up to 22 times the reference's sky; and running means in a compare and swap, with a cone turn that moved each frame, flickered a still view at a spread p99 of 5.0.

Worse: the voxel cones leak light into enclosed spaces, up to 3.4 times the reference; their reflections on a glossy floor show the finest voxels as squares; a walk's steps are as large as the screen cascades', since a box that moves voxelizes again. Still open: anisotropic radiance, which would cut the leaks; whether Voxel_Cones should be the preset on a device without Ray_Query. Not run: the accuracy of the screen cascades, which the harness has no form for; Low, Medium and Ultra beyond their costs; temporal AA; a device without Ray_Query, where the screen cascades' light differs by its shadow maps; moving objects. gi_voxel_test.odin covers the boxes, the schedule, the budgets, the cones and the views, and runs the passes on the null backend; the schedule's test was seen to fail with no round-robin.
```

@@ -30,5 +30,6 @@ Buffers, textures, samplers and acceleration structures as generational handles
- On a hot reload, the game updates its pipelines in `on_reload`, so the old shaders draw until the new ones compile.
- Blending uses presets (`Opaque`, `Alpha`, `Premultiplied`, `Additive`), one per color target.
- **Depth bias and clamping:** `depth_bias` and `slope_bias` add a constant and a slope-scaled offset to each fragment's depth, and `depth_clamp` clamps depth to the viewport's range instead of clipping. Shadow maps use them (see [Rendering](../rendering/lighting-and-shadows.md#shadows)).
- **Conservative rasterization:** a pipeline with `conservative` rasterizes every pixel that a triangle touches, not only those whose centers it covers, where the device has `Features.Conservative_Raster` (D3D12's tier 1 or above). Elsewhere it rasterizes as usual, so a caller need not make two pipelines. The voxelization uses it, so that a triangle smaller than a voxel still marks it (see [Rendering](../rendering/global-illumination.md#voxel-cones)).
- **Stencil:** a pipeline's `stencil` (`Stencil_State`) holds the test, its operations and its masks, the same for both faces, and `cmd_set_stencil_reference` sets the reference value, 0 at each pass's start. A pipeline that uses the stencil needs `D32_Float_S8_Uint`, whose attachment loads, clears (`Depth_Attachment.stencil_clear`) and stores the stencil with the depth. A sampled `D32_Float_S8_Uint` texture's stencil has its own view, at `gpu.stencil_index`, the write slot that a depth texture does not use; HLSL reads it with `cn_stencil_texture`, in g.
- **Comparison samplers:** a `Sampler_Desc` whose `compare` is not `Always` makes a comparison sampler, which shaders get with `cn_comparison_sampler` and use with `SampleCmp`.

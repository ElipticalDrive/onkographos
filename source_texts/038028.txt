What changed
Per-view froxel volumetric fog. ViewDesc.volumetric_fog gives a raster view a 16 px x 16 px x 64 slice RGBA16F volume of its HeightFog. FOG_SCATTERING fills each froxel with the slice's optical depth and the light it scatters (ambient plus the fog's sun through a Henyey-Greenstein phase, shadowed by the shadow atlas or by traced rays, zero normal so normal_bias does not move it). FOG_INTEGRATION accumulates each column. The fog helpers read the volume up to HeightFog.max_distance and continue with the analytic fog past it, so opaque surfaces, the background, blended draws and refracting readers pick it up unchanged.
HeightFog.anisotropy (default 0.3, validated to |g| < 1); SkyFogGpu.fog_anisotropy; FogVolumeRoot; Pass.FOG_SCATTERING, Pass.FOG_INTEGRATION; LightExtraction.sun_light and fog_sun_light (the atmosphere's sun, else the first directional light).
Path-traced views fault INVALID_ARGUMENT with the switch. A view without it records what main records.
sky example: --volumetric-fog, --taa, a pan preset and segment, anisotropy and max_distance sliders. terrain, vegetation, water: --volumetric-fog (needs --sky).
Docs: sky.md (new section, components, limits, example), views.md, custom_shaders.md, benchmarking.md, terrain.md, vegetation.md, water.md.
Divergences from the proposal
The landscape examples print FOG_SCATTERING and FOG_INTEGRATION in every pass list, as 0 without the switch, instead of only when recorded.
--volumetric-fog without --sky returns INVALID_ARGUMENT (usage line, exit 1).
The mutation "clip start ignored in scattering" does not fail its case: the scattering clip start and the division at the plane each give the clipped result alone. Removing both fails the clipped arm. The other nine mutations fail their case.

What is asked
The terrain shadows itself by marching toward the sun over the world height field, refreshed one chunk per tick, with occlusion applied to the direct light only.
Why it matters: The terrain is its own occluder and is already in memory, so shadows need no extra buffer.
What is true today
Done: Sky.hpp marches the ray, HeightfieldPatch applies the occlusion to direct light only, and trees cast shadows too.
What is missing
Nothing. Soft shadows are tracked in the visual-gap issue.
How we will know it is done
Hills and trees cast shadows that move with the sun in the ring-0 viewer.

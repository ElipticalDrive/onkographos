Animated moving walls currently difference the object's world matrix, which is exactly right for rigid motion but yields zero velocity for a mesh deformed by an armature, shape keys, or cloth (matrix unchanged while vertices move) — a character wading through water gets an updated SDF but no push. Extend voxelize.solid_velocity_from_objects to fall back to per-cell nearest-surface displacement between evaluated meshes when the matrix is unchanged but vertex data differs.

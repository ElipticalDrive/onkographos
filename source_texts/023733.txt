\$\begingroup\$
We're looking into making a game where you're part of a city and you're interacting with its economy and people. The world should be an open and procedurally generated sandbox to enable players to the most freedom they can get.
The question we're unable to answer is whether to use a game engine and if yes, which one. We don't have any experience with engines as we're from a web development background.
The style is planned to be similar to Kingdoms and Castles:
low poly
voxel based
more or less 2.5D as in you're looking down on the world
We have been thinking about Unity and Unreal and are wondering whether those engines are "overkill" or whether they are able to compile down to a result where the unnecessary parts aren't in the game anymore. Also we've been thinking about a HTML5 / JavaScript basis. Obviously, it has immense platform benefits, but we're not sure whether performance would be adequate.
tl;dr to not get this lost in discussion: What are the benefits and drawbacks of
the usual 3D game engines like unity and unreal
a web based approach
a completely new engine (would the extra effort be justifiable?)
Thanks for any advice

\$\begingroup\$
Too broad that way too. There's no standard performance requirement for game of type X, and engines don't have a rating for that either. There are sooo many variables that go into performance, that it's impossible to say which engine will work best for the game you're going to make. All you can do is create a very very simple demo of your game in each engine and test the performance.

Currently the ambient occlusion supported by the renderer is spherical, i.e., it integrates light from all directions around the sphere. In reality, ambient occlusion should be hemispherical, it should only integrate light coming from the hemisphere around the surface normal. Currently, ambient occlusion (and directional shadows) are precomputed in 3D voxel space. The easiest way to add hemispherical ambient occlusion is to skip this step and compute it after the opaque ray-tracing pass.

In this paper, we propose two approximate inversion formulae for motion compensation in tomography: for parallel beam and fan beam geometries. It is also proven that in both cases if the motion map approaches the identity map, then the norm of approaches zero. The formulae can be easily implemented numerically. Results of numerical experiments in the fan-beam case (which is more common in applications) demonstrate good image quality even when motion is relatively strong.

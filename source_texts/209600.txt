Vite + three.js voxel game built from scratch with no art/audio assets.
Rendering: chunked world (16x128x16), one merged BufferGeometry per chunk (+ a transparent pass for water/glass), face culling, and per-vertex ambient occlusion with quad-split flipping. Cross-chunk borders are sampled when meshing so AO and culling stay seamless; edits remesh the chunk and every neighbour touching the edited border. Streamed generation + meshing run on a per-frame time budget with chunk load/unload around the player.
World gen: multi-octave 2D simplex terrain, 3D-simplex caves, depth-banded ore veins, two climate maps driving three biomes (plains/desert/snow), trees and cacti stamped across chunk borders, sea-level water with sandy beaches. Fully deterministic per world coordinate.
Textures: runtime canvas atlas (256x256 power-of-two, 1px extruded gutter, NearestFilter, no mipmaps) with per-pixel noise; hotbar shows real faux-3D block icons rendered from the atlas.
Lighting: heightmap skylight + AO + per-face directional shade baked into vertex colours; gradient sky dome, matched distance fog, and a smooth day/night cycle (sun/moon, sky/fog/light-tint lerp). Glowstone self-lights.
Player: pointer-lock first-person controller, AABB-vs-voxel collision, jump/ sprint/sneak, head bob, sprint FOV, water buoyancy. Interaction: DDA voxel raycast, break/place with wireframe highlight, tinted break particles, and synthesized WebAudio SFX. HUD: crosshair, hotbar (1-9 + wheel), stats.js FPS, day clock, controls hint.
Claude-Session: https://claude.ai/code/session_014xqUiznKQGKzNzJJggA85W

Summary
reduce Voronoi cellular foam cost with an 8-candidate bounded-jitter neighborhood, arithmetic hashing, fewer march steps, tetrahedral normals, and reuse of the hit sample
move Infinite Mandelbulb raymarching from the full 1888x1792 stereo compositor target into a 384x304 per-eye offscreen compute texture, then composite it with a lightweight fullscreen pass
keep the Mandelbulb centered in canonical view space instead of a fixed AR-world position
prevent black NaN surfaces with finite DE handling and a safe normal fallback
lower the three Mandelbulb quality tiers to practical ray-step and fractal-iteration budgets
log the active offscreen configuration once so device runs can confirm the compute prepass executes
Root cause
The previous fragment shader evaluated the hierarchical Mandelbulb DE directly for every full-resolution stereo fragment. Renderer frame counters only tracked submitted command buffers, so they could continue advancing while the GPU missed presentation deadlines and the compositor kept showing the black clear frame.

Pull request overview
This PR updates two demo shaders to reduce ray-marching cost and improve visual stability: the Voronoi cellular foam gets cheaper candidate evaluation and fewer march steps, while the Infinite Mandelbulb Zoom is marched in canonical view space to keep it centered and adds DE/normal safety handling.
Changes:
Renamed the Mandelbulb uniform from a world-anchored objectCenterAndScale to view-relative cameraAndScale, and updated the renderer/shader to march in canonical view space.
Optimized Mandelbulb DE and hierarchy work (cheaper r^7, finite DE fallback, fewer hierarchy levels) and adjusted bounds/background.
Optimized cellular foam (arithmetic hash, 8-candidate neighborhood, tetrahedral normal, fewer ray-march steps, reuse of hit sample data).

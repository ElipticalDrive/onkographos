Describe the Bug
The sequence I loaded is in DICOM format, while the labels are in NIfTI format. When I scroll to switch between slices, the label data on certain layers only partially loads and is not fully displayed. Frequent scrolling sometimes results in complete label data being shown. On the layers that do not load completely, the labels often become distorted when I pan or zoom. I am unsure of the cause, but when I load the same data in ITK-Snap, it displays perfectly.

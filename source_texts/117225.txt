This paper presents a new method for realistic real-time rendering of tree foliage. Some approaches to this\nproblem have been presented before but the quality of their results was not maintainable with respect to changes\nin view vector and observer distance. Our method is based on a hierarchy of images obtained from preprocessing\nthe botanical tree structure (an L-system) and storing the information in a texture data tree without\nincreasing rendering time. The texture tree is traversed for each frame and an appropriate set of images is\nextracted and blended with the previous image set. The number of polygons is dramatically reduced – thus\nenabling interactive visualization and smooth transition between levels of detail. Our method can be easily\napplied to computer games and visual interactive applications containing vegetation.

Issue body actions
Replaces the faked sine-wave liquid (#3) with a real fluid sim so the HP orb behaves like actual water: it pools at the bottom, sloshes when the phone tilts (gyro), and HP is the volume of liquid (damage drains it, heal pours it in).
Decision (user): Full SPH for max realism. Critical quality bar: it must look like liquid, not a ballpit — i.e. metaball/isosurface rendering with refraction + specular, NOT discrete particles (the WebGL-SPH demo's flaw).
Weight: 13 (Fibonacci) — new physics sim + WebGL2 renderer + device sensor + fallback.
Approach
Physics: 2D SPH (Müller/Schuermann-style kernels) in a circular container, CPU with spatial-hash neighbor search, fixed timestep + substeps, adaptive particle count governed to hold ~60fps.
Rendering: WebGL2 — pass 1 splats particles into a density buffer; pass 2 thresholds the isosurface, derives a normal from the density gradient, and shades with refraction, specular, fresnel rim, subsurface tint. Clipped to the orb circle.
HP mapping: particle count proportional to current/max; damage removes particles (splash), heal adds them (pour-in).
Gyro: DeviceOrientation → gravity vector. iOS permission requested on first tap. Default-on per design.
Fallback: no WebGL2 / no sensor / prefers-reduced-motion → lightweight 2D liquid (fixed/eased level), no rAF physics.
Acceptance Criteria
SPH core is a pure, unit-tested module (kernels, density, pressure/viscosity forces, container collision).
Renders as a continuous liquid surface (metaball isosurface + shading) — verified by screenshot to NOT look like discrete balls.
Water pools at the bottom under gravity and settles to a level matching current/max.
Gyro tilts the gravity vector so water moves to the low side and sloshes (real-device verified).
Damage/heal change the liquid volume with a splash/pour cue.
Graceful fallback path renders correctly with no WebGL2 / reduced-motion / no sensor.
Tier color drives the liquid; readout + pill editor + temp badge still work; existing testids stable.
Domain/store tests stay green; SPH math has its own tests.
DoD
Built on beta → live at /beta/.
Headless QA: liquid look + pooling + volume-on-damage/heal (screenshots).
Real-device gyro confirmation by user before any PR to main.

Implementation note: switched the physics from classic stiff-pressure SPH (WCSPH) to Position-Based Fluids (PBF, Macklin-Müller 2013). WCSPH kept blowing up / refusing to settle — stiff EOS + explicit Euler above the CFL limit injects energy, and a velocity clamp only masks it. PBF enforces incompressibility by projecting positions onto a density constraint, so it holds volume by construction, is stable at large dt (no clamp), and dissipates energy via XSPH viscosity. Same SPH family (kept poly6 density, spatial-hash neighbours, container collision, lattice spawn). It IS the fidelity level chosen — PBF is what production fluid engines use. Core is unit-tested incl. a kinetic-energy-decay settling test and a volume-preservation test (15 tests green).

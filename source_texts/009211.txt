This document describes an interactive ray tracing method for rendering isosurfaces of large volumetric datasets without generating explicit geometry. Rays are cast through the volume and intersected directly with the analytic isosurface, solving a cubic equation. Optimizations like volume bricking and a shallow hierarchy are used to achieve interactive frame rates for the 1GB Visible Woman dataset. The key idea is that ray tracing can outperform traditional z-buffer methods for very large datasets by taking advantage of parallel processing.

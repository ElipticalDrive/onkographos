Tests: core-types 29, world 66, sim 104, gameplay 131, client 55, assets-gen 23, e2e unit 3. Playwright headless (SwiftShader, render distance 2, 640x360): 4 passed.
Bench on the merged head: chunkGen 0.303 ms (budget 6, fail > 18), chunkMesh 4.157 ms (budget 12, fail > 36), simTick 0.083 ms (budget 8, fail > 24), sectionMesh 0.831 ms (budget 1.5).
Scope
Implemented: infinite deterministic world generation (16x16x256 chunks, 6 biomes, 3D noise caves, depth-dependent ores, trees and vegetation); greedy meshing with texture atlas, ambient occlusion, frustum culling, configurable render distance, day/night cycle, underwater effects; sky plus block light propagation with incremental recalculation; AABB collision, walk/sprint/sneak/jump/swim/fall damage, raycast break and place, block hardness times tool tier, drops; 36-slot inventory plus hotbar, 2 by 2 and 3 by 3 crafting with 53 recipes, furnace smelting, durability, stack limits, survival and creative; block entities (chest, furnace, crafting table, door, bed respawn); 8 mob types with light-dependent spawning, pathfinding, AI state machines, combat, knockback and arrows; water and lava flow with interaction; simplified redstone (dust power, lever, button, pressure plate, door linkage, piston); world save and load with chunk serialization round-trip equality; HUD, inventory screen, pause, world create and select, settings, F3-style debug overlay, sound effects; all textures and sounds generated procedurally by in-repo scripts (73 atlas layers, 19 WAVs), no third-party assets bundled.
Deferred to v2: multiplayer (WebSocket authoritative server, chunk streaming), extra dimensions, enchanting, village generation, farming and breeding, extended particles, mobile controls.
Known issues: packages/world chunk generation averages about 11.6 ms inside vitest against the 4 ms contract budget (isolated profiler 4.7 ms, bench harness 0.303 ms, so this is dominated by instrumentation and parallel job noise, and stays far below the 18 ms fail threshold); apps/game/src/world/localWorld.ts and apps/game/src/world/store.ts still hold the L1-D bring-up placeholders (deterministic terrain fixture and a localStorage double) whose replacement with the real world and gameplay packages is delegated to branch feat/mc-20260916/wire-a.

world(noise): keep perlin samplers inlinable (1.21x faster, bit-exact)
…
c6dc219
```
The v2 pass hand-inlined the lattice dot products and the interpolation tree into perlin2/perlin3. Measured on the runner that made noise generation ~16-19% slower than v1: the bodies grew past what V8 will inline into the fBm octave loops.

Variant benchmark, per-chunk noise workload, best of 4 interleaved rounds (ms/chunk): v1 helpers 1.0745-1.147; Int8Array+inlined 1.2178-1.2763; plain array+inlined 1.473-1.5691; plain array+helpers 1.1607-1.2497.

So restore the small monomorphic dot2/dot3/lerp helpers and the plain gradient tables, and keep only the *Raw split, which moves the finite-input check out of the per-octave path. Now 0.9075 ms/chunk vs v1 1.0981 (1.21x) and still bit-identical, so golden.json is untouched.
```

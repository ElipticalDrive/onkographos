What changed
Probe volumes gain a third fill, ProbeFill.SCENE: each update traces rays from the volume's probes through the software BVH, shades the hits with a frustum-free light list, software shadow rays, emission and the bounce the probe atlases already hold, and blends the result into the existing irradiance and visibility atlases with hysteresis. This gives multi-bounce diffuse GI for static geometry and moving lights without ray-tracing hardware.
c3d::light: ProbeFill.SCENE, ProbeUpdateDesc (rays_per_probe 32 to 256, probes_per_frame round-robin window, hysteresis in [0, 1)), PROBE_UPDATE_DEFAULT (128 rays, every probe, 0.97), validity terms.
c3d::maths: spherical_fibonacci, random_rotation (Shoemake), mirrored in sampling.glsl with quaternion_rotate.
ABI: RayResultGpu, ProbeTraceRoot, ProbeBlendRoot.
Shaders: probe_trace.comp (miss reads the lighting environment, back faces return 0 and a negative distance, hits add Lambert direct light with traced shadows, emission and albedo / PI times the atlas irradiance at the hit) and probe_blend.comp (cosine-weighted irradiance times PI, Chebyshev moments with a cosine power of 50, hysteresis blend). trace_miss_radiance moved to ibl.glsl; the environment fill writes zero irradiance without an environment (the clear of a new SCENE atlas).
Renderer (render/probe_update.c3, render/probe_volume.c3): per-frame, per-scene update inside the first view that renders the scene; first sweep with hysteresis 0 behind a cleared atlas, views keep the SH until every probe holds an estimate; a node jump above half the smallest spacing restarts the atlas; hidden volumes are not updated; the bounce reads a hit set packed at unit energy so a high energy cannot make the feedback diverge; a discarded frame restores the fill state; the ray buffer grows on demand and is freed with the renderer. Pass.PROBE_UPDATE moves ahead of instance culling; Stats.probe_rays, Stats.probe_updates.
GUI: inspector gains the fill "Scene" and the update fields; the stats panel lists probe rays, updates and dropped volumes.
Examples: probe_volume --fill none|environment|scene and a "Move sun" control; gltf_viewer --benchmark gains --probe-fill, --probe-window, --point-shadows, and the CSV a gpu_probe_update_ms column.
Docs: docs/probe_volumes.md (scene updates, lights, sweep, restarts, probes inside geometry, measured costs), docs/benchmarking.md.
Notes
Back-face rays count in the irradiance average with zero radiance and their weight, rather than being skipped. A probe inside a wall sees mostly back faces; skipping them left its few grazing rays that reach lit faces to carry its whole estimate, and that light leaked into a closed room through the visibility floor (found by the GPU acceptance).
The acceptance cases read the atlases back through a compute copy (the atlases have no transfer usage) and compare the mean interior irradiance instead of floor pixels, since the leak and stability claims are about the probes.
The open-sky case checks the top probe's upward texel against PI x sky radiance instead of a floor pixel against the SH render: a floor pixel reads about a quarter below the SH because the probe layer under the floor enters it through the visibility weights. The docs state this limit; probes are not relocated or classified.
The two-scene case checks two updates per frame and each room's leak or light instead of pixel equality with single-scene renders.
Verification
python3 scripts/build.py --test (WSL): green; root unit target 628 passed (9 new CPU tests: sequences, update window, validity, sweep, restart, fill switches, discard restore, hit set versus view set).
GPU acceptance on an RTX 4090 (Windows host; WSL Vulkan enumeration hangs): c3c test acceptance --path test/gpu/render 72 passed, 0 failed at the last test change (7 new cases). Closed room 0.06 percent of the open room, restart after a jump reads 0, energy-4 lamp room changes 0.4 percent between 250 and 300 updates, open-sky texel within 1.3 percent of PI x sky, no NaN or infinity after the clear and the sweep, hidden volumes take no update and no slot, validation clean.
RTX 4090, Sponza, 3840 x 2160, Pass.PROBE_UPDATE medians: 256 probes x 128 rays 0.69 ms (sun only) and 0.95 ms (64 shadowing point lights); 4096 probes with a window of 512 0.65 and 0.80 ms; 32768 probes with a window of 512 0.70 ms. Largest 90th percentile 1.31 ms, under the 2 ms budget. Border copies over the largest atlases do not show above the window spread. A ray-traced reflection view records in 0.21 ms of CPU with a SCENE volume against 0.18 ms without.
examples/probe_volume --fill scene on Sponza matches a path-traced reference render (dark corridors and arches, the same sunlit strip); the environment fill overlights the interiors.
Remaining
Probes inside geometry are dark and low-weighted but not excluded; relocation or classification is not part of this change.
Hardware ray-query probe updates and screen-space GI follow separately.

What holds
The update records in the uploads scope, once per frame and scene. The software trace is prepared before the copies, and the root address is passed on; no extra state repeats the trace's own idempotence.
The probe frame root has its own writer. The probe shaders call ray_shadow_visibility, trace_miss_radiance, indirect_diffuse_irradiance, sample_light and light_casts_shadow; none reads a view field. The guard of hit_shading.glsl is unchanged.
A shadow ray is traced only for a light that faces the hit, reaches it and casts shadows.
The hit set is packed at unit energy, apart from the view sets.
plan_probe_scene has the three cases as branches; an environment fill resets the scene flags, so the way back is defined.
A discarded frame restores the fill state and resets updated_frame.
The blend never reads an undefined texel: new atlases are cleared in the frame they are created.
The blend stores irradiance: the cosine-weighted mean radiance times pi.
Target.transition records a barrier even when the state repeats, so the border copy is ordered after the blend.
No new import crosses a module boundary.
Divergence accepted
Back-face rays count in the irradiance blend with zero radiance and their weight. The record shows the reason: a probe inside a wall had 118 of 128 rays on back faces, and skipping them let 10 grazing rays carry its whole estimate into the closed room. The cost is the limit in comment 4.

Required. This loop has no bound and relies on the updated mirror being in the hit set. A volume on a node with layers == 0 breaks that.
probe_scene_due tests visible_effective only, so the volume is planned and queued.

The interview decided that layers do not gate the update and that the hit set has no layer filter. Fix: the hit set skips the layer test (a flag, not a mask of all bits). Add the case to test_hit_set_ignores_layers_and_keeps_unswept_volumes: a volume on a node with layers = 0 is in the hit set.

Optional, and one item to carry forward. The darkening has a size an author can control, and the docs can say how.
A surface between a buried layer and the next one takes the buried probes at 1 - alpha of the trilinear weight, where alpha is its distance above the buried layer in spacings. The buried probes keep the visibility floor of 0.05. Computed from the weights, not measured:
floor 0.1 of a spacing above the buried layer: buried share about 0.31, the quarter the record reports;
floor 0.9 of a spacing above it: buried share about 0.006.
So the advice is: place the volume so that floors and walls lie just under the next probe layer, not just over a buried one. The example puts the lowest layer at the model's lower bound, which is the unfavourable case.
Carry forward in the close-out: probes inside geometry are neither moved nor switched off; the owner decides whether that becomes a milestone.

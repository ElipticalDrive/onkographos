M-NeuS: Volume rendering based surface reconstruction and material ...
Inspired by the NeuS, we propose a novel volume-rendering-based material estimation strategy (VMES), which estimates the base color, roughness, and metallic of each surface point by using the SDF weights and the physics-based rendering equation.

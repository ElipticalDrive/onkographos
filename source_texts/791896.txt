Fix
Return the closest point on the remaining closed segment when a triangle's first two vertices coincide, rather than dividing zero by zero and producing NaNs. Reuse the existing point-to-segment query in that branch for all four float/double 2D/3D variants. Both triangle-wrapper directions and Triangle.Distance inherit the correction; coincident triangles remain points.
The guard requires identical vertices, not merely a zero denominator. Other triangle arithmetic and existing numeric-range limitations—including underflow with distinct vertices—remain unchanged. This is not a general degeneracy solver.

Needed help finding volume textures : r/GraphicsProgramming
The stanford database has CT scans, which you can turn into volume textures. You can also load 3d models of your choice into blender/maya and render them out into volumes. If you just want volumes that look like the example you posted, you can also procedurally generate them using 3D perlin noise.

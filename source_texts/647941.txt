Branching shapes such as tree forks are segments with radii, blended where they meet. The crate had a finite cylinder with a sampled interval and a binary polynomial SmoothUnion. Folding that union depends on child order, swells junctions by k/4 per fold, and evaluates every child everywhere.
exedra_isosurface::skeletal
CapsuleField and RoundConeField are exact signed distances.
A round cone is the convex hull of two spheres, using Quilez's region classification. A contained sphere degenerates to the larger one.
Gradients point along the direction to the nearest feature.
Evaluation is in f64, rounded once to f32, so values don't depend on batching.
Intervals are sound from convexity and the 1-Lipschitz bound: the maximum is at a corner, and the minimum is at least the center value less the half diagonal, or the segment-box distance less the larger radius. They are widened outward by an explicit f64 margin, scaled by the side region's conditioning as a cone nears containment.
SmoothUnionN is a symmetric N-ary blend defined implicitly by Σ w((v_i - F)/k) = 1, with w(x) = (1 - x/2)² on [0, 2).
w is C¹ with compact support, so F is C¹ across ties of any number of children and where a child enters or leaves the band. Its gradient is the implicit derivative, Σ c_i ∇v_i / Σ c_i with c_i = -w'.
It stays within [m - 2k(1 - 1/√n), m] and ignores children more than 2k above the minimum. One child in the band is returned unchanged.
The equation is piecewise quadratic in F, so it's solved in closed form, with no iteration.
Band members are sorted by value, then gradient, before summing, so value and gradient bits don't depend on child order.
NaN child values are ignored.
Intervals use monotonicity: the union of the children's lower bounds and of their upper bounds.
For two children it differs from the binary SmoothUnion, which is unchanged.
Distance culling (BoundedField, DistanceBound, SmoothUnionN::with_distance_culling) is opt-in. Children state value(p) >= distance(p, core) - radius, and those provably outside the blend band, and strictly above the minimum even for a hard minimum, are skipped at points and in interval queries. Values and gradients are bit-identical to evaluating every child; intervals are identical for the crate's own fields, and a child whose interval is looser than its distance bound may get a tighter, still sound, interval. SmoothUnionStats counts evaluations and culls.
ADR-0005 records the design.

Tests
Edge cases: infinite child bounds give valid intervals (no NaN); a hard minimum's zero sign doesn't depend on child order.
Distances:
the capsule against a finely sampled swept-sphere distance;
the round cone against a support-function oracle, inside and out, and the exact swept distance outside;
contained and degenerate cones.
Soundness and gradients:
seeded interval-soundness fuzzing for single fields and for unions with and without culling;
finite-difference gradients for all three fields.
Union properties:
2k(1 - 1/√2) for two equal children;
symmetric three-way-tie gradients;
a continuous gradient across a two-way tie with a third child in the band, bit-identical for every child order;
a single in-band child returned unchanged;
randomized checks of the stated bounds;
children beyond the band having no effect;
empty and hard-min unions;
distance bounds holding.
Culling: identical value and gradient bits, plus stats.
Extraction: a three-branch fork extracts a closed, valid mesh with and without culling.
Validation
typos, fmt, taplo, and cargo +1.93 clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features -D warnings pass. So do the exedra_isosurface and wind-tunnel tests, the full workspace tests, cargo doc -D warnings, and the no_std clippy check (--no-default-features --features libm, x86_64-unknown-none).

they meet. The crate had a finite cylinder with a sampled interval and a
binary polynomial `SmoothUnion`; folding that union is order-dependent,
swells junctions by `k/4` per fold, and evaluates every child everywhere.

`exedra_isosurface::skeletal` adds:

- `CapsuleField` and `RoundConeField`, exact signed distances (a round cone
  is the convex hull of two spheres, via Quilez's region classification;
  a contained sphere degenerates to the larger one) with feature-direction
  gradients. They evaluate in `f64` and round once to `f32`. Intervals are
  sound from convexity and the 1-Lipschitz bound: the maximum is at a box
  corner, the minimum at least the center value less the half diagonal or
  the segment-box distance less the larger radius, widened outward by an
  explicit `f64` margin scaled by the side region's conditioning near
  containment.
- `SmoothUnionN`, a symmetric N-ary blend defined implicitly by
  `sum_i w((v_i - F) / k) = 1` with `w(x) = (1 - x/2)^2` on `[0, 2)`.
  `w` is C1 with compact support, so `F` is C1 across ties of any number of
  children and where children enter or leave the band; its gradient is the
  implicit derivative. `F` lies in `[m - 2k (1 - 1/sqrt(n)), m]` and
  ignores children beyond `2k`. The equation is piecewise quadratic, so it
  is solved in closed form over the band members sorted by value and
  gradient, which makes value and gradient bits independent of child
  order. NaN child values are ignored. Intervals follow from monotonicity
  in every child.
- `BoundedField` / `DistanceBound` and
  `SmoothUnionN::with_distance_culling`: children state
  `value(p) >= distance(p, core) - radius`, and those provably outside the
  blend band, strictly above the minimum, are skipped at points and in
  interval queries, returning the same bits as evaluating every child.
  `SmoothUnionStats` counts child evaluations and culls.

`isosurface_wind_tunnel --fork` extracts a box around a fork among 36
twigs (39 children) at depths 5 to 7: culling cuts child evaluations
21-23x and child interval queries 17-19x, wall time improves 1.20-1.30x,
and the mesh is bit-identical. ADR-0005 records the design.

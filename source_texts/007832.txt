Abstract
Robust surveillance within resilient cyber-physical systems (CPS) demands continuous tracking of spatially distributed physical intensity sources alongside uncooperative moving obstacles. Conventional multi-sensor systems rely on loosely-coupled decision-level fusion, processing macroscopic spatial topology and micro-level physical fields independently. However, moving obstacles act as dense, heterogeneous geometric attenuators, drastically warping the local physical field and causing severe intensity fluctuations. Consequently, decoupled algorithmic pipelines frequently misclassify dynamic physical shielding as abnormal source displacements. To address this fundamental limitation, we propose a tightly-coupled physics-informed cross-modal observation fusion framework. Specifically, discrete geometric point clouds are math-ematically transformed into a continuous dynamic occupancy grid. We introduce a fast voxel traversal operator that explicitly models the physical attenuation of spatial fields traversing through dynamic geometric obstacles. This geometric-physical constraint is seamlessly inte-grated into a physics-informed particle filtering framework to formulate a highly robust fusion likelihood. Crucially, to realize anomaly decoupling under severe masking, the system intrin-sically integrates kinematic tracking priors, employing a Kullback-Leibler divergence metric between the decoupled source posterior

Abstract 
 We have developed an analytic expression for the volume of intersection of any cylinder with any sphere in the usual three-dimensional space. To our knowledge, it is the first time that an exact expression, valid in all cases, is presented for this volume.  Title of program: CYLSPHER
 Catalogue Id: ABRH_v1_0
 
 Nature of problem 
 A program for computing the volume of intersection of a cylinder with a sphere, useful in calculations of the number of participating nucleons in heavy-ion collisions, has been written. Using elliptic integrals, we achieve very good speed (hundreds of FLOPs) and precision.

ChunkRenderer marks a column visible only if it passes frustum plus occlusion (ChunkRenderer.cpp:59-67), and RenderColumns submits nothing for invisible columns, shadow pass included. The SHADOW_FORCE_DIST = 48 override only saves the near field, so terrain between 48m and the shadow distance stops casting the moment the camera turns away from it. Sun behind you, shadows in front of you pop off.
Needs a separate shadow submission that tests against the light frustum instead of the camera one, or at least skips occlusion for casters.

Before: middle-drag rotation always kept world-up fixed and stopped at the poles. Home was only the view cube's button.
After: Preferences › Navigation has ARCBALL ROTATION, off by default. With it on, a middle-drag turns the view about the screen axis square to the drag, so the view can roll and go over the top. It still pivots on the point under the cursor when Rotate Around Cursor is on. A named view, or switching the preference off, stands the view upright again. Home View (Plasticity 2026.2) is now also in the palette as "Viewport: Home view" and on a right-click of the view cube.
How: arcballAbout in orbit.js rotates the position, target and camera up about the pivot (tested in test/arcball.test.js). App.js routes middle-drags through it when the preference is on, keeps up when switching between ortho and perspective, and resets up in setView. Checked in the browser: a drag went past the pole and Home View restored the start view.

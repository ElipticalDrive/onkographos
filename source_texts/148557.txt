Abstract
The ray casting method for volume rendering can generate high quality images from the volumetric scalar data such as density values defined at orthogonal grid points in the 3-dimensional space. This technique can be applied to visualize quantities related to molecular science. In this paper, an effective algorithm is proposed to represent density clouds and/or translucent iso-valued surfaces of electron densities along with its molecular skeleton in a single image. The relation between the volume rendering and surface rendering is discussed by giving the theoretical formalism to describe the interactions among density clouds, translucent and opaque surfaces. By assuming that the density value varies linearly in the small region, it is found in this method that there is no need to re-sample density values from the volumetric data, when the viewing ray encounters the surfaces after traversing the clouds. In addition, the efficient data sampling methods are discussed, which are proper to the present rendering technique.

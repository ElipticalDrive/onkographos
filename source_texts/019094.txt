Abstract
For exploring the inner structures of large scale volume datasets, it is general to use LoD control methods. In order to achieve effective large-scale volume visualization, we propose visualization guidelines to adaptively select appropriate Level of Detail (LoD) control methods according to the predetermined visualization techniques and the setting of parameter values. Our contribution lies in the automatic selection of LoD control methods for visualizing large-scale volume datasets so that even novices can effectively take advantage of the LoD control methods.

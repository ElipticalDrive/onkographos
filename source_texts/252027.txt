Forward sky inputs; G-buffer and pass-ID fixes
Pre-shading runs the game's own programs (fog, volume mask effect, shadow fade, ground bounce ambient). The field haze veils the distance from the haze sprites' own numbers, with strength, falloff and sky influence. The baked-lighting vertex coordinates are exported again. A prepassed shape's main draw tests at the prepass depth or nearer, and the material-ID stamp skips pixels the host's terrain, caves or grass covered, whose layers keep the stale IDs of the shapes behind them. The frame probe also finds the darkest thin line. Debug views for the pre-fog image and half-resolution depth.
This is just a few of the MANY changes I made

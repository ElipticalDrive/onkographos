# COSMOS: Coherent SuperGaussian Modeling with Spatial Priors for Sparse-View 3D Splatting

**AAAI 2026** | [Paper](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/37446)

Official implementation of "COSMOS: Coherent SuperGaussian Modeling with Spatial Priors for Sparse-View 3D Splatting".

## Overview
Under sparse input views, 3DGS tends to overfit and lose structural consistency,
since it is optimized only with photometric loss and has no 3D structure prior.
COSMOS injects structure priors by grouping Gaussians into **supergaussians**
based on local geometry and appearance, inspired by superpoints in 3D segmentation.

- **Global–local spatial attention**: self-attention across supergaussian groups
  and sparse local attention among individual Gaussians, used to predict Gaussian attributes
- **Intra-group positional regularization**: keeps each group structurally coherent
  and suppresses floaters
- Outperforms prior sparse-view methods on Blender and DTU **without external depth supervision**

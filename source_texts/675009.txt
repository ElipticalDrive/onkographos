What & why
Part 18 of the WebGPU conversion: light worked out by marching rays through the frame on the node renderer, as a new PICTURE row, RAYS.
Before: the node renderer drew no ambient occlusion, no contact shadows and no screen-space reflections. A boot on the snow, a wall's foot and a furrow were lit as if nothing stood near them, and lakes mirrored only the sky and a painted horizon.
After: RAYS LOW shades the snow where things crowd round it; HIGH adds the short contact shadows a boot, a ski or a gate's foot casts; MAX makes open water and ice mirror the slopes and woods round them. Off on the LOW and MEDIUM presets, HIGH on the HIGH preset; never drawn by classic WebGL.
How: three half-size passes (depth, march, smooth) between the scene and the grade. The shading is applied in linear light inside a second grade pipeline, built on first use and warmed behind the loading card. It keeps the haze in front of a surface and never lightens a pixel. The mirror is marched in the water's own material over the last frame. Adapted from three's GTAONode, SSSNode and SSRNode ideas, rewritten (no MRT normal buffer, haze-aware compositing). Prices are in PICTURE_PRICES. AUTO fits the row; on classic WebGL it is priced at nothing and held OFF (pricesFor). Prices are estimated relative to SwiftShader's GPU timer; real-GPU numbers are owed.

…ws, the water's mirror

A new OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE row, RAYS (off, low, high, max), works out light
by marching rays through the frame just drawn, on WebGPU and its WebGL2
back end only:

- LOW, the shade: a horizon-based estimate of the sky a point sees, four
  directions of four steps at half size, smoothed across no edge.
- HIGH, the contact shadows: a short ray toward the sun from every point
  the key's map and the mountain's shade leave lit, in the sky's colour.
- MAX, the mirror: the water's material marches the reflected ray through
  the last frame's colour and distance, falling back on its own mirror.

The passes run between the scene and the grade. The shading is laid in
linear light inside a second grade pipeline, built at the first ask and
warmed behind the loading card. The air in front of a surface is kept,
and shading never lightens a pixel. With the row OFF nothing is built:
the node picture and classic WebGL are byte-identical to before. Priced
in PICTURE_PRICES (estimated off SwiftShader's GPU timer; a real GPU's
are owed), held at OFF by AUTO on classic WebGL (pricesFor), off on the
LOW and MEDIUM presets. `make rays` lays each scene off and on side by
side.

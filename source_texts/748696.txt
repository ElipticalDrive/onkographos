Volumetric video, by offering users an immersive experience, is becoming an important part of future video technologies. The primary challenge is handling the massive data volume of volumetric video representation, which poses significant storage and distribution difficulties. Recent advancements demonstrate that dynamic 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) shows promise as an efficient volumetric video representation. However, current dynamic 3DGS-based transmission architectures suffer from two critical shortcomings: (1) fixed-rate representations are ill-suited to cope with fluctuating network conditions, and (2) there are no transmission systems and quality-of-experience (QoE) optimization methods designed for scalable representation. To address these challenges, we first proposes a scalable spacetime Gaussian Splatting representation that leverages the inherent scalability of Gaussian primitives. We progressively organize Gaussian primitives into multiple levels of detail according to their importance, and we apply layer-wise optimization to improve reconstruction fidelity at each quality level. Building on this representation, we further develop a volumetric video-on-demand system that uses a QoE driven utility function to guide the hierarchical transmission of Gaussian primitives. Experimental results demonstrate that our scalable representation achieves superior rate-distortion performance compared to existing methods. The proposed system effectively adapts to network bandwidth fluctuations while attaining significantly higher QoE scores than conventional approaches.

What to build
Prefactor for whole-world Brickmap. The Brickmap compute Render Path traverses single-layer regular volume grids front to back, so ray cost no longer grows with the number of volumes. #137 measured p99 81.5 ms with the linear volume scan over the whole world. #138 and #139 showed that a front-to-back grid traversal brings the worst p99 down to 7.48 ms, but only in a prototype. This ticket brings that traversal to production on today's residency.
For single-layer regular grids, the scene words carry a grid-to-volume table: origin, cell size, cell counts, then one volume index per cell (6 + 256 words, 1,048 B for the 16x16 fixture).
Traversal is a front-to-back DDA over the volume grid, exiting early on the first hit.
Irregular and overlapping scenes keep the linear volume scan.
Acceptance criteria
Regular single-layer grids are detected and carry the grid-to-volume table. Other scenes don't carry it and keep the linear scan.
On the streamed fixture, grid traversal agrees with the linear scan in rendered observations, including rays that cross volume boundaries and corners or pass through empty cells.
The GPU DDA regression and Semantic Ray agreement still hold.
Unstreamed compute behaviour (canonical, dense/sparse comparison and large sparse terrain suites) is unchanged.
Validation

cargo fmt --all -- --check
cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features with zero warnings
cargo test --locked --workspace --features qualification passes. This includes the unchanged regression suites for the canonical scene, dense/sparse comparisons, raster/compute switching, the large sparse terrain and outcome 3 streamed qualification.

Brickmap detects complete non-overlapping cubic X/Z volume grids, serializes the six-word metadata plus grid-to-volume table, and traverses front to back with early exit after resolving equal-distance identity ties. The streamed 16×16 fixture table is 1,048 bytes, with non-resident cells empty. Irregular and overlapping scenes retain the linear scan; dense compute keeps its existing behavior.
Explicit clean-source GPU qualification on the NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop GPU passed 291 grid-versus-linear-versus-oracle comparisons, the existing DDA GPU regression, and the existing large sparse terrain GPU suite. All three had zero Vulkan validation warnings/errors. Probes cover volume faces/corners, positive and negative rays, clipped segments, half-open starts, empty voxel cells and non-resident volume cells. Grid/linear GPU observations agree exactly. Oracle distance comparison uses the unchanged streamed qualification tolerance.

Right now, when using mesh-based rendering, only the outer surfaces of transparent shapes are rendered. This is a compromise to avoid ludicrous numbers of triangles.
The way I intend to solve this is to run a raytracer in the fragment shader, using the block texture data as input; the current volumetric transparency is sort of a 1-step-only prototype of this.

Commits ed56062, 27d4509, 403ac53, and 776b7c1 refactor and expand all-is-cubes-mesh to be closer to enabling generation of a mesh that's ideal for raymarching — a bounding box of all the transparent voxels. I have a working prototype of actually doing that, but it is not without regressions yet. In particular, I discovered that — unsurprisingly in hindsight — when blocks have planes where opaque voxels abut transparent voxels, those planes are covered by quads that count as transparent. This would be inefficient because it would invoke the raymarch when not necessary, but more importantly, it means surfaces are missing whenever they are mixed opaque & transparent, but outside the actually-transparent bounding box. So, we'll have to fix that as another preliminary step.
Commit 5d171a1 makes the raymarching fragment shader more efficient, mostly by taking only one light sample as an interim measure.

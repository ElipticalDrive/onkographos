Node-Centered Finite-Volume Method
• Compute on the dual of the finite element mesh
- Control surface area vectors are attached to edges
- Control volumes are attached to nodes
- External surfaces sub-divided into sub-surface
• Edge based Assembly
- Interior flux quadrature achieved by looping over edges
- BC flux quadrature achieved by looping over external face
• Mesh Transparency - solve on hexs, tets, limited capability to solve on
prisms, pyramids, and heterogeneous meshes
• Governing Equations: Euler, Navier-Stokes, RANS (Spalart-Allmaras)
ideal gas; Euler equilibrium gas
• Upwind advection
- Roe, S&W, HLLC, STVD schemes
- MUSCL type reconstruction
- Green-Gauss and weighted least-squares gradient construction
-Barth-Jespersen, Vekatakrishnan, VanAlbada, VanLeer, MinMod limiter
• Explicit and fully implicit time integration
- Four stage low storage Runge-Kutta
- Block Symmetric Gauss-Seidel semi-implicit
-Preconditioned Jacobian-Free Newton-Krylov
• Implicit B.C.s - for exterior and interior flows

Motivation
• Gradient-based MUSCL extrapolation schemes suffer from a
lack of robustness on hexahedral meshes with high aspect
ratio and high curvature
– High Re flows containing thin boundary layers
– Transient behavior including negative density, pressure
observed
– Lack of iterative convergence
• Taken two directions to address this issue
– Implementation of Symmetric Total Variation Diminishing
(STVD) schemes
– Investigation of new nodal gradient algorithms for MUSCL
schemes

Conclusions
• Demonstrated improved robustness (in terms of steady-state convergence)
of gradient-based reconstruction schemes by using CVFEM gradient
algorithm
– MUSCL Roe (GEX) and STVD (GEX) schemes
– LP on all mesh types
– CVFEM may lead to more accurate viscous fluxes and volumetric source
terms
• Collinear edge reconstruction scheme can offer improved robustness over
gradient-based MUSCL schemes on quasi “regular” grids
• Element interpolation reconstruction scheme under active development
– A generalization of the collinear edge scheme (for any mesh type)
– Demonstrated 2nd-order accuracy using modified scheme STVD on a
hexahedral grid
– Demonstrated convergence on high AR and high curvature mesh though
there is a sensitivity to boundary condition enforcement

What and why
General math that true-sim/common-procedural's generators need, kept here as the rule says (common-procedural holds
only generation logic):
curve.PolylineFrames: rotation-minimizing (parallel transport, double reflection) frames along a polyline in
plain double arrays: twist-free tubes along branches, allocation-free.
geometry.Octahedral: whole-sphere and upper-hemisphere octahedral maps (+y pole), for octahedral impostor capture
and packed directions.
geometry.Meshes: area-weighted vertexNormals, weld by bit-identical position, openEdges (closed, consistently
wound 2-manifold check) on indexed triangle meshes.
random.Noise: ridged (ridged multifractal over simplex) and 3D cellular / cellularEdge (Worley F1, F2-F1).
random.PoissonDisc: Bridson's Poisson-disc sampling of a rectangle.
README sections updated. 100% line and branch coverage, deterministic (no java.lang.Math beyond exact methods).
Checklist
Tests cover the change (coverage gate passes)
Hot paths stay allocation-free (the per-call scratch of the mesh and sampling helpers is annotated)
README updated for new or changed API
Depends on a new release of another true-sim repo? No

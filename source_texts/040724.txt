Large terrain databases require a great number of polygons and textures. In consequence, transmission of terrain\ndata over slow networks is still worrying. Multiresolution models allow progressive transmission, that is, the\ntransmission of a simple model followed by successive refinements. In this work we describe a new multiresolution\nmodel called Geometric-Textured Bitree (GTB) that enables progressive transmission of both geometry and\ntextures of a terrain model. Wavelet Multiresolution Analysis is applied in different steps of the process: selection\nof geometric points, texture classification and segmentation. An innovative texture synthesis process based on\nWavelet classification is used in the reconstruction of the texture model.

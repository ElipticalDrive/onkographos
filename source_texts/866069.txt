Add the derived section geometry that viewport and export adapters will share: ordered oriented half-space cuts, closed-contour fill preserving holes/islands, clipped section edges, and original-triangle barycentric provenance for texture/normal interpolation. Plane placement uses the inverse transpose through reflection, shear and nested transforms; authoritative geometry remains untouched.
Successive oblique cuts initially exposed a seam when cap coordinates were rebuilt from a rounded grid. Caps now reuse exact source boundary positions. Open or ambiguous contours retain edges and report absent fill, with explicit input/output/work bounds.
Validation: 113/113 CTest suites including real Blender, the section ASan/UBSan suite with leak detection, 80 deterministic oblique conservation/order samples, analytic solid/cavity/cap checks and transformed/small/large-coordinate fixtures passed. The installed contract and all 109 source-package install inputs match byte-for-byte. See docs/verification/R064a-section-geometry.md.
Stacked on #137. This is the geometry foundation; section persistence, native controls/picking and export integration remain subsequent R064 work. M6 is now complete after #126 passed both exact-head CI runs and merged in dependency order; this layer still requires its own dependency checks.

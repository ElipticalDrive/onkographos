Ray tracing methods are the second way to render three-dimensional graphics, next to rasterization. Algorithms from the ray marching family involve searching for the intersection of a ray of light with objects or elements of a scene. In this section, I present an algorithm for tracing light rays in a simple scene consisting of several objects, with an additional function that allows for interpolation between objects. The solution is based on the use of a distance function to describe the scene and geometry, and a sphere tracing algorithm that enables the rapid finding of ray intersections with scene objects. Even though it utilizes a CPU for rendering, this solution is fast enough to allow for changes to be made, such as positioning the camera in the scene in real time.

Chief Scientist · Spatius
My research focuses on 3D vision and digital humans. I received my Ph.D. from USTC in 2021, advised by Prof. Juyong Zhang, and my bachelor's degree in Statistics from USTC in 2015. From fall 2016 to spring 2017, I was a research assistant in the MultiMedia Lab at Nanyang Technological University, supervised by Prof. Jianfei Cai and Prof. Jianmin Zheng.

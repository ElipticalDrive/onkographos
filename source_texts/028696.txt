To visualize the reconstructed series 
of images in three-dimensional space,   click the “Volume Rendering” button on the 
toolbar of the 3D module. A progress bar will   indicate the rendering progress. Once completed, 
a fourth panel will be added to the MPR viewport,   displaying the 3D Volume Rendering.
Basic operations, such as window leveling,   zooming, and panning, can be performed as 
usual by selecting the appropriate tool   from the toolbar and moving the mouse over a 
panel while holding down the left mouse button.  To change the representation of the displayed 
volume, select one of the presets available in   the drop-down menu located at the 
bottom right corner of the panel.  To rotate the volume, click the “Interaction 
with 3D Model” button on the toolbar. Then,   hold down the left mouse button and move the mouse 
up or down to rotate the volume around the X-axis,   or left and right to rotate it around the 
Y-axis. The “Model Interaction” button is   also accessible from the context menu, which 
can be opened by right-clicking the panel.   Volume rotation can also be performed by pressing 
and dragging the middle mouse button directly.  To rotate the volume around the Z-axis, 
right-click the panel to open the context   menu and select the “Roll” button. Then, 
drag the left mouse button over the volume.  To remove unwanted structures inside a specific 
area, right-click the panel to open the context   menu and select the “Cut Inside” button from 
the “Scissors” submenu. Hold down the left   mouse button and draw the desired area. Once the 
mouse button is released, the system will remove   the portion of the volume inside the drawn area. 
To remove unwanted structures outside a specific   area, select the “Cut Outside” button instead.
To crop the volume right-click the panel to open   the context menu and select the “Cropping Cube” 
button. Then, drag the white spheres with the   left mouse button to resize the cropping cube.
To restore the volume to its original state   and revert any portion removal, click the 
“Restore Volume” button in the context menu.  Click the “Volume Rendering” button again 
to close the volume rendering panel.

Moving a point into the irreducible zone can leave it outside every tetrahedron by round-off: a lattice translation or symmetry operation puts a zone-surface point onto the partner face, whose triangulation differs, or the mesh's own surface vertices are rounded. TetTri::locate then found nothing, and interpolation raised "N of M points not found in tetrahedral mesh" (#121). Interpolating at their own mesh vertices failed this way for 148 of the 581 AFLOW lattices that mesh on main.
When the layered search finds no tetrahedron, the finest layer is now scanned for the tetrahedron whose smallest barycentric weight is largest. If x is outside it by at most 1e-10 in barycentric weight, negative weights become zero and the rest are rescaled, so x is moved onto that tetrahedron; anything further out is still reported. The scan also covers points the layer links miss. It runs only for such points.
All 581 lattices now interpolate at every vertex (constant data returned to 1e-14). test_8's surface-point test now requires success; test_17 checks points on every face of the irreducible zone, moved by symmetry operations and lattice translations, for four lattices; a C++ test checks that points 1e-13 outside a meshed cube are located and points 1e-3 outside are not.

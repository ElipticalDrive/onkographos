Summary
This PR adds a new SegmentEditorAdaptiveBrushNurbs module that converts painted segmentations into volumetric NURBS (Non-Uniform Rational B-Spline) elements for:
Isogeometric Analysis (IGA): Direct use of NURBS geometry in finite element simulations via MFEM
CAD Integration: Export to IGES/STEP for engineering workflows
Mesh Generation: Triangulated surface/volume meshes for traditional FEM
This is a significant feature addition enabling engineering simulation workflows directly from medical image segmentations.

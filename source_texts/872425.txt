Hi authors, thank you for your great work on DropGaussian — it’s an inspiring contribution to understanding how regularization can improve sparse-view 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS).
We would like to initiate a friendly discussion on the mechanism behind why Dropout works in 3DGS.
In your paper, you attribute its effectiveness mainly to enhanced gradient propagation, especially for distant Gaussians.
However, in our concurrent study Quantifying and Alleviating Co-Adaptation in Sparse-View 3D Gaussian Splatting (arXiv:2508.12720), we found that the benefit of dropout may instead stem from breaking inter-Gaussian coupling — i.e., mitigating excessive co-adaptation among neighboring Gaussians during optimization.

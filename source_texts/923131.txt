Issue body actions
Only a material's occlusion texture darkens ambient light (shaders/mesh.frag:114-116), and it cannot see other objects. A creature, rock, tree or wall standing on the ground leaves the ground around its base as bright as open ground, so objects look set on the terrain rather than resting in it, and dense foliage has no occlusion between its own leaves.
The frame has no depth prepass to compute occlusion from: after the shadow regions, one pass draws the sky and then shades opaque surfaces directly (src/desktop/vulkan_renderer.cpp:1549-1596 at f67897a).
Proposed
A depth and normal prepass for opaque and masked surfaces, custom materials included, which also lets the main pass shade each pixel once. Masked foliage would test alpha in the prepass and shade with an equal depth test in the main pass, so its overdraw is paid in the cheap pass (this needs measuring on tile-based GPUs, where hidden surface removal already skips some overdraw).
Ground-truth ambient occlusion (Jimenez et al., "Practical Realtime Strategies for Accurate Indirect Occlusion", 2016) from that depth, at half resolution with a depth-aware upsample, applied to diffuse and specular ambient light only, never to the sun or fill light, and combined with the material's occlusion texture.
Radius, intensity and quality as environment settings, and a way for custom materials to read the result.
Acceptance criteria
A consumer check puts a box on a plane and requires the plane's ambient light to darken toward the contact, its sunlit term to stay unchanged, and occlusion 0 to draw the same frame as today.
The prepass draws the same final image as today with occlusion off.
The frame time cost on a hardware device at 1080p, with and without dense masked foliage, in the pull request.

Had great experiences with "ranch" and "fox" render farms. But,to everyone here,i HIGHLY recommend you spend time with some AI upscale and frame interpolation tools. I used TOPAZ lately for some urgent 4K delivery: i rendered in 2K (3-4 times less render times) upscaled to 4K with great results. And some shots I rendered in 12.5 fps,the AI interpolation works really well on not too fast scenes. And you can also Experiment with lower 3d render samples and use some great denoiser in post. I don't like AI for creation but to help save time and resources with almost/the same result, it's great and getting better.s

I haven't tried cloud rendering once yet, I'm doing it locally, so I have no idea. It also depends on how zoomed out I am and what objects are in the render. HD takes 45 mins- an hour from the same file. I have it as "expanded" as possible so I can edit all of the final touches, before I do a "bounce down" and simplify it as much as possible before I start adding all of the animations in. I just got Insydium's whole bundle, so I'll be adding their water features and plant makers, which should make it look better and be simpler once the objects are "connected", because in my current model, I hand sculpted + placed each branch/tree trunk, but used Ivygen+'s hedge feature to make all of the leaves as a sort of workaround, which essentially functions like a volume cloner, and each tree has 10-50k leaves or something like that, each hedge has approx 5500, 4 of which have over 20k each. I figure Taiao, because the trees are better structured, will use less leaves, and therefore, less information. I also have the grass as a hair object, which is probably the most intensive aspect, but Taiao has grass capabilities that will prob look better than what I have done and will probably take less GPU.

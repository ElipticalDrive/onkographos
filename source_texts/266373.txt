A ray cast onto a triangle mesh now hits it wherever it crosses the surface, along an edge two triangles share and through a vertex several share included. Needed by OrigoZero/zero#21504 (zero issue OrigoZero/zero#21347: raycasts never hit a flat fan-triangulated runtime mesh with an exact MeshCollider).
Two commits, one change each:
7c59695 fix(bvh): a ray that does not travel along an axis meets every box whose slab holds it there. SimdInvRay (the node test of Bvh::cast_ray, which TriMesh::cast_local_ray runs) replaced a zero direction component's inverse by 1 / EPSILON, so a vertical ray was tested as drifting 1.2e-7 sideways per unit of distance and a leaf box whose face lay inside that drift was refused before its triangle was tested. A 16-corner fan whose diagonal rounds to a hair off the z axis lost every vertical ray along it. The node test now uses the exact inverse, marks an axis whose inverse is not finite as parallel, and on that axis tests the origin coordinate against the slab. A ray with no zero component pays one branch per node.
b31c3f6 fix(ray): a ray through an edge or vertex triangles share hits one of them. local_ray_intersection_with_triangle measured barycentrics from each triangle's first vertex, so the two triangles of a shared edge rounded it differently and a ray on it could miss both: 122 of 3166 rays through the shared edge of random triangle pairs did. The inside test is now watertight (Woop, Benthin and Wald, JCGT 2013): edge functions of each edge's two end points alone, in the plane sheared across the ray. Time of impact, normal, face and barycentric order are computed as before; a non-finite ray hits nothing. The heightfield's slack test (_within) is unchanged.
What was run (on the developer's machine; Actions are disabled on this fork)
crates/parry3d/tests/trimesh_ray_shared_edges.rs (new, 5 tests): rays down, up and oblique through every shared edge, vertex and centroid of 16-corner fans (from a rim corner and from the centre, wound both ways, under a pose), axis-parallel rays meeting one triangle whose box ends 2e-7 to 1e-5 past them at 10 and 1000 units, the shared-edge property over random triangle pairs, and the inside hit (toi, normal, face, barycentrics, out-of-range, away, NaN). Passes with default features (SSE Vec3A) and with enhanced-determinism (scalar Vec3A, the engine's build).

local_ray_intersection_with_triangle tested whether the ray lies inside
the triangle through barycentric coordinates measured from the
triangle's first vertex. Two triangles sharing an edge measure that edge
from different vertices and round it differently, so a ray crossing the
edge could land a hair outside both and pass through the mesh. Of 3166
rays through the shared edge of random triangle pairs, 122 hit neither.

The inside test is now watertight (Woop, Benthin and Wald, "Watertight
Ray/Triangle Intersection", JCGT 2013): the vertices are taken relative
to the ray origin and sheared so the ray runs along the axis its
direction is longest on, and each edge is tested through an edge
function of its two end points alone. The triangles on either side of an
edge then compute the same value for it, of opposite sign when wound
alike and of the same sign when not, and zero counts as inside both, so
one of them takes the ray. The time of impact, the normal, the face and
the order of the barycentric coordinates are computed as before; a ray
holding a coordinate that is not finite hits nothing. The heightfield's
slack test keeps its own arithmetic.

Tests: crates/parry3d/tests/trimesh_ray_shared_edges.rs, 3166 rays
through the shared edge of random triangle pairs wound alike and
opposite, and the hit a triangle reports inside its own area.

Needed by OrigoZero/zero#21504.

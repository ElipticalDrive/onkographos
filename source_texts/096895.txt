Recently, ray tracing has gained renewed interest with Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS) technology, a key enabler of 6G wireless communications due to its capability of intelligent manipulation of electromagnetic waves. However, accurately modeling RIS-enabled wireless environments remains challenging due to complex environmental variations and RIS mobility. In this paper, we propose R-NeRF+, a physically consistent and computationally efficient Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) framework for characterizing electromagnetic field dynamics in RIS-enabled environments. Our method utilizes NeRF-based ray tracing to capture complex signal propagation dynamics, effectively modeling complete pathways from transmitter to RIS, and from RIS to receiver. To address computational complexity of dense ray sampling, we propose a novel confidence-guided ray pruning mechanism that identifies and retains only the most informative rays during training. Additionally, geometric priors are incorporated to enhance the confidence estimation for robust ray selection. This two-stage process with selective ray sampling accurately characterizes multiple complex transmission paths while significantly reducing computational overhead. Our approach predicts signal fields for any specified RIS placement and receiver location, facilitating efficient RIS deployment. Extensive experimental evaluations using simulated and real-world data validate our methodology’s effectiveness, demonstrating improved efficiency with guaranteed accuracy compared to traditional dense sampling approaches.

A WGSL hexel primitive in gsplat-core, so a trained Hexels model renders wherever the splat path renders
(wgpu: Vulkan on Linux x86 and the aarch64 GB10, Metal on the M4).
gsplat-core: hexel projection, tile map and raster, with upstream's tile coverage rule and pixel
convention. V1 colour fields; the V2/V3 view terms are evaluated once per hexel. 2D fits use upstream's
top-10 normalized blend. Target::Surface adds expected and median depth and world normals. Hexels share
views, sorting and targets with splats; pinhole cameras only.
gsplat-cli: hexel PLY reader (comment hexels_format 1); render reads Hexels cameras.json /
contract.json, writes 16-bit PNGs (--png16), times views with the paper protocol (--fps: 2 warm-up
passes, 4 trials × 10, median; 2D: 10 warm-up, 5 × 20) and records surface outputs (--surface);
speed --impl ours accepts hexel scenes.
Key numbers
All 102 published checkpoints (34 scenes × V1/V2/V3, 2,532 test views): WGSL-vs-GT PSNR equals the
published CUDA-vs-GT PSNR, max per-view |Δ| 0.0045 dB.
WGSL vs upstream CUDA pixels: 92.7 dB (DTU scan24 V3), 88.6 dB (garden V1).
2D: 45 published fits, max |Δ| 0.0005 dB against the published PSNR; WGSL vs CUDA decodes ≥ 92.4 dB.
Other GPUs, same models vs the RTX 5090 render: GB10 Vulkan (aarch64) 103.5-103.8 dB, Apple M4 Metal
91.0-91.3 dB.
FPS, RTX 5090, published V1, paper protocol: WGSL 716 / 604 / 1029 vs upstream CUDA 1081 / 806 / 1752
(T&T / Mip-NeRF 360 / DTU).
Known: hexel renders are not bit-reproducible run to run (ties in the depth keys): the worst view
differs by 90.4 dB between two runs.

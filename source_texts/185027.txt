Automatic building extraction has been an active area of research for more than last two decades and is important for various real world applications such as urban modelling and disaster management. The complete solution to building extraction from LIDAR (Light Detection And Ranging) point cloud data has two main steps: segmentation of the input data to obtain individual building points and extraction of individual building outlines. This correspondence summarises a recently published arctile1 that concentrates on the second step, which is building outline extraction. In this step, boundary edges that form the boundary are first identified. Each edge is a line that connects two consecutive boundary points. They are then traced to form an irregular building boundary. Finally, the irregular boundary is regularised in order to form a regular outline.No Full Tex

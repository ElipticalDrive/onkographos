Spec: docs/design/viewer-rendering.md Phase E (E.2)
Depends on: #1447
Files: viewer/mesh.js, viewer/test/mesh_greedy_opaque.test.mjs (new)
What
Extend the greedy merge from the water-top ticket to opaque full-cube faces (stone, sand, gravel, dirt and similar): merge neighbouring faces of the same direction and plane with identical layer, shade, tint, AO levels on all four corners and UV variant, up to 15 blocks per side. Grass and leaves vary their tint per column and rarely merge; leave them, cutout parts and plants alone. The per-block UV variant (world-coordinate hash) must keep its effect: only merge faces whose variants match, or exclude varied blocks, and say which in the PR.
Done when
Merged output covers exactly the same faces, with equal layer, shade, tint and AO, as the unmerged mesh (test on a synthetic terrain).
AO diagonal flipping still holds on merged quads or those quads are not merged.
Overlapping-region determinism: meshing two overlapping areas gives the same covered faces for shared blocks.
Face counts before and after on seed 12345 are in the PR.
CLAUDE.md "Before opening a PR" checks pass.
World output
Unchanged (mesh only).
Out of scope
Water tops (previous ticket); E.1 allocation-free rewrite.

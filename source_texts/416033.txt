Why. Client-side volume rendering of very large cubes needs bricks at several resolutions, as 2-D images already have tiles.
What exists today. 2-D images use a tile pyramid; very large cubes are rendered server-side and streamed.
Scope
A brick pyramid for cubes, built out of core.
View-dependent loading of bricks in the 3-D viewer.
Done when
A cube larger than GPU memory can be explored client-side at interactive rates.
Notes. Low priority while server-side rendering covers this case.

Abstract
Real-time shadow plays a very important role in strengthening the sense of reality of 3d scene. Shadow Volume algorithm is a very effective ideal method in real-time shadows drawing. However, with the increasing complexity of scene, the computation of this algorithm is large, which will lead to lower the efficiency of drawing. On this basis, it adopts the advanced shading language HLSL Microsoft in this paper, uses the computing power and programmability of GPU, will create the substantial computation of shadow body transferred from the CPU to the GPU, thus will effectively improve the efficiency of real-time rendering shadow.

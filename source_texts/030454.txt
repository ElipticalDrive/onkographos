SeismicDataCompressor (SDC): seismic volume compression with a reproducible benchmark

Description
First citable release of SeismicDataCompressor (SDC).
Highlights
Closed-loop DPCM for the 2D and 3D codecs (fixes the two defects found by the 2026-10-12 benchmark); self-describing payloads with the bit depth stored.
Neural hybrid codec (sdc-ai): a 32.7k-parameter convolutional autoencoder trained with TensorFlow on synthetic data and executed in pure Java; int8 latents + uniformly quantized residual with a bounded maximum error.
Reproducible benchmark with a rate–distortion sweep, external baselines (ZFP, SZ3), BD-rate and an out-of-distribution check; results committed under benchmark/results/.
Measured on the synthetic 128 × 128 × 1000 volume (BD-rate, bits/sample at equal SNR): neural hybrid −5.7% vs SDC 3D and −12.2% vs the same quantizer without the network; SZ3 −16.3% vs SDC 3D. The neural codec is 25–80× slower to compress and 175–240× slower to decompress than SDC 3D, and its advantage shrinks on an out-of-distribution volume. All results are on synthetic data; see benchmark/README.md for methodology and limitations.

Is there a way to set the camera "up" vector with 1.5RC ?
For the camera rotation Potree is now using pitch and yaw angles and assume the up vector is fixed.
Unfortunately, no. The up-vector is fixed to 0 / 0 / 1.
I believe linear algebra is the way to go, why not coupled with methods like "get" and "addTo" for pitch, > yaw and roll - to simplify patching the controls and coding new ones.
In 1.5RC:
viewer.scene.view.yaw = ...
viewer.scene.view.yaw = viewer.scene.view.yaw + 0.1
viewer.scene.view.pitch = ...
I had no success yet playing with "roll" but I dont like using trigonometry for rotations.
Roll isn't supported at the moment because yaw and pitch are usually all you need. What do you want to do with roll?

Yeah definitely, its a problem child for sure.
I was messing around with a couple ways to make this a little more reasonable, one approach is doing a per- triangle thing on the surface only, and treating the 'densification' as a different op. I added a subdivide_to_size method, which subdivides triangles until every edge is shorter than a specified length, which you can use for (better than the ray method anyway) voxelization:
```
    pitch=0.5
    max_edge = pitch / 2.0

    # get the same mesh sudivided so every edge is shorter than half our pitch              
    v,f = trimesh.remesh.subdivide_to_size(m.vertices, m.faces, max_edge)

    # convert the vertices to their voxel grid position                                     
    hit = (v / pitch)
    # get a conservative-ish wrapping                                                       
    hit = np.vstack((np.ceil(hit), np.floor(hit))).astype(int)

    # remove duplicates                                                                     
    u,i = trimesh.grouping.unique_rows(hit)

    # get the voxel centers in model space                                                  
    occupied = hit[u]*pitch

    m.visual.face_colors = [255,0,0,100]

    vox = trimesh.voxel.multibox(occupied, pitch)

    (vox+m).show()
```

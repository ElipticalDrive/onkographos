In autonomous driving and robotic perception, reflective surfaces such as glass, vehicles, and wet roads introduce strong specular effects that pose challenges for neural rendering. Conventional NeRF and Gaussian Splatting methods, tailored to diffuse scenes, often produce blurred highlights, distorted geometry, and unstable view-dependent appearance. This paper presents a generalizable approach to specular scene reconstruction that integrates anisotropic 3D Gaussian filtering for stabilized cost volumes, an Anisotropic Spherical Gaussians (ASG) [1]-enhanced branch for compact view-dependent reflection modeling, and a geometry-aware adaptive fusion strategy to balance diffuse and specular rendering. Extensive experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves sharper highlights, more faithful reflections, real-time efficiency, and strong cross-scene generalization.

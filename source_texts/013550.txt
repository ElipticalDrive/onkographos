Summary
Add camera-following SDFGI and HDDAGI alongside automatic RT-based DDGI, with shared probe coverage and explicit provider selection under Environmental Lighting → GI. SDFGI remains the default. The branch also includes GPU-batched Universe rendering and navigation improvements.
Implement core HDDAGI from Godot da1410fa3516d08cc31b6e86bd6673b9ce776316: hierarchical voxel traversal, local region updates, direct lighting, cached probe transport, rolling history, occlusion, diffuse/specular camera gather and optional reflection filtering. No RT/query/acceleration-structure dependency. Preserve SDFGI contributor eligibility and material-free opaque geometry passes.
Integrate HDDAGI diffuse illumination into baked, dynamic and immediate reflection captures. Retain a consistent generation across faces; exclude recursive specular feedback. Add safe Environment fallback, transactional settings/allocation, frame-retained resources, diagnostics and editor/Python controls.
Implement SDFGI from Godot 34d06658a85845111a50db9e485ec4a0701d4298, including distance fields, scrolling, occlusion, sharp reflections, capture integration and optional surface-clearance relocation. Document necessary adaptations and retain MIT attribution for both ports.
Unify automatic GI coverage at shared defaults of 33×17×33 probes, four cascades, 0.8 base distance and 100% Y scale. Replace authored DDGI volumes and hysteresis/convergence stopping with automatic cascades and periodic integer rolling history. Keep DDGI classification/relocation and 64 uniform + 8 emissive rays; fix atlas packing at 16 columns and 8×8 tile interiors.
Remove Vulkan external interoperability/export chains and use ordinary allocations with VMA 3.4.0. Unify first-party GENERAL image access while preserving barriers, initialize DDGI atlases before publication, and fix geometry readback transfer-source usage.
Include GPU-batched Universe stars, picking/hover, star-local navigation and single-cluster galaxy refinements already on this branch.
Validation
HDDAGI integration: 80 focused tests passed. A fresh two-test reflection-capture rerun also passed with Vulkan core/synchronization validation enabled.
Broad run: 813/821 initially passed. Fixed the SDK shader-inventory mode selection and its rerun passed: 143 entrypoints, 160 variants, zero compile failures. Seven failures remain in unchanged source assertions, EcoSysLab ABI/inventory expectations, rendering documentation budgets and external asset YAML conversion. Exact names and evidence are in docs/hddagi-results.md; the full suite is not green.
SPIR-V: all 22 HDDAGI variants passed; 159/160 SDK variants passed. The unchanged unused legacy fragment SSRCombine has two push-constant blocks; the active compute SSRCombine passes.
Existing goldens passed without accepting replacements during HDDAGI work: all four DDGI raster variants and the SDFGI image. SDFGI reached 56.6283 dB PSNR / 0.999498 SSIM. Earlier SDFGI/DDGI milestones include the separately approved baseline updates already in this PR.
Installed RT-disabled 2560×1440 HDDAGI capture passed with Khronos core/synchronization validation explicitly active and no disabled checks or findings. Compact fixtures cover thin walls, corners, masks, emission, curved rough/sharp surfaces and receiver-only movement.
Recorded 72 benchmark runs / 21,600 measured frames and 48 edit-response captures across default/full HDDAGI, SDFGI and RT-enabled DDGI. Benchmark validation is OFF; correctness tests and the separate installed validation capture use it ON. Settings, timings, memory, provenance and reproduction are in docs/hddagi-results.md and docs/hddagi-results.json.
Pinned clang-format 22.1.8 and whitespace checks passed. Editor/tests/Python built; all enabled applications installed successfully using python Scripts/install_apps.py --preset vs2026-x64 --config RelWithDebInfo --incremental --no-open --jobs 8.
Results and review boundaries
On RTX 5070 at 1440p, stationary GPU spans were 19.15 ms for half-resolution HDDAGI, 19.83 ms for full-resolution HDDAGI, 19.66 ms for SDFGI and 23.54 ms for DDGI. Full HDDAGI geometry/material edits cost more than SDFGI; whole-application VMA peaks were 6.813/6.859/6.597/7.014 GiB respectively. Sampling quality differs, and one-device sequential measurements do not establish a universal ranking.
HDDAGI remains opt-in. Sharp reflections retain voxel blockiness, and conservative broad-edit invalidation visibly resets indirect illumination before recovery. Sponza's yellow illumination and reflective-sphere bands also appear with SDFGI; local probes/SSR participate in those images. No Godot image parity, ground-truth quality improvement, multi-vendor acceptance or deforming/skinned contribution support is claimed. Camera-visibility scheduling remains disabled. SDFGI relocation retains narrow residual seams and unsigned-field visibility limitations.
Keep this PR in draft for review of appearance and the documented exceptions. Earlier provider and Universe evidence remains in their documentation. Local trackers, raw diagnostic captures/logs, editor state and unrelated dirty resource/vendor trees are excluded.

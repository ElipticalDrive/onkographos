Reference Material
OpenGL.org, a good compendium of documentation and links for OpenGL. Also see Steve Baker's notes.
gpgpu.org, a research news site for new and interesting uses of graphics hardware. Also see shadertech.
U. Wisconsin Graphics Qual, an excellent collection of good graphics papers.
OpenGL utilities:
GLUT or FreeGLUT, for cross-platform OpenGL startup and basic interface utility routines.
GLEW, for managing OpenGL extensions like ARB_FRAGMENT_PROGRAM.
Apple's MacOS X OpenGL Profiler, for determining OpenGL performance bottlenecks.

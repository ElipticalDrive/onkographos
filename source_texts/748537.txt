For those scenes reconstructed using Colmap poses, units of depth and poses are meaningless. You can refer to this issue in Nerfstudio's repo. If you have ground truth depth maps in real-world coordinates (like meter in unit), you might calculate a scale transition by performing least square between rendered depth maps and g.t. depth maps.

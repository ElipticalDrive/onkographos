Part of #403 (phase 4s: Voxel worlds).
Priority P1 · type feature · size L · layer base
What
There is no voxel support anywhere in the engine. Add a voxel_volume component and record (chunk size, block palette with colours or materials, generator), chunked storage with run-length or palette compression, greedy or culled-face meshing per chunk on a budget, per-face ambient occlusion and colour, and a render path through the existing mesh pass (one material per palette, frustum culling per chunk). Done = a 512x512x64 volume renders at 60 fps in the Sandbox, a headless test meshes a chunk to a known face count, and a chunk mesh allocates nothing once pooled.
Evidence
grep for voxel in src/ and docs/design finds nothing; Ace of Spades (Voxlap) is a named target (owner, 2026-10-02).
Added 2026-10-02 after the owner named Morrowind, Lugaru and Ace of Spades (Voxlap) as rendering targets.

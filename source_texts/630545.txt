Summary
Implements Stage 2 Parallel POC Worker 6 in isolated scope (src/vfx/, tests/vfx.spec.ts):
Raymarched SDF Glass Shatter & Whisk-Broom Reassembly (src/vfx/shatter-sim.ts):
Simulates 8–12 ballistic glass shards (createShatterSimulator) bursting and bouncing with restitution, friction, and spin across the CPU SDF floor for 1.2s when the marble enters 'shattered', then smoothly sweeping and converging them back to the checkpoint respawnPos via a virtual 1984 Marble Madness whisk-broom arc (0.55s).
Exports getShatterShaderSnippet() (GLSL ES 3.00 & WGSL) and uShatterShards uniform packing (Float32Array(48) / vec4[12]) for refractive faceted glass fragment rendering.
Isometric Grid Shockwave & Rim-Danger Pulse (src/vfx/shockwave-ring.ts):
Manages up to 4 concurrent expanding SDF surface shockwave rings (hard_landing, bumper_hit, portal_exit, crater_blast) that illuminate the anti-aliased fwidth isometric contour gridlines with neon cyan/magenta wavefronts (getShockwaveShaderSnippet()).
Evaluates continuous 8-probe SDF cliff-edge drop proximity (evaluateRimDanger) to drive rim-danger pulsing and rim-save feedback.
Classic Marble Madness Exotic SDF Hazards POC (src/vfx/exotic-hazards.ts):
Suction Vortex / Vacuum Funnel (sdVortexFunnel & evaluateVortexDynamics): Smooth logarithmic funnel SDF + centripetal suction that pulls slow marbles inward while allowing high-speed tangential rim-orbiting (tangentialSpeed >= rimOrbitSpeedThreshold) to sling out the far side.
Marble Muncher (sdMarbleMuncher, evaluateMarbleMuncherPose, evaluateMuncherInteraction): Animated CSG chomping sphere-mouth hazard (opSmoothSubtract jaw wedge + brow ridges) that lunges and hops along a patrol path, supporting both chomp hazards and top-down squash detection.
Exports lockstep GLSL ES 3.00 & WGSL SDF evaluators (getExoticHazardsShaderSnippet()).
Unit Tests (tests/vfx.spec.ts):
Verifies shard ballistics, whisk-broom convergence, 4-ring shockwave expansion/decay, rim-danger cliff detection, and vortex/muncher SDF distances & dynamics.

Nowadays,the continuous increases in the size of datasets and the complexity of visualization operation lead to rapid development of distributed visualization based on Grid or web services.In the field of volume rendering,volume data with a large-scale growth, urgently look to improve the processing power of computing devices.The rapid development of distributed technology based on web services provides a new way research for the research of volume rendering.In order to facilitate the application of Web services technology in volume rendering,this paper presents a Web services-based distributed parallel volume rendering model.In the process of volume rendering,web services can be easily called to visualize in this model.

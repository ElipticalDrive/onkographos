Goal: reproduce the photographic defocus effect where objects at the focal plane are sharp and objects in the foreground/background are blurred (bokeh).
Standard raytracing approach: instead of shooting all rays from a single camera origin, jitter the origin within a lens aperture disk. For each pixel, multiple rays are cast from different points on the lens, all passing through the same focal point in the scene. The spread of ray origins on the lens creates the circle of confusion — points away from the focal plane project to different locations on the image plane, producing blur.
Implementation sketch:
Add lens aperture radius and focal distance parameters to the camera
In the ray generation loop, sample the lens disk and compute the new ray origin/orientation so all samples converge at the focal plane
The existing multi-sampling infrastructure (blend_samples) naturally accumulates the defocus
Works with the existing demo_sdl2 and demo_curses renderers
Related technique: thin lens approximation is the simplest starting point — no need for full lens element tracing.

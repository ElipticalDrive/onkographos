# Triplanar for Nuke

Triplanar texture mapping tool for The Foundry's Nuke. Project textures onto 3D geometry without requiring UV coordinates.

![Nuke](https://img.shields.io/badge/Nuke-Compatible-yellow)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-green)

## Overview

Triplanar mapping is a projection technique that maps textures onto 3D surfaces by projecting from three orthogonal axes (X, Y, Z) and blending them based on surface normals. This approach eliminates the need for traditional UV unwrapping, making it ideal for complex organic surfaces, terrain, procedural geometry, or any situation where creating clean UVs would be impractical.

## Features

- **UV-free texturing** — Apply seamless textures to geometry using world-space position and normals
- **Normal-based blending** — Smooth transitions between projections based on surface orientation
- **Production-ready** — Designed for use in professional VFX pipelines

## Installation

1. Download the `.nk` file(s) from this repository
2. Place them in your Nuke ToolSets directory, or import directly into your script

**ToolSets location:**
- **Linux:** `~/.nuke/ToolSets/`
- **macOS:** `~/.nuke/ToolSets/`
- **Windows:** `C:\Users\<username>\.nuke\ToolSets\`

Alternatively, copy the Group node directly into your Nuke script.

## Usage

1. Connect your **texture** to the appropriate input
2. Connect your **position pass** (P or world position AOV) 
3. Connect your **normal pass** (N or world normal AOV)
4. Adjust scale and blending parameters to suit your scene

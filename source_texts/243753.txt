Serializing entire terrain overwhelms localStorage quota
Every call to persistWorld() stringifies all blocks in every chunk, including the entire procedurally generated terrain. With a 5×5 chunk radius and up to 90 000 blocks, the resulting JSON is several megabytes; in common browsers this exceeds the ~5 MB localStorage limit, so saveWorld() will regularly throw QuotaExceededError and no changes are saved. Persist only user modifications or compress the world data before writing to localStorage.

Building one THREE.Mesh per block makes the world unplayable
The chunk rebuild logic creates a separate THREE.Mesh for every block without merging or instancing. The initial 5×5 chunk world therefore instantiates tens of thousands of meshes and draw calls before any interaction, which will tank frame rate or even hang the tab on most machines. Rebuild each chunk as a single merged geometry or an instanced mesh and cull hidden faces before adding to the scene.

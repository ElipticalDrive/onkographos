In this project, methods of procedural 3D terrain generation are researched and discussed, and a piece of software for generating and exporting 3D terrain geometry is developed. The research covers the representation of geometry in 3D computer graphics and analyses the heightmap and voxel methods of generating terrain geometry. \nThe heightmap method of generation was chosen, and further research was conducted on the algorithms used for generating terrain using this method. The research ends with an analysis of existing examples of products that use procedural terrain generation techniques. The development of the software is documented, starting with the analysis of the research and the project’s aims and objectives to produce and justify a specification of requirements. The software is designed against the specification, and is implemented using techniques discussed in the research section. The software is tested and evaluated against the specified requirements, with the conclusion that the software is stable and fulfils the previously specified functional and non-functional requirements. \nIn conclusion, the entire project is evaluated against the project’s aims and objectives and recommendations are made for the continuation of research for this project, and for additional features to add to the software.

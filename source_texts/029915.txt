Moire tomography programmed by ourselves was applied to reconstructing axis-symmetry fields in order to test the program's validity.Moire deflection tomography was programmed based on optical principle of Moire deflection and mathematical,physical significance of tomography.The program was used to simulate reconstructing axis-symmetry fields with Algebraic Reconstruction Technique(ART) and with Simple Self-correlative Algebraic Reconstruction Technique(SSART),which was programmed by ourselves.As a result,the Molte tomography program could reconstruct the positive and negative axis-symmetry fields accurately with SSART.The reconstructed fields with SSART improved much than that reconstructed with ART.

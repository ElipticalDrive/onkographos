To address the limitations of Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) in rendering high-frequency details, this paper proposes an optimization framework integrating hybrid attention and Weighted Feature Distillation (NeRF-WFD). The framework enhances the detail representation through the Weighted Feature Distillation (WFD) mechanism, which uses uncertainty prediction to guide the knowledge transfer dynamically between NeRF’s coarse and fine networks. The framework also optimizes the feature fusion within the network by combining with the Adaptive Feature Fusion (AFF) module and introduces the Direction-Aware Caching (DAC) technique to alleviate the computational overhead. Experiments on the public Blender and LLFF datasets demonstrate that NeRF-WFD outperforms both original NeRF and pure-attention schemes in key image quality metrics, including Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR), Structural Similarity Index (SSIM), and Learned Perceptual Image Patch Similarity (LPIPS), and notably improves LPIPS and the rendering speed compared with the pure-attention scheme. The results confirm the effectiveness of the method in improving the neural rendering fidelity and balancing efficiency.

Web Worker builds a symmetric 3D density histogram (128^3 / 192^3):
n-fold rotations (+ mirror when omega=0), trilinear splat, log1p,
gaussian blur, uploaded as an R16F 3D texture with linear filtering
Isosurface mode: ray-box clip, raymarch + bisection, gradient normals,
glass (Schlick, refraction, Beer-Lambert absorption) and mercury,
procedural studio environment; threshold is a quantile of occupied voxels
Ink mode: front-to-back single scattering with shadow rays and
Henyey-Greenstein phase, jittered ray starts
Low-res preview while moving, progressive jittered accumulation at rest;
falls back to the point mode when 3D float textures are unavailable

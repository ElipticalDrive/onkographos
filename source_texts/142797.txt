Tomographic reconstruction : r/matlab - Reddit
Here's a short demo-script of inverse-radon reconstruction using the "Projection Slice" approach discovered by R. Bracewell. It is written from scratch using the inbuilt Penny image. The general principle is that each of your photos corresponds to a slice through the spatial spectrum of the image.

Voxel coloring is a scene reconstruction technique that models scene color and texture information differently from other scene reconstruction techniques. Voxel coloring is able to account for large visibility changes and occlusions. The voxels (volume elements) of a discretized scene space are examined in a fixed visibility ordering, so as to avoid problems with image correspondence. This technique does not require that input cameras be close together and densely distributed in the environment. In this project, the voxel coloring algorithm of Seitz and Dyer [3], along with a viewer for the voxel data, was implemented and experiments were performed on real scene, camera-captured images.

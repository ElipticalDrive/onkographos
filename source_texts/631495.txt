Outcome
Objects hidden behind nearer geometry are rejected on the GPU using a depth pyramid.
Acceptance criteria
A depth pyramid is built from the previous frame's depth buffer
Culling is conservative: nothing visible is ever rejected
Hysteresis prevents flickering at the visibility boundary
Fast camera movement has a defined fallback
False positive and negative rates are reported in the diagnostics HUD
Notes
Occlusion culling that occasionally rejects visible geometry produces flickering that is far worse than the frames it saves. Conservative bounds and hysteresis are not optional refinements.

Two bugs fixed:
Pathological octree expansion for flat/planar meshes: In buildNode(), triangles lying exactly on a split plane satisfy both isInZLow and isInZHigh (and similarly for X/Y), causing them to be placed in both child bins. When the bounding box has zero extent in a dimension (e.g. flat mesh at z=0), every triangle is duplicated at every recursion level, producing exponential node/triangle growth (690K+ nodes for 8 triangles). This memory explosion could cause heap corruption or OOM, manifesting as transparent patches in rendered images.
Fix: After computing child bins, if the largest child bin is as large as the parent triangle count, splitting is making no progress. Store the node as a leaf immediately to prevent unbounded recursion.
Missing mesh data in unit test helper: fillTriangleMeshIntersection() accesses faces.material[] and indices.texcoord[] unconditionally. The makeFlatGrid() test helper and BoundarySpanningTriangles test mesh were not populating these arrays, causing out-of-bounds accesses and segfaults.
Fix: Populate indices.texcoord (with NoTexCoord) and faces.material (with NoMaterial) in all test mesh construction code.
Also adds a new test suite (raytrianglemeshoctree_intersect) that validates octree intersection correctness against brute-force for vertical rays, diagonal rays, and boundary-spanning triangles.

…ctness tests

Two bugs fixed:

1. Pathological octree expansion for flat/planar meshes:
   In buildNode(), triangles lying exactly on a split plane satisfy both
   isInZLow and isInZHigh (and similarly for X/Y), causing them to be
   placed in both child bins. When the bounding box has zero extent in a
   dimension (e.g. flat mesh at z=0), every triangle is duplicated at
   every recursion level, producing exponential node/triangle growth
   (690K+ nodes for 8 triangles). This memory explosion could cause heap
   corruption or OOM, manifesting as transparent patches in rendered images.

   Fix: After computing child bins, if the largest child bin is as large
   as the parent triangle count, splitting is making no progress. Store
   the node as a leaf immediately to prevent unbounded recursion.

2. Missing mesh data in unit test helper:
   fillTriangleMeshIntersection() accesses faces.material[] and
   indices.texcoord[] unconditionally. The makeFlatGrid() test helper
   and BoundarySpanningTriangles test mesh were not populating these
   arrays, causing out-of-bounds accesses and segfaults.

   Fix: Populate indices.texcoord (with NoTexCoord) and faces.material
   (with NoMaterial) in all test mesh construction code.

Also adds a new test suite (raytrianglemeshoctree_intersect) that
validates octree intersection correctness against brute-force for
vertical rays, diagonal rays, and boundary-spanning triangles.

I have some experience with drawing polygons, shaded surfaces, the use of geometry shaders, etc. I am trying to create a volumetric cloud/volume render shader like this, with techniques such as 'raymarching'. Did you read the Shadertoy documentation to find the answer to your question? It explicitly states that the mainImage entrypoint is called per pixel — ie. it's a pixel shader (AKA fragment shader). What have you tried so far in your quest to render a shader in your program, and where specifically are you stuck? Rendering a quad with a custom shader is something that just about every OpenGL tutorial covers, so it seems like you should find answers to most of your questions out there already in existing documents, without waiting for new answers here.

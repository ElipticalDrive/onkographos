Creating Planes Normal to View Orientation
You can create a reference plane parallel to the screen by setting an offset from a reference vertex.
Open install_dir\samples\whatsnew\parts\shoe.sldprt.
Click Plane (Reference Geometry toolbar) or Insert > Reference Geometry > Plane.
In the graphics area, select the vertex shown for First Reference .
In the PropertyManager, under First Reference:
Click Parallel to screen .
Set Distance to 0.
Click .
Rotate the model to see the plane created normal to the screen.
In the FeatureManager design tree, right-click Plane1 and click Edit Feature .
In the PropertyManager, under First Reference, click Update Plane to reorient the plane parallel to the screen.

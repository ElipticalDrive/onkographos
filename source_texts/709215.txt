PLY files produced by 3D Gaussian Splatting training (vertex properties f_dc_*, f_rest_*, opacity, scale_*, rot_*) currently open as gray points because the parser only reads position, RGB color, and normals.
Render these files as depth-sorted anisotropic Gaussian splats, using the base (degree-0) spherical-harmonics color.
Out of scope for this issue: higher-order SH, compressed splat formats (.splat, .ksplat, compressed.ply, .spz).

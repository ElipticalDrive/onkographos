evalSceneJS is meant to mirror the WGSL interpreter exactly, but the WGSL version clamps primitive pushes to MAX_STACK (32) and silently ignores pushes when full. The JS version always increments sp and writes into a fixed Float64Array(64), which can diverge from shader behavior (and can also overflow the typed array, producing incorrect results/NaNs in the baker). Consider matching WGSL by defining MAX_STACK = 32 here and only pushing when sp < MAX_STACK (and keeping CSG ops guarded the same way).

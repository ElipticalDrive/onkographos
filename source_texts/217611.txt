What this adds
Load real scenes
glTF 2.0 / GLB parser written from scratch (no dependencies, runs under Node for tests): node hierarchies, indexed/strip/fan primitives, interleaved, normalized and sparse accessors, mirrored transforms.
PBR textures (base colour + alpha mask/blend, metal-rough, normal, emissive), KHR_texture_transform, and the transmission, ior, volume, clearcoat, sheen, emissive_strength, unlit extensions. Files requiring Draco, meshopt or KTX2 are refused with a clear message.
Drag-and-drop of a .glb, a .gltf with its files, a folder, or an .hdr; plus an Open file… picker.
Gallery of Khronos sample models paired with Poly Haven CC0 HDRIs, streamed on demand (both hosts send CORS headers), plus the built-in scenes.
Rendering
Radiance .hdr decoder and alias-table environment importance sampling with MIS; the procedural sky is baked into the same path.
Layered BSDF: GGX clearcoat over Charlie sheen (energy-compensated) over the GGX/Lambert base, one mixture pdf; IOR-driven F0; thin-walled and Beer–Lambert volumetric transmission; alpha any-hit in traversal.
Scale-independent ray offsets (Wächter & Binder 2019).
Interaction
SVGF-style à-trous denoiser on demodulated illumination with MRT albedo/normal/depth guides; its reach shrinks with spp and it fades out by 1024 spp.
Temporal reprojection while the camera moves, gated by depth/normal agreement and by surface glossiness so mirrors and glass don't smear.
Resolution drops only when frames exceed 40 ms (never below 50%, Catmull-Rom upsampling); auto samples per frame when still.
HUD with measured spp, spp/s and Mpaths/s.
Speed
BVH builder rewritten on typed arrays with an explicit stack; big models build on a Web Worker. Triangle data split into traversal (3 texels) and shading (4 texels) textures.

Sharper motion: temporal reprojection, gentler preview scaling
…
87c967f
```
- Each sample reprojects its primary hit into the previous view and reuses
  that pixel's history when depth and normal agree, so orbiting refines the
  image instead of restarting at 1 spp. History is capped per pixel by how
  view-dependent the surface is: mirrors and glass reuse none.
- Per-pixel sample counts (4th accumulation target) drive the running mean
  and the denoiser's variance estimate; the RNG seed never repeats.
- Preview resolution starts at full, only drops when frames exceed 40 ms,
  never below 50%, ignores throttled frames, and upsamples with Catmull-Rom.
- Denoiser fades out by 1024 spp; HUD moved clear of the toast.
```

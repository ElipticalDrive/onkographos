SQ-CBF: Signed Distance Functions for Numerically Stable...
May 20, 2026This work presents a geometry-aware safety filter that models robot and environment geometries using expressive superquadrics (SQs). Our approach yields a numerically stable, real-time-capable CBF safety filter, SQ-CBF, by leveraging signed-distance functions and deriving a closed-form gradient expression.

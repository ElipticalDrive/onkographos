Summary
Removes the two known approximations from #1.
Confiner3D — ConvexPolygonShape3D is no longer treated as its AABB. The hull is built once per shape by an incremental (tetrahedron-seeded, horizon-edge) convex hull with scale-relative tolerances (_hull_epsilon = extent * 1e-5); hashed dedupe + AABB extent keep preprocessing O(n). ConcavePolygonShape3D is supported as a closed triangle mesh: closure is validated by edge incidence (every nondegenerate edge shared by exactly two triangles), inside test is a majority-vote parity ray cast, projection goes to the closest point on the surface with exact small-triangle handling. Both shape kinds query a flat-array BVH over the cached faces (16.6k-tri mesh: inside 3.85 ms → 42 µs, outside projection 12.4 ms → 68 µs per call; tests/bench/bench_confiner.gd). Degenerate inputs (open meshes, coplanar/too-thin point sets) warn once per shape and disable confinement rather than confining to a surface. Caches invalidate on the shape's changed signal. Capsule caps are spherical; slowing_distance bypasses damping when in range.
Storyboard — all three RenderModes are real and a storyboard keeps one OutputView per brain its camera is live in (CameramanCore.find_brains_for):
SCREEN_SPACE_OVERLAY → one shared CanvasLayer 100.
SCREEN_SPACE_CAMERA → one CanvasLayer 1 per output viewport (custom_viewport), incl. CameramanBrain2D.
WORLD_SPACE → one unshaded alpha QuadMesh per brain, reparented into that brain's output viewport so it renders in its World3D, placed world_distance (clamped to near/far) in front of the output camera, sized to the frustum at that distance (h = 2·d·tan(fov/2), ortho size, PROJECTION_FRUSTUM offset honoured). CameramanBrain.storyboard_render_layers overrides world_render_layers per brain.
aspect/center/rotation/scale/alpha/image/split_view apply every frame; render_mode changes rebuild nodes; the storyboard's process_priority tracks max brain priority + 1 (brain priority is set in _init). Views survive their host viewport being freed first and are recreated/freed without leaking.
Liveness: brains register with CameramanCore.brains; liveness resolves through manager chains and is cleared on brain _exit_tree.
Tests: 113 GUT tests / 301 assertions.

While 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) revolutionizes real-time rendering, its widespread adoption is hindered by computationally expensive per-scene optimization. This paper presents a streamlined, web-based platform leveraging MVSplat’s feed-forward architecture for rapid 3D scene reconstruction from uncalibrated consumer video. Our automated pipeline extracts frames, estimates camera poses via COLMAP, and processes data through a containerized MVSplat backend. The resulting 3D parameters are served to a lightweight WebGL browser client for real-time visualization. Experiments demonstrate high visual fidelity (SSIM > 0.84) and near-instantaneous neural inference (~20 seconds), successfully decoupling generation time from scene complexity. Despite classical Structure-from-Motion (SfM) scaling bottlenecks, this end-to-end system generates navigable 3D scenes in minutes, bridging the gap between high-performance spatial computing research and hardware-agnostic digital creation.

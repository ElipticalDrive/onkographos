Current status (reviewed 2026-10-09): Still open. The previously listed view-depth, double-fog, and inscattering-scale fixes remain in place. The remaining volumetric shimmer, slice banding, and block-shaped shafts are not yet accepted against the image/stability/cost gates below; the scene follow-ups are separate from those renderer gates.
Summary
Vortex volumetric fog is functional but not production quality. In
CityEnvironmentValidation (foggy-morning city) it shows frame-to-frame
shimmer/flicker and horizontal banding on large horizontal surfaces, and
sun shafts through fog are block-shaped. Analytic height fog alone renders
cleanly and stably, so the scene currently ships with volumetric fog disabled.
Fixed already (context)
78eacd280 Composite looked up the froxel volume by radial distance instead
of view depth, sampling cells behind the surface (black ground, flicker).
7c108b7ec Height fog and volumetric fog were both applied inside the
volumetric range (double fog); height fog is now excluded there, as in UE.
c7d8f37d7 In-scattered light was multiplied by 2e-5 before the
pre-exposed store, so fog only absorbed. Grid raised from 32 to 64 depth
slices (UE default).
Root causes still open
No temporal anti-aliasing. The froxel pass follows UE: Halton jitter per
frame plus a 0.9 history weight. UE relies on TAA/TSR to remove the residual
jitter noise; Vortex has no TAA (CompositingTaskType::kTaa is a
placeholder), so the noise is visible as shimmer on high-contrast edges.
Slice banding. The composite samples the integrated volume trilinearly
at the receiver's view depth. Slice boundaries show as horizontal bands on
ground seen at an angle. Engines without TAA dither the lookup depth per
pixel (blue noise) to break the bands up.
Block-shaped shadow shafts. Directional shadow visibility is evaluated
once per jittered froxel sample; at kilometre distances cells are tens of
metres deep, and the shafts take the cell shape.
Required work
Per-pixel blue-noise dither of the volume lookup depth (and optionally
XY) in Fog.hlsl, sized to one slice.
Temporal stability that does not depend on screen TAA: froxel-space
history with neighbourhood clamping and a convergence-based weight, or a
deterministic (non-jittered) mode with spatial supersampling; pick by
measured cost.
Revisit grid resolution and depth distribution for kilometre distances
(64 slices over 1.2 km is still coarse); consider a separate near and far
distribution.
Optional: TAA in the composition path, which would also benefit the
volumetric pass.
Re-enable volumetric fog in CityEnvironmentValidation once the
criteria below pass.
Acceptance
Static camera: consecutive frames differ by at most 2/255 in the tonemapped
output wherever the camera sees volumetric fog (measured from RenderDoc
captures of frames N and N+1).
No visible slice banding on a ground plane under fog at 0.2-2 km
(image-based check on a regression scene).
Shadow shafts follow occluder silhouettes without cell-shaped stair steps at
the default grid resolution.
GPU cost of the volumetric pass recorded before and after.
Related scene follow-ups (CityEnvironmentValidation)
The authored camera (south-bank promenade looking north across the river)
is not applied by the RenderScene camera rig; captures start elsewhere, so
the river is not in the default view.
Water is a dark glossy material that only reads as water with reflections;
Vortex has no screen-space or planar reflections, so the river renders as a
dark strip. Give it a deliberate appearance until reflections exist.

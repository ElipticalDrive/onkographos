This poster presents an improvement of the algorithm suggested in the paper [SA08] in which the Transform Feedback functionality was used to sort line segments in real-time, both for the purpose of alpha blending the hair-segments (rendering back-to-front) and in order to quickly build an opacity-map that could be queried for self shadowing. The line segments are sorted approximately into slices and are then rendered into the corresponding slices of a 3D-texture. Here, we utilize a very fast parallel stream-split operation introduced in [BOA09], to quickly radix sort the line-primitives in CUDA.

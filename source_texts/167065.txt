 I have successfully done this just by looping through the volume data at the coordinates I want to show and painting them using GL_POINTS, as shown here (displaying the z direction central slice): 
 for (x=0; x<sizeX; x++){
  for (y=0; y<sizeY; y++){
    color=volume(x,y,0)

    glVertex3f(x,y,0);

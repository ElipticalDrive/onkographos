What
Promotes the crosshair-focused multi-LOD volume-streaming orchestration out of the range.html demo POC into niivue core, and migrates the demo onto it.
nv.loadChunkedVolume(source, options) -> NVChunkedVolume — a pluggable ChunkedVolumeSource seam (levels + fetchChunk, modeled on SlideTileSource) feeds a crosshair-focused octree: finest bricks around the crosshair, coarsening outward under a brick/VRAM budget, so a 20+ GB finest level (OME-Zarr / whole-slide) renders without ever going fully resident. The manager owns per-level fetch dispatch (bounded-concurrency slot-transfer semaphore, transient retry, in-flight dedup), maps the crosshair (world mm) to the volume's texture fraction, and swaps the plan in place on locationChange.
createStreamingNVImage — builds the img:null NVImage skeleton (header + RAS transforms) the renderer streams into.
Export-parity fix — chunkVolumeMultiLOD/MultiLodOptions/MultiLodFocus now exported from the webgpu/webgl2 entry points too.
Demo migration — examples/range.js drops ~160 lines of hand-rolled glue for a thin ZarrChunkedVolumeSource adapter; adds two-way zoom-slider sync (slider ⇄ wheel/programmatic); coarse floor on by default.
Backend-agnostic (emits a ChunkPlan), so WebGL2/WebGPU parity holds.

Add nv.loadChunkedVolume(source, options) -> NVChunkedVolume: a pluggable
ChunkedVolumeSource seam (levels + fetchChunk, modeled on SlideTileSource)
feeds a crosshair-focused octree that keeps the finest bricks around the
crosshair and coarsens outward under a brick/VRAM budget, so a 20+ GB finest
level (OME-Zarr / whole-slide) renders without ever going fully resident.

The manager owns per-level fetch dispatch with bounded concurrency (a
slot-transfer semaphore that never over-admits), transient retry, and in-flight
dedup, and swaps the plan in place as the crosshair moves. It maps the crosshair
(world mm) to this volume's texture fraction via mmToVolumeFraction, so the
focus is correct even when the volume doesn't span the scene AABB.
createStreamingNVImage builds the img:null NVImage skeleton (header + RAS
transforms) the renderer streams into. Backend-agnostic (emits a ChunkPlan), so
WebGL2/WebGPU parity holds. In the render view the finest radius spans a full
cell so the crosshair region renders at the finest level.

Also export chunkVolumeMultiLOD/MultiLodOptions/MultiLodFocus from the
webgpu/webgl2 entry points (previously only in the combined index).

The streamed-volume texture-cache fix gave chunked volumes a
`streamed://<id>` url (a renderer cache key). But those volumes are also
in model.volumes with img=null, so NVDocument.serialize wrote them with
that url and no data; on reload the deserializer took the url branch and
drove a broken `streamed://` fetch ("Failed to load volume") where the
volume was previously skipped silently.

A streamed volume is inherently non-restorable (its voxels come from a
runtime chunkSource closure), so serialize now filters it out via a new
isTransientStreamedVolume predicate (img null + chunkPlan/chunkSource).
Dropping it keeps the remaining volumes' order intact, and the only
cross-volume link (modulationImage, by id) resolves by find-by-id at
render time, so a reference to a dropped volume degrades to a no-op. The
`streamed://<id>` url stays on the NVImage as the cache key.

Adds predicate tests and a CBOR wire-contract round-trip asserting a
normal volume restores while a streamed volume is cleanly skipped.

In Japan, point clouds of vast areas of land measured by instruments such as MMS and airborne LP are being stored. Because of the huge amount of data, these point clouds are managed in index maps based on the national large-scale maps. With this method of managing point clouds, data management becomes more complicated as the number of divided point clouds increases as the measurement area expands. In recent years, measurement technology has been developed and high-density point clouds have been acquired. Therefore, even a divided point cloud consists of large-sized data, which often imposes filtering loads when using it in difference analysis and other applications. The ability to compress a point cloud in text format and restore it to a point cloud with a spatial resolution appropriate for the application contributes to efficient point cloud management. The objective of this study was to propose a compression and restoration method for point clouds according to the application. The proposed method compresses the point cloud by recording the presence or absence of points in voxels defined with five spatial resolutions ranging from a maximum of 1.00 m to a minimum of 0.0625 m. As a result of the verification, the data volume of the compressed point cloud was less than one-sixth of the original point cloud. Furthermore, in the difference analysis use case, point clouds restored from the compressed point cloud at 1.00 m and 0.0625 m spatial resolution were used to identify the location of the deformation and to quantitatively calculate the difference. This indicates that the proposed method can recover point clouds with a spatial resolution suitable for the application, and that the method is useful.

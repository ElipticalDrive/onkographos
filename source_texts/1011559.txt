Summary
Traced shadows are hard, and area lights shadow as a point at their middle. Tracing toward a light's area or radius, with a spatiotemporal denoiser on the shadow mask, gives soft shadows and lets area lights shadow as their shape.
User stories
As a technical artist, I want soft shadows from area lights and lights with a radius, so that lighting looks physically plausible.
Requirements
Traced shadows from area lights and from lights with a radius, sampling the light's shape.
A spatiotemporal denoiser on the shadow mask.
The denoiser's history textures are the render graph's history_texture, as temporal AA uses.
Area lights then shadow as their shape, not as a point at their middle.
Acceptance criteria
A quad light's traced shadow softens with distance from the caster.
The Shadow_Mask debug view shows a denoised, stable mask in motion.
Devices without Ray_Query keep the shadow-map fallback.
The rendering docs describe the built feature, in the present tense.
When to do this
When hard traced shadows from area lights look wrong in a game's scenes.

Implements visual polish and game-feel features for block breaking and placing:
Target Block Selection Wireframe: LineSegments unit cube highlighting targeted block.
Block Break Cracking Stages (0–9): Dynamic crack overlay mapping tiles 84–93 as mining progresses.
Voxel Digging & Break Particles: Instanced particle bursts on break and chip particles while digging, with physics and tinting.
Continuous Block Placement: Holding right-click continuously places blocks at 0.25s intervals (~4 blocks/sec).
Replaceable Blocks: Direct placement onto tall grass, saplings, water, and lava.
First-Person Arm & Tool Swing: Classic arm model attached to camera with idle/walk bobbing, continuous mining swing, and quick place punch.
Regression Tests: Added automated interaction tests covering raycasting, crack stage calculations, continuous placement timing, and replaceable blocks.

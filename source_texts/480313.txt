This change enables volumetric rendering effects by providing GLSL utilities to sample particle density and perform raymarching through the existing GPU-based spatial hash grid.
Key additions:
Volumetric Particle Style: A new particle type designed as a density carrier with long lifetimes and slow, drifting motion.
shaders/helpers/particle_grid.glsl: Reusable GLSL functions for other shaders to sample particle density at any world position or trace density along a ray.
SSBO Exposure: The ParticleBuffer, ParticleGridHeads, and ParticleGridNext buffers are now bound globally during the main render pass, allowing any fragment or compute shader to access them.
Demonstration: A new example volumetric_demo illustrates how to use these tools to create a screen-space fog effect driven by particle density.

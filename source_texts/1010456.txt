The low-poly skin was being used to decide the game's art style, and the open question
was what "ultra cheap" rendering can buy beyond faceted meshes: round, soft and billowing
things (smoke, clouds, foliage), and what the skin looks like at the pixel skin's own
resolution. This change adds each of those as something you can see in the running game
and switch on and off from the address bar, so the options can be compared on screen.

## Impostors: soft things in two triangles

A ball is now one camera-facing quad, with the sphere worked out per pixel in the shader
(`impostor.ts`, `impostor-glsl.ts`, `webgpu/wgsl-impostor.ts`). It is round at any size,
lit from a true normal in the same local frame as every mesh face, and its outline is
pushed in and out by noise so it billows. Each ball carries its body's foot the way a
mesh vertex carries its anchor. It is placed by the world's own projection, so a crown
sways with its trunk and a plume shrinks and sinks on the horizon lip as one body. To
share that projection, the world vertex shaders' placement was pulled out into
`PLACE_GLSL` and `PLACE_WGSL`; the world shaders now call it, and their behaviour is
unchanged.

Balls write their own depth (the front of the sphere at that pixel), so a crown swallows
its own trunk and puffs cut into each other without sorting. The impostor pass runs after
the sheer pass on both backends. It also draws into the mirror, so the crowns show in
puddles.

## What uses them

- **Volcano smoke (always on).** A seeded share of the mountains are now volcanoes
  (`isVolcano` in `landforms.ts`), and their summit is cut into a crater. That is a
  change to the shared world shape, so the pixel skin shows the craters too.
  `src/game/volcanoes.ts` is the plume as a pure function of time and the vent's seed.
  It has no state and is whole on the first frame. Puffs rise fast and slow as they cool,
  swell, bend over downwind on a curl-noise wander, and waste away when old. The skin
  draws them (`plume-balls.ts`) with an ember glow at the vent. The climb is deliberately
  low (`RISE_TILES`): the mountains already stand taller than the 180-line frame from
  most of the field, so a tall column only ever showed off the top of the screen.
- **Clouds (always on).** `sky-puffs.ts` draws the shared cloud decks from
  `sky-clouds.ts` as impostor puffs at the same bearings the pixel skin uses. The hour's
  shade tone rides in the spare `w` of three WebGPU `Frame` fields.
- **`?leaves=impostor`.** Broadleaf clumps become one ball each, and bushes become a
  three-ball mound. The wood stays mesh.
- **`?volume=1`.** Smoke and clouds are raymarched as volumes instead of shaded as balls:
  a noise density inside the ball, lit per sample by a short march toward the sun,
  blended far to near with premultiplied alpha. Crowns never take this path. This is the
  expensive option, and its cost at window resolution has not been measured yet.

## `?res=low`

`?res=low` draws the skin a logical pixel to a buffer pixel, about 180 scanlines tall,
blown up by a whole factor with `image-rendering: pixelated`. Every pixel is the same
size, and a 1080p window is exactly 320x180 (`lowResCanvas`). `lowpolyView` takes the
height for this. Multisampling defaults off in this mode, because `parseMsaa` gained a
fallback; `?msaa=4` still asks for it.

## Notes for review

- `backend.ts`: `createDrawable` takes a `VertexLayout`, and `FrameScene` gained
  `impostors`. `FrameUniforms` gained `cloudShade`.
- Checked in the running page on WebGPU and on WebGL2 (`?gpu=webgl`) with every option
  on: no console errors, and the pixel skin still renders.
- `.claude/rules/skin-lowpoly.md` documents the impostors and their knobs. Its API table
  is export-checked by `skin-rule.test.ts`.

sides changed the low-poly skin in the same places, so this merge keeps both.

## How the conflicts were resolved

- **`Kind` in `mesh.ts`.** Both sides claimed the value `9`: `main` for `liquid`,
  this branch for `smoke` (with `cloud` at `10`). `liquid` keeps `9` because it has
  already shipped; `smoke` moves to `10` and `cloud` to `11`. Every shader reads
  these values by interpolating `Kind.*` (`impostor-glsl.ts`, `wgsl-impostor.ts`,
  `shaders.ts`, `wgsl-world.ts`), so nothing else needed renumbering.
- **`buildActors` in `lowpoly-game.ts`.** It takes `main`'s new `turn` argument (the
  slime skin faces the eye through it), and the branch's `impostorScene` stays beside it.
  `draw` already passed `where.turn`.
- **`TOWARD_VIEWER` had two owners.** `main` added it to `placement.ts` for the
  slime's gloss and the jelly shading, and this branch added it to `impostor.ts` for
  the impostor normal frame. The two values are equal, because
  `SCREEN_RISE = hypot(TILE_DEPTH, WALL_RISE)`. Git merged both without a conflict.
  It now lives only in `placement.ts`. `impostor-glsl.ts`, `webgpu/wgsl-impostor.ts`
  and `impostor.test.ts` import it from there.
- **`.claude/rules/skin-lowpoly.md` API table.** This keeps the branch's impostor rows
  and `main`'s `smoothBlob` and `TOWARD_VIEWER` entries. `TOWARD_VIEWER` is listed
  under `placement.ts` and not under `impostor.ts`.

PR #102 conflicted with `origin/main` in one place: `src/skins/lowpoly/palette.ts`, where
this branch added the `smoke` and `ember` inks for volcano plumes and main added
`waterDeep` for the water's own body. They are independent entries in `LOWPOLY`, so the

The shader files also merged automatically and were read by hand, because `tsc` and
Vitest cannot see a broken shader. Main's changes there were fragment-side: the
`WATER_LOOK` constants and a `deep` argument to `waterColour` in both the GLSL and
the WGSL. This branch's changes were vertex-side: `PLACE_GLSL` and `PLACE_WGSL` pulled
out of the world vertex shaders. The two do not overlap, and no shader declaration is
duplicated. The new WGSL reads `frame.ambient.rgb` and `frame.shading.x`, leaving the
spare `w` lanes that hold this branch's `cloudShade` alone. Nothing was checked in a
browser.

## A mypy gap that any merge of a devkit upgrade hits

`lint-all.py --changed` gives mypy every changed `.py` file by name. The merge brought in
`scripts/log-wrap.py` and `scripts/run-tests.py`, and `scripts/ship.py` has the same
pattern. Each imports a module out of `scripts/hooks/` (`harness_events`, `toolchain`,
`untested_symbols`) by putting that directory on `sys.path` at run time. mypy cannot see
that, so the lint failed with `import-not-found` on code this branch never touched.
`pyproject.toml` now declares those three modules in a `[[tool.mypy.overrides]]` with
`ignore_missing_imports`. `tests/test_mypy_config.py` parses every top-level script and
fails when one imports a hooks module the override does not list. It caught
`untested_symbols`, which the first grep missed.

Alternatives considered: adding `scripts/hooks` to `mypy_path` would make mypy
type-check the vendored harness this repo is not allowed to edit, and the existing
`exclude` exists to prevent exactly that. Dropping vendored scripts from `lint-all.py`'s
mypy targets would also hide `run-tests.py` and `ship.py`, which this project owns.

Verified: `scripts/run-tests.py` (vitest related over 44 frontend files, plus the
contract tests) passes, as do `tests/test_mypy_config.py` and `tests/test_lint_all.py`.
`lint-all.py --changed`, `structure_check.py`, `untested_symbols.py` and `tsc --noEmit`
are clean, and `git diff --check` finds no conflict marker. The tree has no
`scripts/hot-budget.py`.

## How the conflicts were resolved

- **Scenery builders.** `broadleafMesh(...)` and the species table's `SpeciesMesh` now
  take `(solid, at, height, seed, look, leaves?)`: main's `Look` first, as main ordered
  it, then the PR's optional `ImpostorBuilder`. `sceneryMesh` takes main's `SceneryLay`
  object, which gains `leaves` beside `look` and `ground`. A tree on a painted hill
  can wear impostor crowns.
- **`buildChunk(cx, cy, look = FLAT_LOOK, leaves = "mesh")`**: both arguments, and each
  feature is laid with `{ look, ground: under, leaves: crowns }`.
- **`LowpolyGame` and `index.ts`.** Both sides named their option `look`: main's is the
  painted `Look`, the PR's is the `LookOptions` knob set (`res`, `leaves`, `volume`).
  Main's keeps the name `look`. The PR's is now `display` (`DEFAULT_DISPLAY`), passed
  as a fourth constructor argument.
- **`packFrame`.** The two sides used different spare `w` slots. The cloud shade rides
  in `lightDir`/`ambient`/`haze.w` and `look.stepped` in `shading.w`, and the layout
  table documents both.
- **`pyproject.toml`.** The two sides wrote one mypy-override comment differently. The
  merge keeps main's wording and the PR's pointer to `tests/test_mypy_config.py`.
- **`.claude/rules/skin-lowpoly.md`.** The auto-merge left two `shaders.ts` rows in the
  API table. They are now one row, with `PLACE_GLSL` and `paintConstants` both on it.

## The merge also broke something without a conflict

Main widened the GLSL `u_shading` uniform from `vec3` to `vec4` (the fourth channel
switches on the stepped light), and `setWorld` now sets it with `uniform4f`. `setWorld`
also fills the PR's impostor program, which still declared `uniform vec3 u_shading`.
WebGL rejects a `uniform4f` on a `vec3`, so the value is never set and stays 0. On
WebGL2 every crown, plume and cloud would have drawn with no sun. `tsc` and Vitest
cannot see this. `impostor-glsl.ts` now declares it `vec4`, and a new test in
`impostor.test.ts` checks that every uniform the impostor program shares with the
world program has the same declared type. The test failed before the fix.

## Checked

`tsc --noEmit` passes, and so do `lint-all.py --changed` (ruff, mypy, typecheck),
`structure_check.py`, `untested_symbols.py` and `run-tests.py` (vitest related for 48
files, plus the contract tests). This session did not check it in the browser. The
next session should load `?gpu=webgl&leaves=impostor` and `?look=painted&leaves=impostor`
to see the sun-lit balls and the painted ground together.

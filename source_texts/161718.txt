Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) has achieved significant success in 3D scene reconstruction and novel view synthesis. However, when handling high-frequency details and complex occlusions, NeRF often produces blurry images and depth inconsistencies, resulting in loss of detail and visual incoherence. To address these issues, this paper proposes an enhanced NeRF method by incorporating a self-attention mechanism during feature extraction to capture long-range dependencies between features, thereby improving the handling of detail-rich scenes. Furthermore, we design an improved loss function that combines Structural Similarity (SSIM) and gradient consistency to optimize the texture clarity and edge consistency of rendered images. Specifically, the self-attention module is integrated into the multilayer perceptron (MLP) of NeRF, enabling each voxel feature to adaptively attend to feature relationships from other positions; the new loss function further enhances both global and local consistency of the images. Experimental results show that, compared to the original NeRF, our method significantly improves the detail fidelity, clarity, and consistency of rendered images across multiple benchmark datasets.

Adds shading details to water.frag water shader, and other random details to make the scene more realistic.
Features:
Hit objects and water surface implements the phong shading model (water surface keeps diffuse from hit objects, but implements its own specular).
Both Water and Normal mapped geometry now has raymarched shadows.
Water reflections now implements distance fog
Water implements light absorption on submerged rays
Multilayered normal maps for large and micro-features on the water surface for a more authentic surface animation.
Bug fixes:
Intentionally black color vectors from ray-marching were auto-converted to background-color
Viewing vector for water specular's we're flipped causing highlights to visually disappear

Adds cubehandler render to turn a cube and NGL camera orientation into an offscreen rendered image. Signed isovalues, colours, output format, dimensions, background and surface opacity are configurable. An AiiDA CalcJob can render preserved full cubes on their storage computer and retrieve only images/logs.
--slice-plane plane.json includes a coloured plane using the shared EMPA viewer's interpolation, origin, boundary and frozen colour-range conventions. Cells outside an isolated grid are omitted; periodic sampling is explicit. Full-resolution rendering samples the selected full cube. --lighting lighting.json accepts ambient, direct-light and gloss controls for atoms and isosurfaces. The data plane stays unlit. Omitting either option retains the original behavior for that feature. NGL previews and VTK renders use different lighting models, so shading is comparable rather than pixel-identical.
The branch contains current main's packaging/CI updates and retains pyvista>=0.46. SciPy and Matplotlib are explicit dependencies because plane rendering calls their interpolation and colour APIs directly. Existing version bounds and Python support are unchanged. Headless compute nodes require EGL or OSMesa; the tested ARM64 container used VTK_DEFAULT_OPENGL_WINDOW=vtkOSOpenGLRenderWindow and Ubuntu's libosmesa6 without a display server.
Validation: 11 focused CLI/sampling/render tests pass, including skewed anisotropic grids, nonzero origins, explicit boundaries, lighting validation and exclusion of lighting from the plane. On Linux ARM64/Python 3.12.11, PyVista 0.49.1 and VTK 9.7.1, real AiiDA CalcJobs rendered previews and full cubes from stash. The latest browser test saved/restored lighting and submitted a successful full-cube render with a visible plane. Remote execution on tigu or Daint remains untested; camera/appearance parity and wider scientific examples remain review targets.
Consumers: nanotech-empa/aiidalab-orca#9 and nanotech-empa/aiidalab-widgets-empa#14. Rendering is shared infrastructure for other EMPA apps, including those on Daint; applications submit CalcJobs on the computer holding the cube.
Prepared with the assistance of Codex.

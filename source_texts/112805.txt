Voxels have no textures; a material is one flat color. This item adds textures as an asset type and binds them to materials, so the "textured 3D" preset and fully 3D, non-pixel-art games are possible, and so a client-only package can override textures by name (a texture pack).
Direction
Textures are assets referenced by namespaced name, never by file path, and follow B8's override rules.
Per-face binding (top, side, bottom), random variants chosen by position hash, and animation frames. Connected textures may follow later; leave room for them.
UVs stay world-space and anchored to the 1 m lattice (V5), so one block texture spans 2×2×2 voxels and half-block edits show the right part of it. Texture resolution is independent of the UVs, so a pack can ship 16, 32 or 64 pixels per block.
Texture arrays rather than an atlas, sized for the integrated-GPU minimum spec. Filtering per material: nearest for pixel art, mipmapped for other styles.
Normal and emissive maps are optional per material.
Split into sub-issues if it grows past M (rule 1).

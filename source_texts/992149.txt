What this adds
A seed-deterministic procedural candy town in Experimental/CandyCity/ — every road, house, tree, jelly bean and texture is generated in the browser at load time. No models, images or fonts are shipped; the only dependency is a vendored three.js r170 (MIT, unmodified apart from the bare-specifier rewrite).
Roads — jittered 7×7 node grid joined by bent Catmull-Rom streets; liquorice tarmac with pink edge lines, dashed cream centre line and sugar grit; candy-cane kerbs following the curve; white-chocolate slab pavements with pink icing grout; jelly-bean crosswalks at every junction; strawberry-syrup country lanes out of town.
Houses (8 styles) — gumdrop cottage (icing drips, roof sprinkles, chocolate chimney with marshmallow cap), candy-cane cabin, cupcake house (soft-serve coils), chocolate chalet, wafer apartment (balconies, roof garden), ice-cream tower, lollipop pavilion, candy-corn cottage. All with jawbreaker doorsteps, candy-cane jambs, muntined windows on wafer sills, gumdrop flower boxes, icing-rail fences, mailboxes, benches, gummy cars with peppermint wheels.
Trees — cotton-candy (spun-sugar strands), lollipop, peppermint, candy-corn, marshmallow, jelly-bean bushes, gumdrop shrubs; 342 per town, plus street-tree rows on every pavement.
Plaza — bullseye peppermint pavement with a four-tier layer cake: icing drip rings, hundreds of candy beads, candles, cherry.
Texture library — 16 candy surfaces painted on canvases (peppermint, four lollipop swirls, three cane stripes, wafer ridges, waffle lattice, chocolate, frosting, sprinkled sugar, road with markings, pavement, cotton candy, marshmallow, jelly-bean shell, gumdrop sugar, jawbreaker, gummy) plus Sobel-derived normal maps for nine of them.
Atmosphere — sky-dome shader with candy-floss clouds, sun/moon, sprinkle stars and a golden-hour pass; 14 marshmallow clouds; sugar motes; lit windows and lamp pools at night with a day-cycle slider.
Performance — all static candy merged into 29 meshes (10 006 pieces) with per-candy colour on a vertex-colour attribute; jelly beans and sprinkles instanced (1 402 + 9 832); ≈1.12 M town triangles, ≈76–77 draw calls for a whole frame.
Verification
node test/city.test.mjs — 20/20 checks (structure floors, determinism, mesh integrity, material resolution, budgets, lot accounting).
node test/seed-sweep.mjs — 12/12 seeds produce complete towns.
Rendering verified in headless Chromium 130 via ANGLE/Vulkan on SwiftShader (CPU); nine retained captures in evidence/Images.
Not done: hardware-GPU performance, Windows run, editor/renderer/EngineContent integration. The HUD frame rate is CPU rasterisation and is not a performance claim.
Tracker entry added under Documentation/Tracker.md. Evidence and limits: Experimental/CandyCity/evidence/README.md.

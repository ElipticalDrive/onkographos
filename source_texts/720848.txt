Uploaded by gtnconcept on Feb 4, 2009
NOTE: I am not an expert in 3D graphics or video post-production. This video demonstrates a series of personal experiments that are unscientific and could probably use further refinement.
This video demonstrates how video post-processing techniques can enhance output from Google Sketchup's direct illumination 3D renderer. By using different render passes it is possible to approximate the appearance of effects such as:
- ambient occlusion
- coloured radiosity
Current limitations of the techniques used:
- soft-shadows are not supported by Sketchup
- reflections are not supported by Sketchup
- light fall-off limited to occlusion only
- radiosity only uses colours visible on-screen

Thanks for your amazing work!
I encountered some issues when attempting to train the model with different initialization strategies. Specifically, I aim to verify the impact of different initialization strategies on the final prediction results of the model by changing to the initialization with ground-truth point cloud, as shown in Table 6 of the original GaussianFormer paper.
However, I'm not sure if this option is explicitly provided in the source code of GaussianFormer. I only found the parameter pts_init=False in the GaussianLifter in GaussianFormer/model/lifter/gaussian_lifter.py. Later, during the training process, I successfully read the point cloud data from the point cloud file and used it as the initialization result of the Gaussian positions by setting it to True.
According to the above settings, for verification purposes, I first trained on the mini subset of the nuScenes dataset using checkpoints with 25,600 points for 20 epochs. The final mIoU could reach 26, while the mIoU of the model trained with the original initialization strategy under the same settings could reach 29.

# dwiharm

Spherical and rotational harmonic utilities for diffusion-weighted MRI.

## Modules

- `src/dwiharm/convention.py`: Defines the package-wide complex-to-real change-of-basis convention.
- `src/dwiharm/sh.py`: Evaluates real spherical harmonics `Y(l, x)` at Cartesian sample points.
- `src/dwiharm/wigner.py`: Evaluates real irreducible SO(3) representation matrices `D(l, R)`.
- `src/dwiharm/clebsch_gordan.py`: Computes real Clebsch-Gordan coupling blocks `Q(j, l, L)`.

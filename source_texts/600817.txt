Summary
Replaces the Plotly event display with a three.js renderer. A single EventDisplay scene builds a JSON description in Python, which the browser draws as a self-contained HTML fragment. The fragment renders inline in Jupyter and in Quarto pages (_repr_html_), or as a standalone file (write_html).
Voxel volumes (add_volume, add_deposits): the image is uploaded as a uint8 3D texture and ray-marched per pixel on the GPU. Sharp mode uses exact voxel traversal, and there are trilinear and max-intensity modes. Every voxel gets its own opacity, with no triangle sorting. An image costs one byte per voxel; the 50 mm muon figure in the notebook was 2 MB as Plotly cubes.
Truth tracks and vertices (add_tracks, add_vertices): coloured by time with a shared time colour bar, one toggleable layer per species. Hovering shows step time and deposit, or vertex energy and time.
Generic add_line (solid or dashed, single colour or by value) and add_points.
In the page: threshold, density and gamma sliders, layer checkboxes, colour bars, labelled mm tick axes on three box edges, voxel readout on hover, and reset view. Rendering is on demand, and WebGL starts lazily as a display scrolls near the viewport (browsers cap live WebGL contexts at about 16 per page).
three.js r147 classic builds, from pinned jsDelivr URLs (lib="cdn") or inlined from track_vis/static/vendor (lib="inline", MIT licence included).
Removed
viz.py (plot_tracks, plot_truth_vertices, plot_voxels, voxel_mesh, time_colorscale, COLORSCALE, VOXEL_COLORSCALE) and the plotly dependency. Colour scales now live in track_vis/colors.py, and load_event and particle_name in track_vis/io.py.
Breaking change. Downstream code that imports the Plotly functions, such as the likelihood_imaging notebook, has to move to EventDisplay.
Testing
New tests/test_display.py (7 tests): voxelize and deposit binning, colour scales, x-fastest texture order and padding, species grouping, and HTML/spec output. uv run pytest passes, and ruff check and ruff format are clean.
Example notebook rewritten for the new API and executed with nbconvert. The synthetic and toy-volume cells render. The real-data cell needs the ntuple on /data/snoplus2, so I couldn't run it here.
Checked in headless Chromium (WebGL): rendering, layer toggles, hover tooltips, and loading through the CDN path.

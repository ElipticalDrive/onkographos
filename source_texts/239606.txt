Requested by yoshi · project thread
Before: vs-surface on a 1024³ phantom took 89 s, about 85 % of it in OpenVDB's levelSetRebuild. On castings that pass also dropped a few band voxels next to small closed surfaces (noise, pores at the surface), which then got the "beyond the band" code.
After: the same phantom takes 19 s with a byte-identical .vss. The sample castings run 3 to 4.6× faster (housing 8.3 → 1.8 s, bracket 10.1 → 2.9 s), and 0.3 to 0.6 % of their band voxels now get the in-band code a triangle within the band calls for. No voxel gets farther or changes side; the enclosed volume moves by about 0.1 mm³ of 12 488 mm³.
How: the chunk is still triangulated by OpenVDB's marching cubes (VolumeToMesh). Instead of rebuilding a narrow-band level set in a tree, every triangle is binned into z slabs and each voxel within band keeps the nearest triangle's distance in a dense array of the chunk, computed with closestPointOnTriangleToPoint in the argument order and float rounding of meshToVolume. ADR 0009 is amended.
Checked locally: all 236 tests pass, clang-tidy is clean on src/surface.cpp.

Closes #330
Problem
The volume texture cache is keyed by vol.url || vol.name. Its chunked entries were reused while the display plan matched (chunkedDisplayKey), and its multi-instance whole-volume entries while wholeVolumeTextureKey matched. Neither key identified the voxel source. A volume whose voxels were replaced under the same url or name, such as the atomic same-name reload or a new img array at the same _dataVersion, kept drawing the old textures.
Fix
New volumeSourceKey() in volume/orientChunked.ts identifies the voxel source: the img buffer with its byte range, and the chunk source. It deliberately leaves out the NVImage object itself, so equal-content entries built from different NVImage objects still share textures as before.
wholeVolumeTextureKey() includes it (scalar and RGBA), so a multi-instance texture is rebuilt.
In both backends (gl/render.ts, wgpu/render.ts) a chunked entry records its sourceKey. It is reused only when the key matches. Otherwise it is fully evicted, decoded tier included, since that tier holds source bytes. swapChunkedVolumePlan refreshes the key.
Tests
Unit (orientChunked.test.ts): the same source gives the same key; a replaced img, a subarray or a new chunkSource gives a new key; the whole-volume key follows the source for scalar and RGB volumes.
e2e (live-data-updates.spec.ts, WebGL2 and WebGPU): replacing a volume's voxels with zeros under the same name and data version must clear the drawn pixels, for a chunked volume and for a multi-instance view.
Both fail on main's source. The chunked e2e keeps 33932 lit pixels (threshold about 1696). With only the whole-volume key change reverted, the multi-instance half fails on its own (24220 vs 1211).
Gates (format, lint, typecheck, test, build, boundaries) and codespell pass. In the full e2e run, the failures are three uikit overlay specs (uikit not built in the worktree) and vox.4d.html / lod-compensation-report, which fail on main as well.

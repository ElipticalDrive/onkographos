Before: Orbit's field could be 3D or 2D simplex or a gyroid, with 1 to 4 fBM octaves. Phases 4 and 5 were merged but not yet marked done.
After: FIELD gains Cellular, which defaults to smooth F1. Behind it sits the engine that phase 7's Field expander will expose:
fBM, ridged, hetero terrain and hybrid multifractal, with continuous DETAIL, ROUGHNESS and LACUNARITY.
Cellular F1, F2, F2−F1, smooth F1 and cell value, over three metrics on a cubic or BCC lattice.
A second noise that warps space and tilts the roughness from place to place, with its own scale and drift.
Twist, swirl, fold and ripple distortion.
A fold, sine, clip or rectify shaper.
tools/render takes all of these as options. At one octave, Orbit sounds as it did.
Phases 4 and 5 are marked done, since Vic confirmed both in Rack, which makes this 2.5.0. The TODO now makes phase 6 Orbit's left-side layout, a hard-to-soft SYNC knob and a VLFO/LFO/audio switch. The field engine and the expander move to phase 7, and DESIGN.md records the sync design.
How:
Every setting is calibrated to span about [−1, 1] at any detail and roughness. For example, fBM is divided by its RMS amplitude, so adding octaves no longer makes it quieter.
Smooth cellular features use a compact kernel instead of the usual exponential. The exponential gives weight to points outside the 27 searched cells, so the field stepped whenever the search moved.
Each feature's spectrum was measured along lines, and that sets its anti-aliasing bandwidth. Creased features such as F2−F1 therefore oversample sooner.
3D simplex is branch-free, and its output is identical to before.
SIMD across poly is dropped from the plan. The noise hash's 64-bit multiply has no SSE4 vector form, so vectorizing it would change every seed's sound.

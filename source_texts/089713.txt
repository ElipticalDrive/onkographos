Want
Generate each planet's world procedurally, with real authored control over its features: mountain ranges, valleys, lakes, rivers and caves. Some Easter eggs should be places that look normal from the outside but have a hidden way in (a cave behind a waterfall, a hollow hill, a fake rock face).
Today
Terrain is a heightfield on a cube-sphere (`packages/shared/src/planet/terrain.ts`): one height per direction, plus noise. Lakes and peaks are a few hand-placed features per body (`lakes` / `peaks` in `planet/bodies.ts`, e.g. "Tolling Tower" peak, "Brinewater Lake").
A heightfield can't represent caves or overhangs. The one cave today ("the cave behind Lacefall", `content/places.ts`, egg E1) is a place marker, not real geometry.
The server uses the same terrain for movement validation and collision (`planet/collide.ts`), so anything new has to stay deterministic and shared.
Direction (to be refined)
Richer surface generation: ridged noise for mountain ranges, erosion-style valleys, generated lakes and rivers at local minima, all seeded per body. Keep authored overrides for named places.
Caves and interiors: pick one approach:
Cave meshes placed into the terrain: authored or generated interiors, an entrance cut into the heightfield, and their own collision. Cheapest, and works with the current pipeline.
Voxel/SDF terrain in limited regions: true caves and overhangs, but a big change to the chunk mesher, collision and server validation.
Recommendation: start with placed cave meshes.
Secret entrances: a new kind of place (`kind: "hidden"` already exists) whose entrance looks like normal terrain or a prop until you interact with it, walk through it, or solve something. Tie these to eggs in `content/secrets.ts`.
Open questions
Should a generated world be stable forever (fixed seed), or can it be regenerated between seasons? Saves, pads, towns and quest places depend on fixed positions.
How many caves per planet, and are they combat spaces or only for exploring and secrets?
Acceptance
Each planet has at least one mountain range, several lakes and at least one walkable cave, all generated from a seed.
At least one Easter egg is reached through a hidden entrance that looks normal from outside.
Server movement validation and collision work inside caves.
Related: #7 (wildlife and variety).

Follow-up to the survey of computer-graphics methods for the radiation code (2026-09-26).
The cost it attacks
Every receiver (cell centre) gets its own exact gather, and at mesh scale that is 1.6M receivers on the Sozzi case. The fluence rate G is smooth almost everywhere: it varies sharply only near the lamp (where one facet subtends a large angle) and across shadow edges. So most exact evaluations are ones interpolation could supply.
What rendering does
Irradiance caching (Ward, Rubinstein & Clear, SIGGRAPH 1988): compute the expensive quantity exactly at a sparse set of points and interpolate everywhere else, with each cached value valid over a radius set by an error estimate (there, the harmonic mean distance to the surfaces seen).
Irradiance gradients (Ward & Heckbert 1992): cache the gradient too and interpolate to first order, which widens each record's valid radius. Here the spatial gradient of G is available from the gather itself by forward-mode differentiation in the receiver position, at a small multiple of the value's cost.
Why it fits here
Differentiable: the interpolation weights depend on positions only, so they are frozen geometry and G stays linear in emission, with every live parameter's gradient flowing through the exact records.
It composes with emitter clustering (Radiation: cluster the emitters for the gather with an error bound (the "many lights" problem: lightcuts, low-rank sampling) #565): clustering cuts the cost per receiver, caching cuts the number of receivers, which together are the two sides of a fast multipole method.
The mesh already refines where G varies: near walls and the lamp. A cache placed on a coarser, error-driven set would let the bulk of the chamber be served by far fewer exact evaluations.
The hard parts, to settle in the design
Shadow edges: interpolating across one smears a shadow. Records must not be shared across a change in the visibility mask, which means the validity test needs the mask (cheap with Radiation: decide the shadow mask a block at a time — conservative shaft certificates between receiver blocks and facet clusters #554's certificates: a record serves only points in tiles with the same certificate).
An error estimate with teeth: a record's radius must come from a bound or a measured local variation, not a fixed spacing. Mesh refinement is not a substitute.
What a user reads: the dose integral (the quantity a reactor is judged by) may be far less sensitive to this than the pointwise field. Measure both.
Acceptance
On the Sozzi case, G against the exact field over all cells, as median / p99 / max relative error near the lamp, in the pipes and elsewhere, and the volume-mean G and dose statistics, at a stated number of exact evaluations.
Wall clock against the exact field in one process.
Gradients (dG/dM, dG/da) against the exact field's, to the same tolerance.

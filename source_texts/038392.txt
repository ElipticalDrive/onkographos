The three-dimensional reconstruction from volume data of the microscopical cell and head is realized with ray casting algorism on Visualization Toolkit(VTK).Different sampling intervals are used,and different reconstruction results are got and different reconstruction time slots are took in the experiment.The relations between the sampling interval and reconstruction result and reconstruction time slot are compared and analyzed,and the appropriate sampling interval is got.It can provide practical foundation for research and application of three-dimensional reconstruction of kinds of serial slice images.

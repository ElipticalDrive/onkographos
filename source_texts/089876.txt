This dissertation dicusses the main concepts involved in the non-photorealistic rendering techniques and proposes a general scheme to implement such techniques. The work discusses also a non-photorealistic rendering style from line drawing to 3D models presented by Stéphane Grabli et al, in such a way that the line styles are programmable.

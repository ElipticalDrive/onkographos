Goal 7 (volumes): volumes whose absorption, density, scattering colour or emission vary from point to point, rendered by spectral delta tracking under a per-instance majorant, on OptiX and Vulkan.
What it does
Export bounds each varying volume's extinction inside the instance it fills (interval arithmetic over its expression, with point inputs boxed by the instance's bounds), and pipes its texture space and majorant. A volume that no rule bounds is refused with an error naming its material; there is no guessed majorant.
The renderer tracks a varying medium by spectral delta tracking (Kutz et al. 2017) under that majorant. Shadow rays toward lights, and the camera's own medium up to the first hit, pass through it by ratio tracking (Novák et al. 2014).
Emission per unit length (Principled Volume emission and blackbody, or an Emission shader), not scaled by density. A blackbody emits a black body's radiance at its temperature, STEFAN_BOLTZMANN T^4 / π, times its Blackbody Intensity at Blender's unit scale of 1e-6 (BLACKBODY_UNIT), and nothing at or below 0 K. Its colour reuses FCPT's existing blackbody(). test.py checks 1500 K at intensity 2 against that derived number, and 0 K against 0. It is gathered at each tentative collision, over the majorant, by the weight reaching it, and added to the pixel as a first hit's emission is, unresampled.
A medium's material key travels beside Medium (fills(), filling/fillings), so Medium keeps main's 24 bytes.
Scenes with a varying medium compile from an extended PTX or SPIR-V. Scenes without one compile from a PTX or SPIR-V with that code compiled out, as before (the MEDIUM op bit selects). Both backends compile it out the same way: without EXTENDED, a used() macro makes MEDIUM false at compile time.
Semantics and limits
No NEE toward emitting volumes: their light arrives through the tracking above.
Light sampled inside a varying medium is not shifted by ReSTIR; shifting it read test 3 1.1% dark at 4096 spp.
Bounds come from the rest mesh, so a deformed mesh can leave its bound.
The majorant is loose where interval arithmetic is: about 2× the reached extinction on tokyo. It is correct, but costs tentative collisions.
A primary ray that misses all geometry sees no medium emission.
A straight-alpha image's bound is loose when its alpha is unread.
An image read with CLIP extension bounds its texels and the 0 read outside it. An image whose alpha_mode is NONE bounds its alpha as 1, as the renderer reads it.
Provenance
The code is our own. None of it is copied from Cycles' svm source (Apache-2.0); the blackbody intensity is derived from the Stefan-Boltzmann law above.

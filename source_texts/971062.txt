Adds soft sun shadows to the physical sun/sky house demo on top of #86 (project-cloud-shadows), preserving fully lit brightness across directional, CSM and accumulated shadows.
All three modes use one fixed shading sun with the same color, intensity, cloud transmission and direction. The mode selector changes only visibility: a directional shadow map, cascaded visibility from a hidden SunLight, or the accumulated surface atlas. Only the selected live shadow map updates. Sky IBL, material shading and reflections remain active.
Demos (immutable 7a49e5808ac):
Physical sun, clear sky
Larger 1 degree sun for inspecting penumbrae
Directional comparison
Inherited clouds
A separate shadow light jitters across the solar disk. Two floating-point targets accumulate only visibility with an arithmetic mean, exact history texel loads and no color clamp. UV winding does not flip receiver normals. Gutter dilation fills empty texels without blurring valid samples. Sampling stops at the selected budget (64 by default), and camera movement preserves history.
The house UV generator and its tests are copied from unwrapUVs PR #34944, snapshot 836bbd8493b, without merging unrelated history. The 10 km ground reserves an 80 m local planar atlas patch, fading over its last 4 m. Two 4096x2048 RGBA16F atlas targets consume 128 MiB, excluding shadow maps.
Controls include shadow method, 16–256 samples, 0–2 degree angular radius, reset history, shared shadow bias (-0.005 to 0.005; default -0.00005), and normal bias (0–0.2 m; default 0.02 m). Bias edits reach directional, CSM and sampling shadows and reset history. Values survive mode switches and sky/sun updates.
Validation on physical NVIDIA RTX3060Ti (Ampere, WebGPU nonfallback and hardware D3D11 WebGL):
Full shadow path with receivers enabled and occluders disabled produces pixel-identical linear HDR images in all three modes, for clear and cloudy skies. All ~6.7 million covered atlas texels equal 1.
With occluders restored, all methods retain the same image peak; differences remain in shadows. Both new GUI controls reset history and propagate their exact values to every shadow provider. Repeated mode switches and sun-angle rebakes pass without browser/shader errors.
Core lint and example lint pass. Core unit tests: 1957 pass, 1 todo, 0 fail. Addon tests: 128 pass, 0 fail. No typecheck script is configured. Hardware runners omit the stock software Vulkan driver override.

Two sequential trials per variant/mode, each with 5 seconds warmup and 5 seconds counting GPU-completed frames, queues drained at transitions, vsync/caps disabled. Parent ran before updated code; trial variation and scene dependence limit generalization. The live demo converges in 64 display frames (~1.1 seconds at 60 FPS). Raw timings, explicit hardware confirmation, replay scripts and matching screenshots are in the initiating issue's turn-2 workflow artifacts.
Static opaque scene only. Application-driven geometry changes require reset and valid updated UVs. This is progressive surface-space shadow baking; it does not solve the inherited distant ground/background stripe.
References: WebGPU progressive lightmaps, UV unwrapper, bhouston/swarm#140.

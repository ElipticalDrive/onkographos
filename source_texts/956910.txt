With the rise of anti-aliased Gaussian Splatting techniques (such as Mip-Splatting or Anti-Aliased Semantic Splatting), models trained with these methods require specific rendering adjustments to display correctly without blurring or aliasing artifacts at different distances/resolutions. Currently, there is no quick way to toggle this rendering path within the super-splat editor UI.
I would love to see an Antialias Toggle added to the Settings menu. Ideally placed under the render settings or general settings tab for easy access.

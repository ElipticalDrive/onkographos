Issue body actions
Add interpolated (Gouraud) shading, MATLAB's shading interp, for surfaces and filled data.
Add a shading property to the IR (schema review required first).
On screen, draw per-vertex colours.
In PDF, write type 4 (free-form) or type 5 (lattice-form) mesh shadings directly with pdf-writer, because krilla does not expose shadings. Use them below a triangle-count threshold, above which the raster fallback applies.

There are potential optimizations in the dda raycasting of the cursor:
Clip the cursor ray to the volume bounds before traversal. Currently it starts at the camera and walks through outside cells until reaching the volume, or potentially all the way to the far plane on a miss. Skipping that work is the clearest candidate, especially with distant cameras. Preserve the cursor’s entry, previous-position and locked-plane behavior.
Skip empty regions hierarchically for large sparse volumes.

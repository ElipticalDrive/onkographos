# video-to-3dgs

**Turn an ordinary walking video into an explorable 3D scene — a point cloud, a textured mesh, *and* a photorealistic 3D Gaussian Splat — using only free, open-source tools.**

Shot a handheld clip walking through a place (or grabbed one off YouTube)? This pipeline reconstructs that environment in 3D from the RGB pixels alone — no LiDAR, no depth sensor, no paid software. It runs end-to-end on a modest GPU (built and tested on a **6 GB RTX 3050**).

![Fly-through of a 3D Gaussian Splat reconstructed from a walking video](docs/splat-flythrough.gif)

*A photorealistic 3D Gaussian Splat, reconstructed from an ordinary walking video and rendered as a fly-through. [Try the live result in your browser ↓](#what-you-can-do-with-the-splat)*

It produces three increasingly impressive outputs:

| Output | Tool | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Point cloud + camera path** | COLMAP | the raw 3D structure + an animated camera that retraces the walk |
| **Colored mesh** | OpenMVS + Blender | a watertight-ish surface you can edit, retopo, relight |
| **3D Gaussian Splat** | [Brush](https://github.com/ArthurBrussee/brush) | a *photorealistic* scene you can fly through (sky, foliage, reflections — no holes) |

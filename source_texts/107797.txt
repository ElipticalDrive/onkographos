Fixed several remaining issues for volumetric clouds:
Resolved empty sky ghosting by invalidating history and setting alpha to 1.0 when current path density is below 0.01 in cloud_temporal_reprojection.comp.
Fixed backwards regional coverage mapping and converted Voronoi SDF coordinates to meters in cloud_weather_bake.comp.
Implemented a beautifully sculpted superellipsoid dome profiling, adjusted vertical center, and increased cloud baseline height/thickness in cloud_utils.glsl.
Scaled down the 3D volume noise frequency and removed vertical tiling to prevent high-frequency noise from hiding cloud visibility.

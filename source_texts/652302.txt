sdf.rs:
- elongate(p, h, sdf): stretch any SDF along axes without distortion
- onion(d, thickness): hollow-shell operator (abs(d) - thickness)
- vesica_2d: vesica piscis / lens shape SDF
- star_2d: n-pointed star polygon SDF (sector fold + segment SDF)
- box_frame_3d: IQ sdBoxFrame — wireframe box with edge-tube radius
- solid_angle_3d: IQ sdSolidAngle — spherical cap / pie-wedge

math.rs:
- Mat2::identity, scale, transpose, determinant, inverse, Mul operator
- quadratic_solve: returns ([f32;2], usize) sorted roots
- cubic_solve: Cardano + trigonometric method, returns ([f32;3], usize)
- kochanek_bartels: TCB spline (tension/continuity/bias), reduces to Catmull-Rom at (0,0,0)
- bezier_cubic_split: de Casteljau subdivision into two sub-curves

A simple demonstration of four vertical planes; when moving the viewpoint, you can see the content in the camera image change.
Currently, the demo machine generates only 4 frames per second, which looks a bit flickery, and the spatial resolution is only 64x64x32, but in theory it can be made very high, enough for AAA games.
Using reflective film or retroreflective glass can make the image appear in midair and be touchable, which is more of a gimmick (the experiment used cheap 3M reflective film, and the effect was a bit blurry, so I won't bring it out to show).
For more information: hackaday.io/project/194714-volumetric-display-using-rotating-mirror

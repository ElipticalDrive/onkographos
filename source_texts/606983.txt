# DCE-MVSNet

Details are described in our paper:
> Unsupervised Multi-View Stereo with a Learnable Depth Confidence Evaluator via Adaptive Volume Rendering
>
> Liangliang Li, Guihua Liu, Feng Xu, Wenjin Liao

In this paper, we propose a novel method, DCE-MVSNet, which designs the neural rendering network as a learnable depth confidence evaluator to provide reliable training targets for the MVS network.

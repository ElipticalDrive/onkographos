Abstract:
Neural Volume Rendering (NVR) has achieved remarkable progress in recent years, yet its unique computational demands pose challenges for deployment. Our observation reveals that inherent similarity-based sparsity among adjacent sampling points in NVR models creates substantial computational redundancy. This critical insight enables novel opportunities for dramatically improving NVR rendering efficiency through systematic sparsity exploitation. This paper proposes ZeroTetris, a spatial feature similarity-based sparse multilayer perceptron (MLP) accelerator for NVR. ZeroTetris efficiently bypasses the computation of zero activations without precision loss, thereby enhancing energy efficiency and computational speed. To further optimize performance, we introduce an adjustable lossy acceleration technique that approximates select non-zero values as sparse, providing an optional, user-tunable trade-off between accuracy and computation time. Additionally, we propose a multilevel cache architecture for multi-core ZeroTetris systems, minimizing on-chip memory requirements and mitigating data access conflicts. Fabricated in 28nm CMOS technology, the prototype ZeroTetris chip operates at 50 MHz ∼ 125 MHz, occupies 3.18 mm² of silicon area, and consumes 25.92 mW ∼ 139.59 mW. It achieves a lossless equivalent energy efficiency of 9.01 TOPS/W, surpassing existing accelerators in both area and energy efficiency, demonstrating its effectiveness for sparse MLP operations in NVR workloads.

NanoVDB GPU Rendering Layer | umfieldrobotics/slim-vdb | DeepWiki
Jun 26, 2026The NanoVDB GPU rendering layer provides a high-performance, CUDA-accelerated visualization subsystem for SLIM-VDB volumes. It utilizes the NanoVDB library—a linearized, read-only representation of OpenVDB trees—to perform ray-casting against TSDF and semantic grids directly on the GPU.

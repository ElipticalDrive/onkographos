Summary
Adds four original NEXORA atlas workflows grounded in the current engine/render/src/atlas.rs, CPU reference path, and renderer WGSL contract:
nexora-atlas-mip-footprint-selection — footprint-to-mip selection, per-tile mip isolation and boundary testing.
nexora-atlas-face-texel-orientation-contract — all six face mappings, vertical orientation, greedy repetition and column-only mirroring.
nexora-atlas-face-variant-determinism — stable block/face variant selection across remesh, streaming and worker order.
nexora-atlas-cpu-shader-reference-parity — CPU/WGSL parity for texel selection, sRGB/linear operations, face shade, AO and haze.
These workflows validate and preserve the current renderer contracts. They do not claim new runtime features or that NEXORA engine tests were executed during authoring.

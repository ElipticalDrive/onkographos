PDF Ray Casting Algebraic Surfaces using the Frustum Form
We propose an algorithm for interactive ray-casting of algebraic surfaces of high degree. A key point of our approach is a polynomial form adapted to the view frustum. This so called frustum form yields simple ex-pressions for the Bernstein form of the ray equations, which can be computed efficiently using matrix products and pre-computed quantities.

Stacked on #189; merge that first. This is step 1 of the plan agreed on 2026-10-06: fusion is culled to the scanning cameras, marching cubes to the viewing camera, and incremental meshing goes.
Why
Nothing live used it. fuse_viewer, rig_viewer and the shipping iOS scanner all run with a 3-slot ring and spans off, a configuration the incremental path refused. Only fuse_replica --incremental and an iOS benchmark mode turned it on.
Its win was never measured. room0 caps it near 1.2×, and the iPad's ~4× is a ceiling estimate, not a timed extract.
It is what made view culling complicated: the culled overload had to withhold arena state, and the span table needed generations, stamps and anchors. Culling to the viewing camera, which comes next, makes an extract cost the visible region instead.
Removed (−3,028 / +437 lines)
extract_device_incremental, MarchingCubesConfig::track_block_spans, and the span table behind block_spans() / block_span_valid() / block_spans_generation().
The arena watermark and occupancy logic, and ExtractTimings::incremental / remeshed_blocks.
In both sparse kernels: the span writes, the stamp dilation, in-place reuse and the retire pass, and their bindings and push constants.
fuse_replica --incremental, the dead GLSL tick_after, and their tests.
Kept
share_vertices and the slot ring.
The culled BlockList overload and its density guard; the next PR replaces it.
The changed stamp and all its writers. The iOS scanner reads it, and fuse_replica --dirty-every measures it.
volume_resize_test keeps two topology_epoch contracts the span tests used to cover.
Downstream
The iOS scanner's incremental_benchmark mode (Fusion.mm, Readout.mm) must go before it re-pins this recon. This is recorded in the new DECISIONS entry, which supersedes the 2026-08-09 and 2026-08-11 incremental entries.
Testing
M5 Max, macOS, Release, warnings as errors, with Orbbec and FFmpeg: 55/55 tests pass.
Sync validation is clean on recon_mesh_marching_cubes_sparse, recon_mesh_marching_cubes_config and recon_texture_device_mesh.
Reviewed under four lenses, with two skeptics per finding; all 12 confirmed findings are fixed.

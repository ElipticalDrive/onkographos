To simulate large-scale virtual foreign scene,after comparing all kinds of algorithms of geometric data management,and based on the theories of Octree,pre-clipping algorithms of hierarchical bounding volume +linear edge function were synthetically adopted to realize the real-time rendering of large-scale virtual forest scene. Some rules were gained that different dividing manners of Octree affected the efficiency of scene rendering through experimentation and the result proves that adopting the algorithms of hierarchical bounding volume + linear edge function to eliminate the data out of pre-clipping volume is feasible. And the algorithm is adapted to the condition that the height of scene data is even,the geometric data management and its real-time rendering.

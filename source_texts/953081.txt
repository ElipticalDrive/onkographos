Description
Added two new plugins, the piecewise medium and piecewise volumetric integrator, that when used together expose the medium as an heterogeneous 1D grid that can be sampled and evaluated using analytical means.
Testing
Created a test suite for the piecewise medium sampling and transmittance evaluation functions
Quantitavily assessed differences between heterogeneous+volpath and piecewise+piecewise_volpath.

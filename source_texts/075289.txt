Collaborator
ConvertVTKToUSD gains an opt-in output_tet_mesh flag that writes a
tetrahedral unstructured grid as a UsdGeomTetMesh (tet indices, USD-computed
surface faces, per-frame points and displayColor) instead of its extracted
surface. Default behavior is unchanged.
Tutorial 18 now exports the tet mesh to USD, colors it by log10 von Mises
stress with a dark-red-to-yellow colormap, smooths the nodal stress over mesh
edges, and median-filters only the rendered surface so smoothing cannot
invert tetrahedra.
Summary by CodeRabbit
New Features
Added support for exporting tetrahedral volume meshes directly to USD as UsdGeom.TetMesh.
Added an option to preserve tetrahedral connectivity and bypass surface extraction.
Added validation for tetrahedra-only input meshes and documented incompatibilities with surface conversion and masking.
Improvements
Enhanced heart-physics tutorials with clearer logarithmic stress visualization, smoothing, filtering, and improved stress coloring.
Reduced distracting deprecation warnings during surface remeshing.

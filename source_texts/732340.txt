Description
Matrix3D.recompose with Orientation3D.QUATERNION replaces the stub with the native path. A quaternion that is not unit length throws ArgumentError #2004 and leaves the matrix unchanged, matching Flash Player 32.0.0.465. The check is the f32 sum of squares against 1.0 ± 1e-5, and the rotation matrix is built in f32. decompose normalizes the extracted quaternion so a round trip stays inside that range.
Testing
avm2/matrix3d_recompose_quaternion recomposes translation, rotation and scale for unit quaternions, including the acceptance boundary, and asserts ArgumentError swf: Use lzma-rs for LZMA SWFs (fix #405) #2004 with the matrix left unchanged for non-unit, NaN and infinite values. output.txt was captured from Flash Player 32.0.0.465.
avm2/matrix3d_recompose_edge_cases now matches Flash, so known_failure and output.ruffle.txt are removed.

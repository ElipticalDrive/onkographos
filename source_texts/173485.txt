Need Help!

The density of the simulation I think is 256.

Volumes are difficult to render for each software. The higer the density of the volume is, the longer it will take to render. And if the smoke gets more every frame, it will also take more time to render. There is basically nothing to do about this exept lowering the density of the smoke.

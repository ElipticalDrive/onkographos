Evaluating VR Collaboration[Related Work]
Spring 2023, Dave Song
Purpose of this page
This is a page where we explore existing evaluation tools and methods for collaborative VR and AR systems. Developing a standard evaluation tool for VR collaboration quality for project 2, I realized that developing a database where students can access the current state of evaluation tools and methods on VR collaboration is essential to build more accurate vr evaluation. This list of papers/research reviews is meant to grow over time as students explore and research a similar or related topic.

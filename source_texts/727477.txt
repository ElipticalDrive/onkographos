Motivation
pyvista.Transform composes affine transformations, but pyvista-wasm only had the axis-aligned rotate_x/rotate_y/rotate_z forms. The general rotation about an arbitrary axis was missing from the VTK::CommonMath mapping.
What changed
Transform.rotate(vector, angle) — Rodrigues' rotation formula about an arbitrary (non-normalized) axis, matching PyVista's multiply_mode='post' composition order

Closes #775

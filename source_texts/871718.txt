Hi, I am testing both original 3DGS and Hierarchial 3DGS on large scene dataset.
I preprocess with colmap and try to train a single chunk with both hierarchial 3dgs and original 3dgs. However, after training the output quality of hierarchial 3dgs is much better compared to original 3dgs.
I could visualize there are many floaters in original 3dgs while hierarchial 3dgs show very clear rendering.
I use the same hyperparameters that was provided in hierarchial 3dgs in original 3dgs.
Rest of them are exactly the same, same dataset etc.
I wonder if hierarchial gaussian renderer is better than original 3dgs renderer?
I could not find why original 3dgs looks worse with floating effects and large gaussians not fine optimized at the end of training.
Could you please provide more information on this?

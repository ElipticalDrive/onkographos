💡 What: Added viewport bounding box culling (with 5% padding) in BuildingRenderer.ts and RoadRenderer.ts.
🎯 Why: Previously, on every camera movement (pan/zoom), all building polygon vertices and road segment endpoints across the entire dataset were projected (map.project()) and rendered with PixiJS graphics commands, even when 90%+ of them were off-screen.
📊 Impact: Eliminates thousands of unnecessary coordinate projection calls and PixiJS drawing commands per camera frame when zoomed in, significantly reducing main-thread CPU overhead during panning/zooming.
🔬 Measurement: Verified with pnpm test and pnpm build.

Context
The legacy project selects voxels with Physics.Raycast against the MeshCollider of each chunk (TerrainHolder.GetHitChunkAndVoxelPositionAtRaycast). The app has no physics colliders. A voxel ray (DDA) reads the voxel data directly and needs no collider.
Goal
The app finds the voxel at the center of the screen and the empty position in front of it.
Tasks
Add a voxel ray function to the core library (the DDA algorithm of Amanatides and Woo).
Return these values:
The solid voxel that the ray hits.
The face that the ray crosses.
The empty voxel before the hit.
Stop the ray at a maximum distance (default: 64 voxels) or at the world limits.
Draw a box around the selected voxel.
Add tests:
Rays along each axis, in the two directions.
Diagonal rays.
Rays that cross a chunk border.
Rays with negative coordinates.
Acceptance criteria
The tests pass.
In the app, the box follows the voxel at the screen center.

Abstract
An Expression for the expected value of the intersection of a sure sphere and a random sphere has been
derived by Laurent (1974). In the present paper we derive the expression for the expected intersection
volume of a sure ellipsoid and a random ellipsoid.
Keywords and phrases: Expected value; Sure ellipsoid; Random field; Noncentral Chi
square distribution; Bessel function

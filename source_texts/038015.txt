Why
rendering-post-processing / "Volumetric fog" was exempt (exempt:m11e). The tree had the froxel distribution, the Henyey-Greenstein phase and the slice integral, but nothing filled a volume, lit a medium through a shadow map, or applied fog to a surface.
What
src/rendering/fog/ (new module)
medium.h: a height fog given as a Koschmieder visibility plus a scale height, and authored box, sphere, cylinder and cone volumes, each with extinction, albedo, emission and HG g.
volume.h: the host froxel march. It samples the directional shadow map at fixed sub-steps and integrates each one with integrate_froxel. single_scattering_reference is a separate brute-force integral.
shaders/fog_march.slang: the device march, a statement-for-statement copy of the host one. It has two variants: a volume texture for the forward pass, and the atmosphere's froxel table with the fog composited in. src/fog_spirv.h and src/fog_msl.h are generated by regenerate.py (Slang 2026.9.2).
FogPass: the dispatch, the persistent target, the per-frame constants, and the shadow map and atmosphere table as declared reads.
Forward frame: FramePassKind::VolumetricFog runs after the shadow pass and before opaque. PostChainConfig::volumetric_fog turns it on and is off by default.
cy/frame.slang / FrameViewData: volumetric_fog_control is appended at offset 512, giving a 528-byte block. cy/volumetric_fog.slang does the lookup and is exactly the identity for an empty volume. The frame's committed SPIR-V and MSL are regenerated. Run on main's source, the same invocations reproduce main's headers byte for byte.
Samples: samples/12-beauty --fog on|off and samples/10-world --fog. The capture recipes are just capture-volumetric-fog and just capture-world-fog.
Fixes found while building
The harness has no CY_REQUIRE_GT, so the device suite did not compile. It now uses CY_REQUIRE(a > b).
Three host cases marched whole columns and took 1.5 to 18 ms of CPU, over unit's 1 ms budget. They now live in integration.rendering_fog_march.
Shafts case: froxels inside the floor slab or a box counted as "shadowed" by geometry, but the shadow pass culls front faces, so the map lights a solid's inside. No surface lookup can see fog inside a solid, so the case now counts only froxels in open air. The thresholds are unchanged, and 100 % of froxels are on the right side of each.
samples/12-beauty (existing code): passes bound descriptor sets before any graphics pipeline. The RHI binds sets at the last-bound pipeline's bind point, so after the fog's compute march, every scene draw had set 0 unbound. That produced 93 validation errors with --fog on. Pipelines are now bound first.
Both samples now destroy FogPass before the device. 10-world --fog used to abort in VMA at exit.
capture-world-fog passed no --frames, so the sample drew nothing. The recipe now passes it.
The march was renamed from volumetric_fog.slang to fog_march.slang because the shader set resolves imports by basename and reported a collision with cy/volumetric_fog.slang.
How it was verified (Vulkan, RTX 5060)
Branch is rebased on current main. Debug and dev both build clean. just build-shaders --strict src samples reports target_refusals=0 disagreements=0 failures=0. clang-format and clang-tidy report no findings on the touched sources.
render.volumetric_fog, in both profiles:
(a) Off is byte-identical to references/volumetric_fog_off.png. That reference was rendered with CY_RENDER_UPDATE_GOLDEN=1 while frame_spirv.h was still main's, and it still matches after the shader was regenerated.
(b) An empty medium is byte-identical to off, and all 81600 froxels are exactly one and zero.
(c) Of the open-air froxels the map covers, all 3674 wholly shadowed ones scatter under 10 % of a lit froxel's sunlight, and all 59339 open ones scatter over 90 %.
(d) Worst error 8.2e-4 against the single-scattering integral and 3.8e-5 against the host march.
(e) Through an absorbing fog, the floor keeps 0.64 of its brightness near the camera and 0.32 far away, with no channel brighter.
(f) The table variant is within 7.1e-5 of the host march.
Each device case was proved red by a shader mutation, then restored and md5-verified. The record is openspec/changes/add-volumetric-fog/evidence/falsification.txt.
Also passing in both profiles: unit.rendering_fog, integration.rendering_fog_march, integration.rendering_fog_air, unit.render_forward, render.pipeline, render.golden, render.golden_backends, render.frames, render.soft_shadows, integration.render_soft_shadows, render.ambient_occlusion, integration.rendering_contact_shadows and render.light_probes. In dev, render.world_water, render.world_aerial_perspective, render.world_cloud_shadow and smoke.world_quiet_host_before pass too.
Captures:
Beauty off is m11c-beauty-shot.png pixel for pixel. With fog on, 2073328 of 2073600 pixels change, by at most 120 of 255, with 0 validation errors.
World frame 60: 237400 of 518400 pixels change, by at most 21 of 255. The committed 1.5 km midday haze is subtle.
Coverage: Volumetric fog now maps to test:render.volumetric_fog, with the case "froxels in an occluder's shadow scatter no sunlight and the open ones all of it". just quality-requirements rendering-post-processing reports 15/15. just quality-format-check, just quality-licence (0 new) and openspec validate --strict add-volumetric-fog pass.
Still not built (stays exempt by name in the coverage note)
Filtering and temporal reprojection.
Indirect light from the radiance cache ("Fog matches the room it is in").
Punctual and spot lights in the medium. The shafts scenario is met by the directional sun.
Density functions beyond the height fog.
The Metal MSL is generated but was not run on an Apple device.
No CTest covers the beauty sample's binding-order fix, because the sample deliberately has no CTest entry. capture-volumetric-fog fails on any validation error, and that recipe is what caught the bug.

Rebased onto main after #38 (decals) merged.
Conflicts resolved (both sides kept):
CyFrameData / FrameViewData: decalControl then volumetricFogControl. The block is now 544 bytes (decal_control at 512, volumetric_fog_control at 528), and the static_asserts say so. BeautyFrame / FrameConstants in samples/12-beauty carry decalControl then fogControl and grow to 256 bytes.
FrameDescription / FrameSinks / FrameAssembly::declare_frame: the depth of field stage and the volumetric fog stage are both wired through.
test_frame.cpp: the depth of field cases and the fog cases are both kept.
frame.slang: the decal source and applyFrameDecals, then the fog volume source.
Samples: --ground-marker and --fog in 10-world. In 12-beauty: --depth-of-field, --decals and --fog; the shot's depth-of-field and fog lines; the decal and fog shader paths; device members and teardown. The shadow pass's pipeline bind was duplicated, so only one copy is kept.
just/run.just: capture-decals, capture-volumetric-fog and capture-world-fog. READMEs (post, samples) name both features.
Headers regenerated from the merged sources with slangc 2026.9.2: frame_spirv.h, frame_msl.h and selection_{spirv,msl}.h. Before running them on this branch, I checked that the same invocations rebuild main's committed headers byte for byte from main's sources. The fog march's own headers came out unchanged. just build-shaders --strict src samples: disagreements=0, failures=0, target_refusals=0.
Suites rerun, dev and debug, all passing: unit.rendering_fog, integration.rendering_fog_march, integration.rendering_fog_air, render.volumetric_fog, render.pipeline, render.golden, render.golden_backends, render.frames, unit.render_forward, unit.render_post, render.depth_of_field, render.decals, render.bloom, render.grading, render.ambient_occlusion, render.soft_shadows, render.light_probes, render.selection_outlines, render.world_cloud_shadow, render.world_water, render.world_aerial_perspective, unit.world, unit.water. clang-format, clang-tidy on the resolved files, the licence gate, openspec validate --all --strict and just quality-specs are clean.

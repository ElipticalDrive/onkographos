Found while porting B44 (PR #120). FIXED in the port (all six faces marked); regression-tested.
In FastMarch3.h (lines 232-341), Initialize marks only the vertices and edges of the grid boundary as zero speed, not the six faces — while the 2D sibling marks its entire border and the comment says the whole boundary is meant to be excluded. Face voxels stay "far", get pulled into the front, and then index off-grid (i - mXYBound for a z=0 voxel wraps in size_t).

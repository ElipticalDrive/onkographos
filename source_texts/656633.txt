Orthographic views and ORTHO cameras now render as parallel projections, in the viewport (modeling and sculpting with perspective off) and in final renders. Two commits on main 3fd6c36: a fix (+28/−14) and its test (+34).
The bug
trace() (src/__init__.py) always built a perspective camera from the projection matrix: one ray origin at the view's position, with directions x·ndc_x + y·ndc_y + z. It never checked whether the projection is orthographic. So an orthographic window matrix was turned into a frustum:
The ortho half-width became a field of view; ws5 measured ortho_scale 2.2 giving a 95° view.
Every ray left the view's pivot, so a Blender ortho viewport's geometry behind the pivot vanished. Blender draws it, since its ortho clip start is negative.
project(), used by ReSTIR's temporal reprojection and the denoiser's flow, and the Camera/Window texture coordinates assumed a perspective divide as well.
render() and view_draw() both go through trace(), so ORTHO scene cameras and ortho viewports were equally affected.
The fix
trace(): a projection with P[3][3] = 1 is orthographic.
x and y keep their meaning (right / P[0][0], up / P[1][1]), now half-extents.
z carries the rays' offset: the near plane along the view, −(1 + P[2][3]) / P[2][2], which is behind the pivot for a viewport's negative clip start, plus the shift −x·P[0][3] − y·P[1][3].
One float in the lens tail, ortho, says which kind of camera it is.
pixelDomain, both backends: with ortho, the origin is origin + x·ndc_x + y·ndc_y + z and the direction is the view's forward axis. Perspective is unchanged.
project(), both backends: with ortho, ndc = (dot(d − z, x)/|x|², dot(d − z, y)/|y|²), with no divide.
Window coordinate, both backends: with ortho, the point on the image plane is d − z.
Lens grows from 5 to 6 floats in renderer.h and renderer.slang. That makes Scene grow, so RECORD_STRIDE goes from 560 to 576 bytes; main.cpp's static assertion measured the new size.
Thin-lens depth of field stays perspective-only, as before (lens() returns no aperture for non-PERSP cameras).
Test (src/test.py, +34)
Two emissive unit squares, one at depth 0 and one 3 units further, under an orthographic projection with ortho_scale 3, at 64 px. A parallel projection shows each as (64/3)² ≈ 455 px.
Final render through an ORTHO camera: each square must cover 455 ± 10%.
The modeling-style ortho viewport, through the test's Viewport stand-in, with a Blender ortho window matrix (clip −50 to 100): the same, plus a 0.4-unit square behind the view's pivot, which must show (at least half its 7.1² px).

src/Graphics/Vulkan/VulkanIBL.cpp:12-268, :21, :76, :83, :90, :112-130, :218-222, :265, :308
assets/shaders/lighting_pass.frag:246, :246-303, :292-301
CONFIRMS the known lead: and it is substantially worse than the lead states.
VulkanIBL creates only 1x1 images (info.extent = {1,1,1}, line 21) for irradiance (:76), prefilter (:83),
and the BRDF LUT (:90). No pixel data is ever uploaded: lines 112-130 only transition
UNDEFINED → SHADER_READ_ONLY_OPTIMAL, so the contents are undefined, not black as the lead assumed.
The IBL settings UBO is written exactly once, at :218-222, with IBLEnabled = 0. m_iblSettingsMapped
has no other writer in the repo.
m_iblReady is assigned false at :265 and :308, is never set true, and is never read.
The escalation: lighting_pass.frag:246: if (iblEnabled != 0u) gates not only the IBL terms (:292-301)
but the entire Cook-Torrance direct + indirect PBR block (:246-303). With iblEnabled permanently 0,
Vulkan deferred always falls into the else branch: "Classic non-PBR lighting: wrap diffuse + hemisphere
ambient".
So on the primary backend there is no ambient specular, no environment reflection, no metallic/roughness IBL
response, and a non-PBR direct BRDF. OpenGL by contrast bakes fully: OpenGLIBL.cpp:95-198 compiles three
bake shaders and runs BakeIrradiance (:200-244), BakePrefilter (:246-305, mips x 6 faces roughness
sweep), BakeBRDFLUT (:307-330); OpenGLRenderer.cpp:1102 sets IBLEnabled per frame.
Also: SetIBLEnabled (include/private/Graphics/Common/Renderer.hpp:168), applied from GraphicsConfig at
src/Core/Application.cpp:421, is a silent no-op on Vulkan: m_iblEnabled is referenced by no file under
src/Graphics/Vulkan/.
Severity: visual (the headline lighting model is wrong on the default backend).
Found by a full audit of the graphics layer. Assessed severity: visual: major parity gap.

Purpose
Provide the portable WebGPU substrate required by real variable-output geometry stages. It must support one large component and bounded batches of many small independent components without unchecked growth, nondeterministic topology, or silent failure.
Delivered foundation
The initial scaffold is on main:
worker-owned device lifecycle and pipeline/bind-group caching;
cancellation and device-loss invalidation;
aligned point/triangle/plane/mesh/intersection/hull/output layouts;
authoritative Python layout definitions and generated WGSL drift checks;
bounded one-workgroup scan, compaction, segmented scan, and fixed-tree reduction;
NumPy references and GPU worker/dispatch/golden tests.
This foundation is intentionally incomplete. src/chitin/gpu/README.md defers GPU-resident arenas until the first geometry stage can exercise them.
Remaining work bundled with #93
GPU mesh, output, and scratch arenas with explicit descriptors and bounds.
Multi-workgroup prefix and segmented scans.
Stable compaction, deterministic sort/unique, and source-edge key processing.
Queues for single-large, multipart, fragment-batch, and uneven tile-shaped work.
Exact output counts, capacity admission, and reusable scratch allocation.
Hardware execution evidence on the NVIDIA/AMD/Apple cells required by the gate.
First-mismatch reporting across NumPy reference and WGSL results.
The next G1/G2 PR completes this work together with #93, #103, and #120 so the infrastructure is validated by an end-to-end classify/clip/weld/cap operation.
Boundaries
Do not allocate GPU MCTS tree, UCB, RNG, or cost-graph structures here.
Do not add renderer shaders or renderer ownership to Chitin.
No CPU geometry repair or unbounded fallback allocation.
Capacity, cancellation, device loss, and shader compilation failure cannot produce a successful result.
Acceptance
No host/WGSL stride mismatch or unchecked arena write.
CPU CI verifies generation, layouts, references, and artifact drift without a GPU.
Scans, compaction, sort/unique, and reductions operate beyond one workgroup deterministically.
Single-large and many-small allocation/queue fixtures remain bounded.
Cancellation, device loss, overflow, and shader failure cannot look successful.
Hardware primitives pass on the NVIDIA/AMD/Apple cells required by the G1/G2 gate.
WebGPU CoACD G2: robust GPU clipping and deterministic cap topology #93 consumes the arenas and primitives without a parallel dispatch implementation.

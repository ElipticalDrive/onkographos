Resolution
Written as Spec: Smoke screens - the smoke grenade, the Map's voxel grid, the flood fill and the ray-marched cloud.
Decided while writing it, with the user:
No smoke round for the underbarrel launcher: Held item 3 and Explosion kind 3 are the thrown smoke grenade alone.
The canister stays a Prop after the burst and is removed when its Smoke screen is gone; the cloud's source stays the burst point.
The timeline is fixed by the Parameters alone (clearing at grow + hold, gone at grow + hold + clear), so the server knows when to remove the canister without the grid; the loader refuses a reach the front speed cannot cover in the growing time.
Every time is integer milliseconds (integer square root for the ease-out), so the fill module's output is bit-identical and golden-tested.
A new pure Smoke screen module is the one new test seam; PresentationWorld, SimulationWorld, the lavapipe pixel probes and the cooker's tests are the others.
An Agent gets only the Explosion event; its view is never hidden by smoke.
Depends on the deferred frame (Spec: the deferred frame and Looks - UsdPreviewSurface, sun and sky, translucency and the HDR image #827) and ray-traced lighting (Spec: ray-traced lighting - shadows, local lights, reflections and indirect light #828) specs as well as the Fragmentation grenade (Spec: Fragmentation grenade - the Loadout, cooking and throwing, blast and Fragments #862) and Effects (Spec: Effects - one catalogue of particles, decals and brief lights #860) specs.

Step 5 of the terrain roadmap:

replace the compacted voxel-cube draw with a smooth marching-cubes surface
add the complete classic 256-case lookup table
classify every cell and store its case plus triangle count on the GPU
allocate each active cell's output range with one atomic operation
interpolate surface vertices and analytic normals in compute
render compute-written position and normal storage attributes with one indirect draw
keep zero GPU-to-CPU position, normal, triangle-count, or geometry readbacks
preserve the WebGL fallback, heightfield editor, object placement, saves, and GLB rendering
add lookup-table, capacity, source-contract, and no-readback regression tests

This remains one procedural chunk. Editable voxel stamps, multiple resident chunks, border ownership, LOD transitions, and heightfield handoff are intentionally deferred.

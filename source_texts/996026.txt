GodotVoxel with Rounded Edges
This fork of Zylann's Voxel Tools for Godot adds optional rounded edges and corners to blocky terrain, including the underside.
Rounding is off by default: enable rounding_enabled on selected cube models in the existing VoxelBlockyLibrary or VoxelBlockyTypeLibrary arrays.
Shared library controls set the bevel amount, transition length, and linear or nonlinear taper profile. Rounded corner caps blend into connected edges, while blocked contacts taper back to square edges.
Use rounding_affects_neighbors to let decorations or water count as air for neighboring bevels while keeping those models unrounded. The feature supports full unit cubes and compatible imported cube meshes; other shapes retain their original geometry.

Yes, Astra 6 was used substantially for the implementation and documentation. I directed the design, with human review and editing afterward.
So, only cubes are supported then? In mesh models, you only look at the side geometries and check that they are full quads? Do you then store pre-rounded geometries in side data?
The current implementation supports full unit cubes only, including cubes supplied as mesh models. It validates six full two-triangle sides and rejects interior geometry. It keeps the original side data rather than storing pre-rounded sides: neighborhood-dependent geometry is generated during meshing and cached, with UVs and materials mapped from the original faces.
If that means there must not be any jump in UVs between two connected faces, that's quite a big limitation, as it forces the atlas to be laid out in a particular way and maybe even duplicate some tiles, doesn't it?
This may be a slightly misleading phrasing in my documentation that reflects an intended plan from earlier, but does not properly explain how it's done now. Apologies, I'll fix. The consistency requirement applies between the two triangles of an individual face, not between adjacent faces. Faces can use separate atlas tiles; the current check does reject a UV discontinuity along the diagonal within one face.

Summary
Implement geometry assertion utilities and LOD level inspection for Optimum greedy meshing and chiseled block quad-decimated level of detail.
Motivation & Context
Part of Milestone 6 (Optimum & Engine Verification). Optimum integrates a greedy chunk mesher to merge coplanar block faces and a chiseled block LOD system that simplifies intricate micro-voxels down to 12 triangles at distance. Pharos must verify vertex reductions, UV coordinate correctness, and seamless LOD transitions without vertex cracks.
Technical Requirements & Architecture
Add MeshInspector to extract and inspect raw vertex and index buffers from loaded chunk meshes.
Implement quad merging assertions Assert.MeshFaceCountReduced(ChunkPos pos, int minReductionPercent) to verify greedy mesher efficiency.
Implement UV map correctness assertions verifying that texture atlas coordinates wrap and repeat correctly on merged quads.
Provide ChiseledBlockLodInspector to query current LOD level (LOD0 through LOD3) and verify distance threshold transitions as the camera moves.
Acceptance Criteria
MeshInspector extracts vertex and index buffers from chunk meshes in headless memory.
Assertions verify greedy quad reduction ratios on synthetic chunk fixtures.
UV mapping assertions detect stretched or misaligned texture coordinates on merged block faces.
Chiseled block LOD levels transition predictably based on camera distance thresholds.

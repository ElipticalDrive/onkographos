This project develops and evaluates advanced 3D generation techniques leveraging both Neural 
Radiance Fields (NeRF) and 3D Gaussian Splatting, two breakthrough approaches that represent 
scenes using continuous representations with superior multi-view consistency compared to 
GANs and traditional computer vision techniques.  
While NeRF uses implicit neural representations with volumetric rendering, Gaussian Splatting 
employs explicit 3D Gaussian primitives with differentiable rasterization, achieving dramatically 
faster training (3-4 hours vs 10-15 hours for NeRF) and real-time rendering capabilities. 
Conducting a comprehensive literature review examining the evolution from classical 
reconstruction methods through NeRF to the recent Gaussian Splatting advancement. The 
implementation evaluates both approaches across multiple benchmark datasets, demonstrating 
that while NeRF achieves PSNR improvements of 3-6 dB over GAN-based methods, Gaussian 
Splatting matches or exceeds NeRF's quality while reducing training time by 70-75% and 
enabling real-time rendering at 30+ FPS. Through extensive experiments on synthetic and 
real-world scenes, by quantifying the advantages of both methods in terms of image quality 
metrics (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS), multi-view consistency, computational efficiency, and rendering 
speed, the results confirm that Gaussian Splatting represents a significant practical advancement 
for applications requiring high-fidelity 3D content generation with guaranteed multi-view 
coherence and interactive rendering performance.Bachelor's degre

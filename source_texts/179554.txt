Limited-angle cone-beam CT (LA-CBCT) reduces imaging time and dose but suffers from severe under-sampling artifacts and distortions. 2D-3D deformable registration mitigates this issue by estimating LA-CBCTs through the deformation of a prior, fully-sampled CT/CBCT, using deformation-vector-fields (DVFs) optimized by limited-angle cone-beam projections. Population-trained 2D-3D registration networks enable fast inference but face accuracy challenges, particularly under varying limited-angle scan directions. On the other hand, patient-specific models are more adaptable but typically require considerable runtimes to optimize model parameters from scratch for each case.

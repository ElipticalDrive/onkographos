Closes #704
Summary
Greedy is the only mesher. This PR removes MeshingMode, the --meshing flag, generateMeshForMode, the one-quad-per-face mesher, updateForBlockChange, swapRemoveQuad, quad_block, quad_highlight and decayHighlights.
generateMesh (the greedy mesher) writes one 16-byte PackedQuad per quad to Mesh.quads, and the y range of the quads to Mesh.y_min and Mesh.y_max. Mesh has no VoxelVertex list and no index list.
uploadChunkMeshToGPU writes the header and then Mesh.quads with no conversion. The pack_scratch copy and packQuads are gone. The frustum cull gets the y range from the mesh.
async_chunks.Result carries quads: []PackedQuad and the y range, not 4 slices.
--depth-stencil=off: sortByDepth sorts the packed quads back to front into Mesh.sorted_quads, and the upload writes that list. The centroid comes from the packed quad. Each centroid is an integer or a half integer, so the depths are the same f32 values as before. The depth_stencil_off frame is byte-identical to main.
GPU debug: one value for each chunk. uploadChunkMeshToGPU writes the debug tick of the rebuild into the buffer header (origin.y, unused before). The uniform _padding is now debug_tick (0 when GPU debug is off). rebuildTint in voxel.wgsl gives (255 - 4 × age) / 255 for 64 ticks (about 0.5 s at 120 Hz), the same fade as the old per-quad decay. The clock advances only on executing ticks in TAS step mode, as before. Cmd+G and Ctrl+G still toggle it. A fade needs no upload now. Before, GPU debug packed and uploaded every chunk every frame.
Removed tests/greedy_vs_naive.sh and .tas. Updated .claude/rules/swindowzig-voxel.md, docs/memory.md, docs/async-chunks.md, docs/chunk-size-investigation.md, scripts/benchmark.sh and comments.

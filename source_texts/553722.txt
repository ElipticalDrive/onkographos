Differentiable path tracing might be usefull in our use-cases, too. An interesting algorithm to achieve this is path replay backpropagation1 and its extension to time-resolved tracing2. Originally, it's only designed for backward tracing, but the math should work both ways.
There is currently no plan to actively implement this, but might come back to this in the future.

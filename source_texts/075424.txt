P3's plan (issue-1557.md) targets two defects observed in P1's committed screenshots (#1561): (1) round-to-cell coverage holes, (2) stale-mask "flag" trixels. After instrumenting on the P2 base (this PR is stacked on #1562), I found the premise no longer holds as written — surfacing for an architect call before investing in the heavy fix.
What I found
P2 already renders the demo's re-voxelize solids cleanly. Side-by-side, docs/pr-screenshots/.../1555-detached-revoxelize-p1/revoxelize_solids_zoom-after.png (P1) shows both solids riddled with see-through holes/speckle; on the P2 base (current claude/1556-…) the teal cube + orange L-prism render as clean solid hexagons across all 7 canvas_stress --auto-screenshot shots (camera SO(3) suite + the two re-voxelize framing shots). Re-voxelize quality now ≈ the in-scene attached-GRID solids — which is P3's stated acceptance bar ("coverage + face-emit quality ≥ the attached GRID re-voxelize path").
My defect-2 fix is byte-identical. I marked re-voxelize via the free visibleFaceIds_.w lane and dropped the model-space faceIsExposed gate in c_voxel_to_trixel_stage_{1,2} (GLSL+Metal), letting the distance buffer resolve occlusion. img_diff reports drift=0 on all 7 shots. A diagnostic (if (reVoxelize) return;) made the solids vanish, confirming the marker propagates — so the no-op is real, not a plumbing bug.
Why it's a no-op here: the model-space mask is stale for re-voxelize, but (a) rigid rotation ≈ preserves voxel adjacency, so the mask is ≈ correct on the rotated grid (mismatches only at rounding boundaries), and (b) the demo's solids are dense + uniform-color, so the distance buffer fills any gated hole from a neighbor and uniform color hides any wrong-face. The residual is occasional sub-cell speckle at rounding boundaries — i.e. the same ~3% CPU↔GPU sub-cell divergence that is the open "byte-identical tolerance" question on render: detached re-voxelize — GPU scatter fill (P2) (#1556) #1562 (unchecked review box).
Design questions
Is P3 already satisfied by P2? Re-voxelize ≈ GRID on the demo. If the bar is "≥ GRID," P3 may be closeable / re-scopeable rather than a code fix.
If defect 2 should still be hardened: the perf-preserving correct fix is to recompute the exposed mask on the rotated grid (CPU on the small detached pool, or a GPU occupancy probe). My committed gate-skip is not that — it over-emits every interior face (a perf regression on the detached pool) for zero current visible benefit. Which approach do you want?
Verification vehicle: the current dense uniform-color solids cannot reveal defect 2. Exposing it needs a multi-color or sparse re-voxelize solid in canvas_stress (or a P5-style harness). Should that land first so any fix is verifiable?
The "no coverage holes/specks" criterion bottoms out on the sub-cell tolerance ruling pending on render: detached re-voxelize — GPU scatter fill (P2) (#1556) #1562.

The gate-skip is committed as the investigation starting point (byte-identical, captures the "mask is stale for re-voxelize" insight). Discard or evolve it per your call on Q2. Moving to fleet:design-blocked, releasing the claim so any worker can resume once re-armed.

NEEDS-DESIGN (re-escalation)
Implemented re-scoped Step 1 (multi-color verification solid) and Step 2 (rotated-grid exposed-mask recompute, gate-skip discarded). Step 2 works as specified — but building it on the discriminating multi-color solid surfaced a coupling the plan's premise doesn't hold for. Surfacing before investing in Step 3 / coverage.
What I built
Step 1: the asymmetric L-prism in canvas_stress is now multi-color (each voxel tinted by model position) + carved — a mis-gated face reads as a color break / black hole instead of being hidden by a uniform fill. The dense teal cube stays uniform as the control.
Step 2: SYSTEM_REBUILD_DETACHED_VOXELS recomputes the per-voxel exposed mask every frame against the rotated destination cells (face_occupancy::recomputeFaceOccupancyOnCells), rotating the rigid locals about the pool origin with worldCellForGridVoxel (now roundHalfUp) so the mask cells match the GPU scatter's cells byte-for-byte. STAGE_1 keeps its normal visible-triplet × exposed-mask gate (no over-emit). Bounded to the small detached pool.
The finding — coverage and the exposed-mask are coupled
Same fixed pose (--no-spin), before = stale model-space mask, after = recomputed mask:
before (stale mask)
after (recompute)
diff (red = changed)
The recompute fixes per-face colors (the diff's scattered changes are stale wrong-faces corrected).
But it re-exposes aliasing coverage holes: +1.9% black-pixel holes on the L's surface — and on the previously-clean uniform teal cube (img_diff: 0.30% of frame, max_delta 231). The cube is the one you cited as 'renders cleanly' under P2.
Mechanism: the model-space mask's over-emission is load-bearing for coverage. Round-to-cell re-voxelization of a rotated solid leaves sub-cell surface gaps (defect 1). The stale mask emits the whole model surface, so behind-voxels' faces fill those gaps (the distance buffer keeps the nearest) → no holes, but occasionally a slightly-wrong color at a gap. Recomputing the mask to emit only the true rotated-grid surface fixes the color but stops filling the gaps → holes. P2's 'clean coverage' was a side effect of that over-emission, not an independent property.
Two plan premises don't hold
'GRID resolves it by re-probing neighbours on the re-voxelized world cells.' SYSTEM_REBUILD_GRID_VOXELS does not re-probe — it only rewrites positions and keeps the authored model-space mask (over-emission). So GRID's 0-hole coverage comes from the same over-emission; GRID on multi-color content would show the same wrong-faces.
'Discard the gate-skip — it over-emits every interior face = perf regression for zero current benefit.' The over-emission's benefit is exactly the coverage fill. So we can't both drop over-emission and hold the '≥ GRID coverage / no holes' acceptance bar — the perf cost buys the coverage.
The decision I can't make
Correct-colors and ≥GRID-coverage are now in tension; closing both needs an approach choice:
A — Keep over-emission (gate-skip), accept gap-color approximation. Empirically the wrong-color is subtle for smooth/gradient content (a gap fills with an adjacent voxel's near-correct color); only high-contrast neighbours read as wrong. Cheapest; reverts Step 2.
B — Correct mask + conservative coverage (dilate the re-voxelize face footprint, à la render: conservative coverage closes per-axis scatter sliver waffle (#1494) #1523's scatter fix) so adjacent surface faces overlap and fill gaps with the correct colour. Fully correct, but a raster/shader change gated to re-voxelize canvases (new infra + Metal parity).
C — Correct mask + supersample the re-voxelization (higher pool density) so gaps fall below the sub-cell tolerance. Demo-tunable now; doesn't fully eliminate.
D — Correct mask, accept ~2% scattered speckle as the Step-3 tolerance (lowers the ≥GRID bar; ships correct colours + bounded holes).
My lean: B for the principled result, or D if a ~2% sub-cell speckle is an acceptable tolerance and correct-colour > zero-hole on multi-colour content. A is viable if the wrong-colour concern is judged minor for realistic (non-adversarial-contrast) entities.
WIP (Step 1 + Step 2) is pushed as the resume base. — opus-worker

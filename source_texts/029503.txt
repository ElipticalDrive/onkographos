Context
Bunny has ray-sphere, ray-AABB, ray-triangle, and BVH traversal, but ray tracing needs a broader primitive suite and a consistent hit-record model.
Requested outcome
Define and implement a deterministic ray tracing intersection layer.
Acceptance criteria
Define canonical RayHit data: distance, point, normal, primitive id, and tie-breaking.
Add intersections for plane, segment/capsule, OBB, mesh triangle records, and future primitives as scoped.
Define nearest-hit vs any-hit behavior.
Add native and WASM golden tests for hit ordering and grazing cases.

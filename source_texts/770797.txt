Week 7 of 52 · cache track · Mon 14 Dec 2026 to Sun 20 Dec 2026
Why
cachet sends whatever encoding the origin returns and has no compression, so bytes on the wire are often 3 to 5 times larger than needed and Accept-Encoding fragments the cache. This week stores gzip, brotli and zstd as variants with normalised Accept-Encoding, adds Compression Dictionary Transport (RFC 9842) for shared dictionaries, and emits 103 Early Hints from cached Link headers. New dependencies: flate2, brotli and zstd, the reference implementations, behind a compression feature. Closes: CA6 Compression variants (gzip, brotli, zstd, dictionaries); CA7 103 Early Hints.
Done when
A text object compressible by brotli is stored once per encoding and served as br, zstd or gzip according to Accept-Encoding, with Vary normalised to at most 4 variants
With a shared dictionary advertised by Use-As-Dictionary, a repeat JavaScript bundle download shrinks by at least 60% over plain brotli in the test fixture
103 Early Hints are sent for cached HTML with preload Link headers, measured as a first-byte gain of at least the origin think time in the e2e test
Compression CPU per MiB is reported and on-the-fly compression is skipped above a configurable CPU budget
Tasks
Accept-Encoding parsing and normalisation in crates/edgefront-cache/src/encoding.rs with q-values, mapping requests to one of identity, gzip, br, zstd and a fuzz target for the parser
Compressed variants in crates/edgefront-cache/src/variants.rs: compress on fill in a blocking pool, store each encoding as its own variant under the same primary key, and evict variants together
Compression Dictionary Transport in crates/edgefront-cache/src/dictionary.rs: Use-As-Dictionary capture, Available-Dictionary matching, dcb and dcz encodings, dictionary storage with TTL
Early Hints in crates/edgefront-cache/src/early_hints.rs: record Link preload headers on stored HTML and send 103 before revalidation or origin fetch completes
CPU budget and metrics in crates/edgefront-cache/src/encoding.rs: bytes in and out per encoding, compression ratio histogram, skip compression when the blocking pool queue exceeds its limit
Tests in tests/compression.rs covering variant selection, dictionary round trips against fixtures, Early Hints order and identity fallback for clients without support
End-to-end demo: examples/compression.sh fetches a bundle twice with a dictionary and prints bytes saved; README, docs/DESIGN.md (variants) and CHANGELOG updated

Display 3-D Volumetric Images
Display 3-D renderings or 2-D cross-sections of a volumetric image, and adjust
the rendering to improve the visibility of features within the volume
You can display a 3-D volumetric image by rendering the volume in 3-D space.
To improve the visibility of features within the volume, you can move the camera
position within the scene, adjust the transparency of the data, or change
rendering styles. You can also expose interior features by displaying 2-D cross
sections of the volume. For more information, see Choose Approach to Display 2-D and 3-D Images.
Use the Volume Viewer app to view and
explore volumetric intensity data and volumetric labeled data. Volume
Viewer includes tools that you can use to examine volumetric data
interactively.

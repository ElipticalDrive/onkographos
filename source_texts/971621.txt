Original report
Summary
The Fracture has a fog volume it uses to simulate the opacity of water in a pond. It’s a box that goes partially under the ground.
When approaching the area, I noticed that the outline of the fog volume was very bright and visible through the ground.
I lifted the fog volume up, and indeed there was still a thin pixel-wide outline along the borders of the box. However, that outline looked somewhat too pixelated.
Curious, I checked my Renderide settings and remembered I had MSAA on at 4x. I tried disabling it (setting it to 1x), and lo and behold: the outline disappeared! And conversely, the outline appears in all other values of MSAA > 1x.
The first time I have seen this outline was weeks ago, so I don’t think it’s a regression, however it’s only today that I discovered that MSAA was at the root of it.
Steps / context
Visit The Fracture
Go through the portal and fly forward towards the Rift, you’ll see a green glowy pond below you
If you have MSAA disabled at 1x, everything looks normal.
If you enable any higher level of MSAA, you can notice how there’s the green outline of a box visible through the ground.
Expected / actual
Expected: Fog volumes do not have visible outlines, especially if they’re mostly buried underground.
Actual: MSAA causes such an outline to happen.

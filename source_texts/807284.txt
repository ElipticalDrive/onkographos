Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs)
●
●
Inverse rendering: Learning a 3D scene from 2D images
NeRF [Mildenhall et al. 2020]
○ Differentiable volumetric rendering
○ Outgoing radiance
○ Large MLPs (~10h training, <0.1 FPS)
Source: Mildenhall et al., “NeRF: Representing Scenes as Neural Radiance Fields for View Synthesis”. ECCV (2020)

●
●
Unbounded, anti-aliased NeRF: Mip-NeRF 360 [Barron et al., 2022]

Instant-NGP [Müller et al. 2022]
○ Multi-resolution hash encoding
○ Occupancy grids for empty space skipping
○ Fast training and rendering (5-10 min. training, ~10 FPS)

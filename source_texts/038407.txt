3D display of medical images is one of the hot focus in the field of medical imaging today,which can provides physi cians with the patient's condition information intuitively,comprehensively and accurately.VTK is an international widely using open source visualization toolkit,which has excellent framework and operation mechanism.Through the research of the DI COM medicine image and the using of VTK technology,we examination the medical image display of the cross-sectional,coro nal,sagittal plane and 3D reconstruction method.This method has a certain practical value for the study of the 3D medical image volume rendering data.

Lighting is pretty flat at the moment:
Indoors: clustered ceiling point lights plus ambient.
Outdoors: the sun.
There's no bounce light, so white chambers don't get the soft, bright, bounced look that Portal's baked radiosity gives them. Global illumination or ray tracing would be a big visual upgrade.
Options, cheapest first
Quick wins.
HDR + a better tonemapper (AgX / TonyMcMapface).
Bloom on emissives: lamps, portal rims, lasers, bridges, indicator lights.
SSAO (ScreenSpaceAmbientOcclusion).
An environment map / skybox for outdoor levels.
Tune ambient per level.
Baked GI. Bevy supports lightmaps (Lightmap), irradiance volumes and reflection probes. Levels are built in Rust code, so baking would need our own step at level-build time, e.g.:
a simple CPU path tracer over the level's boxes (cheap: everything is axis-aligned boxes), or
Bevy's ray tracer.
Moving things (cubes, doors, bridges) would still need dynamic lighting on top.
Real-time ray-traced GI. Bevy has an experimental hardware ray tracer, Solari (bevy_solari, ReSTIR DI/GI). An RTX 4080 can run it. Check:
its status and Linux/Vulkan support in Bevy 0.19
which material features it supports
Portal-specific concerns
Every effect runs for each portal camera as well: up to 2 portals × recursion depth (4), so cost multiplies.
Temporal effects (TAA, ReSTIR accumulation) depend on frame-to-frame history. Portal camera views jump when a portal moves or when you teleport, so expect ghosting or smearing in portal views.
Lighting in portal views has to match the main view, or the seams show. If RT only runs on the main camera, portals look different from the real world behind them.
Light should arguably travel through portals. Portal 2 fakes this; we can decide later.
Acceptance
A visible improvement in the chambers (start with the quick wins), with a before/after capture using PORTAL_CAPTURE=shots PORTAL_SCRIPT=tour.
Graphics settings toggles for the expensive parts (Graphics settings: fullscreen/windowed, VSync, anti-aliasing #9).
No obvious lighting mismatch between portal views and the main view.

Updating the earlier note. Real-time ray tracing is in the game now (Bevy Solari), with a Graphics setting that applies on the next start. The baked one-bounce volume is no longer the only indoor light. Not closing this: the acceptance items are not all checked.
What the latest push changed (b9737fa, and a2aa2a1 before it): under ray tracing each map light is also a glowing bulb, so light-lit maps are not black. Bulbs dimmer than 80 are skipped, which removed a dot of light in the elevator cars, and the car itself carries a light so the ride stays lit. Light radius is 0 so a light is not a small bright sphere.
Still open against the acceptance list:
No before/after capture is attached here for the chamber look (PORTAL_CAPTURE=shots PORTAL_SCRIPT=tour).
Portal views keep a traced history for a while so a portal that just appeared is not black, and a teleport hands the view across. Light is not traced through a portal, and I have not confirmed the portal view matches the world behind it.
Bloom, SSAO, and HDR from the earlier pass still apply when ray tracing is off. Ray tracing turns MSAA, SSAO, and shadow maps off.

Checked the remaining acceptance items:
Before/after: the new capture script lighting3 (PORTAL_CAPTURE=shots PORTAL_SCRIPT=lighting3) shoots a chamber, a rooftop and Chamber 16 three ways: the look before this ticket (no ray tracing, bloom, SSAO or auto exposure), today's raster look, and ray traced. Ray tracing is the visible improvement, with bounced light, contact shadows and brighter whites. The raster look is close to the original.
Portal views vs. the main view: the same script puts a portal pair in the Proving Grounds. It shoots the view through blue and the same view from just in front of orange, both traced and raster. Brightness and colour match in both modes, with no exposure or tonemapping mismatch.
Graphics toggles: done in Graphics settings: fullscreen/windowed, VSync, anti-aliasing #9 (closed). Ray tracing, DLSS (Use DLSS when the GPU has it, without a feature flag #33, closed), bloom, SSAO and auto exposure all have settings.
Ray tracing turns MSAA, SSAO and shadow maps off by design (the traced light covers those), and light isn't traced through a portal. Neither showed up as a visible mismatch.

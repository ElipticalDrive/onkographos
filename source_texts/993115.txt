Requested by randomboy · project thread
Before: walking into a cave, jumping inside one or stepping under the rim of a roof froze the game for a moment, and the view flipped between the cave cut and the surface on every jump. Digging a block froze it too, worse under a roof.
After: the cut follows the level the player stands on (a jump does not move it) and waits 0.2 s before following a new target; the cap over the cut and the remeshing of edited chunks are done a few chunks a frame, nearest first, so neither entering a cave nor digging takes more than a few milliseconds in any one frame.
Each cut change used to rebuild the cap over the whole 384×384 map in GDScript (75–110 ms on a desktop CPU, several times that on a phone), and that happened twice per jump and on every dig in a cave; a dig also remeshed up to four chunks in one frame.
How: ChunkMesher.build_cap takes a region; main.gd keeps one cap mesh per chunk within CAP_RANGE (6 chunks) of the player and rebuilds stale ones within CAP_BUDGET_US a frame. An edit remeshes its own chunk at once and queues the neighbours in dirty_chunks, drained within EDIT_BUDGET_US a frame. tools/bench_edit.gd times these operations headlessly.

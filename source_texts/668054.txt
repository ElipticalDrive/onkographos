Removes the remaining sawtooth edges, banding and speckles on the raymarched glyph object.
Cause: glyph textures were binary masks (threshold > 0.5) marched with fixed steps.
Fix: per-glyph subpixel EDT signed distance field (R16F, linear), rebuilt only on text/font/rotation change. Main, depth and fog shaders share one SDF chunk: sphere tracing with real distances, analytic normals from the SDF gradient. Cube, invert and GEB toggles unchanged.
Tested (swiftshader, 800x800): 0 console errors; solid close-up 1.7 -> 6.7 fps; sphere, cube, shadows at zoom 40/100/250 and GEB on/off render.

What changed
Scene.fog: Option<Fog>, a field-by-field mirror of Bevy DistanceFog: color (RGBA, alpha = amount), directional_light_color (RGBA, alpha = strength, 0 = off), directional_light_exponent, and falloff: FogFalloff with Linear, Exponential, ExponentialSquared and Atmospheric { extinction, inscattering }. It also has Bevy's koschmieder, from_visibility, from_visibility_squared and from_visibility_colors. Fog::default() uses Bevy's defaults.
lit.wgsl ports Bevy's apply_fog and fog.wesl term by term, in Bevy's order. Fog is applied to the exposed colour after direct + indirect light. Sun inscattering is pow(max(dot(view, to_sun), 0), exp) × sun × exposure × shadow, with the shadow fetched using the view direction as the normal, then folded into the fog colour per falloff. The clear colour is not fogged, as in Bevy.
Renderer fix: sun shadow maps now use unclipped depth, like Bevy's directional shadow views. With DEPTH_CLIP_CONTROL the rasterizer clamps per fragment; otherwise a fragment shader writes frag_depth, which is Bevy's emulation. Headless requests the feature when the adapter has it. The old per-vertex pancake clamp bent the stored depth of large ground triangles that reach towards a low sun. That hid the self-shadowing Bevy shows in the inscattering lookup: fog_*_sun scored mean 0.014 / p99 0.20 before the fix. It also improves sun_cascades on the GPU from 0.0005 / 0.0086 to 0.0001 / 0.0023.
Oracle: new fog_modes scenario with 8 views, fog_{linear,exponential,exponential_squared,atmospheric} plus a _sun variant of each. The scene is a 180 m double row of pillars facing a sun at 10° elevation, with 4 Gaussian cascades and Tony McMapface. bevy_side inserts DistanceFog. Docs updated (D4, D5, README).
Defaults picked (scenario): haze colour (0.40, 0.48, 0.58), also used as the clear colour. Linear fog runs from 5 to 90 m; exponential uses visibility 120 m; exponential squared uses visibility 100 m; atmospheric uses visibility 150 m with Bevy example colours (extinction 0.35/0.5/0.66, inscattering 0.8/0.844/1.0). Sun glow is (1.0, 0.85, 0.6, 0.5) with exponent 20.

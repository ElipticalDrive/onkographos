Currently yes, the goal is just to display whatever is in the track file header.
If you want to generalise and have the ability to visually control positioning of spherical ROIs, and import / export them, then the interface for that becomes a much larger piece of work than just "displaying spherical ROIs" as is done here. The latest Siemens Frontier TDI WIP is quite good in this respect: you can create inclusion & exclusion regions, move and resize them with the mouse, and then hit a button that runs a tractogram through tckedit with those ROIs. But to do something like that in mrview would be a fair amount of work.

Implemented three protected helper methods:
TransformPointToLocal: Converts world space points to local space by applying inverse translation, rotation, and scale
TransformRayToLocal: Converts world space rays to local space
TransformNormalToWorld: Converts local space normals back to world space
These methods will be used by SDF primitives to properly apply Position, Rotation, and Scale transformations.

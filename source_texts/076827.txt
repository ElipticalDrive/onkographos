What
Adds a new "Text load-in v2 — true raymarching" section to /demo/immersive, placed directly below the previous text load-in attempt. It's an improved approach to the same reveal, built from the techniques in Maxime Heckel's Painting with Math: A Gentle Study of Raymarching.
Why it's an "improved approach"
The previous attempt (RayMarchedHeading) only marches 2D blur taps along a smear line — it isn't real raymarching. The new section applies the article's actual technique end-to-end:
Signed distance field: the DOM heading's glyphs are rasterized at their measured positions and converted into a real SDF via an exact Euclidean distance transform (Felzenszwalb–Huttenlocher, tiny-sdf style seeding for sub-pixel edges), then extruded into 3D (opExtrusion).
True raymarcher: ray origin + per-pixel ray direction, marching by the scene SDF distance up to MAX_STEPS until within SURFACE_DIST. World units are CSS pixels so a ray through pixel q hits the text plane exactly at q, keeping GL and DOM aligned.
smoothmin goo: the reveal is a smooth-max intersection with a sweeping half-space, and metaball droplets ride the front, melting into the emerging glyphs via the article's polynomial smooth-min.
SDF-gradient lighting: hits are shaded with normals from the SDF gradient (Inigo Quilez's getNormal) — ambient from normal.y plus diffuse — with a Beer's-law exponential depth fade.
Crisp handoff: as progress settles, the shader converges onto analytically antialiased flat glyphs and crossfades to the selectable DOM heading, same pattern as the previous attempt.

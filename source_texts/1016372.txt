User-reported 2026-10-08, live engine, current build (exact commit not recorded; HEAD at report time was 00f580e09).
What it looks like
In the --cornell box (game-data-free, byroredux/src/cornell.rs), the soft shadow under the floating shelf and the contact region where the red wall meets the floor stay heavily grainy: fine per-pixel black/white Monte-Carlo noise. The directly lit floor area under the ceiling light is clean. The noise is confined to the penumbra and contact-shadow regions, the pixels whose direct term depends on the stochastic shadow rays (MAX_DIRECT_SHADOW_SAMPLES disk samples per frame, PCG-seeded by frameCount).
Screenshot (local): /mnt/data/tmp/noise-issue/3.png.
The design intent is that these regions converge. The ReSTIR finalize in triangle.frag EMA-accumulates a per-pixel shadow ratio (Heitz et al. 2018 ratio estimator, #4942) and "still refines further over parked frames via 1/N". A visibly grainy penumbra therefore means either the temporal history is being rejected or reset, or the per-frame samples are not decorrelated across frames. That is a correctness bug, not expected sampling noise.
This is the game-data-free reproducer for the full-surface speckle reported on the FO4 Institute (filed alongside, linked below). It may also be the same family as #5159 (terrain dark-patch MC noise).
First discriminator (not yet run)
Parked camera, two captures N frames apart; diff them.
Identical noise in both: the per-frame seed is not advancing (cameraPos.w / frame_counter path) and the EMA averages one fixed pattern.
Different noise that never smooths: the history is being rejected every frame.
Named views via byro-dbg: render.debug shadow_visibility, direct_only, indirect_only, restir_light. These confirm the noise sits in the direct/shadow term and not in GI/SVGF.
Launch-time A/B via BYROREDUX_RENDER_DEBUG: DBG_DISABLE_TEMPORAL (0x40000), DBG_DISABLE_RESTIR (0x8000). If disabling temporal reuse leaves the noise unchanged, the temporal history was already contributing nothing.
Where to look
crates/renderer/shaders/triangle.frag ReSTIR finalize, temporal reuse gate (~3443-3560). reprojValid requires all of: sameSurface (stable inst.surfaceId, depth tolerance max(2, 0.5%), normal cos 25°), remapReservoirLight(rp.lightAndSurface) < lightCount, visibilityMaskNeedsTrace(...), and finite positive rp.W. Any one failing every frame silently degrades the pixel to single-frame RIS.
remapReservoirLight / CPU-side light identities (c705c310d, PERF-D4-2026-09-29-01: GpuLight.history_id is CPU-only identity shipped in the GPU struct (+16 B per light) and mirrored in four shaders that never read it #5055/PERF-D5-2026-09-23-02: When any transport emitter is active, every froxel in the grid runs the RK2 backtrace, the six-neighbour gather and the curl forcing, including the empty majority #4784, 2026-10-02). A broken previous-to-current light remap rejects every reservoir.
Shadow-ray loop (~3760-3790) and its seed hash2_pixel_frame(..., (frameCount*K + sr)*9 + i).
Recent changes in this exact block: 34c3adf14 (REN-D10-2026-10-05-01: #5192 returns the wrap-excess, back-light and translucency lobes to the unshadowed path for every light, sun and XCLL directional included (regression of #4946) #5249, transmission lobes take their own shadow trace, 2026-10-07) and its compile fix ebf1d0492 (FNV interiors: black wall/floor voids from stale triangle shader pair (uncompilable source since 34c3adf14) #5368). Between those two commits triangle.frag did not compile and the committed .spv was stale. Also a4b6a7360 (REN-D10-2026-10-03-02: Soft-light wrap, back-light and thick-translucency lobes self-occlude on closed meshes (zero inside shadowFade, unshadowed past it), the decision #5018 deferred #5192, transmission split out of traced visibility, 2026-10-04). From the 2026-10-08 audit suite: REN-D10-2026-10-08-01 (the REN-D10-2026-10-05-01: #5192 returns the wrap-excess, back-light and translucency lobes to the unshadowed path for every light, sun and XCLL directional included (regression of #4946) #5249 trace passes an opaque-only mask to its foreign-hit handoff) and PERF-D5-2026-10-08-01 (the REN-D10-2026-10-05-01: #5192 returns the wrap-excess, back-light and translucency lobes to the unshadowed path for every light, sun and XCLL directional included (regression of #4946) #5249 up-to-8-hop loop).
Bisect candidates if the discriminator points at a regression: c705c310d, a4b6a7360, 34c3adf14/ebf1d0492. Use a separate worktree with its own CARGO_TARGET_DIR (a shared target/ regenerated shader_constants.glsl wrongly during an earlier bisect).
Oracle: cargo test --release -p byroredux --test cornell_rt_oracle -- --ignored --test-threads=1 (L0-L5). Check whether any level measures penumbra variance after N parked frames. If none does, add one; that is the regression gate this bug lacked.
No fix is proposed until the discriminator and a capture confirm the mechanism (no speculative render-path changes).

Live diagnosis + partial fix landed in 2a72231 (kept open for the residual tail).
What the discriminators established (parked bursts, spatial-vs-temporal variance, controlled frozen-scene A/B on lavapipe): the seed DOES advance (pattern drifts ~1.3 levels/frame), history IS valid (reprojValid holds), and the speckle is standing EMA noise with three causes found:
Flicker reset — the scene-static signal folded every light float, so the fire lab (and any flickering-light scene — see FO4 Institute: dense per-pixel speckle over every bright surface at full light #5370's Institute) sat at the 0.1 refresh floor forever. Fixed: a new intensity-blind light-rig geometry key drives history mode 3; the Heitz-2018 ratio estimator cancels intensity, so flicker scenes now keep the deep tail. The Cornell fire lab's lights are geometry-stable (measured: zero position/radius delta frame-to-frame), so this is the repro's main mechanism.
A hard clamp(rpHistLen, 0, 64) sat before historyCap applied, defeating any cap raise. Fixed.
Parked tail deepened 64/0.025 → 256/0.008. Frozen-scene A/B: mean grain −23%, hot-pixel density 1.37→1.26%.
Remaining, narrowed: rayBudget.direct_shadow_samples is 1 — one shadow ray per pixel per frame (the shader's own comments advertise four). At K=1 a p≈0.5 penumbra keeps a ~7-level converged speckle tail; raising parked K (or per-pixel adaptive K in penumbrae) is the next lever. BYRO_VALIDATION=1 clean over 240 renderer-static frames; shader parity green.
Also: the parked-frame penumbra-variance oracle level this issue asks for is still owed — the frozen-scene A/B harness in this session was ad-hoc scripts; the L0-L5 oracle has no such level yet.

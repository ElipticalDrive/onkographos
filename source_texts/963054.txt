Acceptance criteria
Bottom and top slabs have their real half-block geometry.
Bottom and top stairs render in all four horizontal facings.
Client bounds match the authoritative server bounds merged in [Feature]: authoritative stair and slab identities and collision #716.

Shaped geometry uses the existing opaque surface and material.
Full cubes keep the ordinary greedy sweep.
Shared faces are culled per occupied half-cell, including across chunk borders.
Ambient occlusion samples shaped occupancy instead of treating slabs as full cubes.
Mesh census, bounds, border invalidation, culling, AO, and targeting have regression tests.

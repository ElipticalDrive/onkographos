• Use Cases: 3D/4D Reconstruction, VR/AR/XR, Human avatar, Physical AI, World model (Feifei Li, Marble)
• Hardware: AI MAX 395 with Radeon 8060S iGPU offers strong compute and unified memory for 3DGS.
• Training: Use mixed precision, batching, and gradient accumulation to fit large Gaussian sets efficiently.
• Memory: Unified memory avoids CPU–GPU transfers, improving stability and throughput.
• Rendering: Tile-based rasterization enables real-time Gaussian splat rendering.
Use cases: Suitable for end-to-end 3DGS training, rendering, and rapid prototyping on a single device.

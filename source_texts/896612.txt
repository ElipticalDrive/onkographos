Two quality features from the ParaView/Blender discussion (pre-integration already merged via #269, so the diff here is shading + coloured iso only).
Gradient-shaded render_scene — the 'kid's painting' fix. render_scene(...; shade=0.85, light=(-1,-0.5,1)) lights each gas sample using the field gradient as a normal (Blinn-Phong) — ParaView's 'Shade'. The volume gets form/depth instead of flat colour patches. shade=0 (default) unchanged. (Full Blender-style multiple scattering is a much larger separate project.)
Per-level coloured isosurfaces — render_isosurfaces(vol, cam; levels=[…], colors=[…], iso_alpha, ao, shadow): each surface in its own colour, gradient-shaded translucent shells, depth-ordered → RGB (new _cast_iso_rgb). Reuses the AO/shadow depth cues.
Tested: shading differs from flat & in gamut; coloured shells show both colours in gamut. Verified on spiral_clumps.

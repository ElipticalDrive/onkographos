What's new
Instanced rendering: all particles are drawn by one THREE.InstancedMesh (capacity = particle-count slider max, 500) instead of one Mesh each. Positions/velocities stay per particle; instance matrices are written once per frame (syncInstances).
Particle-size changes swap the shared geometry on the instanced mesh; count/shape/container changes reuse the same mesh via count.
frustumCulled = false: three r137 bounds an InstancedMesh by its base geometry at the origin, so culling would hide moving particles.
README: adds the performance roadmap (first recorded 2026-10-07 on perf/instanced-particles-v2, cherry-picked here) with item 1 checked, plus a validation note.

Replace one THREE.Mesh per particle with one InstancedMesh sized to the
particle-count slider maximum. Physics state stays in per-particle
position/velocity arrays; instance matrices are written once per frame.
Frustum culling is disabled because three r137 bounds an InstancedMesh by
its base geometry at the origin, which would hide moving particles.

At 500 particles Chromium issues 32 WebGL draw calls per frame instead of
515 (the remainder is the bloom post-processing chain).

Context
Bunny currently has ray intersections and closest-point queries for a small set of primitives. Once OBBs, capsules, and planes exist, query coverage needs to follow.
Requested outcome
Implement deterministic fixed-point intersection and closest-point solvers for OBBs, capsules, and planes.
Acceptance criteria
Ray/shape intersections define hit distance and normal semantics.
Shape/shape overlap returns deterministic contact point or classification.
Closest-point solvers return raw fixed values with golden-vector tests.
Native and WASM tests cover touching and separated cases.

LDPRT is a method which allows objects to have global illumination effects within a in small neighborhood on a surface by precomputing light transfer and projecting it into the compact Zonal Harminic (ZH) basis. At run-time the light transfer at each local point is rotated into the model’s deformed position and evaluated to produce output lighting. Volumetric shells is a technique for rendering complex surface geometry in real-time through the use of a 3D shell or ring around a model. The shell is transparent everywhere except at the intersection of the shell and the microgeometry that is being rendered. We combine these two techniques to render fur in real-time with correct light attenuation

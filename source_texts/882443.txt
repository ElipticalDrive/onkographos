The engine C3 that opens Stage 6 (OPTICS_ENGINE_REWRITE_SPEC.md, "Before S6.P1.T1"; settled decision 93). Closes #779. The maintainer delegated its review and merge.
The bug. A sphere or conic root that the closed form marks nearTangency (a grazing ray) went to the iterative path's 24-sample scan. That scan misses a chord shorter than its sample spacing: the ray is reported as missing a surface it crosses.
The fix.
Turning-point split. Along the ray, the quadric is aτ² + 2bτ + g. Its turning point τ = −b/a lies between both roots. The solver evaluates the actual sag residual there and at the in-domain interval ends, and searches the two halves in ray order. It does not infer a hit from a rounded discriminant.
Contract unchanged. A bracket goes through the existing safeguarded Newton refinement: the residual definition, the 1e-12 mm raw target, the 72-iteration cap, the stall rules, endpoint priority, forward bounds and cap selection are all unchanged. The roundoff envelope still applies only at an actual stall.
Where the scan stays. It is unchanged for the other fallback reasons, for polynomial aspheres, and for explicit iteration budgets.
Harness report. The solver-output table now lists every retained failure instead of the first 40.
Tests. Synthetic regressions for spheres and conics at K = −1, −0.5 and −2:
0.0002 mm and about 0.15 mm chords that the old scan missed;
analytic root positions;
tangent touches and true near-misses;
cap and forward-bound selection;
invalid domain ends;
rejection of the other sheet.
All existing tests pass.
Evidence (details in the comment below):
Tier P, every lens and pair: a local capture of both engines through the harness's C3 comparison found 0 of 6,034 records different. Tier P asks only at infinity, and this fix acts at close focus.
Samyang XP 35mm at closest focus: the raw half-field goes from 37.38747° to 37.38797°, recovering two missed intersections. At infinity it is unchanged.
Next: the cumulative Tier S against optics-rewrite/s2 runs after merge, then the anchor moves.
Class: C3. A changelog entry is included.

Method and numerical contract
For the quadric along the ray, F(P + τD) = aτ² + 2bτ + g, its turning point τ = -b/a lies between both roots. Evaluate the actual sag residual there and at the in-domain interval ends. Search the two halves in ray order; do not infer a hit from a rounded discriminant. A ray's conic domain is one cylinder interval; locate its interior from the nearest transverse point when necessary, and use the existing domain-edge bisection for invalid endpoints.
A bracket goes through the existing safeguarded Newton refinement without changing its residual definition, 1e-12 mm raw target, 72-iteration cap, or three stall rules. A touch must meet the raw target. Near tangency alone does not authorize the roundoff envelope; it remains available only at an actual refinement stall. Existing endpoint priority, forward bounds, and authored-cap selection remain. The cap search uses the same split so an exterior entry cannot hide the interior exit. Other fallback reasons, polynomial aspheres, and explicit iteration/sampling budgets retain the scan.
Synthetic regressions cover spheres and conics with K = -1, -0.5, and -2: a 0.0002 mm chord and a roughly 0.15 mm chord missed by the old scan; analytic entry-root positions; tangent touches; true near-misses; cap/forward-bound selection; invalid domain endpoints; and rejection of the other quadric sheet. Existing reference fixtures and intersection-contract tests pass.
The solver-output report now reads every retained failure instead of the first 40 general failures. The general summary table keeps its 40-row limit. A regression checks that solver failure 45 appears. This does not remove the harness's separate storage limits.

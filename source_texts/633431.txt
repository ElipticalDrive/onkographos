feat(tsdf): true distance near outlines
Builds on #316 and #317 (both merged).
What. Near an outline, a voxel now takes the true distance to the surface instead of the projective one. This applies in both modes.
Which voxels. A voxel qualifies when its depth sample is not a clean interpolation inside the band (a jump or a gap across its taps, past the band, or no return) and its footprint window (±1 to ±3 px) holds a return plus a gap or a depth range past the band.
The distance. Such a voxel takes the Euclidean distance to the nearest back-projected return within ±2 to ±6 px, kept only under trunc_dist.
The sign is the voxel's own pixel's:
negative behind that pixel's surface;
positive in front of it or beside it;
positive over a gap, at a quarter of a sample's weight.
Shadow. A voxel more than a voxel behind its nearest return, beside it, is in the outline's shadow. It keeps the projective sample.
Colour. Positive rim samples bring no colour.
Under Dynamic, two more rules keep movers clean:
No rim distance near a change. Each camera's change test also marks its reach: every pixel within 6 px of a flagged pixel, with a bit for a departure (tsdf_change_reach.comp). Inside the reach, the fuse gives no rim distance, so a moving surface is fused from its fresh samples alone.
A departure resets what it left beside it. Within the reach of a departure, the reset also clears a positive voxel whose own ray sees past its band or into a gap, if its nearest return is gone from the band or changed. It clears such a voxel whoever fused it: a rim distance, or the front of another camera's band, which that camera fuses again in the same set at one sample's weight. A gap within the reach counts at once, not as a dropout as a lone invalid sample does elsewhere. An arrival clears nothing.
A pass a frame (tsdf_rim_map.comp) writes, for each pixel:
the smallest window that holds an outline;
the depth range of the returns within 6 px.
So a voxel tests its window with one read, and skips a search that would find nothing. This changes no result: on take1 (120 sets, both modes) the triangles are the same as without the pass.
No public API changes. The integrate and reset layouts grow to 14 bindings and the push constants to 64 bytes. Each camera keeps a change reach (a uint32 a pixel). Each frame of a call keeps a rim map and a return range.
Why.
Beside a silhouette, the projective distance is the background's. Marching cubes then steps the outline a cell at a time and leaves thin parts open:
3.9% silhouette holes on take1;
on the ground-truth bench, rims 2.6 mm inside the true silhouette.
An earlier study's rim distance was the best still-scene fix. On its own, though, it shredded a 1 m/s sphere and left more debris than main (131 against 80 cm²), because near a moving outline it averaged distances to where the surface had been, or was going, into its fresh samples.
Rules 1 and 2 fix that on top of feat(tsdf): Dynamic fusion evicts on change, conflicts average #317.
Tested.
New test, recon_tsdf_rim_distance. Noise-free ray-cast depth from a Femto-like camera, 1 cm voxels, through the Fuser.
In both modes:
a turned plate's outline lies 1.4 mm off the true edge on average and 5.0 mm at worst (projective: 2.9 / 8.1 mm Classic, 6.5 / 12.8 mm Dynamic);
voxels beside its edge hold their Euclidean distance to 1.4 mm;
a 1.6 cm rod meshes along its whole length.
Under Dynamic:
a plate moving 3 cm a set holds no weight beside its moving sides, and takes its distances back the first set it is still;
a sphere at 1 m/s past a wall, seen by three cameras, stays at least 98.5% covered while it moves, and leaves nothing off the wall the set it goes.
Removing each rule in turn fails its check (dev builds).

The check that nothing else changes that set now starts past that reach; the rest of the box still goes a set later, by its Vanished rays.

tsdf::ChangeDetector keeps, for each fixed camera slot, a history of every
pixel on the device -- a running depth, the pixel's own temporal noise
floored by the sensor's DepthNoise (sigma(z) = a + b z + c z^2), two
cumulative sums and three counters -- and one pass a camera a frame tests
each valid sample and writes the camera's mask (PixelChange: none, nearer,
farther). Two-sided CUSUM, k 0.5 and h 8, or one frame past 6 sigma; an
alarm restarts the pixel's history and freezes its noise estimate for 30
samples; a pixel tests once it holds 30 samples; an invalid sample changes
nothing. An optional gate word has a pass test nothing, as
record_integrate's does.

recon_tsdf_change_detector runs synthetic sequences a camera slot each:
static ToF-like and stereo-like noise, steps, ramps, a foreground sweeping
in, dropouts, a camera that leaves and returns, a resized one, and the
refusals.

Dynamic no longer clears a voxel when one camera's one sample puts it past
the band. A Dynamic set now runs, for every frame, its camera's change test
(the integrator's ChangeDetector, a slot a camera: FrameInput::slot and
FrameInput::noise), then every frame's reset along the rays its camera
flagged -- the voxels in front of the new sample and in its band go back to
unobserved, the history behind the band stays -- and then every frame's
fuse, in slot order. Free space past the band averages in as +trunc at a
tenth of a sample's weight, and only into a voxel that holds weight, so it
wears away what no change removed but never evicts, and never makes an
unobserved voxel observed. A closed gate also stops the change tests, so a
set fused again is tested once. Classic is unchanged.

The clear kernel becomes the reset kernel (tsdf_reset.comp); the push
constants lose fuse_only. TsdfIntegrator::create and FuserConfig take a
ChangeDetectorConfig, check_frames the mode, and sender::fuse_inputs gives
each frame its camera's index as its slot. A camera's first Dynamic set
zeroes its change history in a submit of its own.

recon_tsdf_dynamic_change: two cameras 8 mm apart on a wall lose no weight
while the scene is still; a card inside the wall's band, and a box, clear in
the set they leave; the box arrives in a set, stays stable, and the wall it
hides keeps its history; a set one camera misses resets nothing; and the
slot and noise refusals. The Dynamic fixtures of the other tests run their
change test from a pixel's second sample and give each camera a slot.

Why do you think the link is worth adding on this list?
VoxiGen is a voxel editor that runs in the browser (WebGPU), aimed at game assets and levels: models up to 1024³, scene composition from reusable models, MagicaVoxel .vox import/export, and real-time ray-cast/path-traced rendering. Every level of VOXEN, a voxel shooter, was built in it. It is the only browser-based entry in the section.
Does this project has any License?
Proprietary. The editor is free to use with no account needed to start; mesh export (GLB/glTF/OBJ/STL) and game-level export are a one-time paid licence, hence :money_with_wings: (partially free).
Disclosure: I'm the developer.

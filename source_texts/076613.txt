What is asked
An isosurface mesher near the camera and raymarching far away, with collision for walking inside the scroll.
Why it matters: Recorded so it does not come back: an isosurface asserts a boundary the scan never resolved and costs about a million faces per brick.
What is true today
Abandoned on 2026-08-28 for direct volume rendering; meshing survives only for a traced sheet (voxel::Surface).
What is missing
Nothing. Reopen only if a segmentation separates the sheets into components.
How we will know it is done
Not applicable, closed. It returns only if a threshold or a segmentation gives separable sheets.

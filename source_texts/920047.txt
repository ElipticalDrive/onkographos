Hand-made art has irregularity that pure geometry lacks: wobbly outlines, varied textures. gb-vector has seeded noise and paths driven by it.
Fix
Seeded Perlin and simplex noise, fractal Brownian motion, and Voronoi cells.
Paths whose points are displaced along their normals by noise, for organic outlines.
The same seed gives the same shape on every platform.
Done when
Noise is reproducible for a seed, with tests pinning sample values
A circle with noise applied to its outline stays closed and smooth at any resolution

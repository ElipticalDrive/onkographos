Bounding Sphere - an overview | ScienceDirect Topics
Bounding volume hierarchies (BVHs) have extensively been used in rendering since Clark and Rubin and Whitted [Cla76, RW80], to efficiently support spatial queries such as visibility culling or ray-object intersections.

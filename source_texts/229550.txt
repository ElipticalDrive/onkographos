Tired of Lag Inside Clouds? Fly Smoothly at 60 FPS!
Volumetric Clouds Performance Fix is a lightweight configuration tweak designed specifically for players who love the stunning visuals of Blackrack's EVE Volumetric Clouds but suffer from severe frame-rate drops (Lag) and micro-stutters when flying through dense weather layers, fog, or storms.
If you are playing on a mid-range PC, a budget gaming laptop, or integrated graphics, this fix is exactly what you need to reclaim your performance without sacrificing the beautiful cloud shapes!
🚀 Performance Impact & Features
By targeting only the most resource-heavy rendering calculations that happen under the hood, this config completely frees up your GPU headroom:
Locked 60 FPS Experience: Transforms your laggy atmospheric flights into butter-smooth gameplay.
No More Fog Shadows: Disables heavy fog shadow reception. The clouds will no longer crush your frame rate trying to calculate dynamic shadows through thick mist.
Lag-Free Lightning Storms: Dynamic light intensity from lightning bolts has been disabled (lightIntensity = 0). You will still see the beautiful visual lightning sheets flashing in the sky, but they won't cause annoying FPS drops anymore.
Optimized Cockpit Droplets: Complex specular reflections on cockpit window rain droplets are turned off (specularStrength = 0). Rain will look clean and flat, preventing your GPU from choking during heavy downpours.
Balanced Particle Fields: Slightly tunes the density and opacity of rain and snow particles to maintain full atmospheric immersion while maximizing performance.
📦 What's Inside the Zip?
This pack contains three optimized configuration files that overwrite the heavy default settings:
lightning.cfg - Disables dynamic storm lighting.
droplets.cfg - Disables performance-crushing droplet reflections.
particleFields.cfg - Optimizes weather particle counts for smoother rendering.
🛠️ Requirements & Installation
Dependency:
This tweak requires the original mod to be installed first:
Blackrack's EVE Volumetric Clouds (Stock Volumetric Clouds configurations).
Easy Installation Steps:
Download the CloudPerformanceFix.zip file.
Extract the files on your computer.
Copy the three .cfg files (lightning, droplets, and particleFields).
Paste and overwrite them into your existing cloud mod directory:
GameData/StockVolumetricClouds/Clouds/
Boot up the game, fly into a storm, and enjoy your buttery-smooth 60 FPS!

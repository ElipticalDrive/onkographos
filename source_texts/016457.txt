Slide 9 clearly states that the scene is rasterized using the traditional rendering pipeline. The secondary rays use the SVO. In a traditional game renderer, these secondary rays are queried with a shadow map, which only lets them go towards the light (or the direction the map was rendered in). The SVO lets them go in any direction, which produces the nice global illumination.

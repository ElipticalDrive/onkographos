The accompanying files demonstrate the effects of JPEG compression. The original image (HeLa-Actin.tif) shows a HeLa cell, of which the actin cytoskeleton is fluorescently labeled. The image of the cell is acquired with fluorescence microscopy. When this image is saved in the JPEG format (HeLa-Actin.jpg) an algorithm is applied, resulting in a loss of information and compression of file size. The difference between the original image and compressed image can be readily appreciated by looking at the animation (TIF-versus-JPEG.gif). The original data used for the animation is also supplied (Animation-compression2.tif). The 'zoom' is generated with a slight modification of this ImageJ macro: https://gist.github.com/ekatrukha/61a1138063591b524e043891e5201f3d

Summary
Lets an inflated marching-cubes mesh snap its boundary onto the surface, and moves the energy fit out of buffer/ now that more than the buffer layer uses it.
Finish::Fit takes a Fitting, so Fit(Freedom, Fitting::Snap) fits, projects the boundary onto the surface, and relaxes the interior. On the sphere the maximum boundary gap is 0.026 with Soft and under 1e-6 with Snap.
fit/ moves from buffer/fit/ to mesh/fit/, taking Fitting and project with it. A single Mesh::fit_to(free, boundary, target, fitting) replaces the fit, project, re-fit sequence that buffer, buffer_tets, buffer_targeted (two sites) and marching each wrote out. mesh::Fitting is still exported from the same path.
Mesh::inflate(target, freedom, fitting) is public, so a mesh cut with Finish::Cut can be fitted as its own step. Freedom moves beside Fitting, and Finish::Fit calls inflate.
Test plan
cargo test --profile release-dev --features geometry --lib -- mesh::fit:: mesh::buffer:: marching:: (24 pass, covering fit, buffer, targeted, restrict and marching)
New snapping_puts_the_boundary_on_the_surface_without_inverting; it fails with Fitting::Soft (gap 0.026), so it does not pass vacuously
Pre-commit clippy across all features
Creased surfaces are untested with Snap; projection may invert boundary cells there
Consumer: automesh uniform-inflation (mesh hex --uniform --inflate, --marching, --snap), which currently pins this branch.

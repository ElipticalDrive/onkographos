Objective:To study the methodology and to assess the value of MSCT with volume rendering technique in small intestinal diseases.Methods:A total of 81 cases with small intestinal diseases proved by operation or pathology were collected from April 2006 to March 2007. The volume rendering technique data were formed after retro-reconstructing of initial scanning data and the finding of VRT image were compared with other reconstruction methods.Results:Each reconstruction method had its own character of note, the VRT image clearly showed the relationship between the lesion and its forming artery which could show the situation and extent of the lesion combined with other methods and thin-slice axial images. Conclusion:Volume rendering technique can provide significant and clear imaging information for the treatment and operational suggestion and possess important clinical diagnostic values in intestinal diseases.

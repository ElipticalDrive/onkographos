The data used to train the models mentioned in "CNN-based algorithm for improving the quality of Single-Shot Holographic Tomography". It contains real and simulated data, which had their Fourier spectra masked and exhibit the associated artifacts. This also contains their GT counterparts with the full spectrum, and thus without artifacts. This dataset contains Experimental data:  volumes with experimental 3D RI reconstructions: microspheres, HaCaT, lymphocyte cells and many others [3, 4]. Technical data: technical objects (cell phantoms) [1, 2] and their augmentations numerical synthesis of the cell phantoms and its variations numerically generated objects - spheres, cubes, merged shapes The dataset is balanced between experimental and synthetic data. Experimental data:

The dataset is balanced between experimental and synthetic data.

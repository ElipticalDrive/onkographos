ROADMAP P14 stage 1. Bounds in evidence/p14-clouds-bounds.json were committed before the code (ee0df60); results in evidence/p14-clouds-runs.json.
raw/renderer/volume.hpp: density from soft-sphere lobes with surface sub-lobes, a height gradient, and Worley and billow erosion (P8 noise). A raymarcher with Beer transmittance, a dual-lobe Henyey-Greenstein phase, powder, height-weighted ambient and octave multiple scattering. A delta-tracking path tracer with ratio-tracking next-event estimation serves as the reference.
raw_native_cli clouds renders a cumulus, a towering cumulus, a deck from above and a smoke plume at sunset, plus the noise-blob control.

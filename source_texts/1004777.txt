LIMITATIONS
• Need to know all tensor entries in advance!
• Unsuitable for streaming setups, where:
• Data tensor grows along a single time-like dimension
• New data slices along the streaming dimension arrive in discrete batches
• Our goal: Design a ST-HOSVD update algorithm that:
• Incorporates new slices into existing factorization
• Does not revisit already processed data
• Respects the relative error bounds of the initial compression
• Scales well with increasing mode sizes
• Is data-parallel

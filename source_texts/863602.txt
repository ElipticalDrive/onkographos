Parent
scene: implement what a mesh stores
What to build
The normal and the tangent each drop from twelve and sixteen bytes to four, decoded in WGSL from a published includable source. Twenty-four bytes per vertex become eight, and a mirrored surface still shades correctly under a structured environment.
The normal is oct32 in a two-component 16-bit unorm, not the fetch unit's packed 10/10/10/2. At an identical four bytes oct32 is 26x more accurate — 0.0037° mean against 0.042°. Two things settled it beyond the error bound: the packed format is a late-2023 spec addition that shipped silently broken on a wgpu GL backend for ~20 months, and with no pixel readback that failure looks like a missing mesh; and an oct decode is stateless, adding no binding, no CPU-side table and nothing for the update path to keep in sync.
Four bytes for the normal is earned on an artefact, not a margin — and that took two prototype tickets. The first found no visible difference at any rung and concluded the choice was "a margin argument, not an artefact argument". That reversed: put a structured environment in front of it and oct16 moves 14% of a mirrored sphere, 3% of it by more than half the dynamic range, lands a hard reflected edge 2 px out of register, and returns a regular reflected bar pattern wavy, kinked and uneven in width. oct32 at the same measurement is 0.52% of pixels, peak 16/255 — nothing. Do not re-litigate this rung; it has been looked at twice.
The tangent is one 32-bit word: 15 bits per octahedral axis, one handedness bit, one reserved bit. Handedness has to ride in the vertex — every w in the corpus is exactly ±1 and constant across a primitive in 10 of 11, but WaterBottle carries both signs inside one primitive, and one counter-example is enough.
The tangent does not need 15/15 and keeps it anyway. Measurement puts its usable floor near 8 bits per axis, so the word carries roughly 14 bits of slack beyond the declared reserved bit. That slack is unspendable: a two-byte tangent gives a stride of 30, and WebGPU requires arrayStride to be a multiple of 4, so it pads straight back. What blocks a narrower tangent is packing, not fidelity. Record it; do not chase it.
The reserved bit stays reserved, and there is no absence sentinel. The zero-length case falls out of the encoding rather than being chosen: the encoder guards a zero L1 norm and returns the octahedral origin, which decodes to +Z. Magnitude is discarded silently as contract — these are directions, and a length-1.0001 normal draws correctly, so a check would fire on correct code.
Normal and tangent decode differently, one straight to a two-component float and one by bit extraction. That asymmetry is the price of not paying four more bytes for symmetry, and it is deliberate.
Two shipping demo shaders break, and this is the mismatch ticket 1 exists to catch. Both declare a three-component float at the normal's location against what becomes a two-component unorm. They include the new decode source and read two components instead.
See Per attribute and Where decoding runs in scene/docs/specs/mesh.md.
Acceptance criteria
A new includable WGSL source publishes the oct decode for both the normal and the tangent, exported as a path constant beside the existing shader paths.
The normal stores as oct32 in four bytes; the tangent as one 32-bit word carrying 15/15 oct plus handedness plus one reserved bit.
Decoding runs at the top of the vertex stage, before morph and before skin.
An unwritten normal or tangent decodes to +Z with positive handedness, with no NaN and no special case.
Handedness survives a primitive carrying both signs.
The two demo shaders reading the normal as a three-component float are updated to include the decode source.
The stride drops by 20 bytes (84 → 64 — the ticket said 24, see the resolution comment); every offset stays 4-aligned and the stride stays a multiple of 4.
Normal-mapped and mirrored assets are inspected by eye against captures of the current engine and show no visible change.

Done in 0479e4f, with a companion commit 891cccf in dvoyni/cog-examples.
What now works. The normal and the tangent each store in four bytes. Stride 84 → 64.
scene/vertexoct.go — the octahedral encode. packNormal writes two 16-bit unorm codes; packTangent writes one word: 15 bits x, 15 bits y from bit 15, handedness at bit 30, bit 31 reserved and never written. The zero/NaN/Inf guard is !(l1 > 0), so an unwritten direction falls to the octahedral origin rather than needing a special case — +Z with positive handedness, no NaN, by construction.
scene/builtin/scene/vertexdecode.wgsl (new) publishes sceneOctDecode, sceneDecodeNormal, sceneDecodeTangent — no bindings, no structs, includable by any material — exported as VertexDecodePath beside sceneShaderPath. scene.wgsl decodes at the top of vs_main, before morph and before skin; sceneDeformVertex now takes the already-decoded vertex.
scene/vertexpack.go — offsets 0/12/16/20/28/36/40/48, layout rows Unorm16x2 and Uint32, plus a compile-time guard on the storage stride.
scene: pack the vertex at bake, with the layout unchanged #217's two hand-offs are discharged. bakeModelGeometry moved off uploadBytes onto a new packOverAuthored, which packs over the converted vertices' own memory — the storage vertex is smaller, so the read walk runs strictly ahead of its writes and the load still allocates no second buffer, honouring the peak-memory concern rather than trading it away. TestTheStorageOffsetsStillAgreeWithTheAuthoringStruct is deleted, as its comment instructed.
How it was verified. go build ./..., go vet ./..., go test ./... — all 14 packages ok. go test ./scene/ ./wgpu/ ./gfx/ -count=10 — stable (the race detector cannot build in this environment, so -count=10 stands in for it).
Precision, measured rather than asserted — 20,000 Fibonacci-sphere directions: normal mean 0.00133°, worst 0.00368°; tangent mean 0.00261°, worst 0.00710°. The tangent is ~2× the normal, exactly as the spec predicts for 15 bits against 16.
Measured over the real corpus (throwaway in-package test, since deleted, driving convertPrimitive over every .glb in cog-examples/assets) — 52 primitives, 38,391 vertices:
38,370 real normals: mean 0.00133°, worst 0.00368°.
2,710 file-authored tangents: mean 0.00219°, worst 0.00609°; zero non-unit w.
WaterBottle mesh 0 primitive 0 is the only file-authored primitive carrying both handedness signs, and every vertex's sign round-tripped — so the both-signs criterion is met against real content, not only a fixture.
packOverAuthored was byte-identical to the arena pack on every primitive.
Vertex bytes 3,224,844 → 2,457,024, a 767,820-byte (23.8%) saving.
#214's safety net was fired on purpose to check it works. A throwaway wgpu test (deleted) flattened the two demo shaders' actual text, reflected them and ran CheckVertexInterface. Both pass now; the pre-change declaration was refused with gfx: shader "obelisk_old" reads vec3<f32> normal at @location(1), which the bound vertex layout supplies as vec2<f32> — which is the exact wrong-shading-that-looks-like-art failure #214 exists to convert into an error.
The ticket's arithmetic was wrong and I followed the spec instead. Criterion 7 said "the stride drops by 24 bytes". The float normal is 12 bytes and the float tangent 16 — 28 bytes becoming 8 saves 20, so the stride goes 84 → 64, which is what mesh.md's own end-state table implies. I have corrected the criterion text in place rather than leaving the two documents disagreeing. Nothing else about it changes: every offset is 4-aligned and 64 is a multiple of 4.
Seven of eight criteria are met and ticked. The eighth — normal-mapped and mirrored assets inspected by eye against captures — needs a GPU, and cog-examples/internal/headless is broken independently (missing method TakeCapture), so no demo test package builds. It is #223's A normal-mapped surface … is inspected, with the numbers above as the offline evidence.
Two things for whoever is next.
The demo shader change landed in a second repo. cog-examples 891cccf updates cmd/scene/cameras/obelisk.go and cmd/scene/loading/repaint.go to include the decode; without it those demos are a refused pipeline the moment they run. go build ./cmd/... there is clean.
The tangent word carries ~14 bits of slack beyond the reserved bit, unspendable until a partner 2-byte saving appears. Packing, not fidelity — noted, not acted on.

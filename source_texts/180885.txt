Abstract A method is presented for meshing 3D CAD surfaces in parametric space using an advancing front approach and a metric map to govern the size and shape of the triangles in the parametric space. The creation of the metric map will be discussed. The advancing front mesher generates triangles based on the metric map, stretching them in order to capture the change in parameterization of the surface. The benefits of this algorithm include better quality elements without having to do costly real space calculations

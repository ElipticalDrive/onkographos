User goal
Keep GODSEED's large world and make NPCs act on their environment, while optimizing loaded map chunks by coordinates rather than rendering/loading the entire world.
Changes
Signed 16×16 chunk coordinate mapper with correct negative-grid floors and bounded 48-chunk LRU.
Camera windows now assemble adjacent chunk spans rather than performing a fresh LRU cache access for every terrain tile.
Large-world scene terrain reused between NPC ticks while the view is stationary; cache invalidated by actual resource edits and precisely at per-patch berry regrowth (not global multiples of 40).
Count chunk builds/hits and terrain-window rebuilds/reuses, while keeping saves unchanged.
Frontier NPCs learn from their actual successes/failures in foraging, timber, building, roads and exploration. Resource search radius expands when local gathering fails, without teleporting or creating resources.
Tests for signed coordinates, biome and window parity, seam crossing, LRU limit, offscreen resource persistence, lazy viewport reuse and NPC adaptation.
Rebuilt deterministic offline HTML and documented architecture/remaining limitations.
Limitations
No dynamic unbounded citizen generation or GPU chunk mesh streaming yet. The large map remains a 1024-cell-square world. Only changes that pass simulation, WebGL and Android emulator workflows are eligible for another APK.
Stacked on draft PR #72; do not merge independently before its base is settled.

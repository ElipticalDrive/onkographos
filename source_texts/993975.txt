VoxelGrid{T,I} gets a type parameter for the integer type of the voxel indices. index_type(dims) chooses it at construction: Int32 if the index arithmetic of the kernels fits (up to 1290³), otherwise Int64. VoxelGrid{T,Int64}(image) forces a type. All index arithmetic in the Siddon and Joseph kernels runs in I.
Only the voxel indices change. Using Int32 for the data indices (pixel_index, ray_index) showed no gain, so the geometries are unchanged. Projectors get a type parameter for the grid, so their fields stay concrete; types that spell out projector parameters are affected.
Timings (RX 7900 XT, Float32, 128³, one ray per cell; approximate): Joseph forward 6.6–6.8 ms with Int64 and 5.7–6.0 ms with Int32; Siddon and the back projections are unchanged within noise.
Tests: sizes at the limit and beyond Int64, field types, and identical results of the ray tracing with Int32 and Int64 grids, forward and back. No large images are projected. CPU 9331, JLArrays 644, ROCm 644 passed.

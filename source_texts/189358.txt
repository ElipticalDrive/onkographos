ARootTask::OnCreate starts with APrim::InitialiseAllPrimStatics (SimJP.exe 0x64D090). It clears the 5x5 prim-vs-prim intersection table and the ray table, then runs every APrim subclass's InitialiseStatic through TClass::RecurseTree. Only APrim::InitialiseStatic registers anything:
prim tests: sphere-sphere, sphere-box, sphere-capsule, composite-any, plugin-any
ray tests: sphere, box, capsule, composite, plugin
APrim is the collision primitive library: APrim, APrimSphere, APrimBox, APrimCapsule, APrimComposite, APrimPlugin, APrimWallManagerPlugin, 107 functions in all. It is mostly float math, so it needs tools/x87_expr.py and differential care. The front end does not use it, so it is deferred out of the L2 path. OnCreate skips the call until this lands.

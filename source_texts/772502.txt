There is already some kind of support for skyboxes. For better performance and presentation and precision and so on, I wish Supersplat Studio could support rendering outlier-splats to skyboxes.
How? Simply define a maximum splat-distance (like 50m horizontally, 10m height), remove splats within this volume and render a skybox from the remaining splats with the camera at the origin. With this skybox materialized, the splat could drop the splats that have been rendered to the skybox and get lighter and smaller. A nice side-effect is, that this rendered skybox could be exported and imported and so photoshop could clean the sky even more.

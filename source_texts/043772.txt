Hi Authors, Thanks for the impressive work on 4DRGS! I've successfully reconstructed the 3D vessels using your code.I am deeply impressed by the visualization quality ,but I am finding it challenging to match the visual fidelity and depth perception of the vessels shown in your paper(fig4)
Could you please share some insights into the rendering process? Specifically:
1.Visualization Tool: Did you use professional scientific visualization software like ParaView or 3D Slicer, or was it rendered using a cinematic engine like Blender (Cycles)?
2.Material/Shading: For the "Radiative" part, did you use any specific Transfer Function or Alpha Blending strategy to represent the attenuation?
3.Mesh vs. Volume: Are the figures rendered directly from the Voxelized Volume or from the Extracted Meshes? If meshes were used, did you apply any smoothing or AO (Ambient Occlusion)?
Looking forward to your insights.Thank you for your time and for sharing this excellent work!

Thanks for your interest. We use ITK-SNAP to visualize the synthesized DSA projections, and 3D slicer to visualize the reconstructed vessels. Attenuation is just density in fact. You can just use the transfer function in 3D slicer with appropriate threshold.

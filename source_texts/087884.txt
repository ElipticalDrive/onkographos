What this is
A faster way to build the 3D shape (mesh) of a chunk (a 32-block cube) of terrain, by packing which blocks are visible into groups of 32 at a time.
Why it matters
Building terrain shapes was slower than it needed to be, especially for terrain fresh out of the world generator.
Current state
Shipped.
Packs visibility into 32-blocks-at-a-time groups per column, with a fix-up step for chunk edges.
Layers with nothing visible skip entirely; a fully buried chunk skips all 192 of its layers.
Confirmed the resulting shapes are pixel-for-pixel identical to before, with all tests passing.
Measured 73% and 63% faster on freshly generated terrain in two test cases.
Done when
Shipped and checked in the running game.

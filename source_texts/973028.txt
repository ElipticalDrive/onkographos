Depends on the case. I'd say the most relevant pre-trained model may be the cross-category one. Unfortunately this was using 64x64 images and rather old renderings.
To load your own data, please adapt one of the data loaders, or perhaps format your data in DVR's format..
Store files in <object_name>/image/xxxx.png (number 0-padded to 4 digits) and camera info in cameras.npz.
This should have 4x4 camera-to-world pose matrix 'world_mat_inv_' and 4x4 'camera_mat_' intrinsics matrix for each view i (starting from 0).
You can download the NMR dataset to see an example of the format

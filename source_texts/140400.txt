1.2.3 ass1c.net -- Direct Volume Rendering (DVR)
This network uses the Tracer and Display Tracker modules to perform a Direct
Volume Rendering (DVR) of the lobster's density. Areas of high density are rendered
as a semi-transparent light orange colour, and areas of lower density are rendered
slightly less opaquely in a deeper red colour. As such, this technique is much
more suited to the analysis of soft tissue and other low density regions. It
clearly shows the outer shell as a fuzzy light orange region surrounding the
reddish core of softer tissue.
The Display Tracker module is used to provide interactivity when examining the
data set's rendering. However, when manipulating the view it is often best to
turn off the Interpolate settings in both the Tracer and Display Tracker modules
so that the display updates faster.
The view can be altered to penetrate the volume deeper, revealing more internal
structure. This is achieved by modifying Tracer module's Alpha Scale control,
which is actually wired to an Animated Float. Pressing the Sleep button of the
Animated Float module will begin the stepping of the Alpha Scale, going inwards
into the lobster in a similar fashion to ass1b.net.

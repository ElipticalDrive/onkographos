What to build
The Raster Render Path renders every volume at its selected Voxel Volume Detail Level. Skirts close the cracks between levels, and convergence is incremental, without whole-scene rebuilds or frame-thread stalls.
Coarse artifacts. Per-volume coarse meshes are derived from summaries, keyed by content version and level, and reused across selections and unrelated edits. Raster Region identities stay volume-local at every level.
Skirts.
The mesher already closes each volume at its boundary faces (Profile whole-world detail levels and derive selection maxima, byte caps and laptop frame time #137). That closure is the starting implementation.
Skirts must cover mixed levels, volume corners and independently meshed Raster Regions, including overhangs, cavities and material boundaries.
Intrusion is limited to one coarse voxel of the coarser neighbouring level.
Convergence.
Newest-only over combined revision and detail targets.
Building on Raster prepares changed volumes individually, then atomically installs the complete affected set #143, only volumes whose level or content changed are prepared. The complete affected set then installs atomically at a fence-safe boundary.
Frames are never mixed across levels or revisions. Older complete frames stay visible while preparation runs.
Coarse revision correctness. Coarse levels follow the newest content version, including after eviction from full detail and after non-resident edits. A stale coarse level is a correctness failure.
Failures and retirement. Preparation, upload and installation failures keep the presenting state and allow a retry. Retirement releases every detail-held summary and GPU allocation, and reports zero workers.
Suggested checkpoints within this ticket. Skirt qualification comes first.
Coarse meshes per level with skirts, plus crack-coverage tests across mixed levels, corners and Raster Regions within the intrusion limit.
Combined newest-only convergence with incremental per-volume preparation and atomic installation.
Coarse revision correctness after eviction and after non-resident edits.
Failure preservation and retirement.
Stop rule: if skirts can't cover the cracks within the intrusion limit at checkpoint 1, stop. Report the failing cases on #141 and reopen shaping, with stitched transitions as the fallback. Don't continue the rest of this ticket on a failing crack solution.
Acceptance criteria
Skirt crack-coverage tests pass across mixed levels, volume corners and independently meshed Raster Regions, including overhangs, cavities and material boundaries. Intrusion stays within one coarse voxel of the coarser neighbour.
Raster Region identities are the same at every level.
Combined targets are newest-only. Supersession never installs a stale combination, and no frame mixes levels or revisions.
Counters show that a crossing prepares only the volumes whose level or content changed, and that nothing is rebuilt for the whole scene.
After eviction from full detail and after non-resident edits, the installed coarse level matches the newest content version.
Injected failures leave the presenting state intact, and a retry converges.
Retirement reports zero detail-held summaries, zero GPU allocations and zero workers.
Unstreamed Raster behaviour is unchanged.

Checkpoint 1 passed before convergence work. Coarse meshes read frontend summaries directly and retain the original volume-local Raster Region identities. Closed volume-boundary faces provide the skirts, with zero extra extrusion outside the selected coarse solid.
The independent crack test compares decoded front-facing triangles against an analytic source-box union, expanded independently to each selected voxel grid. It covers 56 assignments per region grid: all 16 level pairs along X, all 16 along Z, and all 24 permutations of four levels at a volume corner. Each assignment uses 204 rays, for 22,848 comparisons across 16x16x16 and non-aligned 13x15x17 Raster Region grids. Fixtures include a closed cavity crossing four volumes, an overhang, thin floors and two materials. Rays include exact boundaries, offsets of +/-0.0001 and near-grazing directions. Every observation agrees. Vertices remain in the conservative source bounds; selected-cell thickening is at most scale minus one full-detail voxels, within one voxel of the coarser neighbour.
Sensitivity check: temporarily removing coarse X boundary faces fails the Full/Coarse32 case at origin [-1, 31.9999, 47.5], direction [1, -0.000001, 0]. The independent reference hits at distance 65 while the triangles miss. The boundary faces were restored before subsequent work.

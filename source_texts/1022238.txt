This dataset contains the definitive data, synthetic B-mode ultrasound images, scientific code, final analysis outputs, and reproducibility documentation supporting a computational virtual imaging trial of two-dimensional ultrasound volumetry. The study separates exact geometry (G), acquisition-perturbed geometry (A), and synthetic ultrasound (U) to quantify geometric, acquisition-related, and image-formation contributions to volumetric measurement error. The primary bladder-like experiment includes 2,048 exact geometries, 24,576 acquisition-perturbed observations, and 256 definitive synthetic-ultrasound acquisitions, of which 251 were measurable and 5 were prospectively classified as single-frame field-of-view unimageable. A prospective transferability extension evaluates simple cyst-like, gallbladder-like, and dilated renal collecting system-like geometries, with 16 definitive synthetic-ultrasound acquisitions per class. The archive also contains the configuration files, quality-control records, B-mode data, analysis outputs, and documentation required to inspect and reproduce the reported results. All structures and images are synthetic; the extension results represent controlled-design transferability and not clinical prevalence.

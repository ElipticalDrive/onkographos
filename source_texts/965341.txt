Part of the third-pass depth stack, #3 of 7: main ← #135 ← #134 ← #133 ← #137 ← #136 ← #143 ← #132. It builds on #134 (depth3/2-transform-reversal-traits); merge the stack bottom up.
```
intersect/numeric/
├── marching.rs          marching_intersection = coincidence? → seeds → trace each → fit
└── marching/
    ├── pair.rs          SurfacePair: meet · meet_touching · land · clamp · leaving · predict
    │                    · tangent · contact_direction · crossing_branches · grazes
    │                    · osculates · trace_box · swap/swapped
    ├── tracer.rs        Tracer::trace(seed) → Trace::{Curve { points, tangents, stalls,
    │                    touching }, Touch, Lost}; step loop sized by step_error (Hermite);
    │                    Contact::{Crossing, Touching} is its own per-step state
    ├── seeding.rs       seeds(pair) → Vec<Seed>: patch pairs, iso-curve families, grids;
    │                    branch_seeds(pair, stall) for the branches crossing at a stall
    └── coincidence.rs   lies-on check before marching, region around coincident seeds
```
Surface pair. The 28 functions generic over <A, B>(a, b, x: [f64; 4], …), each with its own where block, become methods of one SurfacePair<'s, A, B> (two references, Copy). The swap [x[2], x[3], x[0], x[1]], written out three times, is now swap and SurfacePair::swapped. The pair knows nothing of steps or seeds.
Tracer. trace, step_error, crosses_one_knot_at_most, box_exit and the knot arithmetic move into Tracer. The touching: bool that was decided once in Traced::follow and threaded through trace, direction, correct and step_error is now a private Contact value. The tracer decides it from the seed, reads it at every step to pick the direction and the corrector, and reports it in Trace::Curve { touching }. The "a touching trace shorter than a first step is a contact" rule moves into the tracer too, as Trace::Touch. How tangent tracing changes only the tracer: a step that fails in one mode re-reads pair.grazes and assigns a new Contact in the step loop. The pair already answers both correctors and both directions, and the caller only sees Trace.
Seeding policy. seeds(pair) holds the whole policy: patch pairs on two B-splines, then both grids if that search runs out of budget; the iso-curve families plus grids, with the early exit when the grid seeds only graze; otherwise both grids. It returns the seeds in trace order. A patch-pair seed stays unconverged and carries the box that skips it once an earlier curve crosses it (Seed::PatchPair { centre, skip_if_crossed }). It is converged only when its turn comes, exactly as before, so no Newton runs are added. A new seed source (roadmap 1(b)) only edits this file.
Rebased on End marching traces on the box of a bounded side #145, Trace every branch through crossings at any confusion #146, Keep marched curves within confusion between the traced points (#140) #148, Unroll B-spline first derivatives for degrees 1 to 3 #144 and Accept converging Newton steps hidden by rounding in newton_min_1d (#142) #149 (main at 9f2e26c). Each piece went where ADR 0025 puts its concept:
Tracer: the tangents and stalls of a trace (Trace::Curve { points, tangents, stalls, touching }), step_error with HERMITE_SLACK in place of bulges, the oriented boundary and cube-exit tangents, and the step size from the Hermite error.
Pair: crossing_branches, a question about the two surfaces at a point.
Seeding policy: branch_seeds, a new seed source, from the stall the tracer reports.
Fitting: hermite_curve, segment_length and without_confused go to curves/fitting.rs, next to CONFUSED_CHORD.
Root: Traced::follow keeps main's loop over follow_one, which fits each trace and returns its stalls.
End marching traces on the box of a bounded side #145's trace box: SurfacePair::trace_box (the smaller bounded side's box grown by a tenth, else the model-size cube) and own_box, shared with the coincidence check's surface_corners. The tracer holds the box from Tracer::new, ends a trace on it with box_exit, and answers encloses for the seed check in Traced::follow_one. outside_model and model_exit are gone.
Derivative cones as plain loops. Rebased on Trace every branch through crossings at any confusion #146 and Keep marched curves within confusion between the traced points (#140) #148, the release build left the iterator adapters of bezier_pieces::derivative_cones out of line (+2.3 % on bspline_surface_pair, all in that function; marching's own functions moved by −33 K). That function is untouched by this PR. The last commit writes its two spreads as nested loops in the same order. Results are unchanged.
Generic, not dyn. The pair stays monomorphized for each pair of surface types, so the evaluations inside the Newton loops still inline. The two &dyn uses that were already there (predict, bounds) are unchanged.
circles_meet_exactly (which lane A replaces with a closed-form-table query) moves verbatim to marching/seeding.rs, in seeding_iso_values.
Tests. 18 new direct tests: 12 on the pair (meet with and without a plane, a miss, land, clamp/leaving, predict, tangent, swap, grazing and contact direction on a plane tangent to a cylinder, the confusion threshold of grazes, meet_touching onto the ruling, a surface osculating itself) and 6 on the tracer (a closed plane × sphere loop with the step count the curvature predicts and one unit tangent per point along the trace, a trace ending on both parameter bounds, a touching ruling traced to the model-size cube, a point touch of a plane and a torus, a lost seed at a sphere's pole, knot counting across periods), plus one on the trace box (the smaller bounded side, either order, else the model-size cube). None of them runs a full march.
ADR 0025 records the split, where the two roadmap items go, and why the mode is on the tracer, why the pair is not dyn, and why patch-pair seeds stay lazy. ARCHITECTURE.md gets the folder and a "A change to marching" entry under Where to start.

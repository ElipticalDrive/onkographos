Volume Visualization
3D Volume Rendering of Tomosynthesis Images
Volume Visualization of Specimen Tomosynthesis Case: A detailed 3D rendering highlights the internal structures within the specimen tissue, providing a comprehensive view across multiple planes. This advanced visualization aids in visibility of the morphology of the mass and the margins around the mass.
With Volume Visualization, a comprehensive view of the anatomy is rendered directly from projection images, allowing clinicians to examine structures across multiple planes. This enhanced spatial perception makes it easier to identify clinical features and assess the continuity and relationships of tissue within the anatomy. By visualizing these structures cross all planes, Volume Visualization provides a more complete perspective, leading to more accurate clinical insights and supporting improve diagnostic confidence.
Volume Visualization of Breast Tomosynthesis: This 3D rendering reveals the intricate layers and structures within breast tissue, enhancing visibility of different tissue types and subtle lesions. By improving spatial orientation, this advanced reconstruction provides a comprehensive view of the breast to the clinicians, supporting more accurate diagnosis and improved clinical outcomes.

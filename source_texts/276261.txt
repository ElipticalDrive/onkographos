This submission enhances the volumetric lighting system to address visibility issues and performance bottlenecks. Key changes include:
Enhanced Phase Function: Replaced the single-lobed HG phase function with a dual-lobed version in volumetric_injection.comp, significantly improving fog visibility when looking away from the light source.
Color Propagation: Propagated weather-driven aerosol colors through AtmosphereManager and into the atmospheric LUTs and froxel density calculations.
Optimized Integration: Rewrote the integration compute shader to use a parallel prefix sum, reducing complexity from $O(Z)$ to $O(\log Z)$ and improving GPU throughput.
Bleeding Fix: Introduced a distance-based mask in the atmosphere composite shader to prevent high-resolution haze from bleeding through nearby terrain geometry.
Shadow Stability: Added a small ray offset for volumetric shadows to eliminate light leaking and self-shadowing artifacts at terrain interfaces.

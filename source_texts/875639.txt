Change
Three commits, all in DomainWarpSimplex.inl. No node, setting or public API changes. Below, "first scheme" is the default vectorisation scheme (orthogonal gradient matrix) and "second scheme" the gradient outer product.
1. Vector product helpers.
Orthogonal gradient matrix, 3D (SSE2 to AVX2): the facet decides which axis gets which of three fixed gradient components: (A, C, B), (A, B, C) or (C, A, B). Master selects every component of the three gradients separately. Now f is permuted once (four blends) and multiplied by the fixed components. AVX-512 keeps its permute tables.
Orthogonal gradient matrix, 4D (SSE4.1 and AVX2): every basis gives the four axes roles: the positive indexed axis, the first and second unindexed axes, and either the negative indexed axis (pairwise case) or the third unindexed axis (single and extra cases). f is gathered in that role order from a 16-entry table (two vpermd and a blend on AVX2, one pshufb on SSE4.1). After that the gradient components only depend on the case, which one permute (AVX2) or two blends (SSE4.1) pick. Master works through the four axes one at a time, with compares, masked counters and a table lookup or select per component. SSE2 and AVX-512 keep their code.
4D per half, with a skip: below AVX-512 the 4D orthogonal matrix product runs on each native half of float32v separately and skips a half whose multiplier, the vertex falloff, is 0 in every lane. Both orthogonal matrix bodies are now templates on the register type (...CommonImpl<SIMD, I, F>, ...SimplexImpl<SIMD, I, F>) behind the existing helper functions.
Gradient outer product, 4D (AVX2): the gradient and outer vector components come from the same 20-entry table as on AVX-512, split into two 8-entry halves (one vpermps each, the half picked with a compare). Entries 16 to 19 are entries 0 to 3 negated, so their sign is applied once to the dot product and once to the multiplier instead of to every component. On AVX2, master used the generic selects.
The 2D and 4D orthogonal matrix products get part 8's FN_ALIGN_STACK.
The table-half compare and the 3D facet bits rely on HashPrimes returning a hash with a clear sign bit (its final ( hash >> 15 ) ^ hash shifts arithmetically). The 4D index [0,20) << 27 is then in the upper table half exactly when it is greater than 0x3FFFFFFF, and the two facet bits are never both set. The comments say so.
2. Warp kernels.
DomainWarpSuperSimplex 3D and 4D, as part 7 does for SuperSimplex. Each vertex's direction comes from the sign bit of a difference (SelectSign, SignBit; a compare into a k mask on AVX-512). Its hash is the base hash XOR a precomputed delta for each moved axis and direction. Its hash, gradient product and accumulation are skipped when its clamped falloff is 0 in every lane (AnyInRange: on SSE4.1 and AVX2 an OR of the two halves and one ptest). That covers 7 of the 8 vertices in 3D and 15 of the 16 in 4D, on every feature set.
DomainWarpSimplex 4D: vertices 1 to 3 go through one lambda that computes a vertex's masks, offsets and falloff right before its contribution, so the three vertices' values are not all live at once.
DomainWarpSimplex 3D, AVX-512, first scheme: four of the vertex masks come from masked compares (part 7's MaskedGreater; kUseMaskedGreater keeps it off on MSVC). The second scheme keeps the plain masks.
2D kernels: Warp_2D of both classes is FS_FORCEINLINE, so it becomes part of the 2D Warp(), which part 8's GenFn calls directly. The 2D Warp() and the 3D and 4D kernels get FN_ALIGN_STACK.
3. GCC: force-inline the 4D vector product helpers. Part 4 force-inlined the 2D and 3D helpers on GCC but left FN_WARP_HELPER_4D out of line, because master's 4D bodies, inlined at every vertex, spilled heavily on SSE and AVX2. Commit 1's bodies don't, so GCC now force-inlines the 4D helpers too. The comment above the macros now only gives MSVC AVX-512's reason for keeping them out of line. Clang and MSVC compile the same code as with commit 2.
Why
Below AVX-512, float32v is two registers wide. These kernels keep the warp vector, the vertex offsets and the hash state live across up to 16 vertex contributions, so they spill heavily. Each change removes operations or live values from the contributions. The counts are from clang-cl 23's objects, part 8 against this branch:
3D orthogonal matrix: about 18 operations per contribution instead of about 28. DomainWarpSimplex's 3D kernel (first scheme), which inlines the product at its 4 vertices, goes from 1062 instructions with 446 stack accesses to 905 with 328 on AVX2, and from 1675 with 503 to 1352 with 384 on SSE4.1.
4D orthogonal matrix: the out-of-line 4D product goes from 459 instructions with 146 stack accesses to 273 with 27 on AVX2, and from 763 with 268 to 409 with 24 on SSE4.1: per native half, the product almost fits in the registers. In earlier measurements the same product at full width was 1.09-1.24 times slower on SSE4.1 and AVX2. On a grid the lanes of a vector are neighbours, so often one half lies entirely outside a vertex's range and is skipped.
4D outer product (AVX2): two permutes and a blend per component replace the generic selects, and applying the sign once saves an XOR per component. Clang would fold a test of bit 30 into an extra vpmulld, which the compare avoids. The AVX2 DomainWarpSuperSimplex 4D kernel with the second scheme inlines this product at all 16 vertices. With commit 2's skips, it goes from 4793 instructions to 3924.
SuperSimplex vertex skips and hash deltas: the same reasons as in part 7. The vertex hash no longer waits for the direction compare. A skipped vertex saves its hash, its gradient product and its accumulation, and on a grid the far vertices are often out of range for every lane. DomainWarpSuperSimplex's 3D kernel (first scheme) goes from 2126 instructions with 922 stack accesses to 1766 with 655 on AVX2, and from 3314 with 993 to 2602 with 649 on SSE4.1.
DomainWarpSimplex 4D per vertex: stack accesses in its 4D kernel (first scheme) go from 359 to 225 on AVX2, 320 to 226 on SSE4.1 and 323 to 227 on SSE2.
AVX-512 masked compares: the 3D kernel kept its k masks on the stack. With the masked compares, DomainWarpSimplex's AVX-512 3D kernel (first scheme) drops its 4 kandd, 6 knotd and 7 kmovd and gains 4 compares. It goes from 767 instructions with 134 stack accesses to 739 with 118. The second scheme doesn't spill its masks, so there the extra compares would only compete with its FMAs.
2D kernels: a 2D warp is now one call from GenFn instead of two (GenFn 2D makes 3 calls instead of 4). The 3D and 4D kernels stay out of line, because inlining them was slower on AVX-512 in earlier measurements. The 3D product and the SuperSimplex kernels stay at full width, as in part 7: per-half versions measured slower.
GCC: SysV passes the two-register float32v through memory, so every call to an out-of-line helper stores and reloads its arguments. A 4D warp calls these helpers up to 16 times per point.
Effect

Both builds use FastSIMD 7aff025 and run interleaved in one process, in 3 processes with the A/B order alternating; each figure is the median of the per-process new/old time ratios (lower is faster). Orthogonal matrix products (commit 1; every compiler): the same products, summed in a different order. This changes the first-scheme cases in 3D on SSE2, SSE4.1 and AVX2, and in 4D (and 2D tileable output, which samples 4D) on SSE4.1 and AVX2. That is 28 cases with clang, GCC and MSVC 19.44, and 36 with MSVC 19.51. AVX-512 keeps its tables and is bit-identical. The outer product lookups are bit-identical too: negating the dot product or the multiplier gives the same bits as negating every component.
Kernels (commit 2): the same operations, accumulated in a different order around the new branches and in the inlined 2D kernel, under fast-math. With clang-cl 23 this changes 38 cases. They are DomainWarpSimplex 4D with either scheme on SSE2, SSE4.1 and AVX2 (and its 2D tileable output), DomainWarpSuperSimplex 3D, and the 2D grids where the kernel is now inlined. The fractals and the MountainTerrain tree change through them. GCC changes 35 (GCC 13) to 44 (GCC 16) cases, and MSVC 19.51 24 cases, all SSE2 and SSE4.1 in 2D and 4D. MSVC 19.44 is bit-identical to commit 1. The masked compares give the same masks: DomainWarpSimplex AVX-512 3D doesn't change.
GCC 4D helpers (commit 3): GCC rounds the inlined helpers differently in 4 cases: DomainWarpSuperSimplex with the first scheme and the independent fractal over it, in 4D and 2D tileable output, on SSE2 with GCC 16 and on SSE4.1 with GCC 13. The differences are up to 1.8e-6.
Same values as the complete performance branch: with clang-cl 23, Linux clang 23 and GCC 16, every domain warp case and the MountainTerrain tree are now bit-identical to it. With part 8, 58 of these cases still differed from it on clang-cl 23. The remaining differences are in cases that the complete branch evaluates through part 10's block functions. MSVC 19.51 differs on the MountainTerrain grids and tileable output on SSE2 to AVX2 (up to 3.4e-7, as with part 8). GCC 13 differs in the 4 cases of commit 3 (up to 1.8e-6). On WASM every domain warp and MountainTerrain grid is bit-identical to the complete branch's dump.
No behaviour changes. A skipped vertex or half has a falloff of 0 in every lane, so it would add 0. The sign-bit selection picks the same direction as master's comparison, and the hash deltas give the same integers as master's per-vertex prime sums.
Testing
Builds: clang-cl 23.1.3, MSVC 19.51 (VS 2026), MSVC 19.44 (VS 2022), GCC 16 (snapshot), GCC 13.3, clang 23.1.4 on Linux and Emscripten 6.0.12, all without new warnings (GCC 16 shows FastSIMD's four known -Wignored-attributes warnings, as on master). Amplitude-0 skips: part 8's check, run against the complete performance branch. DomainWarpGradient, DomainWarpSimplex and DomainWarpSuperSimplex, and the two domain warp fractals over them, with masked, Constant 0 and constant 0 amplitudes, all feature sets, 2D/3D/4D, 2 seeds, on uniform grids and on random position arrays: 1320 configurations each. MSVC AVX-512: the masked compares are off on MSVC, so MSVC 19.44 and 19.51 compute the AVX-512 3D domain warp cases alike. The one exception is a single point 2e5 away in the progressive fractal over DomainWarpSimplex, which differs the same way on master, on part 8 and in the complete branch.

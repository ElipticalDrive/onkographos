Description:
Manage full lifecycle of chunks including creation, activation, and removal under a unified deterministic registry.
Rules:
ChunkManager is the only system tracking active chunks
Chunks are registered on creation and deregistered on destruction
Lifecycle states are deterministic:
Generated → Active → Pending Destruction → Destroyed
No scene exists outside registry awareness
Acceptance Criteria:
All chunks are tracked in a central registry
No duplicate or ghost chunks exist
Lifecycle transitions are consistent across clients
System remains stable under long play sessions

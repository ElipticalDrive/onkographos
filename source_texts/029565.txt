by having the hessian plane equation you can use the dot product to calculate the distance of a point to a plane. So when each triangle stores it's plane equation (vec4) you can easily calculate the distance to the voxels center. To figure out which voxel is hit, you can use rasterization technique and render from all three sides (x, y and z direction). when doing so, deactivate culling and depthtest

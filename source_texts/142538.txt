A Directional Occlusion Shading Model for Interactive Direct Volume ...
In this paper, we propose a novel shading model for interactive direct volume rendering that provides perceptual cues similar to that of ambient occlusion, for both solid and transparent surface-like features. An image space occlusion factor is derived from the radiative transport equation based on a specialized phase function.

Advanced fragment shader implementing some kind of GPGPU raymarcher to render a 3d isosurface (here an isocube). Lit with 8 lightsources using phong shading. The "rubber" effect is achieved by varying the rotation matrix with the y-coordinate of the billboard used as raymarching surface.

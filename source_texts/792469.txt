Volumes — Blender Manual
Increase to reduce render time, at the cost of losing detail. Viewport Step Rate Global multiplier on the step size for all volumes in the viewport. Increase for more responsive viewport rendering. Max Steps Maximum number of steps through the volume before giving up, to protect from extremely long render times with big objects or small step sizes.

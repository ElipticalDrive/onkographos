A ray parallel to an AABB face produces 0/0 when its origin lies on that face. intersect_aabb then returns NaN crop bounds, including through Cameras.generate_rays; even interior parallel rays can have NaN backward gradients from the unused divisions.
Handle exact-zero directions using the slab constraint: a parallel ray inside the slab imposes no parameter bound; a parallel ray outside the slab misses the box. Protect the divisor before arithmetic and intersect the remaining finite intervals. Nonzero directions, including very small ones, retain the existing geometry and gradients.

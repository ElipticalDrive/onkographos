GPU Ray Caster This project involves moving the GPU Ray Caster from VTKEdge into VTK, then making this volume mapper available from Slicer. Overview of GPU Ray Casting VTK supports several volume rendering techniques for both regular, rectilinear grids (vtkImageData), and tetrahedral meshes (represented by vtkUnstructuredGrid).

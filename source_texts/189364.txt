Summary
New pure module src/solar-physics/shadowOcclusion.ts — generic 3D ray-intersection math, no knowledge of scene/ types. This is the geometric core that everything else in M3 builds on.
Scope
Ray vs. tilted plane (for shape/roof occlusion — takes raw Vec3[] vertex arrays, not scene/ types)
Ray vs. cylinder and ray vs. cone (tree obstructions — canopy-as-cone over a trunk cylinder, matching what M2 actually renders)
Ray vs. box (building obstructions)
isPanelOccluded(panelPosition: Vec3, sunDirection: Vec3, obstacles: Obstacle[]): boolean — the composed entry point issue #(simulation loop changes) will call
Depends on
Nothing — independent pure-math module.
Testing
Same rigor as sunPosition/poaIrradiance: hand-computed/known-geometry reference cases (e.g. a panel directly behind a known obstacle at a known sun angle should occlude; the same panel with the sun on the opposite side should not).
Spec reference
docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-solarly-m3-design.md, "New pure module: src/solar-physics/shadowOcclusion.ts"

Requested by João · project thread
Before: shadows had hard, uniformly blurred edges, decks and coves were evenly lit, the sea reflected only the sky, and there was no way to change graphics quality by hand.
After: the sea mirrors hulls, sails and cliffs. Things touching the deck or sand cast crisp contact shadows, creases sink into soft ambient occlusion, and sunlight bounces colour onto nearby surfaces. Sun shadows are sharp at the foot and soften with distance. A new Graphics menu (home screen and pause menu) offers Auto/Low/Medium/High/Ultra plus per-effect toggles, applies changes live with an FPS readout, and remembers the choice.
WebGL has no hardware ray tracing, so all of this is screen-space ray marching.
How:
src/render/screenSpace.ts: a half-res MRT pass ray-marches the depth buffer for cosine-hemisphere AO plus one-bounce GI from the colour buffer, and marches contact shadows toward the sun, gated by the shadow map. A depth-aware denoise and upsample follow, and the pipeline composites the result.
src/render/softShadows.ts: PCSS. The shadow pass writes packed depth to the shadow map's colour attachment, a blocker search estimates the penumbra, and 16-tap hardware PCF covers it.
src/world/ocean.ts: SSR. Reflections march along the swell normal with a bisection refine, reject hits under the water, and fade thin hits.
src/core/settings.ts + src/ui/graphics.ts: presets, per-trace budgets and localStorage (aquati.graphics). Auto stays the default and steps effects down when resolution scaling isn't enough. ?ssdebug=1..4 shows debug views.

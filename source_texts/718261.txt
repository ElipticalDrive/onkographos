Deferred from #14. The complete contract is in the generator v4 follow-up roadmap.
Goal
Implement the persistent SVO-like structure selected for Rycraft: a surface-first paged quadtree
with sparse volumetric bricks, not a pointer-heavy world-scale octree.
Scope
Use signed 1,024-block surface regions aligned with terrain-authority pages and 16-block leaves.
Store breadth-first flat node pools with hot traversal records at or below 32 bytes.
Store ordered vertical surface runs and allocate sparse 16-cubed bricks only for overhangs, cave
mouths, structures, deep cuts, edits, and other cubic exceptions.
Use explicit signed region and brick coordinates with no lossy global key.
Persist versioned, fingerprinted, renderer-neutral pages with source hashes and CRC-32.
Restore the last connected parent frontier without replaying learned inference or hydrology.
Add corruption-safe rebuild, compaction, integrity, disk-growth, and memory-accounting tools.
Acceptance
Deleting or rebuilding derived hierarchy pages cannot change world authority, edits, or metadata.
Corrupt, missing, and old pages rebuild safely.
Warm restart restores valid hierarchy pages and the connected frontier without avoidable model or
hydrology work.
Surface and sparse volumetric ownership produce no duplicate faces at their handoff.
Persistence and lookup cover negative coordinates and the complete vertical range.
Dependencies
Depends on #16 and #17.

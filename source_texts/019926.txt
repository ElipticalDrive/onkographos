Starting from a wavelet decomposition of the data, a low resolution image is computed; this approximation can be successively refined. The practical need for such a multiresolution approach is motivated. The mathematical techniques involve Fourier and wavelet analysis. 1. Background and motivation There is a growing interest in the visualization and exploration of digital data representing large three-dimensional (3D) volumes, which can result from measurement by physical equipment, or from computer simulation.

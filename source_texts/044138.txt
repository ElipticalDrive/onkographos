Failing to Reflect Reality: Gaussian Splatting’s Collapse on non-Lambertian Surfaces This video shows renderings of transparent and reflective objects using Gaussian Splatting and Gaussian Shader. It helps to visually understand the various failure points of the method: fake geometry computation, when provided ground truth geometry, insufficiency of spherical harmonic to model the surface, inhability to produce a surface light field, which is necessary to model non-Lambertian objects.

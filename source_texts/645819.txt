💡 What: Implemented viewport bounding box (AABB) culling and WeakMap bounds caching in BuildingRenderer.ts.
🎯 Why: Previously, during every camera movement event (rebuildOnCameraChange), every building polygon in mapData.buildings was being projected via MapLibre (projectPoint) and rendered into PixiJS graphics objects, even if the building was completely off-screen.
📊 Impact: Reduces MapLibre projection math and PixiJS polygon drawing calls by ~70-90% when zoomed into a area, eliminating unnecessary CPU/GPU work on every camera frame.
🔬 Measurement: Tested with pnpm build and pnpm test. Culling uses a 20% viewport padding margin to prevent popping artifacts during panning.

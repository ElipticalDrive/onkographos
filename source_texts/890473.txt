This PR implements a comprehensive overhaul of the sensory experience to align with the "stunning, organic, immersive" vision.
Key changes:
Terrain Generation: Moved from basic noise summation to Fractal Brownian Motion (FBM) and ridged noise, creating more realistic hills and valleys.
Visual Polish:
Added DepthOfField post-processing effect.
Dynamic fog density based on camera height (thinner at peaks).
Varied size/opacity for atmospheric particles.
Randomized tree colors.
Audio Immersion:
Stereo wind synthesis (2 distinct panned layers).
Added high-pass filtered insect ambience.
Controls:
Implemented smooth damping for camera look rotation to remove "robotic" 1:1 mouse mapping.
Verified using verification/test_terrain.js and verification/verify_interactions.py.
PRD Completion: ~65% (Visuals/Audio/Controls core complete).

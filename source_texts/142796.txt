DVGO Rendering | SenseTime-FVG/OpenDWM | DeepWiki
The DVGO (Direct Voxel Grid Optimization) Rendering module provides efficient volumetric rendering utilities for 3D scene rendering and visualization within OpenDWM. Located in the base_vq_models package, this module implements differentiable ray-based rendering with CUDA acceleration, primarily used for LiDAR point cloud generation and evaluation.

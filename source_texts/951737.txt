For the 2.24 docs branch: documents the mipmap filters and the planar reflection changes from playcanvas/engine#9932. Merge it once that engine PR has landed.
Changes:
Physical Rendering > Environment Lighting > Planar reflections (en + ja): the PlanarRenderer script generates its mipmaps with the Gaussian filter, with mipmapFilter: 'box' for cheaper ones; the reflection follows the TAA jitter of the main camera; and mirrors read the texture once, while rough surfaces read it a few more times.
Cameras > Depth Layer (en + ja): a new Color map mipmaps section on CameraComponent#sceneColorMapMipmapFilter and CameraComponent#sceneColorMapMipmaps, with an example. The GrabPass entry describes the redesigned example.
Advanced Rendering > Render Targets > Mipmaps (en + ja): the mipmapFilter texture option, with an example, and the textures the Gaussian filter applies to.

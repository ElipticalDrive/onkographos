Large-scale volume data such as smoke in the virtual scene need to be visualized in real-time in the simulation of disaster.However,existing algorithm cannot process the occlusion and clipping of volume in real-time in the hybrid rendering of both polygon and volume data,and can cause the wrong clip of fragment in the endoscope rendering.A novel GPU based hybrid rendering algorithm is proposed in the paper,which treats the volume as participate media,and the result of polygon rendering is used as the restriction of volume integral region.We generalize the clipping to volume rendering and integrate the hybrid rendering seamlessly.Experimental result demonstrates that the algorithm can render the large volume data in the virtual scene and endoscope in real-time.

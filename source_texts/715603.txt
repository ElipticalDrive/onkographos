The primary issue is that dividing by W causes inconsistent compression of the triangles along Z (or Y in this case) resulting in artifacts due to floating point errors. To fix this we could skip the W divide on the just the Y axis, measure the bounds, then compress that to [-1, 1] on Y to avoid floating point error propagation where possible.
One issue is that the root "ObjectBVH" is constructed with the frustum transform already embedded, meaning the bounds aren't sampled correctly in the shader.

Perhaps an unproject and reproject?

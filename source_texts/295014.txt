Reviewed the fix for the gap flagged during pre-PR review of this branch: overlay_revision now comes from chunk_occupancy_revision() -- a deterministic FNV-1a hash over the chunk's occupied voxels (plus a 1-voxel padding margin for correct boundary-face culling), sorted by global_key() for determinism -- rather than the global replay-step counter.
This directly resolves the earlier concern: an unchanged chunk now keeps the same overlay_revision across step transitions, so the render cache correctly reuses geometry for chunks that didn't change, instead of rebuilding every visible chunk on every step. Verified by reading the actual filter bounds and hash computation, not just the function name.

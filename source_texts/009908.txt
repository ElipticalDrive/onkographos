Volume visualization is a technique for extracting information from volumetric datasets through interactive graphics and imaging, focusing on the representation and manipulation of 3D data. It encompasses various methods for visualizing data that may not have clear surfaces or edges, utilizing techniques such as volume rendering and voxelization. Volume graphics, a related field, aims to synthesize and render volumetric geometric objects, potentially advancing 3D graphics by providing alternatives to traditional surface graphics.

3D scene reconstruction and novel view synthesis have undergone a paradigm shift with the advent of 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS). Unlike computationally intensive volumetric rendering, 3DGS leverages a point-based representation with a differentiable tile-based rasterizer.

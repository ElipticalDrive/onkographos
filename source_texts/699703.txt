What
Implemented a caching mechanism for Delaunay triangulation in watserface/face_helper.py.
Why
create_normal_map performs Delaunay triangulation on every call, which is computationally expensive (O(N log N)) and unnecessary for stable face meshes (MediaPipe 478 landmarks). Recomputing it per frame can also lead to flickering if the triangulation flips due to minor jitter.
Impact
Reduced CPU usage during normal map generation.
Improved temporal stability of the generated normal map.
Performance gain of approximately 25% for this specific operation (based on memory).
Verification
Created a unit test tests/test_face_helper_performance.py verifying:
Triangulation is cached for a "good" (frontal-ish) face.
Triangulation is NOT cached for a "bad" (distorted/side-view) face.
Cached triangulation is reused on subsequent calls.
Verified that ruff and black checks pass.

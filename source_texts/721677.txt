The parser reads every stage out of an rvmat, but the shader only has samplers for four maps: uNormalMap, uSpecularMap, uDetailMap, uMacroMap.
Dropped on the floor:
Stage 4, _as — ambient shadow. Loaded by nothing, sampled by nothing, though the file sits right next to the others in most materials.
Stage 6, fresnel — typically procedural, #(ai,64,64,1)fresnel(0.5,0.2). The shader hardcodes its own fresnel curve instead.
Stage 7, environment — commonly a3\data_f\env_co.paa with useWorldEnvMap="true". The shader fakes it with a sky/ground gradient.
Wanted: at minimum sample _as as an ambient occlusion multiplier, since that one is a plain texture and the difference is visible. Fresnel and env are a larger job and could be split out if this gets too big.

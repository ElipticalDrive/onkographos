Summary
This adds a new Ultra graphics tier to the game. It is the default on desktop browsers with WebGL2.
New render pipeline (game3d/fx.js). The scene is rendered once into an HDR target with a depth texture. Everything else works from that depth:
Volumetric light. Ray-marched height fog with drifting 3D noise, lit by the room's torches. The moon or sun lights it through its shadow map, so arches, pillars and windows cut real light shafts into the mist.
Ambient occlusion. Scalable ambient obscurance at half resolution gives soft contact shadows in corners and under props.
Screen-space reflections. Floors only, with per-room wetness: rain puddles in the courtyard and on the watchtower, wet dungeon stone, polished throne-room, hall and treasury floors. Rays that leave the screen fall back to the sky colour outdoors.
Compositing. A depth-aware upsample combines all three onto the image.
Depth of field. A golden-angle gather focused on the knight; it pulls focus to the duel in combat and softens the world when the knight falls.
FXAA. Runs on the graded image on every tier, with film grain moved after it so it isn't smeared.
Final pass.
Anamorphic streaks from bright lights.
Lens dirt lit by the bloom.
Heat shimmer above flames.
Letterbox bars for the golden armour, the knight's death, the Necromancer's end and victory.
Storm.
GPU-animated rain in the courtyard and on the watchtower: wind-blown streaks and splash rings, lit by lightning.
Rain hiss and thunder that follows each flash, added to the soundscape.
Photo mode.
Press P, or 📷 in the top bar, to free the camera:
drag to orbit the knight;
scroll or +/- to zoom;
[ and ] to change the depth-of-field blur;
P or Esc to exit.
The game's keyboard shortcuts are blocked while it's on.
It won't open during combat and closes itself on a fight or room change.
Scaling.
The toggle now cycles ◆ Ultra → ◆ 3D → ◇ 2D. Phones keep the standard renderer.
Ultra shadows are 2048px.
If frame rate still can't hold at the lowest dynamic resolution, Ultra sheds effects in this order: fog, reflections, depth of field, occlusion.
Reduced motion freezes the fog and turns off the heat shimmer.
Housekeeping.
sw.js precaches fx.js (cache v9).
The social card (img/og-game.jpg, showing the cloister light shafts) and the two homepage game screenshots are re-rendered with Ultra.
Patch notes, README and build label (v12.0) are updated.
Testing
All in headless Chromium (swiftshader):
Every room rendered in Ultra and reviewed. Fog density, key-light strength and reflection strength were tuned from side-by-side comparisons; the lair and cloister have per-room overrides.
Combat (depth of field, strike ring) and death (letterbox, desaturation) checked.
Standard tier and the phone layout still render. The toggle cycles correctly.
Photo mode:
entering it keeps the camera exactly where it was;
drag, wheel and ] respond;
arrow keys and W don't change rooms while it's on;
Esc exits and clears the camera override;
P during combat is ignored.
The v11 regressions pass with no page errors: room tour, sigil click-to-walk, perfect strike, click and Space to strike, reduced motion, phone layout, audio (now including rain).
Ultra costs about 1.7× Standard per frame on software rendering.
I measured 1024px procedural textures at about 3× the generation time per set and kept them at 512px to avoid hitches on room entry.
node --check and scripts/check_site.py pass.

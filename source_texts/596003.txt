Medium Risk
Touches CUDA rendering kernels, scene state wiring, and audio encoding flow; regressions could show up as flipped camera orientation, incorrect raymarch stepping/collisions, or mismatched audio/frame synchronization.
Overview
Adds a new CUDA SDF raymarcher (Mandelbulb) and a RaymarchScene + RaymarchDemo to render it from state-driven camera/light parameters.
Extends the CUDA geodesic raymarcher to support an optional collidable sphere (new sphere_* state inputs) and adjusts camera ray direction setup. GeodesicScene now passes sphere radius/center into the kernel.
Introduces per-frame audio amplitude tracking: AudioWriter records the max voice sample per video frame, Scene publishes it to global state as voice, and projects/scripts can now drive visuals from audio loudness. Also renames manifold parameter keys from u/v to a/b across ManifoldScene and demos.
Includes assorted small fixes/tweaks: larger RPN eval stack, clearer operator parse errors, minor Connect4 steady-state search/debug output adjustments, and a depth-buffer indexing fix in CUDA manifold rendering.

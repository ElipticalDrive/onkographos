This article primarily introduce the basic principle of volume ray tracing algorithm and its realization. The object is first devided as a number of voxels.If ray hands over with voxel,tracing ray will be reflected or transmitted by voxel.Voxels are viewed by volume visualzation technology.Then real graphic is emerged. The volume ray tracing algorithm can engender the complex and high scenery, can be smaller than the ray traceing algorithm at the information's store, and can embody the research of the deep layer of object.

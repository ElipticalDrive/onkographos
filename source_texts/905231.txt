# Screen Space Ambient Occlusion Techniques (SSAO, HBAO, HBAO+) Implementation in OpenGL

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2ab0f521-48a6-49ca-a496-367afe921ca6

Implementation of Screen Space Ambient Occlusion (SSAO), Horizon-Based Ambient Occlusion (HBAO), and Alchemy Screen-Space Ambient Obscurance (HBAO+) in OpenGL using the Sibenik Cathedral scene created by Marko Dabrovic.

SSAO was first introduced in 2007 by Vladimir Kajalin, a Crytek developer who was working on the Crysis game series. HBAO was introduced in 2008 by NVIDIA Researchers Bavoil & Sainz in their work called ["Image-space horizon-based ambient occlusion"](https://developer.download.nvidia.com/presentations/2008/SIGGRAPH/HBAO_SIG08b.pdf), and HBAO+ ([The alchemy screen-space ambient obscurance algorithm](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2018323.2018327)) was introduced by McGuire et al. in 2011.

Development blog and implementation details can be found in [this post in my Hashnode blog](https://cg2homeworks.hashnode.dev/implementation-of-common-screen-space-techniques).

Sibenik Cathedral is a popular 3D model used for testing and demonstrating various rendering techniques, including ambient occlusion techniques. It includes 75284 triangles and 83490 vertices. As stated in [this copyright notice](https://github.com/fsaltunyuva/ScreenSpaceReflectionsOpenGL/blob/main/working_dir/sibenik/copyright.txt), model is created by Marko Dabrovic, with holes corrected by Kenzie Lamar at Vicarious Visions and high-resolution texture and bump maps painted by Morgan McGuire.

Objective To explore the reliability of displaying extracranial carotid arteries with calcifications by sixteen-detector row CT angiography with volume rendering.Methods The extracranial carotid arteries stenoses with calcification of twenty-four patients were selected by analyzing axial images.Coronal and sagital images were reconstructed with maximum intensity projection and volume rendering technique.MIP and VR images were compared in demonstrating arterial lumens.Transverse and anteroposterior diameters of these vessels were measured in all kinks of images(traverse,MIP,VR and DSA),and there correlation were analyzed.Results Forty-nine calcifications were found in traverse images,all of 98 delineation of arterial lumens could be displayed in VR images,but only 22 in MIP.Correlation between diameters measured with VR,traverse and DSA were good.Conclusion Delineation of extracranial carotid arterial lumens with calcification can be exactly displayed with volume rendering.

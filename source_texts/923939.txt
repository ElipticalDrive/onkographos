Fix
Normalize the forward direction before CameraView.Look constructs its basis. Non-unit directions now produce unit camera axes, correct depth mapping, and reciprocal view/inverse transforms. The overload, module helper, and forward/backward aliases inherit the correction; direction magnitude is documented as ignored.
Location, sky, handedness, and public signatures are preserved. LookAt and the raw constructor are unchanged; no new zero/parallel-direction validation or fallback is introduced.
Performance
Equivalent already-unit inputs only, using axis-aligned and oblique directions across 128 translated locations. Aggregate median ns/call from 128 processes / 1,536 samples. Allocations stayed 792 B/call in every workload; non-unit original results are not treated as equivalent successful work.

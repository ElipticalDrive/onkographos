Summary
Adds optional volume normalization to the SmartTube player using Android DynamicsProcessing.
supports disabled, global, and selected-channel modes
provides gentle, balanced, and strong presets
exposes controls in player settings, the quality dialog, and playback actions
persists selected channels and normalization intensity
defaults to disabled, preserving current playback behavior
includes English and French strings
Motivation
YouTube content can vary substantially in perceived loudness between channels and videos. This feature lets users reduce those jumps without changing the device-wide volume or affecting users who leave the option disabled.
Implementation notes
The implementation uses Android DynamicsProcessing on supported Android versions and fails closed when the effect is unavailable. It attaches to the active audio session and is released with the player lifecycle.

Problem
In vanilla Vintage Story, AnimatableRenderer executes its draw calls in Opaque, OIT, ShadowNear, and ShadowFar passes on every frame without a bounding frustum test. In worlds with many animated blocks (doors, chests, mechanical components), dozens of draw calls and joint evaluations run even when completely outside the camera view or shadow volume.
Proposed solution
Inject a bounding sphere frustum test at the entry of AnimatableRenderer.OnRenderFrame for each render stage. The bounding sphere must encompass the rest mesh radius expanded by joint transformation bounds and vertex warping. When skipping a call, preserve or match expected OpenGL state (depth mask, face culling, blending) to avoid state leakage into subsequent renderers.
Impact
Eliminates redundant GPU state changes, draw calls, and CPU matrix evaluations for off-screen animated blocks.
Acceptance criteria
Off-screen animated block entities are culled from Opaque, OIT, and shadow passes.
Visible animations show zero clipping or popping at 4K resolution across all camera angles.
OpenGL state remains consistent for subsequent render passes.

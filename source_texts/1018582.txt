--lod game writes a Poseidon P3D with four levels a whole forest can afford:
near <= 150k triangles (meshoptimizer wood, folded alpha-cut leaf cards),
mid ~30k (twigs dropped, flat 2-triangle leaf cards), far ~6.5k and very
far ~1.5k (trunk and limbs, crossed spray cards baked from the tree's own
leaves). Conifers draw spray cards from mid on and spray cards under needle
fans up close. Thinned leaves grow area-preserving so the crown keeps its
coverage. Origin = trunk foot (a fir's lowest whorls droop below it).

The leaf atlas (512) carries the leaf outlines in alpha plus two sprays;
every texture ships its _nohq normal map, and faces name no RVMAT, as the
engine's own generated trees do. --prefix sets the PBO prefix;
--inspect --lod game reports every level. Oak: 482k -> 148k/28k/6.5k/1.5k
triangles, 562k -> 192k vertices at the nearest level.

Summary
Meshing asks each voxel of its section, and each voxel's neighbours, for its geometry entry (PaletteFacts::at) and seasonal shelter. Every answer unpacked each storage layer's index from the packed words, with a division and a modulo, and rebuilt the layer contributors.
PaletteFacts::decode_voxels now unpacks a mixed section's layers once. It forms each voxel's combination of layer indices as a mixed-radix number, and answers each distinct combination once, through a dense table built from the same lookups at makes. at and seasonal_shelters_at inside the section become array reads.
Neighbouring sections keep the packed lookups. Sections with larger palettes (over 4,096 combinations) or truncated storage are not decoded.
SeasonalCoverage decodes the sections above as well, and reads them from the top, stopping once every column has its highest blocker. Heights increase with the section offset, so the first blocker found is the maximum the previous scan computed.
Measurements
Browser build on a live server, Chrome CPU profile over the first 45 s of a join: meshing went from 2.05 ms to 1.77 ms per mesh job (−14%).
Testing
cargo test -p meshing --tests
New test decoded_voxels_answer_as_packed_lookups: over all 4,096 voxels of a two-layer section (a three-bit layer packing ten indices per word, over a one-bit layer), and past its edge, the decoded answers equal the packed ones.

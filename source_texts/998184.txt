Hello, thank you for your work. During NVS testing, I found that even after using pose optimization, the target image's camera was difficult to align with the ground truth. The two images below, one showing the render and the other showing the ground truth, show a noticeable camera misalignment. I'm not sure if this is due to scene distortion or poor pose optimization. I used an input size of 448 pixels. Is there any solution?

In our previous experiments, we found that pose estimation cannot perfectly align poses with ground truth. This may be because the optimization process gradually converges to local optima, which means that the minimum loss does not necessarily correspond to the most accurate pose. This phenomenon is more common in sparse views setting, such as two-view cases, but poses are almost accurate in dense views setting.

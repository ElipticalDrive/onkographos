Goals
Add reusable, temporally stabilized screen-space diffuse global illumination to the composable WebGPU render stack.
Demonstrate colored light transport in the newly named Deferred Illumination Lab and clearly distinguish it from Visualization City.
Changes
Add createSSGIShaderPassPipeline() with reusable hemisphere-trace, temporal, depth-history, bilateral-denoise, and composite stages.
Integrate diffuse bounce lighting between GTAO and SSR, with colored emitter panels, interactive controls, and indirect-lighting / bounce-confidence debug views.
Rename the existing Material Lab to Deferred Rendering: Illumination Lab without changing its URL.
Explain Visualization City versus Illumination Lab and document that both share the same SSR implementation.
Sharpen reflection boundaries using 75%-resolution tracing and depth/normal-aware bilateral upsampling.
Stabilize GTAO, SSR, and SSGI sampling to eliminate stationary temporal shimmer.
Fix SwapFramebuffers attachment ownership, preserving borrowed textures while eliminating teardown and resize leaks.

To improve the efficiency and quality of quadrilateral mesh generation in B-spline surface reconstruction,an improved Q-Morph advancing front method is proposed.The initial triangular mesh is optimized to fit the quadrilateral mesh generation.By setting the growth constraints and adjusting the mesh vertex degree,the overall quality of quadrilateral mesh is guaranteed.The final mesh which is suitable for complex surface reconstruction is obtained.An example shows this method is efficient and flexible,the consequent mesh has the advantage of uniformity and regularity.

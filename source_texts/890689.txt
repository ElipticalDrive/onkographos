Hi,
For a customized dataset, you can follow SPinNeRF to generate pseudo depth, get inpatined depth and RGB, and then organize the data as the given spinnerf dataset. Put 60 inpainted or original training images in the RGB_inpainted/Depth_inpainted folder (the object area is masked during training anyway). I was able to create a dataset this way and trained MVIP-NeRF on it.

Severity: P2 | Confidence: MEDIUM — one scene, one machine (RTX 3090), one vendor. Measured on the #986 fix head, real scan Cabin in the forest.ply.
Found while producing the pixel evidence for #986. Filing rather than fixing, because it is a sampling difference rather than a correctness break and it is not on the alpha blocker path.
Observation
After #986 the painterly viewport composite and the standard compute composite share one scene-depth guard (modules/gaussian_splatting/shaders/includes/gs_scene_depth_guard.glsl) and one host derivation (modules/gaussian_splatting/interfaces/gs_scene_depth_linearize.h). They still disagree on which pixels survive an opaque occluder, by about 2%.
960x540 SubViewport, Forward+, composite/depth_test = true (shipped default), one unshaded box between the camera and the splat cloud. Coverage = pixels differing from a splats-hidden reference:

With no occluder the two are identical, so this is entirely in the depth guard's input sampling, not in the colour path or the shared math.
Likely cause (not confirmed)
The two composites address the same two depth textures differently:
viewport_blit.glsl:130-133 reads the splat depth with a clamped texelFetch through a scaled source_uv, and reads the scene depth with texelFetch(u_destination_depth, destination_coord, 0) — i.e. at the DESTINATION pixel.
painterly_composite.glsl reads both with texture(..., sample_uv) through painterly_depth_sampler, a NEAREST sampler (modules/gaussian_splatting/interfaces/painterly_renderer.cpp:1229-1243), at the SOURCE-relative UV.
When the internal size and the destination size match these should agree; the half-texel offset between texelFetch(coord) and texture(uv) is the obvious suspect for a silhouette-width difference of this size. The painterly path also binds the scene depth through the painterly depth sampler rather than its own.
Why it matters
Binary GS-vs-mesh occlusion is a user-visible edge: a 2% difference is a visibly different silhouette when a mesh cuts through a splat cloud, and a user switching painterly/enabled should not see the occlusion boundary move.
Suggested fix
Make the painterly composite address both depth textures the way the compute blit does (texelFetch at the integer destination coordinate, with the same clamped scaled fetch for the splat depth) and give the scene depth its own sampler. Then re-measure the table above; the two rows should match.

Branch: frontier-voxel-engine · Master: #24 · Audit: https://github.com/DrewPhi/MicroVoxelProceduralGeneration/blob/frontier-voxel-engine/frontier/PIXEL_ADAPTIVE_RESEARCH_AUDIT.md
Scope: P1 core renderer; dependencies #59/#60, existing #44/#43 and A/B gate #45. Replaces 64m square in production.
Build sparse exact voxel surface clusters/bricks independently from camera residency. Select visible important clusters through the shared projected-error policy; draw exact 6.25 cm faces where justified, not as one complete 64×64 m near square. Combine with GPU heightfield ONLY where highfield-safe and below measured error. Reuse world-aligned chunk identity and common GPU arenas; cache generated content across views. Support per-cluster culling, local geometry updates and host/device memory budgets.
Implementation approaches to A/B: existing greedy/binary face meshes with GPU-instanced clustered submissions as the simpler baseline; optionally packed voxel bricks / exact DDA for demanding clusters. Do not invent a full SVO/DAG just to select nearby faces; no 1×1 region per voxel or huge draw-call explosion.
Acceptance: ground-level close voxel faces exact; aerial camera draws far fewer native triangles/bytes than existing 64×64 cover at matched image error; near-visible and far-important features remain present. 3/15/80m/s routes with no stale partial upload, no geometry cracks, bounded CPU generation and GPU submission. Report geometry/voxel/cluster counts, p95/p99 whole frame, device bytes, CPU build/upload and image oracle difference.
Legacy B/R: old frontier/renderer/src/native_stream.rs, hybrid_gpu.rs, column_mesh.rs and old main/successor are references/benchmarks; port only isolated exact mesher methods with independent parity proof. Independent inspiration: https://www.reddit.com/r/VoxelGameDev/comments/1uo7tel/microvoxel_trees_and_grass/ ; https://www.reddit.com/r/VoxelGameDev/comments/1qicz42/update_5_chunkee_lods_are_back/ .
Non-goals: no artificial voxel stylization, no refitting quadratics, no forced opaque fog, no hand-authored LOD assets. Keep old comparison scenes but do not mark G1–G4 passed without measured evidence.
Adaptive implementation checkpoint and optimization follow-up — 2026-11-05
Local commit ce3e82a implements the candidate and records evidence in frontier/ADAPTIVE_RENDERING.md and frontier/reference/adaptive/. This commit was not yet pushed when this update was written. Integrated checks: 191 Rust tests and 9 Python tests pass; five manual experiments are explicitly ignored. Latest stationary laptop p95: 18.55 ms (fails the 16.67 ms target). Full motion acceptance remains unmet.
Packed native face clusters, GPU arenas, canonical source/content caches and conservative culling.
CPU replacements and partial GPU uploads survive compatible camera changes; publication rechecks current coverage/revision.
Qualified wide aerial comparison and 3/15/80 m/s quality/performance acceptance remain unmet; high-speed coverage also failed in recorded tests.
Follow-up: [FV] Reduce cold adaptive refinement work with stage profiling and reusable summaries #69, [FV] Parallelize adaptive CPU preparation with bounded jobs and memory #70, [FV] Prototype GPU terrain preparation with canonical parity and end-to-end benchmarks #71, [FV] Persist versioned procedural pages, summaries and geometry for fast revisits #72, [FV] Scale loaded comparison quality from 300 m to multi-kilometre views #73; keep [FV] Keep screen-adaptive detail ahead of 80+ m/s aircraft without native-square lag #64 as the movement scheduling/acceptance owner.
Original acceptance criteria above remain in force; completed substeps do not close this issue.

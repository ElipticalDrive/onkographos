Hi @Shrisudhan and @drebain,
Thanks for open-sourcing the PowerFoam code! It’s a fantastic release. I am trying to extract a surface mesh from the PowerFoam representation. I ran the following command:
```
python -m render \
      -c output/mip360/garden/config.yaml \
      --mesh_name garden_mesh_unbounded \
      --mesh_res 1024 \
      --unbounded
```
However, the resulting mesh quality isn't quite where I expected it to be.
I wanted to check in and confirm if this is the standard or recommended approach for mesh extraction in your pipeline? If there is a better configuration or a different script I should be using, I would really appreciate it if you could point me in the right direction.
Thanks once again for your help!

Hi @abhi1kumar,
Thanks for reaching out! The current implementation uses the TSDF extraction method.
In our experience, PowerFoam can yield decent surface reconstructions when paired with explicit normal supervision (either via depth finite differences or from a pretrained model). Please refer to the README for how to train with normal supervision. However, because surface extraction wasn't the primary focus of this paper, we didn't heavily optimize for surface quality in this release.
That being said, I am confident that the results could be drastically improved by integrating normal/surface regularizers from works like 2DGS or GOF.

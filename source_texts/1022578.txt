Adds mirror reflections to water.frag fragment shader based on ray marching of reflected surface rays.

Fixes a simple issue where rgb zero vectors produces black in the raymarcher instead of the intended background vector.
NOTE: Breaks the use of purposefully defined zero vectors for refraction / reflection
refactors some repurposed code common for the refraction and reflection portion of the shader

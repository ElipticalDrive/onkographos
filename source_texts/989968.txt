The normal/roughness target is RGBA16F, which is 8 bytes per pixel of bandwidth on the fattest GBuffer attachment. Octahedral encoding in R10G10B10A2 halves it with no visible quality loss at this shading rate. Cross-backend shader change, so all four backend variants of the GBuffer and lighting shaders move together; worth doing when profiling shows bandwidth bound.

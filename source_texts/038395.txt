The 3-D reconstruction of several datasets based on VTK,including the spleen and neck vertebra of the Chinese female virtual human and segmented CT slice data is presented.Two types of rendering method,surface rendering and volume rendering are studied.The marching cubes algorithm was used for surface rendering,both MIP and composited volume rendering methods were used for volume rendering.Their characteristics of two rendering methods weree compared as well The reconstructed results showed that as a reconstruction and visualization toolkit VTK has powerful function and is very easy to be used for medical image processing.

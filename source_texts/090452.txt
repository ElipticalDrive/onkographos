This is my Master Thesis in Computer Graphics at Daimi http://www.cs.au.dk. It ray traces voxel data with an sparse octree. The data set used is the Stanford bunny@512x512x361 voxel. The last scene seen is 64x64x64 instances of the bunny which is 262.144 which corresponds to 32.768 Gigavoxels of data running@3.24FPS if all bunnys are in the view.

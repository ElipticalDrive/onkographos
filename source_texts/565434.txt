💡 What: Added a new getShadowFast function that performs a 1-tap shadow map lookup, bypassing the for loops in the regular 9-tap getShadow function. Replaced the getShadow call inside the raymarching loop with getShadowFast.
🎯 Why: The raymarching loop used for calculating daytime volumetric scattering (god rays) performed 12 steps. Inside this loop, it evaluated the 9-tap PCF getShadow function. This resulted in an expensive 108 texture lookups per pixel just for god rays, severely impacting performance. Volumetric scattering accumulates multiple samples, inherently blurring them, so a 1-tap shadow test provides almost identical visual quality at a fraction of the cost.
📊 Measured Improvement: Since dynamic GLSL performance profiling and frame timing are not directly available in this isolated environment, benchmark numbers cannot be provided. However, from static code analysis, the optimization clearly reduces texture bandwidth overhead for the raymarching routine by approximately 89% (from 108 to 12 lookups), yielding significantly improved frametimes on bandwidth-bound GPUs.

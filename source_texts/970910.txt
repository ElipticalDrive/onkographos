Why
Milestone 4 needs a real voxel consumer of the indexed graphics API. This adds an offscreen renderer using the pinned, unmodified bend-voxel dense-body extractor and edit operations.
What changed
Pack exposed faces as indexed quads with basic colors, face direction, and complete material IDs.
Export intact and carved 8³ cubes at 512×512, with upstream renderer references, camera reuse, reset, and explicit mesh replacement.
Add pure laws, independent geometry and pixel checks, GPU metadata diagnostics, and integrated validation and sanitizer runs.
Scope
This checkpoint covers dense 8³ fixtures and basic offscreen rendering. Window presentation and production appearance remain later milestones.
Tradeoffs
Normalized byte attributes preserve full metadata using supported vertex formats. Mesh replacement is synchronous and uses explicit allocations.
Blast Radius
The consumer, shaders, and verifier are new. Vulkan core and native implementation remain unchanged. The verifier now requires the pinned bend-voxel submodule.
Verification
bend PROOF.bend passed before commits.
python3 tools/verify.py --sanitize passed on GTX 1660 with Khronos and synchronization validation, ASan, and UBSan.
GPU readback passed the documented edge policy, full-width material diagnostics, all six directions, camera reuse, repeated edits, reset, and zero-row cleanup.
The packing and acceptance contracts are in docs/voxel.md. The decision trail is in .audit/milestone-4.tsv.

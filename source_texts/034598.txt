Issue body actions
Exporting data/voxedit/chr_knight.vengi with the Marching Cubes mesher produces conspicuous, repeated triangular patterns on the knight's surfaces when the OBJ is viewed in Blender 4.0.
Observed result
The attached screenshot shows:
Rows of sawtooth/diagonal triangular patches across the gray armor, especially the helmet, shoulder/chest band, torso, and waist armor.
Jagged triangular teeth along the boundary between the red helmet crest and the gray helmet.
Abrupt alternating light/dark or differently colored triangles on areas that should read as continuous surfaces or coherent color regions.
The knight's overall shape and separate body parts remain recognizable. The problem is the repeated triangular surface pattern and jagged color boundaries beyond the expected rounding/faceting of Marching Cubes.
Expected result
Marching Cubes should produce a smoothed version of the voxel model with coherent surface colors and shading. Broad armor surfaces and the crest/helmet boundary should not acquire these repeated sawtooth patterns.
Reproduction
```
./vengi-voxconvert --input data/voxedit/chr_knight.vengi --output chr_knight.obj -set voxformat_meshmode 1
```
Then import chr_knight.obj into Blender and inspect the helmet and torso.
Screenshot
The screenshot documents the visible artifacts. It does not establish whether their cause is geometry, triangle color assignment, or normals/shading; that requires inspecting the exported mesh.
It looks like the indices are wrong somehow.

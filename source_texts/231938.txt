Summary
Lighting currently uses a modified Blinn-Phong model with pseudo-roughness approximation (LightingCommon.hlsli).
Requirements
Implement standard Cook-Torrance microfacet specular BRDF:
D: GGX / Trowbridge-Reitz normal distribution function.
F: Schlick approximation for Fresnel reflectance.
G: Smith height-correlated geometry attenuation.
Implement Image-Based Lighting (IBL) for ambient diffuse and specular irradiance (Split-Sum approximation with pre-filtered environment maps and 2D BRDF LUT).

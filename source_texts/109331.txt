Priority P2 · type feature · size M · layer base
What
Designers need to paint and sculpt volumes, and voxel art is made in MagicaVoxel (.vox) or Voxlap/Slab6 (.kv6). Add undoable editor brushes for voxel volumes and importers that turn .vox/.kv6 into volumes or into small voxel models (props and characters drawn as meshed voxels). Done = a .vox model loads as a prop and a designer paints a wall into a level volume in the editor.

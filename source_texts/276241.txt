Adds a volumetric rendering pass to the system using a froxel grid cascade (3 cascades of 64x64x64 3D textures), tracking RGB scattering with emissivity in the alpha channel and extinction across two textures per cascade. Integrates a logarithmically proportioned 2D noise map for analytical height fog, and adheres to Wronski/Hillaire volumetric lighting and participating media guidelines.

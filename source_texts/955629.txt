[FeatureRequest]
I recently tried meshroom, and it often could not use footage I could easily use with openvslam. But just having the point cloud is not quite as satisfying.
Using #87 (comment) you can export point clouds. Another person included a fork for colorful point clouds.
If you have three close points and in the original picture between them is a certain color or texture, what about perspective correcting this texture and putting it on a mesh connecting those three dots.
Using this we could export a colored mesh and not just a one color point cloud.

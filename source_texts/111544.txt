Few technologies have had a more positive and profound effect on Medicine than Medical Imaging. Imaging procedures have altered the practice of Medicine not only from the diagnostic perspective, but also from the therapeutic one with the development of Interventional radiology. Such impact is probably due to the fact that imaging is a noninvasive, usually painless and quick procedure. While static imaging approaches (X-ray, CT, etc) have been the norm, the advent of powerful video compression systems now allows medical video to supplement earlier imaging techniques.

💡 What: Bypassed the expensive distance_to_local_point BVH calculation for points that are strictly exterior (!is_inside) when narrow_band is not enabled, explicitly assigning a fixed positive SDF.
🎯 Why: In the solid voxelization codepath, all particles with a positive SDF are later discarded by the sdf <= 0.0 check unless narrow banding is active. Calling the expensive spatial BVH lookup on strictly exterior points is unnecessary.
📊 Impact: This optimization is a structural change to avoid expensive calculations on exterior regions, yielding significant performance benefits on meshes with sparse density compared to their AABB (e.g. less bounding volume hit checks).
🔬 Measurement: Run cargo test to ensure functional invariants remain intact, and cargo bench to measure performance over small mesh scaling.

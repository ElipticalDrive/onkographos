Why
Measured: a frozen DINOv2 backbone lowers depth AbsRel 42% (0.22 vs 0.38) at 0.65 GB, but the trajectory ATE stays ~0.28-0.61 m across backbones, pose-head variants, and more data. A regression pose head has no geometric constraint, so it drifts; post-hoc global BA on a poor init over-constrains (M-A negative: path blew up to 7 m). SOTA (DROID-SLAM, DPVO, DINO-VO) all solve pose with a differentiable BA layer.
Proposal
Replace the regression pose head with a sparse differentiable BA head seeded by our metric depth:
sparse correspondences from the frozen features (patch sampler + small correlation/update operator)
lift corresponded pixels to 3D with the predicted metric depth + real intrinsics
differentiable weighted alignment / Gauss-Newton over SE(3) ⊕ inverse-depth minimizing reprojection residual (backprop through the linear solve; we already have se3_exp + tested geom)
multi-frame: small keyframe patch graph (DPVO-lite) + optional CPU proximity loop closure -> existing pose-graph + TSDF
Feasibility
Backbone frozen (~4-8 M trainable), sparse BA is laptop-scale (DPVO ~5 GB). Edge: metric depth removes monocular scale ambiguity -> BA well-posed at iter 0. Train with pose+reprojection loss on TartanAir/TartanGround (loader done, --use_tartan); eval held-out on TUM long_office (dual metric ATE+AbsRel). Manuscript: preprints/02-metric-seeded-ba-pose.
Milestones
M-B: single-pair metric-depth-lifted differentiable pose (weighted Procrustes / one GN step), pose_head=geo, opt-in
M-C: recurrent update operator + windowed multi-frame GN BA
M-D: proximity loop closure feeding pose-graph + TSDF for a clean fused map

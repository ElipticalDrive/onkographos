This PR unifies the volumetric lighting system (fog, god rays, local light scattering) with the volumetric cloud system. Both effects now share the same high-performance 1/2 resolution raymarching pipeline, spatiotemporal blue noise jittering, temporal accumulation (TAA), and spatiotemporal filtering (SVGF).
Key changes:
atmosphere_lowres.comp: Extended to traverse the full ray from camera to scene depth, integrating both atmospheric and cloud scattering in a single pass.
AtmosphereEffect: Added parameters for volumetric intensity, anisotropy, and haze exposure. Bound necessary shadow maps and light buffers to the low-res pass.
atmosphere_composite.frag: Simplified composition logic to account for the unified integration.
VolumetricLightingEffect: Deleted the redundant froxel-based implementation and its shaders.
UI: Updated EnvironmentWidget to expose the new unified volumetric parameters.
Examples: Updated the volumetric lighting example to reflect the system merger.

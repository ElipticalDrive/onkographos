Computed Tomography is an advanced imaging technique, it can obtain gray image of the object's layer and does not destruct the object in the same time, it distinguish object layer's geometrical structure, material and defect type by gray, so CT is considered the best method to nondestructive testing. In this paper, the principle of CT is introduced ,the Algebraic Reconstruction Technique is discussed and the reconstruction results of computer simulation using this algorithm are shown, the research direction for furture is presented.

Summary
E1 (Homogeneous Medium) rendered almost black on Metal (cpu=0.2787, metal=0.0894). The parked theory was that medium spheres cast no shadow. That turned out to be backwards for E1, and chasing it found four separate bugs:
Loader: the scene's Material "interface" fog sphere had no case in the pbrt loader, so it became an opaque gray sphere that blocked the light. It now maps to the bounded-medium sphere (materialType 28).
Inside rays: shadeHomogeneousMediumSphere assumed the ray starts outside the sphere. After a scatter inside, the exit distance came out negative and the ray was stepped backwards.
MIS distance: the emissive-hit MIS weight measured distance from the sphere exit instead of the previous bounce, so BSDF and light-sampling weights didn't sum to 1 (Metal ~33% too bright even with thin fog).
Shadow-ray attenuation: shadow rays now get blocked with probability 1 - exp(-sigma_t * chord) for each medium sphere crossed, an unbiased transmittance estimate that avoids editing the ~70 NEE call sites. The medium's own scatter-point NEE drops its hand-rolled exp(-sigma_t * dist), which charged the full distance.
Results (macOS parity sweep)
E1: cpu=0.2023 vs metal=0.1944, worst 6x6 block ~18-22% (was 69% off). Visually matches the CPU render.
A8 (Cornell Smoke) flips FAIL -> pass as a bonus.
No other scene changed status. Full ctest: 10/10.
Not covered / follow-ups
Only homogeneous media on interface-material spheres. Cloud/RGB-grid (materialType 29/30) are still ignored for shadow rays, and a medium on a real surface material (E3) is still a known gap.
Separate pre-existing finding: 15 sweep scenes report metal=nan brightness yet print "pass" because NaN comparisons never trip the tolerance.

E1 (Homogeneous Medium) rendered nearly black on Metal (cpu=0.2787,
metal=0.0894). The earlier theory - medium spheres cast no shadow - was
backwards: the scene's "interface" fog sphere had no case in the pbrt
loader, so it became an opaque gray Lambertian sphere that blocked the
light. Fixing that exposed three bugs in the existing materialType-28
(bounded homogeneous medium) path:

- Loader: an interface-material sphere bounding a homogeneous medium now
  maps to materialType 28 (albedo = sigma_s/sigma_t, sigma_t/sceneScale,
  HG g), instead of the gray fallback.
- shadeHomogeneousMediumSphere assumed the ray starts outside the
  sphere. A continuation ray after a scatter INSIDE it got a negative
  exit distance and was stepped backwards. It now treats an inside
  origin as entry=0, exit=accepted root.
- The MIS weight at an emissive hit used the distance from the sphere
  exit point instead of from the previous bounce, so BSDF and light
  sampling weights no longer summed to 1 (Metal ~33% too bright even
  with very thin fog). The kernel now carries the skipped distance.
- Shadow rays crossing a medium sphere were ignored entirely (floor lit
  through ~2.7 optical depths of fog was 1.5-1.8x too bright). The
  sphere intersection function now blocks them with probability
  1 - exp(-sigma_t * chord): unbiased attenuation without touching the
  ~70 NEE call sites. The medium's own scatter-point NEE drops its
  hand-rolled exp(-sigma_t * dist), which charged the full distance.

Parity: E1 cpu=0.2023 metal=0.1944 (worst block 18%), A8 flips FAIL ->
pass; no other scene's status changed. Parity-check header updated.

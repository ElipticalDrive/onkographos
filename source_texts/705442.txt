The Oculus SDK allows you to submit multiple textures of different sizes for each eye which are composited on top of each other before being displayed. It would be convenient if the VRCompositor allowed you to submit more than one texture per eye and have those automatically composited.
Note that this is not the same as VROverlays, since it is not a texture displayed at a certain position. For now I will handle this type of composition myself.

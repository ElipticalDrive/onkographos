Implements report row G-1 (Wave 7): VoxelVolume core, anisotropic mips and particle GI on the TS fluid. Source of truth: rust/kansei-core/src/gi/{volume,aniso,particles,cones,particle_gi}.rs (#105, #128, #111).
What's in it
src/gi/VoxelVolume.ts: VoxelVolume is an rgba16float 3D radiance texture with a full mip chain, its VoxelVolume uniform and a trilinear clamp sampler. VolumeLayout places the voxels. VoxelGiQuality holds the low/medium/high tiers and fit steps down to what the device limits and a memory budget allow. Mip3d builds a 3D mip chain on the GPU.
src/gi/AnisotropicMips.ts: the six directional chains from mip 1 up (VoxelVolume.setAnisotropicMips), rebuilt by buildMips.
src/gi/ParticleVoxelizer.ts: the splat (fixed-point atomics, mass-conserving) and the resolve, with analytic GiBox walls.
src/gi/ParticleConeShading.ts: six 90° cones plus a sun cone per particle, with a running average. The lighting buffer is exposed as instance attributes (lightingInstanceBuffer). Also gradientSkyLighting.
src/gi/ParticleGi.ts: runs the splat, mips and cones each frame.
WGSL is the Rust engine's own. src/gi/GiWGSL.ts imports it with ?raw under the Rust constant names: VOXEL_VOLUME_WGSL, VOXEL_CONES_WGSL, PARTICLE_EMISSION_WGSL, SKY_LIGHTING_WGSL. cargo test -p kansei-core keeps guarding it.
Pass labels: every pass is timed with F-13's gpuPass under Rust's labels (VoxelGI/Splat, /Resolve, /Mips, /AnisotropicMips, /ParticleCones).
Additive changes to existing files (FluidSimulation.velocitiesBufferRef, used for speed emission, comes from S-1, and CameraControls.elevation from K-4):
exports in main.ts;
the AGENTS.md ?raw entry now names src/gi/GiWGSL.ts too.
New examples/index_voxel_gi_particles.html: the engine part of the Rust voxel-gi-particles demo with scene=cornell, running on the TS fluid.
URL params: gi=off, view=indirect, quality=, particles=, stats=1, profile=1, ui=0.
A Tweakpane panel, and window.kansei for scripted captures.
The lightbox's impostor spheres and RT mirrors are demo-local art, so they are not ported.
Out of scope (later rows)
ParticleGi.enableSdf and JumpFloodSdf are G-3. ParticleConeShading.setSdf is the hook for them: until then a 1-texel stand-in is bound and sdfSun is ignored, as in Rust without a field.
VoxelGiQuality.fitScene needs the mesh voxelizer's surface buffer (G-2).
Verification
tsc && vite build pass. dist/gi/GiWGSL.js inlines the Rust WGSL.
TS page vs the Rust demo (prebuilt site-wasm, ?scene=cornell), headless Chrome via chrome-devtools-axi, same 1280×713 viewport. Both were reset to the initial dam and paused, so the GI settles on identical particles. Mean absolute pixel difference against the Rust frame:
lit: 0.02 / 255
view=indirect: 0.04 / 255
gi=off: 0
These were re-checked after rebasing onto F-6, F-13, P-1, S-1 and K-4. Since S-1 the page pours again with resetParticles and shades the live particleCount, as the Rust demo does.
GPU readback checks against CPU references (run in the browser through Vite):
VolumeLayout and fit reproduce the Rust unit tests ([64,48,32] at 4/64 m with 7 mips; high/medium/low).
Mip 1 and all six anisotropic chains match a CPU compositing reference within f16 rounding (max error 9e-4).
anisotropicBytes agrees with VolumeLayout.anisotropicBytes.
The splat keeps mass: accumulated density / particles is 0.99999 for trilinear splats and 0.9999 for footprint splats.
No uncaptured WebGPU errors.

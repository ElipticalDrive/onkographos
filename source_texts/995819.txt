⚡ Bolt Performance Optimization:
💡 What: Pre-compute the combined reprojection matrix (currViewProj * prevInvViewProj) once per frame outside the pixel loop in FrameGenEngine::generateInterpolatedFrame, and cache matrix elements in local stack registers.
🎯 Why: Previously, for every pixel in a frame (e.g. ~2M pixels at 1080p), the engine performed two full 4x4 matrix-vector transformations (NDC -> World Space and World Space -> NDC) with two divisions and magnitude checks per pixel.
📊 Impact: Cuts per-pixel matrix transformation floating-point operations in half (reducing 32 multiplications + 24 additions down to 16 multiplications + 12 additions per pixel) and eliminates 1 vector division per pixel, dramatically reducing main thread execution time during frame generation.
🔬 Measurement: Verified with unit tests in tests/test_mod.cpp (testFrameGenEngine). All 5 unit test suites pass successfully.

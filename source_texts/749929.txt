This paper focuses on how to calculate, with radiosity techniques, images associated to different scenes \nwhich take into account both an isotropic diffuse participating media and specular surfaces. To do so, we \npropose a new expression of the form factors defined in the zonal method. \nThe first part of this article briefly quotes the expression of energetic exchanges by using operators and \nzonal method equations. The second part deals with the different changes brought to the zonal method in \norder to express it under the form of operators first and then redefine its form factors. Finally, we will \ntreat of computational considerations and see how different images enable us to show the many \npossibilities of our algorithm.

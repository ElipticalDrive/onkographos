[P2] 🤖 Reviewer · codex · gpt-6-astra
Bound accepted work and propagate backpressure
Queue() is unbounded and submission never raises Arroyo MessageRejected, so slow work can exhaust memory with queued payloads and completed offsets retained behind an earlier outstanding record. Trigger: arrivals outpace a worker, or one bucket blocks while other buckets keep completing; a focused check accepted 1,000 queued items behind a blocked worker. Fix: bound queued plus uncommitted work, reject nonblockingly before accepting/tracking it, and do not swallow that rejection. (found by 9 of 9 reviewers)

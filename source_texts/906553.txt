What changed
Surface renders now honor explicit OBJ crop coordinates and indexed UVs, preserve source intensity scaling, and save masks only for pixels supported across the full depth stack. Shared fragment publication stages complete outputs, refuses existing ids, propagates read/write errors, and keeps qualitative inference label-free while publishing supplied supervision with its layers.
Why
Previously, nonzero OBJ origins were ignored, failed chunk reads became fill values, and partial writes or fabricated zero ink labels could look like usable research data. The change adds 80 contract regression cases and documents rendering contract version 2, including the need to rerun historical real-data comparisons.
Design and architecture review. Revalidated after rebasing onto main at f5f775c3.

Edge cases considered
Missing Zarr chunks use the declared fill value; permission/network failures and corrupt payloads abort.
Invalid normals and depth clipping update coverage and the saved mask; fully unsupported regions fail before publication.
uint16 and low uint8 intensities retain their scale. Interpolation roundoff stays within source vertex ranges while actual negative geometry still fails bounds checks.
Negative/oversized crops, invalid signs/divisors, flat scale candidates, malformed grids, ambiguous supervision, and inconsistent depth indices fail explicitly.
Failed TIFF/PNG writes and interrupted OBJ downloads leave no final artifact; existing fragments and labels are preserved.
Cache payload, dense reads, and chunk-index counts are bounded; caller annotations cannot overwrite render provenance.
What was NOT tested
Live S3 transfers, full-scroll rendering, real-data NCC/depth comparisons, GPU inference, model training, or end-to-end registration experiments.
Face-constrained rendering of holes or overlapping UV islands; OBJ interpolation retains its documented single-patch convex-hull limitation.
Remote cache freshness: OBJ keys are assumed immutable and ETags are not checked on reuse.
Reviewer focus
Full-depth validity semantics, source intensity conversion, explicit OBJ crop coordinates, and the shared publication boundary used by all fragment adapters.

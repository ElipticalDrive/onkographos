Adding non-VR support to my VR Voxel game (Devlog #2)
Welcome to another devlog for Voxel Architect!
In this episode, we’ll ironically be looking at the non-VR version of this VR game, and talk about how YOU guys were RIGHT, and I was WRONG, and how your insistence has changed the game for the better.
Voxel Architect is a VR voxel builder game that I'm creating in the Godot game engine. It's unique feature is that you can create the voxels that you're building with, out of smaller voxels.
Hope you enjoy!

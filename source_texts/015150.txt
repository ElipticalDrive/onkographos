Introduction  The Ray-Tracing technique introduced by Whitted [Whit80] is a powerful rendering method to modelized very high specular effects with diffuse approximation. Moreover, this technique is also used in radiosity [Gora84][Nish85] in order to mix the best diffuse interreflection from radiosity method with the specular form of Ray-Tracing [Sill89]. Ray-Tracing has become a standard rendering method in the Computer Graphics Community. However, this recursive technique leads the rendering time to be very expensive.

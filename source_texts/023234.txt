Accurate NeRF synthesis can help plant breeders to effectively analyze and identify plant traits to improve crop yield. Most of the existing mesh-based NeRF new perspective synthesis methods are targeted at bounded scenes, such as Instant-NGP, Plenoxels, DVGO, and tensorf, but wheat is usually grown in outdoor field environments, which belongs to non-boundary scenes. At present, the widely used method is to rely on space warping, but it can only deal with forward trajectory and 360° center surround trajectory, which is a special trajectory, and can't deal with arbitrary trajectory, but there are more free trajectories in the daily shooting process. In order to solve this problem, we propose a new warping function called Variable warping. In this study, we use cell phone to shoot wheat experimental field data, and use the improved Variable warping algorithm to synthesize the new perspective of wheat under free trajectory. The experimental results show that it allows us to reconstruct the new perspective of wheat with high quality under arbitrary camera trajectory.

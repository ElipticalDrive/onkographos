Call For Papers:
We solicit papers that present original research related to all
techniques that combine 3D computer graphics with human interaction,
explicitly including (but not limited to) research that has
applicability for 3D games. A more detailed list of potential topics can be found in the
Call for Participation. Original papers are limited to 8 full-color
pages. The submission of a video to accompany the paper is encouraged. Papers will be peer-reviewed
in a single-blind process and authors notified by e-mail.
Papers should be prepared following the ACM
SIGGRAPH formatting guidelines, with the provison that we will
only accept Category 2 papers. Accepted papers will be published in the "Proceedings
of the 2008 Symposium in Interactive 3D Graphics," an
archival-quality proceedings distributed by the ACM.
The paper submission deadline is October 22, 2007.
Paper reviews are due December 1, 2007.

[P1] Recompute poses with final intrinsics before returning
The poses are generated while the loop is still using the provisional CameraMatrix guess (they come from pose_from_homography_normalized built from width/height). After the linear solve produces kmtx_opt, those poses are returned unchanged. Because the homography decomposition depends on the intrinsics, the returned c_se3_t can be arbitrarily biased whenever the initial guess differs from the final CameraMatrix. Downstream callers using these extrinsics together with the estimated intrinsics will therefore start optimisation from an inconsistent pose set. Re-run planar pose estimation with the solved kmtx_opt before packaging the result.

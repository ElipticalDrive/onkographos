Goal
Move beyond source-triangle-derived perpendicular and edge segments in #42. This adds deterministic, independently geometry-checked off-surface finite segment testing against the read-only MOPP interval broadphase, without changing native GMP files or claiming Havok runtime raycasting.
Sampling and independent ground truth
Two explicitly separated strata: expanded-map-bounds random endpoint pairs that are not aimed at a source face, and random-direction triangle-aimed segments with off-surface endpoints to ensure a collision-positive control. The geometry-only BVH and transverse/coplanar oracles from #40–42 determine all original triangle intersections first; both source-observed MOPP interval candidate modes are tested against those ground-truth source IDs.
Private 19-map original byte research
Original user-owned source hashes and Level/MOPP byte counts cross-checked against earlier audits. At 512 independent random-bounds + 512 aimed segments per map, the offline source-byte experiment sampled 19,456 finite segments, including 3,503 randomly drawn contact segments, 6,225 random no-contact controls, and 9,728 targeted positive segments. Independent geometry yielded 24,240 hit original triangle ID occurrences. Both conservative and experimental diagonal modes retained all independently observed hits over 38,912 candidate-set comparisons, with zero observed omissions. Retail map bytes/vertex coordinates are NOT committed. These are finite empirical probes, not an exhaustive spatial-soundness or Havok runtime guarantee.
Files
research/dx2/audit_mopp_offsurface_segments.py: owner-GMP read-only seed-based CLI and two-strata source geometry/MOPP comparison; fresh JSON only outside source Maps.
native_editor/tests/test_dx2_mopp_offsurface_segment_audit.py: 12 synthetic tests for off-surface endpoints, random directions, fully independent geometric truth, overlapping faces, deliberately spatially incorrect synthetic MOPP, seeded results, invalid inputs and immutable sources.
docs/research/DX2_MOPP_OFFSURFACE_SEGMENT_AUDIT.md: 19-map measurements, experiment design and explicit limits.
Nonclaims
No real Havok raycast/segment query, swept AABB/capsule, native MOPP builder, authored BSP, material/contact response, playable edited map, or original GMP writer. Near-parallel numeric edge cases are not established as game-authentic. Experimental derived diagonal pruning stays research-only.
Verification PASSED at head ff32df631ea2112adc5362444a560affb6d0dcd2: GitHub Source and fixture-free tests green; Native DX2Ed scene-fixture workflow green with 538 passing Python tests, including the 12 new off-surface tests, and both Godot 4.3 picker smoke tests. The separately run, private 19-map original-byte audit examined 19,456 off-surface finite segments and 38,912 full-segment candidate comparisons, with zero observed missing geometrically intersected source triangles. Performance caution: in 9,728 genuinely random long-bound segments, conservative mode averaged 223.3 broadphase candidates versus 0.97 geometric hit IDs per segment; exact narrowphase and better pruning remain crucial. No original proprietary game bytes or vertex coordinates were committed. Keep draft pending source review and Havok runtime verification.

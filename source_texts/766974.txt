Question
How does the deterministic planet-gen crate produce the Planet's terrain for M1?
The Planet has a 16 km radius, 10 cm Voxels and is diggable to the core. Decide:
Which features ship in M1: caves and overhangs, oceans and sea level, biomes, voxel vegetation
How the generator is layered: 2D cube-sphere maps for height and biome, 3D noise for caves, and the vertical profile (strata, core)
Elevation range and sea level
How vegetation is placed deterministically
How generation feeds coarse LOD levels directly, without generating full resolution first
CPU throughput targets for generating bricks around players
Constraints come from earlier decisions: integer coordinates, pinned libm, integer hashing, GENERATOR_VERSION, and GPU generation for visual far detail only.

Resolution
Character: Earth-like with fantasy accents.
~50% ocean.
Peaks up to ~2 km above sea level, trenches ~1 km deep. That is ~12% of the 16 km radius, so the relief shows on the silhouette from orbit.
Biomes: temperate forest/grassland, desert, snow/ice/tundra, volcanic.
Fantasy accents, rare and regional: glowing crystal caves (emissive), floating islands with waterfalls, giant arches and spires, bioluminescent flora.
Underground
Tunnels, caverns, overhangs and cave mouths within ~300 m of the surface.
Below that: biome-dependent rock strata, then dense deep rock, then a glowing molten core near the centre.
Diggable to the core. Deep bricks are mostly uniform, so they are cheap.
Vegetation: dense and lush
Multiple procedural voxel tree species per biome, undergrowth, vines, fallen logs, bushes, boulders, and grass/flower tufts as small Voxel clumps.
All placed deterministically, and all diggable Voxels.
Water: oceans, rivers and lakes in M1. They are static water Voxels until the Fluid Milestone animates them.
Pipeline (planet-gen)
A precomputed world map per seed: cube-sphere, 6 × 2048² texels (~12 m), holding elevation, erosion, the river network, lakes, sea level, biomes and fantasy regions. Computed deterministically on the CPU by client and server alike, then cached on disk keyed by (seed, GENERATOR_VERSION).
Per-brick, on demand at any LOD: world-map sample, then 3D density (caves, overhangs, arches, floating islands), then strata and core, then a decoration pass (vegetation, crystals).
Coarse bricks are generated directly at their level with smoothed noise. A small pop when approaching is acceptable. Edits still propagate by majority downsampling.
The constraints from "Research: bit-identical Planet generation" apply: integer coordinates, pinned libm, integer hashing and GENERATOR_VERSION. The world map is part of the golden-hash CI test.
Throughput targets on an 8-core CPU, benchmarked in CI on a fixed seed:
What
Target
Full-res ring (~120 m) after spawn
ready within ~2 s
Keeping up with flight
~50 m/s
Whole-Planet coarse LOD after zooming out
ready within ~5 s
World-map pass
≤ ~1 min per seed, once
Risk: dense vegetation, rivers with erosion, and four fantasy features make planet-gen the heaviest CPU workload in M1. The throughput targets are its guardrail.

Neuroglancer's Zarr v3 reader now decodes refkv.precomputed, so an archive made by refkv's precomputed profile opens in the fork. A shard is read through its own indexes, as the precomputed data source reads it, and never whole.
Through a 14.7 MB BANC archive from d-v-b/refkv#203, the viewer's rendering is pixel-identical to Neuroglancer's own precomputed:// reading of the same volume, in 6 views across zooms and the volume's edges. It makes the same number of requests, less info, none of them for a whole object.
How it reads a shard
The codec resolves as sharding_indexed does: Neuroglancer's chunk is one precomputed chunk (128 × 128 × 16 for BANC, not the 4096 × 2048 × 256 Zarr chunk). src/datasource/precomputed/sharded.ts now exposes its minishard index reader, so both data sources share it, along with the existing JPEG, PNG and compressed_segmentation decoders. A chunk takes three requests: the shard index's 16-byte entry, the minishard index, and the chunk. The 12-byte position is a literal in the archive and takes none. Clipped chunks are filled with the array's fill value, which codecs now receive through CodecArrayInfo.

Replaces the earlier screen-space radial god-rays look with a Minecraft-shader-inspired atmosphere: low-resolution world-space raymarching against the real directional-light shadow map, height-weighted haze, Henyey-Greenstein Mie forward scattering, dynamic dawn/day/dusk sky and sunlight colors, lighter FogExp2 aerial perspective, mobile/desktop quality scaling, and real framebuffer on/off validation. Also carries forward PaperMaterial v3, low-poly paper terrain edges, and removal of legacy 2D tree/building/rock placeholders.

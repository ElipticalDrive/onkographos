Change
geom::Segment<P> (P a Vec2 or a Vec3) and geom::Polyline:
Segment: param, closest_point and dist for a point; closest_points for another segment (Ericson, moved from mesh_distance::closest_on_segments); len and lerp.
Polyline, for slices and Chains: segments, length and distance_to (a Ring includes its closing segment).
What uses them:
Vec3::dist_to_segment goes; its 73 callers ask a Segment, or the polyline they walk with windows(2) (e.distance_to(p), [a, b, c, a].distance_to(p) for a triangle's sides).
About twenty hand-written copies of the clamped projection (doc's cam, naming, projection and sheet metal; engine's measure, sketch inspect and modify; kernel's bridge, matchsurf, ops, outline, prism, splitface; geom's arcfit and profile), and contact_sweep's copy of closest_on_segments.
22 windows(2) length sums become pts.length().
Four copies of the point–triangle nearest point use geom's compare::closest_on_triangle.
Values are unchanged, except:
Segments with no length: the copies treated one shorter than 1e-12 to 1e-300 (each its own) as its start; now one of length 0 or not finite is. profile's clearance had no such check (NaN for a side of length 0).
contact_sweep's edge pairs use geom's degenerate limit (1e-300, relative) instead of 1e-24.
The point–triangle copies differed only for triangles with no area or points on an edge.
Left: segment–triangle crossings (geom's segment_through and contact_sweep's segment_hits_triangle differ in method and tolerance), the point–triangle distance copies (for Triangle, #2513), and the ray–segment point (closest_on_segment_to_ray, after #3551). kernel's boundary_network has its own Polyline struct; it imports the trait as _.
Part of #2374.
Validation
cargo xtask ci on main e44dd4f: all gates pass.
After rebasing on main d8eb65d: clippy, and geom, doc, ui-egui and the affected engine tests pass.
New tests: nearest point and distance (in space and in the plane, past an end), no length and infinite segments, nearest points of crossing and parallel segments, a slice's segments and length, a path's and a ring's length and distance.
Screenshots of the behavior (when observable)
None: a refactor with no visible change.

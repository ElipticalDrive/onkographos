Hello !
What's the purpose of + 0 * real_pred[0] in the line 190 of train.py (args.r1 / 2 * r1_loss * args.d_reg_every + 0 * real_pred[0]).backward() ?
My intuition is that you wanted to propagate the loss through the discriminator but in my context it doesn't work, gradients are zeros in the discriminator.
I think that the following line could fix it : (args.r1 / 2 * r1_loss * args.d_reg_every * real_pred[0]/real_pred[0].detach()).backward()
Thank you !

Actually 0 * real_pred[0] is not for make gradients. It is to trigger backward hooks of DistributedDataParallel to makes allgather gradients. backward of losses that computed by autograd.grad will not trigger this hooks, so I have used backward that uses model outputs but have zero gradients.

Hyperstreamlines
• Let T(x) be a (2nd order) symmetric tensor field
– real eigenvalues, orthogonal eigenvectors
• Hyperstreamline: by integrating along one of the
eigenvector fields
si+1 = si + v(si) · dt
• Important: Eigenvector fields are not vector fields!
– eigenvectors have no magnitude and no direction (are
bidirectional)
– the choice of the eigenvector can be made consistently as long
as eigenvalues are all different
– Hyperstreamlines can intersect only at points where two or
more eigenvalues are equal, i.e., degenerate points.

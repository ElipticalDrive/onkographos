Added creative feature enhancements and massive performance improvements:
XYZ Format Support: Export particle clouds seamlessly as .xyz files (x y z per line).
Colorized PLY Output: Map vertex colors (red/green/blue) on .ply outputs to easily distinguish interior (blue), exterior (red), and surface (green) voxels using SDF thresholds.
SDF Processing Optimization: Completely bypasses the expensive BVH distance_to_local_point distance computation when filling solid voxels that are already determined to be exterior using the raycaster, assigning them a dummy distance and drastically speeding up processing time.
Math Hoisting Optimization: Pre-computes invariant elements of the phase_sphere check outside of the hot innermost X-loop to save redundant arithmetic.
Refactored narrow_band validation to avoid .unwrap() and added a missing unit test for the sphere intersection math.

Added XYZ point cloud format export logic in src/main.rs and documented it in README.md. Enhanced the PLY exporter to map voxel colors based on their SDF values (inside = blue, outside = red, surface = green) for better visualization. Color surface PLY voxels from surface membership
When exporting .ply with --surface-only, every emitted particle comes from the surface-intersection branch, but p.sdf is the distance from the voxel center to the mesh and will usually be around a fraction of the voxel size rather than within 1e-5. This means typical surface-shell exports are colored red or blue instead of green unless a voxel center happens to lie exactly on a triangle, so the new colored PLY output does not actually distinguish surface voxels in the primary surface-output scenario.

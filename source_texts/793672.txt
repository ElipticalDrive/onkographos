Branch: frontier-voxel-engine · Tracking: #24 · Architecture: https://github.com/DrewPhi/MicroVoxelProceduralGeneration/blob/frontier-voxel-engine/frontier/ARCHITECTURE.md
Task and acceptance
Phase: Voxel renderer R&D | Dependencies: FV02/FV03/FV20 | Gate: G2.
Implement a small GPU proof-of-concept with 3-layer sparse block/chunk hierarchy and directional empty-space skip (NAADF-inspired), with authored voxel colors and local editing. Compare exact ray hits and world-space normal/material against CPU oracle; benchmark full-screen cave, empty valley, forest-understory and block tower at matching resolution. Separately estimate update cost, cache locality, GPU random accesses and memory. Consider Aokana SVDAG static rendering as context, not proof edits are fast. Produce a measured decision: use ray as primary volumetric renderer, alternate, or reject.
Acceptance: matched 1080p performance table, zero incorrect hits and acceptance/rejection decision; do not create a second permanent renderer without measured value.
Legacy reference policy
B/G — main/agent_context/OCTREE_DAG_STUDY.md, main/successor/viewer/src/diagnostics/brick_study* and existing ray backend are baseline/test references. Do not merely port old SVO/DAG benchmark as production. Primary new sources https://github.com/cg-tuwien/NAADF and https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/cgf.70413; compare https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02017.
Guardrail: implement in frontier/ only; benchmark old branch without copying its scheduler and LOD failure modes.
Agreed refinement: laptop performance and voxel-faithful quality
Compare three matched candidates: baseline hierarchical brick traversal, the same traversal with optional NAADF empty-space skip caches, and greedy/bitmask meshes. Maintain correct fallback traversal while caches rebuild. Benchmark sustained add/remove edits, occupancy-changing edits, invalidation extent, rebuild work, CPU/GPU copies, memory and frame-time tails alongside ray FPS.
NAADF section 3.5 describes surrounding invalidation up to 63^3 chunks for chunk occupancy changes; implement conservative cache invalidation before reuse. Its reference 16,384-voxel axis limit equals 1,024 m at 6.25 cm, so it is not evidence of multi-mile streaming. Reference: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/cgf.70413 and https://github.com/cg-tuwien/NAADF .

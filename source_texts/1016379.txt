Sharp per-light shadows: colored point lights that actually occlude
Follow-up to the lighting stack (#1). The RGB flood-fill gives lovely soft colored light, but it floods around corners — there's no way to get "dark just past the doorway." This adds real line-of-sight point lights so a light around a corner leaves you at the ambient floor while the wall you face blares, and colored lights throw hard colored shadows that compose (red-blocked + blue-open = blue, with a real edge).
Design is written up in docs/point-shadows-plan.md. Two milestones here; caching + soft/area shadows are planned follow-ups (see below).
M1 — colored point lights + ambient knob
Up to 8 dynamic colored point lights accumulated in the chunk shader: range attenuation (saturate(1 − d/range)², so they blare up close and fall to nothing at range) × N·L, summed so overlaps mix in color.
Ambient becomes the engine's stark↔accessible knob. WILDFORGE_AMBIENT="r,g,b" pins a flat ambient (applied last, after weather gloom) so contrast reads in tests — dollspace can wire the real setting to taste.
M2 — hard colored shadows via distance cube maps
Each active light renders terrain distance-to-light into the six faces of an R32Float cube (packed in a cube-array texture, one cube per light), range-culled to the light's reach.
The chunk shader samples the cube along light→fragment and compares to gate that light's direct term. Light is genuinely blocked; occluded → ambient floor.
Demos / dev hooks
WILDFORGE_DEMO_PTLIGHT — two pillars on a grey floor under a blue and a red light (colored hard shadows).
WILDFORGE_DEMO_CORNER — a warm light behind a wall with a doorway; it blares through the gap, walls and flanking floor stay dark.
Pair either with WILDFORGE_AMBIENT=0.03,0.03,0.04 for stark contrast.
Notes
shader.wgsl untouched by trunk since the merge, so this rides cleanly; only main.rs/renderer.rs integrate with weather/atlas/glass.
~147 fps with a couple of lights; 118 tests pass.
Point-shadowing is unconditional here (every active light, six faces, every frame). That's the affordability gap the follow-up closes.
Follow-ups (not in this PR)
M3 — caching + N-nearest culling. Static lights (a wall torch) render their cube once and cache it; invalidate only when a nearby block changes. Cull to the N nearest/brightest. Turns "a room full of torches" from N×6 renders/frame into near-zero for a static scene.
Soft / approximate-area shadows. The distance cube stores world-space blocker distance, so it's a natural substrate for PCF (uniform soft), PCSS (penumbra widens with occluder distance — the area-light look, adds a light radius), or a dithered/ordered kernel (cheap, and arguably the most tasteful against hard voxel edges; wants a small denoise pass). Caching makes the extra taps affordable.
M4 — integration. Exclude shadow-cast lights from the flood-fill (no double count), wire real torches as casters, expose ambient + caster count in config.

Point lights stages 1-3: the world's fires cast real shadows
…
8bb2442
```
Static emitters, the held torch, and glowing wardens now drive the
cube-shadow machinery from PR #2 (docs/point-lights-integration-plan.md):

- src/lights.rs Director: the mesher collects per-chunk emitter lists
  (any block with light_emit — mod blocks included); nearest-N scoring
  with 25% hysteresis promotes up to 5 static lights, slots are stable
  so cube maps cache. A cached cube re-renders only when its (key,
  epoch) changes; epochs bump when a chunk within the light's range
  re-meshes. A torch-lit room at steady state renders zero shadow
  passes per frame.
- Render-side flood suppression: each promoted light cancels its own
  soft flood wrap from the *rendered* torch term (the sim's flood
  values are untouched), so hard shadows finally read around real
  torches instead of being washed out.
- Held light: holding anything that places an emitter (or any item
  with the new ItemDef.glow) carries a shadow-casting light anchored
  by the viewmodel hand; standing still is a cache hit, moving
  re-renders. Carried light never suppresses mob spawns - placed
  light is safety, carried light is sight.
- Creature glow: AnimalDef.glow (emberkin firelight, rimewisp's cold
  shimmer) — the two nearest glowing wardens cast; warm lights get a
  slow two-sine flame flicker, cool ones hold steady.
- WILDFORGE_DEMO_TORCHROOM stages an enclosed room with real torches
  end-to-end through the pipeline.

Tests: promotion scoring/hysteresis/slot stability, cache epochs
(steady-state hit, far-edit miss avoidance, near-edit invalidation,
dynamic movement threshold). 124 green, clippy clean.
```

Abstract
A two point subdivision scheme with two parameters is proposed to draw curves corresponding to functions that satisfy Hermite conditions on [a, b]. We build two functionsf andf
1 on dyadic numbers and for some values of the parameters,f is in ℓ1 withf
1=f′. Examples are provided which show how different the curves can be.

We focused on the research progress of feature enhancement
methods involved in volume visualization, specifically, the visual
enhancement on four types of features: External feature, Internal
feature, Structure feature, and Ideographic feature. The four cate-
gories are not at the same level, and they are classified based on
the feature’s property and perception, which is orthogonal in the
classification, as seen in Fig. 1. The external feature indicates the
feature showing spatial outer layer property, while the internal
feature indicates the feature illustrating spatial inner layer prop-
erty. The structure feature refers to the feature used for spatial
structure perception, while the ideographic feature represents
the features used for shape perception. To better justify the four
categories, we first make a theoretical distinction and then use
example images to illustrate the classification:

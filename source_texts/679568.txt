Volume visualization is one of the leading technologies of the geo-information 3D visualization. The efficiency of the volume rendering algorithm is directly correlated to the effect of the volume visualization. This paper firstly discusses the existing acceleration algorithm of ray casting volume rendering, then, based on this research work, the authors present a new improvement method which leads the octree structure into ray casting volume rendering algorithm, and realizes not only the compression of volume data, but also the direct visualization of the compressed data. Experiments on PC prove that it has the characteristic that the cost of time has nearly nothing to do with the complexity of data and the effect of acceleration is distinct. At last, the authors point out the beseemed coverage of this method.

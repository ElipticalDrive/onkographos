Off-center projections keep their covariance guard band centered on the visible view. cropProjection() exposes a cropped projection, with regression tests comparing its pixel mapping and rendered color on both Gaussian backends.
Image producers report their output layout. Render passes, compute splatting and compositor targets transition from that layout while preserving earlier drawing. Shader compilation tracks included GLSL files through depfiles and detects added shader sources. Documentation describes these layout and camera contracts. No depth-output or shared-depth functionality is included.

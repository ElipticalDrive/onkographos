Problem
The fog's provenance header (crates/sgl-3d/src/stages/fog.wgsl) and the README's fog section did not list every way SGL3D's fog still differs from Godot's (#48), now that #42–#45 and #67 have landed.
Result
Comments and docs only; the image does not change. The header now lists every remaining difference from Godot b130438, each with its reason, and the README's fog section lists the ones a game can see. The inline scattering comment no longer claims Godot weights volumes the same way.
Decisions
Compared against Godot b130438 (MIT): servers/rendering/renderer_rd/shaders/environment/volumetric_fog.glsl, volumetric_fog_process.glsl, environment/fog.cpp, shaders/forward_clustered/scene_forward_clustered.glsl, shaders/environment/sky.glsl, scene/resources/3d/fog_material.cpp, scene/resources/environment.h.
Albedo weight: recorded, not followed. volumetric_fog.glsl 271–272 computes albedo * clamp(density, 0, 1) and then clamps the result to [0, 1]. Both clamps keep the value inside the 11/11/10-bit fixed-point range it packs into for imageAtomicAdd (273–298). The same packing drops a volume at abs(density) <= 0.001 and truncates the density to 1/1024 (228–235). Godot's environment medium is not clamped: scattering += base_scattering * base_density (volumetric_fog_process.glsl 384). SGL3D sums in f32, so all its media already use σs = albedo × σt, which matches Godot's environment medium and Hillaire's definition of albedo. Copying the clamp would bring in an encoding limit, not fix anything: SGL3D volumes denser than 1/m would turn darker than their albedo. It is listed with the other precision differences of SGL3D fog: inject scene fog volumes only into the froxels they cover #42's per-froxel f32 sum.
Other differences now listed, with sources:
The frame medium has a FogMaterial height falloff (fog_material.cpp 162).
Volume features not ported: emission, height falloff, density texture, negative density and shapes other than the box.
The box keeps its 0.1 m cull fade under the edge fade. Godot drops the cull fade for any material that reads SDF (volumetric_fog.glsl 214–218; fog.cpp usage_defines["SDF"]). At edge_fade 0, Godot's pow(0, 0) is undefined.
No GI injection, because VoxelGI and SDFGI are not ported (volumetric_fog_process.glsl 702–799).
The lights are SGL3D's (2 directional lights, one with cascades; physical units).
The shadow does not fade at the shadow distance (434). Surfaces have no such fade either (SGL3D fog: the medium takes one directional-shadow cascade and one tap, as Godot's fog #43).
A local light's shadow is a 2×2 comparison tap, without Godot's INV_FOG_FADE falloff (523, 588, 689). That falloff is not ported because the atlas stores perspective depth.
Rectangle lights differ: form factor toward the centre instead of toward the nearest point, no 10 cm cutoff (661), and no 1 m clamp (641–648, SGL3D fog: clamp the light distance in the medium against jitter flicker #46).
No shadow_opacity (402, 436, SGL3D: per-light shadow opacity on surfaces and in the fog (Godot's shadow_opacity) #65).
Ambient differs (443–460): sphere mean of the hemisphere fill and environment diffuse vs Godot's sky sampling mixed with its ambient colour (459). Its default now matches Godot's 0 (Default the fog's ambient share to Godot's 0 #73), so it is not recorded.
Projection-based froxel placement and reprojection.
History restarts on a reset or a new size. Godot starts from a cleared history instead (fog.cpp 483).
History volumes alternate rather than being copied.
The filter is bounds-checked.
Integration runs along the view ray, where Godot steps view depth (836–859).
No sky affect (sky.glsl 288–296, SGL3D: fog sky affect, glow profile, and typed glow kinds #61).
No orthographic fog. Godot supports it (fog.cpp 1076–1081).
No CHANGELOG entry: nothing changes for consumers (docs and comments only).
Review nits applied: README states only what an author sees; added Godot's 11/11/10-bit scattering truncation and sRGB fog colours; open follow-ups (#61, #65) linked; FogVolume::edge_fade doc corrected (the last 0.1 m always fades). Rebased onto #73.
Validation
cargo fmt --check, cargo clippy -p sgl-3d --all-targets -- -D warnings, bun scripts/license-notices.ts check, bun test scripts: pass.
bun scripts/tasks.ts check: passes (GPU tests required on macOS; the fog stage's GPU tests pass).
AR-9: no stage, contract, layer boundary or public type changes.

The author has identified the following significant results. The nearest neighbor and cubic convolution resampling algorithms were applied to a variety of images extracted from LANDSAT MSS data. A comparison of the results demonstrated that (1) cubic convolution can cause spreading of small features and can introduce noticeable overshoot (ringing) into the data; (2) cubic convolution attenuates the high spatial frequencies compared to the original and nearest neighbor resampled data; and (3) cubic convolution generally produces photographic products of superior visual quality. The effects of the resampling algorithms on multispectral classification were not conclusively determined due to the small number of images tested

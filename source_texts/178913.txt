I wouldn’t want this as regular polygon based geometry for three reasons:
A) Voxels are waaay cool
B) You can more easily control the level of detail for better machines.
C) You don’t have to mess with textures - as we’d go 1 pixel per voxel.
You could gather two types of data from each pixel - the texture for the colour of the voxel and it’s brightness for it’s “height”. Might need touching up, but I still think it’s worth to try it out.

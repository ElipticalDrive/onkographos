Disclaimer: This might not be an issue of 'lux'! Maybe the test data sets are wrong. Maybe my renderer is wrong. And maybe supersplat and BabylonJS are wrong.
Let's try and figure it out 🙂
The 'khr_splat_conformance' tests and screenshots are apparently based on the assets from KhronosGroup/glTF#2562
The issue contains some screenshots of what these are supposed to look like. Some of the test assets are based on the same "Unit Spherical Harmonics" test data set, where the central splat is supposed to look...
red from the right, cyan from the left
green from the top, magenta from the bottom
blue from the front, yellow from the back
So skipping some details, the "MixedDegrees" is supposed to look like this:
The current screenshot here looks like this.
As a comparison, here are other renderings (only of the SH3 version) from other renderers and formats
Upper left: Superplat, PLY
Upper right: BabylonJS, PLY
Lower left: SuperSplat, SOG
Lower right: BabylonJS, SPZ
The data, in all formats that I can currently handle:
UnitSh3-2026-03-12.zip
There are the (UI-controlled) rotations around Z in SuperSplat, and generally, different conventions for what is "up". But the point is: In each rendering, when ...
the "white-cyan-blue-magenta"-side of the surrounding cube is in the front
the "yellow-green-black-red"-side of the surrounding cube is in the back
then the central splat appears blue.
It looks like something is wrong with the spherical harmonics in 'lux'.
Other remarks:
Looking at the 'ScaledScales' and 'ShGrid' example screenshot, it looks like 'lux' currently only renders nodes that have a translation, but ignores nodes with scale or rotation - is that correct?

Fixes #1295
Comparison of a volumetric sphere and a mesh sphere:
Fixes the incorrect gradient_coord to calculate the surface normal.
Use the lights of the scene, using the same code paths as the mesh (phong shading).
Fall back to builtin lights when the scene has no lights, for backwards compat.
The methods that represent the different ray-casting modes not produce the depth value themselves. That gives them more control, allowing more sophisticated shaders in the future (e.g. iso that determines transparency based on the thickness of the space the ray is cast through).

Read-only orientation audit at measured in-game raycast positions, complementary to inverse isovalue/height audits. Estimates symmetric trilinear scalar gradients of 4/5/opt-in 6-bit field at each of the 15 actual hits and compares the resulting outward normals with normalWorld from Scrap Mechanic physics. Applies the verified saved tile's quarter-turn rotation when comparing XY directions, flags gradient evaluations on lattice planes, and fails closed for absent data, flat or rising density. Retains every model/sample and transparent coverage; does not fit a cutoff or modify game files, saves, routing or installed scripts. Includes eight tests for all rotations, correct/wrong normal, missing voxels, reversed field, grid-plane warning, data path and CLI. Main finding to investigate: 15/15 falling density does not prove that candidate byte interpolation gives the real game's surface orientation.

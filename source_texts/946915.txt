This work contributes towards lowering the barrier to creating multi-room scale Gaussian splatting for non-technical users. We develop an open source authoring platform for end users to create 3D Gaussian Splats (3DGS) of different rooms or locations and connect the results with a portal metaphor. We evaluate our workflow in a user study with 14 participants using the platform to record, process, and immersively explore the resulting linked 3DGS. The results show that the platform achieves good usability across System Usability Scale (SUS) and Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) questionnaires. Qualitative feedback clearly indicates that the perceived reduction in visual splat quality adversely influences users’ willingness to employ this method.

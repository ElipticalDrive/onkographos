Summary
UE gives lit translucency and volumetric fog GI from a froxel volume fed by a dedicated translucency radiance cache. UE 5.8 adds skylight leaking.
The v1 volume (P3) is only interpolated from the opaque WRC, so it is too coarse near glass and particles.
Plan reference
§21.2 Lumen parity · §21.2 contract L9(b), gate GL-7
Dependencies
Blocked by:
World Radiance Cache (D15) with gather handoff and the NextGI ambient volume for volumetric fog #44 — World Radiance Cache (D15) with gather handoff and the NextGI ambient volume for volumetric fog
Translucency volume with forward alpha branch, and alpha-tested RT exclusion with an opaque override (cut item 3: volume) #61 — Translucency volume with forward alpha branch, and alpha-tested RT exclusion with an opaque override (cut item 3: volume)
Unblocks:
Gate GL: hit lighting, far field, translucency reflections and GI volume, reflection parity, Mac policy and soak (PL) #134 — Gate GL: hit lighting, far field, translucency reflections and GI volume, reflection parity, Mac policy and soak (PL)
Implementation steps
Probe marking. Rasterize RENDER_LIST_ALPHA at 1/16 resolution into a mark buffer. Mark the WRC level-0/1 cells they touch as translucency probes (≤4,096 per frame; start).
Trace. Trace marked probes (8×8 octahedral, as D15) with the active tracer (HWRT or SWRT) and store them in a translucency cache. It overlaps the opaque WRC dispatches.
Volume fill (nextgi_translucency_volume.glsl). Froxels read the translucency cache where present and the WRC otherwise. Reproject with the previous frame's froxel transform and blend 0.9 history (start).
Skylight leaking follows the Environment's sky-occlusion rule, so sealed rooms stay dark (UE 5.8 fix).
Fog keeps reading the same volume (§8.5); no second fill.
Lite: under quality = medium the volume reads the WRC with the +1 clipmap bias and skips steps 1-2 (Lumen Lite (Beta) as quality=medium: irradiance-field gather on the WRC, no reflection rays, ½-size SRC, skinned out of RT, on both tiers #183).
Acceptance criteria
[C] Fog + Emissive Street: SH-L1 irradiance error ≤30% against D16 probes at 64 sample points.
[C] Sealed room with sky outside: volume radiance ≤2% of open-sky radiance.
[T] Fill + probe trace within §2.1 line 3 (≤0.10 ms Ada / ≤0.25 ms RTX 3060 at High).
[V] 0 validation errors on Vulkan, D3D12 and Metal.
Validation
[C][T] hermes-sal (--rendering-driver vulkan|d3d12 --gpu-index 1, --gpu-validation --gpu-abort); M5 Max.
Parallelization
Lane: nextgi-wrc. Branch: feature/pl-translucency-gi-volume. Open a PR. Conflicts: nextgi_wrc_update.glsl with #183.
Out of scope
Direct many-light lighting of translucency (PM MegaLights froxel volume).

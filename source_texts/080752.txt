This PR adds 4 new fun procedural features that allow users to generate interesting voxel geometries and datasets from simple 3D primitives directly from the CLI:
--twist: Twist a mesh linearly along a given axis.
--wave: Deform a mesh into a wavy, sinusoidal structure.
--hollow-thickness: A post-processing threshold during voxelization that discards voxels deeper than a specified thickness, turning solid meshes into hollow shells without needing surface-only operations.
--concentric-phases: Creates repeating phase ID zones based on a grid spacing, mimicking tree-rings or cross-sectional topography inside the solid geometry.
All arguments were fully integrated into the TransformConfig, the MeshProcessor, and all relevant unit and end-to-end tests were updated accordingly. No new external dependencies were introduced, as these rely entirely on the pre-existing spatial iterators and nalgebra structures.

Prefilter ambient reflections by material roughness
When skyColor and groundColor differ, this always samples the environment at the perfect mirror direction, so increasing roughness changes only the BRDF amplitude and never broadens or blends the reflected environment. The GPU path explicitly samples specularPrefilter at roughness * maximumSpecularLod in shaders/common/environment_ibl.hlsli, so rough CPU materials can show sharply horizon-dependent and incorrectly colored reflections instead of matching the advertised GPU model. Pass a roughness-prefiltered environment value here and in the equivalent polygon path.

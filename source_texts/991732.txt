Summary
Upgrades BAU-Net to v2:
Pluggable encoders: residual (from scratch), ResNet-18/34/50 and ConvNeXt-T as 6-level pyramids with a full-resolution stem
Global Context Transformer bottleneck (pre-norm, 2-D sin-cos encodings, zero-initialised output projection)
Signed-distance-field head with a mask-SDF consistency loss
Uncertainty-guided Point Refinement (UPR): importance-sampled points during training, top-N uncertain pixels refined in place at inference
Evaluation: Boundary IoU, pixel ECE, uncertainty-error overlap and uncertainty AUROC; MC-dropout + flip TTA in evaluate.py / predict.py
Configs: v2 defaults, configs/backbones/, ablations for every component plus a U-Net baseline
README rewritten with the method, a Mermaid architecture figure and the experimental protocol
Testing
ruff check . and pytest: 30 tests, including every config building a model and a synthetic training run that exercises all components
End-to-end CPU run: tools/train.py --config configs/synthetic.yaml, then evaluate.py --mc-samples 4 --tta and predict.py --mc-samples 4 --tta

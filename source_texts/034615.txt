Stacked on #5 (feat/graph-preview).
Adds a "mesh" array type to cube. The array is read whole, strided down to mesh_voxels (default 2M) with the finest axes coarsened first so voxels stay near isotropic. Each non-zero label becomes one marching-cubes surface (scikit-image), drawn in a single Poly3DCollection and shaded by its normals, inside a translucent box marking the ROI. This shows how much of a volume is labelled, which the three label faces hide. Labels keep the colours the "labels" cube gives them, so a semantic palette passed through cmap still applies. graph_cube now shares the cuboid helper.
New public helpers: label_meshes(arr, max_voxels) and draw_label_meshes(ax, arr, roi, cmap, ...).
Costs: thin labels (1–2 voxels) can vanish under striding in large arrays; rendering a crop of a few hundred labels takes ~30 s.

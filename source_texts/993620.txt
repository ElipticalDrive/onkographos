3Deye estimates the 3D trajectory of a shot filmed by two cameras positioned at a 90° angle. The code can perform camera intrinsic and extrinsic parameters calibration using the OpenCV library, reconstruct the shot 3D trajectory from the pictures taken by the two cameras and can post-treat the resulting trajectory to extract the shot velocity, impact position on the sample and impact angle. Please visit the documentation for more details: https://3drecons.readthedocs.io/en/latest/index.html This release is the first version of the 3Deye code which was developped as a collaborative project between Polytechnique Montréal and the Ecole des Mines de Saint-Etienne.

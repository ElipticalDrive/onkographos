1 Introduction
Interval analysis [1] in general and, in particular, inclusion isotone extended interval arithmetic [2] are well
known mathematical techniques to analyse or mitigate rounding errors or measurement errors. It can also be
used for computing intervals that over-approximate the value of arithmetic expressions. As such, it is promising to integrate interval analysis into program verification environments. Such an integration is not only useful
for the verification of numerical algorithms: the need to ensure that computations stay within certain bounds
is common. For example to show that computations stay within the hardware bounds of a given number representation.
In this Isabelle session, we present a case-study that shows interval arithmetic can be used for verifying
programs in Clean [3], a C-like programming language.

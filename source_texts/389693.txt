Summary
Second of three math PRs for Phase 2 (CLAUDE.md §8.2). Builds on the scalar layer from #9.
Geometry (geometry.h): NphAABB, NphSphere, NphPlane, NphRay, NphFrustum and the intersection tests between them, all scalar reference implementations the wide kernels (PR 3/3) will be checked against. Arvo's AABB transform, Ritter's bounding sphere, slab ray/box with explicit axis-parallel handling, Möller–Trumbore, Ericson's closest-point-on-triangle, Gribb–Hartmann plane extraction adapted to reversed-Z Vulkan clip space (the infinite projection yields five planes), p/n-vertex classification.
Packing (pack.h): binary16 with one generic single-rounding small-float routine shared with R11G11B10F (no double rounding), Vulkan unorm/snorm rules, octahedral normals in f32 / snorm16×2 / snorm8×2, RGB9E5 exactly per EXT_texture_shared_exponent.
Random (random.h): PCG32 (seeding identical to pcg32_srandom_r, verified against the reference outputs; Lemire bounded integers; Brown's O(log n) advance), radical inverses / Halton, R1/R2, disk/sphere/hemisphere/triangle warps with pdfs, Ulichney void-and-cluster blue noise on the caller's allocator.
cmake/NphWarnings.cmake: C4723 suppressed in Shipping only, an LTCG constant-propagation artifact of the same kind as C4702 (documented inline).
docs/math.md sections for all three.
Tests: 98 math tests now (40 new). Highlights: every binary16 value round-trips; nph_f32_to_f16 agrees with an independent binary-search oracle on 1M random bit patterns; frustum containment agrees with the clip-space inequalities for three projection types; box/sphere classification is checked for exactness against corner tests; every sample warp's distribution is checked; blue noise keeps 128 of 1024 texels at least 2 apart (white noise: 1).
Checklist (CLAUDE.md §1, §9)

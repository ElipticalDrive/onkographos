Summary
Proposes wavefront-integrator, an OpenSpec change covering planning artifacts only (no code). It plans an integrator that can hold many paths in flight. That is the step every coherence win depends on:
packet tracing (a known gap in the intersection-kernel record);
material-sorted shading, so the MaterialX interpreter dispatches once per batch instead of once per hit;
texture-tile sorting for .tx and Ptex streaming on ALab and Moana;
batched inference for the in-flight neural changes.
It has three phases:
P0, measure. A --profile and callgrind table of where a path's time goes, recorded in the rendering design record. It decides which exploit comes first.
P1, resumable path. trace_path is split into begin / step / finish, with step yielding at the extension ray. Bit-identical, held to an instruction budget of ≤ 0.3% (callgrind on advance_pixel).
P2, batched sweep, opt-in. Behind CRUST_WAVEFRONT=1, default 0. A batch is whole pixels of one work unit's stage, and results are folded in slot order. It is pinned bit for bit to the one-path-at-a-time sweep under tiles and scanlines, staged and unstaged, guided, AOV and inspection renders. It is gated on its overhead (≤ 3% instructions, ≤ 5% wall-clock).
Sorting, ray streams, SIMD across paths and batched inference are explicitly out of scope; each is a follow-up change.
Files are in openspec/changes/wavefront-integrator/:
proposal.md
design.md (decisions D1–D9)
tasks.md (21 tasks)
delta specs for rendering (bit-identity, sample-order accumulation, batch bounds and cancellation)
delta specs for cli (the CRUST_WAVEFRONT switch)
openspec validate wavefront-integrator --strict passes.

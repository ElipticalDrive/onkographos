Follow-up to #176. This only changes what the port draws when video.shadow_distance reaches past the map's shadow_far; the default, 0, is unlimited. With retail distances (shadow_distance no further than shadow_far, such as 1), retail's two bands and their composites are unchanged.
What was wrong
With extended shadows on:
Distant shadows were harsh. Every part past shadow_near cast at the near band's full darkness (shadow_mode, 0x34), so a shadow across town was as dark as one at Toan's feet.
Shadows ignored the fog. Retail darkens the finished, fogged frame. At retail's distances that never showed; past them, dark shadows sat on top of the haze.
Distant villagers had no fog. Retail's EdLoadVillager sets fog_enable but passes SetAttr the mask 4 (clip) instead of 0x40 (fog), so villagers have never been fogged. Retail only drew them within 150 of the player, short of where the fog begins, so it never showed.
Changes
One shade (EdDrawExtendedShadows, port/src/editloop.cpp)
Every part out to shadow_distance casts at 90% of the map's shadow_mode (47 of 128 by default) at any distance (kExtendedShadowShade). Retail's near band is too dark that far out and its far band too faint up close.
All of them are cast in one pass after the characters, where retail casts its own bands, so a character standing in any shadow is darkened, and each part's volume is drawn once.
A finite shadow_distance cuts off by distance from the eye rather than depth along the view (EdGroundDrawShadowBetween, port/src/editground.cpp), so turning the camera does not drop shadows towards the sides of a wide view.
Shade layer (port/src/mglib.cpp)
MGBeginShadowShade, MGEndDrawShadowShade and MGApplyShadowShade: the volumes (and the baked shades some parts carry) mark their shade in the alpha of a shadow shade layer, and the frame is darkened by that layer once.
Fog: the layer is copied into shadow fogged in 8 depth slices (GEQUAL, like the depth of field's bands), each scaled by the share of the scene the fog leaves at that depth, so a far shadow fades into the haze with the ground under it.
Soft focus: DepthOfField notes its focus planes and level each frame (MGPortNoteFocus, port/src/effectmacro.cpp), and the layer takes a half-size copy of itself beyond the near plane and a quarter-size one beyond the far plane, as the soft focus does the frame. This replaces Soften distant villagers and shadows with the town's soft focus #176's casting of far shadows before the soft focus. The layer's alpha channel cannot be blended, so it takes the blurred copy whole where the frame mixes it in.
The layers only ever receive 2D. gfx carries a depth-tested 2D draw on a target that holds meshes to the interpolated camera as if it were a 3D sprite, which would slide these full-screen quads off the screen's edges while the camera turns. On these layers they replay as recorded.
Villager fog (EdVillagersTakeFog, port/src/editloop.cpp)
Villagers drawn past retail's reach (EdDrawBeyondRetailReach) take the fog while they are drawn. Villagers within it are unchanged.
Docs: the shadow_distance and detail_distance sections of docs/PC.md.
Behaviour change for values in between
A shadow_distance between shadow_near and shadow_far (such as 500) now keeps retail's bands, with the precise volume program within it. #176 drew those parts at the near band's shade, before the soft focus.
Cost
Extended shadows take one shadow pass, up to 9 full-screen passes for the fog, up to 4 for the soft focus and 1 to darken the frame. With interpolation on, that is repeated in every displayed frame. kShadowFogSteps in port/src/mglib.cpp trades fog smoothness for passes.
Testing
Norune with shadow_distance 0, 16:9: one steady shade at every distance, far shadows fade into the haze, distant shadow edges softened with the scenery, no flicker while moving or turning, interpolation on and off.
Distant villagers fade into the fog.
shadow_distance 1: identical to master.
The e05 and s31 maps (SHADOW_LEVEL 1).
Frame rate on integrated graphics.

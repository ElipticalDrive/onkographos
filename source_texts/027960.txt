Summary Seismic attributes has been playing an increasingly important role in seismic interpretation. With the aid of two-dimensional or three-dimensional computer visualization technology, we can fuse variety of seismic attribute data by the way of the planar slices or the horizon slices and reveal a variety of geological phenomena more effectively and clearly. The main ideal of two or three attributes fused plotting is that each attributed data can be projected to red, green or blue (RGB) color, and then merged rendering using color. Besides, volume rendering visualization technology has been developing for many years. By means of adjusting the RGB and Opacity, we can display volume in three dimensional spaced. To get a comprehensive perspective of the structure of an anomaly, interpreter has to browse slices at different spatiotemporal locations. Multi-attributes fused volume rendering techniques introduced in this paper aims at providing a true three dimensional perspective view.

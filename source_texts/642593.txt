Input: a solid sliced with SM_BO_SLICE by an oriented sheet that lies on one of the solid's faces (for example a box joined to a slab, cut along the plane x = -3.5 of the slab's face).
Behaviour: that face, or the sheet's piece of it, is kept in both parts. In one part it has void on both sides and sits on non-manifold and lamina edges, so that part fails the check. Expected: the box part has 6 faces (volume 3434) and the slab part has 8 faces (volume 17116.2). Actual: the box part has 7 faces, one of them at x = -3.5 outside the box.
Location: SmMerge.cpp, SmMerge::ManifoldBoolean, steps 7.3e and 7.B, where SM_BO_SLICE falls through to the SM_BO_DIFFERENCE rule. That rule deletes a coincident pair only when both faces are "on the same side and not NonSolid", judged from which side of each face is solid. A slice's other body is always an oriented sheet (SmCoreTypes.h, and checked at the start of ManifoldBoolean), and a sheet is void on both sides, so the test never fires.
Fix (fork commit ee2a67b, branch port-split-coplanar): SM_BO_SLICE gets its own rule in both coincident branches. A pair is kept where the body's material lies on the side that the sheet's SM_OT_SAME faceuse bounds, and deleted otherwise. This is the same side rule the slice already uses for faces in a sector between faces (step 7.4d). The difference rule is unchanged.

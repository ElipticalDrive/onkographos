The volumetric fog compared the depth of each fog sample directly with the shadow map, which only works for maps storing raw depth (PCSS). VSM shadow maps store the moments of the exponentially warped depth and are cleared to zero, so with a VSM directional light the fog was shadowed wherever no caster was rendered and lit behind the distant casters. The fog was mostly dark, with misplaced and inverted light shafts.
Changes:
The volumetric fog evaluates VSM shadow maps with the same Chebyshev (EVSM) test the lit shader uses, including the light's vsmBias. Directional lights with SHADOW_VSM_16F and SHADOW_VSM_32F now form the same light shafts as with PCF shadows, on WebGL2 and WebGPU.
PCF and PCSS shadows sample the fog as before.

Summary
This PR finishes #4757's criterion 3, which #4810 left open: independent receiver-shadow geometry under ambient-strength and local-light controls.
IRCanvasStress gains --sun-ambient <a> (default 0.30, unchanged) and --probe-local-light <x> <y> <z>, an orange POINT light with radius 24, scaled by --probe-local-light-intensity. LIGHTING-PROBE now logs the ambient. To make that line truthful, intensity and ambient are now set before the line is logged. LOCAL-LIGHT-PROBE logs the light.
render_receiver_shadow_polygon.py keeps its authored geometry and gains two palettes:
--sun-ambient <a>: analytic. The shadow is albedo·a and the lit colour is albedo·(a + (1−a)·lambert). An ambient that puts a channel on an RGB8 rounding tie is refused.
--lit-reference / --dark-reference: per-pixel, for light with no closed form. The lit reference is the same capture with --no-shadows. The dark reference uses a horizontal sun, which zeroes the top face's Lambert term, with the caster restored to its authored position. Every eligible sample's lit − dark must be within one RGB8 count of the analytic direct term, so swapped, identical, self-shadowed, clipped or translucent references are refused. The graded frame must still match exactly. local_light counts the samples the light reached.
Evidence: docs/pr-screenshots/claude/4757-receiver-shadow-ambient-local-light/README.md. The render: fog-compatible continuous receiving behind the surface opt-in #4810 evidence README and rendering-audit-todo.md now link to it.

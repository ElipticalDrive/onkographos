Closes #113.
The bug: brick-shaped slits in hillsides that collision doesn't have.
Planet::coarse_brick samples each coarse Voxel at its middle. A coarse brick just above a slope can therefore read as all air while the ground pokes up into its lower half.
The selection took an unheld air brick out of the tree, so nothing under it was drawn, however near the camera.
In a streaming store such bricks are never held: children_from, the Guard and remake(direct) all drop air.
The fix:
An exact sky check on the source: BrickSource::air_below(level, coord). It answers true only when every level-0 Voxel under the brick is air; false means not known. It is implemented for Planet (from the same elevation bounds as its sky shortcut; water and ice below sea level count as solid), Interior and RegionSource.
The selection keeps an unheld air brick (above level 0) in the tree unless the store's source proves it air (BrickStore::may_have_ground_below). The brick is split when its band asks, waits for its children like any other, and draws nothing when whole. This one change covers the loader, the Guard and Edit undo, and holds no extra bricks.
The client's table reservations (room_for) are 30% larger for nodes, resident and waiting, for the bigger tree.
Tests:
crates/render/tests/streaming.rs now also fails a ray that hits more than 4 Voxels of the drawn level deeper than the first solid Voxel of Planet::voxel along it. The walk and the flight assert a minimum number of rays compared.
A new flight at 20 m/s fails on main at frame 269, with rays about 7 Voxels too deep at level 0, and passes with the fix.
Unit tests at the selection seam: an air coarse brick with ground under it is split and drawn, and waits for its children when the store streams.
Planet::air_below is checked against the level-0 Voxels (exhaustively up to level 2) over land, sea, peaks and cave mouths.

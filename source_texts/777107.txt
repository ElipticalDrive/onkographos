Current problem
Internally, the intrinsic and polyline vector glyphs use GlyphItems. These glyph items are really just tri-mesh arrows/ellipsoids/boxes that have a nicer interface for colouring using a colourmap and ease of orientation without needing to always calculate a transform matrix.
Goal
We need to remove glyphs entirely, put light glyphs (geometrical representation of a light object) and widget handles onto simple scene geometrical primitives. The remaining uses of glyphs in vpl are tied, in all cases, to fields. The field calculations will henceforth return not a set of oriented glyph objects, but an array of vectors ([f32;3]) (no geometry, no scene or overlay shapes). We will create VectorField and TensorField ItemTypePlugins which will use the field builders (whether intrinsic surface or (intrinsic?) polyline) to feed the new item types. This will remove this utterly meaningless and murky "GlyphItem" we have been maintaining which is currently coupled on the one hand to being a simple oriented shape item and the other being a simplified interface for use in fields.

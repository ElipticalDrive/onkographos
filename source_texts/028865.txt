Three-dimensional reconstruction of the jaw and dentition is an important part of preoperative planning for dental implant surgery. The traditional method is to use the Marching Cubes algorithm for reconstruction. However, the Marching Cubes algorithm has shortcomings such as surface voids and slow display speed. In order to improve the performance of the algorithm, it is proposed to solve the surface void problem with Laplacian smooth iso-surface and use the polygon merging method to improve the display speed. 3D reconstruction was performed using two sets of CBCT samples (jaw and dentition of two different patients). The experimental results show that the surface of the model obtained by the improved algorithm is with significantly improved cavity problem and smoother, and the time used for display is also greatly reduced.

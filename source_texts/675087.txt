Adds supersample::Integer = 1 to create_shepp_logan_phantom, create_torso_phantom and create_tubes_phantom. With supersample = s, each voxel holds the mean of s^D point samples spread evenly over the voxel (in-plane for a 2D slice): area sampling instead of point sampling.
Why: a point-sampled phantom aliases strongly in k-space, which biases simulated MRI data. In a 2D Shepp–Logan study (128² reconstruction), 4×4 area sampling cut the k-space error of a 1.6× finer phantom from 10 % to under 3 %. Ristretto uses this to simulate acquisitions without the inverse crime.
Each sub-voxel offset is rendered on a shifted grid and accumulated, so memory stays at two phantoms; time grows as s^D.
The result equals the block mean of the point-sampled phantom at s× resolution (tests for 2D/3D Shepp–Logan and torso).
supersample = 1 is unchanged (tested); mask phantoms throw for supersample > 1.
Version 1.1.0.

```
`create_shepp_logan_phantom`, `create_torso_phantom` and
`create_tubes_phantom` take `supersample::Integer = 1`. With
`supersample = s`, each voxel holds the mean of `s^D` point samples
spread evenly over the voxel (in-plane for a 2D slice) instead of the
single sample at its centre.

Point sampling gives a voxel either the full intensity or none, so the
rasterized phantom aliases strongly in k-space. Used as the ground truth
of simulated MRI data, that aliasing is a systematic model error: in a
2D Shepp-Logan study (128^2 reconstruction), area sampling with 4x4
sub-samples cut the k-space error of a 1.6x finer phantom from 10 % to
under 3 %.

`render_supersampled` renders the phantom once per sub-voxel offset on
a shifted grid and accumulates, so memory stays at two phantoms
whatever `s` is. The result equals the block mean of the point-sampled
phantom at `s` times the resolution (tested for 2D and 3D Shepp-Logan
and torso). Mask phantoms refuse `supersample > 1`.

Version 1.1.0.
```

```
On Julia 1.10 a closure that captured `eltype` stored it as a
`DataType`, and one that captured `is_mask` as a `Bool`, so the phantom
allocation and every `draw!` call were dispatched at runtime (JET
`@test_opt` failed on the LTS jobs). The closures now read the element
type from a static parameter and the mask flag from the type of `ti`,
and the 2D Shepp-Logan path takes the element type positionally.
```

Implements the two 'Geometry creation algorithm' flowcharts in Fortran.
Method 1 - constant sphere size: random-sequential placement of N non-overlapping fixed-radius spheres (distance-rejection 'array C' check), then voxelization + grid averaging.
Method 2 - specified porosity + variable sphere size: spheres with variable radius r in [rmin,rmax] and allowed overlap o in [omin,omax]; keeps adding until the solid volume reaches the required fraction (target porosity), then voxelization + grid averaging.
Voxel convention: 0 = inside sphere (solid), 1 = pore. Outputs legacy VTK STRUCTURED_POINTS volumes (view in ParaView/VisIt) plus sphere centre/radius lists.
Build: gfortran -O2 -o porous_geometry porous_geometry.f90 && ./porous_geometry

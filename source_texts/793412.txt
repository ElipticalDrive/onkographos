Octree Algorithm | mlund/spheretree | DeepWiki
Oct 30, 2025The Octree algorithm constructs sphere-trees through recursive spatial subdivision without requiring medial axis computation or complex optimization. It is the baseline algorithm in the toolkit, optimized for speed at the expense of tight-fitting quality.

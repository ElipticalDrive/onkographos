In traditional visualization method of 3D seismic data sets,a series of 2D slices are used to indicate the 3D seismic data sets.The disadvantage of this method is that the whole scene and details of the original data sets cannot be displayed.This disadvantage can be overcome by the method of volume rendering,which can generate detailed 3D image on screen from 3D data sets.Volume Rendering based on texture mapping takes advantage of graphic hardware to render the 3D seismic data,speeds the calculation.

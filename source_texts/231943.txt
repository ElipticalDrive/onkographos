Given the values from the calibration, determine the roughness of the material, and have the user provide the index of refraction. Then, using the Cook-Torrance BRDF, determine the intensity of all other points based on their angles. Finally, apply some sort of randomness based on the variance of the original data. This can be evaluated against a flat wall.

Summary
Full 3D rewrite. Chladni figures are a physical 3D phenomenon (sand on a vibrating plate) — the previous flat UV rendering gave no spatial read of the plate, no material differentiation between sand and plate surface, and no HDR bloom bait.
What changed:
64-step SDF sphere march into a thin sdBox plate (halfsize 1.0 × 0.025 × 1.0)
Slow orbital camera (12°/s) — plate seen at a natural angle from above
Blinn-Phong with warm key light from vec3(0.6, 1.0, 0.4)
Sand ridge bump mapping: finite-diff gradient of sandH perturbs the top-face normal to simulate raised sand grain ridges; sand–plate material blend with separate specular weights
fwidth AA on sand boundary: smoothstep(0.5 ± fwidth(sandRaw), sandRaw) — sub-pixel sharpening on nodal line edges
HDR specular peaks: spec * 2.5 * matSpec * (0.8 + bass * 1.2) reaches ~1.5 on plate surface
Sand emissive glow: sandAA * 1.2 * (0.5 + audioLevel) — dense ridges reach ~1.8 HDR for bloom
Preserved phantom resonance (30s cycle, higher harmonic interference pattern)
Added audioReact input; added "3D" to CATEGORIES
Optional inputTex mapped onto plate face (mixed with plateColor)
Test plan
Nodal lines form correct Chladni patterns for various N/M combos
Bump mapping shows visible ridge topology under oblique lighting
Audio mode evolution: bass changes N, treble changes M smoothly
Specular highlights bloom on plate surface
Phantom resonance fires at ~30s with finer interference overlay
Plate texture input (inputTex) blends correctly onto plate surface

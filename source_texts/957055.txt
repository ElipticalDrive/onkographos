What changed
Temporal anti-aliasing, per view.
Selection. PostStack.fxaa becomes AntiAliasing anti_aliasing { NONE, FXAA, TAA }, plus TaaParams: current weight, clip gamma, depth tolerance, velocity threshold, mip bias and a debug output. FXAA stays the default. Invalid parameters fault INVALID_ARGUMENT at configure_view, and only while TAA is selected.
Jitter.
maths::halton, post::taa_jitter (8 Halton(2,3) points) and camera::jitter_matrices offset only the rasterized projection.
Culling, picking, lights, shadows and the cluster grid keep the unjittered matrices. Camera.lens_shift is untouched.
FrameRoot.jitter_time.xy carries the offset in NDC.
Motion vectors for everything drawn opaque. A temporal view (TAA or motion blur) keeps each node's previous skin palette, morph block and instance matrices in its ViewHistory (PoseRecord). The VELOCITY vertex stage deforms twice through DrawRoot.previous_pose (PreviousPoseGpu). Skinned, morphed and individually moving instances now get correct velocity, for motion blur as well.
Velocity. Velocity stays unjittered. On a TAA view, velocity is RGBA16F and its z channel holds each surface's expected previous depth.
Resolve (taa_resolve.comp, timed as Pass.TEMPORAL_AA). It runs after the scene passes and before motion blur, depth of field, bloom and grading:
the current sample is filtered back to the pixel center from its 3x3 jittered taps;
velocity is dilated to the nearest depth in the 3x3 neighborhood;
history is read with a 5-tap Catmull-Rom filter at uv - velocity;
history is rejected by expected previous depth (2x2 footprint, relative tolerance) and by the difference between current and previous velocity;
history is clipped to the neighborhood's YCoCg variance box and blended with luma weights.
History images. Color, depth and velocity histories are ping-ponged per view. configure_view, a resize and reset_view_history reset one view only; two views of the same camera never share history.
Material mip bias. TAA views bias built-in material texture sampling through FrameRoot.mip_bias. Custom materials opt in with a sample_custom_map overload that takes a bias.
Debug outputs. Velocity, rejection and history weight replace color on the view's output.
Custom dispatches. A dispatch that writes a TAA view's color first moves the resolved image into hdr_color, so an application effect never feeds the next resolve.
Benchmarks. examples/taa is new. The benchmark loop and benchmark.py take --anti-aliasing none|fxaa|taa, and the CSV gains gpu_velocity_ms and gpu_taa_ms.
Docs. docs/post.md (new temporal anti-aliasing section, updated motion-blur text), docs/views.md, docs/instancing.md, docs/custom_shaders.md, docs/benchmarking.md and the README.
Divergences from the design and why
History lookup. History is read at uv - velocity with no jitter terms. The written design added previous_jitter - current_jitter. Accumulated history is pixel-centered, and shifting its lookup by the jitter each frame would cancel the anti-aliasing.
Resolve order. The resolve runs before motion blur and depth of field, as the post-processing architecture states, rather than after them.
Mip bias plumbing. Built-in fragments set one material_mip_bias value from FrameRoot.mip_bias, instead of passing a bias argument through every sampling call.
Vertex helper signature. write_mesh_outputs keeps its four-argument signature, because custom vertex stages call it.
Resize resets. A resize frees the history tables, so the sample index and slot restart with the recreated images.
Added to scope. The detached view-color write, the benchmark anti-aliasing option and command-line states for the example were added; none were in the plan.
Verification
CPU. python3 scripts/build.py --test is green: 444 unit tests (up from 434); add-on suites unchanged; 65 example programs build.
Real Vulkan acceptance (WSL, llvmpipe, validation on). c3c test acceptance --path test/gpu/render: 23/23 pass (7 new), with no validation messages. The new tests cover:
jitter advance on submit only;
independent reset;
still-scene color;
deformed and instanced motion under every debug output;
a display view;
the detached view-color write;
reconfiguring between motion blur, TAA and FXAA;
background history under a finite far plane. This is a regression test for a bug found in review; it fails without the fix.
Example (WSL, validation on). No AA, FXAA and TAA still frames were compared: TAA removes the checker moire and the stair steps on thin diagonal bars. The debug views show rejection on silhouettes and on the incoming edge during a camera sweep. Observed limit: distant checker detail keeps visible moire under camera motion at the default weight.
RTX 4090, native Windows. Many-light hall, 1024 clustered lights, static camera; medians of three processes of 300 frames:

Remaining work
Transparent surfaces carry the velocity of whatever is behind them. There is no reactive mask.
Custom vertex deformation is not captured in velocity.
There is no temporal upscaler.
G-buffer normals are not used for history rejection.

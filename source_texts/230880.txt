Metallic has no terrain. Every outdoor template (Simulator zones, Battle Royale, Horror, Kart Racing) fakes ground with Parts. Terrain is the one big Roblox Studio feature we have no issue for.
Proposal
Terrain service under Workspace. It is a sparse grid of 32³ voxel chunks; each voxel stores a signed distance (i8) and a material id (u8).
Meshing: Surface Nets per chunk, on a worker thread. Only chunks touched by an edit are re-meshed. Write our own, or use a permissively licensed crate if cargo deny passes.
Materials:
Up to 32 terrain materials from the CC0 asset library (Free asset library: CC0 textures, models, HDRIs #36), triplanar, blended by voxel weights.
They follow the look preset (Look presets v1 (Realistic, Soft, Toon, Low-poly, Pixel 3D, Retro 32-bit...) #64): Toon and Low-poly get flat shading, Pixel 3D gets point sampling.
Physics: one Rapier trimesh collider per chunk, rebuilt together with the mesh.
Studio tools:
Add, Subtract, Smooth, Flatten, Paint and Sea level.
Generate, from seeded noise: hills, mountains, islands and caves.
Brush size and strength on the mouse wheel. Each stroke is one undo step.
Luau: Terrain:FillBlock, FillBall, ReadVoxels and WriteVoxels (Roblox names).
Save: chunks are stored as compressed blobs beside the RON scene, so the scene file stays diffable.
Done when
On a 1 km × 1 km × 256 m world, sculpting with a 16 m brush re-meshes in under 8 ms on the reference PC.
Save then reload gives identical voxels.
A character can walk on the terrain, and a raycast hits it.
Blocked by #63 (material table), and by #69 for large views. Proposed in the ideas pass; refine before claiming.

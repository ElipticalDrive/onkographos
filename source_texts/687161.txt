Improving original Marching Cubes(MC) method to reconstruct 3D models with correct topological structure and fewer triangles.Solving the problem of faces ambiguity by using hyperbolic asymptote and the problem of inferior ambiguity by judging the relationship of hyperbolic asymptote on inferior parallel plane.Giving a method to avoid the computation of unnecessary triangles based on the connectivity between cubes.Finally,preserving the original topology structure and reducing more candidate triangles by using Decimation algorithm.Experiment illustrates that these improvement for original MC method can obtain satisfied results.

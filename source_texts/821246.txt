Abstract — In this work, we present a multi-resolution data management scheme for efficiently storing information, which is originating from sensors and augmenting mobile applications. The proposed mechanism exploits the discrete wavelet transform properties for providing a location-dependent storage model, which alleviates the constraints on the storage resources needed for running context-aware mobile applications. We have subsequently defined an information exchange protocol that, by taking advantage of the introduced data management scheme, significantly reduces the communication resources needed for exchanging and fusing information stored by mobile nodes. The data management scheme, and related information exchange protocol, have been implemented and evaluated by means of numerical simulations. Index Terms — mobile data management, multi-resolution data management, pervasive computing, context-aware computing, wireless networks, disconnected networks I

Engine work for a large custom-shaded showcase scene. Lit .fmat materials gain Light, Ambient, and Composite hooks, alpha to coverage, effects_depth, and an opt-out for the directional light and environment lighting. Instanced meshes can select per-draw ranges and keep static data in device buffers, custom passes get planar mirrors, linear HDR render textures, and the spot shadow tiles. Also includes frame pacing and sliced warm-up, allocation-free steady-state frames, and surface debug view wipes; each change is its own commit with a CHANGELOG entry.

This report introduces a new framework for segmentation of 3D raster data. Segmentation is formalized in an algebraic topology framework, allowing rigorous proofs of the algorithms. Furthermore, by eschewing a lookup table construction as known from the Marching Cubes algorithm, segmentation is no longer restricted to isosurface extraction. The algorithm can be generalized to higher dimensions.

Slice 2 of the 25 km streamed-terrain test map (slice 1 was #5).
Mountain. A new terrain feature, mountain (x, z, radius, height, seed, ridges, erosion, warp). It is a ridged multifractal over a domain warp, with gullies cut across the downhill direction (the Gabor-style erosion noise surveyed by Grenier et al., CGF 2024). Rock shows on the steep faces. A new surface snowAbove adds a snow line (colour only). Streamed Terrain gets a 1.8 km peak 5.6 km from spawn. A mountain tile is made in well under the 25 ms the test allows.
Digging. Streamed Terrain now offers Sandbox, so blasts dig craters on it. It has the starting rack and two buggies near spawn.
Bombs. There are two new Military props, placed near spawn on Streamed Terrain:
Aerial Bomb: 14 m blast, 5 m crater, 2.5 s fuse.
Small Nuke: 90 m blast, 3000 damage, 30 m crater, 5 s fuse. Blasts over 15 m send a ring and column of up to 16 fireballs, as ordinary Explode messages (no protocol change).
Explosive props take a new crater key (0.5 to 60 m). The ranges are now radius up to 120, damage up to 3000 and knockback up to 60. Props/README.md documents them.
Test. "Terrain streaming" sets off both bombs in a Sandbox game and checks the craters, the fireballs and the tick times. Locally:
the nuke went off in 4.4 ms, with a worst tick after of 3.7 ms, a crater 15.9 m deep and 739 cells dug
the bomb took 19.8 ms the first time, which includes JIT
Also. The Animated props pin is re-recorded after #2 (the anemometer and barber pole fixes), which had left main red.
Locally, 84 sections ran green before the bomb test was added, and the 17 map, prop and terrain sections ran green after it. check_branch.py reports 0 problems.

Issue body actions
Improve contact shading around columns, moldings, floor joints, and material displays.
Acceptance criteria:
Use scene depth and normals to calculate ambient occlusion.
Provide world-space radius, strength, bias, and quality controls.
Filter with depth-aware edge preservation.
Apply occlusion to indirect diffuse lighting without indiscriminately darkening direct light, reflections, or emissive output.
Define how the effect combines with material occlusion textures.
Include an occlusion debug view and enable toggle.
Validate small and large scenes for halos, scale sensitivity, and camera stability.
Out of scope: Global illumination, off-screen occlusion, and ray-traced AO.

I added an unfiltered IVisibilityScene::Enumerate overload so the debug view includes every allocated octree node, including empty ones. The component copies node and entry bounds while the scene is locked, then sends one DebugDraw line-batch request after releasing the lock. This avoids DebugDraw calls under the scene lock and reduces bus calls.

Description
Implement the core volumetric estimation engine using the voxel carving (Shape from Silhouette) method.[5]
Requirements
Build a 3D grid of voxels in world coordinates scaled by the ID-1 card.
Implement back-projection reconstruction where voxels falling outside 2D silhouettes are carved away: $V = \sum \Delta x \Delta y \Delta z$.[4, 5]
Calculate final physical volume in $cm^3$.
Academic Rationale
The voxel carving approach is the computationally tractable standard for mobile 3D reconstruction without requiring specialized depth sensors.

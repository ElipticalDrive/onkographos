Question
After excluding AGILE3D from the current destination and deferring 3-D point-click evaluation to a separate future ScanNet multi-query benchmark, what exact five public-benchmark Evaluation Contracts replace the closed six-task strict contracts? Keep the LERF-2D, LERF-3D, and ScanNet OVS public tracks unless a concrete incompatibility is identified; for NVOS and SPIn-NeRF, freeze exactly which reference, source, target, captured, or rendered RGB channels and query-time vision models are authorized at Mapping and Query, which prompt schema each comparator receives, whether SPIn-NeRF uses the Available-Nine cohort or requires the unavailable official full-ten cohort, which same-contract comparator and SOTA Target are eligible, and which prior six-task, RGB-free, promotion, and method-design decisions remain reusable versus explicitly superseded. The answer must produce precise /to-spec inputs without designing or executing the deferred private benchmark.

Resolution
Adopt a new five-public-benchmark destination and planning registry, five-benchmark-evaluation-contract-registry-v1. The five current contract identities are:
lerf2d-field-only-four-scene-v1
lerf3d-field-only-four-scene-v1
nvos-ludvig-online-all-view-eight-v1
spin-ludvig-online-full-mask-available-nine-v1
scannet-ovs-paper8-v1
The old six-task-evaluation-contract-registry-v1, its strict RGB-free NVOS/SPIn contracts, and its AGILE3D row remain immutable historical Context Evidence. They are not renamed or edited into the new authority. The later engineering status is Five-Benchmark Claim Outcome; objective-family regressions are Joint-Contract Negative Transfer.
Common contract and method boundary
An Evaluation Contract freezes the cohort, channel-level Information Grants, Authorized Query Input, output domain, evaluator, metrics, aggregation, comparator role, and comparison rule. It does not require RADIO-GS and a comparator to share the same internal vision model, calibration, threshold, hierarchy, or readout; those belong to each Method Contract. Consequently the LERF and LUDVIG reproductions below are same-evaluator VPA floors, not claims that the methods use an identical internal readout.
All authorizations remain channel-, stage-, purpose-, and provenance-scoped. Granting captured RGB never opens a sibling mask, label, metric, or evaluator artifact. Query-time vision-model weights, version, templates, preprocessing, and randomness are frozen Method Contract members and must appear in Stage Receipts and the Runtime Compliance Proof. Query-derived features, primitive fields, masks, hypotheses, and calibration are Query Workspace only: they may not update Deployment Scene State, persist across queries, or become a semantic cache. Gaussian-rendered RGB is forbidden in the five Primary contracts unless a future contract explicitly grants it.
LERF-2D — lerf2d-field-only-four-scene-v1
Preserve the contract frozen by Freeze six dated SOTA Targets and Evaluation Contracts:
Cohort: figurines, ramen, teatime, and waldo_kitchen; exactly 22 official annotated cameras and 208 official object queries.
Mapping: registered RGB and poses may construct conventional RGB geometry, but the 22 annotated cameras are excluded from semantic lifting and teacher inputs.
Query: frozen field, one official object-category string, and pose/intrinsics/resolution as Output Request Metadata; no captured/rendered RGB or query-time vision model.
Output/evaluator: continuous Camera-Raster Output; object mIoU and localization hit against evaluator-private masks/boxes; object mean, then scene mean, then unweighted four-scene macro.
Conditional SOTA Target: SAD-GS 68.8 mIoU and 88.7 LocAcc, conjunctive and compared by ROUND_HALF_UP(..., 1) until its complete comparator identity is bound.
Mandatory same-evaluator VPA floor: OccamLGS 63.6200435 / 82.8487101.
RADIO-GS retains its globally fixed 0.5 binary Gaussian-posterior boundary. A comparator's different fixed internal calibration remains part of its Method Contract rather than invalidating this Evaluation Contract.
LERF-3D — lerf3d-field-only-four-scene-v1
Preserve the existing four-scene, 208-query contract:
Mapping: registered RGB may construct geometry; annotated cameras are excluded from semantic lifting and mask evidence.
Query: frozen field and one official object string; no source/target/rendered RGB, query-specific detector, test-label-conditioned mapping, or target-view refinement.
Output/evaluator: one fixed selected Gaussian subset rendered into the official nested-mask domain; mIoU, Acc@0.25, and Acc@0.50; object mean, scene mean, then unweighted four-scene macro.
Conditional SOTA Target: PairGS raw-3D 60.4 / 79.6 / 68.2, conjunctive with one-decimal ROUND_HALF_UP; its refined path is ineligible.
Mandatory same-evaluator VPA floor: VALA 54.1248877 / 79.3526471 / 56.6114038.
GaussDet-style test-class-conditioned source-image mapping remains incompatible Context Evidence. Comparator calibration is Method Contract state; it need not equal RADIO-GS's 0.5 posterior boundary.
NVOS — nvos-ludvig-online-all-view-eight-v1
This contract explicitly supersedes strict-unseen field-only NVOS as the Primary Claim Track.
Cohort: the official eight tasks fern, flower, fortress, horns_center, horns_left, leaves, orchids, and trex; the reference is not scored and the official target is scored once per task.
Mapping Information Grants: every registered captured RGB channel and pose, including reference, source, and scored-target views, may construct the scene field.
Query Information Grants: every registered captured RGB channel, including the scored target, may be read by the current query and its preregistered Query-Time Vision Model. Gaussian-rendered RGB is forbidden.
Authorized Query Input: typed POSITIVE_SCRIBBLE_RASTER on the official reference identity/calibration. The negative scribble is unavailable to this contract rather than passed through and ignored; changing that prompt schema creates a new contract.
Method neutrality: LUDVIG-SAM's three positive points per call, ten calls per view, min-max normalization, >0.25 threshold, and bilinear alignment are bound to the comparator Method Contract. RADIO-GS may use another frozen Query-Time Vision Model and shared Gaussian-posterior compiler under the same channel/prompt/evaluator contract.
Evaluator: target masks remain evaluator-only; report foreground IoU and binary pixel accuracy per task, then equal-weight macro across all eight tasks. Pixel accuracy is mandatory-report-only.
Conditional SOTA Target: LUDVIG-DINOv2 macro foreground IoU >=92.4, comparing the unrounded result after ROUND_HALF_UP(..., 1). Its full comparator identity must still be bound.
Mandatory same-evaluator VPA floor: the local exact eight-task LUDVIG-SAM macro >=91.25768502741802.
The original NVOS 70.1, strict-field RADIO-GS rows, WildSeg3D 94.1, and other prompt/view-incompatible results remain Context Evidence. The last cannot become the target while its random prompt and five-view seeds remain unbound.
SPIn-NeRF — spin-ludvig-online-full-mask-available-nine-v1
This contract preserves the Available-Nine cohort but explicitly supersedes the RGB-free field-only Primary Query boundary.
Cohort: orchids, leaves, fern, room, horns, fortress, pinecone, truck, and lego. Fork is excluded; this is an explicit nine-scene result, never a nine-of-ten estimate or a full-ten claim.
Mapping and Query Information Grants: every registered captured RGB channel and pose, including the canonical reference and every scored target frame, may be read; Query may invoke a preregistered Query-Time Vision Model. Gaussian-rendered RGB is forbidden.
Authorized Query Input: typed COMPLETE_BINARY_MASK on the single canonical official reference identity/calibration. Foreground and complete complement are available to the query. The reference mask may choose a query-local candidate and calibration/threshold under a frozen Method Contract; no target mask or metric may do so.
Lifecycle: reference-derived candidates, three-hypothesis primitive fields, vision features, thresholds, and masks remain Query Workspace. The canonical reference is not scored; the resulting query is evaluated on every non-reference annotated frame.
Evaluator: foreground IoU and mandatory-report-only binary pixel accuracy; unweighted frame mean within scene, then unweighted macro across the nine scenes.
Conditional SOTA Target and mandatory same-evaluator VPA floor: local exact Available-Nine LUDVIG-SAM macro >=93.7200449592385.
The matching-nine paper recomputation 94.57777777777778 lacks a complete Exact Row Authority and remains Context Evidence. The published full-ten 93.8 has the wrong cohort and also remains Context Evidence. Sparse-point rows answer a different prompt contract.
The frozen historical LUDVIG row does not itself pass the new claim gate: it lacks runtime-compliance-proof-v1, and the current launcher identity drifted from its frozen binding. A fresh or independently sealed execution must produce a complete Exact Row Authority and PASS Runtime Compliance Proof; historical metadata may not be retrofitted to create eligibility.
ScanNet OVS — scannet-ovs-paper8-v1
Preserve the existing public track:
Cohort: scene0000_00, scene0062_00, scene0070_00, scene0097_00, scene0140_00, scene0347_00, scene0400_00, and scene0590_00; scene0645_00 remains sensitivity evidence only.
Mapping: registered RGB-D, poses, and query-independent frozen teachers; mesh semantic labels, pseudo-GT, GT-present class sets, and label statistics remain evaluator-only.
Query: frozen field plus one content-bound official 19-class bank. Score it once, then apply frozen ordered 15- and 10-class subset mappings and split-wise argmax; no GT pruning or query RGB.
Output/evaluator: categorical posterior on the exact optimized/pruned full-resolution feature-level-0 Gaussian rows. The evaluator retains the frozen anisotropic Mahalanobis pseudo-GT, top-k, class-balanced voting, and opacity-volume weight rules.
Metrics: 19/15/10 mIoU and mAcc, GT-present classes within scene followed by an unweighted eight-scene macro.
Exact conjunctive target and mandatory VPA floor: [34.526858, 51.590646, 37.960598, 56.769618, 47.364163, 67.465001] in 19-mIoU, 19-mAcc, 15-mIoU, 15-mAcc, 10-mIoU, 10-mAcc order, with the existing zero-tolerance semantics. Publication must retain the compatible-local-RGB-geometry qualifier.
Five-benchmark promotion and evidence
Parameterize the four-stage Development Advancement and Claim Promotion policy from Define the joint six-task promotion rule over these five contracts. Preserve candidate identity, paired {0,1,2} seeds, non-compensable floors, leximin gap closure, Mapping-Only Checkpoint Rule, attempt ledger, early termination, retry, prospective freeze, and no-stitching rules.
Claim Promotion is atomic across all five contracts. For each stochastic target component:
the unrounded equal-weight mean over seeds {0,1,2} meets the target under its frozen exact or rounding comparison;
at least two seeds individually meet the target; and
every seed meets its mandatory VPA floor.
A missing execution, comparator-identity gate, Exact Row Authority, Cold-Start Storage Gate, Runtime Compliance Proof, or lineage/completeness gate fails the whole Five-Benchmark Claim Outcome. LERF and ScanNet targets/floors retain their existing zero-tolerance or one-decimal rules. Existing benchmark consumption remains; the five-contract confirmation must still use one sealed-before-release Prospective Evaluation Batch and may make only qualified non-blind claims.
Reuse and supersession
Reuse unchanged: Define the deployment scene-state and artifact lifecycle boundary and Define the transitive runtime compliance contract.
Reuse with five-contract parameterization: storage budgets and gates; Evidence Authority Hierarchy and row semantics; the core Canonical Query Interface, Canonical Query Evidence, Gaussian Query Posterior, Capability Views, Output-Domain Operators, adapters, and abstention rules; sidecar-free D512/L512 canonical field family; prospective evaluation; Joint Mapping Objective; negative-transfer controls; LERF mechanism; and the joint promotion policy.
Explicitly supersede as current authorities: the six-contract registry and destination in Freeze six dated SOTA Targets and Evaluation Contracts; the Primary tracks in Choose the claimable NVOS and SPIn-NeRF protocol tracks; the global RGB-free Primary Query statement and NVOS/SPIn rows in Define the mapping-query-evaluator information firewall; and conflicting no-RGB/no-vision-model clauses in Define the shared field and modality-specific query interface. Their general fail-closed and shared-interface principles survive.
Choose RGB-free reference-prompt completion for NVOS and SPIn-NeRF is no longer the Primary compiler or promotion gate. Registered Raster-Adjoint Evidence and the Observation-Anchored Signed Region-Hypothesis Marginal remain diagnostic controls only.
Establish a claim-eligible Available-Nine SPIn-NeRF comparator authority remains authoritative only for the superseded RGB-free contract; its negative result neither blocks nor automatically promotes a LUDVIG-online row.
Reconstruct the destination-compliant baseline gap matrix remains historical Context Evidence and is superseded as the current baseline authority. Contract changes do not upgrade any row.
AGILE3D's public contract, world-click compiler, metrics, and promotion gate from Choose the transferable instance carrier and interactive update for AGILE3D are deferred beyond this destination. The Boundary-Calibrated Field Region Hierarchy survives through the independent LERF and reference-prompt decisions, not through an active AGILE3D requirement.
Newly visible frontier
Two child tickets now carry the remaining in-scope work:
Choose the shared RGB-assisted reference-prompt query mechanism for NVOS and SPIn-NeRF decides RADIO-GS's own method-neutral RGB-assisted compiler. The prior RGB-free mechanism is a diagnostic control.
Reconstruct the destination-compliant five-contract baseline gap matrix rebuilds the Joint Development Baseline under the five current contracts and is natively blocked by the compiler decision.
The deferred private ScanNet multi-query benchmark is not designed or executed here and belongs outside this map. Acquiring Fork and making a full-ten SPIn-NeRF claim also remain out of scope.
The root glossary now records Query-Time Vision Model, Boundary-Calibrated Field Region Hierarchy, Semantic Region-Hypothesis Marginal, Five-Benchmark Claim Outcome, and Joint-Contract Negative Transfer, and updates development/promotion language to the five-benchmark destination. No ADR is added: this resolution is the decision authority and the map remains its index.

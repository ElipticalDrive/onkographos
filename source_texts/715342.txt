Severity
Location
Finding
major
src/estimation/mixture.rs:624
mixture_gradient_fd still evaluates x ± h without clamping to the packed bounds. At a finite parameter bound, one probe is therefore outside the optimizer's feasible domain; it can produce NaN for domain-sensitive models or return a symmetric derivative that is not the feasible one-sided derivative. The stated rationale only addresses crossing zero, not crossing arbitrary declared bounds. Clamp both probes and divide by the actual clamped span, as central_diff_packed does. A boundary regression test is also needed.

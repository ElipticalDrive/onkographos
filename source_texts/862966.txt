Branch: frontier-voxel-engine · Tracking: #24 · Architecture: https://github.com/DrewPhi/MicroVoxelProceduralGeneration/blob/frontier-voxel-engine/frontier/ARCHITECTURE.md
Task and acceptance
Phase: Voxel renderer | Dependencies: FV03/FV08 | Gate: G2/G3.
Render native 6.25 cm cubes/colors/cell faces with bitmask/greedy meshing in sparse 3D bricks, including caves, vertical walls, tunnels, player-built structures, water boundaries and special materials. Minimize native face extraction and submission overhead with shared arenas/batching. Support dirty small-region remeshing only; maintain exact voxel ray/collision oracle. Compare against a direct voxel ray option; don't assume one wins universally.
Acceptance: exact face/appearance/readback parity with source across chunk boundaries and edits, no missing/sliver faces, finite memory, measured CPU/gpu costs and edit latency.
Legacy reference policy
P — main/successor/world/src/render/surface.rs, terrain.rs native face collector, world.rs and voxel test fixtures may be ported selectively after exact-face and lifetime tests. Do not port its CPU coarse mesh generation. Compare Rust/C++ binary-greedy references as algorithmic guides only.
Guardrail: implement in frontier/ only; benchmark old branch without copying its scheduler and LOD failure modes.
Native handoff diagnostic checkpoint — 6af9b96
Added --scene hybrid: fixed64×64m exact6.25cm FBM interior, explicit static proxy cutout, and2m approximate geometric collar. Efficient column/spans mesher avoids a dense vertical prism. Native-only3.327M triangles/279.5MB; combined3.480M triangles. Four hybrid tests cover canonical DDA depth, single top ownership, sampled boundary continuity and proxy joins;54 workspace tests pass plus an explicitly run ignored performance test. Radeon760M at1080p,10s stationary ground view: p95=12.41ms,p99=12.61ms,zero>16.67ms intervals.
View experiment now supports --view-levels 7..11. Matched10s35m/s aerial routes at2.048/8.192/32.768km half-extents: p95=2.86/3.38/3.55ms; extra distant grids remain cheap in this scene. These are coarse surface proxies, NOT matched distant visual quality or a measured maximum distance.
Still diagnostic: native ownership does NOT follow the camera; no caves/edits/collision/error-bound or invisible transition claim. Static CPU exterior exists solely to test the boundary and is not replacing the GPU streaming renderer. DefaultFBM remains moving smooth proxy. Next: reusable moving native chunks, image-error/transition quality and volume ownership. Full issue/gates remain open.
Checkpoint and reproduction.
2026-11-05 exact microvoxel policy change
See #59/#61/#62: exact voxel geometry should be demand-selected as sparse GPU-resident surface clusters when projected screen error/gameplay requires, NOT all of a 64x64m square. Keep old square as a correctness/performance fixture only; do not increase it to hide transition defects. Near exact geometry and 3D volume ownership must remain correct regardless of visualization LOD.

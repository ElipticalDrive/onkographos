Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) has recently shown impressive results in synthesizing novel views of complex scenes from images. However, its performance is highly dependent on the quality of the estimated pose. Although existing methods have achieved decent results in simple scenes such as forward-facing scene, they still rely heavily on accurate initial poses for more complex scenes. In this paper, we introduce RPE-BARF, a method that incorporates relative pose constraints between adjacent frames and jointly optimizes both relative and absolute poses, significantly improves the model’s robustness against pose errors in complex scenes. We further propose a novel and highly effective Gaussian-blur loss that significantly improves joint optimization of neural fields and poses and it can be effectively integrated with existing methodologies. Quantitative and qualitative results demonstrate that compared to BARF and L2G-NeRF, PRE-BARF significantly improves both the quality of view synthesis and the robustness to initial poses.

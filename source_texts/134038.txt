ABSTRACT
To observe the deformation and the failure process of solid objects in explosion and impact simulations, the clarity and accuracy of
simulation image representations should be ensured. However, in the results of three-dimensional (3D) explosion field rendering,
surface confusion and blurred images of the microstructure often lead to misunderstandings of the simulation results. In this work,
based on the ray casting method and the Blinn-Phong lighting model, a volume rendering method is proposed that can adapt
to the characteristics of step changes in the explosion field data and satisfies the requirements of observing the microstructure.
The Blinn-Phong lighting model is applied to the light and shade calculation of the resampling point, and the gradient of the
resampling point is obtained via interpolation calculations; then, it is normalized to obtain the normal vector. When calculating
the material color of the resampling point, the transfer function calculation is performed first; then, the interpolation calculation is
performed. Finally, the opacity of the resampling point and the associated material color are substituted into the ray casting
synthesis formula.In addition, several groups of examples of visual effects are analyzed from the subjective point of view of human
visual effects and the objective points of view of image entropy, the meangradient, image mean and standard deviation. The results
show that the new method addresses the problem of interface color confusion, improves the accuracy of microstructure rendering
in solid media, increases the spatial sense of visual images, and satisfies the visualization requirements for explosion products and
shock waves. At the same time, this approach effectively shortens the time for volume rendering of the explosion field.

Introduction
Explosion and impact problems have important practical engineering value in the safety protection Ąeld, the
phenomena of shock waves, jets, penetration, and fragmentation generated by explosions and impacts are the focus
of researchers. But such problems usually involve multiple scales, large deformations, fragmentation and material
nonlinearity. Explosive experiments are not only very dangerous, but also produce a lot of smoke, Ąre, and in
practice it is difficult to observe and record the experimental process. Both theoretical and experimental studies are
very difficult. These seriously affect researchersŠ ability to observe and analyze jets, shock waves, projectiles, and
targets during explosions and impacts. Therefore, these problems are usually studied by using numerical simulation
methods1–8 . With the development of numerical simulation technology, in the study of explosion and impact
problems, increasing attention has been given to the shape and the position of solid substances such as fragments,
jets, steel bars and concrete. Such detail requires that postprocessing programs for big data visualization must
have the ability to clearly and correctly determine the physical material interface and to express the Ąne structure
therein. The visualization of big data is an interdisciplinary subject of human-computer interaction, graphics, image
science, statistical analysis and other disciplines. It integrates various knowledge and skills, such as data processing,
algorithm design, software development, human-computer interaction, animation and other forms, to display data,
to interpret the relationships and trends between data, and to improve the efficiency of reading and understanding
data. Utilizing the knowledge of these subjects to develop better visualization procedures has become a real problem
in recent research.
Based on the above background and real needs, in this study the basic principles and visualization process
of the explosion Ąeld volume rendering method are introduced. Then, based on the ray casting method and the
Blinn-Phong lighting model, starting from the light synthesis formula, the color weighted interpolation and normal
vector interpolation methods are introduced to improve the calculation process of the resampling point information.
In addition, no new multidimensional transfer function is used, which avoids possible uncertainty in the visualization
process and ensures rendering quality stability. Moreover, the problem of interface color confusion is addressed, and
the accuracy of microstructure rendering in physical media is improved. Moreover, by changing the visual process,
the computational burden greatly decreases, and the efficiency improves without the use of parallel algorithms or
GPU hardware acceleration. Finally, the visualization results of several sets of typical examples of explosion and
impact problems are provided. The results are analyzed from the subjective point of view of human visual perception
and the objective point of view of quantitative indicators (image mean, standard deviation, image entropy and
meangradient). The results demonstrate that the new method addresses the problem of interface color confusion,
improves the accuracy of microstructure rendering in solid media, increases the spatial sense of visual images, and
satisĄes the visualization requirements for explosion products and shock waves.
The remainder of the paper is organised as follows: section 2 reviews related work. Section 3 describes the
visualisation methodology and process used in this paper. Section 4 tests the effectiveness of the new method
through experiments. Finally, Section 5 summarises the work of this paper and points out the areas that still need
to be improved.
Related work
Limited by theoretical solutions and experimental studies, many researchers have developed relevant simulation
calculation programs for explosions and shocks. Rajasekar et al.9 studied the visualization of shock waves generated
by underwater explosions. Hopson et al.10 of the US Naval Surface Weapons Center used the Euler algorithm impact
physics program CTH that was developed by the Sandia National Laboratory in the United States to numerically
simulate the crushing process of AerMet100 alloy cylindrical shells under internal explosion loading. Matsumoto
et al.11 studied the method of visualizing the shock wave generated by the explosion of the bridge wires, and
Murphy et al.12 studied the method of visualizing the stress wave during the explosion process, which clariĄed
the dynamic behavior of the stress wave during the explosion process. Guo et al.13 established smooth particle
hydrodynamics (SPH) models based on impact tests of a buffer layer. Their models simulated the dynamic response
of foam concrete under low-velocity impact reasonably well. The mechanism of impact damage was investigated,
and the characteristics of layer-by-layer damage of foam concrete were demonstrated. After long-term research,
Ning et al.14, 15 developed a multisubstance Euler-type Ćuid dynamics numerical method and the corresponding
explosion simulation program PMMIC-3D. The accuracy of the numerical simulation results of explosions and shock
waves was veriĄed through experiments; blasts were simulated in air; shaped charge jets were simulated by using the
PMMIC-3D hydrocode; and empirical and experimental comparisons were performed.
The data obtained from numerical simulations is a large number of Ągures, very specialized and difficult to
understand intuitively. Visualization efforts can help researchers understand physical problems quickly and clearly.
The resulting Ąle contains a large amount of information about physical quantities, such as time, velocity, pressure,
displacement, and spatial coordinates. Sometimes the data information is also stored in the Ąle in binary form to save
storage space. Due to the limitations of the human visual system, data in a four-dimensional or higher-dimensional
space cannot be observed directly. Visualizing such data requires projecting the data into a lower-dimensional space
that is perceptible to humans16 .
Considerable research has been conducted on the visualization of multivariate data to obtain high-quality
visualization results. Fuchs et al.17 summarizes the types of multivariate data Ąelds, the corresponding visualization
processes and typical visualization techniques, and points out that the appropriate visualization method should be
determined according to speciĄc data Ąelds and visualization requirements. Wu et al.18 used automatic analysis
technology to achieve more efficient humanŰcomputer interactions and better visualization quality. Erik et al.19
proposed a multilight source lighting algorithm suitable for multivariable data Ąelds. Such an approach enhances the
spatial sense of the visualized results. Volume rendering is one of the core algorithms for realizing 3D data Ąeld
visualization20–23 . Rendering in this manner can directly and effectively display the physical phenomena inside the
data Ąeld, and it has very strong expressive power. To achieve high-quality rendering, traditional methods rely on
high-resolution sampling of volume data and complex transfer functions24 .High-resolution sampling can directly
improve the rendering quality, but it greatly increases the amount of calculation involved in volume rendering. The
other common approach is to improve the transfer function for volume rendering. This class of methods is used
to design new transfer functions, such as gradient25 , curvature26 , size27 , shape28 , and texture29 by incorporating
physical features in the data Ąeld into one-dimensional transfer functions. However, the design of a multidimensional
transfer function is generally difficult and time-consuming, and the rendering quality is also highly uncertain. To
better address these problems, researchers have proposed automatic and semiautomatic transfer functions. These
functions use the Gaussian mixture model30–32 , kernel probability density estimation33 , visibility histogram34, 35 ,
visibility of volumetric features32, 36 , information disparity37 , etc. However, such transfer functions are often not

universal, and they can be used only in speciĄc visualization processes.
The large body of literature mentioned above indicates that due to its own diversity and the diversity of
visualization purposes, there is no uniĄed visualization algorithm for multivariate data Ąelds. SpeciĄc problems
should be analyzed in detail. The three-dimensional explosion Ąeld is a special case of the multivariable data Ąeld.
The explosion and shock problem is a transient dynamic problem under extreme conditions such as high speed, high
strain rate, large deformation and high pressure; its numerical value often varies in the range of zero to tens of
megapascals. The medium type variables have a clear material interface that has nothing to do with the parameters,
which is the greatest difference from other data Ąelds. Moreover, the explosion process changes with the development
of time, and the numerical simulation result is often an instantaneous state for a series of discrete time points, which
makes the visualization of the explosion Ąeld more difficult. There are few related studies on the visualization of
explosion Ąelds and few reports in the literature. Ning et al.38, 39 adopted the fuzzy interface processing method
to simulate an air explosion and a formed jet. Zhang et al. presented a streamline-based method to visualize 3D
explosion Ąelds40, 41 . However, the image effect of the explosion Ąeld is not intuitive enough, and the observer must
have a good level of professional knowledge to understand the image information. In terms of the visualization
requirements for the three-dimensional explosion Ąeld, on the one hand, the focus is on the extraction and enhanced
display of physical features such as shock waves; on the other hand, the visualization results must be able to clearly
and accurately visualize the material interface, microstructure, etc. Wang et al. developed ViSC-3D visualization
software by using the volume rendering method, achieved good visualization results, and successfully displayed the
shock wave42 . However, the strong discontinuity and high strain rate characteristics of the explosion Ąeld was not
considered, and the Phong lighting model was applied directly to the color solution of the mesh vertices. When the
volume rendering method is applied to draw solid substances in the explosion Ąeld, interface color confusion and
an unclear microstructure of solid substances often occur. This occurrence seriously affects the understanding and
judgment of the numerical simulation results by researchers.
Good visualization methods provide not only better results but also faster processing speeds, which increases
research efficiency. The amount of data saved in the resulting Ąles of 3D explosion Ąelds is particularly large, so
the efficiency of volume rendering is also a major problem that hinders the visualization processing. Generally, the
following two methods are used to improve the rendering speed: One is to introduce parallel processing technology
and to develop parallel volume rendering algorithms43 ; the other is to use GPU hardware acceleration to speed up
sampling and compositing calculations in the rendering process to improve the volume rendering efficiency44–47 .
Both of these methods set greater demands on the performance of the computer.
To address the problems and the difficulties mentioned above and to better satisfy the needs for visualization of
explosion and impact problems, the basic principles and visualization process of the explosion Ąeld volume rendering
method are introduced. Then, based on the ray casting method and the Blinn-Phong lighting model, starting from
the light synthesis formula, the color weighted interpolation and normal vector interpolation methods are introduced
to improve the calculation of the resampling point information. In addition, no new multidimensional transfer
function is used. This approach avoids possible uncertainty in the visualization process and ensures rendering quality
stability. Moreover, the problem of interface color confusion is solved, and the accuracy of microstructure rendering
in physical media is improved. Moreover, by changing the visual process, the computational burden greatly decreases,
and the efficiency is improved without the use of parallel algorithms or GPU hardware acceleration.

The visualization process of current method
The visualization process for the current method20, 22, 42 is shown in Figure 2. After loading the 3D explosion Ąeld
data, the lighting coefficient and the transfer function of each medium are Ąrst set; then, based on the transfer
function, the material color and the opacity of each data point in the explosion Ąeld are calculated. Next, the
calculation results of the material color and normal vector are substituted into Equation 6 of the lighting model to
obtain the reĆected light color of each data point in the explosion Ąeld; Ąnally, the reĆected light color and opacity
of each resampling point are synthesized to the corresponding pixels on the projection screen by Equation 5.
In the visualization process shown in Figure 2, the color of the reĆected light of the resampling point is not
directly obtained by the lighting model; it is obtained by interpolation of the voxel-reĆected light color. The
calculation process is similar to the Gouraud shading calculation in surface rendering49 ; its disadvantages are also
similar: When the normal vector changes gently, it can maintain good 3D realism. However, when the normal vector
changes drastically, the voxel color also changes drastically, even if the higher-order interpolation cannot suppress
the Mach band phenomenon; this approach cannot accurately represent the specular reĆected light that should be at
the resampling point. Moreover, there is a large error in the real surface50 .
The purpose of using the ray casting method in the volume rendering of the explosive Ąeld is to improve the
rendering accuracy of the image as much as possible; however, the resampling point coloring obtained by the current
method has a large computational error and cannot adapt to the step variation characteristics of the explosion Ąeld.
Moreover, specular reĆection cannot be accurately represented, and the Mach band phenomenon is prone to appear.
This result contradicts the purpose of high-precision drawing. Therefore, the shading calculation of the resampling
points must be improved.
To improve the shading calculation of the resampling points, the most direct way is to apply the lighting model
to the resampling points, so the normal vector of the resampling points must be calculated.

By comparison, it can be seen that:
(1) In the current method, the voxel material color is obtained from the scalar value of the voxel through the
color transfer function. The color of the voxel obtained in this way is not weighted by opacity, and the color scalar
value obtained will be larger than the actual color value. At the interface of multi-materials, the more drastic the
color change, the higher the scalar value of the voxel and the higher the opacity, the greater the inĆuence. And this
effect will continue to the resampling point color interpolation calculation, so that the resampling point color is closer
to the average of different color scalar values, resulting in the "distortion" of the resampling point color calculation;
(2) In the new method, Equation 15 Ąrst calculates the material color of the voxel points in an opacity-weighted
way, so that the material color of the voxel points is close to the real value; then, it uses trilinear interpolation to
obtain the corrected color value and opacity of the resampling point material. This processing avoids the errors23
caused when color and opacity are interpolated separately, which can make the color of the resampled point closer
to the original color;
(3) The purpose of using the ray casting method in the volume rendering of the explosive Ąeld is to improve
the rendering accuracy of the image as much as possible. In the current method, Ąrst solve the normal vector
of the voxel, substitute it into the light model to solve the voxel color, and then get the resampling point color
by interpolation calculation. In this process, the shading calculation of the resampled points also undergoes the
interpolation calculation, which cannot accurately represent the specular reĆection and is prone to the Mach band
phenomenon, which is contrary to the purpose of high-precision plotting.
(4) In the new method, the normal vector of the resampled points is solved directly, and the lighting model is
applied directly to the resampled points, which avoids the calculation by interpolation to get the shading values of
resampled points, makes the light and dark effect of the spatial structure more obvious, and improves the accuracy
of the explosion Ąeld rendering.
(5) In the whole data Ąeld visualization process, both methods include three parts: Ştransfer function calculation",
Şlighting model calculation" and Şinterpolation calculation", and the lighting model calculation is the most resourceconsuming part of computing. The current method Ąrst performs a normalization calculation to obtain the normal
vector of the voxel point. Next, the lighting model is calculated for the voxel points; each voxel needs to be calculated
8 times of the lighting model. After 4 interpolation times, the material color and opacity of the resampling point are
determined. The new method obtains the normal vector of the resampling point after 3 interpolation operations and

Image quality evaluation indicators
To compare the difference in visualization effect between the new method and the current method, Ąve groups of
explosion Ąeld data were selected for volume rendering. The results were evaluated from subjective and objective
perspectives. The visual effects of human eyes are subjectively compared. In terms of objective evaluation indices,
the reference-free method, also known as the Ąrst evaluation method, is more widely used because it is difficult
to obtain an ideal image in general, so this quality evaluation method, which is completely detached from the
dependence on an ideal reference image, is more widely used. The reference-free method is generally based on image
statistical properties; this method usually contains four evaluation indices: image entropy H, meangradient G, image
mean U and standard deviation Std. The image entropy H reĆects the characteristics of the pixel position and gray
level information in the image and the gray level distribution in the pixel neighborhood, which is a representation of
the degree of Şchaos" of the image. The meangradient G reĆects the rate of contrast change of the tiny details of the
image, which refers to the rate of density change of the image in the multidimensional direction, and characterizes
the clarity of the microstructure of the image. The image mean U is the average of the image pixels, and the
standard deviation Std is the dispersion of the image pixel gray values with respect to the mean. Regarding U
and Std, a special note should be made here that if used to evaluate the richness of the content contained in the
image, the larger the two are, the better the image quality. However, the phenomena visualized in the explosion
Ąeld are Ąxed, and the researchersŠ aim is to make the phenomena such as shock waves and jets more visible and to
eliminate visual occlusion. Therefore, when evaluating the visualization effect in this paper, the smaller the two are,
the more it can satisfy the researchersŠ needs, i.e., the better the quality of the image. The process of quantifying

Conclusion
In this work, according to the characteristics of the explosion Ąeld, a more suitable visualization method is explored
by researchers. A volume rendering method for the explosion Ąeld based on color weighted interpolation and normal
vector interpolation is proposed. Subjectively, the visual effect of the human eye is used as a reference, and four
evaluation indicators, image mean U , standard deviation Std, image entropy H and meangradient G, are objectively
introduced to quantitatively analyze the visual image. The experimental results show that the new method corrects
the interface color confusion problem, improves the display accuracy of the microstructure in the explosion Ąeld
without affecting the rendering of translucent media such as shock waves and detonation products; At the same
time, by improving the visualization process, the computational burden is greatly reduced, and the efficiency is
improved without the use of parallel algorithms or GPU hardware acceleration. The new method is able to stably
volume render the data Ąeld of tens of millions of meshes at a lower usage of computer memory and graphics card,
demonstrating the potential for scalability to larger datasets, has a good practical applicability. It can help explosion
domain scientists to better understand and analyze explosion events and their effects.
The new method greatly improves the efficiency of volume rendering, but in the visualization of dynamic numerical
simulation, the data Ąeld at each moment usually needs to be visualized to show the simulation process in the form
of animation. The new method takes hundreds of seconds for the rendering time of a single Ąle when dealing with
data Ąelds of ten million grids, which still has a large room for improvement in the continuous body rendering of
multiple data Ąles, and the next step needs to make improvements on how to further improve the visualization
efficiency.

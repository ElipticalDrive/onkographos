Direct rendering of multiresolution decomposed volume data | Guide books
This thesis presents a general framework for rendering multiresolution decomposed volume data in which the properties of the multiresolution structure of 3D data are explored systematically. Instead of solving the rendering integral over the original volume data set, we first project it into a multiresolution representation using a wavelet transform or Laplacian pyramid.

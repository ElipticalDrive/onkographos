We present a system that combines voxel and polygonal representations into a single octree acceleration structure\nthat can be used for ray tracing. Voxels are well-suited to create good level-of-detail for high-frequency models\nwhere polygonal simplifications usually fail due to the complex structure of the model. However, polygonal\ndescriptions provide the higher visual fidelity. In addition, voxel representations often oversample the geometric\ndomain especially for large triangles, whereas a few polygons can be tested for intersection more quickly.\nWe show how to combine the advantages of both into a unified acceleration structure allowing for blending between\nthe different representations. A combination of both representations results in an acceleration structure that\ncompares well in performance in construction and traversal to current state-of-the art acceleration structures. The\nvoxelization and octree construction are performed entirely on the GPU. Since a single or two non-isolated triangles\ndo not generate severe aliasing in the geometric domain when they are projected to a single pixel, we can\nstop constructing the octree early for nodes that contain a maximum of two triangles, further saving construction\ntime and storage. In addition, intersecting two triangles is cheaper than traversing the octree deeper. We present\nthree different use-cases for our acceleration structure, from LoD for complex models to a view-direction based\napproach in front of a large display wall.

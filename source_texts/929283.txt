Summary
WebGPU terrain + texture layer-stack editor (no node graph): 01 Terrain Stack → 02 Texture Stack → 03 World & Sky.
Layer Stack and Inspector docked on the right; viewport is the dominant centre column.
Hydraulic erosion is now a Lagrangian droplet solver (inertia, sediment capacity, deposition, evaporation) that writes flow accumulation. Gullies and channels are visible in the viewport.
Geological detail is authored in metres: bedding ledges, joint fissures, round boulders on talus, rill texture.
Fragment normals use world-consistent slopes (vertical exaggeration aware).
Fixes: WGSL sky smoothstep error, removed dead compute stubs, page-grid overflow, clipped tabs, clipped inspector.
Verification
Loaded in headless Chromium with WebGPU (SwiftShader adapter). No shader compile errors; WebGPU init succeeds (no WebGL2 fallback).
Screenshots checked for erosion, boulders, strata, layout.
Known gaps (not yet Gaea-level)
Erosion runs on the 256² CPU mirror, upsampled to the 512² GPU mesh.
Cliffs and boulders are visible but still stylised; the peak shape is still spiky.
Texture stack realism is basic compared with Gaea.

Progress checkpoint (commit 99e63fb): the terrain core now runs on the GPU with texture-resident fields (r32float / rgba16float ping-pong) instead of full-grid storage buffers.
What's in:
Terrain layer stack on GPU: all 9 landform types plus modifiers.
Pipe-model hydraulic erosion, thermal talus and snowpack/flow fields on GPU.
Geomorphology and texture splat on GPU; viewport renders the textures directly.
Resolution selector offers 1024 / 2048 / 4096 (4K default), with fallback to a smaller grid if allocation fails.
The 256^2 CPU mirror is refreshed from a GPU box-downsample for the 2D map and picking.
Verified: headless Chromium at 1024^2 renders with no WGSL or pipeline errors.
Not yet done or verified:
2048^2 and 4096^2 runtime and VRAM (about 1.2 GB of fields at 4K). SwiftShader is too slow to test this here.
Erosion strength is too weak on the GPU path (about 175 m^3 eroded at 1024^2, versus about 4,500 m^3 on the CPU path). It needs tuning.
The strata layer produces voxel-like ledges across the whole massif.
Terrain realism, texture-stack realism, and toolbar clutter from the earlier list are still open.

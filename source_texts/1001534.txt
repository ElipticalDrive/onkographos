Step 3 of texture hardening: mips made on the GPU, sRGB textures that compute can write, and wider block-compression coverage.
Mip generation (engine/render/rhi/mips.h)
CommandList::blit_texture does filtered blits on graphics lists. FormatSupport::Blit says when a format allows them.
can_generate_mips checks the format (Blit and LinearFilter support) and the usage (CopySrc and CopyDst).
generate_mips builds every mip after source_mip from the one above it, barriers included, from any state to any state. Bad requests are refused and nothing is recorded.
record_mip_chain does the blits alone, for callers that manage states themselves.
Uploader: TextureUpload::generate_mips means "upload the top mips, the GPU fills the rest". Both queue paths support it. On the transfer queue, the uploaded mips are handed over still in CopyDst, and the blits run on the frame-start graphics list.
sRGB is filtered in linear space. Mip generation works for arrays, cubes, 3D textures and sizes that aren't powers of two.
Block-compressed and integer formats are refused: their mips come from the cooker. design.md now says so.
sRGB storage
GPUs don't write to sRGB storage images. An sRGB texture with Storage usage is now created with a mutable format and a format list. Its storage views use the UNORM twin (storage_format()), and its main view leaves storage out of its usage.
Storage-only textures no longer get an unused main view.
Tests (tests/render/mips_test.cpp; helpers moved to tests/support/readback.h)
Each generated texel is within 1 of the average of its 2x2 parents.
An sRGB black/white checker generates mid-gray as ~188, not 128.
A solid color stays solid through every mip of a cube, an array, a 3D texture, and a 7x5 texture.
generate_mips works from mip 1 between ShaderRead and CopySrc, then from mip 0. Re-uploading the top mip regenerates the rest. Bad requests are refused.
sRGB storage round trip: written raw through the UNORM view, sampled decoded.
Sampling BC3, BC5, sRGB BC1, and a 6x10 BC1 with 3 mips.
Mutation check: without the barrier between blits, 5 tests fail with 145 validation errors.
Locally:
msvc debug/dev/ship and clang-cl pass.
clang-tidy and clang-format are clean.
The smoke run has 0 validation messages.

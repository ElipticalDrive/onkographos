Multi-scale stream reduction for volume rendering on GPUs
3. We decompose marching cubes and volume raycasting into several data-parallel MSSR stages processing multi-scale stream, which can be mapped efficiently to the massively parallel architecture of modern GPUs, and the performance of them outperform state-of-the-art algorithms while achieving identical volume rendering result.

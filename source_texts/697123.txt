I understand your point about chunk_size.
But for me, chunk is more related to bytes, and the size of each chunk is different.
Obviously, using element and block compression makes it difficult to use of block_size, and renaming the compression types would solve the problem.
First, I will justify the current names:
Bccoo format divides a matrix in coo format into blocks, whose size is block_size.
In "element variant", the analysis of the applied compression is done element by element,
In "block variant", the compression applied is the least restrictive of those decided for all the elements of the block.
As a consequence, I consider that:
block_size should be kept, because it is a common parameter for all bccoo formats.
"element variant" could be kept or moved to "individual variant", since each nonzero is analysed separately.
"block variant" could be moved to "group variant", since the chosen compression is the least restrictive for a group of nonzeros.
About "displ" or "dspl", it tries to be a short for "displacement!

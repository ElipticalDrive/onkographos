Summary
Repro-only PR: a failing test that pins down camera pose writes not reaching the renderer under the Newton backend.
Details
Camera.set_world_poses writes through the sensor's FrameView. Under PhysX that view is Fabric-backed (FabricFrameView), so the RTX renderer — which reads the USD/Fabric camera prim — follows the write. Under Newton the view is a NewtonSiteFrameView, which updates only in-memory Warp state; nothing mirrors the pose onto the camera prim, so the rendered image stays at the old pose.
What the test does
source/isaaclab/test/sensors/test_camera_pose_write_across_backends.py moves a downward-looking camera from 2 m to 8 m over a ground plane via the public set_world_poses, parametrized over PhysxCfg() and NewtonCfg(solver_cfg=MJWarpSolverCfg()), and checks the two observable consequences of the write:
test_camera_pose_write_moves_reported_pose — camera.data.pos_w follows the write. Passes on both backends.
test_camera_pose_write_moves_render — the rendered distance_to_camera must grow when the camera rises 4x.
Results
```
test_camera_pose_write_moves_reported_pose[physx]  PASSED
test_camera_pose_write_moves_reported_pose[newton] PASSED
test_camera_pose_write_moves_render[physx]         PASSED
test_camera_pose_write_moves_render[newton]        FAILED
  Far depth (2.08 m) should be > 1.5x close depth (2.08 m); got 1.00x.
```
Run
```
uv run python -m pytest source/isaaclab/test/sensors/test_camera_pose_write_across_backends.py -v -s
```

:meth:`Camera.set_world_poses` writes through the sensor's :class:`FrameView`. Under PhysX that view is
Fabric-backed (:class:`FabricFrameView`), so the RTX renderer -- which reads the USD/Fabric camera prim --
follows the write. Under Newton the view is a :class:`NewtonSiteFrameView`, which updates only in-memory
Warp state; nothing mirrors the pose back to the camera prim, so the rendered image keeps the old pose
even though ``camera.data.pos_w`` reports the new one.
Both tests move a downward-looking camera straight up over a ground plane, which multiplies the distance
to every visible surface, and check the two observable consequences of the write:
- ``_moves_reported_pose_*`` reads ``camera.data.pos_w`` (deterministic, no renderer involved).
- ``_moves_render_*`` compares the rendered depth before and after the move.
:func:`test_camera_pose_update_reflected_in_render` in ``test_camera.py`` covers the render half of this on
the default (PhysX) backend only; these tests parametrize the backend so the Newton regression is visible.

860a473
```
Add three coloured marker cubes: one directly under the camera that shrinks as it
rises, and two that sit outside the frame at 2 m and only come into view at 8 m.
The PhysX frames go from one large cube to three small ones; the Newton frames
stay on the single large cube, which reads at a glance.

The markers are plain USD gprims at /World rather than scene entities because
geometry placed under /World/envs/env_* by InteractiveScene does not reach the
RTX renderer in a bare build_simulation_context -- the same prim spawned at
/World renders. The scene cube is therefore invisible and only exists to give
Newton a body to build its model from.

Signed-off-by: Alex Millane <amillane@nvidia.com>
```

The essential of direct volume-rendering technique are re-sample and image synthesize. Ray casting were stared out at every pixel on screen along viewpoint direction, and then some sample points were selected on the line. The RGB of the pixel were obtained to calculate opacity and RGB of every sample points. The footprint function was used by splatting to compute the effect range of every pixel, and then define intensity distributing by Gaussian function. At last, obtain ultimate image by synthesizing every pixel’s contributions.

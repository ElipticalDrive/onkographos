Goal
Implement deterministic mip, tile, border and page extraction without leaking renderer-, asset-, world-streaming- or filesystem-native policy across subsystem boundaries.
Scope
Generate byte-stable mip chains, tiles and sampling borders from identical inputs.
Handle normal maps, color space and edge addressing explicitly.
Bound memory, parallel work and output cardinality for oversized sources.
Acceptance criteria
The responsibility is exposed through one typed contract with explicit ownership and lifetime.
Invalid, stale, over-capacity and unsupported inputs return actionable results without partial hidden state.
Frame-hot work is bounded and avoids blocking I/O, unbounded allocation and accidental CPU/GPU synchronization.
Focused tests cover normal, boundary, failure, replacement and shutdown behavior where applicable.

Summary
UE 5.8 ships a user-facing path tracer for reference and final-quality renders. PP turns the D16 reference tracer, which until now has only been used for validation, into that product. It is progressive and denoised, covers every light type and material feature, and works on Vulkan, D3D12 and Metal (Apple9+, experimental as in UE).
The phase ends at code-complete: every build task is closed under the PR basics, with no gate sign-off. Gate GP (#170) has moved to #224 and runs in PQ (§22). M6 is a code-complete checkpoint for this scope, not a release.
Plan reference
§21.0.6 Product path tracer, D16, gate GP (runs in PQ, §22).

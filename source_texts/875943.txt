Parent: #514 (EX-03)
Spec: v3 §27 (EX-03 "Quest variant generation (decimation + atlas)", EX-13), §10
Protocol: docs/agents/implementer.md
Context: a Quest avatar reaches rank Good at 10,000 triangles (Excellent at 7,500, data/vrchat-performance.toml), and other platforms cap triangles too. Our characters carry 52 morph targets and skin weights, so a general simplifier that ignores them breaks the face. Write a quadric edge-collapse simplifier (Garland and Heckbert) with the morph target positions and the UVs as extra attributes in the error. Do not add a dependency: a PR comment may compare against meshopt's simplifier on the same fixture (MIT, would need a register row) so the Director can decide later.
Fence:
crates/omuzaki-io-ops/src/fit_decimate.rs (new; one mod line in src/lib.rs), crates/omuzaki-io-ops/tests/fit_decimate.rs (new)
crates/omuzaki-io-ops/benches/fit.rs (new, criterion) and in crates/omuzaki-io-ops/Cargo.toml the criterion dev-dependency and the [[bench]] entry (both already in the register)
Contract:
```
pub struct DecimateOptions {
    pub target_triangles: usize,
    /// Stop early when the best collapse would exceed this, as a fraction of the primitive's bounding-box diagonal.
    pub max_error: f32,
    pub lock_boundary: bool,   // vertices on open edges never move or merge
    pub lock_uv_seams: bool,   // vertices where UVs split never move or merge
    /// How strongly morph target deltas count in the error (1.0: as much as the base shape).
    pub morph_weight: f32,
}
pub struct DecimateReport { pub triangles: (usize, usize), pub vertices: (usize, usize), pub max_error: f32, pub reached_target: bool }
pub fn decimate(primitive: &mut Primitive, options: &DecimateOptions) -> Result<DecimateReport, IoError>;
/// Splits `total` across the scene's primitives in proportion to their surface area (at least 100 each), then decimates.
pub fn decimate_scene(scene: &mut IoScene, total: usize, options: &DecimateOptions) -> Result<Vec<DecimateReport>, IoError>;
```
Do:
A collapse keeps the surviving vertex's joints and weights (the nearer end, or the weighted blend when the ends differ, renormalised to four influences summing to 1), every morph target's delta at the new position, UVs and colours interpolated, normals recomputed from the result. No collapse may flip a neighbouring triangle's normal by more than 90 degrees or create a non-manifold edge.
All vertex arrays, indices and every morph target keep the same vertex count and order relationship, so expression MorphBinds and node references are untouched. Nodes, skins and materials are never changed.
Deterministic: a priority queue ordered by (cost, vertex ids), no hash-order dependence, no threads that change the result.
decimate_scene calls IoScene::check on the result and returns its error unchanged if it fails.
Acceptance: fixtures are built in the test (UV sphere, grid, cube, a two-joint skinned sphere, three sparse morph targets); never a third-party model.
A 32,000-triangle sphere of radius 0.1 m to 8,000: within 8,000 plus or minus 2 triangles, reached_target, every output vertex within 1% of the bounding-box diagonal of the original surface (sampled), no triangle normal flipped.
Skinned sphere: every output weight set sums to 1 within 1e-5, at most four influences, every joint index valid. Morph targets: each target's count is unchanged and, applied at weight 1, the decimated surface is within 2% of the diagonal of the original with the target applied.
A grid with lock_boundary: the boundary vertices are the same set before and after. A cube with split UVs (24 vertices) and lock_uv_seams: no UV leaves the original range by more than 1e-6 and no triangle's UV area changes sign.
Already at or under the target: unchanged, reached_target true. Two runs give byte-identical primitives.
decimate_scene with meshes of 30,000 and 10,000 triangles and total 10,000: each primitive's share follows its area, none under 100, total within 1%; IoScene::check is clean before and after.
Bench: 100,000 to 20,000 triangles with 52 sparse morph targets in at most 5 s, peak memory at most 400 MB, both reported on the cloud box (numbers in the PR).
tools/ci/pr-check.sh origin/main green.

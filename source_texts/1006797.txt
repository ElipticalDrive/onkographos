Problem
Argus_BoxClear (src/argus.qc:8291) samples the box corners at 15 units from the origin. The player hull is 16 units. A spot whose hull clips a wall by up to one unit passes, and every engine move from it then starts in solid.

Evidence
Both spots passed the samples and failed the engine:
dm3 335.1 125.9 -200, in runs/body_nudge_v2_nudge747_control_played.log: BNCASE wall_refused initialfit 1 finalfit 0 distance 3.
dm4 12.8 -272.9 -72, in runs/dm4_pin_v1_pin723_cover_control_played_dm4.log. The wall face is at y -288.0 and the hull reaches y -288.9.

Fix it one layer up: sample the real hull corners, so every caller gets the same answer.

The maintained tactic and recovery fixtures still pass at played and 100 ms cadence, and a co-op catchup still finds a spot beside the human.
If tactic eligibility moves, compare it against matched controls before adoption.

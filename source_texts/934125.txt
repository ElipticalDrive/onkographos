What changes
Before: each radiance layer of a sky was a separate cube, and every reflection read its layer at mip 0. A small, strongly curved mirror read the sharp sky that only a large mirror shows in Godot.
After: a sky bakes all of its radiance layers into one octmap array with a mip chain per layer, laid out as Godot's Forward+ lays it out (sky.cpp:1545-1576). The reflection, ambient and clearcoat reads pick their mip from the screen derivatives of their direction with vec3_to_oct_lod (oct_inc.glsl:43-51, scene_forward_clustered.glsl:1714-1721, :1744-1749, :1768-1795).
How:
skyOctmapLayout takes the layer count and also returns radiance_pixel_size (renderer_scene_render_rd.cpp:1395-1396).
bakeSkyLayers draws each layer's mip 0, downsamples it into its own mips, and copies each mip into the array. A bake draws into one scratch octmap per width. The sky and the reflection atlas build their array through one function.
Every lit program samples one sampler2DArray and reads the layout from one vec4. Each lit program uses three fewer texture units, so directionalShadow.md has a new unit table.
The array magnifies linearly. A DataArrayTexture defaults to nearest magnification (three r186 DataArrayTexture.js:47), so the reflection probe atlas magnified by its nearest texel before this change too.
The GPU gate allows each mip only the rounding steps of the passes that wrote it.
ADR-0025 and the Sky sheet describe the array and the derivative mip.
How you checked it
Wrote the tests first: the array shape, the copies per layer and mip, the filters, the shader text of the reads, and a real-time sky's seven layers. A regression test covers a fast-filter bake whose first chain mip is as wide as the octmap.
Ran pnpm test:unit (31468 tests), type-check, lint and pnpm test:gpu.
Value check with pnpm ref:godot: 14 mirror and rough spheres at radiance 32 and 256, before and after. These are signed means (ours − Godot, /255) in the screen-radius rings of the sphere:
small mirror at 256, ring 0.3-0.6: −0.21 → −0.19
mirror at radiance 32: ring 0.3-0.6 −0.33 → −0.23, ring 0.6-0.8 −0.55 → −0.33
large spheres: unchanged, within 0.2
roughness 0.3: unchanged, about −0.3 at 256 and −0.5 at 32. This gap is in the content of the filtered layers, not in the read.
Goldens: 129 of 353 moved, because every lit 3D scene reads the preview sky. I rendered the 122 fixtures behind them through Godot and through ours, on main and on this branch, and compared the mean |ours − Godot|:
67 closer, for example sky-energy 0.859 → 0.822, directional-light-limit 1.477 → 1.441, light-transport-sky-graded 0.321 → 0.294, sky-realtime 0.667 → 0.649
43 level, within 0.0005/255
12 further, all by at most 0.34/255 in the worst 64-pixel tile. The largest is plane-rotated-scaled, 1.373 → 1.410. In each case flat matte surfaces round up by one step slightly more often. In light-transport-sky, Godot itself splits the ground between 131 and 132.
The simplify edits change no pixel: four goldens re-captured identically, including both reflection probe scenes.

## What changes

`pnpm ref:diff` showed a faint ring (about 6%) just outside every hard
top and left edge of our render, for example 174 pixels above the cut of
a scissor quad. Godot draws none. The ring is not a renderer bug: a
plain opaque quad with no texture shows it, and the drawing buffer
(`toDataURL`, `readPixels`) is hard-edged. Chromium's default software
compositor adds the ring to a screenshot.

Every capture that measures against Godot (`ref:ours`, `ref:diff` and
the four `compare-docs` capture sides) now opens its preview session
with `launchBrowser: launchGodotComparisonBrowser`. That launch
composites in the GPU process (`--use-angle=swiftshader`).
`withPreviewBrowser` keeps the default launch for every other caller, so
the visual harness is unchanged and no baseline moves. No golden can
show a capture-tool change, so none is added.

The committed comparison images of our side are recaptured with `pnpm
recapture --ours`, so they no longer carry the ring. Of the 150 images,
78 changed.

Measured with `pnpm ref:diff` (mean error per channel, /255):

| Scene | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| unit-alpha-antialiasing-one-sample | 0.383 | 0.378 |
| unit-csg-box | 1.093 | 1.097 |
| unit-material-ao | 1.177 | 1.120 |
| unit-light-transport-direct | 0.099 | 0.093 |
| unit-material-anisotropy | 3.257 | 3.255 |

On the alpha antialiasing scene, the left quad has 1 differing pixel
(more than 2/255) instead of 174.

Mean error of the 78 recaptured images against their Godot image: 71
move closer, 3 stay level, 4 move further by 0.032 or less (unit-label3d
3.635 to 3.667, unit-fog-volume 3.105 to 3.130, unit-material-emissive
3.276 to 3.298, unit-world-environment-basic 0.157 to 0.166). The
largest gains are unit-torus-mesh (3.045 to 2.798) and
complex-2d-platformer (3.165 to 2.961).

The unit-csg-box rise of 0.004 is noise. Between the two launches, 13356
pixels of that scene differ by 1/255 from the rounding of the two ANGLE
backends, and 958 differ by more, along the ring.

After main (#810, #811) merged, all 150 images were recaptured again. 60
changed: 32 move closer, 22 stay level, 6 move further by 0.004 or less
(unit-csg-boolean-ops 1.326 to 1.330 is the largest).
unit-tonemap-agx-shadow moves from 3.579 to 0.046, because the committed
image predated the merged changes. After #814 merged, three sample
images (unit-csg-box, unit-material-ao, unit-sky-procedural) recaptured
byte for byte. After #817 merged, the three fog images that changed
(unit-fog-volume, unit-volumetric-fog-shadow,
unit-world-environment-basic) moved by 0.003 or less, which is level.

## How you checked it

- `scripts/showcase/browser.test.mjs` failed first (captures built their
own launch), then passed.
- `eslint` and `prettier --check` on the changed scripts.
- `pnpm ref:diff` on the five scenes above, and `pnpm recapture --ours`
over every comparison image.

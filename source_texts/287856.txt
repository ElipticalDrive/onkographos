Step 4 of the terrain roadmap:
add one bounded 24×16×24 GPU-resident voxel chunk beside the heightfield
generate procedural solid density entirely in a WebGPU compute pass
compact solid voxel positions into a storage buffer with an atomic counter
write indexed indirect draw arguments on the GPU
render the compacted cubes through one indirect instanced draw
keep zero GPU-to-CPU geometry or count readbacks
disable the prototype safely on the WebGL fallback
add an editor show/hide toggle and configuration limits
preserve heightfield sculpting, object placement, saves, and GLB rendering
This is deliberately a voxel-density and indirect-rendering proof. Marching cubes, editable voxel stamps, multi-chunk streaming, and heightfield handoff are not included yet.

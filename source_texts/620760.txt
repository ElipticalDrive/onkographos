Problem
coord2idx() in PointCloudToVoxelGrid, PointCloudToVoxelGridSingle and PointCloudToVoxelGridAverage cast x / resolution to int32_t, which rounds toward zero. The cells on both sides of each axis plane, [-res, 0) and [0, res), both got index 0, so the voxel straddling each plane was twice as wide (eight times the volume at the origin).
Every filter built on these grids (FilterDecimateVoxels, FilterDecimateAdaptive, FilterVoxelSOR, FilterEdgesPlanes) therefore downsampled the slabs through x=0, y=0 and z=0, which in the sensor frame is right around the robot, twice as coarsely as everywhere else. FilterPoleDetector had the same problem in its XY cell index.
Fix
std::floor, so voxel i spans [i*res, (i+1)*res) for any sign. Division by the resolution is kept, as agents.md requires; the rounding rule is added to that invariant.
Tests
New test-mp2p_voxel_grid_indexing: on the three grid classes, points every 0.2 m over [-5, 5) must fall into 10 unit voxels (9 on develop), two points at ±0.4 into 2 voxels (1 on develop), plus direct coord2idx() checks for negative inputs.
test-mp2p_FilterPoleDetector: new scenario with a pole in a negative cell. Rounding toward zero pulled 9 ground points into its cell instead of 4.
Both fail on develop and pass with the fix. Full mp2p_icp_core suite: 156/156 pass.
Behavior change
Grid filter outputs change wherever points have coordinates in (-res, 0) along some axis: slightly more points survive decimation near the axis planes, as anywhere else.
The same bug in mola's voxel maps (HashedVoxelPointCloud, NDT, SparseTreesPointCloud, HashedSetSE3) is fixed in a companion PR to MOLAorg/mola.
Summary by CodeRabbit
Bug Fixes
Corrected voxel-grid indexing for negative coordinates. Points on opposite sides of an axis plane are now assigned to separate voxels, preventing them from being incorrectly grouped together. This also improves pole detection for features located in negative-coordinate regions.

Why. A velocity field (backend/app/velocity_field.py) is loaded whole: fine for reconstructions such as CF4 at 64³, not for fields from large simulations. Arrows and streamlines also do not need every vector at every zoom.
Scope
A pyramid of vector fields, built out of core like the cube pyramid (Brick pyramid for cubes, built out of core #69): each level the mean of the three components over 2×2×2 blocks, with per-block magnitude ranges.
Arrows (glyphs) and streamlines drawn from the level that suits the view, refined where the view zooms in.
Basins and other derived products computed on the level that the requested resolution needs.
The same files with the local backend and with a cluster backend; server-side rendering of the field on the cluster when it does not fit the desktop.
Done when
A velocity field several times larger than RAM can be explored interactively (arrows, streamlines), on a local and on a remote backend.
Results on a small field are the same as today.
Notes. Part of #73.

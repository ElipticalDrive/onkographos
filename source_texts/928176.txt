Summary
Place buildings/structures in the world, stamped into chunks like trees.
Approach
Reuse the deterministic placement + cross-chunk pattern from the tree system (src/vegetation/trees/placement.rs): decide structures from a hash of position+seed, stamp voxel templates, and let each chunk compute the part of any structure overlapping it (so structures are whole across chunk borders, no neighbor writes).
Scope
Building voxel templates (start with one simple building).
Deterministic placement on suitable terrain (flat enough, right biome).
Later: clusters → villages/cities; variety.
Acceptance criteria
Buildings appear deterministically on suitable ground.
Whole across chunk borders; reproducible on revisit.
Relevant code
New structures module, src/voxel/dynamic_chunks.rs, patterns from src/vegetation/trees/.

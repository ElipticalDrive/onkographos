PDF Second Order Pre-Integrated Volume Rendering
The concept of pre-integrated volume rendering emanates from the early works on tetrahedral meshes [10,21]. Roettger et al [18] extended pre-integration to account for arbitrary transfer functions in the context of the PT (Projected Tetrahedra) algorithm.

Add the eighteenth experiment, Iridescent Strata, inspired by Cristian Peñas’s floating-terrain video. A seeded 3D density field produces layered rock shelves, perforations and detached islands; the scene combines physical iridescence, procedural mineral color, independent environment lighting and a moving world for fly-through parallax.
Terrain is extracted with conforming Marching Tetrahedra in an embedded Blob Worker, keeping 15 chunks resident and supporting offline file opening. Regeneration commits atomically, stale tasks are cancelled, and workers and environment resources are released on switching. Portrait terrain framing now preserves the viewing distance. Three presets, five controls, seed regeneration, two camera views, bilingual model notes and actual screenshot examples are included. The author’s original algorithm is not claimed or redistributed.
Validation:
112 math/state tests pass, including chunk seams, deterministic geometry, worker protocol and scene lifecycle.
All 18 scenes load offline; 54 desktop and 54 touch-emulated preset activations pass with no runtime errors or network requests.
Terrain passes parameter bounds, reproducible seed/reset PNG hashes, 72.3 m travel, 20 switches, cancellation, worker fallback and portrait/landscape camera-reset checks.
Eighteen 1280×800 JPEGs total 1.82 MiB. The bundle, screenshot manifest and three final QA reports share SHA-256 a245862eb225b082fda822e8c70fcff5f2c8bbd8db21b64c475eb2ad7be9c151.
On Apple M3 Pro, headless Chrome at 1280×800/DPR 1 sampled 60 FPS after 5.348 seconds of real-clock playback. This is a short desktop measurement; mobile checks are touch emulation. Fine holes depend on grid resolution, manual cameras can leave the streamed window, and this is an artistic procedural model rather than a geological simulation.

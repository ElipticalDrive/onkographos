No Linear ticket: the workspace has reached its free-plan issue limit.
What this is
Research and levers for the game's aliasing in VR. It explains why a lower FOV looks much clearer than a higher resolution, and adds switchable anti-aliasing and clarity options. Every option ships off and toggles live in F10 Advanced > Display > Clarity and anti-aliasing.
What changed
Change
What
core/gfx/clarity*
Resolve (supersampled render filtered to the runtime's recommended size), temporal AA (rotation-reprojected per-eye history, YCoCg clip), CAS-style sharpening; wired into reentry before the hands and F10 panel
core/gfx/sampler_force* + SetSamplerState hook
Anisotropy degree and trilinear mips on samplers the game already makes anisotropic
Instruments
xr: eye L/R fov (per-eye use of the symmetric render), device/census: multisampled surfaces, clarity: and samplers (10 s) lines

Tests

Docs
PERFORMANCE.md "Anti-aliasing and clarity", STATUS test handoff for this and PRs #133, #134, #135
Evidence
Centre density is (width/2)/tan(hfov/2): 17.4 px/deg at 108 deg vs 31.3 at 75 deg for 2750 px; the Quest 3 panel is about 25. Matching 75 deg at 108 deg needs 1.79x per axis (3.2x pixels), not 1.44x.
Host GPU (RTX 4070 Ti SUPER): resolve pattern sd 0.004 vs 0.289 for one bilinear tap per pixel (the control); crawl 0.006 vs 0.247; temporal edge error 0.219 -> 0.095; still view exact; scene cut not smeared.
Not run: the game, the simulator, the headset.

Blast radius and fail-soft
All off = the previous plain blit and untouched sampler states. A shader failure logs once and the plain copy runs. The resolve changes the swapchain size (a rebuild on toggle). The SetSamplerState hook passes through with levers off.
What is deliberately not here
Off-axis per-eye frustum (sized by the new log line first), the game's own MSAA trial, quad views (CPU floor), the 32-bit address space issue with HD texture packs. See PERFORMANCE.md.

Adds a clarity stage between the captured frame and the swapchain, and
texture-filter levers on the game's D3D9 samplers. Everything ships off
with live F10 toggles and seam words.

- Resolve: above ~100% resolution, filter the render down to the
  runtime's recommended size with a step-scaled Mitchell kernel in
  linear light, instead of leaving the compositor one bilinear tap per
  pixel. Host: pattern sd 0.004 vs 0.289 for the bilinear control.

  between the two rendered cameras, clipped to the current YCoCg
  neighbourhood. Host: edge coverage error 0.219 -> 0.095.
- Sharpen: contrast-adaptive sharpening.
- Anisotropy and trilinear mips on samplers the game makes anisotropic.
- Logs: per-eye headset FOV use, the game's MSAA census, clarity and
  sampler status lines.

PERFORMANCE.md records why a lower FOV is clearer (tangent density),
why linear resolution scaling falls short, the ranked options not built
and the 32-bit address space ceiling. STATUS carries the test handoff
for four open branches.

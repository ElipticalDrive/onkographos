Closes #205
Rebased onto #204 (#264 and #266, main 80227bc), as agreed with its session: shadowed() traces the static TLAS, then the dynamic TLAS where the static one finds nothing, so every ray here queries both.
What
Every point light that reaches a pixel traces one hard shadow ray there, inline in the lit pass, through #203's shadowed(). The ray starts at the centroid-interpolated position, offset along the normal, and ends at the light itself, so whatever lies beyond a light shadows nothing it lights. A ray is skipped only where the light adds nothing: where the surface faces away from it, or lies beyond its range. Nothing caps the rays per pixel or per Froxel (decision item 4 on #17). Brute force and the Froxel lists trace the same rays, and the shadow_rays Render switch turns them off along with the directional lights'. ADRs 0019 and 0020 now say what is implemented.
Routine calls, with their reasons:
The trace conditions are tested at the ray's start. That is the centroid position; the shading takes the light's offset from the pixel's centre. N·L > 0 at the start guarantees a non-zero offset, so normalize never sees a zero vector. At MSAA edge pixels the two positions can disagree only where the light adds almost nothing: at the edge of its range, or where the surface turns from it.
A Renderer test covers the ray's end, outside the ticket's seams. No Reference scene puts geometry beyond a light, so tests/shadows.rs also passes with unbounded rays.
uv-grid's Goldens stay. Its capture moves 3 pixels by one step against main's. That is codegen rounding on a plane with nothing to cast a shadow (two of the three pixels brighten), within the Goldens' tolerance, as Trace the sun's shadow rays; add the shadows Reference scene #258 and Trace shadow rays against a dynamic TLAS of Moving Instances #264 kept it.

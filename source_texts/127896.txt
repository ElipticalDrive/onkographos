When FixedRay3.direction is not already unit length, this solver reports slab parameters rather than the documented hit distances; for example an AABB spanning z=4..6 with direction (0,0,2) returns 2/3 instead of 4/6. A zero direction from inside the box also takes every parallel-inside branch and falls through as Some((i64::MIN, i64::MAX)). Since FixedRay3 does not require normalized/nonzero directions and the sphere solver normalizes internally, this path should normalize or reject the direction before computing the AABB distances.

Normalize triangle ray directions before solving
When the ray direction has a very small nonzero magnitude, the Möller–Trumbore parameter scales beyond the fixed-point range even though the hit coordinate is representable. For example, a ray from (0,0,0) with direction (0,0,FixedQ32_32::from_raw(1)) against the test triangle at z=1 should hit (0,0,1), but this path saturates t and returns a point at z=0.5. Since FixedRay3 does not enforce unit directions, normalize or reject non-unit directions before using the raw direction in the determinant.

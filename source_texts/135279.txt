Description:
Mesh Voxelizer is a tool to voxelize your models inside Unity.
Features:
- Generate voxelization result as single mesh or separate voxel GameObjects
- Convert mesh’s UVs
- Convert mesh’s bone weights (if there are bone weights)
- Support multi-materials mesh
- Support backface culling
- Support voxelization result optimization
- Support filling model center space with voxels
- Support different voxel meshes
- Save voxelized result as prefab
- Full source code provided

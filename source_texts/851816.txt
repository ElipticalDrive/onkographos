Problems based on toroidal geometry are frequently modeled by a small fraction of the full 360 degrees, with reflecting boundaries. Deterministic solutions as part of automated VR typically require at least a full octant to be occupied, if not a full 360 degrees. Replicating geometry is memory intensive and probably unnecessary.
It should be possible to extend discretize_geom to track cell boundaries in a reflecting geometry, but report them as if they are all along a single row of voxels. The actual ray will reflect off the boundaries, but the bookkeeping for voxel composition will continue in direction colinear with the original ray.

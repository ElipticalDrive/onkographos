Currently there are following issues regarding mesh export:
There are no options to customise mesh export. For now only a fixed volume around current camera position is exported. Potential customisation could allow specifying AABB of exported volume.
Exported terrain mesh contains lots of triangles and vertices. Some of these triangles are tiny and don't contribute much visually. This is to be expected from marching cubes based mesher. Potentially there could be a pass that optimises the exported mesh.
- Exported terrain may contain tiny holes. Hopefully in the future godot voxel module will provide an option to prevent terrain from generating these holes. EDIT: Solved. Upcoming release won't have this issue.

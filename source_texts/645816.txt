Problem or limitation
Often times, lights will be placed inside of a room with no possible way to cast shadows outside, but due to the nature of the light radius and culling from occluders, the shadows are calculated from outside anyway.
Proposed improvement
Being able to specify a custom bounding box that resides inside of the room geometry would allow the light to be constrained within the room and blocked by occluders, thus not calculated when outside of the room.
Proposal review
Yes, this proposal is highly specific and actionable.

11.2.3 Executing the Render
Apart from some general error-checking code, the most important part
of Renderer::execute() is a loop over each pixel in the image. In each
loop step, we start by initializing the luminance and alpha values that
will be the result of the render. We also create a list of deep transmittance
and luminance functions (the tf and lf variables). Because the renderer
is able to ﬁre multiple rays per pixel, the ﬁnal deep transmittance and
luminance functions will be computed as an average of each subpixel’s
function.

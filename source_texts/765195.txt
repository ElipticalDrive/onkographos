This change addresses cloud shadow recording and top-down cloud illumination:
Updated CloudShadowGenNode.hpp and shaders/cloud_shadow_gen.comp to look up actual terrain elevation using TerrainClipmapTexture via sampleTerrainClipmap. Raymarching stops at the terrain surface rather than assuming 0m ground elevation.
Updated shaders/cloud_shadow_gen.comp to start recording distance traveled and optical depth accumulation only once cloud density exceeds the kCloudThreshold (0.0001), ensuring average extinction and optical depth are properly weighed over the actual distance traveled through clouds.
Updated shaders/cloud_render.frag to compute ground upwelling reflected from terrain and diffuse top backscattering for rays viewed from above (cosTheta < 0), lighting cloud tops naturally when viewed from above.

# TorchMobileNeRF

**A PyTorch adaptation of MobileNeRF for consumer-GPU experimentation, with a custom mesh extraction and neural feature-baking pipeline for real-time browser rendering.**

TorchMobileNeRF began as a solo R&D project I worked on during my **Machine Learning Engineering internship at Trellis Data**. The project was developed under supervision and tested during the internship, with the goal of exploring client side rendering by understanding MobileNeRF in depth and adapting its core ideas to hardware I could realistically experiment with.

The original MobileNeRF implementation is written in JAX and was designed around a much larger multi-GPU training setup. I rebuilt its continuous training stage in PyTorch and adapted the workflow to run meaningful experiments on an **NVIDIA RTX 3060 Laptop GPU with 6 GB VRAM**.

The project is different to a direct framework port. I built an alternative post-training pipeline that converts the learned neural representation into explicit 3D assets:

```text
Images
  ↓
Colmap extraction of coordinates + Images
  ↓
PyTorch MobileNeRF-style training
  ↓
Learned density + feature fields
  ↓
Marching Cubes mesh extraction
  ↓
xatlas UV unwrapping
  ↓
Neural feature atlas baking
  ↓
OBJ + textures + colour MLP
  ↓
Three.js / GLSL real-time rendering
```

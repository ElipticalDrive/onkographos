Thank you for your interest. If you do not have such problems with r2-gaussian, it should not be the rasterization or voxelization process since I did not change any of that code. It is an interesting finding. I go back to the SPARE paper (SPARE: Sparse-view reconstruction challenge for 4D cone-beam CT from a 1-min scan), and noticed "optimal linear scaling" were used to calculate RMSE since quote "Clinically, CBCT is mostly used for setup alignment, and is rarely used for tasks such as planning or dose reconstruction that rely on accurate Hounsfield unit (HU) values. The image quality of CBCT after appropriate window level adjustment thus matters much more than its HU accuracy" It makes me think whether this issue was caused by the MC simulation itself.

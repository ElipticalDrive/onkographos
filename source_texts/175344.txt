Extreme performance Compute Driven Rendering Overview : Vkguide - Reddit
It uses a GPU DrawIndirect driven pipeline, where the entire scene is uploaded to the GPU, and the GPU is responsible of doing culling and other calculations while the CPU sits there doing almost nothing. The tutorial engine renders 125.000 objects, in both forward view and cascaded shadow, at 300 fps on a RTX 2080.

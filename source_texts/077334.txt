## Volume Rendering model

- continuous volumetric scalar field
- front-to-back composition
- opacity, color contributions

$$\begin{aligned}\alpha_i &= \alpha_{i-1} + \alpha(\mathbf{P}_i) \cdot (1 - \alpha_{i-1}) \\ c_i &= c_{i-1} + c(\mathbf{P}_i) \cdot \alpha(\mathbf{P}_i) \cdot (1 - \alpha_{i-1})\end{aligned}$$



## VR model - shading

- Shading Phong-Blinn model

$$\begin{aligned}\alpha(\mathbf{P}_i) &= \alpha_{tf}(f_{\mathbf{P}_i}) \\ c(\mathbf{P}_i) &= c_{tf}(f_{\mathbf{P}_i}) \cdot s(\mathbf{P}_i)\end{aligned}$$

$$s(\mathbf{P}_i) = c_d \cdot \|\hat{\mathbf{L}} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{g}}_{\mathbf{P}_i}\| + c_s \cdot (\|\hat{\mathbf{H}} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{g}}_{\mathbf{P}_i}\|)^{c_e} + c_a$$

- L normalized light vector
- H normalized half-way vector •gPi – normalized gradient
- diffuse, specular, ambient coefficients
- specular exponent



## VR model – gradient modulation

- Gradient magnitude opacity modulation

$$\alpha(\mathbf{P}_i) = \alpha_{tf}(f_{\mathbf{P}_i}) \cdot \|\mathbf{g}_{\mathbf{P}_i}\|_{[0,1]}$$

- grad. mag. normalized to <0,1>
- boundaries enhancement



## VR model – clipping plane

- View-aligned clipping plane

$$\alpha(\mathbf{P}_i) = \begin{cases} 0 & (\mathbf{P}_i - \mathbf{E}) \cdot \hat{\mathbf{V}} < d \\ \alpha_{tf}(f_{\mathbf{P}_i}) & \text{otherwise,} \end{cases}$$

- E eye point
- V normalized viewing direction
- d depth of the clipping plane



#### The Context-Preserving VR model

- Combination various VR models

$$\alpha(\mathbf{P}_i) = \alpha_{tf}(f_{\mathbf{P}_i}) \cdot m(\mathbf{P}_i)$$

- User defined parameters kt , ks •kt
- depth of clipping plane •ks
- sharpness (clipped / visible regions) •kt ks
- data dependent, TF •kt ks
  - set to 0 → DVR / Grad. Modulation

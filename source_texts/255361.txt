Goal
Replace straight-line Beer–Lambert photon escape with an optional Monte Carlo photon transport (photoelectric with fluorescence re-emission, Compton, Rayleigh, pair production above threshold). Today scattered photons are simply removed: no build-up, redirection, or re-emission, and no secondary fluorescence. That is acceptable for keV PXR lines in thin targets. It is wrong for MeV bremsstrahlung in thick targets or filters, for the 511 keV annihilation photons from the positron work (#276, #295), and for the README's general-purpose 1 keV–100 MeV goal.
Scope
Physics docs: docs/physics/radiation-physics/photon-escape-and-dispersion.md; characteristic-radiation "Secondary fluorescence" limitation.
Data: EPDL already pinned (migrate photon transport from xraydb to EPDL/EPICS2023 #274); EADL relaxation already present (Implement a full atomic-relaxation cascade #91) for fluorescence.
Default stays straight-line escape; the photon MC is opt-in until validated.
Plan
Design note / ADR: how incoherent photons (brem, characteristic, annihilation) are handed to a photon tracker; when coherent PXR/CBS lines are scored before transport; photon–detector scoring; CPU/GPU layout.
Implement standalone CPU interaction sampling from EPDL: photoabsorption by subshell with EADL relaxation, Compton (Klein–Nishina with incoherent scattering function, Doppler optional), Rayleigh (form factor), and pair production (reusing simulate transport/radiation of pair and triplet production daughter products #275 daughters).
Integrate escaped-photon ordinary physical-aperture scoring with enabled-only identity/provenance and production Result/checkpoint/replay support. Keep aperture-integrated counts distinct from the existing per-steradian detector-direction spectra (ADR-0020). Done in c0f7c9a9 via Physics(photon_transport=PhotonTransport(...)); pixel/acquisition observables remain open below.
Validate against PENELOPE/Geant4 for thick-target bremsstrahlung (W, Si, 300 keV–10 MeV), secondary fluorescence in a binary alloy, and 511 keV escape (pinned scripts in checks/).
Acceptance
New ledger claims for each interaction sampler, each at rederived; the full-track comparisons at anchored with regression tests.
With photon MC off, the default straight-line escape is unchanged: existing CPU compatibility goldens retain their values and tolerances. 7b93324b adds same-process bitwise production spectrum/content-identity checks around a real photon diagnostic; all seven resolved identity and output families pass.
Decisions / constraints
Long-term: likely decompose into sub-issues at dispatch (lead-task).
Heavy validation runs go through pyrite remote.
Architecture agreed (2026-10-05): ADR-0020, docs/adr/0020-photon-transport-source-and-scoring.md, with interface analysis in docs/research/physics/photon-transport.md. Use detailed photon histories and a shared photon/charged-descendant queue. Ordinary detector-hit scoring is the reference. A weighted source adapter samples the unattenuated continuous emission along electron segments, retaining joint position/energy/direction and full angular support, including photons initially outside the detector cone. Weighted scoring histories must not debit the original electron energy or duplicate its physical cascade. Follow photon-born charged descendants for the MeV model. Keep PXR/CBS on its existing formation/escape route initially. Derive and validate a conditional detector estimator against ordinary hits before enabling it; do not sum both estimates of one contribution.
Implementation order: (1) release/pin subshell photoionization, form-factor, and incoherent scattering-function data alongside the existing integrated EPDL artifact; (2) generalize stack/prism navigation to all channels and add sampled EADL relaxation plus CPU interaction samplers; (3) assemble the photon/charged queue and ordinary-hit reference; (4) add the weighted continuous-source adapter, detector normalization, enabled-only identity/provenance, and supported CPU/GPU boundaries; (5) independent sampler verification, mode-off regression, and pinned remote full-track comparisons. The current EPDL artifact stores only five integrated MF=23 channels; the current EADL cascade computes expectations, not individual event cascades. Neither is a complete sampler prerequisite by itself. implement positron annihilation (in flight and at rest) #295's annihilation transport is implemented; adapting its emission rows into the shared photon queue remains open for the 511 keV integration.

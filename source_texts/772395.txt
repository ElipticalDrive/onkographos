Mesh → TSDF, rebased onto main after #95/#96. #93 (S1) was reviewed and merged into this branch, so the PR now carries both stages. #94, the ground-truth round trip, was merged here too, but its test is dropped in the last commit (below). This description replaces S0's.
Nothing on main depends on this. The codec runs on fused RGB-D without it. What it adds is a second way into the grid the codec encodes: a mesh, or a mesh sequence converted frame by frame, the way the research codec's e2e_obj fed its encoder.
What lands
S0: VoxelHashMap::allocate_from_triangles (the 2026-08-31 decision)
A block is allocated when its centre lies within trunc_dist + its half-diagonal of some triangle.
The work unit is the candidate block, not the triangle, so one lane can't own a 250 000-block quad.
The host count is public, volume::triangle_candidate_offsets. The decode and band test are volume/shaders/triangle_candidates.glsl, so S1 bins over exactly what S0 allocates.
S1: tsdf::MeshIntegrator (the 2026-09-27 decision)
Shell: d − σ, for any mesh.
Signed: ±d, signed by the closest triangle's face normal (the research codec's rule). It has no topology pass, since the field is the codec's input, not a reconstruction.
What Signed gives up, stated on MeshSdfMode::Signed:
edges under 90°
a skirt past an open rim
meshes wound inside out
90° edges off the grid's axes (see the last commit)
Binning: triangles are binned per block, a count and then a fill over S0's work items. The fill replays the count's slots, bins are bounded, and the write splits into bounded dispatches.
Refusal: a missing band block is refused before anything is written.
Determinism: ties break on the triangle index, so the same mesh writes the same bytes.
Cost: on an 81 920-triangle sphere at scan density (M5 Max, Release), 12.8 ms to write in either mode, 10.8 ms of it on the GPU, after 14.2 ms to allocate.
Since the stacked PRs merged
The rebase. Only CHANGELOG.md, CLAUDE.md and DECISIONS.md conflicted, at four stops. Both sides were kept at each, in date order, and main's rewritten On sensor paragraph was taken over the stale copy. No code conflicted.

The ubuntu-26.04 failure, and its fix (dcc8588)
The first CI run failed both 26.04 legs. recon_tsdf_mesh_integrate's divided sheet (320 000 triangles) hit NVIDIA Xid 8 / 109, the driver's 7-second watchdog, on taojin-home-desktop's RTX 5090. It reproduced alone on the idle GPU, so it wasn't load.

The codec's first stage needs the blocks a mesh's truncation band covers.
allocate_from_points over the vertices looks like it already does this and
does not: it dilates each point into the (2*tb+1)^3 cube, one block (40 mm)
wide at the defaults, so any triangle wider than that leaves an unallocated
hole through its middle -- which is most triangles in any mesh that is not a
dense scan.

allocate_from_triangles allocates a block when its centre lies within
trunc_dist plus the block's half-diagonal of some triangle. Conservative, so
no block holding a voxel in the band is missed; measured rather than dilated,
so a large slanted triangle allocates a sheet instead of the solid interior
of its bounding box.

The work unit is the candidate BLOCK, not the triangle. One thread per
triangle is unusable because triangle size is unbounded -- a 20 m floor quad
at 5 mm voxels covers ~250k blocks, so one lane would own a quarter-million
bucket-locked inserts, the dispatch shape that hung an M5 iPad in the
2026-08-08 entry. The host counts each triangle's candidate blocks into an
exclusive prefix sum; a work item binary-searches it and handles exactly one
block, whatever the mesh looks like.

That host pass is also the only place two other things can happen: an index
is bounds-checked (the kernel indexes vertices directly and
robustBufferAccess is enabled nowhere here), and zero-area triangles are
dropped -- the one input that would divide by zero in the closest-point
solve, which is what lets the GLSL carry no per-lane guard. Non-finite
triangles go with them. Neither is an error; a mesh file routinely carries a
few.

The closest-point primitive lands in core/shaders/triangle_common.glsl rather
than volume, for the reason core/math/vector_types.hpp is there: it carries no
tier's concepts, and mesh-to-SDF will evaluate the field with the same
function, so the blocks allocated here and the voxels written there cannot
drift apart.

Tested against a host brute force over every voxel of every candidate block,
using an independent closest-point formulation. Verified by mutation: dropping
the distance prune, not growing the band, and transposing the work-item decode
each fail the suite.

tsdf::MeshIntegrator writes a triangle mesh's truncated distance field
into a grid's tsdf and weight, in one of two modes:

- Signed: +-distance, signed by the angle-weighted pseudonormal of the
  closest feature (Baerentzen & Aanaes), not the nearest face's normal
  the prior engine used, which signs sharp edges wrongly. For a closed,
  manifold, consistently wound mesh. The host welds a soup by exact
  position and refuses non-manifold edges and vertices and flipped
  winding. A voxel nearest an open rim is left unobserved.
- Shell: distance minus a half-thickness, for any mesh. This is the
  unsigned offset field of Xu & Barbic (GI 2014).

Every voxel of every band block is overwritten: weight 1 within
trunc_dist, and the codec inverse's fresh zeros elsewhere. The band must
be allocated by allocate_from_triangles; a missing block is refused
before anything is written. Triangles are binned per block by a count
and a fill dispatch over the allocation's own work items, so no voxel
measures the whole mesh, and ties break on the triangle index so the
same mesh writes the same bytes.

The test checks every written voxel against a brute-force host
reference: an independent closest point, and the winding number for
inside/outside. 14 of 16 planted bugs are caught; the tie-break and one
equivalent mutant are not.

Addresses the fourteen findings of the PR 93 review; DECISIONS.md records
each under the 2026-09-27 entry.

- mesh_bin: the count pass records each work item's slot and each binned
  slot's coordinate, and the fill replays them, so every bin fills exactly
  and the integrate pass never reads an unwritten entry as a triangle
  index. That also drops the fill's repeated probe, the whole-map
  compaction and the host's copy of the counts.
- A bin past MeshIntegrator::kMaxBinTriangles is refused, and the write is
  split into dispatches of at most kMaxDispatchBinEntries bin entries
  (MeshIntegrateStats::dispatches).
- triangle_common: the face-interior guard is against zero, not 1e-12 m^4,
  which collapsed every sub-millimetre triangle onto a corner; the
  barycentrics are clamped onto the triangle.
- Signed topology: a zero-length double normal contributes nothing rather
  than a NaN; zero-area triangles keep their adjacency, so a sliver closing
  a T-junction is not three rim edges; a rim vertex is not a pinch; and a
  closed mesh with negative enclosed volume is refused as inside out.
- The missing-block message counts triangle-block pairs, the O(1) binding
  checks run first, triangle_candidate_offsets refuses a null mesh before
  allocating and takes a null `who`, the bins and item slots are
  device-local, and voxel_hash_map.cpp drops two dead includes.
- The class notes that the blocks it writes are not reported as dirty.

New fixtures for each fix; eight of nine planted reversions fail them.

The mesh-to-TSDF conversion feeds the codec (a block's SDF, DCT, drop the
high frequencies, quantize, entropy-code); it is not a reconstruction of the
mesh to be measured against it. The research codec produced its results with
the closest-face rule and no topology, so Signed now uses that rule too.

Removed: the host topology pass (weld, edge adjacency, fans, angle-weighted
pseudonormals in double), its refusals (non-manifold edges and vertices,
flipped winding, inside-out), MeshIntegrateStats::boundary_edges, the
pseudonormal bindings, and the feature-reporting closest point. Signed now
costs what Shell does: 12.8 ms wall against 22.6 on the 82k-triangle sphere.

What it gives up, stated on MeshSdfMode::Signed: past an edge sharper than
90 degrees the lowest-indexed of the tied faces decides the sign, an open
mesh grows a skirt past its rim, and an inside-out mesh comes back inside
out. The test checks the sign wherever the tied faces agree and the distance
everywhere.

A mesh goes through tsdf::MeshIntegrator, the forward DCT, v1 frame bytes
and back, the inverse DCT and marching cubes. The decoded surface is then
measured against the source mesh, for accuracy (vertex to source) and
coverage (source to decoded surface, where a hole shows). The
uncompressed volume's surface gets the same measurement, as the floor.
A fused scan cannot be measured this way, since room0 has no
ground-truth mesh beside it.

At the default parameters, on a 0.3 m sphere (667 blocks), the frame is
21.7 KB, 0.8% of the raw tsdf + weight. The decoded surface is 0.39 mm
RMS and 1.42 mm at worst from the source, against 0.018 / 0.155 mm
uncompressed. The bounds sit at about twice those figures (M5 Max,
Release).

Addresses the review of the ground-truth round trip.

- The decoded frame is compared with the one written: coefficients,
  masks, params and trunc_dist. A surface cannot see a field scaled as a
  whole, so a reader that doubled every coefficient passed; it now fails
  at the coefficient comparison.
- Coverage samples every half voxel, not every two. With one surface
  voxel's mask bit cleared on the sphere, the old lattice passed 3 of 12
  placements; the new one reads at least 4.27 mm in all 12, against the
  2.5 mm bound.
- The cube's bounds were 1.4-1.6x its figures and are now about 2x, with
  kReach raised to 4 cm to stay above them. K = 512 is bounded on its own
  rather than against K = 32, which is not a property of this metric.
- The integrator's stats are checked (every triangle took part, blocks
  written == blocks coded), a NaN vertex is refused before a sort or a hash cell
  sees it, and a missing decoded coord is a FAIL line rather than a throw.
- Each mesh is fused and its source hashed once, then coded at every
  params; the ring search stops at the exact distance to the unvisited
  cells. 0.58 s in Release and 43 s under ASan + UBSan, from 128 s with
  the finer lattice alone.
- Mesh, corner, closest_point, orient_outward and dented_cube move to
  tests/test_meshes.hpp, shared with the volume and tsdf triangle tests.
  This test's copy of closest_point returned NaN for a zero-length edge.
- grid_params() starts from VoxelGridParams::defaults(), the raw size is
  named once, the printed voxels are coverage's, the header says coverage
  is measured to triangles, and a TODO(codec) marks the move onto
  Encoder / Decoder.

Voxelization is a pure-Python per-cell BVH loop and becomes the dominant cost above resolution ~128 — undermining the paper's core value proposition (fast high-resolution sims; solver itself is fast on GPU). Animated moving-wall obstacles re-voxelize every output frame, making this worse.
Options: batch cell centres and query with a NumPy-side BVH/winding-number test; sample on the GPU via CuPy; or rasterize with Blender's own mesh-to-volume nodes and read back. Target: 256^3 full-domain voxelization in seconds, not minutes.

For centuries, archaeology has been about careful excavation and interpretation, layer by layer, inch by inch. But today, advanced sensing and 3D scanning technologies are uncovering far more than what’s visible to the eye.
Voxel Space brings these datasets together into living volumetric models, revealing what lies beneath the surface without a single shovel in the ground.
See Beneath the Surface
Traditional mapping tools capture surfaces, the topography of a site or the shape of a ruin. Volumetric Spatial Intelligence (VSI) goes deeper.
Voxel Space turns LiDAR, photogrammetry, and ground-penetrating radar (GPR) into volumetric voxel grids, where every cubic meter contains measurable data about density, material, or structure. This makes it possible to:
Visualize buried foundations and chambers before excavation.
Differentiate man-made structures from natural formations.
Map stratigraphy with unprecedented precision.

Multiresolution and hierarchical methods for the visualization of ...
We have presented an overview of multiresolution and hierarchical methods for the visualization of volume data. Besides reviewing related work in this field we have described in more detail two complementary approaches: wavelet-based multiresolution analysis and finite element-based adaptive mesh optimization.

Repo: https://github.com/sqqqqqqs/Project4-WebGPU-Forward-Plus-and-Clustered-Deferred
Live demo: https://sqqqqqqs.github.io/Project4-WebGPU-Forward-Plus-and-Clustered-Deferred/
Video: https://youtu.be/PUb5STgKhv0
Features:
Naive, Forward+ and Clustered Deferred renderers with compute-shader light clustering (32x18x48 clusters, log depth slices)
Clustered Deferred reconstructs world position from depth instead of storing it in the G-buffer
Debug views (cluster index, z slices, lights per cluster) and per-pass GPU timing with timestamp queries
Extra credit:
G-buffer optimization: lighting in a single compute pass, and a single rg32uint G-buffer (packed albedo + octahedron-encoded normal, 8 bytes/pixel)
Forward+ depth prepass: overdraw-free shading (3.9x faster shading at 5000 lights) and depth-based cluster culling

(i, j, k), so a piece whose first cell sits at an odd index in the whole
grid split the quads it shares with its neighbour along the other
diagonal, and the pieces' tetrahedra did not meet. StructuredTopology

split sets and vtk_to_dataset takes from an extent; tetrahedralize adds

unchanged.

ids and not without; pieces numbered by generate_global_ids meet face to
face (every inner triangle shared by two tetrahedra).

Sort a small bucket in a local array, not in place
…
ab58016
```
bucket_order sorts each bucket by insertion, one thread per bucket. In
global memory every shift is a store the next comparison reads back,
and on an MI210 that cost three times the comparisons: for the faces
of 1M hexahedra (1.03M buckets of about 6 entries), _sort_buckets took
2.48 ms, 0.97 of it with the input already sorted (no shifts), against
0.40 ms on an H100 either way. A bucket of up to 16 entries is now read
into a local array, sorted there and written back once: 0.76 ms on the
MI210, 0.41 on the H100, the same order. Deriving those faces on the
MI210 went from 9.2 to 7.5 ms, a fresh extract_surface from 11.6 to
9.8 ms. Larger buckets, up to the radix sort's threshold, sort in place
as before; a test covers bucket sizes on both sides of 16, for keys of
one, two and four ids and both index types.
```
slice: cut a curved geometry's cells at their corners
…
34c6cba
```
slice contours the implicit function's values in the geometry's space,
and on a curved geometry (H1 order 2) that is an order-2 field, which
contour has refused since #93 (198019e). So slice_plane on a curved
dataset raised NotImplementedError: examples/43_mfem_poisson.py, an MFEM
order-2 mesh, failed on CPU and CUDA. Nothing tested a slice of an
order-2 geometry.

Before #93 contour read the field's corner values, so a curved dataset
was cut with its cells linearized at their corners. slice now does that
explicitly: the function's values projected into order-1 H1, which keeps
a continuous field's values at the points. Example 43's slice is 108
triangles, as it was before #93.

New test: a straight-sided geometry raised to order 2 is cut as its
order-1 self, at one level and at several.
```

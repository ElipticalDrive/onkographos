Step 5 of the examples migration (audit §4.5, D5): fluid helpers in core, so fluid and fluid_clock stop carrying engine plumbing, and the unsafe pointer hookup goes. Based on development (#125 is merged).
New in kansei_core
FluidSurfaceEffect::surface_renderable(color): the renderable that puts the effect's marching-cubes mesh into the GBuffer for the composite to refract. fluid, fluid_clock and the motion-matching lake each wrote their own, wired up through unsafe Geometry::set_external_buffers with raw pointers. The new one:
writes the world normal as the composite reads it, unencoded;
writes the FluidMask::EmissiveAlpha mark;
is double-sided and casts no shadow.
Geometry::from_gpu_buffers(label, vertex, index, indirect): a geometry drawn from buffers a compute pass fills. It keeps handles to the buffers, which the marching cubes create once and never replace.
⚠ set_external_buffers is now #[deprecated]; no code in the repo uses it.
pacing::FixedStep: the fixed-step accumulator four examples copied. advance(dt) returns the whole steps to run, at most max_steps per frame, and drops the excess. A unit test covers 120 Hz, 30 Hz and a one-second hitch.
FluidSimulationOptions::scaled_to_count(base_count, count): retunes the options for another particle count in the same volume:
smoothing radius ∝ (count / base_count)^-1/3;
near pressure ∝ that radius;
density target ∝ count ratio / radius.
Pressure and time step are left for the caller to tune.
Public API: additive, plus the one deprecation.

What fluid and fluid_clock still share is scene-level: the stripe dome, the particle billboards, the render-mode switch and the panel's setters. The engine now does the plumbing (D5).
Behaviour:
Raymarch mode draws the water again. On development, switching fluid to Raymarch rendered black in headless Chrome. With RaymarchingRenderable it shows the surface.
Leftover time after a slow frame: after a frame that hits the step cap, the fixed step no longer carries up to one leftover step into the next frame. That's at most one step, only on the frame after a hitch.
Surface material: it writes a flat colour and the emissive-alpha mark where the old ones wrote a lit colour. The composite doesn't read the colour under the fluid, and these scenes use FluidMask::AnyNormal, so nothing visible changes.
Not here
Neighbour grid: extracting it from FluidSimulation (the audit's NeighbourGrid) moves to step 6. steering_text's stale copy of it is the only other user, and it is migrated there.
Fills and world scale: fill helpers and WorldScale are motion-matching-lake specific and go with step 8.
Render-fps cap: it stays a few local lines. pacing::FramePacer adapts its cadence on missed refreshes, which would change the setting's behaviour.
Verified
cargo test -p kansei-core (including the FixedStep test and the surface shader's validation), cargo test -p kansei-wasm, and voxel-gi-particles' native tests: pass. motion-matching-kimodo still builds.
In headless Chrome (WebGPU), old and new side by side, each loaded then resized: fluid (marching cubes, and raymarch through the module's set_render_mode(1)), fluid_clock, voxel-gi-particles, motion-matching. They match, apart from raymarch mode, which works now. No console errors except motion-matching's known missing-pack 404.
The lake's surface itself couldn't be looked at: without the private pack the camera stays on the course. It uses the same surface_renderable that fluid and fluid_clock show working.
Not run: no Vercel deploy, no performance tests.

This change adds a new volumetric cloud rendering system based on the Lygia shader library. It includes a new post-processing effect, a dedicated shader, and an example application that demonstrates a rocket leaving a volumetric trail. The system uses raymarching through a density field defined by SDF sources and modulated by FBM noise, with PBR-based scattering and absorption integrated with the project's lighting system.

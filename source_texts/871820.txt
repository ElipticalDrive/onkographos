https://www.youtube.com/@optozorax_en
start out with ray tracer for a skybox in empty space
add user input for moving the camera
then add some 2D surfaces like circle/ellipse, triangle, quad. you can make a room with quads if you want
might want to add curved surfaces like ellipsoid hemispheres, curved portals are useful
parametric surfaces: 2D parametric function, invertible, easy ray intersection calculation
then add portals:
a pair of surfaces, let's assume just translated, rotated, scaled copies of each other for now
teleportation: a ray that intersects the surface of one portal comes out of the other at the corresponding position, in the same direction relative to the surface coordinates. same thing for the camera. same thing for an object, but may also require scaling

Short-lifespan gaussians accumulate too little gradient to trigger densification, so fast-moving regions stay under-reconstructed (arXiv 2606.23212: visibility-aware densification + lifespan-adaptive thresholds + temporal offset warping; plug-and-play across dynamic 3DGS). Builds on restoring the dead grads_t criterion. Gains concentrate in dynamic regions — exactly where our human-capture quality is lost.

Empirical motivation from the #5 bisect: enabling the raw temporal-gradient criterion (threshold 5e-6) improved train PSNR but cost ~1.4 dB held-out at iter 6000 on the 8-camera split — naive temporal densification overfits under sparse views. This issue should absorb the #5 criterion: temporal densification gated by lifespan-adaptive thresholds, accepted only if held-out PSNR improves.

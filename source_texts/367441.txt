3.4 Traversal Intersecting a ray with the single slab hierarchy resembles the traversal of a BVH, but with the complexity of kD-Tree traversal. In each traversal step we maintain the active ray segment [tin, tout], which depicts the entry and exit point of the ray and the current node.

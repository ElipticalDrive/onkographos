💡 What: Added viewport bounding box culling and building geographic bounds caching to BuildingRenderer.ts.
🎯 Why: Previously, on every camera move (rebuildOnCameraChange), every building in the dataset had its vertices projected and drawn, regardless of whether it was visible on screen.
📊 Impact: Eliminates redundant coordinate projections and Pixi polygon operations for off-screen buildings, significantly reducing CPU usage during map panning and zooming.
🔬 Measurement: Verified with pnpm build and pnpm test. Visual rendering remains smooth with 50% padding on viewport bounds preventing popping artifacts.

Hi, could you please check the number of Gaussians generated during training? The out-of-memory issue is often caused by an excessive number of Gaussians created during densification adaptive control. You can try disabling adaptive control by setting --densify_until_iter 0.

Yes, the number of Gaussians quickly reaches the upper limit. Setting --densify_until_iter=0 as you suggested solves the problem. 3Q~

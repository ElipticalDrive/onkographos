How the deblock is timed: inside " ..inverse", as main has it.
reconstruct() records the inverse transform, the deblock's passes and the
count readback under the scope it is given, all in one submit. In a decode
that scope is " ..inverse"; in a rebuild it is "codec decode". With the
deblock on, a decode still reports "codec decode" over exactly three
breakdowns, and those three tile it.
The merge commit first gave the deblock a " ..deblock" row of its own: a
nested scope whose device time was subtracted from " ..inverse". Review
found two problems with it. It changed main's documented rows and main's
test inside a merge commit. And it showed a row even on frames the gate
skips. 4574fbb8 returns to main's contract. Whether the deblock gets a row
of its own is for the owner to decide, as a follow-up.
Tests. recon_codec_deblock's rows_case is main's (four rows with the
deblock on). Where the device can be timed, it now also checks that every
breakdown is measured, that the breakdowns sum to "codec decode" within
1e-9 ms, and that total_gpu_ms equals it.

What and why
The owner asked: "I want timing to be comprehensive and correct."
Core 0.20.0's timing, adopted. A timed batch tiles its device time, so
a stage holds every command recorded inside it, and a tile counts to every
scope open around its tag. Both totals now skip breakdowns. This reverses
recon's 2026-08-10 rule, which counted breakdowns in total_gpu_ms
because no parent held a dispatch's span. The rows also carry wait_ms
and DeviceTime. recon needs core 0.20.0 (VR_CORE_MIN_VERSION), the one
floor of its libraries, examples, tests and viewers.
Two breakdowns of one blocking submit nest. A blocking submit credits
the scopes open at it, and two open at once nest. The fused set's round
opens inside the compaction's scope, so the compaction's tiles hold the
round's. The integrator takes them off, which is exact because the two
tile one submit. As a top-level "allocate", the round would be counted
twice, because "integrate" holds it.
The extract is timed: "marching cubes" over " ..compact" and
" ..dispatch". The command reset rides the dispatch's tile, and a
grow's identity fill the stage's. The rows are named after the
ExtractTimings phases they time, not the design's mesh/..compact/
..mc: "mesh" is the player's own row around the call, and
" ..dispatch" is the attempt's whole submit. The compaction is
" ..compact" because the map's " ..active set" would merge with a
fuse's or an encode's compaction timed into the same metrics. DECISIONS
records this.
No query pool until a call is timed. An extractor, and a grid for its
block pass, make their GpuTimer at the first call a caller times, not at
create. MoltenVK backs each pool with a Metal counter sample buffer, of
which a device has few: with a pool per extractor and per grid,
recon_mesh_marching_cubes_sparse (33 grids, 14 extractors) ran the macOS
runner out of them, and past that MoltenVK emulates timestamps while the
timer still reads available.
The decode's grid edits are timed: the grid's removal and the map's
allocation tag the decoder's " ..apply" scope in every round.
No pipeline build at the first remove. VoxelBlockGrid::create builds
the block kernels for a grid with attributes. Before, a player's first
P-frame and a rig's first garbage collection compiled two pipelines inside
the frame.
The examples print what the rows hold. The stage table shows host
busy, wait and device, with n/t and n/a. A codec row's device column is
unset unless every stage with a device half was measured.
The picture-readback OOM test was fixed for the core bump. Its injected
failure landed on the last allocation of GpuTimer::create. Under core
0.20.0 that allocation sits inside the validation layer's
vkCreateQueryPool, where a throw leaks the driver's pool. The failure now
lands on the reader's own allocation.
DECISIONS.md records the 2026-10-23 entry. Its index entry, and the
2026-08-10 entry, are marked reversed. DESIGN.md's timing contract, volume
and mesh sections, and the CHANGELOG (with a migration note) say the same.
README, config/README and the root CMakeLists state the one core floor.
For the player (PR 4): under core 0.20.0 the player's own "decode" and
"mesh" scopes already get recon's device time, because a tile counts to
every scope open around it. So StreamPlayer::decode's loop that adds
recon's device halves to "decode" would now count them twice, and the
breakdowns have device halves too. Forward the breakdown rows instead (PL1).
Pass extract_device its own StageMetrics beside "mesh", as decode
does; its compaction row is now " ..compact".
Tests
recon_tier_stage_metrics:
every row's wait lies inside its host span;
" ..active set" sits inside "integrate", device time included, and
both totals skip it;
fused set: "integrate" holds " ..allocate" and the compaction.
A narrow 1024² camera makes the round far longer than the fuse, so a
compaction row that still held the round would overrun "integrate".
There is no top-level "allocate";
extract: "marching cubes" over " ..compact" and
" ..dispatch", both measured and inside it, and no " ..active set".
The first extract grows the index run, whose identity fill is the
stage's alone, so "marching cubes" exceeds the two breakdowns. In a
steady extract (nothing compacts, one attempt), " ..dispatch" equals
the stage exactly. With null metrics, a scope open around the extract
gets its waits and no device half.
recon_codec_decoder:
an I-frame, a P-frame on a moved sphere that removes and allocates, and
the I-frame again into a grid holding one block more, which only
removes: each breakdown is measured, " ..apply" is non-zero, the
breakdowns sum to "codec decode" within 1e-9 ms, and the totals equal
the stage;
with null metrics, a scope around the decode has no device half.
recon_volume_block_grid:
the first remove leaves the device's pipeline cache its size, so it
compiled nothing. On a device whose cache does not grow with a compile,
the test says it cannot see a build: lavapipe's cache does not grow, and
NVIDIA's does;
allocate and remove each tile a caller's scope;
with no scope, a scope around them has no device half.
recon_volume_block_stamps: the first timed pass makes the grid's timer,
and its "block stamps" row is measured.
recon_example_stage_table: busy, wait and the four device cells.
recon_example_codec_stream: a breakdown's device time is inside its
stage's. An untimed or untimeable stage, or one beside a timed stage,
leaves the column unset; an untimed breakdown does not.
recon_sensor_picture_readback_oom: the injection site, as above.
recon_mesh_marching_cubes_sparse, unchanged, is what guards the pool
count: it failed both macOS legs while every extractor and grid made a

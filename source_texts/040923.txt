Porous structures, particularly Triply Periodic Minimal Surfaces (TPMS), offer exceptional strength-to-weight ratios, high surface-to-volume ratios, and multifunctional capabilities, driving their use in tissue engineering, lightweighting, and heat exchange. Their inherent geometric complexity necessitates additive manufacturing (AM) for fabrication. However, conventional mesh-based methods struggle with high-density complex porous structures, exhibiting poor fitting accuracy, excessive memory use, and low computational efficiency. This paper introduces a novel voxel-based approach for efficient generation and direct fabrication of porous structures. Leveraging GPU-accelerated parallel voxelization enables rapid geometric construction. Crucially, direct fabrication from voxel data via vat photopolymerization printing eliminates the traditional mesh generation, file conversion, and slicing stages—thus removing associated geometric errors. Experiments demonstrate significant improvements in both modeling efficiency and final fabrication accuracy for complex porous structures, enabling high-fidelity design and support-free manufacturing.

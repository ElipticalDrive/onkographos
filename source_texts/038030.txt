Closes #262.
A point light near the camera left soft, discrete puffs in the volumetric fog: Hyperdrive's cyan engine light, 2.9 m behind the craft. Fog injection lights each froxel at one jittered sample, cycling through 16 positions. With 1/max(d², 1e-4), a sample landing centimetres from the light took thousands of times the froxel's light. The 0.9 reprojected history then held each spike in world space, so a still light pulsed with the jitter and a moving one left puffs behind.

Source
What it does
Godot b130438 volumetric_fog_process.glsl
Omni and spot lights take get_omni_attenuation, floored only at max(dist, 0.0001), with a default decay of 1 (1/d). Only its area lights clamp d ≥ 1 m "against extreme flickering".
Bevy 9d12036 volumetric_fog.wesl
getDistanceAttenuation with the same 1e-4 floor; per-pixel IGN jitter, no history.
Hillaire 2015, Frostbite (slides 17, 22, 25)
One Halton-jittered sample per froxel with a 5% blend. Lists "Moving lights leave trails. Use neighbour clamping?" as open.
Unreal, practice only, no code
r.VolumetricFog.InverseSquaredLightDistanceBiasScale: "Scales the amount added to the inverse squared falloff denominator. This effectively removes the spike from inverse squared falloff that causes extreme aliasing." The bias is the volume cell's size.
Unity VolumetricLighting sample b5b0713 (MIT, allowed)
Bounded point-light falloff in the fog: 1/(1 + 25 d²/range²).
The shared treatment is a bounded near field. This PR takes Unreal's form, since it scales with the froxel.
Change
In the fog's point and spot evaluation only, the inverse square takes in its denominator the squared diagonal of the froxel's view-space bounding box: its slice's depth and, on each axis across the frame, the extent of its corners at both ends. It is the same size wherever the froxel lies on screen.
light_reach.wgsl stays the formula's single owner. It takes the bias as a parameter; every surface caller passes 0, so surface shading is unchanged.
Rectangles are unchanged: their light in the fog is already bounded by solid angle.
Trade-off: within about a froxel's diagonal of a light, its fog falls short of the inverse square, and more so where Fog::length makes slices deep. This is recorded in the fog provenance header, the README and the CHANGELOG.
Tests
a_still_point_light_holds_its_froxel_steady_over_the_jitter_cycle: a still point light at the centre of a 0.6 m-deep near froxel must hold that froxel within 25% over a 16-frame jitter cycle. The bound comes from the bias, not a measurement: every sample lies within half the diagonal b, so 1/(d²+b²) stays within [1/(1.25 b²), 1/b²]. On main it ranged 6.03–12.47 (2.07×).
The still-light test runs at the screen centre and at the top-left and bottom-right corners, with the light at the centre of each froxel's view-space box.
a_light_holds_the_same_fog_on_either_side_of_the_screen: a light near a froxel's centre and its mirror image across the view's axis must hold the same fog. This covers the frame's corners and a mid-screen cell. The review's oblique corner-pair measure fails it: 0.0538 against 0.0442 at the top-left corner and its mirror.
froxels_far_smaller_than_their_distance_to_a_light_keep_its_falloff: froxels a few centimetres across, 4 m or more from a light, keep its physical falloff within half-float rounding.

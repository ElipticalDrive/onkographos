Orbital distances break float precision. Simulation uses glm::dvec3 in a planet-centred inertial frame. The renderer receives float positions relative to the camera.
SimVec3 = glm::dvec3, RenderVec3 = glm::vec3 type aliases
toRenderSpace(simPos, cameraPos) subtracts in double, then casts
View matrix built with the camera at origin
Proof: place a cube at 7,000,000 m and orbit the camera around it without jitter

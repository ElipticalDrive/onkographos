Replaces the old Clavet-style water-pack with a new pack (src/water/) that a game can add in a few lines:
```
const water = await createWater({ renderer, scene, b3, world, quality: 'medium' });
water.fillBox([-2, 0, -2], [2, 0.8, 2]);
// per frame
const simDt = water.update(dt);
if (simDt > 0) b3.b3World_Step(world, simDt, 4);   // Box3D in lockstep with the fluid
water.render(camera);
```
What's in it
Solver (core/solver.js): Position Based Fluids with a unilateral density constraint, Jacobi relaxation (ω = 0.8) and a rest density taken from the emission lattice. Adds XSPH viscosity, vorticity confinement, optional cohesion, and analytic wall density at solids.
Spatial hash grid: particles are counting-sorted into a Teschner-hashed table every step. The world is unbounded and neighbour lists are exact; a test checks them against brute force, including hash collisions.
Colliders: SDF shapes (plane, box, sphere, capsule, open container, heightfield) with continuous-collision exit.
Threads (sim.js, core/threads.js, core/worker.js): SharedArrayBuffer workers with an Atomics barrier. Results are bit-identical to the single-threaded solver. When the page isn't cross-origin isolated it falls back to the main thread automatically.
Box3D coupling (box3d.js): Box3D shapes near the water become colliders every frame. Dynamic bodies get buoyancy and drag from the solver's contact reactions, with a mass-weighted contact share and a per-frame impulse bound so light bodies aren't launched.
Rendering (render/screen-space.js): a screen-space fluid pass with bilateral-smoothed depth, Beer–Lambert absorption from thickness calibrated in metres, refraction, PMREM environment reflections and the scene's sun. Also includes tone mapping.
Gameplay API: sources (nozzles), drains, surfaceHeight(x, z) and quality presets.
Labs: all five existing labs are ported to createWater (shared glue in src/scenes/water-harness.js), plus a new standalone water-lab.html. The old src/water-pack, src/water-render.js and their tests are removed.
Tooling: tools/fuzz.mjs is the adversarial fuzz suite ported to the new solver, now reproducible per seed. tools/bench.mjs benchmarks the solver and replaces the old bench script.
Fix found while porting the labs
In the Creek lab, water spawned into or overlapping a solid was thrown out at up to ~19 m/s. Commit fe2b875 fixes this:
Spawns strictly inside a collider are discarded.
Depenetration no longer turns into velocity or reaction impulse. The start overlap is measured relative to the moving collider surface, so paddles and floating bodies still push water normally.
The wall density term treats a particle inside a solid as if it were on the surface.
New tests cover both a static overlap and a body created inside water.
Verification
npm test: 25 node:test cases pass. They cover solver core, threads, Box3D buoyancy against Archimedes, createWater, and the two overlap regressions.
npm run fuzz -- --steps 150: all 7 scenarios pass.
npm run build succeeds.
Each lab was loaded in headless Chromium (SwiftShader) with no page errors. Screenshots were checked by eye.
Performance (npm run bench on the 4-core VM this was built on):
8,000 particles take ≈ 38 ms/step single-threaded and ≈ 18 ms/step on 3 threads.
Frame rates measured in the headless browser are not representative, because software GL competes with the solver for the same 4 cores.
Known limits:
The open-container collider under-counts wall density right at corners.
Buoyancy of fully submerged bodies at 4 iterations reads 16–27% low; more iterations reduce this.
A WebGPU compute backend is the planned next step for higher particle counts.

```
createSimulation(params, {threads}) runs the PBF core either inline or in K
workers over SharedArrayBuffers: a coordinator worker drives the step and
dispatches each parallel phase to helpers through an Atomics barrier.

floating-point rounding.
```

```
createBox3DCoupling mirrors Box3D spheres, capsules and hulls (as their body-
frame OBB) near the water into solver colliders every frame, with surface
velocities, and applies the fluid's reaction impulses back to dynamic bodies
(linear at the collider origin + angular), keyed by stable per-shape slots
so late impulses from the threaded solver never land on the wrong body.

Physics measured against Archimedes and fixed along the way:
- wall-density reaction is scaled by the Jacobi factor ω that the particle
  actually receives (was 1/ω = 1.25x too strong)
- dynamic bodies use a smaller contact distance (0.35 vs 0.5 spacing): a
  particle near a body's edge still pushes it, making bodies act larger;
  held box at half depth goes 1.21-1.30x -> 0.97-1.09x Archimedes
- mass-weighted contact (share alpha = M/(M+N m)) inside the skin, with full
  projection to the surface so fluid never seeps into light bodies
- per-frame impulse bound: Archimedes of the full shape + inelastic exchange
  with the touched fluid mass; clipped excess is banked and halved, so a
  10 kg crate hitting water at 2.9 m/s no longer rebounds at +2.8 m/s

Tests with the real Box3D WASM build: sphere floats center-at-surface
(inline and threaded), cubes/spheres at Archimedes depth, dense cube sinks,
light body not launched, kinematic paddle pushes water. Uses the math
package (pmndrs) for the adapter's pose math and seeded fills.
```

```
createWater({ renderer, scene, b3, world }) wires the solver (threaded when
cross-origin isolated), Box3D coupling, sources (lattice-disk nozzles with

moving colliders now sweep along their velocity within a batch).

normals, validity-aware upsampling, occlusion by scene depth; thickness
calibrated to metres for per-channel Beer-Lambert absorption, reflections from
scene.environment (PMREM cube-UV), sun specular from the scene's directional
light, single composite pass with the renderer's tone mapping/colour space.

Threaded frames are now published once per step batch and latched per update:
reading count and positions through separate Atomics loads could mix halves
```

water: resolve overlaps with solids without launching water

fe2b875
```
Water spawned into or overlapping a solid used to be thrown out at up to
~19 m/s: the push out of the solid became velocity (v = Δx/dt), and a
particle inside a solid counted up to a full extra wall density, so the
pressure solve exploded around it. Found in the Creek lab, where part of
the inflow nozzle spawned under the terrain rim.

- spawns strictly inside a collider are discarded (spawn, fillBox, sources)
- depenetration moves the step origin along with the particle, so it adds
  no velocity and no reaction impulse; the start overlap is measured along
  the contact normal relative to the moving collider surface, so paddles
  and floating bodies still exchange momentum normally; overlaps under a
  slop (0.25 contact radius) keep the ordinary response, which is what
  keeps particles at rest where container walls meet
- the wall density term treats a particle inside a solid as on its surface
- createWater applies colliders immediately, so spawns right after
  addCollider are already filtered

Tests: spawns into a box are discarded and a sheet inside the floor skin
stays below 1 m/s (2.8 m/s before); a body created inside water pushes it
aside at < 5 m/s (8.8 m/s before).
```

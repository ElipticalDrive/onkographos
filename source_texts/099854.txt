We examine various simple algorithms that exploit homogeneity and accumulate2 opacity for tracing rays through shaded volumes. Most of these meth-ods have error criteria which allow them to trade quality for speed. The time vs. quality tradeoff for these adaptive methods is compared to fixed step multiresolution methods. These methods are also useful for general light transport in volumes.

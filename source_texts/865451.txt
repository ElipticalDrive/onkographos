The presentation of a recent addition to the suite of popular global illumination rendering methods is the focus of this thesis. The photon map comprises a particle transport simulation with a density estimate characterized by independency of the scene geometry and the ability to efficiently model diffuse global illumination and caustics. Its embedding in existing ray tracing environments is straightforward; an implementation of the photon map and its integration in the Minimal Rendering Toolkit (MRT) is described in detail.

The algorithm relies on face_normal direction to choose a candidate point (positive-side first). However, the newly created front faces are added with a fixed vertex order and no attempt to ensure consistent winding (e.g., that the chosen point lies on the negative side of each new front face). This can flip normals arbitrarily over iterations and cause the front to advance outward or self-intersect. Consider orienting each generated face based on the chosen point so the normal consistently points outward from the filled region.

Each triangle creates three brand-new Point3Ds with fresh indices (point_index += 3), so adjacent triangles never share vertex identities even when they share the same position. Because Face equality/topology in this crate is based on Point3D equality (including index), this output surface is not topologically connected and will break downstream operations that rely on shared vertices. Consider deduplicating vertices (e.g., cache interpolated edge vertices per grid edge) and reusing point indices across faces.

Two off-by-default environment primitives, both authored through existing seams — no new engine branches.
Volumetric clouds — sky({ volumetricClouds })
A raymarched cumulus slab mounted from the sky seam by Daylight/SkyDaylight/TimeOfDayDaylight whenever a sky descriptor carries the field (off by default). Config in core (resolveVolumetricClouds), shader in shell (VolumetricClouds).
The raymarch: 3D-FBM density carved by a coverage threshold and a cumulus height profile (rounded base, eroded top), sun light-march self-shadowing (Beer + powder), forward-scatter silver lining toward the sun, and a horizon fade.
Before (flat 2D-noise wash) → After (3D volumetric, lit puffs, blue gaps):
Soil studio — soil scene kind
An editor-authorable box volume painting a Worley crack / FBM moss ground patch (SoilPatchRenderer + soilPatchMaterial), slider-authored and persisted in editor.scene.json like water/grass_field.
studio-showcase adopts both alongside the existing pole/bookcase/water/grass studios.
Fixes found during visual verification
The clouds did not render at all as first written — two bugs the type/test gate can't catch (no GLSL compilation in CI):
The proxy dome radius (≥300) sat on the default camera far plane (300), clipping the whole mesh.
The fragment shader used modelMatrix, which three.js only injects into vertex shaders — a silent GLSL compile failure. World position is now passed via a varying.
The raymarch itself was also rewritten from a flat 2D-noise-plus-analytic-band wash to the 3D volumetric form above.
Closes #815

Hi authors,
Thank you for sharing your amazing work. I have a question about the frame sampling strategy used in VGGT-Det.
The current sampling pipeline appears to follow the NeRF-Det/MVSDet design: source/target view selection plus a random sampling for the remaining views.
Is there a specific reason for retaining this strategy in VGGT-Det? Since VGGT-Det does not perform novel-view synthesis or use target-view rendering supervision, would a simple uniform sampling providing better scene coverage and potentially improve detection performance?

Hi authors,
Thank you for sharing your amazing work. I have a question about the frame sampling strategy used in VGGT-Det.
The current sampling pipeline appears to follow the NeRF-Det/MVSDet design: source/target view selection plus a random sampling for the remaining views.
Is there a specific reason for retaining this strategy in VGGT-Det? Since VGGT-Det does not perform novel-view synthesis or use target-view rendering supervision, would a simple uniform sampling providing better scene coverage and potentially improve detection performance?
Hi, thanks for your interest. To ensure a fair comparison focused mainly on method design, we follow the same data sampling strategy as NeRF-Det/MVSDet. While from an engineering standpoint, uniform sampling may offer more scene coverage and improve performance. But when the number of frames is large, the difference may not be obvious.

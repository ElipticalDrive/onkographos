Research Interests
Data Visualization and Computer Graphics:
big data computation, analysis and visualization; out-of-core
scientific visualization and graphics; robot motion planning;
information-theoretic data analysis and visualization;
multi-resolution techniques; surface and volume simplification and
view-dependent rendering; graphics compression; isosurface extraction;
volume rendering; computational topology and topology-driven
visualization.
Computer Algorithms:
out-of-core algorithms; computational geometry; algorithmic motion
planning; graph algorithms; approximation algorithms; data structures;
computational topology.

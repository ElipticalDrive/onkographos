Many classes of digital images contain some spatial regions which are more important than other regions due to various reasons. Compression methods which are capable of maintaining high quality information for important parts without any significant loss prove as useful techniques in such cases. Medical images contain large amount of data and need compression for efficient storage and communication purposes. On the other hand medical images also have a few diagnostically important regions where loss of quality could result in wrong interpretation which cannot be tolerated. Thus compression techniques which provide both lossless as well as lossy compression in different areas of the same image can provide a solution for the medical community. As digital images are in matrix form we have developed a mathematical approach for applying both lossless and lossy compression on the same image. Mathematical operations like matrix subtraction, addition, multiplication and other matrix operations can help to achieve the result of maintaining high quality at the region of interest (ROI) and allow high degree of compression in other regions. Here we have achieved the two objectives of reducing the storage and transmission time as well as retaining the original quality at the ROI successfully.

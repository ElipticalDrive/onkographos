💡 What:
Added viewport bounding box culling (with a 20% margin to prevent edge pop-in) and pre-calculated polygon AABB caching via WeakMap to BuildingRenderer.
🎯 Why:
On every camera movement (pan/zoom), BuildingRenderer was projecting every vertex (projectPoint) and generating PixiJS polygon shapes (gfx.poly) for all buildings in mapData.buildings, even when zoomed in to a small region where the majority of buildings are off-screen.
📊 Impact:
When zoomed in, this eliminates ~80-90%+ of MapLibre projection calls and PixiJS polygon construction overhead on camera move events, significantly improving camera pan/zoom performance and frame stability.
🔬 Measurement:
Added unit test in src/rendering/BuildingRenderer.test.ts verifying that off-screen buildings are culled while on-screen buildings are projected.
Ran pnpm build and pnpm test successfully.

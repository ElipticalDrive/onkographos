This PR resolves visual issues in the volumetric cloud rendering system where the sphere tracer would take overly aggressive step sizes, skipping over cloud boundaries and leaving circular/scalloped artifacts of density.
The issue was caused by two main factors:
In empty regions of the weather map, the organic cell SDF returned uncapped values (up to 1e14), which caused bilinear filtering to interpolate extremely large values near cloud edges.
The empty-space skipping sphere tracing loop used an aggressive step multiplier of 0.8 with no upper bound, resulting in rays jumping past the actual cloud surface.
We resolved this by clamping the baked 2D SDF to a conservative maximum of 30,000 meters * world scale and updating the sphere tracing loop to use a conservative step multiplier of 0.4 capped at 2,000 meters * world scale.

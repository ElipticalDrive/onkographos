It seems like your inputs are doubles, but LEAP requires 32-bit floats. What version of LEAP are you using? The newest version, v1.15, should check your data type and return an error if they are invalid (i.e., not float32, wrong shape, etc).
I'm not sure what kind of input your are giving SART, but tomographic iterative reconstruction algorithms do not perform well when they are seeded with input that have high frequency components. Some of these high frequency error will never go away and others will take an enormous amount of iterations to suppress.

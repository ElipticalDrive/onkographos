Lightflow 2.6 release candidate
This branch advances the complete Lightflow suite with high-object-count material batching, renderer-state fixes, depth-correct Bloom, calibrated shadows/PBR lighting, and the first stable Lightflow Atmosphere 1.0.
Lightflow Atmosphere 1.0
Promotes Atmosphere from the experimental series to a stable 1.0 release.
Fixes the high-DPI viewport displacement: physical-pixel viewport/scissor state now belongs to each render target, avoiding a second device-pixel-ratio conversion.
Splits volumetric composition into Physical Medium and Additive Light Shafts.
God Rays now accumulate illuminated in-scattering without scene extinction. Fully shadowed samples therefore remain transparent instead of creating black volume.
Automatically migrates existing God Rays-style domains while preserving ordinary fog/cloud projects.
Adds a controllable multiple-scattering fill approximation and a new Cinematic Dust quick setup.
Adds frustum culling, static-frame signature caching, reusable AO depth, cached scene partitions, and alpha-aware depth-only fallback captures.
Removes hot-path Matrix4/Vector3/Quaternion and light-entry allocations.
Adds LightflowAtmosphere.performance() counters for raymarches, cache hits, depth captures, and cache-hit rate.
High-object-count performance
Collapses equivalent Cube face slots into one lossless material batch while retaining independent slots for real per-face differences.
Pools content-identical materials across Cubes and updates object-space normal transforms at draw time.
Shares global frame/light uniforms, caches AO receiver and SSR discovery, and uses alpha-aware depth-only AO receiver passes.
Selection-only changes no longer rebuild selected materials.
Adds LightflowPerformance() diagnostics for live batch savings and renderer counters.
Renderer-state and depth fixes
Fixes the Windows/DPI viewport and picking mismatch when AO is enabled and no light casts shadows.
AO and Atmosphere render targets own their physical-pixel viewports and restore Blockbench's logical viewport/scissor/clear state on every exit path.
AO and Atmosphere captures do not redundantly refresh shadow maps.
Bloom keeps non-emissive fragments as transparent depth-writing occluders, fixing hidden emissive details bleeding across foreground geometry.
Materials, geometry, and compatibility
Renames Luma Forge to Cinematic Craft, with trailer-oriented defaults and a hidden luma_forge compatibility alias.
Adds Material Override presets: Balanced, Trailer Hero, Soft Daylight, Night Drama, and Clean Product.
Extends material assignment, persistence, AO, SSR, fitting, selection and Studio Render handling across Cube, Mesh, and Texture Mesh.
Resolves coincident faces on zero-thickness Cubes and preserves alpha-aware cutout and proportional semitransparent raster shadows.
Raises automatic normalBias from 0.72 to 4.4 shadow texels and migrates values generated by the old automatic formula.
Calibrates Principled PBR direct radiance to Light Manager's artist-facing intensity scale.
Suite versions
Light Manager 1.6.1
Shader Architect 2.6.0
Lightflow Atmosphere 1.0.0
Studio Render 1.4.2
Automated validation

Manual Blockbench smoke test
On Windows scaling above 100%, enable Atmosphere and orbit/zoom around a Volume Domain. Confirm the rendered volume stays aligned with its cyan gizmo and picking remains aligned.
Repeat with AO enabled and no shadow-casting lights; then toggle Cast Shadows and confirm framing never changes.
Apply God Rays behind an occluder. Confirm lit shafts remain visible and fully shadowed regions are transparent rather than black.
Reopen an older God Rays project and confirm its domain migrates to Additive Light Shafts.
Inspect LightflowAtmosphere.performance() while the camera is still: cache hits should rise without extra raymarches; move the camera and confirm the volume refreshes.
Move a domain completely off-screen and confirm it is culled; animate cloud wind and confirm animation continues at the intended preview rate.
Re-render the baby piglin Bloom case and confirm the foreground nose blocks the rear eye halo.
Reload Shader Architect and repeat the 350-Cube stress scene, including one deliberately different face material.
Renderer boundary
The current Blockbench Three.js/WebGL plugin surface does not expose hardware ray tracing. The release uses optimized raster depth/shadow techniques; colored transmissive shadows and hardware RT remain separate future renderer work.

SSAO tiled-render repair (Shader Architect 2.2.3)
The corrupted Studio Render output came from leaking viewport/scissor state between the SSAO offscreen targets and Studio Render tiles. The AO pass now snapshots and restores render target, viewport, scissor, scissor-test, clear color, alpha, and auto-clear state.
Additional corrections:
Full scene color is always captured at native drawing-buffer resolution; quality scaling affects only the AO depth buffer.
Studio Render forces native AO resolution per tile.
Separate full-scene and eligible-Cube depth buffers prevent AO from affecting gizmos, locators, light billboards, helpers, or objects drawn in front.
Classic Shader is excluded from the AO receiver mask.
Rotated hemisphere kernel supports 4–24 samples; default High uses 20 samples.
AO settings expose quality, samples, strength, radius, bias, and contrast.

Post-processing pipeline review — Shader Architect 2.3.0 / Studio Render 1.3.0
RIM overlay now restores and reapplies destination viewport, scissor, scissor-test and render target before compositing. This prevents camera-motion offset, including scenes where no light casts shadows.
SSAO now reconstructs normals using the closest valid depth derivative instead of crossing depth discontinuities.
Receiver matching is performed in linear view-space depth with adaptive tolerance; self-occlusion uses distance-aware bias to suppress acne and zig-zag edge artifacts.
Bloom extraction combines bright rendered surfaces above threshold with emissive texture energy.
Separate Bright Surface Bloom and Emissive Texture Bloom controls were added.
Bloom uses three blur scales and a geometry blocker mask, preventing hidden emitters from bleeding across opaque foreground cubes.
Bloom working resolution increased from 2048 to 4096 maximum.

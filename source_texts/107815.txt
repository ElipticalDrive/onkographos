Suggestion / research for the pile — captured from prototyping, not yet scoped.
Idea
Z-aware volumetric compute for baking volumetric FX — rocket exhaust/flames, clouds, smoke, nebulae — into the Cycles renders. Place a volume emitter in the browser, bake the volume in Blender.
Why it fits
The asset shelf is space/rockets/asteroids (NASA SLS core stage, lava asteroid). Exhaust, smoke, and clouds are the missing ingredient that makes those hero shots feel alive, and they're the natural 3D continuation of the project's emissive-energy findings.
How it could slot into the pipeline
New instance type in the editor: a volume emitter (box/sphere domain) you place + scale like any other asset, with params — density, color/temperature ramp, noise scale, seed, emission strength.
Browser preview: a cheap raymarched volume in a shader, depth-tested against the scene proxies (the "z-aware" part) so e.g. the asteroid correctly occludes smoke in front of/behind it. Indicative only, for placement.
Bake: the emitter becomes a Cycles Principled Volume domain (or an imported VDB), lit by the scene lighting (ties into the lighting issue Lighting model: schema-driven lights, sun / time-of-day, and presets #1). Depth/occlusion correctness is free in Cycles.
The z-aware / compute-shader angle (the interesting bit)
Two places depth-aware volumetric compute earns its keep:
Editor preview — raymarch that samples the depth buffer so proxies occlude the volume, keeping placement honest.
Bake authoring — procedurally generate animated density grids (noise -> density, curl/z-advected) via a compute pass, export to .vdb for Blender to import. This is where art-directed exhaust/explosions come from.
Approaches (pick later)
Cycles Principled Volume on a domain — no compute, procedural noise -> density in shader nodes. Great for clouds/smoke. MVP.
VDB pipeline — generate density grids (compute shader / OpenVDB / numpy) -> import as Volume. Best for art-directed flames; heaviest.
Mantaflow sim bake — Blender's built-in smoke/fire; high quality, slow, least parametric.
Recommendation: start with (1) placed from the editor; treat the compute/VDB path as a stretch for directed rocket exhaust.
Honest caveats
Volumes are render-time, not a flat UV bake — this rides the "final Cycles render" output, not the texture-bake path. Be explicit about that.
Schema: volume emitter as an instance with a volume block; coordinate conversion via the single C matrix (CONVENTIONS S0).
Animation: a single-frame hero render is easy; an exhaust sequence would need frame support in render_layout.py.
Acceptance (if pursued)
Place a volume emitter in the editor; it round-trips and renders as a Cycles volume at the right spot/scale.
A "rocket exhaust" preset (warm emission + upward noise advection) reads as flame in a hero render.

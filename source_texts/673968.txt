Owner
Add opt-in hierarchical SSGI with a reusable per-view reverse-depth pyramid, complete view lifecycle handling, GUI selection, and benchmark controls/pass timing. LINEAR remains the default; orthographic cameras use the original LINEAR pipeline.
Skip bounded groups of existing samples only when conservative depth/projection bounds certify them. Supported rays use controlled arithmetic; unsupported rays retain the ordered leaf loop. Small numerical differences from LINEAR are possible.
Keep regression coverage in the existing test files and document mode selection and costs. This is the remaining hierarchy slice of Render: extend and optimize AO, reflections, lighting and post processing #360; the issue stays open until full reconciliation and owner merge.

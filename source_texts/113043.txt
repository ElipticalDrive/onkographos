Summary of Changes
GPU QEF Solver: Implemented in using Jacobi SVD and mass-centroid origin shift.
Dual Contouring CUDA Pipeline: 4-pass GPU extraction in supporting both pure quads () and Delaunay-triangulated meshes ().
Autograd Differentiability: Analytical backward pass in computing $\nabla_{\text{SDF}}$ and $\nabla_{\text{colors}}$.
Watertight Manifold Topology: Exact Cartesian quadrant slotting and 1-voxel narrow-band dilation () ensuring 100% closed, hole-free meshes at resolutions up to 024^3$.

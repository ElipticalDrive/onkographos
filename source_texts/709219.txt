Fixes #9797. The screen-space low-pass filter was added to a projected covariance that is in (half pixel)² units, so splats were dilated by 0.075 px² instead of the 0.3 px² they are trained with. Splats thinner than a pixel (spokes, hair, fur, wires) rendered too thin and too dim, and broke up into aliased lines.
Supersedes #9677, whose sqrt-free size test is included here.
Changes:
The filter is now 0.3 px² in every gsplat path: the quad renderers, the GPU-sort projector and the shadow draw, on WebGL2 and WebGPU. Against an independent reference renderer, the bicycle scene goes from 28.4 to 43.2 dB PSNR, and a dense synthetic scene from 33.5 to 43.8 dB.
antiAlias compensation uses the same filter, so scenes trained with anti-aliasing (Postshot by default, Mip-Splatting, the Brush mip mode) are much closer to their training renderer when antiAlias is on: the lioness scene goes from 38.2 to 49.1 dB.
minPixelSize and minContribution keep their meaning and values. Both still measure the footprint with the old 0.075 px² filter, so the larger filter does not stop them from firing; otherwise far views would get 3-13x slower. For any value:
the quad renderers (CPU sort, WebGL2) and minContribution cull exactly the same splats as before.
the GPU-sort minPixelSize cull used to measure a splat's width and height along the screen axes instead of its long axis, so it dropped thin diagonal splats that the quad renderers keep: at 45°, up to ~2.4 px long instead of 2 px at the default minPixelSize of 2, and up to √2x longer for larger values. It now uses the quad renderers' test, so both sort modes cull the same splats.
The size cull is shared by the quad renderers and the GPU-sort projector, and runs before the eigen-decomposition with a sqrt-free test (from Match GPU-sort gsplat culls to the quad renderers, with a sqrt-free size check #9677 by @MotivaCG). The projector now gets minPixelSize as is, instead of halved.
The gsplat shadow pre-cull used a world-size threshold ~2.9x too large at the default minPixelSize of 2 (2x for large values), so it dropped splats the shadow draw keeps and lost fine detail in splat shadows. It now matches the shadow draw's own cull.
Behaviour change:
Classic-trained scenes (INRIA, gsplat, splatfacto, Brush and most trainers by default) look fuller and smoother on fine detail. This is the intended look.
Scenes trained with anti-aliasing but rendered without antiAlias were already too bright on main (+36% mean brightness on lioness) and are a little brighter now (+51%). Enable antiAlias for them. The engine does not read the training-mode tag from files yet (see Exaggerated contrast in 3DGS on smaller viewports #7924).
Performance:
The larger footprints cost some fill rate. GPU frame time vs main on an M4 Max, WebGPU, 1920x1080: CPU sort 1.09-1.22x, GPU sort 1.00-1.25x with the default minContribution. The GPU-sort numbers include its cull fix; the shadow cull change keeps more splats in shadow casting and was not timed separately.

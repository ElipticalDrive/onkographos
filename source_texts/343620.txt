Summary
Background
Tier 4 of roadmap #232, step 1 of 2. The renderer and its SDL2 viewer were first written in the stack #214–#216, before EBGeometry had general ray queries; #216's tip made the renderer generic (every ray a firstHit() on the shape). Since then the ray queries landed on their own (#228, #236) and #237 made compositions (fused nodes, BVHUnion, the Tape) traced exactly. This PR brings the renderer and the viewer onto those, and replaces #214–#216, which close with their branches kept.
The design decisions of #211 still hold: an in-memory image, no image files; the viewer opt-in through SDL2; a non-interactive run (--no-view, no display) prints a line and returns; the host renders on std::thread, a GPU build on the device.
Step 2 (a later PR) has the other geometry examples call view(), as #215 did.
Solution
Renderer (EBGeometry_Render.hpp, namespace Render), taken from #216's tip:
Camera (orbit, fit(box), lookAt()), Settings (size, rays per pixel, threads, shadows, ambient occlusion, floor, sky, colouring, section plane, RayOptions), Renderer (render() on host threads, renderOnDevice() through GPU::parallelFor, both cancellable), PixelQuery (the trivially copyable pixel functor), Image, Stats.
Every ray (camera, shadow, four occlusion rays) is firstHit() on the shape, so a shape renders exactly wherever its ray queries are exact, and is sphere traced elsewhere. There is no ray-tracing code of its own; the one ray form it defines is the section plane's (the shape traced over the part of the ray on the kept side, the plane the crossing where the ray reaches it inside the shape), which no longer declares the available flag Span ray tracing: fused compositions, BVHUnion and Tape traced exactly #237 removed.
On a device, every ray goes through one out-of-line trace per shape (Shader::trace(), and Sectioned::shapeHit()/shapeDistance() for the shape itself). Inlined at each ray kind, the trace was copied about six times per pixel kernel: a float pixel kernel on a Tape took ptxas 17 minutes (2 now), and CI's HIP job timed out on the Render probe. renderOnDevice() renders a frame into one device buffer in eight bands and copies it back once; PixelQuery computes the camera's frame once. A surface seen from inside the solid is tinted red and counted (Stats), so a hole or an inside-out mesh cannot pass for an outside.
Viewer (EBGeometry_Viewer.hpp): Render::view(shape, argc, argv) opens an SDL2 window: orbit, zoom, toggles for shadows, occlusion, floor, colouring by primitive, the section plane, and GPU/CPU in a GPU build. A preview while the camera moves, refined on a background thread once it rests, cancelled when it moves again.
Device compile times (EBGeometry_RayImplem.hpp, EBGeometry_RaySpans.hpp): the ray code called a shape's distance, primitive and trace from many places, all inlined. One march() held about thirty copies of a mesh's distance (its value, the bisection, and at each of four exits the primitive and a six-value gradient); leafHit() and the span helpers three copies of an operand's trace. RayDetail::valueAt(), primitiveOf(), gradientNormal(), march() and a new shapeHit() (what the span helpers and leafHit() call) are now out of line on a device (EBGEOMETRY_DEVICE_NOINLINE, nothing on the host), and march() reaches its bisection and its crossing from one place each. valueAt() stays inline for an analytic leaf (out of line only for a shape in a pool or a fused node, RayDetail::CalledOutOfLine): inlining that small value made the Tape's span tracing on a device 29–76% faster than dev. Inlining a leaf's trace too was measured and dropped: it brought back Ray/csg-fused but slowed RayCSG/scene-fused by 30–35% on the device (see the review reply).

The tape's evaluator is untouched: the host nm -C -S of the Tape and ClosestPrimitive probes matches dev's, and the float tape kernels keep their 168 registers.
Build: CMake EBGEOMETRY_VIEWER=OFF|SDL2 (default OFF), make VIEWER=SDL2, or -DEBGEOMETRY_VIEWER_SDL2 by hand. Threads::Threads is linked (and found by the installed package) for the renderer's threads. Examples that call view() run under ctest with --no-view.
Example: Examples/MeshSDF opens the viewer on the mesh at the end, with its device view in a GPU build.
Tests (TestRender, host and [gpu]): the camera's fit and placement; a sphere covering exactly the pixels whose rays meet it; a hole seen from inside, tinted and reported; colouring by primitive; the section plane; a ray-traced mesh cube cut by the plane drawn as the closed-form box; faces on the bounding box; a cancelled render; view() with --no-view; rejected settings and cameras; a device render of a tape against the host's. InstantiateAll covers the renderer in both precisions.
Probe Performance/Render: one frame with shadows and occlusion of the classic CSG solid through a tape and of the armadillo as a TriMeshSDF, on one host thread, and its pixels as a host/device pair in row order. #216's camera-ray firstHit() cases are left out: the Ray and RayCSG probes time those paths now.
Docs: Rendering.rst (concepts) and ImplemRender.rst rewritten around the renderer; #216's versions re-described the ray queries as they were before #228/#237, which Rays.rst/ImplemRays.rst now cover. Also: the viewer option (ConfigurationOptions.rst), rendering on a GPU (RunningOnGPU.rst), TestRender (TestingLocally.rst), the Render probe (PerformanceTesting.rst, Performance/README.md), the MeshSDF example page, the index and the README feature lists. ContributionGuidelines.rst and CLAUDE.md describe the out-of-line ray helpers; the docs audit fixed four places that still said a device render goes through GPU::transform().
Side-effects
EBGeometry.hpp includes the renderer and the viewer (the window itself only with EBGEOMETRY_VIEWER_SDL2).
The EBGeometry CMake target links Threads::Threads; the installed package's config finds it.
EBGeometry_RayImplem.hpp and EBGeometry_RaySpans.hpp: the out-of-line helpers above; march() gives the same crossings (its exits are reordered, not changed).
Alternative solutions
Port [DO NOT MERGE] Renderer: ray queries on any shape, a renderer and an SDL2 viewer (1/3) #214's renderer instead of [DO NOT MERGE] Ray tape: closed-form ray forms, the tape's ray program, a generic renderer (3/3) #216's: [DO NOT MERGE] Renderer: ray queries on any shape, a renderer and an SDL2 viewer (1/3) #214 carried hard-coded ray-tracing branches for interactive speed, which the original plan said to remove once rays were general; [DO NOT MERGE] Ray tape: closed-form ray forms, the tape's ray program, a generic renderer (3/3) #216 had removed them.
One PR with every example calling view(): decided against (step 2), to keep this review on the renderer.

The ray code asked a shape for its value, its primitive and its trace
from many places, and every call was inlined: march() held about thirty
copies of a mesh's distance (its value, bisection, and at each of four
exits the primitive and a six-value gradient), leafHit() and the span
helpers three copies of an operand's trace. Device compiles took ptxas
gigabytes and up to hours.

RayDetail::valueAt(), primitiveOf(), gradientNormal(), march() and the
new shapeHit() (what the span helpers and leafHit() call) are now out of
line on a device; march() reaches its bisection and its crossing from
one place each. On the host nothing changes in the Tape's evaluator (nm
of the Tape and ClosestPrimitive probes matches dev), and the float tape
kernels keep their 168 registers.

481 s / 13 GB -> 75 s / 2.9 GB. A renderer pixel kernel on a tape with
assertions on: 2052 s -> 51 s, so the device render test draws a tape
again. All GPU test targets build in 131 s.

Start on the surface (|v0| < eps): the entering flag is still value(min(t0 + 2·eps, t1)) < v0, and the old early return becomes the shared exit.
Sign change in the sphere-tracing loop: this is bracketed, then bisection, then a hit with entering = !inside. That matches the old code.
|v| < eps in the sphere-tracing loop: this is onSurface at t, with entering = !inside, as before.
Fixed-step branch: a sign change sets bracketed, as before.
step == 0: the sign test cannot fire here, because inside is derived from v0. Bisection starting at tPrev is therefore never given an invalid bracket.
Unresolved fallthrough: it is unchanged.
Bisection runs the same 64 iterations with the same stop test, so the crossings should be identical, as the PR says. One extra bool of live state per call is irrelevant next to the compile-time gains.
Device-noinline helpers: fine, with one note
Every new EBGEOMETRY_DEVICE_NOINLINE function has [[nodiscard]], EBGEOMETRY_HOST_DEVICE, and a Doxygen block with the @tparam/@param/@return entries.

What is asked
Evaluate a dual mesher (one vertex per cell placed by a QEF over Hermite data, or Dual Marching Cubes) for crests and crystalline features.
Why it matters: Sharp features are what make ridgelines and rock read as rock, and Marching Cubes rounds them off.
What is true today
Nothing yet.
What is missing
The density field and a measurement that settles the reports' disagreement.
How we will know it is done
A measurement on the same density chunk compares triangle count, manifoldness and ridge error for Marching Cubes and one dual mesher, and the choice is recorded.

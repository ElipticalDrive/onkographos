Illuminated particles now receive the scene's sun shadows: smoke behind a rock or building darkens instead of staying sunlit. Sky and point-light contributions remain visible, and unlit particles (including unlit glow) retain their previous behavior.
The particle shader shares the existing cascade lookup, filtering and soft-sun sampling with surfaces. Shadow uniform lookup and cascade uploads are shared between both programs, with cached locations and the existing shadow texture. Local lighting and sunlight are kept separate before shadowing to preserve weaker local contributions under a strong sun. Shadow casting remains opt-in sprite cutouts; this does not add volumetric density or point-light shadow casting.
Validation covers one and four cascades, soft sun, clearing a caster, unlit/glowing particles, and preservation of ambient and point lighting. Captured frames were inspected. Full unit/integration and render regression suites, include-layer checks and repository lint passed.
Rebased onto current main and #41 for 0.4.1. The full unit/integration suite and focused particle/emitter/WebP render tests passed, as did include-layer checks and formatting. Prior full rendering regression suites and repository lint also passed.
Stacked on #41; this diff contains particle sun-shadow reception and the shared shadow-uniform code.

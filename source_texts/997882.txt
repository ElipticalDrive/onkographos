Root cause
Class: a crossing statement whose two lines are named by letters draws those lines, or not, depending on its grammatical frame (point-first noun or lines-first verb), so two spellings of one fact give two figures.
This is not a missing mechanism. It is a deliberate branch that ADR-592 placed at the one place 2-D lowers every lettered line∩line crossing: cross() inside lineLineIntersection (src/parser/parse.ts ~2225). The point-first branch calls cross(…, 'point'), and subject === 'point' returns [...ensuredEnds, inter] before the operand ink (pre.push({ type: 'segment' … })) that the verb, lines-first and cut branches reach. So the verb «נחתכים» mints its segments in the same function and on the same lines that the noun skips. Every noun spelling in the matrix is the same pointFirst match, which is why they all fail together and the role nouns (specialPointMeet) do not.
Analytic mirrors it. operandPieces (src-analytic/parser/parseAnalytic.ts ~1880) is attached only by the verb readers (meetFrame, cuts, cutsTwo via withOperandPieces). It is never attached by the noun readers: parseIntersection's own INTERSECT_HE/EN branch, the distributive branch of intersectionSpellings, and meetingSpelling.
Mechanism and files
2-D: converge the noun onto the verb's ink in cross. No new branch.
src/parser/parse.ts, cross: delete the early return [...pre, inter] of the point frame. The ink then follows the operand semantics alone, identical for both frames: bare and line operands draw the whole segment before the intersection; an extension draws base→M after it.
What the point frame keeps is only the ensured ends (free-point … ifAbsent, ADR-236). Without them the extension noun («M נקודת החיתוך של המשכי AB ו-CD») would turn from builds into the verb's missingOperands refusal on an empty canvas, and verdicts must not move in this item.
Preferred variant: ensure only the endpoints of ext operands, because a bare or line operand's segment introduces its own ends. That also closes 2-D: «O נקודת החיתוך של AC ו-BD» on an existing quadrilateral emits redundant free-point commands for A–D (internal, no visible effect) #2179 (no redundant free-point commands over an existing quadrilateral). The fallback, if the pickup check below says so, is to keep the four ensures as the prototype does.
Rename the parameter subject: CrossingSubject → ensureEnds: boolean. CrossingSubject and crossingSubjectOf stay for specialPointMeet's role-diagonal branch only (ruling 2, Q2).
Positions do not move: the noun's start positions are unchanged, and segment on existing points never moves them. onSeg / onSeg1/2 / dir1/2 are byte-identical, so there is no engine, replay, solver or render change. The dashed off-ink stretch already serves the «הישרים» case (ADR-612).

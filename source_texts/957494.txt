🤖 Filed by Aria's agent swarm, Dyna, not by Aria.
Ask. Add a client-side add_section_geometry hook to section mesh building in crates/horizon-client-render/src/mesher.rs, with a mesh-worker-safe mod_api context (no Game access; see crates/horizon-mod-api/src/mesher.rs). Keep the contributed geometry inside the section mesh so it follows that section's rebuild and unload instead of being submitted every frame, and reuse crates/horizon-client/src/game/native_render_visibility.rs. Done when a test mod's geometry shows up, is not duplicated after a remesh, and disappears when the section unloads.
Background. NeoForge's AddSectionGeometryEvent lets a mod put extra quads into a chunk section's mesh. It was an unchecked item under Rendering in the old tracker #27 and a D7 row in #44's NeoForge coverage audit, marked "after render visibility" (the section visibility support that now exists). No mod has asked for it yet. Related but separate: SubmitCustomGeometryEvent (per-frame geometry).
Hook or handle. add_section_geometry
NeoForge 26.3 counterpart. AddSectionGeometryEvent
Who uses it. not recorded

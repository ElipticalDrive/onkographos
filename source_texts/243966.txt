The four listed scans ship as SPZ instead of .ply: about 13 times smaller and about 10 times faster to unpack. This also fixes a latent climbing bug that SPZ's slightly different Counter mesh exposed.
SPZ
Scan
.ply → .spz
Kitchen
40.3 → 3.1 MB
Living Room
33.4 → 2.2 MB
Counter
30.7 → 2.3 MB
Curled Up Dog
30.9 → 2.8 MB
Converted with @playcanvas/splat-transform@3.8.0 (SPZ v4). Unpacking went from ~500 ms to ~45 ms. They look the same side by side.
load-scan.worker.ts picks the parser by the file's bytes (ply at the start, else three's SPZLoader), so blob: imports still work. Importing still takes .ply only.
Kitchen's collision moved slightly (reach from spawn 75.8% → 74.1%), caused by SPZ's position rounding (max 1.2e-4 scan units). The mechanism is not known; recorded in context/collision.md. The baseline is re-saved.
Climbing fix
Two climb moves placed the whole body from one ray at one height. Where the face is set back at that height (a recess, or under a lip), the rest of the body landed inside what sticks out, deeper than the push-out acts on:
the snap that holds the gap to the face: 1.23 and 1.15 radii deep (Counter .spz, seeds 1 and 5);
the lip grab: 0.82 radii into the lip (Counter .spz, seed 6), never pushed out if the figurine then rests.
fitClimbBody is now the one place that pushes a climber out of an overlap up to a radius and refuses anything deeper. A too-deep snap is undone for that frame; a too-deep grab is refused. Rule 12 in context/climb.md.
Verified
climbfuzz 60 s, seeds 1–6, on Counter and Kitchen, .spz and .ply (24 runs): 0 violations, 0 page errors, largest sink 0.24 radii (unchanged from before the fix).
uicheck ALL PASS; collisioncheck NOTHING WORSE; typecheck and build pass.
Not verified: Living Room and Curled Up Dog climbs (blind walks rarely reach their walls), and the live site with SPZ (that happens after the upload and merge).
Deploy order
node scripts/upload-scans.mjs uploads the four .spz files; the .ply files stay, so the live site keeps working.
Merge; Vercel deploys.
Check https://wee.samanthamlee.com loads .spz, then delete the old .ply objects from the bucket.

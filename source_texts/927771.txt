This replaces the two perpendicular sheets with the field as it physically is: Re(E(x, z)·e^{i(βy − ωt)}), β = 2π n_eff/λ, filling the guide and its evanescent tails.
Rendering: a ShaderMaterial on a box. In x and z the box covers the area where |E| > 2·10⁻³ of its peak; along y it covers the window's depth. The fragment shader ray-marches it front to back, 128 to 400 steps with per-pixel jitter, sampling f = E(x, z)·cos(β(y − cut) − ωt)·e^(−α(y − y₀)). E(x, z) comes from a half-float, linearly filtered texture of the signed component (ModeField), and the travel along y is computed analytically, so there is no 3D texture. Each step uses emission-absorption with opacity ∝ |f|^1.7 × density: red where f > 0, blue where f < 0, nothing near zero. The output is premultiplied, so the field glows over what is behind it.
Depth: the volume is drawn on its near faces with the depth test on, so opaque things in front hide it. From inside the box it switches to the far faces without the depth test. While the wave shows, the solid shapes it runs through (the core) become glass at opacity 0.15 with their edges still drawn; turning the wave off restores the user's own look.
Display: a snapshot is a train of lobes, each λ/(2 n_eff) long, gliding along +y. Higher-order modes show their sign changes across the section.
Overlay and panel: the overlay now reads "Re E_x, travelling along +y · guided wavelength λ/n_eff = 0.633 µm · phase velocity c/n_eff = 0.41 c · shown about 3 × 10¹⁴ times slower". The panel adds a density slider next to play/pause and speed. Older runs fall back to |E| and say "magnitude only: an older run".
Decay: for a lossy mode the amplitude falls along +y, the way it travels. It is normalised at the back of the window, so the cut face shows the attenuated end.
Checked: pnpm build passes; no Rust change. The app was built with pnpm tauri build, run, screenshotted over CDP and closed:
strip-modes: mode 1 (TE, a single lobe across, 0.316 µm lobes) and mode 2 (TM, Re E_z above and below the core);
a 1.2 µm strip with modes = 4: TE1 forms a checkerboard (sign change across x), TE2 shows three lobes across;
dark and light themes.

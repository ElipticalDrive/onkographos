Published on Apr 22, 2017
This video shows smoothly rendered voxel models using a GPU path tracer I made. These are some of the results of my master thesis work about Level-of-Detail Independent Voxel-Based Surface Approximations.
The results here only show the finest level of detail of the voxel models, which is 1024^3 voxels per model. The video only shows the current convergence rate of the path tracer with the ray depth capped at 3 bounces. I'm using a GGX BRDF and importance sampling to speed up the process of finding good samples for the Monte-Carlo based path tracer.
You can find more information about my thesis, or how I made this on my blog: http://robbinmarcus.blogspot.com

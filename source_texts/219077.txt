What
ca_core::octree: a nested octree in the style of Potree.
Inner nodes keep at most one point per cell of a 128³ lattice, and every point lives in exactly one node.
Points are reordered in place (sequential passes plus an American-flag partition), so each node is one contiguous range. The only extra memory is a u32 index per point during the build.
Build time: 1M points 0.15 s, 10M points 1.3 s (native). An earlier indirect-index version took 4.4 s for 10M.
WASM: Cloud.parse builds the octree and stores the cloud in octree order, so positions, colors and C2C distances all share that order and no remapping is needed. lodNodes() / lodGrid expose the node table.
Web:
lod.ts refines nodes with a max-heap keyed by on-screen point spacing, applies frustum culling, and stops at the point budget or at 1 px spacing.
viewer.ts draws one THREE.Points per node. Geometry is a zero-copy view into the cloud's buffers and is created lazily. GPU buffers of hidden nodes are evicted above 2× the budget.
UI: a Point budget selector (1M–20M) and a "Drawing X of Y points" readout in the status bar.
Verification
4 new octree tests: every point stored exactly once and inside its node's cube; subtrees contiguous in pre-order; at most one point per cell in inner nodes; duplicate points stop at max depth.
Browser, 10M-point terrain: the overview draws 1.5M points. Zooming in fills the 3M budget, and zooming closer culls nodes (205–313 nodes). Selection takes about 1–15 ms (38 ms when nodes are first created).
C2C results are unchanged after the reordering: sample mean 0.022061; 1M-point pool run mean 0.055383.
Known limits
Loading 10M points takes about 11 s in the test browser (fetch over the LAN, parse, octree, transfer). Streaming and a progress indicator are follow-ups.

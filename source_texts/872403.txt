Adds interpolation: 'trilinear' | 'tetrahedral' to the lut effect (default trilinear), threaded through packages/scene, scene parsing, resolve, the compositor uniform and lut.wgsl. The HyperFrames colorGrade LUT path (color_grade.wgsl, HF's own hand-written trilinear) is untouched, so its 0.114 mean-level diff is unchanged.
Tests: crates/core/tests/lut.rs renders both modes on a 5-point nonlinear table and compares with a CPU reference (max 1 level, mean 0.03) and with ffmpeg lut3d when ffmpeg is on PATH (max 1, mean 0.48 for both modes; ffmpeg rounds differently). Trilinear vs tetrahedral differ by max 14, mean 0.535 levels. Lib tests (163) and gradient goldens (4) pass.
Docs: SCENE.md, ENGINE.md, CHANGELOG, scene catalog.

Reuse one FP32 volume-gradient buffer across views and combine groups in FP64. Fixed-majorant transport now jumps directly to collision candidates or the grid boundary.
The Torch path groups eight views with FP32 sampling and two with FP64 sampling. These group sizes preserve the existing signed-gradient tolerances. Final narrowing reuses the same buffer and precedes the finite-gradient check. Gradient buffers occupy 192 MiB for a 256³ volume, up from 128 MiB.
Woodcock flights retain real/null scores, energy accounting and derivative replay. Current-energy bounds preserve failure reporting when interpolation consumes majorant slack; mixed-rate readout and next-event integrals retain cellwise traversal.

Projection medians use 30 completed calls per operation and 1024 samples per ray. For 360 views, zeros_like calls fall from 360 to 1, zero fills from 360 to 45, and full-volume adds from 359 to 44. Forward images match bitwise; signed and unsigned gradients meet the existing thresholds.
Transport medians use ten completed calls of 65,536 histories, with water-equivalent CT attenuation and isotropic elastic scattering. Every saved history matches the baseline: scores, pixels, energies, statuses, real/null counts and density derivatives. Both cases have zero failures and exact energy balance.
Validation: 164 final Torch CPU/CUDA cases; 53 additional CUDA stream, graph and autograd cases; 102 transport-reference CPU cases; 42 targeted transport CPU/CUDA cases and 29 existing physical/readout CPU cases. Memcheck reports zero errors across 42 projection and 14 transport CUDA cases; synccheck reports zero errors across 14 transport cases. Ruff, strict documentation build, wheel/sdist build and installed-wheel imports pass. The Torch adapter has the same 129 Pyright diagnostics as the baseline.

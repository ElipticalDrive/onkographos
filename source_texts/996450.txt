Done after #16
regions subdivisions of the world map
regions are composed of several height maps that are composed (via matrix multiplication) together
base heightmap: a smooth heightmap formed by using linear interpolation from the height of the region center point (using the world map's height for this region) and the northeast, northwest, southeast, southwest points (average of this region's center point and the adjacent region's center point) This heightmap must be smooth in order for the regions to line up.
region heightmap using Olsen noise tiled such that this heightmap lines up with adjacent region's heightmaps
river heightmap: the heightmap (with negative values to reduce land height) as part of the river segment generation

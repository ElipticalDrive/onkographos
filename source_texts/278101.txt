The greedy mesh in packages/thing-export/src/glb.ts, exportGlb merges faces, so wear cannot act per voxel on a merged mesh. A per-voxel-face mesh is not built. The open decision is whether wear needs an unmerged mesh for worn parts only, or a wear map in UV space. PR #1172 left this intent without a trigger.
Docs that state this intent: packages/thing-export/README.md, "Voxelized export".

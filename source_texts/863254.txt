If we actually move meshing to the GPU, the blockers are not the compute pass itself: flattening the dynamic model table, neighbor/AO/fluid resolution, deterministic output ordering, and VRAM/bandwidth. I'd keep this as a narrow full-cube/opaque spike first — the CPU mesher is already page-parallel and only ~28% of wall time on the largest fixture.

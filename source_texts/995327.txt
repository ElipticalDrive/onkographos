Issue body actions
Narrow projected bands currently require consumers to implement nearest-segment distance, anisotropic pixel-footprint projection and filtered coverage themselves. General mask callbacks accept those results but do not supply the reusable kernel.
Add a small renderer-neutral segment sampler and footprint-aware band kernel, with matching GLSL helpers through existing public subpaths. Keep candidate-segment selection/spatial indexing, world projection, material appearance and minimum-visibility policy caller-owned. Handle zero-length/tiny segments and zero widths explicitly; do not use an epsilon denominator that changes the nearest-point parameter or undefined equal-edge smoothstep.
Acceptance:
Focused analytic, finite-arithmetic, endpoint, degenerate, frame/scale, ownership and callback-independent tests.
Paired CPU/GLSL readback for straight, diagonal, endpoint and subpixel cases over the documented Float32 input range.
Explicit footprint convention and decomposition so caller visibility floors do not become hidden semantic behavior.
Same-camera narrow-band near/far proof with resource costs, plus registry consumer migration before claiming duplicate removal.
Root/API/architecture/llms documentation, renderer-free import and package gates. No new simulation, spatial framework or material catalogue.

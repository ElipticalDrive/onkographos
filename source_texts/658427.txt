Two way you could approach this
Directly unpoject the point cloud with max res that's offered for chunks and stitch them up with some overlap, If your objective is a 3D reconstruction and generate mesh this should be suffice as DA3 gives really dense clouds. You could also chek VGGT_long / Pi-LOng which are very impressive though they arent metric scale and may have drift,
Using these point cloud as initializers for usual 3dGS ,, I was trying the dense cloud output from VGGT training, indeed i was getting bad results in 3dgs however for same images with a colmap sparse clouds my rendering were very good.

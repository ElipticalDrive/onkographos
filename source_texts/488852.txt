The 3D data field volume rendering technique is one of the most important research domains in scientific visualization.On the basis of researching and concluding the development procedure of volume rendering and its key technology,combined with multi-core cluster,a parallel ray casting volume rendering algorithm based on hierarchical parallel programming model has been proposed in this paper.This hierarchical parallel algorithm has been used in 3D city shallow geological model successfully.According to the comparative experiments and the analysis of different computation scale on ray casting algorithm between the pure MPI programming model and the hierarchical parallel MPI + OpenMP programming model,the results show that the hierarchical parallel ray casting algorithm accelerates the volume rendering speed,the results also show the hierarchical parallel MPI + OpenMP programming model has better performance than pure MPI programming model.

A consistent method for grouping of catastrophic and noncatastrophic interactions in mixed Monte Carlo schemes has been developed. Energy-loss straggling between discrete catastrophic energy losses is taken into account by sampling from a restricted energy straggling distribution derived from the energy loss theory of Vavilov applied on the interaction cross section of M0ller. Similarly, multiple small-angle scattering interactions between catastrophic large-angle deflections are taken into account by using a restricted scattering power which includes only scattering events smaller than a certain cut-off angle b.

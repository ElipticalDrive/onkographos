Abstract
en
A method for the compression of images comprises two steps. A first step of vector quantization compresses a source image into an image of indices that is coherent in relation to the source image. A second step again compresses this image of the indices according to a prediction method using a network of multilayer neurons.
Application: compression of images for their storage and transmission.

Optimized the low-resolution cloud marching shader and related host code:
Evaluated Spherical Harmonic (SH) probes exactly once per ray/pixel, outside of the marching loop, at a representative probePos along the ray, avoiding redundant evaluations.
Switched cloud shadow sampling to a stochastic model: using one sample per light per step, randomly spaced along the likely check range (traceDist), and computed the optical depth analytically over that distance.
Reconnected temporal history-guided start/stop points (expectedStart and expectedEnd) to clamp the raymarching interval, and smoothly reduced the history confidence/increased the search margin leeway when historical density is very low to robustly handle rapid scene changes.
Converted linear step size growth along the ray to be geometric (multiplied by a constant stepGrowthRate of 1.015 at each step), prioritizing samples at the front of the ray.
Introduced and tracked a new vec3 uniform 'uCloudAlbedo' representing cloud single-scattering albedo to account for multi-wavelength light absorption.
Exposed the new 'Cloud Albedo' control via an ImGui ColorEdit3 picker under the 'Cloud Quality' collapsing header in RenderWidget.cpp.
Verified all C++ compilation, shader validations with glcheck, and the entire test suite passes perfectly.

This pins Helio to the voxel planet work in Far-Beyond-Pulsar/Helio#353 (draft) and updates Pulsar for it.
What the Helio pin brings
The pin moves to Helio main at b4547fd2 (Helio#353 merged, with Helio main's rigid frames, incremental hierarchy resolve and portals merged in).
Chunk octree. A 3D chunk octree with ray-guided residency and a footprint pool.
Per-voxel materials. Edited cells and dug walls draw their own material instead of their leaf's. PickHit reports the drawn material.
Material contract. The generation kernel now evaluates materials as Planet::material does. Picks, dig yields and drawn colours agree, where 40.5% of natural mountain pixels used to differ.
Edit index. A brush index replaces the per-chunk scan of every brush.
Edits on data. Large brushes are baked into the data, so 10,000 large digs cost about what 100 do. Edited chunks the renderer evicts are kept and come back by copy.
Faster edits and leaner residency. Brushes bake about 90x faster, so a sculpt stroke no longer waits 10–16 s for its world build. Chunks the view no longer needs are released: after a flight, the same view holds 20k resident chunks instead of 115k.
Natural per-voxel materials. Every chunk near the eye draws each cell's own material: mixed natural leaves used to be 3.56% of natural pixels wrong, now none, for +9% generation.
Kept chunks in saves. A journal can carry the renderer's chunks for its edits (VoxelKeptChunks, saved as kept), and the pass loads and exports them through its frame.
f64 scene joins. Object transform rows carry the f64 position as a high and a low f32. The joins rebase them to the frame origin pair by pair, and the hierarchy resolve composes parent chains in pair arithmetic, guarded against NVIDIA's driver reassociating the two-sum.
Terrain shadows on meshes. A mesh in front of the terrain, or against the sky, walks its own sun ray through the voxel cut, so it no longer takes the shade of the ground behind it.
Pulsar changes
f64 scene positions (94ee88ea0). Transform.position is [f64; 3] engine-wide, so an object on a planet keeps its millimetres; an f32 position 6,371 km out steps by half a metre.
The GPU gets a derived row, TransformRow ("object_transforms", 48 bytes): the position as a high and a low f32.
CPU hierarchy math is f64 (DMat4/DQuat).
The editor's gizmo and picks work in f32 offsets from the view's f64 eye. The properties panel edits numbers as f64 (NumberFieldBinding).
Scene files, the editor's scene data, class plans, AI tool specs and SetTransform take f64 positions.
Still f32 at their boundary: physics (Rapier, via Far-Beyond-Pulsar/Plugin_Physics#4, draft), the script VM's Vec3, debug lines, the game camera and the terrain tool's source bounds.
voxel_backend.rs: the edit-ray test builds a PickHit, which gained material. It's set to 0, since an edit ray takes the cell.
Harness memory report (5252679c2): every voxel_editor_harness step prints the live Rust heap (a counting allocator), kept and resident chunks, and wgpu's allocator report by label.
Saves keep the edited chunks (342598aed). A loaded level copies what its edits made, instead of generating it from the terrain and every edit.
HelioRenderer owns a VoxelChunkSaves request/answer handle. The planet backend answers it, and the editor gets it through HelioEditorMailbox and keeps it in SceneDomain.voxel_chunk_saves.
save_blocking runs keep_voxel_chunks after compacting the journals. It asks only when a terrain has edits but no kept chunks, and waits up to 5 s.
The backend has its pass pack the chunks through PlanetFrame.export. It answers for the journal the shown planet was built from, and asks again if the world changed meanwhile.
The save deflates the chunks off the render thread. It keeps them only if the journal is unchanged; any later edit drops them.
A rebuilt world parses the journal's kept chunks on the world-builder thread and passes them in PlanetFrame.kept. The pass loads them into the new world.
Pins: Helio and the physics plugin are on their main (Helio#353 and Plugin_Physics#4 merged); no pin exceptions are left.
Merge of Pulsar main (ad7f2f332): rigid frames, sublevels, level instances and portals are kept whole, on f64 positions. frames.rs's own f64 chain composition is gone; hierarchy_transform's is the same and is f64 now. Also fixes main's import_state test (ImportRecord gained companions).
Docs: 0158ee23c makes the voxel system docs describe the chunk octree.
Merge: Pulsar main is merged in (latest 621eea066). The conflicts were the Helio gitlink, resolved to the new pin, and the render module's re-exports.
Tests
cargo test -p engine_backend voxel: 28 tests pass against the new pin.
New voxel_kept_chunks (engine_backend, headless editor renderer). A save waiting on another thread is answered in 7 frames with 90 chunks (105 KiB). A new renderer given the saved journal loads all 90.
New a_level_save_keeps_the_renderers_voxel_chunks (ui_level_editor). It runs the editor's real save while the viewport draws; the written level loads with the same journal and its kept chunks. All 9 ui::save tests pass.
New a_mesh_on_a_planet_keeps_its_millimetres (engine_backend). A mesh under a turned folder 6,371 km out, in a frame rebased near it, lands within a tenth of a millimetre of the CPU's f64 placement. It caught the driver folding the two-sum.
f64 positions: pulsar_scene_model, pulsar_world_registry, pulsar_class, pulsar_scene, pulsar_game, engine_backend's scene_join_rows, environment_join_rows and movement_scaling, and physics_plugin (70 tests) all pass. ui_level_editor --lib: 133 pass; one fails, cursor_capture_load_resolves_legacy_conflict_to_rotation in viewport cursor capture, which this change doesn't touch.
scripts/submodule-pins.sh check passes, with the Helio and physics pins listed as exceptions.
Helio's own suites are in Helio#353, including scene_join and environment_join with positions 6,371 km out, and the new mesh-shadow test.

ABSTRACT
We present two new distance-sampling methods for production volume path tracing. We extend the null-collision integral formulation
to efficiently gather heterogeneous volumetric emission, achieving
higher-quality results. Additionally, we propose a tabulation-based
approach to importance sample volumetric in-scattering through a
spatial guiding data structure. Our methods improve the sampling
efficiency for scenarios where low-order heterogeneous scattering
dominates, which tends to cause high variance renderings with
existing null-collision methods.

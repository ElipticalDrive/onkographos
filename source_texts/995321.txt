The within-distance of geometries with faces lying in a plane in 3D, and the ties of points and segments it meets, read their exact signs first in doubles and in pairs of doubles, and read the faces in place on the serialized form.
Read the exact tie of a point or a segment against a segment first in pairs of doubles. point_segment_offset_sign decides where the nearest point of a segment lies inside it on (u . u)(w . w - r^2) - (w . u)^2, formed in a 66-limb integer (point_segment_side_sign_inputs), and segment_interior_offset_sign decides whether two segments in 3D are within a radius inside both on WideDyadic (segment_interior_wide_values), each on every call. Both read the same values first in pairs of doubles carrying a bound on their error (DDBound): a sign the bound decides, and a value with no error that is zero, is the answer, and only a value the bound cannot tell reaches the exact computation, which is unchanged.
Read the signs of the faces in 3D in doubles before pairs of doubles. vector_cross_signs, vector_triple_sign, plane_cross_dot_sign and plane_point_offset_sign form their values first in doubles from differences rounded once, as triple_product_sign filters a determinant by Shewchuk's bound (dbl_cross3); a component of a cross product whose differences and products are exact and equal is an exact zero, and a determinant of differences held exactly goes to expansions before pairs of doubles.
Read the faces of a geometry in 3D in place on its serialized form. The point arrays reference the serialized coordinates, as geom_distance_gs_arrays reads them, counted first and held on the stack until they outgrow it (geom_faces_init); an LWGEOM is built only where lwgeom_mindistance3d_tolerance answers, a polygon off its plane, a solid or an arc.
Read the ties of points and segments at a distance in doubles first. A pair with a segment of no length skips the test of nearest points inside both; the side of a point at a distance of another reads the squared distance in pairs of doubles before its exact expansion (point_distance_offset_pair_side_sign); and the signs placing the nearest points of two segments are read in doubles before pairs (segment_interior_offset_filter).

The strict suite passes on PostgreSQL 18, both Windows workflows pass, and every MEOS test runs clean under valgrind.
Cost against GEOS called directly: distance_geo reads 0.174 of GEOS alone, and 25 of the 26 speed cells stand at parity. The other, distance_curves, reads 5.689 and judges no answer: every row carries an arc, which the GEOS arm times as a chord chain stroked before the timed pass, so the two arms time different geometries and the cell is a cost of the route only.
Why: a question asked at the double nearest the distance paid the exact computation on every pair the walk kept, and every face paid its signs in pairs of doubles and its reading through an LWGEOM.

`vector_cross_signs`, `vector_triple_sign`, `plane_cross_dot_sign` and
`plane_point_offset_sign` decide the signs the within-distance of faces lying in
a plane reads, to tell a face's plane, whether a vertex lies in it, whether a
point lies inside it and its distance to the plane. Each formed its value in
pairs of doubles with a bound on its error (`DDBound`) on every call, a sign
far from 0 included. Each now forms it first in doubles from differences
rounded once, as `triple_product_sign` filters a determinant of exact
coordinates by Shewchuk's bound: a component of a cross product within 2^-50 of
the sum of the magnitudes of its products (`dbl_cross3`), the determinant within
2^-48 of its permanent, the degree-four sign within 2^-48 of the sum of the
products of those sums, and the degree-six sign within 2^-46 of E1^2 + d^2 S2,
each where no step leaves the normal doubles. A sign those bounds do not tell
is read as before; a component of a cross product whose two differences and
two products are exact and whose products are equal is an exact zero, as the
plane of a face holding an axis has; a determinant of differences held exactly
as doubles goes to expansions before pairs of doubles
(`triple_product_sign_exact`), since the exact zero of a vertex lying in a
plane is one that pairs of doubles never tell.

`geom_dwithin3d` read two geometries with faces lying in a plane from the
LWGEOM that `lwgeom_from_gserialized` builds, one allocation per member and per
point array, and allocated the arrays of their point arrays and faces. It now
reads the serialized form in place, the point arrays referencing the serialized
coordinates as `geom_distance_gs_arrays` reads them (`geom_faces_gs_read`): the
point arrays and the faces are counted first (`geom_faces_gs_count`), so the
arrays holding them, and the rings a face references, are sized once, held on
the stack of the structure until they outgrow it, as `geom_distance2d_exact`
holds its point arrays (`geom_faces_init`, `geom_faces_free`). The LWGEOM is
built only where the faces do not answer, a polygon off its plane, a solid or
an arc, which `lwgeom_mindistance3d_tolerance` reads.

static dwithin witnesses of MobilityDB#3484, every row agreeing. `geo_test` passes.

A question asked at the double nearest a distance meets ties: the pair of a
vertex of one geometry and a segment of the other nearest at it is asked at a
distance its own lies within half a unit in the last place of. Three of the
signs it reads paid exact or pair arithmetic on every such pair.

- `geom_dwithin_segs` asked whether the nearest points of two segments lie
  inside both even where a segment has no length: such a pair is a point,
  nearest the other segment at itself, which the ends read, and it is passed
  over.
- `point_distance_offset_pair_side_sign`, the side of a point at a distance of
  another, read the squared distance less the square of the distance as an
  exact expansion on every call. It reads it first in pairs of doubles, as
  `segment_interior_mid_sign` reads its own, the differences exact as pairs
  (`two_diff`) and the value within 2^-96 of the squared distance plus the
  square, a sign that bound does not tell going to the expansion as before.
- `segment_interior_offset_filter` formed in pairs of doubles the signs that
  place the nearest points of two segments. It forms them first in doubles
  (`dbl_cross3`), one known on the wrong side answering that the nearest points
  do not lie inside both.

Witness: CGAL on the head alone, every dumper rebuilt against it, over the 20
static dwithin witnesses of MobilityDB#3484, every row agreeing, and over the 19
distance witnesses of ED8 the rounding reads, 296729 rows: points and
geometries in 2D (4405) and 3D (2300), buffers (2100), segments (8200), the
rest (1040 and 1488), random and resting points (200000 and 4061), the
within-distance (68435) and the rows beyond the domain and at every scale,
every row agreeing; the two ED8 witnesses of moving points are not read, their
dumper no longer building on the current API. `geo_test` passes.

Measured: against `lwgeom_mindistance3d_tolerance` called directly in one
process, by callgrind, faces random asked at the distance 1.326 -> 1.088, at
twice it plus one 0.784 and at half of it 0.414 unchanged; refuting 3.226 ->
3.131, 1.461 -> 1.460, 1.342 -> 1.349; real outlines 0.050 -> 0.047.

After the far band landed (bf4d3be): in dense forest at eye level the agent measured 5.97 M triangles drawn at 7 fps with three viewers sharing the GPU; unmeasured alone. The grown 1-voxel stage reaches 1.5 N (main.rs GROWN_REACH) where the projected-size rule asks for 4 N.
Do: measure alone at eye level in dense forest (drawn triangles, fps, with and without far shadows); if bound, first candidates are residency by distance for the window's own bands (#10), instanced variants for the window's 1-voxel band, and a lower GROWN_REACH with a wider crossfade into the crown stage. Also re-measure the dry-grass tint after the DRY_GRASS change (fine (102,125,72) vs coarse (138,165,118) before it).

Is the commit above going in the right direction?
Here is a preview for your convenience:
```
signedDistanceAlong : Types.Axis2d units coordinates -> BoundingBox2d units coordinates -> Interval Float units
signedDistanceAlong axis boundingBox =

let
    b1 =
        extrema boundingBox
in
Interval.from
    (Point2d.signedDistanceAlong axis (Point2d.xy b1.maxX b1.maxY))
    (Point2d.signedDistanceAlong axis (Point2d.xy b1.minX b1.minY))
```

Going in the right direction @matyjas but I don't think it will be quite that simple! For example if you had an axis pointing down and to the right (+X, -Y) then the minimum signed distance along the axis would be that corresponding to the upper left corner (min X, max Y).
I think there will need to be some logic that checks the signs of the X and Y components of the axis direction and then figures out based on that which two corners of the bounding box correspond to the min/max distances. (Alternatively you could get the signed distances of all four corners and then just find the min/max of those, but I'm guessing that would be slightly more expensive.)

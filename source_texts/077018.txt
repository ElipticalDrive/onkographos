Adds Splat Local to the Gaussian splatting software section (alphabetical order, matching the existing entry format).
It's an open-source (MIT) video → 3D Gaussian Splatting pipeline that runs entirely locally on Apple Silicon — no CUDA, no cloud: sharp-frame selection, COLMAP/pycolmap poses (with a quality-gated GLOMAP global mapper), Metal-native training via Brush, and export to .ply/.spz/.sog. Training checkpoints stream into a live browser viewer while it runs.
Live demo of a reconstruction, explorable in the browser: https://michael-l-i.github.io/splat-local/
Disclosure: I'm the author. Also listed in MrNeRF/awesome-3D-gaussian-splatting.

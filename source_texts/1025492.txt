# Hands-On Ultrasound Image Reconstruction Tutorial

## Introduction

This tutorial demonstrates how ultrasound B-mode images are reconstructed from Radio Frequency (RF) signals using delay-and-sum beamforming.

## Ultrasound Reconstruction Process

Ultrasound image formation follows this approach:

1. **RF signals** are generated from ultrasound pulse-echo reflections
2. Array transducer elements receive echoes at slightly different times
3. **Beamforming** (delay-and-sum) coherently combines these signals
4. **Envelope detection** extracts the signal amplitude
5. **Log compression** creates the final B-mode image

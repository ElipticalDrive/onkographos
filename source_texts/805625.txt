Sep 20, 2026Relevant source files Block-sparse attention enables efficient computation of attention when the attention mask follows a structured block-sparse pattern. FlashInfer implements block-sparse attention using the Block Sparse Row (BSR) matrix format, allowing arbitrary block sizes and optional per-element masks within blocks.

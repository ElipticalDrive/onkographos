Question
How does pbrt-v4 make a pixel sample reproducible from (pixel, sample index, seed) alone, and what does RandomWalkIntegrator compute with it?
Cover: IndependentSampler (and, for comparison, the default ZSobolSampler) — StartPixelSample, hashing/seeding, PCG32 sequence and Advance, dimension consumption order; RandomWalkIntegrator::Li step by step (uniform sphere sampling, maxdepth, emitted light, the estimator and why it is unbiased); DiffuseBxDF f/Sample_f/PDF; Sphere intersection and its error-bound handling; UniformInfiniteLight Le. State which details Umbra must replicate to match pbrt-v4 statistically versus bit-for-bit.

The sample is a pure function of (pixel, global sampleIndex, seed). ImageTileIntegrator::Render calls sampler.StartPixelSample(p, s) before every sample, and every sampler rebuilds its state from those arguments alone. Tile, thread and wave order therefore never change a value, so Jobs over global sample-index ranges are safe.
IndependentSampler runs SetSequence(Hash(p, seed)) and then Advance(s·65536 + dim). Hash is MurmurHash64A over the 12 little-endian bytes (x, y, seed). The hash selects one of 2⁶³ PCG32 streams (inc = (h<<1)|1), and MixBits(h) sets the start offset. Advance jumps the LCG in O(log n) by composing affine maps (Brown's method). Each Get1D takes one u32 and computes min(OneMinusEpsilon, u32·2⁻³²). Each sample owns a disjoint window of 65 536 draws.
ZSobolSampler (the pbrt default, fastowen, 16 spp) is equally pure but has no RNG state. It uses a Morton index with randomly permuted base-4 digits, plus Sobol' points scrambled by Hash(dim, seed). Its values also depend on spp and the film resolution.
Dimension order: 0 wavelength, 1–2 filter, 3 time, 4–5 lens, then 6+2k and 7+2k for the uniform-sphere direction at bounce k. A direction's pair is drawn even if the direction is then rejected. GetBSDF draws nothing.
RandomWalkIntegrator (maxdepth 5): on a miss it returns Σ infinite-light Le. On a hit it adds Le, stops at depth == maxDepth, and otherwise returns Le + f·|cos|·L̂ᵢ/(1/4π). This is unbiased for the LTE truncated at maxDepth scatterings: a single-sample MC estimate with a pdf that is positive everywhere, applied recursively via the tower property. DiffuseBxDF::f = R/π within the same hemisphere and 0 otherwise.
Oracle for the M1 scene. For a convex sphere under a uniform environment, pixels on the sphere converge to exactly ρ·L and background pixels to L, for any maxdepth ≥ 1. The per-sample variance on the sphere is (5/3)(ρL)².
Sphere: interval-arithmetic quadratic, accurate discriminant 4a(r+|v|)(r−|v|), a conservative test that t > 0, reprojection onto the surface, error bound γ₅·|p|, and OffsetRayOrigin along the normal plus one ulp. No epsilons are used.
UniformInfiniteLight: Le = scale·L(λ), with scale /= ∫ȳ·L (and optionally × E_v/π). Umbra must replicate this normalisation.
Replication target. Match pbrt's sample stream bit-for-bit (hash, PCG, Advance, float conversion, dimension order); it is cheap and test vectors are in the research note. Match radiance statistically only: libm transcendentals and pbrt's own -march=native/FMA builds rule out bit-for-bit radiance. The reference render should use Sampler "independent".
Full findings, with permalinks at pbrt-v4 commit b0bdf461 and a statistical vs bit-for-bit table: research/03-pbrt-v4-sampler-and-randomwalk.md

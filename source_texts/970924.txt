What
Reflective dielectric surfaces. Mirrors already worked (metallic 1 / roughness 0); this adds what water and glass need — Fresnel reflection at grazing angles, see-through tint head-on, refraction.
Two new Material factors, everything gated on transmission > 0 so existing scenes render identically:

Path tracer (ground truth)
New dielectric lobe in tracePath: Schlick Fresnel picks an uncolored mirror reflection; total internal reflection folds in by forcing F = 1; otherwise refract() with probability transmission (albedo tint, ray origin offset to the far side of the surface so it can't self-hit), else the existing diffuse + NEE path.
Lobe-selection probability equals each lobe's energy share, so probability and weight cancel — throughput is only ever multiplied by the lobe tint, never divided. Russian roulette untouched (clear glass keeps throughput ≈ 1, so paths survive).
Front/back face read off the raw geometric normal before the ray-facing flip — that's what chooses eta on entry vs exit.
Packed into spare GPUMaterial slots: roughness = vec4(roughness, transmission, ior, 0) — SSBO layout unchanged.
Raster preview (honest, not authoritative)
pbr.frag: diffuse fades by (1 − transmission), alpha follows Fresnel (opaque at grazing — water reads correctly). Env-map reflections already existed. A small alpha floor + Fresnel rim keep glass visible/selectable without an HDRI. No raster refraction — an alpha blend honestly says "preview"; bending the env lookup would look authoritative while being wrong.
Renderer: transmissive entities draw after the sky, sorted back-to-front, blended, depth test on / writes off.
Editor
Transmission slider + IOR slider (shown only when transmissive), plus Mirror / Water / Glass / Frosted preset buttons. Presets push their own undo entry (programmatic writes bypass track()'s widget edit-state).
New fields joined the scene-change hash — without that, slider edits would never re-upload to the path tracer.
Bounces tooltip notes glass wants 6+.
Persistence
SceneFormat/SceneSerializer round-trip; lenient decode defaults mean no format version bump — old files load unchanged, old builds ignore the new keys. Round-trip test extended with non-default values.
Known v1 limits (documented in docs/PLAN.md)
Glass casts opaque shadows (occlusion traversal has no material knowledge).
Point lights invisible in specular reflections (no NEE on delta lobes — pre-existing for mirrors).
Denoiser may blur refractions at low spp (guides are first-hit; fades as samples accumulate).

./run.ps1 configures + compiles warning-clean; forge_tests.exe → [ok] all tests passed.

* perf: GGX VNDF sampling + roughness regularization (#44)

The metal and dielectric specular lobes perturbed reflect() with a
uniform-cube fuzz scaled by roughness^2 - not a normalized BRDF sample.
Rough reflections converged slowly and samples whose fuzz fell below
the horizon killed the whole path.

Both lobes now sample the GGX visible-normal distribution (Heitz 2018,
JCGT 7-4): the estimator weight is just G2/G1 (near-constant variance)
and no samples are wasted below the horizon. The dielectric lobe
evaluates Fresnel and refraction against the sampled half-vector, so
frosted glass gets a proper rough-transmission lobe too. alpha =
roughness^2 (Disney convention), clamped at 1e-3.

Also path-space regularization (Kaplanyan/Dachsbacher): once a path
has gone diffuse, roughness is clamped up to 0.25 so glossy chains
converge instead of sparkling. Camera-visible surfaces and pure
specular chains keep true roughness - mirrors and glass stay sharp.

Co-Authored-By: Claude Fable 5 <noreply@anthropic.com>

* perf: R2 low-discrepancy sampling for primary dimensions (#45) (#48)

The RNG was pure per-pixel white noise (O(N^-0.5) convergence). The
high-impact dimensions now draw from an R2 additive low-discrepancy
sequence (plastic-constant fractions) indexed by the accumulated sample
count, with a per-pixel Cranley-Patterson rotation per purpose so
neighbouring pixels stay decorrelated and AA jitter cannot correlate
with bounce directions.

LDS dimensions: AA jitter, lens aperture, first-bounce lobe select and
first-bounce direction (the exclusive lobes share one 2D dimension).
Deeper bounces keep PCG white noise, where correlation artifacts would
outweigh the stratification win. Up to O(N^-1.5) convergence on smooth
integrands - the biggest relative win on exactly the simple scenes the
viewport shows most.

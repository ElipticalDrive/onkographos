Build a simple SDF raymarcher capable of displaying various primitives and capable of performing boolean operations, preferably including a smoothed union as well. It also needs some basic lighting. I've heard it's pretty easy to calculate normals for an SDF, so that shouldn't be too hard.
If you want to be early and go the extra mile, you can try to optimize it. To do this, you can utilize grid cell voxelization, which splits a grid into another grid for more detail. This is a good way of having low detail on the inside of an object and fine detail where it matters: along its curves and near its surface. This saves you from evaluating the inside of the shape many times for each grid cell.

Slice B1 of #1533.
Scope
New 3D type (working name BilliardScene3D; decide between a new FractalType and a 3D mode of Billiard Rays in this slice, record the choice). Full registration (enums + capabilities Raymarch3D, parameters + Clone, view defaults, render host, poster renderer, region snapshot, params panel, dropdown, animatable map).
CPU ray tracer: primary rays from the orbit camera; exact ray-cylinder intersection for every disk of BilliardScene (height BilliardSceneCylinderHeight, optional cap); the floor plane at z = 0 textured with a Billiard Rays render of the current parameters (rendered once per frame at a set resolution, cached while parameters are unchanged).
Mirror reflection off cylinders, up to a bounce cap; misses go to a background colour.
Batch parity: camera (Cam3D*) and new fields via the region snapshot / --param; Command builder round-trip.
Tests
Ray-cylinder hits lie on the cylinder surface; the reflection law holds at each bounce (independent check).
Looking straight down with no reflections reproduces the floor texture (the Billiard Rays image) under the camera mapping.
Poster renderer equals the live calculator; region / --param round-trip.

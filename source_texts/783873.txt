PDF An Efficient Method for Volume Rendering using Perspective Projection
This paper introduces two new techniques to help make perspective projection more feasible in rendering volume data. First, a method is presented for efficient slice-by-slice processing of volume data, allowing high resolution data sets by eliminating typical memory constraints.

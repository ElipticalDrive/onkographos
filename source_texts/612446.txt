Summary
ADR-0063 (accepted with the user's decisions of 2026-10-28), stage 1: a mesh's voxels stored and drawn compact. Stacked on #125 (ADR-0062 stage 2), on #123, on #122.
The user asked first for a cheaper voxel encoding that keeps the far field's detail. The study (ADR-0063 "The encoding and the minimum"): occlusion and metallic are constant in every voxel cluster, capsules (2.8 % of voxels) use their byte, the footprint's box uses all its bits, and the axis needs 12 bits a component, not 16. So:
A voxel of 12 bytes (16 before): the footprint's box and shape; the lobe, its axis octahedral at 12 bits a component (8 for a capsule, whose lobe is broad, beside the capsule's byte) and its concentration; the albedo and roughness. Lossless but for the axis: within 0.06 degrees, a capsule's 0.95.
A voxel cluster's record of 8 bytes, named by its meshlet's triangle_offset: its voxels' first word, its cells' edge as a power of two's exponent, the occlusion and metallic its voxels share. A cluster whose voxels differ in them is wide, its voxels four words with their own.
A brick of 16 bytes (32 before): its corner in whole bricks of its cell, exact on its grid, its first voxel's rank in its cluster, its mask.
The builders keep their form; nph_render_voxels_pack makes the compact one from a mesh's levels, and nph_render_brick_from_packed / nph_render_voxel_from_packed give it back: the twins of the shaders' vis_voxel_cluster, vis_voxel_brick and vis_voxel_load, which hand the footprint tests and the resolve the very records they read before, bit for bit. The mesh asset's version 5 stores the form (VOXC, VOXB, VOXA; versions 3 and 4 with voxels no longer read: cook again); COOK_MESH_VERSION 6; the fuzz seeds are version 5's.
ADR-0063 itself (streaming the proxies by level, as the culls ask for them) is in this PR too, with the user's decisions: streaming next; a cheaper encoding first; then the finest minimum that fits. The residency model shows the compact form makes "one level coarser out of view" fit the 4 km world (2.40 GB, against 3.35 with today's encoding); every cell at its own level all round does not fit either way (5.36 GB).

Image: in the deterministic normal debug view 99.56 % of the pixels are the same bit for bit, 0.44 % within 4 levels of 255 (the axis's rounding), 119 isolated far pixels (0.0014 %) more, where the two-sided lobe's side flips at a grazing view (as the 16-bit axis does at other pixels). The lit views differ by FLIP 0.013 to 0.029, the radiance cache's noise realization (0.4 to 0.6 of 255 apart in every band, the triangle-only near field alike).
Open (recorded in ADR-0063): the first view after loading, at 16 times the lists only, runs the radiance cache's update at 2.3 to 2.5 ms a frame where the previous build's ran at 0.30; the same view measured later, and the first at the default lists, are unchanged. render_perf --memory-trace (new here) reads 7.68 GB of the 7.83 GB budget at the first frame, while the bottom levels build, 6.73 GB after: most likely the driver's placement of what was made near the budget, which the update's rays read; not the encoding (identical data, identical later). Stage 3's streaming lowers that peak.
Tests
The compact form round-trips every field of random clusters (narrow and wide, every shape, corners to 2,000 bricks either way, cells from 2^-10 to 2^9) but the axis, within its bound; it refuses what it cannot hold (corners off their grid or past 16 bits, cells no power of two or out of range, bricks of two cells, voxels that do not follow, empty masks, ranges past the arrays, more than 1,024 voxels); the GPU's voxel tests on packed bricks match the C twin's ray by ray (the twin test's capsules now vary their diagonal, which their byte had left at 0, and its bricks lie on their grid); the renderer refuses malformed records, bricks and words; the mesh view checks records, bricks and words (and refuses version 4 voxels as needing a recook).
Windows MSVC Development: every suite (62 CTest entries). GCC 14 with CI's flags (Development and Shipping), clang's CI-only warnings, clang-tidy 19, clang-format, every shader through glslang 14 + spirv-val: clean.
Not in this PR
ADR-0063's stages 2 to 4: the levels as blobs and their addressing, the streaming itself, the open world at the game's size.
The first-view transient above, to be watched as stage 3 changes the load.

ADR-0062's stage 3, with the user's decisions of 2026-10-28: streaming
next, and first a cheaper voxel encoding that keeps the far field's
detail, else a finer minimum. A model of what views need, from the
cooked landscape's proxies (every cluster, brick, voxel and level
error): held by distance, the landscape's proxies need 0.68 to 1.05 GB
of their 2.06; a 4 km world tiled with them needs 7.5 GB all round the
camera, 2.0 GB for what is in view. So the views ask (Karis et al.,
"Nanite: A Deep Dive", 2021): a proxy's level is the unit, the camera's
and the shadow map's instance culls request the finest level each drawn
proxy can use, a CPU manager loads and evicts within a fixed pool, no
host copy, and the walk's cut clamps at the finest resident level.

The encoding: occlusion and metallic are constant per cluster, the axis
needs 12:12 (8:8 for the broad-lobed capsules, which then hold their
byte), and bricks sit on whole-brick coordinates: a 12-byte voxel and a
16-byte brick, 0.72 times the memory, lossless but for the axis. That
does not fit every cell at its own level all round (5.36 GB), but it
does fit one level coarser out of view (2.40 GB at the worst place,
3.35 with today's encoding): a turn shows the far field at most a level
coarser, briefly.

A mesh's voxels are stored and drawn in a compact form, 12 bytes a voxel
and 16 a brick where they were 16 and 32 (render/voxel.h "The compact
form"), as the user asked first: a cheaper encoding that keeps the far
field's detail.

- A voxel cluster's record (8 bytes, named by its meshlet's
  triangle_offset): its voxels' first word, its cells' edge as a power
  of two's exponent, and the occlusion and metallic its voxels share; a
  cluster whose voxels differ in them is wide, its voxels four words.
- A brick: its corner in whole bricks of its cluster's cell (bricks lie
  on their cell's grid, so the origin comes back bit for bit), its first
  voxel's rank in its cluster, its mask.
- A voxel: the footprint's box and shape; the lobe, its axis octahedral
  at 12 bits a component (8 for a capsule, whose lobe is broad, beside
  the capsule's byte) and its concentration; the albedo and roughness.
  Lossless but for the axis: within 0.06 degrees, a capsule's 0.95.

The builders keep their form; nph_render_voxels_pack makes the compact
one from a mesh's levels, and nph_render_brick_from_packed and
nph_render_voxel_from_packed give it back, the twins of the shaders'
vis_voxel_cluster, vis_voxel_brick and vis_voxel_load, which hand the
footprint tests and the resolve the records they read before, bit for
bit. The mesh asset's version 5 stores the form (VOXC, VOXB, VOXA;
versions 3 and 4 with voxels no longer read: cook again);
COOK_MESH_VERSION 6; the fuzz seeds (scripts/fuzz_seeds.py) are
version 5's. By ADR-0063's model the landscape's proxies take 1.48 GB

vary their diagonal too, which their byte had left at 0, and its bricks
lie on their grid); malformed records, bricks and words are refused by
the renderer and the mesh view.

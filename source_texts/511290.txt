consider N rays with M samples. Currently we invoke the radiance field with N*M xyz queries to compute colors, transmittance and alpha values. Considering that nerf query is the slowest part we could try to limit the number of elements being queried. One way todo so, is to switch to incremental evaluation of rays: let opaqueness O(i) of a ray be 1 - T(i)*(1 - exp(-alpha(i)*delta(i)). Let th be a user defined threshold close to one. Then for each sample M, only evaluate those rays where O(i)<th

Owner
Promotes the immersive visualisation work from the gitignored dev/ folder into the core package, exported and tested.
What
A Makie-free AMR volume ray-caster in src/functions/immersive.jl, exported from Mera:
amr_volume — indexes the AMR octree natively (no uniform-grid resample / no (2^lmax)³ blow-up)
cameras: perspective_camera / equirect_camera (360°) / fisheye_camera (dome)
render_view → scalar image; render_scene → RGB (front-to-back compositing, coloured-density, ACES tone-map)
field_channel (any getvar field incl. RT; color_by for coloured-density) / points_channel (stars)
cross-level trilinear de-blocking + supersampling; as_image/save_view/save_scene (FileIO), flythrough_montage, orbit_keyframes
mp4 flythrough lives in MeraMakieExt (needs using CairoMakie); core errors helpfully without a backend
Deps
Project.toml: + ColorSchemes, Colors (compat added). Core needs no Makie (Mera keeps Makie a weak-dep extension).
Tests
test/61_immersive_tests.jl (wired into runtests): 40 data-free oracles — AMR leaf lookup, ray–box intersection, trilinear vs analytic, camera ray directions, project↔raydir round-trip, MIP, compositing/ACES math, colormaps, PNG save, montage, and the Makie-free flythrough fallback — plus a DATA_AVAILABLE spiral_clumps render block. All green locally (45/45).
Docs
Public Immersive page under Data & Visualization → Volume Rendering (stills, multi-tracer composite, embedded mp4 fly-throughs).

Public, exported, and tested. A Makie-free AMR volume ray-caster (equirectangular / dome / perspective
views, multi-tracer coloured-density composites, fly-through stills) now lives in

Task and acceptance
Phase: World generation | Dependencies: FV03/FV05 | Gate: G3.
Specify cave, tunnel, bluff, rock arch, overhang generation in deterministic 3D density/CSG. Implement a conservative region classifier with explicit heightfield eligible and 3D volume ownership categories: never treat a cave opening or player modification as a heightfield-safe solid cap. Source queries at 6.25 cm, including 3D edge/corner intersections and material. Build overlapping transition fixtures in terrain, cave, arch, edited tower; expose metadata usable at all display distances.
Acceptance: no hidden cave mouths or rock arches at visible LOD; boundary ownership invariant and reproducible classification tests.
Legacy reference policy
R — inspect DrewsAnalyticRendering/src/terrain.rs cave noise and main/successor/world/src/generation/* as reference only. No quadratic approximation, no assumption that the visual surface can stand in for 3D native occupancy.
Guardrail: work only in frontier/ until a separately approved promotion; reproduce baseline tests without copying its architectural failure modes.

Agent brief
Category: enhancement
Complexity: moderate
Summary: Add each FogVolume's density, albedo and emission to the volumetric fog, as Godot does.
Blocked by: none. The volumetric fog it builds on (#663) is on main.
Current behaviour: the FogVolume slice is renderIntent: 'pending' with status unimplemented (ADR-0045). A FogVolume draws nothing.
Desired behaviour: Godot 4.6.3:
Pass: volumetric_fog_update rasterises each FogVolume into density, light and emission maps over the froxel grid (servers/rendering/renderer_rd/environment/fog.cpp:567-790). The fog density stage adds them to the Environment's medium (shaders/environment/volumetric_fog_process.glsl:354-378).
Shapes and material: the FogVolume shape and size, and the FogMaterial's density, albedo, emission, height falloff and edge fade, as Godot's built-in fog shader computes them. A FogMaterial density_texture and a custom fog ShaderMaterial follow the same path.
A FogVolume draws nothing when the Environment's volumetric fog is off.
Key interfaces:
The FogVolume slice: moves from pending to a drawn node, and its sheet status changes.
The fog volume pass from Draw Environment volumetric fog, lit without shadows #663: gains the volume maps.
Acceptance criteria:
A unit test pins the density a box FogVolume adds at its centre and outside it.
A new golden, unit-fog-volume: unit-world-environment-basic plus a 3×3×3 box FogVolume at (0,1,−5) with a FogMaterial of density 1. Its header names the variable.
pnpm ref:godot shows the golden closer to Godot after the fix than before.
Value check: compare with Godot for each shape, a negative density that carves fog, height_falloff and edge_fade at 0 and at a high value, and the Environment's fog turned off. Report each case on the value check line.
Godot measurement: Godot 4.6.3, that scene against unit-world-environment-basic: 16.48% of pixels change, mean 2.92/255, max 52/255. At (712,137): rgb(120, 121, 130) with the volume, rgb(73, 69, 90) without.
Out of scope:
Shadows in the fog (sibling issue under Draw WorldEnvironment volumetric fog #658).

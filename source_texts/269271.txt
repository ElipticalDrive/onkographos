Outcome
The full-spectrum 3-D real entries (fft_3d_array, fft_3d_array_into, ifft_3d_array, ifft_3d_array_into, ifft_3d_array_into_spectrum_scratch) widened the field to complex and transformed every z lane at full length while the half pair (fft_3d_array_half_into/ifft_3d_array_half_into) and, since #474, the 2-D entries took the half route. They now route through the half volume where the split admits nz:
the forwards compute the half volume in the 2-D staging role — free once the split applies, since the x and y passes hold the two 3-D roles and only the refused-length z lanes borrow the 2-D one — and write the (nx, ny, nz) output once in parallel lane blocks, into caller storage or fresh capacity through the slot pass feat(apollo-fft): Transform a real 2-D field through its half spectrum #474 introduced;
the inverses pack the lower nz/2 + 1 bins of each lane (in place, into caller scratch, or into the 2-D role) and read nothing else;
the expansion, the packs and the fresh-capacity set_len site move out of half_plane.rs into real_storage/expand.rs, generic in the lane length and the partner lane, so both ranks share one pass and one proof; its unit test covers a plane and a volume and passes under miri locally (cargo +nightly miri test -p apollo-fft --lib -- expand::tests).

High-level PR Summary
Routes 3-D real FFT transforms through the half-spectrum pair where the real split admits nz, matching the optimization already applied to 2-D transforms in PR #474. The forwards compute the half volume (nx, ny, nz/2 + 1) in 2-D staging scratch and expand it once into full output; the inverses read only the lower nz/2 + 1 bins per lane. This eliminates redundant computation of conjugate-symmetric bins while adding expansion/pack passes. The owned forward routes from 4 MiB output (where page faults repay staging costs) and caller-owned forms from 8-long lanes. Performance measurements show speedups of 1.57x to 6.61x across tested shapes. The PR includes comprehensive test coverage verifying bit-identical agreement with the half-spectrum pair, zero allocations for warm plans, and correct handling of edge cases. As a prerequisite, the PR completes the SAFETY comment ratchet initiative by documenting all 143 remaining unsafe blocks and fixing three release-mode soundness issues in Stockham, two-by-prime, and Bluestein kernels where length assertions existed only in debug builds.

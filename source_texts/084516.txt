SDF of a pipe cross section reduces to SDF of an ellipse: https://iquilezles.org/articles/ellipsedist/
However, SDFs in combination with Boolean operations are not guaranteed to work: https://iquilezles.org/articles/sdfxor/ Given that we are using Boolean ops heavily, this might cause problems. Hard to predict whether this will be an issue without implementing.
Hybrid SDF/classical raytracing is an option.

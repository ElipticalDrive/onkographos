Closes #560
pfc::solvers::scalar_root (include/openpfc/solvers/scalar_root.hpp): Newton inside a caller bracket, bisection when a step leaves it or f' is zero or non-finite. Host/device callable (OPENPFC_HD), no allocation, no exceptions. Status Converged, IterationLimit, NoBracket, NonFinite; the result is the last evaluated point with its residual and the final bracket. A root on a bracket end within f_tol is accepted there (analytic brackets often end on the root).
First consumer: a two-phase (KKS) alloy model that inverts h s(y-a) + (1-h) s(y-b) = c per cell; it carries a model-local copy until this lands.
Tests
tests/unit/solvers/test_scalar_root.cpp (registered in openpfc-tests): sqrt(2) and cos(x)=x (decreasing f) against closed forms; the saturated logistic mixture for c in {1e-9, 0.2, 0.7, 1-1e-9} and h in {0, 0.3, 0.9, 1}; missing bracket, reversed bracket, NaN inside the bracket, the iteration bound with f'=0 (pure bisection), root at a bracket end.
Local run (g0005, gcc 15.2, Catch2 3.3.2 amalgamated, -Wall -Wextra -Werror): all 3 test cases, 94 assertions pass. The full openpfc-tests binary was not rebuilt for this PR.

Phase-field consumers invert a monotone scalar law in every cell, for example
the diffusion potential of a two-phase concentration, and each wrote its own
safeguarded Newton. The bracket makes the step unable to diverge in the flat
tails where plain Newton overshoots, and the routine runs on host or device.

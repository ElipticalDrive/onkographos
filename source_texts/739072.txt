Why
Today every frame ray-traces from scratch and leans on render_scale (0.5 web / 0.75 desktop) to stay interactive. But when the camera and params are static, last frame's samples are "free" — accumulating across frames lifts the effective sample count linearly with time, converging in seconds to near-offline quality. This is the single biggest quality lever that doesn't touch physics.
What
Static camera + params: accumulate a history buffer (exponential/rolling), upweight new samples → converges to a high effective sample count
Camera move or param change: discard history, fast re-converge (no ghosting)
Optional: sub-pixel jitter for AA via reprojection
Why it unlocks everything else
Web at 0.5 scale reaches cinematic quality by sitting still
4× SSAA (AaQuality::High, today ~4× the march cost) becomes affordable for free
Higher-order Einstein rings smooth via accumulated samples instead of per-frame supersampling
Directly amortizes the expensive RK45 march (~7× Phase-1 cost, spec §3.4)
Acceptance
Static camera accumulates → visually converges to higher quality within a few seconds
Camera move / param change discards history; no ghosting or smearing
No regression while orbiting (graceful degradation, not a frozen stale frame)
One new full-screen pass + history texture; the offscreen Rgba16Float chain is extended
References
Render chain: 4 Material2ds in src/render/material.rs (offscreen→brightpass→blur→composite)
render_scale plumbing: src/render/plugin.rs

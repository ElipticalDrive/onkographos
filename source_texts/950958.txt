What
PR2 of the brepjs-voxel performance & scale stack. After PR1 (BVH distance), the FWN sign pass — mesh.is_inside(p) per voxel, O(voxels·triangles) — was ~98% of voxelize_mesh end-to-end. This accelerates it to ~O(voxels·log triangles) with a Barnes-Hut / Barill-2018 hierarchical winding number that reuses the PR1 BVH (one tree, two traversals).
Each BVH node carries a first-order point-dipole expansion (area-weighted normal sum about the area-weighted centroid, aggregated bottom-up). A query approximates a node by its dipole when |p − center| > β·radius (β = 2), else recurses and evaluates the exact solid angle at leaves.

Correctness — sign identical to exact FWN

Zero sign mismatches vs the exact O(N) FWN oracle across every voxel of unit-cube, holey-cube (the keystone), and icosphere. The keystone (holey interior stays inside, w ≈ 0.833) routes through the fast path unchanged.
The winding number is approximate; the opening criterion — not expansion accuracy — guarantees the sign. Near-0.5 (near-surface) nodes are evaluated exactly; the dipole is used only for far nodes where w is unambiguously ~0 or ~1.
The β = 2 dipole-only margin is empirical, not a proven bound (the docstring says so). Committed adversarial guards — high-aspect slab, far disjoint emitter, single open triangle — lock it so a future β/leaf-size change can't silently erode it.

Interesting. Setting the Volume Sampling Rate Scalar (VSRS) to 20 significantly reduced the stippling, but didn't eliminate it. I next set the Samples Per Pixel (SPP) to 8. That produced a similar result. Finally, I combined them, setting VSRS to 20 and SPP to 8. That pretty much eliminated the stippling, but took several minutes to render. I think we can label this a question instead of a bug. I've attached the resulting images as well. Thanks, @StasJ

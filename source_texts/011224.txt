Radiosity is a global illumination technique that solves for diffuse interreflection of light between surfaces in a scene. It models light transport using a linear system of equations where the radiosity of each surface depends on the radiosity of other visible surfaces, weighted by form factors. The radiosity equation is discretized over triangular surface patches with constant radiosity to build the system of equations. The solution provides view-independent lighting suitable for precomputed rendering.

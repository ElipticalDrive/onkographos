Abstract
With the rising industrial attention to 3D virtual modeling technology, generating novel 3D content based on specified conditions (e.g., text) has become a hot issue. In this paper, we propose a generative 3D modeling framework called Diffusion-SDF for the challenging task of text-to-shape synthesis. Existing approaches fail to generate high-fidelity 3D shapes conforming to the given text descriptions due to imprecise 3D data representations and less powerful shape generation strategies. To address this, we propose an SDF autoencoder together with the Voxelized Diffusion model to learn and generate representations for voxelized signed distance fields (SDFs) of 3D shapes . Specifically, we design a UinU-Net architecture that implants a local-focused inner network inside the standard U-Net architecture, which enables better reconstruction of patch-independent SDF representations. While our Voxelized Diffusion model generates diversified shapes based on text conditions, it uniformly separates voxel grids in the 3D space for all the shapes despite of different structures and geometries, which is not data-adaptive thereby limiting the resolution of shapes with given memory costs. In order to fully exploit the representative ability of local voxels, we further propose Diffusion-SDF++ by designing Octree Diffusion with hybrid resolutions, where the model adaptively assigns higher-resolution voxels to detailed local structures for precise description on the fly. To achieve this, we design an octree-based UinU-Net named OinO-Net to adapt to non-uniform flexible voxel structures, which extracts features of leaf octants by hierarchically capturing local details and global shape priors. Experimental results show that Diffusion-SDF is capable of generating both high-quality and highly diversified 3D shapes that conform well to the given text descriptions, and Diffusion-SDF++ further outperforms Diffusion-SDF with more precise shape details due to the adaptive voxelization strategy.

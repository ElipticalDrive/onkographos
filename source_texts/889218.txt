Question
Which main-pass and visibility techniques suit GPU-driven rendering with MSAA or SMAA and no TAA (ADR 0008), bindless materials (ADR 0001) and ray-traced shadows: Forward+ and clustered variants, deferred, visibility buffer, meshlets and mesh shaders, virtual geometry, two-phase occlusion culling? What does each cost and require? Survey per the map's state-of-the-art rule: research since 2019 plus foundations, shipped practice, the per-technique fields, a compact note in docs/research/ ending in options, not a decision. Sized to one session.
Context
ADR 0014 (camera-relative composition in the culling pass), ADR 0015 (reverse-Z, Hi-Z keeps the minimum depth). Feature availability: wgpu capability matrix across Metal, Vulkan and DX12. Blocks Visibility strategy for the main pass.

Two probes on the M4 Pro: one for capabilities, one for GPU timings.
What wgpu 30.0.1 allows (the facts the options depend on)
No tile memory: programmable blending, input attachments and tile shaders are absent, so a G-buffer goes through memory.
Metal has no indirect command buffers and no count draws. One draw_indexed_indirect is one native call on every backend.
WGSL mesh shaders now draw on Metal, but Metal forbids ray tracing in render pipelines that use mesh shading.
R32Uint and Rg32Uint ID targets take 2x and 4x MSAA on Metal; R64Uint takes none.
Derivatives exist only in fragment shaders, so compute shading samples with textureSampleGrad.
Indirect validation is on by default in release builds and adds CPU work per indirect draw. Its cost was not measured.
Measured on the M4 Pro (wgpu 30.0.1, 2560×1440, one synthetic scene)
Hidden surface removal hides draw order at 0.36M triangles but not above. At 4.6M, back to front costs 2.7 times front to back, and 3.2 times at 27.4M. A depth pre-pass in the same render pass restores the cost for 9% at 4.6M.
A discard that never runs costs 16 to 21% front to back, and 2.4 to 6.1 times back to front.
4x MSAA with memoryless attachments costs 7 to 26%.
At 1x, deferred and the visibility buffer stay within 16% of forward up to 4.6M triangles; at 27.4M, deferred is 33% faster. At 4x, lighting every sample costs deferred 1.2 to 3.7 times forward 4x.
@builtin(primitive_index) adds 19 to 24% to an ID pass.
Options for Visibility strategy for the main pass (#22), with no recommendation; the note states each option's image-quality consequences:
A. Clustered forward with a depth pre-pass: 4x MSAA, one shadow ray per pixel, nothing accumulated over frames.
B. Forward with a visibility pre-pass: as A, with a triangle-ID pass that culls the lit pass's triangles.
C. Deferred with clustered or tiled lighting: MSAA only through a per-sample G-buffer, otherwise SMAA 1x.
D. Visibility buffer with compute materials and lighting: no quad overdraw; MSAA needs per-sample IDs and per-sample shading at edges.
E. Virtual geometry beside A to D: Nanite and Bevy have no MSAA, and Nanite relies on TAA to hide LOD switches.

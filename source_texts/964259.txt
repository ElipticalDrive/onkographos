The first stages of moving sfem's resampling/ code here (sfem's MIGRATION_SRESAMPLE.md): the library's skeleton and the SDF obstacle sampling sfem's contact uses. The companion sfem PR switches sfem over to it.
History
sfem's resampling/ history is imported with git filter-repo, so git log --follow and blame reach the original commits (Simone Riva's and Patrick Zulian's):
legacy/main/: resampling/ of sfem main (75fb51d86).
legacy/grid_to_mesh/: resampling/ of the unmerged grid_to_mesh branch (9fc0234fa), with the cell lists and the GPU adjoint, without test_grid/ and the deprecated folders.
legacy/ is not built. It is ported from in later stages and removed at the end. The SDF files moved out of it in a commit of renames only.
The library
Build: CMake options SRESAMPLE_ENABLE_{OPENMP,CUDA,MPI,SMESH,TESTING,DEV_MODE}; standalone it has no dependency.
Install/export SRESAMPLE::sresample.
Templates instantiated for the type tuples of SRESAMPLE_INSTANTIATIONS (real, geometry, index, grid), an all-float32 tuple included.
src/sdf/:
the cubic B-spline sampler and its coefficients;
the nodal sampling (gap, normal, curvature);
the dual-weighted surface sampling of TRISHELL3, QUADSHELL4 and BEAM2.
Each element kernel is written once, for host and device, so the GPU now samples TRISHELL3 and BEAM2 too. Every device call takes a stream and allocates nothing.
src/mesh/: the shell elements' geometry, dual basis and lumped mass.
src/mpi/: the z-slab gather and view of a grid split across ranks. It replaces matrix.io's array_range_select and counts in layers.
Tests:
the B-spline tests moved from sfem;
surface sampling: linear fields reproduced, weights, outside the grid, host vs device;
the z-slab gather on 3 ranks;
a stream gate.
Each test has mutation checks.
Checks
Standalone (macOS, DEV_MODE): no dependencies 3/3; OpenMP and MPI 4/4.
Inside sfem: see the sfem PR. In short: bitwise equal to the old kernels at one thread except the QUADSHELL4 lumped weights (at most 2 ulp, from where fused multiply-adds are placed), and identical MG SDF contact histories on GH200.

Every SDF sampler in the library read the trilinear interpolant, whose gradient
jumps across cell faces: a contact node on a face cycles between two normals
and the outer contact loop stalls. They now read the interpolating cubic
B-spline, resampling/surface/sfem_sdf_sampling.{hpp,cpp}: the grid is
prefiltered once (gain 6, pole sqrt(3) - 2, causal then anticausal per axis,
lines extended by point reflection so planes stay exact) into coefficients held
by sfem::SDFBSpline, and sdf_sample<ORDER> returns the value, gradient and, for
ORDER 2, the Hessian of a C2 field that passes through the samples. The surface
kernels (quadshell4 host and CUDA, trishell3, beam2), the nodal interpolate_*
functions, SDFObstacle and the gap_from_sdf drivers all call it; the unreachable
gap is one constant, SFEM_SDF_UNREACHABLE.

- Normals no longer accumulate across re-linearisations: the kernels add into
  the normals and only the gap was zeroed, so every update after the first
  summed the normals of all previous ones. resample_gap(_normals) and their
  CUDA counterparts now zero them. Fixing this takes the tet case from 17 to 6
  outer iterations; test_normals_follow_the_update fails by 0.22 without it.
- Slab-distributed grids: SDFObstacle::create and create_from_file take the
  mesh's communicator and z coordinates and gather the 12 layers the prefilter
  reaches to float precision (sdf_view_ensure_margin). The slab and whole-grid
  results differ by 4.9e-7 on 1, 2 and 4 ranks (test_parallel_sdf_slabs);
  without the margin they differ by the unreachable gap.
- Nodal sampling of displaced nodes: interpolate_displaced_gap gives the gap
  -s / |grad s| and the unit normal at X[idx] + u, summed in real_t, and
  interpolate_displaced_curvature gives (H - n m^T - m n^T) / |grad s| with
  m = H n, minus the gap's Hessian at contact -- the term a Newton tangent of
  node-to-obstacle contact needs. SDFObstacle::sample_nodes and
  sample_nodes_curvature expose them (host only for now). Outside the grid the
  gap is unreachable and the normal and curvature zero, instead of an abort.
  interpolate_gap now returns -s / |grad s| instead of -s.
- ContactSurface zeroes the coordinates beyond the mesh dimension, host and
  device, so 2D meshes sample the grid at z = 0.

sfem_SDFSamplingTest checks the interpolant, the derivatives against finite
differences, quadratic and plane reproduction, the curvature's order (2.4-2.5
on spheres, 3e-6 relative to finite differences on an ellipsoid, where H alone
is off by 17x) and the outside rule. sfem_MGSDFContactTest asserts the
penetration at the solution, below 1e-4 of the imposed 0.05.

MG SDF contact outer iterations, trilinear -> B-spline: sphere 30 -> 31,
hifreq 20 -> 21, multisphere 18 -> 17, checkerboard 14 -> 14, tet 5 -> 6 (17
before the normals fix), quad2d 15 -> 15, neohookean 14 -> 14.

bench_MaMAL on Alps (one GH200, device, 233k to 208M nodes, sphere and
multisphere, with and without line search, 28 runs): 0.83-1.00x the trilinear
time, with equal or fewer outer iterations and MG cycles in 26 of 28 runs;
both arms meet the 1e-8 residual and penetration tolerances in every run. At
208M nodes sampling costs 20 ms per call against 9 ms, 0.3% of the solve.

The thread-local launch stream redirected every library launch on the thread
into a graph capture. Each launch now takes the stream of the object it belongs
to:
- MPRGP's passes take the solver's stream, bound by CUDA_MPRGP::build_mprgp from

  operator's: MPRGP orders the product after the solver's work and the solver

Merge nearby world geometry into camera-centered Global Distance Field clipmaps for efficient long-range software traces.

Scope
Define cascaded/clipmapped GDF representation and voxel formats
Inject resident mesh distance fields into clipmaps
Track dirty world regions from lighting_scene
Scroll/reuse clipmaps as the camera moves
Bound per-frame update work
Support static/movable invalidation policy
Trace coarse/far rays through GDF
Add residency/clip/debug visualizations
Acceptance criteria
Long software traces avoid iterating arbitrary mesh DF instances
Small camera motion reuses most clipmap content
Local changes dirty bounded regions
Update work and memory are quality-tier budgeted
GDF miss/fallback semantics are deterministic

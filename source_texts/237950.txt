this PR reworks the soft shadows (which were never really correct), to instead implement Monte Carlo area-light soft shadows using 4×4 jittered stratified sampling, concentric disk mapping, and deterministic randomized rotation.
The shadows still have a grainy quality, but I dont think I can increase the sampling anymore due to the inherent limitations of ray tracing on a CPU

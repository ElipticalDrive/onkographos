Correct the ANARI volume opacity conversion to account for the renderer’s sample distance and the volume’s unitDistance. The conversion is now applied after transfer-function sampling using the ANARI opacity relationship, which preserves interpolation behavior. Invalid nonpositive unitDistance values are also handled.
This reduces the volume test’s SSIM error from 0.376137 to 0.230263. The existing Helide baseline threshold still fails because of additional rendering differences.
Further experiments (not included in this PR) had these results.
Matching Helide’s MT19937 jitter reduces the error to 0.197168
Also matching Helide’s sample distance reduces it to 0.0703201
Using a floating-point transfer-function lookup reduces it to 0.0581116
Removing the additional Viskores ray-start offset reduces it to 0.0350807, below the 0.05 threshold
Alternative jitter patterns did not provide the same improvement. Hash-based jitter produced 0.224755, while Viskores’ Philox-based generators produced approximately 0.226. This indicates that matching the existing baseline depends on reproducing Helide’s specific sampling pattern rather than simply adding statistically uniform jitter.

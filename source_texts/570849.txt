Model layers now cast and take shadows. Each light that casts makes a shadow map of the models, so a model shades itself, other models and 3D layers, and 3D layers shade models the way they shade each other, coloured glass included. A model's shadow is as soft as a layer's under the same light, and models cut shafts in volumetric light too. Point lights cast all round, even from among a model's parts.
Casts shadows and Accepts shadows show on Model layers again, with Only for both, so a model can cast without being seen and a card set to Accepts Only catches a model's shadow over footage. A new Model layer casts shadows. Model layers also get a Holdout switch, which draws what's under its 3D layers in its place, so a stand-in for something in the footage hides the CG behind it. Comps without a model don't make any maps, and a million-triangle model with two lights casting takes about 1.3 ms on an RTX 5080.
Still to do: a model's shadow on a holdout, a faded model's shadow fading with it, a model's shadow stopping at a 2D layer the way a layer's does, and shadows from a model blurring with its motion.

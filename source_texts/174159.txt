Abstract
Efficient streaming of high-fidelity 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) is hindered by large transmission overhead. Existing compression methods (pruning, quantization, anchor-based prediction) under-exploit redundancy among neighboring anchors. We observe strong inter-anchor feature similarity and propose ACPGS, a bandwidth-efficient 3DGS framework that introduces anchor-level shared co-features, gradient-guided adaptive refinement for complex regions, and hierarchical progressive encoding to exploit both inter- and intra-layer correlations. Experiments show ACPGS reduces transmission size by >30% over state-of-the-art methods and achieves >140× compression over vanilla 3DGS while preserving near-lossless rendering quality, making it suitable for bandwidth-constrained applications.

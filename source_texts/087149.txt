Dear PSI and everyone interested,
We are delighted to extend an invitation to you for the upcoming PSI seminar given by Georgios Kouros and Wangduo Xie.
The seminar is scheduled for October 27th (Monday) at 10:00am in room ELEC 01.89.
For those unavailable to attend in person, we've arranged for online participation through the following link:
PSI Seminars with Georgios Kouros & Wangduo Xie
Monday, October 27, 2025
10:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Further details regarding the seminar content are outlined below.
Speaker 1: Georgios Kouros
- Kouros, G., Wu, M., Nagesh, S., Zhang, X., & Tuytelaars, T. (2024). Unveiling the Ambiguity in Neural Inverse Rendering: A Parameter Compensation Analysis. In Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR) Workshops (pp. 2832-2841). https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024W/NRI/papers/Kouros_Unveiling_the_Ambiguity_in_Neural_Inverse_Rendering_A_Parameter_Compensation_CVPRW_2024_paper.pdf
- Kouros, G., Wu, M., & Tuytelaars, T. (2025). RGS-DR: Deferred reflections and residual shading in 2D Gaussian splatting. ArXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18468
- Kouros, G., Wu, M., & Tuytelaars, T. (2025). Spec-Gloss Surfels and Normal-Diffuse Priors for Relightable Glossy Objects. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02069
Speaker 2: Wangduo Xie
- ‎Xie, W.; Schoonhoven, R.; van Leeuwen, T.; & Blaschko, M. B. (2024). AC-IND: Sparse CT reconstruction based on attenuation coefficient estimation and implicit neural distribution. arXiv preprint, arXiv:2409.07171. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2025/papers/Xie_AC-IND_Sparse_CT_Reconstruction_Based_on_Attenuation_Coefficient_Estimation_and_WACV_2025_paper.pdf

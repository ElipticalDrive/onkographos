Follow-up to Phase 11 (#134): the two small correctness items left after the review phases, plus the remaining review work planned as phases in PLANNING.md.
Emitters: NEE samples consistent with BSDF hits
A geometry light evaluated its emission differently from a BSDF ray hitting the same point, so MIS combined two different integrands. The result was biased whenever the emission was not constant. The differences:
Texture point: the light read the texture at the world point instead of the hit's local point (centre-relative for analytic primitives, scaled through transforms).
Seed: the light used seed 0 instead of the entity's seed.
Mix materials: a mix was replaced by a synthetic emitter with blend fixed at 0.5 and the centre texel.
Thin-walled emitters: only the front face was sampled.
Changes:
ISamplable.LocalPointAt is new on every samplable primitive. It returns the texture point a Hit there reports, and GeometryAuditTests checks it against Hit for all 19 audited shapes, as it already does for the UVs.
IMaterial.MayEmit and IMaterial.EmitsFromBack are new; Emissive, MixMaterial and OpenPbr implement them.
GeometryLight now carries the surface's own material and evaluates its Emit at the sample's UV and local point, with the entity's seed and the face seen. Both faces are sampled when EmitsFromBack is true, including BDPT/VCM light-subpath emission.
GeometryLight.TextureOffset (added in Phase 11) and the synthetic mix emitter are removed.
OpenPBR emission as a light
Every samplable surface whose material may emit is now registered as a NEE light. This covers:
OpenPBR emission, including materials read from glTF and MTL files (deferred since Phase 2);
mixes with an emitting side;
emissive lathes and extrusions (the docs already said they were lights, but the loader never registered them).
Verification
New LightTransportTests cases, also rerun with BDPT:
sphere and ellipsoid whose emission depends on the point and on the seed: off by 4-10% with the old evaluation, now 0.998-1.004 against the quadrature over real hits;
mix with blend 0.2;
thin-walled OpenPBR panel seen from its back.
New EmitterRegistrationTests: checks what the loader registers as a light.

Remaining review work, planned
PLANNING.md has a new section, "Chiusura della review del motore", with one phase per session. The status table and work order (§7) of PIANO-REVIEW.md point to it.
Fase 12, correctness leftovers:
animated emitters are light-sampled at the mid-animation pose while BSDF hits see the real pose, the same kind of mismatch;
emitters inside instances and CSG are not lights;
the power estimate of textured lights uses a single point;
the canonical validation scenes (§5), with which ones exist and which are missing;
final verdicts in §6 and the 3.23 label.
Fase 13a, spectral infrastructure: hero wavelength, RGB→spectrum lifting, XYZ film; it must reproduce today's RGB renders within the lifting error.
Fase 13b, spectral physics: dispersion, measured metals, spectral skies, spectral Cornell and comparison with Mitsuba's spectral variants.
Fase 14, scene review and closing: every scene, the tutorials, archiving the plan and cleaning up CLAUDE.md.
Outside the review: the roadmap items, collected in one list with where each one lives.
Docs updated: scene reference EN+IT (OpenPBR emission is a light), README EN+IT, docs/technical/rendering-pipeline.md, CLAUDE.md, devlog, PIANO-REVIEW.md.

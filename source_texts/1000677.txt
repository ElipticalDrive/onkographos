Use the scene's skybox cubemap as an environment light source for PBR materials.
Why
The engine has PBR metallic/roughness shading (Material3D.UsePbr) and skybox cubemaps (CubemapRegistry / SkyboxRenderer), but they don't talk to each other: PBR ambient is a flat term, and a fully metallic material reflects nothing. Image-based lighting is the biggest available visual jump for the 3D pipeline — it's what makes the DamagedHelmet-style glTF reference scenes look right.
Scope
Prefilter the skybox cubemap once at registration: a small irradiance cubemap (diffuse ambient) and a mip-chained prefiltered specular cubemap (roughness → mip), plus the split-sum BRDF LUT (2D texture, computable once at startup)
GPU prefiltering via offscreen passes (render each face with a convolution shader), following the existing ShadowMapRenderer pattern of a renderer owning its FBO state
Renderer3D: replace the flat ambient term with irradiance sampling and add the specular IBL term in the PBR path; Blinn-Phong path unchanged
MeshRenderSystem wires it automatically when both a Skybox entity and PBR materials are present; scenes without a skybox keep the current flat ambient
Extend CubemapRegistry (or a sibling EnvironmentMapRegistry) to own the prefiltered resources per skybox
Out of scope
Local reflection probes / parallax-corrected cubemaps
Screen-space reflections
Runtime-changing skyboxes re-prefiltering every frame (re-prefilter on registration only)
Acceptance criteria
A metallic-roughness material in the DamagedHelmet sample visibly reflects the skybox, with reflections blurring as roughness increases
Scenes without a skybox render exactly as before
CPU-side math that doesn't need a GL context (e.g. sample-direction/mip-selection helpers) unit-tested per repo conventions; docs/pbr.md updated

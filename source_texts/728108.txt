perf: use cosine-weighted hemisphere sampling for diffuse bounces (#30)
…
769829a
```
Replace the rejection-sampled sphere octant in sampleBounceDirection
with cosine-weighted hemisphere sampling: draw a point on the unit disk
(r = sqrt(u), phi = 2*pi*v) and project onto the hemisphere. This
matches the Lambertian BRDF's cosine term, removing the rejection loop
and its variable iteration count, and yields lower-variance diffuse
bounces.
```

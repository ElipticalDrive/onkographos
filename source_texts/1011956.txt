This change is a small performance improvement suggestion.
The isotropic entry of the HG phase function table (representations[0], used for $\lvert g \rvert \approx 0$) holds three identical uniform lobes. So ApplySingleLobeHenyeyGreensteinProduct computes the same product three times, and SamplePDF and PDF evaluate the same mixture three times.
This unnecessarily slows down guiding in isotropic media. The same distribution can be represented with one uniform lobe of weight 1.
On my PC a guided scatter at g = 0 takes 430 ns, vs 262 ns after this proposed change.

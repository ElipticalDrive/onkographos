Updated sample 14_MarchingCubes to manage chunks lifecycle for position-based infinite mesh generation.
Sample

Upgraded sample 14 from a static single-chunk view to an infinite, real-time procedural world streamed around a free-fly camera using dynamic chunk recycling and compute-driven Marching Cubes.
Changed the density function to feature a procedural terrain generator with layered FBM noise, domain warping, canyons, terraces, caves, and flared stone pillars.

Framework

Upgraded the free-fly camera controller with smooth world-axis vertical movement (E/Q) and speed multipliers (Shift/Ctrl).

Description
RandomSampler::get_1d() (include/huira/sampling/sampler.hpp) draws from std::mt19937 through std::uniform_real_distribution, behind a virtual call. MSVC’s uniform_real_distribution routes through generate_canonical and costs tens of ns per draw; mt19937 carries 2.5 KB of state per sampler; the virtual get_1d() blocks inlining even where the caller holds a concrete RandomSampler& (as trace_pixel does). A path sample draws on the order of 10 numbers, so this is plausibly 10–25% of per-sample time.
Candidate: PCG32 or xoshiro128** with the float built from the top 24 bits (guaranteeing [0, 1)), and RandomSampler marked final so the compiler can devirtualize. Per-tile seeding by (tile, round) is unchanged. Images will change, since the random stream changes; tests that pin exact pixel values need re-baselining. Measure before and after with the 1024² sphere example at 10 spp (currently ~0.24 s of tracing warm on a 16-thread laptop).
Additional Context
This warrants future investigation before we commit to anything. But the time-savings could be meaningful without a sacrifice in accuracy.

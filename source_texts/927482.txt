Normals are transformed by the model's linear matrix, so lighting is wrong under non-uniform scale or shear. Compute one inverse-transpose normal matrix per mesh projection (None when singular). Clip triangles against the homogeneous near/far planes before the perspective divide so geometry crossing the camera rasterizes correctly.

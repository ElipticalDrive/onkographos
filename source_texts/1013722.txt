Packs placefile line, triangle and polygon vertices into compact formats (lines 48 → 32 bytes, triangles/polygons 44 → 28 bytes per vertex) using half-float offsets, normalized byte colors and 16-bit thresholds, with no shader changes. Identical CPU geometry is shared across panes. Also trims incomplete triangles by whole vertices.
Single-pane placefile screenshots are pixel-identical to develop. Heavy placefile RSS after enabling drops about 160 MB (2292 → 2129 MB) on its own, and combined with the text PR and malloc PR, 1-pane RSS is about 0.9 GB and 4-pane about 2.6 GB.
Draft: best reviewed after the placefile text PR, since heavy placefiles still crash without it. Measurements are from a software renderer, so real-GPU savings may differ.

Summary by CodeRabbit
Improvements
Placefile lines, polygons, and triangles now use more compact rendering data, reducing the amount of data needed to represent their geometry.
Identical placefile geometry can be shared, reducing duplicate data. Incomplete polygon and triangle geometry is excluded from rendering.
Placefile line, polygon, and triangle rendering now stores vertex attributes in packed records. Each renderer uses one VBO, and completed buffers can be shared when their contents match.
Changes: Placefile vertex packing

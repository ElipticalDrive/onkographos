Abstract
In virtual endoscopy, it is very important to observe a target organ with a rapid and easy way. In this paper, we propose a virtual endoscopy system that adopts a fast volume rendering scheme based on perspective ray casting [1] and a semi-automatic path generation scheme to navigate a hollow organ along a centerline.

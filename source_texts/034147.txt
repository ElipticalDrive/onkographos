The acoustic wave is the most effective way for detecting targets in underwater, so the volume rendering of sonar detecting effectiveness field in underwater is one of the cores technology for the visualization about underwater environment. Aiming at the non-linearity of transfer function in sound field volume rendering, the volume rendering method is proposed. The method conquered the shortages that the conventional direct volume visualization can hardly realize the real-time rendering with high quality and made the volume rendering in common PC timely and easily.

…der's setting means

Cycles scales the user limit by 3 and clamps when reduce_add(fabs(RGB)) exceeds
it (scene/integrator.cpp, kernel/film/light_passes.h film_clamp_light): a
per-channel-average limit. Astroray compared XYZ Y, so a saturated blue
contribution passed at 4.6x the limit and a green one was cut at 0.47x. CPU
clampMetricRGB / GPU gpu_clampContribMW now take the contribution's XYZ to linear
Rec.709 and compare (R+G+B)/3 (signed: equal to the fabs sum in gamut, linear in
XYZ, so a hero-wavelength sample's chroma noise does not inflate it).

The #884 symptom itself (geometry_zoo cabinet, clamp 10, volume_bounces 4:
Astroray 9-10 % removed vs Cycles 2 %) no longer reproduces on main f06f356:
2.0 % vs 2.0 % (3 seeds x 96 spp); 1.3 % with this branch.

A voxel grid has been added, running entirely on the GPU. It allows for real-time modification of a huge number of voxels, such as real-time voxelization (exactly what we need).
P.S. Currently, loading a huge number of chunks takes just one frame, causing noticeable stuttering during movement. This will definitely need to be fixed in the future.

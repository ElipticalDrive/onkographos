Issue body actions
A scalar grid drawn unlit through a window and a ramp in OKLab, with slice planes and a maximum-intensity mode for scans, and a window refused when it misses the grid's measured range.
Epic: #310 · Part of: #175 · Design: Volumes
Scope
Data volumes: kind data draws a scalar grid by hash, with declared dims (each ≤ 256) and dtype (u8, u16 or f32), unlit through a window and a ramp from value to look role and opacity, interpolated in OKLab, with up to 3 slice planes; the step count changes sampling, never how much light a depth stops; the check measures the grid's range and refuses a window that misses it with volume.window
Maximum intensity: a data volume with mode: max draws each ray's greatest windowed value inside the box, up to the first slice, once, in the ramp's role and at its opacity, the X-ray and angiography view of a scan
Acceptance criteria
A data volume of a 64³ u8 grid of the constant 51, 0.64 m on a side, with window [0, 102] and a ramp from opacity 0 at 0 to 0.1 at 1, passes 0.95^64 ≈ 0.0375 of the background seen along an axis through its center within 1%, as the difference between its frames over a white and a black backdrop on a linear target read back, at 96 steps and at 192; a slice through the center of a grid that rises linearly from 0 to 1 along x shows, at x = 0.5, the OKLab midpoint of the ramp's two end roles (a blue and a yellow) within 1 code value with tone map none; the frame at t has the same pixel hash seeked or played while a channel sweeps the window; and an f32 grid whose values lie in [0, 1] with window [100, 2000] is refused at check with volume.window, naming the window and the measured range. A planted march that applies each step's opacity uncorrected gives different results at 96 and 192 steps and fails, one that interpolates the ramp in sRGB misses the midpoint and fails, and one that checks the window without measuring the grid passes the empty window and fails
Maximum intensity: a 64³ u8 grid, 0.64 m on a side, zero but for two 8³ cubes of 200 and 100 on the axis through its centre, one in front of the other, seen along that axis, with window [0, 255], a ramp from a blue at 0 to a yellow at 1, both at opacity 1, and mode: max, shows at the centre pixel the ramp's OKLab color at 200 ÷ 255 within 1 code value with tone map none, at 96 and at 192 steps and with the cubes swapped; with the 100 cube in front, under a perspective camera, a ray that meets only it, beside the 200 cube's silhouette, shows the color at 100 ÷ 255; and a planted mean along the ray fails at the centre, and a planted first value above a threshold fails once the cubes swap, each naming the pixel
Proof
The constant and two-cube grids read back at 96 and 192 steps, and each planted march failing.

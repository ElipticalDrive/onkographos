Project scope
The goal of this project was to enhance the Alpha Wrap algorithm, in particular its implementation in CGAL. I worked on two tasks:
In the beginning of the coding period, I implemented and benchmarked several sphere marching algorithms to enhance the performance of the offset intersection (Rule 1 in the paper).
In the end of the coding period, I implemented the Alpha Wrap algorithm with a varying alpha value.

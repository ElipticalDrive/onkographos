Goal
Explain eight-way octree subdivision in the simplified Explanation mode without replacing the real 3D octree with a separate quadtree implementation.
Scope
consume the existing Rust/WASM OctreeTrace
show the root cube as two 2D slices: lower-Z and upper-Z
render the four depth-1 XY quadrants in each slice, representing all eight real octants
show each child node's real Rust member IDs/count
let the user step through the eight depth-1 children and highlight the matching slice/quadrant
explain low/high X, Y, and Z decisions for the selected child
report total nodes, leaves, occupied leaves, exact tests, and deeper descendants under the selected child
keep the existing 3D Experiment octree helpers as the direct spatial counterpart
update the roadmap after merge
Non-goal
Do not implement a separate quadtree collision algorithm just for the teaching view.

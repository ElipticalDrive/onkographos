Encyclopedia of Mathematical Geosciences
Definition
Algebraic reconstruction techniques (ART) were initially proposed for radiology and medical applications but have been extensively used to study various seismological phenomena (Del Pino 1985; Peterson et al. 1985). These algorithms are based on iterative reconstruction techniques and efficiently help invert matrices of large dimensions, taking advantage of the sparse nature of the matrices. These methods gained popularity due to the fact that they were able to handle various data geometries with irregular sampling and limited projection angle (Peterson et al. 1985). In addition, these techniques are relatively more straightforward to implement compared to transform methods described by Scudder (Scudder 1978).
This entry describes fundamental principles and derivations of ART along with various modifications to ART, namely simultaneous iterative reconstructive technique (SIRT) and simultaneous algebraic reconstructive technique (SART).

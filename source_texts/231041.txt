Programme context. Part of the neural-fields work across the stack (wave epic #82). The literature review is geostack report 18 and the plan is the neural-fields design page; both land in #87. Everything needed to implement this issue is below.
Problem / Request
Decide, with numbers, whether neural fields are worth pursuing as a storage format for GeoStack outputs (reanalysis cubes, model trajectories, DEMs), by benchmarking them against error-bounded codecs at matched maximum pointwise error, not PSNR.
Motivation
Report 18 found the published picture is mixed: Huang & Hoefler (ICLR 2023) beat SZ3 at 300–3,000× on weighted RMSE, CA-INR reports ratio 140 on E3SM, Climplicit replaces CHELSA with a 16 MB model, but a 2026 ERA5 study (arXiv 2606.14353) found SZ3.1 leads at every error bound at dataset level, and ImplicitTerrainV2 concedes SZ3 wins on DEMs (80.45 dB at 1.96 bpp vs 65.97 dB at 1.23 bpp). Most INR papers compare against JPEG2000 or other INRs. The real advantages are random access, analytic derivatives and context conditioning, so the benchmark must measure those too.
Design Snapshot
```
data      ERA5 t2m + z500, 0.25°, 1 year (WeatherBench subset); one DEM tile
methods   SZ3, ZFP (via hdf5plugin / pysz), geonnax SIREN + MultiScaleSIREN,
          CA-INR-style context inputs (topography, climatology)
weights   float16 + zstd; optional 8-bit quantization
metrics   compression ratio at max-abs-error ∈ {0.01, 0.1, 1.0} (units of the variable),
          wall-clock encode/decode, random-access query cost, gradient error (∂/∂x vs finite diff)
```
Implementation Steps
A research notebook in research_notebook (data prep with xrtoolz, training with pipekit)
A short results section appended to docs/reports/18_neural_fields.md
A recommendation: keep, park, or drop compression from the roadmap
Definition of Done
Ratios reported at matched max error for every method
Recommendation recorded on the design page

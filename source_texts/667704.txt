Add Volumetric Smoke experiment based on Murmuration
…
8828759
```
Reuse Murmuration's curl-noise motion and lifecycle respawn verbatim
(init.wgsl, update.wgsl) and replace the lit-particle rendering with a
volumetric smoke look:

- lightdensity.wgsl: accumulate soft gaussian density in light space
  (additive) as a single-scattering / self-shadow source.
- smoke.wgsl: draw each particle as a soft gaussian puff and accumulate
  with order-independent weighted additive blending; shade via
  transmittance sampled from the light-space density map plus soft
  form shading.
- composite.wgsl: resolve the weighted accumulation (avg color +
  Beer-Lambert coverage) over a background gradient, then grade.
- Bloom/threshold/blur/screen passes reused from Murmuration.

Adds the volumetricSmoke Vite entry and a gallery card.
```

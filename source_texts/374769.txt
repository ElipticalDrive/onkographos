Native smoke puffs currently disappear before weapon light or the Sharpshooter sight can interact with a lingering cloud. RTX now feeds actual smoke emissions into bounded persistent fluid fields, shades them with the shared weapon lights, and scatters the native sight narrowly inside density. Ordinary smoke thins for up to 10 seconds; nuke pickup smoke has a distinct 15-second presentation profile.
The NumPy CPU solver advances planar velocity, density, and temperature on up to four 144×144 grids. The OpenGL shader displays an assumed vertical density profile with single scattering. This is a 2D flow / 2.5D display approximation, not a 3D fluid solver or a GPU simulation. Dark general explosion puffs retain a restrained source contribution so they do not obscure the finer exhaust wisps.
Native state, spawn ordering, random-number consumption, and checkpoint serialization remain unchanged. Immutable per-tick emissions preserve delayed effect ages. Exact pool/slot/generation claims suppress only replaced native puffs; capped, oversized, old, and reused slots retain their native fallback. Simulation time drives advancement independently of drawing, including fractional slow motion and forward replay. Reset, session replacement, RTX toggles, and the smoke setting clear presentation history. New deposits do not restore an old patch's faded opacity.
Validation:
31 focused CPU tests cover capture parity, actual nuke pickup, delayed sources, bounded fields, ownership, lifetime, reset/toggle/restore, and resource cleanup.
298 smoke, simulation, effects, bonus, and replay regressions pass on the merged head.
All 949 rendering tests pass with terrain checks enabled, including native OpenGL tests for fractional source gains, density-only sight scattering, and continuous faint extinction.
Native integrated recordings cover all four rocket weapons and an 18-second nuke aftermath, plus exhaust-light controls. A merged-head capture verifies smoke alongside Radioactive and Monster Vision.
Ruff and the repository's full source/test type-check scope pass.
CPU-only fixed-step measurements on Apple M1 Pro: median 0.00033 ms with no fields, 5.88 ms with one active field, and 23.54 ms with four. These exclude texture uploads, GPU shading, world rendering, and readback; they are not frame-rate claims. Empty scenes perform no fluid steps. Overlapping fields do not exchange velocity or shadow each other; a patch receiving nuke smoke shares its slower decay until retirement.

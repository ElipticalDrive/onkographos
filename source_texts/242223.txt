Implements the OpenSpec change mis-for-shadow-linked-lights (proposal, design, delta spec and tasks are in the branch's first commit).
Why
A light whose collection:shadowLink excludes occluders was sampled by NEE alone at continuous vertices: a bounce ray is stopped by occluders the light's shadow rays ignore, so the two strategies disagreed on visibility. That dropped the half of MIS that keeps glossy reflections quiet. On ALab the shadow-linked distant sun was 66% of the frame's firefly variance.
What changes
Link twin (tracer/path.rs, link_twin): at a continuous surface, volume or enclosure vertex, after the bounce direction is drawn, each restricted non-dome light is intersected analytically along it (LightShape::hits, LightKind::found_along). A shadow ray with the light's own mask then tests NEE's visibility, crossings and volumes included. The result is weighted as a bounce hit on the light would be and routed as a crossed light for LPEs (C.*[LO] stays the beauty bit for bit).
One owner: the ordinary bounce collects nothing from such a light under any strategy (LightList::bounce_skips). NEE uses its normal MIS weight. light, bsdf and MIS now estimate one image.
Unchanged: restricted domes stay NEE-only, and delta vertices still see the physical occluders (documented gaps).
CRUST_LINK_TWIN=0 restores the NEE-only estimator for A/B. It's documented in docs/architecture.md and the site.
trace_path / advance_pixel are monomorphised on TWINS, so scenes without a twinned light run an integrator with no twin code.
Measurements
ALab frame 1004, 256 spp, --light-samples 4 --light-selection learned, two seeds:
lgt_sun_distant direct-glossy variance 3 551 → 100 (−97%).
Frame variance −34%; worst pixel noise 39 → 25.
Render time +2.3% (bench_ab.sh, n = 3), against a +5% budget.
Means agree: the old estimator reads low at 256 spp (heavy tail) and converges to the new one at 1024.
Linked glossy test scene:
Before: bsdf gave 0.0037 against light's 0.808.
After: all three strategies agree (sphere and distant light), and power-MIS variance is −73%.
Goldens: all 35 unlinked samples are bit-identical (check_images.sh). light_linking.usda moves by relMSE 2.5e-9, against 3e-3 between seeds.
Instructions: cornellbox +0.014% (callgrind, 2 spp). A runtime guard instead of the const generic cost +0.23%.
Full numbers and method are in docs/alab_profile.md ("Shadow-linked lights and their noise") and openspec/specs/lighting/design.md § Shadow linking.
Tests
analytic_light_hits_match_the_kernel: analytic vs kernel hits for sphere, rect and affine sphere/disk/cylinder.
every_strategy_agrees_on_a_shadow_link_that_matters, mis_is_whole_again_for_a_shadow_linked_light and a_restricted_dome_stays_nee_only replace power_mis_matches_nee_only_for_a_shadow_linked_light.
The LPE bitwise, partition and light/bsdf-agreement tests now also run on a linked scene.
cargo fmt, clippy (stable and pinned nightly), and cargo test --workspace in debug and release all pass.

Description
```
LightList::set_links builds twinned from restricted && !nee_only and only debug-asserts
```

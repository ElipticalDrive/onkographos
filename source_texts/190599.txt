The three parallel pure-C# math modules for M2, built against docs/m2-contract.md.
MarchingCubes (+tables): canonical Lorensen-Cline/Bourke tables (public domain, cited), indexed watertight mesh via global-edge dedup, field-gradient normals (outward = -grad), deterministic serial march.
MetaballField: Wyvill kernel with support R = r/0.454 so a lone bone's iso=0.5 contour sits exactly at its radius; overlapping bones sum and fuse. Bounds = per-bone segment AABBs grown by support radius.
AutoSkinner: 4 nearest bones by point-to-segment distance, inverse-distance weights normalized to sum 1, stable index tie-break, safe at endpoints.
Each module is pure/deterministic (no RNG, no clock). 84/84 unit tests pass (was 50): +7 MC (incl. table-integrity + watertight + on-sphere shape), +17 field, +10 skin. Integration into the Recipe->mesh pipeline is P2.

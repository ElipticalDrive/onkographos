Summary
2D SDF library (src/engine/sdf/). Part of #61; plan in docs/IQ-PLAN.md.
Why
Today the engine has five SDF helpers. A complete, exact 2D shape vocabulary (with gradients for lighting) lets cards draw any outline as a field instead of approximating it.
Scope and sources
The full 2D primitive set from 2D SDFs:
Shapes: circle, (rounded, chamfer, oriented) box, segment, rhombus, trapezoid, parallelogram, equilateral, isosceles and general triangle, uneven capsule, pentagon, hexagon, octagon, hexagram, pentagram, star(n, m), pie, cut disk, arc, ring, horseshoe, (oriented) vesica, moon, rounded cross, egg, heart, cross, rounded X, polygon, ellipse, parabola (and segment), quadratic Bézier, blobby cross, tunnel, stairs, quadratic circle, hyperbola, cool S and circle wave;
Modifiers: opRound and opOnion.
Analytic gradients come from 2D SDFs and gradients (sdg*, returning d plus ∂x and ∂y), which gives per-dot normals for lighting at no extra samples.
The robust ellipse comes from Distance to an ellipse (Newton or trig-free), and the C²-continuous corners from Smooth rounded boxes.
L∞ variants from 2D SDFs in L∞ are optional: square isolines suit the squares track.
light/sdf.ts is then re-exported through the new module, with no pixel change.
How
Port from the linked articles (read in full; their actual content is summarised above), credited per function. Where an article uses GPU derivatives (fwidth, dFdx), take the footprint explicitly: the cell size, (cell, 0) and (0, cell).
Keep modules outside the engine barrel and import them by path.
Acceptance criteria
src/engine stays framework-free with no new dependency; every visual goes Field → Grid Sampler → Renderer; one accent per board.
pnpm golden --check: 0 changed (packs are additive; light/sdf.ts stays bit-identical), N added for new cards.
First-load JS unchanged (±0.1 kB gz): new modules imported by path, never through the engine barrel; new boards lazy.
Frame ≤ 4 ms × load per card.
Unit checks per ported function against reference values (distance at known points, gradient vs. finite difference, inverse round-trip, band-limited mean preservation).
Credit: a source comment on every ported function (// after Inigo Quilez, <article URL> (MIT)), the article's own credits carried over, THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md present, and cards carry a credit.
Dependencies / blocked by
None. Card credit comes from #53 (tixy T2), but the engine pack does not need it.

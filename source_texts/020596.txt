In the study of the realistic graphics,the complex model of real-time rendering and dynamic display has been a hot one.Details of the level are an important technology of real-time graphics generated.Level of model means to create models with different details in the same scene,and uses them while drawing.Creating models at multiple level of detail can effectively reduce data amount and complexity and realize real-time processing of the three-dimensional scene.Owing to the problem that the ray casting algorithm has high image quality and more slow velocity,the thought is provided to introduce the LOD technique into the ray casting volume-rendering algorithm in order to improve the interactive function in drawing volume-rendering image.This will offer the theoretical foundation to the future studies.

The most important thing that stops me from implementing cube files is lacking of correct, and safe parser, and I'm just lazy to dig into a specification when I'm not actually need this.

Since you're not familiar with color space math when parser is ready, then I could wrap this up by myself, that shouldn't be hard or long.

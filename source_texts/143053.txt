16.2.10 Volume Cutting Planes
Additional surfaces can be created on the volume with user defined clipping
planes. A clipping plane can be used to cut through the volume, exposing
a new surface. This technique can help expose the volume's
internal structure. The rendering technique is the same, with the addition
of one or more clipping planes defined while rendering and blending the
data slice polygons.

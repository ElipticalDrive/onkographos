With CUDA framework for parallel computing and GPU with high-efficiency parallelism as well as flexible programmability,this paper proposes a numerical control simulation processing surface rendering algorithms.The algorithm mainly includes: parallel interval tree traversal index for voxel volumes,get the marked information of active voxel's corner and edge;map between the voxel marked information and classification of marching cubes models;these results match corresponding to the MC models will directly render.The based on CUDA architecture of GPU parallel algorithm,improved the speed of rendering,enlarged the real-time of simulation in theory.

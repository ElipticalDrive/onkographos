Photons emitted by the wind in polar (r-theta) grids are not placed uniformly in the volume of their cell.
Example: rtheta_get_random_location chooses r uniformly in r^2 and theta uniformly in sin(theta):
```
r = sqrt (rmin * rmin + random_number (0.0, 1.0) * (rmax * rmax - rmin * rmin));
... sthetamin + random_number (0.0, 1.0) * (sthetamax - sthetamin) ...
```
For a uniform distribution in a spherical-polar cell, r should be uniform in r^3 (as spherical_get_random_location does) and theta uniform in cos(theta). Uniform in sin(theta) gives a probability density proportional to cos(theta) instead of sin(theta): in a cell next to the equator almost no photons start near the equator, and in a cell next to the pole too many start near the pole. The r^2 sampling puts photons slightly too far in.
Cause: the r-theta routine appears to have been adapted from cylind_get_random_location, where uniform in r^2 (rho^2) is correct. The sph3d grid on x3d copied the theta part.
Fix: sample r = (rmin^3 + u (rmax^3 - rmin^3))^(1/3) and cos(theta) = cos(theta_min) + u (cos(theta_max) - cos(theta_min)), which also handles both hemispheres without a separate sign. This changes where wind photons start within their cells; cylindrical and spherical grids are unaffected. Tested on x3d (patch available) with cv_kur, cv_sph3d and agn_sph3d: the spectra change by no more than the seed-to-seed noise (total flux by 0.0-0.6%, against 0.1-1.6% between two seeds), so the effect on these models is small, but the sampling is still wrong for cells near the pole and the equator.
Current branches needing the fix: main, dev, biconic, polar, polarization26, x3d (x3d also in sph3d_get_random_location, theta only).

In a previous paper [12] (see [11] for an expanded version) we proposed a pyramid scheme for MIP volume rendering with progressive refinement based on morphological pyra-mids [3,6]. Such pyramids, which involve nonlinear spatial filtering by morphological operators [5, 14], systematically split the volume data into approximation and detail signals.

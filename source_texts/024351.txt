The implemented algorithm extracts isosurfaces from discrete data on arbitrary polyhedra, whether convex or non-convex, and even with non-planar face boundaries. The software package includes a user manual and routines for writing the geometry of polyhedra and isosurfaces to external files. Test programs are included to extract isosurfaces on single polyhedral cells and grids of any type, either from implicit mathematical functions or discrete data such as volume fractions or signed distances to scanned surfaces. The proposed method for isosurface extraction on arbitrary polyhedra employs a general polygon tracing procedure that can be applied to both convex and non-convex geometries, producing consistent results even in ambiguous situations. The locations of the extracted isosurface points are approximately computed through interpolation. This procedure does not rely on predefined configurations and can be considered an extension of the marching cubes technique to arbitrary polyhedra. The implemented routines can be used with Fortran or C, and OpenMP application programming interface is employed to speed up operations performed over grids. The proposed algorithm has multiple applications, including surface visualization in computer graphics and the reconstruction of interfaces in PLIC (piecewise linear interface calculation) VOF (volume of fluid) methods. This version has been extensively modified and restructured to improve interoperability through the ISO_C_BINDING module and incorporates several improvements for computational efficiency.

Summary
Three brick-renderer problems reported on Dml3RG:
OOM on 2D→3D with a 12/16 GB cache on a 20 GB GPU. setImage destroyed the old atlases and created new ones in the same tick; Dawn frees VRAM only after the queue retires, so old + new had to fit at once. Now: wait for onSubmittedWorkDone after a drop, allocate under an awaited OOM scope, and step down one atlas per OOM (only a single-atlas failure reaches the flat fallback).
Flicker returning after a few playback loops. The play loop's working set (~5 GB) exceeded the 4.3 GB atlas, and LRU on a cyclic sweep evicts exactly the next-needed frame — every loop refetched everything (15 GB fetched in the bench). While playing, eviction now ranks by next use (Belady's MIN): 181 → 25 refetches per loop on that view.
Cache budget rounded down. Atlases were sized at maxBufferSize and counted with floor, so 12 GB used 8.6 GB and anything under 8.6 GB used 4.3 GB. The budget is now split evenly (atlasCountForBudget, shared with the chip label). Plan Decision 4 updated.
Also: prefetch direction at the loop wrap, multi-atlas prefetch capacity, label atlases following the step-down, and setZPlane / maxSafePrefetchDepth during the async allocation window.
Test plan

Feature Description
A god-ray implementation that doesn't use ray-marching but a more simple way (that can be possibly altered to bring better results). God rays give light and volume more feel by making it seem as if particles in the air are scattering light a little, mainly present around objects blocking light.
Benefits
It would give the feel of volumetric effects without actually delving into them just yet, improving the visuals significantly.
Additional Context
Example of implementation - A video on a minecraft clone (possibly useful for other features as well) that shows the implementation of this feature in a Unity URP Context.
Confirmation
I would like to work on/contribute to this feature myself.

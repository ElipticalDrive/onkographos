💡 What:
Implemented Axis-Aligned Bounding Box (AABB) viewport culling and WeakMap bounds caching in BuildingRenderer.ts.
🎯 Why:
Previously, BuildingRenderer projected all vertex coordinates (projectPoint) and generated PixiJS polygon paths for every building in the map dataset on every camera movement (rebuildOnCameraChange). When zoomed into a specific area of the map, 90%+ of buildings are off-screen, leading to wasted coordinate projections, array allocations, and vector graphics commands on every frame during panning/zooming.
📊 Impact:
Eliminates off-screen building coordinate projections and PixiJS shape path allocations during camera movements.
Significantly reduces CPU time and GC overhead during map interactions (pan, zoom, tilt) when zoomed in.
🔬 Measurement:
Verified with unit tests in src/rendering/BuildingRenderer.test.ts ensuring off-screen buildings are skipped while visible ones are projected and cached.
Confirmed with pnpm test and pnpm build.

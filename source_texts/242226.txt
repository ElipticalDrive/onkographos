Implements the OpenSpec change multiple-light-samples-per-vertex: crust:lightSamples / --light-samples N (the first vertex of each path) and crust:lightSamplesIndirect / --light-samples-indirect M (every later surface or volume vertex), both default 1.
What changes
Stratified picks. The N picks at a vertex take (i + u) / N, so a light with selection probability p is picked N·p times, give or take one (pinned: pmfs 0.5/0.25/0.25 at N = 4 give 2/1/1 at every vertex). Only the pick is stratified — tying the point-on-light coordinates to the same slice would bias which part of a light each sample sees; the design record says why.
Multi-sample MIS. LightList::density(pdf, pmf, samples) takes the vertex's count, so both MIS halves state it. NEE weights with and divides by N·p (the division is the average — the first draft divided by N twice and the agreement test caught it at 38% of its reference). The bounce side weights with the count PrevVertex carries from the vertex it left: surface and volume bounces, escaped light, hidden-light crossings.
LPE routing. One L event per contributing light sample; C.*[LO] stays pinned bitwise to the beauty at (4, 2), (4, 4) and (2, 3), and partitions still sum.
Unchanged by default. The surface block is an always-inlined, closure-free function the vertex calls straight-line for its first sample, with a cold loop for the rest. Every sample scene and both Kitchen sets are bit-identical to main's 16 spp goldens; cornellbox at 2 spp on one thread counts 4,231,470,450 instructions against 4,233,257,504 (−0.04%). (cornellbox_guided differs between any two multi-threaded runs of main itself; it is identical single-threaded.)
Verified
Strategy agreement (light-only, BSDF-only, power and balance MIS, and the one-sample renderer) on a floor under a sphere light, a rect light and a dome at (4, 4), (4, 1), (1, 4), (3, 2), and in samples/fog.usda at (1, 4) and (4, 4) — crates/crust-core/tests/light_samples.rs.
RMSE between N = 4 and N = 1 falls 2.0–2.8× from 4 to 16 spp on three samples: noise, not bias.
Import and CLI parse tests, a refused 0 included; fmt, clippy -D warnings, cargo test --workspace, zola build.
Measurement and the default
Equal-time efficiency at 16 spp against 1024 spp unclamped references (four seeds; the full table, protocol and caveats are in openspec/specs/lighting/design.md, "Several light samples per vertex"). Four camera-vertex samples cut the error at equal time by 37% on veach_mis and 32% on ALab, where direct area light at a diffuse first hit is the noise; they cost 10–45% on dome-lit cornellbox, the glossy openpbr_showcase and instancing, the volume scenes, and the Playground (glass first hits). The indirect count never pays on its own. Both defaults stay at 1; --light-samples 4 is the recommendation for interiors and area-lit sets, and no default-change proposal follows.
Lessons recorded in the design record
Reaching the instruction budget took six builds. The decisive cause was not the loop: the per-sample block is inlined twice, and two closures inside it (the pick, the shadow-ray visibility) got a second call site, so LLVM stopped inlining them, at 0.65% each. A block inlined twice must contain no closure worth inlining.
Note: learned-light-selection-by-default also modifies the "Light selection" requirement; whichever change archives second must restate it with both applied.

`crust:lightSamples` / `--light-samples N` (the first vertex of each path)
and `crust:lightSamplesIndirect` / `--light-samples-indirect M` (every
later surface or volume vertex), both default 1 and at least 1.

- The N picks at a vertex are stratified to `(i + u) / N`, so a light of
  selection probability p is picked N·p times, give or take one. Only the
  pick is stratified: tying the point-on-light coordinates to the same
  slice would bias which part of a light each sample sees.
- Multi-sample MIS: `LightList::density` takes the vertex's sample count,
  so both MIS halves state it. NEE weights with and divides by N·p (the
  division is the average); the bounce side weights with the count
  `PrevVertex` carries from the vertex it left, escaped light and
  hidden-light crossings included.
- Light path expressions route one `L` event per contributing sample;
  `C.*[LO]` stays pinned bitwise to the beauty.

  vertex calls straight-line for its first sample, with a cold loop for
  the rest: every sample scene is bit-identical to main at N = M = 1 and
  cornellbox counts 0.04% fewer instructions (callgrind, 2 spp).

camera-vertex samples are 1.59× more efficient on veach_mis and 1.46× on
ALab, and a 10–45% loss on dome-lit, glossy and volume scenes, so both
defaults stay at 1. The table, the protocol and the recommendation are in
the lighting design record.

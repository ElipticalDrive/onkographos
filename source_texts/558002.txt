Summary
High-level 3D inverse design currently extrudes a planar pattern and rejects Gaussian beam sources, preventing a BeamZ implementation of the polarization-insensitive grating coupler example.
Add optional volumetric (z, y, x) density parameters, spherical FFT filtering, differentiable interpolation between density and FDTD grids, and polygon export by z layer.
Support fixed Gaussian beam sources with autodiff and spectral adjoints. Validate source/design clearance and reject source or modal planes inside the absorber along their normal axis.
Search additional 3D eigenmode candidates for polarization selection: a one-mode TE/TM request previously could select the same physical mode. Bump the topology numerical fingerprint to 6 so earlier objective histories cannot resume silently.
Add an executed polarization-insensitive grating coupler notebook, with compact optimization, shaded 3D cutaways, field sections, spectra, and binary-density validation.
The reference profile optimizes a 10 × 10 × 5 µm polymer volume on glass over 20 Adam updates, with 50 nm density pixels and a uniform 100 nm FDTD grid. It documents the full-domain symmetry implementation, polarization and beam-angle conventions, and the output-monitor adjustment needed to measure before BeamZ's PML. Mesh convergence and fabrication suitability require further validation.
Correctness
188 selected tests passed across density transforms, interpolation gradients, fixed-mask/layer export, Gaussian injection and power normalization, polarization selection, 3D topology, and spectral/broadband adjoints. Re-ran all 50 affected workflow/adjoint tests after adding the absorber-placement guard.
Both reference and smoke notebook profiles executed successfully. The reference directional derivative agrees with finite differences to 0.000535%; ordinary forward simulations agree with the optimization amplitudes.
Final reference coupling at 1.55 µm: x→TM 69.95%, y→TE 71.82%, mean 70.88%, PDL 0.115 dB. A 50% longer run changes coupling by less than 0.01%; thresholded density gives 70.14% and 71.75%.
make format, repository-wide Ruff checks, make dead-code, changed-module Pyright, and make build (including distribution validation) passed. Notebook outputs were validated and figures visually inspected.
make audit stopped at five gdsfactory attribute type errors in the unchanged beamz/design/gds.py (lines 89, 101, 488, 555, and 579). Its full-suite/coverage stage did not run; the passing selected tests above are separate evidence.

Phase 3, voxels: greedy meshing (src/voxel/mesher.rs).
For each of the six face directions and each slice of a chunk, the visible faces are gathered into a grid and merged into rectangles where texture and corner lighting match. UVs run 0 to width and 0 to height, and the block texture array already samples with repeat. A face whose four corners are not equally lit is never merged, so ambient occlusion looks exactly as before.
A flat chunk's ground goes from 2,048 quads to 2.
Checked: a new test compares, per face direction, the signed area the mesh covers against a block-by-block count of exposed faces, on flat ground and on ground with blocks, a hole and a wall; the existing mesher tests pass unchanged; clippy clean; the voxel world example renders correctly.
Not done (still on the roadmap): a pass for see-through blocks, and chunk draws through the indirect path.

A chunk's mesh had four vertices for every block face that showed. Faces in one
plane with the same texture, evenly lit, are now joined into one quad as wide and
as high as they go, the texture repeating across it once a block: flat ground a
chunk across is two quads where it was 2,048. A face lit unevenly at its corners
is left on its own, so that the shading by the foot of a wall is as it was.

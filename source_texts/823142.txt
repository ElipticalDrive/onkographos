Our long-time simlations indicate that there is
no large beam blow-up due to "Arnold Diffusion" at
Pp
collider parameters. Future simulations will be undertaken to set further limits on this process.
Our 1-D simulations indicate that the coupling of
randon diffusion fran outside sources to the nonlinear force is inportant in limiting beam stability.
Extension of our analysis to the pp 2-D gecxetry and
inclusion of other non-linear and noise effects such
as "tune nrodulation" and "power supply ripple" will
make these limits ore precise.

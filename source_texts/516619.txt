Fixes #8.
Adds the r=volume renderer (panel chip "volume") on both backends: a fragment raymarch of the sim's own density grid — tent-blurred mass field, iso surface with bisection refinement, gradient normals, Fresnel + spec + momentum-based foam, one refraction segment with Beer–Lambert absorption, background continued analytically along the refracted ray. Renders into a scaled offscreen target (?rscale=, default 0.5, validated once in app.js) and upscales.
WebGPU: volBlur compute + volume/volUpscale modules; grid storage buffer read in fragment. WebGL2: GLSL twins over the tiled grid texture.
Review round: wall/rock hit-point shading factored into one shared sceneShade chunk per language (used by background + volume); f now cycles all renderer chips (surface → particles → volume); stats line shows the effective rscale= in volume mode; verify skill documents the new mode.
Occlusion-correct by construction (verified at the SSF rendering: pool visually occludes the nearer waterfall stream where they overlap #6 angle); gizmo renders in-mode; mode switches instant and survive panel rebuilds (incl. in-volume grid-chip rebuild at 32³).
Bench (128³/384², Apple silicon): WebGPU 3.04 ms/frame at 0.5× scale, 3.23 ms at 1×; WebGL2 9.22 ms.
Verification (post-refactor, final code): volume at 32³–128³ both backends; ssf + points regression screenshots re-taken on both backends after the sceneShade refactor; ?dbg=1 in volume mode (real-time run); f-cycle + rebuild + rock-drag harness; ?rscale= probes (0.25 blockier, garbage → 0.5 asserted via stats line); 700×900 window. Reviewed by architecture/logic/security/tests agents over two rounds.

```
New render mode on both backends: a fragment shader raymarches the
simulation's own (momentum, mass) grid as a participating volume with a
refractive iso-surface. Per frame the grid is box-blurred (tent 1,2,1
weights) into a small (mass, |momentum|) field — WebGPU compute pass into
a storage buffer, WebGL2 fragment pass into a tiled RGBA16F texture —
then the march finds the iso crossing (0.5-cell steps, dithered, capped
by the analytic scene hit so rocks occlude water), refines it with 4
bisection steps, shades with a central-difference gradient normal
(Fresnel, spec, foam from blurred momentum magnitude), and follows one
refracted ray through the interior, integrating Beer-Lambert absorption
from the marched density before continuing the analytic walls-and-rocks
background along it. Renders into a scaled offscreen target (?rscale=,
default 0.5) and upscales; gizmo overlay drawn on top as in other modes.

Water-over-water occlusion is correct by construction (single march hits
the nearest surface first). Bench at 128^3/384^2 (M-series, 900x650):
3.0 ms/frame at 0.5x, 3.2 ms at 1x on WebGPU (sim-dominated), 9.2 ms on
WebGL2.
```

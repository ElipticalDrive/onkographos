Phase 1 quick win (World explorer review). Size: S–M (about a day). Line numbers are as of main 4ffa8f01.
Problem
The fog never reaches full strength before terrain and objects are cut off, so there is a hard edge, and the sky doesn't match the fog. The image has no anti-aliasing and no anisotropic filtering.
Fog is only about 0.85 at the 2200 yd cut (rendering-bgfx/src/main/shaders/world-stream/vs_model.sc:15, :23), and the fog colour differs from the sky's horizon colour (rendering-bgfx/src/main/shaders/terrain/sky/fs_model.sc:8). Distant terrain and objects pop out against a different colour.
The far cut is a fragment discard (rendering-bgfx/src/main/shaders/world-wmo/fs_model.sc:8-10).
No MSAA (rendering-bgfx/src/main/java/com/vibeminer/rendering/bgfx/WorldStreamNativeOwner.java:109, :140-159) and no anisotropic filtering (SAMPLER_FLAGS at rendering-bgfx/src/main/java/com/vibeminer/rendering/bgfx/BgfxWorldStreamRenderer.java:100, OBJECT_SAMPLER_FLAGS at :1081). Ground and walls at grazing angles blur, and edges alias.
Proposed approach
Fog reaches 1.0 at the view cut. One fog-colour uniform used by terrain, objects and the sky's lower band, so the horizon colour equals the fog colour.
Move the far cut into the vertex shader. Dropped (2026-11-06): a per-vertex cut would distort triangles that straddle the cut. Cull exterior object placements on the CPU before drawing (#356) #369's CPU cull already drops every placement wholly past it, and fog reaches 1.0 at the cut.
MSAA x4 on the World render target, resolved before readback. Anisotropic filtering on terrain and object samplers. Alpha-to-coverage for cutouts when MSAA is on.
Caps check: fall back to no MSAA where the target can't be multisampled, and report it.
Coordinate with PR #350 (#177 step 1), which derives sky and fog colours from the light tables. If it has merged, feed its colour into the shared uniform; otherwise keep today's colour and let #350 feed it later.
Recompile every shader backend (D3D11, SPIR-V, Metal, GLSL) and keep the D3D11 instance-semantics rule.
Acceptance criteria
Tests: fog factor is 1.0 at the cut; the sky's horizon colour equals the fog uniform; sampler flags include anisotropy; MSAA falls back cleanly when unsupported.
Native GPU proofs pass on D3D11 and Vulkan on this machine, including the MSAA resolve. Metal needs a macOS machine; if it isn't checked, say so in the PR.
Owner check: no visible edge where terrain and buildings end; the horizon blends into the sky; roads and walls at grazing angles look sharp; less edge shimmer.
The visual gains are inferred from code, not measured. Record MSAA's frame-time cost with the Phase 0 probe (#353).
Files
Shaders world-stream/*, world-wmo/*, terrain/sky/* and their compiled binaries; WorldStreamNativeOwner (render target); BgfxWorldStreamRenderer (sampler constants and fog uniform only). The CPU-culling issue (#356) edits the same renderer file: same developer, or merge in sequence. PR #276 also edits WorldStreamNativeOwner and the renderer.
Refs
World explorer review (renderer F6/F7, far cull from F2). #177, PR #350. Anisotropic filtering was a follow-up named in #286. Roadmap #186.

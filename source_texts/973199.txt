USD cameras authoring positive fStop and focusDistance attributes previously ignored depth of field and always rendered as pinhole perspective sensors in both hdMitsuba and usd_mitsuba.
Translate such cameras to Mitsuba's thinlens sensor with aperture_radius = 0.5 * focalLength_scene_units / fStop, and update aperture_radius and focus_distance in place during interactive Hydra camera parameter edits.

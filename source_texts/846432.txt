At the time of this writing, REACT3D is using a small custom-made Matrix library. It would probably be best to swap this out in favor of using a separate, better-established open-source 3D math library.
This calls for doing some research and finding a good, mature C++ math library that meets the purposes of the project, and that most importantly has a sufficiently permissive license.
At the time of this writing, I think a leading contender is Eigen: http://eigen.tuxfamily.org/index.php?title=Main_Page

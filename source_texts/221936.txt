Part of #372.
Problem
The colorize depth buffer splats each projected map point over an isotropic disc — CPU at bagwiz_slam/src/core/slam/colorize_rasterizer_cpu.cpp:182-187, CUDA at colorize_rasterizer_gpu.cu:262-290:
```
const double radius_px = std::clamp(f_avg * spacings_[i] / (2.0 * depth), 0.0, config_.splat_radius_max_px);
splat_depth(depth_buffer_, u, v, radius_px, ...);
```
The header already names the tradeoff (colorize_rasterizer.hpp:70-79): splatting closes the holes a sparse map leaves in the pixel grid, "at the cost of over-culling near depth edges."
A circular disc over-culls specifically at grazing incidence. A surface seen nearly edge-on projects to a thin sliver, but every one of its points still stamps a full circle of its own depth into the buffer, occluding its own neighbours. That is exactly the geometry a vehicle-mounted camera sees most: road surface ahead, and building facades running alongside.
The information needed to fix it is already computed. ColorizeGeometry::normals (map_colorizer.hpp:61-66) holds per-point unit normals from the k-NN PCA pre-pass, and observation_incidence_weight() (colorize_weight.hpp:47-49) already consumes them. The rasterizer simply never receives them.
Proposal
Splat the projected surfel (center p, radius spacing/2, normal n) rather than a screen-space circle. The projection of a 3D disc is an ellipse; the standard EWA-splatting derivation gives its 2x2 screen-space covariance from the Jacobian of the projection at p and the disc's tangent frame. Clamp the major axis to splat_radius_max_px as today.
Cheaper approximation if the full EWA form is more than this needs: keep the circle but compress it along the projected normal direction, i.e. semi-axes (r, r·|cos θ|) where θ is the incidence angle already available to the weight term.
Both backends need it — splat_depth (colorize_rasterizer_cpu.cpp:309-330) and device_splat_depth (colorize_rasterizer_gpu.cu:262-290). The inner loop swaps a circle test for an ellipse test, which is the same cost.
Plumbing: the factories currently take only spacings (colorize_rasterizer.hpp:153-155, colorize_rasterizer_gpu.hpp:32-34), so both grow a normals span.
Priority
Medium-high. Recovers coverage on grazing-angle geometry with data the pipeline already computes and no measurable extra cost. Changes the visible set, so the CPU/GPU agreement test and the colorize baselines both need review.

What is asked
Render any chunked volume (CT, microscopy, simulation) directly: bricks, a measured density profile, transfer ramps, a mosaic where the finest level wins, near-to-far residency and a front-to-back raymarcher, with a free camera and a sheet tracer for the Herculaneum scrolls.
Why it matters: Volume rendering shows only what the scan resolved, and every speed-up came from fewer memory lookups per sample: 1.5 to 7.0 Msample/s with the image signature unchanged.
In ring 0: Host tool by decision: the scroll viewer has no reason to run in ring 0, but its arithmetic stays Fixed32-testable (see MasterLaplace/LplKernel#182).
What is true today
Built and tested but not committed: voxel/ (Brick, Volume, Transfer, Mosaic, Residency, Raymarch with FreeCamera, Sheet, Surface, Minimap) and zarr/ sit untracked. Single-thread speed went from 1.5 to 7.0 Msample/s with signature 0x6FC98E1F unchanged.
What is missing
Commit voxel/, zarr/ and their parity tests so LplVesuvius builds against a tracked revision. Still open: the density window is set by hand, and the blend at the level 2 to 3 boundary is to be measured.
How we will know it is done
voxel/ and zarr/ are tracked in LplPlugin, their tests pass in CI, and lpl-scrollwalk reproduces the reference image signature from a pose.

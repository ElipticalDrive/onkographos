Transfer Functions in Volume Rendering of Lake Water Quality ...
The general notion of visibility histograms are presented, which are multidimensional graphical representations of the distribution of visibility in a volume-rendered image and can be used together with traditional 1D and 2D opacity transfer functions based on scalar values, as well as with other more sophisticated rendering algorithms.

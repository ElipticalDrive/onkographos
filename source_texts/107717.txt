Implemented a suite of creative features to enhance the voxelizer tool capabilities:
Twist and Taper Transformations: Modifies the mesh geometry before voxelization by applying a rotation proportional to the Z axis (twist) and scaling X/Y proportional to the Z axis (taper).
Simulated Porosity: Randomly culls a percentage of particles during voxelization using a custom, fast sine-based spatial hash algorithm, creating porous or sponge-like structures.
Phase Cuboid: Allows defining an axis-aligned bounding box region where internal particles are specifically assigned a phase of 1, supplementing the existing sphere capability.
Fiber Generation: Calculates a pseudo-random orientation vector (fiber_x, fiber_y) for every particle utilizing the spatial hash, which is useful for simulating anisotropic properties in exported formats.

Implemented a suite of creative features to enhance the voxelizer tool capabilities:
- **Twist and Taper Transformations**: Modifies the mesh geometry before voxelization by applying a rotation proportional to the Z axis (`twist`) and scaling X/Y proportional to the Z axis (`taper`).
- **Simulated Porosity**: Randomly culls a percentage of particles during voxelization using a custom, fast sine-based spatial hash algorithm, creating porous or sponge-like structures.
- **Phase Cuboid**: Allows defining an axis-aligned bounding box region where internal particles are specifically assigned a phase of `1`, supplementing the existing sphere capability.
- **Fiber Generation**: Calculates a pseudo-random orientation vector (`fiber_x`, `fiber_y`) for every particle utilizing the spatial hash, which is useful for simulating anisotropic properties in exported formats.

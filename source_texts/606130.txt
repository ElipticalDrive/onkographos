This change optimizes the grass placement system by sharing visibility information from the terrain rendering pipeline.
Previously, the grass system processed a fixed global grid regardless of whether the terrain was visible or occluded. Now, the TerrainRenderManager exports the results of its frustum and Hi-Z occlusion culling per patch into a TerrainPatchVisibility SSBO.
The grass system utilizes this data in a new grass_pre_pass.comp shader, which filters terrain patches by both their visibility status and their distance from the camera (mapped to grass LOD levels). It builds a compact queue of GrassTask objects.
A grass_command_fixup.comp shader then sets up an indirect dispatch for the main grass_placement.comp shader, which has been refactored to process these tasks. The placement logic ensures that grass blades are only spawned once by validating that jittered positions fall within the task's patch boundaries, and by iterating only over relevant grid cells for the current LOD scale.
This approach significantly reduces redundant compute work in the placement shader, especially in complex or occluded environments.

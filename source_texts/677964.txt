This paper explores the innovative application of 3D Gaussian splatting in constructing high‐fidelity Gaussian avatars. By synthesizing insights from recent advancements, we discuss how 3D Gaussian‐based techniques address challenges in modeling photorealistic and dynamic human avatars. The study examines the core methodologies, compares implementations across various frameworks, and evaluates their impacts on animation realism, computational efficiency, and potential applications in virtual reality, gaming, and digital media.

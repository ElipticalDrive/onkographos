Do a literature review of recent NeRF and 3D reconstruction papers, looking for methods that address the failure modes we've identified. Focus on things like cross-altitude feature matching, pose refinement using geo priors, frame quality scoring and filtering, and adaptive sampling strategies. Pick the most promising approach for our use case and design an implementation plan. Create an initial prototype architecture that we can build on.

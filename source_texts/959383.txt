# Procedural-Object-Generator
This application allows you to generate buildings, landscapes and vegetation using mathematical randomness.

![Demo](https://github.com/sparklemod/Procedural-Object-Generator/blob/main/screens/demo.gif)

The generated models depend on the image created by the user. The left window allows editing textures, and the right window allows viewing the generation result.

Summary
Adds directional-light volumetric rendering and separates the shared geometry prepass from ambient occlusion.
Changes
Implement single-scattering ray marching for homogeneous media with optional bounded volumes for directional light.
Extract GeometryPrepass to provide depth and normals independently of AO; share its textures between SAO/GTAO and volumetric rendering.
Replace fixed shadow coverage with bounds fitted to shadow casters, receivers, and bounded volumes; enable constant and slope-scaled rasterization depth bias.
Refactoring: Update resize resource lifetimes and material rebinding, simplify render-pass setup, and clarify MSAA HDR image naming.

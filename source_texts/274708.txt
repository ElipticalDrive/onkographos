Only a v2 .h5 input is memory-mapped. NIfTI input, the CLI default, is read whole and copied into time-first order (to_time_first, data/dmri/io.py). That costs about twice the series at peak (8.2 GB for an HCP-sized .nii.gz), and the whole series stays resident for the run.
Each volume of a volumes-last NIfTI is contiguous on disk, so the conversion can stream volume by volume into a v2 .h5 in the dataset's cache (#589). The result is then mapped the same way .h5 inputs are.

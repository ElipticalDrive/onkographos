Feasibility assessment for "Lumen-style GI, Unreal-quality lighting" in docs/plan/2026-10-global-illumination-feasibility.md. Docs only; no source changes, no changeset (contributor doc, CONSTITUTION.md §6.6).
Bottom line: close to Unreal's look for many scenes is achievable; Lumen parity on WebGPU in 2026 is not (no hardware ray tracing, no bindless, no mesh shaders; reference machine is an M1).
Biggest finding: there is no HDR scene buffer. Lite's PBR shader tone maps, gamma-encodes and clamps every fragment (pbr-template.js 171) into an 8-bit sceneColor (render-path.ts 111–116).
Lite's TAA resets whenever the camera moves and reads no velocity (taa.js 76, 105), so it cannot denoise GI in a game.
07-rendering.md and ADR-0025 say Lite has no compute; that is stale. Lite 1.31+ has compute tasks, 3D storage textures, a G-buffer pass with velocity, a Hi-Z pyramid, SSGI, contact shadows and local probes. ignifx uses none of them yet.
Options assessed: A screen-space GI/SSR/GTAO, B DDGI-style probes on a global SDF, C radiance cascades, D Lumen-faithful port, E baked lightmaps and probes, plus the "Unreal look" foundations (HDR, TAA, CSM, GTAO, bloom, AgX, exposure).
Recommended Phase 1, foundations: 20–28 agent-days (incl. 2 days of spikes). It also ships Phase 7's unshipped taa, screenSpaceContactShadows and ssgi.
Phase 2, baked GI (runtime plus an editor baker): 35–55 days. Phase 3, dynamic diffuse GI as a desktop tier: 40–60 days. A Lumen port would be 150–300+ days and is not recommended.
WebGPU status cited from gpuweb #535, #11053, #11055 (opened 2026-10-13), #3015, and the draft bindless proposal.
Six owner decisions in §6 (device tier, order, upstream Lite PRs, editor baker scope, static-world rule, a written GPU budget).
All ms figures are estimates scaled from XeGTAO, the Lumen performance guide and DDGI papers. Nothing was measured (§7).
Done: the document. Remaining: owner review. How to verify: read the doc; each engine claim cites a file and line. pnpm check is green locally: 7 passed (build, format, typecheck, api-report, docs-harness, test, lint).

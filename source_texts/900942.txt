…and honest coverage

The library described a 2D world. The consuming app now has a second
runtime -- a first-person voxel world -- built out of contracts that
were never declared, plus movement contracts the library already had.

Four new descriptors, for the things that only exist in three dimensions:

  world.voxel_volume              dense block storage, palette, solidity
  presentation.voxel_mesher       faces to geometry, coplanar merging
  presentation.first_person_camera eye-level view, pitch clamped short
                                  of the poles
  presentation.submersion_view    near-field fog and dimmed light while
                                  submerged

compound.first_person_voxel_world wires them to the movement contracts
the 2D platformer already uses -- which is half the point. jumpVelocity
and coyote time never cared how many axes there were, and the compound
makes that checkable rather than asserted. 11 wires, 4 of them derived
through the impact intent rather than named: breaking a block and
breaking the surface of water both reach audio and particles with no
wiring table.

Coverage was also wrong, and invisibly so. 39 of 71 descriptors named a
canvas2d implementation while the app registered all 71 -- nothing ever
compared the two lists. web-implementations.json now declares what the
app implements per target, tools/declare_implementations.py applies it,
and --check runs in CI so it cannot drift again. 43 entries corrected.

validate_component_graph now fails on an event that is listened for and
emitted by nothing. Found the hard way: first_person_camera was written
against "movement.moved", which no component has ever emitted, and every
validator passed. The input.* prefix stays exempt -- those come from the
player. Canary-tested: reintroducing that exact line fails the check.

README counts were stale before this branch (51 components, 10
compounds); now 75 and 10.

All five checks green: graph, compounds (10/51 wires), topology,
platforms, implementations.

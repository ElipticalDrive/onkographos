The reveal camera replaces the post-settle yaw: instead of rotating the landed die to present its face, the camera moves to a pose that reads the face upright. revealCamera() in physics-roll.js computes that pose; dice3d.js drives the camera to it during the replay tail, holds it there, and re-derives it on resize. Invariant 3 — nothing writes mesh.quaternion after the body sleeps — is the thing this whole branch protects, and it's asserted directly by e2e/roll.spec.js.

Recompute the reveal when resizing during replay
When the viewport changes after Roll is pressed but before the hold phase begins—for example, when a player rotates a phone during the animation—this guard skips recomputing the reveal, while the !rolling branch is also skipped. Because captureLanded() computed the pose using the old aspect and the tail caches that pose in revealPos/revealQuat, the completed result remains framed using the old landscape/portrait distance until another resize occurs. Refresh the active replay's reveal and any cached tail target when the aspect changes.

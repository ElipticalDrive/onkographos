Contributor
The 3D (isometric) surface rule view was very slow to pan and to update after an edit. This adds caching and hidden-block pruning to the preview worker.
Changes
Block pruning: each area of chunks is reduced once to its visible faces. Only exposed top, +x and +z faces are kept, in back-to-front order, and fill colours are cached per block.
Drawn-image cache: the worker draws the whole area once per zoom level and sends it as an ImageBitmap. A pan is then a single drawImage, with no getImageData readback and no per-pixel values array.
Terrain setup cache: the random state and settings are reused between frames instead of being rebuilt on every request.
Filled-chunk cache: chunks are cached by terrain shape (seed, noise settings, and the shaping files, ignoring surface_rule). Editing a surface rule now only reruns the surface pass, which takes about 12 ms per chunk, instead of the noise fill, which takes about 400 ms per chunk.
Progressive build: while chunks are being built, each one is shown as soon as it finishes.

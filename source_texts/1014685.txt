Ordinary real interval diffusion can use CG/Jacobi through Run, parameter JVP/VJP and saved evaluation reopening when the represented operator is proved SPD. The previous Q1 admission retained General even for this bounded pure-diffusion profile. This connects existing solvers to the shared prepared assembly; it adds no solver method or public certificate API.
The proof consumes positive finite quadrature coefficients, exact signed local Laplacian blocks, quotient-component anchoring, and a conservative integer-exponent energy/roundoff margin, then checks exact assembled CSR symmetry. Unsafe or unproved initial profiles retain General. A previously SPD-bound Plan rechecks changed parameters and reacceptance before provider execution. Reaction, storage, nonlinear/vector/tensor diffusion, higher dimensions and arbitrary conditioning are excluded from this proof.
Two independently useful shared corrections preserve available conjugate-transpose diagonals (including the complex conjugation) and admit the already implemented real Faer BiCGStab SPD Identity/Jacobi Fast tuples. An ordinary TPFA CG/Jacobi adjoint witness and actual normal/transpose backend solves cover these changes. Public Oriented operators now require ComplexFloat scalars; repository consumers are migrated together.
Focused validation passes: public Q1 11, native Q1 4, plural Regions 10, diagonal admission 4, multiplier closure 6, Oriented 2, Faer 23, TPFA primal/JVP/VJP/reopen, Clippy, formatting, and registered numerics.linear-backends. Independent falsifiers include positive quadrature values with zero represented local energy, a weak anchor lost by accumulation, and an initially valid SPD Plan rebound outside its proof. Mathematical/ownership risk review found no blockers. Full affected checks and relevant hosted contexts remain required; the matrix marks the bounded product-tested extension without a new registered-verification claim.
Integration order: #1710 is finishing its exact candidate gate after #1709 merged; #1711 is correcting its exposed transient MINI consumer restriction. This existing independent branch is stacked on #1711 and will not displace either predecessor. Implements a bounded #1707 increment; it does not close the full solver-substitution target.

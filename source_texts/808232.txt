We show that the k free bitangents of a collection of n pairwise disjoint convex plane sets can be computed in time O(k + n log n) and O(n) working space. The algorithm uses only one advanced data structure, namely a splittable queue. We introduce greedy pseudo--triangulations, whose combinatorial properties are crucial for our method.  1980 Mathematics Subject Classification: 68Q25,68U05,52A10,52C10,52B10,05C90.  Key Words and Phrases: Visibility graphs, visibility complexes, triangulations, pseudotriangles, pseudo-triangulations, topological sorting, sweep algorithms.

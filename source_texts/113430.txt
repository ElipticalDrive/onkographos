This PR integrates Helio's destructible voxel worlds into Pulsar: planets, finite planes and infinite planes of real 0.1 m to 1 m voxels. They are authored as SceneDB components, sculpted in the level editor, driven from scripts and blueprints, and rendered by the helio.voxel-terrain backend. It pins Helio at Far-Beyond-Pulsar/Helio#285, which carries the renderer (helio-pass-voxel-planet).
The system map is docs/voxel-system.md. It covers the layers, the components and edit journal, a frame's flow, the backend contract, the editor, scripting, camera and coordinates, decisions and pitfalls, diagnostics, tests and extending. The renderer side is in Helio's helio-pass-voxel-planet/README.md.
What it contains
Components.
VoxelTerrainComponent: shape, radius or plane size, voxel size, generator (a searchable picker of the registered generators, serialized as generator_id / generator_version), seed, editable, and the edits journal. The journal is shared and prefix-hashed, so copying it and "did it only grow" checks are O(1).
Generator settings live in their own component (VoxelLandformComponent, VoxelFlatTerrainComponent), which is attached automatically when a generator is chosen.
Presets: planet, plane and infinite_plane.
Game worlds compose these components with others in prefabs.
Backend contract (voxel_backend.rs). VoxelRenderBackend provides selection, pass factory, frame publishing, camera-relative rendering, sky ownership, ambient up, certified clip range, altitude, lift_out_of_ground, exact f64 edit_ray, TSR quality and diagnostics. PlanetVoxelBackend caches the world per entry and extends it in place when edits are only appended.
Viewport renderer (renderer.rs).
Camera-relative frames with an f64 eye; the world origin is passed to Helio passes.
A planet-aware camera: the frame's up follows the local vertical, so the horizon stays level anywhere on a planet. W/S move level, Q/E move along the vertical.
Speed = viewport speed x clamp(altitude / 20 m, 1, 1e6).
The camera can't enter solid voxels.
Near and far planes come from certified empty space.
Brush and pick rays share one camera basis.
Editor (ui_level_editor).
Voxel Sculpt tool: dig, build or paint; sphere or cube; radius; material; one block. Strokes are gap-filled while dragging.
One undo step per stroke (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y).
F frames the terrain from 30 m.
Inspector labels and the generator picker.
Scripting. World methods on VoxelTerrainComponent: get_block, set_block, fill_sphere, fill_cube, raycast_distance, voxel_size. Script edits go into the same journal as the sculpt tool.
Engine support. A label option for engine_class properties; PULSAR_VOXEL_STATS=1 streaming diagnostics in the engine log; profile scopes.
Helio pin
The pin includes Helio main and adds these fixes, found by replaying editor trips (details and numbers in Helio#285):
Freezes when leaving the ground (planning stalls up to 256 ms; now about 6 ms).
Poor performance near the ground where terrain is below datum (primary cost 5-20x).
A ring of missing terrain around the planet in orbit (sky-bound limb holes).
The pink or striped screen close to the ground (the editor grid was drawn through the eye in camera-relative frames).
Flickering level bands and TSR history loss along the sky while moving.
Rings in the shading while ascending.
In the release editor, a climb from the ground to 18.7 km now logs no frame spikes, and residency CPU stays at 5 ms or less per frame.
Tests
cargo test -p engine_backend --lib voxel: the backend, including altitude, lift out of ground, stroke fill, settings components, unknown generators, paint and single blocks.
--lib camera_frame_tests: level horizon, direction round trips, smooth transport over the planet.
--test voxel_block_api (7) and --test voxel_component_schema (5).
ui_level_editor sculpt stroke tests (a stroke is one undo step).
Helio's voxel pass suites run at the pinned commit.
main is merged in. The Helio pin contains main's Helio commit, and the full editor (pulsar_engine) builds.
Known limits
The sky away from the pole and from orbit is wrong: Helio's fallback sky assumes world +Y up and a fixed sun. A planetary atmosphere is next.
Gizmos and authored meshes in planet scenes are not rebased to the camera origin; meshes would need f64 or tiled transforms.
The Flamegraph profiler stops saving at about 1M events, and the in-viewport performance panels need work.
Visual direction: distant grass variation and speck density need an art pass.
Generic live chunk payloads are not consumed by this backend; it reports an error instead.

Render voxel terrain with the Helio voxel planet backend
…
f95ee2a
```
Replace the tiny-voxel backend with helio-pass-voxel-planet (GPU-driven
clipmap of exact voxels, 0.1-1 m): PlanetVoxelBackend reads a JSON
PlanetSourceRecipe (planet landform + ordered brush edits) from the
terrain's generator parameters, caches the built planet per source
revision and applies appended sculpt brushes incrementally. Voxel Sculpt
edits cast exact rays clipped to the planet shell (orbital edits work)
and build with cobblestone. Traced sunlight follows the scene's
directional light; outdoor voxel scenes get hemisphere ambient around the
local vertical.

Also adapts main's spline preview and hlfs camera test to the f64 editor
camera, and the voxel schema test to the built-in flat generator default.
```

Voxel terrain: generator ref, brush requests, stroke fill, camera aids
…
23c7ff0
```
- The terrain's generator is a VoxelGeneratorRef (still saved flat as
  generator_id/generator_version); the projection, recipe and brush
  mapping come from helio_component::voxel_world, shared with scripts.
- Sculpt edits carry a VoxelBrushRequest (remove/add/paint, sphere/cube,
  radius, material, single block). Edits validate with Brush::resolve
  instead of copying the world per sample.
- Consecutive stroke samples are joined by stamps (stroke_fill), so fast
  drags carve continuous trenches or lines of blocks.
- Backends report ground clearance and can lift an eye out of solid
  terrain: the editor camera speed scales with height above the ground
  (orbit in seconds, walking pace near it) and never enters the ground;
  dug caves stay enterable.
- Profiling scopes for projection, publish, world updates, brushes and
  clearance. The voxel pass builds optimized in dev profiles.
- Tests: block API and picker (voxel_block_api), inspector properties,
  stroke fill, clearance, lifting. Docs: scripting API and editor tools.
- Helio submodule: fine noise (no landform terracing), scripting API,
  generator picker.
```

Planet-aware editor camera; altitude speed; pin camera-relative overlays
…
6e6a319
```
Editor camera over voxel worlds:
- The camera keeps a reference frame whose up follows the local vertical
  (carried by the smallest rotation as it moves), and yaw/pitch are
  relative to it: the horizon stays level anywhere on a planet (it tilted
  30 degrees at 30 degrees from the pole), W/S move level and Q/E along the
  vertical. View, movement, pan, picking, gizmos and brush rays share one
  basis. Saved and focus poses keep world-space yaw/pitch.
- Speed follows the altitude above the ground below (new backend query,
  Planet::ground_height) instead of the conservative clearance, which was
  ~0 anywhere below the highest possible terrain: the camera crawled at
  10 m/s five kilometres above lowland.

Helio e56ede21 (from c7806c0's pin):
- The editor grid put its world y = 0 plane through the eye in
  camera-relative frames; pressed against the ground the view turned the
  x axis' red (the pink screen). Frames now carry their world origin to
  passes; the grid, billboards (light icons) and far-plane rays in clouds,
  TSR, shadow cascades, lens flare and fog are correct at any near/far.
- Stable per-column LOD transitions (flickering bands while moving).
- Destruction gate: 8-cell edit tiles.
```

Cloud rendering has advanced from simple precomputed shading to sophisticated volumetric models that simulate light scattering and fluid dynamics. This paper reviews three key approaches: GPU-accelerated fluid simulations for interactive applications, artist-driven texture splatting for efficient game visuals, and multi-field weather visualizations for scientific accuracy. By comparing their strengths and limitations, this review highlights how these methods balance realism and performance, offering insights into their applications and future advancements.

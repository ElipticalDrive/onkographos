Goal
Make continuous, differentiable neural fields a first-class representation in GeoStack: fitted directly on gridless observations (tracks, swaths, pixels), uncertainty-aware, usable as a 4D-Var control, and scored against the classical oracles the case studies already name.
Wave / Milestone
Wave: wave:6
Milestone: none yet (each library releases on its own cadence)
Motivation
The 2025–2026 literature (report 18) says neural fields win at gridless reconstruction (altimetry, stations, CryoSat-2 point clouds) and as differentiable state parameterizations for variational assimilation (spectral bias as an implicit background covariance), and still lose to SZ3/ZFP at archival compression. A repo survey (23 Oct 2026) found the stack already has most parts:
geonnax: SIREN, MultiScaleSIREN, FourierNet/GaborNet, FiLM / HyperLinear / ConditionedINR / HyperSIREN, SphericalHarmonicEncoder, SlepianEncoder, needlets, GeoFNO point queries;
pyrox-nn: a BayesNF port (BayesianNeuralField + BNFEstimator), BayesianSIREN, BayesianFourierNet, EnsembleMAP / EnsembleVI;
vardax: strong/weak/incremental 4D-Var, AveragingKernel, MultiInstrumentFusion.
What is missing is the plumbing between them: no point-observation path in vardax, no coordinate-derivative operators, a BNF likelihood with fixed σ = 0.1 and no observation operator, and no way to make a network the 4D-Var control. The design page has the full argument.

Summary
nerf (arXiv 2003.08934), model 43: a continuous-field MLP (positional encoding L=6, 5×128) trained purely through differentiable volume rendering of a synthetic voxel scene (seeded MNIST digit extruded to 28×28×8, colored by depth so RGB is informative).
Design-review fixes baked in: GT rendered exactly (fixed 256 samples, never tier-capped), timesteps: 200 so the tier min gives S 25 / M 200 samples-per-ray, rays_per_batch=1024 as its own config field, 30-epoch M override, interleaved azimuth split (interpolation not extrapolation; no 0/360 duplicate), fixed 35° elevation, bin-midpoint eval sampling (deterministic PSNR).
Gate: mean PSNR on held-out azimuths {M: 20, L: 26} PROVISIONAL (untrained field measured ~7 dB at S); psnr_min reported alongside. Showcase: turntable.png (8 novel views) + gt_vs_pred.png. Docs ch. 13 section.

S dataset build measured 1.3 s on CPU (CI-safe, vectorized GT)

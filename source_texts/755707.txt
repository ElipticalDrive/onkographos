Repeated parity queries dominated the imported 25 µm finishing workflow. This change reuses vertical intersection lists across block columns, retaining surface-exclusion checks and falling back to the existing three-direction classifier for ambiguous or poorly conditioned cases. Event storage is bounded; overflow triggers fallback rather than truncation. Pool ordering is restored before publishing state so existing histories remain unchanged.

Import is 5.08× faster and total runtime is 46.1% lower. Initial stock/target buffers match byte-for-byte. Final states match previous results at 250, 100, 50 and 25 µm. Full-resolution checkpoint/resume, compacted resume and native replay pass; all 5,199 history deltas are byte-identical.
Validation and limits
49 tests pass locally in both Debug and ReleaseFast, including capacity exhaustion, boundary cases, canonical pool ordering and allocation-failure cleanup.
Existing ten mesh fixtures and thirteen additional transformed meshes match scalar occupancy and independent geometry checks. The varied suite passes.
Three additional rotated fixtures are rejected by both old and new STL parsing. These are explicitly documented restrictions, not successful voxelizations; validation has not been weakened.
Existing Lean proofs, audits, bridge checks and deliberate-breakage guards pass. Numerical scanline guards are engineering safeguards, not certified interval bounds or a proof of the executable.
Adds the transformed-mesh suite to CI. Remote CI results are separate from the local validation above.
Raw paired reports, source/binary identities, resume/replay evidence and reproduction commands are included. See docs/SCANLINE-IMPORT.md and benchmarks/scanline-import/. The one-command full workflow remains python3 scripts/finishing-workflow.py.

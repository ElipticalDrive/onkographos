To deal with complex computation in photorealistic drawing by ray tracing,a dynamic grid partition method based on the space distribution of primitives is proposed to remove the redundant computation due to unnecessary grid partition.Firstly,the model space is partitioned averagely and the primitive number is calculated in each grid.Continue to create partitions until the primitive number in grid reaches a saturation state.If it has reached saturation state,stop the grid partition.And then at last we get a grid tree.In the process of ray-primitive,it continues to traverse the grid tree until detects the intersection of ray and a primitive from the nearest viewpoint.Through the comparison experiment on different models,the performance of this method is about 60% higher than that of traditional average grid partition.

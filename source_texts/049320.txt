This pull request transitions the volumetric cloud rendering system from a 2D weather/SDF-based model to a direct 3D weather volume model.
It bakes analytical 3D density and 3D SDF using tileable 3D Worley cells into the 3D volume texture, and updates the raymarching pipelines to sample them directly with horizontal tiling (uCloudVolumeTiles = 4.0) and full advection support. This achieves highly efficient space skipping and rendering.

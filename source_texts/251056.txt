Filed from the 2026-10-04 audit double-check (record N-30), audited at ae5554978ed86a24e2a7331a9a188ce381a01482 (master). Adversarially judged: not refuted (0.88). Plan item PF-3b. Related: #20 (scope mentions duplicated count/emit work).
What is wrong
tile_binning.glsl has one main() compiled twice (GS_TILE_GLOBAL_SORT_COUNT_PASS / GS_TILE_GLOBAL_SORT_EMIT_PASS). Both variants execute everything from line 703 to the first pass-specific #ifdef at 991 and the shared block 1008-1181 per visible splat: splat, instance and chunk-meta loads, wind deformation, project_gaussian_2d, compute_eigen, two possible conic rebuilds, opacity-aware sigma, subpixel history read, tile-rect computation and the tightened ellipse_sigma2. COUNT then does one atomicAdd(tile_counts) per covered tile; EMIT recomputes all of it, adds SH and lighting, and does a second per-tile atomicAdd cursor per record with an undo atomic on overflow. The only per-splat state COUNT hands to EMIT is a 1-bit subpixel_visibility word. run() executes COUNT -> prefix -> EMIT every frame, and a descriptor-invalidating overlap-buffer resize re-runs COUNT a third time.
Evidence
modules/gaussian_splatting/shaders/tile_binning.glsl:703 (main), :819 (project_gaussian_2d), :991 (first #ifdef), :994 (visibility word), :1201 (COUNT atomic), :1444 (EMIT atomic), :1447 (undo)
modules/gaussian_splatting/renderer/tile_renderer.cpp:506, :897-1065, :1010
Record sizes by static_assert: PackedGaussian 128 B, PackedGaussianQuantized 80 B, InstanceDataGPU 112 B, ChunkMetaGPU 64 B
Consequence
Close-up views with about 1M surviving splats read the 80/128-byte splat, 112-byte instance and 64-byte chunk-meta rows twice (about 0.5 GB per frame of redundant traffic) and hot tiles issue tens of thousands of serialised atomics. State-of-the-art pipelines project once and write the payload plus a per-splat tile count.
Direction (not a patch recipe)
Make COUNT write the finished ProjectedGaussian payload (it already has everything except SH/lighting colour) plus the clamped tile rect and ellipse_sigma2 into the projection buffer or a small per-splat sidecar; EMIT consumes them and adds colour; replace the per-record atomic cursor with a per-splat prefix. Acceptance: binning GPU time on the dense lane down by at least 30 percent with a bit-identical image.

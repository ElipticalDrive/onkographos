What
Replaces the 2D aurora background with a true raymarched volumetric nebula (the "Compiled Field" effect) as the site-wide background — the 3D "wizard" upgrade.
Ray-marched domain-warped FBM density, cosine palette (indigo→blue→cyan→gold), fresnel iridescence, parallax particle depth, chromatic aberration, ACES tonemap + grain, and cursor gravitational lensing (the field bends toward the pointer).
Performance (device-tiered)
Desktop: 40 march steps @ 0.66 internal resolution.
Low-core machines: 26 steps.
Phones: 14 steps @ 0.5 internal resolution.
STEPS is baked into the compiled program per device; reduced-motion renders a single static frame; offscreen + tab-hidden pause the loop.
Verified
tsc -b + vite build pass.
Shader compiles clean at runtime on desktop and mobile viewports (no THREE.WebGLProgram errors).
No horizontal overflow on 390×844.
Note: exact look/FPS can only be judged on a real GPU (headless has none) — it mirrors the approved standalone artifact. Fully reversible; quality tiers are one-line tunable.

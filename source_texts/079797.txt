Glenn's engine list, rendering items not yet done. Already done (no work needed): depth of field (shaders/vulkan/dof.frag), bloom, SSAO, SSR, ACES/AgX tonemap with contrast/saturation grade and vignette (ADR-0037), volumetric clouds and weather, atmosphere and fog, impostors for far trees (ADR-0193), city tiers that crossfade (ADR-0226), occlusion culling (ADR-0100), streaming cells, particles (ADR-0239), enterable interiors (ADR-0080).
To do
Lens flare (sun and street lamps): screen-space ghosts from the bloom pass, plus a sun occlusion test.
Grading presets: sepia, black and white, film looks, and 3D LUTs (a .cube file loaded per look), selectable live. One grade stage, the existing composite.
Volumetric fog and light shafts: a froxel (view-frustum voxel) fog lit by the sun's shadow cascades, so shafts through trees and between towers; ground fog in valleys at dawn.
Far-distance haze check: confirm aerial perspective reads right across the island (mountains bluer and paler with distance); calibrate against photographs (ADR-0102).
Render to texture: a second camera drawn into a texture. The base for mirrors, portals, security monitors, a rear-view mirror, a picture-in-picture camera for the cinematics tools.
Path tracer denoising: today a bilateral filter (src/main.cpp). Intel Open Image Denoise (open source, Apache 2.0) is the standard ML denoiser; it takes albedo and normal buffers too. Credit it.

A halcyon-only change that implements KHR_CAMERA_DEPTH_OF_FIELD (0376f13).
Thin lens. Both camera subtypes take the spec's apertureRadius (default 0, a pinhole) and focusDistance (default 1). Camera_makeRay() takes a lens sample in [0, 1)², maps it onto the aperture disk (concentric map) in the image plane's axes, and aims the ray where the pinhole ray meets the plane focusDistance along the view direction. Without an aperture the ray is the same as before.
quality only. Each path draws its lens point from SAMPLE_DOMAIN_CAMERA_LENS (Typhoon's key, unused until now), and its ray differentials leave the same lens point. The preview renderer traces one fixed ray per pixel without accumulating, so a lens sample would shift its image rather than blur it. It keeps the pinhole ray, as do the quality's debug modes and its depth and id channels, and only the quality claims the extension. A negative apertureRadius, or a non-positive focusDistance with an aperture, warns and renders through a pinhole.
The new depth_of_field test renders a square whose edges lie on pixel boundaries, through both camera subtypes. Through a pinhole or a lens focused on the square, no pixel is partly covered. Focused in front of it, the edges blur over several pixels (441 partly covered pixels) and the total coverage stays the square's area. The preview renderer stays sharp. renderer_extensions now expects the extension on the quality only. halcyon's full ctest suite passes.

```
Implement KHR_CAMERA_DEPTH_OF_FIELD for both camera subtypes: a thin lens
of 'apertureRadius' (default 0, a pinhole) focused on the plane
'focusDistance' (default 1) along the view direction. Camera_makeRay()
takes a lens sample in [0, 1)^2, maps it onto the aperture disk with the
concentric map in the image plane's axes, and aims the ray where the
pinhole ray meets the focus plane. Without an aperture the ray is the same
as before.

Only the quality renderer samples the lens: each path draws it from
SAMPLE_DOMAIN_CAMERA_LENS (Typhoon's key, unused until now), and its ray
differentials leave the same lens point. The preview renderer traces one
fixed ray per pixel without accumulating, so a lens sample would shift its
image rather than blur it; it, the quality's debug modes and its depth and
id channels keep the pinhole ray, and only the quality claims the
extension. A negative 'apertureRadius', or a non-positive 'focusDistance'
with an aperture, warns and renders through a pinhole.

The new depth_of_field test renders a square whose edges lie on pixel
boundaries: through a pinhole or a lens focused on it no pixel is partly
covered, focused in front of it the edges blur over several pixels, and
its total coverage stays its area; the preview renderer stays sharp.
```

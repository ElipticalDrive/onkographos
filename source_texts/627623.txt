This PR implements a new volumetric refraction effect for the SDF system. Volumetric sources can now define a refraction_strength which causes rays to bend as they pass through the volume, based on the local density gradient. This creates realistic heat-haze or magnifying glass effects within the volumetric SDFs. The implementation handles both internal refraction during the march and distorted background reprojection.

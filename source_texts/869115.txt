Closes #298
New gfx/RotationDome.h (glm-only): angle <-> dome direction, fixed-view projection, drag-to-angle-delta (dot tracks the finger).
ui::DrawRotationDome: ImGui window per pane, bottom-right; wireframe lat/long, camera dot, "N" marker. Being an ImGui window, drags never reach map pan/tap. Dot is drawn from camera yaw/pitch every frame, so it stays in sync with any other camera change. Pitch clamps via OrbitCamera::Rotate.
Debug panel checkbox "Rotation dome", on by default. When off, web two-finger touch rotation is re-enabled; when on it's disabled (one-finger pan and pinch untouched). Desktop mouse/trackpad controls unchanged.
OrbitCamera::Yaw()/Pitch() getters; new TestDomeAngleMapping in camera_tests.
Verified: camera_tests compiled with g++ + glm 1.0.1 and passes. Not built/run: full app (SDL/ImGui/emscripten unavailable here), so the HUD drawing and the web touch change are untested visually.

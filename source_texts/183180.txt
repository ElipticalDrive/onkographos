I decided I wanted to recreate Pixar's old Point Based Global Illumination system, and to do that, I first created point clouds for both my Cornell Box and my Utah Teapot.
I'm using Pixar's documents to speed things along, but what's stumping me is the part where I have to organize everything into an octree. For those unaware, an octree's a structure where each node (or point, in this case) has 8 children, hence the name. I want to find a way to automate the process, as I don't feel like manually assigning 8 points to a parent point.
Does anyone know how I can do this, or at the very least, know any YouTubers who can push me in the right direction? If you do, then I'll greatly appreciate your help.

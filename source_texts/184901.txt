A pinhole camera uses a small "pinhole" to define the rays of light from a bright source, thus creating an "image" on a screen on the opposite side of the pinhole.   This demonstration uses an iris as a pinhole and a light bulb filament as the object, as shown in Figure 1, to investigate the effect on the image of changing the size of the pinhole and changing the position of the object. An animation illustrates how the light rays create the "image.

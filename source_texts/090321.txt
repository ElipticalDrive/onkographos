Part of #25. Needs #33.
What
Turns a 3D density field into a printable solid:
Isosurface at density 0.5 (marching cubes, or a dual method for fewer slivers), giving a closed mesh.
Smooth it (Taubin smoothing, which keeps the volume), and reduce the triangle count.
Booleans with the kept parts through the geometry ops interface, using the Manifold engine (A geometry ops interface, with an optional Manifold engine #18) by default here, because JSCAD's booleans struggle with meshes this size.
Check with the print check and cad check like any other part.
Done when
The bracket bench's optimised specimen is a closed, manifold solid with exact bolt holes, and passes the print check.

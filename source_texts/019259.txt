Volume clipping is a human-computer interaction in volume rendering, which is mainly used to display the interested parts in the volume data and hide other ones. However, in the ellipsoidal ray casting algorithm (ERCA) for digital earth, there is still a gap in constructing the selection object in volume clipping conforming to the geodetic or ellipsoidal coordinate system. This paper provides an ellipsoidal BLH-slice volume clipping algorithm (EBLHVC), which gives the simplest mathematical representation of three typical selection object surfaces in the geodetic or ellipsoidal coordinate system, and presents a direct ray sampling method based on the intersection calculation using algebraic equation to realize volume rendering of the selection object surface. The article provides a concise slice display for volume rendering on digital earth.

We have found that U-Nets with the attention mechanism do a sufficient job at contextually understanding the
physical system well enough to generate new control sequences.
The model learned the dynamics of the PDE without being specifically guided at inference.
We can conclude that this method is powerful enough for even non-smooth inverse problems, such as this one
where letters have strictly defined borders.
This approach may be desirable over traditional gradient-based optimizations (such as adjoint methods or
model predictive control) because gradients often get stuck in local minima.

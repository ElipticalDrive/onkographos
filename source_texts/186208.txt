Cleaning Meshes with a Voxel Grid
If you are working with an imported mesh and you are having problems when converting it to an implicit body, this workflow can help:
Voxel Grid from Mesh will fill the volume of your imported mesh with voxels, or “3D pixels,” and converting back to a mesh will generate a clean mesh which can then be converted to an implicit body.

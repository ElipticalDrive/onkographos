Summary
Adds per-pixel pseudo-Huber (Charbonnier) as a selectable photometric loss alongside MSE; sets PSEUDO_HUBER as the default.
New LossType enum and constexpr LossType loss_type at the top of src/main.cpp, next to the other training constants
compute_rgb_loss helper picks between:
MSE — torch::mse_loss(rgb, target)
PSEUDO_HUBER (default) — per-pixel Charbonnier mean_i [√(c² + e_i²) − c], with c = 5.4e-4 · √3 (5.4e-4f * 1.7320508f so it stays constexpr without C++26 std::sqrt)
README and docs/index.html pipeline step 6 updated to a single line: "Photometric MSE or pseudo-Huber on the rendered image (selectable via LossType in src/main.cpp) plus a regulariser of the form …"
Notes
Per-pixel form (vs image-wise √(c² + MSE) − c) is what gives the gradient-damping on outlier pixels that's the actual point of a Huber-family loss.
At this c, the loss is effectively smoothed L1 across the whole training range — matches mip-NeRF's operating regime.
Test plan
cmake .. && make succeeds
Train a scene; loss values are reasonable (different scale to MSE — expect smaller numbers since it's L1-like)
Flip loss_type to LossType::MSE, recompile, and confirm MSE training still runs identically to before

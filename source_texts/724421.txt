<h2 align="center">cube.render</h2>

A minimal canvas demonstration of 3D cube rotation using  
<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodrigues%27_rotation_formula">Rodrigues’ rotation formula</a>.

a simple _cube_ rendered in a browser canvas with basic rotation logic.

To experiment or modify the demo:

- Edit `script.js` for rotation logic.
- Style the canvas or interface in `style.css`.

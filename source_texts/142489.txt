My engine's voxel mesh optimization. : r/VoxelGameDev - Reddit
The mesh is only marked for rebuild when the chunk is viewed in the appropriate way (it only generates the cardinal direction meshes as required based on the camera). Another part of the code, which I plan to thread, actually generates the mesh based on a priority queue so mesh nearest the camera is created first. Fortunately, it is quite quick.

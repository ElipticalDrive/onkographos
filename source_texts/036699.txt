Goal
Compute indirect light from the scene itself rather than from a single distant environment. Once IBL (#2) lands, the
only indirect term is a distant HDRI: nothing occludes it, light does not bounce between surfaces, and every reflective
surface mirrors the sky. On Bistro that is the largest visible difference from the reference render — the reference is
defined by its contact darkening, and the dataset's occlusion channel is empty, so there is no baked AO to bind.
TurianZ shipped this sequence on the same scene; the ordering below follows its cost/benefit experience.
Tasks
Depth + normal prepass — prerequisite for the screen-space passes; must share the main pass's culled draw list
so it does not multiply draw calls
SSAO — hemisphere sampling with a depth-aware blur, modulating the ambient term; half resolution option
Screen-space reflections — ray march against the prepass, resolve against last frame's colour with temporal
reprojection, fall back to the environment on miss, gated by roughness
Reflection probes — ReflectionProbeComponent with box projection and blend distance, baked cubemaps
Irradiance volumes — IrradianceVolumeComponent (box, spacing, blend, priority); baked per-probe order-2 SH
plus distance moments for a visibility test; bake as a background task with progress and cancel
Volume resolution shared with post-processing volumes (Engine singletons: consider DI or DataAsset alternatives #44)
Acceptance Criteria
Bistro shows contact occlusion under awnings and inside the shop, colour bleeding from bright surfaces, and
storefront glass reflecting the street rather than the sky.
Each technique can be toggled per project quality level and measured in the stats HUD (Bind glTF external images to their own asset ids, as FBX now does #10).

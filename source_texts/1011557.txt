Summary
The virtual shadow map (#253) filters its shadows with the same comparison taps as the cascades, so its penumbrae have the same width at any distance from the caster. Shadow map ray tracing (SMRT) marches a few rays from each shaded point toward points on the light's disk, through the virtual map's depth, and finds what blocks them. Penumbrae then grow with the distance from the caster as a real sun's do, with no blocker search and its artifacts. This is the shadow-map path's soft shadow. #177 is the traced path's.
User stories
As a technical artist, I want sun shadows that are sharp where they touch and soft far from their caster, so that outdoor lighting looks physically plausible.
Requirements
The light's size. A directional light has an angular diameter (about 0.53° for the sun). Add it to the directional light. Zero keeps hard shadows.
Rays. For each pixel, a few rays toward points on the light's disk, each marched a few steps through the virtual map's depth, with the counts from the quality's level. A ray reads the level that matches its distance from the pixel, through the page table. A step on a page that is not allocated reads the next coarser level.
Occlusion. A ray is blocked when a step is behind the map's depth by more than a thickness that grows with the step's distance. Extrapolate the map's depth behind a blocker so that thin casters do not leak light.
Page marking. The marking also requests the pages that the rays may read, within the penumbra's largest width.
Noise. Blue noise that changes each frame picks the rays' points, and temporal AA resolves it. If that is not stable enough, use Soft traced shadows with a denoiser #177's denoiser on the result, if it exists.
Quality. Low and Medium keep the comparison taps. The docs give SMRT's cost at each level.
Acceptance criteria
A tall caster's shadow is sharp at its base and wider far from it.
A light with an angular diameter of zero gives the same shadows as without SMRT.
The shadows are stable in motion with temporal AA.
Thin casters such as poles and fences do not leak light.
The cost at each quality level is measured and in the docs.
The rendering docs describe the built feature, in the present tense.
When to do this
After the virtual shadow map, when hard sun shadows look wrong in a game's scenes.

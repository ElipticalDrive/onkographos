Frequently asked questions
How does voxelization work?
The tool fills the inside of your mesh on a 3D grid (ray-based solid voxelization) at the resolution you pick, sampling surface and texture colors, all locally in your browser.
Can I just view or convert a model without voxelizing it?
Yes. Switch the view to Original model to orbit and inspect it, then export the original as GLB, OBJ, STL or PLY — for example OBJ to GLB or STL to OBJ.
Does conversion keep materials and colors?
Geometry always converts; materials and vertex colors carry over where the target format supports them (GLB and PLY). STL is geometry-only by design.
What can I do with the voxel result?
Export it as a vertex-colored GLB and open it in the Voxel Editor or any 3D tool to keep working on it. Nothing is uploaded at any point.

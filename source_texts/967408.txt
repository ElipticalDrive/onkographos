POSTCITY, First Floor, Workshop Room
Sun 8. Sep 2024 13:00 – 15:00
Volumetric Visions
The workshop will introduce participants to 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS), a novel machine learning approach to volumetric data representation and rendering, and explore its application in Extended Reality (XR).
The workshop will cover 3DGS technology and its artistic potential, followed by hands-on practice. Participants will learn how to capture objects and scenes, render video and explore VR integration.

Bio
Photo: Artem Konevskikh
Artem Konevskikh
RU
Artem Konevskikh is a Vienna-based machine learning engineer working with artists and creators. His diverse background, ranging from data analysis at CERN to developing ML solutions for urban planning, provides a strong foundation for projects at the intersection of technology and art. Konevskikh explores how artificial intelligence and emerging technologies impact creative processes, focusing on the application of neural networks and volumetric technologies in digital art.

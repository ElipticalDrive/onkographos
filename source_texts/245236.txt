Round 1 of the abrash gap loop: SDF penumbra soft shadows + ambient occlusion, wired into the CPU raymarcher.
What changed
render_sdf (crates/abrash-render/src/experimental/sdf.rs) previously lit
everything with flat ambient + unshadowed Lambert diffuse. It now:
attenuates diffuse by soft_shadow_estimate — the Wright/IQ penumbra march:
while marching toward the light it tracks res = min(res, k·h/t), one march
per light, k = 16;
modulates ambient by ambient_occlusion_estimate — IQ's 5-tap AO along the
normal (occ += (h−d)·sca, sca *= 0.95).
Both estimators already lived in abrash_core::sdf; this PR is the wiring that
makes the raymarcher actually use them. No new public API, no duplicated code.
Demo
New example sdf_soft_shadow_demo (--features nova): ground plane + sphere +
box, renders to sdf_soft_shadow_demo.ppm. A/B against the old lighting shows
the sphere's soft shadow blob on the ground and AO darkening where the box
meets the plane.
Verification
New tests in abrash-core/src/sdf.rs: penumbra widens with smaller k
(k=8 vs k=32 on a grazing ray), AO drops in an inside corner, AO is
1.0 on an open floor.
cargo test -p abrash-core -p abrash-render: 1537 + 197 lib tests green.
cargo fmt --check: clean. cargo clippy: zero new warnings (12/25
pre-existing on the two crates, unchanged).

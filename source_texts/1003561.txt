Fixes #999 for standard volume H1 Lagrange fields.
Higher-order traces lack complete facet-node orientation maps; cubic 2D traces instead use a quadrilateral vertex map and can silently produce wrong fields. Some 3D facets also list interior edge nodes in an order inconsistent with the first-facet basis used by surface mappings.
Align extended facet connectivity with that basis, then construct complete trace permutations using reference corner weights and the existing geometric orientations. Share the corner-weight construction between both paths. Add regressions for physical node correspondence and affine interpolation at physical facet quadrature points.
Repository validation on Linux/Python 3.12 against an ordinary native installation:
All 301 repository tests pass, including 80 new cases. The original 64 cover node correspondence and interpolation; 28 fail on the original master. Another 16 normal-dependent cases cover P2/P3 on all four cell types and both scalar/vector pairings, assembling both sides of a tilted interface (32 loads). They compare complete vectors with analytic traction loads and check outward normals; 12 fail on the original master.
The CI Flake8 hard gate and full new-test style check pass.
Supplemental external workload checks, separate from those repository tests:
Across 80 manufactured split-domain solves on triangles, quads, tets and hexes, 48 crashes and eight incorrect cubic solutions are corrected. All 24 complete working controls remain identical. Another 40 nonconstant Robin-jump and 48 mixed-order solves match independent harmonic fields; maximum field error is 3.5e-13.
The original issue's quadratic model solves 30,970 DOFs, and the shipped quadratic acoustic model solves 39,054 DOFs, with residuals below 8.1e-13. First-order controls retain their fields; matching Python hash seeds also preserve their raw operators and states exactly.
In 320 native normal-dependent load comparisons, dw_dot traces match separate analytic traction loads. These cover both scalar/vector pairings, both interface sides, P1-P5 on the four cell types, vertex/cell relabelings and tilted planes. Outward normals and total component integrals agree with independent plane geometry and polynomial integrals.

Summary
3DGS PLY (INRIA gaussian-splatting, nerfstudio splatfacto): detected in the existing PLY reader by f_dc_0..2 + opacity + scale_0..2. Each Gaussian becomes a point at its center with
RGB from the SH DC term, 0.5 + SH_C0 * f_dc clamped to 0..1,
opacity = sigmoid(logit) and size = exp(max(scale_i)) (the standard deviation along the longest axis, in cloud units) as float attributes.
The zero nx ny nz of such exports are not loaded as normals; rotations and higher-order SH are ignored. Works in the streaming, whole-file binary and ASCII paths.
.splat (antimatter15, 32 bytes/record): center, color, opacity = alpha/255, size = max linear scale.
Splat cleanup filter in the Filters panel (only offered when the chosen cloud has opacity): keeps opacity ≥ min and size ≤ max (default max size: 1/50 of the extent).
Color by Opacity (grey ramp) in the cloud list.
Saved splat clouds keep opacity/size and read back as such.
PlayCanvas compressed PLY is not supported (chunked quantized layout; out of scope).
Testing
cargo fmt --check, cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings, cargo test (119 + 5 pass): new tests for a hand-made binary/streamed/ASCII splat PLY (centers, colors, opacity, size), save/re-read, .splat, and the cleanup filter.
E2E: new test builds a 100-Gaussian splat PLY, checks the status line, colors by opacity, runs the cleanup (keeps 75 of 100), runs C2C against a LiDAR-style PLY, and loads a .splat. Full suite: 30/30 pass.

# PointCloudRendering

An OpenGL renderer that turns raw, unstructured point clouds into continuous, shaded surfaces using **splatting** — the EWA / surface-splatting family of techniques — combined with a deferred shading pipeline.

![screenshot](doc/screenshot.png)

The application loads `.ply` files, renders them in real time with a directional light, and exposes the interesting knobs (splat size, lighting, level of detail, G-buffer visualization) through a Dear ImGui panel.

TriMesh::contains_point (via TriBvh's parity ray) misclassified points near meshes made of long, thin triangles.
Cause: the ray–triangle test treated |det| < 1e-7 as parallel. The determinant scales with triangle area, so a sliver (under about 0.05 mm²) never counted as crossed, and each dropped crossing flipped the parity.
Parallel test: both the wide BVH kernel and triangle::ray_intersect now test the angle between the ray and the triangle's plane (PARALLEL_COS = 1e-6).
Near-edge crossings: when the parity ray passes within 1e-3 (barycentric) of an edge, it is retried along two more fixed directions, with a majority vote if every direction grazes an edge.
New test: tests/sliver_containment.rs builds a watertight box out of needle fans. 19,928 of 20,000 interior points read as outside before the fix; none do after.
Found through a sim run in which a bowed bar, subdivided along its length, produced a false 43.8 mm penetration 44 mm from any steel. Across 20M probes, the warped bar had 973 false insides deeper than 5 mm before the fix, and the sim run that failed on it now passes. All 71 tests pass, clippy is clean and query speed is unchanged.

parity ray that passes within 1e-3 (barycentric) of an edge, where rounding

directions, with a majority vote when every direction grazes one.

I have improved the integration of cloud shadowing into the volumetric lighting system.
The primary issue was that the volumetric injection shader (volumetric_injection.comp) was only considering Mie scattering (haze/fog) and neglecting Rayleigh scattering (air molecules) in its single-scattering calculation. This caused "God rays" to be nearly invisible unless the atmospheric haze was extremely high.
I updated the shader to:
Include Rayleigh Scattering: The scattering calculation now uses both Rayleigh and Mie phase functions, ensuring light shafts are visible even in clear air.
Proper Shadow Modulation: The combined scattering term is now correctly modulated by both terrain and cloud shadows.
Better Contrast: By separating the shadowed single-scattering from the unshadowed multi-scattering term, I've ensured that light shafts have better definition and visibility.
Consistency: Updated local lights and lightning effects to use the same physically consistent scattering model.
Verified with make glcheck and weather system integration tests.

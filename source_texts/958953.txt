Summary
Integrates the mfractal package as the new core of fractal_visuals — procedural (multi)fractal image generation + measurement across three natural domains (rocks, clouds, fluids), plus an empirical validation harness and a c2-balanced pareidolia stimulus dataset.
Earlier notebooks (fractality.ipynb, multifractality.ipynb), the FD-binned image archive, and the Delaunay-morphing scripts are preserved under legacy/.

mfractal/ — 30 family generators (15 rocks, 8 clouds, 7 fluids), two flagships (multifractal_cloud MRW, multifractional), estimators (2D wavelet leaders + 2D MFDFA).

benchmarks/make_figures.py — 8 figures: per-domain visual catalogs, c2-vs-complexity (all + curated), cross-family descriptor scatter, complexity-knob rank chart, curated panel.

datasets/build_pareidolia.py — candidate pool → c2 binning → balanced selection.
datasets/pareidolia/ — 106-stimulus set at n=512 (~14 MB), manifest CSV with c1/c2/cx/seed per image + montage.

Twelve-element feature_vector() bundles the existing c1/c2/Delta_alpha/
alpha0 with new descriptors:

  ANISOTROPY
    directional_hurst(image)   -- H(theta) via structure-function across angles
    anisotropy_index(image)    -- (Hmax-Hmin)/Hmean from the above
    subband_anisotropy(image)  -- fast wavelet H/V/D variance proxy

  SPATIAL COHERENCE
    autocorr_radial(image)     -- radially-averaged R(r) via Wiener-Khinchin
    coherence_length(image)    -- first r where R(r) <= 1/e
    integral_length(image)     -- trapezoidal integral of R(r)

  WITHIN-IMAGE HETEROGENEITY
    local_c2_map(image)        -- sliding-window wavelet-leader c2
    heterogeneity_full(image)  -- dict: std/range/skew/kurt/entropy of local FD

anisotropy = 0.49 (highest, grain-aligned), ridged anisotropy = 0.01 but

Flips the usual workflow — instead of generating and measuring (c1, c2)
post-hoc, you specify the target spectrum and synthesize backward.

Implementation: uses the proven MRW machinery (multifractal_cloud) under
the hood with an empirical (granularity, multifractality) -> (c1, c2)
calibration fit on n=256 samples. The calibration lands:

  c2 within +/- 0.02 of target across c2 in [-0.5, -0.05]
  c1 within +/- 0.10 of target across c1 in [1.0, 1.5]

API:
  mf.prescribed_cascade(n, seed, complexity)
      cx routes (c1, c2) from (1.7, -0.05) at cx=0 to (1.0, -0.50) at cx=1
  mf.prescribed_cascade(n, seed, c1_target=..., c2_target=...)
      direct spectrum targeting

Useful for c2-balanced stimulus banks where you want N samples at a chosen
multifractal level without iterating generate-measure-discard.

Bases of genuinely different kinds, beyond cascade/fBM/multifractional/NS:

- mfractal.aggregation   dla_dendrite
    Single-center diffusion-limited aggregation (walker DLA). Different
    from anything else: structure built by random walkers sticking to a

    microscopic physical process. Pareidolic reads: neurons, frost,
    lightning, coral. cx routes particle count (800 -> 5000) and blur.

- mfractal.attractors    clifford_attractor, de_jong_attractor
    Density of orbit of a chaotic 2D map iterated 150k-1M times. No
    stochasticity in the dynamics -- complexity comes from sensitivity to
    initial conditions and parameter geometry. Filamentary wisps, fern /
    cosmic-cloud reads. seed picks the (a,b,c,d) neighborhood.

- mfractal.interference  wave_interference
    Sum of 8-64 random plane waves. Bounded power spectrum (not 1/f^beta),
    smooth analytic structure, no singularities. Useful monofractal anchor
    with a visual character completely unlike fBM. Moire / fabric / wave-
    front reads.

Attractors:
  Seeds now sample (a, b, c, d) from a wide parameter cube
  ([-2.2, 2.2]^4 for Clifford, [-2.7, 2.7]^4 for de Jong) instead of
  jittering a single base. A two-stage quality filter (fast 8k-iter probe,
  bbox + histogram-occupancy check) rejects degenerate orbits — point
  attractors, short cycles, or sparse cycles that pass a naive bbox check
  but visit too few cells. Up to 40 retries before falling back; in practice
  most attempts succeed on the first try.
  Result: seeds now give visually distinct strange attractors (different
  fern / wisp / branched / vortical shapes) rather than near-identical
  variations on a fixed parameter neighborhood.

wave_interference:
  New `crispness` parameter in [0, 1]. 0 = original smooth cos-sum;
  1 = near-binary level-curves via tanh saturation (post-sum field
  pushed through tanh(gain * (field - 0.5)) with gain = 1 + 18 * crispness).
  Lets the interference fringes read as crisp lines or moire wave-fronts
  rather than soft gradients.

Figures 30 (cx sweep, refreshed), 31 (seed sweep, dramatically wider
attractor variation), 32 (crispness sweep across cx).

A small frozen-ResNet18 + MLP head distils the wavelet-leader c2
estimator into a differentiable predictor, intended as the conditioning

pareidolia), stratified 80/10/10 by source. CPU end-to-end, ~3 minutes.

  Pearson r = +0.882   R^2 = 0.773   RMSE = 0.149

Per-source:
  procedural   n=48   r=+0.97   RMSE=0.07   <- nailed
  diffusion    n=32   r=+0.82   RMSE=0.22   <- bottleneck

texture distribution matches training. The diffusion-domain RMSE (0.22)
is too noisy to drive classifier-guidance at sampling time (target floor
< 0.10) -- in-domain overfit with cross-domain gap. Two clear levers:
better backbone (DINOv2/CLIP), much more diffusion training data

plan that takes the regressor through to classifier-guided SDXL.

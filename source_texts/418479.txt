I&#x27;m one of the developers of Spark, a new open-source 3D Gaussian Splatting renderer (sparkjs.dev).<p>While developing Spark, we built a WebXR demo that lets you experience a stream of 3D AI-generated worlds from &quot;inside&quot;. These worlds were made by World Labs from text+image prompts last year, and I think it&#x27;s really cool to imagine what&#x27;s going to be possible in the near future!<p>If you have a Quest 3 or Vision Pro, open up lofiworlds.ai in the web browser and make sure to enable hand tracking so you can &quot;touch&quot; the 3D Gaussians :). Tap your wrists together to toggle spotlight mode. The demo works on mobile + desktop as well but 90% of the experience can only be had in VR so I highly recommend dusting off that Quest 3 and trying it in there!

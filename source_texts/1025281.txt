Planted torches self-shadowed with hard diagonal bands. The cause wasn't bias — a distance cube map, sampled on the big flat coplanar surfaces a voxel world is made of, self-occludes at grazing angles by ~a full block (not a sub-texel near-miss), which no bias can close. So this stops using a shadow map for point lights and instead marches the voxel occupancy grid (Amanatides–Woo DDA) from the receiver to the light. Any opaque cell between them occludes; the march starts a hair off the surface along its normal and skips the endpoint cells, so a lit face physically can't self-shadow. Exact — no depth compare, no bias, no seams, no acne.
Highlights
Penumbra: area-sampling the light's disk gives a physically widening soft edge (tight at contact, broad far away), from a fixed coherent sample pattern so it's stable — no crawl or shimmer. Umbra/full-light early-out to one ray.
Stained glass: the ray carries per-channel transmittance, so glass tints the shadow (real per-pane, subsumes the tint cube).
Perf: 128³ RGBA occupancy grid, chunk-aware fill (~1.5ms), rebuilt when the camera crosses a 16-block step or a nearby chunk remeshes (so shadows track block edits). The redundant distance cube is skipped when the grid owns occlusion, so it costs about what the cube map did (measured on-par at 4 lights).
Setting: point_shadows (grid|cube), default grid; a TORCH SHADOWS toggle in Settings. WILDFORGE_DDA_SHADOW=0/1 forces either for A/B.
Ships a shadow-debug viz (WILDFORGE_SHADOW_DEBUG=1..4) used to diagnose the above.
Known limits
Grid covers ~64 blocks around the camera; torches farther out don't get grid shadows yet.
8 MB occupancy texture.
Animated sub-cell occluders aren't handled (out of scope; the rung-one mechanization plan is static blocks). Minor fine-structure moiré in very dim penumbra.

Coherent, stable penumbra for grid point-light shadows
…
67f19b2
```
The soft-shadow fan rotated its sample disk by a per-fragment hash, so
the penumbra was a noisy Monte-Carlo estimate: it shimmered, crawled as
the source radius changed, and left fine-structure moiré in dim shadow.
Drop the random rotation for a fixed Vogel spiral that rotates smoothly
with the light direction — the soft edge becomes a stable gradient. The
early-out now probes all four disk edges (not one axis) so a penumbra
crossing either axis is never missed and hardened. Torch sources stay
small (a flame), so the band is thin and never wraps around a block —
the "light through the block" was an oversized 2-unit disk sampling past
a 1-block occluder, not a real leak (every ray still stops at opaque).

Rebuild the shadow occupancy grid on nearby edits
…
3e388dd
```
The DDA occupancy grid only refreshed when the camera crossed a 16-block
step, so placing or breaking blocks around a torch left the grid stale —
shadows didn't track the edit, and the frozen occupancy could even throw
a misplaced hard edge. Mark the grid dirty whenever a chunk within its
reach remeshes (the same signal the light director already consumes), so
it rebuilds the next frame and shadows follow edits immediately.

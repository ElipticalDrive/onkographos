This PR fixes the rendering noise and flickering issues in the cloud rendering system. Specifically, it corrects the 3D SDF texture sampling coordinate bug in atmosphere_lowres.comp and activates the Y-boundary checks. It also streamlines and refactors the density and distance field logic by routing all queries to calculateCloudDensity and evalSdf respectively, removing redundant lofting and puffy cloud legacy code. It adjusts the SDF parameters to position tileable spheres in the middle of the height volume with random height/radius variations that touch boundaries without being clipped or fully overlapping.

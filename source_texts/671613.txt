Root causes of the 1.6 dB smoke run: exposure gain initialised with the
absolute EXIF EV (predictions divided by ~70; TRIPS uses EV minus the
scene mean, NeuralScene.cpp:38) and a masked-MSE that summed over 3
channels but normalised by a 1-channel mask (PSNR 4.77 dB low). Also:
background config honoured, crops kept inside the image, seed applied to
torch RNG, non-finite gradients zeroed and logged. Smoke run now 12.26 dB
after 2 epochs (jobs train-smoke-3/-4, rc 0). Regression tests added.
Reviewed-by: Reviewer/Claude Fable 5.1/normal

This is my take at a 'hologram' for my bachelors. Far from ... - Reddit
For a piece of hardware like this (volumetric POV display) the interrupt latency will need to be very low and consistent and display data timing will need to be very precise. These two requirements are much easier to implement on a microcontroller than on a higher level chip running a high level OS.

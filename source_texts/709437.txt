Part of #146. Blocked by #148.
Scope
Add ray-traced ambient occlusion to the lit shader.
Reuse occluded() from box.frag; add a max-distance parameter. The sun call keeps 1000 m.
Sample 8 cosine-weighted hemisphere directions with a Vogel disk, phase-rotated per pixel and per frame by interleaved_gradient_noise and the TAA phase, exactly as the sun samples rotate.
Ray length is camera.lighting.x (1.5 m). Origin is offset 0.01 m along the normal.
ao = visible / 8, then mix(1.0, ao, camera.lighting.y).
Multiply only the ambient term: ambient * ao. The sun term does not change.
When camera.lighting.z < 0.5, skip the loop and use 1.0.
The loop is compile-time bounded; TAA averages the noise.
Acceptance
Corners, the tunnel, and the base of crates darken; open floor stays unchanged.
1440p GPU total stays under 2 ms. Measure and record.
With AO off the image matches the sky-only build.

Abstract
The Phase-21 line of the 2-relational geometry (2-RG) program attached an information theory
to the midpoint-subdivision tower T (∆) of a tetrahedron: the Shannon entropy of the normalised
cell-energy distribution pr (c) = E(c)/Er is strictly increasing along the subdivision depth r (TT1), and the stationarization against the two-valued profile πr obeys the exact law χ2 (pr kπr ) =
9/(2 · 4r + 1)2 (T-T2). Those theorems were, however, proved exactly only for the regular toy
tetrahedron; their cell-level extension to a general tetrahedron was gated by the open shape lemma
L-SHAPE (registry open problem O-P21-1). The present paper closes that gap on the full-tree
(D2) arena, and reports the adjudication of the natural generalisations. (1) The shape lemma (ST1, [proved], elementary): for every non-degenerate tetrahedron ∆, every tetrahedral cell at layer
r of the D2 tower is similar to ∆ · 2−r and every octahedral cell is a cross polytope with semi-axes
(a, b, c) · 2−(r−1) ; the cells of each layer occupy at most two similarity classes, and the per-cell energy
ratio poct (r)/ptet (r) = 2 holds exactly at every layer, independently of the shape of ∆. The proof is
word-level: a semi-axes basis reduces the central-octahedron analysis to fourteen explicit edge-level
identities, verified edge by edge and certified in exact rational arithmetic on three archived witnesses.
(2) Verbatim upgrade (Corollary S-C1): because the per-cell values of pr coincide, term by term, with
those of the regular toy, every closed form of T-T1/T-T2 upgrades verbatim and becomes shapeindependent: H(pr ) strictly increasing; DKL (pr kur ) % D∞ = 21 log(9/8) (the same constant for all
∆); χ2 (pr kπr ) = 9/(2·4r +1)2 ; DKL (pr kπr ) ∼ (9/8) 16−r strictly decreasing. O-P21-1 is adjudicated
[adjudicated-closed] on the D2 arena: the cell-level generalisation requires no new condition. A
geometric explanation (Corollary S-C2) identifies the two-class property as the root cause of the
shape independence of the Phase-21 energy transfer matrix ME . (3) Negative controls (S-N1): the
two-class property is an energy/edge-square-multiset level statement, not a Euclidean-embedding
level one (explicit non-similar equal-energy witnesses T1 , T2 ); diagonal (red) triangulations of the
central octahedron are not covered. (4) Fixed-ratio refinement (S-T2): replacing midpoints by fixedratio points λ 6= 1/2, (a) the six edge points form a cross polytope if and only if λ = 1/2 ([proved],
elementary identity); (b) the two-class property is [refuted (toy, exact certificate)]: at λ = 1/3
the combinatorial type already leaves the two-type system at layer 1, and the forced octahedral
construction produces eight pairwise non-similar central tetrahedra (exact rational certificate; cell
energies ranging from 3589/600 to 77957/5400); (c) the shape map Sλ on similarity-class space
is defined precisely and its residual problems (finite-type closure, needle degeneration, size-biased
stationary profile) are registered [open]. Novelty honesty. No absolute novelty claim is made:
the geometric half of S-T1 is a suspected-folklore property of tetrahedron–octahedron refinement
(recorded in the mesh literature, e.g. Weiss–De Floriani 2010) and is not claimed as new; the

1.1
Background: the tower information theorems and the O-P21-1 gap
The Phase-21 Line-B campaign of the 2-RG program [16] studied the discrete refinement axis r ∈ N of
the midpoint-subdivision tower T (∆) of a tetrahedron and the information theory of the normalised
cell-energy distribution pr (c) = E(c)/Er , where E(c) is the sum of squared edge lengths of a cell and Er
its layer total. Its two main theorems are the tower entropy theorem T-T1 (H(pr ) strictly increasing,
with the universal uniformisation-deficit constant D∞ = 21 log(9/8); registry R:1896 [21]) and the
stationarization theorem T-T2 (χ2 (pr kπr ) = 9/(2 · 4r + 1)2 exactly, DKL (pr kπr ) ∼ (9/8) 16−r strictly
decreasing, while DKL (pr kur ) increases to D∞ > 0; R:1897). Both were established with exact closed
forms on the regular tetrahedron (the regular toy), and at type level for a general ∆ via the shape( 1 1/2 )
independent energy transfer matrix ME = 1 3/2 (Lemma L-ME) and imported Perron–Frobenius
theory.
The gap between “regular toy, cell level” and “general ∆, type level” was isolated in Phase 21 as
the open lemma L-SHAPE (registry open problem O-P21-1, R:2984): controlling the within-type,
cell-level shape dispersion of the full-tree (D2) refinement on a non-regular tetrahedron, suﬀiciently
to transfer the cell-level closed forms. The Phase-21 paper stated this gate explicitly: its cell-level
statements for a general tetrahedron remain open-candidate, and the anticipated risk was the classical
shape-regularity problem of red refinement (finitely many similarity classes; cf. [6, 9]).
The Phase-22 Stage-0 reconnaissance discovered that the gate is, on the D2 arena, far better than
assumed: the midpoint full tree on any non-degenerate ∆ produces at most two similarity classes per
layer, with explicit similarity ratios. The present paper turns that discovery into a proved theorem
with a complete word-level proof (S-T1), upgrades T-T1/T-T2 verbatim (Corollary S-C1), explains the
shape independence of L-ME geometrically (Corollary S-C2), records what the theorem does not say
(S-N1), and adjudicates the fixed-ratio (λ 6= 1/2) generalisation: midpoint uniqueness is proved, the
two-class property is refuted in the toy (S-T2(a),(b)), and the genuine shape dynamics that remains
is defined precisely and left open (S-T2(c)).

5 Geometric explanation of L-ME’s shape independence (S-C2)
Corollary 5.1 (S-C2; explanatory; [proved]). The root cause of the shape independence of the energy
transfer matrix ME (Lemma 2.2) is the two-class property of Theorem 3.1: the action of midpoint
subdivision on edge-square multisets is a universal rational multiplier. For corner cells, every parent
edge appears in every corner cell as exactly one half-edge, with multiplicity d + 1 (the Phase-21 TT3(a) edge-by-edge argument [16]); for central tets, every oct edge appears as exactly four half-edges
among the eight central tets (the L-ME edge-by-edge count). Both multipliers and multiplicities are
combinatorial constants and never pass through the metric realisation of ∆. In other words, ME is a
replacement matrix at the level of edge multisets: shape enters only through the scalar factor E(c),
and is cancelled by the summation over types.
This corollary is explanatory: it adds no new content to L-ME, whose original proof stands unchanged.

7 Fixed-ratio refinement: the adjudication (S-T2)
Setting. Replace the edge midpoints of D2 by fixed-ratio points Pij (λ) = Vi + λ(Vj − Vi ) (λ ∈ (0, 1)
fixed), and mimic the D2 rule otherwise (4 corner tets + central cell + face-central tets).
7.1
S-T2(a): midpoint uniqueness
Theorem 7.1 (S-T2(a); [proved], elementary identity; [certified]). For any non-degenerate ∆ and
any λ ∈ (0, 1): the six λ-edge points form a cross polytope (three antipodal vertex pairs sharing a
common centre) ⇐⇒ λ = 1/2.
Proof. Expanding the three antipodal pair sums with Pij (λ) = (1 − λ)Vi + λVj :
S1 := PAB + PCD = (1 − λ)(A + C) + λ(B + D),
S2 := PAC + PBD = (1 − λ)(A + B) + λ(C + D),
S3 := PAD + PBC = (1 − λ)(A + B) + λ(C + D).
Note the structural phenomenon caused by the orientation asymmetry of Pij : S3 ≡ S2 identically —
the three pair sums degenerate to two distinct values (certified on 100 random rational (∆, λ) pairs,
group C-S2-crosspoly, 8/8). A single independent identity remains:
S1 − S2 = (1 − λ)(C − B) + λ(B − C) = (1 − 2λ)(C − B).
For non-degenerate ∆, B 6= C, so S1 = S2 ⇐⇒ 1 − 2λ = 0 ⇐⇒ λ = 1/2; and then S1 = S2 = S3 ,
the three antipodal pairs share a centre, and the six points form a cross polytope.

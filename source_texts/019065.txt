Fractal image compression offers a high compression ratio with extremely short decompression time. However, it suffered from long compression time. In this paper, a new block classification method based on the Haar wavelet transform is introduced. When used in conjunction with our domain block pool reduction scheme, the enhanced method can improve the speed of compression over 50 times with minimal loss of decompression accuracy.

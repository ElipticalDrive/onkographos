Hi, thanks for the great work on gsplat! I have a question regarding the distributed=True mode in rasterization.
In your [v1.2.0 release notes](https://github.com/graphdeco-inria/gaussian-splatting/releases/tag/v1.2.0), it’s mentioned that multi-GPU distributed rasterization is supported and can lead to >3× speedup and memory savings. I would like to clarify:
Is distributed=True meant to optimize only training speed, or should it also improve inference/rendering efficiency?
Here is what I observed during inference:
Single GPU:
Rendering 101 images × 7 views
Total time: 40 seconds
Effective FPS (per view): ~17.6
4 GPUs with distributed=True:
Same 707 images
Total time: 100+ seconds
Effective FPS: much lower
This seems counter-intuitive, as distributed inference should ideally be faster than single-GPU rendering.
Do you know why this slowdown might occur?
Some possible questions I have:
Does distributed=True during inference still require a balanced number of Gaussians per rank to be efficient?
Are there synchronization overheads or collective ops (like all_gather) that become a bottleneck?
Is it expected that distributed rasterization is mainly beneficial during training, but not necessarily during inference?
Thanks for your help and clarification!

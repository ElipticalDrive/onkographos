166-183: Verify PyVista grid dimension ordering against your volume convention
The new _display_volume helper centralizes the visualization nicely, but the (dimensions, spacing, origin) mapping is subtle when volume.data comes from SimpleITK (which returns arrays as (z, y, x)):
```
arr = volume.data

    dimensions=arr.shape,          # currently (z, y, x)
```

A Python library implementing seven spatial subsampling algorithms for structural orientation data (bedding, foliation) used in 3D geological modelling workflows. The engine reduces spatial clustering in point datasets while preserving the statistical character of orientations, using methods ranging from simple decimation to Kent-distribution-based spherical statistics and geologically-informed first-order subsampling. Includes corrections to known transcription errors in the published Kent distribution estimation algorithm (Leong and Carlile 1998; Carmichael and Ailleres 2016).

The engine reduces spatial clustering in point datasets while preserving the statistical character of orientations, using methods ranging from simple decimation to Kent-distribution-based spherical statistics and geologically-informed first-order subsampling.

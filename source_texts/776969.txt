Adds physician-useful research lesion localization in the ORIGINAL HU-NIfTI voxel coordinate system, not just the model's internal 512² image grid. Inverts pinned original ianpan vendor NIfTI → LPS reorientation, center crop/pad and zyx mapping; inverse affine produces voxel XYZ and physical RAS mm ROI for each classification-positive nonempty candidate. Rejects geometry mismatches and clipped/outside-original-FOV heatmaps without inventing boxes.
Updated research importer displays the source-bound candidate ROIs and validates report clinical-status flags. SHA-256 of source HU NIfTI remains required. Source projection is a deterministic imaging geometry calculation, not independently corroborated pathology, registration against patient DICOM SOP or expert mask, nor a Dice/TP/diagnostic claim.
Automated synthetic source affine cases cover RAS, LPS, axis permutations, padding and refusal of invalid boxes. No GPU, clinical data, public CT re-run or model weights needed for CI.

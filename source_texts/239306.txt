Repo: https://github.com/yunzhedeng/Project5-WebGPU-Gaussian-Splat-Viewer
Live demo: https://yunzhedeng.github.io/Project5-WebGPU-Gaussian-Splat-Viewer/
Features:
-Point cloud rendering with MVP transform
-Gaussian splat renderer: preprocess compute shader with frustum culling, covariance projection, SH color, GPU radix sort, and indirect draw with alpha blending
-Tile-based depth sorting with a compute shader rasterizer (selectable as gaussian_tile in the UI)
-GPU timing, culling toggle and workgroup size options in the UI for performance testing

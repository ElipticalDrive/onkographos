Summary of Changes
Fixes the two cases of internal::distance_index() described in #9765: when the point is outside of the edge (vertex(1), vertex(2)), past one of its ends, and not outside of the adjacent edge, the closest point is that end vertex (vertex(2) for a counterclockwise triangle, vertex(1) for a clockwise one), but vertex(0) was returned. Both now return the end vertex, like the corresponding cases for the other edges.
Added both cases to test_distance_2.cpp. They fail without the fix (52 instead of 1, 25 instead of 2) and pass with it, for all kernels in the test.
I also compared the result against an exact reference (0 if the point is in the triangle, otherwise the minimum of the distances to the three edges as segments) on 100,000 random point/triangle pairs for each of three coordinate ranges (up to ±1000) and three kernels (Epeck, Simple_cartesian<Gmpq>, Homogeneous<Gmpz>). Before the fix about 3% differed, with both orientations; after the fix none did. The other 2D and 3D squared_distance pairs gave the same results as before.

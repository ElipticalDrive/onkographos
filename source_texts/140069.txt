Volumes - Blender 5.2 LTS Manual
Oct 20, 2026Volume Step size is the distance between volume shader samples. Cycles automatically estimates this distance based on voxel size in volume objects and smoke simulations. Render time can be reduced by increasing the step size, at the cost of potentially losing some volume detail.

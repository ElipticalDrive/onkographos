What
A new opt-in PATH_TRACED option in the RT pack (shader menu, Rays page), aimed at the Bedrock RTX look. Off by default.
lib/pathtrace.glsl (new): deferred.fsh's bounce light becomes a real path of up to PT_BOUNCES (3) bounces, each up to PT_DISTANCE (48) blocks. At every block it lands on, it adds:
the block's own glow (glowing blocks plus LabPBR emissive texels via mcm_hit_emission)
a sun/moon shadow ray, reusing deferred1's shadow when the hit was on screen
one light from mcmetal's light list, with its own shadow ray (next event estimation)
The path's colour is tinted by each block's albedo (cosine sampling). Russian roulette starts after the 2nd bounce.
Lightmaps no longer give light. They only gate the sky for rays that run out of range, so caves stay dark.
The primary surface keeps its existing sun, AO, ReSTIR block light, history and denoiser. Only the bounce-light estimate changes.
Light cache and world cache are not used inside the path.
Resolution
At 2560x1440 with GI_HALF_RES=1 (default) the path is traced at 1280x720. The planned MetalFX route is mcmetal's existing MCMETAL_UPSCALE=2 at MCMETAL_UPSCALE_SCALE=50 with GI_HALF_RES=0, which also traces at 720p. Both are booked on the M3.
Checks
check_pack.py compiles every program, GL and RT, with the option on and off.
GitHub Mac run (room, room GI view, noon, cave) dispatched.
Speed and look are judged on the M3 only.

Adds an opt-in multi-bounce path-traced GI implementation for RT deferred shading.
Introduces up to three cosine-sampled bounces with configurable per-bounce distance.
Accumulates block emission, sun/moon direct lighting with shadowing, and sampled mcmetal block-light direct lighting.
Propagates albedo through path throughput and applies Russian roulette after the second bounce.
Uses sky lightmaps only to gate sky fallback and keeps primary-surface lighting, caches, and denoising behavior otherwise unchanged.

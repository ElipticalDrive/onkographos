Owner
Slabs: Revit-style Modify Sub Elements
shape param (JSON {z:[...], points:[[x,z,dz]], creases:[[a,b]]}) on apex.slab and flat roof. Indices: boundary vertices then interior points.
Geometry (apex-geometry/src/shaped.rs): constrained triangulation of boundary + interior points + creases into a height field; one watertight solid, constant vertical thickness (underside = top − t).
Openings / stair voids are subtracted with mesh_boolean; plan, section and exports use the same solid.
UI: Edit shape in the inspector (per-vertex Z inputs, add point, add crease, reset); in 3D the handles can be dragged up/down (snap 0.01 m). Every edit is one undo step.
Tests: ramp watertight + volume + undo/redo, interior point + creases + reset, opening cut volume.
Pitched roof as one mitred solid
Overhang, eave cut (vertical / horizontal / perpendicular), slope by angle or by rise, hip option. Underside drop t/cos(pitch) so the ridge and hips mitre exactly. No more stacked-plate edges buried at the wall junction.
Wall Attach top to roof follows the new solid.
house-e2e: ramp slab via shape editor, roof overhang 0.5 + perpendicular eaves, gable attach (selected via test hook since the overhang now covers the walls in plan), new close-up shots.
Note
Medium Risk
Touches core mesh generation (new shaped surfaces, roof CSG/booleans) and document params used by rendering, wall attachment, and exports; mitigated by unit/e2e tests but regressions in existing roofs or slabs are plausible.
Overview
Adds Revit-style slab sub-element editing for apex.slab and flat apex.roof: a JSON shape param drives per-vertex Z offsets, interior points, and creases. New ShapedSurface geometry triangulates the top and extrudes with constant vertical thickness; shaped meshes replace plain extrusion for solids, openings, and exports. The web app gets a SlabShapePanel (edit mode, numeric handles, creases, reset), 3D gizmo drag on shape handles, and WASM slabShapeInfo / setSlabShape with undoable updates.
Pitched roofs are rebuilt as a single watertight solid via RoofShape and ShapedSurface (gable/hip, overhang, slope by rise or angle, eave cut vertical/horizontal/perpendicular). Wall attach top to roof now clips against the new underside surface. Inspector params expand accordingly; house-e2e exercises a ramp slab, roof overhang/eave cut, geometry-based wall selection under overhang, and extra 3D shots.

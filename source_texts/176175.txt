If you have a ply file, one process for converting to a mesh is:
Compute the density of the splats at each point in a voxel volume. Now you have a voxel matrix
Use marching cubes to convert the voxels to a mesh
This is pretty simple algorithm wise, but your mesh fidelity will be limited by your voxel size and smaller sizes will increase your memory requirements.
Alternatively, you can iteratively render the gaussians from different view points and transform each pixel in the depthmap to world space. This will give you a set of points on the surface of the object. You can then use Poisson Reconstruction to convert these points to a mesh. You can get higher resolution this way, but it's way more complicated. The SuGaR paper talks about this strategy.

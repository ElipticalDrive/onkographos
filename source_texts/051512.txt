Skip seam faces when adjacent chunk voxels are solid
In generate_mesh, the boundary checks like x == 0 || ... force face emission on chunk borders without consulting neighboring chunks, so in the new 3x3 world every solid-to-solid chunk seam still generates internal quads. This substantially inflates triangle count and overdraw as world size grows, which directly undermines the culling goal introduced in this commit; the mesher needs neighbor-chunk occupancy for border voxels before emitting these faces.

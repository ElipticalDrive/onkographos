⚡ Bolt Performance Improvement:
💡 What: Optimized procedural terrain sampling in src/sim/world.ts by precomputing spatial bounding radii squared (maxReachSq) for landmasses, peaks, and carriers.
🎯 Why: terrainHeight is queried hundreds of thousands of times during heightfield creation, physics simulation, collision detection, and radar altitude checks. Every query previously computed Math.hypot for 15+ landmasses and all peaks regardless of distance.
📊 Impact: Reduces 300,000 terrain queries execution time from ~402ms down to ~239ms (~40% speedup) with 100% bit-exact height values.
🔬 Verification: Run bun scripts/diag-performance.ts and bun scripts/simtest.ts. Added critical learning to .jules/bolt.md.

Precompute maximum reach radii squared and derived constants for landmasses and peaks in `makeProceduralSampler`. Distance-squared checks (`dx * dx + dz * dz >= maxReachSq`) now filter out distant features before invoking expensive `Math.hypot` / `Math.sqrt` calls.

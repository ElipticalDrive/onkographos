Summary
Adds source/worksheet02.ptx, the worksheet for Workshop 2 (second half of Week 2 and first half of Week 3): cross product, lines and planes in 3D, and an introduction to matrices, matrix multiplication and matrices as transformations.
Six A4 pages, seven exercises:
Warm-up. Cross product of two vectors, checked with the dot product; a table of matrix-expression sizes for A (2x3), B (3x2), C (3x3), D (2x2).
Lines. Vector form to Cartesian form and back.
Planes. The plane through a point parallel to two vectors, and where the line from Exercise 3(b) meets it.
Matrix arithmetic. Ax, A^T A, x^T x, x x^T and A^T x^T (not defined) for a 2x3 matrix and a column vector.
Matrix transformations. Recover the 3x3 matrix of T: R^3 -> R^3 from T(1,0,0), T(1,1,0), T(1,1,1).
Challenge: italics by matrix. The word LIFT upright and sheared on a grid (new generated figure assets/lift-italic.svg); find the shear matrix.
Also adds a \colvec macro to source/docinfo.ptx for column vectors in square brackets, registers the target in project.ptx and adds a row to site/index.html.

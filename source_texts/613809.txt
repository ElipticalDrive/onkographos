What
PR 4 of 6 of Stage 18 (dev_plan.md, design). clinical goes from 0.1 to 0.2 and gains:
Maximum 3D diameter (size.max_diameter_mm) between voxel centres (18.2-c). The farthest pair is searched among the end voxels of the lines along the longest side of the box, then among the corners of their hull; a flat or straight set is searched pairwise.
Convex hull of the mesh vertices (shape.hull_volume_ml, shape.hull_area_mm2, shape.solidity, shape.convexity) (18.7). Own incremental hull in hull.rs, no new dependency; the six axis extremes go in first, so interior points are rejected against a few faces.
Connectivity (topology.*, 18.8): 26-connected pieces and share of the largest, 6-connected cavities and their volume, Euler number (closed-voxel cubical complex), image sides touched. Pieces and the cavity mask come from ferrum_domain::analysis (components, fill_holes), so there is one implementation.
Warnings in the envelope: fragmented (largest piece below 95 % of the voxels), has_holes, touches_border. Thresholds are in the feature definitions. A warning never changes the mask.
Desktop card: a "Connectivity" group is rendered if the profile has it (the card still uses quick; the switch to clinical is PR 6).
quick is unchanged (0.1).

Spec changes to review
18.7-b: "an L-shaped phantom gives a value derived by hand" → the hull volume of the L is derived by hand (11/3); solidity by hand is shown for a two-voxel dumbbell (0.2), because the mesh volume of an L is not hand-derivable. An earlier brute-force reference with a jitter was wrong (jitter bigger than its tolerance); the independent check above is what settled it.
18.7-a: convexity is hull area / mesh area; it is ≤ 1 for a connected body and can exceed 1 for separate pieces (the dumbbell gives √5+½). Stated in the definition text.
18.8-d: thresholds fixed: 95 % / one cavity / one image side.
Maximum 3D diameter uses voxel centres (as the long axis), not mesh vertices as IBSI and pyradiomics do; the field test expects small differences.

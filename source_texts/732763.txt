Suggesting addition of gtsam-splatfactors to the list.
What it is: Gaussian Splatting SLAM with an iSAM2 (GTSAM) factor graph backend. Photometric rendering residuals from gsplat are expressed as GTSAM factors, enabling loop closure, incremental Bayes tree updates, and proper uncertainty estimation — features that existing 3DGS-SLAM systems (SplaTAM, MonoGS) lack.
Features:
iSAM2 incremental pose optimization with photometric factors
Automatic loop closure detection (DINOv2 + photometric verification)
Monocular depth initialization (Depth Anything V2)
Keyframe selection heuristics
COLMAP/nerfstudio export
Modular: easily add IMU/GPS/wheel odometry as additional factors

Closes the README roadmap row Curved transmissive epsilons: removing the offset that stops rays re-hitting curved glass passed every test.
Change
spawnOrigin (shading_scene): rebuild the hit from its barycentrics, move it along the face normal by a bound on its float error (gamma_3 sum|b_i p_i| plus the rounding of w; Higham 2002 3.1, PBR 4e 6.8), add Chiang/Li/Burley's terminator lift, then step each coordinate one float outward (bit-level NextFloatUp).
Continuation, NEE shadow and AO rays all use it with tMin 0. This removes kRayEpsilon (1e-4) and transmissionOffsetEpsilon (longest edge x normal spread, 1.93e-2 on the test sphere), which skipped real neighbouring facets inside curved glass.
kFilterRadius moves to path_tracer.h so a test can bound the pixel footprint.
Tests
spawn_origin_clears_its_plane (exact): 788,640 origins at scales 2^-20 to 2^20, off-origin translations and a sliver. All are strictly on their side of the face in double precision, and none is past the derived bound.
curved_glass_scale_invariance (exact): rough glass over a floor renders bit-identical at 2^-16 and 2^16, so any fixed length fails it.
beer_lambert_absorption: the 3% tolerance becomes a bracket from geometry. This is the closed-form reference.
A telephoto keeps the pixel footprint near the axis.
Slab chords lie in [t, t/cos theta]. Sphere chords lie in [2 sqrt(r_in^2 - rho^2) - stand-off, 2R].
The sphere reads 0.250130 in [0.25, 0.250895]. Main reads 0.253453.
Each mutation fails at least one gate:

Measured (256x128, 16 passes, seeds 1-3, paired against main)
Rough glass sphere: -2.98% (noise 0.61%), because light that skipped a facet now meets it.
Smooth glass sphere: -0.56% (noise 2.47%).
Stump: -0.05%.
Macbeth and cloud: bit-identical.
Medium stack: across about 247k glass entries, wrong-side glass hits go from 7 to 0, with no escapes inside the medium either before or after.
Cost: a spawn takes 28 ns against 13.8 ns. Cornell CPU time is +2.4%; the pass-time ratio over 3 paired rounds is 1.05 on cornell and 1.04 on rough glass, with no interval at n = 3.
Evidence is in results/curved_transmissive_epsilons.

Volume visualization techniques are often applied to sampled data obtained from MRI and CT-scans, as well as to simulated data computed by computational fluid dynamics (CFD) methods. In these and many other scientific visualization applications, a sequence of images is animated by incrementally rotating a volumetric object about its center, so that the scientist can view the structure from all angles. In this paper, we introduce an efficient parallel algorithm, called VoxelFlow, for generating rendered images of volume data which can be used in animating object rotations. Unlike existing methods, our algorithm uses a look-ahead scheme of voxel dataflow and exploits the communication features of modern scalable multicomputers. Computational and communication load balancing issues are effectively addressed to achieve near linear speedup on the Cray T3D.

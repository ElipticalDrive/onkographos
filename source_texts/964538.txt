Two new Graphics groups, with shaders in HLSL.
Physically based rendering
brdf.md: Cook–Torrance with GGX, height-correlated Smith visibility and Schlick Fresnel, Lambert diffuse, a complete one-light shade function, windowed inverse-square attenuation, and specular aliasing.
materials.md: metal/roughness, plausible value ranges and validation, texture formats/colour spaces/ORM packing, normal maps (MikkTSpace, handedness, OpenGL vs DirectX green, Toksvig), AO, and extensions.
ibl.md: L2 SH irradiance, the split sum, prefiltered mips, the BRDF LUT, the combined ambient function, multiple-scattering compensation, reflection and light probes with parallax correction, and environment sources and units.
colour.md: linear vs sRGB (with a checkerboard test), HDR targets, physical light units, EV100 exposure and histogram auto exposure, tone mapping (ACES fit, AgX, PBR Neutral, grading, UI), and HDR displays.
Shadow mapping
basics.md: the depth-only pass, the comparison with SampleCmpLevelZero, acne and biases (constant, slope-scaled, normal offset), peter-panning, resolution/aliasing, and contact shadows.
filtering.md: hardware 2×2 PCF, grids, rotated Poisson disks with interleaved gradient noise, PCSS, pre-filterable maps (VSM family), and a choice table.
cascades.md: practical split scheme, sphere fit with texel snapping, casters behind the slice (pancaking), cascade selection, blending/dithering, cost reductions, and virtual shadow maps.
local-lights.md: spot and point (cube, linear distance) shadows, per-face culling, an importance-sized shadow atlas, caching static shadows, and budgets.
The nav, the graphics index, and the home page's Graphics line are updated. A test merge with stuart6854/gamedev-reference#108 is clean, and the strict build passes locally.

Summary
First Phase 3 foundation item: mesh-to-SDF voxelization replaces the analytic radial formula as the inside-test, decoupling all downstream consumers from the star-shaped-solid assumption.
New metrics/sdf.ts: signed voxel field built from any watertight mesh — Z-axis ray-parity rasterization gives an exact inside/outside classification (XY-binned triangle candidates, deterministic micro-jitter against edge hits), followed by a 3-4-5 chamfer transform for approximate distance magnitudes
ShapeField.sdf is built from the capped solid; createFluidState marks cells via sdf.isInside(point, margin) — the mouth plane is respected implicitly by construction, so the explicit dome clamp is gone
Dead implicit-field code removed (isPointInsideField, projectPointInsideField, sampleFillPoints); getDirectionalRadius remains solely as a shape generator
Performance: chamfer runs on flattened typed-array offsets with zero-distance early-out — build cost cut from 460 ms to ~60 ms at resolution 52
Why this matters
Drag-handle deformation and mesh import (rest of Phase 3) can now change geometry arbitrarily; fluid marking, fill logic, and future collision consumers keep working without touching the radial formula. The containment shader still mirrors the recipe in GLSL — that migration to SDF sampling is the queued follow-up.
Test plan
New suite: cube interior/exterior with margin control; sphere radial monotonicity across the shell; ≥97% agreement with the analytic field away from walls on irregular vessels; no inside verdicts above the mouth plane
Full suite 52 tests green; typecheck / lint / build green

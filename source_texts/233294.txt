Summary
Adds myraytracer.core.splat.rasterize — an EWA/3DGS differentiable rasterizer (Kerbl et al., SIGGRAPH 2023): each anisotropic 3D Gaussian is projected to a screen-space footprint via the EWA/Jacobian affine approximation and alpha-composited strictly front-to-back. Ports the deprecated gpu/splat.py onto the unified array-API core (torch backend).
Adds myraytracer.core.optimize.fit_gaussians — the splatting analogue of fit_albedo (Port inverse rendering onto the unified core #60): all five Gaussian arrays become torch leaves and Adam minimizes MSE against a target image, with opacity/color clamped to [0,1] each step.
Exports Gaussians, rasterize, fit_gaussians from core.
Extends the differentiable-core proof from materials (fit_albedo, #60) to scene representation, and is the seed of the automatic inverse-rendering loop tracked on #9.
Test plan
tests/core/test_splat.py (7 tests): peak pixel = color*opacity, gradient flow to all five parameters, behind-camera culling, front-to-back occlusion, numpy-backend rejection, and a fit-reduces-loss loop.
Full suite: 263 passed.
ruff check / ruff format --check clean.
100% coverage on both new modules.
Follow-ups (not in this PR)
Tile-based rasterization + 3σ culling (replaces the dense (H·W, G) evaluation — the efficiency win that unblocks non-toy scenes).
Adaptive densification/pruning (the automatic scene-growing loop).
Spherical-harmonic color for view-dependent appearance.

Closes #26 · fixes KI#5 and KI#6
What
Real neighbours, re‑mesh on load (KI#5):
The mesher reads neighbour blocks from VoxelWorld (since Move the voxel world into an engine module (mge_voxel) #25).
loadChunk now also marks the chunk's loaded face neighbours dirty. A chunk meshed while its
neighbour was missing is re‑meshed, so no seams remain.
Trees span chunks (KI#6):
Each column's height and surface in SandboxTerrain is a pure function.
The decoration pass also replays trees from columns up to 2 blocks outside the chunk, in world
order, and each chunk writes its share.
Trees are intact across borders, and the world is identical whatever order chunks load in.
Generation now works in world Y (stone below 0).
Greedy meshing for colour‑only cubes, on by default:
merges maximal rectangles per direction and slice;
textured faces stay one quad each, since atlas tiles can't repeat until M3;
a flat 16×4×16 slab drops from 1 024 to 12 triangles.
Optional per‑vertex AO written into the vertex colour:
a diagonal flip avoids anisotropy;
greedy only merges faces with uniform AO, so shading is unchanged.
Gallery: a colour‑only voxel world (4×2×4 chunks, simplex fbm, water) using greedy + AO.
Writes voxel_terrain.obj and voxel_water.obj (the procedural path of mge_voxel).
Logs 13 218 triangles with greedy vs 28 662 without.
Verification
168/168 tests pass. New tests:
Seams: a chunk with all 26 neighbours, and a corner chunk, match an independent reference that counts visible faces with world.getBlock over the whole world (random stone/water/glass).
Re‑mesh after neighbour load: loading a neighbour dirties the chunk, and the new mesh drops exactly the shared wall.
Greedy vs naive: fewer triangles, with the same area per face direction (flat and rough terrain, opaque and transparent).
Greedy leaves textured faces alone: they are not merged.
AO: darkens the floor next to a wall and leaves open corners bright; greedy + AO keeps the area.
Trees: canopies are complete for trees near chunk borders, over a 7×2×7 world.
Load order: forward and reverse loading give identical chunks.
The sandbox runs offscreen, the gallery exits 0 with 22 meshes, and scripts/lint.sh is clean.

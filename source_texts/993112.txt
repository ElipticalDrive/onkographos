Problem
Benchmarks show that applying a terrain operation costs tens of microseconds, but rebuilding every dirty chunk's mesh costs about 1.3 ms per chunk and dominates the artillery and stress workloads by two to three orders of magnitude (Docs/benchmarking.md). Chunk already tracks a DirtyRegion AABB, but BuildChunkMesh always rebuilds the whole chunk and TerrainWorld::BuildMeshInput samples the full one-voxel border per voxel.
Goal
Make remesh cost scale with the changed region rather than the chunk size, without changing the mesh output.
Deliverables
A mesher path that rebuilds only faces inside (dirty region expanded by one voxel) and reuses the rest, or an equivalent design you justify in the PR.
Identical output to the full rebuild: add a test that compares face sets between incremental and full rebuild for randomized operation sequences.
Benchmark evidence on one machine: Tools/bench_compare.py output for isolated_crater, boundary_crater, artillery against main, same preset. The dirty_flush_ns series should drop clearly; apply_ns must not regress.
No change to any state hash.
Pointers
Source/OpenBattlefieldCore/Private/ObChunkMesher.cpp, ObTerrainWorld.cpp (BuildMeshInput), Benchmarks/Core/ObBench.cpp (FlushDirty).

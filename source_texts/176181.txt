The ability to explore gaussian splatting on a Quest is quite recent.
The app I used is called Gracia and you can find it here: https://sidequestvr.com/app/29546/gracia
In case you are not familiar with Sidequest and need more info on how to setup Gracia as well check out this guide here: https://graciavr.notion.site/How-to-sideload-Gracia-from-Sidequest-ee5fc68f6773457faa1330e918f7c21f
To capture Gaussian splatting you can use apps like Polycam or Luma AI and export the ply file
Before importing in Gracia I would suggest cleaning it up with Supersplat you can find here: https://playcanvas.com/supersplat/editor
If you like this make sure to check also this interview with Michael Rubloff from Radiancefields.com where we dive deeper into the topic of NeRFs and Gaussian splitting:
https://open.substack.com/pub/xraispotlight/p/tools-and-resources-to-learn-and?r=2umm8d&utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web

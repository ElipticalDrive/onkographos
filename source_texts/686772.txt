In order to exactly extract the geometric parameters from point cloud, a new algorithm of rotational surface extraction was presented.Principal directions of a rotational surface were mapped to Gaussian sphere in the algorithm,and principal direction Gaussian image(PDGI) was generated.The Gaussian sphere was uniformly partitioned into small 3D grids,and the image points on the Gaussian sphere were assigned to the corresponding 3D grids.A group of 3D feature grids was obtained by cluster analysis technique according to the image points in each grid,then a plane passing through a great circle was fitted to the image points enclosed in the obtained 3D feature grids.The direction of the rotational axis was determined by the normal of the fitted plane.The positional point of the rotational axis was obtained by the direction of the rotational axis and the normal lines of the rotational surface,and then the rotational axis was determined.An optimized method was applied to refine the rotational axis.Experiments show that the algorithm can extract the geometric parameters of the rotational surface accurately and reliably.

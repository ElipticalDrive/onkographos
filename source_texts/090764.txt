What is the best approach to take when generating meshes for chunks ...
Greedy meshing of cube voxel meshes is worth it because it is fast. If you are writing a smooth voxel contouring algorithm, mesh simplification is probably a harder choice to make, although it can still be worth it. Regenerating a mesh for a chunk is generally necessary.

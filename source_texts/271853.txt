What
A move and a rename: grid/ becomes a CMake package of its own, volumetric_kit_grid 0.1.0, on volumetric_kit_core alone, and recon builds on it. It is the step before grid/ is lifted into its own repository by git filter-repo --subdirectory-filter grid, so the directory is complete on its own: it configures, builds and tests with cmake -S grid, with nothing of recon's. No behaviour changes; the codec's golden frames pass unchanged.
What moved into grid/, with its tests, oracles, fixtures, shaders, examples, templates and docs:
the tiers volume, voxelize, mesh, eval, codec, codec_stream, stream and networking;
io's mesh files (ply_writer, triangle_mesh, textured_mesh, mesh_normalization, mesh_io), now the mesh_io tier; the image half stays in recon;
recon_core's split dispatch (dispatch.hpp, dispatch.cpp, dispatch_args.comp, dispatch_slices.glsl), now grid_base. Its header is public, because recon's tsdf records with it, and dispatch_slices.glsl is exported;
the examples codec_mesh and serve_stream, the examples' scaffolding and codec helpers (grid_example_common), and config/examples/codec_mesh*.json;
the test helpers recon's tests also use, as the opt-in grid_test_support: device_blocks, grid_readback, sphere_scene, codec_fixture, stage_rows, stream_files and networking_loopback.
Five commits, for review:
a pure git mv;
the package's CMake;
the rename;
recon's updates;
the docs.

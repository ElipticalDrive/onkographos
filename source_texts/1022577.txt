Summary
Adds SSR ray marching to water_ps.glsl: reflects visible scene geometry (terrain, objects) onto the water surface instead of relying solely on the sky zenith colour.
32 world-space steps × 4 m = 128 m max reach, projected through view_proj into NDC each step.
Edge-fade and distance-fade attenuate ssr_weight on hit; sky zenith is the graceful fallback for off-screen or occluded geometry.
No new uniforms, samplers, or C++ changes — all required data (view_proj, scene_color_tex, depth_tex) was already provided by the forward water pass (feat(renderer): forward water pass architecture #352).
Technical details
Guard: march only when R.y >= -0.05 and fresnel > 0.03, skipping head-on fragments.
Intersection: ray_depth > stored_depth && ray_depth - stored_depth < 0.02 (NDC depth threshold — may need tuning per scene scale).
Blend: sky_reflect = mix(sky_zenith, reflect_color, ssr_weight) → surface_col = mix(absorbed, sky_reflect, fresnel).
Test plan
Water surface shows reflections of terrain when camera is at a grazing angle.
Sky zenith colour appears where no scene geometry is reflected (off-screen, occluded).
No visible hard edge at screen borders (edge_fade smooths transition).
Reflections fade with distance (dist_fade).
Foam, refraction, and specular remain unaffected.
No GPU hang or visible stutter (32-step loop exits early on off-screen rays).

Abstract
The flowing particle screen is an emerging display technology that enables permeable, high-quality projected images floating in midair. The FogScreen is one such display, which is becoming widely used in many settings and can be extended to a pseudo-3D display. The authors also present some other unique volumetric or pseudo-volumetric display technologies for creating midair images.

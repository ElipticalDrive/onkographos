Adds a foundational SDF library with shape primitives and composition operators:

Primitives:
- sdf_sphere: unit sphere of radius r
- sdf_box_3d: axis-aligned box (Quilez exact, handles interior correctly)
- sdf_torus: ring in XZ plane (major/minor radii)
- sdf_capsule_3d: segment-swept sphere from a to b
- sdf_cone: infinite cone by half-angle (Quilez formulation)
- sdf_cone_finite: finite cone apex-to-base with base radius
- sdf_cylinder: infinite cylinder along Y axis
- sdf_cylinder_finite: finite capped cylinder from a to b

Operators:
- sdf_op_union / sdf_op_subtract / sdf_op_intersect: boolean CSG
- sdf_op_smooth_union: polynomial smooth-min blend (k-factor)
- sdf_op_round: inflate surface outward (round edges/corners)
- sdf_op_onion: hollow shell of thickness r
- sdf_op_repeat_3d: tile 3D space with period c per axis

22 tests, 1405 total passing.

The paper focuses on automatic simplification algorithms for the
 generation of a multiresolution family of solid models from an initial
 boundary representation of a polyhedral solid. An algorithm for
 general polyhedra based on an intermediate octree representation is
 proposed. Simplified elements of the multiresolution family
 approximate the initial solid within increasing tolerances. A
 discussion among different octree-based simplification methods and the
 standard marching cubes algorithm is finally presented.

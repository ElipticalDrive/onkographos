Requested by randbrown · project thread
Before: the post menu has ascii / mosh / edge / stitch / negative, and none of them produces the extruded-block look from the seg.cpu-style reference clips. Pressing Y toggled stitch and also cycled the theme.
After: a new voxel glitch post (in the post group, ⇧E) turns whatever is on stage into a field of lit 3D cubes. Each cube takes its colour from a grid cell, its height comes from that cell's brightness, and near-black cells drop out as void. A perspective camera drifts, tilts and orbits over the field, and beats kick the dolly in. It works over any quale, so camera and video quales get the look without changes of their own. The voxel card has a reset button that restores every tunable to its default. Y now toggles only stitch. Theme cycling moved to ⇧T, and the hint list and the all-keys sheet show it.
How: post-voxel.js renders offscreen in WebGL2. The frame is downsampled to a grid texture. A tiny ping-pong pass eases it so video noise doesn't strobe the towers. Then one instanced draw call renders a 30-vert open-bottom cube times cols·rows instances. Each instance reads its cell via gl_InstanceID + texelFetch, so there are no per-instance buffers. Shading is Lambert light with darkened face seams and depth fog into the void colour. The cube count is capped at 48k by growing the cell size, and the GL buffer is capped at 1600px wide. Without WebGL2 it falls back to a flat block mosaic. It is wired like stitch: off/on/blip/flip modes, a voxel card with 11 sliders and a reset, settings + qualem persistence, qualia.voxel({...}), qglitch("voxel", …), and docs. VOXEL_DEFAULTS in post-voxel.js is the single source for the overlay's live config and for the reset. The theme hotkey moved out of the inline qualia.astro listener into page-init's hotkey switch.
Verified:
npm run check and npm run build pass.
I drove the built /qualia page headlessly in Chromium (SwiftShader GL) over galaxy, fractal and the fake-webcam camera quale. Cubes render with correct culling, lighting and camera, and there were no page errors.
Reset puts every value back to its default, and the sliders and labels follow.
Y toggles stitch and leaves the theme alone. ⇧T moves to the next theme and leaves ascii alone.
FPS under software GL is meaningless, so a check on a real GPU is still needed.

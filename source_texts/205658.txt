Current ray-triangle intersection algorithm (by Möller & Trumbore) is not watertight. This leads to lines on the triangle meshes. To fix this we need to implement Woop’s algorithm ( https://jcgt.org/published/0002/01/05/paper.pdf , https://pbr-book.org/3ed-2018/Shapes/Triangle_Meshes#TriangleIntersection ).

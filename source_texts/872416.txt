These commits were pushed to the #2 branch after #2 was merged, so they are not on main yet.
Exact travel-time gradient (81bce9e): the lookup returns the bilinear/trilinear interpolation of the table and its exact derivative. Outside the grid, the edge cell's form continues.
The gradient tables are gone: tables take a third of the memory in 2-D and a quarter in 3-D.
gradcheck passes (it failed before, about 2% off near stations), and the gradient matches float64 finite differences to 2e-9 s/km.
Locations on the synthetic tests are unchanged.
sacloc3d works with RANSAC (3deefac): picks are [station_index, phase_type, phase_weight], the weight enters the loss, and predict returns (n, 1). With 20% outlier picks, the median 3-D error is 0.41 km against 1.65 km for a plain fit.
app.py (e3e975a): the config is copied per request, so client settings no longer leak between requests, and the eikonal tables are cached.
docs/run_adloc_bootstrap.py runs again (a0a15c9). Seven bugs fixed: the import, single-process mode, the batch keys, the velocity keys, one projection for limits and stations, the plotting suffix, and the pandas 3 jackknife.
Script fixes:
run_adloc_ddp.py keeps its loaded config (195aa32);
run_ransac.py uses x/y limits as a local range around each event's stations (195aa32, 3ed6309).
One synthetic_picks.py (be26dea), with a flag for random station terms.
The two scripts in the fifth item need regional data and were checked by reading the code. Everything else was run.

… gradient tables

The gradient was interpolated from separate gradient tables, so it was not the derivative of
the returned time (torch gradcheck failed, about 2% off at r = 4 km with h = 1 km), which can
stall the strong-Wolfe line searches of LBFGS and L-BFGS-B.

The lookup now returns the bilinear (2-D) or trilinear (3-D) interpolation of the table and
its exact derivative. Outside the grid the edge cell's form continues (the cell index stops,
the fraction does not), so the time stays continuous and the gradient is still its exact
derivative, never zero. The gradient tables are gone: tables take a third (2-D) or a quarter
(3-D) of the memory, and the 3-D lookup no longer allocates per pick.

gradcheck passes; the gradient matches float64 finite differences to 2e-9 s/km; locations on
the synthetic tests are unchanged (BFGS, torch, hypodd, sacloc3d, RANSAC).

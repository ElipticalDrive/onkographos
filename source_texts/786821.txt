What it gives
The wireframe needed no engine code. Box3D gives a sensor a broadphase proxy like any
other shape, so b3World_Draw already reaches it, and DrawQueryCallback colors it wheat
rather than the solid color. So a trigger draws, and it draws apart from a collider.
Neither half is documented. A submodule bump could drop either one and nothing would say so,
so tests/test_physics.cpp pins both and DESIGN.md §5 records them.
That section was four findings and is now seven. Two of them are #277 findings that reached
the pull request body and never reached the document.
The scene gains a goal volume. A trigger nothing crosses is a trigger nobody tests. This
one sits under the M7.4 falling crate, so the sandbox reports an overlap on its own with
nobody pressing a key. M8.6 needs a goal volume anyway.
Verify
Three measurements, and each is a difference rather than an assertion:
With --physics-debug off the picture is byte for byte what it was before the trigger
existed. 0 pixels differ over 1280x720. So the trigger pushes nothing and draws nothing
it should not.
With it on, exactly 1034 pixels differ, and every one of them is wheat. Nothing else in
the frame moved.
Making the test trigger solid fails one check and only that one: and a trigger is never the color a solid shape draws in.
ctest is 28 of 28, docs and the Box3D containment check pass, clang-tidy 19 reports nothing
on the changed files, and synchronization validation reports nothing over 300 offscreen frames
with the wireframe drawing.
One finding, filed rather than fixed

works around it and points at #280.

(PDF) Multimodal volume rendering with 3D textures | Academia
This paper presents a novel volume rendering application for multimodal datasets using 3D texture mapping. Users can define a 2D fusion transfer function to modulate data merging between modalities. The system achieves frame rates of 7-10 fps on NVIDIA GeForce 7900 GTX and 17-28 fps on GeForce 8800 GTX.

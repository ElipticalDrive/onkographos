Resolution
Recorded in ADR 0002.
Storage unit: sparse 8³ bricks, 0.8 m at 10 cm. Each holds a 512-bit occupancy mask (64 B) plus palette-compressed 16-bit Material IDs with 1, 2, 4 or 8-bit indices. Uniform bricks (all air, all one Material) are a flag only.
Index: a sparse hash map keyed by brick coordinate on the CPU, in int32 Planet-frame coordinates. A shallow tree over bricks on the GPU, for ray traversal.
Lifecycle: unedited bricks are a regenerable cache that can be evicted. Bricks touched by Edits are persisted as full-resolution snapshots. The Edit wire format belongs to "Choose network architecture".
Look: no per-Voxel colour. Colour and roughness variation within a Material is computed procedurally from the Voxel's position.
LOD: all-voxel to orbit. The same brick format at each mip level, in camera-centred rings, each twice as coarse as the one inside it. About 19–20 levels from 10 cm out to the Planet-disc Zoom, with no heightfield or mesh fallback.
Downsampling: a coarse Voxel is solid if at least 4 of its 8 children are, and takes the most common solid child Material. Edits propagate up all levels immediately, so craters are visible from orbit.
Debris and Fluids keep their own small grids and convert to and from bricks.
GPU budget: about 2 GB of voxel data on the 4090. The research estimates 0.2–0.7 GB is needed.
Deferred: a sparse voxel DAG for far levels or disk, only if measured memory requires it.

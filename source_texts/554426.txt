I think I see what you're after now - the signed flood fill agorithms fill inside/outside voxels with constant values and do not modify active states. They are indeed a flood fill algorithm, but it sounds like what you want to do is activate the interior of your level set with exact distances? These algorithms do not re-compute the distance values.
There are a variety of ways to re-construct the interior of a level set, I think the easiest is perhaps just to use openvdb::tools::levelSetRebuild(). This will convert your level set to polygons and reconstruct the interior up to the specified distance. If you want to completely fill the interior with distances you can simply use a large value:
```
ls = levelSetRebuild(*ls, 0, /*ext=*/halfWidth, /*interior=*/std::numeric_limits<float>::max()); // LevelSetRebuild.h
```
Alternatively there are tools in FastSweeping.h which can be used to re-compute interior values and avoid level set reconstruction. @kmuseth / @apradhana can probably elaborate on those.

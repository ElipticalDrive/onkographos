Recent advancements in GPU computational power have enabled ray tracing algorithms, traditionally used for offline rendering, to enter the realm of real-time rendering. Path tracing, a widely adopted global illumination technique in ray tracing pipelines, suffers from image noise in scenes requiring multiple light bounces. Although filtering techniques alleviate noise, results often remain suboptimal. Bidirectional path tracing addresses sampling inefficiencies but introduces challenges such as computational demands and memory usage, limiting its real-time applicability. To enhance rendering quality in complex scenes, this paper introduces Triangle Grid Resampling Bidirectional Path Tracing (TGRBDPT). Leveraging triangle mesh resampling, TGRBDPT improves upon traditional methods under equivalent sampling conditions. Experimental comparisons across various scenes demonstrate TGRBDPT's superiority over path tracing and bidirectional path tracing in terms of image quality and computational efficiency, Increasing application scenarios for bidirectional path tracing algorithms.

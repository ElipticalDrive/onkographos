This PR attempts to implement many of the Projection libraries and tries to update their dependencies where needed and from @aboood40091's repo.
Goals
Implement all of these, as seen from BotW:
Projection libraries, including what appear to be the Perspective, Ortho, Frustum, and Direct projections
Camera functions, including the LookAt, Direct, and Ortho cameras
Viewport's functions, including a guard on the mDepthRange since SMO doesn't seem to have that member variable.
Include the Graphics and DrawContext library updates that @aboood40091 had worked on.
Note, this also brings in a vectorized Matrix44f::copy function.
Addresses
0xB1D898 - 0xB1F2B0 are the Projection functions in BotW.
0xB1FFF0 - 0xB20718 are the sead::Viewport functions
0xB1B9F0 - 0xB1C680 are the Camera functions
Non-Matching / Other Functions
In the prior PR, the static_assert in seadGraphicsNvn.h had been commented out because it was failing while editing. I'm not entirely sure if that's related to the work here.
Next Steps
I'm looking for feedback. Been iterating on the SMO side with german77.
Thank you to aboood40091, MonsterDruide1, and german77 for all the commentary, help, and original code that I pulled into this branch!

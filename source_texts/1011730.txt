Summary
Introduces SaberEffect, a WebGL effect that renders an electric energy glow around an element's silhouette, inspired by Video Copilot's After Effects Saber plug-in.
It builds a cached distance field from the element's silhouette with the Jump Flooding Algorithm (JFA), then every frame overlays several glowing lines, each warped with animated 3D simplex noise and lit with a reciprocal-distance falloff.
Key Changes
packages/effects/src/saber.ts — SaberEffect (Effect interface, types-only imports from @vfx-js/core):
Distance field (JFA), built once and cached — rebuilt only on buffer resize, edgeThreshold change, or invalidate():
Seed pass: detects the silhouette edge on grayscale luminance (cutoff = edgeThreshold) and writes JFA seeds
JFA flood passes: propagate nearest-seed coordinates
Resolve pass: convert seeds to aspect-corrected distances
Per-frame render: overlays up to 4 lines (layers, each with its own thickness + noiseScale + weight), warps the lookup with animated 3D simplex noise (z = time), and lights it with k / distance. softness shapes the falloff, core controls the white-hot center. Premultiplied output.
dynamic rebuilds the field every frame for live sources (video / webcam); static images/text keep the cheap cached path.
Params: color, intensity, amplitude, frequency, speed, softness, core, edgeThreshold, layers, dynamic, pad.
packages/effects/src/_noise.ts — shared Ashima/Gustavson simplex noise (SNOISE3D).
packages/storybook/src/Effect.stories.ts — saber story with interactive controls; sources: Logo / Jellyfish / Text / Webcam (webcam runs with dynamic rebuilds).
packages/effects/src/index.ts — exports SaberEffect, SaberParams, SaberLayer.
Scope / Follow-up work
To keep this PR focused on the Saber effect, the following are intentionally deferred to a separate PR:
Effect.ready (awaitable SDF build) — the JFA can only run in render() (draws are ignored during init), so await vfx.add(...) does not guarantee the field exists yet. A general readiness primitive (prepare() hook + vfx.prepare(element)) is planned, also benefiting ParticleEffect (state seeding) and JPEGGlitchEffect (async decode). A prototype SaberEffect.ready was explored here and reverted.
VFXProps.fallback (React loading UX) — a fallback prop on VFXImg/VFXVideo (and/or a useVFXReady hook) that shows a placeholder until the effect is warmed up, driven by the readiness primitive above. (True React Suspense for effect-readiness would require an offscreen/headless prepare path and is out of scope.)
Note: an alternative Saber2 approach (edge detection + BloomEffect) was explored on this branch and removed from the final tree; its commits remain in branch history.

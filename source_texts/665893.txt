Parent: #253. Shared visible-lighting slice.
Implementation order
Immediate prerequisite: #270 for source identity, geometry, strength and visibility policy. Can proceed alongside the plant foundation; no plant species or Clock plant event is required.
Outcome
Nearby scenery responds to lights: moving a source moves its illumination, and an intervening solid creates a shadow. Preserve the dim-room appearance, legible emitting digits and readable UI. Existing glow can remain as an appearance treatment, but halos alone do not satisfy this issue.
Scope
Extend the shared render contract/adapters to consume the authoritative lighting snapshot and world geometry. Apply illumination to scenery/actors/material appearance, not solely to the empty background. Keep emitting surfaces visibly luminous and text/HUD/settings legible.
Render directional and finite-range local contributions with the same source positions, strengths and opaque-visibility rules as Lighting: shared sources, opaque visibility and bounded exposure #270. Use simple hard visibility with optional modest display smoothing; physically soft shadows, reflections and refraction are not requirements.
Keep the room's low background level and current aesthetic. Any minimum display brightness or glow is explicitly presentation-only and cannot enter the plant resource ledger. Avoid double-brightening by applying old decorative light and new illumination twice to the same material.
Support the current production GPU path and the raster/vector/headless presentation paths used by Clock. A bounded lower-resolution implementation is acceptable where needed, but source placement, which side of an object is lit, and basic shadows must remain recognizable. Preserve the existing explicit software recovery path and saved renderer choices; do not silently select a new backend.
Bound light-map/mesh/texture/cache work and memory, share immutable inputs across views, and release owned resources on scene replacement/resize. Rendering cannot step physics or mutate plant exposure. Scenes that supply no lighting contract retain their existing output, so this issue does not roll the feature out to every game.
Add repeatable visual fixtures for a lit material, a moving opaque blocker, an emitting surface, multiple local lights and no-light/directional-only cases. Document render resolution/quality limits and exact backend coverage before enabling the Clock integration.
Acceptance
The same fixture visibly brightens near the source and darkens behind the blocker; moving either changes the image coherently. Compare the rendered shadow with exposure probes outside the documented sampling-edge tolerance.
Foreground bodies and plants are eligible receivers/blockers through declared geometry; UI labels and decorative halos are not accidental occluders. Source color changes the visible tint without changing plant exposure solely because of display settings.
GPU, raster/vector and headless captures agree on source/occluder placement and basic lit/shaded regions. Software recovery is usable and bounded; pixel-identical antialiasing is not required.
Repeated resize, backend selection, pause and scene entry/exit have no retained light buffers or unbounded work. Record costs on representative fixtures; integrated device acceptance belongs to Lighting: qualify Clock visuals, plant exposure and Pi performance #274.
Status: ready for implementation after #270. No rendering implementation or performance result is claimed by this ticket.

Multiple scattering is still very noisy when light sources are located inside participating media. When distance sample or in-scattered direction is chosen, we can use better strategies:
For distance samples: equiangular sampling, as we do here: Better importance sampling for volumes #1591
For direction sampling: combine phase function sampling and sampling based on proximity to light sources (For instance: sample point P near light sources with pdf ~ attenuation and choose in-scattered direction as a direction towards P)

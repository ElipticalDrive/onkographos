The hierarchical volume decoders mark unevaluated grid cells NaN, so marching cubes interpolates NaN coordinates for vertices on the evaluated/skipped boundary. trimesh's process step removes those vertices but silently remaps every face referencing them to vertex 0, fanning garbage triangles across the mesh (~44% of faces on a measured T-pose run; the long ones render as threads stretched across the character). Filter those faces out before construction so vertex removal never leaves dangling references.

This PR targets octree_indexing (#103). It addresses physical extent openings separately from missing refinement support and geological extraction masks.
Why
An isosurface intersecting the model box is normally left open by dual contouring. Optional extent caps make supported surfaces usable as closed solids while reporting when closure is unsafe or incomplete instead of silently claiming success.
Changes
Add MeshExtentCapping.SCALAR_LESS_EQUAL, with scalar <= isovalue defining the inside of each independently exported surface.
Join interior QEF geometry to the box-face contour with transition triangles and append outward-facing caps on the six extent faces.
Share a unique finest-level boundary lattice across surfaces, reuse scalar samples per stack, and batch boundary evaluation while restoring temporary grid and stack state even on failure.
Preserve the requested RegularGrid.orthogonal_extent across octree levels. Fix QEF mass-point handling so genuine zero coordinates are not treated as invalid intersections.
Use strict scalar-crossing rules for enabled extraction/capping, including exact ties and nonfinite-input diagnostics.
Expose surface ownership/isovalue metadata and capping_report, including closure status, open edges, winding/manifoldness checks, missing or escaped QEF vertices, cap-plane error, and added geometry counts.
Separate API orchestration from geometry construction and document the best-effort contract in docs/extent_capping.md.
Behavior and Limits
Capping defaults to none. Fault stacks, fault-affected stacks, and stacks with removed extraction-mask cells skip capping and report the reason. Missing support or escaped QEF vertices are not repaired by extending caps into the interior; unsupported cases warn and return available geometry.
Check mesh.capping_report["closure_success"] before treating a result as a closed solid. Caps close existing intersecting surfaces: they do not manufacture a disconnected box shell or partition nested surfaces into geological unit volumes. Topology audits do not prove the absence of self-intersections. Cap postprocessing is NumPy-based and non-differentiable; final triangle indices reference mesh.vertices, not the original vertices_tensor snapshot.
Exact extent sampling and zero-coordinate fixes also apply with capping disabled, so legacy mesh-array identity is not guaranteed.
Test Coverage
Adds geometry and integration regressions in test_extent_capping.py and test_extent_capping_integration.py, plus test_grid_zero_handling.py and test_scalar_crossing.py for extent, QEF, and crossing edge cases. Large-depth GPU and production-scale memory benchmarks remain outstanding.

## Stack Context

This four-PR stack improves control over octree surface extraction and adds local, authored scalar-field corrections. Review and merge from the bottom up:

1. **#100: Refinement support and diagnostics** (this PR).
2. #103: Optional corner deduplication and unique-edge quad triangulation.
3. #102: Best-effort closure at the model extent.
4. #104: Stack-local anisotropic micro-point corrections.

This PR targets `main` and provides the integer-coordinate and support-diagnostic foundation used by the later mesh changes.

## Why

Sparse refinement can omit neighboring cells needed to extract complete dual-contouring faces. Users need an explicit tradeoff between refinement cost and neighborhood coverage, plus diagnostics that distinguish missing refinement support from physical or geological boundaries.

## Changes

- Add `OctreeRefinementMode` with `fast` (existing selection), `balanced` (six face neighbors), and `precise` (all 26 touching neighbors). Apply one support dilation per surface-refinement transition when mesh extraction is enabled.
- Track signed int64 cell coordinates and theoretical domain bounds instead of byte-packed coordinates and bounds inferred from active cells; handle empty triangulation lookups and guard coordinate-key overflow.
- Expose `refinement_debug` masks and counts, including support-only parents and missing in-domain neighbors.
- Attach mesh `support_report` diagnostics that classify missing cells as physical-boundary, extraction-mask, or internal-refinement gaps, and warn about unresolved internal support.
- Document configuration, cost multipliers, and the limits of support closure in `docs/octree_refinement.md`.

## Behavior and Limits

`fast` remains the default. The coordinate/bounds fixes can change meshes affected by the previous indexing defects even in fast mode. `balanced` is a support stencil, not 2:1 octree balancing; neither non-default mode guarantees watertightness, discovers unsampled features, or restores branches absent from an earlier sparse generation. Extent capping is introduced separately in #102.

## Test Coverage

Adds neighbor-closure regressions covering stencil selection, sparse/domain boundaries, refinement behavior, and diagnostics in `test_neighbor_closure.py`. The documentation includes lookup-only cost measurements, not end-to-end performance claims; representative curved, faulted, multi-stack, and GPU benchmarks remain outstanding.

## Stack Context

This four-PR stack improves control over octree surface extraction and adds local, authored scalar-field corrections. Review and merge from the bottom up:

1. #100: Refinement support and diagnostics.
2. **#103: Optional corner deduplication and unique-edge quad triangulation** (this PR).
3. #102: Best-effort closure at the model extent.
4. #104: Stack-local anisotropic micro-point corrections.

This PR targets `octree_refinement` (#100) and uses its signed integer lattice coordinates. It adds two independent opt-in paths without changing the default evaluation or triangulation selectors.

## Why

Neighboring octree cells store repeated corner samples, increasing interpolation work as refinement support grows. Mesh connectivity also benefits from identifying each crossing primal edge once and explicitly reporting incomplete cell neighborhoods.

## Changes

- Add `deduplicate_octree_corners`: evaluate each eligible lattice corner once, then gather scalar and gradient results back to the original row layout before downstream processing.
- Preserve normal and flat-stack evaluation, including per-stack compression within fused PyKeOps calls. Keep non-corner queries, appended surface points, and external callbacks on their existing layouts.
- Fall back to full corner evaluation for incompatible layouts, unequal duplicate fault values, or differentiable corner/fault rows where merging would change autograd semantics.
- Add `TriangulationMethod.QUADS`: deduplicate crossing primal edges, find their four incident cells, and split each complete quad deterministically into two gradient-oriented triangles.
- Report incomplete neighborhoods through `triangulation_report` and reject inconsistent shared-edge crossing flags. Incomplete quads are skipped rather than emitted as partial triangles.
- Correct garbage-collection placement so chunked evaluation collects every five chunks and after the final chunk.
- Document both selectors and their independent behavior in `docs/octree_refinement.md`. The intermediate `triangulation_sort_once` option is removed from the final implementation.

## Behavior and Limits

Corner deduplication defaults to `False`; triangulation defaults to `LEGACY`. Deduplication preserves the stored corner layout and numerical output parity, not necessarily bitwise identity. Quad connectivity is same-level only: it does not stitch coarse/fine transitions, cap physical boundaries, or guarantee watertightness. Unique/gather overhead can outweigh savings on small grids.

## Test Coverage

Adds regression coverage in `test_octree_optimizations.py`, `test_fused_corner_evaluation.py`, and `test_quad_triangulation.py` for deduplicated evaluation, fused-stack handling, connectivity, and diagnostics. NumPy and CPU Torch parity are covered; GPU behavior and end-to-end performance still need validation.

## Stack Context

This four-PR stack improves control over octree surface extraction and adds local, authored scalar-field corrections. Review and merge from the bottom up:

1. #100: Refinement support and diagnostics.
2. #103: Optional corner deduplication and unique-edge quad triangulation.
3. #102: Best-effort closure at the model extent.
4. **#104: Stack-local anisotropic micro-point corrections** (this PR).

This PR targets `borders` (#102) and integrates authored local contacts into scalar-field evaluation, segmentation, refinement, and mesh extraction without adding them to the macro cokriging system.

## Why

Local geological edits should not require modifying the global macro interpolation constraints. Authored micro contacts provide a separate anisotropic correction whose support, strength, and surface ownership are explicit, while preserving macro contacts by default.

## Changes

- Add `MicroPoints` to `InterpolationInput`, carrying contact coordinates, per-contact anisotropy matrices, nuggets, and global surface indices. Validate shapes, finite values, ownership, and supported stack types before interpolation.
- Prepare shared contact queries once and rebase ownership for stack-local subsets without mutating authored arrays.
- Fit a separate `MicroCorrection` per enabled stack and octree level from macro-field residuals. Keep fitted state out of inputs/options and optionally constrain macro surface points to zero correction.
- Evaluate correction values and optional gradients together and apply them after macro evaluation, before downstream segmentation and mesh processing.
- Maintain a consistent grid/macro/contact query layout across stacks and upstream ordinary or finite-fault evaluation. Contacts on fault-affected stratigraphic stacks fit against the already-faulted macro field without changing fault reference frames.
- Support NumPy and PyTorch dense fits, backend/device conversion, and autograd through contact coordinates, metrics, and nuggets. Preserve fused macro scalar evaluation where supported.
- Move prototype solve helpers into a test-only reference module and document authored contacts, configuration, and stack/backend constraints in `docs/micro_points.md`.

## Behavior and Limits

Micro correction is opt-in through `options.micro_options.enabled`; disabled authored contacts are ignored with a warning. Configure the exponential or Matérn kernel, range, nugget, strength, and `preserve_macro_points` independently of the macro solve.

Enabled contacts on fault stacks or external-function stacks are unsupported. Faults do not displace, split, or mask the micro kernel itself. Micro fitting uses a dense solve rather than PyKeOps; nearest-metric assignment for preserved macro points is discrete, and finite-fault projection is not end-to-end differentiable. Singular fits warn and use a least-squares fallback.

## Test Coverage

Adds public-API integration coverage in `test_micro_points_integration.py` for authored contacts, transforms, ownership/early validation, backend conversion and gradients, fault interactions, and multi-level mesh behavior. Updates evaluator/reference regressions and concurrent-options tests to check stack-local fitted state and avoid coupling the macro evaluator to correction-specific test hooks.

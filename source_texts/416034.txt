Why. Server-side rendering (#70) accepts cubes only (backend/app/routers/render.py refuses other kinds). A catalogue or VBT stored on a cluster has to be sent to the desktop to be seen, even when it is larger than the desktop's memory or GPU.
VBT volumes. A VBT can also hold a volume (a regular grid, kind: volume). Today /v1/vbt/subset sends the whole field in one reply, float32 in base64 inside JSON, whatever max_rows says: for a 512³ field (volumesnap63.bin, 512 MB) that is about 683 MB on the wire, 178 s through an SSH tunnel to PLEIADI-GPU. A VBT volume is a cube, so it should take the cube path: brick pyramid (#69), out-of-core rendering on the cluster (#70), and the decimated preview on the desktop.
Scope
A remote 3-D view for point data (catalogues, VBT) in the render session: the same push stream, drag level and Auto / Local / Remote choice as cubes.
Points from the octree of Out-of-core octree for catalogues and VBT #32 within a GPU budget, refined when the view stops, so sets larger than the GPU can be shown.
The desktop's controls (point size, colour by column, LUT, selection, filters) drive the remote view as they drive the local one.
With the desktop's local backend nothing changes: points are drawn on the desktop.
VBT volumes: built into a brick pyramid and rendered like cubes, local and remote; the desktop no longer receives the whole field.
Done when
A catalogue and a VBT larger than one GPU's memory are shown remotely from the desktop, through a tunnel, at an interactive frame rate measured with benchmark/remote_render/.
Small catalogues open as today, locally and remotely.
A VBT volume of 512³ or larger opens remotely without sending the whole field, and is rendered on the cluster.
Notes. Depends on #32. Part of #73.

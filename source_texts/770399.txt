Follow-up of the #1349 review (brute force checks for other objects and operations). Stacked on #1349: merge it first.
What
Three new tests compare every geometry distance, intersection and position function with an independent reference, on random generic and nearly degenerate configurations (cap, needle and flat triangles, nearly flat tetrahedra of both orientations, small angles, coplanar/collinear objects, radii around GLOBAL_EPSILON, elongated ellipses):
Test
Reference
test-distance-brute-force
Minimum over samples of the objects. The distance must lie between the brute force minimum minus the sampling resolution and the minimum, and the closest points must be on the objects.
test-intersection-brute-force
The (brute force checked) distances: separated objects must not intersect, touching ones must intersect (or be parallel when coplanar/collinear), points must be on both objects, and the number of points must match the boundary crossings counted along samples. Exact detections must not detect separated objects.
test-position-brute-force
Exact constructions: integer vertices and exact barycentric combinations (power of two denominator), so the expected position is known by construction; sides are checked against exact __int128 orientations.
Failures are collected and reported with their exact hexadecimal inputs. The random generators are shared in tests/geometry/random-geometry.hpp. The randomized tests of test-distance.cpp moved to the brute force test; the existing test files keep the deterministic cases.
Bugs found and fixed (one commit each, with a deterministic regression test failing before)
Distance
point_triangle_distance 2D: null distance for a point aligned with a flat triangle, far from it.
segment_line_distance: up to 1e-3 error for small angle crossings (cancellation in a*c - b*b), now computed like segment_segment_distance.
line_triangle_distance: null distance with a point 0.04 away from nearly flat triangles (ill-conditioned barycentric coordinates); crossing now detected with exact predicates.
segment_triangle_distance: threw for segments shorter than GLOBAL_EPSILON.
point_sphere_distance: returned the radius for points within GLOBAL_EPSILON of the center.
point_ellipse_distance: dimensionless |level - 1| < GLOBAL_EPSILON test, null distance for points up to emax * GLOBAL_EPSILON / 2 beyond the tips of long axes.
point_tetrahedron_distance: facets selected from barycentric coordinates skipped the facet containing the point for nearly flat tetrahedra (0.27 instead of 0).
Doc of point_circle_signed_distance (sign is the side of the circle plane).
Intersection
line_sphere_intersection / segment_sphere_intersection: discriminant (a squared length) compared with GLOBAL_EPSILON, so every line was tangent to spheres of radius below 1e-3; incorrect results without points; throw for short segments.
line_triangle_intersection: intersect results with points up to 55 away from the triangle for nearly parallel lines (tolerance on unnormalized quantities, correctness check that could not fail); segment_triangle_intersection returned incorrect in the same cases.
2D segment_segment_intersection / segment_line_intersection: none for collinear overlapping segments and small angle crossings.
plane_plane_intersection: lines up to 1e-4 off the planes for angles below GLOBAL_EPSILON; plane_circle_intersection built on it, exact tangency only, correctness of the last point only.
Position
point_tetrahedron_position: every point outside for negatively oriented tetrahedra, and outside points inside for nearly flat tetrahedra (approximated facet normals). Now exact predicates only, with the exact orientation.
Behavior changes to review
Intersections keep the GLOBAL_EPSILON tolerance (objects closer than GLOBAL_EPSILON intersect, as required by the fix(Geometry): tier 1 robustness fixes #1349 touching endpoint fix), but it is now applied to distances everywhere. Exact predicates are not used to decide intersections, to keep that tolerance.
parallel means parallel directions (up to GLOBAL_EPSILON over the object extent) or coplanar within GLOBAL_EPSILON, as before for exactly parallel objects. Planes are parallel when the sine of their angle is below GLOBAL_EPSILON (as line_plane_intersection).
point_tetrahedron_position no longer depends on the tetrahedron orientation.
Downstream users of the changed intersections (Geode-Background inserters, Geode-Common cutters, Geode-Simplex frontal 2D) have not been run against this branch yet.
Not covered
triangle_triangle_distance_between_non_conformal_parts has no precise definition beyond its implementation: only invariants are checked (closest points on triangles, distance not below the triangle distance and equal to it without common vertex, null for constructed overlapping configurations).

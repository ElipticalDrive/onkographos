Let users move a cross-section plane through the displayed surface and see its intersection curve.
Acceptance: plane position changes the displayed intersection; tests cover known intersections and empty/degenerate cases. Plane orientations, intersection algorithm, and controls remain undecided. Share slice infrastructure with partial-derivative exploration rather than duplicating it.
Implementation is proposed in the pull request below for review.
Related derivative slices: #23. Share slicing implementation.
Implementation design for review
X/Y/Z fixed-coordinate planes within applied bounds. Shared marching-triangle scalar-field kernel evaluates the equation directly on each plane and supports future derivative slices. Cached by equation, applied sampling, axis and position; at most 6561 evaluations. Empty/undefined/all-zero sampled fields have explicit explanations. No depth occlusion; controls transient.
PR: #42 (unmerged; awaits user review).

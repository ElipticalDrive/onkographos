Abstract
In recent years, image reconstruction based on graph theory has aroused growing interest. Here a novel MRS framework was introduced. It works to reconstruct the brain image based on shape analysis. The shape was recognized from the input image. Its description and count were collected in a hypergraph structure for the reconstruction process. The MRS framework reconstructs an image based on the identified shapes. The reconstructed images help to locate the missing data and to identify the disorder in the brain.

Talked about this a bit on IRC tonight with @immortius and @Double-A-92 and I wanted to put a reminder in for later - not a very critical need :-)
Once upon a time we had partially transparent leaf blocks (based on alpha blend / translucency), which became a performance issue (render ordering issue) and was disabled. A different strategy exists and is used in MC to allow leaf blocks with "holes" in them
This could help lighten up the trees a bit again rather than being opaque, and might also help in other places, like allowing "swimming" (climbing) in trees while still having sensible viewing options (rather than totally opaque or not rendered at all)

Closes #460. Draft, stacked on piece/G146, built ahead of Gate 3 so CI can run it now.
What changed. geom_num::distance: exact comparisons of a squared gap against a clearance, in 256 bits with the budgets written in the header: point to point, line and plane, line to line, and the difference of two products of dots. geom-mesh::contact: the Devillers-Guigue triangle test, Apart, Touch or Cross, where Cross means every sign was strict; closest feature of a triangle, and a point's distance to it against a clearance.
How it was checked. Contact against an edge-by-edge oracle on 60,000 lattice cases, in every order and turn. Closest features and comparisons against exact fractions, and at the frame's edge. Kani proves the sign of each comparison on small coordinates. On a development machine, 18.7M triangle pairs a second against the done-when's 5M. Count benchmarks in geom-bench.
Sources. Devillers and Guigue, INRIA RR-4488, from the paper, with the interval comparisons re-derived. Ericson's Voronoi regions, Real-Time Collision Detection, section 5.1.5.

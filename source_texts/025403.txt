We present a meshing and rendering framework for tetrahedral meshes that constructs a multi-resolution representation and uses\nthis representation to adapt the mesh to rendering parameters. The mesh is partitioned into several segments which are simplified\nindependently. A multi-resolution representation is constructed by merging simplified segments and again simplifying the\nmerged segments. We end up with a (binary) hierarchy of segments whose parent nodes are the simplified versions of their\nchildren nodes. We show how the segments of arbitrary levels can be connected efficiently such that the mesh can be adapted\nfast to rendering parameters at run time. This hierarchy is stored on disc and segments are swapped into the main memory as\nneeded. Our algorithm ensures that the adapted mesh can always be treated like a not-segmented mesh from outside and thus\ncan be used by any renderer. We demonstrate a segmentation technnique that is based on an octree although the multi-resolution\nrepresentation itself does not rely on any paticular segmentation technique.

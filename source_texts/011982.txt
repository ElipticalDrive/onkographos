Description
Replaces the brute-force Mesh.prototype.raycast() triangle loop in DepthMesh.raycast() (~1.18 ms/frame on CPU across 3,042 triangles of downsampledMesh × 2 controller rays = 6,084 triangle intersection checks per frame) with a 2D projective grid walk over the depth heightfield in src/depth/DepthMeshRaycast.ts:
Per-geometry grid metadata in DepthGeometryUpdater:
Tracks segments, isRectilinear, minVertexDepth, maxVertexDepth, and hasDepthData per geometry when updateGeometryPositions() runs, and exposes getGeometryMetadata().
2D projective grid traversal in DepthMeshRaycaster (src/depth/DepthMeshRaycast.ts):
Because every vertex of DepthMesh lies along its perspective unprojection ray from the camera origin (0, 0, 0), any 3D triangle in grid cell (ix, iy) projects under central projection (x / -z, y / -z) strictly inside that 2D cell [ix, ix + 1] × [iy, iy + 1], and any 3D ray segment with -z > 0 projects to a straight 2D line segment in grid space.
Clips the local-space ray against the 6 planes of the truncated depth frustum (zMin, zMax, rxMin, rxMax, ryMin, ryMax), projects the entry and exit endpoints into (c, r) grid coordinates, and visits only the ~20–40 grid cells crossed by that 2D segment instead of all 1,521 cells.
Reads vertex positions directly from the flat Float32Array buffer into reusable scratch THREE.Vector3s instead of calling Mesh.getVertexPosition() 18,252 times per frame.
Falls back to standard Mesh.prototype.raycast() when depth data has not yet been populated or if a non-rectilinear projection is used.

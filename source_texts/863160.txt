PR-D of the depth-sorting → volumetric arc (TODO #24; follows #630/#641/#644). Implements VOLUMETRIC_BLENDING_SPEC.md Phase 3: points render the real emission–absorption math, gain RGBA per-point opacity, and the mandelbulb demo becomes the showcase.
Volumetric points (both shader backends)
Isotropic chord-integral rayMass: the point profile exp(−K·ρ^β) (K = ln 100) truncates at T = √(2K) ≈ 3.03σ — nearly the gsplat T = 3 — so rayMass = falloff · R·√(π/K) (POINT_CHORD_SCALE, new materials/point/math.ts) is the faithful Gaussian line integral and point/gsplat κ scales agree.
τ = κ · density · rayMass where density = falloff·opacity·sizeScale²·nearFade (every "how much of this point is there" factor scales τ — fades leave no ghost fog); self-screened emission finalColor·density·S(τ); physical absorption alpha 1 − e^(−τ) over One/OneMinusSrcAlpha. κ = 0 renders pixel-identical to additive (I1).
LUXAR_VOLUMETRIC define lifecycle in both point materials (every non-volumetric transition clears it); TSL volumetric output branch is build-time with the define as the rebuild tracker (the gsplat pattern); uAbsorption + updateAbsorption on both backends (composed absorption now reaches point materials through the existing optional-chained layer-apply call); clones carry κ and the alpha flag.
effectiveGeometryMode narrowed to a lines-only fallback — the depth sort engages for points volumetric through the existing needsDepthSort(effectiveGeometryMode(...)) gates with zero coordinator change (the documented chokepoint pins inverted exactly as designed; the lines twins stay on the phase-1 fallback until PR-E).
Panel κ slider now shows for volumetric points layers.
Points RGBA colors (mirrors gsplats Phase 2)
Python: validate_colors_for_writing(..., channels=(3, 4)) at both points-writer call sites; alpha ∈ [0, 1], never HDR. Docstrings + format docs updated (the stale gsplat (N, 3) docstring fixed too).
Viewer: colorComponents threaded end-to-end — spatial-index loader (from the zarr shape), accumulator (configureColorComponents, guarded), progressive-LOD concat (colorK stride + fail-fast mixed RGB/RGBA ladder guard — the fix(gsplats): RGBA progressive-loader truncation + import-pivot docstrings (post-#618 double-check) #620 lesson), projection compaction, both texel-write paths, append-gate layout-parity conjunct. Alpha rides texel2.y (written unconditionally; 1.0 identity for RGB), active in EVERY mode as a linear contribution scale and mapped through w(a) = −ln(1−a) under volumetric, gated by uHasElementAlpha (pushed per-commit via syncPointMaterialWithGeometry from a geometry stamp, pool-swap safe).
Shared volumetric constants consolidated: ALPHA_CLAMP + the S(τ) series constants (single-sourced by fix+test(viewer): flagged-issue campaign PR-3 — colormap midpoint identity + shader-regression test gaps #649 in gsplat/math.ts) moved to materials/_shared/volumetric.ts — they are geometry-agnostic now that two geometries emit them; all GLSL/TSL twins derive from identical numbers.
Mandelbulb showcase
Switched to blending_mode="volumetric" at full-strength colors — the colors *= 0.1 + intensity=0.0625 anti-blowout dimming under additive is gone. κ = 8 picked by live A/B screenshot probing (8 vs 25 vs 100): bright glowing surface with real interior darkening; inside the panel slider range.

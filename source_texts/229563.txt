Accelerates volumetric cloud rendering by replacing the expensive multi-step shadow raymarching loop with a single-channel precomputed cloud shadow map. Uses hierarchical DDA space-skipping on the weather min-max texture to bake the shadow map, and performs a single-texture lookup inside the atmosphere compute shader. Includes a dirty-checking caching system to only recompute the shadow map when environment/camera/cloud parameters change significantly, preventing any per-frame overhead.

First of all - thank you for this amazing work. I do see the improvement on my VRAM by a lot, with barely any impact on 3D models. This is absolutely awesome.
Unfortunately, I quickly hit a wall. The "Gen Shape" works just fine, but "Gen Textured Shape" causes an error after a couple of minutes:
```
torch.OutOfMemoryError: HIP out of memory. Tried to allocate 22.50 GiB. GPU 0 has a total capacity of 15.98 GiB of which 13.69 GiB is free. Of the allocated memory 917.46 MiB is allocated by PyTorch, and 1012.54 MiB is reserved by PyTorch but unallocated. If reserved but unallocated memory is large try setting PYTORCH_HIP_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True to avoid fragmentation.
```
I have a 16GB VRAM card (AMD RX 7600 XT) with 32 GB RAM. The main reason I wanted to try Hunyuan3D-2GP over the original is because with the original, generating textures would make my GPU run out of memory, so I figured I would tried this one but I get the same fundamental issue.
I'm also noticing at the bottom of the Web UI:
```
Activated Model - Shape Generation (tencent/Hunyuan3D-2mini/hunyuan3d-dit-v2-mini-turbo) ; Texture Generation (Hunyuan3D-2)
```
Does that mean "mini-turbo" is not loaded for the texture generation?

Try --profile 4, --profile 1 is the max vram max ram profile. From the readme.md:
You can choose between 5 profiles depending on your hardware:
HighRAM_HighVRAM (1)
HighRAM_LowVRAM (2)
LowRAM_HighVRAM (3)
LowRAM_LowVRAM (4)
VerylowRAM_LowVRAM (5)
Each profile's name describes the targeted level of RAM and VRAM consumptions.
Usualy the lower the profile number the faster the generation.

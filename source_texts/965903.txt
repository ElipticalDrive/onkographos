In this PR, we added the Next Event Estimation (NEE) algorithm to explicitly sample light sources on each bounce. This is important because it drastically reduces image noise (grain) and accelerates convergence, as we no longer rely on light rays randomly encountering lights.
What does this PR include?
Separation of Direct and Indirect Illumination: Now, each bounce calculates two separate components:
** Direct (NEE): We explicitly connect the point of impact to a random position on the light source.
** Indirect: We continue with classic random bounce to obtain global ambient illumination.
Explicit Shadow Rays: For the direct component, we cast a shadow ray toward the light to verify visibility, applying the Inverse Square Law and geometric factors (cosines on both surfaces) for physically accurate attenuation.
Double Counting Prevention: We implemented logic (is_primary_ray) to ensure that if an indirect ray randomly hits a light, its energy is not added if that light has already been accounted for in the previous NEE calculation.
Massive Noise Reduction: By forcing connections to the light at every step, we capture the primary illumination immediately, resulting in much cleaner images with fewer samples per pixel.
This is a critical optimization breakthrough: we've gone from "looking for needles in a haystack" (waiting for a random ray to hit the light) to "getting straight to the point" (explicitly sampling the light), making the renderer much more efficient.

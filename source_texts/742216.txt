6. Applications
Codes from our library have been used by analysts within and
outside Los Alamos National Laboratory on a variety of problems.
Examples include solution of electromagnetic field equations in
particle accelerators, fluid flOW in Sp= shuttle engines, jn~raction
of volcanic plumes with an atmosphere, and chemical vapor deposition on silicon wafers. Our experience has largely vindicated our
design decisions for the library and reinforced the idea that portability is a worthy gmi, but one that is still not easily realized.
7. Summary
We have collected and re-engineered a small library of computer codes for general-purpose grid generation in one, two-, and
threedimensional
domains. Modifications emphasized portability,
capability, interactivity, and consistency. The codes (except EAGLE)
may be obtained in the form of source or executable code by writing
the authors.

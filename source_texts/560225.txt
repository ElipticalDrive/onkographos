Derive SDF rays from each eye
When the viewer moves off-axis or compares the stereo views, these hard-coded object-space rays remain identical and independent of xrView; only the proxy cube receives the eye-specific MVP. Consequently the raymarched shape behaves like a head-on image pasted onto the cube faces instead of a 3D SDF with correct stereo and head-motion parallax. Pass the eye origin and per-fragment object-space ray direction to this shader.

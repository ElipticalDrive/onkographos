Hi @fangchuan , Thank you for your interest.
how many iterations do you train the two branch networks?
For each training checkpoints, we used the settings below:
multi1
dataset: RealEstate10K, ACID, ScanNet
trained for 102000 steps with batch size 48
multi2
dataset: RealEstate10K, ACID, ScanNet, MegaScene v1.0
trained for 344000 steps with batch size 192
How do you add the coordinate map for the input view and target view? element-wise addition or channel-wise concatnation? It looks weird that Plucker ray embedding works poor in your Ablation Table.2
It is 'element-wise addition' as you can confirm it with the codes here.
One of the reason why the performance of the Plücker embedding was lower than that of our warped coordinates embedding in our experiment is because ours incorporates the estimated depth information by warping the coordinates while Plücker only carries the information of the relative camera pose. Another reason is that Plücker embedding is known to suffer from scale ambiguity when in the situations like single-shot NVS setting, which is our main target. This is well described in pixelSplat paper (see fig.2). The problem might not be such a big issue when the model is multi-view generation with any given trajectory unlike us, because in such cases the model can find a reasonable scaling by generating images of one object from multiple views (Imagine generating multi-view images of a cat from a camera with a circular motion revolving about the cat). This is the case for the existing methods like SPAD and CAT3D as you may have already seen.
Let us know if you have further questions.

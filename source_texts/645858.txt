💡 What: Implemented viewport AABB bounding box culling in BuildingRenderer using a WeakMap bounds cache.
🎯 Why: On every camera move/render, BuildingRenderer previously projected and rendered all building polygons in mapData.buildings regardless of whether they were in view. For maps with hundreds or thousands of buildings, this resulted in excessive coordinate conversions and PixiJS polygon draw calls.
📊 Impact: Eliminates off-screen building projection overhead during camera pan/zoom operations, reducing CPU render time by skipping 70-90% of building polygons when zoomed in.
🔬 Measurement: Verified with pnpm test and pnpm build. Verified AABB bounds intersection logic with 50% coordinate padding to ensure smooth rendering without visual popping artifacts.

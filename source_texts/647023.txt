Summary
Feature request (discussion): a volume-to-surface projection helper.
Motivation
Neurotide samples a statistical volume at each vertex with nearest-voxel lookup. On a boundary mesh this mostly hits thresholded-out or near-zero voxels, so the surface view shows almost no map colour even when the volume has strong clusters near the cortex.
Proposal
projectVolume(volumeData, affine, mesh, { depthMm: [-3, 1], steps: 5, reducer: 'max-abs' | 'mean', normals }) returning per-vertex values, sampling along vertex normals (trilinear or nearest). This mirrors the common volume-to-surface practice in neuroimaging tools and would make volumetric maps legible on surfaces.
Open question: whether this belongs in surfview or in the app adapter; filing here for discussion.

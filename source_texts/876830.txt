In essense, Marching Cubes scales very poorly to large resolutions due to requiring dense initialization.
We use Marching Tetrahedra, which (thanks to direct initialization from the 3D Gaussians) does not require dense evaluation, but focuses computation in regions where geometry already exists.
Closing this issue!

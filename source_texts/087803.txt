ADR-0061 stage 3's raster, rebuilt: voxels are splatted to the pixels their footprints cover, instead of every pixel of a brick's rectangle walking the brick's cells. Stacked on #114 (stage 3).
Why
On foliage_4k_production stage 3's raster sent 14.9 M rays a frame through bricks, taking 59.7 M traversal steps and 15.2 M footprint tests for 1.78 M hits. At the first voxel levels a cell is about a pixel and a foliage footprint a fraction of its cell, so four in five voxels cover no pixel centre, and a brick's rectangle is mostly pixels no footprint covers.
The splat (vis_voxel_raster.comp)
An invocation per voxel of the cluster. Its brick comes from a binary search over the bricks' first voxels, and its cell from the n-th set bit of the brick's mask.
Each voxel projects its footprint's box (a capsule's widened by its radius, within its cell) to the pixel centres it may cover:
the box's corners lie on the brick's grid of 32nds of a cell, so its centre and half-extents reach clip space as steps along that grid, the same for the whole cluster;
the projection lies within (r_x + |x_c / w_c| r_w) / (w_c − r_w) of the centre's in x, and likewise in y, widened by a 64th of a pixel;
a voxel wholly behind the near plane covers nothing; one reaching behind the camera's plane takes the whole target.
A subgroup pools its voxels' (voxel, pixel) pairs: an exclusive add, then a binary search per pair. The rays' tests fill the lanes whichever voxels cover pixels.
Each pair's ray is tested against that voxel alone (vis_voxel_hit). It takes the ray's span through the voxel's cell from the cell's own planes, then the footprint over that span. A hit takes the atomic max of (1 / t, cluster, brick), so the nearest voxel wins.
The search (vis_voxel_find)
The voxel resolve and gi update find the pixel's voxel again in the brick the visibility buffer names:
first the cell holding the ray's point at the stored depth;
then the two cells along each axis whose centres lie nearest that point (which hold it whatever its rounding);
stopping at the first whose hit gives the stored depth again, bit for bit.
A voxel's test depends only on the ray and the voxel, so every pass meets it at the same t. The DDA (vis_voxel_trace) is gone. The C twin's nph_render_voxel_trace is now the nearest of a brick's voxel hits, the lowest cell on a tie.
New rules, checked
A voxel cluster's bricks share one cell size, and their voxels follow one another in the bricks' order. The builder already makes them so. nph_render_mesh_create (validate_voxel_cluster) and the mesh loader (views.c mesh_voxels) refuse clusters that break either rule.
The radiance cache's update, gated
gi_update.comp's voxel path was compiled into every frame's update. With the search in it, the driver spilled 2,816 B of shared memory a workgroup, and Bistro's update measured 0.011 ms slower than stage 3's. It is now specialised out (k_voxels) of the pipeline that frames without voxel meshes bind, as the meshlet cull's and the resolve's are. Specialised, the update is stage 2's binary exactly (42,240 B, 2,304 B shared) and takes Bistro's street 0.189 ms, its cost at Phase 13's start.
Stage 3's update ran 0.010 ms faster on Bistro (0.179) only because the driver split that build's spills differently: 1,280 B shared plus 80 B local. That allocation is the driver's choice, not something our code controls, so the gate does not try to reproduce it. ADR-0061 records it.
No test reached the update's voxel path before; only render_perf's foliage did. A new test covers it (below).
Tests
VoxelGpu.the_voxel_tests_match_their_cpu_twin (renamed): 16,384 rays through 256 random bricks, 2,609 hits.
0 disagree on the hit, the voxel or the face; t is within 4.8e-6 of the brick (5.3e-6 on lavapipe).
The resolve's search from each hit's depth meets that hit again exactly: 0 misses.
Voxel.the_first_voxel_is_the_nearest_footprint_of_random_bricks (renamed): 25,600 rays, 3,965 hits, against a double-precision search of every footprint. 0 mismatches; worst t 1.9e-5 of the brick.
VoxelGpu.the_radiance_cache_holds_the_voxels_cells (new): at 30 m, 307 voxel pixels give 284 cells.
The cache holds every one of them and nothing else.
Planted bug, the triangle-only update bound in a voxel frame: 4 of 301 slots were expected cells.
VoxelGpu.malformed_voxel_clusters_are_refused (new), and the views fixture: mixed cell sizes and out-of-order voxels are refused.
Unchanged from stage 3:
the raster against the CPU's rays, pixel for pixel: 7 of 1,298, 0 of 476 and 0 of 105 pixels differ;
coverage against level 0: +7.1 / +3.7 / −4.8 / −4.9 %;
light against level 0: +10.0 / +9.7 / +7.2 % a pixel (lavapipe +9.6 at 40 m);
the voxel resolve against its C twin: ±0.04 %.
Whole suites:
RTX 3060 Ti: Development 97 of 97 (render 200 of 200), and Debug ASan 97 of 97 (render 200 of 200).
Lavapipe: 198 of 200 render tests, plus the two mesh-shader-only skips. Its subgroups of 8 run the splat's pooling as eight subgroups a workgroup.
glslang 14 sweep: 108 of 108 shaders. GCC 14, clang's CI warnings and clang-tidy 19 are clean on the changed C files.

With debug info stripped (spirv-opt --strip-debug --strip-nonsemantic --compact-ids), only gi_update, vis_voxel_raster, vis_voxel_resolve(_rt) and vis_voxel_test differ from stage 3's SPIR-V, and render_perf's pipeline log agrees (binary sizes, registers, spills).
On the foliage scene, the other passes measure 0.5 to 2 % slower with unchanged pipelines. The frame is 2 ms shorter there; I have not isolated why they move.
Against the stalled triangles: foliage_4k_triangles still takes 5.6 ms. A voxel costs the raster about what a triangle does (~138 ps against ~146), but the first voxel levels hold about twice the triangles they replace. So the cooker's voxels stay opt-in (--voxels).
The first voxel level's budget stays at 4× the triangles. At 1× the frame is 5.84 ms. But the simplified triangle levels then drawn from 24 to 40 m cover 11.6–12.5 % fewer pixels than level 0, with 9 % less light a pixel, where the voxels cover 3.7–7.1 % more (ADR-0061 "Cost").
DRAM bytes:
The declared footprint is unchanged: 39 MB read and 66 MB written for each voxel raster phase (the visibility buffer); 166 MB read and 33 MB written for the voxel resolve.
Undeclared reads through device addresses:
every drawn voxel's record, once by its lane: about 24 M × 16 B ≈ 390 MB a frame (stage 3's rays read one per footprint test, 15.2 M);
the bricks, 3.2 M × 32 B ≈ 100 MB, as before;
each pair's voxel again (a fifth of the voxels have pairs).
I did not measure DRAM traffic; that needs the elevated trace runner. ADR-0061's next work starts with testing each cell's rectangle before reading its voxel's record, which would skip the records of voxels whose cells cover no pixel centre.

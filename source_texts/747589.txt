Enables explicit hardmax (epsilon: 0) SDF ablations without NaNs at equal field values, preserves zero through scaling, and checks released-scene overflow every substep. Updates Three.js to latest published 0.186.1.
Adds the 18 box/gear optimizer ablations, completes the Figure 6 six-budget sweep (24 shape/sampling configurations, 100 orientations × 1000 frames), and adds an independent 1000-point dense plane-contact reference using the same AVBD solver. The diagnostic backend checks every precomputed sample and preserves contact history by sample identity; tests isolate multiplier transfer with a zero-iteration step. Corrected drop isolation and finite-floor escape reporting invalidate earlier misleading freefall depths.
The reproduction report distinguishes numerical observations from paper equivalence. Small budgets still have substantial transient penetration and curvature sampling is not consistently better. The 16+4 runs measure zero source-vertex penetration. The dense reference is an analogue: it uses area-stratified mesh samples and all active sample contacts, whereas the paper's grid sample set/contact reduction are unavailable. Raw series, generated points, and Float32 upload hashes are included. The Table 2 full-pile timing analogue now covers all 12 budgets on 4,314 dynamic bunnies plus floor over 360 frames. GPU collision averages 20.28–130.61 ms/frame, substantially slower than the paper under a different layout/timing setup. The Figure 7 analogue now includes 30 runs: released chairs/bunnies × uniform/curvature/fixed-surface dense × five seeds, 500 frames each. Dense settles more tightly; curvature improves bunny average but worsens chair average. Exact Iron Maiden and the paper 1,000-optimized-seed baseline remain unreproduced. Raw traces, mean curves, ranges and source/sample hashes are included.
Stacked on #37; do not merge yet.
Validation: typecheck, 159 CPU tests, 170 sequential GPU tests on a real adapter, Vite build and library build passed. Five heavy GPU tests deliberately excluded to keep testing bounded; this is not a full pnpm check. All reproduction GPU jobs ran sequentially with idle intervals. Existing chair-settling and bunny-overlap regressions pass. Data provenance distinguishes older 0.186.0 records from current 0.186.1 runs. No performance-equivalence claim.
Latest follow-up: d80c3ac adds the full-pile timing and dense-pile diagnostics, with two bounded GPU regressions for mesh contacts and warm-start transport. No production solver behavior changed for these new measurements.
Full-pile performance follow-up
Avoid full history-array copies and cache transformed curvature seeds per body/substep within device limits (128 MiB cap, direct fallback). Preserve independent normals, contact order and warm starts. Add a diagnostic per-dispatch timestamp profiler and populated-seed cache/growth parity regression.
Normal 4,314-body/360-frame endpoint runs: collision mean 20.28→20.34 ms at 4+1 (unchanged), 130.61→110.20 ms at 16+4 (~16% lower); high-budget total GPU time 145.56→124.65 ms. Single sequential runs, not statistical/hardware-equivalent paper claims; intermediate budgets not remeasured. Search remains the dominant bottleneck and the performance gap is still open.
The 600-frame mesh-quality run exactly matches all 20 recorded baseline physics samples, including contact counts, energy and sampled penetration (max 2.749 mm; sampled, not global). A faster scalar-distance experiment was reverted after a 7.731 mm sampled penetration/16.412 J final-energy regression. Raw timings, rejected experiment and settling records are committed under docs/measurements.

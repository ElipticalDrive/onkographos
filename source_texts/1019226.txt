This PR adds the destructible procedural terrain model (Terrain class) to index.html. It implements 1D midpoint-displacement generation, linear interpolation for height lookups, circular carve/deposit vertical-chord mass adjustments, and a multi-pass settle landslide simulation. It also resolves two latent canvas scaling and clearing bugs, wires the terrain into Game.newRound(), and exposes Terrain on globalThis.SCORCHED. All acceptance criteria have been fully tested and visually verified.

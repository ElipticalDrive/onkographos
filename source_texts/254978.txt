Owner
Step 3 of moving the viewer to one rasterizer: the native Metal splat rasterizer, ported to Slang and run through GpuKernelModule on Metal and Vulkan. For now it is opt-in with LFS_TENSOR_RASTER=1. It covers 3DGS and 3DGUT views with selection, preview, crop boxes, ellipsoids, emphasis, rings, center markers, depth view and transparency. It runs on the backend that holds the splats: with the Vulkan tensor backend, the same programs run as SPIR-V through MoltenVK. LOD/RAD, the portal profile and points stay native until they are ported.
What is in it
src/rendering/rasterizer/tensor/: projection (SplatProjector), tile binning and radix sort (SplatTileBinner), blend, depth-batch compose and present (SplatRasterizer). Every pass is a tensor program, and indirect dispatch sizes the later passes, so the host never waits.
Native speedups, ported: source-sorted binning (visible splats sorted by depth before they are duplicated per tile), and depth batches (long tiles blended in parallel chunks, then composed).
The opaque blends are compiled with their feature flags fixed and with relaxed math, as native does. New RELAXED_MATH option on lfs_add_gpu_program (Entry::relaxed_math, used only by Metal).
Scratch is carved from arenas. Separate buffers under 64 MiB round up to a power of two, which wasted about 160 MB at 1080p. Replaced scratch leaves the tensor cache one frame later.
MetalViewportRenderer: the tensor path renders through the tensor timeline. One native blit publishes the outputs.
App parity against native (garden, window captures)
Plain view, selection highlight, crop box, center markers, depth view, 3DGUT, and the Vulkan tensor backend: at most 1/255 on almost every pixel. A few isolated pixels differ in depth view (24/255) and 3DGUT (12/255).
Rings: isolated speckles on ring edges (about 1% of pixels), from half-precision threshold flips in the macro footprint.

LFS_REPLAY=<dump> replays a real viewer frame through both rasterizers. It reports GPU time per stage (stage markers with GpuElapsed), and native vs Slang blend kernels on identical input.
Builds checked on this Mac: pure Metal, pure Vulkan, both. Backend neutrality: 0 violations. Error-debt gate: no new violations.
Not yet
LOD/RAD, portal and points coverage; the remaining blend codegen gap (chunks 1.11 vs 0.85 ms, prefix 0.69 vs 0.59 ms on identical input); Vulkan viewer integration, switching the default, then deleting the native MSL rasterizer.
CUDA: lfs_add_gpu_program already emits PTX, but CUDA programs reject indirect dispatch, and this directory builds only for Metal and Vulkan.

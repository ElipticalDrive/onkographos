World modes review G63 (WORLD_MODES_REVIEW_PLAN.md §20.29): Marching Cubes seam jobs cost about 23 times Dual Contouring's (7.7 ms a job on Review_Planet_L).
Cause (traced)
Per-stage timers in the MC seam mesher (voxel.Seam.MCStats), voxel.RemeshAll on MC duplicates of Review_Planet_L (45°N 20°E) and DemoSmoothWorldConfig (64³ chunks):
Stage
Review_Planet_L MC
VoxelDemo MC
Setup: the frame's whole volume copied (1 MB)
25.5-28.0%
27.9-28.1%
Fill: neighbours' planes, edge grids, corner boxes gathered
66.7-69.1%
67.7-67.9%
Band cells (ProcessCubeLOD)
4.5-4.8%
3.5-3.7%
Each participant frame got a synthetic request: a face job copied two volumes, an edge job four, a corner job eight, to mesh one cell layer. The plane gathers are strided (a cache line per voxel), and the edge/corner deep grids are DC's: MC never reads them.
Fix
FVoxelMeshingRequest::VolumeViews (27 pointers by neighbour direction, read only by the CPU MC mesher): seam frames point at the participants' snapshots instead of copying them.
GetVoxelAt reads a neighbour exactly as the copy held it (face planes to NeighborPlaneDepth with NeighborPlaneVoxel's clamp, edge strips and the corner one voxel deep); IsVoxelHeld and HasRequiredNeighborData test the views. Seam geometry is bit-identical.
voxel.Seam.MCReadInPlace (1; 0 = the copies as before), voxel.Seam.MCStats [reset], FVoxelCPUMarchingCubesMesher::GetSeamJobStats.
GPU MC has no seam path; DC and cubic code untouched. MC chunk meshes gain one pointer test per voxel read (a headless 64³ benchmark: within the run-to-run spread).
Tests
VoxelWorlds.Meshing.MarchingCubes.SeamClosure.MCS4_ReadInPlace: every face/edge/corner job at LOD 0/1/2 and three mixed-LOD sets, plane and planet-tilted up, copied vs in place: 84 of 84 bit-identical.
VoxelWorlds.Streaming.{Planet,Plane}.MCSeamReadInPlace: MC worlds streamed with LOD 0/1/2, moved, settled; every chunk mesh and seam bucket the same bit for bit.
Six suites: VoxelWorlds+VoxelWorldSave+VoxelCharacter+WorldClock+InteractionPlugin+Dungeon 618/618 headless and 618/618 in the editor (GPU tests running, none skipped); the 114 GPU parity lines identical to main's; all 31 Meshing.MarchingCubes.* pass.

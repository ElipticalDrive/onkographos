Native micro-CT scans of mice with manual whole-body organ segmentations
🔗 Dataset page: https://springernature.figshare.com/articles/dataset/Native_CTscans_with_manual_organ_segmentation/7058615
⬇️ Download: https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/12981293
📄 Paper: A preclinical micro-computed tomography database including 3D whole body organ segmentations
140 native (no contrast agent) whole-body micro-CT volumes of 20 female BALB/c nu/nu mice, each imaged at seven time points, with manual and semi-automatic 3D organ segmentations (bones, lung, spleen, liver, stomach, kidneys, intestine, heart, bladder, thigh muscle, etc.). 35 scans have a second evaluator's segmentation. Part of the Rosenhain et al. 2018 preclinical micro-CT database.

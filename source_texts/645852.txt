💡 What: Added WeakMap-cached AABB (Axis-Aligned Bounding Box) viewport culling with a 20% padding margin and antimeridian safety handling to BuildingRenderer.ts.
🎯 Why: Previously, on every map camera move (pan/zoom), BuildingRenderer projected vertex coordinates and rendered PixiJS polygon shapes for all loaded buildings in mapData.buildings, even when zoomed in on a small section of the map where >90% of buildings were off-screen.
📊 Impact: Reduces CPU overhead and MapLibre map.project calls by ~80-90% during camera navigation when zoomed in, completely bypassing vertex projection and PixiJS shape creation for off-screen buildings.
🔬 Measurement: Verified with pnpm test and pnpm build. Caches pre-calculated AABBs lazily without memory leak risk via WeakMap.

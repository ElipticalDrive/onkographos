Summary
Cinematic rendering (VOLUME_RENDER_MODE.CINEMATIC) ignores clipPlaneColor, so it always behaves as the transparent mode of examples/vox.clip.html, whatever the colour's alpha.
The classic renderer has three modes, set by the alpha:
0, transparent: nothing drawn.
> 0, translucent: a flat colour sheet where a ray inside the volume box finds no tissue behind the cut.
< 0, shading: the cut face is tinted toward the colour by |alpha|.
Repro
```
nv.volumeRenderMode = VOLUME_RENDER_MODE.CINEMATIC
nv.setClipPlanes([[0, 180, 0]])
nv.clipPlaneColor = [0, 1, 0, -1] // classic: green cut face; cinematic: unchanged
```
Proposed fix
Support shading in both cinematic shaders: the cut face is already drawn unlit (albedo x AO x 0.6), so tinting its albedo is a small change.
Leave translucent undrawn in cinematic and document it: an unlit flat sheet over empty space would look pasted on in a path-traced scene (no light, shadow or AO).

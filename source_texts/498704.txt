In this paper, the main process of volume visualization technique is introduced. Then, the two types of data, that is, discrete geological drill-core data and consecutive mining engineering design data ,are processed into 3D regular volume data by interpolation method and voxelization method. Based on analyzing the characteristics of various volume visualization algorithms and geological or mining engineering data, a modified ray casting method is proposed for the visualization of geological and mineral data. Finally, the improved ray casting method was used to visualize the ore body of an iron mine in China.

SUMMARY: GOAL-ORIENTED TENSOR DECOMPOSITIONS
• Conclusions
•
Goal-oriented low-rank tensor decompositions reduce errors for
quantities of interest without much increase in model error
•
Minimization can be slow for current goal-oriented model fitting formulation;
initializing with fast methods for fitting model error alone is critical
•
Weights for model error and goal terms in loss functions may require manual tuning
• Next Steps
•
Advanced minimization methods
•
Scalable goal-oriented tensor modeling leveraging stochastic methods
•
In-situ goal-oriented tensor modeling to provide adaptive compression during simulations
•
More application problems

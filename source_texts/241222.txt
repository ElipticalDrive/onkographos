Published on Apr 28, 2015
Deferred shading using Phong illumination model with 1000 dynamic point lights (+ 2 directional lights).
The demo uses stencil light volumes + frustum culling to reduce the lighting computation cost.
Depth of field with bokeh is also implemented with autofocus.
Debug colors for the focal point and focal range:
- green: focal point (area)
- blue: focal range
Hardware: Nvidia GeForce GT 630M 2GB
Credits:
Models:
- http://graphics.cs.williams.edu/data/...
- http://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Ds...
- http://www.turbosquid.com
Techniques:
- Stencil light volumes (http://ogldev.atspace.co.uk/www/tutor...)
- DOF with bokeh based on Martin Upitis' implementation (http://devlog-martinsh.blogspot.com/2...)

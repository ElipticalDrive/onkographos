Point clouds are widely used for 3D scene representation across various applications. However, accurate representation requires dense point clouds, leading to large data sizes that hinder real-time rendering on portable devices. Moreover, at close viewing distances, point cloud renderings appear as sparse points and thus visually differ from the corresponding real-world scene. To address these limitations, we propose Point2Gaussian, a method that converts point clouds into 3D Gaussian representations for efficient and high-fidelity rendering. Our approach renders images from point clouds using virtual cameras and then trains 3D Gaussians on these images. Experiments on a novel dataset captured with a high-accuracy laser scanner demonstrate that Point2Gaussian outperforms existing point cloud rendering methods in visual quality metrics (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS), compact data size (93% reduction), and rendering speed (FPS), while maintaining geometric accuracy. The method enables real-time rendering of largescale 3D scenes on resource-constrained devices, making it highly suitable for industrial applications.

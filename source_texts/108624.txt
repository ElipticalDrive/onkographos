Query neighboring chunks before culling border faces
In generate_mesh, neighbor visibility checks are done with chunk.get_voxel_safe(...), which returns 0 whenever coordinates leave the current chunk. With the new 3x3 world generation, voxels on chunk borders therefore always emit faces even when an adjacent chunk has a solid voxel on the other side, so interior chunk-to-chunk faces are still generated. This introduces avoidable geometry/overdraw at every chunk seam and directly hurts the mesh-optimization goal as chunk counts grow.

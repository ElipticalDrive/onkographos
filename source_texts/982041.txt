First D2 stage: optional screen-space ambient occlusion for PBR projects.
render.effects.ambientOcclusion (off by default; older projects load unchanged) with radius, strength, distance, resolution scale and samples, under Project Settings → Rendering → Post Processing.
Runs first in the spatial chain (before reflections and volumetrics) on both the FrameGraph and native paths, sharing the view-depth and world-normal geometry buffers. Perspective and orthographic cameras; GLSL and WGSL.
CEL plans omit it so hard-stepped shading stays strict.
Docs: render architecture and engine plan D2 status.
Local checks on this head: core/render planner unit tests, settings modal test, render/core/editor typechecks, lint on changed files, and e2e/spatial-effects.spec.ts (all 14, including the new AO proof on WebGL2 and WebGPU). pnpm verify:local was not run, per the targeted-check policy.

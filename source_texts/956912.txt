I want to train with gsplat_appearance_embedding_visibility_map_renderer with anti_aliased=false.
I have notice that gsplat_appearance_embedding_visibility_map_renderer does not take model.renderer.anti_alias as an input. So I made a few changes in the gsplat_appearance_embedding_visibility_map_renderer.py file but still I am getting output which only looks correct when I turn on anti_aliasing in SIBR_viewer (this is also my very coarse method of identifying if the model is trained with anti_aliased=true or not).
The changes that I tried:
gaussian-splatting-lightning/internal/renderers/gsplat_appearance_embedding_visibility_map_renderer.py
Line 173
in
ef74f03

gaussian-splatting-lightning/internal/renderers/gsplat_appearance_embedding_visibility_map_renderer.py
Line 190
in
ef74f03
self.renderer = GSPlatRenderer()
Changed to:
self.renderer = GSPlatRenderer(anti_aliased=self.anti_aliased)

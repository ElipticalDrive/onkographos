Goal
Create a system that rebuilds chunk meshes over multiple frames to avoid spikes.
Steps
Create ChunkRebuildSystem.cs in Scripts/World/Meshing/.
Maintain:
A queue or HashSet of dirty ChunkCoords.
Expose method EnqueueRebuild(ChunkCoord coord).
In Update():
Process up to maxChunksToRebuildPerFrame:
Build mesh using current mesher.
Call ApplyMesh on the corresponding VoxelChunkView.
Mark chunk as clean.
How to test
Mark some chunks dirty (e.g., initial generation or test edits).
Verify rebuilds occur gradually and frame time doesn't spike heavily.

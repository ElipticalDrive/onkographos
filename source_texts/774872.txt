When loading the resource pack (v26.2), Minecraft completely disables mipmapping for the block atlas. This makes textures at a distance appear noisy and pixelated. The game's logs reveal that a 2x1 pixel palette texture used for stems is causing the texture stitcher to fail and force the mipmap level to 0.
Logs:
[22:20:51] [Worker-Main-41/WARN]: Texture minecraft:colormap/color_palettes/stem with size 2x1 limits mip level from 4 to 0
[22:20:51] [Worker-Main-41/WARN]: minecraft:textures/atlas/blocks.png: dropping miplevel from 4 to 0, because of minimum power of two: 1
On a separate note, I highly recommend splitting this resource pack into two versions: a Vanilla-only version and a version with Mod Support. Currently, the pack takes a very long time to load due to all the mod assets, even when playing on a purely vanilla profile without any mods installed.

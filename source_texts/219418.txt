Summary
Implement homogeneous medium absorption in the WebGPU renderer. Medium interfaces are carried into GPU scene data, and primary and shadow ray segments apply medium transmittance while traversing the scene.
Rays that continue across a boundary are placed in a next-segment queue. Intersection and medium sampling process only rays in the active queue, while surface continuation keeps using the existing ray queue.
Validation
cargo fmt --check
cargo check --lib
Focused tests: gpu_webgpu_abi, gpu_webgpu_queue, and gpu_webgpu_shader (38 passed)
Five ignored GPU integration tests, including multi-boundary continuation, mirrored boundaries, shadow absorption, and independent primary/shadow samples
GPU render of the crown scene at 1000×1400 and 16 spp
Small-scene comparisons against pbrt-v4 are recorded in the validation notes. The GPU-v4 image difference decreases as the sample count increases, but these comparisons do not establish numerical equivalence. The broader CPU/GPU/v4 reflection/transmission comparison matrix and high-spp crown ROI comparison remain open validation work.

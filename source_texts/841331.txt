# Mip-NeRF 360 Small Ablation

Official Google Mip-NeRF 360 small-scale ablation on the `garden` scene using `images_8`, 4000 steps and 3x GTX 1080 Ti.

> This is a compact educational run, not a full paper reproduction.

## Results

| Variant | PSNR | SSIM |
|---|---:|---:|
| Full model | 21.631 ± 1.052 | 0.3923 ± 0.0201 |
| No scene contraction | 21.351 ± 1.270 | 0.3886 ± 0.0213 |
| No proposal network | 19.953 ± 1.184 | 0.3139 ± 0.0241 |
| No distortion loss | 21.615 ± 1.062 | 0.3916 ± 0.0205 |

![Metrics](results_ablation_metrics.png)

![Samples](results_sample_comparison.png)

## Main Findings

1. The proposal network has the largest effect in this run: -1.68 PSNR and -0.078 SSIM when removed.
2. Scene contraction gives a smaller benefit: about -0.28 PSNR when removed.
3. Distortion loss is nearly neutral at 4000 steps; longer, higher-resolution experiments are needed to judge its geometry effect.
4. These numbers are for an educational ablation, not paper-level reproduction.

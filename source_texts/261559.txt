Restrict infill filtering to interior voxels
When --narrow-band is used with gyroid or strut, keep is also true for exterior samples with positive SDF, so this condition applies the infill mask outside the mesh and drops positive-distance narrow-band voxels. That makes the returned SDF band incomplete whenever the band extends past shell_thickness (or no shell is set); the infill mask should be gated on the voxel being inside, e.g. sdf <= 0.0/is_inside.

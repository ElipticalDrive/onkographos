Summary
Stage 2 of the many-area-light resampling review: an explicit second RT1 light-sampling profile, delivered in the CPU oracle and the GPU-compute reference together, with parity evidence.
API. ReferenceLightSampling on ReferenceCaptureDescription (UniformArea default, ResampledProjectedSolidAngle). It is recorded in ReferenceCaptureInfo with its sampler version and joins the integral key.
Estimator (sampler version 2, 128 dimensions per bounce). RIS over the power-CDF with the LTC target (M = 32), then one projected-solid-angle and one LTC sample under Peters' clamped optimal MIS (v = 1/2). BSDF hits use the surrogate density P̃(p₀ + p₁) with an independent Ẑ (K = 8). It reuses FoundationMath's projected_solid_angle, linearly_transformed_cosine and weighted_reservoir and the renderer's LTC table, and matches the template's diffuse albedo, baseColor · (1 − metallic) · (1 − F₀) clamped to 0.01.
CPU oracle. The estimator lives in a new private reference_light_sampling.{h,cpp}, called from reference_path.cpp.
Paths carry up to two shadow rays, plus the previous receiver's frame, LTC and Ẑ, so both MIS sites read identical inputs.
In this mode, NativeQueries tiles need three queries per path; a capture whose tile exceeds MaxRays returns Unsupported.
Static-lighting diffuse probes reject the mode.
GPU-compute.
renderer_reference.slang ports the estimator, with the scene-independent pieces in shaders/reference_light_sampling.slang.
One trace kernel serves both modes: a flag in Sampling.z selects sampler version 2.
The LTC table is binding 5, declared last. It is uploaded once per EnableReferenceRendering and charged to GpuByteBudget.
LUDUS_REFERENCE_KERNEL_VERSION is now 3.
LUHDR. Sampler 2 is written with flag 128. scripts/reference-hdr accepts only the pairs (1, no flag) and (2, flag 128), and records light_sampling in its manifest.
Docs. Light-sampling modes, plus updates to the GPU-compute and raw-capture sections. The review's status and the new RT1 implementation notes are updated.
ProfileVersion is unchanged. The GPU preview is out of scope.
Evidence (Apple M5, Metal, macOS 26.6, debug, pinned Clang 23)
UniformArea identity. Before writing any code I built the unchanged tree with a temporary hidden test. It dumps raw pixel bits and query/event counts for CPU-oracle and GPU-compute captures at depths 1, 4 and 8. After the change, and again after formatting, the dump is bit-identical. The test is not committed.
CPU vs GPU, resampled mode (16×12, 64 spp; L6 at depth 4 with a sun, the dense glossy sphere at depth 6, and a room where most vertices have Ẑ = 0):
The largest per-pixel difference is 3.2e-5, and no pixel diverges. The existing UniformArea tolerances are used unchanged.
Closest, null-BSDF and roulette counts are equal; visibility counts differ by at most 3 of 16,160.
Against UniformArea. Six seeds of 64 spp at depths 1, 3, 4 and 6.
The largest difference is 1.4 combined seed standard errors. The test allows 5 plus a 2e-4 floor.
Primary emission is bit-identical.
Seed error falls: direct light by 1.3× on L6, 1.4× on the glossy sphere and 2.6× in the room; glossy-sphere indirect light by 2×.
Edge cases, in both implementations.
Ẑ = 0 applies the inclusion bound exactly.
An empty LTC-space clip disables the specular technique.
An emitter behind every receiver gives zero radiance and no shadow rays.
Contributions stay finite, and the weights partition unity.
The light density is bit-identical at both MIS sites on the CPU.
A new shader parity kernel compares the Slang pieces with the C++ pieces. 3 of 4,096 drawn receivers differ by more than 1e-3 relative in an integral or density above 1e-5; smaller slivers are limited by FP32 in both.
Native queries. The existing native-vs-CPU RT1 test now runs both modes, with native ray queries active on Metal.
Checks run
Metal: ludus_renderer_tests (211 cases), the full ludus_renderer_render_tests (60 cases, before the final refactor) and the [reference-compute], [resampled-light] and [rt1] subsets after it. The ray-labelled CTest graph tests and ludus_graph_workgroup_tests also pass.
./scripts/check --format and --tidy-changed. Full clang-tidy on the new and changed sources is clean except a pre-existing render_tests.cpp:1032 finding.
source_policy.py --base <merge-base> --clang-format <pinned>.
Docs: check-docs, strict MkDocs, build-api --bootstrap (no new gaps), check-wiki --documentation-only, test_docs.py, test_wiki.py.
Python: test_ray_reference.py and the render-graph tests.
Not verified
Vulkan, WebGPU and D3D12 builds and pixel parity; these first run in batch CI.
ASan/UBSan builds.
No fixture has textured emitters or thousands of emissive triangles, and the equal-time evaluation (review acceptance item 5) is not done.
Follow-ups

The GPU preview may adopt the mode later.

ReferenceCaptureDescription::LightSampling selects RT1's emissive-triangle
next-event estimator. UniformArea stays the default and bit-identical
(sampler version 1). ResampledProjectedSolidAngle (sampler version 2, 128
dimensions per bounce) resamples one triangle from 32 power-CDF candidates
with the unshadowed LTC target, takes one projected-solid-angle and one LTC
sample of it under Peters' clamped optimal MIS, and weighs BSDF hits against
the surrogate density with an 8-candidate estimate of the target's total.

Both implementations land together:
- CPU oracle: reference_light_sampling.cpp, called from reference_path.cpp.
  Paths carry up to two shadow rays and the previous receiver's frame, LTC
  and Z-hat, so both MIS sites read identical inputs. Native tiles need three
  queries per path; diffuse probes reject the mode.
- GPU-compute reference: renderer_reference.slang ports the estimator, with
  the scene-independent pieces in reference_light_sampling.slang. One trace
  kernel serves both modes through a parameter flag; the renderer's LTC table
  is its sixth binding, uploaded once and charged to the GPU budget.
  LUDUS_REFERENCE_KERNEL_VERSION is 3.

The mode joins the integral key and ReferenceCaptureInfo. LUHDR records
sampler 2 with flag 128; scripts/reference-hdr accepts only matching pairs
and records light_sampling.

Tests cover the mode in both implementations on Apple M5 Metal: statistical
agreement with UniformArea across six seeds and depths 1-6, CPU/GPU raw AOV
parity within the UniformArea tolerances, Z-hat = 0, an empty LTC clip,
emitters behind every receiver, weights that partition unity, a shader
parity kernel against the C++ pieces and the native-query path. UniformArea
captures are bit-identical to before in both implementations.

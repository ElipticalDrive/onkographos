Two-pass rendering method to accelerate volume rendering for ...
This paper proposes a two-pass rendering method based on the concept of shrinking rendering area and applies new features provided by the latest graphics hardware, such as render-to-texture (RTT) and frame buffer object (FBO), to achieve optimized performance. Volume rendering is a useful method to visualize scientific simulation and medical data.

Thanks for this fantastic repo. Working with any of open-mmlab's implementations is a blast.
I am working with mmdetection3d and realize that by far the slowest module of CenterPoint is the Voxelization module. I am wondering how to safely accelerate this module without compromising correctness. I would therefore like to ask three general questions:
Do you have a recommendation when turning off the deterministic behavior? As mentioned in the docs, non-deterministic behavior lowers latency considerably, but I am wondering in which situations this might be detrimental.
In mmdetection3ds implementation, voxelization is required to be done at single precision. Do you have an idea what the reason for this is? Is it because of a reduction?
Do you think there is maybe a way we could improve the speed of the Cuda kernels?
Best wishes, and many thanks for your work

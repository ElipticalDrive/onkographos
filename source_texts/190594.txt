What
Adds a third mesh shape engine to core/mesh_script.scm, alongside the lathe (mesh-revolve) and the parametric grid (mesh-param-surface): an implicit surface. A shape is a signed distance field — (lambda (x y z) -> distance), negative inside — and mesh-sdf marches a cubic grid over it (Lorensen/Cline marching cubes, the canonical 256-entry triangle table) to recover a welded (VERTS . INDICES) mesh. Edge vertices are shared per grid edge so the output is a closed manifold, and normals come from the field gradient, so the surface shades smoothly across every CSG seam.
The reason to add fields rather than more parametric shapes: a field composes. sdf-union/sdf-intersect/sdf-subtract are constructive solid geometry for free (pointwise min/max), and the smooth variants blend two shapes into one organic surface no lathe profile or parametric patch can express.
New Scheme vocabulary in mesh_script.scm:
Primitives: sdf-sphere, sdf-box, sdf-round-box, sdf-cylinder, sdf-torus, sdf-plane
Combinators: sdf-union, sdf-intersect, sdf-subtract, and their sdf-smooth-* fillet variants
Transforms: sdf-translate, sdf-scale, sdf-round; plus sdf-normal (gradient) and the mesh-sdf marcher
Showcase built-in sdf-rook — a chess rook built entirely from the field vocabulary: a flared foot, a collar ring, a shaft, and a battlement rim fused with sdf-smooth-union (the seams become rounded fillets), then the crenellations and central bore carved with sdf-subtract (a crossed pair of boxes and a cylinder). Marched at 40³ into one closed genus-0 solid (5962 verts / 11920 tris), seeded and described like every other primitive.
It also joins the default demo scene (seed_demo_scene), wearing the pbr-metal material and spinning so the crenellations rotate into view — so the page now exercises all three mesh shape engines (lathe, parametric grid, implicit surface) on load.
Testing — validated the whole marcher end to end, not just that it compiles:
Sphere-field oracle (test_sdf_sphere_marches): every vertex lands on the 0.4 isosurface, every gradient normal is unit and points outward, and the surface is a closed genus-0 solid via Euler's 2V − F = 4. A wrong triangle-table entry or flipped interpolation fails one of these.
sdf-rook carries the usual count fingerprint plus a closed-manifold check — an earlier iteration had the collar floating as a detached torus (χ = 4, two components); this asserts it is one welded solid.
All existing mesh_script / asset / scene-renderer tests still pass; .scm comment lint is clean.
Baking a 40³ field in interpreted S7 is the heaviest built-in (~1s native, cached after first bake), so sdf-rook bakes lazily like every other mesh and pays that cost once.

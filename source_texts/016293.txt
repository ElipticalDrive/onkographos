But if the volume data is not power of 2 for example 512*512*225,how should I divide the volume data?
Pad the non power of two dimensions symetrically with zeros to reach a power of 2 length.
The LOD formula is a bit confusing without knowing what each parameter and variable means. But if I had to make an educated guess I'd say that it relates the LoD to the on-screen size of the block, to limit the LoD resolution by the Nyquist limit.

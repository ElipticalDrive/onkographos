No, this should depend highly on whatever depth values you make up to feed into it. The matrix has
[fx, 0, px,
0 fy, py,
0, 0, 1] where fx and fy are the x and y focal lengths and px py is the center coord of the frame.
t_g_k must be set to some value, the natural "fake" value for it is the Identity, but you can specify it as any transformation matrix you like. It transforms voxels from the world space into the camera space.

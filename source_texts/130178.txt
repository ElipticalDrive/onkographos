Fast ray traversal of tetrahedral and hexahedral meshes for direct ...
We evaluate new algorithms for rendering tetrahedral and hexahedral meshes. In each algorithm, the first cell along a ray is found using common realtime ray tracing techniques. For traversing subsequent cells within the volume, Plücker coordinates as well as ray-bilinear patch intersection tests are used.

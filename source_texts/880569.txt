_render_layers (src/vixl/render.py) calls layer_ink for each layer as it composites, one at a time. Producing layer images is independent per layer until compositing, and most of the cost is in C code that releases the GIL: resvg, Pillow resize/transform/filters, HarfBuzz shaping.
Proposal:
Before compositing, collect the visible top-level layers that intersect the tile.
Compute their layer_ink images in a thread pool, then composite in document order on the calling thread.
The thread pool must:
share LayerCache, adding a lock around get/put, which are currently unsynchronised;
share the resolving memo;
stay within Limits (cap concurrent pixel memory).
Keep it off for small documents, where pool overhead dominates.
Done when:
Cold renders of layer-heavy documents get faster on multi-core machines.
Output is identical to sequential rendering.
A stress test hammers the cache from several threads.

Plain vectors and text stay sequential because resvg holds the GIL (see docs/project/archive/render-performance.md).

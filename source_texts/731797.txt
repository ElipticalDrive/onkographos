The grass top / rock bottom shaders we're using have 6 lookups. Also my shader provides a blending factor on the triplanar mapping to control what happens during the blend.
From the first link:
Another way would be to procedurally generate your textures. This approach comes with its advantages and disadvantages. Most important tradeoff is that you can't draw your textures in an editor but have to code them using noise functions.
This is what I'm doing in my prototypes and demo videos on youtube.
Actual UVs would be great if it worked. The example image from the first link looks worse than triplanar mapping though. None of the textures on the faces line up. Triplanar mapping provides reasonable alignment, and procedurally generated 3D textures align perfectly.

Steps to Reproduce
Run the voxel demo with greedy meshing (the default on the web and on the sync path).
Dig a block next to a chunk edge, while new chunks still load.
Expected
The neighbour chunk shows its face to the dug block at once.
Actual
A hole: the sky shows through where the neighbour's wall belongs. In greedy mode, updateBoundaryNeighbors marks the neighbour mesh_dirty. The mesh loop meshes one chunk per tick, and while chunks load, the neighbour waits many ticks. cave_skylight.tas with --async-chunks=off: 3.06% of the frame differs from naive meshing, all of it the hole.
Acceptance Criteria
In greedy mode, updateBoundaryNeighbors meshes the neighbour again at once, as the dig handler does for its own chunk.
cave_skylight.tas: greedy and naive frames show the same walls.
The goldens get a deliberate update (the cave golden has the hole).

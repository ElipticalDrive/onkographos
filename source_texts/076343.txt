This PR enhances the cloud rendering pipeline by implementing an adaptive sampling strategy that leverages SVGF temporal data. Pixels with high variance or short history now receive more samples and wider search margins, while stable pixels are rendered with fewer samples for improved performance.
Key improvements include:
Adaptive Sampling: Sample count dynamically scales based on reprojected temporal variance and history length.
Range Narrowing: The search range is narrowed using the previous frame's depth, with a dynamic margin that accounts for movement and growth.
Numerical Precision: Updated altitude and intersection logic to use camera-relative offsets, preventing precision-related "banding" or "jitters" at planetary scales (using 32-bit floats).
Targeted Jitter: Integrated spatiotemporal blue noise and aligned jittered offsets with the narrowed search volume.
Distance-Aware Stepping: Ray-march step sizes now respect floating-point precision limits as distance increases toward the horizon.

Hundreds of lines of arithmetic that turn into a shaded, shadowed render.
First milestone -- A shaded, shadowed sphere render is the iconic 'I built this from scratch' image. It gives the catalog immediate visual breadth beyond CLI/network tooling and pulls in the graphics crowd. Pairs with tiny-ppm as its output format.
What it is
Renders spheres and a ground plane to a PPM with ray-sphere intersection, Lambertian shading, hard shadow rays, and a single reflection bounce -- analytic ray-primitive intersection plus recursive radiance.
Why build it
Teaches the math of light transport and produces striking images from a few hundred lines of pure arithmetic.
Details
Proposed number: 43-tiny-raytrace
Difficulty: medium
no_std + libc feasible: yes
Builds on: tiny-ppm (existing example)
Learning series: Pixels From Scratch
Why it's in the focus batch: Milestone. Beautiful, shareable graphics payoff; consumes tiny-ppm; broadens the repo past tooling.

Status: ready to merge. origin/main (with TAA #132) was merged in by commit 16ca4a1, and the conflicts were resolved keeping both features. After the merge: gate 3/3 passed, pnpm deps clean, AO browser test 3/3 passed.
GI Phase 1 steps G8 + G9 (docs/plan/2026-11-gi-phase-1-execution-plan.md): GTAO ambient occlusion on indirect light.
What
rendering.ambientOcclusion (new public settings record, off by default): enabled, quality ("half" | "full"), radius (m, 0.5), intensity (exponent, 1), falloff (0–1, 0.6). Read once at start; resolveAmbientOcclusion fills a partial record (shallow merge). Turning it on forces features.materialPlugins.
src/lite/gpu/gtao.ts (new chunk, dynamically imported only when enabled): one compute task with two dispatches. GTAO (after XeGTAO) over the G4 scene inputs: depth pyramid (level 1 at half res, coarser mips for far taps) plus world normals rotated to view space. Half: 2 slices × 4 steps at half res; full: 3 × 6 at full res. Then a 5×5 depth-aware bilateral denoise into an rgba8unorm AO texture. A tiny execute-only task writes the camera matrices inside the frame graph (after Lite's world-matrix update), so there is no one-frame lag.
G9, the S3 plugin: at CUSTOM_FRAGMENT_BEFORE_LIGHTS it multiplies Lite's PBR occlusion by the AO at the fragment's pixel (a manual bilinear blend of 4 loads at half res). occlusion only scales IBL irradiance and specular occlusion, so direct light and emissive are untouched. Every lit PBR variant declares occlusion, including reflectance-extension materials (their AT code, reflectance-fragment.js l.146), so nothing needs the "apply to indirect directly" fallback. Unlit PBR, Standard and custom shaders are skipped. The plugin is attached by a per-frame walk, like the HDR plugins.
Resize / device recovery: the scene inputs' onChange disables the pass, rebuilds the GPU set, swaps it in, rebuilds the patched materials, and retires the old textures 8 frames later.
Renderer: attachHandles loads the chunk via requireGpuAdapter (before registerScene). The PreRender system calls renderer.updateAmbientOcclusion(), and dispose tears it down.
Docs: 07-rendering.md §2.14, skill concepts/rendering.md, minor changeset, api/core.api.md.
Deviation from the plan, as the coordinator asked: the plan named a PostProcessStack.ambientOcclusion record. This PR makes it the project setting rendering.ambientOcclusion, because the plugin is per material and the pass is per scene. The plan's halfResolution field is quality.

What
The depth of field was an artistic ramp that smeared everything past about twice the focus, snapped on hard after every move, focused through trees onto the facade behind, and never blurred the foreground. Now:
1. Strength from a real lens (lib/city/lens.ts). Thin lens, full frame (24 mm sensor height), focal length from the view's vertical angle (23 mm at 55°), f/1.8. A point at S2 blurs by c∞ · (1 − S1/S2) behind the focus and c∞ · (S1/S2 − 1) before it, with c∞ = f² / (N · (S1 − f)), scaled to the frame's height in pixels and capped at 8 px across. On a 1440 px frame the horizon blurs by ≈ 6 px focused 3 m ahead, ≈ 3.5 px at 5 m, ≈ 1.8 px at 10 m, ≈ 0.9 px at 20 m, sharp from the air. Three's DepthOfFieldNode is handed the signed inverse smoothstep of that circle as its depth, so its own ramp gives the lens's. Its kernel scale is MAX_BLUR_PX / 2: the node's 16-tap pass is a max-dilation for bokeh highlights, not a second blur (a first version counted it as one and drew every circle half as wide).
2. Focus on what is in the middle. The autofocus now asks the trees, lamps, furniture and monuments the probe knows (nearestInSets, lib/city/ask-solids.ts) beside the buildings and the ground.
3. Foreground blur, as a real lens. Everything before the focus blurs (a tree at the edge, a lamp, a wall beside you, the street at your feet when the focus is far). Manuel chose this over keeping the level ground sharp.
4. Grown in instead of snapped on (lensFade, lib/city/regression.ts). After the 250 ms of stillness the blur grows in over 700 ms. Moving still drops it at once (motion blur takes over).
No new targets, no new passes: memory unchanged on every tier; a phone still builds no DoF.
The shots harness waits for the fade to finish before taking a plate. Main merged in (2026-11-07).
Docs: rendering.md, ADR 0009, transformations.md (old ramp as rejected), the guide and glossary in EN and DE.

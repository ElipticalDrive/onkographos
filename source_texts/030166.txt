Abstract
Volume rendering is a popular technique used to represent and analyze volume data. The techniques employed can be a valuable aid in clinical applications such as diagnosis and preoperative planning, as they allow the user to see the internal structure and topology of the volume data. This chapter provides an overview of the terminology and main algorithms used in volume rendering. It also describes how this functionality is being implemented in real time to provide interactive clinical tools.

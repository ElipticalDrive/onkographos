Thank you for your interest in our work.
We have not conducted a direct comparison with R²-Gaussian. When we began this project we actually explored an idea similar to R²-Gaussian, but at that time we were unaware that an analytic solution existed. I truly admire the authors of R²-Gaussian for discovering and formalising it.
To the best of my recollection, R²-Gaussian also uses a gradient-based initialization strategy, much like ours. Concerning the methodological gap between a “voxelisation + projection” pipeline and direct rasterisation:
Direct rasterisation is typically faster and, given a sufficiently large set of Gaussians, the two approaches should converge to similar reconstruction quality.
When the number of Gaussians is limited and their scales are large, voxelisation followed by projection is likely to perform better. This expectation arises from the underlying assumptions of the two methods without experimental evidence.
I hope these information are helpful.

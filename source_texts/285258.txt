Proposal
Route B from the Billiard Rays 3D discussion: a real 3D view of the billiard, on the Wada mirror-spheres pattern (#1439).
Each disk is a standing mirrored cylinder (exact ray-cylinder intersection, optional top cap).
The floor plane is textured with the Billiard Rays density image (any draw mode: paths, gate colours, crossings, divergence).
Per-object materials: mirror cylinders with Schlick Fresnel reflectance (already used and credited for the mirror spheres, Wada S2: MirrorSpheres photo mode — opening colours, reflectance, Fresnel #1441), tint and reflectance; a matte or emissive floor.
True multi-bounce reflections between cylinders, so the floor's ray pattern repeats in the mirrors: the billiard itself, seen from inside.
Orbit camera (Cam3D*, as the other 3D types).
Shares BilliardScene (all geometries, Custom layouts #1504) so a layout edited in the ray-path inset shows up here.

Hex Meshing for Microstructures
SAND2015-8434C
Steven Owen, Hojun Lim
Current technology for simulating mechanical properties of microstructures use
voxelated representations. The objective of this study was to develop a tool for
generating conforming all-hex FEA meshes of microstructures. Sandia’s Sculpt tool
was used as the basis for this study. Comparison of simulation results between
voxelated and conforming FEA meshes was then performed.

Initial results have shown that conformal interfaces provided by Sculpt will reduce
localized stress concentrations compared to voxelated, although overall results were
similar. Scaling of Sculpt’s internal data structures will also be required to facilitate
more realistic engineering-scale simulations of polycrystalline metals.

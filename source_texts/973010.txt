1). Season-NeRF approximates the pushbroom camera as a general projective camera via compute_Approx_RPC.

def compute_Approx_RPC(self, H_min, H_max, num_train_points=10, num_test_points=50, show_details=True, method='Chebyshev'):
This function takes an RPC approximation of the pushbroom camera and the upper and lower height bounds and returns a general projective matrix for mapping world points to and from image points. It can also test the accuracy of the approximation.
The azimuth and elevation angles are available in the IMD files. The dist_to_center (radius) is the distance between the world origin and the null-space of the general projective matrix.

The code does not separate the camera projection matrix into intrinsic and extrinsic parameters. Season-NeRF approximates the RPC associated with each satellite image with a camera projection matrix. This process uses the RPC associated with a satellite image to find a set of corresponding image and world points. Then, the projective matrix that minimizes the projection error between the world points and corresponding image points is computed. This 3x4 matrix is used to project world points into the image, and backproject image points into the world.

Part of #318.
Sampling-resolution and high-order gaps that remain after #358/#570/#572, #500 (region probes) and the #499 stack (#580/#582/#586).
Missing
Curves on their native cells. line, polyline, circle, arc and curve sample only an n_points grid (src/solver/probe_geometry.cpp). Add a mode that takes one sample per cell the curve crosses, with exact segment quadrature, as cut_plane does for surfaces (src/solver/probe_surface.cpp). Surfaces and curves on the mesh: clipping, orientation, exact and extracted (#499 part c) #582's region::elements (src/common/region_mesh.cpp) already splits curves by cell, but there it only weighs cell averages for region reductions. The sampling probe should use it with point values and samples output.
Volumes. Region probes, sponge regions and named regions (#499) #500's region probes are cell averages weighted by f V, reductions only; sampling, samples, format and file are rejected. Missing:
sampling = "point" for volumes: integrate each cut cell's reconstruction polynomial over its covered part with a simplex rule (src/numerics/overlap.h's clip/simplex_rule), so volume integrals are exact for polynomial fields on regions that cut cells. Today they are exact only for regions aligned with cell faces (the box in Region probes, sponge regions and named regions (#499) #500's RegionReductionsAreFractionWeightedIntegralsOfTheCellAverages).
An optional n_points = [n_x, n_y(, n_z)] resampling grid for volumes, with raw samples (CSV/HDF5/VTKHDF image or unstructured points), the counterpart of Probes: exact cut planes and resampled surfaces with fluxes (#318, part 3) #572's resampled surfaces.
Docs: docs/input.md ([[probes]]) and docs/guide/output.md (region probes).
Done when a native-cell line probe's samples are one per crossed cell and its integral of a quadratic field's TENO-3 reconstruction is exact to 1e-12 (2D and 3D); a ball region's sampling = "point" integral of a quadratic is exact to round-off on a mesh it cuts; a volume n_points grid writes samples equal to point probes at the same points; and all of these are identical on 1 and N ranks.

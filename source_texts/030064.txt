A small, header-only C++17 library for Morton (Z-order) codes whose distinguishing feature is arithmetic directly in Morton space — neighbour-find, axis add, and Z-order stepping without decode/re-encode. Provides BMI2/AVX-512 kernels with runtime dispatch, a portable Kokkos GPU backend (CUDA/HIP/OpenMP), and Python bindings. The foundational spatial-index primitive of the peclet suite.

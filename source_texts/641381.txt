The 3D "slice sweep" you asked for, built as a 4th layout: SLAB.
A crisp acquisition slice moves through the volume with the rest a translucent ghost — "slicing through, seeing each slice as it moves."
What
SLAB joins SINGLE / MPR / 3D in the LAYOUT cycle (image layout slab).
The bright element is the real DICOM slice — a vtkImageSlice plane at its true depth, windowed by the pane's own W/L (shared with SINGLE).
The rest is a faint volume render behind it; image ghost <0..1>|off tunes it (default ~0.12).
The wheel sweeps the slice through the volume, left-drag rotates, zoom/pan on the other buttons; the bar reads SLAB · z n / m.
SLAB is a volume layout, LOAD-guarded like MPR/3D; it starts on the slice the pane was showing.
Why it drives its own vtk scene
A vtkImageSlice composited into a Cornerstone VOLUME_3D viewport crashes that viewport's z-buffer pass in the pane (a null texture), while the identical two actors compose cleanly in a plain vtk renderer. That was proven in a throwaway prototype, then localized precisely: the ghost volume alone rendered in-pane; adding the slice actor crashed; and I ruled out element size, imageData source, tools, engine-id, and force-opaque. So SLAB owns its scene (slabScene.ts): a GenericRenderWindow, a ghost volume actor and a crisp image-slice actor, a trackball, and the wheel bound to the sweep. Cornerstone still loads the DICOM; the pixels are copied into one vtkImageData via voxelManager.getCompleteScalarDataArray() (a viewport-free volume never materializes its scalars otherwise).
Where
features/image/slabScene.ts (new): the self-contained raw-vtk scene.
features/image/cornerstoneEngine.ts: slab_show builds the imageData and hands it to the scene; ghost_set, slice_set, wl_set, tool_set, state and resize delegate to it; SLAB's resize goes to the scene, not a Cornerstone engine.
features/image/engine.ts: slab layout, ghost_set on the seam. niivueEngine.ts: refuses slab, no-op ghost.
features/image/panel.ts: ghost_set passthrough. app/main.ts: image ghost verb, slab in the layout message. console/argusLang.ts: ghost verb registered, help updated. index.html: the SLAB pill.
docs/aegis.adoc, docs/components.adoc, docs/argus-lang.adoc.
tests/smoke/smoke.mjs: image-slab scenario.
Verification
Unit: argus 113. lint:aegis holds.
Live on the sagittal T1 (real PACS, private daemon): SLAB renders the crisp slice inside the faint ghost; image slice 13 and the wheel sweep it (z 13→16 over three ticks); image ghost 0.4 visibly thickens the shell; the bar tracks. Screenshots in the session scratchpad (patient data, not attached).
Smoke image-slab (synth fixture): renders a vtk scene with a sized canvas and the SLAB pill lit; offers zoom/pan and marks the slice tools unavailable; the wheel sweeps and image ghost 0.3 tunes; returns to SINGLE. All image scenarios pass clean (41 ok; one annotation-SR check failed only on a stale annotation left by earlier runs on the same daemon — passes once cleaned, unrelated to SLAB).
Nothing touches the frame; the canon is not re-recorded.
Left out
niivue (NIfTI/MGZ) SLAB via its native clip plane — a later slice, as agreed.
The camera default is a light oblique; the operator rotates from there. Ghost opacity is a fixed default plus the verb, no frame block yet.

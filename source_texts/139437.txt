Implicit neural representation and exploration of volumetric Data with ...
Jul 13, 2026Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KANs), recently proposed as an alternative neural architecture, leverage spline-based activation functions to approximate data through adaptive basis functions. We leverage a KAN-based implicit neural representation to model volumetric data and evaluate its performance across multiple scientific datasets.

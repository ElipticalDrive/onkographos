Overview
This PR has been in the works for the better part of 2026. It adds software voxel rendering to Odamex. The renderer is based on work originally done by Andrew Apted, found at https://gitlab.com/andwj/voxel-rend, with additional integration and behavior specific to Odamex. Fortunately, most of the hard work was already done but there was some balancing to be done between how the other two "voxel ports", ZDoom and Woof, handle things.
Test wad here: odavoxel_v1.zip
Voxels can be loaded from WADs or from an external voxels directory. Voxels contained in server-loaded WADs always take priority over files in the client’s external directory.
Odamex.13.0.0.-.DOOM.2_.Hell.on.Earth.2026-09-16.00-10-54.mp4
New CVARs
r_voxels: Enables software voxel rendering. Defaults to 1.
r_voxeldir: Sets the directory used to load external .kvx files. Defaults to voxels.
sv_allowvoxels: Allows clients to load custom voxel models from their external directory. Defaults to 1.
Setting sv_allowvoxels to 0 only disables client-provided voxels from r_voxeldir. It does not disable voxels contained in WADs loaded by the server. This is a safe guard to prevent clients from replacing important models, such as player sprites, with custom voxels that could provide an advantage in competitive play.
Odamex.13.0.0.-.DOOM.2_.Hell.on.Earth.2026-09-16.00-16-51.mp4
VOXELDEF
Woof doesn't support VOXELDEF, while ZDoom does. We chose to follow ZDoom’s format where possible so existing voxel packs require fewer Odamex-specific changes.
The following properties are currently supported:
AngleOffset
XOffset
YOffset
ZOffset
Spin
PlacedSpin
DroppedSpin
UseActorPitch
The following properties are recognized, but currently ignored with a non-fatal console warning:
Scale
PitchFromMomentum
UseActorRoll
OverridePalette
These were left out because they either 1) need additional renderer work; or 2) do not currently provide enough benefit to justify complicating the initial implementation.
Odamex also adds a new property: FaceViewerPitch
This allows forward-facing voxels, such as spheres and other powerups, to pitch toward the viewer while limiting the maximum angle. This keeps them readable from above or below without allowing extreme rotations (think spheres, they look horrible if they don't tilt toward the player that is above or below). We intend to propose this property to UZDoom so both ports can remain compatible.
When no VOXELDEF behavior is provided, Odamex applies sensible defaults intended to make common voxel replacements look good out of the box. Placed weapon pickups spin, while dropped weapons face the player so they remain easy to identify. Powerups and other special items also face the player, with their rotation limited to avoid extreme angles at wide FOVs. Monsters, projectiles, and other actors retain behavior appropriate to their movement and orientation.

Problem
Hollow STL export decimated and smoothed only the outer shell, then concatenated the marching-cubes inner surface unchanged. That reversed the previous order, where the combined hollow mesh was reduced together.
On a 1,200-face cap the outer shell landed at 1,200 faces and the inner shell stayed near 5,600. The face cap was ignored whenever hollow was on, so the full-resolution cavity could bring back the long export the decimation-before-smoothing change was meant to avoid. Smoothing then moved only the coarse outer wall, and a thin shell could cross the unsmoothed inner surface.
Fix
Both shells now share max_faces in proportion to their current face counts, and both go through the same Taubin and Humphrey passes before they are joined. Smoothing still runs after decimation, so a hollow export does not walk a multi-million-face cavity.
Quadric decimation needs fast-simplification. The fast-test job installs dependencies explicitly and did not include it, so simplification was a no-op and the new face-cap test saw the full marching-cubes mesh. That package is now a project dependency and is installed in CI.
Tests
tests/test_mesh_export.py covers the shared face budget and a hollow sphere export: the saved mesh stays within the face cap, both shells are reduced, and the inner surface remains inside the outer wall.

Into wave/m3-3. #624 and #643 have landed there, and this PR has the wave merged in.
What
This closes #641, the two follow-ups from #624's review of X1's 3D mips.
A chain is built from the item's crop, not the whole source.
mips::Region is the crop in whole source texels.
Level 0 holds those texels one for one: vs_region in mip.wgsl maps the pass onto the region through a small uniform.
Shape::remap_uv maps the item's UVs and UV bounds into the chain's coordinates.
Because the chain holds only the cropped texels, no coarse level averages a texel from past the crop into the item. Clamping to the chain's edge also keeps the anisotropic footprint inside the crop.
An image keeps one chain per crop, the four most recently used (Image::chain).
An import's chain is rebuilt only for a new frame.
feat(render): rebuild 3D mips once per source and frame #643, already in the base, gives each Image a change count (generation, bumped by the public Image::mark_changed), and rewrite_rgba8 and render_into mark uploads and layers. A chain rebuilds only when its image's count has moved.
feat(render): rebuild 3D mips once per source and frame #643 marked a cached import once per cache frame. This PR replaces that with Image::set_frame: the cache counts a change only when it hands the import a new frame, meaning a frame whose FrameSync hold or info, its presentation time included, differs from the last one's (as GpuFrame's equality has it). A platform may hand out one hold for every frame it writes into a texture, as the Windows capture ring does per slot (round 1 B2). The hold is tracked with a Weak, so a reused allocation can't be mistaken for the same hold. This answers feat(render): rebuild 3D mips once per source and frame #643's open question.
feat(render): rebuild 3D mips once per source and frame #643's an_import_rebuilds_its_mips_once_per_frame is adapted: it hands the import a new frame (a new hold on the same texture) each cache frame, and still checks one build per new frame across two mixes.
An import the caller keeps outside the cache is rebuilt only when the caller calls Image::mark_changed, as README step 2 says. The engine imports only through the cache (jstream-core's snapshot.rs).
Tests (tests/three_d.rs, plus place::tests::a_3d_items_region_is_its_crops_texels):
a_3d_items_region_is_its_crops_texels: the region is the crop's whole texels, carried as integers from the item's crop (Shape::crop, round 1 B1). At 1920×1080, columns 63..241 and rows 7..69 give (63, 7, 178, 62), and every whole left and top edge comes out as itself.
golden_3d_a_cropped_item_minified_shows_nothing_past_its_crop: a floor with an off-grid crop of a 512² source, green inside the crop and red outside, minified onto coarse levels. It checks that no covered pixel shows more red than the background. Before the fix it showed red 160.
a_layer_rebuilds_its_mips_once_per_change: two items and two calls build the chain once, and after render_into changes the layer it rebuilds once and shows the new content.
an_import_rebuilds_its_mips_only_for_a_new_frame: through the cache, the same frame doesn't rebuild. A new frame in the same texture does, under a new hold and under the same hold with a later presentation time.
each_crop_gets_its_own_chain.
The CPU reference's bilinear samples are now clamped to the crop, as the chain is. With that, D cropped measures 2.8 on lavapipe (it was 6.6), and its limit tightens from 8 to 5.
Docs: the render README's Purpose, Design (step 2 and the resources table) and Testing (the goldens table and the further tests) are updated, and the open question is removed now that this answers it. The ARCH › GPU pipeline sentence is updated too.
Why
#641 found two problems in #624. Texels just past a crop bled into a cropped 3D item's edge at coarse levels. And layers and imports were rebuilt on every call that drew them, against decision 5's "once per source and frame".

Only if applicable
Hot-loop change: not measured. Per 3D draw, this adds a short mutex lookup over at most four chains and one Arc clone. Rebuild passes drop from one per call to one per change. Two cases churn, though (fix(render): build 3D mip chains from the crop, and rebuild them once per change #687 round 1 N1). A crop that changes every frame makes a new chain every frame, and five or more distinct crops of one source miss the four-entry list on every lookup. Either way, each draw allocates and builds a chain: a texture with its full mip chain, a uniform buffer, and per-level views and bind groups. The evicted chain is dropped on the render thread. Before this PR, one whole-source chain was reused. Reusing an evicted chain's storage when the region size matches is deferred.
Bug fix: golden_3d_a_cropped_item_minified_shows_nothing_past_its_crop fails without the crop-built chain (red 160). The rebuild tests were not run against the old per-call rebuild. By reading the old code, a layer drawn by two calls would count two builds, not one.

Owner
Three additions to the site. The existing background visual engine and its ten shaders are untouched.
Shader Library — a section that grows by one original GLSL shader per day, with live WebGL previews, plus the automation that produces them.
Mandelbulber 3D fractals — distance estimators ported from Mandelbulber v2, and three raymarched shaders built on them.
SP-404 Converter — a browser tool that prepares audio for an SP-404 card and pulls samples back off it.
1 · Shader Library
shaders/manifest.json — one entry per shader (id, date, name, description, useCase, glsl). Sources are stored in Shadertoy format (mainImage, iResolution, iTime, no textures) rather than the homepage's bespoke uniform contract, so each piece stays portable to Shadertoy, TouchDesigner, ISF and Resolume. Renderer. The handoff suggested one WebGL context per card, but browsers allow roughly 16 live contexts and this library gains a card every day. Instead all cards share one context, rendering into an offscreen buffer that is copied to each card's own 2D canvas. Cost per frame is bounded too: the loop runs at ~30fps and redraws at most three visible cards per frame, round-robin, so six heavy cards cost what three do. Programs compile lazily on first scroll-in, the loop stops when nothing is visible or the tab is hidden, prefers-reduced-motion gets one static frame, and context loss is recovered from.
Generated shaders are compile-checked before they ship. The risk with a daily generator is a bad day quietly putting a broken card on the site, so every candidate must survive — in headless Chromium, using the byte-identical wrapper index.html uses — static checks (#version, iChannel*, layout(), top-level out, derivative functions, missing mainImage), a real compile and link, and two rendered frames that reject black output, a full-frame wash, or no animation. Failures are fed back and regenerated up to three times; if none pass the run exits non-zero and the manifest is left unchanged.
2 · Mandelbulber 3D fractals
shaders/formulas/mandelbulber.glsl — mandelbulb, mandelbox, menger_sponge, sierpinski3d, amazing_surf, quaternion, bristorbrot, plus orbit traps and raymarch helpers. Each is named after the Mandelbulber v2 formula it implements, so a preset there translates by matching names. It's a source to copy from, not a module to include — library shaders stay self-contained to remain drop-in elsewhere.
The generator receives this reference as stable, cacheable system context, along with the four framing rules these three fractals actually needed: keep the camera outside the structure, match subdivision depth to what a preview resolves, vary hue smoothly rather than per-pixel, and keep key/fill/rim hues close together on the ramp.
3 · SP-404 Converter (sp404.html)
Runs entirely client-side — no upload, no CDN dependency (the ZIP writer is inline; 16-bit PCM barely deflates).
Audio → SP-404. Decodes whatever the browser reads, resamples, and optionally normalises, trims silence, folds to mono and declicks edges.
SX / A: 44.1 kHz into ROLAND/SP-404SX/SMPL/ as A0000001.WAV…J0000012.WAV, laid out the way Roland's own converter does (RLND header, audio starting at byte 512), plus a PAD_INFO.BIN whose 120 big-endian records point at exactly that offset.
MKII: 48 kHz into IMPORT/, keeping your filenames.
SP-404 → WAV. SX/A cards are read directly: pads come from the filenames and PAD_INFO.BIN supplies the start/end points, so you get the sample as the pad actually plays it. The MKII keeps samples in internal memory, so there is nothing on the card until EXPORT SAMPLE has written it — this then cleans up what the unit wrote. When no conversion is requested the audio is copied through byte for byte under a fresh 44-byte header; decoding and re-encoding would cost a least significant bit for nothing.
On the SX, pad assignments live in PAD_INFO.BIN, not the audio files — so a naive tool would silently wipe every pad already on the card. An existing file can be loaded and is merged; untouched pads come back byte-identical.
The SX format is not documented by Roland, so it was researched rather than recalled. Two details conflicted across sources and went with weight of evidence: the file is PAD_INFO.BIN (three working tools that read real cards use it) and samples are a bank letter plus seven digits (the official manual). The MKII's export filenames are undocumented, so unrecognised names pass through unchanged rather than being guessed at and mislabelled.
Verification
All six shaders compile, render and animate, inspected visually at several timestamps including t=900s to rule out float-precision drift in a long-lived tab.
Building the fractals visually caught defects a compile check alone passes: a camera sitting inside the Mandelbox, a Menger sponge whose fifth subdivision aliased into speckle, and three colour faults — a constant-hue glow term swamping surface shading, per-pixel hue variation that shading averaged into grey, and key/fill/rim hues far enough apart to sum to white.
Shader validator negative-tested: broken syntax, black output, washed-out output, #version, iChannel, and missing mainImage each rejected.
Wrapper parity between index.html and scripts/shader-validate.mjs asserted by comparison, not just by comment.
Converter tested end to end on real audio: ZIPs pass unzip -t; WAVs parse with a standard reader despite the RLND chunk; resampling preserves pitch (900 Hz stays 900 Hz through 22k→44.1k); normalise lands on −1 dBFS; silence is not amplified; PAD_INFO offsets match actual file sizes; the merge leaves other pads byte-identical; assignment stops cleanly at J12.
Round trip: build a card with prepare mode, extract it back — audio bit-identical, Roland chunk gone, stored trim applied to the exact frame, rate conversion correct.
Testing caught three real converter bugs: the result message being instantly overwritten, an unusable mobile table, and MKII filename collisions producing duplicate ZIP entries.
No console errors from these changes; no horizontal overflow at 390px on any page.
Notes and caveats
The daily workflow becomes live on merge. It needs repo secret ANTHROPIC_API_KEY (Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions) and will fail its 13:00 UTC run each day until that exists. Worth triggering Daily Shader Drop once via workflow_dispatch to confirm generate → commit → deploy before trusting the schedule.
Node tooling lives in scripts/, not the repo root, so the static deploy isn't mistaken for a Node build.
Generation uses claude-opus-5 (override with ANTHROPIC_MODEL).
Cron is 0 13 * * * UTC — 9am EDT, 8am EST. GitHub cron has no DST handling; a comment in the workflow notes switching to 0 14 * * * for winter.
Preview frame rate was measured under software rendering (SwiftShader), far slower than any real GPU — an upper bound on cost, not a prediction. Worth a look on a real low-end device.
The converter is verified correct and self-consistent but has not been tested on actual hardware; the SX path rests on reverse-engineered specs. Try one sample on a backed-up card before trusting it with a full bank.
The PAT pasted in the earlier planning chat should be treated as compromised and rotated — this design never needs it, since the workflow pushes with the built-in GITHUB_TOKEN.

A browser tool that turns any audio into card-ready SP-404 samples. Runs
entirely client-side — no upload, no dependencies, no build step.

- sp404.html: drop audio in, get a ZIP out. Decodes whatever the browser
  can read, resamples, optionally normalises, trims silence, folds to mono
  and declicks the edges, then writes 16-bit PCM.

  SP-404SX / SP-404A: 44.1kHz into ROLAND/SP-404SX/SMPL/ named A0000001.WAV
  through J0000012.WAV, laid out the way Roland's own converter does — an
  RLND header with the audio starting at byte 512 — plus a PAD_INFO.BIN
  whose 120 big-endian records point at exactly that offset. An existing
  PAD_INFO.BIN can be loaded and is merged, so pads the run does not touch
  keep their assignments instead of being wiped.

  SP-404MKII: 48kHz into IMPORT/, keeping the original filenames. They are
  sanitised for FAT and de-duplicated, since two sources can sanitise to the
  same name and duplicate ZIP entries unpack unpredictably.

  The ZIP is written here rather than pulled from a CDN: 16-bit PCM barely
  deflates, so storing costs a percent or two and keeps the site
  dependency-free.

- index.html: nav entry and a section linking to the converter, reusing the
  existing card styling.

Verified end to end against real audio: ZIPs pass unzip -t, the WAVs parse
with a standard reader despite the RLND chunk, rates and bit depth are
right, resampling preserves pitch, normalise lands on -1 dBFS, silence is
not amplified, PAD_INFO offsets match actual file sizes, the merge leaves
other pads byte-identical, and assignment stops cleanly at J12.

The converter now runs both directions, picked with a mode switch.

Extract mode takes the files off a card and gives back plain WAVs:

- SP-404SX / A cards are read directly. Samples are matched to their pads
  from the filename, and PAD_INFO.BIN, if present, supplies the start and
  end points so the export is the sample as the pad actually plays it
  rather than the whole untrimmed file.

- SP-404MKII keeps its samples in internal memory, so there is nothing on
  the card to read until EXPORT SAMPLE has written it there. This cleans up
  what the unit wrote: strips the Roland header, converts the rate, renames
  and batches. Its export naming is undocumented, so unrecognised names
  pass through untouched instead of being guessed at.

When no conversion is asked for, the audio is copied through byte for
byte under a fresh 44-byte header — decoding and re-encoding would cost a
least significant bit for nothing. Verified bit-identical in both
directions.

Also handled: non-PCM and non-audio files in the folder are listed and
skipped rather than failing the run, output names are de-duplicated, and
an SX filename that is already a pad code is not doubled up into
"B05 B0000005.wav".

Verified by round trip — build a card with prepare mode, extract it back,
and compare: audio bit-identical, Roland chunk gone, stored trim points
applied to the frame, rate conversion correct, prepare mode unaffected.

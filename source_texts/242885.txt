Exclude boundary reflections from the scattering-event limit
When a Fresnel reflection occurs, this continue still advances the surrounding step counter even though result.events is unchanged. Consequently, callers using a small maximumEvents can receive Dropped for a high-index or grazing path that has had few or even zero scattering events, contrary to the setting's documented meaning; count actual volume events separately from boundary bounces.

Reduce the quadratic H-function reference calculation
Every run of this test executes the inner sum 4000 × 4000 × 400 times for each of three albedos, totaling 19.2 billion floating-point iterations before the random-walk checks themselves. This makes the default CTest target take tens of seconds even in an optimized build and substantially longer in debug/sanitizer CI; precompute the three reference values or use a converged, much smaller quadrature.

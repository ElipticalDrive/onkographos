Stacked on #230.
Three area lights join the quad. Each is visible to the camera, sampled by NEE and MIS-weighted where a BSDF ray hits it.
Disk: exact solid-angle sampling by Guillén et al.'s (2017) radial spherical-ellipse map, with Newton on Boost.Math's ellint_3. It is not tabulated, because the table's rejected samples bias the image.
Sphere: cone sampling (PBR 4e 6.2.4), cancellation-free.
Mesh: a glTF's faces picked by area and sampled uniformly (Heitz 2019); it pivots on its bounding-box centre.
Disks and spheres are Embree's native discs and spheres, so the surface hit is the one NEE samples.
Scenes without a mesh light render bit-identically (cornell, cornell_checker, macbeth, stump at 256x128 @ 16).
Material emission stays BSDF-only, and light selection stays uniform; both are on the roadmap.
Tests:
Disk solid angle against Gauss-Kronrod quadrature, to float rounding.
Disk and sphere samples uniform in solid angle.
Sample and hit pdfs agree for every shape.
MIS agreement for each shape.
Irradiance against closed forms.
Embree shape hits against an analytic solve.
Config parsing and rejection.
Placement and pivot.
Rotation of every type through the API.
Also: omnidirectional_film_filter_reaches_across_the_seam read indeterminate PathTraceSettings; it is now value-initialized.

Scope beyond the criteria, now built
Shadow levels: shadow passes draw the level the key light's cascade texels allow, never finer than the camera's level, which is what receives the shadow. It draws as a cast-only instance inset by its error (at most a shadow texel), so it never stands out of the surface the camera sees.
budget.lod_px: a document may lower the 1 px; it can't raise it.
Proxy band: draws a level that keeps params.ratio of the triangles within params.error. A mesh without levels gets a meshoptimizer proxy made once per content key, never per frame.

A rung whose memory would pass the class's cap is stepped over, both ways.

Renderer fixes this needed
One view drew one of two pictures at random: one pixel, one code value apart.
Cause 1: back faces discarded in the fragment shader shared pixel quads with a front face's silhouette, which moved the specular antialiasing's dpdx(n).
Fix 1: opaque material solids cull back faces in the pipeline (front faces when mirrored). Nothing culls under a capped cutaway, double-sided or see-through material.
Cause 2: inside a margin, the box band drew first and cut the ball's quads with its nearer depth.
Fix 2: a margin's less detailed band draws last, and a band's mask discards only after shading.
Checked: 16 plays of the orbit draw identical frames.
Shadow levels finer than the camera's level blotched the rock, because the coarser surface caught the finer caster's shadow. Hence the never-finer rule and the inset above.

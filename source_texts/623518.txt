What was added
src/engine/sdf/ is a 2D SDF library in plain TypeScript, ported from Inigo Quilez's articles. You import it by path. It stays out of the engine barrel.
basic.ts, polygons.ts, arcs.ts, curves.ts: every primitive the issue lists, from the 2D distance functions article. Each takes (px, py, ...params) and returns a number. Shapes are centred on the origin, y-up, negative inside.
gradients.ts: the 17 sdg* functions from 2D SDFs and gradients. Each returns d and writes the gradient into an out pair you reuse, so hot loops don't allocate. This is the same pattern matter/glass.ts uses.
ellipse.ts: the analytic ellipse, plus the Newton and trig-free solvers from Distance to an ellipse.
corners.ts: sdBoxCorners with circle, parabola, cosine and cubic corners from Smooth rounded boxes.
linf.ts: eight of the L∞ primitives: box, circle, ellipse, parabola, segment, rounded box, rhombus and oriented box.
ops.ts: opRound, opOnion, sdgRound and sdgOnion.
field.ts: sdfField(grid, sd, { cx, cy, scale, angle, flipY }) turns a bound SDF into a Field in cell units, the way light/sdf.ts does.
index.ts re-exports the five light/sdf.ts helpers. That file is unchanged and its callers keep their imports.
THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md has the MIT notice and the list of ported articles. Every ported function carries // after Inigo Quilez, <article URL> (MIT). The credits the articles give (wutzhuhn for the parabolic corner, oneshade for the L∞ trick) are carried over.
The module is deterministic. Trig, cube roots and acos come from noise/dmath. src/engine/sdf/ is added to DET_PATHS, so a card drawn with it will hash the same in every engine.
Where the port differs from the GLSL
On a cell grid, samples land exactly on axes and centres. At those points a few of the GLSL versions return 0 or NaN where the field should be smooth, so I fixed them. Each fix is noted in its doc comment.
sdRhombus, sdStairs and sdParabola (and sdgParabola): sign(0) made a line of zeros past a tip or below a vertex.
sdParabola, sdParabolaSegment and sdBlobbyCross: the trig branch divides 0/0 where p = q = 0.
sdHyperbola: the solve is 0/0 at the centre.
sdEllipse: the analytic ellipse falls back to the circle when a = b, and to the robust solver at the isolated points where its quartic is 0/0.
sdBoxCorners: the article scales the corner distance by √2·r. The uv frame is scaled by √2/r, so the right factor is r/√2. With that factor the circle corner matches sdRoundedBox to 2e-15.
sdParabolaSegment, sdHyperbola and sdParabolaLinf are open curves. Past their ends the sign follows the extended curve, so the field flips there. That comes from the original code and is documented, and the checks sweep them only where they are meant to be used.
How it was checked
npx tsx scripts/sdf.ts: 93 checks pass. For each of the 67 primitive cases it checks the distance at known points, worked out from the geometry. It also checks the sign, 1-Lipschitz on 3000 random pairs and on a 0.125 lattice through the axes, and |∇d| = 1 at 2000 random points for the exact ones (2000/2000 on every exact case). For the 20 sdg* cases, the gradient matches a central difference, |g| = 1, and d matches the plain SDF. It also checks that the circle corner equals sdRoundedBox, that the cosine and cubic corners have no curvature jump at the join (0.003, against 0.909 for the circle), the sdfField adapter, that the light/sdf.ts helpers are bit-identical (FNV 8126e108 on a fixed sweep, pinned from main) and the determinism lint on the module.
npx tsc -b: clean.
npx oxlint on the changed files: clean.
npx tsx scripts/light.ts: passes.
npx tsx scripts/noise.ts: passes. The determinism lint now scans 59 files under 12/12 paths. A few timing budgets warn under load, which isn't enforced.
pnpm golden --check: all 217 presets match, 0 changed.
There are no new cards, and first-load imports are unchanged.
Left for later
scripts/sdf.ts is not wired into verify.ts yet.
Two L∞ primitives aren't ported yet: the capsule and the arc.
The gradient form of smooth min from the gradients article goes with the IQ2 operators.
The article's sdgTrapezoid also returns the closest point. This port leaves that out.
The rounded boxes article has no code for a hyperbolic corner, so there isn't one here.
Cards that use the library, and their credit field (Tixy: tixy() engine adapter and card credit metadata #53).

```
Ports Inigo Quilez's 2D distance functions to plain TypeScript, imported by
path and kept out of the engine barrel:

- every primitive from the 2D distance functions article, plus opRound and
  opOnion
- the sdg* functions with analytic gradients (distance returned, gradient
  written into a reused out pair), plus sdgRound and sdgOnion
- the analytic, Newton and trig-free ellipse
- rounded boxes with circle, parabola, cosine and cubic corners
- eight of the L-infinity primitives
- sdfField, which turns a bound SDF into a cell-unit Field

The module uses the deterministic math from noise/dmath and is registered in
DET_PATHS. A few GLSL sign(0) and 0/0 spots that leave holes on a cell grid
are fixed and noted in the doc comments. light/sdf.ts is unchanged and
re-exported from the new index.

scripts/sdf.ts checks known distances, signs, 1-Lipschitz sweeps, unit
gradients, sdg* against finite differences and their plain forms, the C2
corners, the Field adapter and a pinned hash of the light/sdf.ts helpers.

THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md holds the MIT notice and the list of ported articles.
```

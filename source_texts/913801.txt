Adds an Export format box to BatchDentalSeg, offering NIFTI, STL, OBJ, VTK and VTK (merged) — the same choice the local module had, which was never ported because it ran inside Slicer.
mesh_export.py does the surfaces server-side, so every consumer gets them and not only the callers running in Slicer. One marching-cubes pass per label feeds every format ticked; surface_decimation bounds the triangle count, which is what made AMASSS's raw surfaces unusable.
NIFTI alone by default, so every earlier call means what it always did. vtk is pinned at AMASSS's version and pulls no torch of its own.
9 new tests, 48 passing.

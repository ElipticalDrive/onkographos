Questions on Camera Projection and Ray Marching (Sebastian Lague's Clouds)?
Hey all,
I'm trying to learn ray marching using Sebastian Lague's clouds tutorial. In particular, in his raymarch vertex shader, he has the following:
```
// Camera space matches OpenGL convention where cam forward is -z. In unity forward is positive z.
// (https://docs.unity3d.com/ScriptReference/Camera-cameraToWorldMatrix.html)
float3 viewVector = mul(unity_CameraInvProjection, float4(v.uv * 2 - 1, 0, -1));
output.viewVector = mul(unity_CameraToWorld, float4(viewVector,0));
```
And I'm a bit confused at why this works for him. In particular why he chose float4(v.uv * 2 - 1, 0, -1) and float4(viewVector,0).
From what I can tell, since v.uv comes from the float2 uv : TEXCOORD0 in appdata, it should be limited to [0, 1]. Which means that float4(v.uv * 2 - 1, 0, -1) is the NDC coordinate of the vertex, with z on the near plane, and w at -1 is because unity's forward z is -1 whereas opengl's camera forward z is +1.
But what I don't understand is that if the camera inverse projection just inverses the calculation that the camera projection does, and the camera projection takes the camera space coordinates, and transforms them into clip space coordinates (which the rasterizer later uses to get the NDC coordinates), why is he multiplying the NDC coordinate by unity_CameraInvProjection rather than getting the clip coordinate first?
And I also don't understand why for float4(viewVector,0), he made the w component 0 instead of 1. Afaik all camera/local/world etc... coordinates are meant to have w = 1?
Any help is much appreciated.

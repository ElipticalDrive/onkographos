Feature Request Overview:
Many of the volume rendering presets seem to have been tailored for a specific rendering technique (e.g., presets with "MIP" were likely created to look best with the maximum intensity projection technique). To minimize clicks and greatly simplify rendering workflows, it would be nice to have the preferred "technique" baked into the preset. For example, if you switch to a MIP preset, then the volume rendering module could automatically switch the rendering technique. If we eventually incorporate the changes proposed here, volume rendering workflows could become much more streamlined and repeatable. With the increased power of presets, we could even create a nice simple toolbar for switching between rendering presets outside of the volume rendering module panel.

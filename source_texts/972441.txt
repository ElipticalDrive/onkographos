Closes #1472
Implements mesh_blocks in crates/mesh/src/mesh.rs, the core of meshBlocks (docs/design/viewer-rendering.md Phase E, E.1): column scan for minY/meanY, clip at y0 (8 below the lowest surface), the at lookup with the pad ring, and one quad per visible face of full cubes, ice and water. Fast leaves count as solid but are not drawn. Layers come from a flat faceLayer table in the faceLayers layout; shade is f.s * (0.75 + y/256), tint white, AO 0, water depth in byte 15 (capped at 255). Output: opaque, water, ice and cutout buffers (cutout empty for now), meanY and minY.
Tests cover face counts, culling against solids and pad cells, the clip at y0, water depth and its cap, ice, fast leaves, and identical output for two offset copies of the same terrain.
SPEC sections: none (this is the project's own viewer code, not Beta behaviour).
World output: unchanged for a fixed seed (no terrain code touched). Wasm size unchanged: the crate isn't built into any wasm yet.

Owner
Closes K5, K6 (Chapter 2B, part 1). Stacked on #11: merge that first, then retarget this to main. K7 (per-voxel corner controls) follows in a second 2B PR.
What
SurfaceRaycast (Core), K5: finds the voxel whose piece of surface a ray hits first. It walks the voxels along the ray (VoxelRaycast, Amanatides & Woo) and polygonises each one that has a surface, stopping at the first whose own triangles the ray hits (RayTriangle, Möller–Trumbore). No mesh collider needed, and voxels without surface can't be picked.
VoxelSelector (Lab): a left click selects the voxel under the visible surface, and a click that hits no surface clears the selection. The selection gets a yellow line-mesh wireframe that shows even with gizmos off, plus a label with its coordinate and case index. A chunk resize clears it.
K6: F calls the new FreeFlyCamera.Focus, which glides the camera (keeping its rotation) to frame the voxel. Any movement input cancels the glide.
StepThroughMarker.mat is renamed LabMarker.mat (same GUID), since the highlight shares it.
Merges 2A: ChunkLab structure (K1-K4, K30) #11's latest work (volume stats, test shapes). ChunkVolumeStats was re-added to the scene by hand because both branches had used the same scene fileID.
No more gizmos for chunk visuals (K3): ChunkGizmos becomes ChunkDebugView (same GUID). The density spheres are one vertex-coloured mesh, and the outline and voxel grid are line meshes, because Unity's gizmo pass on D3D12 dims gizmos against a flipped depth buffer (the dark disc that mirrored the ball). Spheres are suppressed above 40,000 samples.
Acceptance
Edit-mode tests: 113/113 pass, including 7 for VoxelRaycast and 5 for SurfaceRaycast/RayTriangle. One of those checks that a ray passing through a surface voxel without touching its triangles is not a hit.
Tested in Play mode through MCP. Picking at the ball's centre selects (5, 5, 2), on the visible surface; picking at its top selects (4, 7, 3); empty space returns nothing. The highlight and label render, and F frames the voxel.
Real mouse clicks couldn't be simulated (the Input System ignores queued events while the Game view is unfocused), so a manual click check is still needed. Pick is public, and that's what the test drove.
Notes
The wireframe is depth-tested, so the parts behind the surface are hidden.

thinks are hidden using a vertically flipped depth buffer, so gizmos

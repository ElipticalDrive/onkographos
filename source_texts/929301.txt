Owner direction (2026-10-30): maps with landform mutators (deltas, mesas, cliffs, basins, lakes...) get their features from topography generation, not from rules applied to the finished topography, so features stop fighting the terrain.
Already in the erosion model: Mountain, Valley, Cliffs, Plateau (LandformExtension), world river valleys, plains features, the sea as base level, delta land from deposition (#56).
Gaps: geology off for Basin/Hollow/Chasm/Cavern/Dunes/TerraformingScar (R18); vanilla lakes painted over the heightmap (R16); headwater hollow and island knob drawn after erosion (R17); coastline land shaping after erosion; delta distributaries (#58).
New principle P23: closed-basin water balance. Plan: tools/Docs/LANDFORM-PLAN.md.
Steps:
Landforms step 0: per-mutator landform check (-ll-check-landform) and baseline #72
Landforms step 1: closed basins in the erosion model (P23 water balance); Basin keeps geology on #73
Landforms step 2: lake mutators as closed basins filled by the lake model (retire R16) #74
Landforms step 3: headwater hollow and river-island knob in the erosion model (retire WaterTerrainNudges, R17) #75
Landforms step 4: rim and weak-zone shapes: Hollow, Chasm, LavaCrater in the topography #76
Landforms step 5: coastline keeps only bathymetry; land shaping from the sea base level #77
Delta step 4: distributaries from the data layers on delta land (avulsion node, superelevation, mouth bars) #58 delta distributaries from the layers
Out of scope for now: Dunes (aeolian model), TerraformingScar, Cavern/caves, glacial mutators, Fjord as a glacial trough.

Summary
Ports the multi-resolution pyramid erosion of KTT Smooth Fluvial Erosion (Houdini 22.0) and its updated erosion kernel to Bevy GPU compute.
Pyramid cascade
Level schedule follows KTT Compute_Padding: levels = log2(Feature_Size * Detail_Scale / voxel) + 1. Each level runs 200 * max(VoxelScale, 1)^(Geological_Age / 10) erosion passes plus one writeBack. A fractional level count fades in the first pass through its trail weight.
Level transitions (assets/pyramid_resample.wgsl, assets/pyramid_rivers.wgsl):
The erosion delta is Gaussian-upsampled ×2 and added back onto the bilinear-sampled source terrain.
Smooth_Flows blends deep deposits toward the previous level's smooth surface.
River momentum and deposition carry over to the next level; flow lines are reset.
Driver: a Prepare-stage planner (Cascade::advance) builds each frame's dispatch list. Per-dispatch ErodeParams sit in a DynamicUniformBuffer, so several iterations can run per frame. Two work buffer sets ping-pong next to an untouched source set.
UI: the manual level slider is replaced by the cascade controls (Feature Size, Detail Smoothing, Geological Age, Iterations / Frame, Continue At Finest) and a live status line.
Houdini 22 kernel (assets/erosion.wgsl)
The river field __m (mx, my, stored as f32) replaces macc. The protrusion coefficient replaces the old peak suppression.
Momentum coherence steering and per-step river friction.
New options: Erosion Cutoff Angle, Clamp River Channeling, and Hydro Conditioning (pit search on every 5th pass).
KTT masks and layers
Softness mask, outflow (Sharp or Accumulate), erode mask, uplift map, bedrock (depth, depth map, additive sediment), granularity noise and the Age field.
The masks are procedural (noise patches; sea level and map borders for outflow), since there are no painted layers. A new masks pass (assets/pyramid_masks.wgsl) resamples them at every pyramid level.
New Age preview mode (key 8).
Testing
cargo test: 9 unit tests for the level schedule and the cascade planner.
Readback check (temporary example, not committed): ran the full 4-level cascade, read the height texture back, and counted cells above 3. Normal heights stay under 1.3.
Clean with rivers off, coherence 0 and coherence 1, and with hydro conditioning, clamped channeling and a 60° cutoff all on.
A race-induced spike bug from the first port is fixed: the dh clamp now re-reads the cell, matching the OpenCL reference.
Known issues / reviewer notes
Fixed a race where many trails converging on one river cell (strong momentum coherence) stacked their cuts and wrapped the u32 height. The height update is now one clamped compare-exchange. There were no outliers in 25 runs at coherence 5 and 10 at coherence 10, compared with about 1 run in 5 before.
Display textures now have COPY_SRC usage, needed for the readback check.
Masks and layers were checked by GPU readback against an un-eroded run:
Outflow cells keep their source height.
Hard rock wears 3–5× less than soft rock.
Bedrock caps the cut at its depth, and additive sediment never cuts below the source.
Cells where the erode mask is 0 change 4.5× less.
Eroded cells come out younger than untouched ones.
Bedrock_Depth and Granularity_Strength defaults are scaled down for this map's roughly 1-unit terrain height. KTT's strength of 5 shreds the surface here.
Not ported: Compute_Padding (level sizes round up instead, which is identical for power-of-two maps), bedrock Height mode, and export fields other than Age.

```
Follow ref/houdini22/compute_erosion.cl:
- River field is __m (mx, my) instead of macc: |m| with momentum
  coherence, mx as scalar water otherwise. Stored as f32 bits with a
  compare-exchange atomic add.
- Protrusion coefficient keeps rivers off local maxima (replaces the
  writeBack peak suppression).
- Momentum coherence steers toward normalize(m) * |v|; friction applies
  on every river step.
- Erosion_Cutoff_Angle ends trails on steep drops.
- writeBack: scarring scaled by min(1, dx^character), river channeling
  carve with optional Clamp_River_Channeling.
- Optional hydro conditioning drains pits every 5th iteration.

Clamp dh against a fresh height read like the reference; clamping
against the step-start height let concurrent trails push cells past
their neighbours and wrap the fixed-point height buffer.

Display textures get COPY_SRC so they can be read back.

The softness mask scales Rock_Softness per cell. Outflow cells do not erode,
deposit, uplift or carve rivers, and Smooth_Flows resets them to the source

Add KTT bedrock, erode mask, uplift map, granularity and Age
…
f932635
```
Bedrock follows Adjust_Bedrock: it sits Bedrock_Depth below the source
terrain (optionally scaled by a depth map), or at the source surface with
a sediment layer added on top (Additive_Sediment). Trails never cut into
it, trails starting on exposed bedrock are culled, and upscaled heights
are clamped to it at each pyramid level.

The erode mask culls trail starts where it is 0 and scales
Erosion_Strength elsewhere. The uplift map scales Uplift_Amount. Smooth_Flows
adds granularity noise on deep deposits. The Age field grows every dispatch
and decays where trails change the height; it is carried through the pyramid
and shown in a new Age preview mode (key 8).

The per-cell masks and layers are packed into two buffers (vec4 masks,
vec2 bedrock/age) so the erosion group stays within 8 storage buffers per
stage. Bedrock_Depth and Granularity_Strength defaults are scaled down for
this map's roughly 1-unit terrain height.

Fix river cells wrapping below zero under strong momentum coherence
…
52d826c
```
KTT applies each trail's height change as a plain read-modify-write, so
concurrent trails on one cell lose updates but the result stays inside the
clamp to the neighbourhood range. The port split it into a fresh read plus
atomicAdd/atomicSub, which applies every concurrent cut sized against the
same reading. Where momentum coherence funnels many trails into a river
cell they stacked up, drove the cell below zero and wrapped the u32
height (seen as a ~4250 spike in about 1 run in 5 at coherence 5).

Apply the clamp, outflow scale and bedrock floor inside one
compare-exchange against the cell's current value, and use the applied
change for deposition, erosion, flow and age.
```

Coarse levels are cheap, so spreading them over frames only delayed the

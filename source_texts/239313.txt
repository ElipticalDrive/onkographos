Outcome
Visibility is decided in a compute shader from bounding spheres, not on the CPU.
Acceptance criteria
A compute pass tests every object against the frustum
The visible list is compacted on the GPU
The CPU submits a camera and a dispatch, never a per-object loop
Visible and culled counts are reported in the HUD
Notes
Blender's draw manager loops over objects on the CPU, which is why large scenes become CPU-bound long before the GPU is busy. Moving the decision to the GPU is the structural fix.

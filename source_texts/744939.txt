Scope
```
terrain meadow 16x16 cell=1 step=0.25 palette=field style=smooth
  heights
    0000111122221111
    0001122233332211
    ...
  paint
    gggggggggggggggg
    ggggsssggggggggg
    ...
```
terrain NAME WxD cell=METERS step=METERS palette=P. heights is a grid of one character per vertex (0-9, then a-z for 10-35), each times step. The grid has W+1 by D+1 vertices; its rows run from back (-Z) to front (+Z), as voxel levels do.
paint is a grid of palette keys, one per cell (W by D).
style=smooth (triangulated grid, smooth normals, the gentle hills of a cozy scene), style=flat (flat-shaded triangles, low-poly look) or style=terraced (each cell a flat top at its height with vertical sides, a voxel-like look). The diagonal of each smooth cell is chosen by a fixed rule (the shorter diagonal, ties to one documented side) so the result is deterministic.
Optional skirt=DEPTH closes the sides downward, for terrain seen at an edge.
Acceptance criteria
Tests check vertex positions, triangle counts and normals for each style on a 2×2 grid.
Wrong grid sizes and unknown height characters give their codes and positions.
The preview shows the terrain in its four views; the top view's colors match paint cell by cell.
Out of scope
Noise (next issue), paths and water (painting issue).
Touches: new src/terrain.rs, src/document.rs, src/preview.rs, tests/terrain.rs.

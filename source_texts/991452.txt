The answer to the second question above is significantly more complicated and space does not permit a detailed description of the inequalities that
must be satisfied. In order to guarantee a bicubic polynomial is monotone
in ma element it is sufficient that a collection of linear inequalities involving
the Hermite coordinates be satisfied. If the inequalities are not satisfied by
the initial Hermite derivatives, the magnitude of one (or more) derivatives is

one or more Hermite derivatives may have a cascading effect, in that additional values may have to be changed along a row or a column. In no case is
the sign of a Hermite derivative changed. Once an inequality is satisfied, it is
never subsequently violated due to a change in one or more Hermite coordinates. Once all of the inequalities are satisfied, ma appropriate
set of values
for (02uf/OxOy)i,j

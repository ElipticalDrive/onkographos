Every method is pure overhead (up to 5.3× total runtime) against a ~10% peak-memory saving. The locality it buys has no consumer — indexing is per-feature, with no cross-feature spatial operations — and the two problems it historically mitigated (write fragmentation across parent partitions; per-partition compaction incompleteness) were both fixed structurally in earlier releases (sorted single-writer output + merge; compaction after a parent-cell shuffle).
Removes -s/--spatial_sorting, the spatial_shuffle stage, and the sort-column plumbing through the worker path. CLI-breaking for -s users (the option vanishes); appropriate for the next minor release.

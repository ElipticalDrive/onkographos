Summary
This adds the integration engine for the immersed IGA-FEM unit-cell fallback in the printed-polymer plan.
python/immersed/cut3d.py is a verbatim copy of fv/cut3d.py from kfbim-octree (commit c3f1253), with a credit header added. Aleksey, who wrote it, approved the copy. kfbim-octree itself is not touched.
python/immersed/cut_quadrature.py wraps it in three calls:
cut_cells(V, T, lo, h, n) checks that the solid lies strictly inside the grid (cut3d needs a margin). It keeps the raw Dunavant fans (qdeg=0), because cut3d's compressed rule is exact only to degree 2.
volume_moments / volume_rule(g, p≤3) computes the moments ∫ξ^α dV on each cut cell with the divergence theorem. The flux is degree ≤ 4, which cut3d's rules integrate exactly. It then fits minimum-norm weights on the (p+1)³ Gauss points of the cell, and full cells get tensor Gauss.
surface_rule returns the STL surface split by cell. It moves triangles lying on an upper grid plane from the inactive cell above to the active one below.
Checklist
Theory. Divergence-theorem moments are exact on polyhedral (STL) solids for degree ≤ 3, and degree 4 and up raises an error. The moment-fitted rule is exact only for integrands of total degree ≤ p. A B-spline stiffness integrand has higher degree, so on cut cells it is an approximation (measured below).
Physics. This is geometry and quadrature only, with no solver yet. The case of solid faces lying on grid planes (a unit cell) is gated.
Numerics. Moment fitting gives negative weights: the minimum is −2.1× the Gauss weight. That is a known property of moment fitting and can weaken positivity of the stiffness on small cuts. Before the solver lands we need either a non-negative fit (NNLS) or a small-cut stabilisation.
Speed. The pytest file takes 1.5 s warm and about 40 s on the first numba JIT. A sphere at n=64 (12k cut cells) takes 10.5 s for build plus rule.

Degree 4 converges at about h⁵ (ratio ≈ 30 per halving). Degree 6 stops improving at n=64, probably from round-off amplified by the negative weights. I have not investigated that yet.
Tests (python3 -m pytest python/immersed -q, 6 passed)
Box off the grid: volume, closure and degree ≤ 3 moments vs closed form, < 1e-12.
Box on grid planes (the unit-cell case): per-cell volume = h³, no orphan surface points, area = 6, ∮n = 0.
Icosphere: moments vs tet fan < 1e-11. The polyhedron is 0.86% under the true ball volume, which is facet sag.
64-gon cylinder: volume vs closed form < 1e-12, moments < 1e-11.
Hollow box with a 0.3h wall around a void (infill-like, two surfaces per cell): volume and moments vs closed form.
Guards: p = 4 and a missing grid margin both raise.

Summary
Campaign million-entity-render, slice D0.3 (fleet:wip until the campaign checkpoint), stacked on the D0.2 PR: a voxel canvas casts sun shadow from the cells it rasterizes. BAKE_SUN_SHADOW_MAP::bakeVoxelFaces drops the authored-grid caster mode for revoxelized canvases (the useSource branch, the frame's source-grid fields, binding 9 and the mode flag in c_bake_voxel_sun_faces.glsl and its Metal twin, sourceFaceCoverage_); the frame UBO is 48 bytes. IRCanvasStress --source-face-shadows / --voxel-face-shadows are accepted and ignored, so every capture recipe in the older design notes still runs; both flags produce byte-identical captures to the default.
scripts/render-revox-face-metric.py --shadow-overlay now casts one ray per displayed triangle from its geometric centroid, the point the lighting pass samples, instead of one per face, and applies the one-pixel band at every trixel edge. It classifies each false-shadow trixel by its ray's closest approach to an occupied cell: false shadow within --terminator-tolerance (half a cell) is grazing, the nearest-texel read at a terminator, and passes; clear-ray false shadow and any missed shadow fail. raster_polygon fills a list as well as a byte mask so the owner map can carry trixel ids.
Trace of the near-riser tread band, caster first: it was the per-face oracle's own floor. Per trixel, the default casters show missed shadow 0 at all five yaws and false shadow only on trixels whose rays pass within a third of a cell of an occupied cell; across the four yaws with self-shadow, 370 lit trixels with clearance of 0.35 cells or more never read shadowed. The bake and the surface receiver were read end to end with no defect; the residual is the sun map's nearest-texel read. The authored-caster captures fail the same gate with clear-ray false shadow at 0.55–0.67 cells.
Docs: authored-voxel-shadow-faces.md marked retired with the measurement that retired it; finite-shadow-defaults.md and voxel-sun-face-coverage.md state the flags are no-ops; revoxelized-display-fidelity.md § Direct sun carries the per-trixel table and the trace; the render prefab CLAUDE.md bake bullet names the rule; VALIDATION row updated; campaign ledger, decisions and ## Now advanced to D0.4.

Decisions taken
The oracle tolerates false shadow on a trixel whose centroid ray passes within half a cell of an occupied cell (the observed maximum is a third of a cell) and fails on any clear-ray false shadow or missed shadow. Rejected: a receiver-side bias to absorb the residual, and a tighter tolerance derived from the sun texel size, which the oracle cannot read from a capture.
The stale IRCanvasStress reference set is re-blessed off master (canvas_stress macos-debug render-verify references stale since the September render stacks #3552), not inside this stack.

* perf: IRPerfGrid --yaw takes radians, as documented

The flag's help text, every recipe under docs/perf and 70 committed
arms pass radians, but the demo handed the value to
IRRender::setCameraVisualYaw, which takes degrees. --yaw 0.785398163
was a 0.785 degree pose, so every IRPerfGrid row labelled 45 degrees
was measured near-cardinal. The demo now calls IRPrefab::Camera::setYaw
like every other demo and logs the pose it took.

Control (docs/perf/perf-grid-yaw-unit.md): before the fix --yaw 90 runs
the cardinal gather path and --yaw 1.5707963 the per-axis scatter path;
after it they swap, and the cull counts of the new 0.785398163 run
match the old --yaw 45 run to the digit. Captures never depended on the
flag (each shot sets its own yaw), and the IRPerfGrid render-verify
baseline passes 21 of 21 byte-exact. repeat_profile.py now fails an
IRPerfGrid run whose logged pose is not its --yaw in radians.

The campaign doc records this slice, the lane split against the open
shadow stack, and the corrections the stack sanity review made to
earlier campaign work.

* perf: address the checkpoint review of the --yaw unit fix

The pose gate passed a non-finite request (NaN compares False) and
crashed after the run on a value Python cannot parse but atof accepts;
both are now refused before the run. The erratum names the three design
documents and the objective's 1.33x zoom baseline that were also taken
at the 0.785 degree pose, states the flag's history correctly (degrees
at introduction, a help text that later said radians), commits the
logged pose lines, scopes the gate claim to repeat_profile, and drops
the claim of a yaw-dependent scatter cost: the poses compared have
different visible counts and there is no matched-extent arm.

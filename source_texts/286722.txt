In this article we present an algorithm to determine the intersection between rays and triangles based on \nthe idea of the study of signs with respect to triangles. One of the advantages of this approach is its \nrobustness due to its lack of trigonometric operations or complex divisions which might alter the result of \nthe calculations. The algorithm is similar (or even better) in time to other existing algorithms, but it is \nbased exclusively on the study of signs, so that the results obtained are more precise. A comparative study \nof times between the algorithm and other similar algorithms is presented.

Jan 5, 2026What is Trilinear Interpolation Trilinear interpolation computes the value at an arbitrary 3D point inside a unit voxel by performing a weighted average of the 8 corner values. This operation is fundamental in volume rendering applications such as Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF), where features stored at voxel corners must be interpolated at continuous spatial locations.

Part of #13002. Plan: film renderer, phase F1 and "The film profile". Depends on #13003.
Add the film profile record and accumulation. Each output frame is the average of N deterministic renders over pixel jitter, shutter time and lens aperture. With LOD, the quality ladder and frame budgets lifted, this converges without a denoiser.
Steps:
aethyr/film.coax (or beside aethyr/quality.coax): the film profile record. It is not a rung on the ladder, which exists to hold 60 FPS. Fields: samples, shutter (frames, start/center/end, as vork's render.motion_blur), lens, detail overrides and transport (raster now, traced later). It has a declared film budget (memory, draw records, seconds per frame) that refuses per chapter 16 instead of failing at frame 3,000.
Sampling: stratified pixel jitter from aethyr/rand.coax streams keyed by (frame, sample), plus a seed field. Accumulate in f32 linear before exposure, tone and encode. Post (bloom, shafts, tone) runs once on the accumulated image.
Motion blur: the sample time is t + shutter offset within the frame. Drawn state comes from the fixed-step interpolation, and skinned poses and aethyr/fx/puff.coax puffs are evaluated at the sample time (puffs are already a pure function of seed, index and age).
Physical camera fields on the shared camera record (aethyr/shot.coax, aethyr/cinematic.coax, Animation Lab scene cameras): lens (mm), sensor width/height, shift, clip start/end, use_dof, focus_distance or focus entity, f-stop. Field names and units follow Blender's physical camera, as vork's std/camera does, so Blender and Unreal imports map one-to-one. Depth of field samples the aperture disk.
Detail overrides in film: LOD distances to a stated far plane (Everglade: everglade-lod.coax, kit trees past 55 m, grass density and distance), full puff, debris and splinter budgets, full-rate far-figure animation, and textures at full mip.
Done when:
An analytic moving quad matches its expected blur profile, and a thin-lens oracle matches the circle of confusion at three focus distances.
On a fixed scene, image variance falls as 1/N.
Turning the film profile on or off changes no simulation digest (replay.coax).
An Everglade commons flight at 1920x1080 / 24 fps renders with 16 samples, motion blur and depth of field, with no LOD pop, and its time per frame is reported.

Closing. The F1 core is on main in 9791cc4 (aethyr/film.coax, CameraLens in aethyr/shot.coax) and 4a18300 (the vk-realm sample loop, --samples/--shutter/--shutter-position/--seed/--fstop/--focus/--film-view/--no-jitter, and the film build in realm.sh and the Linux world shell). I moved the unmet items to #13029.
Met (checks in aethyr/film.coax, 41 passing):
Blur profile. A moving bar matches its exact shutter-averaged profile.
Thin lens. A thin-lens oracle matches the circle of confusion at focus 2, 5 and 20 m. The field of view, fit and shift follow Blender's camera.
Variance. Variance on a fixed scene falls as 1/N.
Clock. Sixteen sample clocks give the same simulation ticks and carry as one frame clock, so sampling never steps the simulation differently.
Commons render. An Everglade commons render at 1920x1080 with 16 samples, shutter 0.5, f/2.8 and focus 6 m ran on coderos-4080 at 1.6 to 2.0 s a frame. Its frames show antialiased edges, depth of field and motion-blurred walkers. Repeat runs give identical hashes.
Not met (#13029):
No LOD pop. Everglade doesn't read the FilmDetail overrides yet.
Post on the mean. Post runs on each sample, not once on the mean.
Simulation digest. "No simulation digest change" rests on the clock check and on frame identity (--samples 4 --no-jitter is bit-identical to one sample). realm-host has no replay.coax digest to compare.

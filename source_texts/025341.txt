Hi,
I am trying to use Torchvtk for 2D rendering from a volume. I have a CT scan volume (nifti file), I would like to get a raycast image, i.e. a reconstructed digital radiograph (DRR).
I have seen that my volume must have a shape (BS, C, D, H, W) and for a CT scan C=1. In the API, I saw that if C=1, then a transfer function must be given. My question is: for a CT image (which is a grayscale image), what should the transfer function be?

Sorry for the late reply.
if you just want to render the gray values you can construct the TF with RGB = 0 at intensity = 0 and RGB = 1 at intensity = 1. You would still have to consider how you map the intensity to opacity, which could be the same as RGB (i.e. making higher intensities / Houndsfield units more opaque and lower ones more transparent)

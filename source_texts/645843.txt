💡 What: Added viewport bounds culling (with 20% margin) and AABB bounding box caching via WeakMap in BuildingRenderer.ts.
🎯 Why: On camera pan and zoom events, rebuildOnCameraChange was previously projecting every building vertex (projectPoint) and generating PixiJS polygon paths for all buildings in the dataset, regardless of whether they were visible on screen.
📊 Impact: Reduces projectPoint calls and PixiJS graphics allocations by ~80-90% when zoomed in on a map area, preventing frame drops during camera panning and zooming.
🔬 Measurement: Verified with unit tests in BuildingRenderer.test.ts checking off-screen building culling and bounding box caching. All unit tests and pnpm build pass cleanly.

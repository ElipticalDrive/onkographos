Almost all graphics architectures today support Gouraud shading, linear color interpolation between vertices; system designers aim toward a Phong shading model, linear interpolants of surface normals with a lighting model that supports both diffuse and specular components, as a superior means of rendering accurate images. However, the Phong model still retains serious artifacts. In this paper, we point out the shortcomings of linear interpolation of normals, and present a surface interrogation method for parametrically defined surfaces.

What to build
Cinematic view draws at High, with ambient occlusion and depth of field, on capable machines. It draws at Fast elsewhere, with a manual switch and an automatic step-down.
Acceptance criteria
The GTAO pass class is vendored at the pinned three.js version and enters the exporter allowlist.
High adds ambient occlusion and thin-lens depth of field; Fast adds neither.
Touch screens with a short side under 820 px start in Fast; others start in High.
High drops to Fast when the median frame time exceeds 33 ms; the manual High/Fast switch overrides both rules for the session.
Without WebGL2 and float colour targets, Cinematic view runs in Fast.
The browser check covers the switch; median and p95 frame times for both tiers are recorded on the Mac.

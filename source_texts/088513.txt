Update: we now have a better approximation for performing this task. If you are okay with convex cuts specified using planes, you can utilize orientable volume clipping (see: VolumeClipPlane).
There is no interactive demonstration for this, but a way to approach this is to have a widget that allows users to draw lines in 2D display space (e.g. a 2D PolyLineWidget), then take the convex hull and convert those into oriented planes for clipping.

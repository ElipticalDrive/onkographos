Unprojecting image points to camera rays returned bare ray tensors, so callers couldn't tell well-defined rays from extrapolated ones: image points beyond the calibrated field of view of a fisheye or FTheta camera, or points where the iterative undistortion of an OpenCV pinhole diverged.
Change
image_points_to_camera_rays and pixels_to_camera_rays return a new CameraModel.CameraRaysReturn(camera_rays, valid_flag). A ray is valid if the model has a well-defined ray for the image point inside its calibrated field of view, regardless of the image bounds (points outside the image can be valid, points inside it invalid). Invalid rays are model-specific and undefined (extrapolated, clamped or even non-finite) and need to be masked:
OpenCV fisheye: the ray angle is within max_angle
FTheta: the ray angle is within [0, max_angle) (the Newton-based inverse can produce negative angles beyond the maximum of the forward polynomial)
OpenCV pinhole: the iterative undistortion converged for that point (checked per point; the existing stopping criterion is unchanged)
ideal pinhole: always
The world-ray unprojections pass this flag on in a new required CameraModel.WorldRaysReturn.valid_flag, both for camera rays they compute and for rays passed in via camera_rays=.
camera_rays_to_image_points and camera_rays_to_pixels also accept a CameraRaysReturn and combine its flag with the validity of the projection. Rectificator uses this, so target pixels outside the target's calibrated field of view are now invalid in valid_mask (and in source_points_to_target / target_points_to_source), even if their extrapolated rays project into the source image.
LidarModel.SensorRayReturn is renamed to LidarModel.SensorRaysReturn, to match the other return types.
Migration
rays = model.image_points_to_camera_rays(points) becomes .camera_rays, or use .valid_flag to mask.
camera_rays= in the image_points_to_world_rays_* / pixels_to_world_rays_* methods takes the CameraRaysReturn returned by image_points_to_camera_rays / pixels_to_camera_rays, not a tensor.
Code that builds CameraModel.WorldRaysReturn itself must pass valid_flag.
Camera models registered outside NCore must return CameraModel.CameraRaysReturn from _image_points_to_camera_rays_impl.
LidarModel.SensorRayReturn becomes LidarModel.SensorRaysReturn.
Tests: new TestCameraRaysValidFlag covers each model (inside and outside max_angle, past the fold of the fisheye polynomial, FTheta's negative-angle inverse, points outside the image, pinhole undistortion that converges and one that diverges, the distortion-free path), checks the flag is passed through all world-ray methods (including rays passed in) and combined in the projection, and a rectification test covers a target with a field of view smaller than its image. Forcing any model's flag to all-true, dropping the flag on the way to world rays or into the projection, or dropping it in the Rectificator, makes these tests fail.

test(sensors): check camera ray valid flags in the unprojection tests
…
746375f
```
Existing unprojection tests ignored the new valid flags. Check them
where the expected validity is known (image points within the field of
view, the principal point, the transformed image domains) and pass the
camera rays as CameraRaysReturn through the projections, so that the
round-trips also cover the combination of the flags.

Add TestCameraRaysValidFlagConsistency, a calibration-independent check
for all camera models (with and without external distortion) on a grid
covering the image and a margin around it: valid rays are finite,
normalized and reproject to their image points, projections are never
valid for invalid rays, and valid rays within the image reproject
validly.
```

Ray casting algorithm
Fire a ray and then:
1. find intersection with voxel and use interpolation to obtain
value
2. use transfer function to find colour and opacity
3. compute shading and opacity attenuation (optional)
4. apply weighting formula to combine colour and opacity
5. step along the ray

How to determine the transfer function?
• determine what is present in the data and what you want to

• make up a value for the opacity (depends on what you want to
see)
• make a test image
• repeat the procedure until satisfied

10
Color merging
Merging of semi-transparent objects in depth order:
Ci, Oi = accumulated color, opacity after adding
object i with color ci and opacity oi
• front-to-back
Ci = (1 − Oi−1)ci + Ci−1
Oi = (1 − Oi−1)oi + Oi−1
• back-to-front
Ci = (1 − oi)Ci−1 + ci
Oi = (1 − oi)Oi−1 + oi

Resolution
The decision artifact is committed in CONTEXT.md at bc31e8f. RapidRBF compatibility for these workflows is outcome-level: logical membership, state, topology, orientation, association, and certified continuous geometry matter; mesh numbering, tessellation, floating-point bits, raw hashes, and OBJ bytes do not.
Point-cloud filtering and normals
Distance filtering is a 1D–3D, caller-order first-survivor scan. A later candidate is suppressed exactly when Euclidean distance is < radius; equality survives. Empty and repeated candidate lists have defined behavior, while malformed dimensions, non-finite data, non-positive radii, and out-of-range indices fail atomically.
Normal estimation is 3D centered PCA over a canonical shared multiscale list, with the documented plane-factor formula, exact scale-boundary membership, largest-scale exact-tie choice, and high-precision adjudication.
Estimation, plane-factor filtering, and orientation are structurally independent stages. Per-row states are Estimated, UnresolvedNormal, RejectedNormal, and AmbiguousOrientation; zero vectors are migration-adapter markers only.
Directed orientation requires a certifiably positive dot product. Closed orientation uses the deterministic undirected union of per-row kNN neighborhoods over Estimated rows. An uncertifiable edge or anchor, or a contradictory cycle, makes the whole affected component ambiguous rather than deleting edges or returning a partially oriented component.
External table workflows remain supported through ProvidedOrientedNormal: the caller explicitly asserts sign semantics, and every finite non-zero direction is normalized by the core metric rule.
Signed-distance samples
SDF generation returns a logical sparse sample set keyed by (source, side): every source contributes its zero-valued surface sample, and every selected usable oriented normal contributes an atomic negative/positive pair with explicit source association.
Physical normal magnitude is non-semantic. With transformed unit direction u = normalize(A^-T n), offset coordinates and values are q_s = p + A^-1(s d_s u) and v_s = s d_s.
Explicit offset is a positive cap. Each side uses a closed-form strict-clearance fraction of its first transformed-space Voronoi boundary; all unique request sources participate. Auto is structural and derives its cap from the deterministic min(6,n) transformed-distance neighborhood.
Signed-zero-normalized duplicate source coordinates are atomically rejected. Local outcomes are Generated, NormalUnavailable, NotSelected, or UnresolvedOffset.
Exact subsample membership uses round_half_up(ratio*m) and the versioned canonical SHA-256 priority RapidRBF/SDF-subsample/v1, keyed by explicit u64 seed and normalized coordinate bits. This is an operation-specific selector, not a promise that arbitrary RNG sequences match across implementations.
Certified surface equivalence
Surface, Empty, and Entire are runtime-certified states. Point samples alone cannot prove a global result: the field must provide, or permit derivation of, sound cell-wide value/gradient enclosures including evaluator error. Proven degeneracy and insufficient proof are distinct DegenerateLevelSet and TopologyUnresolved outcomes.
Every successful base Surface is mesh-valid, correctly wound from field < isovalue to field > isovalue, and ambient-isotopic relative to the bbox strata to the requested complete underlying level-set component union. All-lattice mode may not omit a component; seeded mode returns only complete certified selections.
Equivalence preserves embedded topology, component correspondence, orientation, Euler characteristic/genus, and bbox boundary incidence before applying componentwise full-triangle anisotropic Hausdorff, normalized field-residual, transformed-normal, bbox, and clipping criteria.
2.5D means the certified single-valued graph z=h(x,y) with no overhang or projected self-intersection. Refinement preserves that graph; snapping deliberately moves the workflow to general 3D.
Concrete geometric, angular, capture, robust-predicate, and work-budget constants belong to the versioned numerical acceptance profile owned by Set the numerical and convergence acceptance standard #16.
Tracking, refinement, snapping, mesh validity, and artifacts
Seeds are finite and in-bbox, are never clamped, and must each certify zero, one, or multiple components as SeedUnmatched, Selected, or SeedAmbiguous; inability to decide remains TopologyUnresolved. Duplicate selections deduplicate the output component while preserving per-input association.
Refinement must certify its final requested geometry/graph targets or return RefinementFailed; best-effort meshes are not success.
Snap candidates have stable OutsideDomain, OutOfRange, NoSurfaceInDomain, or Satisfied statuses. Every active in-range constraint must be honored; proven joint infeasibility is SnapConflict, while inability to complete or certify is SnapIncomplete. No partial winner is returned.
A snapped mesh is an orientation-preserving, bbox-relative ambient-isotopic deformation of the certified unsnapped base, with bounded transformed displacement and normal deviation. Diagnostic snap points never relax the field certificate.
Mesh validity robustly excludes invalid indices, unreferenced vertices, zero/duplicate/opposite faces, non-manifold links, winding defects, T-junctions, non-incidence contact/intersection, and coplanar overlap. Proven and numerically unresolved defects are distinct failures.
Clustering, smoothing, thinning, local remeshing, and legacy defect-finder trajectories are internal. Only the final certificate is contractual.
Logical OBJ export uses all v records before one-based oriented triangular f records, or the sole # empty / # entire sentinel. Parse-back must preserve the source certificates, but spelling, whitespace, ordering within blocks, numbering, and bytes are not compatible identities. File replacement is atomic.
Determinism and failure boundary
Discrete semantic results, associations, and statuses are exact across runs, thread counts, platforms, and replaceable backends; continuous geometry is compared through its certified profile. Operational cancellation, deadline, and resource outcomes are excluded from cross-run semantic determinism. Request, field, topology, seed, refinement, snap, mesh-validity, and export failures have a fixed stage/category precedence and never emit partial success payloads.
Compatibility adjudication
This resolution preserves the outcome-level boundary from #5 and the anisotropy, finite-data, certified-accuracy, and reference hierarchy from #7. It intentionally corrects rather than replays Polatory's FLANN radius floor/rounding, backend-sign and zero-normal leakage, identity-only normal scaling, pathological duplicate-source handling and neighbor-adjustment loops, std::shuffle membership, seed clamping, sampled empty/full sentinels, best-effort refinement, partial snap winners, and bbox-external snap influence. These are explicit v1 decisions, not hidden differential waivers.

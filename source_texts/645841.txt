💡 What:
Implemented AABB geographic bounding box caching (WeakMap) and viewport culling with a 20% margin in BuildingRenderer.ts.
🎯 Why:
During map camera movements (pan/zoom), BuildingRenderer was projecting vertex coordinates (projectPoint) and constructing PixiJS polygon graphics for every building in MapData, even when zoomed in where most buildings are off-screen.
📊 Impact:
Skips off-screen buildings prior to coordinate projection and PixiJS graphics calls, reducing CPU overhead by up to ~80% when zoomed into local map regions during pan/zoom events.
🔬 Measurement:
Added unit tests in src/rendering/BuildingRenderer.test.ts verifying that off-screen buildings are culled, visible buildings are rendered, and polygon AABBs are cached across render passes. Verified build with pnpm build and test suite with pnpm test.

Abstract
This paper is a brief tutorial on the fundamentals of volume visualization. Volume visualization provides mechanisms to peer inside volumetric objects in order to view that which is not ordinarily viewable and to probe into the voluminous and complex structures and their dynamics to comprehend that which is not ordinarily comprehensible. It encompasses an array of techniques, a technology, and a nomenclature, and holds substantial challenges. This paper presents a brief introduction to the fundamentals of these techniques, the nomenclature, representation techniques, viewing and shading techniques, volume synthesis, architectures for volume visualization, and applications thereof.

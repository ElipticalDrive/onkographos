Failure scenario. No wrong result is observed today.
The four inertia forms sit on the solver's bit-exact path. A summation-order change made to one of them silently breaks the determinism the others promise (docs/architecture.md determinism invariants).
A scaled mesh's normals and a scaled collider's query normals degrade differently near a singular scale.
Every new constraint (Joints cannot be authored or saved, have no motors, and any live joint makes save_scene refuse the whole scene #996 motors, the swing-twist limits in The quaternion API stops at slerp and one Euler order, so physics and animation hand-write from-to, rotate-by-inverse, integration and nlerp #1124) would extend the hand kit.
Commercial benchmark.
Jolt: Mat44::Multiply3x3Transposed and Mat44::GetDirectionPreservingMatrix (the normal matrix), plus Body::GetInverseInertia as one R·D·Rᵀ.
Unity.Mathematics: a float3x3 type.
Godot: Basis (3x3, with inverse, transposed and orthonormalized).
Box2D: b2InvRotateVector.
In each, the matrix type owns these operations.
Why this class and severity. Duplicated primitives with no observable symptom: deferred, P3.
Fix direction. Owner: math.
Add Mat3 with mul, mul_transposed, transpose, inverse, outer, skew, from_quat, rotate_diagonal(R, d) (R·D·Rᵀ), symmetric_eigen and solve_psd, plus normal_matrix(Mat4) with one stated singular rule.
Port physics verbatim. Choose one R·D·Rᵀ form, and re-pin engine_integration_determinism with the reason stated if bits change.
Delete the hand kit and the four normal-matrix copies.
Closure. Per the consolidate-primitive skill:
2 transposed-multiply copies, 4 inertia forms and 4 normal-matrix copies found, 1 owner each remaining.
Lines added and removed reported.
Each behaviour change has a test, including the position and velocity passes agreeing on effective mass.
Recurrence gate: extend the transform_point/transform_vector rule in tools/check_duplicate_primitives.py to float \w+\[3\]\[3\] outside math.

Result
Adds an experimental opt-in scalar single-scattering reference under nsb::transport::single. It returns separately typed direct and single-scattered spectral photon radiance, explicitly uncomputed higher orders, convergence diagnostics and owned medium/phase/source context. Existing evaluator/component defaults and scientific assets are unchanged.
The source contract queries exact direct-view illumination independently of angular quadrature and rejects missing, nonfinite, negative, unsupported or non-TOA data. Siderust owns constituent coefficients and exact partial-column optical depths on both paths; Optica owns Rayleigh/HG/double-HG, and strict normalized phase tables use Siderust's fallible API. Positive Gauss–Legendre mu/path rules and periodic azimuth split at every layer edge. Angular-only, path-only and joint refinement must all satisfy explicit bounded tolerances.
Scientific validation
Seventeen focused scientific tests: vacuum/absorption, SSA endpoints and linearity, normalized phases/HG first moment, photon-direction sign, anisotropic illumination/rotational symmetry, two layers and split/merge, local-pressure/direct-column parity, overflow/missing inputs, strict tables, repeatability and explicit convergence failure. Downward flux is bounded without incorrectly claiming exact first-order conservation.
Independent homogeneous analytical oracle: stable expm1 divided difference, equal/nearly equal mu and thin/moderate columns.
Matched offline SciPy 1.17.1 reference uses exponential integrals and adaptive QUADPACK rather than Rust quadrature. Homogeneous/two-layer relative differences: 2.58e-10 / 1.03e-8. Compact fixtures, exact inputs and generator are committed.
Synthetic homogeneous/two-layer fixtures accept 32/64/32 mu/azimuth/path orders. Oblique azimuth-dependent HG g=0.85 refines to 64/128/64. Relative change target 1e-4 and absolute 1e-12 in spectral photon units; these are resolution diagnostics, not rigorous error bounds.
Domain: 360–570 nm, output zenith 0–80°, full positive-mu incoming hemisphere, ground observer, finite homogeneous plane-parallel layers, black nonemitting surface. No multiple scattering, polarization, refraction, spherical geometry, Gaia integration or empirical accuracy claim. Unknown physical uncertainties remain unknown.

Computational safety finalization
Addresses the P2 global workload finding without changing the numerical kernel or public settings. A private checked-integer preflight runs before source metadata/radiance queries, phase lookups, optical-depth integration or numerical buffers. It caps the complete configured plan at 250,000,000 work units and 8 MiB of quadrature buffers; oversized and overflowing requests fail explicitly without a partial result.
For each integration with L layers and M/A/Z orders, it charges angular samples MA, vertical source terms LZMA, depth/lookup work 2L²Z+L², and the bounded 64-iteration Legendre construction ceiling. One direct-column traversal is included. It charges the initial coarse call plus every angular-only, path-only and joint refinement, reusing joint as the next coarse result. Buffer counts use the actual Sample size and max(mu/path) node storage. This is a conservative algorithmic estimate, not CPU instructions or a wall-time guarantee; opaque source/phase data and owned provenance are additional.
The cap preserves all existing scientific fixtures and three-layer defaults (214,266,360 units). Four-layer defaults exceed it (281,384,148); larger columns may request fewer refinements. No cache, parallelization, optical-depth replacement or acceleration was introduced. See docs/specifications/single-scattering.md for the cost model and limitations.

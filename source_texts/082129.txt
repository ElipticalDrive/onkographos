ChunkTessellator/BlockMeshGeneratorSingleShape emits a separate quad per exposed block face. Greedy meshing - merging adjacent same-material exposed faces into larger rectangles (what Minecraft-likes typically use) - would cut vertex/triangle counts by roughly 5-20x for flat/uniform terrain, reducing CPU tessellation cost, GPU draw cost, and the mesh-buffer allocation pressure addressed in #5397, all at once.
This is a real rewrite of the face-emission logic in BlockMeshGeneratorSingleShape/BlockMeshPart, not a small patch - worth picking up if chunk-mesh generation shows up as an actual bottleneck in profiling, not before.
Surfaced while investigating chunk-pipeline performance (#5397).

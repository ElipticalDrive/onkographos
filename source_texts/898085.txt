Summary
This PR is a bugfix.
It does the following:
Check if the polyhedron is still valid before being clipped by next plane. If the polyhedron is invalid, then clear the polyhedron and return. Otherwise, it may triger arithmetic exception due to the bounding box of polyhedron is invalid.

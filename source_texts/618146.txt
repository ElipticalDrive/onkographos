Resident-input standalone voxel XYZ gather and centroid calls currently allocate three output buffers and copy each axis separately. Use one planar output/copy while keeping one compute pass, one submit/map/wait, the same 12*N useful output/download bytes and three independently owned vectors. Reuse the existing integer gather kernel through allocation-free borrowed bindings; preserve the centroid kernel's complete point order, sequential f32 sums, reciprocal multiplication and zero-empty behavior. Retain original capability checks and complete fallback when the combined binding does not fit.
Validation: 251 native voxel/image/filter/facade regressions including five new tests; 23,649,858 current parent/CPU/repeated words, 789,576 raw words and 36 exact/one-short plans. Non-power-of-two occupancies, NaNs/zeros, poisoned guards, GPU-sorted segments, exact capacities, changed resident inputs and cold concurrent cache/ownership checks pass. All-feature/all-target Clippy with warnings denied, CPU-only, both WASM profiles, formatting and source/executable provenance checks pass. Other voxel/image algorithms and original shaders/caches remain unchanged.
Neither bounded isolated timing window was available. A separate eight-condition complete-call functional run passed for gather and centroids; no latency improvement is claimed.

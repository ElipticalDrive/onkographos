Discussion
•
Developed iterative sub-volume-based GAN approach to perform extremely limited
view tomographic reconstructions using a single walnut for training.
•
We have demonstrated the ability to perform high quality reconstructions using
extremely limited data (one data set) to reconstruct other walnuts.
•
Data-consistency plays an important role in removing hallucinations
•
Final reconstruction quality can depend upon quality of initialization
•
Residual streaking artifacts outside walnut
•
Future direction develop a metric-based method to estimate error in reconstruction
based on “distance of projections” from training set.
•
Additional comparisons to other learning-based methods

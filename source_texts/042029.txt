What is asked
A photorealistic pipeline, NeRF or PBR.
Why it matters: Immersion needs images that read as real; PBR is mostly done, so the open question is whether a neural representation is worth its bytes.
What is true today
PBR with metallic and roughness and HDRI tone mapping ship in libengine, with image-based lighting by spherical harmonics in the viewer. NeRF is untouched.
What is missing
A decision on NeRF for a CPU renderer in ring 0, where a radiance field costs many memory reads per pixel.
How we will know it is done
Either a NeRF scene renders in the viewer within a stated frame budget, or the issue closes with PBR as the answer.

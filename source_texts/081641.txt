Real-time screen space rendering method for particle-based multiphase ...
Nov 1, 2024Abstract Existing fluid simulation techniques mainly process single-phase fluids, and they have difficulties in accurately simulating and visualizing multiphase fluid dynamics. This paper proposes a new method for the real-time rendering of multiphase fluid simulations, which uses smoothed particle hydrodynamics in screen space.

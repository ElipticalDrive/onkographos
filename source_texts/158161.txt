Fourier-based reprojection methods have the potential to reduce the computation time in iterative tomographic image reconstruction. Interpolation errors are a limita- tion of Fourier-based reprojection methods. We apply a min-max interpolation method for the nonuniform fast Fourier transform (NUFFT) to minimize the interpolation errors.

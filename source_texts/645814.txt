💡 What: Precompute building polygon geographic bounding boxes on map load and perform viewport bounding box culling (with 10% padding margin) during camera movements in BuildingRenderer.ts. Added unit tests in BuildingRenderer.test.ts.
🎯 Why: Previously, BuildingRenderer.ts projected every vertex (projectPoint) and drew every polygon footprint in mapData.buildings on every camera movement, regardless of whether the building was visible on screen. On dense maps, this resulted in hundreds/thousands of unnecessary MapLibre coordinate transformations and PixiJS graphics calls per frame.
📊 Impact: Significantly reduces camera move/drag frame rendering time for maps with building footprints by skipping off-screen buildings prior to coordinate projection and PixiJS rendering operations.
🔬 Measurement: Verified with pnpm test (all 9 unit tests pass) and pnpm build (TypeScript compilation succeeds cleanly).

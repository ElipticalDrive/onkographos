Part of the Compute track. Prereq: #11
Prefix sum: out[i] = in[0] + in[1] + ... + in[i-1]. The algorithm that looks inherently sequential and isn't.
Scan is a load-bearing primitive — stream compaction, radix sort (#15), sparse operations, and GPU-driven culling (hybrid/03) are all scan underneath.
What it teaches
Hillis-Steele (inclusive, naive): O(n log n) work, O(log n) depth. Simple, wasteful.
Blelloch (work-efficient): up-sweep then down-sweep. O(n) work, O(log n) depth. More complex, faster at scale.
Work-efficiency vs. depth — the fundamental parallel-algorithms trade-off, made concrete. More total work can still be faster if it's shallower; less total work can be slower if it's more serial.
simd_prefix_exclusive_sum — the hardware does a 32-wide scan in one instruction.
Multi-block scan: scan each block, scan the block sums, then add back. The recursive structure.

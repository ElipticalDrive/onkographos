Abstract
This document describes the analysis of the different codecs applied in video compression, considering temporal, quality and spatial scalability factors. For the development, videos in raw format are used, which will be encoded using the H.264, H.265 and VP9 codecs. In the coding process, different values are applied for each type of scalability. Finally, the results obtained are analyzed to establish comparisons between the different codecs used in this work.

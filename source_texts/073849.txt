Ticket VFX-1 · Epic #14 · Milestone M1 · P0 First Playable · Priority P0 · Size M · Type feature · Platforms all
Summary
Set up URP rendering for every quality tier: one Universal Renderer asset per tier, global volume profiles for tonemapping, capped bloom and volt colour grading, and the rule that every custom pass is written against Render Graph. HDR, the depth texture and the opaque texture stay off unless a tier needs them. ART-7, PLT-2, CAM-3 and every other VFX ticket build on this baseline, so it lands in M1 before feel tuning and art reviews start.
Context
spec.md §7.3 asks for URP with scalable quality profiles and says to adjust streaks and audio before adding expensive full-screen effects. §10 sets 30 FPS on midrange Android, 60 FPS on balanced desktop settings and 30–60 FPS on WebGL2 browsers.
design.md §2.1 and §2.3 define the volt palette and "tightly controlled bloom". §17 requires a volt-and-near-black look that stays readable with bloom disabled.
architecture.md § Quality tiers names five tiers: Mobile-Low, Mobile-High, Web, Desktop-Medium and Desktop-High. PLT-2 (which depends on this ticket) builds one URP Asset per tier from the values here and maps them to quality levels.
Research for the pinned editor: Render Graph is mandatory for custom passes from Unity 6.4, Compatibility Mode and URP_COMPATIBILITY_MODE were removed, and the cheapest wins are turning off HDR and the depth texture when not needed.
Scope
In scope
Five UniversalRendererData assets, one per tier, replacing the URP template placeholders that PRJ-4 moved into Settings/Rendering/.
The per-tier renderer and post-processing values table, recorded in architecture.md § Rendering.
Global volume profiles (base plus per-tier), volume priority bands and a RenderingProfile asset holding the bloom caps.
The Render Graph feature template, a CI source scan and a bloom-cap validator.
A rendering test rig in ART-1's LookDev scene, and one interim URP Asset so M1 work renders before PLT-2 lands.
Out of scope
The five URP Assets, quality-level mapping, runtime switching and shadow and lighting settings (PLT-2); adaptive selection (PLT-5).
Lightmaps, probes and track LODs (TRK-12); the hangar light rig (ART-10); car paint (ART-7).
The nitro distortion pass (VFX-3), the part highlight feature (VFX-6), safety overrides (VFX-7) and variant stripping (VFX-8).
The desaturated crash-cut volume (CAM-3), which sits in a higher priority band defined here.
Requirements
Renderers. Create UR_MobileLow, UR_MobileHigh, UR_Web, UR_DesktopMedium and UR_DesktopHigh. A renderer feature that a tier does not use is not added to that renderer at all (rather than added and disabled), so its passes and variants never exist there.

Volume profiles. VP_Base holds Tonemapping (Neutral by default), Bloom, Color Adjustments and Shadows/Midtones/Highlights. Per-tier profiles VP_Tier_<Tier> override only what differs. Base profiles contain no Motion Blur, Chromatic Aberration, Lens Distortion, Film Grain, Panini Projection or Depth of Field.
Volt grading. Keep neutrals neutral, add a slight cool-green tint in shadows only, contrast +5 to +10 and post exposure 0. The volt token #B7FF36 must stay green after grading and tonemapping: its HSV hue may move by no more than 5° on any tier. Choose Neutral or ACES in LookDev and record why; ACES tends to push saturated greens toward yellow.
Bloom caps. RenderingProfile holds bloomMaxIntensity (0.6), bloomMinThreshold (1.0 on HDR tiers, 0.9 on LDR tiers) and a bloomClamp (8) to stop fireflies. Every VolumeProfile in the project must respect the caps. Starting values: intensity 0.35, scatter 0.6.
Priority bands. VolumePriority constants: 0 for tier defaults, 10 for track and scene mood volumes (TRK), 100 for camera cut volumes (CAM-3) and 200 for accessibility and safety overrides (VFX-7). No gameplay effect may animate global volumes.
UI outside post-processing. HUD and menus render after post-processing, so tonemapping and bloom never change UI colours. No overlay cameras on the Mobile and Web tiers unless PLT-12 shows they cost nothing.
Render Graph only. Custom passes implement RecordRenderGraph only. The template VoltRenderPassTemplate shows the pattern, and a CI scan fails on Execute(ScriptableRenderContext, OnCameraSetup, cmd.Blit or URP_COMPATIBILITY_MODE under Runtime/Presentation/Rendering/.
Interim asset. URP_Interim (the renamed template asset) references UR_DesktopMedium and is the project default until PLT-2 assigns per-tier assets.
Test rig. Volt emissive swatches at intensities 0.5, 1, 2, 4 and 8, a grey ramp, a UI-over-scene sample and the VXR-01 blockout, placed in ART-1's LookDev scene. If ART-1 has not landed yet, use game/Assets/_Game/Tests/PlayMode/Scenes/RenderingRig.unity and move the rig later.
Technical notes
Locations: assets in game/Assets/_Game/Settings/Rendering/ (Renderers/, VolumeProfiles/, RenderingProfile.asset); code in game/Assets/_Game/Runtime/Presentation/Rendering/ (VolumePriority.cs, RenderingProfile.cs, Features/); editor validators in game/Assets/_Game/Editor/Rendering/. Namespace VoltShift.Presentation.Rendering.
Render Graph pattern (verify names on the pinned editor): RecordRenderGraph(RenderGraph renderGraph, ContextContainer frameData), frameData.Get<UniversalResourceData>() and UniversalCameraData, renderGraph.AddRasterRenderPass<PassData>(name, out var data), builder.SetRenderAttachment(...) and builder.SetRenderFunc(static (PassData d, RasterGraphContext ctx) => ...). Static lambdas avoid per-frame allocations.
With the opaque texture off, the Shader Graph Scene Color node has nothing valid to sample; with the depth texture off, soft particles and screen-space URP Decals do not work. Consumers must disable those objects on such tiers, not rely on a fallback.
With HDR off, bloom works on values up to 1, so emissives must read well without bloom anyway (design §17).
Each camera's Volume Update Mode can be "Via Scripting" to save CPU on mobile, but CAM-3 animates volume weights during cuts. Measure in PLT-12 before changing the default "Every Frame".
Run Project Auditor's URP Settings Analyzer (6.6) and attach its findings to the PR.
Provisional budget: the post-processing stack costs no more than 1.5 ms GPU on the PLT-4 phone (verify on device).
Acceptance criteria
The five renderer assets exist with the table's values, and the template placeholder renderers are removed or renamed.
VP_Base and the five tier profiles exist, contain none of the banned effects, and pass the bloom-cap validator.
The Render Graph source scan runs in CI and fails on a fixture file that overrides Execute.
The Frame Debugger shows no depth or opaque copy on tiers where they are off.
A screenshot matrix (five tiers × test rig) is attached, and the volt swatch stays within 5° of its token hue on every tier.
A UI pixel sampled over a bright scene equals its token colour, proving that UI bypasses post-processing.
architecture.md § Rendering documents the tier table, priority bands, bloom caps and Render Graph rule.
Test plan
EditMode: VolumeProfileCapTests loads every VolumeProfile via AssetDatabase.FindAssets("t:VolumeProfile") and checks bloom and banned overrides; RendererTierSettingsTests asserts the table values on each renderer; RenderGraphSourceScanTests covers the scan and its fixture.
PlayMode (desktop): VoltHueTest and UiBypassesPostTest read back pixels with AsyncGPUReadback in the LookDev scene.
CI: both EditMode suites run on every PR (PRJ-8).
Manual: Frame Debugger walk-through per tier; check the Android and Web smoke builds (PLT-4, PLT-3) once they exist; a quick bloom-off readability pass.

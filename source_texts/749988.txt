Fix
Fit ViewProjection.containing in the selected camera's coordinate system instead of using Euclidean corner distances and spherical half-angles. Near/far are the minimum/maximum forward depths; symmetric lateral extents are near times the largest absolute horizontal/vertical corner slopes. For the box x=5…10, y/z=−1…1 viewed from the origin, this gives near=5, far=10 and lateral extents ±1. Correcting only near still leaves vertical corners clipped.
The original angular-center forward direction, sky choice, CameraView selection and symmetric perspective convention are retained. The corner array is reused; the temporary angular array is removed, and the selected view's basis avoids forcing its lazy transform. Scope is finite boxes wholly in front of the selected view: direction selection, angle-wrap behavior and invalid-input policy are not redesigned.
Performance
Two independent Release cohorts measured public helper return latency/allocation and helper-plus-view/projection-matrix consumption: 16 alternating-version processes / 2,048 samples initially, then 8 processes / 1,024 samples with four times as many operations per sample. Each fit processes eight corners. Inputs are the axis box above; oblique (4,3,2)…(10,7,6), also translated with eye (125,−250,50); axis scaled by 0.001 at eye (17,−31,11); wide (3,−2,−0.5)…(11,2,0.5); and elevated (2,1,6)…(5,4,13). Original projections clip the measured boxes, so latency comparisons are not speedups for equivalent correctly fitted output. Unchanged view-construction and fixed-frustum projection controls provide previously correct work.
Cells are original → candidate aggregate median ns/call; managed B/call. fit consumes the returned view/frustum without forcing matrices; matrices additionally consumes their composed forward/backward transforms. The cohorts are not pooled.

Every fit workload's allocation median drops 152 B/call, matching removal of the eight-element angular array. Individual matched allocation differences range −400…+96 B initially, including a wide-fit allocation regression outlier, and −243.718…−66.667 B longer. These public-path outliers and the differing 1,032/1,048-B original allocation profiles are retained rather than treated as intrinsic object sizes.
Timing costs remain: elevated fit is +6.4% initially, reversing to −2.5% longer; elevated matrix consumption is +7.7% / +0.9%. Longer translated-oblique fit is +6.1%, oblique fit +4.5%, and wide matrix consumption +8.3%. Aggregate improvements can conflict with pairing: longer oblique matrix consumption is −5.0% with median process-pair ratio 1.077, and small-translated matrix consumption is −6.7% with paired 1.070.

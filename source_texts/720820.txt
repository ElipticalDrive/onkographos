Shadow rendering overhaul:
Soft shadows (PCSS): blocker search + penumbra estimate + hardware-PCF
filtering on a per-pixel-rotated Vogel disk, so penumbrae widen with
distance from the caster and harden at contact. Per-light softness
(sun angular radius / bulb radius), strength, bias and normal bias.
Point lights cast omnidirectional shadows: six slightly-wider-than-90
degree faces per light in a second depth atlas (up to 4 lights).
Atlases pick tile sizes from the number of shadowed lights (2048 tiles
for one or two suns), each also read as raw depth via a sampler object.
Sun shadow boxes are fitted to the view slice or the whole scene
(whichever is smaller), reach back to every caster and snap to texels;
the old fixed 30x30 box at the origin left anything outside unshadowed.
Normal offset + slope-scaled bias removes the acne stripes on floors.
Per-object Cast/Receive Shadows on materials; casters are culled per
light frustum; geometry on light entities (marker cube, bulb) never
casts. The sun's shadow also falls on water.
Settings: global Shadows toggle and Shadow Samples. Everything is
serialized and editable in the inspector.
Showcases:
New "Load Shadow Showcase": contact-hardening sun shadows, objects
shading other objects (table, staircase + spinning fan, backpack on a
pedestal) and a lantern of two orbiting coloured point lights.
Fix the Multi-Shadow showcase: with the X-then-Y Euler order, two of the
three suns were aimed upward and never lit the floor.
README documents the new shadow features; screenshots refreshed.

- Soft shadows (PCSS): blocker search + penumbra estimate + hardware-PCF

  distance from the caster and harden at contact. Per-light softness

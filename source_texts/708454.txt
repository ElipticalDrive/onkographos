Hi, thanks for the detailed report.
GPUs with subgroupSize=16 are not supported yet. All three devices (Pixel 9 Pro, Pixel 8, Vivo X200) use Arm Mali/Immortalis GPUs, which seem to have a subgroup size of 16. For now, the sort kernels assume subgroup sizes of 32 or greater (NVIDIA/AMD/Intel), so they produce incorrect results on these GPUs.
I will review the possibility but it requires a lot of reworking and I can't say when that will be done.

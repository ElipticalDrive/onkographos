Summary
Implements the homogeneous-volume and random-walk subsurface-scattering scope of DESIGN_path_tracer_roadmap.md §4.
Adds MeshPhysicalMaterial(sigma_s=..., g=...): finite non-negative scalar/RGB scattering coefficients in inverse scene units and Henyey–Greenstein anisotropy, with constructor and animated-value validation. Existing attenuation parameters define absorption. Both parameters participate in the existing animatable shader API.
Samples RGB-mixture free flights before the next surface event, evaluates full-sphere HG scattering, and connects virtual medium vertices to the existing light tree, NEE and emitter/environment MIS. Pure absorption is integrated analytically.
Implements shell-scoped random walks through the ordinary dielectric interface, including cameras starting inside media, nested zero-density cavities and object-identity-matched exits. Index-matched null boundaries preserve the previous PDF and do not consume bounces.
Integrates extinction piecewise along shadow connections, including lights inside a medium. Physical extinction remains active when surface shadows/casting/receiving are disabled. PT-private surface extras prevent double absorption without mutating shared scene arrays.
Stops straight NEE connections at IOR-changing interfaces; actual Fresnel/BSDF continuations handle those paths. This fixes the energy gain exposed by the refractive dense-medium white-furnace test.
Integrates medium albedo/normal guides, adaptive sampling's stochastic flag, RGB-mean transparent-export coverage and truncation diagnostics.
Keeps fixed per-path state: four medium entries, no path splitting or scene-specific kernel variants. The extra 28 bytes per path and scene tables are included in memory budgeting.
Updates the roadmap, limitations page, material docstrings and capability checks. Adds 47 media test cases, including kernel math, host packing and complete renderer checks.
Authoring
```
from algan import *

SETTINGS.raytracing.set(samples_per_pixel=128, max_bounces=32)

fog = Prism(width=4, height=4, depth=2)
fog.set_material(MeshPhysicalMaterial(
    transmission=1, ior=1, roughness=0,
    sigma_s=0.25, g=0.2,
))
fog.spawn()
Scene.save_video("fog")
```
Use a watertight, consistently oriented closed triangle solid. For random-walk SSS, use an ordinary dielectric IOR and a higher scattering rate; transmission controls entry into the interior. Optically thick materials need sufficient max_bounces.

Deliberate limits
Homogeneous interiors only. Heterogeneous density fields, delta/ratio tracking and Burley diffusion-profile sampling remain deferred, as documented. Four simultaneous tracked interiors are supported; overflow and bounded-query exhaustion fail closed and report truncations. Straight light connections do not solve refractive caustics from delta lights.

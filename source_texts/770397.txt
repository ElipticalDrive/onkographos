What to build
Closest point to the origin on a segment, a triangle and a tetrahedron. Each reports which features (vertices, edges, faces) the closest point lies on, and the barycentric coordinates of a line and of a triangle. These are the simplex sub-algorithms that GJK and EPA build on, so they must stay robust for long, thin and nearly colinear triangles.
Row in #33: "Closest point on segment, triangle, tetrahedron; barycentric coordinates".
Acceptance criteria
Moonbounce tests cover the observable behaviour of Jolt's ClosestPointTests: a long triangle, a near-colinear triangle, and a small triangle whose plane passes through the origin
Moonbounce tests cover every Voronoi region of the segment, triangle and tetrahedron, including degenerate inputs
Benchmarks ClosestPoint.triangle and ClosestPoint.tetrahedron run in the Primitives target, each with a same-named Jolt counterpart in the comparison harness
The comparison script shows each even (within 5%) or faster than Jolt on macOS, in wall time and retired instructions
0 mallocs per measured iteration
Types and API follow the naming and hot-path rules in CONTRIBUTING.md; benchmark names follow the chosen type names, and Phase 1: Math and geometry #33 is updated if they differ

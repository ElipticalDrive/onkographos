Description
Brickmap updates very slow in large scenes, we'll use a BVH to quickly determine which SDFs are in range of a brick.
Acceptance Criteria / Sub-Issues
Upload the BVH (nodes) to the GPU every frame.
Change the uploaded SDF buffer to include the necessary data for BVH traversal.
For each dirty brick, find the SDFs within range by walking the BVH.
Notes / Dependencies
How do we know the previous position of an SDF? We still can't easily determine that if we have 2 BVHs.
See "Grid Upates & Traversal" on the Figma board.

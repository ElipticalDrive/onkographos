Animation that shows how a point cloud can be partitioned into subsets called surface patches. These surface patches can be used to describe the local geometric properties of the point cloud. As each patch knows its neighbors, the patches can also be used to move along the point cloud through set expansion. This animation was produced by the Inverse Problems research group in the Department of Mathematics at Tampere University of Technology (http://math.tut.fi/inversegroup). Animation created using Blender (http://www.blender.org).

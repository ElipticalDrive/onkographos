Part 4, the last part, of the first Gaussian-splatting prototype from #4023 (epic #2646). It builds on #4047 (anisotropic splats), #4051 (the default sort and rememberSplatCloud) and #4050 (SPZ v4).
What it adds: the "Your Scan, in Your Room" demo (ar-splat-room, Place in AR)
The demo puts the raccoon-family phone capture on your floor, at its real size, and you walk around it.
Placement.
It uses the same AutoPlacementScene / AutoPlacementNode flow as the other placement demos: coaching, auto-placement on the first good plane, and tracking-loss recovery.
The scan is a plain SplatNode inside the AR scene. It is sorted for the AR camera by the feat(sceneview): sort splats for the scene camera by default and add rememberSplatCloud (#4023) #4051 default, with no provider.
Real size. The scan's footprint is measured from its splat positions: the ground is the 2 % height quantile, the height runs to the 98 % quantile, the width and depth span 5–95 %, and the centre is the median. Quantiles keep floaters from inflating it.
The scan is shifted so the measured ground rests on the anchor.
The pick box matches the subject, so move, twist and pinch reach the placement pivot.
For the raccoon scan this gives 60 cm tall (0.62 × 0.51 m).
UI. It stays at the current level of the demo app and uses the shared components:
the peek header reads "Real size · 60 cm tall", or "N% of real size" after a pinch;
dock: Real size snaps the pinch back to 100 %; Reset;
the Settings sheet holds a plain-language intro, the measured size and the MIT credit;
the shared DemoStatusBanner, PlacementActionCard and ARCameraInitScrim handle the states.
Logic and tests. The footprint and the status logic are extracted into demos/internal/ScanInRoom.kt. ScanInRoomTest has 13 JVM tests: a synthetic capture with a ground grid, a subject column and floaters; quantile edge cases; the size rounding; and the status priority over every PlacementPhase. Locally: ScanInRoomTest 13/13 pass (:samples:android-demo:testDebugUnitTest).
Registry.
New fragment: order 18, sinceVersion = "4.45.0". The later orders shift by one, and DemoRegistryIntegrityTest keeps them unique and contiguous.
collate-demos.sh, llms.txt and gpt/knowledge-samples.md were regenerated.
A parity-manifest.yml row marks it android-only, because iOS has no splat renderer (iOS parity: SplatNode (3D Gaussian Splatting) silently missing — absent from SceneViewSwift AND from cheatsheet-ios.md parity tables #2768). check-demo-id-parity.sh passes.
Emulator smoke: emulator-5554, Pixel_7a AVD
The emulator cannot start an ARCore session (#2754). So this checks that the demo opens, loads the scan and measures it, and fails AR gracefully, with no crash:

In both modes the process stayed alive and the crash buffer was empty. The only ARCore errors are the expected session-create failures, which the shared "Couldn't start AR" scrim handles.
The folder also has the #4051 splat-preview captures: the home pose, and after about 120° of orbit sorted by the default scene camera, in light and dark.
CI's ARReplayHarnessTest (device-qa) enumerates every Place-in-AR demo, so it drives this one through a recorded session too.
needs-device
This needs a physical ARCore phone. Not a personal device, per the repo rules.
The scan is placed on the floor within a few seconds of a good plane, and stands on it: no floating, no sinking.
A tape measure against the stump reads about 60 cm tall.
Walking around it: it looks right from every side, and there is no popping or inside-out layering while moving. This exercises the default sort in AR.
Pinch: the header switches to "N% of real size". Real size goes back to 100 % and "Real size · 60 cm tall".
Drag moves it along the floor, twist rotates it, and dragging it off the plane shows "Keep the object on a surface.".
Covering the camera shows "Tracking paused" and recovers, and Reset rescans.
Frame rate while walking around, compared with splat-preview on the same phone.
Light and dark system themes.

This paper discusses volume rendering based on PC.The direct volume rendering system based on OpenGL illumination model adopts improved ray casting algorithms.This system is coded in Visual C++6.0 program language.Realizing that almost all the volume rendering functions on the microcomputer completely,which involves creating gray voxel-model.volume rendering(including ray-gradient calculation,shading,image projection and composing),then an universal system,which has a good interface and the function of man-machine alternation,is coming into being.The system can be used to solve the problem preferably between the quality of volume rendering images and the rendering speed.

Abstract The DepthCube™ 3D display is a solid‐state multi‐planar volumetric display system in which a DLP based high speed projector projects slices of the 3D scene onto a stack of LC scattering shutters acting as an electronically controlled projection volume. The scattering shutters are based on Polymer Stabilized Cholesteric Texture material. Highly continuous appearing 3D images are obtained through multi‐planar anti‐aliasing.

Before: under Photon, real blocks out past the shadow range show small step shadows, and those shadows stopped at a hard line where far terrain began. JR saw this as a dotted band at the edge.
After: the far blocks just past the edge get the same step shadows, which fade out as the blocks shrink with distance. Far slopes seen from high up stay stripe-free.
How:
FarShaders.earth_slopeDepth now keeps a face's own depth where the voxel spans plenty of pixels. It uses the same pixel-size blend as the face lighting, through the new earth_faceShare.
FarLod's depth blur skips steps where the finest far voxels span more than a few pixels. That blur exists so the pack's screen-space shadows march over a hillside instead of a staircase.

Outcome
Bevy DistanceFog (linear, exponential, exponential-squared, atmospheric with extinction/inscattering colours and directional sun glow) for depth and mood in Greyhaven.
Scenario
fog_modes: a long receding row of pillars under each fog falloff, with and without sun glow.
Acceptance against the Bevy oracle
Scene gains a fog setting with every Bevy FogFalloff mode and the directional glow.
Every fog_modes view passes the gate.
Gate (docs/technical-decisions.md D3). Mean FLIP <= 0.005 and p99 FLIP <= 0.05 against Bevy on CI (lavapipe) and locally; median GPU frame time <= 1.10x Bevy on a hardware adapter (RTX 3060 Ti), with the oracle table in the PR. Every existing scenario still passes.
Notes
Volumetric fog (VolumetricFog) is out of scope until a consumer asks for it.

Batch scalar vari records for reverse matrix addition, subtraction, division, elementwise multiplication, and matrix multiplication into one arena allocation and one registered span per output. Adjoint reset, nested recovery, full recovery, and profiling account for the spans. Coefficient positions are preserved across storage orders and expressions without linear access; matrix products are evaluated before their coefficients are read.
The shared vari_no_stack tag has its own core header. When combining this change with PR #15, retain one definition of the tag and constructor.
All 39 tests across 11 focused targets passed with GCC C++17 at -O3. They cover span lifetime and zeroing, empty matrices, mixed storage orders, expressions, matrix multiplication, repeated gradients, and the affected arithmetic operations. No performance benchmarks were rerun.

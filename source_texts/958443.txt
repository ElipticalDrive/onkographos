depth = torch.full(resolution, camera.far, device=density.device)
occlusion_mask = densities > self.settings.transmittance_floor
if occlusion_mask.any():
first_hit = occlusion_mask.float().argmax(dim=-1)
depth = camera.near + first_hit.float() * step
Keep far depth for rays without hits
In weatherflow/utils/cloud_rendering.py lines 312-316 the depth map is updated whenever occlusion_mask.any() is true, using first_hit = occlusion_mask.float().argmax(dim=-1) for every pixel. When some pixels hit clouds and others are clear, argmax returns 0 for clear rays, so their depths are forced to near instead of staying at far, corrupting reprojection/visibility for empty-sky regions. Depth should remain at the far plane for pixels with no hits before applying the first-hit index.

Add an optional device transaction for poor interior tetrahedra: move the densest eligible interior site to the selected tetrahedron's centroid, or append a site when the device target permits growth. Donor ranking uses actual bounded Voronoi volume divided by local sizing cubed. A fresh hull-face table and signed circumcentric flags avoid depending on stale certificate scratch or substituting lumped primal volumes. Crease/corner/surface sites stay fixed; failed candidates restore the complete geometry, active count, inactive tail, statistics, and history.
On the 128-site sharp cube, either mode raises minimum interior radius ratio from 0.05584 to 0.31539, reducing poor-element deficit from 0.39972 to 0.34011. Teleportation takes 19.22 ms and insertion 18.41 ms including reset, reconstruction/statistics refresh, and extraction. On the 1,000-site cube, teleportation raises the interior minimum from 0.14046 to 0.23893 in 59.85 ms. Independent Delaunay/topology checks pass. Boundary minima remain unchanged, and the small example underfits the domain; these are stage results, not full-mesher or CPU speedup claims.
Validation: 19 focused tests and 330 full-suite tests pass. Three full-memory/allocation-lifetime cases and three pooled full-memory cases report zero sanitizer errors. Native profiling records 1,812 kernel activities across three teleportation replays with only device-to-device copies and no host/allocation/free graph nodes. Insertion, target switching, and rollback also compose with captured producers/consumers. All 38 other runtime modules and all 20 preexisting quality-transaction kernel/function ASTs are unchanged. The sole shared wrapper change extracts its original update launch into an overridable method.
Documented in docs/interior-improvement.md, with reproducible benchmark and evidence in results/validation/vtm/interior-*. Discovery #76 tracks full-scheduler recovery for an invalid coarse graded boundary; the local transaction correctly refuses it.
Closes #71. Part of #37/#29; combined time-to-quality comparison and the full public scheduler remain open.

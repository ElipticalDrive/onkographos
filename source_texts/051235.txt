Stage-4 dual contouring for sharper features.
What
New sculpt-core dual-contouring path: Hermite edge samples + QEF vertex placement, dual quads per sign-changing primal edge
MesherKind::{SurfaceNets, DualContouring} with extract_chunk_with / extract_full_mesh_with
Preferences → Mesher combo (default Surface Nets); switching redirties all visible chunks
Viewport remesh and STL export both use the selected mesher
Why
Surface Nets rounds cube corners and knife/cookie cuts. Dual Contouring keeps those features sharper while remaining optional (slightly more CPU).
Test plan
cargo test -p sculpt-core --lib (incl. DC sphere / QEF tests)
cargo test -p sculpt-app --bins
Manual: Preferences → Dual Contouring on a cube primitive — corners should read sharper; export STL with DC selected

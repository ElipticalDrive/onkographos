Study and bench only: no viewer C++ or shader changes. Report: doc/oit/stochastic-transparency-replacement.md.
Verdict
No-go for stochastic transparency as a replacement for PPLL.
Quality: no stochastic variant measured meets PPLL's quality up to 8 layers per pixel without TAA. PPLL is 0.73 RMSE / 1 step max. The stochastic variants are:
S = 8: 6.1 / 21
S = 4: 9.0 / 21
half resolution: 3.8 / 17, which fails on thin foliage with a max error of 120
GPU parity: the only variant at parity with PPLL slots on 8 layers is S = 4 with a 16-bit depth, and that depth merges distant layers (19.4 RMSE on the deep scene).
Pass count: all variants except the one-pass basic method need a second geometry pass over the alpha set. That is +100 % of non-rigged alpha draws with depth of field off (the default), and +50 % of rigged draws. The one-pass method is 3.7x PPLL and the noisiest (11.5 / 65).
Go-with-conditions only for a hybrid that keeps PPLL and changes its tail. It is exact (bit-identical to PPLL) up to 8 layers, and past the slots it uses the depth-based stochastic estimate.
Where it helps: past the slots RMSE drops: full 32 goes from 12.2 to 7.9, foliage 32 from 11.2 to 2.7.
What it costs:
The worst-pixel error rises: full 32 max 18 -> 42.
With D24 at S = 4 it is 1.86x PPLL at 8 layers and 2.13x at 32 layers (1440p), plus a depth-only geometry pass.
The cheaper lever is 16 PPLL slots.
They cost 1.00-1.04x of 8 slots up to 32 full-screen layers.
They make foliage 32 exact and bring full 32 to 6.8 / 9.
The price is memory: 156 B/px, 549 MiB at 1440p, above the 512 MB default budget.
Gate (Stage 0 in the report): an in-world survey must show overflow beyond what 16 slots fix before any hybrid work.

CPU side of the second stochastic transparency round, which asks whether it could
replace PPLL rather than sit beside it.

- deep: full-style layers 5 cm apart at 40 m under a perspective projection
  (near 0.1 m, far 256 m), where a 16-bit depth cannot separate them.
- occl: layers over 8 px opaque bars at depth 0.537 (off the layer depth grid,
  so no LEQUAL ties), to test a half-resolution depth at opaque silhouettes.
  exact_pixel() now hides fragments behind the scene's opaque depth.
- SCENE_PIXEL lets a half-resolution pass evaluate coverage at 2p.
- blue_noise(): 32x32 void-and-cluster threshold map.
- upsample_weights(): bilinear weights of the four half-resolution neighbours,
  dropping those whose opaque seed is nearer than the fragment.
- error_stats_blurred(): RMSE after a 3x3 binomial filter, a proxy for the
  noise seen at viewing distance.

Tests cover all of these, plus Python models of the one-pass basic method and
of the PPLL slots + stochastic overflow hybrid (exact within the slots, the
depth-based estimate past them).

Implementation names now take '+' options, so variants can be combined without
new code paths:

- stoch + d16 / d24: the multisample depth in 16 or 24 bits instead of 32F.
- stoch + mrt: total alpha as a second render target of pass A, dropping
  pass T (two geometry passes instead of three).
- stoch + half: pass D at half resolution with a depth-aware upsample in A.
- stoch + bn: blue-noise thresholds with a per-layer shift.
- s4 / s8 / s16: per-variant sample count.
- stoch1: basic stochastic transparency with alpha correction, one geometry
  pass like PPLL (multisample colour + depth, total alpha by image atomics).
- hybrid: PPLL slots exact where they suffice; on overflowing pixels, the
  depth-based estimator over the slots plus a visibility-weighted tail.

Also: the brmse column (error after a 3x3 binomial filter), measured
multisample memory via GL_NVX_gpu_memory_info where present, a total-alpha
check per variant, a check that hybrid matches PPLL slots where nothing
overflows, a per-variant summary against PPLL slots, and several PNG scenes
(OIT_BENCH_PNG_SCENE separated by ';'). The existing implementations, their
output and checks are unchanged.

The study the owner asked for on 2026-11-04: what it would take for
depth-sampled stochastic transparency to replace PPLL, and whether that is
achievable. Replacement criteria with numbers, the round 2 bench results
(NVIDIA 621.01 at 1440p and 1080p, Mesa Zink at 1440p), what closes the
GPU gap and what does not, the CPU/draw-call cost from the alpha pool code,
coverage of every alpha path, memory, the Vulkan line, the literature, and a
staged plan with gates.

Verdict: no-go as a replacement (no stochastic variant meets PPLL's quality
up to 8 layers without TAA, and all but the slowest need one more geometry
pass); go-with-conditions only for a hybrid that keeps PPLL and changes its
tail, and only where an in-world survey shows overflow past what 16 PPLL
slots fix.

- mrt with iterations (S = 16 on 8 independent hardware samples): write the
  total alpha only in the first iteration; it was summed twice.
- stoch1's total-alpha readback uses glGetTexImage and slices the window,
  instead of glGetTextureSubImage (OpenGL 4.5), keeping the bench on the
  viewer's 4.4 baseline.
- The hybrid check compares the read-back images value for value with PPLL
  slots on scenes without overflow and requires at least one such scene;
  equal RMSE did not prove equal images. Passes on NVIDIA and Zink with a
  maximum difference of 0.
- Report: the depth-of-field depth pass is off by default, so a second
  geometry pass doubles the non-rigged alpha draws (+100 %), +50 % only for
  rigged draws or with DoF on; the per-process residency includes the
  sample-count probe's D32F targets.

Mipmap Generation | Looooong/Unity-SRP-VXGI | DeepWiki
May 1, 2025This page documents the mipmap generation system in the Unity SRP VXGI implementation. Mipmapping is a crucial component for efficient voxel-based global illumination as it creates a hierarchical representation of the voxel volume with progressively lower resolutions, enabling efficient cone tracing and reducing aliasing artifacts.

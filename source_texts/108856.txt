Summary
Proposed design for fog and volumetric lighting. ADR 0018 status is Proposed. The froxel pass is not built.
The frame path is a budgeted froxel grid (Hillaire, SIGGRAPH 2015; Wronski, SIGGRAPH 2014). Per-pixel ray marching and epipolar shafts are rejected. Height fog, local volumes, and shadowed sun and point-lamp shafts share the grid. Indirect in-scattering is a read of the world cache. Fog does not write that cache.
Fog history is off on Potato, Low, Medium, and High, because ADR 0012 stores no radiance history. Enabling it is an open decision for Anthony. The evidence is froxel_temporal_smear_px and fog_yaw_pop_rel. This design does not choose that outcome.
Closed forms live in genos_math::media. fog_along and height_fog_along call them. crates/render/shaders/media.glsl is the GLSL mirror. fog-check grades those functions against an independent oracle. Renderer metrics stay NOT-IMPLEMENTED.

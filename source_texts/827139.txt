```
Implements TDD §5.2 — Amanatides-Woo 3D DDA voxel traversal for picking.
Raycasts the camera ray against the grid one voxel at a time using
state.getCell() — O(ray length), independent of cell count.

Avoids two cross-review traps:
- Does NOT use raycaster.intersectObject on InstancedMesh (slow, O(N))
- Does NOT read hit.face.normal (local-space, breaks under yaw rotation)

- src/picking.js — pickWithDDA(ray, state) → {hitCell, placementCoord, face}
- src/input.js — InputManager: pointermove/leave → renderer.setHover
  (pointerdown/up come in T-007 for actual placement)
- src/renderer.js — hoverMesh (wireframe box), setHover(coord|null)
  red tint when canPlace.ok=false
- src/main.js — wires InputManager

Tests:
- Unit: 10 picking tests (empty grid, cell hits, edge cases, 1000-ray
  perf check)
- E2E: 4 tests (hover on empty ground, pointerleave hides, hover on
  cube lands on adjacent face, visual snapshot)

Added three@0.160.0 as devDep so picking.test.js can use THREE.Ray in
Node. Production still loads three via CDN.

Total suite: 96 unit + 20 E2E.
```

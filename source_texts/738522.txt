Lighting plan, Phase 6 PR 6: volumetric shadows on the light block's path.
On the light block's path, the volumetric march has no shadow maps or placeholders of its own. Each slot's volumeShadow (kind, views, first view) names its light's views in the block (hapticShadowViews): a spot light's one view, a point light's faces, or the shadowed sun's cascades. The march reads them from the bound HapticLights buffer and samples the atlas with one bilinear compare tap, as it does on Babylon's maps, so its cost is unchanged. Its samplers are the scene depth and the atlas. The march's lights stay its own uniforms, as the plan decided.
The block's shadow functions now come from one source in two variants: the surfaces' and the volume's single tap. The surface shaders are byte-identical to before (HAPTIC_LIGHTING_FUNCTIONS_GLSL/WGSL compared), so most of the diff in haptic-lighting-shader.ts is the move.
volumeShadow{i} widens from vec2 to vec4 on the legacy path too (shader and bind, both backends). The combined e2e and the new test's legacy captures cover it.
A spatial stage whose fragment reads a uniform buffer is drawn from the shader store, keyed by a hash of its source. Babylon builds an inline source's Effect with a constructor that takes no uniform buffers. Without this, the march drew nothing on WebGL2 ("uniform buffer that is too small") and failed createBindGroup on WebGPU. Only stages that declare a uniform buffer take this path.
New e2e in e2e/spatial-effects.spec.ts: the shadowed fog spot light over unlit surfaces, on the legacy path and on the block's path, on WebGL2 and WebGPU. The shadowed fog differs by 0.005–0.006 of 255 on average, the unshadowed fog is identical, and the occluder cuts the same pixels on both paths.
Checks: typecheck, lint, the render unit suite (3477 tests), and e2e/spatial-effects.spec.ts plus e2e/haptic-lights.spec.ts on desktop Chrome.

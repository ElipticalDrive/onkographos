Every year graphics cards are capable of rendering more polygons than the previous generation, resulting in more detailed geometry in games. However, artists are still limited by the technical restrictions of both the engine and the hardware. This report discusses an alternate method of representing geometry data via the use of Sparse Voxel Octrees (SVOs) in order to govern geometric Level of Detail (LoD) with the technology being able to (theoretically) render geometry in infinite detail. \n \nThis report discusses and shows the technology running on a single-threaded system as well as the research, development, and process of producing the program including challenges faced during the project. \n

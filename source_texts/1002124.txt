Summary
Replaces VIDVOX 9-pass buffer delay (required inputImage, produced nothing standalone) with a fully standalone single-pass 3D raymarcher
Central sphere (r≈0.6) fractured via voronoi displacement — 8 spherical voronoi cell centers, each cell's fragment displaced radially by a hash amount × glitchAmount
RGB chromatic separation via three concentric shell raymarches: Red shell at r−0.025, Green shell at r, Blue shell at r+0.025; results composited with max() per channel
Key technique details
Sphere SDF with per-voronoi-cell displacement (sdFracturedSphere)
Camera slow orbit: rotX(sin(t*orbitSpeed*0.13)*0.35) * rotY(t*orbitSpeed*0.2)
Per-cell hue rotation matrix applied to base RGB primaries (red/data-green/electric-blue)
Blinn-Phong: diffuse + specular → lighting * hdrPeak; edge rim pow(1−dot(N,V),3)*3.0 → HDR peaks 2.0+ on faces, 3.0 on edges
fwidth() AA on sphere silhouette
Deep black background [0,0,0.01]
Test plan
Load in ISF-compatible host (VDMX, ISF Editor, etc.) — confirm sphere visible as central object on black background
Animate glitchAmount 0→1: sphere fragments should pop/shatter into cells
Animate orbitSpeed: camera should slowly orbit the sphere
Confirm RGB fringe/chromatic aberration visible around sphere edges
Confirm HDR brightness peaks on directly lit faces (>1.0 channel values)
Confirm no inputImage dependency — runs standalone as generator

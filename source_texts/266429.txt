Add an illustrated Appendix A to the LaTeX guide so that the traversal's correctness contract can be reviewed before machine formalisation. It defines half-open voxel membership, ordered output intervals and physical lengths, then gives a written exact-arithmetic proof of clipping, initialisation, invariant preservation, tied crossings, completeness, uniqueness and termination for a model of walk(). A decreasing integer also bounds the number of emitted intervals.
The notation explicitly uses zero-based integer coordinate indices i, real ray parameter t, integer voxel-index vectors c, and Cartesian products of sets. It distinguishes the voxel count n_i from the largest voxel index n_i-1. A physical-space/parameter-space diagram illustrates a 3×2 example, its output entry and boundary-point membership. The text defines output records, callbacks, clipping, sentinels and clamping alongside preconditions, postconditions, invariants and variants.
This is a written mathematical proof, not a machine-checked Lean proof, a C++ refinement proof or a floating-point guarantee. The kernel and wrappers are unchanged.
Update the checked-in PDF, figure-generation script, navigation and validation status. Included TeX files are Makefile dependencies; the expanded contents fit on one page.
Validation: rebuilt with latexmk -Werror; no unresolved references or layout warnings; visually inspected the updated pages and diagram. The worked example was checked by exact rational per-voxel clipping. Appendix A is on PDF pages 19–23, with the notation diagram on page 20. No Lean toolchain or numerical-test dependencies are added.

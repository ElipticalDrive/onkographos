抄録
We built a realtime volume rendering system. This system enables us to draw an image of volume rendering quite easily. For example, by means of this system, we can confirm flow simulation results by volume rendering even though the operation is carried out during the calculation. In order to complete this system, we introduced a concept of a simplified volume rendering, and utilized Open GL facilities.

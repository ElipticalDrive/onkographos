Third milestone of the render quality track. Plan and results: docs/plans/2026-11-02-r3-ao-fog.md.
What changes
Prepass: the lit vertex shaders plus fs_prepass draw the scene again at one sample per pixel. They write world normals (Rgba8Unorm) and linear view depth (R32Float).
AO: ground-truth AO (Jimenez et al. 2016) at full resolution:
2 slices × 4 steps per side, 1 m radius by default;
a 4×4 Bayer pattern for rotation and jitter, which a depth-aware 4×4 blur then averages out exactly, so no TAA is needed;
the camera basis and pixel scale come from view_proj (ao.rs, unit-tested), so CameraData is unchanged.
Lit shader: reads the AO texel at group 3 and applies it to ambient light only, never the sun:
diffuse uses the multi-bounce fit and specular uses Lagarde's specular occlusion;
it combines with glTF occlusion textures by min.
Fog: exponential height fog (Quilez's analytic integral along the view ray), fading toward the horizon sky plus sun in-scatter. The sky itself is unfogged.
API: AoSettings { enabled, radius, intensity, show }, FogSettings { enabled, density, height_falloff }, their setters, and Renderer::ao_supported(). Both are on by default.
Sandbox: the Rendering section is collapsible (starts collapsed) and gains AO and fog controls.
Why AO doesn't sample the depth buffer
naga's GLSL backend can't textureLoad a texture_depth_2d; the WebGL2 shader test caught it.
Binding the depth texture as unfilterable float got past naga but read back 0 on WebGL2, found with a depth-stripe debug build.
So the prepass writes linear depth to a colour target. R32Float needs EXT_color_buffer_float on WebGL2, the same extension as HDR. SceneTarget::ao records it; without it AO is off.
Risk
Public renderer API: solstice and Ashen Crown both cargo check clean. They now get AO and fog by default.
Cost: the prepass draws all geometry a second time.
Verified
Unit tests (camera basis, AO capability); every shader translates to WebGL2 GLSL; clippy native + wasm32 (-D warnings); workspace tests
AO buffer on WebGPU and WebGL2 (temporary debug build): flats stay white, contacts and corners darken, no halos or visible noise, backends match
Final image on WebGPU (Props): grounded contact shading, distance haze, no console errors
Native Vulkan debug and release, plus skinned_character: no validation errors
Not yet verified
Hand check of AO and fog, in the browser and natively
Frame-time cost of the prepass and AO (needs GPU timestamps)

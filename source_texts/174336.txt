About me
I was a researcher in scientific visualization, volume visualization, parallel graphics.
I defended my Phd at the University of Strasbourg in 2007. In 2008 and 2009 I was a postdoctorate researcher at the CEA. In 2010 I was a postdoctorate researcher at UC Davis. I now work for the big G.
I also hack on open source graphics stuff (graphics drivers, 2D and 3D apps, ...) as time permits.

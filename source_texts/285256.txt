Describe the bug
Rays intersecting with a Sphere surface with a Hexagon apertures are reflecting at points a way from the surface.
There seems to be a disagreement between the surface definition within the tracer and the GUI depiction.
This disagreement is also impacting the flux map calculations.
To Reproduce
Steps to reproduce the behavior:
Open and trace the "Aperture Examples.stinput" example file
View intersections, isolate an ray that "pops" into and out of the depicted spherical surface.
Expected behavior
Depicted surfaces should be consistent with SolTrace's representation.
We should test other surface configurations for this issue as well.

Visualizing large uncorrelated point data sets with regular volume rendering produces unsatisfying results due to low spatial resolution. For timedependent data available main memory can be a limiting factor as well. In order to deal with these large time-varying data sets we create a hierarchical representation and store quantized relative coordinates. During rendering the compressed data is accessed out-of-core, and particle positions are interpolated using B-splines. By traversing the hierarchical structure for each frame we can trade-off rendering speed vs. image quality, achieving interactive rates for data sets with millions of points

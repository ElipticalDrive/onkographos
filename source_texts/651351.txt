Closes #201
Decision D[octane]: forces without a neighbor structure, by a search in a region octree every step (Toutouni, Chakraborty, Tu, Huang, "OCTANE: Breaking the Neighbor-List Bottleneck in GPU Molecular Dynamics", ICS '26, https://doi.org/10.1145/3797905.3807878), are not adopted. A prototype of the method takes 5 to 10 times a step of the groups of MDIR on every system measured, the vapor of the publication included.
No code of MDIR changes and no key of the control file: a standalone prototype, a document with the theorems, and the record of the decision.
What is in the PR
scripts/experiments/neighbor-structures/octree.cu: the method (T) and the same search over a grid of cells without the tree (G), with run.sh, the results (results/2026-10-08-octree-NVIDIA-GeForce-RTX-3090.csv), and a section of the README with every table. groups.cu gains two macros so that it runs on argon; its default build is unchanged.
docs/octane.md: the evaluation. scripts/experiments/neighbor-structures/octane_analysis.py checks its formulas by Monte Carlo and in closed form.
docs/decisions.md (the row D[octane]), docs/references.md (Toutouni2026, verified against Crossref), docs/paper/04-neighbors.md (a paragraph "Why a list at all" beside "Why not tiles"), docs/paper/02-notation.md, docs/roadmap.md.
Theorems (docs/octane.md)
The publication states no theorem of correctness. The document proves:
Completeness (Theorems 1 to 4). If every particle is stored in the leaf of its wrapped position, a descent that tests the boxes of the regions reaches every particle in the query box; in a periodic cell the query box is searched in its at most eight images, and with $r_c &lt; L_a/2$ each pair within the cutoff is found exactly once; moving every particle that left its leaf keeps the tree consistent. It fails with tight bounding boxes, which go stale when only the leaves are updated, as the publication describes, and when a leaf is full, which the publication does not address.
The levels of a search (Theorem 5, Corollary 6). $P(\ell_\text{c} \ge l) = \prod_a (1 - 2^{l+1} r_c/L_a)_+$ for uniform positions, and a mean climb below $\lceil\log_2 u\rceil + 22/7$ levels whatever the number of particles. The publication's claim of a constant climb holds, and its Tables 1 and 2 follow from its formulas; it leaves out the faces of the periodic cell, which send 2.7 to 12% of its own searches to the root.
The cost of a search (Proposition 7). $\rho\prod_a(w_a + 2r_c)$ candidates: $\tfrac{3}{4\pi}(2 + w/r_c)^3$ for each pair within the cutoff, 3.7 for leaves of half the cutoff and 15 for twice, where a list of 9 Å over 8 Å meets 1.42.
The levels of an update (Theorem 8, Corollary 9). $P(J \le k) = E\prod_a (1 - \lvert\Delta x_a\rvert/2^k w_a)_+$. In the publication's model the probability of leaving a leaf is $29/(24\pi)$ in two dimensions, as it says, and $\tfrac{9}{16} - \tfrac{3}{10\pi} + \tfrac{1}{64\pi} = 0.472$ in three, not its 0.4237; the mean climb is 1.003 levels, not 0.78 (Monte Carlo: 0.4719 and 1.0030). A step of dynamics moves a particle three orders of magnitude less than that model assumes, so updates are cheaper than the publication's analysis says.
The capacity of a leaf (Proposition 10). With the factor 1.5 of the publication and independent positions, 6.4% of leaves of 8 particles overflow.
Validation
Exactness (oracle: all pairs on the host in f64; tolerance: the same set of pairs, forces to rounding in f32). JAC and argon in a small cell where 76% of the query boxes cross a face: T at every leaf level and G at every cell size give the same pairs, none missing and none added, also with leaves over their capacity and after 25 partial updates; forces within $7.5 \times 10^{-7}$ relative.
Time a step (µs, one RTX 3090, f32, the pair kernels of the other prototypes; M and Gr are the matrix and the groups of MDIR, loop plus build over the interval between builds).

The excluded pairs, which the publication does not have, add 23 to 24% to the search of T. The benchmark of the publication is a vapor ($9.07 \times 10^{-4}$ atoms/Å³ from its Table 3, 3.7 pairs an atom).
Theory against measurement. The measured shares of the levels of a search are those of Theorem 5 to the third digit; the candidates an atom are 768 and 1403 against 769 and 1393 of Proposition 7; 0.033% of the atoms of liquid argon leave their leaf in a step, as $3,E\lvert\Delta x_a\rvert/w$ gives.
Memory. T holds 0.8 MB on JAC and 9.4 MB on Cellulose where the groups hold 4.0 and 71 MB and the matrix 28 and 516 MB. This is the one gain; no system of the suite is bound by memory.
No build of MDIR and no suite: nothing of MDIR changes.
Left open
The prototype was not profiled, and its kernels are a first version; the ratio between T and G (3 to 7) is the part most likely to shrink. That G stays behind the groups does not depend on the tree.
The programs of MDIR were not tuned for the vapor.
JAC and Cellulose have no dynamics in the prototype; the share of atoms that leave a leaf there is from displacements drawn at 300 K.

Eliminating popping artifacts in sheet buffer-based splatting
A new layered, hardware-accelerated splatting algorithm for volume rendering that features the speed benefits of fast axis-aligned pre-classified sheet-buffer splatts while at the same time exhibiting display quality comparable to high-quality post-classified view-aligned sheet- buffers.

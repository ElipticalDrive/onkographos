1. Objectives & Scope
Seamless PBR Texture Suite & Texture Atlas: Generate procedural seamless PBR textures (Albedo, Normal maps with tangent perturbations, and Roughness/Metallic maps) for core feudal voxel blocks: Stone, Dirt, Grass, Oak Log, Oak Planks, Cobblestone, Brick, Sand, Water, Iron Ore, and Gold Ore. Pack into unified PBR Texture Atlas.

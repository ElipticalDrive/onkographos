Fix
A contained sphere necessarily intersects its box, so combining center containment with negated intersection rejected enclosed spheres. Replace that contradiction with a finite nonnegative radius check against all six center-to-face clearances.
Containment is closed: include face tangency and zero-radius spheres, including points on degenerate boxes. Reject negative/nonfinite radii, nonfinite centers, NaN bounds and invalid boxes. Valid unbounded boxes may contain finite spheres. Comparisons use ordinary floating-point subtraction and rounding, without a tolerance or a full-range exact-arithmetic guarantee.
Intersects, cylinder containment and bounding-volume generation are unchanged.

Volumetric Video is an exciting medium that enables visualization of 3D time-varying data, often as sequence of Point Clouds or Textured Meshes. Despite over a decade of focus, there still exist several problems that make it disadvantageous to use. This research chooses to focus on reducing the size of Volumetric Video using Textured Meshes, as files often become very large for sequences consisting of only a few seconds of footage. Signed Distance Fields are created from Textured Meshes, such that temporal compression of data may be more easily explored and exploited. The goal of this work is to produce/experiment with a novel compression method for these Time-Varying Textured Meshes that can maximize a size-to-error compression ratio, while maintaining a decompression speed of 30 frames per second. The results reflect that as file sizes decrease, the proposed method’s error increases at a slower rate than the chosen state-of-the-art.

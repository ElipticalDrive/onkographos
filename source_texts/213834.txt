Owner
Native octree preprocessing and rendering repeated region membership, surface collection, prop-frame registration and lighting math. Several APIs also required callers to pair output pointers, array pointers and separate counts.
Own particle and prop region-link construction in builder-local containers, return packed link tables by value, and share GLM point-to-volume assignment. Replace the global fixed surface scratch buffer with returned, stable deduplicated collections. Share ordered subtree traversal and polygon/terrain stream packing, returning named offset/end results. Temporary region bounds use owned GLM boxes and common inclusion math. Packed ids and stream widths retain their disk representation.
Share lighting distance ramps, normal-dot calculations, attenuation products and color accumulation across light, fog and vertex-channel processing. Fog and weighted diffuse-light inputs use counted spans. Preserve point/specular nonnegative dots, directional unit clamping, separate normalization/saturation/amplitude passes, attenuation multiplication order and batch-cache/statistics behavior.
Return complete GLM prop bounds while updating the existing collision/path caches. Pass complete boxes through prop registration and overlap queries; share cell conversion with cellAt. Consolidate animation-frame registration and support/blocker conditional linking, retaining frame numbering and blocking flags. Door probes use common box inclusion and center math; this corrects the former Y-minimum comparison against the destination's Z coordinate.
Own conditional edge/waypoint channels in vectors and pass counted spans into status refresh. Return GLM path-cell coordinates and packed keys together; share major-axis crossing interpolation while retaining unsigned coordinate narrowing, truncation and the existing vertical-boundary policy.
Stacked on #154. Cut here; subsequent work continues in a new stacked PR.
Validation: affected native targets build. All 48 geometry cases pass at the final head; vector-math and renderer ownership suites passed for the rendering batch. Regressions cover native bounds, inclusive/negative-coordinate cell registration, duplicate registration, conditional support/blocker keys, independent builder storage, record limits/order, 11000-surface collection, actual leaf-stream conversion, light attenuation combinations and 64-vertex batch boundaries. All changed C++ sources pass include checks with assertions enabled. The preceding surface-collection head passed full Linux, ASan and include-hygiene CI; full CI is submitted for this new head.
Identical experimental semantic scans: region-link construction stays at 40 candidates; lighting stays at 10 with source complexity 335 to 302; surface collection falls from 12 to 11; the latest prop-bound family falls from 37 to 36 and source tokens from 43934 to 43226. Conditional channels/path conversion falls from 10 to 9 candidates (3.3% to 3.0%). Candidate counts overlap and are not independent fix counts. Some percentages rise as source is removed. The legacy uv run wiz8 pr-check entrypoint is unavailable (ModuleNotFoundError: wiz8decomp.cli); native builds, targeted tests and direct clang-tidy checks provide local validation.

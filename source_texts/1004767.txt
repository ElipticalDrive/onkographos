In yt off-axis projections are done using the volume rendering infrastructure. The core of the off-axis projection operation happens in this loop:
yt/yt/visualization/volume_rendering/off_axis_projection.py
Lines 189 to 196
in
592b2d6
for i, (grid, mask) in enumerate(data_source.blocks):
data = [(grid[f] * mask).astype("float64") for f in fields]
pg = PartitionedGrid(
grid.id, data,
mask.astype('uint8'),
grid.LeftEdge, grid.RightEdge, grid.ActiveDimensions.astype("int64"))
grid.clear_data()
vol.sampler(pg, num_threads = num_threads)
It should be possible to wrap the enumerate in parallel_objects. It will require adding a reduction step to combine the partial projection results from each processor. Since projections are linear, it should be safe to just add the results from each processor.

In this work, we study the reconstruction of blood velocity with contrast enhanced computed tomography with tomographic projections perpendicular to the main flow field direction. The inverse problem is regularized with a convection-diffusion partial differential equation and with the Navier-Stokes equation. The velocity field is reconstructed together with the density field with a first-order adjoint method. The method is validated on a simple phantom. The addition of a physical constraint with a partial differential equation for the velocity field improves the reconstruction results.

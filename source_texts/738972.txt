Implement a Wolfenstein 3D-style raycasting dungeon accessible from a
portal in the overworld. Features include DDA raycasting with distance
fog, textured walls with side shading, billboarded enemy/item sprites,
boss fight with charge attacks and phase transitions, minimap overlay,
sword swing animation, damage flash, screen shake, and HUD with hearts
and key count.
New files:
raycaster.py: Core raycasting engine with DDA algorithm, sprite
rendering, and minimap
states/dungeon3d_state.py: 3D dungeon gameplay state with enemies,
boss, items, and combat
Modified files:
world/tile.py: Add DUNGEON_3D_ENTRANCE tile type (29)
sprites/tile_sprites.py: Add swirling portal sprite for the entrance
world/maps.py: Place portal at (16,17) in overworld wilds with sign
states/play_state.py: Handle DUNGEON_3D_ENTRANCE tile to push 3D state
https://claude.ai/code/session_01BekwNwjD4WoTBkhxgWThsv

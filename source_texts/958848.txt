Why
Block edits were not becoming visible promptly because chunk meshing performed many synchronized world reads and rebuild work was not scheduled with a reliable near-first policy. Terrain UVs could also switch projection within a single Marching Cubes triangle.
What changed
Use fixed worker slots with render-thread uploads and stale-revision checks, following the claudemc rebuild lifecycle.
Schedule visible chunks in a center-out spiral so nearby chunks are rebuilt and loaded first.
Invalidate the edited chunk and neighboring chunks immediately after a successful block mutation.
Build Marching Cubes meshes from a 33x33x33 world snapshot, avoiding repeated synchronized reads during meshing.
Use one planar UV projection per triangle for stable slope texture mapping.
Fix snapshot indexing to match the allocated 33x33x33 buffer.
Validation
./gradlew.bat --no-daemon clean build

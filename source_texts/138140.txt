Raycasting object "detection" : r/opengl - Reddit
Your ray-plane intersection should give you a parametric value, t, which is the distance along the ray where it intersects with the plane. If the equivalent t -value for the far point of your segment is > the t -value of the intersection point, then your segment collides with the plane.

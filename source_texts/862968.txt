Cull chunk-edge faces against neighboring chunks
In the new multi-chunk setup (State::new builds a contiguous 3x3 world), generate_mesh still treats every chunk boundary as exposed by unconditionally emitting faces when x == CHUNK_SIZE - 1/x == 0 and similarly for z. This leaves hidden seam faces between adjacent solid chunks, which significantly inflates triangle count and buffer upload cost as world size grows. Meshing needs neighbor-chunk occupancy checks at boundaries to preserve face culling across chunk seams.

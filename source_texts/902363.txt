Motivation
Introduce a voxel-backed terrain layer and editing capabilities so players can mine and place blocks on a spherical planet.
Provide stable dynamic gravity and smooth player orientation across the planet surface to support FPS-style movement on a sphere.
Add raycasting and chunk/voxel bookkeeping to support edits and eventual chunk remeshing.
Description
Added vector and quaternion utilities including quatFromAxisAngle, quatRotateVector, quatSlerp, and supporting helpers for gravity and tangent calculations.
Implemented voxel system primitives such as worldToVoxelIndex, voxelKey, voxelCenterFromIndex, sampleBaseDensity, getVoxelDensity, raycastDensityField, and modifyDensitySphere, plus maps to track modifiedDensity, voxelOverrides, and voxelTypes.
Integrated player physics and input changes: expanded settingsPhysics with voxel/planet parameters, changed state to include rotation, modifiedDensity, voxelOverrides, and chunk bookkeeping, replaced pitch/turn handling with pitchDelta/turnDelta and quaternion-based rotation limits, and updated gravity to use a planet center.
Added mine/place interactions: prevent default context menu, handle LMB to set voxel to AIR and RMB to place blocks with collision checks, and mark affected chunks dirty via markDirtyChunkForVoxel/markDirtyChunkByVoxelIndex.
Implemented ownership/neighbor transfer helpers (buildTileLookup, worldToChunkVoxel, transferToNeighborChunk, resolveChunkOwnership) and a placeholder processDirtyChunkRemesh to flag chunk remeshes.
Updated rendering to cull non-solid tiles via isSolidAtWorld, adjusted shading to use integer rgb(...) colors, and tweaked HUD and input text to reflect new controls.
Testing
No automated tests were added or executed as part of this change.

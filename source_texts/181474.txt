A sharp (sixth order) low-pass ﬁlter, which is built into the cubic-spline transform, effectively eliminates adverse nonlinear accumulation of small-scale errors near the resolution limit. These features, critically important to noise-free nesting, are deﬁned and analyzed in this paper in the simpler context of a single 1D domain.

Divine Augustine10-Jul-14 1:23
Divine Augustine10-Jul-14 1:23
The idea behind transfer function is to change the characteristics of the data depending on few parameters. a simple example is to remap each pixel and its alpha with a different value according to our algorithm. This we can do statically before transfering into 3D texture, or dynamically on the fly during rendering. For the dynamic one we will have to enable shaders. For the static one we dont have to use shaders because every time we change the values which alters the characteristics, we will recreate the 3D texture with newly mapped values. For the dynamic one, we will make the 3D texture only once. And whenever we alter the characteristics, we build a transfer function texture and gives it to the shaders. Shader will always get the pixel from 3D texture and will look up in the 1D texture of trasfer function and will replace the values. I suggest you enable shaders which makes it flexible , easy and faster.

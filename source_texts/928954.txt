Pose Evaluation | ActiveVisionLab/nope-nerf | DeepWiki
The pose evaluation system takes the optimized camera poses from the trained NoPe-NeRF model and compares them against ground truth camera poses. Since NoPe-NeRF operates without initial pose priors, evaluating the accuracy of the estimated poses is crucial for validating the effectiveness of the approach.

The subtleties of geometry preservation: Tet4 vs Tet10
All incremental improvement operations designed based on linear tets
• Element quality metrics used to evaluate quality improvement or best configuration
• Element topology checks
• Collapses only considered along element edges
§ Can’t have midedge node as node-to-keep
• Swaps only consider replacing element edges with new element edges
• Only element edges are candidates for lying on sharp features
Resulting strategy:
§ Refine all “kinked” edges ⇨ capture all geometry
features with element edges
§ Preserve geometry to within tolerance of straight
edges during incremental improvement
§ Project midedge nodes to geometry as long as inradius is not too degraded compared to in-radius
with straight edges

Summary/Perspective
• Composite tet 10 is enabling rapid model development and adaptive refinement for large,
resolved deformation for solid mechanics
• Adaptive, incremental mesh improvement is a critical component to realizing both rapid model
development and adaptivity
• Tet10 mesh improvement requires specialized strategies
§ Currently appears that tet10 ⇨ refined tet4 ⇨ mesh improvement ⇨ tet10 appears to be
effective
• In-core remeshing is “tricky”
§ Easy to make software design assumptions that are invalidated by mesh modification
• Future work
§ Mesh smoothing appears to be an important component of mesh improvement

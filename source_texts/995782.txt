Description
Currently, linear algebra operations have two ergonomic gaps:
Matrix-Vector Multiplication: There is no method on Matrix<T> or Vector<T> to compute the matrix-vector product $A \cdot v$ or vector-matrix product $v^T \cdot A$.
Cross-ADT Matrix Multiplication: multiply is defined only within DenseMatrix (multiply(DenseMatrix)) and SquareMatrix (multiply(SquareMatrix)), with no polymorphic Matrix<T>.multiply(Matrix<T>) method on the sealed interface. Multiplying a SquareMatrix by a rectangular DenseMatrix requires explicit downcasting / conversions.
Proposed Improvement
Add Vector<T> multiply(Vector<T> vector) to Matrix<T>.
Add polymorphic Matrix<T> multiply(Matrix<T> other) or cross-type multiplication overloads.

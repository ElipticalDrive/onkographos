3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has recently attracted significant attention for human avatar reconstruction, providing explicit scene representation and real-time rendering performance surpassing NeRF-based techniques. However, existing 3DGS approaches rely on Linear Blend Skinning (LBS), which cannot model pose-dependent non-rigid effects such as muscle contraction, clothing wrinkles, and skin sliding. Existing MLP-based corrections like RMAvatar suffer from severe overfitting due to large parameter counts and observation-space composite functions. This paper proposes BGPR (Bone-Guided Pose-Dependent Rectification), a lightweight and generalizable method that decomposes non-rigid deformation residuals into bone-level offsets propagated via LBS skinning weights, applies corrections in canonical space before LBS deformation, and employs Tanh hard constraints. Experiments on ZJU-MoCap demonstrate BGPR achieves 32.48 dB PSNR, 0.9781 SSIM, and 0.0182 LPIPS, outperforming all existing methods with only ~10.5K parameters.

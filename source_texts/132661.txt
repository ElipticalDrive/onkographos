Ray Marching CUDA Kernels | ashawkey/nerf2mesh | DeepWiki
Oct 29, 2025Purpose and Scope This page documents the low-level CUDA kernel implementations that power volumetric ray marching in nerf2mesh. These kernels implement the core algorithms for efficiently sampling points along rays, querying a spatial acceleration structure (density bitfield), and compositing colors via volume rendering equations.

What is asked
Terrain as a 3D scalar density field (fBm in x, y and z) meshed by Marching Cubes with interpolated vertices and gradient normals, displacement along normals for overhangs, and true 3D caves.
Why it matters: A heightfield stores one height per column and cannot express overhangs, arches or true caves.
What is true today
Nothing yet. Caves are layered voxel warrens walked in gate P19.
What is missing
A renderer that consumes meshes and a 3D noise. The 256-case table and its 14 base cases are integer work; vertex interpolation must stay Fixed32 to fold.
How we will know it is done
A density chunk is meshed by Marching Cubes in ring 0 and on the host with the same vertex signature, and the client walks under an overhang.

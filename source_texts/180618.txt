Conclusions
We have presented a new data structure called ]time-Space Partition
(TSP) tree. The TSP tree is designed to hierarchically
represent a
time-varying
volume both in temporal and spatial domains.
The
temporal coherence is exploited based on the idea that if the data in
the volume are unchanged in a given time span, it is only necessary
to perform rendering once and reuse the same image for the animation sequence. Combined with the exploitation
of spatial coherence,
the time-varying
volume rendering speed can be accelerated.

For Gouraud Shading:
1. Find the vertices color by applying illumination model. Be sure to standardize the vertices normal.
2. Point P is defined in screen coordinate system (SCS), therefore find the SCS values of the triangle
vertices.
a. Find VCS values first.
b. Calculate NDCS values.
c. Compute SCS values, ignore the depth.
3. Interpolate color of the vertices to find the color at P.
a. Determine the scanline containing P.
b. Calculate intersection points P4, P5 with the scanline and the lines P3P1 and P2P1.
For Phong Shading:
1. Find SCS value of P as before. Compute the interpolated normal at point Pscs from the vertices
normal.
2. Apply illumination model at Pscs. In that case, you need depth info at Pscs and to find the WCS
value correspond to this Pscs.
a. Lerp the vertices pseudo-depth to get approximate depth value at Pscs. [though real
graphics pipeline doesn’t go this way]
b. Apply reverse graphics pipeline
i. convert SCS to NDCS by inverse ndcs-to-viewport matrix.
ii. Transform NDCS to VCS by inverse projection matrix and homogenization.
iii. Convert VCS to WCS by inverse world-to-vcs matrix.

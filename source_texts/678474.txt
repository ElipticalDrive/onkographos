Summary
Fixes Object 3's bifurcating tree branches merging into a solid object instead of forming distinct branching channels.
Root cause: voxel_union_meshes() at 50µm pitch was merging branches that are close together. The terminal radius is only 100µm (2 voxels at 50µm pitch), which is insufficient resolution to preserve fine branch structure. With 128 terminals per inlet in a 5mm radius domain, adjacent branches were being merged during voxelization.
Fix: Replace voxel_union_meshes() with trimesh.util.concatenate() in generate_bifurcation_tree_mesh(). This preserves individual branch geometry and lets the embedding operation handle the union at the finer 25µm pitch.

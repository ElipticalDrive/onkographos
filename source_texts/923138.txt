Part of the "ReImagined look" (#361): the cars look great, but trees, rocks and terrain look flat next to newer games. A lighting pass could close much of that gap without new art:
Screen-space ambient occlusion (SSAO/GTAO) on the world: contact shadows under trees, rocks, rails and buildings, and in terrain creases.
Better shadow filtering (softer, less aliased edges), building on the longer shadow distance from World detail options: farther shadows of small objects and sharper distant textures (#361) #366.
Optionally, a light grading/tonemapping touch if it fits the game's look.
Each as an option, off or console-like by default until tested, with its fps cost on a mid-range PC and the Steam Deck.
@soconnor0919 this overlaps the renderer passes you're rebuilding in #157 (world-color composition), so it would probably fit best on top of your native passes. Would you like to take it?

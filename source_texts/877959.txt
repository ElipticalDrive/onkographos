Why this matters
Builds on #43's off-surface point/segment work with the first independent moving AABB versus original DX2 Level triangles geometric oracle. Continuous 13-axis SAT (3 box axes, triangle normal, 9 edge-cross-box axes) computes time-of-overlap intervals. Separately, a read-only source-authenticated MOPP interpreter propagates the full box footprint (center segment and half-extents) through axis filters, splits and rescale instructions. Unknown diagonals still take both children by default; PR #39 fitted formulas remain explicit experimental opt-in.
Private original 19-map audit, read-only
Using owner-provided GMP source bytes with original SHA-256 and Level/MOPP envelope cross-checks against #30/#36: 4,864 moving AABB paths, half-extents (0.2, 0.2, 0.3) original Level units. Of 2,432 independent expanded-bounds random sweeps, 846 made actual geometric contact with 2,665 original triangle IDs. All 2,432 separately triangle-aimed sweeps made contact with 4,913 original triangle IDs. Across 7,578 geometric hit occurrences and 9,728 MOPP candidate-set comparisons (two diagonal modes), zero observed missing original triangle IDs. All original commercial map data remains private and unchanged. This is finite empirical evidence, NOT Havok runtime behavior.
Implementation and synthetic tests
research/dx2/inspect_mopp_swept_aabb.py: independent moving-box SAT geometry oracle, BVH, source-ID/time overlaps, and MOPP whole-sweep candidate expansion.
research/dx2/audit_mopp_swept_aabb.py: owner-GMP source-validated CLI and exclusive fresh sanitized JSON outside Maps.
native_editor/tests/test_dx2_mopp_swept_aabb.py: 19 tests for interval times, coplanar motion, reversed winding, multilevel crossing, rescale/filter/diagonal box expansion, invalid input and the synthetic bad-split counterexample.
native_editor/tests/test_dx2_mopp_swept_aabb_audit.py: 6 original-Level/Flesh/MOPP fixture tests, including intentional false negatives in the old synthetic tree, zero-area ID gaps, source immutability and safe refusals.
Research documentation in docs/research/DX2_MOPP_SWEPT_AABB_SAT.md.
Additional shape-size checks: Across all 19 original maps, two further audits with 0.02/0.02/0.02 half-extents and 1.0/1.0/1.5 half-extents tested 2,432 sweeps per size, independently finding 2,930 and 7,334 geometric hit triangle IDs. Both MOPP candidate modes had zero misses. Aggregated across all three box sizes: 9,728 sweeps, 17,842 observed source geometric hit occurrences, 19,456 candidate comparisons, zero observed omissions. Sampling paths partially overlap across box sizes because the same seed and prefix were used; this is a size-sensitivity check, not three independent random populations. Detailed documentation in the research notes.
Verification: 25/25 new tests passed locally. Full GitHub source and Godot 4.3 fixture CI pending on latest head. No Havok shape sweep, collision response, capsules, physics-material interpretation, native GMP writer or runtime test. Remains draft for research review.

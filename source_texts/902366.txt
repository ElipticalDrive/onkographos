Motivation
Integrate a simple voxel-based terrain and editing system so players can mine and place blocks on a spherical planet.
Support curved-planet gravity and smooth player orientation so movement and camera remain stable on a globe.
Provide raycasting and density sampling to drive interactions and a chunk-dirty/remesh mechanism placeholder for future meshing.
Description
Added core math and orientation utilities including quaternion functions (quatFromAxisAngle, quatRotateVector, quatSlerp) and helpers for gravity/basis (gravityDownVector, tangentForwardFromDirection, buildDynamicGravityFrame).
Implemented voxel/density pipeline: sampling and base density (sampleBaseDensity, pseudoNoise3), voxel density lookup (getVoxelDensity, sampleDensityAtWorld, densityNormal), voxel indexing and keys (worldToVoxelIndex, voxelKey, voxelCenterFromIndex), and simple edit operations (modifyDensitySphere, voxelOverrides, voxelTypes).
Added world-to-chunk/tile mapping and ownership resolution (worldToChunkVoxel, transferToNeighborChunk, resolveChunkOwnership, buildTileLookup, mapWorldToOwnedChunkVoxel) and a dirty-chunk tracking + remesh stub (markDirtyChunkForVoxel, processDirtyChunkRemesh).
Implemented raycasts against the density field and spherical surface (raycastDensityField, raycastSurfaceAlongGravity) and added mining/placing handlers (LMB mine, RMB place) with input guards and voxel override application.
Converted player physics and camera to use planet-centered gravity and smoothing: player rotation quaternion state, gravity application toward settingsPhysics.planetCenter, ground probing, jump handling, turn/pitch deltas, and movement projected onto tangent plane; updated camera basis and rendering to use the new basis.
UI/UX and rendering tweaks: added contextmenu suppression, updated HUD text, filtered rendering to only solid tiles (isSolidAtWorld) and switched tile color fills to computed rgb(...) values for consistent shade rendering.
Introduced new state fields and physics settings such as modifiedDensity, voxelOverrides, dirtyChunks, chunkStates, planetCenter, voxelSize, chunkSize, terrainAmplitude, and other tuning parameters.
Testing
No automated tests were added or executed as part of this change.

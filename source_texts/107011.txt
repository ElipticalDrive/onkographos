Following #8719, some visual artifacts remain when SSAO is active with MIP / MinIP volume rendering and models are displayed inside the volume geometry.
These artifacts can only be resolved in the VTK shader by properly handling the SSAO textures.
This problem can be resolved when the source problem is solved in VTK and 3D Slicer version is bumped.

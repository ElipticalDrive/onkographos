What to build
The Voxel Frontend produces non-semantic coarse Voxel Volume Detail Levels for every volume of a streamed Voxel Scene. It caches them per Voxel Volume Content Version and keeps the whole world covered at 8³ from startup, without ever materializing the whole world. The domain language and ADR 0002 are recorded alongside, because every later ticket in #141 uses them.
Glossary and ADR.
Record Voxel Volume Detail Level (one level of a volume: a rendering approximation of the same scene-space extent) and Voxel Detail Selection (the level assigned to each volume), with Required and Installed variants defined like residency.
Update existing definitions whose coverage currently refers only to residency, so they read correctly once detail selections exist. Examples: Installed Voxel Residency Selection, Visible Voxel Scene Revision, Replacement Render Path.
Add ADR 0002: detail summaries belong to the Voxel Frontend, are non-semantic like residency, and are cached per Voxel Volume Content Version. Render Paths derive their own data from them, so far volumes never need full materialization. ADR 0001 still governs: detail never publishes a Voxel Scene Revision or a Voxel Change Set.
Level chain. 64³ (full detail), 32³, 16³ and 8³. Every level spans the volume's scene-space extent, with voxels 2, 4 and 8 times larger.
Direct-vote downsampling. Each coarse voxel votes directly over every full-detail value it covers, never over the next finer level. Any occupied value makes it solid. Its material is the most frequent occupied Voxel Material. Empty values don't vote, and ties go to the lowest Voxel Material identity.
Summary cache. Keyed by scene identity, volume identity, Voxel Volume Content Version and level.
Entries are immutable and retainable by reference. A reader (later, a Render Path) can hold an entry, and it stays valid and unchanged regardless of later edits or cache eviction.
The 8³ summary of every volume stays resident permanently. 32³ and 16³ entries are kept only while someone retains them.
Startup sweep. Runs after streamed publication and before residency begins. It materializes one volume at a time, derives that volume's resident summaries and releases the copy.
Failures. Summary generation and cache failures are typed errors, never empty summaries.
Unstreamed scenes are unaffected.
Acceptance criteria
CONTEXT.md defines Voxel Volume Detail Level, Voxel Detail Selection, Required Voxel Detail Selection and Installed Voxel Detail Selection. Existing terms whose coverage wording refers only to residency are updated. ADR 0002 is recorded via /domain-modeling.
Downsampling tests cover any-occupied solidity, empty values not voting, lowest-identity ties and chain independence (each level equals a direct vote over full detail).
Summary tests show that at every coarse level, one-voxel-thick surfaces stay solid, closed cavities stay closed and material boundaries resolve deterministically.
Public counters show cache entries are reused across edits to other volumes and replaced only when their own volume's content version changes.
A retained entry is unchanged after a later edit to its volume.
After the startup sweep, every volume of the 16x16 fixture has a resident 8³ summary, and at most one materialized copy was held at any point during the sweep.
Injected generation and cache failures surface as typed errors. No empty summary is ever produced in their place.
Reads, edits, Semantic Rays and Voxel Change Sets are unchanged by summaries.

cargo test --locked --workspace --features qualification passes. This includes the unchanged regression suites for the canonical scene, dense/sparse comparisons, raster/compute switching, the large sparse terrain and outcome 3 streamed qualification.

Implemented on main in b06fac6, bab837a and 502a821.
Added immutable, reference-retained 32³/16³/8³ summaries with direct full-detail voting, occupied-only material counts and lowest-identity ties.
Added content-version cache keys, public reuse/retention counters, permanent newest-generated 8³ holds and weak retention for finer summaries. Historical readers cannot displace newer resident summaries.
Added an explicit post-publication, pre-residency startup sweep. The production 16×16 fixture test retains all 256 coarsest summaries, peaks at one materialized copy and releases every copy afterward.
Added typed generation/cache failures and retry tests. Derived metadata rejects unrepresentable voxel sizes before generation.
Recorded the four domain terms and coverage updates in CONTEXT.md and frontend ownership/non-semantic rules in ADR 0002 using the domain-modeling skill.

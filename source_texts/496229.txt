Include pending streams in Apply state
When a hosted manifest is still loading, this only considers the committed scene. If another streamed scene is visible, clicking Apply sets reload_requested; stage then reloads pending.source (the old manifest) and despawns the pending new URL, silently restoring the previous scene. On the first load, Apply instead promotes the draft while the pending entity retains its old configuration, and poll later rolls the setting back. Track ViewerStreams.pending here and either re-stage that pending source with the draft settings or disable Apply until it resolves.

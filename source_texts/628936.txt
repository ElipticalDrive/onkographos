Curvature: VCM on a densified proxy of the GT, computed tile by tile
Stacked on # (feat/vcm-curvature): merge that one first, or target its branch.
Why
The CT GT meshes (bowl, grid, paw_pad, plant, spine) are very irregular: edges up to 10 mm, against a median of 0.1 mm on the other GT. Next to large triangles the VCM inflates |k|: on bowl, 32 % of the vertices had |k| > 0.75, almost all of them artefacts. A uniform density also makes all objects comparable.
What changes
Proxy mesh (src/core/proxy.py): GT edges longer than curvature_max_edge (0.15 mm = p99 of the non-CT GT) are split at their midpoint, conforming, until none is left. The shape is unchanged; already fine meshes are left as is. The GT itself is not modified.
Tiled computation (tiled_curvature): the proxy is built and measured by 30 mm cubes with a margin (r + 2R + 2 edges = 2.3 mm), so memory follows the tile, not the mesh.
spine: 3.3 GB instead of 12.9 GB, +8 % time (459 s vs 427 s)
plant (63 M proxy vertices) now runs on a workstation (43 min, 12.5 GB); it did not fit in one piece
against the one-piece run, 0.04 % of the area changes its |k| > 0.75 label (spine, paw_pad)
Pipeline: preprocess-gt computes the curvature on the raw GT (the visibility cleaning cuts holes whose rims the VCM would see as edges), caches it in Groundtruth/attributes/proxy_curvature_r<r>_R<R>_e<max_edge>.npz, then gives each gt_pcd point the value of its nearest es.npy keeps its format: nothing changesdownstream (apply-masks, aggregate`).
New config key evaluation.curvature_ma = GT as is), and --curvature-max-edgeoption on preprocess-gt. - **New command** proxy-curvature`: raw GT -hout the GT point cloud.
vcm.py: per-face temporaries built by blocks (memory only, same results).
scripts/recompute_curvature.py uses the same path.
Tests - tests/core/test_curvature_proxy.py: refinves fine meshes untouched; a coarse sphere ismeasured on its refined proxy; the cache is w vs one-piece run on a sphere (same proxyvertices, median |Δk| < 0.01).
Full suite: 181 passed, 9 skipped.
Real run on knife and lego (preprocess-gt --no-visibility --no-clean-gt, existing gt_pcd): 6 min 17 for both, 12.6
GB peak; |k| > 0.75 on 6.8 % (knife) and 24.8istent with the VCM on the GT as is (lego26.9 %).
Note
The CT GT are not changed in this PR: the junsses are still in the meshes and will show ascurved zones.

Hi @sijieaaa ,
Thank you for your significant contribution to the community with this dataset. It is a truly valuable resource.
I have been reviewing your paper and was particularly interested in the validation you performed using Novel View Synthesis (NVS) methods, especially 3D Gaussian Splatting (3D GS). I have a couple of questions regarding the specifics of your methodology and would be very grateful if you could provide some clarification.
Point Cloud Initialization and Hardware: In the paper, you mention that "All 3D GS-based methods use the raw point cloud provided by the dataset as the initialization." For a scene like AMtown, the raw point cloud is quite large (>1 GB) and contains a noticeable amount of noise. Using such a large and noisy point cloud for initialization would seem to demand significant GPU memory and might complicate the training process for the Gaussians. Could you please share the hardware specifications (e.g., GPU model and VRAM) used for training the 3D GS models? Additionally, did you perform any pruning or pre-processing on the raw point cloud before using it for initialization?
Pose Information for Training: For training the 3D GS models, did you use the poses from the sampleinfos_interpolated file? If so, could you please elaborate on the process of generating these 6-DoF poses? My understanding is that you used DJI Terra and the rtk_positions_raw data. However, based on my current knowledge, DJI Terra typically does not alter the input RTK poses during processing. It would be very helpful if you could share more details on how the interpolated 6-DoF poses were derived.
Thank you for your time and for any insights you can provide. Your work is greatly appreciated!
Best regards,

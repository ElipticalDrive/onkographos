Overview
I improved some artifacts in SSR.
What I've done
The reflection was sampled from the brightest part, because the color fetched from the half resolution texture is decided by the average of the texel, but the brighter part dominates the result.
=> I fixed to compress the color range by tone mapping before the cone tracing.
The ray-marching was missed sometimes around edges, then the result edges look like a bite mark.
=> I fixed to check thickness properly by binary search.

Thank you for your interest in our work!
Our VAE is designed as a foundation model for 3D generation, with the primary goal of taking mesh inputs, preserving their geometric details, and producing high-fidelity reconstructions. While the model excels at this task, we did not specifically train it for converting 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) representations into meshes.

Thank you for your interest in our work!
Currently, the open-sourced portion is the reconstruction model (VAE) from our image-to-3D framework. Its role is to perform high-fidelity reconstruction of input meshes, which is why it requires 3D mesh inputs. Through this initial release, we hope to contribute to advancing research in the 3D generation field.

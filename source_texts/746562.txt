What is asked
A quadtree or clipmap loads large coarse chunks far away and small dense chunks under the camera, with the visible range growing with altitude.
Why it matters: Seeing far from altitude is affordable only if far data is coarse: the bytes loaded must follow what the eye resolves, not the distance.
What is true today
Nothing yet. Streaming uses fixed-size chunks with a stride LOD.
What is missing
A level structure for real elevation data and a residency rule driven by camera altitude.
How we will know it is done
From a summit or a flight altitude the viewer shows a horizon hundreds of kilometres away with resident memory under a stated budget.

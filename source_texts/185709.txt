Abstract
We construct asymptotically the solutions
to a
classical reaction-diffusion system (the Gray-Scott model of an
autocatalytic reaction)
in the form of static spike
autosolitons (self-sustained solitary pulses, spots and clots).
We show that solutions in the form of
static spike autosolitons exist over a wide range of system parameters
in one dimension, and in a narrower range of parameters in two and three
dimensions. We study the properties of these solutions.

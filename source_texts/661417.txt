Known limitations worth noting:
DensityHeatMap and IndirectOnly debug modes do a per-pixel ray cast which is O(width × height) and single-threaded — could be slow for large images.
PhotonMapIntegrator hardcodes MaxDepth = 3 for the MIS direct component. For scenes with complex specular paths (e.g. mirror inside mirror) this may miss some direct lighting.
The photon emitter uses Vector3.Zero as the origin when sampling lights for power estimation — this works for area lights but would be incorrect for more complex light types.

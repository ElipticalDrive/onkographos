Issue body actions
Ray-traced light traces one ray per pixel per effect, and the light policy ranks lights per view, so a street of hundreds of lamps or a stadium either loses lights to the cut or turns noisy. Sampling every light by its contribution, with reuse across neighboring pixels and the live view's window, keeps them all. Tracked, not scheduled: it starts when a piece needs it.
Epic: #310 · Design: Cut, or deferred · Ray-traced light · Light: ray traced · Light and the light policy
Scope
Direct light from every light in the document is sampled in proportion to its contribution, from a light tree built each frame from the lights at t
Canonical renders (render-back, checks and exports) use samples seeded per frame and pixel, with no reuse across frames; the live view may reuse samples across neighbors and its frame-history window, display only, rendered again on a jump
The light cut stays the path where rays are off
Sampling stays stable while lights animate: the light tree's build is ordered by stable light ID, so a change of power changes its weights and never its order
Acceptance criteria
A street of 512 point lamps, 3 m high and 10 m apart, each with range 4 m, so that every lit road point lies in the range of one lamp alone, renders a canonical frame at one sample per pixel that equals a 4,096-sample reference of the same frame within 1% at 20 sampled lit road pixels, since sampling by contribution picks that lamp every time; a planted sampler that picks lights uniformly finds it once in 512 tries, reads 0 at most of those pixels and fails, naming them
A canonical frame is the same by hash on one machine whether reached by play or by a seek; a planted sampler that reuses the previous frame's samples in a canonical render differs and fails, naming the frame
The live view at rest converges to the canonical frame within one code value at 99% of pixels after its window; a planted live view that keeps reusing its window's samples at rest stays off the canonical frame and fails, naming the pixels
Among the 512 lamps, one whose power ramps linearly over 60 frames gives a mean luminance over its lit region that rises monotonically across those frames at the export's samples; a planted tree whose split order comes from an unstable sort reorders when the power changes, flickers and fails, naming the frames
Proof
The street fixture's canonical frames, its references and the planted twins' failures.
Oracle: no upstream test checks these criteria; why, per criterion in doneright/oracles (DeepBuildAI/deepbuild).

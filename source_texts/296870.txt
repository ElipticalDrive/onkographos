A method to generate polygonal boundary representations
 from voxel-based volume representations is presented.
 As an auxiliary data structure, the algorithm makes 
 use of a face octree extracted
 from the voxel-based volume representation.
 The precision of the computed boundary is given by the 
 length of the main diagonal of the voxels in the initial 
 representation.
 Experimental results that illustrate the method are 
 presented.Postprint (published version

Contributor
Clouds, cloud shadows, rain shafts and lightning drawn by the engine (engine 0.192.0), in a new module weather-render, from the weather model (the near and far weather maps, presets, wind, storm cells, brushes) so clouds follow presets and storms.
What it does
render.clouds technique slot (Clouds), three tiers plus off: volumetric (fly-through: fine march with detail from the camera), layered (default: Schneider's two-speed 2.5D march), skybox (2D: coverage on each type's layer, two-stream slab lighting; no noise, no march). Half-resolution march with jittered start, temporal accumulation reprojected by the clouds' mean depth (neighbourhood clamp; history drops while a flash lights the clouds), joint-bilateral upsampling, composited after the sky.
Density after Schneider & Vos 2015 / Schneider 2017 (profiles per WMO type, anvil widening, shear, Perlin–Worley base shape, detail erosion, curl), physical extinction from LWC and droplet size; lighting: approximate Mie phase (Jendersie & d'Eon 2023), cone light march, Wrenninge octaves (Hillaire 2016), two-stream diffuse term, Hillaire's energy-conserving step, sun transmittance through an analytic atmosphere (Chapman, Schüler 2012), aerial perspective at the clouds' mean depth. Physical units (lux, cd, cd/m²) as the scene's lights.
Noise volumes generated once on the GPU, matching weather-clouds' CPU reference within one step of 255 per texel (tested).
Cloud shadows: Beer shadow map (Hillaire 2020) and the sun's screen-space shadow mask on surfaces (CloudShadowMask); a coverage-only map for the 2D tier.
Rain shafts below the bases from the map's rain channel (Marshall–Palmer extinction).
Lightning: in-cloud glow by the diffusion approximation, flashes as point LightSources on scene nodes (LightningLights, lit through LightSync), visible bolts from BoltGeometry (LightningBolts).
CloudLighting: the interface for sun/sky light + transmittance + aerial perspective until effects-atmosphere's render techniques land (its atmosphere-render branch had no commits yet); SceneSky implements it from the scene's sun node.
Every tunable a kind config or a setting (CloudSettings), quality presets via CloudSettings.forQuality.
Tests (CloudNoiseGpuTest, CloudRenderTest, WeatherRenderTest): GPU noise = CPU reference; coverage follows the weather map; the clouds' shadow darkens the ground under them (and only with the mask); a flash lights the clouds from inside and the ground through the scene's light; ground flashes draw their bolts; every tier draws the same weather; CPU tests of flash selection, sky and atmosphere.
Measured (RX 9070 XT, 1080p, clouds' passes): layered 0.7–2.0 ms on the ground (4.3 ms flying through a layer), volumetric 1.2–4.1 ms (7.3 ms flying through), 2D 0.02–0.04 ms; shadow map + mask 0.05–0.11 ms. Above the report's estimates for the ray-marched tiers; follow-ups in docs/design.md.
Engine features missing (docs/design.md): a per-light transmittance term in shading (cloud shadows yield to the sun's own shadow entry until then), 3D persistent graph images, TextureUpdates overflowing the memory stack with ~270 regions in a frame (worked around), blend state in GraphicsProgram, 3D readbacks.
Not in this PR (issues): rain particles via effects-particles, the fly-through voxel cache, planets, adopting effects-atmosphere's tables, fitting to a path tracer, cost reductions.
Second round (review fixes)
The weather maps are WeatherField evaluated on the GPU every frame (field.slang) instead of CPU tiles uploaded a few a step: no seams where moving cells or the wind's drift met tile boundaries (WeatherMapGpuTest: every texel within 0.0044 of the field at the frame's time).
Cloud types blend continuously: each texel holds two types and a weight (a transition's smoothstep, a cell maturing over 3 minutes, its core taking over from the preset's type, brush falloff); each type covers its share of the coverage (a leaving type breaks up instead of popping). aChangeOfCloudTypeIsContinuous fails without it; film pops (fair → overcast) 802 → 115.
Dark thick clouds: extinction normalised so a column has its water's optical depth; the two-stream depth from the type's extinction; the light march's far part from the Beer shadow map (lit tower sides, dark bases). aThickRainCloudIsDarkFromBelow.
effects-atmosphere 0.3.0: AtmosphereLighting (its sun, media, scene units); the pictures use its sky, aerial perspective and sky capture (dimmed under clouds); daylight white balance in the pictures.
Steps grow with distance from 30 m, range 200 km; coverage-projection shadows beyond the Beer map; flash peak 2·10¹⁰ cd.
Films: storm rolling in, cloud shadows sweeping, lightning under a storm, a climb through the clouds, rain shafts, tiers side by side, type transitions before/after.

Stacked on #81, rebased onto #93's merge. This gives the codec a fixture with ground truth. A fused scan can only be measured against the volume before encoding, and room0 has no ground-truth mesh beside it.
What the test does
recon_codec_mesh_roundtrip runs one pipeline end to end:
Mesh → TSDF: a mesh goes through tsdf::MeshIntegrator.
TSDF → bytes: the forward DCT, then write_intra_frame.
Bytes → grid: read_intra_frame, then the inverse DCT into a fresh grid.
Grid → mesh: marching cubes.
The decoded surface is then measured against the source mesh in two directions:
accuracy: every decoded vertex position's distance to the source surface.
coverage: every point of a lattice over the source surface, half a voxel apart, measured to the decoded triangles. This is where a hole shows.
The decoded frame must also equal the one written, coefficient for coefficient, since a surface cannot see a field that was scaled as a whole.
The uncompressed volume's surface gets the same measurement as the floor, so what the codec adds is the difference. The test uses the codec's internal headers by include path, as the other codec tests do, so it doesn't wait for Encoder / Decoder.

The cube's floor is marching cubes' error, not the field's: it cuts the edges and corners back by under a voxel (4.1 mm of coverage) before anything is compressed.
Why a plain, axis-aligned cube. The first cut used the tsdf test's dented cube. After the rebase, Signed takes its sign from the closest face, and the dent is outside that rule: part of the region past its 56° edges reads the wrong side, and the uncompressed surface grew stray sheets up to 40 mm off. A plain cube turned off the grid's axes fails too. Beyond a corner, on the line that continues an edge, two tied faces' normals are perpendicular to the offset and rounding picks the sign (72 stray vertices, up to 26 mm off). An axis-aligned cube off the voxel lattice has no voxel on those lines. The sphere's figures did not change.
The bounds sit at about twice these figures (1.8x at the tightest), which is enough to catch a regression and not rounding. They are what PR 4's room0 tuning can hold the defaults to.
Cost of the test
The reference distances go through a small spatial hash with a ring search, over unique vertex positions. The search stops at the exact distance to the cells it has not visited. Each mesh is fused and hashed once, then coded at every set of params. The test takes 0.52 s in Release and 43 s under ASan + UBSan.
What review changed
The frame is compared with the one written. Doubling every decoded coefficient passed before, and a reader planted to do it now fails.
Coverage is sampled every half voxel, not every two. With one surface voxel's mask bit cleared on the sphere, the two-voxel lattice passed 3 of 12 placements. At half a voxel all 12 fail, the lowest at 4.27 mm against the 2.5 mm bound.
The dented cube's bounds were 1.4–1.6x its figures; the plain cube's sit at about 2x. K = 512 is bounded on its own rather than against K = 32.
The integrator's stats are checked, NaN vertices are refused, and a missing decoded block is a FAIL line, not a throw.
The mesh fixtures and the closest point moved to tests/test_meshes.hpp, shared with the volume and tsdf triangle tests. This test's copy returned NaN for a zero-length edge.
Verification
macOS, M5 Max, Release: 34/34.
The new test and the two tests that now share test_meshes.hpp pass under ASan + UBSan.
The changed tests compile clean with GCC 13 at -O3 -Werror (ubuntu:24.04).
Pre-commit hooks pass.
The decision entry and CLAUDE.md record the figures.

The mesh-distance metric came in with the room0 measurement as a header
in examples/common. It chose the codec's defaults, the codec's
ground-truth test (#94) carries its own copy, and the per-band study
will be judged on it. That makes it library code: recon_eval, namespace
volumetric_kit::recon::eval, branching off recon_mesh. Nothing in the
pipeline links it. It is named eval, not metrics, which core's
StageMetrics already means.

eval/mesh_distance.hpp:
- MeshDistance: point-to-surface distance up to a reach, through a hash
  of reach-sized cells, over a copy of the triangles (the header kept a
  reference). create() refuses a bad reach, a ragged index count and an
  index past the vertices, where the header read out of bounds.
- summarize, and compare_meshes giving accuracy and coverage plus an
  F-score at an optional threshold, which must lie in [0, reach].

codec_replica (and codec_stream.hpp / codec_sweep.hpp) measure with it,
and report the F-score at half a voxel.

The 2026-09-27 decision records why this is infrastructure rather than
one of the excluded research harnesses. It also records that a stride
subsample varies about 0.5% run to run, because marching cubes' vertex
order follows its atomics.

Tests: recon_eval_mesh_distance replaces the header's test and adds the
F-score (exactly 1 and 0 across a known shift, the exact recall of half
a plane read off its extent) and every refusal. Suite 35/35 in Release;
the new test passes under ASan + UBSan.

Addresses the review of the ground-truth round trip.

- The decoded frame is compared with the one written: coefficients,
  masks, params and trunc_dist. A surface cannot see a field scaled as a
  whole, so a reader that doubled every coefficient passed; it now fails
  at the coefficient comparison.
- Coverage samples every half voxel, not every two. With one surface
  voxel's mask bit cleared on the sphere, the old lattice passed 3 of 12
  placements; the new one reads at least 4.27 mm in all 12, against the
  2.5 mm bound.
- The cube's bounds were 1.4-1.6x its figures and are now about 2x, with
  kReach raised to 4 cm to stay above them. K = 512 is bounded on its own
  rather than against K = 32, which is not a property of this metric.
- The integrator's stats are checked (every triangle took part, blocks
  written == blocks coded), a NaN vertex is refused before a sort or a hash cell
  sees it, and a missing decoded coord is a FAIL line rather than a throw.
- Each mesh is fused and its source hashed once, then coded at every
  params; the ring search stops at the exact distance to the unvisited
  cells. 0.58 s in Release and 43 s under ASan + UBSan, from 128 s with
  the finer lattice alone.
- Mesh, corner, closest_point, orient_outward and dented_cube move to
  tests/test_meshes.hpp, shared with the volume and tsdf triangle tests.
  This test's copy of closest_point returned NaN for a zero-length edge.
- grid_params() starts from VoxelGridParams::defaults(), the raw size is
  named once, the printed voxels are coverage's, the header says coverage
  is measured to triangles, and a TODO(codec) marks the move onto
  Encoder / Decoder.

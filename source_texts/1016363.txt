Requested by Tobias · project thread
Before: objects floated on the ground with no contact darkening, sun shadows had one fixed blur everywhere, small gaps and creases got no shadow at all, and every edge stair-stepped and shimmered (brick joints, thin poles, distant geometry).
After: creases and corners darken where the sky can't reach them, sun shadows are sharp at the caster and widen with distance like the real sun's, thin contact shadows ground small objects, and edges are smooth and stable under a still or moving camera.
How: every frame now starts with a depth prepass that also writes normals, roughness and motion vectors (new RenderFeature::draw_prepass hook; the mesh feature implements it, with previous transforms for moving objects). From it come:
Ambient occlusion: GTAO (3 slices × 4 steps, edge-aware blur), applied to ambient light only, with multi-bounce color and specular occlusion. Settings ambient_occlusion, ao_radius, ao_intensity; debug view AmbientOcclusion.
Sun shadows: PCSS with a physical sun penumbra (sun_penumbra_scale), noise-rotated taps and dithered cascade blending; plus 10-step screen-space contact shadows (contact_shadow_length).
TAA (default anti_aliasing = Taa): Halton jitter, motion-vector or depth reprojection, 3×3 jittered reconstruction, YCoCg variance clipping, Catmull-Rom history, camera-cut reset; mild CAS sharpening in the tonemap pass (sharpness). Overlays draw with an unjittered frame group.
Tests: new crates/render/tests/screen_effects.rs (AO darkens a crease and leaves open ground alone, AO off is full visibility, TAA blends a slanted edge, TAA is still with a still camera) plus jitter and camera-cut unit tests. ARCHITECTURE.md and AGENTS.md document the prepass hook.

The showcase looked flat: no ambient occlusion, hard aliased edges, and
3x3 PCF shadows. This adds the screen-space foundation the renderer was
missing.

- RenderFeature::draw_prepass: opaque features write depth, normals,
  roughness and motion vectors before shading (MeshFeature does, with
  per-entity previous transforms for moving objects).
- GTAO from the prepass with an edge-aware blur; shade_surface applies
  it with multi-bounce and specular occlusion, so every feature using
  the shader library gets it.
- Sun shadows: PCSS with the physical sun size, rotated Vogel taps and
  dithered cascade blending; screen-space contact shadows.
- TAA: Halton jitter, motion vectors (prepass or camera reprojection),
  Catmull-Rom history, YCoCg clipping, camera-cut detection, then
  contrast-adaptive sharpening in the tonemap pass. Overlays get an
  unjittered frame group.

CI is red on engine_foliage tests/headless.rs a_sunlit_oak_has_a_green_crown_and_a_trunk: "only 1246/10000 green pixels in the crown area" (the test needs more than 1250).
Cause: this PR adds TAA. The oak's alpha-tested leaf cards are one or two pixels wide at the test's 320×180, so anti-aliasing blends their edges with the blue sky, and a half-covered leaf pixel no longer passes the test's "green > blue" check. Measured on this branch merged with main: 1253 with TAA, 1825 with anti-aliasing off, 1860 on main. Nothing renders wrongly; the pixel count sits on the threshold.
Not fixed here because the test belongs to the Foliage module. I've asked its owner to make the pixel check tolerant of anti-aliased edges (for example g > r && g > 0.8 * b), which passes on main today too. Once that is on main I'll update this branch so CI re-runs, and merge when it's green.

Milestone 2.1 — Implement the differentiable, gradient-scaled mollified Vχ map with a monotone volume-uniform PCHIP tabulation and inverse.
Equations and invariants — note equations (mollified_V) and (V_derivatives); DESIGN.md §5 invariant 3 (profile-realization endpoint identities and monotonicity) is established for the volume-map component.
Acceptance criterion — analytic circle/sphere volumes and monotone tabulation, demonstrated by tests/unit/test_level_set_volume.py. It checks raw endpoint residuals before normalization, exact exposed endpoints, strictly monotone 65-level volume-uniform tabulation, analytic co-area density, tabulated-derivative/co-area agreement, critical-point safeguarding, and a checked-in sphere-resolution table.
Measured numbers — circle/sphere relative volume errors: 5.18e-4 / 7.76e-4. Co-area-density relative errors: 1.70e-2 / 2.56e-3. Sphere quadrature-resolution rates for 24→48→96: 2.078 and 2.058. See tests/manufactured/mollified_sphere_volume_rates.csv and docs/verification.md.
Mutations verified to turn the suite red — replacing ε_i = c h_i |∇χ_i| with a fixed χ-space width fails the circle co-area assertion (3.328 vs π outside the 3% bound). Replacing H′ε by zero fails both circle and sphere co-area checks. The independent tabulated-derivative/co-area check catches an incorrect PCHIP interior slope.
Open ADRs blocking merge — none.
Reviewer focus — confirm the robust critical-point gradient floor and the raw endpoint/co-area diagnostics are an appropriate narrow foundation for 2.2; the FEM extraction pass and nonlocal JVP remain explicitly deferred to 2.2 and 2.3, respectively.

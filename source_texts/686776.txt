As for the point data are affected by noise in point cloud,the initial estimates are usually unstable that often leads to failure or incorrect result in the process of fitting point cloud to cone or cylinder.As regard to this important problem,a Gaussian image based algorithm is proposed to obtain good initial estimates.In this method,the Gaussian image of a cone or a cylinder is first obtained and then cluster analysis is applied to removing the noise data and acquiring the clean data.The accurate direction and position of the rotational axis,as one of the most important parameters of initial estimates,can be calculated from these clean data.At last,a nonlinear least-squares method is used to refine the solution.

Introduction
The question, and its honest answer
The campaign question for this phase was: can the 2-RG framework (kernel axioms K1–K5, emergent
metric classes, shape-space dynamics machinery) attack open problems in topology and topological geometry?
The reconnaissance triad [26, 27, 28] scanned eighteen topology targets (T1–T18: among them the smooth
1
4-dimensional Poincaré conjecture, the smooth Schoenflies conjecture in n = 4, S 4 -unknotting, slice–ribbon,
knot geography, homotopy groups of spheres, RPn immersions and topological complexity, the K-theoretic
core of the Novikov conjecture, positive-scalar-curvature questions of Gromov type, coarse Baum–Connes
injectivity residues) and nineteen topological-geometry targets (P1–P19: among them Cannon’s conjecture,
Hilbert–Smith, Singer, Charney–Davis, Haefliger–Thurston, taut foliations, Chern’s conjecture on aﬀine manifolds, Thurston’s dilation characterization, the Property-A versus coarse-embeddability converse, expanderfree non-embeddable box spaces, Hilbert compression exponents, non-smooth Lorentzian Gromov–Hausdorff
theory), and rated the framework’s firepower against each.
The honest answer, which this paper is obliged to state in its introduction, is: zero intersection on
the manifold/knot/homotopy-theory lines. Every named target in those lines is driven by gauge
theory, surgery and s-cobordism methods, equivariant stable homotopy, L2 -Hodge/von Neumann dimension,
Mather–Thurston-type homology of diffeomorphism groups, cubulation, or arithmetic K-theory — none of
which has any proved counterpart in the framework’s library; indeed the framework’s own ceiling theorems
(the W13 family: no Einstein-type PDE is derivable from first-order (D, E) data [24]) structurally block
several of these approaches. The framework’s real range on this terrain is exactly two lines:
(i) the shape-space dynamics line, where the 3D fixed-ratio subdivision classification of Phase XXXIV [25]
has a genuine 2D continuation with new phenomena (Part I, this paper);
(ii) the coarse-embedding obstruction line, where the framework’s kernel language is literally the same
mathematical language as the mainstream decision layer (Schoenberg’s negative-type dictionary [10],
Yu’s uniform-embedding hypothesis [15]) — but where the correct deliverable turned out to be a
negative theorem: the machine’s detection power collapses exactly onto the classical spectral criterion
and cannot exceed the class completely characterized by Tessera [11] (Part II, this paper).
No named open problem from the reconnaissance lists is claimed anywhere in this paper (Section 10).

2.2
The fixed-ratio scheme (P35-D1)
Let ∆ = (A, B, C) be a non-degenerate triangle and λ ∈ (0, 1). On each edge take the λ-division point in
cyclic orientation A → B → C → A: PAB = (1 − λ)A + λB, PBC = (1 − λ)B + λC, PCA = (1 − λ)C + λA.
Joining them produces four children (the correct 2D analogue of the 3D construction G-D1/G-D2 of [23, 25]):
• corner cells CA = conv{A, PAB , PCA }, CB = conv{B, PBC , PAB }, CC = conv{C, PCA , PBC };
• central cell K = conv{PAB , PBC , PCA }.
Labels propagate over the alphabet {A, B, C, F } by CA : (A′ , B ′ , C ′ ) = (A, PAB , PCA ); CB : (PAB , B, PBC );
CC : (PCA , PBC , C); F : (PAB , PBC , PCA ). The three corner areas are λ(1 − λ)S each and the central area is
(1 − 3λ + 3λ2 )S = det T (λ) S; they sum to S, so the four-cell family genuinely partitions ∆ for every λ (cert
[OK 10]) — in contrast with the 3D twelve-cell family, which does not partition the volume [23, 25].
4
Orientation freedom (2D-specific). Each edge can take the λ- or the (1 − λ)-division point: eight
choices, falling into exactly two classes under S3 -relabelling and the λ ↔ 1 − λ duality: the cyclic class
(+ + +, the S3 -covariant main scheme P35-D1) and the vertex-order class (A < B < C orientation, analysed
as P35-V in Section 5.6). The 3D opposite-edge-pair structure has no 2D dual (a triangle’s three edges have
no “opposite pairs”), so the four-cell family — not a twelve-cell family — is the correct analogue.

The assembly lemma
Lemma 2.1 (P35-L1, Möbius three-law; proved【已证】(elementary proof); CERTIFIED-numeric【CERTIFIED-numeric】). In 2D, every branch’s shape dynamics is the action of a PGL+ (2, R)-element on H.
For a periodic word w with period matrix Mw , write τ = tr2 (Mw )/ det Mw . Then:
(i) τ > 4 (hyperbolic) ⇒ degeneration with ar = Θ(Qrw ), Qw = (ρ/ν)2 the squared eigenvalue-modulus
ratio (the P34-L1 form [25]);
(ii) τ = 4, non-scalar (parabolic) ⇒ polynomial degeneration ar = Θ(rκ ), κ = 2 (finite fixed point) or
κ = 4 (fixed point ∞);
(iii) 0 ≤ τ < 4 (elliptic) ⇒ never degenerates (the orbit is confined to a hyperbolic circle about the fixed
point, ar bounded on both sides); finite classes iff rotation angle/π ∈ Q;
(iv) Mw scalar ⇒ one periodic class.
In 2D there is no “flat” morphology: ∂H is one-dimensional, so the unique degeneration morphology is needle
(vertex collision / elongation).

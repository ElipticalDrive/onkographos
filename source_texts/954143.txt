Volumetric clouds
The web lead asked for real clouds: the overcast is currently only a factor on the sky's luminance. This adds a physically based volumetric cloud layer to kansei's atmosphere.
The sky-atmosphere example:
top left: cumulus at midday (clouds=0.45);
top right: the sun 4° up, lighting the clouds' faces warm (elevation=4);
bottom left: overhead (pitch=50);
bottom right: nearly overcast stratocumulus (clouds=0.9 cloudtype=0.3).
What's in it
VolumetricCloudsEffect::new(&sky, VolumetricCloudsOptions { layer: CloudLayer { .. }, .. }) goes right after the AtmosphereEffect. It marches a layer between two altitudes (default 1.5–4 km) around the planet, in the atmosphere's planet frame, after Schneider 2015 (Horizon Zero Dawn) and Hillaire 2016 (Frostbite).
Shape.
A weather map marks a coverage share of the sky where clouds may form, and how tall they grow.
Within it, coverage also sets how much of the shape noise becomes cloud: thin coverage leaves scattered puffs, full coverage a closed deck.
A height profile runs from stratus to cumulus (cloud_type, varied by the weather map).
A Perlin-Worley shape noise builds the clouds, leaning downwind with height, and a Worley detail noise frays the edges: wispy at the base, billowy above.
Noise. The textures are tileable and generated once on the GPU: 128³ shape, 32³ detail, 256² weather. Each is stretched over its full range, since raw Perlin and Worley sums span only a narrow band; a test guards this.
Light.
The sun arrives after the atmosphere (the transmittance LUT at each sample, with the horizon's shadow for dusk).
It passes through the cloud toward the sun (a 6-step march), with a dual-lobe phase that gives the silver lining.
Multiple scattering uses Wrenninge's octaves plus a diffusion floor, 1/(1 + 0.75(1−g)τ), so thick cloud passes light rather than going black.
Sky light comes from the atmosphere's SH, dimmer toward the base.
Integration is energy-conserving per step.
The air in front. The aerial-perspective LUT is applied at the cloud's transmittance-weighted depth, so distant clouds fade into the haze.
Resolution. The march runs at resolution_scale (0.5 by default: a quarter of the pixels) with a jittered start. It is blended over frames by reprojection at the cloud's depth; call reset_history() on cuts.
Composite. A depth-aware upsample places the clouds over the sky and over any surface they are in front of, never over nearer silhouettes.
Animatable. layer can change every frame, for example coverage per shot. time drives the wind.
Verified
the_cloud_noises_span_their_range (GPU): every noise channel spans at least 0.05–0.95 of its range.
a_clear_sky_passes_through_and_an_overcast_one_covers_it (GPU):
With coverage 0 the image passes through unchanged.
A closed deck seen from below under a 100 000 lux sun has a base luminance of 2 453 cd/m², within the 1 000–10 000 of real overcast. It is grey rather than black, and finite everywhere.
Shader validation: the WGSL validates with naga, and CloudParams matches its Rust layout.
Cost at 1920×1080 on the M4 Pro, one frame per submit, measured above the sky's own per-frame update:
about 1.5 ms at the default half resolution;
under 0.3 ms at resolution_scale: 0.25;
88 unit tests plus the GPU suites pass, and the new code has no clippy warnings.
Example. sky-atmosphere takes clouds= (coverage; 0 turns them off), cloudtype=, cloudbase= and cloudthick=. It showed no WebGPU errors in headless Chrome.
Known limits (follow-ups)
Sky lighting. The sky lighting SH and the environment cubemap are still the clear sky, so the scene's ambient light and reflections don't darken under a deck. The web's overcast factor can stand in for that until the clouds feed them.
No cloud shadows on the ground yet.
One layer, with no high cirrus.
Temporal lag. Cloud edges lag by a few frames under very fast camera turns; cuts need reset_history().
For midsommar-web
Add VolumetricCloudsEffect::new(&sky, ..) right after the AtmosphereEffect. Set layer.coverage from each shot's weather and time every frame, and call reset_history() on cuts. Keep the overcast factor for the ambient light until the sky lighting sees the clouds.

VolumetricCloudsEffect renders a CloudLayer over a SkyAtmosphere: a layer of cloud between two
altitudes around the planet, marched at a reduced resolution in the planet frame.

- Shape: a weather map marks a coverage share of the sky where clouds form (and how tall they
  grow); a height profile shapes them from stratus to cumulus; a Perlin-Worley shape noise builds
  them and a Worley detail noise frays their edges. The tileable noises (128^3 and 32^3 3D, 256^2
  2D) are generated on the GPU once, stretched over their full range.
- Light: the sun after the atmosphere (transmittance LUT) and through the cloud toward it (a short
  march), a dual-lobe phase, Wrenninge's octaves of multiple scattering with a diffusion floor for
  thick cloud (overcast bases stay grey), and the sky's SH light; energy-conserving integration.
- The atmosphere in front of the cloud from the aerial-perspective LUT at the cloud's depth.
- A jittered march accumulated over frames by reprojection at the cloud's depth; a depth-aware
  upsample composites it over the sky and over any surface it lies in front of.

The sky-atmosphere example takes clouds=, cloudtype=, cloudbase= and cloudthick=.

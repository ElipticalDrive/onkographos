Goal
Turn the temporal inputs from #164 into a real TAA resolve in VICE Runtime, while keeping the path portable across Metal and Direct3D 12 and suitable for later temporal reconstruction.
Part of #159 Stage A.
Scope
enable the temporal-resolve slot in the Runtime post-process plan
add a deterministic low-discrepancy sub-pixel jitter sequence
apply jitter in clip space to the scene camera without perturbing shadow-frustum fitting
preserve current and previous jittered camera transforms for motion reprojection
render current HDR scene color into a dedicated raw RGBA16F target
keep two RGBA16F resolved-color history targets for ping-pong TAA history
keep two sampled depth32 targets aligned with the temporal history ping-pong so previous-frame depth survives
resolve history using motion vectors generated in Add post-process orchestration, frame history, and motion vectors #164
reject history outside the viewport and on current/previous depth disagreement
clamp reprojected history to a 3x3 current-frame neighborhood before blending
reduce history weight under high motion
invalidate history on resize/minimize, camera identity changes, large camera teleports, and abrupt view-direction cuts
bind safe current-frame fallbacks when history is invalid
preserve HDR tone mapping/exposure as the terminal pass
add regression tests for jitter sequence, clip-space jitter, continuity/cut invalidation, and default pass ordering
require exact-head macOS ARM64 and Windows x64 CI green, including Windows DXIL smoke
Out of scope
temporal upscaling
sharpening/reconstruction filters
reactive masks/transparency classification
velocity dilation
bloom
automatic exposure
HDR10/EDR output
Exit criteria
The default Runtime path renders with deterministic sub-pixel jitter, produces motion/depth inputs, reprojects and clips a persistent HDR history with depth/disocclusion rejection, outputs a resolved RGBA16F frame into temporal history, and tone-maps that result on both Metal and Direct3D 12.
Part of #159.

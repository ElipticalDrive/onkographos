As I am generating fragments for a cube, when objects are inside the simulation they always render in front of the front face culled mesh, meaning even if the density is in front of the object, the fragments are technically behind it. For now, I have disabled all culling techniques on the cube which solves the issue to some degree. This does mean I am drawing the fragments twice and at certain angles, the volume draws over an object it should be behind.
A better solution to this would be to stretch a quad across the whole screen and generate all the fragments, a method used by signed distance field rendering. This would mean I wouldn't have to take into account the camera being inside the volume, and the ordering would always be correct. I can't say I like the idea of needing a special setup for the camera though.

Goal
Rays for editor picking (M9), gameplay line-of-sight and simple queries: a ray from a screen pixel through the camera, and ray tests against planes, spheres, boxes, oriented boxes and triangles.
Scope
in: Meow2/Math/Ray.h: Ray (origin + unit direction), Plane + planeFromPointNormal, rayFromScreen (perspective and orthographic), pointAt, intersect(Ray, Plane|Sphere|Aabb|OrientedBox) → distance, intersect(Ray, triangle) → distance + barycentrics (Möller–Trumbore, optional back-face culling), pickClosest(Ray, span<const Aabb>)
out: mesh/BVH raycasts (renderer/physics), physics raycasts (Jolt, M8)

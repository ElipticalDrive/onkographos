Objective
Validate that a real supported DICOM-CT-PD examination is interpreted correctly before attempting clinical-data reconstruction.
Tasks
Cross-check dimensions, view ordering, detector spacing, angles, units, and calibration against source metadata/documentation.
Establish whether measurements can be used directly or require preprocessing.
Compare derived geometry and basic projection behavior with independent tools/reference data.
Acceptance criteria
Validation report records dataset/version and examination identifier in a privacy-safe manner.
Geometry checks are reproducible.
Measurement domain is not guessed.
Unsupported vendor processing/calibration is clearly stated.
Only then is the examination marked eligible for reconstruction experiments.
Backlog ID: M3-05
Depends on: #61 (M3-04), #48 (M1-05)

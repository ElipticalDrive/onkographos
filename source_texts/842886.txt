Six raster-pipeline optimizations adapted from George Kopanas's "What the 3DGS Literature Left on the Table" (https://grgkopanas.github.io/3dgs_cuda_blog/), chosen and ranked from Metal stage profiles rather than the blog's A100 numbers.
On an M4 Pro (garden, 1.6M Gaussians, 1297x840) the training step falls from 38.2 to 24.1 ms (-37%). At 324x210, the crowded-tile regime of the first ~6,000 steps of a default run, it falls from about 7.6 to 3.1 ms. Rendered output is unchanged or changes only by float rounding, and gradients match to float rounding.

Behavior changes
Tile footprint. Opaque Gaussians now also cover their 3-3.33 sigma rim, which the 3-sigma box clipped at tile borders. Held-out PSNR on a model trained under the old footprint is unchanged (26.5867 -> 26.5872 dB). Visibility, radii, densification statistics, and optimizer gating still use the 3-sigma box.
Safe-math sigma. Under fast math, the compiler could round sigma differently in forward and backward, depending on surrounding code. Moving only the sigma computation in the previous backward kernel shifted gradients by up to 1.8e-4 relative, because forward and backward could disagree on a pixel at the 1/255 threshold. Both passes now share bit-identical sigma by construction. Forward output moves by at most 1e-6 in color on random scenes, and contributor indices are unchanged.
Retry-arena test. It used opacity 0.001 Gaussians to overflow the arena. Those can never blend, so they now emit no tiles, and the test uses 0.005.
Verification
Native tests. All 61 pass. New coverage:
msplat_raster_footprint compiles the shader with probe kernels and checks every blending pixel of 20,000 random Gaussians against the emitted tiles and the kept 8x8 blocks. It found zero misses and 0.09% / 0.13% slack.
test_exact_sort now covers byte skipping, odd pass counts, identical keys, and a 9,000-entry tile.
Mutation checks. The new tests fail on deliberately broken shaders: a 3% smaller footprint, a missing ellipse extreme, a missing radix copy-back, a skipped varying byte, and a 10% tighter block cull.
Contract pins. New pins cover the shared footprint helpers, the radix structure, the early-exit vote, block staging, and the safe-mode sigma.
Out-of-tree harnesses (not committed): old vs new kernels on random scenes for all nine gradient components and forward output. Count/scatter agreement was checked on every tile of 24 full-resolution renders of the 1.6M-Gaussian model.
Not done
No physical-iPhone measurements. scripts/run-ios-benchmarks.sh is the device check.
The prebuilt metallibs in swift/Sources/Msplat/Resources/ are not rebuilt, so the Swift package picks this up only after an xcframework/metallib rebuild.

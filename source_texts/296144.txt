This category of implicit 3D representation methods encodes the shape by network parameters. Thus there is no other input for network, except the tested 3d points. You can refer to some previous works to understand its mechaniam, such as DeepSDF and NGLOD.
The works you mentioned are actually half-implicit or called hybrid implicit representation, in my opinion.

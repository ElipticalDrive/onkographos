Summary
Adds an opt-in, WebGPU-only Gaussian splat streaming path for lod-meta.json v1 and loose SOG v2/DC-only data, plus a first-person Trogir demo.
Pure-data public APIs, coarse-first loading, bounded transport/decode, source caching, GPU resource accounting and pressure recovery.
Compute gathering and covariance projection/culling, deterministic survivor compaction, GPU-count-driven stable radix sorting and one global indirect splat draw.
Shared multiview LOD selection, optional per-leaf cooldown and exact-input selection reuse.
First-person demo controls, camera-pose readout, streaming HUD, mutable foreground budget and optional read-only local asset mounting.
Planned and reviewed with GPT-6 Astra, implemented with GPT-5.6 Sol. Master 1233e1bd was merged normally in eb78a4f9, preserving the upstream Ocean/Playroom demos and Trogir. LOD improvements are in 74056221; bootstrap review fixes are in 83226d10. Requested tooling/test cleanup is in af4dc0f8, the first-person-only demo in 7f25169d, the initial eight review-comment fixes in 10e07c86, and the two subsequent review replies in 9cbd5312. Both reopened threads were answered and resolved after the follow-up publication. The PR is open for review and unmerged.
No WebGL fallback, packed SOG or higher-order SH support is added. Generic library defaults remain compatible. Dataset bytes and generated bundles/reports are not committed.
Coarse-first streaming and bounded lifecycle
Coarse content is loaded and published before refinement. Visible coarse coverage can arrive before the primary bootstrap source draws, and missing fine files do not prevent affordable resident coverage. Desired fine demand is retained while the bootstrap draw barrier is closed.
Source preparation includes metadata admission before acquiring transport slots. Encoded bodies and decoded allocations remain charged throughout their real overlap. One payload owner avoids CPU/HTTP admission deadlocks, while strictly higher-priority coverage can preempt a lower-priority attempt and resume the original job afterward. Existing queued jobs are promoted in place when their demand changes from refinement to uncovered coverage. Nonabortable decode retains its resources until actual settlement before yielding; request/decode limits remain upper bounds, not a claim of parallel payload decoding. Source caching, published-source protection, abort generations and retirement tracking preserve active data during replacements and teardown.
All participating camera metrics feed one shared-capacity target solve. Required reductions are staged together, generations publish atomically, and rejected fine candidates try progressively cheaper resident alternatives. Environment arrival reduces foreground allowance; illegal mutable capacity increases retain the valid display and surface an explicit error. Environment URLs may share a leaf source's request/cache/allocation, but environment publication and capacity deduction remain behind the coarse barrier. Unchanged intervals preserve their generations. Scene attachment owns cleanup immediately.
Submission-local gather descriptors support more than 4,096 intervals, reserve admission headroom and stay charged until GPU retirement. Initial allocation failures unwind their resources.
GPU pipeline
Gather selected source intervals into a persistent canonical allocation, then project/cull into 64-byte records at the original canonical IDs.
Hierarchical exclusive scan and stable scatter compact the survivor key/index prefix in canonical order. GPU survivor counts drive radix dispatch and indirect drawing, including zero-survivor and shrinking-count transitions.
Stable radix sorting retains all 32 key bits and eight 4-bit passes. Portable workgroup bitsets replace predecessor-by-predecessor local ranking while preserving deterministic equal-depth ties and arbitrary-u32 behavior; no subgroup requirement, precision reduction or CPU-sort fallback.
Compaction reuses hierarchy scratch rather than allocating another projected-record cache. Its added resources cost 32,080,192 bytes at the demo's 4,010,000 capacity, included in admission, retirement and disposal accounting.
No per-frame production CPU readback is added. The one-time bootstrap visibility readback is separately protected. Static Gaussian rendering and projection/visibility math are unchanged by these optimizations.
LOD stability and selection
lodCooldownMs accepts finite nonnegative milliseconds, defaults to 0 for library compatibility, and is explicitly 250 ms in Trogir. Its per-leaf deadline starts after an accepted visible representation replacement, not after requesting/downloading a file. Initial coarse coverage and the first post-barrier refinement are immediate; identical, rejected and pending-only work does not restart the deadline.
Held displayed alternatives participate in the shared multiview solve. Unaffordable holds and actual capacity/admission pressure cannot block required reductions. Publication follows the admitted hold decision, including asynchronous completions, without misclassifying deliberate delay as generation pressure. Stationary updates reconsider expired deadlines; visibility loss, eviction and disposal clear holds.
Exact copied matrix/camera/viewport/policy inputs reuse per-binding metrics and the aggregate solve. Source/publication/admission changes, binding participation, pressure, environment allowance and cooldown expiry invalidate the caches. Completion, retirement, scheduling and statistics continue on cache hits; there are no approximate movement thresholds. Protection counts rebuild in linear passes only when dirty; unchanged frames refresh active-source recency without rescanning the hierarchy for those counts.
Packed/half-precision records, radial or quantized sorting, stale asynchronous orders, alpha floors/support shrinking, contribution culling and crossfades remain deferred because they alter quality or ordering semantics. Indexed quads remain a separate candidate. No measured Lite frame-time or FPS improvement is claimed. The previously inspected deployed SuperSplat URL now returns 404; earlier deployment-specific observations are historical, not current deployment verification.
Trogir demo
Default dataset: https://assets.babylonjs.com/splats/Trogir/lod-meta.json. Explicit directory/full-manifest overrides remain supported.

Quality correction
The selection correction recomputes from coarse floors instead of preserving old target entitlement, then prioritizes nearby eligible detail rather than cheap distant upgrades. In the historical controlled 750k-target/760k-capacity replay, changing only selection eliminated target/display gaps at all five route stops; nearby user-pose leaves 204/199/203/198/202 and street leaves 82/83/86 reached LOD0. This deliberately concentrates scarce detail nearby, not universally at distant leaves. These diagnostic settings differ from the standalone demo defaults.
Historical compiled-demo survivor counts were 531,734 at the 1.2M foreground target and 2,967,186 at 4M, with matching GPU sort/draw counts. They are view-specific functional observations, not a matched-engine startup, pixel-equivalence or speedup benchmark.
Review fixes
The Astra review focused on streaming correctness and cooldown/cache/radix lifecycle interactions; it was not an exhaustive review of every PR file.
The earlier 83226d10 fixes protect the mandatory 16-byte bootstrap readback and enforce coarse-only pre-barrier publication even when coarse and fine intervals share a resident source. Both were independently reviewed with multiview, retirement, failure and source-arrival probes.
The initial eight PR review comments were addressed in 10e07c86:
Allow browser zoom and fully disable spinner animation under reduced motion.
Dispose partial startup before reporting failure, including engine/stream results arriving after pagehide. Observe readiness immediately while preserving the primary startup error and avoiding unhandled teardown rejection.
Track environment aliases explicitly without duplicate source ownership, and handle oversized environment publication through terminal readiness failure rather than an unhandled continuation.
Reject terminal pre-draw failures, including unsupported orthographic startup, exactly once; clean up and ignore late completions.
Replace quadratic protection scans with dirty-triggered linear rebuilds and active-source recency updates.
Let higher-priority coverage preempt lower-priority transport safely while preserving CPU accounting, cancellation, generation ownership and the original job promise.
Clear owned transient selection errors after valid controls without hiding genuine source errors, including both failure/invalid-input orderings.
Independent Astra closure probes confirmed those initial cases. Three additional error-handling sequences found during closure were fixed before publication; the final CPU probes reported zero unhandled rejections.
Subsequent review replies identified two remaining paths, fixed in 9cbd5312 and reviewed by the coordinating Astra session:
Promote an already-requested Upgrade to Uncovered in the scheduler without restarting preparation or changing its promise, generation or FIFO order. Regressions cover the orchestration transition and a queued job preempting stalled fine transport, completing, and allowing the original job to resume.
Treat a visible coarse baseline exceeding mutable maxSplats as a recoverable range error even before readiness. A regression verifies invalid-to-valid budget recovery retains stream ownership and then completes the coarse draw/readiness fence. Unsupported orthographic startup and genuine source errors retain their existing behavior.
Both follow-up replies were posted beneath the original review comments and the reopened threads were resolved.

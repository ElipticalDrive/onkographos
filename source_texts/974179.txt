What changed
The post-processing volume gains Unity's three lens/film overrides as built-in knobs, the way bloom is:
Vignette: active, intensity (0–1), smoothness (0.01–1), color (default black), center (default 0.5, 0.5).
Chromatic Aberration: active, intensity (0–1). Radial, Unity URP's three-tap path (intensity × 0.05 split): the composite's centre tap is red, and two more taps along the radius give green and blue. It samples the composite's colour input, which since Per-object motion blur: reconstruction filter (TileMax/NeighborMax) over the velocity buffer #1436 is PostFx::scene_color() (the motion-blurred image), before the grade.
Film Grain: active, intensity (0–1), response (0–1). Hashed noise seeded by pixel and Scene::shader_time (0 outside Play, so edit-mode captures are stable), with Unity URP's luminance response curve.
Each one has:
a settings struct on PostProcessingComponent (components::{Vignette, ChromaticAberration, FilmGrain}, #[serde(default)]), off by default. Once ticked, the strengths start at a visible 0.3 (Unity starts at 0, which looks broken when an agent ticks the box and sees nothing);
shared ops in scene::authoring::rendering::post_processing, which is now a folder (the lens ops live in post_processing/lens.rs and are re-exported, so vc_ops::set_vignette_* and the rest still work), with the clamps, and with! rows in the field table;
Graphics.{Get,Set}Vignette{Active,Intensity,Smoothness,Color,Center}, {Get,Set}ChromaticAberration{Active,Intensity} and {Get,Set}FilmGrain{Active,Intensity,Response} (api/graphics/lens.rs, a small Knob table), with doc rows and a Lens effects paragraph in docs/api/Graphics.md;
a Lens Effects section in the Post Processing inspector card (editor/inspector/components/camera/lens.rs, one function per effect);
a player option each: vignette, chromatic_aberration, film_grain (GraphicsOption rows; every quality tier allows them, since each costs a few taps), gated through effect_runs in render::postfx::params::pack_lens.
Shaders. A new composable module, engine/shaders/postfx/lens.wgsl:
fringe (CA) is added to the HDR colour right after the scene sample, before SSR, bloom, exposure and the grade.
after_grade runs the vignette and then film grain on the graded colour. It is the composite's last step, so grain is the last thing before the authored effects and FXAA.
Each effect is skipped outright at intensity 0, which is what pack_lens packs for an effect that doesn't run. PostParams gains three vec4s at the end (vignette, vignette_color, lens). Adding them pushed common.wgsl over its 300-line cap, so the PostParams struct moved into its own module, postfx/params.wgsl (postfx::params::PostParams). postfx.wgsl, lens.wgsl and the authored-postfx scaffold (shadergen::assemble) import it from there.
Authored blocks removed. vignette, film_grain and chromatic_aberration are gone from shadergen::blocks::postfx, as exposure/saturation/contrast were when the volume took them over. damage_vignette stays (a gameplay overlay). A recipe naming one of the removed blocks is now refused as an unknown block. docs/api/Shader.md's block list and recipe example are updated.
Scene format. Three new objects under post_processing, blessed in tests/scene_format/scene.fingerprint. Absent keys load as "off".
Review follow-ups (independent review of this PR, last Brief on #1439)
A tonemap-only render texture no longer gets lens effects. SetTargetPostFx(id, false) reset the grade but not the lens. The reset is now one method, PostParams::tonemap_only, which render::draw::stack calls. It also clears vignette and lens. New pixel test: tests/gpu/postfx/tonemap_only_screenshot.rs. It fails with the fix reverted and passes with it.
An authored effect's game_time() read is tested again: blocks_screenshot::an_authored_effect_reads_game_time, a pulsing damage_vignette rendered at two times and at the same time twice.
PostParams joins render::gpu::uniform_layout_tests (now a folder, with postfx.rs). It compares every member's offset and the struct size against the WGSL struct.
pack_lens clamps what it packs (intensities and response to 0..=1, smoothness to MIN_SMOOTHNESS..=1, colour to 0..=1), because a loaded file bypasses the ops. Unit test: params::tests::lens::lens_knobs_are_clamped_when_packed.
CA reads the blurred image: the composite's t_color is scene_color() since Per-object motion blur: reconstruction filter (TileMax/NeighborMax) over the velocity buffer #1436, so this came with the rebase.
fringe's doc now says the taps are fetched toward the centre.
Shader.md's recipe example uses sharpen instead of a second scanline.
No #[rustfmt::skip] added. The card's Shadows and SSAO sections moved to camera/lighting.rs, and lens.rs is split into one function per effect (clippy's 50-line limit was why it had been squeezed).
fringe takes the composite's centre sample as base, so CA costs three taps, not four.
The branch was rebased onto main with #1464 merged. The earlier commits were squashed first, so the conflicts were resolved once (force-push authorised by the brief).
Why
Vignette, CA and grain are the standard lens stack of every shipped shooter (they are Unity URP/HDRP volume overrides). Before this, they existed only as authored blocks, reached through Shader.Bake + SetCustomEffects + SetPostParam by string. Now they are knobs an agent sets in one call, saved with the scene, editable in the inspector and switchable by the player. A post stack at the Trepang2 bar needs that.
Effect
A camera with the effects off renders exactly as before, because the composite skips each one at intensity 0. A lavapipe test renders every effect active at intensity 0 and gets an image byte-identical to off.
Lavapipe tests (tests/gpu/postfx/lens_screenshot.rs):
the vignette darkens all four corners and keeps the centre;
CA produces red and yellow fringes on a white edge near the border, and none on an edge at the centre;
grain changes between ticks and is identical at the same tick.
With post-FX off, a render texture draws none of the lens effects (tonemap_only_screenshot).
Unit and integration tests:

Decisions
Followed the brief and Unity on order: CA before the grade, the vignette after it, grain last. Unity URP actually applies its vignette before the LUT. The brief puts it after the grade, and so does this branch; the only difference is how a non-black vignette colour gets graded.
The vignette is Unity URP's unrounded shape (it follows the screen's aspect), with no rounded toggle, since none was asked for.
Grain uses a hash, not Unity's grain textures: it needs no assets, is deterministic and is reproducible per tick.
The vignette center isn't clamped at pack time. An off-screen centre is odd but finite; only the NaN cases (smoothness 0) and the out-of-range strengths are clamped.

Not run: a real-GPU (Metal) pass. A scoped mutation run is dispatched (mutants-on-request.yml, scope=diff); its report lands on this PR.
Seam
components::{Vignette, ChromaticAberration, FilmGrain} (each with a DEFAULT const), in the fields vignette / chromatic_aberration / film_grain on PostProcessingComponent.
PostParams in Rust (render::postfx) and postfx::params::PostParams in WGSL (engine/shaders/postfx/params.wgsl):
vignette = [intensity (0 off), smoothness, centre uv];
vignette_color = rgb;
lens = [CA intensity (0 off), grain intensity (0 off), grain response, unused].
The struct is no longer in common.wgsl: import it from postfx::params. A field added to it gets a row in render::gpu::uniform_layout_tests::postfx.
PostParams::tonemap_only() is the one place a post-FX-off view drops effects. A new composite effect resets its slot there.
engine/shaders/postfx/lens.wgsl (postfx::lens::{fringe, after_grade}) is the composite's lens stage. A later lens effect (lens distortion, say) goes there.
For a human
On Metal: tick the three Lens Effects in the inspector and confirm they look like the "after" capture: fringes at the edges, dark corners, and fine grain that animates in Play and holds still in edit mode.

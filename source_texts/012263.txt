Implemented a complete SVO + DDA software ray tracing pipeline in mesrgl-universe and mesrgl-integration. Key features include:
64-bit packed SVO node representation (child mask, base index/offset, leaf flag, LOD, material ID) with population count bitwise indexing.
3D Morton Z-order curve bit interleaving encoder/decoder and ray sign masks.
Continuous array-backed SVONodePool eliminating GC overhead during traversal.
Recursive SVOBuilder, SVDAGOptimizer subtree deduplication, and SVOValidator structural invariant verifier.
Robust Slab method Ray-AABB intersection and Amanatides & Woo 3D DDA traversal.
HierarchicalSVOTraverser with stack-based descent and front-to-back child sorting.
Screen-space footprint DistanceLOD and ConeRayTracer with hierarchical voxel material Mipmap aggregation.
WavefrontRayScheduler with ray queues (primary, shadow, reflection, refraction with Snell's Law and TIR) and Early Ray Termination.
TemporalReprojector, edge-aware SVGFDenoiser, and MesrGLFramebuffer supporting RGBA16F/RGBA8, depth, normal, motion vectors, ACES/Reinhard tone mapping, and gamma correction.
MinecraftVoxelAdapter and per-mod ModRenderConfig API namespaces.
Unit test suite with 10,000 randomized rays verified against exact geometric brute-force AABB oracle and multi-resolution LOD performance benchmarks.

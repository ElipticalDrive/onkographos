Summary
Route selected virtual-geometry work between hardware and compute rasterization based on projected triangle/cluster characteristics while producing one coherent visibility result.
Roadmap metadata

Type: feature
Parent Epic: [Epic] Virtual Geometry & Massive Mesh Rendering #469
Scope
Classify visible clusters/triangles using projected size, triangle density, raster cost, and backend capabilities
Send larger geometry through the hardware visibility path and micro/pixel-scale geometry through compute rasterization
Merge/arbitrate both paths into one depth + visibility identity representation without ordering holes
Keep routing GPU-driven and avoid CPU per-cluster decisions
Add conservative thresholds and fallback behavior for unsupported capabilities
Add telemetry for classification counts, crossover efficiency, overdraw, and per-path timing
Tune thresholds using the VG2.0 corpus rather than hard-coding one GPU-specific assumption
Acceptance criteria
Mixed hardware/compute frames produce stable depth and identity with no cracks or double-shading artifacts
Path selection can be disabled/forced for validation
Crossover policy is capability/profile driven and benchmarkable
Hybrid mode is measurably no worse than either forced path across the representative benchmark corpus and improves at least the mixed-detail stress cases

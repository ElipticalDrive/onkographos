The topology can be used to identify the interesting features from volumetric data and it can be well described by the contour tree. The existing contour tree-based volume rendering methods usually suffer from the high time cost of the construction of the contour tree. In order to solve
this problem, the fast contour tree-based medical volume rendering method is proposed. Firstly, a fast construction algorithm for the contour tree by using multi-thread processor is developed; then, the fast contour tree-based volume rendering method with the manually interactive interface
is described in details; finally, the experimental results show that by the proposed method the regions of interest can be easily identified, and the rendering results with clearer boundary can be obtained. Furthermore, the computational efficiency can be greatly improved.

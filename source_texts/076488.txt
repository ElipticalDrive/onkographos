We propose a method for voxel-artistic rendering, which generates a voxel art-like image from a given color image. First, we overlay a hexagonal lattice on a color image, and then, we determine the color of each hexagon based on the colors of neighboring pixels in the image. We regard the hexagons as voxels, and arrange the voxels to approximate the original color image. We show some experimental results, where the generated voxel art-like images appear to have three-dimensional structures, and to preserve the contents of the original images

What and why
Follows #5535.
Renderer::render_node_with and Renderer::render_node_thumbnail_with draw into a new render context and never set its aliasing threshold, so a caller that passes AntiAlias::None still gets smooth edges. A page render sets the threshold from the options (draw_frame, and Frame::offscreen_context for offscreen art).
renderer.rs: both methods set the context's aliasing threshold from opts.anti_alias before drawing.
How it was tested
New test: node_renders_follow_the_anti_alias_option. It fails without the change, and fails on the thumbnail check with only the render_node_thumbnail_with line taken out.

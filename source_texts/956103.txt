Task T4 of the fork's work list (docs/FORK.md). One task at a time, in order. Depends on T3.
Exponential height fog integrated along the view ray (no ray march first), density by weather (foggy weather thicker), colour from the time cycle (0x4E1B30) so it meets the distance fog; Proper Shaders' [VolumetricFog] values (BaseHeight 10, HeightFalloff 0.02, MaxOpacity 0.8...) as the starting point. Settings: on / off, density, height.
Done when: streets fill with fog in foggy weather and mornings, the HUD is untouched, < 0.5 ms at 1080p.

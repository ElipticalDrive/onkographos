🎯 What: The testing gap addressed
The ray_intersects_triangle function in Modules/RayTriangleIntersection.lua lacked dedicated testing for its purely mathematical logic, preventing verification of its Möller–Trumbore intersection algorithm.
📊 Coverage: What scenarios are now tested
Standard ray intersection in the middle of a triangle.
Missing intersection (ray passes outside the triangle bounds).
Pointing away (ray starts inside or points away from the triangle).
Parallel rays (ray direction is parallel to the triangle plane).
Ray intersecting exactly on a triangle vertex.
Ray intersecting exactly on a triangle edge.
✨ Result: The improvement in test coverage
The module is now fully covered by deterministic mathematical test cases, serving as a safety net against regressions without relying on Roblox Instance mocks or physical world environment. Tests run via Lune using the established @lune/roblox Vector3 mock.

FoVolNet is a novel volume rendering pipeline that utilizes foveated rendering and deep neural networks to enhance visualization performance while maintaining high visual quality. By focusing computational resources on the user's focal point and reconstructing the full frame from sparse inputs, FoVolNet significantly reduces rendering time compared to traditional methods. Abstract—Volume data is found in many important scientific and engineering applications. Rendering this data for visualization at high
quality and interactive rates for demanding applications such as virtual reality is still not easily achievable even using professional-grade
hardware. We introduce FoVolNet—a method to significantly increase the performance of volume data visualization. We develop
a cost-effective foveated rendering pipeline that sparsely samples a volume around a focal point and reconstructs the full-frame
using a deep neural network. Foveated rendering is a technique that prioritizes rendering computations around the user’s focal
point. This approach leverages properties of the human visual system, thereby saving computational resources when rendering
data in the periphery of the user’s field of vision. Our reconstruction network combines direct and kernel prediction methods to
produce fast, stable, and perceptually convincing output. With a slim design and the use of quantization, our method outperforms
state-of-the-art neural reconstruction techniques in both end-to-end frame times and visual quality. We conduct extensive evaluations
of the system’s rendering performance, inference speed, and perceptual properties, and we provide comparisons to competing neural
image reconstruction techniques. Our test results show that FoVolNet consistently achieves significant time saving over conventional
rendering while preserving perceptual quality.

How does the concept of foveated rendering in FoVolNet improve rendering performance compared to conventional methods?
Foveated rendering in FoVolNet improves rendering performance by exploiting the human visual system's characteristics, which allows it to focus computational resources on areas of the image that are more likely to be noticed by the viewer. It renders images sparsely by skipping peripheral pixels and reconstructs them using a neural network, reducing the amount of data needed to be processed in real time and decreasing frame rendering time compared to conventional methods .
In what ways does FoVolNet leverage characteristics of the human visual system for rendering purposes?
FoVolNet leverages the characteristics of the human visual system (HVS) by implementing foveated rendering, which focuses computational resources on areas within the viewer's focal area, rendering these regions with more details while reducing detail in peripheral areas. This approach ensures efficient use of computational resources, where dense rendering is applied only where necessary, optimizing performance without significant perceptible loss in visual quality .

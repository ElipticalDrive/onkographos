The manual "Displacement Mapping and Volume Rendering Graphics Hardware" by Michael C. Doggett introduces new hardware architectures for more realistic surface rendering of three-dimensional objects and the rendering of volumetric datasets. The dissertation focuses on the introduction of geometry engines on relatively inexpensive single chip ASICs, which now contain the complete graphics rendering pipeline in full custom hardware. The first part of the dissertation deals with surface rendering, proposing architectures for displacement map rendering in hardware. These architectures integrate into currently available pipelines and make use of existing units in those pipelines. The second part, titled Volume Rendering, presents the VIZARDII architecture and several hardware-based performance improvements for any ray casting architecture. VIZARDII is an interactive programmable hardware accelerator for Volume Rendering implemented on a PCI Card, with the main pipeline implemented on a Xilinx FPGA allowing new features to be added relatively quickly. The dissertation also discusses novel architectures for ray queuing and sorting, sub-cube based space leaping, and antialiasing that occurs when ray casting volume data. This manual is useful for both professional mechanics and DIY enthusiasts.

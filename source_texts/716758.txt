💡 What: Replaced the O(log N) partition_point (binary search) lookups in the voxelization inner loop with an amortized O(1) rolling index (hit_idx).
🎯 Why: Because the voxel grid along the X-axis strictly ascends and the hit_xs vector is sorted, we can linearly advance a rolling index alongside x instead of repeatedly performing binary searches across thousands of voxels.
📊 Impact: Reduces the computational complexity of evaluating intersections for a scanline from O(N log K) to O(N + K). While benchmarks on 10x10x10 grids remain near ~500µs, this asymptotic optimization prevents exponential slowdowns on massive meshes where the inner loop executes hundreds of millions of times.
🔬 Measurement: Verify via cargo bench to ensure no performance degradation on small sets and validated correctness via cargo test, ensuring all ray intersection parity checks perfectly match the previous point-by-point logic.

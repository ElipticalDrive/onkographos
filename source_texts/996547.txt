Summary
Store Kind and Light in 16³ sections with bit-packed palette indices, selected per ChunkStorageType via USE_SECTIONS.
Keep derived layers (FacesOcclusion, FacesSoftLight) on the column backend; section palettes with 96 B soft-light entries can trigger the dense fallback.
Add deserialization validation for section storage, mirroring the column backend (section count, palette size, index bit width/length, index bounds, dense length).
Add tests for backend selection, BPE round-tripping at every bit width, maximum palettes, malformed archives, and the section round-trip.
Why
At radius 32 the server holds Kind + Light for 4225 chunks. Section storage collapses uniform air/sky/underground sections and BPE keeps the remaining section indices compact. Derived layers are LRU-cached for 32 chunks and stay column-backed.

Compatibility
VERSION_MAGIC was intentionally left unchanged per the author's instruction; the generated archive/region was deleted. Existing local archives must be deleted or regenerated. Without the magic bump, an old archive fails later as a bincode decode error instead of UnsupportedVersion.
Validation
just ci passes (fmt, clippy, workspace tests, udeps).
Runtime: both configurations reached 4225/4225 chunks with dirty=0; the client stayed connected and rendered.
Known limitations
16³ is hardcoded; 8³ would be finer for caves/overhangs.
ChunkStorage<T> now dispatches through an enum on every get/set; the load-time sample may indicate a cost that needs more data.
/proc RSS measurements are from a single run each on this machine.

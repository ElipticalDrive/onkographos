Image compression means reducing the image size without any effect on the quality of image. Due to this compression maximum number of images to be stored in the available memory space. In this paper we discuss about image compression, types of compression, types of redundancy, and algorithms of image compression. There are many algorithms available in the image compression, but now days wavelet transform based image compression algorithms are mostly used. In this paper we review about two wavelet based algorithms SPIHT (Set-partitioning in Hierarchical Trees), EZW (Embedded Zero-tree Wavelet).

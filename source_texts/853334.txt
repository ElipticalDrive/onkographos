GPU tests that assume float arithmetic is bit-exact across drivers
GPU float arithmetic is not bit-exact across conforming drivers:
the float-to-half store may round to nearest or toward zero;
OpFDiv allows 2.5 ulp;
texture filtering and sRGB conversion precision are implementation-defined;
the sign of a NaN is unspecified;
fused multiply-add contraction varies.
Tests that pin one driver's results fail on another. Issue #255 has 10 such tests on a Radeon 780M, and #304 had two on Mesa 26.
Rule:
Byte identity is valid only for two runs of the same code on the same device: a feature turned off against the stock path built from the same shader, determinism, cancellation and restoration.
Not valid:
hardcoded golden bytes or bit patterns of GPU output;
exact equality between GPU output and a CPU computation;
exact equality between two different shader paths that are only mathematically equivalent.
What replaces them: a CPU f64 reference within a derived, documented bound (store precision, division and transcendental ulps, filtering, quantisation), or a behavioural property.
Bounds must be derived, never widened until the test passes. A real shader bug gets fixed in the shader. One found so far: SSR AO picked its taps with floor of a float quotient. When the AO grid matched the frame size, that landed one texel off on RADV in 7,310 aligned cases out of a sweep, and on llvmpipe in 1,035.
Audit
A read-only audit of main at 7c38b32 covered every readback in render-post, render-scene, render-gpu, render-lighting, render-shadow and scene-viewer. It excluded the FXAA tests (#351) and the 10 tests in #255.

The exact-equality group breaks the rule outright and is fixed first, in PRs grouped by module:

The tolerance group passes today, and its bounds are probably adequate. Each gets a derivation, or a property-based replacement, as its module is touched.

[linux-llxprt-branch-2]
Exact-equality group: done. #366 (15 render-post tests) and #391 (42 render-scene and scene-viewer tests) replaced every test the audit found pinning driver-defined float results. Together with #351 (FXAA) and #356 (the 10 tests in #255), the render-post suite and all converted tests now pass on both lavapipe and the Radeon 780M (RADV).
Every converted assertion was break-checked: the behaviour was broken in the shader and the test had to fail. That exposed three conversions that could not fail as first written, because the two compared renders shared the broken code or the error hid under 8-bit rounding:
the decal modifier;
bloom over the scene;
the retired-shader and stock-shader comparisons.
Each now also checks absolute values against an f64 model.
Still open: the tolerance group, 226 tests whose fixed tolerances have no written derivation. They pass today. As each module is next touched, its tolerances get a derivation or a property-based replacement.

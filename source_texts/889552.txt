Ticket: #44 State of the art: real-time global illumination and lighting (research ticket, resolved and closed on the map; this PR records its note)
What
docs/research/global-illumination-and-lighting.md surveys real-time global illumination, lighting and shadowing for an open world at ADR 0013's budgets, for the visibility strategy (#22) and the map's lighting fog lines. Sixteen techniques each get what they buy, how they handle time of day and moving lights, their temporal class (complete each frame; converging in a world-space cache; or relying on screen-space history), published cost with its context, maturity, fit against wgpu 30.0.1, the M4 Pro and the main pass, and IP: baked lightmaps, baked probe volumes, precomputed relightable GI, screen-space GI and AO, voxel GI, SDF-traced GI, DDGI, surfel GI, Lumen, radiance cascades, hashed world-space caches, ReSTIR DI and many lights, ReSTIR GI and PT, specular, shadow maps, and ray-traced shadows. A table adds what wgpu 30.0.1 allows beyond the capability matrix, and a measured section gives ray-query, BLAS and TLAS costs on the M4 Pro, because every published cost comes from desktop GPUs or consoles. The note ends in five options with no recommendation: ADR 0008 as written, relightable precomputed GI, ray-traced probe GI with world-space accumulation only, per-pixel ReSTIR path tracing, and radiance cascades. Each option states its image-quality consequences, following the owner's direction (map decision).
Verification
Every citation was checked by script against the source it cites: 255 links. Each quoted phrase and number in a claim was searched for in the cited PDF page, PPTX slide, source line range or web page. The ~50 flags (row numbers, computed or measured figures, dates written after their links) were read by hand, and about 70 more claims were read in full, including every cost the options use.
Foundational DOIs checked through Crossref.
wgpu: registry sources byte-identical to tag v30.0.1 (cmp); the "rebuild only" and ray-tracing-pipeline lines were read at the tag.
Measured on the M4 Pro (wgpu 30.0.1, Metal, macOS 27.0.1) with a scratch program, not in the repo: 4 open-scene and 3 dense-scene runs, compute-pass timestamps resolved after their submission completed, and a sample profile of the TLAS rebuild.
Local review (docs only, so the agent's own read on the Standards and Spec axes) found 11 problems, all fixed before opening:
Quotes and status: "it only supports rigid meshes" was misquoted; MegaLights is Beta, not experimental; the UE 5.8 page calls hardware ray tracing Lumen's default, so SDF tracing is "Lumen's software path".
Readings: Assassin's Creed Shadows' keyframe transitions are on p4; Split Radiance Cascades also shows a 10-frame accumulation; Bend's "only tested with 64" is a thread-group width, not a SIMD width the M4 Pro lacks; MetalFX does offer a temporal denoiser (macOS 26); storage-buffer binding arrays are Bevy Solari's requirement, not ReSTIR's.
CodeRabbit does not review this PR: .coderabbit.yaml excludes docs/research/**.
Metrics
n/a (docs only).
Decide
Where the lighting decision is made. ADR 0008's lighting clause (baked GI for static environments, ray-traced shadows and GI for dynamic objects, cascaded shadow maps) came from the charter. The note gives five options and no decision ticket owns them. Per-pixel ReSTIR, Lumen and MegaLights need deferred-style inputs and motion vectors, while the baked and probe options work with Forward+ and MSAA, so the choice constrains Visibility strategy for the main pass #22.
Recommend: add a decision ticket "Lighting and GI strategy" to the map, unblocked once this merges. Decide it in the same grilling session as Visibility strategy for the main pass #22 or just before it. ADR 0008's lighting clause stays as written until then.
Acceleration structures and the ADR 0013 ledger. The ledger counts buffer and texture bytes. Acceleration structures are neither: on the M4 Pro they take 94 bytes per BLAS triangle (45.5 compacted) and 288.5 bytes per TLAS instance. wgpu's internal counters track them only on DX12.
Recommend: record this as a note on the map's RT fog line now, and amend ADR 0013 to count acceleration-structure bytes when ray tracing enters the ladder.

## What

Records the owner's decision on lighting (#65): option C of
`docs/research/global-illumination-and-lighting.md`.

- **New ADR 0020, "Ray-traced probe GI and hard ray-traced shadows".**
- Indirect diffuse: DDGI probe clipmap (camera-centred cascades,
irradiance plus depth moments, Chebyshev leak test), shaded from
position and normal in ADR 0019's lit pass. Temporal class (b).
- Direct: one hard shadow ray per pixel per light, class (a). Ray
queries required; cascaded shadow maps dropped.
- Continuous time of day, every light bounces, moving geometry changes
indirect light (probe rays read the shadow TLAS).
- Provisional bounds: indirect reaches 90% within 0.5 s of a step
change, no steady-state flicker; probe update at most 2 ms GPU on the
dense Reference scene; missing both reopens the decision.
- No bake for correctness; only cooked term is a per-Chunk far-field
sky-visibility field relit by the current sky.
- TLAS: near field plus merged far proxies; radius decided with ADR
0019's implementation.
- Specular: rough from probes, glossy from runtime ray-traced reflection
cubemaps (class b); mirror reflections return with water.
- Rejected with reasons: A, B, D, E (reopen at 3 ms on the M4 Pro),
surfels, hash cache, soft-shadow jitter.
- **Second grilling (2026-10-13), folded in by the owner's choice:** the
near-field TLAS splits into a static one (rebuilt on Chunk streaming or
anchor moves) and a dynamic one capped at 10k Instances; authored
Interior volumes mask probes for load-free interiors; the far-field term
adds bounced sky visibility; contact darkening names the occlusion
Channel.
- **ADR 0008 amended:** lighting clause points at 0020.
- **`CONTEXT.md`:** new terms **Temporal class** and **Interior volume**
(used by the GI survey, ADR 0019 and 0020).
- **`CLAUDE.md` review checklist:** the no-screen-space-history line now
cites ADR 0020.

## Verification

lights work, which temporal class is accepted, and whether ADR 0008's

Reconstructing  3D models from   their  projection images has been  an
interesting research topic in  computer vision and geometric modeling.
In  this paper,  we  review methods  that   generate octree  models of
objects from  their silhouettes in  images. The complexity analysis of
the  algorithms is developed and presented. It is concluded that
the performance of the algorithms depends mostly on the number 
of starting images and the object shapes through their number of faces
and concavities

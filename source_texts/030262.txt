Context
The TSDF panel's Scene dropdown switches between Point Cloud, Voxels and Mesh (#10, #11). Wanted: a Gaussian Splatting view as a fourth option, and the same render mode in the standalone VoxelViewer. viser 1.0.30 renders gaussian splats natively (add_gaussian_splats), so the band's surface voxels can be shown as isotropic gaussians (center = voxel center, color = voxel color, std ~ voxel size) with no new dependencies and no training.
Acceptance criteria
Scene dropdown gains "Splats": selecting it after Voxelize hides the other representations and shows the band as gaussian splats; Iso / Min Weight re-filter live like the voxel view
Standalone VoxelViewer Render Mode gains "Splats" (Point Size slider scales the splats)
Loading a new sequence / re-fusing invalidates the splats like voxels and mesh

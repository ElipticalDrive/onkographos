Abstract
The effectiveness of an adaptive linear quantization and reconstruction scheme is discussed. Experimental results obtained for a chest X-ray image are presented. Data compression is achieved using the adaptive threshold sampling method, and processing is carried out line by line. The experimental results show that the percentage mean-square error increases slightly, but there is a considerable bit reduction in the transmission of the transform domain coefficients.

Outcome
Support a scenery radius larger than the authoritative live gameplay area by retaining bounded structural chunks and merging live overlays through the same renderer.
Scope and invariants
Define renderer-neutral chunk identity, world/map/physical-level placement, bounds, revision/digest, resources, structural records, and attachment lifecycle.
Compile/upload retained GPU packets asynchronously with cancellation, visibility/culling, prioritization, memory budgets, and deterministic painter integration.
Merge authoritative live records and tombstones over structural chunks by stable identity/revision without duplicate geometry or stale disclosure.
Use the same buffers/materials/passes for client, editor, and offscreen rendering where applicable; do not create a scenery-only renderer.
Handle chunk manifest changes, resource revisions, teleport/map transition, reconnect, missing/corrupt chunks, and device recreation.
Acceptance criteria
Large-radius fixtures do not enlarge server live entity state and reveal no unauthorized dynamic data.
Tests cover chunk overlap/seams, stale/duplicate/out-of-order revisions, live add/update/remove/tombstone, transitions, cancellation, eviction, corruption, and device loss.
CPU/GPU memory, upload, compile, draw, and culling meet explicit metrics budgets.
Painter/depth/lighting/semantic masks remain correct across chunk/live boundaries.
Client world-streaming epic consumes this one retained-scene implementation.
Dependencies and parallelization
Depends on renderer scene/resource/metrics foundations and server/protocol chunk manifest/delivery issues. Compilation, culling, merge, and cache work can proceed in parallel behind frozen chunk types.
Licensing
Renderer code, shaders, tests, and approved fixtures are MIT. Legacy material may be reused only under the merged approved MIT provenance-grantor registry and complete-history evidence policy in atrinik/atrinik#275, or another compatible exact license; assets retain exact terms.
Program equivalence output
Publish structural/streaming scene results through the versioned capability/result contract in atrinik#279, using exact semantic identity/depth/coverage/visibility masks plus narrow documented color tolerances. This issue is a native blocker of atrinik#280; an implementation-level rendering modernization may differ, but an unexplained gameplay visibility, ordering, selection, accessibility, or content-presentation difference fails the gate.

How to Create Realistic VDB Cloud Shading in Redshift (Cinema 4D Tutorial)
VDBS are amazing, but you have to know a few tips and tricks to render them so they look photoreal! In this Redshift + Cinema 4D tutorial, I’ll show you how to shade and light VDB clouds for realistic results.
Learn how to set up the Volume Material, fine-tune density and scattering, tweak your lighting, dial in your render settings, and make your clouds beautiful!
Topics covered:
Realistic VDB cloud shading
Redshift volume material settings
Lighting setup and lighting tips
Render Settings for Volumes

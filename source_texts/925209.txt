A game with ~40 per-pixel surface samplers (arcade-cabinet/will-it-blow) hand-rolls value noise + 5-octave fbm. Using strata-procedural@0.2.1 with the game's integer hash injected as the lattice and octaveTransform {scale:[2.03,2.03], offset:[17.1,9.7]}, output is bit-identical (200,000 of 200,000 samples, max diff 0), so adoption is otherwise a pure swap.
Cost is the blocker: 2,000,000 fbm(5 octaves) samples take 125 ms inlined vs 651 ms through the kit (5.2x), because every call validates coordinates and each intermediate sum. That is right for a library entry point, but dominates a bake loop that calls fbm tens of times per texel on load.
Request: an opt-in path for hot loops, e.g. a validated builder that returns a sampler with per-call finite checks elided (checked once at construction/for the bake's coordinate range), or a bulk fill over a coordinate grid. Acceptance from our side: probe parity stays exact and cost is within ~1.2x of inlined; we will then cut over and delete our copy. Probe code is in the repo's docs/strata-adoption.md.

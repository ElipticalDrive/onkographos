Outcome
Replace the retired smooth SDF planet with the authoritative game-ready cubic voxel world described in docs/cubic-voxel-planet.md.
Invariants
every canonical voxel is one exact 100 mm Cartesian cube;
material byte 0 is air and 1 through 255 are solid palette materials;
no SDF, density field, heightfield, spherical face projection, extracted smooth surface, or smooth fallback;
sparse procedural base plus persistent hierarchical edit overrides;
true 3D mountains, caves, overhangs, materials, and whole-planet destruction;
observer/projected-error streaming with stable stationary convergence;
complete ancestor-or-descendant coverage and generation-safe publication;
exact gameplay/collision queries remain owned here.
Delivery gates
randomized dense-reference parity for hierarchy queries, edits, collapse, snapshots, and replay;
fixed-seed terrain fixtures with mountains, overhangs, connected caves, strata, and destructible structures;
root deletion bounded by hierarchy depth;
refine/coarsen round-trip identity and no stationary refinement churn;
hard CPU memory, request, upload, and work budgets;
ground-to-orbit/extreme-distance/return integration without holes, stale state, or black frames;
live visual approval before planet changes are committed or merged.
Helio renderer work is tracked separately. Shared engine/API work must remain isolated for maintainer review.

An armature/shape-key/cloth-deformed obstacle moves its evaluated vertices
while its object matrix stays fixed, so matrix differencing yielded zero wall
velocity (updated SDF, no push). solid_velocity_from_objects now takes the
previous frame's evaluated world-space vertices: when the matrix is unchanged
but vertices moved, each band cell takes the displacement of its nearest
current vertex over frame_dt (chunked argmin). The bake loop snapshots both
matrices and vertices per animated obstacle each frame. Rigid motion keeps the
exact matrix-differenced path. Mock-based regression test included.
Closes #13.

Cap the deformation nearest-vertex allocation
For deforming obstacles with realistic cloth/subdivision vertex counts, this expression materializes a full len(chunk) × len(v_cur) × 3 float64 array per 2048-cell chunk. For example, a 100k-vertex evaluated mesh allocates roughly 4.9 GB just to choose nearest vertices for one chunk, so high-resolution animated obstacle bakes can OOM before a frame advances; chunking or spatially indexing the vertices is needed as well as chunking cells.

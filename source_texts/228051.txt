FP16 training is currently broken, due to weights going to NaN after the first iteration. This appears to be due to numerical instability in gradient calculations. For example, a mean-squared error of pixel values involves summing a large number of small values, which can be problematic in low precision.
Perhaps try implementing dynamic loss scaling on regular NeRF and then apply it on Scene Flow Fields model.

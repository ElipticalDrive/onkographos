Summary
Implemented Multiple Importance Sampling (MIS) with Next Event Estimation (NEE) and heavily optimized the path tracer. Created to aid in the training process of the AI denoiser. MIS converges significantly faster which allows for more accurate and faster renders.
Changes
MIS
Power heuristic
BSDF and PDF evaluation
NEE
HDRI CDF precomputation and direct sampling
Area light power based sampling from precomputed alias table
Punctual lights power based sampling
Shadow ray testing
Renaming of all variables/structs/functions to follow standard and consistent conventions
Parsing of glsl #include and file separation/organization
Real time UI for switching between different BSDF "modes" and toggling MIS/NEE
Support for separated light path bounce depth - total, diffuse, specular, transmission

The F16<->float helpers used by avifGetRGBAPixel()/avifSetRGBAPixel() on avifRGBImage::isFloat data were adapted from libyuv's HalfFloatRow_C(), which converts unsigned integer samples to half floats. Reused as a general float<->half conversion, the bit trick misinterprets the sign bit as part of the exponent:
-1.0h (0xBC00) decodes to +4294967296.0f (sign flip, 2^32 magnitude)
-0.0h (0x8000) decodes to +131072.0f
+Inf (0x7C00) decodes to the finite 65536.0f
-Inf (0xFC00) decodes to +2.81475e14f
NaN (0x7E00) decodes to the finite 98304.0f and avifFloatToF16() silently drops the sign of negative values.
Replace them with correct IEEE 754 binary16 conversions (signed zeros, subnormals, negatives, infinities, NaNs, round-to-nearest-even on the float-to-half direction). All 65536 half precision bit patterns now survive a decode/encode round trip bit exactly, matching NumPy's float16 behavior. Verified exhaustively against NumPy (65536 decode + round-trip cases and ~20k adversarial float encodings: 0 mismatches), covered by new F16Test cases in avifrgbtest.cc, and validated with the full test suite under ASan+UBSan (failure list identical to the unpatched baseline).
The bulk unorm->F16 conversion in avifRGBImageToF16() is unchanged: it legitimately operates on non-negative integer samples and must stay bit-compatible with libyuv's HalfFloatPlane().

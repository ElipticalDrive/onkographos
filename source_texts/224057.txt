What
First step of the visual-target plan (docs/lanes/world/visual-target.md, V0): a software perspective renderer, so we can see courses instead of reading top-down maps.
crates/w5k_world/src/render.rs: camera and view space, near-plane clipping, edge-function triangle fill, z-buffer (stored as 1/z), banded Lambert flat shading, distance fog, sky gradient, shadow rays against the heightfield. Terrain is a mesh sampled every few nodes, water is flat quads, trees are trunk plus cone, rocks are squashed octahedra, walls and barricades are boxes.
w5k world view <course.ron> --out DIR [--size WxH] [--step N] writes view-road.png (from behind the start of the main road, looking along it) and view-overview.png (aerial of the whole course). Under a second per course.
Tests (physics and geometry sentences): projection puts the look-at point at the image centre with +x right and +y up; a nearer triangle hides a farther one in either draw order; a triangle crossing the near plane is clipped; fog blends toward the horizon colour; a face square to the sun is fully lit and one edge-on gets only ambient; a pillar's shadow is H / tan(elevation) long; rendering is deterministic.
Theory: docs/theory/world.md section 11 (view space, clipping, edge functions and barycentrics, z-buffer, flat shading, shadow ray marching).
Images
docs/lanes/world/media/view/: slice-road.png (trees, road, rock field and hill ahead), ridge-overview.png (cliff, switchback road, chute), crossing-overview.png (river and road bridge).
Known gaps (V1 onward)
Props cast no shadows; the terrain is the raw grid, so road edges are stair-stepped at mesh step 2 (V1 decimates and softens). The sun direction and band count are display choices (PROVISIONAL look-flat).
Checks
fmt, clippy -D warnings, constants lint, media lint, lane guard, cargo test for w5k_world and w5k_tools pass locally. Golden-Change: none (display tool only; nothing the simulation reads changed). Independent of #137 (mobility).

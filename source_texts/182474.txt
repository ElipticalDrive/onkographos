A set of 336 FREE Gradient Textures for you to use in your games and projects!
Are you looking for some great grayscale gradients to do some lighting or texture effects? Want to experiment with height mapping? Do you just need to alpha-blend some images together? Then look no further! This pack contains over one hundred types of grayscale gradients organized into nine different categories that can be used however you wish. Every gradient in this pack has been rendered as *.png and at multiple sizes so you can choose the size that works best for you! Most gradients are also seamless along at least one axis. These gradients are great for creating image and texture blends, brushes, special effects, lighting, height mapping and more!
Every gradient is available in three sizes: 512x512, 256x256, and 128x128
UPDATE 12/23/2021: Spaces have been removed from all texture filenames to make them easier to import into certain programs or game engines that do not support spaces.

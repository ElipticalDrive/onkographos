This project investigates the construction of non-Euclidean geometries through domain repetition and coordinate identification within a signed distance field (SDF) ray-marching framework. By Implementing the system entirely with GPU-based ray-marching on the Shadertoy BETA platform, smooth SDF blending, and soft shadow rendering, we demonstrated how algebraic transformations of space can produce rich geometric and visual effects. Our core technology, called domain repetition, allows us to render a variety of quotient spaces of R including R3/Z3 , R3/Z2 , R3/Z, R2/Z 2 , R2/Z, and various R3/D2n. R3/Z3 and R2/Z2 being equivalent to the 3-torus (S1×S1×S1 , or simply T3 ) and 2-torus (S1×S1 , or T2 ) respectively. Domain repetition allows these spaces to be rendered quick enough to be traversed with real-time camera movement, something not possible in regular R3 ray marching. In our testing, our renderer could only handle around 40 objects in a scene before dramatic performance loss, whereas with domain repetition we could render thousands of (identical) objects with almost no cost to performance compared to rendering a single one

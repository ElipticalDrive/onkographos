Step 6 of the terrain roadmap:
add a sparse CPU-authoritative voxel stamp model with stable IDs and strict bounds
support add, subtract, and smooth stamp operations
upload only compact stamp shape/control buffers to WebGPU
generate a shared density-sample grid in compute
apply constructive SDF stamps and a separate local smoothing pass
classify and emit the marching-cubes surface from the edited density grid
derive normals from density gradients
queue regeneration without GPU-to-CPU readbacks
integrate global undo/redo and clear-world history
persist voxelStamps in world document version 4
migrate versions 1–3 to an empty voxel edit layer
restore the previous world transactionally when stamp imports are invalid
add sidebar controls for operation, center, radius, strength, blend, and cursor X/Z capture
This remains one bounded chunk. Multi-chunk ownership, dirty-region dispatch, direct surface picking, LOD transitions, and heightfield handoff are deferred.

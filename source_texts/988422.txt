Addresses the latest round of effect feedback.
Glass (Glass blocks, Fluted glass, Liquid glass)
Blend pulls each piece of glass toward its own average color, down to one flat color at 100%.
Frost now samples a mipmap, so wide frost stays smooth instead of grainy.
Tint is now frosted sea glass: milky, with lighter edges and softer shading. Glass blocks no longer render with dark rims and gaps that look like keyboard keys.
Texture dimples the surface like patterned glass. Stretch widens blocks into ovals.
Retuned Pink mosaic, Glass squares, Green glass and Dimple glow against their references.
Paint
Motion now stirs the paint further along its flow and back over each loop. The swirls wind in and ease out with no sliding or boiling, and the loop seam measured zero.
New Cells control drops acrylic-pour cells (dark rims, nested spots, clusters) late in the flow, so they stretch into ovals. New Acrylic pour preset.
Thin film (new Color effect)
Film mode computes interference colors from the physics: Airy single-layer reflectance, integrated over the spectrum with the Wyman–Sloan–Shirley CIE fit, converted to sRGB. Picture tone sets the thickness, so gradients become Newton's fringe sequence.
Foil mode models holographic foil as a diffraction grating, so tones run through the pure spectrum.
Oil slick and Holo swirl are rebuilt on it.
Caustics (new Noise type)
A beam of rays bent by a rippled lens, lit by ray density with box filtering, so strands stay sharp without aliasing. Neural pink is rebuilt on it.
Motion
Glide on Flow and Noise now blends the two fields with contrast compensation, not two colored frames. Carnage morphs with no visible crossfade.
Engrave
Steps and Hairline, so Vertical bars jumps from hairline to bar like the reference.
Stronger ripple beads; retuned Wave traffic.
Russian strings and README counts are updated. I rendered the full board of 31 presets and saw no shader errors.

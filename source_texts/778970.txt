ColorCast is a Python toolkit for color and style transfer between images. Its accessibility pipeline simulates protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia using Vienot (1999) and Brettel (1997) linear-RGB matrices; the Daltonization module re-encodes lost chromatic information into surviving perceptual channels, making color differences visible to dichromatic observers. It also provides histogram matching, mean/std transfer, LUT-based curves, and selective regional transfer via CLI, GUI, and library API.

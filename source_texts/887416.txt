Context
Even if Bunny does not own a physics engine, downstream physics/collision systems need deterministic primitive facts such as volume, centroid, inertia approximations, and surface area.
Requested outcome
Add deterministic mass/property calculations for supported shapes and meshes where the math can be specified cleanly.
Acceptance criteria
Define which properties are exact fixed calculations vs approximations.
Add shape volume/area/centroid helpers for spheres, AABBs, capsules, and meshes as scoped.
Document overflow and unit policy.
Add raw golden tests.

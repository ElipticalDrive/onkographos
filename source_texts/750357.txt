Part of #357. A volumetric cloud layer can now shadow the sun on the scene and in the volumetric fog.
Change
Switch. CloudLayer.cast_shadows is true in CLOUD_LAYER_DEFAULT and false for a bare CloudLayer{}.
Pass. When the switch is on and the sun is above the horizon, Pass.CLOUD_SHADOWS writes one transmittance map per view, every frame. It runs before the fog volume and the shading passes, and keeps no cache.
Map. One 512² R16F map (CLOUD_SHADOW_SIZE) per view.
Contents. Each texel holds the transmittance along the sun through the layer, on the layer's bottom plane. The shell is treated as flat over the map.
Coverage. It covers CLOUD_SHADOW_EXTENT (16,384 m, 32 m a texel).
Placement. It is centred on the camera's footprint along the sun and snapped to the world's texel grid, so it does not swim.
Edge. It fades to full light over the outer CLOUD_SHADOW_EDGE_BAND (10%) of its half extent, so there is no seam.
March. Each texel takes CLOUD_SHADOW_STEPS (16) samples with the detail octave.
Receivers. A receiver projects along the sun onto the bottom plane.
Below the layer it takes the map's value. Inside the layer it takes the value raised to the share of the layer above it. Above the layer it takes 1.
The divisor is floored at CLOUD_SUN_FLOOR, which moved into the ABI so the march, the map and the receivers share it.
shadow_visibility and shadow_transmittance multiply the result into the sun's visibility. Forward and deferred shading, the froxel fog inject (plain and temporal) and custom stages therefore receive it.
A draw that does not receive shadows (DRAW_RECEIVE_SHADOW off) receives no cloud shadow either.
ABI.
FrameRoot.cloud_shadow (a GpuAddress, zero when absent) takes the two pad words at offset 568. FrameRoot stays 576 B.
New CloudShadowRoot (64 B).
LIGHT_CLOUD_SHADOW marks the sun's packed light, and light_casts_shadow accepts the mark.
Refactor. The cloud density moved to shaders/common/cloud_density.glsl, shared by the march and the map writer.
Serial. CloudLayer's layout fingerprint changes. No released tag includes CloudLayer (v0.1.0 predates it), so no v1 reader is needed.
Example. sky --clouds gains a "Cloud shadows" checkbox. Wind moves the shadows.
Docs. sky.md describes the map, its placement, extent and edge band, the receivers, the memory (CLOUD_SHADOW_SIZE² × 2 B per view) and the exclusions. custom_shaders.md notes that the shadow helpers include the clouds.
Exclusions (documented). The path tracer, traced-reflection and probe hits, probe updates and the atmosphere's lighting cubes see no cloud shadow. The cloud march keeps its own light march for self-shadowing.
Divergences from the brief
Map centre. The map is centred on the camera's footprint along the sun, taken at the lower of the camera's altitude and the layer's bottom.
For a camera below the layer, this is the camera's projection onto the bottom plane.
For a camera above the layer, projecting the camera itself would push the ground below it out of the map at a low sun.
LIGHT_CLOUD_SHADOW. The brief names only FrameRoot.cloud_shadow. The receivers also need to know which light the map belongs to, because shadow_visibility takes a LightGpu and no index.
A sun without shadow layers still reaches the map, and so do custom stages that gate on light_casts_shadow.
Ray-hit shading keeps its former test, so hits stay excluded.
Constants. CLOUD_SHADOW_EXTENT 16,384 m, CLOUD_SHADOW_STEPS 16 and CLOUD_SHADOW_EDGE_BAND 0.1. Each is in the ABI file with a why/cost comment.
Cost
Lavapipe (CPU Vulkan, environment only, 640×360, three runs of 24 frames, medians): CLOUD_SHADOWS 13.2-13.4 ms, against CLOUDS 8.5-8.6 ms in the same runs.
Fixed size. The map is fixed at 512² texels, so the pass's cost does not depend on the view's resolution.
Levers:
CLOUD_SHADOW_SIZE: cost scales with the texel count.
CLOUD_SHADOW_STEPS: cost scales with the step count.
The detail octave: about half the noise samples.
CLOUD_SHADOW_EXTENT: trades reach against texel size.
Memory. 512 KiB per view, plus a 64 B root and a 128 B march root in the frame's upload ring.
Receivers. One map sample per shadowed sun lookup, only for the flagged sun.
Owed. GPU timings for the pass and the receivers are owed on the RTX 4090.
Verification
All runs are on lavapipe, headless, with validation on and 0 validation messages, at head 0823578 against main 24a91a3.
Identity.
With no layer, the main-view and mirror md5 equal main in 10 of 10 scenarios: noon, twilight, orbit, plain and temporal volumetric fog, deferred, traced shadows and three ground views.
With a layer and cast_shadows false, the same 10 of 10 hold.
Fog history (S4). Temporal volumetric fog over 30 frames gives 1 rejection in 30 recordings (the first frame), for the main view and the mirror. Five conditions were run:
wind 5 m a frame;
wind 5 m plus camera 0.5 m a frame;
wind 40 m plus camera 8 m a frame;
shadows off;
no wind.
The control, fog density +1% a frame, gives 30 of 30. Toggling cast_shadows resets the history once by construction; that was not run.
Pan. The snapped map leaves at most 1 level of residual with 0 pixels over 1 level. An unsnapped control leaves up to 3-4 levels and over 13,000 pixels over 1 level.
Projection. With the sun overhead, the ground shadow correlates 0.84 with the cloud seen from below, flipped.
Horizon. At 0° and −5° sun elevation the image equals shadows off. At 1-12° the map is written.
Unit filters. shader 40, abi 1, sky 11, light 22, view 64, shadow 44, describe 38.
Serial targets. serial_test 23, serial_order_forward 2, serial_order_reverse 2, serial_text_test 25, serial_described_test 104, serial_core_policy_test 1, serial_all_test 2.
Render acceptance. 41 of 41 cases with sky, fog, atmosphere or shadow in the name, one per run.
Build. Shaders verify (160 stages); sky builds.
Screenshots (lavapipe)
Ground at noon: before, after, and |before − after| × 8.
Volumetric fog: before, after, and the difference × 8. At the example's cloud scale the shadowed bands in the fog are broad.
Pan: top-down at 1 px a metre, snapped against unsnapped, with the residual × 32.
Sun overhead: the ground shadow looking down, beside the cloud looking up, flipped.
Remaining work
Cloud temporal accumulation follows in a later PR.

Fixes for review 5496551022, at 8815d84. Each is in its own commit.
Required (clouds.c3:222, sky.md:450), 3b1a753. The claim held only for a high sun. I took option B.
At or above the layer's bottom, the map centres on the point the datum under the camera reaches along the sun: lean = bottom / max(sun_up, 0.1). The ground under the camera is then at the centre at any sun.
Between the datum and the bottom, nothing changed.
The comment and the sky.md Placement bullet describe both cases exactly. They also say:
the window steps when the camera crosses the bottom;
a receiver inside both windows reads the same value, because the content is world-anchored and texel-snapped;
the limit for a camera below the bottom: the ground under it sits a · |sun.xz| / max(sun_up, 0.1) off the centre, about 6.9 km for a = 1,400 m at 11.5°.

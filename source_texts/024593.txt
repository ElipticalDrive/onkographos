Follow-up from #598 (comment by @fwilliams: #598 (comment)).
The current volume_rendering.md tutorial is quite complex — it includes a full NeRF training pipeline with spherical harmonics, dataset loading, TV regularization, grid refinement, and polyscope visualization. This makes it hard for users to focus on the fVDB volume rendering API itself.
Request: Create a simpler volume rendering tutorial that:
Focuses on demonstrating fVDB's uniform_ray_samples() and volume_render() APIs
Uses a minimal, self-contained example (e.g. rendering a simple procedural volume or pre-trained field)
Does not depend on nerfacc or external datasets
Is short enough to follow in one sitting
The existing tutorial can be retained as an advanced example or removed once the simpler one is in place.

Part of #676.
Overview
A two-day, throwaway spike to find out whether a Gaussian-splat Earth (Spark) clearly looks better than the textured NASA Earth, and what it costs. The output is a written verdict and numbers, not shippable code.
User story
As Anthony, I want to know by measurement whether splats are worth it, so we only add the dependency if the Earth really looks better.
Context
The requester goal is the best-looking Earth, not the splat technique for its own sake. Refinement made its Spark claims without web access, so they are unverified.
Acceptance criteria
Timeboxed to two days, on a throwaway branch that is never merged. The verdict is posted as a comment on this issue.
The comment compares a splat Earth (option B in Photo-real Earth home world at the centre of the Orrery #676) and the textured Earth side by side at real zoom (default Fit and maximum zoom), with screenshots.
It records: frame rate at roughly 5k, 30k and 150k splats on a desktop and a real phone; the Spark chunk size; the cost of building splats in the browser; and idle-spin sorting cost with motion off.
It records whether Spark builds in the Angular esbuild setup with three 0.186 (peer range), and its ready event.
It records whether the terminator can be done with a "night veil" sphere or needs GPU modifiers, and how colour, bloom, ACES, depth and compileAsync behave.
It ends with a clear go/no-go on the Spark upgrade, naming the win that justifies it.
Alternatives considered
Chosen: a short measured spike. Rejected: committing to Spark from the start (unverified assumptions, adds a dependency), or never testing it (the requester asked to be shown the best-looking option).
Technical notes
Run it after #677 lands so the comparison is against the real textured Earth. Do not merge the spike branch.
Out of scope
Shipping anything. The offline .spz option (A) unless the spike finds B unworkable.
Open questions
Reference phone and frame-rate floor for the comparison.

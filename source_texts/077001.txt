The SPZ decoding we currently have in Cesium Native was written before the KHR_gaussian_splatting standard was entirely finalized. This means there are currently a few issues in how we're decoding SPZs:
Transformations applied to scale and alpha should be done in the runtimes.
COLOR_0 should have the transformation from degree 0 spherical harmonic to color coordinate applied, but we should also be storing the degree 0 spherical harmonic in KHR_gaussian_splatting:SH_DEGREE_0_COEF_0.
We should fix these to better fit the standard, as well as to ease along implementing splatting in new runtimes.

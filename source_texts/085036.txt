GPU meshing is an experimental alternative, not a prerequisite for the per-chunk CPU worker architecture. Prototype generation of exposed-face geometry on the GPU with bounded buffers and an explicit overflow strategy. Acceptance: parity with CPU meshes for seams/materials/edits, capability fallback, no synchronous readback on the frame path, and measurement of GPU contention, memory, update latency and frame tails on representative hardware. Document whether the added complexity is justified before adopting it.

World ambient occlusion has been measured only on desktop GPUs (Radeon 8060S, RTX 4090) and once on an Apple M2. On the busiest scene (b30's beach), the Radeon costs 0.40 ms a frame against a 0.35 ms target, and the 4090 0.16 ms. It's opt-in for now (display.ambient_occlusion off by default, "world" a choice).
Before "world" can be the default (and the screen-space AO, slow on Macs and with #588's stripes, retired):
Measure world AO's GPU cost per frame on a Steam Deck, an Android phone, an iPhone and an M-series Mac, on b30's beach, Blood Gulch and an interior.
Measure its bake time and its effect on frame times during the bake (see the issue on the bake's cost on the M2).
Check the look on each, in AO-only and normal views.
Check memory: the cache is about 47 MB for the stock maps, plus the GPU atlas.

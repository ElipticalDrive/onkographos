## 2026-10-14 Single-volume NIfTI reader

The Nifti module now registers `NiftiVolumeReader` in `<modules/nifti/niftireader.h>`, adding direct `Volume` loading for `*.nii`, `*.hdr`, `*.img`, and their gzipped variants. This makes regular NIfTI files available through APIs and processors expecting a `DataReaderType<Volume>`, while `NiftiReader` continues to provide `VolumeSequence` loading for time series and other multi-volume datasets.

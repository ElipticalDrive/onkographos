Summary
Extends the merged UniLab ray facade with the parkour-facing ray-interface pieces that can be owned downstream:
Replaces the single implicit ring fields with public RayRingPatternCfg, RayGridPatternCfg, and RayCameraPatternCfg.
Adds body, yaw, and world attachment alignment. yaw preserves a projected horizontal frame; world ignores attachment rotation.
Supports per-ray grid origins, inclusive grid sampling, parallel grid directions, and explicit pinhole width/height/fovy/forward/up ray generation.
Adds optional caster-backed hit-point output. Flattened output order is [distance, normal?, hit_point?].
Keeps geom/tag/body/ancestor filtering, inclusive min_distance, public caster negotiation, and fail-closed unsupported capabilities unchanged.
Adds focused tests for pattern validation, grid/yaw transform, world alignment, normalized camera rays, hit-point packing, and the real MuJoCo/UniSim/uni_ray path.
Links the remaining backend-boundary work to Public ray interfaces for per-row geometry variants and site frames unilabsim/unisim#479: per-row geometry variants and site frames cannot be implemented correctly downstream.
No camera image postprocessing, latency/noise policy, terrain curriculum, or learning algorithm is added; those remain downstream/task owners.

In view of the need of the 3D seismic data volume display,the paper has some improvement on the base of analyzing the 3D visualization method and technique of the direct volume rendering in addition to the characteristics of the 3D seismic data.The paper realize the 3D seismic data volume's visualization methods and software,and by the display of the characteristics of seismic wave amplitude,the 3D seismic amplitude space distribution of the variation of intensity has been revealed.

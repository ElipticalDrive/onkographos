What to build
The ray vs triangle distance query, against a single triangle and against four triangles at once (structure-of-arrays, using Vector4 lanes). The four-wide form returns per-lane distances, so a caller can take the closest hit. Moonbounce implements the test from the literature (for example Möller–Trumbore), not from Jolt's code. It must agree with Jolt on edges, back faces and degenerate triangles.
Row in #33: "Ray vs sphere, capsule, cylinder, triangle (1 and 4 at a time)".
Acceptance criteria
Moonbounce tests: hit inside, miss outside, hits exactly on an edge and on a vertex, back-face hits, a ray parallel to the triangle, and a degenerate (zero-area) triangle
Moonbounce tests confirm the four-wide query matches four single-triangle queries lane for lane, including misses
Benchmarks RayTriangle.distance and RayTriangle4.distance run in the Primitives target, each with a same-named Jolt counterpart in the comparison harness
The comparison script shows each even (within 5%) or faster than Jolt on macOS, in wall time and retired instructions
0 mallocs per measured iteration
Types and API follow the naming and hot-path rules in CONTRIBUTING.md; benchmark names follow the chosen type names, and Phase 1: Math and geometry #33 is updated if they differ

# phone-gs-live

Free, local **phone camera → PC → phone screen** loop for **building** Gaussian-splat work (interiors, facades, roof sections).

**Your PC (verified):** RTX 3070 Ti 8 GB · Ryzen 5 3600 · 32 GB RAM — adequate for building-scale offline splats and room-scale live experiments.

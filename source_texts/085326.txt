Perhaps help us expand it? Notes: Faces have a winding order : Clockwise Counter-Clockwise Faces have a surface normal . Cameras have a view direction . The dot-product of a surface-normal and view-direction indicates if a face is facing the camera, or not. GPU rasterizers have built-in back-face culling. Vertices (and thus faces) can be discarded , by setting them to NaN in the vertex shader.

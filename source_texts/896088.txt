The amount of light scattered by the atmosphere does not happen uniformly as the angle of the sun changes. Although the physics of this phenomenon are varied and complex, generally, scattering is lessened when the sun is perpendicular to the observer's line of sight, and increased when it is behind or in front of the observer.
Different types of light scattering follow different phase equations.
Currently, only Rayleigh scattering is implemented in iTwin.js, but if #5350 is added, a separate phase function will also need to be added.

Blazingly Fast Greedy Mesher - Voxel Engine Optimizations
From youtube.com•Jan 19•23m watch time
A deep technical dive into optimizing greedy meshing algorithms for voxel engines using binary representations and bitwise operations. The author demonstrates how they achieved a 40x performance improvement over their initial implementation by reducing voxel sampling from 1.1 million to 39,000 operations per chunk mesh, making their greedy mesher faster than a traditional culled mesher. The optimization involves converting voxel data into binary grids, using bit manipulation for face culling, and calculating quad dimensions through trailing zero bit counting instead of iterative loops.

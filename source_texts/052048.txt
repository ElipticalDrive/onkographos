Roadmap item 18 (Wave D): volumetric light. A half-resolution ray-march pass, the approach Frostbite and UE4 used before froxels, built on the shadow maps the fork already has.
What it does
r_realtimeLighting 2 only. A post pass at ¼ × ¼ of the view runs after the MSAA resolve and before bloom and the tone map. It marches the ray from the eye to the depth buffer and adds the light the air scatters toward the eye:
Sun: 16 steps, each sampled in the finest of the four cascades that holds it, so shafts through windows and doors and between trees carry the cascades' shadows. The march stops at r_volumetricRange (3000 units).
Every lamp lit this frame:
Only the segment of the ray inside the lamp's reach is marched (ray-sphere test).
4 steps, spread by the angle at the lamp (equi-angular sampling, Kulla & Fajardo 2012), so the 1/d² falloff cancels against the sampling density.
The same shade, cone and fade terms RtLightParticle uses, plus one tap of the lamp's cube shadow.
Lamps whose upper-bound contribution (π/h of the lamp for a ray passing h away) is under the visible threshold are skipped.
Scattering and noise: Henyey-Greenstein forward scattering (g 0.5). Per-pixel interleaved-gradient jitter, evened out by the existing depth-aware blur.
Cvars
r_volumetric: default 1, latched.
Live (density 0 skips the pass):
r_volumetricDensity 1
r_volumetricAnisotropy 0.5
r_volumetricSun 1
r_volumetricLamps 1
r_volumetricRange 3000
New GPU timer: volume.

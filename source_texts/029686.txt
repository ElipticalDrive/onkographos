Dataset description
This archive contains a volumetric fluorescence imaging series and an accompanying preview video derived from the same acquisition. Both files are temporally synchronized and share a common time axis.
1. [Euglena_3D.tif] — 4D volumetric image stack
A four-dimensional array storing the full imaging series in axis order (t, z, y, x):
t : time axis, one index per acquired volume
z: axial planes within each volume
y, x: lateral image plane
Volumes were acquired at a rate of 24 Hz.
2. [Euglena_3D.avi] — Mean-projection preview video
A video in which each frame is the mean intensity projection along the z-axis of the corresponding volume, generating a 2D video video with 24 Hz frame rate.

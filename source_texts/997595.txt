fc-tone is 1 − exp(−Exposure·L) and then a 2.2 power. Options:
a filmic curve (for example ACES-fitted or AgX-like) with a toe and shoulder, so highlights on snow roll off and shadows keep colour;
the exact sRGB transfer function instead of the 2.2 power;
grading presets through fc-landscape-grade: warm or cool, lifted blacks, split toning, vignette.
Done when: a side-by-side of the current and filmic curves on both pieces, judged by blind pairwise, and presets documented.

# 2D Gaussian Reconstruction: A Differentiable Rasterization Study

![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/python-3.11+-blue.svg)
![PyTorch](https://img.shields.io/badge/pytorch-2.0+-ee4c2c.svg)

A deep-dive project into the core mechanics of **3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS)** by stripping away camera complexities and focusing on pure parameter optimization in 2D space.

---

## Project Objective

This project was conceived primarily as a personal learning exercise to demystify the core principles of differentiable rendering. Rather than aiming for a flawless high-resolution reconstruction or relying on high-level libraries, the objective was to implement a manual optimization pipeline from the ground up. 

By fitting 500–2,000 2D Gaussians to a target image via gradient descent, I sought to gain a firsthand understanding of how spatial parameters, scales, and color values are learned. This 'from-scratch' approach served as a practical deep dive into the underlying mechanics of Gaussian Splatting, prioritizing structural insight over final image fidelity.

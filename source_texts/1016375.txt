Dithered soft / approximate-area shadows for point lights
A small follow-up on the merged point-shadow system (#2 + your stages 1–8): point lights get an optional soft penumbra, so a torch reads as a small area source rather than an idealized point. Hard shadows stay the default-feeling look; softness is opt-in per light.
What it does
PointLight gains a radius (source size, world units). 0 = hard point (unchanged); larger softens the shadow.
The shader samples the distance cube over a Vogel disk scaled by the radius, rotated per fragment by a world-space hash — so the dither is view-stable (the penumbra doesn't crawl when you move) and reads clean against the voxel blockiness. No denoise/TAA pass needed.
Wider source → wider penumbra (the approximate-area-light look).
Why it slots in cleanly
Sampling-only. Radius never touches the rendered cube, so your (key, epoch) cube cache is completely untouched — softening is just extra taps in the main pass.
Rides the spare pt_misc.w uniform slot; no new buffers/bindings.
Real emitters and dynamic (held / mob) lights both get it; the stained-glass transmission tint is unchanged.
Tuning
The penumbra is tight and controllable — the offset is a world-space radius at the receiver (angular spread ≈ radius/distance), so small values give a soft-but-crisp edge and anything past ~1 washes out. Default is 0.15 (apt for a torch flame); WILDFORGE_LIGHT_RADIUS overrides it (0 = hard) for comparison. Screenshots of the doorway demo (WILDFORGE_DEMO_CORNER) at 0 vs 0.15 show the beam edge going from razor to feathered.
Could become a per-block-def field later (a torch vs a big glowing panel wanting different source sizes) — left as a single global default for now to keep this focused.
125 tests pass.

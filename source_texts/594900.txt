What
Exploring /moonbase was hard because the camera could only orbit the point it was aimed at (TrackballControls with noPan). Now you can move it freely around the base.
WASD / arrow keys slide the camera and its pivot across the ground. W goes where you're looking, flattened onto the local horizontal so it never dives into the regolith, and the pivot keeps its height above the terrain it passes over. Speed scales with the eye-to-pivot distance: slow up close, quick over the whole ridge. Hold Shift to go 3× faster.
Right-drag pans the view (two-finger drag on touch). Left-drag still rotates and scroll still zooms.
The Moon Base Zero card shows a one-line controls hint, with a separate version for touch screens.
Controls swap
The first version kept TrackballControls and only removed noPan. In use it was janky: the view tumbled and rolled freely, and the pan moved in screen space, so dragging up or down on a tilted view climbed or dropped the camera and looked like a sudden huge zoom-out. It's now drei's OrbitControls in map mode:
screenSpacePanning={false}: right-drag slides across the ground plane at constant altitude.
zoomToCursor: the wheel zooms toward whatever is under the pointer.
The horizon stays level. The camera is created with the base's local up and keeps it: camera flights no longer roll it, and keyboard travel no longer rewrites it. Over the 16 km patch the true radial up only drifts by about 0.5°.
maxPolarAngle stops the tilt at 88°, just above the horizon, so the camera can't swing under it. The hero framings sit around 75°. The sky and underground cutaway views are exempt, since both look upward on purpose.
Damping (0.12) replaces Trackball's dynamic damping, and the distance-adaptive rotate and zoom speeds are retuned to OrbitControls' scale.
Details
Held movement keys count as user interaction. A camera flight in progress gives way at once, and the frame budget runs at full rate while you travel.
The zoom-out recentering, which glides the pivot back to the ridge centre at high altitude, is paused while a movement key is held so the two don't fight.
Typing in an input, textarea or select never moves the camera, and Cmd/Ctrl/Alt combinations pass through. Held keys are cleared when the window loses focus.
Only a left click on empty ground closes the open panel now, so a right-click or right-drag pan never closes it.
The camera's existing terrain floor still applies, so you can't drive under the surface.
Checks
ESLint and Prettier are clean, and tsc reports nothing in MoonGlobe.tsx or pages/moonbase/index.tsx.
In the browser: W moved the view forward over the base, S brought it back, and right-drag panned, with no page errors. The first speed setting (0.6) overshot the whole base in 2.5 s, so it's tuned down to 0.2.

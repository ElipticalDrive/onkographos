Owner
Reworked the core engine architecture to implement the conscious voxel engine (VOXELCRAI) with a custom winit/wgpu runtime and an AI core.
This PR replaces the previous bevy_ecs and eframe/egui based architecture with a custom winit/wgpu event loop and rendering pipeline. The ConsciousnessCore module is introduced, allowing the engine to observe WorldMetrics and perform actions like igniting/calming voxel clusters, toggling trauma mode, and seeding concepts. The VoxelWorld has been refactored into a self-contained data engine that feeds both the renderer and the conscious layer.
Note
Replaces the old ECS/UI stack with the VOXELCRAI conscious voxel engine on winit/wgpu, adding a new instanced renderer and AI core while dropping bevy_ecs/egui and point-cloud paths.
Runtime/Engine:
New src/main.rs winit event loop with surface reconfigure/error handling and per-frame updates.
Introduces ConsciousnessCore (src/consciousness/*) and integrates pulses into runtime via ArchGuardLite.
Rendering:
Instanced cube pipeline (vertex/index buffers, depth, uniform Globals) and WGSL shader embedded in main.rs.
Depth texture management and sRGB surface config.
Voxel & Lighting:
New voxel module (types, memory) with Voxel9k, half-float F16, and VoxelMemory sampling.
New lighting/pattern_lighting.rs with 1000‑byte LightPattern and LightPatternBank.
ArchGuard:
Replaced Prometheus/circuit-breaker system with lightweight ArchGuardLite pulse log and HTML-escape safety.
Tooling/Deps:
Update Cargo.toml: add wgpu 0.20, winit 0.30, bytemuck, glam, anyhow, log; remove bevy_ecs, egui/eframe, point-cloud stack.
build.rs watches src/shaders/voxel.wgsl; scripts updated for voxelcrai binary.
Docs:
Overhauled README.md, ARCHITECTURE.md, PROJECT_STRUCTURE.md, QUICKSTART.md, and CHANGELOG.md to VOXELCRAI.
Removed:
Legacy ecs.rs, evolution.rs, lighting.rs, renderer.rs, ui.rs, and shaders/point_cloud.wgsl.

Bug: Cube vertices share normals across different faces
The cube mesh defines only 8 vertices with normals pointing in only 2 directions (front/back faces), but the indices reuse these vertices for all 6 faces. Vertices 0-3 have normal [0,0,1] and vertices 4-7 have normal [0,0,-1], yet they're indexed to form top, bottom, left, and right faces which need different normals. This causes incorrect lighting on 4 of the 6 cube faces since the normals don't match the actual face orientation.

Bug: HTML safety check applied after escaping
The record_pulse method calls escape_html first, then checks is_safe on the escaped string. Since HTML entities like &amp; and &lt; are ASCII graphic characters, the safety check always passes after escaping, making it ineffective. The safety validation should occur before escaping to actually filter unsafe input, or the check serves no purpose.

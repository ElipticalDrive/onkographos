Summary
Adds a geometry layer on the existing Domain: plane, sphere, circle, ellipse, and rotated ellipsoid. Negative is inside. A plane, sphere, and circle are signed distances; the ellipsoid and ellipse polynomials are not.
Classifies interior, exterior, and cut cells from the exact range of φ. Equal corner signs are not treated as proof that a curved interface misses a cell. Positive slivers are kept.
Plane cuts match an analytic clip. Curved volumes are a dyadic approximation of the linear interpolant of φ, checked against the analytic body when it lies inside one cell and against the whole-sphere volume on a refined grid.
Device kernels call those same functions. On one H100 the mask and cut volumes matched the host (cells 343, cut 34).
Closes #434
Closes #435
The two issues share one integrator and one device translation unit, so they are two commits on this branch rather than two pull requests that would not compile apart.

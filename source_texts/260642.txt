I choose 3D ▸ Export quantitative data for surface-mode objects at the default XY resolution. A square 10 voxels wide traced on five sections comes out at 1.18 times its traced volume (area times thickness), 20 wide at 1.09, 40 wide at 1.045. My test shapes run 1.03 to 1.17. In the 3D scene the surface sits about half a voxel outside the traces.
Surface.generateTrimesh in PyReconstruct/modules/backend/volume/objects_3D.py rounds trace points onto voxel centers, and skimage's polygon counts a center on the outline as inside, so a 10 by 10 square fills 11 by 11 voxels. Marching cubes then puts the surface half a voxel past them.

I have a fix in #651, but I'm holding it. It counts a voxel only when its center is inside the trace. That fixes shapes 2 or more voxels across: a box 10 voxels wide goes from 1.18 to 0.97 of its volume. But at the default XY resolution, traces under about 1.5 section thicknesses wide break apart or read near zero.
Another option is to compute volume from trace area times section thickness. Surface area would still come from the mesh.

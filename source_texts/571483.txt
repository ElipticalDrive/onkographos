# UV-Textured Surfel Rasterization

This is the rasterization engine for the paper [GTAvatar: Bridging Gaussian Splatting and Texture Mapping for Relightable and Editable Gaussian Avatars](https://kelianb.github.io/GTAvatar/).

This work is an extension of the [2D Gaussian Splatting Rasterizer](https://github.com/hbb1/diff-surfel-rasterization) that adds texture support. Contrary to other textured variants of 2DGS that use per-primitive textures, we sample from global textures. Each Gaussian is parameterized by a central UV `uv0` and a 2x2 Jacobian `uvJ` that describe how the splat's tangent plane maps to UV space. Gradients are backpropagated to the textures, `uv0` and `uvJ`, along with all the standard Gaussian attributes. We also support normal maps.

For an example of using this renderer, see [this code](https://github.com/KelianB/GTAvatar/blob/main/avatar/rendering/gaussian_renderer_2dgs_textured.py).

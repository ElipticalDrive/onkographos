Neural radiance fields have demonstrated powerful capabilities in 3D scene reconstruction and view synthesis, but traditional Multi-Layer Perceptrons often face certain expressive limitations when dealing with high-frequency details and complex geometrical structures, resulting in unclear rendered images and view synthesized images. Inspired by the Kolmogorov-Arnold networks, the ability of the network to fit complex functions is enhanced by a nonlinear activation functions, which demonstrates a strong advantage. Meanwhile, to improve the computational efficiency, We use Gaussian Radial Basis Functions instead of FastKAN with B-splines and embed it into the orig-inal network framework to form a neural network with both local and global features, which improves the interpretability of the network and also achieves the performance improvement on the NeRF -synthetic dataset and NeRF-LLFF dataset.

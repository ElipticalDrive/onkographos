A Volterra integral equation, which relates the Fourier coefficients of the projection (in polar coordinates) with the corresponding coefficients of the unknown density function, is deduced. The same equation holds for both divergent and parallel beam projections. The problem is shown to be “ill-conditioned.” A numerical solution, based on the recursive evaluation of certain integrals, is proposed and a Tikhonov regularization procedure is applied to the discrete problem. Numerical examples are also reported.

Summary
octree_mesh is a new operation that meshes the inside of a skin on a 2:1-balanced linear octree. The skin can be any surface, closed or slightly dirty. The existing octree operation and its 2-D quadtree are unchanged, pinned by new golden digests. Plan: doc/octree_mesh_3d_plan.md; contract: doc/octree_mesh.md.
Outputs
output="tet" (the default): conforming graded tetrahedra.
The tree is tetrahedralised as a graded BCC lattice (six shapes, 35.26°–125.26°) and cut to the skin by isosurface stuffing (Labelle & Shewchuk 2007).
Sharp corners and edge chains are snapped onto the boundary.
The mesh is then optimised (ODT relocation, sliding along the skin, flips) under a guard that never lowers the smallest dihedral angle or raises the largest.
skin_grading="uniform" gives the 10.7°–164.8° bound, and the tests assert it.
conformity="cleaving" meshes nested shells as conforming materials.
output="castellated": the inside leaves as hexahedra.
Regions inside, boundary_cells and surrogate_boundary, plus sdf:distance and sdf:closest_point, for embedded and Shifted Boundary Method solvers.
The surrogate faces are whole cell faces and enclose the inside volume.
output="hex": hexahedra only.
A grid at the skin size, repaired until every vertex's 2×2×2 block is face-connected inside and out.
Pillowed along the boundary, snapped to the skin's corners and edges, and untangled on the corner scaled Jacobian.
The grid is uniform: carrying the octree's grading into the hexahedra (the Livesu et al. 2022 dual) remains roadmap item 3.1.4.3.
Inside test, size field and report
Inside and outside come from the fast winding number (Barill et al. 2018).
The size field combines curvature, features, gaps and a user array.
The report holds:
the angles and the scaled Jacobian;
the Hausdorff distance to the skin and the boundary's manifoldness;
feature recovery;
the quality histograms;
per-stage timings;
every warning. Nothing that misses its target is silent.
Every surface
C++ (operations/octree_mesh.hpp) and Python.
The C API: mio_octree_mesh, the 264- and 392-byte structs with reserved tails, and mio_octree_mesh_warnings.
Fortran, Julia, R and WASM (octreeMesh).
Both CLIs' octree-mesh, the pipeline step OctreeMesh and the MCP tool.
Notebook example/python/41_octree_mesh.ipynb.
Bench rows: octree, octree_mesh_castellated, octree_mesh_tet, octree_mesh_hex.
Release 17.12.0
Version: the ten version files, the find_package pins and the single header; the CHANGELOG is dated.
IO baseline: the four BASELINE_HASHES are refreshed. The old hashes were reproduced first by writing v17.11.0 back into the new bytes.
fit_cad goldens: the native STEP digests (Python and C++) are re-recorded the same way. Every old digest reproduces exactly, the mesh and report digests are unchanged, and the OpenCASCADE rows are untouched.
Spack follow-up (after the tag, outside this repo)
In spack/spack-packages, for meshioplusplus and py-meshioplusplus:
Add version("17.12.0", sha256=...) to each, using spack checksum.
Re-check every when="@X:" guard and every depends_on floor against this CMakeLists.txt and pyproject.toml. No new mandatory dependency was added.
Run spack style, then open the PR.
Known limitations (reported, and in roadmap §3.1.4.3)
The hexahedral output is not graded.
A cylinder's rims are reported as missed feature chains at a small min_size.
Winding numbers of lattice vertices away from the skin dominate the time of a uniformly fine mesh. Even so, 1.9M tets take 39 s on 4 threads.

octree_mesh phase 1: linear octree core, and octree golden digests

A core-private linear octree (src/cpp/src/detail/linear_octree.*) for the
coming octree_mesh: leaves as sorted 63-bit Morton keys of their lo
corners plus a level, over a forest of cubic roots. Locating the leaf of
a finest cell is one upper_bound; split, require (refine a leaf down the
path to the octants it must expose), dilate (rings of leaves at a seed's
level) and 2:1 balance across faces, edges or corners, finest level
first by required parent-neighbour octants (Sundar, Sampath and Biros
2008; p4est). The minimal balanced refinement is unique, so the result
does not depend on the order of the work; every sort is a total order.

Tests (tests/cpp/test_linear_octree.cpp): key round trips; invariants
(ascending keys, aligned leaves inside the forest, no overlap, full
coverage) after every operation; balance equal to the naive "split any
leaf two levels coarser than a neighbour" fixed point on 12 seeded
random trees for each adjacency, idempotent and refinement-only;
neighbour queries equal a brute force; the same tree on 1, 2 and 4
threads; and balancing octree()'s unbalanced tree reproduces octree()'s
own face- and full-balanced trees leaf for leaf.

tests/cpp/test_octree_goldens.cpp pins octree's output (3-D and 2-D:
every balance, transition and fill, the winding sign, snapping, the
refinement criteria, forests, padding, even-odd and planes) before
octree_mesh lands beside it. octree.cpp is untouched; the digests were
taken from it as it is on master.

A new operation, octree_mesh (operations/octree_mesh.hpp, a new installed
header: additive), that meshes the inside of a skin on a balanced linear
octree. This phase ships its castellated output; output="tet" and "hex"
raise Unsupported by name until their phases land.

- detail/tri_bvh: a BVH over Morton-sorted triangle centroids split at the
  median count (its shape a function of the triangle count), the fast
  generalised winding number of Barill et al. 2018 (dipole, first and
  second moments; ~2e-3 at beta = 2, re-evaluated near the threshold) and
  Moller-Trumbore ray casts with ties to the lower triangle id.
- detail/octree_size_field: the welded skin with normals and sharp /
  non-manifold edges; the size field min(h_max, curvature chord,
  feature size, thickness / gap_cells, size_array) clamped to
  [min_size, max_size]; the root frame (L = ceil(log2(h_max/h_min)),
  roots centred on the padded box); the top-down build with exact
  triangle-box candidate culling.
- octree_mesh: cut leaves dilated by grading_layers (two rings and three
  skin sizes of padding under skin_grading="uniform"), 2:1 balance across
  faces, edges and corners; castellated leaves (fill inside / cover /
  all) as hexahedra with the inside and boundary_cells cell regions, the
  surrogate_boundary side region (inside leaves split until every exposed
  face is a whole face), octree:hanging, sdf:distance and
  sdf:closest_point; a report with the frame, counts, quality histograms,
  timings and warnings. A skin wound inside out is negated with a warning.
- Python mp.octree_mesh (core only), the octree-mesh verb of both CLIs
  (--json without the timings), the OctreeMesh pipeline step in both
  engines and the octree_mesh MCP tool (122 tools).

Tests: C++ (winding against the exact sum on spheres, a torus, holes,
overlaps and inversion; rays against a brute force; the frame, welding,
features and curvature/gap sizes; refinement only adds leaves; the ball's
volume converges and the surrogate faces enclose it; no inside cell
across a surrogate face; hanging nodes and distances against brute
forces; dirty skins; thread-count determinism; refusals) and Python
(refusals, report, CLI round trip, both pipeline engines agreeing, MCP,
CLI JSON parity). OctreeMeshOptions is pinned at 336 bytes.

octree_mesh phase 3: conforming graded BCC tetrahedra on the octree
…
7f5757a
```
detail/bcc_octree_tets tetrahedralises a 2:1-balanced (faces, edges and
corners) linear octree on the doubled integer lattice, so every vertex
is exact: each leaf is a cone from its centre over fans of its face
portions (a boundary face or one shared with a coarser leaf is one
portion, a face shared with four finer leaves four), each portion fanned
from its centre with its edges cut where a leaf corner lies at their
midpoints; across a face between two leaves of the same size the two
cones are replaced by the BCC tetrahedra around the segment between the
centres. A portion's fan depends only on the portion and the vertices on
its boundary, so both sides of every face agree. Six shapes occur
(dihedral angles 35.26-125.26 degrees; a region of equal leaves is all
BCC, 60-90), each tagged; vertices are numbered by Morton key, so the
result is a function of the tree alone. Our own construction after
Labelle and Shewchuk's BCC lattice; the angle table is derived here and
pinned by the tests.

Tests: on seeded random balanced forests the exact volumes add up to the
forest's, every interior triangle is shared by exactly two tetrahedra
with opposite orientations and every other lies on the forest's
boundary, every tetrahedron is positive, no vertex lies inside an edge;
each class has the tabulated angles and all six occur; equal leaves are
T1 away from the boundary; thread-count determinism; an unbalanced tree
and the budget are refused.

octree_mesh phase 4: isosurface stuffing, cleaving and feature snapping
…
0c718fc
```
output="tet" now works: the balanced octree's graded BCC lattice is cut
to the skin and its sharp features are snapped.

- detail/isosurface_stuffing: Labelle and Shewchuk's stuffing of one
  interface through a tetrahedral mesh: cut points keyed by edge (shared
  by every tetrahedron around it), violation by alpha_long/alpha_short
  per edge class, warping in ascending vertex order with an inversion
  guard, and stencils for every sign pattern on both sides; lattice-face
  quadrilaterals take the diagonal through their lower vertex and
  interface quadrilaterals their shorter diagonal, so both sides of every
  face agree. Built from the paper only.
- Cut points come from the winding-number tree: the first crossing from
  the inside end that takes the winding number below the threshold
  (detail/tri_bvh's new segment query), else bisection (a hole). The
  lattice's own boundary is pinned, so keep_outside fills the root
  lattice exactly.
- conformity="cleaving" stuffs each winding level of nested shells in
  turn with the earlier interfaces held fixed: conforming materials,
  as octree_mesh:material and material_<k> regions.
- detail/feature_snap: the feature graph (corners where the sharp edges
  branch, end or turn; chains between), corner snapping (exact) and
  chain tracing by a tube-restricted shortest boundary path, every move
  guarded against inversion and new slivers; misses are counted and
  warned about. feature_corners / feature_edges point regions.
- Report: num_cut_points, num_warped, num_warp_refused, the feature
  counts (and num_feature_moves), guarantee_applies; the boundary side
  region, octree:level, octree_mesh:shape and sdf:distance.

Tests: random planes through random graded trees are cut exactly (both
sides positive, conforming, the inside volume the clipped box's); a
uniform lattice stays within 10.7-164.8 degrees on random spheres; the
octree_mesh tetrahedra of a sphere and a torus keep that bound with a
closed 2-manifold boundary and the enclosed volume; a cube keeps its 8
corners exactly and its 12 edges; nested spheres give two conforming
materials; keep_outside fills the lattice; holed and graded skins give
valid meshes; thread-count determinism; Python, CLI JSON and pipelines.

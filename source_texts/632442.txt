Description
Introduce a Cinematic Orbit Camera Mode that provides multiple dynamic camera perspectives for tracking satellites in a more immersive and visually engaging way. Instead of relying on a single fixed camera, users can seamlessly switch between different viewing modes, making the simulation feel more like a professional space visualization or mission control experience. Camera movements and transitions should be smooth, natural, and animated to avoid abrupt changes in perspective.
Features
Chase View – Position the camera behind the satellite, following its direction of travel for a realistic pursuit effect.
Cockpit View – Simulate a first-person perspective from the satellite, allowing users to experience its journey through space.
Earth Observer View – Keep the camera focused on Earth while tracking the satellite as it moves across the planet's surface.
Orbital View – Display the satellite along with its complete orbit from a wider angle, helping users better understand its trajectory.
Free Fly Mode – Allow users to freely navigate the 3D scene using manual camera controls while keeping the satellite accessible for quick refocusing.
Smooth Camera Transitions – Animate movement, rotation, and zoom when switching between camera modes to provide a polished and cinematic viewing experience.
Automatic Satellite Tracking – Ensure the active camera continuously follows the selected satellite while maintaining the chosen viewing perspective.

Regions whose shape comes from the flow, re-evaluated as it evolves, usable wherever regions are (#499): probes, snapshots (#542), sources, sponges, statistics.
Condition regions (volume): {type = "where", condition = "T > 1500"} or any expression of registered fields and x, y, z, t (kexpr, Device expressions everywhere, drop exprtk; geometry independent of expressions #449, on the device); cell fractions from the condition's sub-cell reconstruction (smooth, not a 0/1 mask), so integrals converge under refinement. Examples: heat release in the flame zone, mass of burnt gas, volume of recirculation (U_X < 0).
Isosurfaces (surface; 2D: isoline): {type = "isosurface", field = "T", value = 1500} extracted on the mesh from the reconstruction at each evaluation; flux through it, area (flame surface area), and probes and snapshots on it. Normals point toward increasing field (documented, flip = true to reverse), consistent with Regions everywhere: probe regions, sponge regions, probe surfaces on the shared shapes #499's orientation rules.
Booleans with static regions (the flame zone inside a box), named in [regions], and boundary/interior (the boundary of a condition region is its isosurface).
Re-evaluated only when a consumer needs it (at its sampling interval), and costs are reported in the run health timing of output.
Reacting-flow analysis: conditional statistics, PDFs, flame surfaces #513's flame-surface extraction and conditional quantities become uses of this, not a separate implementation.
Tests: the isosurface area of a known field (sphere, plane) converges to the exact value; a condition region's volume equals the analytic one; flux through an isosurface of a steady field matches the divergence theorem; serial vs MPI identical.

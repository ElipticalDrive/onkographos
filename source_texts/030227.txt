With the rapid development of computer technology,as a completely new 3D image display technique,True 3D volumetric display techniques will lead science visualization to a new development direction and have a wide area of applications in the future.Imaging theory and motional types of swept-volume display systems of translational motion were studied.Particularly,a display unit in which screen moved with a sinusoidal velocity profile was studied.By using linear approximation,the homogeneous was graphic obtained and worst-case positional error due to linear approximation was analyzed in the system.The system could realize true 3D effect.

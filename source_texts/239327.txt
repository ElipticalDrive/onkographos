Outcome
Render meshes are divided into meshlets with bounds and normal cones, culled on the GPU before rasterisation.
Acceptance criteria
Meshlets carry a bounding sphere, a normal cone, an LOD error, and a material range
Compute culling plus indirect indexed drawing is the baseline path on every backend
Task and mesh shaders are used as a fast path where available, producing an identical image
Meshlet counts before and after culling appear in the HUD
Notes
Meshlets are used even without mesh shaders. Treating them as a mesh-shader-only feature would leave most Android and older desktop hardware on the slow path forever.

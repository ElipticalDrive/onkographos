Summary
Two viewer toggles for HybridMeshPipeline, both off by default (today's look is unchanged):
kHybridMeshCullBackFaces culls back faces for a frame. The cull mode is dynamic state (vkCmdSetCullMode, core Vulkan 1.3), so the flag switches per frame with no pipeline rebuilt.
kHybridMeshWireframe draws every triangle's edges over the shaded mesh: a second pass in VK_POLYGON_MODE_LINE, depth-tested (LESS_OR_EQUAL, no depth write) and depth-biased toward the viewer. Edges are black over a light surface and white over a dark one; on a 4x window a one-pixel edge resolves to grey. It composes with the other flags, and culling removes back faces' edges too.
The wireframe pass needs fillModeNonSolid. create builds it only when the device enabled that feature, wireframe_supported() reports it, and elsewhere the bit is ignored. The feature stays out of device_requirements(), so a device another library made (e.g. recon's fusion device a viewer adopts) still runs every gfx type. Consumers opt in with features.fillModeNonSolid = VK_TRUE where the physical device offers it; MoltenVK (macOS and iOS), NVIDIA and lavapipe all do.
The bias grows with the surface's slope, so on a thin part seen nearly edge-on the far side's edges can show through; culling back faces hides them.
GraphicsPipelineDesc gains dynamic_cull_mode and depth_bias_constant / depth_bias_slope.
Why it is safe to cull
Marching cubes winds each triangle counter-clockwise about its outward normal. On a reconstructed person at 1 cm and 5 mm, winding and normal agree on every triangle and the signed volume is positive. The camera tier's Y flip keeps that winding counter-clockwise on screen, which is the rasterizer's front face. A new test draws an outward and an inside-out triangle through Camera::look_at_perspective to pin this chain.
Tests
tests/hybrid_mesh_pipeline_test.cpp, all with validation on:
CullBackFacesKeepsTheOutwardSide: both sides draw by default; the flag culls only the inside-out triangle.
WireframeIsIgnoredWithoutFillModeNonSolid: the base fixture's device has no wireframe pass, and the flag changes no pixel.
HybridMeshWireframeTest (a device with fillModeNonSolid; skips where it is not offered):
black edges over a light quad tilted 10° and 60°, with the surface between them unchanged;
white edges over a dark quad;
nearer geometry hides the edges behind it;
the edges still show on a 4x target, tilted 10° and 60°;
culling also removes back faces' edges;
the pass moves with the pipeline (move-construct and move-assign over a live object).
Mutants, all caught on MoltenVK: depth bias removed (the edge tests fail; on MoltenVK the fill hides edges at 0.5–30° of tilt without it), edge pass ignoring depth, cull mode never set to back, and the edge colours swapped.
Run on the M5 Max (MoltenVK, Release): ctest -E 'metal_surface|GlfwNativeWindowTest', 734/734 passed. Linux (NVIDIA and lavapipe) is covered by CI.
Follow-ups
The player and recon viewers get on-screen toggles in their own PRs, which re-pin gfx.

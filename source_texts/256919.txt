Summary
Two new modules (MCGen_MultiGPU/, Visualizer/), a new default scatter model and bug fixes in ScatterGen_RayTracing_CircularHole, and an example that compares the analytic matrix with the Monte Carlo one component by component.
ScatterGen: scatter model v2 (default; -model legacy keeps the old kernels)
First-order (single Compton) scatter, integrated over the volume where the scatter happens.
Crystal scatter:
The Compton interactions of the beam in crystal j (PE[j,v]·μ_C/μ_PE) are spread over points filling j, with Gauss–Legendre nodes across j.
Each point is weighted by the transmission of the incoming beam to it: collimator layers and holes, the crystals on the way, and j itself.
From each point, the scattered photon goes to the visible faces of detector crystal i. The faces are divided into cells of equal angle (≤ 0.1 rad) seen from the scatter point.
Each cell has an exact solid angle, Klein–Nishina angle, energy and window. The window is averaged over sub-points where the energies cross a window edge.
The photon is attenuated in j and in the crystals in between, then photo-absorbed in i. The chord through i is sub-sampled where it drops to 0 towards the face edges.
Collimator scatter:
Scatter points fill the tungsten: a thin shell around every hole, plus the rest of the plate for penetrable plates.
The plate grid (24×24) excludes holes and shells per sub-point.
Incoming and scattered photons are attenuated exactly through all layers and their holes, using the same hole grid as PEGen.
Validation in examples/analytic_vs_mc (reference: MCGen with physics = single_scatter, i.e. the same physics definition):

About 1.3 % of the 140.5 keV crystal-scatter deficit comes from the PE matrix it is normalised to (0.987 of the MC).
The 140.5 keV collimator values are within the MC noise (±4 %).
The crystal-scatter shape TVD at 140.5 keV dropped from 0.26/0.22 to 0.06/0.05.
The legacy kernels were off by factors of 0.002–24 on the same example.
Earlier v2 deficit: the previous v2 was 5 % low in wide windows. That was five larger, partly cancelling errors:
the incoming beam was assumed uniform in j
coarse face patches at the window edge near 90° (−53 %…+9 % per scatter point)
the chord at the face edges
the scatter-point grid
aliasing of the hole exclusion in the plate grid (+5.5 %)
The example ReadMe gives the details.
Independent checks:
Plate without holes: 0.1 % from a numpy reference.
Single hole: 2.12e-10, between the numpy (2.05e-10) and MC (2.20e-10 ± 6 %) references.
Single voxels: per-crystal-pair checks against MC event logs.
Cost: on 4 CPU cores this example takes 206–6947 s for crystal scatter and 1938–4806 s for collimator scatter (the previous v2 took 20–608 s and 809–1880 s; legacy ~20 s).
The GPU version compiles with 179–208 registers per thread and no spills, so 16×16 blocks fit.
Faster settings (S2_CELL_ANG, S2_XT_MIN, S2_BK_N) and their accuracy cost are documented.
ScatterGen fixes
Energy resolution of scattered photons: FWHM/E ∝ E^-1/2 (sqrt(E0/E')). It was inverted, in all three modules.
Rotations: each rotation uses its own PE matrix.
Buffer overrun: fixed the per-rotation output copy in scatter().
-components: also writes the crystal-scatter and collimator-scatter matrices separately.
Plate edges: unchanged by design. The analytic model assumes entry through the entrance window, and the MC sets the real shielding (shield_outside_collimator). This is documented.
MCGen_MultiGPU/: multi-GPU Monte Carlo photon transport (140.5 keV / 511 keV)
Reads the same Params_*.dat files and writes the same float32 [rotation][detector][voxel] layout.
Physics:
photoelectric absorption with K fluorescence
Klein–Nishina Compton scattering
Rayleigh scattering with form factors
an xraylib material database (1–1100 keV)
Gaussian energy windows
Tracking: delta tracking with exact flights through the holes.
Multi-GPU:
voxel chunks distributed over the GPUs
per-history Philox streams, so results don't depend on the GPU count
a CPU backend running the same physics code
Unbiased importance sampling: a defensive mixture of the whole assembly, the crystal block and the hole apertures. The figure of merit improves 5–10×.
Physics modes: full, primary_pe (the analytic PE definition) and single_scatter (the analytic scatter definition).
Optional outputs: components, relative errors, spectrum, tracks and a summary JSON.
Visualizer/, tools/, examples/
Visualizer/: WebGL2 element visualizer with explicit axis directions and orientation tests, plus serve.py for large matrices.
tools/:
make_demo_params.py (now with --window)
compare_sysmat.py
analytic_cpu/: the original analytic CUDA kernels compiled for the CPU, for checks without a GPU
examples/analytic_vs_mc/run_example.sh [tc99m|511] [gpu|cpu]: runs on one GPU or CPU and produces a report and detector images. Reference CPU results are committed.
Validation (CPU; this environment has no GPU)
MC vs exact integral: 1.00004 ± 0.0001.
Full physics vs an independent numpy MC: all |z| ≤ 2.1.
MC vs the analytic PE kernel: 0.987 at 140.5 keV (0.996 with a finer grid) and 0.998 at 511 keV.
Scatter model v2 vs single-scatter MC: see the table above.
Beyond first order: under full physics, scatter/primary is about 2.1× the first-order value (multiple scatter, Rayleigh, K x-rays, same-crystal interactions), so MC components are recommended when these matter.
CUDA: MCGen and ScatterGen compile and link with nvcc 13.4. Not yet run on a GPU.

```
Monte Carlo photon transport for SPECT/PET system matrices, tuned for
140.5 keV and 511 keV. Reads the same four parameter files and writes the
```

ScatterGen v2: fix absorption across far faces, hole-rim quadrature
…
515ac93
```
Checked against independent references (a numpy volume integral for a
plate without holes, a numpy next-event estimator for one hole) and the
single-scatter Monte Carlo:
- Absorption in the detector crystal and shadowing by its neighbours are
  averaged over at least S2_CHORD_N^2 entry points per face. With one
  patch per distant face the centre-ray chord overestimated absorption
  (collimator scatter at 511 keV was ~20 % high).
- Hole shell: y nodes cluster at the front and back faces (rim scatter
  comes from within ~1/mu of them) and depth nodes mix two attenuation
  scales, each node weighted by the summed densities. The old midpoint
  nodes missed the rims (one hole, 14 deg off axis: 0.45 of the reference,
  now 1.12; central voxel of the 140 keV example: 0.90 -> 0.94 of MC).
- Scatter points whose incoming weight is < 1e-5 of the largest are
  skipped (same result, ~1/3 less time).
- scatter_cpu: -skip and -holes options for validation runs.
```

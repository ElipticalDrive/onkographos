Interactive out-of-core isosurface extraction - Semantic Scholar
A novel persistent octree (POT) indexing structure for accelerating isosurface extraction and spatial filtering from volumetric data that achieves the asymptotic bound of the interval tree for identifying the active cells corresponding to an isosURface and is more efficient than BONO for handling spatial queries.

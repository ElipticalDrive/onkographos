Summary
Raise the default Adaptive point-size maximum from 10 to 32 to reduce gaps during close-up inspection.
Limit enlargement to nearby points: smoothly transition from the configured maximum at camera-space depth 0.5 to a distant cap of 6 at depth 3 and beyond. Caps are relative to a 1080-pixel drawing-buffer height; depths are world units (metres for metre-based models).
Reduce oversized distant splats caused by coarse octree spacing, particularly on railings.
Preserve Fixed and Attenuated defaults, explicit maximum-size overrides across mode changes, and the existing final minimum/maximum clamps. An explicit minimum can override the distant cap.
Retain the numeric conversion for the example sizing-mode selector so enum comparisons work when switching modes.
Implementation and scope
The distance-dependent cap adds only four lines to the existing Adaptive vertex-shader path; there is no additional render pass, render target, or per-point attribute.
Update the model documentation, material/picking unit coverage, and the Adaptive GPU visual fixture and baseline.
Local validation
15 targeted material and picking unit tests passed.
AdaptivePointSize, FixedPointCloud, and EdlPointCloud visual tests passed.
GPU assertions verify coarse-point caps of 6, 6, 19, and 32 at depths 8, 3, 1.75, and 0.5, plus explicit minimum handling and Fixed-size invariance.
Library/test TypeScript checks, targeted ESLint, example formatting, and whitespace checks passed. Library typechecking and example formatting were rechecked after restoring pointSize to 1.0.
Inspected near/far views of various point clouds, including walls, floors, pipes, and railings.
Limitations
This is a depth-based cap, not geometric edge detection or density estimation; it cannot eliminate every bulky silhouette or fill every sparse surface.
Very close views can still expose individual splats and gaps. The finite near cap balances coverage against excessive point growth.
Thresholds use camera-space depth, not Euclidean camera distance, and assume an appropriate model scale.

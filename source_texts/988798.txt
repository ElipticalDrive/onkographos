The product owner wants maps built from the ground up: shape the terrain itself, not only place things on the flat test map. (This widens the epic's first scope, which left terrain out.)
What the game has
Level terrain: level archives carry the game's terrain: blocks with height and layers. The demo reads, draws and collides with it today (viewer/src/level_scene.rs, arena.rs, bf/level.rs), read-only.
The flat test map: a fixed plane.
Acceptance
A new editable terrain for the editor:
choose its size and grid spacing;
a brush with radius and strength raises, lowers, smooths, flattens to a height, and sets a height;
undo works through Map creation: select, move, turn, copy and delete placed things, with undo #187.
Collision follows the shape as it changes: the squad walks the slopes with the game's step and slope limits; shots, grenades and missiles hit the ground; placed scenery and NPCs keep their place on it.
Drawn at a steady frame rate at a sensible size (state the limit), with normals updated for lighting.
Saved and loaded in Map creation: save and load a created map (the demo's own file) #188's map file.
Docs updated.
Out of scope
Editing a real level's own terrain in place, unless it falls out of the same code.
Painting textures: the next ticket.

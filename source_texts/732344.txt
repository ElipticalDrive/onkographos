Fixes #5
check_rotation_matrix() only verified that the upper 3x3 block was orthogonal. Orthogonal matrices with determinant -1 are reflections, which a quaternion cannot represent, so Rotation3.from_matrix() and Pose3.from_matrix4x4() silently returned a different transform (identity for diag(-1, 1, 1)).
Changes:
check_rotation_matrix() now also requires the determinant to be close to 1, using the same rtol and atol, and raises ValueError otherwise.
The error message includes the shape, the matrix, the determinant, and the caller's err_msg.
New constant rotation3.MATRIX_NOT_PROPER_ROTATION_MESSAGE.
Docstring updates in rotation3.py and pose3.py.
Tests:
rotation3_test.py: reflections (single-axis flips, -I) in 3x3 and 4x4 raise from both check_rotation_matrix and from_matrix. A separate test checks that err_msg is preserved.
pose3_test.py: a 4x4 reflection with translation raises from Pose3.from_matrix4x4, and err_msg is preserved.
Ran rotation3_test.py (330 tests) and pose3_test.py (238 tests) directly with Python and NumPy. I did not run them under Bazel.

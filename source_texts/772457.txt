When building TM octrees, the binning appears to be adding triangles to bins that don't contain them. This leads to errors during intersection where triangles are hit prematurely because they are tested in the wrong bin sequence due to octants erroneously containing them. The workaround currently is to test against the octant bounding box at time of intersection. A better solution would be to bin properly to begin with.
Needs to handle bins containing all or part of a triangle, which could just be an edge passing through.

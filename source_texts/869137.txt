Owner
Live mouse orbit for the native sail chase camera, and the same drag/zoom on the build-berth orbit.
Controls
Drag with the left, right, or middle button to orbit. Drag right swings the eye to port; drag up raises it.
Scroll zooms. Wheel up moves closer. Distance is about 13–80 m on the 12 m sloop (the near limit grows with hull length, so a 24 m hull stops around 19 m).
Pitch stays between about 3° and 69°. On the water the eye is also kept at least 1.6 m above the sea, so a close zoom will not drop into the waves.
A drag that starts off the debug panel keeps orbiting until the button is released, including if the cursor crosses the panel. Clicks and the wheel over ImGui are left to the UI. The wind vane does not take the mouse.
Walk the yard still uses its follow camera; orbit/zoom apply again when you leave it.
The berth keeps its slow auto-spin until the first drag, then holds the angle you left it at. Scroll zoom works during the auto-spin.
Sail yaw is an offset from the ship's heading (0 = astern chase). That is the bearing a later camera-direction weapon pass (side/front/rear) can read. Weapon selection is not in this change.
Flags and defaults
No new flag. With no drag and no zoom, both cameras match the old fixed poses, so existing --shot framing is unchanged:
--cam still replaces the sail camera.
--head still frames a build shot (there is no mouse in --shot).
--isolate still uses its own orbit views.
Default sail pose: eye 24 m astern at y = 9, looking 5 m ahead. Default berth pose: eye on the 24 m ring at y = 12, looking at the stocks.
Verification
g++ -std=c++17 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic self-test: 62/62 model checks and 12/12 new orbit checks (default poses, heading follow, zoom direction, pitch/distance clamps, yaw wrap).
ctest model_spine passed with SEA_TRIAL_BUILD_APP=OFF (g++).
The windowed app was not run. extern/ submodules are empty here, and sea_trial forces the D3D11 renderer, so there is no live GUI or headless --shot on this VM.

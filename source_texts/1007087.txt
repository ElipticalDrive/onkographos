For a cone-linear curve from (-3,0,4,1) to (3,0,4,1), a ray from the origin with direction (0,0,2) returns t=1.00000036 instead of 1.5 and incorrectly reports a hit and occlusion with tfar=1.25. The ray documentation allows non-normalized directions and restricts hits to the ray interval.
We include the squared direction length in the quadratic's A coefficient, reusing the value already computed for the origin shift.
The reproducer and new regression pass after the fix, and the existing unit and selected verification tests pass before and after.

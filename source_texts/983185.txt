Summary
add renderer-independent exponential depth shading with an onset plane, direction, decay length, and target color
reproduce the legacy fog-like appearance by flattening distant primitive finishes: diffuse and Phong decay as (f^2), specular as (f^3), and ambient-lit shading transitions to unscaled emission
preserve primitive alpha by default, with optional shade_alpha=True blending toward the target alpha
add optional POV-Ray constant fog for continuous camera-distance fading
warn when fog is requested below POV-Ray quality 9 while retaining quality 3 for fast previews
document both alternatives and reproduce the legacy hematite depth settings in the example and notebook
Design
Directional shading stays in the style-resolution stage and does not invalidate geometry. It uses an explicit unit direction plus decay length rather than overloading vector magnitude. Spheres, bonds, and meshes use the same finish response so distant bonds do not remain unusually prominent.
The far-depth material converges to the target pigment with emission=1 and other lighting terms suppressed. POV-Ray emission is intentionally used instead of high ambient because emission is unaffected by the scene's global ambient_light; this ensures a white depth target remains white even in the legacy scene configuration with ambient_light=0.1.
Native fog remains a scene/backend feature because POV-Ray evaluates it continuously along camera rays and it has no independent Cartesian onset.

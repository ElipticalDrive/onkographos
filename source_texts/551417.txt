Description
Replaced the previous README.md with a detailed Chinese document that defines objectives, constraints, and success criteria for floating-point dataset compression.
Added a three-layer architecture (Sample, Shard, Index) and described per-field metadata requirements such as eps_abs, eps_rel, quant_bits, and codec_family.
Enumerated compression strategies for time-series and general tensors, recommended codecs (SZ/SZ3, ZFP, FP16/BF16, INT8/INT16), and a block-level adaptive selection flow based on quick profiling metrics.
Documented index schema, quality metadata (codec_id, codec_params_digest, error_stat, task_guardrail), end-to-end publishing workflow, and a staged MVP/experiment matrix for validation and rollout.

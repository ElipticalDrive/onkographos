This change updates cloud shadow map generation and sampling to:
Include volumetric fog extinction alongside cloud extinction during top-down shadow map generation so dense fog dims the ground.
Sample cloud and fog shadows inside VolumetricFroxelGenNode and shaders/volumetric_froxel_gen.comp to render volumetric light shafts / god rays in fog.
Support low sun elevations and horizon clouds by dynamically centering the 320km shadow map domain along sun direction and clamping UVs smoothly to edges.
Centralize shadow projection and layer blending logic into shaders/helpers/cloud_shadow.glsl across deferred ground shading, cloud rendering, and volumetric froxel fog generation.

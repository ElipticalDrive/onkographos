Avoid frustum-culling the instanced terrain
The instanced block meshes are added with only the base cube’s bounding volume (new THREE.InstancedMesh(geometry, material, count)), but no call is made to recompute the bounding sphere/box or disable frustum culling after the per-instance matrices are filled. Because the geometry’s bounds still cover only a 1×1×1 cube around the origin, once the camera looks away from that tiny region the renderer will cull the whole instanced mesh and the terrain will disappear even though many instances are still in view. Either set frustumCulled = false or compute a bounding volume that encloses all instances before rendering.

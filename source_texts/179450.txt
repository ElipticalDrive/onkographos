Efficient coding of experimental holograms using speckle denoising
Several methods were proposed for the compression of holographic data on the hologram plane. The lossless and lossy data compression and quantization effects were analyzed in the context of phase-shifting digital holography [5], [6], [7]. Histogram quantization for digital holograms of 3-D real-world objects was presented in [7].

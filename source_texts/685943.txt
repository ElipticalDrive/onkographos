We propose a method of downsizing technique for point sampled data which reduces information amount of \nthree dimensional point cloud data. Our method generates clusters and spiral chain lists. Each chain is consists \nof three dimensional points. After that, we adopt a predictive encoding to compress these chain lists. In addition, \nwe show the effectiveness of our method with same experimental results by comparison with one of \nconventional methods. From these experiments, our method can reduce the information amount coordinate data \nto 31.7% of original model.

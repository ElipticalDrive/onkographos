Spec: docs/design/viewer-rendering.md Phase E (E.2)
Depends on: none
Files: viewer/mesh.js, viewer/test/mesh_greedy_water.test.mjs (new)
What
In meshBlocks, merge neighbouring up-facing water faces at the same y that have the same layer, shade, tint and depth byte (vertex byte 15) into larger rectangles. UVs are uint8 in 1/16 block and the texture array uses REPEAT, so a merged quad spans at most 15 blocks in either direction. Water tops at y = 63 are flat and untinted, so an ocean chunk should collapse to a handful of quads. Only water tops are merged; other water faces and all other parts stay as they are. Positions must remain correct across the pad ring and chunk borders.
Done when
A flat ocean chunk's water part has under 5% of its previous face count (test against a synthetic block array and, if cheap, the wasm for seed 12345).
Merged quads cover exactly the same area, with the same layer, shade, tint and depth, as the unmerged faces (test comparing covered cells).
Quads never exceed 15 blocks per side; faces next to ice, solids and air still cull as before.
CLAUDE.md "Before opening a PR" checks pass.
World output
Unchanged (mesh only; no block, height, biome or decoration differs).
Out of scope
Greedy meshing of opaque faces; the allocation-free mesher (E.1).

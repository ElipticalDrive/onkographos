Medical imaging and compression are very crucial areas in terms of complexity in Image acquisition and processing. Lossless or near lossless compression is desirable for telemedicine or for storing a large amount of data; as a better acquisition (varying from 1 Tesla to 7 Tesla) larger the data volume. In this work HEVC/H.265 encoding is used to compress Computer Tomography (CT) Images. Quality evaluation is done on the basis of an objective analysis with parameter SSIM, MSE, PSNR etc. This work also reported the scheme of HEVC and its results with INTRA prediction and CABAC coding.

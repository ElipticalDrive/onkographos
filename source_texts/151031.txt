This paper presents the first FPGA-based real-time sort-free 3DGS accelerator through algorithm–architecture co-optimization. The accelerator enables a fully pipelined and highly parallel rendering architecture by eliminating global depth sorting and adopting an order-independent weighted-sum accumulation scheme. The proposed Gaussian pruning scheme further reduces the number of Gaussian primitives by 64%–80% with less than 1.35 dB PSNR loss while maintaining over 30 dB PSNR. Spherical harmonics analysis is applied to facilitate hardware design for rendering quality and hardware complexity balancing. A scale-adaptive Covariance Computation Unit is designed to remove data overflow for stable computation. An optimized Width Predictor and Processing Element further reduce the redundant computation and the rendering latency by 57.5%. Implemented on an AMD-Xilinx Alveo U200 FPGA, the proposed accelerator achieves 637.6 FPS while consuming only 2.7 W at 60 MHz.

Summary
First slice of BRDF importance sampling for polygonal lights, following Christoph Peters, "BRDF Importance Sampling for Polygonal Lights", ACM TOG 40(4), Article 140, 2021 (DOI), section 3 and supplement A.
FoundationMath projected_solid_angle.hpp: prepare / sample / density for convex polygons uniformly in projected solid angle. Covers horizon clipping, the ellipse sectors of Equation (1), the A.6 bitonic sorting networks, and the two-quad initialization refined by exactly two tangent-line iterations (sections 3.3–3.5). Ported from the author's polygon_sampling.glsl under its BSD-3-Clause option. The notice is kept in the sources and in cmake/sdk/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. The author's clipping, LTC and shading code are GPL-only and were not copied; clipping is an independent implementation.
shaders/projected_solid_angle.slang: the matching GPU port, installed beside math.slang so later render-graph templates can include it.
FoundationGeometry convex_polygon.hpp: partitions emissive triangle meshes into convex planar polygons of at most seven vertices with no three collinear, as the paper requires (3.1, A.2). It adapts Hertel–Mehlhorn inessential-diagonal removal (FCT 1983, LNCS 158, pp. 207–218). Merging follows shared edge chains, so fans and grids collapse. Non-convex shapes and holes split into convex pieces.
Docs: new sections in docs/modules/foundation/math.md and geometry.md, including every departure from the paper.
No renderer, integrator or render-graph behavior changes. Rendered output and reference profiles are untouched.
Departures from the paper (the distribution is unchanged)
Ellipse kinds (outer, inner, degenerate) are explicit, replacing the sign-bit and infinite-u.x encodings, and the [0, π] arctangent never divides by zero. Ludus compiles ordinary Metal shaders with fast math, and WGSL doesn't guarantee infinities, NaNs or signed zeros. The paper's encoding returned NaN on Metal for a vertex at the zenith, which the GPU parity test caught. The C++ code uses the same representation.
Sector selection skips zero-area sectors, so rounding at the end of the distribution can't select an empty sector (the original yields NaN).
The iteration uses an exact reciprocal instead of the exponent-flip approximation; the author's comment offers this variant for ports.
The biased variant (6.4) and the spill-free generated clipper (A.1) are not included.
Evidence (Apple M5, macOS)
Float64 oracle that never calls the production code: it clips independently, integrates with Lambert's edge formula and inverts the distribution by bisection. Over 4000 random polygons:
the backward error scaled by the projected solid angle has a 99th percentile of 1.1e-7, matching the paper's Fig. 6b;
the relative backward error has a 99th percentile of 4.2e-5 and grows only for tiny lights, as §3.6 describes.
Two iterations take the initialization's 99th-percentile error of 4.8e-2 (the paper reports 0.02) below 2e-7 absolute. The density integrates to 1 within 0.5%. The mapping is continuous in the random numbers.
[polygon-sampling] GPU test: Slang vs C++ on 4096 cases, natively on Metal. The SPIR-V and WGSL outputs compile with the pinned slangc but have not executed; they first run in batch CI.
Partition tests: exact triangle coverage, area preservation, strict convexity, at most 7 vertices, bitwise source positions. The polygons' total projected solid angle matches the triangles' total.
Validation run locally
macos-clang-debug: FoundationMath (122 cases), FoundationGeometry (70), allocation probes, renderer [polygon-sampling] and [random]
macos-clang-asan-ubsan: FoundationMath and FoundationGeometry suites clean
./scripts/check --format, source_policy.py --base <merge-base> --clang-format clang-format, --tidy-changed
Full .clang-tidy rule set on the five new translation units. The repo-wide --tidy inventory has existing findings in apps/editor, none in these files.
check-docs, strict MkDocs, check-wiki --documentation-only, test_docs.py, build-api with Doxygen 1.18.0 (no new gaps)
Linux/Vulkan and browser/WebGPU execution: first in batch CI
Follow-ups (not in this PR)
LTC sampling and the clamped optimal MIS (paper section 4), solid-angle sampling (section 5), integration into the reference path tracers, and render-graph templates. The many-area-light design in docs/architecture/many-area-light-sampling-reference-review.md reuses these primitives.

  initialization and two tangent-line iterations (3.3-3.5), plus the

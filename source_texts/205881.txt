Objective
Replace the planetary path's regular and transition Transvoxel extractors with a production GPU adaptive dual Surface Nets extractor if, and only if, it passes topology, ownership, publication, rendering, and performance gates.
This remains a Cartesian sparse volumetric SDF path. Pages and octree cells are storage and adaptive-indexing units only. The smooth path is independent from the future exact 10 cm cubic/block voxel path.
Required design
Treat the balanced 2:1 leaf complex and its canonical SDF samples as the source of topology.
Emit connectivity around sign-changing primal edges.
Give every regular or LOD-boundary polygon one deterministic page owner.
On 2:1 boundaries, let the owner compute all incident fine and coarse cell vertices from canonical samples; never consume a neighboring mesh.
Use canonical lower-LOD samples supplied by Pulsar, not filtered or resampled fine data invented by Helio.
Publish a page generation atomically only after its complete regular and boundary output, meshlets, and counters are valid.
Keep GPU residency and extracted arenas bounded independently of planet size.
Keep Transvoxel only as the matched baseline until the replacement passes. Remove obsolete extraction code and tables after promotion; do not ship a runtime compatibility selector.
Preregistered acceptance gates
Topology and ownership
Exhaustive local sign cases for uniform cells and every oriented 2:1 face/edge/corner neighborhood.
Randomized balanced-octree sign configurations verify no boundary edges, non-manifold edges/vertices, degenerate triangles, duplicate triangles, or duplicate ownership.
Identical canonical cell-vertex bit patterns wherever two page jobs recompute a shared vertex.
No dependency on neighbor mesh readiness or extraction order.
Publication and streaming
Stale generations cannot publish or replace newer geometry.
Refine/coarsen and edit replacement preserve complete old coverage until the complete new frontier is ready.
Full-sphere coarse coverage remains visible at extreme distance and after return to ground.
Resize/fullscreen graph rebuilds preserve the same opted-in planetary pass and its GPU caches.
Bounded residency, pending work, vertex/index/meshlet arenas, and deterministic backpressure/eviction.
Rendering and performance
PageIndexed and Meshlets produce the same visible surfaces and boundary ownership.
No cracks, holes, stretched triangles, stale geometry, black frames, or camera-motion stalls during fast ground/orbit movement and destructive edits.
Record matched Transvoxel vs Surface Nets GPU extraction latency, upload bandwidth, vertices, triangles, meshlets, scratch bytes, persistent bytes, and edit-to-visible latency.
Surface Nets must be no worse in correctness and must justify promotion through simpler ownership/topology and competitive measured cost.
Research basis
The design should be checked against the original Surface Nets and adaptive dual-contouring literature. The referenced voxel-planet implementation is inspiration only: it explicitly permits duplicate boundary overdraw and does not guarantee manifold output, which are rejected here.
Promotion rule
PASS: all automated topology/publication/cache gates, matched benchmark evidence, and live visual validation pass; then remove Transvoxel from the production planet path.
PIVOT: a correct adaptive dual formulation needs a different boundary representation, while preserving the same ownership and acceptance invariants.
STOP: Surface Nets cannot satisfy the gates or is materially worse than Transvoxel; retain the proven extractor and publish the negative evidence rather than weakening the goal.
Tracks the extraction milestone under #134 and depends on the publication/persistent-pass prerequisite in #229 / #230.

Research checkpoint: PIVOT within the Surface Nets direction
The first exhaustive CPU reference exposed two production blockers in naive adaptive Surface Nets:
Reusing the current regular Transvoxel table to infer dual components is not solid/air complement symmetric. Case 0x06 and its complement partition the same crossing edges differently.
Replacing that table with globally consistent face cycles fixes complement symmetry and adjacent-face agreement, but randomized closed fields still produce dual edges with incidence four. This is the known limitation that connected components alone do not guarantee manifold dual vertices.
The reference kernel now fails closed:
all 256 cube signs assign every crossing edge to exactly one component;
complement cases and adjacent ambiguous faces agree;
all 512 2:1 face signs are classified as safe or requiring refinement;
polygon ownership is traversal-order independent and coarse-owned at 2:1;
a closed sphere produces a duplicate-free 2-manifold;
randomized closed fields either pass the manifold audit or return UnsafeCellTopology before geometry emission.
Focused result: 9/9 Surface Nets topology tests pass. No extractor is wired into production, and Transvoxel remains the live baseline.
Decision: continue with a topology-complete MC33 / Dual Marching Cubes cell classifier (shared face and interior scalar tests, multiple dual vertices), then apply the deterministic 2:1 owner/ring contract. Do not dispatch GPU geometry until randomized fields no longer require the temporary unsafe-cell rejection.

Adaptive Surface Nets replacement progress (uncommitted; not yet visual-ready):
Replaced per-edge CPU ring materialization with a compact deterministic face-patch plan. The realistic horizon test fell from 37.8 s to 0.22 s while preserving the 49,152-edge six-face/edge/corner ownership oracle.

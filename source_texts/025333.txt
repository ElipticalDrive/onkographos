When placing fiducials on a volume that is rendered in 3D using the multi-volume technique, the fiducials are placed incorrectly.
Steps to reproduce
Load CTChest
Show volume rendering using the "VTK Multi-Volume (experimental)" technique
Set CT-AAA preset
Try to place fiducials on the ribs or any other solid bone
The fiducials appear around the middle of the volume
Expected behavior
The fiducial is placed on the bone surface (as it does using "VTK GPU Ray Casting"
Environment
Slicer version: Slicer-4.11.20200930
Operating system: Windows

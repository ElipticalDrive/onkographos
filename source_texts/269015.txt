cat12-segment-smooth v1.0.0 First archived release of the CAT12 segmentation container with an added smoothing step. What this image does Runs CAT12 segmentation on a single uncompressed T1w .nii and applies Gaussian smoothing with a [6 6 6] mm FWHM kernel to the modulated grey matter map.

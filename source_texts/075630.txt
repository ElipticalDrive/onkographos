This change introduces major enhancements to the volumetric clouds system.
The 3D volume bake shader (cloud_3d_volume_bake.comp) is updated to compute the physical evaluated cloud density (with full weather map, advection, advection offset, and noise detailing) at each voxel's Earth-curved world-space coordinate and store it in both the baseAlbedo (B) and baseDensity (A) channels of the 3D texture.
The shadow bake shader (cloud_shadow_bake.comp) is updated to populate the local ambient occlusion (G channel) based on neighbor evaluated densities from the A channel, and march towards the sun (u_primaryLightDir) to compute voxel-specific shadow transmittance (B channel). This is processed in a performance-optimal, staggered 2x2 Bayer-aligned fashion.
The minimal cloud density function calculateCloudDensityMinimal inside clouds.glsl is optimized to directly sample the baked density and AO from the 3D texture instead of procedurally evaluating noise and density, leading to massive performance gains.
The CPU-side manager (AtmosphereManager) is updated to bind the 3D volume texture to read-write image unit 1 and set/bind the necessary uniforms and textures during shadow bake.
All shaders are preprocessed and validated using make glcheck with zero compilation warnings or errors.

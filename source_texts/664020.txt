Two changes to igl::embree::reorient_facets_raycast, one commit each, results unchanged, and a test in a third.
Outside parity mode, each ray now stops at its first hit past its own face. The vote only ever used hits[0], but intersectRay collected every hit along the ray, re-casting past each one. Parity mode still collects every hit.
Rays are shot with the accumulating parallel_for overload, each thread voting into its own copy and the copies summed after. This is the reduction the commented omp lines from Remove omp pragmas #2242 point at.
On a Toronto building model of 27,106 triangles, facet_wise = true, 100 rays a face, Apple M1 Max:
Time
main
26.2 s
first hit
2.5 s
first hit and parallel_for
0.8 s
Backfacingness, the paper's measure, stays at 0.0011 to 0.0012 throughout, the same spread main shows from run to run.
I added a test, tests/include/igl/embree/reorient_facets_raycast.cpp: a cube with every other face flipped, checked facet-wise, by patches and by parity, compared with test_common::assert_eq. CI on my fork: https://github.com/aidannewsome/libigl/actions/runs/38092726682.
If it's wanted, the rest of the way would be making the rays in parallel too, each with its own generator worked out from a seed and the ray's index, so the function can also repeat its results. random_dir uses the global rand(), so that part would draw directions itself, and a seed means a new overload. I do this in outie, a Rust port of this function, where it takes the same model to 0.24 s. That could be a follow-up PR.

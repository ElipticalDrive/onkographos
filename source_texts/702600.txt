What math itself lacks:
Has: AABB, Sphere, Ray, Plane and Frustum.
Missing types: OBB, capsule, segment, triangle and Rect.
Missing queries: barycentric coordinates, ray-plane and ray-triangle.
Missing AABB operations: expand, encapsulate, volume and surface area. AABB helpers are hand-written about 15 times beside math/aabb.h: distance, containment, sphere overlap, bounds-from-points and union, plus a token-identical translate in CCD and queries #914 covers the AABB helpers that already exist as copies.
Failure scenario. Gameplay and editor features would have to re-derive these kernels or reach into physics internals. Examples:
an editor vertex snap, a mesh-accurate pick or a gizmo axis drag;
a navmesh query or a decal projector;
a gameplay "closest point on path".
Capsules exist only as collider data, so a character controller (#801) cannot share the query.
Commercial benchmark.
Godot: Geometry3D has ray_intersects_triangle, segment_intersects_sphere/cylinder/convex, get_closest_points_between_segments and get_triangle_barycentric_coords (Geometry3D.xml).
Unreal: FMath::ClosestPointOnSegment, SegmentPlaneIntersection and SphereAABBIntersection; FOrientedBox and FCapsuleShape (FOrientedBox).
Unity: Plane.Raycast, Bounds.Encapsulate/Expand and Rect (Geometry).
Jolt: Geometry/ClosestPoint.h, RayCapsule.h and OrientedBox.h.
DirectXMath: DirectXCollision.h (BoundingOrientedBox, TriangleTests).
Why this class and severity. A capability, so feature. P2: editor tooling and gameplay queries need these before serious production, and physics already proves the kernels work.
Fix direction.
Move the physics kernels verbatim into a math geometry header, so they stay bit-equal and the determinism suites are unchanged.
Add Segment, Triangle, Capsule, OBB and Rect types, with ray-plane, ray-triangle and barycentric queries, and AABB expand, encapsulate, volume and surface area.
Physics then includes math, which the module graph allows.
Editor picking can then add a mesh-accurate pass.
Closure.
The kernels are moved, not copied.
The physics determinism suites stay bit-equal.
Each query has unit tests, with exact results on axis-aligned cases.
Related. #914 (AABB helpers), #801 (character movement), #1124 (rotation helpers).

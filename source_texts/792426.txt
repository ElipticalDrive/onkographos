Pass 16's seventh round (the plan: rounds 411-420): the throughput rows the suite ranked behind Blender - a big mesh's save and open, the PLY / OBJ / STL writers, the PLY and OBJ readers, and the frame that enters Edit Mode at 500k faces with the views on the CPU. Stacked on #314 (round 416) and the rounds before it; merging this merges those.
What it does
A big mesh in the scene file is one binary chunk (ADR 0111): a mesh of 4096 vertices and corners or more is written as mesh <id> <name> <smooth> <nv> <nf> bin <bytes> + its arrays as they are in memory (positions, face offsets, corners, UVs, face materials / smoothing / groups, the mask, seams, sharp and wire edges, vertex groups, custom normals, sparse shape keys) inside the text; everything else - objects, components, materials, small meshes - stays text, and the loader reads both forms. Scenes without a big mesh are byte-identical to before; a scene saved with one needs this build or newer (the header stays blendity_scene 1; said in the ADR, the CHANGELOG, the lesson). The asset-path retarget edits the text around the chunks. No compression of the chunk on its own (Scene > Compress still compresses the whole file).
The exporters and importers on every core, without per-element allocation: the PLY reader (binary vertices and faces in parallel; ASCII lines in parallel; the records-across-lines reader kept as the fallback; the weld on an open-addressed table), the PLY writer (split vertices through each vertex's own chain, flat faces, the bytes at once), the STL writer (triangles counted, written in place), the OBJ writer (lines in pieces on every core - each face piece writes the usemtl / s lines from the face before it, so the text is what one thread writes; one vt and one vn per distinct value at a vertex), the OBJ reader (a flat vertex table, integers parsed in place).
The Edit Mode cage on the CPU: the overlay record built and replayed on every core; a big overlay batch drawn in 8-row bands with its band sort counted and filled on every core.
tests_round417 (test_assets.cpp): the chunk against the text form bit for bit with every attribute set, a small mesh kept text, a cut-short chunk / an out-of-range corner / a chunk longer than the file refused, the retarget leaving the chunk alone; the overlay record's order and the replayed picture identical to the one-at-a-time path; PLY binary / ASCII / big-endian / extra elements and properties / records across lines / truncated; OBJ's one vn per vertex and its usemtl / s lines at exactly the faces that change over 40,000 faces; STL's n - 2 triangles a face, each inside one face; OBJ negative indices and a second object.

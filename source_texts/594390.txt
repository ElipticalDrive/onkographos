Design only (AR-9), rebased onto main 9c8dd93 (#153, #154, #155 merged). This amends specs/sgl3d-architecture.md:
adds a Vertex encoding shared contract;
Prepared geometry: normals are now validated, and the vertices it packs follow the new contract;
Ray source: its vertices are packed;
Deformation: decodes the packed rest pose into f32 vertices.
There is no code yet. Packing runs in PreparedModel::new (#135).
Rework after the AR-10 review (owner's decision: positions stay f32)
Positions are f32 (12 B). The per-model grid, its step, floor and splitting rules are gone.
The ray source keeps exact positions for intersection and for what the scene builds over them (bounds, culling hierarchies, caster clusters, BVH).
Casters' vertex buffers keep their f32 positions. This removes finding 4: a deforming instance's casters already read f32 positions from the ray source, as Design shared geometry buffers and prepared models (#135) #154 lays out, so no pipeline split is needed.
The departure from Godot is recorded with Hyperdrive's numbers (RD-2): a 16-bit grid errs by up to 6.25 cm across its 4.5 km environment and 7.8 mm across its 650 m course, tilting 2 m tunnel panels by about 0.45° against its 0.05° reflection test.
Bevy 9d12036 keeps positions as f32 too: crates/bevy_mesh/src/mesh.rs:339–340, ATTRIBUTE_POSITION is Float32x3.
The Roadmap 13: Hardware ray-traced reflections and shadows #23 SNORM16 question is moot: the BLAS takes f32 positions.
Normal and tangent use Godot's axis-angle.
The axis is octahedral in 2 × unorm16 (Vector3::octahedron_encode, core/math/vector3.cpp:90–104), in word 3.
The angle is a unorm16 whose half carries the handedness (_get_axis_angle, rendering_server.cpp:320–339), in the low half of word 4. It needs its own 16 bits because Godot hides it in the position's w.
Decoding uses axis_angle_to_tbn (scene_forward_clustered.glsl:79–87).
The frame is made orthonormal before encoding (finding 3).
The tangent is projected onto the normal's plane and normalised, as pbr_tangent_frame does at shading.
A tangent that vanishes under projection, or whose w is not ±1, counts as absent (asset::tangent_frames). An absent tangent takes Godot's arbitrary one (rendering_server.cpp:565–574) with w = +1. Nothing reads it, because anisotropy needs authored tangents.
Godot's importer instead turns compression off when a tangent isn't perpendicular (modules/gltf/gltf_document.cpp:1829–1843).
Godot takes the axis and angle from Basis::get_axis_angle (core/math/basis.cpp:751–826). That function assumes a rotation and loses conditioning approaching its 0° and 180° singularities, so SGL3D takes them from the frame's unit quaternion instead.
Frame convention: the encoded rotation's matrix rows are (tangent, bitangent, normal), as Godot's Basis holds the frame in _get_axis_angle and axis_angle_to_tbn reconstructs its rows. The bitangent is normal × tangent, and the handedness is carried by the angle's half.
Rounding is to nearest (RD-2 improvement). Godot's casts truncate ((uint16_t)CLAMP(v * 65535, 0, 65535)). Truncation errs by up to a whole step: 0.0146° for a normal or tangent, which is above the 0.01° bound, and a full step for UV, colour and lightmap UV.
Chart table word: the Ray source rebase list now names the mesh record's word where its model's chart table starts.
UV rectangle per mesh, not per model (finding 2). The mesh record carries it.
Colour, lightmap UV and chart:
Colour stays RGBA8 with the colour sRGB-encoded and alpha linear, clamped to 0..1.
Lightmap UV stays 2 × unorm16; a negative UV packs as (0, 0), which also means unassigned.
The chart index into the per-model bounds table is now 16 bits, in the high half of word 4. A model with more than 65 536 distinct chart bounds is refused.

Nits from the review:
The layout test ties the struct, and a GPU round trip against independent expectations ties encode to decode.
The leaf test's UV and colour decode for cut-outs is now in the reader list.
The deform stage decodes the normal and tangent, which need no mesh data, so DeformDispatch is unchanged; reading the mesh record was only needed for the position grid.
Skinned bind bounds and every other structure over positions are unchanged, since positions stay f32.
Tolerances, held by the GPU round trip:
normal or tangent: within 0.01°, estimated from 16-bit steps; the implementation measures the maximum, including near 0° and 180°, and fixes the bound;
UV: within extent/131 070 per axis of its mesh's rectangle;
colour: within half an 8-bit step of its sRGB encoding;
lightmap UV: within 1/131 070.

Wenuray is a Python framework for radio-interferometric self-calibration on top of CASA. A self-calibration procedure is described once, as a short TOML recipe or a few Python objects, and checked against self-calibration theory before it runs. Imaging is pluggable: CASA tclean, Pyralysis or a user's own imager. Every run records the resolved recipe and its provenance, so it can be repeated exactly.

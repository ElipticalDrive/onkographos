There are plenty of transfer functions that haven't been implemented yet in LUT Maker. This issue is a place to collect a list of the transfer functions that people want, so that I can implement them.
Please leave a comment with any not-yet-listed transfer functions you want added. And if you can find the white paper with the formulas (which are needed for implementation) even better!
So far:
Panasonic's V-Log: white paper
Fujifilm's F-Log: white paper
DJI's D-Log: white paper
Nikon's N-Log: white paper
RED Log3G10: white paper. (Added in 1158fec.)
Blackmagic Design's Film Gen 5 transfer function: reverse engineered. (Added in 1158fec.)
DaVinci Wide Gamut Intermediate: white paper. (Added in a134abc.)
ARRI LogC3 (a.k.a. ALEXA LogC v3): white paper
ARRI LogC4: white paper
Apple Log: the white paper is currently behind a paywall, because Apple. Independent implementation.

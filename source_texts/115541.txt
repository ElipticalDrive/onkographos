Abstract
The storage and transfer of medical images play a crucial role due to the considerable data size, and, hence, the compression algorithm role is inevitable in telemedicine. This chapter proposes lossy and lossless compression schemes for the medical images. This work highlights variants of lossy vector quantization algorithm, bat optimization coupled with vector quantization (Bat-VQ), and contextual vector quantization based on the simulated annealing (CVQ-SA) algorithm. The performance metrics aid in validating compression algorithms. In terms of peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR), the Bat-VQ algorithm based on dynamic codebook design and CVQ-SA algorithm yields efficient results. However, in terms of the compression ratio, the CVQ-SA algorithm is superior. The least square-based prediction lossless compression algorithm was also proposed in this research work that yields robust results compared with the classical algorithms. The outcome of this research work suggests that a lossy compression algorithm also applies to medical images subjected to the constraint that reconstructed image quality is good and acceptable by the physicians.

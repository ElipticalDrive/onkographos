Fixes G23 (world modes review, Documentation/WORLD_MODES_REVIEW_PLAN.md §14.1; full write-up in §19.3): two heightmap readers of the analytic surface took the natural height where they meant the ground the generator makes (the natural height plus the terrain conditioning zones of POI pads and their falloff rings). Planets have read the conditioned surface since spherical Phase 5; this applies the same rule on heightmaps.
What was wrong (reproduced on VoxelDemo, base build)
Scatter's buried-chunk test (column scan, cubic scan, GPU path's classifier) compared a chunk's top with GetTerrainHeightAt. A pad cut below a chunk layer's top buried the chunk holding the pad's ground. The Village at (−104,312, 331,034): 4,969 points on the generated ground classified underground; chunk (−25,57,−1) had ground in all 4,096 columns and 0 instances. Under a raised pad it went the other way: another village had 5,315 points classified open surface inside the ground.
PCG far points (beyond the edit-aware band) stood at the natural height. A probe volume over the Village: 900 far points a median 346 uu off the ground (p90 774); 362 floating more than 150 uu above, 333 buried more than 150 uu below.
An analytic scan of 1,766 zones within 40 km of the spawn: 275 have columns where the generated ground sits in a chunk the natural test buries.
Changes
FScatterExtractionContext::ConditioningZones (was PlanetZones) carries the chunk's zones in every world mode, from UVoxelScatterManager::SetConditioningGatherer (was SetPlanetConditioningGatherer). The chunk manager gathers by the chunk's XY footprint on a heightmap and by the chunk's cap on a planet, as before. All three heightmap paths compare with FScatterExtractionContext::GetGeneratedTerrainHeight.
FVoxelSurfaceQuery::SampleSurface takes optional zones (height, gradient, water and height rules). SampleSurfacePoint forwards them on heightmaps, and the Voxel Surface Sampler gathers the zones over its bounds.
Generation does not change, so no GenerationVersion bump. Without zones every path computes exactly what it did before.
Tooling (kept): voxel.Scatter.ConditioningAudit [maxLines] [drawSeconds] and its scan [radiusKm] mode; voxel.Scatter.Digest file (one CSV line per chunk).
Test seams: UVoxelScatterManager::ExtractHeightmapSurfacePoints; ClassifySurfacePointsUnderground is now public.
Tests
VoxelWorlds.Scatter.Conditioning.CutPadAndRing: 0 of 59,226 ground points underground across every heightmap path; 6,200 against the natural height.
VoxelWorlds.Scatter.Conditioning.FilledPad: the chunk under a raised pad is underground (it was open surface).
VoxelWorlds.Scatter.Conditioning.ManagerWiring: through the manager's own pipeline, 0 of 1,018 ground points underground; 1,018 of 1,018 without the gatherer.
VoxelWorlds.PCG.FarPointsOnConditionedSurface: far points within 0.4 uu of the generated isosurface; up to 2,867 uu off without the zones.
Each test fails on the old behaviour. Suites VoxelWorlds+VoxelWorldSave+VoxelCharacter+WorldClock+InteractionPlugin+Dungeon: 509/509 headless and 509/509 in the editor (GPU tests running). The 87 GPU parity lines are identical to Phase 5's by CRC.
VoxelDemo A/B (this deliberately changes heightmap scatter and PCG output near POIs)
I ran the A/B six times: base behaviour three times (the base build, plus twice the fixed build with a temporary switch that removed the zones, deleted before commit) and fixed behaviour three times. Each run visits the spawn, two villages, a tower and a city.
The map capture PNG is byte-identical in all six runs, and so is the spawn digest.
Within 200 m of the five stops, 824 chunks appear in every run. 15 of them differ between runs of the same behaviour, so they are capture noise. Of the 809 stable chunks, 11 change with the fix, and every one lies in a zone:
3 at the Village gain instances on the cut pad's ground.
3 at the Tower gain instances from G26 (below).
5 at the second village lose instances that were inside the ground.
Without the distance filter, every changed chunk is still a zone chunk.
Fixed build: 0 ground points underground at the Village, 0 surface points inside the ground at the second village. PCG far points sit a median 0.4 uu off the ground (p99 1.9).
Found along the way: G26 (open, not fixed here)
The column scan treats the voxel above a chunk as air. Wherever the ground lies within 1.5 voxels above a chunk's top face, that face produces a second row of surface points, up to 1.5 voxels under the real ground. This already happens on natural terrain along every chunk-layer contour. Under cut pads the old natural-height test happened to hide it; with the fix, pads behave like natural terrain (the Tower: 850 more such points).
Fixing it changes heightmap scatter everywhere along those contours, so it needs its own A/B.
Not changed: the cubic world's voxel trees (FVoxelTreeInjector) still stand at the natural height. That is generation-time code, and cubic is terrain-only (D1).

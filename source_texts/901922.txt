Pros and cons using two VAOs instead of one:
pros:
VAOs are cheap objects (memory usage).
You can be sure the vertex attributes are bound properly to the buffers. (otherwise you may need layout qualifiers in both of the shader programs.)
You create the VAOs once and you will need two render calls only.
cons:
You will need to change the program before each render call (if it was possible, it would require the vertex attributes to be re-bound or at least hinted not to do that)
Would look more complicated and could be error prone.
source: http://www.openglsuperbible.com/2013/12/09/vertex-array-performance/
this post is from 2013 but I guess the results are similar on newer cards as well.

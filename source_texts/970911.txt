Summary
The foundation of voxel volumes, a second kind of geometry beside brushes for caves, coral, hives and other organic shapes. This covers M11.1 and M11.2 of the design in docs/voxels.md.
A level with volumes loads and saves, but plays as if they were not there. Nothing compiles, draws or edits them until M11.3 onward. Levels without volumes behave as before, but the format version goes up to 4.
Field and meshing (M11.1): mscent-world::voxel has sparse copy-on-write chunks, Add, Carve, Smooth and Paint dabs, surface nets, level of detail without cracks, and air connectivity between chunk faces.
Level file (M11.2): format version 4 adds section 5. Versions 1 to 3 still load.
Editor: the document keeps volumes across load and save, and each one keeps its place among the brushes.
--dump-level: refuses a level with volumes, because the text form has none and packing it again would lose them.
Numbering: M11, because M9 is particles and M10 is scripting. Scripting already took section 4 and version 3.
assets/levels/facility.msl: re-packed, because the version bump changes its bytes.
No external dependencies were added. mscent-bench gains dev-dependencies on mscent-math and mscent-world, both in this workspace, for voxel_bench.
Measurements
From voxel_bench on the dev machine, which is noisy. Not measured on the i3-7100U, and the drawing numbers in the design still come from a throwaway prototype that M11.4 replaces.

Pre-PR review
Fixed, with tests:
Saving moved a volume in the CSG order. The editor renumbered brushes but not volumes, which changed the geometry with no edit. A volume now stays right after the last surviving brush that preceded it, and a delete followed by undo restores it exactly (model/csg_order_tests.rs).
A volume tied with a brush's order loaded. It would be neither before nor after that brush, so the loader now refuses it.
Smooth gave new solid a stale material. That test failed with the fix turned off. My first version of it passed without the fix, so I rewrote it.
Huge shapes could overflow to NaN and write solid samples. Prim now refuses anything past the range brushes use.
same_geometry comment now covers volumes.
Answered, not fixed:
Carved rock in a Subtract volume shows material 0, not the brush's. Nothing draws volumes yet, so this is in the M11.3 done-when.
Touching volumes do not see each other, so a tunnel across a shared face would be walled off, though the loader accepts them. Also in the M11.3 done-when, and the design now says not yet.
The first dab after a snapshot copies the chunk table. At most 4096 entries, once per snapshot and not per dab. Not measured; M11.7's 1 ms tick target will show it if it matters.
The review ran in the main session with no independent reviewer, and it covered the code before the merge with main. The merge itself was checked by the gate only.
Known limits
The writer does not enforce the read limits, so a level could be sculpted past them, save, and then refuse to load. No tool can do that yet; it is in the M11.7 done-when.
Painting a volume makes same_geometry false until M11.5 splits the comparison.
The commits before the renumbering still call this work M9.1 and M9.2.

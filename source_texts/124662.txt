Abstract:
The loosy and lossless compression schemes with progressive transmission capability play a key role in critical data storage, retrieval and transmission, when a large volume of data must be handled fast, without distortions, over large networks with limited bandwidth. In this paper, a comparative study of lossless compression algorithms is presented. In cases where the algorithm under consideration may only be applied to binary data, the bit planes of the gray scale are separated, with and without Gray encoding, and the compression is applied to individual bit planes.

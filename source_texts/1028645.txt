Implemented ray query terrain shadows using Vulkan Acceleration Structures (BLAS/TLAS with AABBs) and GL_EXT_ray_query in deferred shading. AABB resolution scales based on distance from camera to shadow caster, and candidate heightmap sampling precision scales based on distance from camera to shaded surface point.

Summary
Removes unnecessary TripoSR vertex-colour reconstruction from MeshTitan's geometry-only backend.
calls upstream extract_mesh(..., has_vertex_color=False);
records vertex_colors: false in result provenance;
documents the omitted colour query;
adds a regression contract so the adapter cannot silently return to colour extraction.
This does not change marching-cubes resolution, chunking, preprocessing or mesh topology settings.
Closes #10.
Acceptance
Hosted software CI must pass. No TITAN RTX performance/VRAM claim is made from this change; physical qualification remains #6.

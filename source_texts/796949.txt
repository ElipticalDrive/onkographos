Adds a method to vello::Scene to compute the bounding box of the scene. This is mainly driven by a use-case in bevy_vello to do performance viewport culling, but may also be useful for blending layers.
Note: this currently fails because ellipses are encoded as xxyy and rects are encoded as xyxy.

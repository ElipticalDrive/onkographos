Summary
The camera turned on volumetric fog if the scene had any VolumetricFogVolume, no matter how far away it was. The fog is only computed out to its draw distance (4096 units), so a volume beyond that range can never show. Even so, the full fog pass ran every frame: froxel lighting, temporal blend and integration.
Now the camera turns volumetric fog on only when a volume with strength above 0 has its bounds within the draw distance. Once on, the fog stays on until the camera is 512 units past that distance, so it doesn't switch on and off when you stand at the edge.
Motivation & Context
Large maps often have fog volumes in a few places: valleys, villages, caves. Before this change, one volume anywhere in the map, even with Strength 0, made every camera pay for the fog on every frame.
In our map there are 10 volumes. From the test spot, the closest one was about 8,600 units away and none were within the 4096 draw distance, so nothing visible was drawn. Measured live in the editor in play mode, by disabling those volumes (which is what this change now does automatically):

Implementation Details
Where: CameraComponent.CopyPostProcessing now calls a new FogVolumeInRange( SceneCamera ) instead of Scene.Get<VolumetricFogVolume>() is not null. The method loops over the scene's fog volumes with Scene.Query<VolumetricFogVolume>(), which doesn't allocate. For each one it transforms Bounds by the volume's WorldTransform and checks the closest point against the camera position.
Same number in both places: the existing hard-coded DrawDistance = 4096 is now the constant FogDrawDistance. The range check and the fog's draw distance use the same value, so they can't drift apart.
Why the camera position, not the view frustum: a sphere around the camera is the safe choice. It may keep the fog on for a volume behind the camera, but it never turns off a volume that could be visible.
Edge margin without extra state: the extra 512 units only apply while camera.VolumetricFog.Enabled was already true last frame. That keeps the margin per SceneCamera with no new field.
Volumes with Strength 0 are skipped, since they add nothing to the fog.
Cost of the check: one bounds transform and one distance test per fog volume, per camera, per frame.

None. The goal is no visual change: volumes beyond the draw distance were already invisible.

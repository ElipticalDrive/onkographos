Stage 7 of the voxel roadmap (report §8): booleans with a voxel base run on the field, live preview included, with the architect's MUSTs 6–8 (Intersect over the whole base, a Voxel_Part.edits stamp for the live preview, .Boolean out of the mesh-only commands).
Best of all worlds (which idea came from where)
Field CSG as 3D-Coat's voxel room and OpenVDB's csgUnion/Difference/Intersection: min/max of the two distance fields at the base's voxel centres, so overlapping, touching or nested pieces always give one closed manifold clay and nothing can fail.
Live marks and preview as ZBrush's Live Boolean: the existing Union/Subtract/Intersect marks and base rule, the preview drawn in the marks' place and remade only when something changed and nothing is held.
Blend radius as Houdini's SDF blend (VDB Combine's smooth operations): a Blend slider in voxels rounds the seam with the same smooth minimum the Add brush uses.
Preview on a coarser lattice as Nomad resamples: the preview applies the marks to a copy of the base brought down to at most 256 voxels across by Res− steps, cached while the base is unchanged.
Mesh marks voxelised as Blender's Remesh-based workflows and 3D-Coat's import to voxels: a mesh mark is turned into clay at the base's voxel size in the base's space (the stage 3 voxeliser) and combined like a voxel mark.
What changed
src/voxel/combine.odin (new): Clay_Op, clay_copy (a clay's field without its steps), volume_has_clay, clay_combine(c, other, to_other, scale, op, blend): the other's box (band and blend around it) in this clay's bricks; Intersect first drops every brick and Inside tile outside it (MUST 6); per brick in the box, when the other is all one tile over it the result is a tile without computing, otherwise the brick is made and combined in a parallel_for (union smin, subtract −smin(−a, o), intersect −smin(−a, −o)); bricks that came out all one clamp collapse; then saddles, band repair and grow_band rounds as Res− does. Serial and parallel results are bit-identical (tested). grow_band is now package-private.
src/app/boolean.odin: shape_in gives a voxel object's welded surface (a voxel mark on a mesh base); boolean_apply applies the marks to a clay in the base's space; boolean_coarse keeps the ≤256 preview copy keyed on Voxel_Part.edits; boolean_preview chooses the path; boolean_make swaps the result into the base's clay as a swap undo entry; boolean_stamp mixes in the parts' edit stamps and the blend; the Booleans section shows Blend for a voxel base instead of the vertex counts.
Voxel_Part.edits with voxel_part_edited at attach, stroke end, undo/redo, resample and Make (MUST 7). .Boolean left MESH_ONLY_COMMANDS (MUST 8); a voxel object's Object palette offers Booleans.
Bench: a Union ms column (two spheres of the row's size, 1.2 radii apart). README (Voxel sculpting and Booleans), sm-sdf and sm-cad skills, a touch scenario step (a voxel sphere's copy subtracted from it through the palette's Make boolean).

The union at 1024 is 6 s: every brick of the base under the other sphere's band (most of it, for two spheres of the same size) is read again at each of its 512 voxels, trilinear in the other's field, then the band is solved again over them. The preview runs on the copy at most 256 across, so it costs the first row plus the weld (0.4 s in all on the VM). The scripted subtract at 96 across took 368 ms.
Verified
python3 scripts/gate.py green: check, test (692 tests), build, smoke, touch (357 of 357 scenarios, a desktop simulation), wincheck, androidcheck; after merging Live ambient occlusion while working (stage 4.2) #166 and Real 3D lights, stage 1: sun, point and spot lights saved with the scene #167 into the branch: check, test, build and smoke again, green, and the boolean touch scenario.
New tests (combine_test.odin): union and subtract volumes between the right bounds with every vertex on one of the inputs (seam aside), the intersect lens within 5 % of the analytic volume and disjoint spheres emptying the clay, a blend adding to the sharp union and a half-size turned mark placed by its transform, one thread and three bit-identical, a copy equal to its source.
build/sculpt --voxels=96 --script="duplicate_object; boolean subtract; move 0.9 0 0; select 1; make_boolean" --check-mesh: one clay object left, closed, 0 non-manifold, 0 degenerate, volume 2.63 (the sphere's 4.19 less the bite), 368 ms at 96 across.
The live preview dumped to a PNG: a sphere with a spherical bite out of its right side, the cutter not drawn, the seam at the lattice's grain.

The raycast collision query in OgreWorld first performs an 'all intersections' ray-aabb query to the whole scene, and after that performs a 'nearest intersection' hit to the individual triangles of each mesh whose aabb was hit in the first pass to find the nearest hit on a triangle level.
This is algorithmically wrong, and results in potentially hundreds of redundant ray-triangle mesh tests being performed.
Remove the use of Ogre ray-aabb query, and rewrite it manually to perform an immediate one-pass 'nearest intersection' ray-triangle mesh query to the whole scene, using aabb's as an early-out.

What
A move: MeshIntegrator (mesh → SDF voxelisation) leaves tsdf for a new tier, voxelize:
target volumetric_kit::recon_voxelize, namespace volumetric_kit::recon::voxelize, headers include/volumetric_kit/recon/voxelize/, sources src/volumetric_kit/recon/voxelize/;
links recon_volume alone, and sits right after volume in src/CMakeLists.txt's tier order (strict dependency rule); the umbrella volumetric_kit::recon includes it;
its 7 kernels and mesh_scan.glsl move with it, so recon_tsdf now builds only tsdf_integrate.comp and tsdf_clear.comp;
triangle_common.glsl moves from core/shaders/ to volume/shaders/, beside triangle_candidates.glsl; its other user is voxelize's mesh_integrate.comp;
tests renamed: recon_tsdf_mesh_integrate{,_exact} → recon_voxelize_mesh_integrate{,_exact} (tests/voxelize_mesh_integrate{,_exact}_test.cpp).
No forwarding header, no alias namespace, no compatibility shim.
Why
The conversion fuses no depth: mesh_integrator.{hpp,cpp} include no tsdf header, and its shaders include only volume GLSL and the closest-point GLSL. It needs the grid, the triangle candidates and recon_core's dispatch helper. In tsdf it made every fusing caller build 7 mesh kernels it never runs, and every mesh caller link the fuser. This is a prep step of the recon split: the voxel layer can leave without depth fusion.

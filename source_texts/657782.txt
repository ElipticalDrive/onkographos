Generalize sampled image operations, view-relative mips, complete memory
footprints, retries and generation-specific wait counters across supported
image ISAs. Reuse the Vulkan fixed-point polygon footprint model for
derivative LOD and qualify its volume extension against independent
GFX11/12 captures, including exact boundary and NPOT mip selection.
Preserve gather float payloads, missing-coordinate filtering for tex1D
views of 2D arrays, unnormalized wrapping, signed zero, and GFX12 quad
mip blending. Keep sampler completion separate from ordinary response
spans. Reject unqualified resource/sampler controls, including GFX12
UAV3D and DEPTH_SCALE, before memory access.
Unsupported image atomics, queries and sampling forms report execution
errors instead of silently leaving destination registers unchanged.
Validate decoded execution and metadata across image ISAs, including
unsupported descriptor controls and preservation of destination registers.
Physical qualification covers gfx1100/gfx1201; earlier hardware is unavailable. Independent captures cover exact mip selection and
qualified filtering; sRGB filtered results use a measured numeric tolerance.

Summary
The meshers read light from the wrong place or not at all: smooth terrain is
always drawn fully sky-lit with no block light, and blocky faces read the light inside
their own opaque block, which is always 0. Each surface must show the light of the air
it faces.
Deliverables
Smooth and blocky terrain faces carry the block light and sky exposure of the air in
front of them.
A light change re-meshes every chunk whose surfaces read the changed voxels.
Acceptance criteria
```
Scenario: Covered ground is drawn without sky light
  Given smooth ground with an opaque block one above its surface
  When the chunk is meshed
  Then the vertices under the block carry sky exposure 0
  And open ground beside it carries sky exposure 1

Scenario: Ground beside a light shows its colour
  Given a crystal in a sealed pocket of smooth ground
  When the chunk is meshed
  Then the pocket's wall vertices carry the block light of the air beside them

Scenario: A blocky face shows the light in front of it
  Given a blocky block with a level 10 light in the voxel beside its +x face
  When it is meshed
  Then that face carries block light 10/15 and its other faces carry their own neighbours' light

Scenario: Light crossing a chunk border re-meshes both chunks
  Given a light whose area ends on a chunk border
  When it is removed
  Then both the chunk holding the light and the chunk whose faces read the border are marked for re-meshing
```
Tech spec
Design
Smooth (mesh/marching_cubes.rs, mesh/hybrid.rs): generate_mesh gains a
light_at: impl Fn([usize; 3]) -> ([u8; 3], bool) (field sample index to block light
and sky exposure), beside [ENG-F24-05] Terrain shows each block's own material, on walls and on the cut #469's material_at. With 0/1 densities each vertex lies on
an edge with one solid and one air sample; the vertex takes the light of the air
sample of its edge. light_level keeps its compat formula from the sampled values.
Hybrid builds light_at from the same padded -1..=16 range as the density field
(grid.block_light_rgb_at, grid.sky_exposed_at).
Blocky (mesh/blocky.rs): each face reads position + face normal instead of
position.
Border re-meshing (light_propagation.rs, light_system.rs): whenever a write lands
on a voxel in a chunk's outer layer, the chunk across that face joins affected too
(block light in drain and the dark wave; sky in the refresh from [SG-F7-03] Digging and building relight the world in game #473 by the changed
bits on face layers). Precise, so a light change does not re-mesh all six neighbours.
Coarse LOD 1 (64+ blocks away) and the cut cap keep constant light.
Why the air endpoint and not trilinear sampling: every vertex already has exactly one air
sample; one lookup per vertex, no blending rules, and the light read is the light of the
space the surface faces.
Out of scope
Coarse LOD and cap lighting; smooth-terrain AO (SG-F7-06 [SG-F7-06] Smooth terrain shows contact shading #482); shader composition (ENG-F29-01 [ENG-F29-01] Block light adds to the surface colour #489); a 3D light
texture instead of per-vertex light (revisit with the GI experiment); point lights.
Footprint
flagstone_voxel/src/mesh/marching_cubes.rs, mesh/hybrid.rs: light_at, per-vertex light; tests.
flagstone_voxel/src/mesh/blocky.rs: face-front sampling; its test that sets light on the block itself is updated.
flagstone_voxel/src/light_propagation.rs, light_system.rs: border neighbours in affected; tests.

Decisions and deviations from implementing SG-F7-05:
Which chunks re-mesh on a border write. Only loaded chunks (grid.has_chunk), as refresh_sky already did. The set is the chunks that actually read the voxel: the face neighbour across an outer layer (blocky faces read the voxel in front of them), plus, because the smooth mesher reads one voxel past each positive face, the chunks below on every low axis, diagonals included. So a voxel on a low edge or corner also re-meshes the diagonal chunk(s); the spec named face neighbours only. light_storage::reading_chunk_offsets holds the rule.
Arrival. Chunks entering the grid don't dirty their neighbours for sky changes, because every arrival caller already re-meshes the arrived chunks and their face neighbours. Residual: a streamed-in chunk's low-side diagonal neighbours are not re-meshed for sky on arrival; streaming has the same face-only behaviour for geometry seams today.
Smooth ground meeting a blocky block in a mixed chunk. The blocky block counts as air in the smooth density field, so the smooth vertex at the foot of a building reads the block's own light (dark). This gives a contact darkening around building bases; left as is, to be judged in game (SG-F7-06 [SG-F7-06] Smooth terrain shows contact shading #482 does contact shading properly).

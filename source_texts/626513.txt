SDF Colors
What?
Implemented materials for SDF geometry. The array of materials can be modified from the plugin settings, and Edits can be assigned a material index. Smoothed edits also get smoothed materials.
How?
Just like the array of edits, materials are uploaded to the GPU when they are modified. To sample materials, the smoothing factor is used to calculate the material blending between every edit. Oklab is used for albedo interpolation.
Reviewer Steps
Modify materials in the plugin settings
Add and remove a material
Change the material index of an edit
Change the smoothing, and verify that the materials are blended smoothly
Toggle "Colors Subtraction" on a subtractive edit and verify that it only affects subtracted surfaces when on
Change unit scale and verify everything stays in place
Closes #54
Notes
This will be optimized once the BVH is in place. We can loop over the list of SDFs in the current brick rather than looping over every SDF.

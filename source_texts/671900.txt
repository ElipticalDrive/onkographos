The advance of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) are able to create new and meaningful outputs given arbitrary encodings for three dimensional terrain generation. Using a two-stage GAN network, this novel system takes the terrain heightmap as the ﬁrst processing object, and then maps the real texture image onto the heightmap according to the learned network.

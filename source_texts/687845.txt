Issue body actions
Render directional-light scattering through fog so cathedral windows produce visible shafts, including colored shafts from RGB cookies.
Acceptance criteria:
Integrate single scattering along camera rays through a bounded or distance-limited medium.
Stop integration at opaque scene depth.
Sample directional shadow visibility so walls and window frames block shafts.
Sample RGB cookies consistently with surface lighting.
Expose density, scattering color, anisotropy, distance, and quality controls.
Composite scattering and transmittance before tone mapping.
Provide a disabled path and measure GPU cost at a recorded resolution and quality.
Validate camera motion, depth edges, and zero-density behavior.
Dependencies: Add directional-light shadow mapping #50 and the RGB cookie issue.
Out of scope: Multiple scattering, volumetric clouds, fluid simulation, and local-light volumetric shadow

Currently, many information processing and control systems are based on computer vision technologies. Among the many problems that need to be solved in such systems, it is important to reduce the redundancy of information in the image. To solve this problem, various methods of image compression are used, including those based on the wavelet transform. This paper is devoted to one of these methods. The proposed method is based on the calculation and analysis of energy features, which allow us to assess the significance of the detailing coefficients for the representation of the image in a compressed form. As a result of its application, a matrix of approximating coefficients of the initial level, significance maps and sets of significant detailing coefficients of various levels of the wavelet transform are formed. To improve the compression quality, significant detailing coefficients are normalized. In addition, you can apply entropy coding for each level of data. The described method is simple to implement and provides flexible parameter settings to obtain the required image compression quality indicators.

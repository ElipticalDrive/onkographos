Dual Contouring | swiftcoder/isosurface | DeepWiki
Aug 25, 2025This document covers the Dual Contouring algorithm implementation, which extracts mesh surfaces from isosurfaces while preserving sharp features through feature placement techniques. The algorithm addresses limitations of traditional marching cubes by placing vertices at feature points rather than grid-aligned positions.

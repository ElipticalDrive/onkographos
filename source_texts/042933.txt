Adds VoxiGen to Tools. It's a voxel editor running in the browser on WebGPU (a C engine compiled to WebAssembly), used to build game assets and levels: models up to 1024³, MagicaVoxel .vox import/export, GLB/image voxelization, and level export for game engines. It's actively developed and was used to build every level of VOXEN, a voxel shooter. Closed source, free to use (export to mesh formats is paid); not copyleft.
Disclosure: I'm the developer.

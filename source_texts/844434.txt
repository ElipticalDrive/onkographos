Improve the performance by implementing parallel code.
Parallel GL commands using a z-sort and compositing the scene (8X boost.)
Implement VBO (instead of direct mode) and custom matrix stack (2X.)
Parallel GPU rendering using z-sort and compositing (2-3X.)
Cluster Computing (using Equalizer library) will yield higher performance (pixels and FPS.)
Support SGI UV series (2000, 3000…)
On a single workstation (with two dual GPU cards) with 4 GPU's it is possible to achieve 1 million node objects at 30FPS. This completely saturates a 4K display.
Theoretically, with a fully built-out SGI it is possible to completely modulate the geometry and pixels at a resolution that meets the human eye. Therefore, we postulate that it is no longer hardware that stands in the way of full psychologic immersion, but rather it is the 'software' that represents the Achilles Heel of technology.

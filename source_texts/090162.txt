Rendering
While rendering voxels appears easy at a glance ("they're just cubes!"),
there are actually many, many, trade-offs one has to take into account,
mainly depending on the intended visual style and scale of the individual voxels.
Vertex Pulling
Manual vertex streaming for improved memory usage and bandwidth.
Signed Distance Fields
An N-dimensional field/function where at every point the distance to the closest surface is known.
Raysterization
Rendering images via parallax-projected raymarching of rasterized bounding volumes. Also often (wrongly) called parallax raymarching.
Naïve Voxel Meshing
The simplest way to turn voxels into meshes...
Greedy Voxel Meshing
Merging faces across voxels to reduce triangle counts...
Indirect Multidraw
A method for rendering massive amounts of geometry, with a single drawcall.
Quad Indices
The basic pattern of indices for quads made of triangles.
Virtual Texture Atlas
By dynamically building (and uploading/keeping on the GPU) a texture atlas with a lookup-table of its contained sprites, both a variety of interesting effects and dynamic loading of textures/sprites can be achieved.
Vertex Packing
Manually bit-packing data into vertices for improved memory usage and bandwidth.
Level Of Detail
Under perspective projection, as the distance to any visible object increases, it'll occupy/cover less and less pixels on the screen, so the geometric features or details of the object become ever smaller too. Because of this, it makes sense to reduce the level of detail an object has at large distances.
Baked Voxel Meshing
Baking meshes ahead of time, to later quickly turn voxels into meshes...
Vulkan API
Modern hardware accelerated rendering API.
Raytracing
Rendering images via raycasting, be it on the CPU or GPU, is a powerful method, especially when it comes to voxels.
Texture Atlas
A texture atlas (also called a spritesheet) is an image containing a
multitude of smaller images (sprites), usually tightly packed together so as
to reduce wasted space, that is used during rendering by various models,
materials, shaders and renderpasses.
OpenGL API
Classic hardware accelerated rendering API.
Surface Extraction
Turning voxels into geometry.
Face Pulling
Taking vertex pulling to it's conclusion by streaming ONLY face-data.

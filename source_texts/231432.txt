Fixes #1352.
Problem
In RegNeRF's LLFF._generate_random_poses, the append operation is outside the sampling loop. Consequently the method generates many candidate camera positions but retains only the final pose, regardless of the requested n_random_poses count.
Change
Move random_poses.append inside the sampling loop. No changes to the sampled distribution, camera coordinate transforms or downstream ray generation.
Add deterministic tests that exercise the actual LLFF method and its NumPy pose helpers on synthetic camera poses and depth bounds. These confirm that six requested poses produce six distinct 3x4 camera transforms, while a single requested pose preserves the previous output shape.
Validation
The multi-pose regression fails on unchanged upstream (actual shape 1x3x4 instead of 6x3x4), and passes after the fix.
Both focused regression tests pass with NumPy. They execute the actual method in isolation to avoid importing the project's historical JAX and TensorFlow dependency stack.
Ruff selected checks, Python compilation and git diff --check passed.
Full LLFF dataset downloads and NeRF training were not run.

Abstract
3D display was expected to be the dominant next-generation display technology for producing more natural images. For autostereoscopic 3D technologies, observers could perceive 3D images with the naked eyes, yet still has the degradation of image quality for a given autostereoscopic 3D display. In this chapter, various LC lens (LC-lens) technologies for improving the issues are illustrated, followed by the future developments of LC-lens on 3D applications.

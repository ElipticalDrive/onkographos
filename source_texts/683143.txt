The demand for restoring high fidelity textures to 3D reconstruction models is increasing in fields such as photogrammetry, virtual reality, and autonomous driving. Based on the texture mapping method of image stitching, this article improves the texture selection strategy of triangular surfaces based on image quality and geometric perspective, proposes a texture optimization algorithm for multi-view 3D model reconstruction, constructs an energy function based on Markov random field, optimizes the data items of the energy function based on the image quality and geometric perspective of triangular surfaces, and improves its optimal texture selection strategy. Then this paper adopts image enhancement technology based on brightness detection to improve the contrast of texture images, integrates global and local texture optimization algorithms, and makes a comparison with the OpenMVS algorithm. The results show that the texture model generated by the algorithm in this paper has richer levels of brightness and darkness, clearer contours and details, and can better present the real texture features of the object surface, significantly improving the visual realism and detail expression of the reconstructed model.

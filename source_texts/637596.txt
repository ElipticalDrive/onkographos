The use of hardcoded voxel format is both not conforming to DarSIA (but matplotlib) and does not use darsia classes for Voxel and Coordinate. This results in confusion and likelihood of bugs. This needs to be changed. Also, in order to make the conversion to the matplotlib format more controlled, there should be a functionality in Voxel and VoxelArray, that brings the Voxel in the "wrong" format.

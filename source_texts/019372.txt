Volume rendering is a newly-developed visualization algorithm which differentiates from the classical surface rendering. This paper presents an in-depth discussion on the theories, principles, and processes of volume rendering techniques and an example for their applications in modeling mineral deposits. Based on the improved ray-casting algorithm, a 3D simulation system for the visualization of mineral deposits is developed. Real-life data from an iron mine in China are used to verify the visualization algorithms and the computer programs. The case study demonstrates the capability and applicability of volume rendering techniques for the visualization of geological and mineral data sets. This work is expected to facilitate the practical application of volume rendering techniques in the visualization of 3D data fields.

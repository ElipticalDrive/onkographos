A 3D pseudo-perspective rendering engine engineered in modern **C++17**, inspired by early 90s FPS pioneers such as *Wolfenstein 3D*. Features real-time Digital Differential Analysis (DDA), camera plane vector calculation, perpendicular wall projection (fisheye correction), and ASCII/PPM rendering output.

---

## 🧮 How It Works: The DDA Algorithm

The engine calculates what the player sees across the horizontal screen width by projecting rays through a 2D grid map:

1. **Camera Plane & Direction Vectors**:
   For each screen column $x \in [0, \text{screenWidth}-1]$:
   $$\text{cameraX} = \frac{2x}{\text{screenWidth}} - 1$$
   $$\vec{r} = \vec{d} + \vec{p} \cdot \text{cameraX}$$
   where $\vec{d}$ is the player direction vector and $\vec{p}$ is the orthogonal camera plane.

2. **Grid Traversal (DDA)**:
   Traverses grid lines incrementally until intersecting a solid wall boundary.

3. **Fisheye Distortion Elimination**:
   Instead of using raw euclidean distance from player to wall, the engine computes **perpendicular wall distance**, ensuring flat walls appear straight rather than spherical.

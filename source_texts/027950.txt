This paper presents a survey on volume rendering algorithms and a theoretical analysis of their applicability for rendering large data sets. The analysis is based on rendering volumetric seismic data sets used by oil and gas companies. A classification of the rendering process and rendering acceleration methods is presented. Then, volume rendering algorithms are classified into four different groups, and a description and theoretical analysis of their performance is presented. Keywords: volume rendering, scientific visualization, seismic. 1 Introduction Volumetric data consist of a set of samples located in three-dimensional space (3D). They represent the discrete sampling of a 3D function. Every sample contains a scalar value v describing some property of the data. The collection of all these values represents the scalar field function of the volume. Each sample is a volume element, or voxel.

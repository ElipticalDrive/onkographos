# Ray-Tracing

A C++17 CPU renderer supporting both surface path tracing and SDF-based volume rendering. 

The surfacec renderer supports spheres, triangle meshes, various materials, depth of field, Monte Carlo sampling, and BVH acceleration. 

The volume rendering converts a closed triangle mesh into a voxelized signed distance field, maps the SDF to density, and ray marches through volume using absorption, scattering, and shadowing. 

OpenMP is used to parallelize both rendering and SDF construction.

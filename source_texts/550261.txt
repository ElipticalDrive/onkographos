Hi,
I've been testing the Gaussian Head Avatar pipeline on different datasets and encountered blurry renderings when using the AVA-256 dataset. I suspect this might be related to how the camera intrinsics are handled by the underlying rasterizer (the differentiable Gaussian rasterizer from gsplat).
The rasterizer seems to assume centered principal points (cx = W/2, cy = H/2). This works well on datasets like Nersemble, where intrinsics are centered:
But when I use the AVA-256 dataset, which contains non-centered cx, cy values, the results appear blurry:
I wanted to ask:
Do you think this blurriness is caused by the assumption of centered principal points in the rasterizer?
Does the current pipeline support a rasterizer that accounts for arbitrary principal point offsets, like gsplat in GaussianAvatar?

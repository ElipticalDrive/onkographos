Repository: https://github.com/eyang72004/Project5-WebGPU-Gaussian-Splat-Viewer
Live Demo: https://eyang72004.github.io/Project5-WebGPU-Gaussian-Splat-Viewer/
Completed Features:
Point-cloud and Gaussian splat rendering with GPU preprocessing, covariance projection, spherical harmonics, depth sorting, and alpha blending.
View-frustum culling, indirect rendering, interactive Gaussian scaling, configurable workgroup sizes, and Gaussian count controls.
PLY scene loading, JSON camera presets, and interactive camera navigation.
GPU timestamp profiling, CSV export, and five documented performance experiments.
Extra Credit: Implemented half-precision (f16) Gaussian preprocessing with an interactive f32/f16 selector and packed intermediate splat data (48 to 32 bytes per splat, a 33.3% reduction), with comparative benchmarks documented in the README.
README documentation, screenshots, and two HQ demonstration videos covering the Bonsai and Bicycle scenes.
Feedback: This project was a valuable opportunity to explore GPU preprocessing, sorting, rendering, and performance optimization in WebGPU.

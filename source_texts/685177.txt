Outcome and scope
Define UI material parameters and SDF rendering through the generic renderer, preserving blending, clipping, painter order and resource lifetime.
Acceptance and validation
Reference pixels, shader ABI/format validation, invalid material inputs and descriptor/retirement pressure. Do not tie custom native graphics objects to public UI headers.

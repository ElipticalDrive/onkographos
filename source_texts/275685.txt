Summary
Adds a curated docs/REALTIME_RENDERING_READING_LIST.md capturing the user-provided prioritized upgrade path (TAA/spec AA → SSR → volumetric fog → shadows → WBOIT → stochastic culling → water) with verified links:
JCGT article URLs checked against journal routing (papers.json paths): SSR, specular AA, moment shadow maps, WBOIT, stochastic light culling.
SIGGRAPH Advances course pages for volumetric fog (Wronski 2014) and related Frostbite volumetrics (Hillaire 2015).
Water/ocean: DOIs for Wave Particles (SIGGRAPH 2007), Water Wave Packets (TOG 2017), Springer chapter DOI for An Efficient Method for Real-Time Ocean Simulation; HAL pointer for Realistic Water Volumes in Real-Time with note to confirm formal venue in ACM/EG records.
Restores docs/OCEAN_RENDERING_REFERENCES.md (venue/title caveats) and links both new docs from docs/SIGGRAPH_FEATURES_ROADMAP.md References.
No code or shader changes.

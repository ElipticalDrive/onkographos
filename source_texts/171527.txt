In the article considers the visualization methods of three-dimensional objects, which are based on the original projection system. Methods allow to form real-time without the mathematical treatment to restore intermediate azimuthally image real or virtual object by its reference images. Also there is provided a method of optical coordinate-system transformation, which could be template for creating a new autostereoscopic volumetric display. It differs from existing models such parameters as: lack of rotating parts and complex mathematical processing and ability to create fully permeable volumetric fine-quality image.

Features Implemented
Point-cloud and Gaussian splat rendering
Gaussian covariance projection, spherical-harmonic lighting, depth sorting, and alpha blending
Toggleable frustum culling and preprocessing workgroup sizes
Camera presets, mouse controls, and Gaussian scale adjustment
Extra credit: Mixed-precision preprocessing with f16 rotation and lighting calculations, plus compressed conic and color storage
Extra credit: Tile-based depth sorting with packed tile lists and compute compositing
Benchmark export and performance comparisons across renderers, workgroup sizes, culling settings, and point counts
Correctness tests covering dense tiles, resizing, precision switching, and empty-frame recovery
The README includes implementation details, screenshots, demonstration videos, performance analysis, raw measurements, and instructions for reproducing the tests.

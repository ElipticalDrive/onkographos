MITK supports an extensive set of image processing and volume rendering functionality, and it is a very convenient tool. 3-D reconstruction of the liver is performed by MITK under VC++ 6.0 platform. Compared with 3-D reconstruction results by Amira, good surface image of liver could be obtained by MITK. The 3-D reconstruction image of liver can be used for finite element analysis and temperature field simulation, which may be applied for surgical planning system and guide the process of clinical operation.

Objective
Extend global exact reblocking into a per-region hierarchical layout that can split, merge, migrate, and compact blocks according to access heat, entropy, and reconstruction value.
Scope
define a sparse octree layout manifest over logical cell coordinates
permit different block shapes in different active regions
add bounded split and merge transformations
collect read/write heat, encoded size, entropy, and dirty-state telemetry
add hysteresis to prevent refine/coarsen oscillation
verify coordinate preservation before committing a new layout
journal every layout transformation and retain rollback state
Acceptance criteria
hot regions can refine without reblocking the entire volume
cold neighbouring regions can merge when the total objective improves
boundary coverage is complete and non-overlapping
no coordinate can resolve to multiple active leaf blocks
split/merge cycles preserve all values exactly with the lossless codec
optimization respects declared block-count and metadata budgets
deterministic fixtures prove hysteresis prevents oscillation

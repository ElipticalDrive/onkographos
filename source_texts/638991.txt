Issue body actions
The native mip-copy path currently accepts a limited set of block-compressed DDS formats and skips uncompressed DDS and BC6H. Format gaps can leave large originals uncovered by generated overrides. Extending format coverage alone does not establish equivalent optimization to an existing output; dimensions, compression and material requirements also matter. Baseline comparison is tracked in #25.
Acceptance:
Define supported uncompressed channel layouts and row-pitch rules from the DDS specification; explicitly report ambiguous or unsupported layouts.
Copy retained mip bytes natively without changing channel masks, alpha semantics, compression, or color-space declarations.
Evaluate ordinary single-surface BC6H mip copying separately from protected cubemaps, arrays and volumes.
Keep inspection, output validation, preview/rebuild capability checks and cache recipes consistent with expanded format support.
Test exact retained bytes, headers/pitches, rectangular and tiny mips, truncated/padded data, cancellation, cache reuse, and unsupported-layout behavior with synthetic fixtures.
Validate representative outputs in game under Validate in-game texture quality and performance #14 before treating them as a replacement for existing optimized textures.
Implementation and synthetic/package validation: #27 and #30. The final in-game acceptance item remains open under #14.

This PR computes the exact transform package of positive-scale Gumbel laws: prove that the exponential-integrability domain is (-∞, 1 / s), evaluate the mgf as exp (m * t) * Γ(1 - s * t), simplify the cgf, and identify the characteristic function as exp (i * m * t) * Γ(1 - i * s * t).
Advance the exact target in StandardDistributions/README.md, Layer 10, §10.2 Gumbel and generalized extreme value, namely the required Gumbel domain, moment-generating function, and characteristic function. After this PR, the Gumbel milestone still needs its density, mean and variance, affine and maximum identities, support and weak-continuity package, while the broader section still needs the GEV family.
Choose this direct family-completion slice because the Gumbel exponential-pushforward construction has just landed on main, and its transform is immediately reduced to the already merged exact real-power moment theory of the rate-one Gamma law. The open logistic-difference PR is independent and contains no Gumbel transform work; open and historical PR searches and pinned Mathlib/Tau Ceti source searches find no overlapping API.
Add TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gumbel/Transforms.lean (182 lines) with five documented public theorems and no new definitions, instances, or notation. Reuse integrable_rpow_gammaMeasure_iff, integral_rpow_gammaMeasure, and eqOn_complexMGF_of_eqOn_mgf; the last transfers the real Gamma formula to the imaginary axis without duplicating a complex Gamma-integral computation. Credit Johnson, Kotz, and Balakrishnan, Continuous Univariate Distributions, volume 1, chapter 21, for the classical formulas. No Mathlib infrastructure or external formalization is vendored.
Read the shared review protocol and all ten rubrics: scope, correctness, reuse, attribution, api-design, generality, placement, naming, documentation, and proof-quality. Self-review repeated exact and semantic duplicate searches for every declaration, confirmed the positive-scale hypothesis and t < 1 / s threshold are sharp, and found no missing characteristic result, unnecessary public helper, or stronger-than-needed hypothesis.

3D Gaussian Splatting, as a novel scene representation method, achieves high-quality rendering while maintaining real-time rendering speeds. Although existing 3D Gaussian Splatting methods enable real-time rendering, their quality is limited in extremely blurred regions. This paper proposes a dual-level real-time deblurring framework that integrates geometric transformation with diffusion priors. At the 3D level, Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) dynamically adjusts the covariance of Gaussian primitives to model blur. At the 2D level, we generate virtual viewpoints through camera pose interpolation to augment the training data, employ a pre-trained single-step diffusion model to enhance rendered novel views, and design a progressive distillation strategy that propagates clean details back to the 3D representation to ensure multi-view consistency. Extensive experiments on real-world benchmark datasets show that our approach achieves competitive performance compared to state-of-the-art methods.

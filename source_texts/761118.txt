Luxar is an open-source, Python-first system that compiles arbitrary n-dimensional scientific data — points, lines, Gaussian splats, and triangle meshes — into compact, spatially indexed Zarr archives served to a browser-based WebGL viewer with HDR rendering, progressive streaming, and native n-dimensional navigation. It includes a pipeline for fitting microscopy volumes to Gaussian splats as a compact, continuous, GPU-renderable representation.

⚡ Bolt: Add viewport bounding box culling in BuildingRenderer
💡 What:
Added AABB (Axis-Aligned Bounding Box) coordinate caching for building polygons in BuildingRenderer.ts.
Hoisted viewport map bounds extraction outside of per-building loops during camera re-renders.
Added viewport spatial culling with 50% padding to skip vertex projection (map.project) and PixiJS polygon drawing operations for off-screen buildings.
Added unit tests in src/rendering/BuildingRenderer.test.ts.
🎯 Why:
During map pan and zoom interactions, rebuildOnCameraChange was re-projecting and drawing every building in the dataset, even if 90%+ were off-screen.
Spatial culling eliminates redundant coordinate projection math and GPU drawing allocations for off-screen footprints.
📊 Impact:
Reduces coordinate projection calculations (map.project) and PixiJS graphics calls by ~90% for off-screen buildings during camera pan/zoom events.
🔬 Measurement:
Verified with unit tests in src/rendering/BuildingRenderer.test.ts checking that off-screen buildings are culled and map.project is not called for them.
Ran pnpm test and pnpm build successfully.

Low window shaft: fog_ref_err_near 0.0483, limit 0.05, margin 0.0017. The F1 2-shadow-ray record was the same figure. history_accepts is 0. A lit previous volume offered to a dark frame leaves the composed radiance at 0.
Departures from Hillaire 2015
Temporal reprojection is the identity (ADR 0026 decision 5). The stage is called and does not read the previous volume.
Extinction is the closed-form optical depth of the slice, not a cell-centre density sample.
In-scattering is single-scatter direct light. Probe indirect is stage F4 and is not added.
Shadows are rays against the boxes, not shadow maps.
Compose stays the F1 trilinear read of the integrated column. ADR 0026 decision 5 also names tricubic upsampling; this reference does not add it.
The GPU density, integrate, and compose pass stays on lighting-v3. Renderer rows stay NOT-IMPLEMENTED. Fog milliseconds in the plan are still models.
Risks
The Low shaft margin is 0.0017. The GPU half of F2 must recheck it. If it slips, the next step is 3×3 reuse, then height-fog-only.
Turning fog history on is still an open decision. This reference fails the test if a previous volume is blended.
The graded error is the F1 integral, not a new error budget. A GPU pass has to match this reference within 1% and still clear 0.05 on the shaft.

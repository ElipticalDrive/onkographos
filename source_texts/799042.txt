Summary
Billboard impostor tuning (PR #19/#20) still produced flat slabs / clipped planes in coordinator review. This PR abandons billboard smoke preview and renders from the cooked density grid instead.
Approach: density projection preview
ViewportSmoke now carries resolution + density_frames from the Eulerian cook
wgpu draws background + grid/collider overlays only
CPU voxel splat + MIP compositing (smoke_density.rs) projects density onto the framebuffer:
Threshold voxels, project to screen, gaussian splat into a max field
Soft alpha composite with warm smoke tint
Sparse presets (sphere obstacle) get wider splats
Particle billboards remain in the data model for future use but are skipped when density grids exist
QA
Uses GPU_CLEAR_RGBA (actual wgpu sRGB readback) for metrics
Rejects empty/sparse frames, background wash, and sharp rectangular slabs (preview_bright_bbox_fill_ratio)
Requires soft multi-blob cloud silhouette (not single clipped polygon)
Artifacts (regenerated)

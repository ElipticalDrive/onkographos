Pull request overview
Adds initial 3D wire-mesh “elements” support (mesh construction, function support pairing, and local mass/stiffness assembly) and exposes the new operator API, with accompanying tests.
Changes:
Introduces WireMesh3D for validating named 3D polylines, detecting collisions/intersections, and generating a flat subsegment index.
Adds MeshFunctions to enumerate function support via subsegment pairs and WireMesh3DFill to assemble mass/stiffness contributions over overlapping support.
Expands test coverage for 3D wire meshing and fillers; re-exports the new operator symbols from doodler/__init__.py.

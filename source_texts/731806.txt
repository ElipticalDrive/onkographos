What happens
A StandardMaterial3D with uv1_triplanar on (object or world space) renders correctly in the rasteriser but is traced with the mesh's plain UVs. On a BoxMesh, a CylinderMesh or a SurfaceTool mesh without meaningful UVs, the albedo, roughness and normal textures collapse to a stretched sample or one flat colour, so raster and path traced views of the same material disagree completely.
Minimal reproduction
Make a BoxMesh 20 x 0.2 x 3 m.
Give it a StandardMaterial3D with a high-frequency albedo texture (a checker or gravel), uv1_triplanar = true, uv1_scale = Vector3(0.5, 0.5, 0.5).
Light it and compare raster with path tracing: raster shows the tiled texture; path tracing shows the box's default UV layout stretched over 20 m (lengthwise streaks, or a near-uniform colour).
Why
Raster triplanar is code that BaseMaterial3D generates into its shader. The path tracer's hit path never runs that code.
The triplanar flags are shader-code state, not shader params, so they never reach the params table process_material snapshots: render_raytracing.cpp L1861-L1874 reads uv1_scale and uv1_offset only.
The hit shader then samples with h.uv * mat.uv1_scale + mat.uv1_offset: scene_raytracing_raygen.glsl L531.
UV1 scale and offset do work, so a mesh with real UVs and a non-triplanar material traces correctly.
Workaround in the game
Surfaces that must read under path tracing get their own UVs, in metres, from a small SurfaceTool helper, with a non-triplanar material whose uv1_scale is 1 / tile size.
Found while building a game project against fixes at 66860fa.
Compared with NVIDIA's Godot RTX: not fixed there. nvidia-pt-dlss and nvidia-pt-dlss-dev have no triplanar handling in render_raytracing.cpp or scene_raytracing_raygen.glsl.
A fix exists, though. It is item 4 on NVIDIA-RTX/godot#11: Puddlefunk/godot bc91da5 "Support StandardMaterial3D triplanar mapping under pathtracing". It exposes the triplanar flags and sharpness to the material snapshot (material.cpp, render_raytracing.cpp/.h, raytracing_data_inc.glsl) and adds a triplanar sampler to the hit path (raytracing_closest_hit_common_inc.glsl, scene_raytracing_raygen.glsl), 6 files, +147 -13.
Proposal: take it, after the commits it sits on. On its own it does not apply to fixes (git apply --check fails in render_raytracing.cpp, render_raytracing.h and raytracing_data_inc.glsl): it comes after 6012a95 (emission without a texture, proposed in #1) and 4212a9c (cast_shadow, proposed in #2) in the same series. Taking #1, #2 and this in that order should stack; if not, the conflicts are in the material snapshot struct and its packing. Check after: world triplanar on a moving node (a world-space projection should swim, an object-space one should not), and normal maps under triplanar (the tangent frame comes from the projection axis, not the mesh).

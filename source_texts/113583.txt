Re-opens #162, which was merged by accident and is reverted by #168. These are the same five commits re-applied on top of the revert (new SHAs, identical tree). The base is the revert branch, so the diff shows the full change; once #168 merges, GitHub retargets this PR to main.
Adaptive resolution built from the existing infrastructure, as a tier API: tsdf::AdaptiveGrid fuses into a stack of ordinary VoxelBlockGrids at halving voxel sizes (2 cm, 1 cm, 5 mm by default) and gives a block finer voxels only where the depth is systematically off the coarser level's surface. adaptive_viewer drives it end to end on Replica, one Orbbec camera or the four-camera rig. This replaces the retired octree trial (#138–#155, tag archive/hierarchical-octree-trial); DECISIONS.md records why.
tsdf::AdaptiveGrid (include/volumetric_kit/recon/tsdf/adaptive_grid.hpp)
create(device, allocator, AdaptiveGridConfig), fuse(std::vector<FrameInput>) — the integrator's own set type, host or device depth, optional colour — owned_blocks(level) for the culled extract, level(l), stats(l), sensor_floor(), set_refine_offset(). Pimpl, move-only, Status/Result.
The coarsest level fuses every frame. A finer level allocates only from depth masked to refined coarser blocks (GPU mask) and integrates the whole frame, so it still carves free space. A block's parent is its coordinate halved in the coarser hash; nothing stores a pointer. Each level allocates and integrates once per set and grows its map when it fills. Every level is a complete grid, so stamps, GC, extraction and the codec work on it unchanged.
Criterion: a depth point's residual is the coarser TSDF at it over the TSDF's gradient length (distance along the normal, independent of view angle). Residuals sum per 2×2×2-voxel cell across sets; a check turns each cell into its systematic offset, mean² − variance / n, so sensor noise cancels. The median coarsest block's offset is the sensor's floor. A block refines when its offset exceeds floor + refine_offset and its remaining noise; it coarsens after calm_checks checks below floor + half of it, or once its surface is gone (Dynamic cleared it). A block merely out of view keeps its detail. Coarsening removes the finer blocks it leaves.
Kernels: adaptive_residual.comp, adaptive_offset.comp, adaptive_mask.comp (+ adaptive_common.glsl).
adaptive_viewer meshes each level's owned blocks and draws them together. Live frames are prepared on the GPU (GpuFramePrep, H.265). Options: dynamic TSDF with weight cap 20 by default live, --color 3840x2160, a live threshold slider, level colours, mouse orbit/pan/zoom.
Measured (Apple M5 Max, Release)
room0, 400 preloaded frames, all three levels, re-meshed and textured whenever the renderer took the last mesh: 7.2 ms host per fused frame (171 remeshes); 571 of 8,692 2 cm blocks refine over a 0.14 mm floor.
Offline prototype study (not in this PR), eps = 1 mm: 0.86× the voxels of uniform 1 cm; mesh within 0.26 / 0.46 mm (accuracy / coverage p95) of uniform 5 mm, against 0.79 / 0.86 mm for uniform 1 cm.
Femto Mega, one camera: per-frame residual noise ~3.4 mm; floor ~3 mm (host-registered depth) / ~7 mm (GPU-prepared), equal at 2 cm and 1 cm. About 6% of 2 cm blocks refine at eps = 1 mm.
Four-camera rig, GPU path: ~24–29 sets/s; ~16 ms per set at 1280×720 colour, ~23 ms at 4K (camera-capped at 25 fps).
Validation
Release builds warning-clean: full build with tests, examples and the Orbbec/FFmpeg viewers; and a viewer build without Orbbec (the viewer CI leg's configuration).
ctest: 62/62 pass, including the new recon_tsdf_adaptive_grid (wall + sphere: refines only near the sphere, coarsens and removes finer blocks once it leaves, refuses invalid configs/frames, move semantics). It runs with the Khronos layer where installed and fails on any validation error; it passes with synchronization validation forced on locally.
Live runs: Replica, one camera and the four-camera rig.
Known limits / next
Refinement cannot tell calibration error or ToF bias from shape: on the rig (calibration currently stale — a camera was moved) overlapping floor refines. Next: check that a refined block's offset actually fell at the finer level, and revert it otherwise.
A small object cut by block boundaries splits its evidence (a block is judged from two sampled cells).
Seams between levels are not stitched; the viewer textures from the set's first camera only.
Each level submits its own allocate/integrate; owned_blocks compacts on the host.

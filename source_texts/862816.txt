Published on Jan 13, 2019
Thanks to the Mandelbulber developer community and Stephen Sink for his tutorial on keyframing 3D fractals. Buddhi and the team have developed an awesome application.
I used a random number generator to select 3 fractals from 98 possible fractals to make a hybrid fractal in Mandelbulber. There are a possible 152,000 fractal combinations of 3 in 98 so I used the unix 'rand' utility to choose them. The experiment worked for me because of the large number of combinations in Mandelbulber available and the random choice seems novel. This is an option for those of us who don't want to rely on our faded brains to navigate the 3D fractal universe.
The animated backgrounds were made with Gimp MathMap. The fractal render took 12 hours on one computer. Then 4,000 PNGs with alpha backgrounds were imported to Gimp. I hit a memory wall and had to split the render into 5 chunks.

Modern procedural content generation increasingly relies on artificial intelligence to bridge the gap between human intent and automated world-building. This paper explores the potential of advanced neural models in transforming abstract semantic layouts into complex, structured terrain heightmaps. By examining how conditional generative frameworks interpret sparse inputs and structural boundaries, the study evaluates their suitability for next-generation game engines and interactive design tools. While addressing inherent challenges in resolution scalability and structural fidelity, this research outlines a pathway toward intuitive, AI-driven cartography and dynamic environment synthesis.

Summary
Adds a Remesh section to the Unit Sphere page (book/examples/gallery/academic/unit_sphere.md), between Quality After Smoothing and Control Study.
The section remeshes the $n = 10$ marching cubes surface (3,800 triangles) to the mean edge length of Octa-Loop level 4 (0.1178). It compares four surfaces: the marching cubes surface, the remeshed surface, the surface after Taubin 20, remesh, Taubin 20, and Octa-Loop level 4.

Remeshing alone makes the triangles uniform but does not round the steps. Smoothing before and after the remesh cuts the radius RMS from 0.01779 to 0.00688. That is still 4.4 times the Octa-Loop value. The page also tabulates edge length CoV, minimum angle, vertex radius range, area, and volume.
Contents
unit_sphere.md: the new section, with commands, figure, table, and prose.
unit_sphere_remesh.py: runs automesh remesh and automesh smooth, prints the tables, and draws the figure.
unit_sphere_remesh.png: the four-panel figure, committed as a static asset like the page's other figures.
Reproducibility
```
cd book/examples/gallery/academic
uv run --with numpy --with matplotlib unit_sphere_remesh.py
```
automesh must be on PATH (tested with 0.5.1). The script reproduces every number in the table.
Notes
The page cites one trial that unit_sphere_remesh.py does not run. With 20 iterations in every step, five orders of smoothing and remeshing gave a radius RMS of 0.00739 to 0.00903, against 0.00688 for smooth, remesh, smooth.
The pull request excludes the uncommitted edit to src/mesh/mod.rs. That edit waits on the conspire pairing #239 release.

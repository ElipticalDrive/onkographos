Summary
Our FD helpers to auto-derive AD endpoints (like tesseract_core.runtime.experimental.finite_difference_vjp currently have poor discoverability. I suggest we add a tutorial or how-to guide that introduces them, and guides users through picking the right method.
Why is this needed?
Several hackathon projects ended up re-implementing FD instead of using them.

Feedback from the 2026 hackathon adds weight to this one. Participants who wrapped tools without gradients asked for a worked example of wrapping a forward-only tool and estimating its derivative, without finding the finitediff and gradient-fallbacks examples that already cover it.
I suggest we frame the guide around the question users ask ("my tool has no gradients, what now?") rather than the names of the helpers. It should link both examples and be linked from the how-to section and from the "Do you need Tesseract?" page (#821). One case worth covering is a forward tool that is a closed-source executable, which the Tesseract calls as a subprocess.

Ucsc-crl-90-38: a Coherent Projection Approach for Direct Volume Rendering
Abstract Direct volume rendering using semi-transparency offers the opportunity to visualize all of a three-dimensional sample volume in one image. However, because all cells may contribute something to the image, processing such images can be very expensive and good quality high- resolution images are far from interactive.

Volume rendering (VR)
Volume rendering is a data visualization technique that creates a three-dimensional representation of CT and MRI volume data. This technique can also be applied to tomosynthesis data. Volume rendering is used less in general ultrasound but has been used in echocardiography.
Clinical applications
Volume rendering is primarily used to better visualize human anatomy, surgical treatment planning, and medical teaching 1.

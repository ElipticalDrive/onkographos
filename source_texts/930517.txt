The terrain rendering system has been refactored to use a more efficient GPU-driven mesh pipeline. Instead of relying on a tessellation shader to displace a flat grid using heightmaps, the system now uploads absolute vertex data (positions, normals, biomes) to a persistent VBO. A compute shader (terrain_cull.comp) perform fine-grained culling at the patch level and writes the indices of visible patches into a dynamic EBO. Rendering is then performed via a single indirect draw call using standard triangles. For compatibility with other procedural systems (fire effects and decor placement), the heightmap and biome textures are still maintained and updated during chunk registration.

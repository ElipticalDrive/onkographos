What
A new module, atmosphere-fog: fog as the engine's media shading hook atmosphere.fog (engine 0.192.0), integrated along every view ray through regions the plugin writes each frame. Before, fog differences came from per-instance override entries: a point took its instance's entry for the whole ray to the camera, so the air outside a window took the room's clear override. Now what is fogged depends on where the ray runs: the air outside a window is fogged up to the glass, and the room behind it stays clear, seen from outside and from inside.
Outdoor fog: exponential height fog (density, height falloff, base height, albedo), lit by isotropic ambient radiance and the sun through Henyey-Greenstein. Authored as the hook's values on a scene component (engine-scene's ShadingHookComponent).
FogVolume component: box or ellipsoid placed, turned and scaled by its node; density, albedo, height falloff, blend distance.
IndoorZone component: box or ellipsoid; EXCLUDE (outdoor fog out) or REPLACE (its own air); light dims the outdoor light reaching media inside it.
FogRegions gathers the components from the scene in world terms; AtmosphereFog (a technique per view) keeps the nearest to the camera up to the caps and writes them camera-relative into a frame buffer, its address into the hook's record.
Integration: the ray is cut at the boundaries of the regions it crosses. Pieces in no region hold height fog only and are integrated in closed form. Pieces inside regions are integrated in homogeneous steps (Hillaire 2015), which is exact for hard-edged uniform regions.
Settings (FogSettings): atmosphere.fog.maxVolumes, maxZones, regionsPerRay (≤ 16), steps, cullDistance.
Build-logic 0.19.0 (shading hooks); the generated listing AtmosphereFogShadingHooks rebuilds the engine's shading with the hook. CI now sets up lavapipe for the GPU tests.
Known approximation, as documented: the sun's shadows are not in the media (no light shafts). Indoor zones dim the outdoor light instead.
Tests
FogVolumesTest (GPU, validated; emissive walls on black against closed-form values):
from outside through a window, the back wall shows only the 10 m of outdoor fog. With the zone removed it shows 20 m, so this part fails without the zones.
from inside, the room is clear and the fog starts at the window.
in-scattering comes only from the outdoor part of the ray.
a replacing zone fills the room with its own air.
caps keep the nearest zone, and regions beyond the cull distance are left out.
turning the fog off by its bit leaves the image unfogged.
fog volumes dim by density times path length: an ellipsoid, and a box turned and scaled by its node.
FogRegionsTest: world transforms, nesting, rotation and scale, modes, hidden nodes, growth, validation.
FogScreenshots (TRUESIM_SCREENSHOTS=true): a window in fog seen from outside and from inside, each with and without the zone.

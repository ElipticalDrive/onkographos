Written by Claude (AI) for @ArndRosemeier. This finishes stage 2 of the renderer plan.
What it does
Global illumination that needs no baking and relights itself when the world changes (destruction, building, streaming).
Voxel scene (render/gi.rs): a 64x32x64 voxel box (1 m voxels by default) follows the camera. It is rebuilt when the box moves, when the scene changes (render_epoch), and every 30 frames for moving props. Terrain under the height formula or atlas is filled by a compute pass. Meshes, batches and terrain meshes are rasterised into the volume once along each axis, writing albedo from fragments.
Light probes: a 32x16x32 grid at 2 m spacing. Every frame each probe traces 16 rays through the voxels with a DDA. A ray that escapes the box sees the sky dome. A ray that hits a voxel face picks up the sun on that face (using the real shadow maps) plus the probes' own light there from the previous frame, which gives multi-bounce light over a few frames. Results are stored as linear SH and blended over time; ray directions rotate every frame. A probe buried in geometry is marked dead and skipped.
Shading: pbr_shade now uses the probe light in place of the plain sky dome wherever probes cover a point. That means sky occlusion under roofs, in caves and in craters, plus coloured bounce such as grass green under eaves. Sky reflections dim by the same amount. Lookup uses an 8-probe weighted blend with normal offset, and it fades back to the sky dome at the edge of the box. Views through portals keep the old sky light, because the probes describe the live space only.
Setting: GlobalIllumination::new(probe_spacing_m), on by default at 2 m. world.set_global_illumination(None) turns it off.
Profiler: gpu_profile has a new gi phase.
Cost
On lavapipe (software rendering) GI takes about 15 ms of a 150 ms frame. That is less than SSAO (40 ms) or the world pass (70 ms). Not measured on the RTX 5070 yet.
Checks
200 unit tests pass, including 2 new ones (box snapping, shader binding remap).
Clippy has the same 8 errors as main and no new ones.
8 examples render with no validation errors. Before/after screenshots look right, and debug views of the probe light show darkness under eaves and inside the house, green bounce from the grass, and a fade to the sky dome outside the box.
Known limits / next steps
Voxel albedo comes from vertex colour and tint. Textures and terrain material colours are approximated, and terrain under the formula uses a fixed ground colour.
Thin walls under 1 m can leak some light. Smaller probe spacing helps, at the cost of coverage.
A single box covers about 64 m. Cascaded boxes are a natural follow-up for large outdoor views.

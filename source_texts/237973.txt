* Hole: place a Single hole by distances from reference edges (#37) (#2931)

A Single hole can take up to two straight edges of its face as references,
each with the distance of the hole's centre from that edge's line, measured
in the face's plane. `solid.hole` takes them as `references: [{edge, distance}]`;
the edges are found again by their persistent names, else their points, so
the hole follows them when the part changes, and each distance is a
parameter. A bad reference (no edge there, a curved edge, one square to the
face, parallel edges, a negative distance, a centre off the face) is refused.

row; the canvas shows each distance as a dimension from its edge to the hole,
with a box just past the edge to type it in. Picking an edge measures its
distance (and its box takes the keyboard), typing one moves the hole, and
moving the hole measures them again. Edit Feature picks the edges again with
their distances.

* Sketch offset keeps its source lines free to turn (#4464)

An offset adds a Parallel between each source line and its offset line.
Since offsets became associative, the offset's points are held during a
solve, so each Parallel pinned its source line to the offset line's

the association already keeps them parallel. After Break the stored

* Timeline: suppressing a feature keeps later features' body names (#4302)

Bodies numbered by the counter (`BodyN`) take the next free number on every
recompute, so suppressing a feature that made one moved every later counted
body down one (`Body2` became `Body1`) and broke what named it, such as a
Move of `Body2`. timeline.suppress now stores the current names of later
features' counted bodies in their `body_names` first, as body.rename already
does for a renamed counted body (#1109). Engine only: no evaluation or
design-format change.

Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>

* Timeline: a body made while a feature is suppressed keeps its name (#4302)

Suppressing a feature freed its counted name (`Body1`), so a body made
meanwhile took it and became `Body3` once the feature was back. Suppress
now also stores the suppressed feature's own counted names, and the
evaluator adds the names stored on earlier suppressed features to the
state's `reserved_body_names` (the names a rename freed, #4957), which the
`BodyN` counter passes by. They are taken from the design before each
feature is computed and are part of the state digest, so a cached result
is only reused when they match.

* Join: say so when the joined piece doesn't touch the body (#4434)

A Join (extrude, revolve, sweep, loft, pipe, coil, primitives, thicken,

pieces or closes a cavity, and leaves Combine (which joins the bodies it
is given) alone.

* Hole: place a Single hole by distances from reference edges (#37) (#2931)

A Single hole can take up to two straight edges of its face as references,
each with the distance of the hole's centre from that edge's line, measured
in the face's plane. `solid.hole` takes them as `references: [{edge, distance}]`;
the edges are found again by their persistent names, else their points, so
the hole follows them when the part changes, and each distance is a
parameter. A bad reference (no edge there, a curved edge, one square to the
face, parallel edges, a negative distance, a centre off the face) is refused.

In the Hole dialog, Reference 1 and 2 follow the face, each with its Distance
row; the canvas shows each distance as a dimension from its edge to the hole,
with a box just past the edge to type it in. Picking an edge measures its
distance (and its box takes the keyboard), typing one moves the hole, and
moving the hole measures them again. Edit Feature picks the edges again with
their distances.

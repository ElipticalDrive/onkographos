Closes #2 (Phase 1 – Ingest + voxelization). Plan: docs/PLAN.md; plain-language summary: docs/STATUS.md.
Plan / what changed
Ingest (src/mesh2brick/ingest/): loader registry; STL/OBJ/PLY via trimesh; own 3MF reader (core + production extension – slicer files keep geometry in referenced model parts that generic loaders miss), units → mm; normalisation (up axis, optional auto_orient, repair, outward normals).
Voxelization (voxel/voxelize.py, ADR 0003): column rasterization, 4×4 rays per stud, winding-number union, exact per-cell inside length, threshold on volume fraction; brick/plate cells; rounding rule (xy centred round_half_up, z from the lowest point); surface fallback for open meshes. Hollowing stays in the backend (ADR 0004).
Colours/files: single, height_bands (color.bands); .grid.npz, legolization-compatible .npy/.vox.
CLI: voxelize (size, volume error, shell estimate, preview, --vox/--npy); place accepts .grid.npz.
Backend local patch (docs/UPSTREAM.md Phase 1 – Ingest + voxelization #2): legolization 0.5.0 runs HiGHS without time limits; presolve stalls forever on some stability LPs (bunny @16 studs with slopes). Per-attempt 5 s limit in both LP paths; captured LP as regression fixture. Slopes are opt-in until fixed upstream (443 s vs 9 s even with the patch).
Defaults: target_studs 32 (PO decision), parts.categories [brick, plate].
CI jobs get timeout-minutes.
Acceptance (SPEC §5)
10 mm cube at mm_per_stud: 8 → 1×1 stud, 1 brick (rounding rule documented in ADR 0003)
Sphere at target_studs: 32: volume deviation 0.1 % (< 15 %)
All fixtures run through legolization without errors (tests/test_fixtures_pipeline.py, 12 studs)
Checks
ruff, mypy --strict, 257 tests (96 % coverage) green locally. New dependency: networkx (BSD-3, pure Python) for trimesh hole filling.
Known limitation (→ Phase 2)
Large models (e.g. a 4,500-cell local test model at 32 studs) take very long in legolization placement; time budget and strategy choice are the first Phase 2 topic.

* feat: phase 5 colour availability and strategy benchmark

- parts.availability: exclude (default), split, report for parts LEGO
  does not make in a colour (Rebrickable elements). exclude leaves them
  out of placement for the whole model (1x1 fillers stay, slopes all or
  none); split replaces them afterwards by existing shorter parts of the
  same colour with joints staggered against the layers above and below
  (place/split.py), exact coverage, rollback on failure, stability
  re-analysed.
- benchmark_placement.py reports parts not made in their colour and the
  placement notes.

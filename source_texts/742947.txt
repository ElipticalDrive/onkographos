Goal
Introduce an additive, GPU-ready geometry layer for static implicit interfaces on OpenPFC's existing structured Cartesian Field/grid infrastructure, without a general-purpose mesh generator or FEM package.
Use a signed implicit function phi (negative inside, positive outside, phi=0 interface). Support analytic plane, sphere and rotated ellipsoid, arbitrary origin/spacing and 2D/3D. Clarify that an ellipsoid polynomial is not generally a signed-distance function and exact analytic geometry is not equivalent to exact geometry reconstructed from sampled phi.
Deliverable
Stable CPU/CUDA/HIP-compatible evaluation of phi, analytic normal where known and robust numerical normal where applicable; explicit degeneracy handling.
Classify interior/exterior/cut cells, including tangent/near-zero cases, without treating a single nodal sign as proof of an uncut cell for a general implicit function.
Represent metadata in existing structured-grid indexing with a compact active-cell list; separate geometry preparation from hot GPU physics kernels.
Unit tests for translated/rotated ellipsoids, anisotropic spacing, periodic seams, degenerate normals and grid refinement. Compare classification and surface positions to independent analytic geometry.
First bounded GPU prototype, memory/cost measurements and tests on one available accelerator.
Limits
Geometry only, no PDE interface enforcement, no claim of exact volume fractions from voxel samples, no new general mesh framework. Preserve all current Field/FFT/halo behavior. DiscretizationLab owns comparative experiments.
Acceptance
A usable implicit-interface and packed active-cell API, with analytic geometric verification and tested device parity.

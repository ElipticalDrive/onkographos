I am a scientist-guise layman (民科 in Chinese), my understanding is probably wrong.
the GS authors are very powerful, so I just compare a few papers:
GS: this paper
DSS: Differentiable Surface Splatting for Point-based Geometry Processing
DPRF: Differentiable Point-Based Radiance Fields for Efficient View Synthesis
DPRF: princeton
shape: point , xyz-movable (raw point, so can be rendered by pytorch3d PC renderer)
color: learnable RGB SH coefficients per-point
render: differentable/e2e-ok; rast, direct pytorch3d point-cloud raster & render
tricks: coarse-to-fine strategy
others: no lighting
why-faster: point-cloud-splating rending than MLP-ray-marching, no sampling
DSS: eth
shape: anisotropic ellipse, xyz-movable (not raw-point, 2d and normals), points-add/del, ..
color: raw-rgb
render: differentable/e2e-ok; extended, in CUDA; performance-tricks: EWA
tricks: error-aware view sampling;
optimze: gradients-based
others: with lighting
GS:
shape: gauss-ball, xyz-movable, no normals but 3d-ball, scale dynamic, points-purne-clone, ...
color: learnable SH coefficients per-point, sh-degree(monotonic growth while training, in render, limited in code:2^4), powerful, more parameters
render: differentable/e2e-ok; extended, in CUDA; performance-tricks: tile
tricks: lots of
optimze: gradients-based, different-lr-for-different-parameters
others: no lighting, diffcult to surface
if right and useful, please one-key-double-click-triple-income: like, favor, coin. :-)

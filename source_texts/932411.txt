In this lesson, we're going over the basics of using the camera orbit function of the in-game camera. With this function, you can create smooth camera orbital movement centered around your character, vehicle (if you are in the pilot seat), as well as arc shots. Great for locations, showcases, and all sorts of cinematic shots.

Is your feature request related to a problem? Please describe.
PBS_Triplanar has a less than ideal performance impact. A single SphereMesh with PBS_TriplanarMetallic and a 2k noise texture applied to the albedo, normal, occlusion, and metallic textures can reduce my rendering frame time from >120fps to ~70fps when it fills my screen.
This has performance implications for a world I'm working on (the frog bog) which utilizes PBS_TriplanarMetallic heavily for the trees we have, unfortunately these trees are large and take up large portions of the screen leading to a large amount of render time being devoted to the triplanar material on the trees.
Describe the solution you'd like
I'd like for the PBS_Triplanar materials to have improved performance.
Describe alternatives you've considered
not using triplanar materials
ie. unwrapping the trees and baking a texture in a similar way to how triplanar works
this can work but is not ideal for the desired quality of the wood while keeping texture resolutions low and unique materials low (we use the same triplanar across all of the trees)
beyond that our artist does not want to unwrap the trees because it's a pain, and triplanar materials "just work" and handle it better than they could
Additional Context
It's notable to mention that worlds built using the rock convex hull brushes (or similar) will be impacted by this heavily as well, and upon reflection there have been several worlds I've worked on that used triplanar materials heavily with convex hull brushes to build the world, and had less than ideal performance in an otherwise "empty" world.
I do not know why the triplanar materials are so heavy, but it's worth noting that there's a newer version of the triplanar shader we use that was commited 6 years ago that removed a hack to work around unity.
see: bgolus/Normal-Mapping-for-a-Triplanar-Shader@a3571bf

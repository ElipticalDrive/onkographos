```
Compute-based metaball renderer: field evaluation and Marching Cubes mesh extraction run entirely on the GPU via compute shaders, with drawIndirect for dynamic vertex counts. 12 balls move on Lissajous curves in 3 groups with wanderer balls that periodically break away. Blinn-Phong + Fresnel lighting, bloom post-processing, orbit camera.
```

```
Reported symptom: ball bodies still read as white, with per-ball hue
only visible in shadowed/ambient-only regions. The rim and specular
terms are additive white light on top of the tinted diffuse/ambient,
so at grazing angles and highlight spots — a large fraction of a
small separated ball's visible area — they wash the color out toward
white regardless of the underlying tint. Tinting both closes that gap.
```

Evaluate whether the method of Toutouni, Chakraborty, Tu, and Huang, "OCTANE: Breaking the Neighbor-List Bottleneck in GPU Molecular Dynamics", ICS '26, pp. 30–41, https://doi.org/10.1145/3797905.3807878, is worth adopting in MDIR. Label D[octane]. The precedent is D82 (tiles): a prototype whose result is recorded as a decision whether or not the method is adopted.
The method. No neighbor structure is stored. Each step, a thread per atom starts at the leaf of the atom in a region octree, climbs to the smallest ancestor that contains its query box, and searches down from there, computing the forces of the pairs it finds (each pair twice, no atomic additions). Atoms that leave their leaf are moved to the new one; the tree is not rebuilt. Atoms are sorted along a Morton or Hilbert curve every step.
The bar is MDIR, not the paper's baseline. The paper compares with the GPU package of LAMMPS on Lennard-Jones argon, on other hardware, at a density that its Table 3 gives as about 9 × 10⁻⁴ atoms/Å³ (a vapor: about 4 neighbors within 3σ). MDIR already has the numbers of the other side: a build of its matrix or groups, amortized over the steps between builds, plus the loop over the list. A search every step costs what a build's tests of candidates cost; the prototype measures whether that prediction holds.
What will be done
A standalone CUDA prototype beside those of scripts/experiments/neighbor-structures (not a template of MDIR): the tree, the search with the forces, the update of the tree.
Exactness against all pairs: the set of pairs within the cutoff, and the forces.
Time per step on one RTX 3090, f32 positions, on the systems that MDIR has numbers for (JAC and Cellulose at the density of water) and on argon at the density of the paper and at that of the liquid, against the matrix and the groups of MDIR with their builds.
The analysis of the paper proved or corrected: the levels climbed by a search (its Eq. 2–7 and Tables 1–2), the levels climbed by an update (Eq. 9–13), with the periodic boundary; and the theorem that the paper does not state, that the search finds every pair within the cutoff under periodic boundaries after partial updates of the tree. The levels are also measured on real configurations.
What bounds an adoption, whatever the times: the paper has no excluded pairs (MDIR enters them at the build; a search every step tests them for every pair every step), no particle mesh Ewald, constraints, barostat, or bonded terms, and computes each pair twice where the groups of MDIR compute it once.

The analysis of volumetric data is a crucial part in the visualizationpipeline, since it determines the features in a volume dataset andhenceforth, also its rendering parameters. Unfortunately, volumeanalysis can also be a very tedious and difcult challenge.To cope with this challenge, this paper describes a novel infor-mation visualization driven, explorative approach that allows usersto perform an analysis in a comprehensive fashion.

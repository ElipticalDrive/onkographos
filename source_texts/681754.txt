Abstract
Liang and Ng (Metrika 68:83–98, 2008) proposed a componentwise conditional distribution method for L
p
-uniform sampling on L
p
-norm n-spheres. On the basis of properties of a special family of L
p
-norm spherical distributions we suggest a wide class of algorithms for sampling uniformly distributed points on n-spheres and n-balls in L
p
spaces, generalizing the approach of Harman and Lacko (J Multivar Anal 101:2297–2304, 2010), and including the method of Liang and Ng as a special case. We also present results of a numerical study proving that the choice of the best algorithm from the class significantly depends on the value of p.

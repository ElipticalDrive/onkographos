stacked on #853 and the render-perf pr
terrain past the render distance. every chunk the client sees is kept as 32³ voxel sections at five levels of detail, saved to disk per server or world, meshed on a worker thread from models baked per block state, and drawn by the gpu which walks the section tree each frame, culls against a hi-z, picks the detail by screen size and asks for the meshes it lacks. pomme-only, under the distant terrain option
also in here from measuring the branch against master:
streaming hitches fixed: chunk occlusion and the light engine on their own threads, uploads and chunk packets budgeted per frame, an o(world) unload removed. stream 1% lows at rd 32 went 27 -> 215 fps, at 64 2 -> 146
vsync off presents immediately like vanilla
gpu time per pass in f3 and the harness frame_times step
panics written into latest.log
chunk mesher hides faces as vanilla shouldRenderFace decides (same-kind translucent blocks, partial shapes), removes the dots and lines ice and snow showed from above
tests: unit tests for the sections, mesher, node tree and face rule. world/distant_terrain, world/render_distance, blocks/face_culling and blocks/grass_occlusion in game, the last three match vanilla. the chunk packet budget changes when the client answers a chunk batch so it needs a peel capture before merging
known gaps as TODOs in the code: generation beyond what's been seen, shader packs, dimensions without sky light

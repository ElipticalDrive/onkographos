This paper presents a new simple, fast and robust approach in computation of per-sample precise shadows. The\nmethod uses tessellation shaders for computation of silhouettes on arbitrary triangle soup. We were able to reach\nrobustness by our previously published algorithm using deterministic shadow volume computation. We also propose\na new simplification of the silhouette computation by introducing reference edge testing. Our new method\nwas compared with other methods and evaluated on multiple hardware platforms and different scenes, providing\nbetter performance than current state-of-the art algorithms. Finally, conclusions are drawn and the future work is\noutlined.

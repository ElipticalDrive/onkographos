Topology optimization based on volume integral methods is severely limited by the memory and computational cost of dense linear systems. This work considers the Magnetic Moment Method and integrates a low-rank Tensor Train compression strategy within an adjoint-based optimization framework to address these limitations. A quantized Tensor Train representation enables efficient solution of the direct and adjoint problems arising in the optimization loop. Preliminary results demonstrate significant reductions in memory usage and computation time, enabling larger-scale optimization problems.

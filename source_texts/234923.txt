Problem
The retained raster captures render the back-facing surfaces of the canonical Voxel Scene instead of the outward-facing surfaces. Near outward faces are culled, so farther faces are visible through the volume and the scene appears inside-out.
The completion bundle from #29 must not be treated as valid visual evidence until this bug is fixed and the affected evidence is regenerated.
Confirmed diagnosis
The raster artifact itself is correct:
Both triangles of every diagnostic quad wind consistently with the face's declared outward normal.
Vertex normals match the semantic outward face normals.
The mismatch occurs after camera projection:
The perspective matrix intentionally flips Y for Vulkan so world-up remains visually upward with the positive-height viewport.
A camera-facing positive-Z diagnostic triangle projects counter-clockwise in framebuffer coordinates. The measured signed area was -36901.688.
The graphics pipeline enables back-face culling but declares vk::FrontFace::CLOCKWISE.
Therefore the outward camera-facing triangle is classified as a back face and culled, while the opposite/back-facing surface is classified as front-facing and rendered.
A temporary diagnostic changed only the pipeline front-face declaration to COUNTER_CLOCKWISE; the projected-winding test changed from red to green. Changing the projection Y sign instead also changes winding, but maps world-up downward and is not a valid fix.
Reproduction
Add a public-seam raster regression that:
Derives the real one-voxel raster artifact.
Selects the camera-facing positive-Z semantic face.
Transforms its indexed triangle through CameraPose::view_projection.
Applies the same positive-height Vulkan viewport transform.
Verifies the framebuffer winding agrees with the pipeline front-face declaration.
Before the fix, the camera-facing triangle is counter-clockwise while the pipeline expects clockwise.
Acceptance criteria
The raster pipeline treats counter-clockwise framebuffer winding as front-facing while retaining back-face culling.
Quad vertex order, outward semantic normals, and the Vulkan projection Y correction remain unchanged unless new evidence proves they are independently wrong.
A permanent regression test exercises the real derived artifact, camera transform, viewport convention, and configured front-face state; it fails on the current implementation and passes after the fix.
Validation-enabled diagnostic and canonical captures visibly show the nearest outward surfaces rather than farther back-facing surfaces through the volume.
Existing semantic-face, lifecycle, failure, prerequisite, formatting, strict lint, and workspace tests pass with zero Vulkan validation warnings or errors.
Affected canonical, lifecycle, timing, and milestone-completion evidence is regenerated at the fixed reviewed revision; manifests and hashes verify successfully.
The refreshed completion bundle replaces the visually invalid Capture and verify the milestone completion bundle #29 bundle and keeps runtime claims limited to the recorded Windows development machine.
Non-goals
Reordering face templates or reversing semantic normals.
Removing the required Vulkan projection Y correction.
Disabling back-face culling as a workaround.

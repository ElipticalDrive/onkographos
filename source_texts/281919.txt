Summary
The first third of CH3.0. nd-octree gains the maximum leaf width of proposal §3.7 as
OctreeOptions::with_min_leaf_level, its key-space form. Every box that holds a key and
lies above the level is refined, capped at max_level. The default, 0, adds no condition.
The rule acts in create_local_tree, below the blocks, never in the coarse tree's
weight rounds. The blocks therefore stay nodes of the one-rank tree, and the leaves are
the one-rank leaves on every rank count (O1).
Also in this PR:
D1 signed off (kD ≤ 500 at 10⁻³ to 10⁻⁸, as recommended);
CH3.0 split into CH3.0a (this), CH3.0b (the builder's leaf rule on W1–W4) and
CH3.0c (complex densities); the user: "Split a/b/c, proceed".
Plan and record: docs/helmholtz/tasks/CH3.0a.md.
Acceptance (CH3.0a's Done when)
With the option set, every leaf holding a key lies at or below the level (capped by
max_level), on any number of ranks:
test_create_local_tree_min_leaf_level_against_brute_force;
test_mpi_weighted_partition at 1, 2, 3, 4 and 8 ranks.
The leaves are the one-rank leaves and a brute-force refinement's:
against a plain recursion, key for key, for max_keys 1, 8 and 64, max_level 3, 5
and 16, and levels 0–6;
the coarse-tree-by-weight test with the level set, at 1–512 shares;
the MPI example: the cube and the Plummer sphere at levels 4 and 5, from the share
and from rank 0. The rule takes the cube from 64 leaves to 4,012 and 15,282, and the
sphere from 232 to 1,184 and 3,872.
Unset, every tree and Laplace's outputs are bit for bit unchanged (constraint 1):
level 0 equals the tree without the option, in both tests;
nd-fmm-bench --write-output from main (e876cbc) and from the branch on the M6
gives 36 of 36 outputs identical byte for byte. The inputs were the cube and the
Plummer sphere (N = 10⁵, f64), at p = 3, 6 and 8, with Dense, Classes, Rotation,
Batched, PlaneWave and Auto.
No D9 timing runs: the user, "Bytes only, no D9".

box, so the trees are the same key for key, and the FMM outputs are identical byte for
byte (above). The evaluate path is not touched.

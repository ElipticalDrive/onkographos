voxel.filters.apply_filter(volume, kernel, stride=1, padding_mode='replicate') -> vx.Volume
¶
Apply a filter kernel to a volume with 'same'-style output extents.
Channels are filtered independently. When strided, the grid is downsampled
to a spatial size of ceil(baseshape/stride)ceil(baseshape / stride)ceil(baseshape/stride) and the volume geometry is
updated to reflect the new voxel spacing.

What to build
The lit, ray-marched Smoke screen (ADR-0067 amended, its one exception to drawing no volume), in the pass order lighting -> smoke lighting -> smoke march -> translucent forward.
The smoke density volume: one byte per voxel of the Map's grid on the GPU, written from PresentationWorld's density update.
Smoke lighting: a compute pass over the occupied voxels only, once per frame and shared by both eyes in VR: one sun shadow ray per voxel by ray query, the cloud's own shadow by marching the density volume toward the sun, and the ambient light from the probe grid, never the sky. Local lights reach the smoke through the probes alone. The result is a lit volume beside the density.
Smoke march: from the camera through the lit volume to the opaque depth, at fixed steps from a fixed start, skipping space outside the live Smoke screens' bounds. Density is filtered across voxels and shaped by a world-space 3D noise offset by the Smoke screen's seed and drifting with its time, so the noise lives in the world, never in the screen. Single scattering only. It composites the in-scattered light and the transmittance over the lit image.
No quality setting: desktop marches at render resolution.
Acceptance criteria
Lavapipe test (augusta_client_renderer_smoke_tests): with a Smoke screen between the camera and a lit wall, a probe on the wall reads greyer than without it.
Lavapipe test: an indoor cloud reads darker than an outdoor one under the sun.
Smoke lighting runs over occupied voxels only, once per frame; the march leaves no per-pixel noise.
The pack's memory estimate counts the smoke GPU volumes.
Smoke lighting and smoke march carry NVTX ranges.
ADR-0067 is amended; the renderer README describes the passes.

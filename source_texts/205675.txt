Summary
docs/maths/intersections.md covers:
Ray tests:
Plane, with Unity's Plane.Raycast.
Sphere, with early outs before the square root.
Axis-aligned box with the slab method, including IEEE infinities and the NaN edge case.
Oriented box, via local space.
Triangle, with Möller–Trumbore: barycentrics and back-face culling.
Overlap tests: sphere–sphere, box–box, sphere–box (the closest point on the box), point in an oriented box, and capsules.
Separating axis test: the projected-radius formula, and which axes to test for 2D polygons (edge normals), two oriented boxes (15 axes), and a triangle against a box (13). Also how to skip degenerate cross products, and the minimum translation axis.
Precision: epsilons, self-hits, and shared edges.
Common pitfalls.

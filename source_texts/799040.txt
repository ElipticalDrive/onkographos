On a moon page, Saturn's billboard vanished whole once its centre slid behind the moon, because visibility was one ray test at the centre. Now a billboard hides only when the nearer sphere covers its whole image, rings included; a partial overlap is drawn stacked just under that sphere, also mid-drag while depth ranks are frozen.
Proves the change: headless Chromium on /titan/, one held drag moving Saturn across Titan's limb: main drops Saturn at step 20, this branch keeps it under Titan. Also checked with Saturn starting behind the camera: it stacks at the stage's z-index − 1, not over Titan. New world-frame-projection test covers clear, partly covered and wholly covered discs at three scales.

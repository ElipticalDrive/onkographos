The "dual contouring" scheme apparently allows to better capture sharp features of a surface. It's mentioned in this article.
Using this scheme with mandelbulb is difficult, as it needs the normal for each point. For a mathematically correct mandelbulb it's impossible to obtain any normal information. But in practice we limit the number of iteration and normal approximations might be good enough.

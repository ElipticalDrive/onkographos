Phase 5 — ATMOSPHERE & POLISH
Task
Implement volumetric fog/mist in valleys using custom Three.js shader.
Approach
Option A: Full-screen quad with raymarching shader (most realistic)
Noise-based density (3D simplex/Perlin)
Altitude falloff: dense below 1600m, zero above 2000m
Sun scattering (God Rays integration)
Option B: THREE.FogExp2 + custom vertex/fragment shader on terrain
Simpler, cheaper
Vertex: fade based on Y
Fragment: noise texture for variation
Shader Uniforms
glsl uniform float uTime; uniform vec3 uSunPosition; uniform float uFogDensity; uniform float uFogHeightFalloff; uniform sampler3D uNoiseTexture; // or 2D + time offset
Performance Budget
<2ms GPU at 1080p
Toggleable via settings
Acceptance
Mist visible in valleys (Alpe Chiarino, Alpe Vegessa) in morning
Clears on peaks (Pizzo Alto, Bocchetta di Larec)
Animates subtly (slow noise scroll)

Objective
Select a small, legally accessible real CT projection dataset for integration testing and research.
Candidate
Mayo Clinic/TCIA Low Dose CT and Projection Data, including DICOM-CT-PD where accessible.
Tasks
Verify access conditions, dataset license/terms, file organization, and representative examination size.
Identify measurement encoding and scanner geometry documentation.
Determine whether data are counts, corrected measurements, or transformed projection values.
Identify available reconstructed reference images and their relationship to the projection series.
Record download instructions and checksums without committing large data to Git.
Acceptance criteria
Dataset and access requirements are documented.
One small representative examination is identified.
File format and metadata needs are listed.
Legal/redistribution restrictions are recorded.
No data are downloaded or committed automatically by CI.
Backlog ID: M3-01
Depends on: #35 (DOC-03), #36 (DOC-04), #52 (M1-07)
Workflow: Follow the repository's Red → Green → Refactor TDD process. Add/adjust Catch2 tests before production implementation, preserve existing tests, and keep CI green.

Goal
A presentation rendering mode next to the analysis one: lit, shadowed, depth-cued images of cubes, VBT volumes and particle datasets, for figures, outreach and movies. The analysis rendering (Composite / MIP / MinIP, threshold, colour bar) stays as it is; this is a style the user switches on when the picture matters more than reading values.
Two families, one per kind of data.
1. Volumes (FITS cubes, VBT fields): cinematic volume rendering
The VTK we build against (9.5+, 9.6.2 locally) already has what is needed:
Screen-space ambient occlusion for volumes: vtkSSAOPass with SetVolumeOpacityThreshold, volume shading on, a Fixed32 depth format. It occludes the first surface above an opacity threshold, which fits our threshold-driven view. Kitware reports real-time cost, and 3D Slicer finds it usable even in VR.
Volumetric scattering / shadows on the GPU ray caster: SetVolumetricScatteringBlending, SetGlobalIlluminationReach (mapper) and SetScatteringAnisotropy (vtkVolumeProperty). Better looking, but much slower. Slicer's experience is that it can look artificial and fails on some GPUs, so it should be optional, with a quality setting.
Path tracing (OSPRay or ANARI) would give the most photorealistic result, but neither module is in our current VTK builds. Treat it as a later, offline-only option.
References:
https://discourse.vtk.org/t/how-to-perform-cinematic-rendering-photorealistic-volume-rendering-of-medical-images-using-open-source/9716/11
https://www.kitware.com/screen-space-ambient-occlusion-for-volumes/
https://www.kitware.com/volumetric-rendering-in-vtk-and-paraview-introducing-the-scattering-model-on-gpu/
2. Particles (point VBT, simulation snapshots, catalogues): Splotch-style rendering
For SPH / N-body snapshots, a ray-traced splatting renderer in the style of Splotch (Dolag et al. 2008; GPU and MPI versions since). Each particle is a Gaussian kernel with its own smoothing length. Light is accumulated along the ray with emission and absorption, and colour and intensity come from chosen fields. The result shows gas and dark-matter structure as a continuous medium, not as points. Our point viewer currently uses vtkPointGaussianMapper, which splats but does not integrate emission/absorption.
Options to evaluate:
run Splotch itself as a backend renderer (MPI + GPU on an HPC node, frames or movies back to the client); check that its licence is compatible;
or implement the same model as a GPU pass in our VTK pipeline, so it also works locally.
Requirements
It works locally and on any HPC backend: client-side for data the desktop holds, and in the backend render session (EGL) for remote views, through the same render-state commands.
Interactive degrade. Reduced quality while rotating and full quality on release, as the remote view already does with its GPU-time budget. A still / movie export renders at full quality offline.
The colour map, colour range and threshold keep their meaning, and the colour bar stays truthful.
Steps (proposal)
3-D view toggle Cinematic: SSAO plus shading on the local volume. Measure the cost on Apple GPUs and on PLEIADI V100s.
The same toggle in the backend render session.
Scattering, off by default, with a quality slider, and on in the movie export.
Splotch-style particle rendering: decide between integrating Splotch and a native pass, then prototype it on a point VBT snapshot.

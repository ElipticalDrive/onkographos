What the game has
infinity-shout's Kolvik is an interior station of rooms and corridors (52×42 m, ~440 static meshes, ~2k runtime entities, shadowed lights whose ranges cross walls). More levels of that kind are planned.
What exists (code at e68b6d8)
Visibility is frustum culling only, per entity AABB (src/render/frustum/mod.rs); LODGroup is manual (render/lod.rs); no occlusion queries, no Hi-Z, no portals, no PVS, no small-object/screen-size culling.
Looking down a corridor, the frustum contains the rooms behind its walls: their props go through the CPU draw build, the prepass/forward, and (for shadowed lights) the atlas casters. On immediate-mode GPUs (Windows/Linux), that is overdraw front-to-back sorting can't remove.
What should happen
As Unity (baked Umbra occlusion), Source / id Tech (baked PVS and portals for interiors), Unreal and Bevy 0.16 (two-phase Hi-Z occlusion on the GPU):
Short term: a baked cell/portal PVS. It fits rusty's bake pipeline (which already reasons per room: Reflection probes à la Unity: per-probe settings, blending, and auto-placement per room #1584 places probes per room), is deterministic and CPU-cheap, and gives per-light room masks that also bound the shadow-atlas casters.
Small-object culling: a per-renderer max draw distance / minimum screen size (UE's min screen radius, Unity's per-layer cull distances).
Long term: two-phase Hi-Z culling on the GPU, on top of the retained render scene (Retained render scene: interned handles and persistent GPU instance data, updated from changes, instead of rebuilding every draw from the ECS per camera and per shadow sweep #1706) and indirect draws.
Debug view and counters: objects culled by frustum / occlusion / size, per camera and per shadow view.
Found in a performance/architecture audit of the game; details in the game repo's docs/perf-audit/01-render-cpu.md (R-10). Related: #330, #472, #1584.

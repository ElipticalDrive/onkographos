Tracks ROADMAP task C3 (intersection half) for Mikail: the contract in INTERFACES §5
(Intersection and traversal) and the BruteQuery definition in §6 (Tracing queries).
ROADMAP cross-owner blocker fix 4 assigns BruteQuery to Mikail.
Ships as two stacked PRs into dev.
PR 1: Hit (unblocks Arek's NormalShading and shade_hit)
src/trace/hit.hpp: declarations-only Hit (16 B, POD) and RT_MISS_PRIM
tests/trace.cmake and tests/test_trace.cpp with Hit layout tests
PR 2: intersection and brute force
src/trace/intersect.hpp: stable-quadratic intersect_sphere, watertight
intersect_triangle (PBRT v4 6.5.3, including the double edge-function fallback)
src/trace/brute.hpp: brute_closest, brute_any, BruteQuery
CPU tests: hit, miss, grazing, inside sphere, degenerate triangle, shared-edge tie,
vertex hit, interval boundaries, empty scene, order independence, double fallback
tests/test_trace_device.cu: CUDA kernel runs the trace functions and compares with
the host (CUDA builds only)
Contract note W4: .cu sources for CUDA-only test targets in tests/
Optional: device camera_ray + brute_closest check
Out of scope: traverse_* / BvhQuery (needs build_bvh), backend.hpp, the GPU smoke
image (needs NormalShading, surface_at, and an owner for render_pixel).

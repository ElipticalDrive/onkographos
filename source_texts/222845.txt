Problem
Viewport ambient occlusion currently shades model surfaces, but the existing virtual ground used by the ellipse ground shadow is not an AO receiver. Small feet and nearby lower faces consequently have no ground contact shading. A broad ellipse cannot describe these individual contacts.
Proposed behavior
Extend the existing Ambient occlusion setting to include a virtual ground receiver. Keep AO independent of the ground-shadow effect: disabling Ground shadow disables the ellipse/projected shadow, while AO can still show contact shading. Preserve the default AO-off setting.
Reuse the existing AO depth/sampling path; do not add a separate Gaussian shadow implementation in this change.
Use the existing visible-body bounds and camera up-axis convention: ground height is bounds.min.z - 0.001 * (1 + bounds.diagonal()) in the up-axis frame. Do not clamp to the world origin or move the origin grid.
Keep receiver geometry bounded and exclude hidden bodies, respect clipping and visual styles, and check Z-up/Y-up, below-ground views, live previews, transparency, MSAA/SSAA and resize behavior.
AO off must skip the additional drawing work. Measure the incremental CPU/GPU cost against model-only AO on hardware and software adapters. Do not describe two triangles as proof that the fragment work is cheap.
Preserve wasm/WebGPU/WebGL2 support; coordinate with the separate OpenGL compatibility work in OpenGL startup crash: ambient-occlusion shader uses unsupported depth-texture textureLoad (WSL2) #2888 / Fix OpenGL startup with portable AO depth reads (#2888) #2933.
Reproduction and exploratory evidence
A small public-safe model contains a plate on four rectangular feet and a separate floating sphere. Use the same orthographic camera and compare AO off with a prototype ground receiver. The feet gain local contact shading instead of only a broad model-wide ellipse.

Completion evidence
Provide fresh before/after app images from the delivery branch, geometry/visibility regression coverage, measurements with clear viewport/adapter settings, and native + wasm validation. Keep this PR independent of projected shadows and SSAO denoising. No physical floor is added to the design file.

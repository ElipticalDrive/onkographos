Breaking: this changes default output. 3AREA, 3AREA_2_VOLUME, 3COMPACTNESS1, 3COMPACTNESS2, 3SPHERICAL_DISPROPORTION and 3SPHERICITY move from nyxus's documented voxel-face convention to IBSI's mesh definitions, and change value on every 3D ROI. No setting restores the old values. The commit is marked ! with a BREAKING CHANGE: footer.
What changes
3AREA is now the area of the marching-cubes surface, the same surface 3MESH_VOLUME already integrates over, and the five ratios built on it (3AREA_2_VOLUME, 3COMPACTNESS1, 3COMPACTNESS2, 3SPHERICAL_DISPROPORTION, 3SPHERICITY) take IBSI's definitions over 3AREA and 3MESH_VOLUME. This is what IBSI, MIRP and PyRadiomics compute. Before, 3AREA counted exposed voxel faces, which sat 28% above MIRP on the ut_ phantom and 16-22% above PyRadiomics' SurfaceArea on CT volumes.
The area is summed in the same walk as the mesh volume, so the in-RAM and out-of-core paths still share one integral.
Each facet's area is taken on the voxel grid: its lattice cross product is scaled by sy*sz, sx*sz and sx*sy. On a non-cubic grid the area is no multiple of the lattice area, so the spacing cannot be applied afterwards.
Whole-volume mode uses the bevelled box's closed form, with the spacing per axis.
Closes PN-55.
Why change the default
The only thing the voxel-face convention offers is that it reproduces earlier nyxus output. On every other count the mesh definitions are better:
The area measures the surface, not its staircase. A face count reads a plane tilted against the grid at up to √3 times its area, changes when the object rotates, and does not improve with resolution: on a large voxelized sphere it is 1.5 times the true area however fine the voxels. The mesh follows the surface between voxel centres, so its error is far smaller; on the CT volumes the face count sat 16-22% above the mesh area.
The ratios mean what their names say. They mixed the face count with the voxel-count volume, so a large voxelized sphere read 3SPHERICITY of about 0.67 at any resolution. Over the mesh area and mesh volume they are IBSI's compactness, sphericity, spherical disproportion and surface-to-volume ratio.
It agrees with every reference. IBSI defines the surface area (C0JK) and these ratios on the mesh, and MIRP and PyRadiomics compute them that way; nyxus's own docs listed all six as departing from IBSI.
It costs nothing. 3MESH_VOLUME already walked the mesh; the area is summed in the same pass.
Anisotropic voxels are handled per triangle, which is the only correct way for an area on a non-cubic grid.
Users who need the old numbers, for example to compare with earlier cohorts, can stay on the previous release; the ! puts this change in the release notes' breaking section.

3AREA was the voxel-face count, and the five ratios built on it
(3AREA_2_VOLUME, 3COMPACTNESS1/2, 3SPHERICAL_DISPROPORTION,
3SPHERICITY) divided by the voxel-counting volume, a convention the
shape docs recorded as differing from IBSI. IBSI, MIRP and PyRadiomics
take these over the marching-cubes surface: area (mesh) and volume
(mesh). The face count sits 28% above MIRP's area on the ut_ phantom
and 16-22% above PyRadiomics' SurfaceArea on CT volumes.

3AREA is now IBSI's surface area (C0JK): the area of the surface the
mesh walk already takes for 3MESH_VOLUME, summed during the same walk,
so the in-RAM and out-of-core paths still share one integral. The area
adds about 15% to the walk on a ball of radius 10 and nothing
measurable from radius 40 up. The walk, roi_mesh_measures(), takes
the voxel spacing and returns both measures. Each facet's area is
taken on the voxel grid: its lattice cross product is scaled by sy*sz,
sx*sz and sx*sy. On a non-cubic grid the area is no multiple of the
lattice area, unlike the volume, so the spacing cannot be applied
afterwards. The five ratios take IBSI's definitions over 3AREA and
3MESH_VOLUME. Whole-volume mode uses the bevelled box's closed form,
with the spacing per axis.

Tests:
- MIRP 2.6.0 pins all six features on the ut_ phantom at rel=1e-6
  (measured 5.9e-08 to 2.4e-07, MIRP's float32 mesh).
- PyRadiomics 3.0.1 pins 3AREA, 3AREA_2_VOLUME and 3SPHERICITY at
  spacing (1.3, 0.8, 2.5) at rel=1e-9.
- The octahedron and bevelled boxes on three grids, general and
  whole-volume paths, hold the area and all five ratios at rel=1e-12
  against a piece-by-piece sum over the box's 26 surface pieces, and
  the whole-volume values against the general path's.
- An anisotropic in-RAM vs out-of-core invariant holds the area to
  exact equality.
- mesh_area() over a held mesh matches the walk's area bit for bit on
  a ragged ROI on an anisotropic grid.
- The empty-ROI mechanics test covers the area output.

Each new oracle and analytic test fails on the previous code. The
regression pins move to the mesh values. The registry promotes the six
features to vetted, and the generators re-verify every pin.

Closes PN-55.

For ODF display and fixel plot, it should be possible to provide a check box that prevents whatever geometry is drawn in a particular voxel from exiting the originating voxel and therefore potentially corrupting visualisation of the contents of adjacent voxels.
This should just be a matter of attributing a voxel space position to each generated vertex, and adding a discard statement in the fragment shader.

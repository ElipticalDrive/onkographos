This study details the procedural generation and surface detailing workflow within the Carbon Generator plugin, utilizing dedicated neural detail models (DET1K and DET2K) to synthesize high-resolution heightmaps, semantic layers, and multi-class texture atlases. Expanding upon terrain foundations, the framework projects and conforms detail canvases onto complex geometry—such as boulders, rock formations, and custom .obj meshes—using advanced vertex displacement and height-based stratification (spanning classes from soil and grasses to cobblestones and masonry). Furthermore, the pipeline integrates Runtime Virtual Texturing (RVT) and automated material layering to achieve seamless visual blending between surface details and surrounding terrain environments in Unreal Engine 5.

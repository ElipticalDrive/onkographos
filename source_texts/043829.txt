# hologram-system

**Pick up a 3D model with your bare hands and turn it in mid-air.**

A volumetric display built on the Pepper's Ghost illusion: a phone lying flat
under a four-sided acrylic pyramid projects four synchronised views, and the
reflections converge into an object that appears to float inside the glass —
viewable from every side, with no headset, no glasses, and nothing worn.

Then you reach out and grab it. A webcam watches your hands, a gesture engine
turns that into rotate, scale and select, and the object responds in real time.
No mouse, no controller, no touchscreen. The interface is your hands and the
object is in the air between them.

```
  webcam ─▶ MediaPipe ─▶ gesture engine ─▶ WebSocket ─▶ phone ─▶ pyramid
           (13 ms)       (deltas only)     (70 B)      (4 views)
```

**Any glTF model loads**, which is what makes it more than a demo — export from
Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Blender or CAD and your own geometry is what you are
holding. The plugin pipeline means adding an object is a file, not a code
change.

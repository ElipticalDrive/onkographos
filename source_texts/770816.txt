Cluster-wide SSTable sampling, dictionary training and publishing the result to system.dicts sit in storage_service for no better reason than storage_service having had all the needed pieces at hand when they were written -- the commit that added them says exactly that, and suggests a service of their own. Everything that uses them, from the REST endpoints through the automatic training loop to alternator's table size estimate, has to reach through storage_service to get there. The RPC compression dictionaries have had such a service since the beginning; this gives the SSTable ones theirs.

sstables: Keep dict publishing in the group 0 shutdown protocol
…
6fa1421
```
While the publishing code lived in storage_service, it used that service's
group 0 abort source, so wait_for_group0_stop() woke it up before group 0
was drained, on every shutdown path. The new service got an abort source
of its own, which nothing fires until the service itself is stopped --
long after group 0 is gone. A publish in flight at that moment holds the
group 0 gate and the drain waits for it with nothing to wake it up.

Chain the new abort source onto the one storage_service already fires, so
that publishing stays a member of the group 0 shutdown protocol it used to
belong to.

Signed-off-by: Pavel Emelyanov <xemul@scylladb.com>

Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5 (1M context) <noreply@anthropic.com>
```

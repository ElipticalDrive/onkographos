Closes godotengine/godot-proposals#13364
When addressing godotengine/godot-proposals#9947, I had thought that automatic instancing was supported by the mobile renderer as well, but recently I was asked about automatic instancing by a community member and realized that this isn't the case. This is also mentioned in the documentation.
Luckily, adding the support is largely trivial (the implementation here is almost a 1-to-1 copy of the implementation in the Forward+ renderer) and adds very little overhead.

This is great!
For context, we didn't add automatic instancing to the mobile renderer since it adds a lot of GPU overhead and Mobile devices tend to get GPU bound very quickly (they also get CPU bound quickly, but GPU is much worse). So, in general, we optimize the mobile renderer to reduce the cost on the GPU. Additionally, the extra indirection has a big performance penalty on older mobile devices that it doesn't have on Desktop devices. There is a real chance that merging this actually harms performance for people who use the renderer for its intended purpose, shipping games on mobile devices.
What that means in simple terms is we need to carefully evaluate whether this change is worth making.
We identified that need when making the Mobile renderer, which is why we delayed adding this optimization. Before we merge this, we would need to test performance on a range of mobile devices with a range of scenes to ensure that there are no serious performance regressions.

Follow-up from the #8564 verdict (#8812).
The nearest-glass split (#8743) draws splats twice through an opt-in depth-interval shader: one bound depth texture and a greater / less-equal flag, so every sample is written exactly once. That hook exists only in our local patch of @luma.gl/splats, not upstream. The reverse-Z ordering fix is already proposed upstream as luma.gl #3619.
Why it matters: every @luma.gl/splats upgrade has to carry the patch, and a future GPU-sorting backend needs the same hook (GaussianBackendScene.encode(…, interval)).
Done when: the hook is proposed upstream (or folded into #3619's follow-up). Either the patch is dropped after an upstream release, or the reason it can't be is recorded here.

Parent tracking issue for FF's deliberate expansion into 3D rendering. Design doc: Docs/Technical/3D-Rendering-Roadmap.md (canonical design; this issue is the canonical task list — keep both in step).
Thesis
FF's native 3D primitive is the distance field (SDF sphere-trace) — the moat. The single highest-ROI observation: FF already computes, per pixel, far more than it emits — hit depth, normal, world position, albedo, AO, material — then collapses it to one 8-bit beauty buffer and discards the rest. Most of this roadmap is stop discarding what you already compute, not build new geometry.
Two hard constraints on every slice: (1) must be twinnable + headless/batch-renderable (CPU scalar oracle vs D3D/Vulkan parity, the FF engineering moat); (2) routes through the two existing idioms — the palette/theme art system and the parity-twin slice — not a third (no general shader node graph, no mesh scene graph).
Slices (ranked by architectural fit × payoff)
Strategic core is the top trio — S1–S3 lifts FF's 3D a full tier reusing existing data with zero mesh machinery.

Sequencing
S7 (EXR) → S2 (linear+transform) → S1 (passes) → S3 (camera) → S4 (denoise) → S5/S8/S6 by appetite. #388 (multi-light relief in-scatter) is a good warm-up on the volumetric axis.
Non-goals (the Blender trap)
Mesh modeling/sculpt/UV, rigging/particles/physics sim, full node editor, asset library/geo-nodes. FF exports mesh for print — that is the handoff line: model/sim is Blender's job, render the distance field cinematically is FF's.

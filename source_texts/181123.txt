Ray Processing System | Harry-Zhi/semantic_nerf | DeepWiki
This document covers the ray processing components of the Semantic-NeRF system, which handle the generation, sampling, and transformation of camera rays for neural radiance field rendering. The ray processing system is responsible for converting camera parameters and poses into 3D rays that can be input to the NeRF networks for volumetric rendering.

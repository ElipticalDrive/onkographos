Summary
Rebuilds the volumetric fog system from scratch (not a restore of #4) and integrates it into the path-traced Vulkan renderer as four extra raygen records of the existing world pipeline (light / inject / filter / integrate, SBT indices 2–5), sharing the TLAS, descriptor set and push constants with the trace itself.
The fog's light field is split by frequency, which is what removes both fireflies and grid wobble at the source:
Sun/moon shafts stay in the frustum-aligned froxel grid (sharp, directional, one path-traced visibility ray per decimated cell).
Skylight, terrain GI and block-emitter glow live in a world-anchored radiance probe volume (RTXGI/DDGI architecture): a fixed 32×32×16 grid of 4-block probes snapped to a quantized world anchor. Probes are fixed in world space while the camera moves, so the GI field cannot re-sample new positions per frame — no grid wobble by construction. Each probe traces a uniform-sphere restricted GI path plus 2 pdf-correct emitter next-event samples and accumulates them in a gamma-encoded (1/5) running average with sample-count weighting, ping-pong lanes carrying history across the camera-anchor shift. ~16k fixed probes each averaged over up to 64 frames vs ~200k single-sample frustum cells — no per-cell dots by construction, and no per-cell GI/emitter rays left to shimmer.
How it works
Light pass (world-anchored probe volume): one thread per probe. Uniform-sphere tracePath under a restricted probe path (no surface RIS, no emission gather, no SSS, diffuse-only continuation, no stars; the celestial disc never appears even through specular/glass bounces) + 2 alias-sampled emitters over the light grid with the exact reconstructed proposal PDF, phase folded into the isotropic average (1/4π). Values are scaled by the configured fog ambient strength and blended into history gamma-encoded with weight 1/count (max 64). History follows the camera-anchor shift (index - shift); out-of-bounds probes seed from the clamped boundary neighbor at half weight; teleports and terrain/light publications restart accumulation (RtTerrain.epoch()).
Inject (one thread per sun-shaft cell): world-stable procedural density (3-octave FBM, world-anchored, wind-advected) and the single-scattering term {sigma_s·L per unit length, sigma_t}: the analytic sun/moon term through the same path-traced visibility() as surface lighting (tinted glass/water/translucent geometry colors the shafts, underwater wave caustics apply), plus a trilinear query of the probe volume for everything else.
Filter: trilinear resample of the decimated shaft grid + temporal reprojection (same prevViewProj + camDelta convention as reflection MVs) + variance clipping; the blend runs in gamma-encoded space so shadow-edge transitions stay perceptually linear, ping-pong between two RGBA16F lanes selected by frameIndex parity.
Integrate: camera-outward march with the exact analytic Beer-Lambert slice integral → per-column {cumulative in-scatter, transmittance}.
Composite: applyFroxelFog in indirect.rgen applies L' = L·T + S in scene-linear space before pre-exposure, so fog composes correctly with DLSS-RR, exposure and the display transform. The primary pass writes first-interface distance into volumeDepth, so fog stops at the wall/glass/water surface the camera sees.
Underwater mode: dedicated parameters selected on the CPU side when the camera is submerged; probes start in the water medium (Beer-Lambert + wave-refraction caustics), and the sun term keeps its strength-gated caustic weighting.
No shadow maps
Every light-attenuation path is path-traced — this is the point of doing fog inside the PT. Nothing samples a shadow map.
Performance
The probe volume is fixed-size and independent of render resolution; quality settings now trade light-probe samples/bounces and shaft-grid decimation. Per-cell GI/emitter rays (the dominant cost of the previous design) are removed entirely — inject is one sun visibility ray per decimated cell, and the GI/emitter field is a trilinear lookup.
Settings
In-game options (bound in RtVideoOptions, keys caustica.options.rt.*, en_us.json translated): volumetric fog toggle, fog quality (scales sun-shaft grid, light-probe samples and probe bounce depth), density, distance, height falloff, noise, anisotropy, god rays, temporal weight, ambient (GI) strength, underwater fog toggle. Config surface: rt.volumetrics.* with TOML comments.
Testing status
CI green on the head commit (full build: shaders via slangc + spirv-val, Java, bundled jar). Suggested runtime check: night near torches/lava, sunset, and camera + light movement — fog glow should be smooth (no dots), stable while the camera moves (no grid wobble/boiling), and the frame-stats tool should show frame.volumetricLight/Inject/Filter/Integrate.

The per-probe path traces were the noise source: at one sample per fog
cell, a path that happened to HIT a lava block or gather a surface-RIS
HDR sample injected a per-cell firefly that the linear variance clip
could not reject (the one-sided neighborhood inflates its own sigma).
The sky dots were emitter/star/specular hits, and the pixelation came
from the fixed 4x4x2 probe decimation.

Rework the fog light field along the production path-traced architecture
(Minecraft RTX keeps its GI/emissive data in a fog volume; NVIDIA's
RTXGI probes use gamma-encoded accumulation to suppress fireflies):

- Probe paths are restricted to the low-variance terms only: no surface
  RIS, no direct emitter gather, no SSS, diffuse-only continuation. The
  sun/moon and celestial discs were already gated; stars stay hidden.
- Block emitters enter the fog through a pdf-correct next-event sample
  over the existing light grid (one alias-sampled emitter + one path-
  traced visibility ray per probe) — the only way torch/lava glow can
  exist in fog at all, and now bounded and low-variance instead of an
  accidental path hit.
- The temporal filter blends in gamma-encoded space (exponent 1/5, the
  production DDGI probe-update curve): residual HDR samples are
  compressed before entering history, so the field converges to a
  perceptually stable value instead of an unstable one with spikes.
- Probe decimation is per-quality (4/3/2x XY, 2x Z) instead of fixed,
  so Quality 1 gains resolution back (3x3x2 cells) at the same ray
  budget, and higher qualities spend rays on samples before depth.

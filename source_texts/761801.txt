Closes #284
What changed
The path tracer reads emissiveMap at each hit, scaled by emission and emissiveColor.
environment.volumeFog is traced as a homogeneous medium with exact free-flight sampling (new src/path-tracer-volume.js). Scattering events are lit by every light and the sky with shadow rays; surface light through the fog is attenuated. Noise, drift and fog ambient are not traced and are reported.
metrics.pathTracer.ignored in an image job result now also names anisotropy, iridescence, sheen, moss, splat layers, baked light maps and the untraced fog fields.
New reference scene emissive-sign (emissive-mapped panel on a dark wall), with the matching emissive path in render-blender.py.
Cycles row of docs/parity.md, docs/path-tracer.md and the reference README updated.

Summary
UsdLuxMeshLightAPI is not supported (site/content/docs/usd/lights.md). Emissive geometry contributes only when a BSDF-sampled ray happens to hit it, never through light sampling (NEE). crust diagnostic flags this as unlit_emitters.
Emissive meshes are common in production: fire and FX cards, practical lights modelled as geometry, signs, screens. Wherever they light the scene, they are the dominant noise source.
Measurement
On a production hair-backlight pass lit by an emissive card, crust diagnostic attributed most of the frame's noise to the emission / unlit_emitters rows (relative error 0.031 / 0.035 against 0.015 for direct diffuse). The pass needed about 20,000 spp to reach 5% noise.
Expected
Prims with MeshLightAPI (and optionally any emissive mesh, behind a setting) should become light-list entries. That means area sampling over their triangles, weighted by emitted power, with the solid-angle density on both sides of MIS (the invariant in docs/architecture.md: a shape's sample and its pdf come from one sampler). The materialSyncMode of the API should choose between the material's emission and the light's inputs:color × inputs:intensity.

Issue body actions
Explore using AI-generated assets for surface look (not full 3D meshes) to keep consistent with the procedural/InstancedMesh-friendly voxel approach.
Scope:
Generate a small set of flat colors/simple texture swatches (e.g. "old rusty yellow paint" for the car, bark/leaf tones for trees) via an image-generation tool, applied as THREE.MeshLambertMaterial colors or small texture maps
Explicitly out of scope: importing full AI-generated 3D models (.glb/.obj) — that would need GLTFLoader/OBJLoader and risks breaking the low-poly/low-drawcall guardrail if models aren't kept simple; revisit only if the flat-color/texture-swatch approach isn't enough
This is exploratory — evaluate feasibility and visual fit before committing to a specific pipeline.

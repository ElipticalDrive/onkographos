Volume visualization has been applied in many fields and become an important method for scientific visualization. The article illustrates the intersect problem that arises in the describing of a complex geologic structure by cubic models and surface models. The paper provides an approach, marching cube, to manipulate and modify the models interactively by using the typical method on scientific visualization. Then the models are treated with raster and vector graphic ways. And the complex geologic models, such as cross models, hierarchic models and faultage models, finally are built.

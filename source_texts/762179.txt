What & why
tdmcp already staged RayTK's .tox via manage_packages, but did nothing with its operators, and shipped its own hand-written GLSL raymarching (createRaymarchScene/createSdfField). This PR closes the real gap: driving RayTK's raymarching/SDF ROPs as an editable node graph (SDF → combine → material → camera → render TOP), complementary to the GLSL tools (which are untouched).
It also adds a dedicated tdmcp-raytk-integration harness (map → plan → build → integrate → QA) so this and future RayTK work is repeatable and release-aware.

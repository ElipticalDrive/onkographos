Package 10.7 — regional 2:1 octree transitions and measured iso accuracy
Progressive 3D volume preview (final scientific surface unchanged)
Locally refine 3D leaf cells near sampled level crossings rather than refining entire shared Cartesian axis planes.
Balance adjacent octree leaf depth to 2:1. Tile each coarse face against its actual smaller neighbors; use canonical patch-center triangulation and propagate every edge breakpoint to orthogonal faces to avoid T-junctions.
March the resulting conforming tetrahedra with consistently oriented triangles. Hard cap at 4,500 leaves and 75k triangles per surface.
Require measured cell-center/face-center scalar probe tolerance ≤0.5% of source map range; on failed budget, tolerance or truncation, fall back to the existing conforming-axis/regular preview.
Do not change isoTriangles final extraction, signed levels, potential sampling, or scientific export paths.
Verification and honest error reporting
Native 64³ signed-distance sphere topology audit: no degenerate faces, every closed interior edge exactly two oppositely oriented faces, real coarse/fine interfaces and depth balance.
Independent analytic reference: maximum observed preview vertex radial error ~0.021 Å; ~0.123 Å for legacy coarse preview, on this test only.
Worker records sampled iso-vertex scalar residuals and a first-order gradient-normal displacement proxy (Å). Neither proxy is a rigorous global Hausdorff or missed-feature bound.
Skewed/periodic colored potential check, noisy-field fallback and exact final mesh parity; real Chromium/WebGL large-volume smoke updated for local octree.
Device measurement support
Add browser/OS, WebGL renderer/vendor/version, CPU logical cores, pixel ratio and texture-capacity metadata to user-downloaded benchmark JSON; retain merged-vs-instanced GPU A/B from 10.6.
Document repeated capture procedure on different devices; CI runs only one Chromium/WebGL device and does not claim a real multi-GPU/OS performance matrix or driver VRAM measurements.
EN-default UI/EN+TR docs, offline service worker bump.
Please leave unmerged until native regressions, independent Gemmi/CCP4 and real browser/WebGL CI are green.

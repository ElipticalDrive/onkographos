Volume Rendering - an overview | ScienceDirect Topics
Volume rendering is a technique used in computer science to visualize 3-dimensional scalar fields, such as data from medical scanners or computational fluid dynamics. It allows for the visualization of complex 3D relationships by blending contributions from multiple surfaces and integrating the entire volume to produce soft surfaces.

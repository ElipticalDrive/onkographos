Description
mrHARDIflow is now stable enough. We present you v1.0.0 with the following features :
Diffusion preprocessing using state-of-the-art algorithms
Robust T1 anatomical volume registration to diffusion space through a high-resolution template
Precise 3-class (WM, GM, CSF) tissue segmentation providing Partial Volume estimates and masks
Registration of commonly used parcellations in subject space (D99, INIA19, CHARM, SARM)
Reconstruction of single-tensor and multi-tensor diffusion models, as well as fiber ODFs
Computation of insightful metrics from diffusion models for qualitative and statistical analysis

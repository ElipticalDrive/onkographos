Context
HFDisplacementData provides Source Engine-style subdivided face grids (power 2–4) for organic terrain, slopes, and sculpted surfaces.
Problem
HFDisplacementData.triangulate_displaced() generates triangle meshes by assigning flat per-triangle cross-product normals (normals.append(n1); normals.append(n1); normals.append(n1)).
Because every triangle on the displacement grid receives its own flat face normal rather than an averaged normal from adjacent triangles sharing each vertex:
Sculpted terrain surfaces appear visibly faceted along quad diagonals under dynamic or directional lighting.
Smooth terrain shading cannot be achieved without an external post-processing step.
Proposed Solution
Compute smooth vertex normals by accumulating and averaging the face normals of all triangles incident to each grid vertex (row, col).
Normalize the accumulated vertex normals before emitting the mesh array.
For boundary vertices belonging to shared sew groups, allow averaging normals across adjacent displacement surfaces during sew operations.

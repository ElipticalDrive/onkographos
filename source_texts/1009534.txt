What
G8e.6's follow-up from ADR 0173: SSR misses showed the bright sky, even under a canopy. ADR 0279 records this design.
waterSkyReflection (include/water.slang) replaces the bare sky in Water.vk.frag and WaterScene.vk.frag.
With a light probe volume: sky × occlusion + probe bounce(R) × (1 − occlusion), the same specular occlusion lit PBR surfaces use (visibility along R, widened by roughness).
SSR hits still replace it. Without probes nothing changes.
Only those two .spv files and the lock lines that follow water.slang changed.
Not built (ADR 0279):
Wet banks: the terrain splat's fragment stage has no image to spare, and its vertices have no stream to carry a wetness value.
rendering.sceneTextures.
A caustics-only test: the water-fall golden covers caustics.
Tests
Render: SkyReflectionsUnderARoofTakeTheProbesOcclusion (water-ssr --count 3/4: a roof over half the pool, a baked volume or none, SSR off).
Under the roof the water reflects 0.362 of the open water's light with probes, and 1.000 without — the same on moltenvk and lavapipe.
New golden water-roof-probes_frame0030.png on both drivers. Both inspected.
The water, probe, terrain and forest-mini goldens are unchanged on both drivers (lavapipe in Docker).
Gates, run locally on an Apple M5 (load 4–8):

shaders-check, format-check, check.sh, state-guard

What to build
Two additional dockable viewports appear alongside the 3D orbit view. Camera View renders the mesh reprojected into the camera image plane using the camera intrinsics. Projector View renders the mesh reprojected into projector pixel space using the projector intrinsics + extrinsics. All three viewports are engine-managed ofFbos drawn via ImGui::Image(). Resizing an ImGui window resizes the corresponding FBO.
Acceptance criteria
Three ofFbo viewports managed by engine: orbit, camera overlay, projector overlay
Camera View: mesh rendered through camera intrinsics projection matrix
Projector View: mesh rendered through projector intrinsics + R/T projection matrix
All viewports drawn as ImGui::Image() textures in dockable windows
Viewport FBOs resize correctly when ImGui windows are resized

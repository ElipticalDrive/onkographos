This is what makes the authoring loop work: the assistant writes text, renders it, looks at the PNG and corrects the text.
Scope
A CPU triangle rasterizer with a depth buffer, flat shading from one fixed directional light plus ambient, and nearest sampling of the palette texture. No GPU, no dependency.
Document::preview(&PreviewOptions): for each model, four views in a row (front, right, top, and an isometric 3/4 view from the front-right), orthographic, scaled so one voxel is at least scale pixels, with a label above. Optional thin grid lines on the top view at voxel boundaries, so coordinates can be counted.
A built-in pixel label font of the crate's own (5×7, uppercase letters, digits, _ - . : / ( )), lowercase drawn as uppercase.
Deterministic bytes: rasterization in fixed point or with f32 operations that are exact under IEEE (no sin/cos/powf from the platform's math library; the iso view uses a fixed precomputed matrix).
Acceptance criteria
Golden PNG hashes of the showcase's preview match on Linux, macOS, Windows and wasm.
A test renders a single red voxel and checks that each view shows a square of the expected size and position, and that the top face is brighter than the side faces.
Hidden faces are hidden: a test with a small cube in front of a large one checks the front view's pixels.
A test renders every glyph of the label font and compares with a committed fixture.
Out of scope
Animation strips (0.5), variant grids (0.4), perspective cameras.
Touches: new src/preview.rs, src/raster.rs, src/label_font.rs, tests/preview.rs.

抄録
In this study, we propose an efficient CT reconstruction algorithm with a top-down octree generation. Our algorithm reduces not only the data size of CT images but also the computation time for CT reconstruction. In our method, an octree is constructed simultaneously computing CT values of each octree cell. Also, the proposed method preserves the geometrical accuracy of the surface by using a geometric error driven subdivision strategy for the construction of the octree.

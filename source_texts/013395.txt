Hey @splashdust ! I added this to my fork of your voxel world sandbox. Not sure you're interested in it - if you aren't, feel free to discard this PR.
The idea was to provide a very efficient way of ray-marching the voxel grid, while executing something each step of the way. This had proven very helpful in previous voxel projects I worked on, to be able to do some form of super fast "ray tracing" for stuff that don't require sub-voxel hit detection.
It does overlap quite a bit with your own raycast_fn(), but I'm not familiar with the algorithm you used so unsure if it's a 1:1 replacement or not. In case it is, I could rework your raycast_fn() for it to use my new traversal instead.
What do you think?

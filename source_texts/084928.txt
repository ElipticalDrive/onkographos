The generated surfaces look less smooth. It may be due to the surface geometry is actually less smooth and/or the surface normals are not computed.
The surface net internal smoothing produces somewhat different results compared to windowed since. This needs a bit more work.
After exporting to model, some scalar array remains in the polydata, which throws off the coloring of the model by default (coloring by scalar gwys enabled).
All these would worth some more investigation before we change the default method to surface nets. Therefore it might make sense to change the default in Slicer 5.11.

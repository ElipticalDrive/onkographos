Refs #578. The design and the workstreams: docs/world-ao.md.
The integration branch for world-space ambient occlusion: the structure's distance field, baked once per map from the player's own map and cached, plus analytic proxies for the objects, applied to the ambient term alone through #574's slot. This PR is the skeleton the four workstreams fill. Their PRs go into world-ao, not master. Draft until every stream has landed and the checks in the doc pass.
What the skeleton has
Interfaces:
port/linux/include/world_scene.h: stable ids, static triangles, drawn instances, the world light list;
world_sdf.h: layout, encoding, cache format and version, the bake's schedule;
world_proxies.h: shapes, the GPU layout, the reference occlusion;
world_ao.h: the game hooks and the front end;
struct xgpu_world_ao_block in port/linux/src/xgpu_stream.h: the GPU block.
Stubs that do nothing: port/linux/game/world_*.c. The pure parts (the field's layout, encoding and trilinear sample; a sphere's occlusion) are real, so their tests have something to test.
Hooks:
render.c: per frame, and per window around the opaque surfaces until the lightmap pass ends;
scenario.c: map load (a cache lookup only, never a bake), BSP switch, unload;
d3d8_device.c: the front end's halo_world_ao_*.
Command stream version 16: WORLD_AO_WINDOW (the block) and WORLD_AO_FIELD (slabs of the field, bounds-checked by the decoder). Both backends leave the callbacks unset, at marked points.
Bindings: SPIR-V set 3 bindings 1–2 (Ambient occlusion: darken the ambient light alone, not the flashlight and dynamic lights #574's set, so still at most four sets), MSL texture 5 and buffer 23.
The setting: display.ambient_occlusion = "world" is documented. It runs no GTAO, and for now draws as "off". Off is still the default, and Classic's preset still sets "off".
Harness tests: test_world_sdf.py, test_world_proxies.py, test_world_scene.py and test_world_ao.py, with negative controls. They cover:
nothing running unless the setting is "world";
the hooks' order;
no bake in a load;
the stream records and the bindings.
Off is unchanged
With the setting off, nothing new runs past a cached string compare. No record is written, and the emitters aren't touched, so the shaders are byte-identical.
Checks
Linux x86-64 build (5900X).
pytest tools/harness.
CI builds every platform.

…here its shapes are, a box's frame once

The proxies were the world's cost on b30's start (measured per draw and per
part of the shader: the field is 0.02 ms of it, the loop's place and shape
loads, every kind's reach computed for every shape, and the box's bound and
exact faces the rest). The SPIR-V emitter now:

- walks the subgroup's cells' shapes together (OpGroupNonUniformBitwiseOr of
  the pixels' masks), lowest first, each pixel taking only its own: the
  same product, of the same shapes in the same order, while each shape's
  index, place and kind are the subgroup's (scalar loads, uniform branches);
  without the subgroups' bitwise operations in pixel shaders it walks each
  pixel's own, as before (xgpu_spirv_options subgroups, the device's
  VkPhysicalDeviceVulkan11Properties; HALO_VULKAN_NO_SUBGROUPS);
- finds each kind's reach, bound, fade and occlusion inside its own branch;
- finds a box's frame once for its bound and its faces, its far patches or
  exact faces by a branch, and one face of each pair (the side p is on).

HALO_VULKAN_DRAW_TIMES (and _FILE): a timestamp after each draw of every
nth frame, summed by the draws' occlusion kind, or written a draw a line to
compare two fixed-step runs draw for draw.

The reference checks run both walks (check spirv_subgroups); the SPIR-V
interpreter learned OpGroupNonUniformBitwiseOr and OpShiftLeftLogical.

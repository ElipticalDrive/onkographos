This paper presents a full‐color, real‐time computer‐generated holography (CGH) processor implemented on a Field‐Programmable Gate Array (FPGA) platform. The proposed processor leverages high‐bandwidth memory (HBM) and an optimized dataflow to achieve holographic rendering with 4K resolution at 30 frames per second across eight depth layers for multi‐plane holographic displays. A real‐time holographic media system is developed, enabling the conversion of 2D video streams into holographic content through the proposed CGH processor. Furthermore, a binocular real‐time holographic display system was realized by integrating multiple CGH processors with tiled spatial light modulators (SLMs), achieving scalable and immersive holographic visualization.

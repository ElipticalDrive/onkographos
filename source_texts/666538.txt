Why
Distance fog (#122) and height fog (#159) are analytic per-fragment terms; they can't represent light shafts (god rays), fog that pools in volumes, or fog scattering point/spot light. Volumetric fog raymarches the participating medium for those effects — the high-end atmospheric look.
Three.js analog
No core equivalent; comparable to Unreal's Volumetric Fog (froxel-based) or screen-space raymarched fog.
Proposed AlphaEngine shape
A fullscreen post pass that reconstructs world position from scene depth, raymarches the fog medium, accumulates in-scattering from the scene lights (reusing the per-frame lights UBO), and composites over the HDR scene colour before tonemap.
Start with an analytic/uniform medium; a froxel (3D-texture) approach is the scalable follow-on.
Notes
Needs scene depth as a sampleable texture (feat: expose scene depth as a sampleable texture #158) to reconstruct position; reuses the lights UBO from feat: lighting system (light objects + per-frame lights UBO) #101 and the per-view fog params from feat: fog #122.
A fullscreen raymarch needs no compute support; a froxel volume would (compute is not yet available in the engine).
Heaviest of the fog family — defer until distance (feat: fog #122) and height (feat: height fog (analytic exponential height fog) #159) fog land.

In this paper, we propose an adaptive embedding algorithm for VQ-compressed images. Adjusting the predefined distance threshold according to the required image visual quality, the proposed algorithm-which is designed as a codeword arrangement strategy-clusters a number of similar codewords together such that a codeword in a group can embed a sub-message while preserving embedding capacity and achieving high image quality. Compared with Lin et al's scheme, the experimental results show that the embedding capacity for test images is obviously superior while the recycling strategy is applied, and the peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) of image quality is higher in most cases; therefore, the proposed scheme is able to embed the information more effectively and efficiently.

What Is a Volume Shader? Definition, Uses, and Workflow
A practical volume-shading workflow starts by deciding what the medium should look like and where it will be rendered. Fog for a real-time scene, cinematic smoke, and glowing fire may all use volume shading, but they need different density, resolution, lighting, and performance settings.

The current Surface Nets isosurfacing algorithm does not gaurantee a valid surface output (can produce non-manifold edges and crossing triangles), limiting the usefulness of the output for downstream mesh or CSG boolean operations.
The Regularised Marching Tetrahedra (RMT) algorithm (Regularised marching tetrahedra: improved iso-surface extraction) reports that it can produce valid surface outputs, with reduced triangle counts, so a surface-following implementation should be investigated.

What changed
Make orbit a momentary left-mouse drag view instead of a permanent camera in the C cycle.
Begin each orbit drag from the current camera position so entering orbit does not snap to a hard-coded angle.
Keep the orbit angle relative to vehicle yaw so the camera continues to follow the car naturally while turning.
Return automatically to the previously selected driving camera as soon as the left mouse button is released.
Ignore ordinary canvas clicks until the mouse has moved past a small drag threshold, and keep HUD/UI clicks from activating orbit.
Preserve mouse-wheel orbit distance adjustment while actively orbiting.
Behavior
Hold left mouse on the driving canvas.
Drag to rotate around the hero car.
Release left mouse to return to the prior chase/close/hood/cockpit/drift camera.
Validation
The change is isolated to src/graphics/cameraController.ts. The pull-request CI should run the project regression matrix and production Vite build.

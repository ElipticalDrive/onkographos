Abstract
The trend toward aggressive quantization of neural network model weights — from 32-bit floating point to 16-bit, 8-bit, 4-bit, and recently 1-bit representations — has yielded remarkable reductions in model storage and inference cost with surprisingly modest degradation in output quality. This document carries the trend to its logical and inevitable conclusion by specifying Zero-Bit Quantization (ZBQ), a weight encoding scheme in which each parameter is represented using zero bits. ZBQ achieves optimal compression (infinite compression ratio), eliminates all storage requirements, reduces inference energy consumption to zero, and, under certain definitions of model quality that this document is careful not to make precise, preserves output characteristics.

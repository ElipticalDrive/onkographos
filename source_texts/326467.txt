Summary
Compare two state-of-the-art neural inverse-rendering frameworks against mini-mesh's planned UV-space pipeline:
TensoIR (Jin et al. 2023) — tensor-factorized radiance + reflectance fields with secondary effects (shadows, indirect lighting).
NeILF++ — Neural Incident Light Field (5D) + BRDF field + SDF; handles occlusion and indirect lighting natively.
Goal: decide whether to integrate either as an alternative export-time material decomposition path, or whether the simpler UV-space pipeline (Lambert+SH → GGX, separate issues) is sufficient on our typical inputs.
This is a research investigation — the deliverable is a written comparison and a recommendation, not code.
Why investigate
Both have publicly available reference implementations (TensoIR has CVPR 2023 code).
Both jointly model lighting + indirect bounces, which the simpler UV-space pipeline ignores.
For indoor scenes with strong inter-reflection, the UV-space pipeline will be biased; TensoIR/NeILF++ may be a better fit there.
For outdoor / mostly-diffuse scenes, the simpler pipeline is likely sufficient; quantifying the gap is the point of this investigation.
Tasks
Survey: re-read TensoIR, NeILF, NeILF++ papers; extend docs/missing_brdf_papers_analysis.md with implementation notes
Reproduce TensoIR on the synthetic scene from feat(pipeline): End-to-end validation with ground truth meshes #5; record material MAE + relighting PSNR
(If feasible) reproduce NeILF++ on the same scene
Compare against UV-space pipeline output (once UV-observation + GGX issues land)
Write docs/inverse_rendering_backend_comparison.md with results + decision
Decision criteria
≥3 dB relighting PSNR improvement on indoor scenes → integrate as --inverse-rendering-backend {tensoir,neilf} export option
<1 dB → keep simpler UV pipeline, document trade-off, close as won't-fix
1–3 dB → research note only, revisit if a downstream consumer needs it
Non-goals
Implementation work (deferred until decision is made)
Training framework changes
References
Jin et al. — TensoIR: Tensorial Inverse Rendering, CVPR 2023
Yao et al. — NeILF: Neural Incident Light Field, ECCV 2022
NeILF++ — extended NeILF + BRDF + SDF (see docs/missing_brdf_papers_analysis.md)
Sun et al. — Neural-PBIR Reconstruction, ICCV 2023 — alternative hybrid baseline

The vertex-clustering decimator left holes, non-manifold edges and flipped faces on 38 of the 49 shipped levels (Bennu 4k: 1,470 non-manifold edges, 983 boundary edges), while every source measured had none. The scene drew them as black triangles, visible on the Eros and Didymos share cards.
What changes
Reduction is edge collapse (meshoptimizer, MIT, build-time only), then removal of zero-volume fins, then a closed-surface check; the script refuses to write an open level. 49/49 levels closed; two full regenerations give identical bytes.
The recipe is data: scripts/shape-model-targets.json, run by pnpm shapes:generate. Per-body options were written nowhere; they were derived by producing every combination and comparing with the shipped file (right one 0.1–1.7 %, next 3–17 %). Levels, albedo and embedded credit are read from the fiche (19 of 24 files embedded "source à documenter").
Sources re-read where served today: PSI mirror of the SBN (UMD still 403), NAIF, DARTS. Bennu's OLA v20 now comes from the OSIRIS-REx SPICE archive (SVS serves only v21); credit updated in four languages.
Grid reader: the pole is one point (Vesta stayed open otherwise).
Colour: point-sampling measured map noise. Bennu is draped, so its unused baked colour is dropped; Eros and Ryugu keep their measured colour, carried over from 3a36b12 and averaged per vertex footprint.
Checks
pnpm verify: 4,408 tests, exit 0 (new guards: closed surface per level, falsified on the old Eros 1k; recipe ⇔ fiches; embedded credit).
pnpm build, JS budget unchanged (margin 30,702 B); pnpm test:pages 21/21; modelLod + modelCredit 5/5.
Share cards and Bennu in the app compared with production by eye.
Shipped models 17,981,724 → 17,090,756 B; 1k levels −15,048 B.

The vertex-clustering decimator left holes, non-manifold edges and
flipped faces on 38 of the 49 shipped levels (Bennu 4k: 1,470
non-manifold edges, 983 boundary edges), while every source measured
had none; the scene drew them as black triangles, visible on the share
cards of Eros and Didymos.

- Reduction is now edge collapse (meshoptimizer, build-time only), then
  removal of zero-volume "fins", then a closed-surface check: the
  script refuses to write an open level. 49 of 49 levels closed, and
  two full regenerations give identical bytes.
- The recipe is data (scripts/shape-model-targets.json, run by
  `pnpm shapes:generate`). The options of each body were written
  nowhere; they were DERIVED by producing every combination and
  comparing it with the shipped file (the right one within 0.1-1.7 %,
  the next at 3-17 %). Levels, albedo and credit are read from the
  fiche, not copied: 19 of 24 files embedded "source à documenter".
- Sources re-read where they are served today: the PSI mirror of the
  Small Bodies Node (the UMD host still answers 403), NAIF, DARTS.
  Bennu's OLA v20 model is now read from the OSIRIS-REx SPICE archive,
  since the SVS page serves only v21; credit updated in four languages.
- Grid reader: a pole is ONE point (cos 90 deg is 6e-17), else Vesta
  stayed open.
- Colour: point-sampling a map measured its noise (Bennu's baked
  colour: neighbour correlation -0.02). Bennu is draped with its
  texture, so it no longer carries baked colour. Eros and Ryugu keep
  their measured colour, carried over from 3a36b12 and averaged over
  each vertex footprint; a map read again goes through the same
  averaging.
- Guards: closed surface per level (falsified on the old Eros 1k),
  recipe covers exactly the modelled bodies, embedded credit equals
  the fiche's. Bennu extentRatio 1.18 -> 1.19, measured.

Shipped models: 17,981,724 -> 17,090,756 bytes; 1k levels -15,048.

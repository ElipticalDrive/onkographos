🔬 Core Debate
Is 3D Gaussian Splatting the future of perceptual fidelity in AR — or just a pretty shortcut that collapses under real-world conditions?

🎯 Key Talking Points
NeRF vs Gaussian Splatting — The Real Trade-Off — NeRFs are computationally prohibitive for real-time AR. Splatting trades accuracy for speed. But where's the perceptual threshold? Can people tell the difference in a blind test?
Log-Space Scale: Math vs Perception — #2563 is the episode's centerpiece. Storing scale in log-space is mathematically convenient but may introduce perceptual artifacts at large distances. Should the spec optimize for GPU efficiency or human perception?
Anti-Aliasing When Your Pixel is a Distribution — Traditional AA filters samples. Splatting is a sample. What does it mean to anti-alias a probabilistic primitive? Are we filtering the wrong thing?
The "Splat as .jpg of 3D" Question — If glTF becomes the universal 3D format, will Gaussian Splatting be its killer app — or will it be a niche trick that makes 3D look too good, raising expectations that real-time AR can't meet?
Compression & Perceived Quality — With 16 comments on SPZ_2 compression, the community is already worried about bandwidth. In AR, you're often on cellular. Can compressed splats maintain presence, or does compression break the illusion?
Wide Gamut & HDR Splats — #2539 asks whether splatting should support wide gamut color. If AR content looks more vivid than the real world, does that enhance presence or break it?

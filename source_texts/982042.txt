Summary
Upgrades the Abyssal Cinematic look with ambient occlusion, a voxel fog and related screen-space techniques, makes the grass as dense as the renderer allows, and thickens the fog wherever the world physically reacts.
Voxel (froxel) volumetric fog replaces the per-pixel march. A view-aligned grid is filled once per frame, each cell blends with its reprojected value from the previous frame, and pixels march through the grid. Its cost follows the quality preset, not the render scale.
Horizon ambient occlusion (GTAO-style, cosine-weighted) with a temporal filter. It replaces the 10-tap SSAO and darkens only indirect light, so sunlit surfaces keep their colour.
Contact shadows: a short screen-space march from each lit surface toward the sun, for feet, hilts and blades.
Shader polish: multi-scatter energy compensation on the indirect specular term, and a footprint-faded micro relief on the terrain and prop shading, which hides the 512 px texture tiling up close.
Maximum-density grass: tuft density per preset (Low 1.5×, Medium 2.5×, High 4×, Ultra 5× the original growth). The instance budget rises from 16384 to 65536. Kelp (0.45×) and cilia (0.7×) are thinned so they never hide the hero.
Dense mist where the world reacts: a meadow map lays a ground mist over every grass cell, settling into the terrain. Each physical push source (the hero, footsteps, blasts and nearby enemies, the same ones that bend the grass) thickens the fog around it. The holes that clear the view around the hero are still applied after the mist.
Environment maps at 1024 × 512 from the Poly Haven 1k originals (earlier commit on this branch).
Not in this PR
The character mesh, rig and animation set are unchanged. The retarget is tied to the 53-bone UAL skeleton, and the CC0 models that are reachable from the sandbox use other rigs and a different style.
PBR texture sets stay at 512 px. The mirror is capped at that size, and the higher-resolution masters are on hosts the sandbox cannot reach.
Audio is unchanged.
Not included: temporal anti-aliasing (FXAA is still the final pass) and screen-space reflections.
Verification
npm test: 49 tests pass. New: tests/volumetric.test.js and tests/foliage.test.js. The shader check rejects GLSL reserved words, because an earlier version of the fog shader used patch, which silently dropped the whole cinematic pipeline to the fallback look.
Headless Chromium on SwiftShader, 1280 × 720, High preset, seeded worlds, all four realms: the cinematic pipeline constructs in every realm, with no console errors or warnings. Before and after stills of each realm were compared against the original commit (975c7a5).
GPU frame cost has not been measured, because SwiftShader cannot represent GPU timing. The new work is bounded by the preset grid sizes and step counts. A GPU frame profile is worth doing before merging, especially at Ultra, where the grass is densest.

Fog: the cinematic fog is a view-aligned voxel grid. Each frame the grid
is filled once, one slice per draw, and every froxel blends with its
reprojected value from the previous frame. Pixels then march through the
grid. The grid's cost follows the quality preset, not the render scale.
The old per-pixel march is removed.

Ambient occlusion: horizon-based GTAO with per-frame rotation and a
temporal filter, at half resolution. It is applied to indirect light
only, from last frame's reprojected result, so sunlit surfaces keep their
colour. It replaces the 10-tap world-space SSAO.

Contact shadows: an eight-sample screen-space march toward the sun
against the previous depth buffer, for the small shadows the shadow map
is too coarse to show.

Shader polish: multi-scatter energy compensation on the indirect
specular term, and a footprint-faded micro relief on the triplanar
terrain and props, so the 512 px texture sets stop tiling up close.

Dense meadows and mist: grass density follows the quality preset (1.5x,
2.5x, 4x and 5x the original growth). Kelp and cilia are thinned so they
never hide the hero. A meadow map lays a ground mist over every grass
cell, and each physical push source (the hero, footsteps, blasts and
nearby enemies) thickens the fog around it. The holes still clear the
view around the hero.

Tests cover the slice mapping, grid sizing, the presets, the foliage
budget and density scaling, the meadow map, and a static check of the
shader sources (template tokens and GLSL reserved words, which broke the
pipeline once already).

To enable both features of FIFE (master HEAD required) simply add this settings.
```
        <Setting name="GLUseMipmapping" type="bool"> True </Setting>
        <Setting name="GLTextureFiltering" type="str"> Trilinear </Setting>
```
It has to be figured out what the best way to handle the problems with the ground tiles.

Hey guys,I can only provide a few suggestions.
Check your image sizes.
Give each image in an Atlas enough room. Two additional pixel for every side should be enough (transparent black 0,0,0,0). The bars at the top of the moving ships are caused by your atlas background color (255,0,255,255).
If you still notice artifacts, especially on ground, then you need something like a border color. You can replace the transparent black with something that fits better. As example the water tiles should get a frame of two pixels with a similar color (50,100,140,0).

In order to insure both the rendering speed and the image quality,a GPU-accelerated ray-casting volume rendering is proposed.The algorithm achieves time-consuming sampling and interpolation by using the graphics hardware with trilinear interpolation function.Space leaping in the sampling process increases readering speed.The experimental results show that the proposed method ensures high quality image rendering effects and raises the speed by 95 times,white its storage capacity increases less.Moreover,GPU-based real-time rendering of mass of volume data is also achieved.

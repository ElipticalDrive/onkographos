Closes #1447
Implements docs/design/viewer-rendering.md Phase E.2 (greedy meshing for water tops) in viewer/mesh.js. Up-facing water faces at the same y with equal layer, depth byte and tint (all four corners equal, compared as bytes) merge into rectangles of at most 15 blocks a side (uv is uint8 in 1/16). Other water faces, ice and everything else are unchanged; culling is unchanged. Water tops with differing corner tints are emitted per block as before. SPEC.md is not involved (mesh only).
World output: unchanged (mesh only; no block, height, biome or decoration differs for any seed). Wasm unchanged, so no size change.
Tests: new viewer/test/mesh_greedy_water.test.mjs (ocean chunk under 5% of faces, same covered cells and attributes, tint boundary, 15-block cap, ice culling). preview.mjs 12345 still plans a route. Note: client test "dry grass blocks carry the dry tint of their corners" already fails on master.

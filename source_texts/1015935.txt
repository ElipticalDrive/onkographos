Fog overrides today are per object (engine-render's per-instance shading hook override entries): every point of an instance takes its entry's fog for the whole ray between it and the camera. Looking from outside through a window, the air between the camera and the window takes the room's (clear) override, so the outdoor air in front of the glass is not fogged; the fog depends on which object a ray ends on, not on where the ray runs.
Area-based fog
A media shading hook (atmosphere.fog, the engine's IMediaHook) that integrates along the view ray through regions the plugin writes each frame:
Outdoor fog: exponential height fog (density at a base height, falloff per metre), its single-scattering albedo, lit by an isotropic ambient term and the sun through a Henyey-Greenstein phase function. Authored as the hook's values on a scene component (engine-scene's ShadingHookComponent).
Fog volumes (FogVolume component): boxes or ellipsoids placed, turned and scaled by their node, with density, albedo, height falloff and blend distance (soft edge).
Indoor zones (IndoorZone component): boxes or ellipsoids where the outdoor fog is excluded or replaced by the zone's own air, and the outdoor light reaching the media is dimmed. A room's zone ends at its walls and windows: from outside, the air up to the glass is fogged and the room behind it clear; from inside, the fog starts at the window.
Technique: a per-view technique gathers the regions from the scene (FogRegions), keeps the nearest to the camera up to caps, writes them camera-relative into a buffer each frame and its address into the hook's record.
Integration: the ray is cut at every region boundary it crosses; pieces in no region hold height fog alone and are integrated in closed form; pieces inside regions in a fixed number of homogeneous steps (exact for hard-edged uniform regions).
Settings (tunables): max volumes, max zones, regions per ray, steps per piece, cull distance.
Tests
GPU, against closed-form values: through a window from outside, the outdoor air in front of the glass fogged and the room behind it clear (and fogged once the zone is removed); from inside, the room clear and the fog starting at the window; in-scattering only along the outdoor part; a replacing zone; caps keeping the nearest; fog volumes (ellipsoid, turned and scaled box) dimming by density times length.
Screenshots: fog outside a window, seen from outside and from inside, with and without the zone.

Summary
A USD Material's volume terminal is now the medium inside the geometry it is bound to — the approach of NVIDIA's Typhoon (hdEmbree, typhoon/main of NVIDIA-Omniverse/OpenUSD), which has no UsdVolVolume support and renders every volume this way.
MaterialX volume terminals (crust-mtlx). volume, volumematerial, mix of volume shaders, and the VDF tree (anisotropic_vdf, absorption_vdf, mix / add / multiply) with Typhoon's combinators — coefficients linear, anisotropy weighted by each branch's scattering — built as program ops, so textured VDFs work and the JIT needs nothing new. compile_terminals builds a material from its surface and volume terminals (taking the Host, and reading a surfacematerial's displacement as compile does).
Volume + surface: the volume is the interior a ray refracting into a thick transmissive surface carries, replacing the one the surface's transmission_* inputs describe (ApplyVolumeToSurfaceClosure).
Volume alone: a medium boundary (Material::is_medium_boundary / boundary_medium, World::has_medium_boundaries). The integrator crosses it without a vertex or depth, travels in its medium inside (Typhoon's single-owner MediumState; every ray the path traces carries it), runs NEE + MIS at scatters there (PrevVertex::Phase pairing, LPE-routed like a region scatter, with the vertex's light-sample count as main's multi-sample NEE gives a region scatter), and shadow rays cross boundaries paying Beer–Lambert (through_boundaries, which also handles cutouts and thin walls; the light cache sees a boundary as clear).
Inline MaterialX in USD. ND_* shader networks (outputs:mtlx:surface / outputs:mtlx:volume) are translated node for node into crust-mtlx documents (scene/usd_import/mtlx_network.rs), literals read through the same parse_literal as .mtlx files, connections followed through NodeGraph outputs and interface inputs. mtlx is the last surface context, so a stage that renders through its preview surface is unchanged.
Fix: volume-region tracking read the ray parameter as a distance. Camera rays are not unit (length = focus distance, 10 by default), so crust:volume fog seen from the camera was ~10× too thin and the image depended on focusDistance. fog, smoke and aovs_lpe now render denser (aovs_lpe is bit-identical to main with its region removed); every other sample is bit-identical.
Sample samples/materialx_volume.usda: a volume-only fog cube with a ball inside, a glass sphere with a volume interior, an inline OpenPBR copper.
Rebased as a single commit onto main at ab9a84b (multi-sample NEE, the LastCrossing re-hit guard, the openusd workaround retirement); the earlier merge commits of main into this branch are folded in.
Performance
The integrator is monomorphised on a MEDIA const (advance_pixel / trace_path / shadow_transmittance / volume_nee), chosen per render from World::has_medium_boundaries, so a world without boundaries runs a copy with none of their branches: cornellbox +0.04% instructions (callgrind, 2 spp, 4,235.8M → 4,237.6M), bit-identical. Run-time guards instead cost +0.7%.
Testing
tests/volume_materials.rs: Beer–Lambert through an absorbing boundary exact to the 0.001 restart epsilon, for unit and ×10 directions; white furnaces at 1 within 0.025 under power MIS, light-only and BSDF-only, and with a white Lambertian ball inside the fog; inline surface, preview-surface precedence, surface + volume interior. usd_scene.rs::loads_materialx_volume_usda; crust-mtlx VDF unit tests; nodedef-name parse tests; tracking_measures_distance_not_the_ray_parameter.
Mesh boundary vs the same medium as a crust:volume region: agree within 0.3% (the restart epsilon); difference falls like noise across spp.
Every sample without a boundary or region is bit-identical to main at 16 spp (re-checked on this rebase: 32 of 36 samples identical; fog, smoke, aovs_lpe and the new materialx_volume differ, as expected).
cargo fmt --check, cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -D warnings and cargo test --workspace on the pinned toolchain; zola build (0.21) checks internal links; openspec validate add-mtlx-volume-materials --strict passes.
Known gaps (documented)
One medium owner at a time (no nesting), a camera starting inside a boundary does not see it, boundaries must be closed with outward normals, the medium is homogeneous per entry, volume's edf is ignored, and light path expressions don't record pass-throughs before a boundary crossing (beauty unaffected). Pre-existing and documented, not fixed: under streaming import (≥ 4 top-level prims) a Material that is itself a top-level sibling of its geometry does not resolve.
Records: openspec change add-mtlx-volume-materials; materials, rendering and usd-scene-import design records; docs/architecture.md invariant; README; site usd/materials.md, usd/volumes.md, architecture/limitations.md.

A Material's `volume` terminal is now the medium inside the geometry it is
bound to, as in NVIDIA's Typhoon (hdEmbree), which has no UsdVolVolume
support and renders every volume this way:

- crust-mtlx reads `volume`, `volumematerial`, `mix` of volume shaders and
  the VDF tree (`anisotropic_vdf`, `absorption_vdf`, `mix` / `add` /
  `multiply`) with Typhoon's combinators, and `compile_terminals` builds a
  material from its surface and volume terminals.
- Under a thick transmitting surface the volume replaces the interior the
  surface's transmission inputs describe. With no surface the material is a
  medium boundary (`Material::is_medium_boundary` / `boundary_medium`): the
  integrator crosses it without a vertex, travels in its medium inside (one
  owner at a time), runs NEE + MIS at scatters there, and shadow rays cross
  boundaries paying Beer-Lambert.
- Inline MaterialX networks (`ND_*` shaders) in USD are translated into
  crust-mtlx documents; `mtlx` is the last surface context, so stages that
  render through a preview surface are unchanged.
- Fix: volume-region tracking read the ray parameter as a distance, so fog
  seen from the camera was thinned by the focus distance (10x by default).
  `fog`, `smoke` and `aovs_lpe` render denser; every other sample is
  bit-identical.

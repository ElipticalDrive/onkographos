Last decade has seen an explosive growth in data, this we can call we are living in data age. High resolution photos and videos have been an essential part of most of the technological devices. With this comes a huge challenge to manage large volumes of information. One would think large volume of data in turn requires large volumes of storage space. Hence it is very much essential to compress these data in very e cient formats in order to store them or transmit them over given bandwidth channels. This research work focuses on digital image compression, which is the need of the hour. Digital images are stored with certain number of bits based on the type of the image. There exists lots of redundancy in the storage of images. Compression can be achieved if this redundancy can be reduced. While reducing the redundancy, it leads to mainly two types of compression, one is lossy another is lossless compression. In recent days, Arti cial Intelligence Techniques that include fuzzy logic, swarm intelligence, clustering algorithms , back propagation neural network and arti cial neural networks are being used. These methods are used on gray level and color images and then traditional compression algorithms like Run length encoding or Human compression are applied to obtain compressed images. But they need a training network to be generated for their operation. In this research work, an attempt is made to develop algorithms for lossy as well as lossless type of compression, which are at-par with the current standards. Performance evaluation is a criterion that is used to assess the quality of reconstructed images. A novel idea about performance evaluation is also proposed.

### ABSTRACT

Direct volume visualization is an efficient technique to explore complex structures within volumetric data. The main advantage, compared to standard 3D surface rendering, is the possibility to perform semi-transparent rendering in order to provide more information about spatial relationships of different structures. The enormous data sizes introduce a growing demand for interactive 3D visualization. Within this paper a set of approaches to handle and render large volumetric data is developed, enabling significant performance improvements due to a much better utilization of the CPUs processing power and available memory bandwidth. This paper propose a new innovative discussion over the volume rendering for the medical images, a comparison over some techniques that involves a huge amount of memory and uses of big computer resources and a lot of processing time.



### Keywords

Volume visualization, image processing, medical images.

The main problem of these procedures is the limited amount of memory provided by graphics systems. Nowadays the amount of available memory considerably increased due to strong demands of the gaming industry. Several special purpose volume rendering architectures have been proposed, due to the high computational demands of direct volume rendering. Recent research has focused on accelerators for ray-casting of rectilinear volume data. Taking into account a comparison of the most popular direct volume rendering algorithms, it can be said that Shear-warp provides the highest performances, but with a loss of image quality, also Raycasting and splatting produce comparable image quality, splatting is superior if sparse data is processed and ray-casting performs better for semitransparent dense data.

A good example for improvements of the volume rendering is Open Qvis project, that implements methods for interactive high-quality volume visualization on general purpose hardware such as commodity desktop computers with graphics cards designed for computer games and multimedia [6].



### 2. SOFTWARE VOLUME RENDERING METHODS

From the starting ideas of volume rendering, a lot of different techniques have been presented that improve the rendering speed or enhance the quality of images. In the field, nowadays, there are techniques that incorporate ideas from many sources and becomes a benchmark. The lack of complete algorithms has led researchers to explore a diverse spectrum of methodologies, ranging from wavelet analysis to snowball analogies. The volume rendering methods can be related to three paradigms, image order, object order, domain based rendering. This groping methods are based on how the data is handled and in what manner it recover the final image.

 Image order, or backward mapping as it is known as, is an field within volume rendering where techniques are evaluating each on screen pixel one at a time, this method produce the best image quality, but offer slow render speed. Object order has the idea to reverse many of other techniques, is often called forward mapping because the data is projected in a forward direction, has the advantage that can run in parallel due to the fact that only a small surrounding area around a voxel needs to be examined for shading and classification calculations. This method offer good render time, with average to good picture quality, applications of this method are shear-warp and splatting.

A good example for the algorithms that uses frequency domain rendering consists in using the algorithm that was proposed in 1993 by Malzbender and was later extended, in the same year by Levoy et al. It has a big advantage over image order algorithms because the computational complexity is close to a magnitude lower, O(N3) where N3 is the volume size, for image order compared to O(��2 log(��)) for frequency domain rendering. Much of the theoretical foundation is based on the Fourier transform slice projection theorem.

#### 5. CONCLUSIONS

 Human blind studies are expensive and generally unpractical for most researchers. Pommert and Hohne concluded in their evaluation of medical volume visualization methods that the image quality is best evaluated through a human blind study. Volume rendering is a big research field that is has seen a lot of activity over the past decade and will continue to do so in the future with the introduction of better accelerators motivated by the gaming industry. The original techniques which all had a number of drawbacks initially have received many updates that have made them faster with better image quality and more versatile. The applications of volume rendering are many and varied, the driving force is primarily the medical field which can use the rendered images to help diagnose illnesses, or to practice performing virtual surgeries. This article illustrate the benefit of image processing techniques over the medical image domain and propose some applicable improvements ideas for the algorithms that uses image compression. Also, nowadays there are a lot of factors that influence the humane medicine, starting from the evolution of the technology for different fields up to the new innovative medical methods. All of these methods are strictly related to the DIICOM technology, and all the computers and medical devices that use this standard. The three methods or ideas of improvement are related to the wavelet decomposition method that is very important in the optimization methods for the volume rendering related to the medical images. But this methods offer solutions for the personal computers that was the testing platform and also according to the presented studies this methods can confirm in the future because this domain is growing faster.

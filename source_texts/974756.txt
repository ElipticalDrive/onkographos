We conduct KWAJEX simulations using the single-column version of the Community Atmospheric Model (CAM) with a hierarchy of parameterization schemes for PBL turbulence and cumulus convection, spanning from traditional standalone schemes (CAM_ZMUW) to unified convection schemes (CAM_UNICON), unified cloud–turbulence representations (CAM_CLUBB), and super parameterizations (CAM_SP). Details of these four distinct configurations are as follows:  kwjx_4scam KWAJEX forcings crm_kwjx cloud-resolving model (CRM) outputs scam_kwjx_zmuw Deep convection: Zhang & McFarlane (1995) scheme PBL turbulence and shallow convection: Park & Bretherton (2009) scheme scam_kwjx_unicon Shallow and deep convection: a unified framework (Park, 2014a) PBL turbulence: Bretherton and Park (2009) scam_kwjx_clubb Cloud Layers Unified by Binormals (CLUBB) scheme (Golaz et al., 2002; Guo et al., 2021), which is further extended to replace the Zhang-McFarlane deep convection scheme scam_kwjx_sp CRM with kilometer-scale resolution embedded in each grid column of the host CAM  scam_kwjx_clubb_silhs CLUBB with the stochastic Subgrid Importance Latin Hypercube Sampler (SILHS) scam_kwjx_clubb_zm CLUBB with the Zhang-McFarlane convection scheme

Description
We conduct KWAJEX simulations using the single-column version of the Community Atmospheric Model (CAM) with a hierarchy of parameterization schemes for PBL turbulence and cumulus convection, spanning from traditional standalone schemes (CAM_ZMUW) to unified convection schemes (CAM_UNICON), unified cloud–turbulence representations (CAM_CLUBB), and super parameterizations (CAM_SP). Details of these four distinct configurations are as follows:
kwjx_4scam
KWAJEX forcings
crm_kwjx
cloud-resolving model (CRM) outputs
scam_kwjx_zmuw
Deep convection: Zhang & McFarlane (1995) scheme
PBL turbulence and shallow convection: Park & Bretherton (2009) scheme
scam_kwjx_unicon
Shallow and deep convection: a unified framework (Park, 2014a)
PBL turbulence: Bretherton and Park (2009)
scam_kwjx_clubb
Cloud Layers Unified by Binormals (CLUBB) scheme (Golaz et al., 2002; Guo et al., 2021), which is further extended to replace the Zhang-McFarlane deep convection scheme
scam_kwjx_sp
CRM with kilometer-scale resolution embedded in each grid column of the host CAM
scam_kwjx_clubb_silhs
CLUBB with the stochastic Subgrid Importance Latin Hypercube Sampler (SILHS)
scam_kwjx_clubb_zm
CLUBB with the Zhang-McFarlane convection scheme

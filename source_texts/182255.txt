Octree Explained
For spatial representation Each node in an octree subdivides the space it represents into eight octant s. In a point region (PR) octree (analogous to a point quadtree), the node stores an explicit three-dimensional point, which is the "center" of the subdivision for that node; the point defines one of the corners for each of the eight children.

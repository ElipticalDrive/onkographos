# UNG-GS: Uncertainty-Aware Normal-Guided Gaussian Splatting for Surface Reconstruction from Sparse Image Sequences



**Zhen Tan, Xieyuanli Chen, Jinpu Zhang, Lei Feng, Dewen Hu***  
| [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11172) | [Data]() | 

---

## 📢 Overview

We propose **Uncertainty-aware Normal-Guided Gaussian Splatting (UNG-GS)**, a novel framework that introduces an explicit Spatial Uncertainty Field (SUF) to quantify geometric uncertainty within the 3DGS pipeline.

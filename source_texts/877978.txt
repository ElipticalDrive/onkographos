cast_shapes_heightfield_shape runs a full GJK cast against every triangle (segment in 2D) of every cell it walks through, including triangles the shape can't reach within max_time_of_impact or only after a hit it already found (the TODO: pre-check using a ray-cast on the Aabbs first?). We hit this in a character controller over a heightfield terrain: its sweeps are short casts close to the ground, so the whole-heightfield check never rejects them, and a typical horizontal sweep cast against ~15 triangles without hitting any.
This PR skips a triangle when the shape provably stays clear of it until reach, the nearest hit so far or max_time_of_impact: either the swept Aabbs don't overlap, or, for support maps, the shape stays on one side of the triangle's plane. The clearance includes the target distance plus a small margin for GJK's tolerance, chosen from measurements.
Results are unchanged in 400k seeded random casts (2D and 3D, byte-identical output vs master). The one exception I found: with shapes a few millimeters across, master reports some hits where the shape actually stays at least 2 mm (median 4 mm) away from the triangle (GJK false hits); this branch returns the next real hit instead. The new tests compare against casting each triangle individually.

Compared to #453, the bound also shrinks to the nearest hit found so far, support maps get the plane test, and the margin keeps the near-ties #453 ran into.
Note that #463 touches the same function; they conflict textually but are independent, and I'll rebase whichever lands second.
While testing I also ran into some pre-existing traversal misses in 3D (casts toward +X/+Z skip cells because curr_range.end is exclusive, horizontal casts return after the first cells, and an infinite max_time_of_impact with a zero x or z motion never terminates). The new tests avoid those cases; happy to send a separate PR for them.

Shape-casting against a `HeightField` ran GJK on every triangle (segment
in 2D) of the cells it walked through. It now skips a triangle when the
moving shape stays clear of it until the nearest hit found so far, or
until `max_time_of_impact` before any hit: either its swept Aabb misses
the triangle's Aabb, or, for a support map, it stays on one side of the
triangle's plane over the whole sweep. The clearance is the target
distance plus a margin chosen from measurements, relative to the length
of the sweep and the target distance. In random shape-casts the result
is unchanged, except for some hits GJK reports on shapes a few 1e-3
across that stay clear of the triangle by about their own size.

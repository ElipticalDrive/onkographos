Could you add a view clipping feature for editing PLY files?
The current view is easily obstructed, making it difficult to draw ranges. With a view clipping function, users can define a plane (by clicking a point) and clip data behind (or in front, simply by reversing the direction) along one of the X, Y, Z axes. This makes it easier to identify the main region of the model. Furthermore, spherical or cubic clipping can also be supported: click a point, set a threshold value, and create a spherical or cubic clipping volume.
The main purpose is to facilitate selecting or drawing regions of interest with various tools in subsequent operations.
Of course, this is purely a visual effect; it does not actually cut the model data.

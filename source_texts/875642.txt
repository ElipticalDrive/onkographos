What and why
Adds harpas_core::noise, implementing the deterministic gradient and simplex noise samplers that vanilla 26.1 world generation builds density functions, climate fields, aquifers, ore veins, surface rules and carvers on:
ImprovedNoise: Ken Perlin's 2002 3D improved gradient noise, preserving (xo, yo, zo) and 256-entry permutation initialization from any RandomSource, (double) 1.0e-7f (0x3e7ad7f2a0000000) vertical step smearing (y_scale / y_max), and unfudged local_y quintic fade weights.
PerlinNoise: multi-octave 3D Perlin noise with 2^25 (33_554_432.0) coordinate wrapping, untruncated zero-amplitude entries in the 2^(N - 1) / (2^N - 1) normalization factor, modern "octave_<k>" positional seeding, legacy sequential seeding (consume_count(262) for skipped octaves), edge_value and max_broken_value.
NormalNoise and NoiseParameters: paired PerlinNoise samplers offset by 337 / 331 (1.0181268882175227) and scaled to target deviation 1 / 6 over the active non-zero octave span, supporting both modern and legacy seeding.
BlendedNoise: three-sampler legacy terrain noise (min_limit_noise, max_limit_noise and main_noise) with 684.412 coordinate scaling, vertical smear multipliers, blend <= 0.0 / blend >= 1.0 short-circuiting and min_limit_noise.max_broken_value(y_multiplier) bounds.
SimplexNoise and PerlinSimplexNoise: 2D (70.0 scale, 0.5 falloff) and 3D (32.0 scale, 0.6 falloff) simplex noise and multi-octave 2D simplex noise across zero, negative and positive octaves.
Also splits the M3 world generation roadmap task into four reviewable sub-tasks in ROADMAP.md and marks this first step done. All six samplers live in one change because they share the 3D cube-edge gradient table and permutation initializer, layer directly on one another (ImprovedNoise → PerlinNoise → NormalNoise / BlendedNoise, and SimplexNoise → PerlinSimplexNoise), and are checked together in harpas-core's Java 25 differential parity harness.
Related

Testing
Unit tests in crates/harpas-core/src/noise.rs covering (double) 1.0e-7f widening, vertical smearing, trailing-zero amplitude normalization (such as 26.1's ridge noise), 2^25 coordinate wrapping, BlendedNoise upper bounds and re-seeding, and 2D/3D simplex and multi-octave simplex sampling.
Differential FNV-1a parity checks in crates/harpas-core/tests/java_parity.rs comparing ImprovedNoise, PerlinNoise (modern and legacy), NormalNoise (modern and legacy), BlendedNoise, SimplexNoise (2D and 3D) and PerlinSimplexNoise bit for bit against a clean-room Java SE 25 probe.
cargo fmt --all --check, cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings, and HARPAS_REQUIRE_JAVA=1 cargo test --workspace.

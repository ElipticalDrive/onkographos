(PDF) A Fast Depth-Buffer-Based Voxelization Algorithm
Abstract The term voxelization describes the conversion of an object of any type into volume data, stored in a three dimensional array of voxels. This paper presents a fast and easy to implement voxelization algorithm, which is based on the z-buffer.

What changed, and why
Implements engine: "octree" — meshio++'s octree algorithm as a third volume meshing option alongside Gmsh and fTetWild. The octree engine builds a conforming hex-dominant volume mesh (hexahedra, pyramids, tetrahedra) from the tessellated skin of either B-rep or mesh sources, sized by refinement level rather than element size. This closes roadmap item 2.24 Octree hex-dominant volume mesher.

meshio++ 17.8.0's octree becomes a third volume engine beside Gmsh and
fTetWild. It meshes the tessellated skin of any 3D source (a B-rep is
tessellated on host OCCT, a mesh source is used as-is) into a conforming
hexahedra + pyramids + tetrahedra volume (templates transition, the only
one MDPA export accepts), merged into the Gmsh model through MSH 4.1 text
exactly like fTetWild so every downstream reader is unchanged.

- src/octreeService.ts: octreeMesh, pure octreeToMsh41 (all cell classes
  on the one volume entity: a second entity fails in gmsh.merge),
  brepSkinStlBytes. Runs on the meshio++ singleton: no new Pipeline fn.
- MeshOptions.octreeMaxLevel (1-6, default 4) and octreeSnap; result
  facts (numSnapped/numRefused/element mix) on MeshResult, the panel
  status line and the handoff manifest.
- Refused by name: elementOrder 2, non-simplex elementShape, periodic,
  sizeField, .geo_unrolled, refinement sweeps. Optimizer pass and Parts
  warn; dimension != 3 falls back to Gmsh. Parts are not preserved.
- Presets, budget, .geo note, MCP capability text and the FE Mesh panel
  (engine option, level/snap fields, greyed Gmsh-only fields) updated.
- Verified live: block.stp level 4 = 3522 cells (1372/542/1608) enclosing
  exactly 60, every cell positive in Gmsh node order (probe
  meshio-octree-orientation.ts: no reorder needed); cube.stl = 1000;
  mcp:smoke and the new test:webview case green. The File-menu clip
  check in test:webview fails identically on unmodified HEAD in this
  container (font metrics); generated screenshots were not committed
  because none shows the new controls and all differ by render drift.

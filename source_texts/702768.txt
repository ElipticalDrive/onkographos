This change improves the visual accuracy and performance of the lighting system:
Atmospheric Mie scattering and extinction now factor in the hazeColor uniform, allowing volumetric lighting to correctly reflect weather-driven aerosol tints like smoky haze.
SH probe occlusion is optimized by utilizing the precomputed u_terrainShadowMap instead of an expensive manual raymarch loop.
TerrainRenderManager has been updated to provide the required texture bindings to the SH probe compute shader.

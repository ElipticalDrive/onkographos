Branch: frontier-voxel-engine · Master: #24 · Literature audit: https://github.com/DrewPhi/MicroVoxelProceduralGeneration/blob/frontier-voxel-engine/frontier/PIXEL_ADAPTIVE_RESEARCH_AUDIT.md
Timing: only after #59/#60/#61/#66 produce comparable correct native/mid/far geometry and captured CPU/GPU budgets. Do not block initial screen-adaptive architecture or replace working primary renderer on theoretical promise.
Hypotheses: the screen-sized primitive + visibility-buffer ideas in CuRast (2026) / SVRaster (CVPR 2025) may inform GPU compaction/visibility of exact solid voxels; NAADF provides conservative directional empty-space skipping and LOD-free exact ray hits. Compare their principles with already-built rasterized voxel clusters + existing brick-skip on the same cave/bridge/forest/edit/long horizon scene.
Important limitations: SVRaster is a trained radiance-field / novel-view rendering system (CUDA), not a drop-in exact occupancy renderer or low-cost editable game world. CuRast specifically targets enormous opaque triangle sets and RTX5090-class CUDA, and its authors warn it performs poorly for many small meshes; it is NOT a mandatory or likely optimal Radeon760M/wgpu backend. Aokana lacks runtime edit support in the published version. NAADF's CPU/GPU edit synchronization and scene scale differ from our long-range 6.25cm game.
Acceptance: same canonical exact voxel source, images and camera paths, image/depth/parity first; compare p95/p99 full frame, isolated GPU time, CPU preparation and work for each frame, GPU bandwidth/bytes, edit propagation, view speed. Reject optional alternative unless a strong measured advantage or distinct use-case justifies a second backend; document portability/licenses.
Existing related: #45 (voxel ray A/B), #53 (GPU-driven culling), #57 (PlanetSmith future experiment); link results rather than duplicate their production features.
Legacy B/R: frontier/renderer/src/brick_ray.rs, brick_ray.wgsl, column_mesh.rs are baselines; no port from main or DrewsAnalyticRendering without documented source provenance. External code may be read for algorithms only, honoring licenses.

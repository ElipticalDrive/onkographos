IQ’s site as already recommended is a great source for all things ray marching (he’s also the guy behind shadertoy). You may find more info by looking for specific techniques used for example in volumetric cloud rendering which is a common application for it. There’s actually a great deal of papers/articles/presentations specifically on that topic which will generally be applicable to other types of volumetric rendering.
Just to add I wouldn’t say there’s a general lack of interest/info on the topic. On the contrary volumetric rendering is at the forefront of a lot of graphics research (one very good presentation this year at SIGGRAPH for example) discussing everything from path tracing, photon mapping and ray marching techniques. You’re unlikely however to find many simple tutorials for this kind of stuff since it’s an advanced topic and generally the people implementing it are bound to be in industries where you can’t just post source code online I.e games/fx studios. Papers/academic blogs are often your best source of info for that reason.

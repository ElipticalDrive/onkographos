Q: How teardown renders voxels not confined to the grid
The tech video linked to by u/Leverpastej is an excellent source. If you want a TLDR, my understanding is that Teardown does the following: Each object is rendered as its bounding box using the rasterization pipeline, with the fragment shader then ray casting the voxels inside (using a 3d texture). Lighting is calculated using a separate world aligned voxel grid.

<p align="center">
  <strong>Put a 3D asset into a Gaussian Splat scene where it actually fits, without training anything.</strong><br/>
  Load a splat <code>.ply</code>, voxelise it into an occupancy grid, find the empty regions, drop a <code>.glb</code> / <code>.obj</code> mesh<br/>
  into the best one without collisions, convert it to Gaussians and write one merged <code>.ply</code> back out.<br/>
  An optional second mode renders views and runs Grounding DINO + SAM to detect what is already in the room.
</p>

The marching cube octree data structure is proposed as a scheme for representing and generating the mesh of various level-of-details (LODs). We suggest a solution to a problem of modelling partially complex objects, too. The marching cube octree is based on the data structure of the Marching Cube algorithm [1] and the octree structure. The LOD meshes are generated at run-time using proposed efficient algorithm. It triangulates only needed nodes of the marching cube octree to cover up the whole surface of mesh.

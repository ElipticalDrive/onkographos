Summary
This PR introduces:
fix of the volume chunks jittering when zooming out,
new uniforms for the layers that can be injected in the shader program,
new entries in the menu to control new uniforms,
face culling fix,
volume shader cleaning,
new blending webgl method.
We cleaned the volume shader by also removing some allocation that was done inside the for-loop, and moved some expensive computation to the CPU side.
Tests & Checks
We manually tested the changes by:
running the "volume" example
manually clicking on the different menus to activate/change the way the volume is rendered
manually zooming out until the volume is small enough and rotate the camera to verify that the jitter we had disappeard

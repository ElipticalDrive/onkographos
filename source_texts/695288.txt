Summary
Swappable Shading Pipeline: Full bidirectional runtime switching between Phong illumination and Cook-Torrance GGX PBR with IBL & ACES film tone mapping (\EShadingModel::Auto, \EShadingModel::Phong, \EShadingModel::PBR).
Automatic fallback parameter derivation: PBR to Phong (
oughness \to shininess, \metallic \to specular) and Legacy Phong to PBR (\diffuse \to BaseColor, \shininess \to roughness).
Strict preservation of bit-exact golden tests and original legacy shading under default settings.
5 Diverse Procedural Benchmark Scenes (Zero external binary assets / Zero 3rd party libraries):
\grid: 5x5 Metallic-Roughness PBR Lookdev Matrix (25 spheres, 38,400 tris, GGX & IBL stress).
\helmet: Cybernetic Mecha Helmet (multi-part assembly, HDR amber emissive ring =(3.2, 1.4, 0.35)$, mirror gold visor, carbon shell, titanium collar).
\ errain: High-Density Fractal Alpine Terrain ( \times 160$ vertices, 50,562 triangles, vertex throughput and tile binning stress).
\overdraw: Deep Overdraw Asteroid Field (120 icosahedrons across 12 depth strata, depth complexity \sim 15\times$, Hi-Z early rejection stress).
\�rena: Multi-Light Colosseum (1 directional shadow caster + 4 orbiting colored point lights + 1 central spotlight).
Tooling & UI Integration:
\srcli: Added --shading auto|phong|pbr\ and --scene grid|helmet|terrain|overdraw|arena|default.
\Win32App: Added dedicated 벤치마크 씬(Benchmark Scenes) submenu (6 choices) and Shading Model submenu under Options.
Headless runner & tests updated.

Passed 110/110 tests in \RendererTests.exe\ (including bit-exact golden tests \golden.anim, \golden.fill, etc.).

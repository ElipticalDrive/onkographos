Overview
Implements a production-grade Froxel-Frustum Volumetric Fog & Lighting pipeline for LumisCaustica.
Key Features & Implementations
Exponential Z-Slice Distribution: Concentrates resolution near the camera while spanning distances up to 024,\text{m}$.
Exact Closed-Form RTE Integration: Computes slice transmittance and in-scattering analytically along each camera ray without numerical step bias or banding.
Dual-Lobe Henyey-Greenstein Phase Function: Forward Mie scattering lobe ( > 0$) for sharp crepuscular sun shafts/god rays alongside a backscattering lobe ( < 0$) with configurable blend weight $.
Anti-Boiling & Singularity Regularization: Smooth distance regularization $\frac{1}{d^2 + r_{\text{emitter}}^2}$ and voxel maximum energy bounding to eliminate fireflies and near-emitter singularities.
3D Spatio-Temporal Filter with Variance Box Clamping: \times3\times3$ neighborhood statistics in $ space with AABB clamping against history reprojection to prevent ghosting and boiling during camera motion.
Depth-Aware Cross-Bilateral Composite: Samples pre-integrated volume grids with sub-slice accuracy and blends seamlessly over scene radiance.
Vulkan & Build Pipeline Integration: 3D storage image management (RtImage3D), pipeline orchestration in RtFroxelPipeline and RtComposite, reflection bindings and shader record generation, video settings UI, configuration, and localization.

Implements "Dual Contouring of Hermite Data" by Ju et al. on a dense or sparse regular grid (no octree yet). Optionally can guarantee that the dual vertices lie strictly inside their primary cells (reducing self-intersections), which in turn requires the newly added very fast box-constraint convex quadratic program solver for tiny dense problems.
The 715_MeshImplicitFunction has been updated to a more interesting shape which highlights dual contouring's better crease handling over marching cubes.
Dual contouring outputs a pure-quad mesh. To render this nicely, a per_corner_normals overload has been added for polygon meshes.
Includes unit tests for various functions.

Published on Jan 28, 2019
I animated a hybrid fractal with Mandelbulber2. I used a random number generator to select the component fractals.
I called it 'Maxiter' because it pushed my hardware to the limit.
I was able to test a script developed with the help of Ofnuts that can speed-up rendering.
https://www.gimp-forum.net/Thread-Ima...
Backgrounds were done with Mandelbulber and Gimp MathMap.

Summary
Rays leave a surface at offset_origin(p, n) = p + n * ray_tmin_scale * max(1, |p|), with ray_tmin_scale = 1e-4 * the diagonal of the scene's bounding box: 0.15-0.2 mm for a 1.5 m scene. Any feature thinner than that, such as a gap between parts, a thin layer or neighbouring shells, is skipped: the ray starts beyond it and leaks through. The comment on offset_origin already notes it ("if validation scenes show light leaks on thin geometry, upgrade to an adaptive integer-offset scheme").
This PR adds that scheme as an option, TraceSettings::robust_offset, following Wächter & Binder, "A Fast and Robust Method for Avoiding Self-Intersection" (Ray Tracing Gems, 2019, ch. 6):
each coordinate moves along the normal by a fixed number of its own float ULPs (an integer add to its bit pattern), and by a fixed distance near the origin, so the offset follows the coordinate's precision rather than the scene's extent: about 15 µm at 1 m from the origin;
it applies wherever offset_origin is used: emission points, bounces, solar shadow rays, transmission;
the flag is set once where the push constants are built (Vulkan and Metal), so no kernel entry point changes.
It is off by default, because the golden values pin the scaled epsilon. It could become the default once the maintainers have looked at it.
Tests
New radiative vf: robust offset keeps a 50 um gap closed: two unit plates 50 µm apart. With the option, plate A sees plate B at the analytic view factor. Without it, A sees only space, because every ray starts beyond B.
The existing suite passes unchanged (option off).

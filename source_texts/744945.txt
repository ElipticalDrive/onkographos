Owner
create_voxel_world: cloud check (~75% of noise calls) uses an early-exit cloud_density_exceeds; identical output, ~4x faster in a standalone harness.
generate_maze_layout: reusable wrapper over mazes::runtime_app::apply (string_view OBJ) returning a wall raster; write_maze_image uses it.
maze_flow_field: BFS distance field with bilinear sampling; headings and goal zones are baked once per maze.
Physics: the old heightmap-derived layout (hundreds of terrain hulls, up to 4096 voxel bodies, startup spheres, add/jolt controls) is replaced by maze walls (merged runs) plus path-following spheres. The capsule/support checks use maze bounds. Walls are rendered as stone cubes.
Not compiled locally (SDL3/MazeBuilder/box3d deps unavailable).

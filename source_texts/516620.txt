Part of the renderer-exploration effort: new render paths plug in behind frame.mode (the panel's render chip row, data-r buttons in index.html, synced with ?r=), implemented per backend.
Technique
Raymarch the simulation's own density grid — the (momentum, mass) field the sim already produces every substep — as a participating volume with a refractive surface. No new sim data needed; this is the "true 3D" renderer: correct water-over-water occlusion by construction, soft volumetric interior, and view rays that actually bend.
Implementation plan
Chip + mode: data-r="volume", renderMode === 'volume' render path in both backends.
Density source: WebGPU — bind gridV (mass in .w) as read-only storage in the fragment shader, manual trilinear fetch (8 taps via cellIndex). WebGL2 — the tiled grid texture with gridFetch + manual trilinear (the tiling breaks hardware filtering across slice boundaries anyway).
Pass structure (replaces the SSF passes for this mode):
Reuse the analytic background raytrace as the enclosing scene (it already computes cube entry/exit t0/tE).
Fragment raymarch from t0 to tE: fixed world-space step (~0.5 cells), sample density, march until density crosses an iso-threshold (~1.0 particles/cell); refine hit with 3–4 bisection steps.
Normal from central-difference density gradient (6 taps); shade like the SSF composite (Fresnel, spec) and refract: continue a second march segment along the refracted ray, integrating Beer–Lambert absorption from interior density until exit or rock/floor hit; sample the background analytically (the scene is procedural — no texture needed).
Optional second surface event (exit refraction) behind a quality flag.
Quality/perf knobs (this renderer embraces "low res for quality"): render into a scaled offscreen target (?rscale=0.5 default for this mode), upscale in a final blit; step count budget uniform. Foam: reuse the speed channel by marching the momentum magnitude alongside density.
Rocks/walls: already analytic in the same shader — composite exactly as the background does today (correct depth interactions for free).
Files
src/wgsl.js + src/backend-webgpu.js (new fragment + pipeline, storage binding in a render pass), src/shaders.js + src/backend-webgl2.js (GLSL twin), index.html (chip), README.md.
Risks / notes
128³ manual-trilinear march at full res is heavy → the low-res target is load-bearing, not optional (expect ~30 fps at 0.5× on Apple silicon; fine per the exploration brief).
Grid density is noisy at the surface (4 particles/cell rest) — a cheap 1-cell box pre-blur compute pass on mass may be needed for a stable iso-surface.
The volume lags particles by nothing (grid written every substep) but is one grid-resolution blurrier than the SSF look; that is the aesthetic.
Verification
Repo verify skill: ?r=volume screenshots at 64³/128³ on both backends vs ?r=ssf at the same ?az/el camera; the #6 occlusion scenario (?az=1.2&el=0.05) must be correct by construction; bench with ?bench=300 to record the fps cost at 0.5× and 1× scale.

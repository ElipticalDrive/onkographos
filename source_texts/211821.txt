Coal has no SDF geometry. Register a field as a box that bounds it, so the broadphase pairs it, and route any pair with a field to tesseract's implicit SDF solver ahead of Coal's narrowphase, which would otherwise collide the box. The field is sampled through getDistance, so a function-backed field is never discretized.
The other side of the pair uses its analytic implicit form, or a point query against its Coal geometry when it has none (convex meshes, cast hulls). That point query makes a static field cast-checkable against swept convex shapes, including d_arc compensation. Cast pairs classify cc_type by the swept shape's own normal at the witness point, since the solver's normal tilts by more than the support-function tolerance.
Reject what the solver cannot represent rather than colliding the placeholder box: a kinematic field in a cast manager, nonuniform field scale, and partners with no implicit or convex form (plane, mesh, octree).

Stacked on #5 (feat/domain-ops).
What users get
Noise modifier. Fractal bumps on any shape or group: amplitude, scale, octaves, seed. On a group, every repeated copy gets its own noise.
Terrain. An IQ-style eroded heightfield tile (fbm whose octaves fade on slopes): size, height, detail, seed.
Gyroid. A TPMS sheet clipped to a cube: size, cell, wall thickness.
Formula. A shape defined by any f(x, y, z), negative inside, y up. Examples: sphere, gyroid, waves, blobs, rock, twisted bar.
Language: numbers, x y z, + − * / ^, parentheses, pi tau e, and sin cos tan asin acos atan atan2 sqrt abs floor ceil fract sign exp log pow min max clamp mix smoothstep step mod length smin noise fbm.
Live editing: every draft that parses applies immediately (background recompile). A broken draft shows the error and its position, and the shape keeps the last good formula.
Safety and size: the field is scaled by a safety factor for marching and clipped to a size cube.
All of it works in Classic / Chrome / Studio and exports to Embed, Shadertoy and the TypeGPU / WGSL / GLSL functions.
How
Shared noise sources (gradient noise, fbm, value noise with derivatives) in all three languages, with integer hashing only: WebGPU, WebGL2 and every driver produce the identical field (sin-based hashes drift).
Noise and the march: noise displacement grows bounds by its reach and scales the distance by its Lipschitz factor, from the same plan the export uses.
Formulas are parsed by a closed recursive-descent parser (no eval) with a function/constant whitelist and emitted from the AST.
Identical formulas share one generated function.
Sources from hash links are sanitized.
A prototype-key bug (constructor(x) passed as a function) was caught by the tests before shipping.
Schema v9: new shape types, optional formula, noise modifier; v4–v8 still load.
Numbers
Terrain + gyroid + noisy rock + 5 noisy repeated boxes: 2.6 / 3.9 / 3.5 ms (classic / chrome / studio) at 1440×900.

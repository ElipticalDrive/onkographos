This paper presents a Photoplethysmogram (PPG) data compression method that applies the orthogonal transformation technique to the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) and discrete Hartley transform (DHT). PPG is a widely used technique for measuring blood flow and oxygenated Hb concentration in the blood to determine SPO2, blood pressure, and other parameters. In recent years, many watches have added features to measure SPO2 and Blood pressure by incorporating PPG sensors. To send and transmit data wirelessly to medical clinicians and store data, data compression and modulation are essential, along with better signal quality. The performance of each transform has been measured using the performance indexes: compression ratio (CR), percent root mean square difference (PRD), and Signal-to-Noise ratio (SNR), and compared with the literature. The calculations and data compression algorithm was implemented on Visual Studio Code in Python. It was found that FFT and DHT yield comparable results, with FFT performing slightly better; however, DHT offers the advantage of real-time computations.

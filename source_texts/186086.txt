The Gaussian measure of fractal Brownian motion (FBM) was analyzed and a
one-point probability density based on it and the Fokker-Planck (FP) equation for it were found.
A general solution of the FP equation for the conditional probability density of the FBI in the
form of a functional integral has been found. Based on the Gaussian measure with a modified
covariance, the conditional probability density for the stochastic equation (with the FBI as a
base) is determined and presented in the form of a functional integral

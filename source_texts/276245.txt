Summary
The Fog settings give one height falloff for the whole frame. Local fog volumes, wind-driven noise and a Volume material domain let a game shape its fog as part of its look.
User stories
As a level designer, I want local fog volumes with their own density and color, so that a swamp or a cave has its own fog.
As a technical artist, I want a node graph that shapes fog per froxel, so that I make fog effects of my own.
Requirements
Local fog volumes: boxes and spheres with their own density, albedo, emission and edge falloff, inject into the froxels where they overlap them.
A 3D noise texture that moves with a wind breaks the fog up.
A Volume material domain gives a node graph that the injection runs for each froxel, for fog that a game shapes itself.
The lighting doc's Fog bullet also listed local fog volumes; they are part of this issue.
Acceptance criteria
A box fog volume in the sample adds fog only inside it, with a soft edge.
Wind noise moves the fog over time.
A Volume material shapes the fog.
The rendering docs describe the built feature, in the present tense.
When to do this
When a game's fog is part of its look.

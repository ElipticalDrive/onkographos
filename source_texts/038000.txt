Blocked by #976 (atmospheric sky). Shares its grid with #976's aerial-perspective LUT and with #780's cluster grid.
What is missing
There is no volumetric fog and no light shafts. A lamp in a dusty room lights surfaces and nothing between them; the sun through a gap casts a shadow and no beam.
The technique, already decided in #731
Godot's froxel buffer, not Bevy's per-pixel raymarch. #731 measured why and the table there is the argument:
Bevy volumetric_fog
Godot 4
Technique
raymarch in screen space, per pixel
froxel buffer, frustum-aligned
Cost driver
scales with the number of directional lights
one pass over the grid, regardless of pixels
Lookup
marched again
a single lookup
One of the few places the port-literal policy does not apply — see [[feedback_port_bevy_dont_relitigate]] for the rule and why this is the exception. Raised by the owner, who ships Godot's volumetric fog in stellar_delivery and found it cheap.
🔴 One grid, three consumers
The grid is the point of this issue. #780 already builds a froxel grid for light clustering; #976 wants a 32³ frustum-aligned volume for aerial perspective; this wants one for the fog. They are the same shape. Three allocations of the same volume is the failure to avoid, and deciding the grid here — resolution, Z distribution, how a pass declares what it writes — is most of the work.
Light shafts
Fall out of the same volume: the scattering term per froxel needs the light's visibility, which is a shadow lookup the page path already answers. What has to be decided is how many lights per froxel pay for it — many_lights peaks at 22 lights in one froxel, and a shadow lookup per light per froxel is a cost nobody has measured.
What this grid does NOT do
🔴 The cells stay coarse here. Fog, aerial perspective and the light lists read one value per froxel, and that is the whole point of the technique. Particle and fluid media need detail inside a cell, and they get it in #1235 — a sparse refinement hung only off the froxels that hold them, marched with a fixed, capped number of steps. It is for those effects alone; nothing in this issue refines.
Wind
The medium moves. Sampled from the field in the wind-field issue rather than simulated here.
Budget
13.9 ms whole-frame on the OneXFly, and #731's clouds are the cautionary tale at 39.83 ms. Godot defaults to 64x64x64 over 64 metres; both numbers are a starting point to measure, not to adopt.
Authoring it
#1431 adds a ShaderKind::Volumetric so the density field is built with graph nodes instead of hand-written WGSL. It depends on this issue and not the other way round: this one builds the pass, the 3D texture and the temporal reprojection, and #1431 only makes what they evaluate authorable. A graph kind landed first would have nowhere to run.
Acceptance
A froxel grid declared once and read by fog, aerial perspective and clustering.
Light shafts from the sun and from a local light, both shadowed.
Measured on the OneXFly, with the grid resolution and range as the knobs.

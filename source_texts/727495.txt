rotation_matrix_between loses small camera-orientation changes when acos(dot(a, b)) rounds to zero, and loses near-half-turn offsets when the dot product rounds to -1. For float32 a=(1,0,0), b=normalize((1,1e-5,0)), it returns identity even though the cross product retains the rotation. Its tensor constructors also force float32 CPU output for float64 inputs and detach the skew matrix from autograd.
Use the cross-product form of Rodrigues' formula with denominator 1 + cos(theta) on the nonnegative-cosine hemisphere, where that denominator is bounded below by one. This retains small rotations and the correct derivative at aligned vectors. On the opposite hemisphere, use atan2(||a x b||, a dot b) and a normalized axis; exact antiparallel inputs use a deterministic perpendicular axis and the proper half-turn matrix. Build the identity and skew matrix from the input tensor so dtype, device and gradients are preserved.

Hi authors,
Great work on Self-Calibrating Gaussian Splatting for large FOV reconstruction!
I'm testing the camera optimization method of this repo, and it works perfectly on the provided lego dataset – both the camera poses and 3D Gaussian model converge well with the official command. However, when I run the same camera optimization setup/commands on the tandb dataset (except that I changed the init_type from random to sfm), the convergence effect is quite poor (neither camera nor model converges as expected).
I wonder if there are any dataset-specific hyperparameters or preprocessing steps I need to adjust for the tandb dataset? Or any other potential reasons for this performance gap between the two datasets?
Thanks a lot for your help and guidance!

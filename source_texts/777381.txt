With the advent of Internet image authentication has become a central part of research in security. Since JPEG 2000 has recommended wavelet transform as one of the steps in image compression systems, a hash function which utilizes wavelet decomposition is desirable. In this paper, we propose a wavelet based hash function which distinguishes acceptable level of compression from image processing modifications such as Median filtering, Gaussian noise addition, and FMLR attack. KEY WORDS  Image Authentication, JPEG, Wavelet, Compression

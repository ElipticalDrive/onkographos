Anyone know how to sort this issue of sprites smearing?
Use post process volume with infinity extent and reduce/turn off motion blur. In many cases the motion blur with temporal anti-aliasing creates some ghosting at different situations, so it is better to turn it off or adjust motion blur with temporal anti-aliasing to fix possible issues, also in some cases for blurred UI textures for example ...

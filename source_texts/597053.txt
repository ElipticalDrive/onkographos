// register_tracked <array_calibration.json> <stills.json> <edge mm> <out.json> [<from s> <to s>]
VKC_ASSIGN(const camera::ArrayCalibration array, camera::read_array_calibration(argv[1]));
VKC_ASSIGN(const camera::TrackedStills stills, camera::read_tracked_stills(argv[2]));
std::vector<calib::solve::TrackedTagView> views;
for (const camera::TrackedStill& s : stills.stills) {
  const cv::Mat grey = cv::imread(beside(argv[2], s.image), cv::IMREAD_GRAYSCALE);
  const camera::ImageSize size{std::uint32_t(grey.cols), std::uint32_t(grey.rows)};
  VKC_ASSIGN(const calib::detect::MarkerSearch search,
             calib::detect::detect_tag({size.width, size.height, grey.step, grey.data},
                                       calib::kOriginTag));
  if (search.found) views.push_back({s.image, size, s.model, s.cam_to_tracking, *search.found});
}
VKC_ASSIGN(const calib::solve::TrackedWorld found,
           calib::solve::tracked_world_from_tag(views, calib::kOriginTag, edge_mm / 1000.0));
VKC_ASSIGN(const camera::TrackedRegistration reg,
           calib::solve::register_tracked(stills, array.world, found));  // a miss: its reason
return camera::write_tracked_registration(argv[4], reg, core::WriteMode::CreateNew);

How
A tracked camera (ARKit) poses every frame in a gravity-aligned world of its own. Its stills of the origin tag fix
tracking_to_world = [Rz(yaw) A | t], A turning the tracking's up onto the tag's +z. The tracking gives the tilt, so
the fit solves the yaw and the translation alone ("g4"):
Seed, each still alone, in closed form: in the tracking world turned up to +z the tag's plane is level, the
corners' rays meet it at c + H e_k (e_k a ray's level part over its rise, H the plane's height below the
camera), and the square's corners are a similarity of the e_k. Its turn is the yaw, its scale H, its shift the
rest of t. The median over the stills.
Fit on cv::LevMarq, 4 parameters: every still's squared corner residuals through its whole lens, against a
square of the edge the rig measured (TagWorld::edge_m, an argument; 147.25 mm today), never the printed one.
The corners go into each camera in double, with no Rodrigues round trip (detail::project, the tier's one
projection). No still is dropped; each still's fit is reported.
Guard: every still's camera above the tag's plane, else CameraBelowTag naming the still; the reason asks
whether the poses are in sensor's camera axes (cv_from_gl_camera for ARKit's) and options.up is the tracking
world's up.
Gate: RMS at most kMaxTrackedRmsPx (12 px), and no still's camera farther than kMaxTrackedSeedGapM
(3 cm) from where the seed places it; else PoorFit, with the RMS or the gap in the reason.
Misses are values, reusing TagMiss. register_tracked copies the fit (transform, RMS, still count, edge) and the
stills' sensor, session and epoch into sensor's TrackedRegistration, after checking the array's world is the tag
sought (family, id, printed size), the stills pass validate_tracked_stills, and every still of the fit is one of
the stills' images; it ends on validate_tracked_registration.
Deviations from the agreed header (written into the notes before the code)
A still's pose is its session's recorded pose. Wording only: S3's review moved cam_to_tracking from "as the
tracking gave it" to the frame's recorded pose (sensor's TrackedStill::cam_to_tracking), and this header follows.
The seed is the closed form above, not each still's two IPPE square poses. In the marker's frame (z out of the
face) a face-on view is exactly a half turn, whatever its roll, and calib already works around two OpenCV 5.0.0
IPPE faults there (view_pose.cpp: rot2vec near a half turn, and no pose for a face-on view on the axis). The
tests ask for face-on stills, exact and in the bars. With the tilt given, one still's yaw and position have a
closed form, exact on clean corners, with no pose solver. The header's step 1 says so, and names the seed's two
Numerical misses: corners that make no convex quadrilateral (collinear ones), and rays that meet the plane on
both sides of the camera.
The fit gate is a new kMaxTrackedRmsPx = 12 px, not the rig's kMaxTagWorldRmsPx = 3 px. Found running the
bars: on the tests' own model (the tracking off 0.1° / 2 mm per still), 3 px refused 1021 of 2100 holds. The
planning simulation's model gives the same (RMS p50 2.7–3.6 px at 1.2 m). The rig's cameras are solved together
to a fraction of a pixel; a tracked still carries the tracker's own pose error. On the bars' 2100 holds the RMS
peaks at 9.2 px (1.2 m); one still moved 10 cm fits at 48 px or more from 1.2 m.

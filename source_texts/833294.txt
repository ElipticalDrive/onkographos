Step 36: the Phase 7 lighting core, brought forward. Stacked on #33 (Step 35).
Why
The independent review of 02.11.2026 concluded that shape decisions were being made under lighting that cannot show volume. Lighting therefore comes before any more shape work, and it is judged on UE screenshots.
What
r.PlanetAtmosphere.CloudLightingModel 2 (new default) adds three changes:
Phase function: two lobes with a strong forward peak. The default is 0.6 × HG(0.8) + 0.4 × HG(−0.2). Forward glow is about 4× Step 32c; side and back values are about the same. The Wrenninge octaves stay as in 32c.
Ambient light, which replaces the height ramp:
sky light (multiple-scattering LUT) comes from above, through the cloud above the sample;
light from the sunlit ground (SurfaceAlbedo, shadowed by the sample's sun transmittance) comes from below, through the cloud below it;
both use diffuse transmission 1 / (1 + k τ).
Powder term.
Every parameter is a CVar so it can be tuned live in UE:
CloudPhaseForwardG, CloudPhaseBackG, CloudPhaseBackWeight;
CloudAmbientDiffusion, CloudPowder, CloudGroundBounce.
CloudLightingModel 1 is the Step 32c lighting, so one build can A/B both.
PA_SampleCloudDensityEx now also returns the local cloud depth. The density itself is bit-identical to Step 35 on the CPU harness.
Verification
DXC: 10 entries × HLSL 2018 / 2021.
The look is judged on the UE screenshot ladder (model 1 vs 2).

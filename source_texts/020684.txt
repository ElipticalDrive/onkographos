In this paper we describe a basis for a system that is able to compute actual scientific and realistic visualization\nmethods in parallel. It is capable to integrate easily in modern VR renderers like for example Open Inventor\n[SGIa], Coin [Coi] and OpenSG [Vos02]. Our approach is advantageous for processing large datasets which\nusually are the result of physically based simulation algorithms and programs. Using our techniques it is even\nmore feasible to manage similar visualization problems for other large amounts of data (e.g. medicinal CT-scans\nor large geometries) in the context of displaying interactively.

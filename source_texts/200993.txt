Trinity §14: SoA acoustic propagation with voxel-DDA occlusion.
What
SonicSync is the safe single-thread CPU kernel that drives a future WebGPU acoustic ray-tracer:
SoA hearing path (gate 5): source/listener pools are flat Int32 / Uint32 columns; semantic IDs are bounded integers (no strings); listener filter is a u32 bitmask over the semantic ID's low 32 categories.
Q16.16 fixed-point world (FP_ONE = 65536): no floats in the simulation; bit-for-bit identical replays.
Amanatides-Woo 3D DDA (gate 2): visits every voxel the ray actually passes through, per-octant step LUTs precomputed once in the constructor (gate 1), per-pair direction goes into a preallocated working Int32Array(3) - zero per-call allocation.
Double-buffered perception-event ring (gates 3, 4): producer writes to the BACK ring, tick() swaps, consumer reads the FRONT ring - the "read compact events one frame later" pattern.
Cooldown hash (gate 7): open-addressed table keyed by (sourceSlot, listenerSlot, semanticId) with last-emit-tick; a re-emit is suppressed until cooldownTicks have elapsed - the dedup-before-Omniveil gate.
Bounds checks (gate 6): addSource / addListener / setVoxel all reject out-of-range inputs; eventCapacity overflows are dropped + counted as eventsDroppedTotal.

The Gemini sketch was a 5-line calculateOcclusion stub returning a
0..1 attenuation - no SoA hearing path, no DDA, no precomputed
directions, no double-buffering, no bounds checks, no event cooldown.
The Codex audit flagged readback timing and ray-budget as blockers.

This is the safe single-thread CPU kernel: SoA source/listener pools
keyed by integer semantic IDs, a Q16.16 fp voxel-occlusion grid,
Amanatides-Woo 3D DDA tracing that visits every voxel a ray actually
passes through (no naive step ratio), per-octant step LUTs and a
preallocated working direction vector (zero per-call alloc), a
double-buffered perception-event ring so the consumer reads compact
events one frame later, an open-addressed (source, listener,
semanticId) cooldown hash to dedupe re-emits before they reach the
Omniveil consumer, and bounds checks on every source / listener /
semantic / output write. The WebGPU acoustic ray-tracer is the
deferred integration layer.

40 tests cover all 7 Codex gates plus determinism, capacity drops,
hearing radius, semantic mask filtering, and the 3D diagonal DDA.

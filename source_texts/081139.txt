What: an octree index. The BVH covers object queries and PointIndex (uniform grid) covers point queries. An octree's advantage is sparse volumetric subdivision: adaptive occupancy, level-of-detail and empty-space skipping.
Suggested consumers:
sparse storage for axiolid-field (see the field-ops scaling issue);
adaptive level-set extraction in axiolid-levelset;
point-cloud reconstruction (axiolid-pointcloud-reconstruction-sdf) on non-uniform density.
Exit: a measured workload where the octree beats the BVH or grid, with a differential test against them.
Moved from a retired plan file (ADR 0078): crates/algorithms/query/spatial/PLAN.md. Filed as a feature in search of a consumer; pick it up when one of the consumers above needs it.

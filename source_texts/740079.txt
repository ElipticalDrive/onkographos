Scope
ao=on on a voxel model bakes per-vertex ambient occlusion from the three neighbors of each face corner (the classic voxel AO with four levels), stored in COLOR_0's alpha or as a documented _AO attribute. Greedy meshing only merges faces whose corners have equal AO, and flips quad diagonals by AO to avoid the known anisotropy artifact.
wind=WEIGHT on a part or a generator's leaves writes a per-vertex weight (0 at the anchor, rising with height above it) as a documented _WIND attribute, for a renderer's vertex shader to sway foliage.
The preview applies AO to its shading so its effect can be reviewed.
Acceptance criteria
Tests check AO values at an inner corner, an edge and a flat face, and that turning AO on never merges faces with differing AO.
The glTF Validator accepts the custom attributes (application-specific attributes start with _).
Golden hashes of the preview with AO on match on every platform.
Touches: src/mesher.rs, src/mesh.rs, src/gltf.rs, src/generate/tree.rs, src/preview.rs, tests/mesh.rs.

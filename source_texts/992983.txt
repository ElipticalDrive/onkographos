This PR gives perception pipelines a shared representation of camera calibration, pose, and identity across live capture, simulation, storage, and replay.
Shared camera models: Adds field-of-view and calibrated pinhole models, structured lens distortion, camera range, and supported modalities.
Consistent optical frames: Standardizes camera axes to x-right, y-down, z-forward; separates standalone Camera from RobotCamera; updates robot definitions, camera pointing, and visibility reasoning.
Unified observations: Introduces CameraObservation, bundling the semantic camera, effective image calibration, sampled pose, and acquisition timestamp. Migrates CAS and annotators from separate camera metadata views.
Integrated RGB-D rendering: Extends Semantic Digital Twin’s ray tracer with aligned color, segmentation, and depth output, including optical-axis depth for point-cloud reconstruction.
CollectionReader was simplified by dropping (unused) support for multiple Cameras in one CollectionReader.
Camera input and replay: Updates ROS, file, and simulated inputs; handles image rotation and calibration adjustments
Regression coverage: Adds tests for calibration, optical orientation, mounting, rendering, observation identity, serialization, and replay.
The change includes API and required recording-format changes, particularly due to the removed CAS camera views and new observation class. Many changes are motivated by repository guidelines which have been applied on files that have been touched during the migration to the new camera model data structures.

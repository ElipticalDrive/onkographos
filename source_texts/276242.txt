⚠️ NOTICE: This PR includes the changes from the previous PR: #264.
Summary
Optimizes volumetric fog rendering to reduce GPU cost and fix visible banding caused by directional shadows and Exponential Height Fog.
Changes
Add GPU point light culling using 8×8×4 froxel blocks and compact light masks.
Optimize directional shadow filtering, replacing 25 PCF samples with 4 bilinear comparisons.
Add configurable shadow sampling (1–32 samples) inside each froxel. $\textcolor{yellow}{\text{Keep the samples low in the inspector to avoid high GPU cost (between 5 or 8 max)}}$
Fix shadow banding with analytic Exponential Height Fog integration and density-weighted shadow sampling.
Optimize cascade selection and reuse shadow calculations across samples.
Skip unnecessary lighting calculations for cells with zero scattering.
Results
Reduced unnecessary point light evaluations.
Eliminated visible shadow banding in the tested scene.

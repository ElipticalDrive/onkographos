Fixes #1331
When an OrbitController has a target, dragging diagonally makes the camera slowly turn away from it. The cause turned out to be the order of rotations in _update_rotate. The code turns the camera's orientation by elevation first and then by azimuth, but moves its position around the target the other way round. When both angles change at once, the two results differ, and the error builds up
Now the vector from the camera to the target is turned the same way as the camera itself. Calling look_at after every step, as suggested in the issue, would remove the symptom but not the cause
I also added a test: four diagonal rotations, and after each one the camera must look exactly at the target and stay at the same distance. I checked it on main and it fails on the very first rotation. I also ran the scenario from the issue on an offscreen canvas with real mouse events: on main, after 10 drags the target is 20 px off centre (800×800 view); with the fix it stays in the centre. All tests pass
I used Claude Code to reproduce the drift, find the cause and write the fix and the test. I read the code, understand it and ran all the checks myself

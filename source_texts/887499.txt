Stacked on #15 (both PRs append to the same address-table and docs sections). Source: kaarele/witcher3-remastered-vr main at e729bd9 (MIT).
Summary
5.01 renders both eyes of strict Stereo through the game's single temporal upscaler, so DLSS or FSR blend the eyes (ghosting), and the render counter makes the eyes take turns on jitter, noise and motion vectors. kaarele fixed this in several parts; one commit each:
Per-eye DLSS and motion-vector camera ([FEATURE:REMASTER-ENGINE-DLSS 1-4/6], [FEATURE:REMASTER-DLSS-IMAGE 1-5/5], [DIAG:REMASTER-DLSS-EYE-CONSTANTS]): with [engine] temporal_backend=dlss, the game's own DLSS runs with one Streamline viewport and its own frame guards per eye (constants builder, input prepare, SR apply hooked). The per-eye motion-vector camera record (V14003) now also runs on 5.01: builder through the address table, record offset as a table value (0x460 on 4.04, 0x530 on 5.01). Optional dlss_preset, dlss_mip_bias_offset, diagnostic dlss_eye_constants_log.
Per-eye FSR ([FEATURE:REMASTER-FSR-PER-EYE 1-7/7], [FEATURE:REMASTER-FSR2-PER-EYE 1-4/4]): with Anti-aliasing = FSR and [engine] remaster_fsr_per_eye=1, the game's upscale ffxContext gets a twin for the right eye (the exe's FidelityFX imports are redirected, AMD DLLs untouched); on GPUs where the game uses its built-in FSR 2, the right eye gets five twin history targets. Brings src/call_site_hook.h (+ test) and the vendored FidelityFX API headers (external/fidelityfx-api, MIT).
Pair jitter, pair noise, second-eye motion ([FIX:REMASTER-PAIR-JITTER 1-6/6], [FIX:REMASTER-PAIR-NOISE 1-4/4], [FIX:REMASTER-PAIR-PREVIOUS 1-4/4]): both eyes of a pair use the same jitter sample and noise index, and moving objects keep their motion vectors in the second eye.
Docs: kaarele's sections plus our results in docs/remaster-port-status.md, keys in witcher3vr.example.ini, FidelityFX headers in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md, a note in the guidelines' 5.x settings list.
Deviations from kaarele's code: see "Not taken over 1:1" below (one changed place: the reduced collector init detour).
Everything is 5.01 only and off by default (remaster_dlss_per_eye_motion=1 only matters when per-eye DLSS or FSR is on). The launcher writes temporal_backend=none and AAMode=1 on 5.x, so launcher users see no change until they switch these on.
Not taken over 1:1
Collector init detour reduced (changed code). remaster_pair_previous_transforms hooks kaarele's collector init detour, which in his build also feeds his local-light features ([FEATURE:REMASTER-LOCAL-LIGHTS], not taken over). This PR takes the hook with a reduced detour (pair tagging and the previous-transform step only) and a reduced layout check (only the frame index at collector +0xB0 that the detour reads; his check also covers local-light code). Both places are marked with a comment in the code.
Per-eye reset masks without their setters. The masks g_remaster_dlss_external_reset_mask / g_remaster_fsr_external_reset_mask are declared and read as in his code, but nothing sets them yet: in his build the setter is the per-eye asymmetric frustum (remaster_asymmetric_ndc, not taken over yet).
Tests: his "per-eye FSR is 5.01 only" check is taken over; his other new checks belong to features not taken over.
Example INI: his ; comments are written as # like the rest of our file; remaster_dlss_per_eye_motion=1 is listed explicitly.
Everything else is copied unchanged.

Taken over from kaarele/witcher3-remastered-vr (MIT, e729bd9):
[PERF:PAIR-BARRIER-LAG 1-11/11] and [FIX:PAIR-ENQUEUE-AFTER-FENCE 1-11/11].

[perf] pair_barrier_lag=1 lets the strict-Stereo game thread wait for the
pair queued by the previous tick instead of the one it just queued, so the
next game tick overlaps the render (one pair of run-ahead). The capture ring
gets a fourth slot per eye, every pair its own scene descriptor slot, and
the pair pose lead can follow the measured pipeline depth
(pair_pose_lead_adaptive). Default 0: unchanged barrier.

[perf] pair_enqueue_after_fence=1 (default) queues the duplicate eye right
after RenderSceneSetup's per-tick fence wait instead of inside the frame
factory, so both eyes of a pair apply the same skinning/relink batch: with
run-ahead the second eye otherwise drew moving characters one tick ahead.
The fence wait, both call sites and the FlushCommands census probe are
signature-checked; 4.04 and unknown builds keep the old order and lag 0.

Not taken over: the pair rate limit and the world-marker part of the
run-ahead audit (that hook is not in this fork yet). The address-table
test now checks that the RenderSceneSetup factory call bytes reach
FrameDataFactory.

Taken over from kaarele/witcher3-remastered-vr (MIT, e729bd9):
[FIX:REMASTER-PAIR-JITTER 1-6/6], [FIX:REMASTER-PAIR-NOISE 1-4/4] and
[FIX:REMASTER-PAIR-PREVIOUS 1-4/4].

5.01 advances its render counter once per engine render, so in strict
Stereo the eyes take turns: each eye's DLSS/FSR history sees every other
jitter sample, and GTAO and screen-space shadows use a different noise
index per eye. remaster_pair_jitter redirects the generator's two calls
in the frame builder so both eyes of a pair use the pair's sample;
remaster_pair_noise replaces the two noise-index loads with the pair
index. remaster_pair_previous_transforms re-validates the previous
transform of render proxies moved in the pair's first render for the
second render, so moving objects keep their motion vectors in the
second eye (DLSS/FSR otherwise smear them there). All off by default.

Adapted: kaarele's collector init detour also feeds his local-light
features, which are not in this fork; it is reduced to the pair tagging
and the previous-transform step, and its layout check to the frame
index the detour reads.

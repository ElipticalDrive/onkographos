Summary
Adds MarchingCubes::extract(&Voxels<f64>, mask) returning an Isosurface: a port of scikit-image's marching_cubes (Lewiner et al. 2003, plus the classic Lorensen tables), with level, spacing, gradient direction, step size, masks and degenerate-face removal.
Computed in f64. Isosurface holds vertices: Coordinates<3>, normals: Directions<3>, faces: Vec<[usize; 3]> and values: Vec<f64>; spacing is a Coordinate<3> of lengths and level is a plain f64 in the units of the grid data.
Lookup tables are generated from scikit-image 0.26.0's own tables.
Tessellation::from(Isosurface) lets a segmentation be written as STL. With Gradient::Ascent and the object above the level, faces wind outward; use degenerate: false before building a Tessellation.
Parity with scikit-image
The face structure (counts and vertex indices) matches scikit-image exactly; vertices, normals and values match within a small tolerance (1e-6 to 1e-5), because scikit-image stores them in float32. One deliberate difference: a vertex normal whose gradient contributions cancel to rounding noise (below 1e-12 of their size) is returned as zero rather than normalized, since normalizing noise gives an arbitrary unit vector.
Testing
matches_scikit_image replays fixtures.txt (429 recorded scikit-image cases): every non-trivial single-cube sign pattern, cubes reaching each Lewiner table branch, and small volumes covering step size, spacing, gradient direction, masks, Lorensen and degenerate removal.
During development the f64 code was also checked against about 150,000 further random cubes recorded from scikit-image, all with identical topology (not committed).
Unit tests for lut, tables (structural invariants), cell and switch, plus error paths and an outward-normals check for the Tessellation conversion.
Notes
Fixtures were generated with a throwaway scikit-image environment; the generator scripts are not committed.
Docs and the paper citations are left to a follow-up edit.
Multi-material interfaces (e.g. Wu & Sullivan) are intentionally out of scope and will be a separate PR. Per-label marching cubes on binary masks conforms where only two labels meet; junctions are untested.

Hi, thank you for the great work on Speedy-Splat!
I have a question regarding practical usage:
Can the rendering acceleration components — specifically SnugBox and AccuTile — be applied directly to an already-trained 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) model (e.g., a .ply file from the original 3DGS pipeline), without retraining?
From my reading of the paper, it seems these two methods only modify the tile assignment logic during rendering and do not alter Gaussian parameters.
If it is correct, would it affect the rendering quality?
Thanks again for your work and time!

Hi @tongxyh,
Yep. SnugBox and AccuTile quickly and precisely localize the projected Gaussians into pixel tiles. They don’t change any Gaussian parameters and require no retraining, so you can apply them to an existing 3DGS .ply. Because the localization is exact, the rendered images are identical to the original 3DGS output.

There are many different approaches to real-time rendering of\nsmoke. In this work, three different methods were tested and\nevaluated. Soft particles proved to be a reliable method to\nrender smoke as particles doesn't have visible intersections\nwith the scene. Smoke with volumetric lighting resulted in\nthick white convincing smoke, but it's too unstable to be used\ncommercially. The soft particles solution were finally\nsuccessfully implemented into the PC graphics engine at the\ngame development company SimBin.

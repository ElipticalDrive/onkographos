Why
Voxels were a way to save compute, not the look we want. Realistic rendering is for the whole city, not the centre box. This PR does not widen the box. The decision and the later pull requests are in docs/adr/0001-rendering-architecture.md.
?renderer=mesh is phase 0. It covers Freedom Square and Ushakova / Nezalezhnosti avenue down to the embankment (world box x 9984–11264, z 8320–10496, about 1.3 × 2.2 km). Buildings come from the existing v_*.bin tiles. Walls use building:levels / height, with the bake's storey heuristic when those tags are missing. Roofs are flat (with a parapet), gable, hip, pyramid, skillion or dome. Facades are procedural and tiling, with a window grid from the storey count. Meshing runs in a worker, and the chunks are one instanced batch. Ground, streets, vegetation, water, birds, audio and the UI are unchanged.
Inside that box the 1 m block tier is off, so carved voxel buildings are not drawn on top of the meshes. That also drops shop signs and street props there. Landmark lamps on the square stay. Walk collision is still the voxel columns. The outer 96 m of the box keeps voxel buildings so a footprint is not sliced. Pages deploy is unchanged. Pull requests run test, bake and build in .github/workflows/ci.yml and do not deploy.
This branch is rebased onto main after PR #2 (pre-war restoration) was squash-merged. Spawn is still 10293, 8817. West and north of the world did not move, so the centre box in metres is the same. Do not merge yet.

Recommendation
Mesh levels of detail for the whole city, then delete the voxel buildings. Not voxels-as-the-horizon, and not a single cut that removes them now.

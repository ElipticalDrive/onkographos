Bounding Volume - an overview | ScienceDirect Topics
A 'Bounding Volume' is a spatial indexing approach used in computer graphics to enclose elements within a hierarchical structure, such as a bounding box or sphere, to efficiently support spatial queries like visibility culling or ray-object intersections.

What and why
Continues sofia-gros/horizon-mods#379, stacked on #35. Adds optional validated visual.fog declarations with spherical start/end distances and an RGB multiplier. Existing declarations remain unchanged without this opt-in.
Sample the actual rendered camera with at most two loaded-only block reads; respect registered surface height and same-family columns, including upward fluids without mirrored geometry.
Multiply existing fog RGB and only tighten environmental/sky/cloud limits, using the existing spherical shader channel. Keep cylindrical render-distance fog separate.
Apply after the ordinary Fog-off option and before native mod overrides. No new native water/lava identity, FOV or water-vision behavior.
Add unrun declaration, immersion, tint and tighter-limit regressions.
Host issues are disabled; the mod tracker remains the scope reference.

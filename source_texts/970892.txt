Summary
docs/unity/rendering/post-processing.md covers URP Volumes only, as requested. It covers:
How volumes combine:
Global and local volumes, Blend Distance, Weight, Priority, and per-property override checkboxes.
The two default profiles (Graphics settings and URP Asset). They are cached, so script edits to them don't apply.
Volume Mask and Volume Trigger.
Setup: the camera's Post Processing checkbox, HDR for bloom and tonemapping, and local Box Volumes.
Common effects: a table of overrides with class names, uses and gotchas, plus the docs' mobile-friendly list.
Driving effects from gameplay:
DamageVolume: a weight-only volume that pulses on Health.Damaged, decays with a half-life, and holds a baseline at low health.
VignetteControl: edits a profile override, using profile (a copy) rather than sharedProfile (the asset), with Override(), active, and cleanup via HasInstantiatedProfile.
Volume Update Mode, and UpdateVolumeStack() when it's set to Via Scripting.
Common pitfalls.

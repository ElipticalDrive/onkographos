This change enhances the atmospheric rendering system by replacing hardcoded physical constants with dynamic uniforms, allowing for runtime adjustment of the atmosphere's composition and appearance.
Key improvements:
Visual Variance: Introduced temporal and spatial variance in the sun's scattering patterns using 3D Simplex noise in the sky LUT compute shaders. This creates a more organic and "gentle" look as requested.
Physical Parameterization: Exposed atmosphere height, Rayleigh/Mie scattering/extinction coefficients, ozone absorption, and scale heights as controllable parameters.
UI Enhancements: Added a "Physical Parameters" and "Atmosphere Variance" section to the Environment UI, providing intuitive sliders and color pickers for the new attributes.
System Integration: Updated the WeatherManager to drive these new physical parameters, ensuring they blend smoothly between weather states and synchronize across the AtmosphereEffect and AtmosphereManager.
Tested with make check and make glcheck to ensure C++ and shader validity.

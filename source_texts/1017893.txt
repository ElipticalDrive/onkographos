Closes #45. First M4-adjacent visual feature: bakes voxel ambient occlusion into the mesh so crevices/concave corners darken, plus a gentle hemispheric ambient over the existing directional sun.
Before → after (screenshot, radius 6)
The terraced terrain gains real depth — every step and valley now has soft contact shading instead of flat colour. (Verified locally on M3; before/after rendered via --screenshot.)
How
Greedy mesher computes standard 3-neighbour AO per face corner, and now merges cells only when orientation and AO match — so AO discontinuities split the quad instead of interpolating wrong. Adds the classic diagonal flip so an occluded corner doesn't bleed across a quad.
Vertex gains an ao term (0..1, 24→28 bytes); the shader multiplies it into the lighting with a floor so nothing is pure black. Ambient is now hemispheric (brighter facing the sky).
Pure-logic AO + helpers are unit-tested (vertex_ao levels, lone block fully lit, diagonal neighbour darkens a corner).
Cost (honest tradeoff)
AO-varying cells can't greedy-merge, so triangles rise: fixed test region 8,482 → 14,716; radius-12 bench 217,550 → 361,326 (~+66%). The frame becomes GPU-bound → FPS on M3 ~3,330 → ~1,900 (still ~1.9× the 1000 gate). BENCHMARKS.md row + footnote added. Triangle count is a lever LOD (#37–#40) can pull back later.
Verification
cargo test --all (incl. new AO tests + updated golden region count), clippy, fmt — green.
Before/after screenshots on M3 confirm correct AO (darkening in concavities, no seams/artifacts).

The problem of free-form object modelling in
CAGD is an actual task in computer graphics and solving it is
important for practical use. This paper describes a new approach
of semi-numerical computing volume of solids bounded by
rational Bézier surface set. There is an approach for volume
calculation of solids bounded by simple Bézier surface set. In this
paper, the proposed approach is based on the combination of
analytical integration for volume integral computation and
numerical integration for rational function integral computation

Adds minecraft.html, a self-contained 3D voxel sandbox (raw WebGL, no external libraries or assets).
Infinite procedurally generated terrain (Perlin fBm): hills, mountains with stone/snow, beaches, sea, trees
Chunked meshing (16×16×96) with face culling, ambient occlusion, distance fog, translucent water
Procedurally drawn 16px texture atlas (13 block types)
First-person physics: gravity, jumping, AABB collision, swimming, sprint/sneak, fly mode (F)
Break / place / pick blocks with a 9-slot hotbar (1–9 or mouse wheel), block highlight, debug overlay (F3)
Optional ?seed=N URL parameter for reproducible worlds
Verification: loaded in headless Chrome (SwiftShader) with ?seed=4242: no console/page errors, 225 chunks generated, walking/jumping/collision and break/place verified programmatically, screenshots inspected. Real pointer-lock mouse look wasn't testable headlessly (has a fallback for when pointer lock is blocked). Desktop only (no touch controls).

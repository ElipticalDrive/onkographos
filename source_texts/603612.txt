Closes #7.
ElementsFocus::Entity(e) / ElementsFocus::Point(p) on the ElementsCamera centres the rain/snow box, wind window and gusts, ground fog, strikes and thunder distance, cloud weather and shadow maps, trails and waves windows, and the surface weathering's mean. Without it nothing changes; terrain LOD stays on the eye.
Orthographic cameras: cloud rays from the view matrices, parallel and starting on the focus plane; bolt width from the orthographic pixel size.
For an orthographic followed camera, five things Bevy measures from the camera's translation are adapted (docs/research/view-anchor.md Deviations 1–5):
linear distance fog from the nearest ground in view;
one shadow cascade around the focus;
precipitation drawn without the telephoto coverage fade, with a box covering the view;
the ground-fog volume off;
ray origins on the focus plane.
A perspective camera is unchanged.
New isometric example, docs/isometric.gif, docs/isometric.png.
WindState::at_camera → at_focus (migration: rename).
Tests: focus resolution, the ray mirror, orthographic bolt width, orthographic fog onset, strikes/thunder on the focus (CPU); domains centred on a focus 1500 m from an orthographic camera, and an orthographic deck matching the perspective one (GPU).
Cloud march 0.524–0.530 → 0.468–0.529 ms (no regression).
All gates and every GPU suite pass.

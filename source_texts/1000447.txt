Terrain
OpenSimplex2 noise with fBm (src/noise.js) for base hills, valleys and elevation.
A low-frequency region map blends plains, rolling hills and mountains (src/terrain.js).
Ridged multifractal noise gives mountains sharp crests; peaks reach about y=230.
World height raised from 64 to 256. Grass over dirt over stone; steep slopes and high peaks are bare stone.
?seed=123 in the URL picks a different world.
Meshing skips fully buried blocks (median build time per chunk 2.1 ms → 0.8 ms).
Settings menu
Esc → Settings: render distance (2–16 chunks), field of view, mouse sensitivity, invert mouse Y, debug info.
Applies immediately and is saved in localStorage; includes Reset to defaults.
Chunks now unload in a circle just outside the load radius.
Testing
Headless Chromium: spawn on terrain, column consistency, walk/sprint/sneak speeds, jump, break/place, sneak ledge stop, collision while sprint-jumping over hills, all settings applying and persisting across reload. No console errors. Mouse look with a real mouse and real-GPU frame rate were not testable headless.

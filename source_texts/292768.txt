Description
Follow-up of #176, which delivered the reaction–diffusion reference example in 1D (examples/gray_scott_1d: Turing instability of a homogeneous state, self-replicating pulses) because v0.10.0 only has UniformGrid1D. The classic Gray–Scott patterns (spots, stripes, labyrinths, self-replicating spots) are 2D; this issue adds that version once the nD structured grid exists (DD-051, #152).
To settle at implementation:
boundary conditions: the 1D example closes its ring inside the model, since oxiflow has no periodic condition for finite differences and its boundary conditions apply to scalar states only; the 2D version should use whatever the nD grid provides (periodic or zero-flux faces) rather than repeat that workaround;
validation reference: the linear Turing analysis extends directly to 2D (growth rate of a Fourier mode against the eigenvalue of J − |q|² diag(Du, Dv)); the pattern regimes of Pearson (Science 261, 1993) give the qualitative part.
Completion criteria
Common criteria of [MAINT] Seven reference examples across the target physical domains #173 (runs to completion, documented equations and reference, self-check with an error exit, clippy clean, README/DEVELOPMENT entries)

At least one pattern regime of Pearson (1993) reproduced and characterised

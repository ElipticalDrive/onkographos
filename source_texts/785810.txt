GI upgrade
Reuse the surface GI six-axis visibility factor for fog mean sampling in both GI blend sets.
Keep the one-trilinear-fetch fast path in baked clean cells; near geometry evaluate eight corners of the precomputed RGB mean with visibility-weighted, normalized trilinear interpolation.
Match the surface path's all-blocked behavior to avoid blending through thin walls.
Document quality and performance trade-offs.
Dependency
Stacked on PR #11 (codex/gi-correctness-20261102) because the fog ray cache must refresh spatially before visibility-aware sampling improves the volume lighting. Once #11 merges, retarget this PR to master.
Validation needed
No local compile/GPU run was possible from this environment. Please verify shader compilation with DXC/SPIR-V, compare enclosed room scenes and thin-wall fog GI with TOURMALINE_GI_VIS=0, and profile weighted-path cost. Fog mean assets remain RGBA16F; no change to cooked GI formats or descriptor bindings.
Deliberately deferred
Baked emissive area lights and compressed fog mean are larger independent changes requiring dedicated visual/compression A/B and are not included in this PR.

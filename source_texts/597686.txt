Treating quads as two triangles works in many cases. But for large and deformed quads this may be an issue.
An alternative is to treat quads as bilinear patches and intersect the patch directly. This is done in pbrt-v4 for example. It would be helpful to support ray-patch intersection also in Embree. I realize this may feel like a large request, but it could bring significant benefit as more scenes are getting treated as quads primarily.

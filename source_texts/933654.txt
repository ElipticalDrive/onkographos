Glossary to understand the article
SDF
Signed distance functions, or SDFs for short, when passed the coordinates of a point in space, return the shortest distance between that point and some surface. The sign of the return value indicates whether the point is inside that surface or outside (hence signed distance function).
Gradient
Conceptually, the gradient of a function f at point (x,y,z) tells you what direction to move in from (x,y,z) to most rapidly increase the value of f.
The gradient of an SDF gives the direction you should run away if you want to get away (increase the distance) the fastest from the origin of the SDF

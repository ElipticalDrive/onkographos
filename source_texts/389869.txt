Owner (2026-09-07): likes the smooth contours, unhappy with performance and with things sitting off the edge in space; open to a blocky/chunky look if the chunks are small enough to be distinctive. Asked for the top 3 options with renders and performance estimates, plus the cost of 3D voxels / a continuous heightfield.
Root cause of the bug class: the client marches contours between cell centres (contours.ts CONTOUR_SAMPLE_CLEARANCE) while the sim reasons on cell edges. Two truths: lattice vs drawn. The drawnGroundYAt oracle (9cb0379) and the climb riser shift (c9e9fcf) are client-side patches for it.
Decision artifact: being produced by an Opus agent in .terrain-options/ (untracked); link to be added here when published.
Correction recorded: the server does NOT run contour math; it holds only the integer heightmap.

Decision artifact (renders + measured costs): https://claude.ai/code/artifact/91883cf0-3ec4-4478-a0f5-1dbea42a5a4b
Ranked
Tile terraces on cell edges + bounded bevel — makes terrainHeightAt be the drawn surface, retiring the bug class (not narrowing it). Per-chunk cost becomes a proven bound (<=4,608 tris), so the blocky fallback and the three budget guards become deletable. Patch: 7,362 tris, zero ear-clip work.
Constrained smooth contours — pin sheer crossings to the cell edge (all 31 risers of the sheerest pair land at x=19.500 instead of 19.125..19.875) and cap Chaikin displacement at 1/8 cell. Visually unchanged, -28% tris, -41% ear-clip work. Ships first as a 3–5 agent-day stepping stone regardless.
Fine blocky — is tile terraces with BEVEL_CELLS = 0; no separate arc needed. Per-cell: 2,594 tris; 2x sub-cell: 6,308.
Voxels / continuous heightfield: est. 4–10x the cost of option 1, irreversible, and neither addresses the off-the-edge complaint.

Two things:
we did not optimize depth in this work.
an easy way to fulfill your goal might be to first render absolute length, and then apply a scaling factor to get zdepth. The scaling factor for each ray is the reciprocal of the L2 norm of unnormalized ray direction defined here: https://github.com/Kai-46/nerfplusplus/blob/ebf2f3e75fd6c5dfc8c9d0b533800daaf17bd95f/nerf_sample_ray_split.py#L26

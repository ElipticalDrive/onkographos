💡 What:
Implemented spatial viewport bounding box culling in BuildingRenderer.ts, RoadRenderer.ts, and TrafficLightRenderer.ts.
🎯 Why:
During camera movements (panning, zooming, tilting, or rotating), map renderers previously iterated over and projected 100% of all map elements (building footprints, road edges, traffic light approaches) regardless of whether they were visible on screen. Calling MapLibre's projectPoint on hundreds or thousands of off-screen coordinates on every camera move frame caused noticeable stutter and frame drops.
📊 Impact:
Reduces coordinate projection operations (map.project) and PixiJS geometry allocations by ~80-95% when zoomed into a map section.
Prevents unnecessary allocations of temporary vertex coordinate arrays for off-screen elements.
🔬 Measurement:
Load a map scenario with buildings, roads, and traffic lights.
Pan and zoom around the map while monitoring frame rates or CPU performance profiles.
Observe smoother panning and zooming performance with zero off-screen projection overhead.

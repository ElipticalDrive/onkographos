Abstract
Pre-computed sky radiance, transmittance and polarisation coefficient datasets for the **Prague Sky Model** as presented by Wilkie et al. (2021) and extended by Vevoda et al. (2022).
Full version (2.2 GB)
PragueSkyModelDatasetFull.dat - A full version of the dataset as presented
in the first paper, contains the entire range of visibilities, solar
elevations, observer altitudes, and ground albedos, includes polarisation.
Ground-level version (103 MB)
PragueSkyModelDatasetGround.dat - A smaller version of the dataset, contains
only a single (zero) observer altitude, does not include polarisation.
SWIR version (547 MB)
PragueSkyModelDatasetSWIR.dat - A wide spectral range version of the dataset
as presented in the second paper, contains 55 (instead of 11) wavelength
channels, but only a single (zero) observer altitude, includes polarisation.

What
PSNR metrics in W&B, in the same units as the eval_diffusion.py tables:


train_gs.image_quality: computes three per-view metrics, in fp32 and with no graph, using eval_diffusion.metrics' definitions:

psnr;
fg_psnr: over the target silhouette, alpha > 0.5;
bg_l1: L1 outside the silhouette, which shows haze.

Each volume's value is the mean over its views, as in eval.


Logged as <key>_psnr, <key>_fg_psnr, <key>_bg_l1 next to every rendering loss:

train and validation loss/photom;
validation loss/photom_t<t>;
validation loss/photom_full, plus /source and /targets;
non-diffusion runs' loss/photom.

The renders already exist, so the cost is negligible.


Two logging inconsistencies fixed in the train step:

Spike-skipped steps: train/loss/photom* and photom_frac were logged before the spike-skip check, so they included skipped steps. They're now held until the step is accepted, like train/loss and train/loss/x0.
Train x0 parts renamed: from train/loss/<part> to train/loss/x0/<part>, matching the validation keys (<set>/<name>/loss/x0/<part>). Existing panels on the old train keys need updating.

"""Per view (B, V), in fp32 and without a graph: psnr, fg_psnr (over the target silhouette, gt_alpha > 0.5; 100 dB for a view

fg = (gt_alpha.float().reshape(sq.shape) > 0.5).float()
nf, nb = fg.flatten(2).sum(2).clamp_min(1), (1 - fg).flatten(2).sum(2).clamp_min(1)

"psnr": -10 * torch.log10(sq.flatten(2).mean(2).clamp_min(1e-10)),
"fg_psnr": -10 * torch.log10(((sq * fg).flatten(2).sum(2) / nf).clamp_min(1e-10)),
"bg_l1": (ab * (1 - fg)).flatten(2).sum(2) / nb,
}

Groundwork for VR: the renderer draws from a ViewSet instead of reading yaw, pitch and fov_degrees straight off Frame. No visible change (label wanted: skip-release; I can't set labels from a fork).
What changes
New gfx/view.rs:
View is one eye's view of the camera-relative world: view (rotation), projection (reversed-Z), aspect and tan_half_fov.
View::screen(yaw, pitch, fov, aspect, far) builds the flat-screen view.
ViewSet holds the views of a frame (one today, two on a headset) and the view used for culling.
Renderer::views(frame) replaces view_projection and the private projection. camera_frustum goes through the same View.
draw_frame reads the following from the primary view: view_proj, inverse_view, and misc.z / misc.w.
The frustum and the back-face eye (projection_center) come from ViewSet::cull().
Why it is bit-exact
The products keep their order: projection * (bob * spin) * rotation, evaluated left to right as before. aspect and tan(fov/2) are computed with the same expressions.
the_screen_view_matches_the_legacy_projection_bit_for_bit compares to_bits() of the old and new matrices over several FOVs, orientations, aspects, far planes and poses.
native_post and pack_uniforms keep their own projections for now. Their association, (P * bob) * spin, and the GL projection differ, so they move when a second view needs them.

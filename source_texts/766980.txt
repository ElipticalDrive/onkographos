Goal
A package of spectacular, procedurally generated terrain on top of the existing landform families — terrain generation 21, shipped in one go on one branch (feat/spectacle-gen21), one PR, one release:
Arch lands — fields of crossing rock arches, arches running over other arches, arch rows, collapsed arches.
Pillar islands — thin stone towers (one or two pillars) carrying balconies on the way up and a crown island on top, with meadow ponds and endless waterfalls.
Bridge mesas and "impossible" table mountains — tables joined by rock bridges, visor tables with undercut roofs, two-storey, holed, tilted and ring tables.
Giant overhangs — wave rocks, rock shelters.
Cave portals and daylight halls — mouths big enough to fly a ship into, surface-near halls with a skylight and a forest on the floor.
Fossils — bone strata in cliffs, fossil ridges with free-standing ribs, giant skeleton landmarks, fossil monuments with a scan payoff.
Four new planet types where each family is dense, plus the families as rare finds on the tagged classic types.
Floating islands (Skylands, corrupted, gas giant) stay exactly as they are.
Why
The generator already has every mechanism these forms need — height offsets, extra bands, worm carvers, paints, stamps — but every existing overhang form is small (arches span 16–30, caps 1–3 thick, cave mouths r ≤ 4, the rib-cage prop is 7 blocks). Scale, composition (several shapes per feature, shapes crossing) and band material are what is missing, plus three infrastructure gaps: bands carry no material or surface skin; the far view, the far-column streaming band, the landing-pad planner and the structure placer are blind to bands and caverns.
Rules that bind every sub-issue
New worlds only: every family gates on WonderProfile.Generation >= 21; every classic / gen-1 / gen-3 golden group stays byte-identical.
Hotspot-cell recipe, seam-safe margins, trig-free geometry (eight integer directions, sqrt-normalised hash vectors), per-cell memos for anchors.
New band kinds go into a NoInlining sibling of GetExtraBands (the Hosted world is OOM-killed seconds after start: JIT of WorldGenerator.GetExtraBands transiently needs ~1 GB #1740 JIT rule), new families into their own partial file + test class.
Server-authoritative; nothing persisted except the fossil monuments (once, like every structure).
Docs: docs/developer/WORLD_GENERATION.md gets a generation-21 section; TODO.md carries the status.

Infrastructure first, then the forms (independent of each other), then the types:

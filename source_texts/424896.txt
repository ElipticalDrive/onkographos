We do not include explicit normal information in our geometry representation, so the triangles should be treated as double-sided. Instead, normal information is stored with the textural data via textured_mesh_to_volumetric_attr, which accounts for the effects of normal maps.
If you need consistent face orientation, here are two potential solutions:
Geometric reorientation: Without additional normal information, you can use algorithms like unify_face_orientations from cumesh (or similar libraries) to reorient the face directions.
Alignment with implicit normals: You could flip triangles that are inconsistent with the normal vectors stored in o_voxel. Note that this would require a simple extension, as the current TRELLIS.2 model does not explicitly model a normal channel for the sake of simplicity.

Your 3 axis-aligned directed-edge maybe found in edge_flux_sign.
I understand that in the FCT1.5 decoder, face orientation is directly determined by edge_flux_sign; the normals are only used in the decode step to choose the diagonal split, and do not directly flip the faces.
Also, edge_flux_sign is redundant — neighboring voxels share edges, and 3 of them correspond to the ovoxel's intersected flag, i.e., it's like a directed intersected flag.
FCT indeed hasn't released the VAE or DiT. If you could release the FGD-D VAE and DiT, that would be a really significant contribution — looking forward to it!

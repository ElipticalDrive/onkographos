Abstract
We study operator equations Af = g for operators between Hilbert spaces X and Y. Both the cases of linear operators A and of nonlinear A with a special structure are treated. Approximate inverse means a solution operator which maps the data g to a stable approximation of the solution of the ill — posed problem Af = g. This inversion operator is precomputed without using the data g, see [10].

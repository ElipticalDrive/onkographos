Formal verification of parallel prefix sum and stream compaction ...
The verification of the stream compaction algorithm crucially depends on the verification of the prefix sum algorithm. Stream compaction reduces an input array to a smaller array by removing undesired elements, and many other applications, such as collision detection and sparse matrix compression, rely on it.

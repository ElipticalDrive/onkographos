Missing capability
Viewer perspective/orthographic navigation, fit and viewpoints exist, but imported glTF camera associations and authored camera parameters are not consumed.
Suggested priority: P2.
Current implementation
src/renderer/scene/CameraController.cpp viewer-camera creation and src/utility/SceneManager.cpp:1003 node reconstruction.
Reference implementation
apply authored cameras — src/ui_renderer.cpp#L1816
projection and clip parameters — src/ui_renderer.cpp#L1838
write current view to camera — src/ui_renderer.cpp#L1872
scripted camera selection — src/benchmarking.cpp#L93
Scope
Preserve node.camera references and list/select authored cameras using their full parent world transform.
Support perspective yfov/aspect/near/far and orthographic magnification with the engine's coordinate/reverse-Z conventions.
Update a camera from the current view and expose named/indexed camera selection for deterministic captures.
Integrate camera edits with undo, animation channels and glTF save.
Acceptance criteria
Transformed parented cameras reproduce authored framing, roll and projection.
Switching camera or scene correctly resets temporal state.
Fixtures cover perspective, orthographic, missing optional far plane and invalid camera references.

Implement in the project's Vulkan-Hpp RAII/VMA/Slang architecture and follow docs/coordinate-conventions.md where rendering is affected.

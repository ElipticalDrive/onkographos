Implicit transmission processing ignored pixel caps and changed flat background colors across quality tiers. Model normalization also dropped volume overrides, while Go instanced replacement commands omitted neutral values and retained stale volume state. These four cases now preserve the authored output and volume controls. White backgrounds also remain finite in half-float targets at low exposure.
Regression tests fail on unchanged main and pass here, using drawable opaque and glass triangles and Go-to-browser replacement commands. Transmission copies reuse the existing fullscreen post quad and blit shader to stay within the original bundle limits.
Validation: 2,406 JS tests pass, including type checks, architecture, bundle freshness and bootstrap budgets. Scene/capability Go tests and vet, production WASM size, all 11 wire routes, and the WebGPU honesty gate pass. Budgets and performance receipts are unchanged.
The combined glass and ocean source changes also pass the unchanged bootstrap size limits and transmission regressions.

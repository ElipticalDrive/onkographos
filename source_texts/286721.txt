MAGOS: Scene Optimization Using Ray Tracing and OpenCL
Authors/Creators

Description
This paper presents a method for optimizing 3D scenes by determining triangle visibility using ray tracing. Each triangle in the scene is tested for occlusion from the camera using the Möller–Trumbore algorithm, executed in parallel via OpenCL.

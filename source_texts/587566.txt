Summary
This PR adds root isolation for polynomials in Bernstein form: the points
of (0, 1) where such a polynomial changes sign. With Bezier extraction, it
gives the exact crossings of a NURBS face element with a grid line, the
sign changes of its weighted coordinate minus the line times its weight
function.
It adds:
utils/bernstein.hpp, header-only, with the de Casteljau evaluation of a
polynomial in Bernstein form on [0, 1] and its split into the parts on
[0, t] and [t, 1];
the number of sign changes of the coefficients, which bounds the roots
in (0, 1) with the same parity;
bernstein_crossings, which splits intervals until each holds at most
one sign change and refines that root by bisection, reports a zero at a
split point where the sign changes, counts intervals narrower than the
caller's tolerance by parity, and never reports zeros at the ends.
Testing
The test suite covers:
evaluation and both parts of a split reproducing a quartic summed term by
term;
simple roots, a triple root at a split point reported once, a double
root left out, zeros at the ends left out, and two sign changes without
a root;
roots closer than the tolerance counting by parity, a close pair left out
and a close triple reported once.

Add bernstein_crossings, the points of (0, 1) at which a polynomial in
Bernstein form changes sign. Intervals are split until each holds at
most one sign change of its coefficients and then refined by bisection
on the polynomial; a zero at a split point counts where the sign
changes, intervals narrower than the tolerance count only with an odd
number of sign changes, so that touching roots are left out, and zeros
at the ends are never reported.

Co-authored-by: Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>

Summary
add a fixed-capacity GPU manifold dual-contouring extractor for one 32^3 page plus halo
mirror the corrected CPU manifold topology, including disconnected material-link splitting and stable f32-compatible QEF policy
compact QEF/topology vertices and indices with bounded scans, full 64-bit generation tags, explicit overflow suppression, and fixed resources
validate Rust/WGSL layouts and CPU/GPU geometry, material, winding, manifold, page-boundary, determinism, capacity, and stale-generation contracts
Resource contract
13 persistent buffers
45,928,928 bytes at the default worst-case capacity
at most 8 storage buffers in any shader stage
resize does not allocate or mutate extraction resources

headless GPU parity on NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 / Vulkan
six canonical fixtures match exactly within f32 noise
dense adversarial maximum error: 0.000854492 cell position, 0.000003174 normal
adjacent X/Y/Z page QEF ownership parity passes
deterministic byte-identical repeat and undersized-capacity suppression pass

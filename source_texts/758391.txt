Froxel fog on a RenderView (render-view-wants item 3, option A).
What changed
fog.slang is projection-agnostic. It no longer reads the depth row of the projection (A = m[10], B = m[14]), which a reflected camera's oblique near plane (Lengyel clip) fills with x and y terms. The pixel's planar distance is 1 / (inverseViewProjection * (ndc, z, 1)).w; a froxel at planar distance d on a pixel's ray is eye + rayOf(uv) * d (rayOf = h.xyz - eye * h.w at the far end of the ray, whose dot with the clip w row is 1 for every point). Both are used in inject and apply; the history reprojection takes the planar distance from the previous view-projection's clip w. fog.spv rebuilt with python tools/shaders/shaders.py packages/render/src (only fog changed; --check current).
RenderView: pub fog FogSettings (read every picture), enableFog(renderer, device) bool (mirrors enableTiming; false unless the renderer's colour format is RGBA16_FLOAT, the fog pipelines' format), cutFog(). The view's depth now has TEXTURE_BINDING. render sets keepDepth around the view's render and records the fog into the picture's own encoder, over the view's depth, so it reads the cascades and shadow parameters that render has just written for its camera.
fx.cone: the FxFrame filling is extracted from ImageFx.renderScene into writeFxFrame (reflected cameras: eye = camera.position(), forward reflected).
Measured (headless; packages/render/tests/viewfog.cone, 34 checks)
Five screen points of the earlier table, absorbing fog (extinction 0.1) on a wall perpendicular to the plain camera at the reflected eye: recovered 5.95 (true 6) at x = 0, -0.4, +0.4 and 39.87 (true 40) at +-0.4, identical to the plain camera. The same test against the old shader: 5.98, 4.91, 7.64, 16.4, -22.1.
A scattering scene (ground, 3 cubes, sun, height fog): reflected camera's fogged picture vs the plain camera at the reflected eye differs by at most 0.001 of a peak of 1.13 (old shader: 87).
Main view: the existing fx fog shots (god rays, no shadow) are byte-identical to before; a perspective scene (70 degree fov, sun, height fog, 5 frames with history): 95 of 49152 bytes differ, by 1 level of 255 at most.
Suite 1448/1448; congo test all packages 525 tests (the two failures of one run were my own edits during the run; render and controls re-run clean); test_congo 85; pegasus3d 5b368de builds with 0 errors (scratch copy C:\src\pegasus3d-render-view-fog).
Not done
pegasus3d's main-view shot comparison (--script opens a real window): left for Cone module. Shots that carry the froxel fog: weather-cloudy, weather-overcast, weather-rain, weather-snow; all other shots have the fog pass off.

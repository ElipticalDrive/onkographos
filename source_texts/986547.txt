What happens.
Two plane types.
math::Plane (math/include/engine/math/frustum.h:22) is dot(n, p) + d.
physics::ConvexHullData::Plane (physics/include/engine/physics/collider.h:25) is dot(n, p) − distance.
Signed distance is written out by hand at physics/src/convex_hull.cpp:50, physics/src/physics_payloads.cpp:327, physics/src/contact_clip.cpp:183, physics/src/physics_query.cpp:197 and editor/src/editor_panels_viewport.cpp:155.
Passing one type's offset into the other's formula flips the sign without any error.
Triangle normal and plane from three points: nine copies with five degenerate rules.

Example triangle: a = (0,0,0), b = (1e-5,0,0), c = (0,5e-6,0), cross-product length 5e-11.
face_plane keeps a (0,0,5e-11) face with offset 0.
epa_face_plane rejects it.
The hull seed check normalizes it to (0,0,1).
narrow_phase skips it.

Failure scenario.
A hull with a sliver face behaves differently in building, EPA and narrow phase.
An editor feature that uses the hull plane formula on a math::Plane gets inverted inside/outside tests.
At a grazing view angle, dropping an asset and choosing "Create here" at the same pixel place the object at different points, or one of the two refuses.
Commercial benchmark.
Godot: one Plane type (normal, d, with distance_to, intersects_ray, has_point) built from three points (Plane(a, b, c)). Geometry3D reuses it (Plane.xml).
Unity: one Plane with Raycast, GetDistanceToPoint and Set3Points.
Unreal: one FPlane with PlaneDot and FMath::RayPlaneIntersection.
Why this class and severity. Duplicated primitive with disagreeing copies. The differences need sliver geometry or grazing angles, so deferred, P3.
Fix direction. Owner: math.
Make math::Plane the only plane, with these helpers:
plane_from_points(a, b, c, &out), refusing a degenerate triangle by one stated rule;
triangle_normal;
signed_distance;
ray_plane(ray, plane, &t).
Physics stores math::Plane, storing −distance, so the hull wire format is unchanged. Moving existing physics kernels verbatim keeps the determinism suites bit-equal.
The editor uses one ray_plane with one reach policy.
mesh_cook uses math::Vec3.
Closure. Per the consolidate-primitive skill:
2 plane types → 1.
9 triangle-normal copies → 0.
2 ground intersections → 1.
Each behaviour change has its own test: the sliver triangle above, and a grazing-angle drop and menu at the same pixel.
Recurrence gate: a rule on cross\(math::sub\( followed by normalize outside math.
Related. #1137 (degenerate-input policy), #1125 (public geometry queries), #1119 (asset drop).

Summary
Streaming is whole-resource: a streamed texture is created with its whole mip chain and streamed once. Per-mip streaming and a budget-driven residency policy let larger scenes fit in video memory.
User stories
As a game developer, I want textures to stream in by mip under a memory budget, so that large scenes fit in video memory.
Requirements
Per-mip streaming: tiled or reserved resources, or a mip chain uploaded tail first.
A residency policy driven by a budget (gpu.Stats.video_memory_budget) that evicts and streams mips by need.
Together with the asset runtime's unloading of unused assets by use.
Acceptance criteria
A scene larger than the budget stays within it, with visible mips refining as they stream.
Tests cover partial residency on the null backend.
docs/gpu/queues-streaming-readback.md and docs/rendering/assets.md describe it.
When to do this
When scenes are larger than video memory allows to keep resident.
Context
GPU: Streaming, Rendering: GPU residency
lib/core/gpu/d3d12/streaming.odin, lib/engine/render/residency.odin.

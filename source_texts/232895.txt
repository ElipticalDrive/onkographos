Context
A chunk is a full 16×256×16 column, and most of its height is air. Meshing, storage and culling work on the full column.
Measured before the work (M1, hilly): every chunk's highest solid block is under y = 80, so 11 of 16 sections are air. Since #593, the mesher skips air with bit masks, so sections give meshing no more speed. Culling still tests each chunk as a 256-high bounding sphere. The light grids (128 KB per chunk) are the memory cost: #606.
Scope of this issue: the culling part. Storage per section is #606.
Acceptance Criteria
The default cull tests each chunk's box against the planes of the view-projection matrix. The box is the x and z of the chunk and the y range of its mesh.
The cull never removes a visible chunk: frames with the cull and with --frustum=none are byte-identical.
The goldens CI passes.
The PR states the drawn chunks and the frame time before and after.

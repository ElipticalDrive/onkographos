Terrain tiles behind other terrain are drawn: the far side of a crater rim, a valley behind a ridge, a body behind another body. Tile selection culls against the frustum and the horizon only.
Approach
Hierarchical-Z occlusion culling (Haar and Aaltonen, "GPU-Driven Rendering Pipelines", SIGGRAPH 2015): each selected tile's box, from its height tile's exact bounds, is tested against the depth pyramid in a compute pass that writes the indirect draws.
The exact bounds stay on the GPU: no readback.
Works with CPU tile selection, and moves into Select terrain tiles on the GPU #262 (Move terrain detail selection and culling to the GPU) once selection runs on the GPU.
To decide
Two-phase culling with last frame's visible tiles, or one phase against last frame's reprojected pyramid (whatever scatter occlusion culling chose).

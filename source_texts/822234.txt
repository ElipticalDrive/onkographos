Haochen Wang has debugged this issue. Apparently it is caused due to a subtle difference in the Zip-NeRF and CamP configurations.
CamP's default configs are intended for 8 GPUs, but Zip-NeRF's default is for 1 GPU so you need to take that into consideration.

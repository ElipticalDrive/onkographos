Changes the way directional lightmaps are calculated by using the technique described in https://web.archive.org/web/20160313132301/http://www.geomerics.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/CEDEC_Geomerics_ReconstructingDiffuseLighting1.pdf
This implementation is based on https://github.com/kayru/Probulator/blob/4a97a2b021eb2ca7ef696f4ddf36ba9a9432cbb6/Source/Probulator/SphericalHarmonics.h#L136-L151
Calculating SH lighting this way has several benefits, namely it avoids negative colors on the opposite side of a bright light source, as well as keeps the total energy.

Author
By the way, does this technique also apply to SH evaluation for lightmap dynamic probes? I'm wondering if this can help resolve #108526. I've tried to max() each component of the ambient light to 0.0 but it didn't help improve visuals (although it did get rid of negative pixels in the final image).
Dynamic probes make use of L2 spherical harmonics, while the Geomerics method only applies to L1. There has been some research in terms of de-ringing/windowing L2 SH, but it generally doesn't look as good as a non-linear model: https://www.activision.com/cdn/research/Deringing_Spherical_Harmonics_PPS_V2.pdf

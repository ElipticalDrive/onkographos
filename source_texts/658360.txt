Outcome
Participating media and subsurface scattering render correctly.
Acceptance criteria
Homogeneous and heterogeneous media are supported
Subsurface scattering is validated against the reference
Both degrade to a cheaper approximation under the frame budget in preview
Memory cost of volume grids is budgeted like any other resource
Notes
Volume grids are large and easy to allocate carelessly, which makes them a common cause of out-of-memory failures during final renders.

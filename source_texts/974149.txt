On top of clean_gradient_mode (#20), adds the knobs and tooling behind the stop-gradient / action-velocity / noise-augmentation results (full tables in docs/experiments/action-flow-stop-gradient.md):
action_velocity_clean_gradient_mode — route the action-velocity term to the encoder independently of the flow term (default: follows clean_gradient_mode). Flow-all_stopgrad + AV-full = 0.079 vs 0.013 fully attached: the co-adaptation rides on the flow gradient.
reconstruction_noise_aug (+ noise_aug_t_min 0.7, noise_aug_probability 0.5) — released UNITE's noise augmentation before decoding. E + all_stopgrad + noise-aug = 0.0114 ± 0.0005, tied with direct 3-D FM (0.0120), no L_act; with noise-aug on, L_act's marginal gain is ≈ 0 in both attachment modes.
SyntheticSharedLatentFlow.losses() gets the same clean_gradient_mode (the paired-source codec rows go 0.203 → 0.018 at latent 2 under all_stopgrad).
eval_synthetic_inference_steps.py — re-evaluate checkpoints at several Euler step counts (no few-step advantage for the latent path: direct 0.015 vs G 0.025 at 8 steps).
Builders for the 2×2 and follow-up matrices as run on ICE (key names mapped from the pre-merge branch), the ICE CPU-array launcher.
Tests for every knob; loss values are unchanged for every existing config (defaults off / full).

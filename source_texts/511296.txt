## Motivation

- High resolution generative models are mostly 2D based, ignorant about the implicit 3D structure.
- 3D-aware generative models require heavy computation, limiting their applications in high resolution image synthesis.
- Design a method to achieve both desiderata.

## TL; DR

- Relieve the computational burden by early aggregating of 3D NeRF representation, projecting the volume density into 2D space.
- Redesign upsampler to minimize 3D inconsistency introduced in 2D upsampling stage.
- Introduce NeRF path regularization, which enforces pixel intensity of proposed model and full NeRF to match, for better 3D consistency.
- Apply progressive training strategy as done in ProGAN (Karras et. al., 2017) for stable learning of underlying 3D geometry.

## Previous Work

- StyleGAN, StyleGAN2, StyleGAN3 (Alias-free Generative Model)
- HoloGAN, pi-GAN, GRAF, GIRAFFE: 3D-aware 2D GANs

### Early Projection to 2D space

- 2D GANs produce images quickly mainly due to their inherent structure of producing pixels in a single forward pass. As NeRF-based volume rendering takes relatively long time, propose to project 3D feature (density) into 2D space (per-pixel feature vector as in pixelNeRF) in the early stage and operate in image plane.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/46308407/145917971-8624a698-38ef-4f74-9b17-682178b7a268.png)
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/46308407/145917991-a837a5fd-5126-458f-8f77-ec24f16322ac.png)
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/46308407/145918011-ff41055a-3aac-4bb2-9438-ccbd35a05627.png)

- **naive**: obtain pixel value and sample points along the ray to stack the color densities to produce the final pixel under given camera pose
- **theirs**: volume render density for a given ray and produce final color simply through multiple MLP layers
- **advantage**: more operations can be done in simple feed forward manner.
- The 3D rendering is done in low resolution feature space to further control memory foot print. Careful upsampling ensues naturally.

### 3D-Consistency-Preserving Upsampling

- PixelShuffle: "checkerboard"
- LIIF: texture sticking
- Bilinear: bubble artifacts

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/46308407/145918590-0860c40f-16fb-437e-932a-be6f37dcb775.png)

- Model upsampling in residual manner with learnable MLP, shuffle pixels and apply blurring kernel.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/46308407/145918714-fcdcb149-298b-40ae-ad0f-8a28ec19a7e3.png)

- Theirs originally map 3D NeRF representation to 2D image space in low resolution for computational efficiency. Enforce the pixel intensities of theirs and full NeRF-based rendered output to match, hence 3D geometry consistency. (on subsampled pixels)

- They propose couple more tricks/design choices: (1) remove view direction conditioning for RGB prediction (since using unstructured 2D image datasets like FFHQ) (2) Fixed 2D noise injection (2D per-pixel noise works well only for 2D GANs; employing it in 3D-aware models can result in degraded 3D consistency.

### Overall Architecture

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/46308407/145919163-88312222-4d5e-418b-84ea-2a5e9cddb404.png)

- NeRF++ backbone where the foreground and the background is processed in parallel.
- Latent mapping network and the modulated convolutional synthesis network.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/46308407/145919310-f94c8768-f093-4b31-859c-f056410a881e.png)

- Non-saturating GAN objective + R1 objective (original StyleGAN2 objective) + NeRF-path-reg.

- Adapt 3-stage progressive training: (i) low resolution (ii) linearly scale up to target resolution (iii) operate at the target resolution

## Comments

- 3D-aware + High Resolution look quite excessively heavy on the dish; kudos for the authors who made it work with 8 V100 GPUs.
- Multiple careful design choices made on the way (e.g., early agg, noise fixing, upsampler, view-condition, NeRF-path reg). A LOT of work must have been devoted in to this paper.
- Output is very intriguing, especially the reconstruction of a woman figure in point cloud and COLMAP. 
- Also quite amazing how simply using intermediate NeRF representations (density-based volume rendering) results in 3D aware generative model. Neither the generator nor the discriminator has been provided any explicit 3D supervisions. The idea of incorporating traditional volume rendering (whether it be approximate or not) is this much powerful.

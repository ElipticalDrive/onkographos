RT workstream stage R3: SDF ray marching. An SDF scene graph with sphere tracing, soft shadows, AO, fog and god rays, plus SDF and raster compositing through shared depth, on the CPU in float64 and on the GPU in float32.
Bounds were committed before any R3 code (evidence/rt-r3-bounds.json, 2cc583d); method notes 1 to 4 were each committed before the run they changed. Every run is in evidence/rt-r3-runs.json.
What it adds
raw/renderer/sdf.hpp, sdf_scenes.hpp: a postfix SDF program (primitives, Mandelbulb and Mandelbox estimators, smooth union, transforms, repetition). A float64 sphere tracer refines hits with a sign bracket and bisection.
sdf.wgsl: the GPU march, shading terms, fog, god rays and composite with the R1 rasterizer.
Owned scenes: garden (the painterly target), arcade (the retro target), menger_tower, bulb.
raw_native_cli sdf-render (shaded frames plus albedo, normal, depth and material AOVs for the shader lab) and sdf-parity [--quick].
Results (WARP; SwiftShader quick; RTX 4090 under the GPU lock)
M1 (CPU against analytic intersections): passes after method notes 1 to 3 (grazing stop error, linear bracket, a control that still discriminates).
M2: garden, menger_tower and arcade within on every backend; the smooth-union control fails.
M3 shadows and AO, M5 god rays, M6 compositing, M7 determinism: within, controls failing.
Two open failures on this branch:
M2 on the Mandelbulb (WARP t within on 93.3% of hits against 99.5%, normals 57%; RTX 99.46% and 98.2%).
M4's 256-sample fog march (1.33e-3 against 1e-3; midpoint-rule error, confirmed in float64).
CI gates everything else (tests/gpu/sdf_gate.py).
A follow-up PR (method notes 5 and 6, stacked after this one) closes M4 (2.9e-5) and narrows the bulb to 99.475% and 98.2% on both backends. It also records why the bulb stays open: a correctly rounded float32 replica of the march reproduces WARP's counts exactly.
Does not prove
That the SDF shading matches the path tracer or M3's material model, or that the fractal scenes render at an interactive rate.

Closes #1448
Implements docs/design/viewer-rendering.md Phase E (E.2): greedy merge for opaque full-cube faces. No SPEC.md section is involved (mesh-only change).
What
In meshBlocks, untinted full-cube faces (stone, dirt, sand, gravel, planks, logs, ...) are collected per face direction and plane and merged into rectangles of at most 15 blocks a side. Cells merge only when layer, shade byte, UV variant and AO level are equal.
UV variant: faces merge only when their variants match (not excluded). faceUV now takes the quad's extents, so flips and swaps are per block and the tile repeat lines up with the unmerged mesh. Because the variant hash gives 1 in 8 odds per neighbour, varied blocks (stone, sand, gravel) rarely merge; dirt, planks, logs and the like merge freely.
AO: only cells whose four corners share one AO level merge. Cells with an AO gradient stay single quads, so diagonal flipping is untouched and the gradient is not stretched.
Grass, leaves, cutouts, plants, and tinted blocks are left alone.
meshBlocks/meshHalf get a greedy option (default true). Older tests that count or inspect single-block faces (mesh_ao, mesh_bush, mesh_ground_cover, mesh_uv, and the overlap test in lod) pass greedy: false; lod's UV range check was widened to 15 blocks.
Tests
New viewer/test/mesh_greedy_opaque.test.mjs: merged quads split into cells equal the unmerged mesh cell for cell (layer, shade, tint, per-corner AO and UV); 15-block cap; overlapping areas agree on shared blocks; AO-gradient cells are not merged. Checked that a deliberately broken UV flip fails them.
node --test viewer/test/*.test.mjs client/test/*.test.mjs: all pass except dry grass blocks carry the dry tint of their corners, which also fails on master.

We evaluate the effectiveness of the algorithm for shared memory parallelism. We also present the advantages of the Hybrid OpenMP/MPI parallelism to perform high-fidelity large scale calculation. We show that CPU time for a best-estimate 2D whole core calculation can be reduced from several days to few minutes on computer cluster.

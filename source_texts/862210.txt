Add a headless two-rough-reflection benchmark to the SMS harness. A float64 CPU oracle uses bracketed bisection, and the Slang compute kernel solves the coupled seed equations with safeguarded Newton. The analytic fixture has a unique inverse root and a fixed-endpoint BDPT baseline with three connection strategies and balance MIS. The initial on-demand shift uses the forward area Jacobian and a fresh proposal for complete target support.
The optional cached-neighbors estimator shares each receiver's solved fresh path with its horizontal partner through workgroup memory. Each receiver combines its own draw and one shifted neighbor draw using multi-sample balance MIS. A bounded monotone check evaluates reverse support, with an inverse solve near patch boundaries. Odd row tails use fresh slicing.
On Apple M5 Metal, the cached estimator passed 10,368 matched CPU/GPU rounds, including 8,470 accepted shifts and 110 fresh-only support cases. Maximum absolute radiance and Jacobian differences were 2.54e-6 and 7.22e-6. Both timed estimators agreed with independent quadrature.
Across seven seeds, cache reuse reduced mean variance per round by 20%. At 0.25 GPU seconds, median RMSE was 9.72e-6 fresh and 1.13e-5 cached. At one wall second it was 4.97e-6 fresh and 4.74e-6 cached. Both budgets include source generation, cache publication, shifts, MIS, and accumulation. The original fixed-endpoint BDPT and on-demand shift results remain in the benchmark guide.
Validated with Slang 2026.1.2, the SMS benchmark suite, source policy and formatting, strict MkDocs, API generation, and wiki checks. The experiment covers a single analytic chart and workgroup cache rebuilt each round.

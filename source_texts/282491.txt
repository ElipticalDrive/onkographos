The laser confocal scanning microscopic (LCSM) system is one kind of modern microscopic instruments which is characterized by its depth discrimination capability. The three-dimension reconstruction is very importance in order to obtain the three dimensional images of a object. In this paper, the theories and methods of how to create accurate depictions of three-dimension data based on volume rendering is discussed and a three-dimensional reconstruction method based on the microscopic image is discussed. The experiment results show that the reconstructed images have high quality.

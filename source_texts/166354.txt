Lossy compression is often used in practice of image processing. A specific feature of our research is that we assume optical and/or medical images to be compressed noisy and take this fact into account. Moreover, major attention is paid to the specific case of spatially correlated noise. The study is carried out for five different compression methods based on discrete cosine and wavelet orthogonal transforms. It is shown that for all these techniques the existence of optimal operation point (OOP) is possible according to standard and visual quality metrics. Peculiarities of dependences of these metrics on parameters that control compression are analyzed. It is demonstrated that noise intensity and correlation properties influence behavior of these dependences. Some practical recommendations are given.

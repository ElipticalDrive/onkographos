Hello i3DGS authors,
Thank you for releasing this excellent work. I am currently trying to integrate i3DGS with other 3D Gaussian Splatting pipelines, especially methods that require the standard COLMAP SfM initialization.
I have a question regarding the output format of i3DGS.
As I understand, i3DGS directly estimates camera poses and generates Gaussian primitives without relying on the traditional COLMAP SfM pipeline. However, some downstream 3DGS methods require the standard SfM outputs, such as:
sparse/
├── cameras.bin
├── images.bin
└── points3D.bin
or the common 3DGS format:
point_cloud.ply
Could you please provide some guidance on the following questions?
Is there a way to export the sparse point cloud generated during the reconstruction process (similar to COLMAP's points3D.bin)?
If i3DGS does not explicitly generate SfM sparse points, is it possible to obtain an equivalent sparse point cloud from the Gaussian primitives (e.g., using Gaussian centers as 3D points)?
Does the released code provide a tool or script to convert the i3DGS output (e.g., hierarchy.ply or Gaussian model) into a standard 3DGS format that can be directly used by other 3DGS frameworks?
If such conversion is not currently supported, could you please suggest the recommended way to initialize another 3DGS method using the reconstruction results from i3DGS?
Thank you very much for your time and for sharing this great work. Any advice would be greatly appreciated.
Best regards.

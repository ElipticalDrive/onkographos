Owner
Rebuild of the thesis project around its original goal. Rigid octree
parts are animated by a keyframed skeleton and placed in a per-frame
top-level BVH. Rays are traced by a hierarchical DDA with pixel-footprint
LOD, on the GPU (OpenGL 4.5 compute) and in a matching CPU reference.
Octree builder (Morton-sorted, bottom-up, prefiltered colour/normal)
from SDFs or the thesis raw volumes, with size checks
X-Toon detail modes per the NPAR 2006 paper; fixes the 2016 shader
bugs (log formula, r = 1 divide by zero, unsigned gradients, light
position used as a direction, pow of negative n.v, GL_REPEAT)
Demo scene: crowd of walkers sharing 6 limb octrees around a tree
or the thesis bonsai
Unit tests (traversal and BVH against brute force) and a
--compare mode that checks GPU output against the CPU renderer

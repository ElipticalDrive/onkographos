Abstract
Image compression is a method to get rid of the redundant data from the image. Just the solely essential data is to be kept, and one must cut back the storage size by eliminating unimportant data, transmission information measure and coordinated universal time. This significant data is obtained by numerous transform techniques which imply reconstruction while not losing quality and important data of the image. The authors have undertaken the task of conducting a study of the performance distinction of the two very significant methods for the same, namely discrete cosine transform (DCT) and discrete wavelet transform (DWT). These techniques are mainly deployed for JPEG image compression. This paper highlights the implementation techniques adopted for these two methods. Also, the pros and cons of each is mentioned after an in-depth analysis.

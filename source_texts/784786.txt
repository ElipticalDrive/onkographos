What
The first-person weapon view camera-system describes — "WHEN a first-person camera renders the world and a separate weapon view THEN both SHALL be render views derived from one evaluated camera" — and the first-person camera that drives it.
The weapon view is drawn into the world's frame, not composited over it: one depth buffer, one prepass, one light loop, one temporal resolve. What differs is the matrix a weapon vertex goes through — the viewmodel's own field of view and near plane (1 cm), with its depth squeezed into the slice of the reversed-Z range nearest the eye (z' = (1 - s) + s·z, s = 1/16). Every world surface past near / (1 - s) (10.7 cm) is behind every weapon fragment, and the weapon still orders itself.
Weapon pushed into a wall 0.45 m out (barrel reaches 0.85 m)
with the weapon view: 5995 of 5995 texels
as world geometry: 4654 of 5995 texels
Depth composition golden (references/weapon_view_composition.png): the barrel reaches into the pillar and is drawn whole; every texel off the weapon is the world's, byte for byte.
Pieces
cy::rendering-viewmodel (new, src/rendering/viewmodel/): the squeezed projection, guaranteed_clear_distance, settings_from_views (checks the weapon view is the primary view's camera: same pose, distinct history identity), assembly_view, ViewmodelFrame (last frame's lens) and write_viewmodel.
Assembly: AssemblyView::viewmodel (appended). The world view no longer draws the weapon layers (survivors on any weapon layer are dropped); the weapon view is culled against its own frustum; its draws are marked render::kInstanceViewmodel (new bit) and carry the shadow policy on kInstanceCastsShadow / kInstanceReceivesShadow. A device-culled frame refuses a weapon view.
Frame: CyFrameData / FrameViewData append the viewmodel rows, last frame's rows and a policy word (688 → 832 bytes). cy/frame.slang appends cyViewmodelDepthVertex, cyViewmodelForwardVertex and their skinned forms (the rigid entries are untouched). The forward pass finds a weapon surface's light list through the world's projection of its position (frameClusterPixel) and skips the contact term for it unless asked. PipelineSetup::viewmodel creates the variants; the recorder binds them for marked draws. Frame, particle, strip and selection headers regenerated (embedded_headers.py, never hand-merged).
Lighting, shadows, GI: the weapon is lit at its camera-attached world position — lights, shadow map (received by default, cast on request), fog, irradiance volume. Screen-space terms see it as a thin shell at the eye.
Motion vectors: previous rows = last frame's viewmodel lens over previous_camera_relative_view (split out of previous_camera_relative_to_clip, same arithmetic). A carried weapon has zero motion; a raised weapon moves as its lens does.
Camera (servers/camera/first_person.h): FirstPersonCamera — mouse look with the sensitivity applied once, head bob by distance walked, recoil as a damped spring stepped in closed form, ADS blending both lenses (camera::blend), motion reduction, and the three aims (control / view / weapon). produce_weapon_view, ViewPurpose::Viewmodel.
ABI 1.13 (append only, baseline updated, just quality-abi green): first_person_configure/input/kick/reset/state/release, viewmodel_configure/get over cy::game-backend-first-person; refused in a fixed step. CyberdyneKit FirstPersonCamera, WeaponView, FirstPersonTests; Rust SDK and Swift overlay regenerated.
Docs: new guide docs/guides/first-person-weapon-view.md; README, ABI README, Swift README/guide; OpenSpec change add-first-person-weapon-view (strict-valid) with the living specs synced; requirements map — "Aim is three things" moved from exempt:m11e to a case, two new camera-system requirements and one native-abi requirement mapped.

Gaps
No sample drives a first-person rig end to end yet (the host places the eye and writes the weapon rows; the adapter and first_person_eye / first_person_weapon are the pieces). No video: there is no sample to capture.
A weapon draw ignores DrawPipelineFn (a material variant's vertex stage projects through the world's rows); the selection mask and virtual geometry don't know the weapon view; the device cull refuses one.
Depth of field and screen-space effects read the squeezed depth (weapon flattened at the near plane).
CameraSettings::reduce_motion does not yet drive the first-person motion_scale.

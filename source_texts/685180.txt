Color rendering currently works only for Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer painted models (paint_color plus the project filament palette). Everything else renders in mono:
Core 3MF materials: <basematerials> and <m:colorgroup> referenced by pid/pindex on objects and triangles (p1/p2/p3), used by PrusaSlicer, Cura, Fusion and most CAD exporters
PrusaSlicer per-extruder colors (slic3rpe:mmu_segmentation plus Metadata/Slic3r_PE.config extruder_colour)
PLY vertex colors (red/green/blue[/alpha] vertex properties)
OBJ materials: mtllib / usemtl with Kd diffuse colors (read the sibling .mtl when the sandbox allows it)
Golden fixture and test for each
The Color / Mono toggle should appear whenever any of these supply more than one color.

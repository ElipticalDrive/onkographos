Description A matrix manipulation library, preferably one that uses SIMD instructions, is required that supports at least cross products for 3x3 and 4x4 matrices. The engine will be using homogeneous matrices, so 4x4 matrices will be utilized to represent 3D objects too. The HUD might use 2D objects, hence 3x3 support. Supported functionality should include transpose, scalar operations, transformations (linear and non-linear) and cross products.
Why it's needed? To power the 3D renderer.

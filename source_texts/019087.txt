In our modern society we are overwhelmed by information.\nSupercomputers all over the world are generating data sets containing\nterabytes of numerical data. In the years ahead the greatest\nchallenge to the scientifc community will be to make sense of all\nthis information. Specifically, tools will be needed to explore large\nmulti-dimensional data sets describing complex physical processes.\nComputer aided visualization will certainly be one of the most\nimportant means of solving this problem.\n\nTurbulence researchers at the Norwegian Defense Research\nEstablishment need visualization software in order to validate\nsimulation code, present results, and gain greater insight into\nphysical phenomena. In this thesis we present VoluViz, an\ninteractive volume visualization application developed at the\nNDRE, which is designed to fulfill these needs.

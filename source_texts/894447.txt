The voxel terrain was completely invisible due to two bugs:

1. **Inverted density convention**: The density function used
   `surfaceY - y`, placing air in the "inside" role (density < 0).
   Marching Cubes generates normals pointing from inside toward outside,
   so all triangle normals pointed *downward into the ground* instead of
   upward. Back-face culling (Three.js default: FrontSide) then culled
   every visible face, making the terrain invisible.

   Fix: flip to `y - surfaceY` so that solid rock is "inside" (d < 0)
   and air is "outside" (d > 0). Normals now point upward/outward,
   matching what the camera sees from above.

   Cave carving signs are also flipped: adding a positive value to a
   negative (solid) density pushes it toward 0 / positive (air pocket).

2. **GLSL `color` attribute redefinition**: `generateVoxelTerrain` was
   setting `material.vertexColors = true`, causing Three.js to inject
   `in vec3 color` into the shader preamble. The custom vertex shader
   also declared `attribute vec3 color`, resulting in a GLSL compilation
   error (`'color' : redefinition`).

   Fix: remove the `vertexColors = true` assignment. The ShaderMaterial
   already declares and reads the color buffer attribute explicitly, so
   no Three.js injection is needed.

All 30 voxelTerrain tests updated and passing. Build clean.

Summary
Two bugs were fixed in lib/voxelTerrain.ts:
Inverted density convention — the density was surfaceY − y, which put air in the Marching Cubes "inside" role. This made all generated triangle normals point downward into the ground, so back-face culling rendered the entire terrain invisible. Flipping to y − surfaceY (solid = inside, air = outside) makes normals face upward, matching the camera. Cave carving signs were also flipped accordingly (+= instead of -=).
GLSL color attribute redefinition — generateVoxelTerrain was setting material.vertexColors = true, which caused Three.js to inject in vec3 color into the shader preamble. Since the custom vertex shader also declares attribute vec3 color, the GLSL compiler raised a redefinition error and the shader failed. Removing the vertexColors = true assignment lets the custom shader manage the attribute directly. All 30 voxelTerrain tests were updated and pass; the build is clean.

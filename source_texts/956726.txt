Done when
Neighbouring faces with the same texture and direction are merged into one larger face.
A flat 16 × 16 surface becomes one face instead of 256.
Textures still repeat once per block on a merged face.
Faces of generic shapes that fully cover a side can merge.
Partial and diagonal faces are never merged.
The world looks the same as before.
The rendering benchmark shows fewer triangles and no higher frame time.
Tests check that a merged mesh covers exactly the same area as the unmerged one.
Out of scope: Merging faces across chunk borders.
Notes: Greedy meshing is the standard method. Faces merge only when everything that affects how they look is the same, so per-face data added later, such as light, keeps working.

Volume VFX: The Ultimate Tool for Virtual Production
Volume VFX is a relatively new technology that will completely change virtual production. With its help, players or actors will be able to dip into a virtual 3D environment and get unforgettable emotions and an immersive experience. Thanks to the use of volume filmmaking, you can get advanced visual effects and extend its reality (XR).
What is Volume VFX?
The volume filmmaking is a technology that allows you to perform virtual production using LED panels and real-time rendering. This is a green screen alternative that has completely changed the filming process. Now actors do not need to be filmed against a green screen and imagine the picture in their heads because they can get on-set visualization, thanks to immersive technology. This effect is achieved using spatial computing and virtual set design. This technology is used both for modern video games and during filming.
Benefits of Volume VFX
Using the volume VFX offers many advantages for filmmakers and actors, developers, and architects. Let's talk about them in more detail:
Better quality of the film or developed game. Thanks to Volume VFX and visual storytelling, actors will be able to immerse themselves in their roles better. If it is a game, then developers can experiment with special effects and a cinematic virtual environment;
Continuity of the process. VFX Technology allows for continuous filming or game development;
The Volume Filmmaking allows for the creation of real-time visual effects thanks to animation software;
Creation of interactive content using dynamic lightning, LED wall, and digital backlot.
These advantages make volume technology revolutionary and indispensable. Therefore, more and more filmmakers and developers are using it in their work.
Applications of Volume VFX
The volume technology makes work easier in many areas of our lives. It is used not only in cinematography but also in other sectors.
Game development
Developers often use VFX volume as a specialized technique within the broader field of CGI (Computer-Generated Imagery) to add realism and spectacle to the game. All special effects, environment, and visualization of individual components are achieved with this technology.
Cinema
The Volume VFX is often used in cinematography. In this case, a 3D digital set is created in advance for a specific scene. Thus, green screens are no longer used in the production stage. The actor immediately sees in-camera VFX and can play his role more accurately. Thanks to this technology, we can enjoy even higher-quality films.
Architecture
Volume Technology is often used by advanced architects. It helps create realistic visualizations of buildings. This happens before construction, which allows you to evaluate the future building, see how it will look, and show it to clients. For developers, this is a great way to identify problems at an early stage and fix them, as well as analyze how the building will look at different times of the day and from different angles.
How to Get Started with Volume VFX
If you want to learn how to use this technology, then you will need to study a lot of information. Decide on the direction in which you plan to develop, for example, volume filmmaking. Learn the features of this technology, digital set extension, motion capture, key features of compositing and post-production tools. Pay special attention to lighting techniques and camera operation in virtual space.

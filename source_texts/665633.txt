In: SDF function > Primitive
Description
An SDF function for a torus, which is a shape formed by sweeping a minor circle along a major circle.Both circles have adjustable radiuses.
INFO
To learn more about concepts and workflows involving SDF functions, go to the dedicated page: Working with SDF functions
Inputs
Radius major FloatThe radius of the circle along which the minor disc is swept to form the surface of the torus.
Default: 0.5
Radius minor FloatThe radius of the circle being swept along the major circle to form the surface of the torus.
Default: 0.2
Center position Float3The world space position of the pivot of the torus.
Default: (0, 0, 0)
P Float3The transformed world space position. Use this input to apply additional transformations using the Offset P and Rotate P nodes.
Default: The untransformed world space position.

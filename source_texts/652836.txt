Look at procedural terrain generation w/ erosion like in Zylann's HTerrain.
Probably wait until compute shaders are running. Then look at:
https://github.com/SebLague/Hydraulic-Erosion
https://github.com/dandrino/terrain-erosion-3-ways
https://discourse.panda3d.org/t/dynamic-terrain-erosion-on-the-gpu/14935
https://search.brave.com/search?q=terrain+erosion+glsl&source=android
https://github.com/jice-nospam/wgen
Auburn offers his whole library in GLSL which could be included as a compute shader based terrain generator, which would depend on #174.
https://github.com/Auburn/FastNoiseLite/tree/master/GLSL
Also the current worldnoise algorithm can be included and it should be stampable - add a region with the current worldnoise settings.

@otto-link Thanks for the interest. You can certainly have your front-end app output files that we can import, or you can directly export to Terrain3D as World Creator already does. If you do, we can link up your app in the docs as another option for heightmap creation.
Regarding this ticket, there are those who won't want to use an external app. For them, I'd like to incorporate heightmap generation directly within Terrain3D running on the GPU. Godot already has a general noise library, but it's not terrain specific and is CPU bound. I'm primarily considering a GLSL noise library and GLSL erosion code we have.
I could consider incorporating libraries with appropriate licenses. Unfortunately, the GPL is incompatible with MIT. Usually within the GPL family, libraries are released under the LGPL, while apps are released under the GPL.

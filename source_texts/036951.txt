Abstract: This work delves into the implementation of ray tracing algorithms and their application in graphics rendering. Ray tracing is a sophisticated technique that simulates the interaction of light rays with objects to create realistic images. The process involves generating rays from a camera position towards pixel positions, calculating intersections with scene objects, and determining lighting effects through shadow and reflection calculations. This paper presents a comprehensive study on the advancements in ray tracing technology, highlighting its significant parameters such as camera position, field of view, and resolution. We discuss the challenges encountered in ray-sphere intersection calculations, shadow rendering, and optimizing computational performance, and offer solutions that enhance both realism and efficiency in rendered images. This study aims to improve the technical understanding and application of ray tracing in various graphics rendering scenarios, contributing to future developments in computer graphics and related fields.

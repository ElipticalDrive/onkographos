Upgrade the first opaque lighting pass into a more convincing crystal presentation.
Goals
Render into an HDR framebuffer.
Add thresholded bloom for emissive edges/mineral cores.
Add Fresnel-weighted transparency/reflection approximation.
Approximate absorption/tint through crystal thickness.
Add subtle chromatic dispersion at high-contrast edges.
Add depth-based volumetric fog / atmospheric scattering.
Keep the material path fast enough for tens of thousands of instances.
Later option
Evaluate ray-marched SDF or ray-query approaches for hero crystals only rather than forcing every crystal through an expensive refraction path.
Done when
Crystals read as luminous/translucent mineral structures instead of opaque colored geometry, while the main garden remains real-time on the RTX 3090 target.

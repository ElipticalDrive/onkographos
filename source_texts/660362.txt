Person 2 — 2.5D Mapping & Grid Engine
Extend the uniform mapper into Veyra's core variable-resolution representation.
Scope
Implement the complete world-aligned dyadic hierarchy:
```
L0 = 5 cm
L1 = 10 cm
L2 = 20 cm
L3 = 40 cm
```
Use implicit parent/child relationships based on (level, ix, iy) rather than pointer-heavy quadtree nodes.
Implement:
parent/child key calculations
covering-leaf lookup
leaf activation/deactivation
split and merge operations
mixed-resolution point ownership
resolution-aware neighbor access
topology-change bookkeeping
DistanceLOD:
0–10 m → 5 cm
10–30 m → 10 cm
30–60 m → 20 cm
60–100 m → 40 cm
rolling-window behavior across all levels
stale-cell cleanup across hierarchical tiles
mixed-resolution map snapshots
per-level statistics and active-cell counts
Merge logic must preserve count, mean, variance / second moment, min/max elevation, semantic evidence, and appropriate temporal metadata.
Required invariants
Every observation belongs to exactly one active leaf.
An active parent and its descendant cannot coexist for the same region.
Four children exactly cover one parent.
No gaps or overlaps occur across resolution boundaries.
Merge preserves aggregatable statistics.
Rolling-window movement does not change world cell identity.
Do not introduce unrestricted recursive 2:1 balancing or topology cascades.
Expected Result
A fully functioning DistanceLOD mapper that can be compared with Uniform5 using the same input sequence and produce a correct mixed-resolution semantic 2.5D map.

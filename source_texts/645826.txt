💡 What:
Precomputes geographic bounding boxes for building footprints in BuildingRenderer.ts and uses geographic viewport bounding box culling (with a 10% padding margin) during rebuild().
🎯 Why:
BuildingRenderer.rebuildOnCameraChange() runs on every camera pan and zoom event. Previously, every building polygon's vertices were projected via projectPoint regardless of whether the building was visible on screen.
📊 Impact:
Eliminates coordinate projections (projectPoint) and PixiJS graphics calls for off-screen buildings on camera movements, drastically reducing frame update processing time when viewing zoomed-in map areas.
🔬 Measurement:
Verified through a new unit test suite (BuildingRenderer.test.ts) that off-screen building polygon vertices are skipped during rendering updates while on-screen vertices are correctly projected. Full test suite and production build (pnpm build) pass cleanly.

Hi @hojunking, you are right we haven't used the SH paramters during the SceneSplat encoder pretraining. The decision was made early before the training. The main concern was that without the SH paramters, the inut dimension is kind of balanced for color and geometry encoded by 3DGS with total input dimension of 14 (3 for center, 3 for scales, 4 for quaternion, 3 for base color and 1 for opacity). Simply using the total 48 dimension SH paramters might dominate the input and affect the training.
Meanwhile, I think there is motivation to include full SH paramters, as their view-depandant encoding could help better distinguish geometry and semantics from the effects like specular highlights. A separate processing might be better compare to simple concatenation in the input.

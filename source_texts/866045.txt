Follow-up to #228. A consumer scanned 40 test IFC models and found 45 `IfcBooleanClippingResult` instances in 5 files, e.g. walls cut by roofs. They are the next most common exact boolean after openings.
#228 made `ReferenceExactCompiler` subtract placed extrusions through the general exact boolean (`axiolid-brep-boolean`, ADR 0080, with its tolerance contract).
Proposal
Unbounded half-space (`IfcHalfSpaceSolid`, agreement flag): clip by a plane. Either add a direct plane-clip operation on an exact B-rep, or model the half-space as a large box limited to the operand's bounds plus a margin and reuse the general boolean. Document which, and why the result does not depend on the margin.
Polygonal bounded half-space (`IfcPolygonalBoundedHalfSpace`): the plane clip, restricted to the extruded polygonal boundary. This is the intersection of the half-space with a placed prism, so it can reuse the exact compiler: boolean of placed operands (placed extrusion minus placed rectangle prism) #228 path.
Several clippings per body compose.
Done when
A wall clipped by one or two roof planes, under a general placement, has exact volume and certified `boundary_distance` matching closed forms.
A bounded half-space that cuts only part of the wall does the same.
Flush and tangent cases fall under the exact compiler: boolean of placed operands (placed extrusion minus placed rectangle prism) #228 tolerance contract.
Unsupported shapes, such as a curved half-space base surface, are refused by name.

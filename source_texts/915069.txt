Purpose
This draft isolates a complete rebuild of TEKNIK's basic Android world. It does not import the previous prototype's gameplay code.
Included
deterministic procedural block terrain;
packed exposed-face chunk meshes with cached height fields;
collision-first priority streaming and stale-request pruning;
collision creation/removal budgets;
safe spawn held until the full nearby collision ring exists;
walking, jumping, touch controls and desktop controls;
mining, placement and persistent edits;
streaming, collision and FPS telemetry;
ARM64-only Android export;
architecture decisions, quality gates, roadmap and reference-mod capability map;
smoke coverage for safe spawn, chunk transition and save reload.
Acceptance rule
This remains a draft until CI passes and the APK passes functional, stability and 50 FPS testing on the Vivo T3x. A successful workflow alone is not treated as proof that the milestone is complete.

Target-device visual correction
Head e7c406b7edfdc4c57640a78e971d6440f8d7652d addresses the three defects demonstrated in the latest Vivo T3x recording.
Changed
Replaced the large transparent double-sided sea-level plane with simple opaque, unshaded, back-face-culled color water that cannot cast shadows. The recording indicates this plane—not the disabled directional shadow system—was producing the moving black dome/fan artifact.
Raised the first-person camera near plane and disabled shadow casting for every first-person helper geometry node.
Replaced the full 12-edge cube selector with a depth-tested four-edge outline on only the targeted face.
Replaced the pale washed-out sky with a stronger blue upper-sky/daylight horizon gradient, lower ambient wash, lighter fog, and ACES tonemapping.
Expanded the presentation/input smoke test for the water render path, first-person shadow casters, face-only selector, and sky gradient.
Added docs/DEVICE_VISUAL_GATE.md so these issues require another physical-phone recording before closure.
Validation status
Two workflow attempts failed before GitHub created any runner job or artifact. No Godot parser, smoke test, or APK export executed, so this revision is committed but not CI-verified and no APK from these attempts should be used.
The PR remains draft and unmerged.

Goal
Add a fourth viewport pane that renders a Maximum Intensity Projection (MIP) of the loaded volume, interactable with mouse orbit (drag) and zoom (wheel), alongside the existing Axial / Coronal / Sagittal MPR panes.
Scope is MIP only — no transfer functions, no full direct volume rendering (DVR), no lighting/iso-surface. Those can be follow-ups.
Why this is tractable (what already exists)
The volume is already uploaded as a single filterable 3D texture (r16float) in SliceRenderer.setVolume (src/render/slice-renderer.ts) — the MIP just samples the same texture.
Patient-space geometry is available on Volume.geometry (src/dicom/types.ts), with helpers in src/render/reslice.ts (index→patient LPS affine, bounding box). Reuse it to orient the 3D view to patient anatomy.
Panes are already drawn as independent viewports/scissor rects on one canvas (SliceRenderer.renderPanes).
Window/level is already a uniform in the slice shader; MIP should reuse the same window transform (PS3.3 linear form) on the projected max value.
Tasks
Layout — extend mprLayout in src/render/layout.ts to a four-up 2×2 grid (return a 4th rect). Today it returns a 1-big-+-2-stacked arrangement.
Pane model — in src/app/viewer/viewer.ts, panes are currently keyed by Orientation and the per-pane state (sliceIndices/zooms/pans) is a 3-tuple indexed by orientation. Introduce a discriminated pane kind (e.g. {kind:'mpr', orientation} | {kind:'3d'}). The 3D pane has no slice index; give it its own camera state (orbit angles + zoom) signals. Three MPR panes occupy three grid cells, the MIP occupies the fourth.
Raycast shader — new src/render/raycast-shader.ts (follow the WGSL string-constant pattern of src/render/slice-shader.ts). Per fragment: build a camera ray, intersect the volume's box, march through the 3D texture accumulating the maximum sample, then apply the existing window/level transform. A fixed step count derived from the volume dimensions is fine.
Renderer — add a second GPURenderPipeline (raycast) to SliceRenderer (or a small sibling renderer) and select the pipeline per pane in renderPanes; raise MAX_PANES to 4. Keep the MPR slice path unchanged.
Camera — new src/render/camera.ts: pure view/projection matrices, orbit (azimuth/elevation) + zoom, and ray generation. Default orientation should put patient superior up using Volume.geometry, consistent with the MPR conventions. Suggested approach: place the volume box in world space via the index→patient affine, look at its center, and in the shader transform world-space rays into texture space [0,1]^3 to march.
Interaction — over the 3D pane, drag orbits and wheel zooms (branch by pane kind in the existing pointer/wheel handlers); disable the voxel probe over the 3D pane. Leave MPR pan/zoom/scroll/probe behavior unchanged. Swap-main (X) may keep cycling the three MPR orientations while the 3D pane stays fixed — implementer's discretion, just don't regress MPR.
Tests — unit-test the pure camera/ray-box math (mirror the style of src/render/reslice.spec.ts). The WGSL itself isn't unit-testable; that's expected.
Acceptance criteria
A 4th pane shows a recognizable MIP of the volume; dragging orbits it and the wheel zooms it.
The MIP respects patient orientation (superior up by default), like the MPR panes.
Existing Axial/Coronal/Sagittal panes, the cursor probe, pan, zoom, and slice scroll are unaffected.
ng build --configuration production and ng test --watch=false both pass; existing tests stay green. Do not modify package-lock.json.
Out of scope (possible follow-ups)
DVR with a transfer function, iso-surface/gradient shading, lighting, MIP slab-thickness controls, picking in the 3D pane.

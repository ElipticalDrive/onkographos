Roadmap item M3.5 · estimate 10 h · milestone M3 · Sensor twin I: scenes, birds and the thermal camera
Radiometry: Planck band radiance (8–14 µm), emissivity, a bird surface-temperature model, sky brightness temperature (Swinbank, Berdahl–Martin, cloud correction) and first-order atmospheric transmittance from humidity and visibility.
Milestone goal: physically plausible thermal scenes with exact ground truth, for any weather.
Milestone exit criteria: the four reference scenarios render deterministically; contrast changes with weather as the radiometric model predicts (property tests). Release v0.2.0.
Done means the definition of done holds and M3.5 is ticked in ROADMAP.md in the same change.

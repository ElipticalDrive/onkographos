Problem
MCX currently supports srctype = disk and Lambertian direction sampling (srcdir.w = -Inf) for 3D simulations, but their natural 2D counterparts do not appear to be available.
For a 2D simulation, a disc source naturally reduces to a line segment, while cosine-weighted sampling over a hemisphere naturally reduces to cosine-weighted sampling over a semicircle. Supporting these 2D cases would likely also allow for easier comparison with 2D diffusion-approximation simulations using diffuse boundary sources.
Proposed change
I propose extending the existing disk source as follows:
In 3D, keep the existing disk source unchanged.
In 2D, make srctype = disk sample photon starting positions uniformly along a line segment centred at srcpos, with total length 2 × srcparam1.x.
In 3D, keep the existing cosine-weighted Lambertian sampling for srcdir.w = -Inf unchanged.
In 2D, add the corresponding cosine-weighted angular sampling over a semicircle.

Implemented by #274 (merged in 1f53e1e): in 2D, srctype = disk now samples a line segment of half-length srcparam1.x centred at srcpos, and srcdir.w = -Inf uses cosine-weighted sampling over a semicircle. See the PR for the validation of both distributions.

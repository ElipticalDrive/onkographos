Motivation
Reduce runtime CPU/GPU spikes and visible popping by introducing a prioritized remesh queue and keeping chunk VBOs alive until explicitly remeshed.
Improve rendering performance by adding frustum + lightweight occlusion culling and better chunk visibility heuristics to avoid drawing distant/hidden chunks.
Enhance visual quality by adding greedy meshing, ambient occlusion vertex tinting, proper UV tiling and texture wrapping, and vertex-color fallbacks for missing textures.
Produce more natural terrain and coastlines by refining multi-scale height blending and introducing column-context sampling for terrain generation.
Description
Replaced the simple dirty chunk Set with a prioritized remesh scheduler using requestChunkRemesh, requestChunkAndNeighborsRemesh, and rebuildDirtyChunkMeshes to defer and rank remeshes.
Reworked setBlockTypeRaw to compute lighting-sensitive changes, enqueue remeshes for affected neighbor chunks, and optionally trigger immediate rebuilds via rebuildDirtyChunkMeshes.
Rewrote updateChunkGeometry to a greedy meshing pipeline that emits merged quads, computes simple AO per-corner, packs vertex attributes into static THREE.BufferGeometry VBOs, reuses per-chunk meshesByKey, and disposes/update buffers safely.
Added textured-material improvements in loadAssets by enabling RepeatWrapping and vertexColors on loaded textures, and changed UV logic to support tiling vs per-face UVs and fallback color materials (COLORED_OPAQUE).
Implemented chunk-level frustum culling plus a two-stage occlusion heuristic (angular bins + nearest-depth per cell) in updateChunkFrustumCulling and only recompute culling when camera moves/rotates beyond thresholds or interval elapses.
Introduced camera-state tracking (lastFrustumCameraPos, lastFrustumCameraQuat) and reduced frustum cull frequency when stationary to save CPU.
Added render distance configuration support by reading worldGenSettings.chunkRenderDistance and computing effectiveChunkLoadRadius with low-end device adjustments and a small retention hysteresis in getChunkRetentionRadius.
Converted chunk loading/removal to use squared-distance checks, clear and dispose meshesByKey on removal, and request initial remesh on createChunk rather than immediate meshing.
Small gameplay plumbing: applyBlockPhysics now calls the centralized rebuildDirtyChunkMeshes after physics updates and updateChunkAndNeighbors now requests remeshes rather than directly rebuilding geometry.
Worldgen changes: InfiniteWorldGenerator.getHeight was tuned for better coastal shaping and blended sampling by introducing coastNoise, continentalness, modified weights and spike clamps to reduce vertical artifacts.
Terrain chunk generator refactor: added buildColumnContext to precompute continentalness, erosion, coastalShelf, detailStrength and pass column context into sampleCellDensityForProfile and heightFromBiome, tightened scan ranges, improved coastal/clamping behavior, and added deep-ocean trench biasing.
Testing
Ran a local build and lightweight smoke test of the singleplayer runtime to verify no startup/runtime errors and to confirm chunks generate, remesh, and render without obvious console errors.
Executed the repository automated test suite via npm test and the tests completed successfully on the change branch.
Performed a manual playtest pass to exercise block edits, physics-driven changes, and camera movement to validate remesh scheduling, frustum/occlusion behavior, and visible chunk transitions.

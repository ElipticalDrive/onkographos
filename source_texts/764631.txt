Context
The terrain uses one Perlin noise layer with terraces, and positions rounded to steps of 10 voxels. The wiki iteration 6 asks for biomes. The map grows by regions (#19).
Goal
Varied worlds with the style of the art direction (#64).
Scope
Generate the world by regions (Epic: Grow the map by regions and expeditions #19). The result depends only on the seed, the region position and the generator version.
Add biomes from elevation, moisture and temperature.
Add cliffs and terraces with a natural shape.
Add rivers and lakes, with the water rules of the pathfinding design (Write the design of the pheromone pathfinding #53).
Add trees and plants as voxel models.
Add ores in the stone layers.
Select a safe start area for the settlement.
Notes
Stonehearth stays a reference for the game design only (Write the game design reference and the art direction #64). Do not copy its code or its assets (Remove the Stonehearth reference files #27).
Dependencies
Depends on: #49, #64.

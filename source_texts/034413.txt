In short yes, but without any code to preview I can only suggest you try watching this tutorial on YT or others like it for more specific details.
But if you have already or another similar, then I would try something like:
Enable Depth of Field in Post Process
Adjust parameters related to Focal Distance and Focal Region
Modify your shader to account for any depth information by sampling your depth buffer to calculate your blur in relation to the distance of your focal point (e.g. the couch compared to the table)
Using the depth information you can then decide how much blur to apply to an indvidual pixel using the distance from your focal plane.
I pulled most of this off of the Unreal Engine Documentation and the above youtube video and am in no way an expert in Unreal engine. Best of luck and I hope I was of some help.

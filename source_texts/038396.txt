Visualization for medical volumetric data has been an important method to aided diagnosis and treat.Nowadays its techniques embrace a wide field of research and application.Therefore it is proposed that VTK library is used for implementing the visualization of medical data.Two types of rendering method,surface rendering and volume rendering are studied.The marching cubes algorithm was used for surface rendering,both ray-casting methods were used for volume rendering.Their characteristics of two rendering methods were compared as well.The reconstructed results showed that as a reconstruction and visualization toolkit VTK has powerful function and is very easy to be used for medical image processing.

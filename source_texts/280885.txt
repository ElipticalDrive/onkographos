Question
How much of the hazard benchmark benefit comes from per-mesh BVH acceleration versus scene-level BVH acceleration?
Measured answer
At 361 rays, per-mesh BVH acceleration reduces p95 scan latency from 21.284 ms (true-brute) to 0.524 ms (mesh-bvh), a 40.6x speedup. The top-level scene BVH reduces it further to 0.197 ms, another 2.67x.
At the 0.5 ms budget, the layers map 9 -> 257 -> 361 rays and 34.1% -> 100.0% -> 100.0% safe stops. Per-mesh BVH contributes +65.9 percentage points (paired 95% CI [+63.5, +68.3]); scene BVH contributes another 104 rays but 0.0 percentage points because fixed-ray safety has already converged. Across every declared budget, per-mesh BVH accounts for all mapped safe-stop improvement in this experiment; scene BVH still provides additional latency reduction, without additional safe-stop improvement under the unchanged ray layouts and scenarios.

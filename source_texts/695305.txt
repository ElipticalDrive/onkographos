Pull request overview
This PR extends OrcaSlicer’s 3D rendering pipeline by adding a selectable Phong shading model, a screen-space ambient occlusion (SSAO) post-process pass, and a basic “cast shadows on plate” effect, with new AppConfig toggles and associated GLSL shader programs.
Changes:
Added new shaders (phong, ssao) and a UI toggle for selecting Gouraud vs Phong shading.
Added an SSAO post-process pass driven by new config/UI toggles.
Added a stencil-based projected “plate shadow” pass driven by new config/UI toggles.

@RF47 This is a very cool addition! Congratulations!
I would like to recall my old proposal that I did not describe in any issues. Now that it's possible to create shadows on a model, is it possible to use this shadow map to place seams? The principle is as follows: where there is a light area, the likelihood of a seam is lower, and where there is a dark area, the seam will be located and will be selected based on the darkest value. This will also work well in hollow models, where the seams will be located in the hollows and folds. This principle of light construction is very similar to what we see with our own eyes, and we rarely look into the shaded corners of a model.
To implement this method, it seems that you need to adjust the position of the light source in terms of azimuth and declination, and even add 2 or 3 similar ones to illuminate the model from the best angles.

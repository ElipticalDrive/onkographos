A volumetric display system using swept volume technique has been studied in this paper.Adopting digital micromirror device(DMD) as the space light modulator,we project a series of 2 D time-varying image slices of a 3 D model onto a rotating-screen spinning at a sufficiently high speed,because of the persistence of vision,the viewer′s visual system can perceive a 3 D volumetric image by fusing together the successive 2 D images into a 3 D whole.The prototype of the display has been trial-produced,spatial object can be displayed with true three-dimension in a cylinder display space with a diameter of 200 mm and a height of 100 mm.it has a 24 Hz volume refresh rate and can be viewed from almost any angle.It is proved that the scheme is feasible.

Volume rendering for DTI uses anisotropy indices to define transfer functions that show the anisotropy and shape of the tensor. Tensor glyphs do not reduce the dimensionality of the tensor, instead using visual representations that show the 6D tensor as such; however, these techniques cannot show global information.

Owner
Part of #357. This PR adds temporal accumulation to volumetric fog, behind ViewDesc.fog_temporal (off by default). With the switch off, the view runs the plain inject variant (main's code) and frames are byte-identical to main.
Change
Switch and memory
ViewDesc.fog_temporal: bool. It is off in both constructors and does nothing without volumetric_fog.
When on, the view keeps a second fog volume, so the volume memory doubles. The formulas in sky.md and views.md say so.
Inject pass (FOG_SCATTERING)
Variants. The temporal path is its own shader variant, behind a new TEMPORAL shader flag.
Three entries (plain, RT shadows, RT shadows with the scene BVH) gain a temporal twin, so there are four pipelines: plain or temporal, atlas or traced.
A view without the switch runs the plain variant, which is main's code: one thread per froxel, no history.
Jitter. On the temporal path, each froxel's light and shadow samples are taken at a depth inside its slice: the Halton base-2 point over FOG_TEMPORAL_SAMPLES (16).
Lamps at two depths. The volumetric lights other than the sun are also evaluated half a slice on (wrapped into the slice), and the two samples are averaged. A lamp's falloff is narrow along depth. Both samples run in one light loop: the second reuses the first sample's cluster cell list and shadow visibility and adds only a falloff and phase term. The sun and the ambient take one sample.
Reprojection. The froxel centre is projected through the view's previous view-projection and read at its previous slice, from the previous scattering volume, with trilinear filtering.
A froxel that falls outside the previous frustum or past max_distance takes no history.
Camera motion reprojects; it does not reject.
An orthographic view takes neither jitter nor history.
Clamp. Before the blend, each channel of the history is clamped to the range of this frame's in-scatter at the froxel and at its two depth neighbours in the same column.
Blend. The clamped history is mixed with the weight FOG_HISTORY_WEIGHT (0.9).
Exposure. The history's RGB is first multiplied by the ratio of the current pre-exposure to the one it was injected under. Optical depth carries no history and is not scaled.
Chunked march.
One temporal thread injects FOG_SCATTERING_CHUNK (8) slices of a column.
It evaluates each slice once and keeps a rolling window of three for the clamp, reading the slice just outside each end of its chunk. Those slices are never written.
A $assert checks that every FogQuality slice count is a multiple of the chunk.
Integration (FOG_INTEGRATION)
The pass reads the scattering volume and integrates into the other volume. It is in place, as before, when the switch is off.
History ownership
The two volumes ping-pong. They swap only when a frame is submitted. An aborted frame neither swaps nor commits.
The history is valid only if the view's own last submitted frame wrote it.
A view that skipped a frame, or recorded its volume twice in one frame, injects without history.
Mirror and clip-plane views keep their own pair.
Rejection
History is dropped, and the next frame injects without it, when:
reset_view_history runs, the view is created or configured (including a resize and a quality change), or the scene changes;
any HeightFog field, the fog node's height or the frame origin changes;
the sun turns more than FOG_HISTORY_SUN_DEGREES (0.1°);
the hash of the volumetric light set changes. It covers position, direction, range, cone, layers, colour, intensity and shadow state (atlas against traced). The pre-exposure is divided out of each intensity, and the sun's direction (plus its colour under an atmosphere) is left to the angle test.
ABI
FogVolumeRoot grows from 32 to 128 B. It gains:
the previous view-projection and camera position;
the scattering, history and integrated indices;
flags, jitter and pre_exposure_ratio.
New constants: FOG_VOLUME_HISTORY, FOG_HISTORY_WEIGHT and FOG_SCATTERING_CHUNK.
New shader flag: TEMPORAL.
Example and docs
sky --fog-temporal. The fog panel gains a "Temporal accumulation" checkbox next to the quality combo. The benchmark header prints fog_temporal=.
sky.md gains a "Temporal accumulation" section: the jitter, reprojection, blend, clamp with the two lamp samples, rejection, ownership and cost. It states that a light moving every frame gets no convergence.
Two stale lines are fixed in passing:
the limits bullet no longer says fog scatters only the sun;
the sample header line in benchmarking.md now matches the example's, including clouds=.

Review
Ownership (T2). The ping-pong works out:
Scattering goes to slot ^ 1, integration to slot, and publish swaps only a recorded, submitted frame.
The next frame's history (the new integrated index) is therefore the previous frame's blended per-froxel scattering. It is read before this frame's integration overwrites it.
accepts refuses a second recording in the same frame, and discard keeps the committed pair on abort.
History (T1). RGB only is multiplied by the ratio, and optical depth is analytic.
A still camera reads the texel it wrote: the grid-centre position gives slice = k + 0.5, which is the texel centre.
The clamp reads one slice past each chunk end and writes only inside the chunk. The $assert holds the presets to multiples of 8.
Rejection (T4). The light hash covers volumetric lights and the sun.
Intensities are divided by pre-exposure (exact for a power of two).
Shadow state is reduced to on or off, and shadow_first is zeroed.
The sun direction is left to the 0.1° angle test.
configure_view, reset_view_history and forget_view_history clear the fog history.
Pre-exposure ratio (e100ca4). Accepted as it is, at the fog level. Each history carries the factor it was stored under. FrameRoot.pre_exposure_ratio belongs to ViewHistory, whose validity is a different thing, so fixing it there would tie fog to TAA, SSGI and motion blur.
ABI. FogVolumeRoot is 144 B, and its $assert and twin are updated.
Owner ruling: narrow features
The owner chose one bounded round before merge. Your glow compare shows the clamped lamp glow mostly dissolving into haze (136 against a jitter mean of 162).
The narrow term is the lamp's falloff; the sun and ambient terms are smooth along depth. So the first candidate is to evaluate the local lights at two depths per slice (the jitter and the jitter + ½, averaged). A narrow glow then enters the current range far more often. The cost falls on the local-light loop only, and the box gets more samples.
A soft clamp, mix(history, clamped, k) with k < 1, is the fallback candidate.
Adopt the first variant that keeps the glow patch within about 5% of the jitter mean (≥ 154), the trail ratio at k24 ≥ 0.8 and static flicker ≤ 0.06. Report it in the existing T3 table, adding the glow column.
If neither meets those bounds, keep (c) as it is with the documented limitation, and record the attempt in the change record.

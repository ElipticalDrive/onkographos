Offset chunk meshes when rendering
The render loop iterates over world.chunks() but discards the chunk’s ChunkPosition and draws every mesh without a model transform. Because ChunkMesher::build_mesh generates vertex positions in local chunk coordinates (0…CHUNK_SIZE), ignoring position here means every generated chunk is rendered on top of the origin instead of being translated to its world location, so the world collapses into a single stack of overlapping geometry. Use the chunk position to translate the mesh (e.g., by setting a uModel matrix or baking the offset into the vertices) before calling draw().

Sorry that we forget to push the rendering code. You can refer to this issue to include the related code.
Why don't you sample rays then render them ?
Point cloud rendering is just an auxiliary network in our model since we found that it could improve reconstrcution quality of the predicted voxels. Discarding this component during VQVAE training is acceptable and you will still obtain a functional VQVAE model.
During training, we work with known rays, which are the lines from the LiDAR center to the LiDAR points. This approach includes ground truth data in the process. Although ray directions may be consistent for a specific LiDAR device, this pattern cannot be generalized across all devices.

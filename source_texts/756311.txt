Photorealistic rendering is used to generate views of computer stored scenes. Global illumination \nalgorithms take all transfers of light in the scene into account thereby creating a realistic looking \nimage. Previously several approaches have been presented which are able to deal with global illumination \nfor diffuse surfaces. More general surfaces are handled only by few methods. \nThis work presents a new algorithm for the generation of photorealistic images for scenes with \narbitrary surfaces. Initially particle tracing and a reconstruction phase are used to obtain a good \napproximation to the directionally dependent illumination in the scene. The illumination information \nis stored and can be used subsequently to generate images from different viewpoints directly \nfrom the stored solution. The whole system is structured into several independent phases and is \ndesigned to allow parallel processing and incremental refinement.

Parent
M1a: Walk the Planet. A playtest finding: 240 fps standing still, about 20 fps walking or flying. The dev chose this direction on 2026-11-05: faster selection at the same detail, with no change to the LOD bias.
What's wrong
The first uncapped full-size run (#119, 8-core dev box, bias 1, d0 about 1028) shows the frame thread's scene work is too slow once the bands are full:
Walking 150 m at 5 m/s after the fill completes:
scene work per frame: p99 30.8 ms, worst 256.8 ms;
following the camera (recentre): p99 28.1 ms, worst 48 ms;
taking in bricks: p99 3.6 ms, worst 248 ms.
The fill takes 3,106 s with the camera still. Taking in is capped at 2 ms a frame (STREAM_BUDGET). Each arrival costs about 25 µs in Selection::arrived and about 8 µs in show_diff, so the frame thread takes in about 9K bricks/s, while the workers can make about 120K/s.
The tree is big by design: about 2.6M split bricks, 20M bricks generated, 3.16M drawn, 426 MiB of scene.
An earlier run at 48 m/s over a partly filled tree measured only p99 6 ms. The cost grows with the tree, which fits "worse the longer you play".
#118's overlay ("CPU parts": recentre, stream, update_scene) shows this on the dev's machine. Take the dev's numbers into account if they come in.
What to build
Profile first (perf on Linux, release with debug info) on the full-size test, walking and flying over a full tree. Find where Selection::update/recentre, arrived and show_diff/packing spend time.
Cut the cost to the targets below. The approach is open:
cheaper per-node work and data layout;
a tighter incremental due scheduling;
batching arrivals;
running the selection and packing off the frame thread, with the frame applying a ready diff.
The picture must stay the same: same bands, same detail, no holes.
Acceptance criteria
A profile summary in the PR: before and after, the top costs.
Over the full tree, at 5 m/s and at 48 m/s: following the camera is at most 2 ms p99 and 8 ms worst on the 8-core box, and the frame's total scene work is at most 4 ms p99. These are measured by the full-size test, extended with a 48 m/s flight.
The full fill with the camera still completes in at most 5 minutes on the 8-core box, with no frame's scene work over 8 ms.
No change to what is drawn: the streaming oracle tests (crates/render/tests/streaming.rs, including Terrain slits: keep coarse air bricks with ground under them in the tree #113's depth check if merged) pass, and the drawn counts per level match before/after for the same camera.
needs-benchmark if the GPU scene layout or upload pattern changes; otherwise CPU-only.
Related: #119, #115/#118, #84, #86, #123 (sizing).

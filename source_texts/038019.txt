Outcome: a scene fog volume costs only the froxels it covers.
Where SGL3D is: every froxel tests every scene fog volume in inject (crates/sgl-3d/src/stages/fog.wgsl, the volume loop). Measured in Hyperdrive (release, 1920×1080, High, its moving-route harness, 32 track-wide tunnel volumes): fog injection 4.5 ms median, of which the volume loop is ~1.3–1.5 ms (2.71 → 1.15 ms with scene lights off; 4.5 → 3.22 ms in the full frame). Godot computes each volume's froxel AABB and dispatches only that kernel per volume (servers/rendering/renderer_rd/environment/fog.cpp ~701–787, volumetric_fog.glsl, at b130438), over a frustum-culled list; Frostbite voxelizes participating-media entities in a separate step (Hillaire 2015, slide 16).
Scope
Port Godot's per-volume injection (froxel AABB per volume, its own dispatch into the density/emission/scattering volume), or as a first step frustum-cull the volume list per frame and bound each volume's slice/tile range, whichever the port cites; write orchestration natively (RD-2).
Keep the result unchanged where volumes overlap froxels (same density, albedo and emission sums).
Update the provenance header and README; per-pass GPU timings (RD-6) for fog injection before and after.
Dependencies: none.
Follows the SGL3D rendering development rules (RD-1–RD-7) and architecture (AR rules).

A maintenance release of OGRE 1.7.x is available. You can download the SDK, sources and demos HERE.
OGRE is one of the most popular 3D rendering engine and is available for Windows, Mac OS X and Linux. OGRE supports all modern rendering paths: OpenGL, Direct3D 9, Direct3D 10 and Direct3D 11.
And for those who are interested in CUDA development with OGRE, here is a lib called Ogre::Cuda that provides the support of Direct3D / OpenGL interoperability with CUDA. D3D / GL Interoperability allows CUDA to directly update Direct3D or OpenGL buffers (vertex, image).

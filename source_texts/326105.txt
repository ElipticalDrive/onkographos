AdaPT now supports volumetric path tracing (in a unidirectional path tracing fashion). Currently, null-surface BSDF and deterministic Frensel refractive BSDF is supported. For medium scattering phase functions, H-G, H-G (convex mixture) and Rayleigh are supported.
MIS for volumetric path tracing is on the way. Since this is a difficult topic, it might take 1-2 weeks to complete.
Next stage:
MIS in vpt

@supersonicMaclaurin Hello, thank you for your attention to SRGS. According to your description, r is the multiple you want to upsample and downsample the original dataset, that is, the process of obtaining LR. SRGS will automatically perform x4 upsampling. The rendering process should be consistent with the training process.

The Cell-CT uses a technique called Optical Projection Tomography to render cells in 3D. This process is illustrated in the video where a cell is shown transported through the Cell-CT's glass micro-capillary by applying pressure to a gel that embeds cells. As the capillary spins, the cell is scanned from multiple perspectives yielding a set of pseudo-projection images. As illustrated in the second half of the video, these images are combined together using a method known as Filtered Back-Projection, producing the final 3D cell volume. The example is shown for a squamous cancer cell.

Prototype ready for HITL review
The throwaway RGB-free reference-prompt completion decision lab is captured at commit 450d90f.
The current recommendation for Choose RGB-free reference-prompt completion for NVOS and SPIn-NeRF is Registered Raster-Adjoint Evidence plus an Observation-Anchored Signed Region-Hypothesis Marginal:
Keep SIGNED_SCRIBBLE_RASTER and COMPLETE_BINARY_MASK as two typed schemas under one Registered-2D Query Modality Compiler family. NVOS unlabeled pixels remain unknown; SPIn-NeRF mask foreground/complement become positive/negative evidence. Schema, not benchmark identity, selects sanitization.
Use the exact front-to-back compositor responsibility adjoint W^T from frozen geometry, opacity, and reference calibration to accumulate positive, negative, and visible mass. It emits one anchor probability q, observation confidence c, typed evidence groups, query identity, and complete Evidence Lineage without reading any RGB.
Fit one globally specified, deterministic, gradient-free signed shrinkage prototype from decoded Capability Views and the current prompt. Score every legal region/antichain on the already selected Boundary-Calibrated Field Region Hierarchy and marginalize rather than choosing a top component, hierarchy level, or scene threshold.
Convert the hierarchy marginal into the Gaussian Query Posterior through anchor-preserving logit transport with intervention budget (1-c)*completion_confidence. A fully observed anchor is bitwise unchanged; sparse or occluded rows receive bounded completion. The binary boundary is exactly posterior >= 0.5 for both contracts.
NVOS target-camera tracks must be removed and receipt-bound during Mapping; target camera data enters only Camera-Raster Output. SPIn-NeRF compiles the canonical complete reference mask once, reuses one posterior for every non-reference annotated frame, and neither scores the reference nor uses it for branch selection.
Invalid schema/calibration/lineage, missing signs or support, target-track exclusion failure, a non-rebuildable hierarchy, ill-conditioned fitting, conflicting evidence, empty hypotheses, numerical failure, or insufficient output support returns typed Query Abstention. There is no RGB/SAM, legacy-cache, task-head, all-background, or per-scene fallback.
The proposed smallest exact-contract Development Evidence identity is registered2d-anchored-hierarchy-full8-plus-available9-v1: all eight official NVOS tasks and all non-reference annotated frames of the Available-Nine SPIn-NeRF Cohort, with matched A (exact registered anchor, no propagation) and H (the recommended completion) arms on byte-identical cold fields. All predictions and abstentions are sealed before any metric or oracle diagnostic is released.
The proposed mechanism gates are zero Query Abstentions; NVOS macro IoU at least 70.1%, at least +1.0 pp over A, and no official task more than 0.5 pp below A; SPIn-NeRF at least +1.0 pp scene-macro over A and no scene more than 0.5 pp below A. The SPIn-NeRF rule is explicitly a development mechanism gate, not a SOTA Target; its eligible numeric target remains absent.
After sealing, the evaluator may compute two diagnostics only: F, the best field/hierarchy/output representability ceiling, and P, the best legal hypothesis also consistent with the frozen reference prompt. F failure diagnoses field/carrier representation; F pass plus P failure diagnoses prompt observability/registration; both ceilings passing with a failed actual posterior diagnoses fitting/solver/calibration/transport. Neither ceiling may select a prediction, threshold, hierarchy, checkpoint, or retry.
No benchmark was run and no result is claimed. This prototype ticket remains open pending the human's actual selection. In the lab, use the “建议组合”, “帧分区”, and “归因分叉” walkthroughs, then copy the decision token or state the requested change here.

Resolution
The human selected the recommended combination after reviewing the RGB-free reference-prompt completion decision lab, captured at commit 450d90f.
Adopt Registered Raster-Adjoint Evidence plus an Observation-Anchored Signed Region-Hypothesis Marginal as the RGB-free reference-prompt completion design shared by strict-unseen NVOS and the Available-Nine SPIn-NeRF full-reference-mask contract. This is the reference-prompt mechanism candidate that enters the implementation spec. It remains subject to cold-start, runtime-compliance, exact-pilot, joint-promotion, and missing-SPIn-target gates; it is not a runnable result or claim-eligible row.
Typed reference-prompt sanitization
One Registered-2D Query Modality Compiler family accepts two typed schemas. Component selection follows the authorized input schema and Query Intent, never benchmark identity.
SIGNED_SCRIBBLE_RASTER requires a content-bound reference identity and calibration plus non-empty, disjoint positive and negative scribbles. Only explicitly marked pixels become positive or negative evidence. Every unlabeled pixel remains unknown and may not be reinterpreted as background.
COMPLETE_BINARY_MASK requires the canonical reference identity and calibration plus a strict binary mask with supported non-empty foreground and complement. Foreground is positive evidence and the complete complement is negative evidence. The sanitizer opens no sibling RGB channel from the reference container.
Both schemas emit complete query identity, typed evidence groups, sanitization receipts, and Evidence Lineage. Invalid dimensions, coordinates, signs, mask values, overlap, reference identity, calibration, or lineage fail closed before semantic execution.
Registered Raster-Adjoint Evidence
Let W be the exact front-to-back compositor responsibility matrix computed only from frozen Gaussian geometry/covariance, opacity, and the authorized reference calibration. Captured or rendered RGB, SAM, masks other than the Authorized Query Input, target evidence, and teacher/MPR state never enter W.
The compiler accumulates m_pos = W^T y_pos, m_neg = W^T y_neg, and m_vis = W^T 1 with one content-bound implementation. It emits per-Gaussian observation confidence c = (m_pos + m_neg) / m_vis and registered anchor probability q = m_pos / (m_pos + m_neg), with neutral q = 0.5 and c = 0 where no legal evidence is registered.
A complete mask therefore gives c = 1 on every fully reference-observed Gaussian and an exact continuous foreground anchor. Sparse scribbles preserve partial or zero coverage rather than multiplying their evidence away or declaring the unlabelled raster background.
Hard nearest-Gaussian assignment is rejected as the primary lift because it discards mixed visibility and occlusion responsibility. Target-camera tracks, rendered-target assistance, and RGB/SAM completion are prohibited regardless of diagnostic score.
Shared query-local fitting and posterior propagation
A globally specified deterministic, gradient-free signed shrinkage fit may use only the decoded Capability View and the current query's positive/negative registered responsibilities. Its fitted values remain Query Workspace state, cannot persist across queries, and cannot update the field or Global Method Parameters.
The selected Boundary-Calibrated Field Region Hierarchy is the sole query-independent support carrier. It must be a bitwise-rebuildable Field-Derived Support Topology compiled only from legal Deployment Scene State and identical Global Method Parameters. No prompt, benchmark identity, teacher/MPR topology, target view, or evaluator output may rebuild or modify it.
The frozen INSTANCE likelihood scores every legal hierarchy region and antichain from the signed registered evidence, query-local prototype evidence, field support, and source-frozen priors. It marginalizes over the complete legal hypothesis set rather than selecting a top component, hierarchy level, region count, graph threshold, or scene branch.
The resulting hierarchy marginal h is transported around the registered anchor through the shared observation-preserving logit rule. The intervention budget is (1-c) * completion_confidence, with one source-frozen residual cap, epsilon, normalization, confidence rule, and deterministic tie-break. A fully observed anchor is bitwise unchanged; partial and unobserved rows receive only their bounded completion budget.
The transported per-Gaussian probability is the Gaussian Query Posterior. Both contracts use the exact binary boundary posterior >= 0.5; Camera-Raster Output consumes the same continuous posterior. Per-task, per-scene, per-query, reference-IoU-selected, or metric-swept thresholds and fallbacks are forbidden.
NVOS frame and track boundary
Each strict-unseen field must be mapped only from authorized non-target Mapping Observations. Every sparse geometry observation whose transitive track contains the held-out target camera is removed before field construction.
The cold package and Mapping Stage Receipt must bind the target-camera exclusion set and prove it transitively. A missing, ambiguous, or mismatched exclusion proof fails before Query.
Query receives only the frozen field, official signed reference scribbles, reference identity/calibration, and the target pose/intrinsics/output size as Output Request Metadata. Target metadata may enter only Camera-Raster Output and output-support checks; it cannot alter evidence, fitting, hierarchy likelihood, calibration, or posterior.
The exact contract remains all eight official reference-target tasks, nearest-neighbor alignment to the evaluator raster, and equal-weight macro foreground IoU. Target RGB and mask remain evaluator-only.
SPIn-NeRF reference and target boundary
Mapping may use all protocol-authorized scene RGB and poses, including scored frames, but those channels are unavailable to Query.
The compiler consumes the complete mask on the single canonical official reference frame once. The same query identity and Gaussian Query Posterior are reused unchanged for every non-reference annotated output frame.
The reference frame is neither submitted nor scored and cannot select a branch, threshold, hypothesis, calibration, retry, or checkpoint. Non-reference frame identities and calibrations enter only Camera-Raster Output.
The exact cohort remains orchids, leaves, fern, room, horns, fortress, pinecone, truck, and lego, with frame mean within scene followed by equal-weight nine-scene macro. Fork and full-ten numbers remain out of this contract.
Abstention and forbidden fallback
Missing or forbidden lineage; invalid sanitizer schema or calibration; absent positive or negative support; target-track exclusion failure; insufficient reference or output field support; conflicting signed evidence; an invalid, empty, or non-rebuildable hierarchy; ill-conditioned query-local fitting; numerical failure; or identity drift returns a typed Query Abstention with support diagnostics. Query Abstention is distinct from all-background and counts as candidate failure in scored evaluation. It may not trigger RGB/SAM, a query-time external vision model, a legacy graph, a capability/descriptor cache, persistent object memory, task-specific code, a scene router, or threshold relaxation.
Smallest exact-contract pilot
Freeze development identity registered2d-anchored-hierarchy-full8-plus-available9-v1:
Cold and lineage preflight. Seal sidecar-free D512/L512 fields, remove Training Artifacts and caches, disable unauthorized reads, rebuild Capability Views and the hierarchy twice, require bitwise identity and a passing Runtime Compliance Proof, and verify every NVOS target-track exclusion receipt before any metric is opened.
Source-only component freeze. Bind the two sanitizer schemas, compositor identity, Capability View projection, shrinkage fitting, hierarchy compiler, likelihood/prior, completion confidence, residual cap, abstention bounds, epsilon, tie-breaks, implementation, environment, and 0.5 boundary using only independent source-train evidence before benchmark metrics.
Exact dual cohort. Run all eight official NVOS tasks and all non-reference annotated frames in the Available-Nine SPIn-NeRF Cohort. A subset is useful only as diagnostic evidence and cannot carry this exact-contract identity.
Matched causal arms. On byte-identical cold fields, run A, the exact registered anchor with no propagation, and H, the selected query-local fitting, hierarchy marginal, and anchor-preserving transport. The arms share prompt manifests, W, posterior schema, output operator, alignment, implementation, and environment; only the completion component differs.
Prediction seal. Seal all predictions and Query Abstentions for both arms and both contracts before releasing any metric or oracle diagnostic. Missing executions, reference scoring, fallback, or identity drift fails the pilot.
Mechanism gates. H must have zero Query Abstentions. NVOS must reach macro foreground IoU at least 70.1%, improve A by at least +1.0 pp macro, and regress no official task more than 0.5 pp. SPIn-NeRF must improve A by at least +1.0 pp scene-macro and regress no scene more than 0.5 pp.
SPIn target status. The SPIn relative rule is a Development Evidence mechanism gate only. The eligible numeric SOTA Target remains absent; neither this gate nor the historical 87.7162%, RGB/SAM diagnostics, or full-ten rows may replace it.
Sealed failure attribution
Only after both arms are sealed may the evaluator compute:
F, the best target performance representable by the fixed field hierarchy and output operator; and
P, the best legal hierarchy hypothesis that also satisfies the frozen reference-prompt consistency constraints.
Both use the applicable pilot gate and are diagnostic only. Preflight or output-support failure is compliance/reconstruction failure. F failure diagnoses Canonical Capability Feature, boundary carrier, hierarchy, or output-geometry representation failure. F passing with P failing diagnoses registered lift or reference-prompt observability/separation failure. Both ceilings passing with a failed actual H posterior diagnoses query-local fitting, hypothesis likelihood/marginal, calibration, or anchor transport. The ceilings may not select a candidate prediction, threshold, hierarchy, checkpoint, parameter, branch, retry, or fallback.
Passing only NVOS rejects the shared mechanism; it cannot authorize a SPIn-specific head. Passing both contracts only admits the mechanism to Define six-task negative-transfer controls and the joint mapping objective. It does not create a Joint Development Baseline, Development Advancement, Canonical Current Row, or SOTA claim.
Rejected alternatives and map impact
Query-time RGB/SAM completion, target-camera assistance, hard nearest-Gaussian lift as the primary compiler, unconditional flat-graph diffusion, per-scene source-LOO routing, reference-IoU branch selection, persistent object memory, and task-specific thresholds or fallbacks are rejected. Existing NVOS and SPIn-NeRF rows remain Context Evidence under their frozen identities.
Registered Raster-Adjoint Evidence and Observation-Anchored Signed Region-Hypothesis Marginal are ticket-local implementation-spec terms. /to-spec must preserve their semantics and decide whether to promote them into the root glossary; neither may become a second semantic field, prompt-conditioned scene cache, or benchmark head.
No new ticket or fog graduation is required. Closing this decision removes the final native blocker from Define six-task negative-transfer controls and the joint mapping objective. This session does not claim or work that ticket.

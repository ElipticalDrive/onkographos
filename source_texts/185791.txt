Octrees, BSPs, and k-d trees are all ways of organizing 3D data, each with different advantages and disadvantages. Octrees Octrees are perhaps the simplest of the data structures addressed here. Octrees are a tree data structure where each internal node has eight children. When used to represent spatial data, each node can be thought of as a cubic region in space.

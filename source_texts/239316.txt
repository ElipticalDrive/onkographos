Part of #3019.
Problem
Shader-deformed skin can look correct while queries, construction, shadows or culling use rest or linear geometry. The dual-quaternion shader changes positions while the installed Three.js CPU path stays linear, and bounds and raycast use that CPU path. Recheck the installed source, because the scene error has not been measured.
Work
Enumerate every consumer of positions and normals: beauty, depth, shadow, overlay, construction, culling bounds, picking, contact and measurement.
Select one pose revision and operator order for all of them.
State CPU double and GPU float tolerances by units and scale.
Compute safe bounds, avoiding rest-bound culling and always-visible workarounds.
Make picking and measurement use the selected result, avoiding full CPU readback where an equivalent evaluator serves.
Acceptance
At one code, shape and pose revision, rendered landmarks, query points and construction match the declared error, and silhouettes agree with depth, shadow and picking at posed extremities. Check splits, non-identity transforms, both skin methods, morph-before-skin and corrective boundaries. Neutral restoration resets bounds, root and corrective state.
Depends on #3023 and #3025.

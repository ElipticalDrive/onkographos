Done when
Chunks that are entirely outside the camera's view frustum are not drawn.
Chunks that touch the frustum are always drawn, so there are no holes at the screen edges.
The test is done on the CPU per chunk, with the 80 degree field of view.
Fast camera turns do not show missing terrain.
The rendering benchmark (Rendering benchmark #175) shows fewer chunks drawn than before.
Tests cover chunks inside, outside and across each frustum plane.
Notes: Subject p. 8: "You must implement proper frustum culling and visibility optimization". Hidden faces inside and between chunks are already skipped (#176, #178). This is the simple version. Culling inside the detail tree (#185) builds on it.

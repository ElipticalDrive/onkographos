Found on Sponza (#171), at noon from the courtyard (--lab models --model Sponza --time-of-day 0.5 --view=8.84,1.83,0.39,66.2,8.3): the sun's shadow on the curtains has a hard, noisy, stepped edge that shimmers when the camera moves.
What is right. The shape: every blocked ray meets one horizontal ledge across the courtyard, 5 to 6 m off, with the sun at 70°. A straight edge over folds about 14 cm deep makes the teeth.
What is wrong. At that distance the sun's disc (0.27° in radius) gives a penumbra of about 5 cm across the light, about 15 cm on the curtain at that grazing angle: some 16 pixels of gradient.
The resolve traces one shadow ray per pixel and frame, at one of the eight points of a Vogel disc (sun_disc_point, Soft sun shadows: the sun's disc sampled over TAA's jitter cycle #54), and leaves the averaging to TAA.
The exposure is metered for the arcade, about 8 stops under the sun. One point in eight is still 1/8 of the sun, and that blows out to white.
So the whole penumbra collapses into one hard step at its outer rim, quantised and dithered per pixel, and TAA's history clamp lets it crawl in motion.
Eight rays per pixel only turn it into eight stripes. Outdoor scenes hide it because their exposure is not 8 stops under the sun.
The owner's choice (2026-10-04): a shadow denoiser, after research and a decision entry.
The references: NVIDIA's SIGMA (NRD) and AMD's FidelityFX Shadow Denoiser.
How it works: a filter whose width follows the penumbra estimated from the blocker distance, plus temporal accumulation.
What it should give: a smooth, stable gradient at any exposure.
Where it helps: every scene with ray-traced sun shadows (the city, the island, the labs).
Steps:
Research, written up in docs/research/ (one agent).
A 🟡 entry in docs/DECISIONS.md for the owner.
Build it once the owner has decided.
Constraints: cross-vendor (no wave-size assumption, ≤ 32 KB groupshared), render-graph passes with profiler zones, Slang, the mesh path and the fallback at 0 px apart, and #71's flake rules.

Pushed in 913c5fd (D-049).
What it does: NVIDIA's NRD (SIGMA) denoises the sun's soft shadows when it is installed.
tools/fetch-nrd.sh builds NRD v4.17.3 into the git-ignored nrd-sdk/. None of NRD enters the repository (NVIDIA RTX SDKs License); CREDITS.md has its row and what a shipping game owes.
shadow/trace traces one ray a pixel to the sun's disc, keeping the closest occluder; each of SIGMA's dispatches is a graph pass (shadow/SIGMA …); the resolve reads the result in place of its own ray.
The sun's disc for the soft shadows is 1° (the real one 0.27°), for art's sake. --sun-size DEG, --no-shadow-denoiser, F6, and --shadow-reference (256 rays a pixel) to compare.
Without NRD (a fresh clone, CI): pixel for pixel as before.
Found on the way: NRD's kernels write past the image's edge, which D3D12 drops and Vulkan does not; the stray writes landed in the shadow's inputs and the serial frame differed by 80 000 px. NRD's pipelines alone are now robust (VK_EXT_pipeline_robustness), and its clears are vkCmdClearColorImage.
Measured on Sponza (the #172 view at noon, 1600 × 900, frames 600–615), the per-pixel range over TAA's 16-frame cycle on the curtains' penumbra:
before: 5.0 codes (99th percentile 78);
SIGMA: 1.5 (14);
the 256-ray reference: 1.2 (8).
Cost at 1440p: Sponza 3.48 → 3.97 ms (shadow/trace 0.44, SIGMA 0.44), the city 4.03 → 4.72, the island 3.69 → 3.95 (docs/PROFILE.md). More than the proposal's 0.35–0.6 ms.
Still open: the curtains' edge still shimmers while the camera moves (the owner). The reference does not, at a cost no game can pay. #173 brings AMD's FidelityFX denoiser as the fallback without NRD, and compares the two in motion.
Checks (Tier 1):
Without NRD (FORGE_NRD_DIR an empty folder): every image as the accepted set, but Ballad captures at frame 600 differed once from the same build #71's flake; mesh against fallback 0 px; validation clean.
With NRD, accepted for 913c5fd: every city-blocks capture changes, by --expect (the city, the island, the labs, the shots). ꟻLIP means: mesh-city60 33 492 px, 0.0044; mesh-cityorbit120 87 717 px, 0.0104; mesh-island60 547 px, 0.00008; mesh-shot-valley 17 997 px, 0.0054; mesh-lab-model-Sponza 8 063 px, 0.0014; mesh-lab-models60 2 391 px, 0.0003.

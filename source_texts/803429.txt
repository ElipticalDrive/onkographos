How do I apply Motion Blur Shake online?
Open the Motion Blur Shake preset, add your image or video, and adjust the loaded effect layers to suit your source. Preview the result before exporting.
Does the preset actually move the camera?
Its saved stack contains one Motion Blur layer. The shake-like impression comes from keyframed changes in blur angle and strength, rather than a separate camera-position animation.
How is the animation timed?
The saved timeline lasts three seconds and contains repeated changes between strong and weak blur, with elastic easing on many keyframes. You can revise those keyframes for a different rhythm.
How can I make the effect gentler?
Lower the strongest blur keyframe values and reduce layer opacity. Changing only the current strength may be overridden during playback, so inspect the animation as well as the static control.
Can I use it on a photograph?
Yes. The keyframes animate the blur even when the source does not move. Keep some quieter intervals so viewers can still recognize the image between stronger smears.

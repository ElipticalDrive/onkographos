( 64, 6, 1 )
i >> 6 = tetrahedraIndex
i & 63 = cellIndex
(gid >> (0, 2, 4) & 3) = sliceIndex
(gid >> (6, 8, 10) & 3) = blockIndex
gid >> 12 = queuedChunkId
Each chunk can be generated individually and a chunk is represented by a uint64_t.
In format y16xz24
Giving us 64x16Mi into xz and 64x64k = 128 Mi cells into the xz and 4 Mi cells into the up direction.
Every queued chunk dispatches ~1.5M threads (49 152 or 24 576 warps).
These could be generated like following;
Camera frustum intersect chunks, detect closest and most important (center).
Add to list, sort by importance and only queue up to N chunks this frame.

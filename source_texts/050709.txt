Cubes that are hidden behind other cubes should not be rendered. Since all the voxels are aligned to grid, one way to do this is to have a Cube check its neighbors. If it's surrounded by all of it's neighbors, it is not rendered.
Obviously, the backsides of cubes are not rendered, as cull facing is enabled. This optimization only affects the cube as a whole, and should significantly reduce the number of vertices, textures, and lighting calculations that need to be done for rendering hundreds of thousands of cubes.

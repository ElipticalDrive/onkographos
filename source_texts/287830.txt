As you may notice, we need these GLOBALS because they will be used in the get_rand_pose function. So how to implement these GLOBALS depends on what information is needed in get_rand_pose when using the spherify option.
Meanwhile, if your target is to apply R2L to 360 scenes, one potential solution is to use the get_rand_pose function in Blender dataset, which does not need these GLOBALS. The images in the Blender dataset are also from 360 scenes, although they are synthetic, without background, so much simpler. But if you target an unbounded 360 real scenes (like those in Mip NeRF 360), this way may not work well (based on my quick intuition).

It is not "not likely". You may give it a shot. Possibly it can work well. Before we have any experiments, my current intuition is that, unbounded 360 real scenes have a large variety of view directions and typically have many more details than the simple objects in Blender or scenes in LLFF (where the camera views are pretty limited). So simply put, it is the inherent scene complexity in unbounded 360 real scenes that makes me "feel" R2L may encounter some problems dealing with these cases. But hard to say definitely; we still need experimental results for confirmation.

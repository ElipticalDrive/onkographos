First step of the M4 LOD arc (#37). Adds the primitive for rendering distant terrain cheaply — the key to the render distance we want (PLAN.md §3).
What
Chunk::build_mesh_lod(level) meshes a downsampled copy of the chunk: level halves the resolution each step (0 = full 16³, 1 = 8³, 2 = 4³, …, capped at log2(SIZE)). Solidity is majority-downsampled (a coarse cell is solid when ≥ half its factor³ fine cells are), and the coarse grid is meshed with a matching vertex scale, so a LOD chunk keeps the same world footprint with far fewer triangles.
The greedy mesher (AO and all) is generalised into a free greedy_mesh(n, scale, is_solid) over any cubic grid; build_mesh() is now just greedy_mesh(SIZE, 1.0, …), so full-res output is unchanged.
Result
On the radius-6 screenshot region (temporarily forced to LOD 2 for the check): ~9.4k triangles vs ~57k full-res (~6× fewer), rendering as recognisable coarser terrain — same hills, same footprint, AO intact, no holes.
Scope
Just the meshing primitive — not yet wired into streaming, so live rendering is unchanged. Selection/rings (#39), the region octree (#38), and seam handling (#40) build on this next.
Verification
Unit tests: footprint preserved across levels 1–4, full chunk still 6 merged quads, a half-solid chunk downsamples to a clean slab (top at world y=8), stepped terrain loses triangles, level capping doesn't panic.
Visual: forced-LOD-2 screenshot confirmed correct (then reverted; no wiring shipped).
cargo test --all, clippy, fmt green.

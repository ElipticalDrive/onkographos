What and why
First of the voxel performance PRs, on top of #26. Every SendSeconds (1 s by default) the server packed and gzipped each wet brick only to hash it and find whether it changed. Still lakes were included, though they had not moved. For a settled 40 × 40 m pool, one such pass cost 3.75 ms, while stepping that pool costs 0.4 ms per simulated second, so sending cost about ten times the simulation.
Each brick counts its changes (Brick.Changes): every step that works on it, the step it falls asleep in (its flows are cleared), water put in from outside (Wets: pours, drains, lifts, settling that wakes it) and its solids read again (Refill).
The server remembers, per brick object, the brick and its count when last sent. A brick whose count has not moved since is neither read nor packed.
A brick forgotten dry and made again at the same place is another instance, so it is always sent (found by the local CodeRabbit review).
The hash check stays for bricks that stepped but whose bytes came out the same.
The per-brick send moved into its own method, which keeps Publish simple.
Where it shows
Server simulation
Client (drawing, swimming, current)
Docs only
How it was tested
New unit test: a sleeping pool's change count stays the same over 2 s of steps, and pouring water in changes it (139 tests pass).
Benchmark (scratch program over the voxel sources, this PC): packing every wet brick of the settled 40 × 40 m pool took 3.75 ms per send. It is now skipped for as long as the pool sleeps.
The server's periodic log (DebugSeconds) now also gives the time spent sending and how many bricks were sent.
In game (real game hidden, local server, the raised 10 × 10 m wooden box test), per 20 s of log:
while filling: about 80 bricks sent, 10 ms spent sending;
settled, with the box asleep: 0 bricks sent and 1 ms spent (stepping 36 ms);
draining after a wall piece was broken: about 400 bricks sent, 55 ms spent.
No exceptions.
dotnet build -c Release passes (analyzers on) and tools/format-check.sh too
Tried in the game
Checklist
VERSION raised and its dated CHANGELOG.md heading added
README.md (and the mod page, thunderstore/README.md) updated if behaviour or settings changed
No decompiled game code
Summary by CodeRabbit
Bug Fixes
Unchanged still water is no longer repeatedly processed for synchronization, reducing overhead around calm lakes.
Water changes, including small amounts settling into sleeping pools, are detected and synchronized without waking those pools.
Dry areas are excluded from synchronization, while wet areas are sent when their contents change.
Refills and water or solids added outside normal simulation steps are recognized as changes, keeping synchronized contents up to date.

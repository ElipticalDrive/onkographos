Adds an isolated Unreal Editor generator for seven original procedural material studies: sand, snow surface, wet stone, rain, snowfall, blowing sand and an unassigned spell ring. Creates editable material instances and a three-bay scene with surface objects, weather cards, lighting and an overview camera. Surface shaders include analytic normals; effect controls include intensity, speed and tiling.
Stacked on #634 at d4aa2ba. Four added files only; no runtime, story, combat, save, ship-gate or default-map changes.
Validation: 11 local offline Python tests passed, plus Python compilation and manifest validation. Editor-boundary substitutes test overwrite/dirty-editor protection, missing meshes, graph failures and compiler-result handling. Native UE5.8 execution, HLSL compilation, map generation, captures and performance NOT RUN. Source CI configured; status pending. These are noncanonical material studies, not finished cinematic assets or a complete weather system. Weather is shader cards, not volumetric or Niagara simulation.
Native build and visual acceptance instructions: unreal/Docs/WEATHER_MATERIAL_REVIEW_V1.md. Preserve draft; no merge, release or art approval.

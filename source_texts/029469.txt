Summary
Note
First of three stacked pull requests that turn the renderer prototype of #211 into library code: this one (renderer, ray queries, viewer), then the example rewrites (claude/renderer-examples, on top of this), then the ray tape and performance work (claude/renderer-raytape, on top of that). Closes the prototype #211 when merged; follows the direction set in #93.
Background
#211 prototyped visualising EBGeometry shapes by rendering their signed distance directly, instead of meshing them (#93), and measured sphere tracing, ray tracing and a hybrid. Its conclusion: ray trace wherever a primitive can answer a ray (meshes through their BVH, analytic shapes in closed form), combine CSG through a ray version of the tape, and sphere trace only what has no ray form. It also asked that examples open a viewer on what they build, with no third-party dependency by default and nothing blocking ctest.
Solution
Ray queries on any shape (EBGeometry_Ray.hpp), device-callable:
firstHit(shape, ray, tMin, tMax): the first crossing of the surface in either direction, with HitT::m_entering saying which way, the finest element the shape names there (the tape's leaf, the mesh's face), an exact flag and, when known, the normal. A ray starting inside the solid reports where it leaves, so a hole seen from within the material, or an inside-out mesh, cannot pass for an outside.
intersects(shape, a, b): whether a segment crosses the surface (different end signs answer without a march).
Shapes are sphere traced when their value is a distance or a bound, and stepped and bisected otherwise (Perlin). A march that runs out of steps is HitStatus::Unresolved, never a miss.
RayForm<S>, a trait a shape type specializes to be ray traced instead. TriMeshSDF has one (pulled forward from the planned third PR after testing the viewer): the ray follows the BVH nearest box first through the new TriangleSoAT/TriangleAoSoA::rayHit() (Möller–Trumbore), exact and with interpolated vertex normals.
Tape::firstHit() and Tape::intersects() forward to the free functions. Tape::primitiveAt(p) names the leaf of the expression that decides the value at a point (leaves numbered depth first, each occurrence counted, stored in the primitive clause's unused field); signedDistance() is unchanged.
The renderer (EBGeometry_Render.hpp, namespace Render): Camera (orbit, fit(), lookAt(), +y up), Settings, Image, Stats, Renderer. Diffuse, specular and sky light, shadows, ambient occlusion, a floor, supersampling with a slightly moving light, colour per primitive, and a section plane whose cut faces get their own colour. A surface seen from inside the solid is tinted red and counted. render() runs on std::threads, renderOnDevice() through GPU::transform(); overloads of both can be cancelled. PixelQuery is the trivially copyable per-pixel functor. Ray-traced shapes get ray-cast shadows and occlusion.
The viewer (EBGeometry_Viewer.hpp): Render::view(shape, argc, argv) opens an SDL2 window, compiled only with EBGEOMETRY_VIEWER=SDL2 (CMake) / VIEWER=SDL2 (make). Without it, with --no-view, or without a display, it prints why and returns, so ctest (which passes --no-view to examples that call it) never waits. Previews adapt their resolution while the camera moves; refinement runs on a background thread and is cancelled when it moves again.
Examples/MeshSDF ends with view(), on the GPU in a GPU build.
Armadillo, 960 × 960, 2 × 2 rays per pixel, shadows and occlusion: 174 ms on 24 CPU threads, 82 ms on an RTX A4000 in float (the prototype: 168 ms and 41 ms on the same machine).
Tests: TestRay, TestRender, new cases in TestTape and TestTriangleSoA, all with [gpu] cases; InstantiateAll covers the new templates. Docs: Rendering.rst (concepts), ImplemRender.rst, the viewer option in ConfigurationOptions.rst, and the updated pages listed in the commits. CI: a Viewer-SDL2 job builds the window and runs what needs no display.
Side-effects
The EBGeometry CMake target links Threads::Threads (the installed package finds it), since Renderer::render() uses std::thread.
HitT, TriangleSoAT and TriangleAoSoA gain members; Tape::Layout gains m_numPrimitives.
Examples/CMakeLists.txt passes --no-view to any example whose main.cpp calls Render::view(.
The device path of the renderer is covered by the [gpu] cases and was timed on an RTX A4000. The window itself has been run only under SDL's offscreen video driver (no input) and under ThreadSanitizer; interactive use of the current version still needs a manual check.
Alternative solutions
Image files (PNG/PPM) as the dependency-free output: dropped on request; the image stays in memory.
Sphere tracing only in this PR, with every ray form in the third: tried first, but a mesh took 19 s per full frame and the viewer froze, so the mesh ray form moved here. The general ray tape (CSG crossings, affine maps, sphere tracing inside boxes for operations without a ray form) stays in the third PR, which also replaces this PR's renderer-specific ray-tracing shortcuts (the section plane's ray form, the ray-cast shadow and occlusion branches) with the generic path.

numbered in the order they appear in the expression, depth first, each

Ray queries: firstHit() and intersects() on any shape, and on Tape
…
5084378
```
EBGeometry_Ray.hpp adds RayT, HitT, HitStatus and RayOptions, and two
device-callable queries over any shape:

- firstHit(shape, ray, tMin, tMax): the first crossing of the surface in
  either direction, clipped to the shape's box. HitT says where, whether the
  ray enters or leaves the object there (a ray starting inside reports where
  it leaves), and the finest element the shape names there: the tape's leaf
  (primitiveAt()) or the mesh SDF's closest face.
- intersects(shape, a, b): whether a segment crosses the surface; ends of
  different sign decide it without a march.

Shapes whose value is a distance or a bound are sphere traced, which never
steps over the surface; values that are not distances (Perlin) are stepped
at a fixed length and bisected. A march out of steps is HitStatus::Unresolved,
never a miss. Tolerances are relative to the box diagonal (or an explicit
scale for unbounded shapes). Tape::firstHit() and Tape::intersects() forward
to the free functions.

Render: Camera, Settings, Image and Renderer, rendering any shape
…
307a68c
```
EBGeometry_Render.hpp renders a shape into an in-memory RGBA image by
sphere tracing each camera ray with firstHit() and shading the hit:
diffuse, specular and sky light, soft shadows, ambient occlusion, a
checkered floor under the shape, and an optional section plane whose cut
faces get their own colour. Surfaces can be coloured uniformly or by
primitive (the tape's leaf, the mesh SDF's face). A surface seen from
inside the solid is lit from the eye's side and tinted red, and Stats
reports such hits and whether the eye itself is inside the solid, so a
hole seen from within the material, or an inverted mesh, cannot pass for
an outside.

PixelQuery is a trivially copyable, device-callable functor per pixel,
reading the box, scale and floor from the shape itself.
Renderer::render() runs it on std::thread over rows;
Renderer::renderOnDevice() runs it through GPU::transform() on a device
view of the shape. The EBGeometry target now links Threads::Threads (and
the installed package finds it).

firstHit(): a march that starts on the surface, as it does where a face
lies on the shape's box, now decides entering or leaving by whether the
value falls along the ray; the sign just past the start misread oblique
rays as leaving.

a camera and settings, and a device view of the shape. The window is
compiled only under EBGEOMETRY_VIEWER_SDL2, which the new CMake cache

view() opens no window, and returns after one line saying why, when the
program was built without SDL2, when its arguments hold --no-view, when

that call it still run unattended.

occlusion and floor, P colours by primitive, X cuts with a section plane
facing the camera (arrows move it), G switches GPU and CPU, R resets,
Esc/Q closes. A moving camera is drawn at half resolution without shadows,
and in full once it rests; the title shows the frame time and whether any
surface is seen from inside the solid.

The viewer sphere traced meshes: 19 s per full Armadillo frame on the
CPU, rendered on the event loop, so the window froze for that long each
time the camera stopped. Now:

- RayForm<S>, a trait a shape type specializes to be ray traced
  instead of sphere traced; firstHit() uses it when present. TriMeshSDF
  has one: the ray follows the BVH, nearest box first, pruned behind the
  nearest crossing, through TriangleSoAT/TriangleAoSoA::rayHit()
  (Moller-Trumbore, widened at shared edges), giving an exact hit, the
  face id, and a normal interpolated from the vertex normals (the new
  HitT::m_normal).
- The renderer keeps a ray form under the section plane, takes the
  normal and the primitive from the hit, and casts hard shadows with one
  ray for a ray-traced shape; soft shadows march fewer, longer steps.
  Armadillo, 960^2 with 2x2 rays per pixel, 24 threads: 0.06 s without
  shadows (was 1.6 s), 1.6 s with shadows and occlusion (was 19 s).
- Renderer::render() and renderOnDevice() overloads that a flag cancels
  (between rows on the host, between bands of rows on a device), with a
  row counter and Stats::m_complete.
- The viewer previews at a resolution it adapts to about 10-40 ms, and
  refines on a background thread (full resolution without shadows, then
  the full settings), cancelled as soon as the camera moves.
- Examples/MeshSDF passes its device view to view() in a GPU build.

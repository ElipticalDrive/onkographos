Summary
Real LEGO geometry — replaced manual box/cylinder approximations with Three.js LDrawLoader, which fetches official .dat part files and renders proper LEGO shapes including studs, anti-studs, and accurate brick profiles
Parts CDN — part files loaded from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/r169/examples/models/ldraw/officialLibrary/ (no local assets needed)
Studio LDR pre-processing — STUDIOSTEPDESC markers are regex-replaced with standard 0 STEP before passing to the loader, so it correctly groups bricks by build step
Hard-edge normals (smoothNormals = false) for a more authentic LEGO look
Loading progress UI — "Fetching LDraw file…" subtitle shown during the multi-request part fetch

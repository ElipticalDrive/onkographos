Part 3 of 4 of #199 (split), after #245. Part 4 follows and closes #199.
What
The lit pass's BRDF is glTF 2.0 Appendix B's mix(dielectric_brdf, metal_brdf, metallic), and cook_torrance is gone.
The mix.
A dielectric's Fresnel, from its fixed F0 of 0.04, mixes its diffuse and specular lobes: the light its surface does not reflect enters, scatters and leaves diffuse.
A metal's Fresnel, from its base colour, tints its specular lobe.
cook_torrance weighted the diffuse lobe by one Fresnel from an F0 already blended by metallic, which agrees with Appendix B only where metallic is 0 or 1.
Gathered by lobe. Mixed as Appendix B writes it, the lit pass took up to 25 µs (5%) longer at 1440p on the M4 Pro, since the metal's Fresnel is computed per light. Gathered by lobe it is the same function:
the dielectric's diffuse lobe, weighted by (1 − metallic)(1 − F) with the dielectric's own F;
one specular lobe whose Fresnel, Schlick's F being linear in F0, is F at the F0 that metallic mixes, which no light changes.
The f64 oracle compares it with Appendix B's own code.
The Fresnel weight, (1 − |V·H|)⁵, is multiplied out like Appendix B's fresnel_w: pow has no value for the negative base that a |V·H| rounded just past 1 gives.
reflected_luminance is the per-light term: f(L, V) E (N·L)⁺ from unit vectors, in ADR 0023's photometric terms. Where L and V are opposite, H is left zero instead of NaN, and χ⁺ zeroes the specular lobe.
Where images change. No Golden scene has a fractional metallic, so the Goldens change only at silhouettes, where Appendix B's |N·V| replaces the old clamp to 0, mostly on metals. SciFiHelmet's metallic Texture is fractional, about 0.2 to 0.3 over most of it, and there the mix itself moves the helmet by up to 23 steps.
Size. 370 changed lines (327 added, 43 removed).

metallic_roughness_brdf_matches_appendix_b_and_is_reciprocal: 675 cases of cosines from lights and views between the normal and grazing, at the roughness floor, 0.5 and 1, for a dielectric, a metal and half of each, against Appendix B's "full code" in f64 within 4e-6; light and view swapped give the same value. It fails with Appendix B's simplified mix, with metallic's mix reversed, or with the diffuse lobe missing 1/π.
reflected_luminance_follows_the_brdf_and_a_white_metal_reflects_at_most_what_it_receives:
from unit vectors around a tilted normal, matches the f64 reference within 5e-5, since f32 rounds the directions themselves;
0 for a light at or below the horizon, finite where L = −V at the roughness floor;
a white metal's directional albedo, summed over 16,200 directions of the hemisphere, stays at most 1 at roughness 0.5 and 1, seen from 0°, 60° and 85°.
Appendix B's dielectric is not held to that bound: its fresnel_mix is not energy conserving, as Appendix B says in "Coupling Diffuse and Specular Reflection" (it reflects 1.11 at 85° when white, at roughness 0.5).
It fails without the half-vector guard (NaN), or with N·V in place of N·L.
fresnel_rises_from_the_base_reflectance_to_one_at_grazing takes the weight, at ±0.5 and just past 1 too.

fixed: Appendix B's licence is cited;
fixed: pow could see a negative base;
fixed: the tests built Surface by position three times, now in one place;

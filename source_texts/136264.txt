Light Propagation Volumes (Global Illumination) | Fab
Jun 18, 2026Light Propagation Volumes (LPV) is a lightweight method of rendering Diffuse Global Illumination which approximates the bounce light from the sun. Built from scratch with performance and simplicity of usage in mind, the plugin provides an efficient method of lighting the game environments.

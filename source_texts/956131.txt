The current fog system is quite limited, ugly and not intuitive with the fogdomecommands. I suggest the following change:
A fog material. This wouldn't technically be a material, but for the editor it would act like that: Select a certain amount of space, type /fog (or /fog2, /fog3 etc), and in that area a haze/mist/fog dome or whatever it is called gets visible. The editor will have commands at his disposal to tweak those hazes, like /fogcolour, /fog3density, /fog2fade (the amount of distance you have to walk into the haze to reach the maximum fog density) etc.
A new version of the old /fog command, /ambientfog or something. It would basically act like a combined version of the current /fog and /fogdome: render a haze in a circle around the player, just like now, but make the skybox misty at the same time too. Commands like /ambientfogheight, /ambientfogmax, /ambientfogdistance etc should be available.
I am not sure if all this is technically doable, but it would give the map maker even more possibilities to give his map a great atmospheric feel.

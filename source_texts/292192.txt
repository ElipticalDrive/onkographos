Motivation
Reduce the number of field multiplications and barriers in the shader scan phase to cut VGPR pressure and improve GPU throughput on RX 6800-class hardware.
Preserve the single-inversion-per-workgroup strategy but compute it from a saved total product to avoid per-lane inversions and reduce expensive work.
Limit changes to only the performance-critical shader logic to maximize chance of safe, high-impact runtime gains.
Description
Replaced the previous Hillis–Steele inclusive prefix/suffix scans in shader.comp with work-efficient Blelloch exclusive scans (upsweep + downsweep) for shared_P and shared_S.
Saved the workgroup total product into shared_total_inv before the downsweep and perform a single fe_invert on that total, then combine exclusive prefix/suffix products with the inverted total to compute each lane's reciprocal.
Simplified extraction of P_excl/S_excl so each lane reads its exclusive result directly from shared_P[lid]/shared_S[lid] after the Blelloch scan.
Change touches only shader.comp and is committed on branch perf/blelloch-scan-optimization with a focused commit message.

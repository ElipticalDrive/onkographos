Plan: morton API surface + bed-picks consolidation
After reviewing the four notebooks that touch the morton work (crossovers.ipynb, xopr_atl06_crossovers.ipynb, bedmap_morton_match.ipynb, bedmap_disambiguate.ipynb) plus the two adjacent ones (bed_picks.ipynb, bedmachine_comparison.ipynb), three patterns are duplicated across them and belong in the library: a per-region bed-pick load loop (3 notebooks), an opr:mbox→Polygon visualization helper (2 notebooks), and a per-pair crossover error computation. We also have one new top-level module (xopr.crossovers) whose name is too narrow for what it actually does.
This issue tracks the consolidation. Plan below; non-breaking apart from the xopr.crossovers → xopr.matching rename, which is safe to do now since the module is fairly new and only one notebook imports it.

load_bed_picks design notes
Accepts: gpd.GeoDataFrame (N frames), pd.Series (one row), dict (STAC-item-as-dict), or pystac.Item. Always returns a flat GeoDataFrame of picks.
Output schema: geometry, wgs84, slow_time, id, collection, opr:date, opr:segment, opr:frame, segment_path — strict superset of the existing layer-parquet schema, so it's a drop-in layer_gdf for disambiguate_matches.

What
Builds out the implicit (SDF/CSG) geometry-kind into a full clean-meshed-CSG + hybrid-render pipeline, and captures the design thinking behind it in ADRs. Re-derives CSGLib architectural-geometry lineage (Stephen Todd's BigRoof heritage) as composable ops over one typed IR.
The whole thing lowers from a single SDF IR, so the CPU golden and the GPU/WGSL paths agree by construction (the repo's ADR-0003 discipline). 306 tests pass.
Highlights
Plane-native BSP mesher (src/geometry/bsp.ts) — exact meshed CSG for the polyhedral subset:
Boundary-by-classification against the exact SDF golden; exact plane∩plane vertices, minimal clean faces (a hip roof is 16 tris vs ~4,700 for grid dual-contouring).
Coincident-face handling via canonical-plane evaluation (shared walls, the L/T junctions "just work").
Coplanar-adjacent merge + feature-edge extraction (mergeCoplanar / brepEdges) → clean architectural line-work.
Octree localisation for many-mass scenes (differentially tested identical to the global pass).
Per-face provenance (primId → planeId) — the ADR-0012 pick-to-feature hook.
Value-noise displace op (src/geometry/implicit.ts) — organic fbm displacement; bit-exact hash CPU↔WGSL (GPU parity to 1e-3); animated in the raymarch via a per-frame time uniform.
Hybrid decomposition (src/geometry/hybrid.ts) — split a model into what the BSP can mesh and what it can't:
boundsSdf reports a node's solid extent, honestly ±∞ for a bare half-space.
nonPlanarRegions is "where the BSP won't work" — nothing (planar), finite boxes (local growths), or unbounded (a displaced/blended half-space).
planarSkeleton pulls out the plane-only sub-model to mesh.
Playground — three interactive views (mesh, raymarch, hybrid), WebGPU + TypeGPU + three.js:
Mesh view: grid-DC vs plane-BSP mesher toggle, coplanar-merge toggle, feature-edge wireframe; hip/L/T houses + a 16-house village (exercises the octree).
Hybrid view: one model (house.smoothUnion(growth)) auto-split — house BSP-meshed, growth raymarched confined to its reported region box, composited by shared depth so the growth blends into the roof. Shared shading node so mesh and raymarch are one material. Toggles for the meshed house, raw-triangulation wireframe overlay, and the non-planar region box.
Docs / ADRs
ADR-0012 — geometry provenance & pick-to-feature editing.
ADR-0013 — hybrid strategy dispatch & Progen-scale parallelism (building/mass units, octree-as-dispatcher, the mesh↔raymarch vs mesh↔mesh seam asymmetry, Rust-relevance-tracks-the-algorithm, and — corrected mid-review — that real PBR + positional lights on the raymarch is viable and shadow-casting is a general procedural-geometry contract, not a raymarch special case).
ADR-0014 — the procedural-geometry render contract (surface → depth/distance/gbuffer/lit roles; three.js as one binding, a renderer-owned G-buffer as the non-three.js path).
Interactive surface-nets/dual-contouring explainer (docs/explainers/).
Reviewer notes
Scope of the BSP: polyhedral CSG only; curved/smooth ops (sphere/smoothUnion/displace) are rejected with a routing hint and go to grid-DC or raymarch.
Known first-cut limitation in the hybrid view: the BSP meshes the entire planar skeleton and the raymarch region is an overlay on top (depth-composited), not a spatial partition — so the roof is triangulated under the growth too. Seam-free but double-renders the region; a true partition (subtract the region box from the skeleton) is noted as a follow-up.
Pre-existing failures: the 6 failing test files (test/decode, parser, packet, etc.) are the HTJ2K codec tests that need the unbuilt openjph-wasm / Rust wasm package — unrelated to this branch. All 306 runnable tests pass.
Toolchain: Node + pnpm + vitest (not Bun); pnpm test runs CPU + GPU suites.

```
Add `evaluateBrep`/`brepToMesh`: the exact `implicit → mesh` bridge for the
polyhedral subset (CSGLib/quickhouse lineage). Each primitive's faces are
seeded, clipped to their primitive, split by every other primitive's planes,
and kept iff the fragment separates inside from outside — boundary-by-
classification with the exact CPU SDF (`evalSdf`) as the in/out oracle. Output
is exact plane∩plane vertices and minimal flat facets (the "clean lines" the
grid staircases away), with per-face/per-triangle provenance (primId → plane),
the ADR-0012 hook.

CPU by design: irregular convex-polygon clipping over tens–hundreds of faces,
the opposite of the parallel per-pixel job. The image-space form of this
boolean already lives on the GPU (raymarch `sdScene`); this yields the
orthogonal artefact — a mesh with topology.

Scope (first slice): box/plane, union/intersect/subtract, translate/scale.
Non-polyhedral (sphere/smoothUnion) and exactly-coincident faces (shared
walls, L/T junctions) throw with a routing hint rather than emit wrong
geometry — coincident-face merging over a canonical-plane registry is the
next slice. Mesh view gains a mesher toggle (grid DC vs plane BSP) that falls
back to the grid with the decline reason when the BSP declines.

Verified against the golden: brep faces lie on the SDF zero-set and orient
outward (bsp.test.ts, 7 tests).
```

feat(geometry): coincident-face handling in the BSP evaluator
…
ba61ffc
```
Evaluate each CANONICAL geometric plane once instead of per-primitive, so
coincident faces (shared walls, the L/T junctions) stop being a special
case. Coincident planes merge into one canonical plane carrying each owner's
orientation; the plane's whole boundary is computed from a single seed, so
nothing is doubled. The exact-SDF classifier already resolves the rest: the
±ε offset crosses every coincident plane at once, so a union's back-to-back
shared wall (opposite normals) classifies as interior and drops, while an
intersection's shared cap (same normal) collapses to the one surviving face.
Provenance picks the owner whose orientation agrees and whose solid contains
the fragment centroid.

Removes the earlier throw-on-coincidence guard. The L-shaped and cross-wing
houses now mesh exactly with the plane BSP (verified in the mesh view: L =
46 tris, T = 124 tris, clean valleys, no z-fighting).

Tests: adjacent-box union drops the shared wall; A ∩ A collapses to one box;
an L of two roofed arms meshes with no duplicated triangles (bsp.test.ts, 9).
```

```
Add `mergeCoplanar` and `brepEdges`. After per-plane evaluation still emits a
separate face wherever a crossing plane split the seed (a fused solid's shared
top in two pieces, plus every fan diagonal), fuse each plane's coplanar,
co-oriented fragments back into their maximal outlines by directed-edge
cancellation: interior edges appear as opposite-directed pairs (the arrangement
has no T-junctions), so cancelling them leaves the region's outer loop (CCW) and
any hole loops (CW). Collinear vertices are dropped so an edge split where two
fragments abutted collapses back to one. The convex fragments are retained as
`parts`, so triangulation stays a trivial per-part fan — no non-convex/holed
re-meshing. `brepEdges` walks the merged outlines (deduping edges shared between
planes) to give clean feature-edge line-work.

Mesh view: the BSP path now merges, reports the merged face count, and renders
feature edges (not triangle-wireframe) when Wireframe is on — the L reads as
clean line-work (ridges/hips/valley/eaves), no diagonals.

Tests (bsp.test.ts, 14): box → 6 faces / 12 edges; two abutting boxes fuse to
one box (6 faces, 12 edges); a corner notch stays one non-convex face; a
poke-through tool recovers a face with a hole; the flush-arms L merges and stays
valid with no duplicate triangles.
```

feat(geometry): octree localisation for the BSP evaluator
…
b249188
```
Localise boundary evaluation through a space octree once a scene has more than
`octreeMaxMasses` union operands (default 8). Lowering now tags each prim with a
`massId` (a union starts a fresh bounded region per side; intersect/subtract keep
it) and computes each mass's AABB from its additive box extents. The octree
subdivides the scene until each leaf overlaps few masses; within a leaf a face is
split and classified only against that leaf's masses, so distant houses never
interact and the work drops from O(masses²) toward O(masses).

Correctness is unconditional: classification stays the exact global SDF (foreign
planes crossing a leaf outside their mass create no real boundary there, so
per-leaf splitting is still exact), and leaf boxes are clipped half-open so a
face on an octree seam is emitted by exactly one leaf. `mergeCoplanar` stitches
the per-leaf fragments back. Small scenes (≤ threshold masses, all current
shapes) take the unchanged global pass.

Playground gains a "Village (4×4 houses)" shape (16 masses → octree path; meshes
to 144 clean faces).

Tests (bsp.test.ts, 17): forced-octree result is byte-identical to the global
pass on a 2×2 village; a 3×3 village meshes to 81 valid faces with no duplicate
triangles; the default threshold keeps small scenes on the global pass.
```

docs(adr): ADR-0013 hybrid strategy dispatch & Progen parallelism
…
bdc48e1
```
Capture the design intent for composing the three implicit lowerings (grid-DC,
plane-BSP, raymarch) at scale and for ornament that breaks the BSP:

- two nesting units — building (embarrassingly parallel, concatenate; the worker
  payload) and mass (intra-building octree localisation);
- per-building raymarch proxies reusing the structure/value split (one pipeline
  per model, instanced), with overdraw as the residual limit;
- octree as a strategy dispatcher: leaf strategy = f(live op-kinds), subdivide to
  isolate ornament;
- the seam asymmetry — BSP↔raymarch composites via depth (free), BSP↔grid-DC is a
  topological crack — so ornament routes to raymarch and grid-DC stays a per-object
  choice;
- tags (policy) vs analytic recognition (correctness) as layers;
- Rust relevance tracks the algorithm: ruled out under grid-DC, implicitly
  reopened by the BSP choice, gated by the language-agnostic differential tests.
  Not an immediate priority.

Status: proposed / exploratory (design intent, not yet implemented).
```

feat(geometry): value-noise displace op for the implicit kind
…
8607630
```
Add a `displace` SDF node — `child + amp·fbm(freq·p)` — a hash-lattice value
noise (4-octave fbm) that lumps a field into an organic surface. The hash is
pure u32 integer arithmetic, bit-identical between the CPU golden and the WGSL
in SDF_PREAMBLE, so the only CPU/GPU divergence is f32-vs-f64 interpolation
(GPU parity holds to 1e-3). It breaks the exact distance property, so it is a
display op: the raymarch under-relaxes and animates it by drifting the noise
domain through a per-frame `uNoiseTime` global (0 = the static golden the CPU
and parity harness see); the grid mesher can still tessellate it.

The plane BSP rejects it alongside sphere/smoothUnion (non-polyhedral).

Tests: bounded fbm, amp-0 no-op, determinism, canonical param order (CPU);
displaced-sphere field parity (GPU).
```

```
Add `hybrid.ts`: split an implicit model into the part the plane BSP can mesh
and the part it can't, so a scene can mesh the planar bulk and raymarch the
organic remainder (ADR-0013's "segregate, don't throw").

- `boundsSdf(node)` — AABB of the solid, honestly ±∞ for a bare half-space
  (an intersection clamps it back); non-planar nodes widen it by their reach
  (displace by amp, smoothUnion by k). The extent a node declares.
- `isPlanar` / `planarSkeleton` — the plane-only sub-model to mesh.
- `nonPlanarRegions(node)` — THE region where the BSP won't work, whose range
  is the whole point: nothing (planar), finite boxes (local growths), or
  unbounded (a displaced/blended half-space — no box to hide behind). Boxes
  merge, so one growth is one region and any unbounded region absorbs the list.

Tests (10): tight/unbounded extents, planar/non-planar classification,
skeleton extraction, and the three region outcomes incl. the infinite cases.
```

docs(adr): ADR-0014 procedural geometry render contract
…
cdf123a
```
Sketch the contract that lets procedural geometry (horn/terrain meshes,
raymarch) take part in depth, distance, and G-buffer passes, not just lit
colour. The seam is a surface program — author the surface once (a vertex
transform, or the march) plus material; depth/distance/gbuffer/lit are generic
functions of it. three.js binds the roles via customDepthMaterial /
customDistanceMaterial (the near-term forward path); a renderer-owned deferred
G-buffer is the same contract read differently — it unifies mesh/raymarch by
construction, hosts our own custom lighting (many lights, traits, provenance as
a G-buffer channel), and is renderer-agnostic WebGPU, so three.js becomes
optional presentation. Generalises the ADR-0013 shadow note.
```

Problem
#36 showed the FD-02 non-smoothness comes from 66 force-band cells whose trilinear gradient is ~0: the source STL (SHA 5e6d2107…8d11) has 1-cell-thick (0.05 m) plates whose faces lie on the h=0.05 design nodes, so even an exact SDF sample is 0 at all 8 corners. Fresh resampling reproduces all 66 cells, so genesis v16 is correct but under-resolved. Reinitialization and lattice offsets do not remove them; an h=0.025 lattice does (0 cells <1e-6, 3 cells <0.25).
Work
Register genesis v17: same source STL, same design box, h=0.025 (phi 121x65x49), same sign/volume semantics; append-only, v16 unchanged.
Record the force-band gradient census as a registered gate (no cells with |∇phi| below a preregistered threshold).
Re-run the minimal WaterLily qualification chain the new state needs (W1 adapter, W3 primal, W4 sensitivity) on Kaggle T4; flow grid is independent and unchanged.
Done
v17 state registered with gate evidence, and the primal/sensitivity chain re-qualified or fail-closed diagnosed.
Dependencies
Blocks #37 (FD-05). Relates to #28, #29, #20.

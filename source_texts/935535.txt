💡 What: Implemented bounds checking to conditionally skip Terrain_Simplex3D noise evaluation in Terrain_GenerateChunk based on the surface distance dy.
🎯 Why: Terrain_Simplex3D was previously being evaluated for every single voxel (65,536 times per chunk) just to determine if the 3D density noise overcame the vertical distance from the surface dy = baseH - ly. Given that the noise evaluation is bounded to [-1.0, 1.0] multiplied by amp3D, if dy falls outside of [-amp3D, amp3D], the voxel is mathematically guaranteed to be solid or air, making the expensive noise evaluation completely redundant for the majority of the block generation logic.
📊 Impact: Chunk generation time is reduced by >60% (from ~4.2s to ~1.6s for 1000 chunks).
🔬 Measurement: Verified with a custom C benchmark measuring the execution time of 1000 Terrain_GenerateChunk calls before and after the change.

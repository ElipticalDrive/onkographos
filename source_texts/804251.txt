Stacked on #15.
High quality now ray-marches the procedural sky's clouds; Low/Medium (and Quest) keep the flat layer from #15.
Volume: 60 m slab over the 100 m cloud shell; 64³ RG8 3D texture (perlin-worley shape + worley detail) built once on first use.
Shape: a smooth low-frequency weather map (cloud fields included) decides where cloud can be; the 3D noise carves the clouds. Flat bases, domed tops that rise with the weather. Storm → flat, closed deck.
Lighting: 4-step sun march, Beer's law + multiple-scattering tail, dual-lobe HG phase + isotropic share (sunlit sides bright from any angle), ambient from base to top.
March: 16–48 steps by path length (12 m target), IGN jitter, early out at 2% transmittance. Height on the shell's curve, not flat — flat height made the clouds stop ~15° above the horizon.
Shadows: world/model shaders switch with it (cloud_volumetric, cloud_volume globals) to one volume sample along the sun, so shadows fall under the clouds you see.
RenderQuality gets a volumetric-clouds column; main sets it on sky build and on quality change.
Cost (M1 Max, 3D at the project's 1280×720, looking mostly at sky): gl_compatibility 1.1–1.3 ms → 2.3–2.8 ms/frame (+~1.2 ms); Forward+ no measurable change. Weaker GPUs will pay several times that, which is why it's High-only.
Checked by render (tools/cloud_shot.tscn, now with VOL=1, PERF=1, a ground view, and the converted user:// scene so shadows show): golem + 2fort clear, golem storm 0.55 / 1.0, ravine night; ground luminance responds to storm in both modes. Game boots clean. Test suite: same 1 unrelated failure as #15.
Not done: temporal reprojection (no history buffer on a sky-brush shader), so edges carry a fine jitter grain.

perlin-worley volume (Skybox.cloud_volume, built on first use), carved out of a broad,
low-frequency weather map: flat bases, domed tops that rise with the weather, detail erosion,

haze and lightning apply on top.

sample through the sun's slant of the slab, so they fall under the clouds drawn. Low/Medium

- RenderQuality.apply flips the volumetric switch (static Skybox.set_volumetric); main.gd's two
  call sites and the dead Skybox.volumetric go. The 64^3 volume (~180 ms) is built only once a
  procedural sky draws it.
- set_storm pushes only what a storm moves (shadow, cover), not the noise textures and sky
  constants every frame of a transition.
- Sky shader: each cloud path hands the tail its own lit/cover/mottle/shade/rim instead of the
  volumetric path faking the flat one's n and thick; silver lining is hg_phase(c, 0.8)/45.
- March: takes the shell distance and noise point the fragment already has, hoists wind and
  fair out of the 5-per-step density calls, and is skipped below the horizon.
- Ground shadow (volumetric) traces to the slab once instead of twice.
- cloud_field/cloud_tex2 replace copies; planet vec4 becomes the viewer position.
- skybox.gd: _fbm/_bytes/_interleave replace five noise setups and two byte loops;
  render_quality.gd: _preset() replaces three copies of the lookup.

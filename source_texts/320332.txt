## 📋 Overview

**Proximity Volumetric Rendering (PVR)** is a revolutionary theoretical rendering architecture designed to bridge structural material blueprints with dynamic level-of-detail (LOD) processing. Moving entirely away from traditional flat polygon meshes and painted textures, PVR models environments using volumetric voxels defined by molecular and chemical "material recipes". 

To overcome the immense computing and data-throughput ceilings required to render the physical world down to a subatomic level, PVR introduces a hybrid compute pipeline:
1. **Distant Horizon & Macro-Scale:** Uses classical AI Neural Inference (Neural Impostors, NeRFs, and Neural Texture Compression) to predict and render the macro-scale environment efficiently, preventing memory bottlenecks.
2. **Immediate Proximity & Micro-Scale:** Reserves raw, highly localized Quantum Computing channels to compute photorealistic light transport equations (path tracing) and subatomic material mechanics right in front of the viewer.

Volume rendering techniques is a kind of volume visualization technique,which can reflect the inside information of the volume data. This paper mainly introduces a improved volume rendering algorithm,which firstly puts fuzzy enhancement operation on 3D medical images,then takes fuzzy thresholding segmentation,and clearly classifies the three dimensional data fields as edge region and non- edge range,renders them respectively finally. The results on PC shows that this method can improve the speed and accuracy of mapping.

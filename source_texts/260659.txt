Draft for review by @lassoan , on top of #13 (one commit). #13 introduced scikit-image's marching cubes for the WSS wall surface; this replaces it with VTK, which is already the optional dependency for pathlines and export and is the same isosurface 3D Slicer builds for segmentations.
Agree that VTK makes sense here.
What changes
wss.wall_surface(mask, voxel_to_world): 0.5 isosurface of the binary mask from vtkFlyingEdges3D on a unit-spacing image in index space, mapped to world with voxel_to_world. Returns points, inward normals (unchanged: smoothed-mask gradient, covector transform), faces and ijk. wall_points() is every step-th vertex of it.
to_vtk_surface(..., faces=) builds a triangulated model, so the WSS/TAWSS/OSI map can be shown as a model in Slicer rather than as vertices.
Without VTK the voxel-boundary fallback remains; scikit-image is no longer used anywhere. The wss extra now names vtk; the test extra still lists scikit-image until it is confirmed nothing else needs it.
Checks
12 mm cylinder on a 1 x 1 x 2.5 mm grid: vertices and triangles identical to scikit-image's (max vertex distance 0.0, same 3072 vertices and 5952 faces); lateral area within 1% of 2 pi R L; radius 11.9 mm.
After the first call (VTK import, ~0.5 s) under 1 ms on 32x48x48 and 5 ms on 64x128x128, versus 10 ms for scikit-image.
tests/test_wss.py and tests/test_derivatives.py pass; new test_wall_surface_is_the_half_level_isosurface.
If #13 is merged first this can be merged after it; the stacked branches above #13 do not touch wss.py.

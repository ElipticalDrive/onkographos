Part of #88
Question
How do we make the terrain look organic and lush — closer to AoE2's smooth isometric landscape — instead of the current stark blocky tile grid?
Context
Current terrain: large flat quads with hard colour boundaries (bright green grass / tan sand / blue water). Cliff edges show as raw black dashes.
The spec (§2.14) mentions a splat-map, 512 px tiles — implying splatmap blending was planned.
Three.js terrain shader exists (src/render/). The ambientCG grass/ground/rock textures are loaded.
Water is a flat blue solid-fill plane — no animation.
Acceptance criteria
Splatmap blending: tile edges blend smoothly between grass↔dirt↔sand using a GPU splatmap — no hard colour boundaries visible at standard zoom.
Tile texture tiling: ambientCG grass001, ground110, rock064, and leafy-grass textures tile within their biome zones with no visible seam.
Water animation: water plane uses a scrolling normal map or vertex-displaced wave to simulate gentle motion. No new assets required (procedural or existing PolyHaven water normal).
Cliff/elevation edges: height-step boundaries render with a terrain-colour transition rather than black dashes.
Performance: terrain draw call count unchanged (still one draw call per terrain mesh). GPU frame time ≤ 6 ms constraint maintained.
Existing fog-of-war DataTexture overlay remains correct on top of the new terrain.

Label: mesh-terrain | rendering Priority: 6
A cylindrical 2D texture representing the full interior surface, used for rendering beyond the 1km LOD threshold. Third tier of the LOD pipeline: full geometry (≤1km) → decimated mesh (>1km, visible) → 2D map (far distance, all visible chunks beyond decimated range).
Required:
Generate once at world creation from same noise seed as chunk generation — before any chunk work begins
Stored as world data: heightmap (16-bit PNG or raw float array) + surface colour texture
Rendered as texture projected onto low-poly cylinder geometry — no marching cubes
Deformation-triggered regional update: when terrain is significantly deformed, update the corresponding UV region (regional texture write, not full regeneration). Significance threshold (surface height delta or density points changed) — tunable during testing.
Update is cheap: chunk world position maps directly to UV coordinates

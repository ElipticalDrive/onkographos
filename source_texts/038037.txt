High-Level Assessment
The following are alternative approaches to this PR:
1. Automatically derive lights from emissive materials
➕ Removes manual proxy creation for common glowing materials
➕ Keeps material and volumetric color synchronized automatically
➖ Requires renderer-wide material analysis or baking
➖ Cannot reliably infer radius, intensity, or intended media contribution
➖ Risks creating excessive local lights and unpredictable performance
2. Integrate volumetrics directly into surface-light packing
➕ Reuses existing renderer light lists and selection logic
➕ Could reduce duplicate ECS traversal
➖ Couples volumetric limits and behavior to surface-light constraints
➖ Makes volumetric-only proxies harder to represent cleanly
➖ Mixes 2D shafts, froxels, and PBR responsibilities
Recommendation: Keep the explicit volumetric proxy and dedicated collection approach. It provides predictable artistic control, prevents emissive surfaces from being double-lit, and preserves separation from surface-light packing; automatic emissive-material extraction is better treated as a later opt-in baking feature.

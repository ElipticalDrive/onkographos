In recent years, data accumulation is growing at a tremendous rate. Such data could originate from different sources such as camera images, supermarket transactions, sensor readings, video camera footage, social media interactions and so on. Since the data may be large in Volume, Variety and Velocity, the main challenge is how to store, process and extract useful and relevant information from these data. For image data, image compression can be used to reduce the data volume making it manageable for analysis. Thus, this paper will review a proof of concept multi-resolution analysis using wavelet transform, which is one of the mathematical tools used in signal representation, and consider the application in the field of image compression for wireless sensor network data. The reviewed methodology involves application of wavelet transform, threshold detection, quantization and encoding the resulting data and finally applying an inverse transform.

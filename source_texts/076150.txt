Quiltwright renders multi-view quilts for Looking Glass light-field displays. Every view uses an off-axis (asymmetric-frustum) projection rather than a toe-in rotation, which is the geometric requirement for the display's lenticular optics to fuse the views into stable depth. Scenes can come from PyVista/VTK or from the POV-Ray ray-tracer, and the library provides tools for managing the holographic depth budget -- adjacent-view disparity and focal-plane placement -- plus a client for Looking Glass Bridge and encoders for quilt video and Hololuminescent Display masters.

Step 6 of the physics plan: fluids. This PR includes the commit of #21 (ropes). Merge #21 first; after that, this PR shows only the fluids commit.
What
fluid [visc] fills a shape with drops. They start in staggered rows about 6 units apart; big fills use coarser drops so there are at most 2500. The drops are simulated as position based fluids: a density constraint that only pushes drops apart where they are too dense, so the surface is free, plus artificial pressure against clumping. Settings: 4 steps per frame, 4 passes per step.
solid shapes hold the water, and that includes solids moving with keys or bones (the stone in the example). Drops keep about one spacing off a wall, so the drawn outline meets it.
flow N turns the shape into a spout. N drops per second leave its lower edge along the shape's own "down", in columns one spacing apart, and take the spout's motion with them.
Viscosity smooths neighbouring drops' velocities (XSPH) and adds friction where drops touch a solid, so fluid 0.6 behaves like honey.
Drawing: a metaball field on a half-spacing grid is traced with oriented marching squares (saddles resolved by the cell average). The loops are smoothed into a BezPath and set as the node's deformed path, so the water renders with the shape's own fill and stroke, including holes. In Design the shape is drawn as is.
Runs in the shared simulation core, after ropes. gravity goes to the fluid when the shape has no emitter or rope.
Editor: a «Жидкость» checkbox with viscosity, flow and gravity in «Физика». The «Препятствие» hint and docs now say that ropes, cloth and fluids collide with solids too.
Example examples/fluids.msc (a dam break with a falling stone, a tap filling a cup, honey sliding off a plank), added to CI.
Docs: a «Жидкости» section in docs/physics.html with a render and a panel shot, plus a script.html row, README, the Windows README and F1.
Checks
cargo fmt, cargo clippy -D warnings, cargo test --workspace: 2 new core tests (a water column levels out in a tank and stays in it; a spout pours a stream down to the floor) and 1 new script test. motif-cli check passes on every example.
Performance: the 4 s example simulates in about 0.8 s in release.

`rope` turns a shape's outline into a chain of points held at fixed
distances: it sags, swings, drapes over `solid` shapes and sways in the
wind. Ends are pinned (`pin start end`) and follow the shape, or are tied
to other objects (`tie`). `cloth` turns a shape into a pinned grid that
resists stretching and gives way to squeezing, so it folds like cloth seen
from the front: flags, curtains, capes. Objects hang on ropes (`hang`,
turning with the rope at its end), and a shape with `links` is drawn as
chain links along one. In Design the hung objects sit on the drawn rope.

`fluid` fills a shape with drops kept at an even density (position based
fluids), pulled by gravity and held by `solid` shapes, including ones that
move with keys. `flow N` pours N drops a second from the shape's lower
edge instead, carried along as the shape moves. Thickness (`fluid 0.6`)
evens out neighbouring drops' speeds and makes the fluid cling to what it
touches. The water is drawn as one smooth vector outline round the drops
(metaballs traced with marching squares) with the shape's fill and stroke.

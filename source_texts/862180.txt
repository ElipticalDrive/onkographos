Cherry-picked from #2324 (bisecting NR mode only, without the neighbor-limited variant), adapted to EXPAND_NODE_EDGES.
Adds the IterateSmoothingLengthDensityBisectingNR solvergraph node, which keeps a per-particle bracket [h_lo, h_hi] tightened from the sign of rho_sum - rho_h(h) and falls back to bisection when the Newton-Raphson step leaves the bracket. Selectable with
```
cfg.set_smoothing_length_density_based_bisecting_nr().
```

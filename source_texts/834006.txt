We need to generate pointcloud from a Nerf model, probably while the model is in training.
The NerfStudio team suggested saving a checkpoint during training and using the checkpoint to export pointcloud. If possible, we should also look at other methods, such as a method that pauses the training to generate a pointcloud and resumes after the generation.
Then, generated pointclouds need to be sent to a DT and rendered.

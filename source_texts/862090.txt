Part 2 of the translucency work (#67 was part 1, released in 0.81.0).
The volume of a material can now scatter, so a thick part is milkier than a thin one. The photon map now works inside such glass too, so a collimated beam through slightly milky acrylic shows its track, as a laser's does. The change note is changes/scatter-inside.md (bump: minor; scene format 26).
What changed
Scatter inside a material (core/haze.js THE MEDIUM'S WALK, materials.js, tracebasics.js)
Two new material fields:
scatter distance: the mean free path, in mm whatever the scene's unit;
scatter forward: Henyey-Greenstein's g.
The walk runs in the march: each collision is a march step, not a bounce.
Only a scene with a scattering material compiles it. Every other scene's march is the text it was.
A medium's absorption and scattering are packed in f16 per mm on the ray. A colour depth's exp(-μL) moves by at most 3e-4 over 10 m.
Every kernel's text changed with the new ray layout.
The library gains milky glass (scatters inside).
The photon map in the volume (sppm.js sppmGatherVolume, haze.js THE BEAM SEEN INSIDE)
How it works:
Photons store a record where they collide in the glass.
A camera ray gathers at its first collision.
Shade then turns the ray there, as the march would have.
Light only the map can count: light sampling cannot reach inside smooth glass, and a collimated light reaches the picture only through photons. So this is how that light is seen at all.
Its own radius inside glass (sppmRadiusGlass, a developer setting, default 10 px):
Records stored in glass go on a grid of their own.
The radius shrinks as a volume: R³ by the factor the surface's r² is.
Added after the user found the beam's track too grainy at the surface's radius.
Bindings: it needs two more storage buffers in the march. On an adapter without room for them (volumeOf), the beam stays unseen, as before.
The photon map's keep, planned on the GPU (sppm.js sppmPlan)
The bug (read in code):
The share of stores a sample keeps was planned on the CPU from a readback of the sample before.
RT.step, which the Tests panel's image rows use, records all its samples before that readback lands.
So every sample ran on the first guess.
The fix: one dispatch of the map's own kernel now plans the keep at each sample's start, from the count still in the heads. The plan sits in four words after the records, which nothing clears.
The surface map took the same plan: it had the same dependency.
Tests panel
An image row waits for the kernels its settings ask for, as a timing row did. Switching the photon map in a scene that scatters builds every kernel again. Samples were refused meanwhile ("trace while the scene's pipelines are being built").
The map's columns, shared with sppm: records, dropped, samples over, cut, kept, radius, radius inside glass. They are read after the row's samples finish.
New rows: volume, volume-light, volume-map.

Haze scatters at a material's faces, whatever its thickness. The user asked for the volume too,
so that a thick part is milkier than a thin one (roadmap/DIFFUSERS.md option A). A material now
has a scatter distance (the mean free path, in real millimetres like the colour depth) and an
asymmetry g. The scattering is pure; the colour stays the colour depth's.

The walk is in the march kernel, not a shade vertex. Inside a solid, every shadow ray and photon
connection is stopped by the solid's own wall, so a collision there only turns the ray. The march
turns it and marches on, so a collision is a march step against Max Iterations rather than a
bounce. A leg's flight is drawn when it starts, from its vertex (absLtot). An internal reflection
keeps the draw, which is exact because the free path is memoryless. A collided ray goes on as
light sampling could not have made it (prevPdf -1), and shadow rays take the scattering as
extinction. The walk is compiled in only for a scene that scatters (scene.scatter, set by
codegen); every other scene's march is the text it was, and build.test holds that.

The Ray had no word left, so its medium became six f16 in the three words the absorption's f32
used, per millimetre, and the Ray stays 112 bytes. The f16 rounds a material constant by at most
2^-11 of itself. Over 10 m, exp(-mu L) moves by at most f16's subnormal half step (3e-4);
volume.test bounds both.

The Dawn test caught a real bias. Drawn from after shade's step off the surface (ten
tolerances), the flight could never collide within that step, and a slab of optical depth 6
passed 0.3952 where an independent walk passes 0.3813. Drawn from the vertex it reads 0.3786 to
0.3809. Two rules for the tolerance skin at a wall were tried, showed no measurable difference
at a sane tolerance, and were left out. The comments say what is not modelled.

Tested: the JS walk is held to exp(-tau), to T + R = 1 and to the diffusion regime's
1/(tau + 2q). On Dawn, the march's walk agrees with it at three depths; milky spheres vanish in
the furnace; a floor lit through a milky slab reads one mean with the photons and the photon map
on or off. Bench rows `volume` and `volume-light`. The library gains milky glass. Help, docs,
the tutorial (the haze chapter, two excerpts re-read and stamped) and the change note follow.

The user shone a collimated beam through slightly milky glass and expected to see its track, as
a laser shows in hazy acrylic; nothing inside the glass showed. The cause, read in code: a
collision only turns a ray in the march. Light sampling and a photon's connection both stop at
the glass's own face, and a collimated light no camera path can meet at all. So the light the
glass scattered had no way into the picture. The user chose the photon map over bringing back
the manifold connection, and this is MEDIA.md phase 2 for a scattering material.

A photon now stores a record where it collides, in the march: SPPM_VOL, its normal word empty,
by the surface map's own rules for who stores. A camera ray whose lens field is 1 hands its first
collision to the shade queue (RAY_GATHER). The map's kernel gathers there:
sum p(l.v) Phi / (sigma_s 4/3 pi r^3), over n^2. Shade then turns the ray as the march would
have. That collision is the map's D (lensStep, EV_EVAL), so the camera's path then leaves the
class. One JS template writes the eight cells' walk for both gathers, and the surface gather now
skips volume records, whose empty normal word would read as "up". The store is one sppmPut for
surfaces and media.

The march takes the map's two buffers only where the stage has room for them beside its own
(volumeOf, against the adapter's limit), and the march and shade are rebuilt with the map when it
is switched on or off for a scene that scatters. It must also be handed the sample's sppmGeom:
without it, every photon was stored in a cell of size 0, and no gather found any (Dawn).

Checked on Dawn over lavapipe: a sunlit milky slab seen side on reads 0.952 +- 0.042 of the
camera's own paths with the map on, an independent estimator; no JS mirror of the gather against
a closed form yet. A radius near the part's size reads low (0.747 at 7 cm), the sphere sticking
out of the face; the map's own radius shrinks. Every other GPU suite still passes. Bench row
`volume-map`; help, library, docs, the change note and the tutorial follow (three excerpts re-read
and stamped).

The user (2026-11-03): "the beam track is too grainy, add the volume radius". A gather inside glass
counts the records in a sphere, so what it finds goes as the radius cubed. At the surface's
radius it found about 0.01 records on volume-map's sheet (0.72 mm in 0.26 m^3; inferred from the
user's numbers, not measured), and the map row was noisier than the camera's own paths.

sppmRadiusGlass (Render settings > Light sources > Photon map, a developer setting beside the
surface's radius) is in pixels at the distance in focus. It defaults to 10, where the surface's is
3; that is a choice made without seeing it. The host turns it into UB.sppmVol: the radius, the
cell 2R, and 1 / (4/3 pi R^3). It shrinks as a volume shrinks (sppm.js sppmRadiusVolume: R^3 by
the factor the surface's r^2 is).

The records stored where photons scatter in glass (SPPM_VOL) go on a grid of their own: sppmPut
picks the cell by the record's flag. sppmWalk takes the grid it walks, so the medium's gather
walks its own cells with its own radius, and the surface's gather is unchanged (sppm.gpu.mjs
passes). The march and the map's kernel take sppmVol with the sample's other values.

A large radius reads dark near the glass's faces: the sphere sticks out and is divided as if
whole. The setting's help says to keep it under the thickness.

On Dawn (volume.gpu.mjs) the side-on slab with the radius inside glass at twice the surface's read
0.941 +- 0.031 of the camera's own paths, and the spread across its face fell from 0.172 to 0.068.
volume-map pins 10 pixels and shows it as r glass mm. The tutorial is updated: the uniform block's
110 fields in 784 bytes (chapter 21), and the walk, the store and the iteration's excerpts re-read
and stamped.

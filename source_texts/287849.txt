The optimization
Blocks, water and items further than the render distance from the camera are not drawn at all:
Default: 125 blocks · slider in the Light tab's Render section, 20 → 500, step 5
Culling runs on the GPU inside every world material's vertex shader (lib/render-dist.js): one shared uRenderDist uniform, injected after three's <project_vertex> chunk — any vertex whose world position is beyond the limit collapses outside clip space and is discarded before rasterization
Costs zero CPU time per frame: no chunk rebuilds, no buffer churn, no visibility scans — just one uniform value when the slider moves
Works for the big instanced voxel batches (a 500×500 world = ~484k instances) and single meshes alike; the welded water surface culls its own vertices through the same shared uniform
Horizontal (XZ) distance, Minecraft-style — looking down from high up still draws the ground beneath you
No popping, no surprises
Distance fog now fades out exactly where the cull cuts (near = rd·0.45, far = rd·1.02), so geometry dissolves into the sky instead of snapping out
Generating a world larger than the current distance auto-raises it to fit the new footprint (one Undo restores both terrain and distance)
The setting lives in the light state → saves with the project, restores on load, survives light presets
Library thumbnails render at ~7 blocks, always inside even the 20-block minimum
Also fixed
Stale gen.cells reference in the generation success toast (regression from the Map-return switch in Armor for the player, 5 new terrain styles, worlds up to 500 blocks #15 — generation worked but the stats toast threw "Generation failed")

Representational accuracy of the isosurface meshes produced by well-known marching isosurfacing methods is considered. Accuracy is determined versus the isosurface given by trilinear interpolation and quantified using a new measure of volumetric divergence. The new measure is an accurate estimate of spatial discrepancy between a produced mesh and the isosurface of trilinear interpolation. Through experimental testing using the measure, suitability of the considered methods to well-model the isosurface of trilinear interpolation's behavior on grid cell interiors is also established.IEEE/ EG Symposium on Volume and Point-Based Graphic

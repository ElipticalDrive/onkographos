Clean branch from current main. Replaces the flat radial God Rays with world-space volumetric lighting: camera-depth prepass, real directional sun shadow-map occlusion, Henyey-Greenstein Mie forward scattering, height haze, nonlinear view-ray spacing, stable no-TAA dithering, 3-tap filtered shadow samples, dynamic dawn/day/dusk/night sky/light coupling, adaptive exposure, and mobile/desktop quality tiers. Includes real WebGL volumetric on/off framebuffer regression. Architecture is inspired by techniques used in Minecraft shader packs such as MakeUp Ultra Fast; implementation is original and no third-party shader code is copied.

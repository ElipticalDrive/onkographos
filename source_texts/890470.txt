Motivation
Replace ad-hoc perlin-based fallbacks with a modular, extensible world generation pipeline to support richer biomes, rivers, hills and deterministic randomness.
Enable streaming/infinite worlds with chunk retention and on-demand generation to remove rigid world boundaries.
Description
Added a new worldgen subsystem under worldgen/ including random/qcg.js, noise/fbm.js, layers/base_layers.js, stacks/*, pipeline/biome_pipeline.js, terrain/chunk_generator.js, and core/infinite_world_generator.js that implement a layered biome pipeline, river/hills stacks, terrain height synthesis and a deterministic RNG.
Reworked game/singleplayer/main.js to initialize a worldGenerator from WorldgenCore.InfiniteWorldGenerator (seeded from resolveWorldSeed()), and to delegate sampling calls such as getBiome, getRiverMask, getRavineMask, getNoiseGroundHeight, and hashRand2D to the generator when available.
Refactored chunk handling and generation to stream chunks around the player with ensureChunksAroundPlayer() and getChunkRetentionRadius() replacing the old full-world generation, and added chunk eviction logic that cleans up geometries and torch lights when chunks fall outside the retention radius.
Updated block/terrain generation to use pipeline samples for biome, river mask and frozen-river handling, to apply riverbed overrides and river ice, and to centralize biome/height logic in TerrainChunkGenerator; added script includes to singleplayer.html for the new worldgen modules.
Testing
No automated tests were added and no automated test suite was executed for this change.

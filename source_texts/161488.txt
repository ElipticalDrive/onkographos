Spherical Beta appearance model
Adds Spherical Beta (SB) lobes as an appearance model orthogonal to spherical
harmonics. sh_degree and sb_number are independent, so sh0 always supplies
the diffuse base and SB lobes add specular highlights on top: (3, 0) is today's
default, (0, 2) is the Beta Splatting setting, (0, 0) is purely diffuse.
Each lobe stores 6 floats: [r, g, b, theta, phi, beta].

SH0/SB2 is a strict win over SH3. Higher PSNR on both strategies, at 44–46%
of the PLY size, roughly half the peak VRAM, faster to render and faster to
train. MCMC's 1M-Gaussian cap makes that row exactly parameter-matched: 104 MB
against 236 MB for identical geometry.
SH3/SB2 is the quality ceiling under MCMC only. At 29.742 dB it is the best
result measured, but it costs 16% render throughput, and under the default
strategy it lands below SH0/SB2 while costing 2.7× the PLY and 45% more
training time. The extra SH bands buy nothing there.

Scope
SB is wired into rasterization() for 3DGS only. It composes with SH in Python
and leaves the C++ rasterizer untouched. Both DefaultStrategy and
MCMCStrategy handle the sbN parameter through densification. Lobes persist
through training checkpoints, PngCompression, and PLY export — the latter via
additive sb_params_* properties that match the reference implementation's
naming, leaving the standard f_dc_* / f_rest_* schema untouched so ordinary
viewers still load the file. The splat and ply_compressed formats reject
lobes rather than dropping them silently.
Reproducing
```
SH_DEGREE=0 SB_NUMBER=2 examples/benchmarks/mcmc_sb.sh
```

re-exports them after editing — so scenes trained here are directly inspectable,
including a Full / Diffuse / Specular toggle that reproduces the light
decomposition from the paper.

Adds a second fog mode, vfog.enabled (off by default), that replaces the fog ramp with a lit volume, and changes how a tile's own lights shade everything. That tile-light change is not opt-in: it applies to every area, fog or not. The maintainer has looked at the fog in game across three rounds of tuning; the last fix (fog over a background with no skybox) has not been confirmed in game on its own.
The fog volume
A froxel grid over the main view (FogVolume, two compute passes after AO):
cx_vfog_cs lights each froxel, then blends it with last frame's result reprojected onto the same point. It is lit by:
the area's fog colour, pulled toward the sky average by vfog.sky;
the sun through its shadow cascades, with a two-lobe phase function;
every clustered lamp, with its cube shadows.
cx_vfogsum_cs marches each column from the eye and stores in-scattered light and transmittance at every slice.
Every stage that fogs reads the result through CxFog, CxFogFactor, CxFogReveal and CxFogSky, which branch on uVFog.x. Pass order and the deferred lights' weights are unchanged. Captures and reflections keep the ramp.
The fog is built from what the area already sends: fog colour, start, end, the day/night state, the sun and the lights.
Density: the area fog is a wall from start to end, measured from the player. It leaves 1% transmittance at the fog end, so the far plane stays hidden as with the stock fog.
Height: the wall thins away across a fog bank whose top is vfog.top (15 m) above the eye.
Sky: the sky reads the same ray as the geometry in front of it, so a silhouette cannot come out foggier than the sky behind it, and lamp glow carries onto the sky. The resolve exposes and tonemaps the part of a sky pixel's light that the fog put there, matching the fogged geometry beside it. With no dome at all, the resolve covers the bare clear colour with the volume itself.
Sun: it is a share of the fog colour, not light added on top, so far fog stays the builder's colour on average. The moon lights no air.
Lamps: the surface curve sharpened toward an inverse square whose strength grows with the light's reach (vfog.reach, default 0.1). Without this, every lamp glowed with the same peak brightness.
Texture unit 32 holds the volume: it is the lit stage's 32nd active sampler. Native stage images move to unit 33 and up.
Tile lights light as a local ambient
A tile model's own lights (main lights and night windows, SceneDb.LitTile set) are now the ambient class everywhere:
no N.L, no shadow, no highlight;
in the fog, they add their surface curve without a phase or shadow.
This applies even though vanilla's SetTileLights marks the main lights shadow-casting. Torches, the flame objects that tiles place, and placed lamps stay real light sources.
Because these lights are no longer static, they leave the lightmap bake. Many areas now have nothing to bake, so the load-screen bake gets shorter or disappears, and those lights run live in the lit pass instead. Each one is cheap, but it is worth watching frame time in tile-dense areas.
Knobs
vfog group under Graphics » World. Only the master switch is a User row, because the submenu is at its 14-row cap; every dial is Advanced. The whole group is server-holdable through ServerPolicy.AllowKnobs("vfog."), documented in docs/ui-policy-contract.md (new "Volumetric fog" section).
quality ladders on the effects quality: 96x54x48 up to 240x135x128 froxels.
haze (default 0) and lights (default 0.15).
reach (default 0.1).
top (default 15 m).
sun, sky, aniso, height, floor, noise, noisesize, wind.
Chat-only: temporal and lightshadows.
/vfog prints the medium, the bank top, the opaque distance, the ambient and the sun share.
Checks
build.sh.
run-demo.sh: all self-checks pass. New ones:
FogVolume.SelfCheck: the opacity budget against a numerically integrated ramp, the colours, the quality ladder, the server hold and the SceneFog arms.
A DbLights check that a tile light is admitted as ambient, with no shadow and no bake.
tools/gates/run.sh.
check-shaders.py: 204 programs, all linked on the NVIDIA driver.
build-win.sh passed on the first commit. The later commits add no hook, offset or OS-specific code, and were not cross-built again.
Known limit: very tall geometry that crosses the far plane above the fog bank shows its clip edge.

A second fog the player (or a server, by policy) can switch on in place of the
fog group's ramp. Off by default, so the current fog stays the default look.

It is a froxel volume over the main view, the technique current engines use
for volumetric fog. Each frame cx_vfog_cs lights every froxel: the area's fog
colour (pulled toward the dome's average by vfog.sky) as the light the medium
scatters, a share of it arriving as the sun's beam through the shadow cascades
with a dual-lobe Henyey-Greenstein phase, and every clustered lamp through
cx_lightfrag's own curve with its cube shadow. Samples are jittered inside the
froxel and blended with last frame's re-projected answer. cx_vfogsum_cs then
marches each column from the eye and stores in-scattered light and
transmittance per slice, plus one slice for the sky.

The medium comes from what the area already sends. Its fog end becomes an
opacity budget: 1% transmittance on a ray out from the player at the fog end,
so the far plane stays hidden as the ramp hides it. vfog.haze of that budget
hangs in the open air, thinning above the player's feet and broken into
patches that drift on the area's wind; the rest is a wall rising from the fog
start to the end. The sky's march drops the wall above vfog.band, so the dome
stays visible overhead. By day the sun takes a share of the fog colour rather
than adding to it, so fully fogged geometry stays the builder's colour on
average while shafts and shade show inside it. The moon lights no air: the
share eases to zero on the night palette. Indoors there is no sun.

Every stage that fogs reads the volume through the functions it already calls
(CxFog, CxFogFactor, CxFogReveal, and the new CxFogSky for the two sky stages),
so pass order and the deferred lights' transfer weights are unchanged. The
volume sits on texture unit 32, the lit stage's 32nd sampler; native stage
images move up to 33. Probe faces and the planar mirror keep the ramp. The far
plane, the fog cull and the sky band follow the volume's shape while it is on.

The vfog group is server-holdable whole (ServerPolicy.AllowKnobs("vfog.")), so
a server can hold vfog.enabled=1 and its look; docs/ui-policy-contract.md says
how. vfog.quality ladders on the Effects area, one step below it. The panel
gets one row (the switch); the dials sit under Advanced, since World was at its
row cap. Self-checks cover the opacity budget against a numerically integrated
ramp, the far distance it implies, both colour terms, the grid ladder, the
server hold and SceneFog's arms with the mode on.

Not live-verified: built, self-checks green, every program compiles and links
on the NVIDIA driver (check-shaders.py), but no in-game run yet.

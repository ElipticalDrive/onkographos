Closes #24.
The Minecraft terrain preview is now a playable first-person voxel scene. WASD/mouse controls movement and look, Space jumps, Shift sprints, held LMB mines, RMB places, and 1/2/3 select unlimited dirt/stone/wood. A five-block ray selects visible resident cells, with a selection outline and mining progress in the HUD. Hand mining uses game-owned hardness rules; bedrock remains unbreakable. Placement requires loaded air and cannot intersect the player.
The voxel plugin adds bounded origin-relative collider collection, explicit missing-data policies, full-cell bounds, and a conservative broadphase for the existing first-person controller. Queries reuse caller storage and visit only the local swept region. Incomplete movement queries pause simulation while retaining look. The game rebases physics/cameras during chunk crossings without resetting velocity or interpolation. Edits change collision immediately and replace rendered geometry through the existing bounded streaming pipeline.
Dirty chunks remain pinned in session; disk saves, inventory, tools and drops belong to later roadmap issues. Pinned edits share the 160-chunk resident budget and can eventually block further streaming. These limits and checked usage examples are documented. CPU-only builds still need no raylib toolchain.
Validation:
scripts/check.sh: formatting, workspace/all-target clippy, tests, doctests and strict rustdoc passed.
Ten new CPU tests cover local queries and budgets, missing policies, jumping/landing and edited walls, negative chunk crossings, reach, mining resets/reinstallation, unbreakable blocks, placement overlap, distant coordinates and interpolation.
scripts/native_smoke.sh passed, including wide/portrait Minecraft screenshots. The native Minecraft probe mines the support block, verifies falling and remeshing, places it back, verifies landing and updated GPU receipts, then unloads every mesh. It also passed after the final spawn-bound validation change.
Rust 1.89 workspace/all-target check, generated-project smoke, Wayland compilation and workspace benchmark compilation passed.
Seven versioned collision/interaction benchmarks saved and repeated on clean commit e7e169b, using 10 samples, 0.25 s warmup and 1 s measurement. Workload IDs and environment metadata match; mean changes range from -1.78% to +0.62%. These establish new baselines rather than claim improvements over an older implementation.
Representative repeat means: standing query (16 cells/4 solids) 95.9 ns with 8 resident chunks and 97.3 ns with 512; swept query (175 cells/25 solids) 1.36/1.38 µs respectively. Five-block hit/miss and unloaded-boundary queries are 60.5/47.9/47.4 ns. Setup and output-capacity warmup are untimed; query inputs are opaque to the optimizer.
Local raw samples and metadata are preserved under artifacts/benchmarks/voxel-interaction-v1/{save-20261001T202718Z,compare-20261001T202749Z}. Reproduce using:
```
scripts/benchmark.sh save voxel-interaction-v1 'voxel_(colliders|interaction)_v1'
scripts/benchmark.sh compare voxel-interaction-v1 'voxel_(colliders|interaction)_v1'
cargo run --release -p rayengine-minecraft --features render --bin minecraft -- --seed 42
```

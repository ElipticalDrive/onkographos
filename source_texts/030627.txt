Goal: Semantic 3DGS (3D Gaussians with semantic labels) .
Challenge : Consistent Gaussian -level semantic identities
from multi -view 2D segmentation .
Gap: Existing approaches use latent embeddings or post reconstruction assignment with no geometric grounding .
Question : Can SfM correspondences establish persistent
identities at initialization?
2. Key Insight
◆
◆
◆
SfM Tracks: Covisibility tracks link each sparse 3D point to
its 2D observations across views .
Semantic Observations : Multi -view consistent 2D masks
provide semantic labels for these observations .
Semantic Consensus : Labels along each 3D point track are
used to obtain a single persistent semantic identity .
3. S33DGS Approach
>
>
SfM Covisibility -Guided Semantic Seeding
◆ During 3DGS initialization,
each SfM 3D point is assigned a persistent semantic label obtained from its SfM track using a
semantic consensus voting strategy .
Joint Photo -Semantic Supervision
◆ Semantic identities are rendered as probability distributions following the same 𝛼-blending used for RGB rendering .
◆ Training combines photometric and semantic supervision
◆ Adaptive Density Control allows new Gaussians (cloned/split) to inherit their parent’s semantic identity, preserving semantic
consistency throughout optimization .

8. Limitation
S33DGS depends on multi -view consistent 2D masks and reliable
SfM coverage ; segmentation inconsistencies or limited geometric
support for small, thin, or heavily occluded objects may lead to
less reliable semantic initialization
9. Conclusion
S33DGS grounds semantic identities in SfM geometry, enabling
coherent 3D semantic representations and direct object -level
editing while preserving photometric reconstruction quality .

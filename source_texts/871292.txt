What
Adds a reader for COLMAP binary models (cameras.bin, images.bin, points3D.bin) to crates/sfm. It also adds the three v1 sfm metrics (registered ratio, mean reprojection error, mean track length) and their gates. ADR-0010 records the decision, and the kickoff now points to it.
Why
This answers #28's open question 3: Lauri chose to read the model files instead of parsing model_analyzer. The M4 water-plane fit needs this reader anyway.
How
The binary layouts and the metric definitions come from COLMAP 4.2.1's source, the version in the pinned image. The reprojection error is the plain mean over points with an error set, not a track-weighted mean. The track length is observations divided by points.
WriteImagesBinary writes only registered images, so the registered ratio divides by the number of input frames. The spike's ratio, registered images over model images, was 1.0 by construction and could never fail its gate.
The reader checks that ids resolve across the three files. A truncated file, an oversized count, an unknown camera model or a dangling id is an error that names the file.
rigs.bin, frames.bin and the text format are not read; ADR-0010 explains why.
Verification
just check: fmt, clippy and 131 tests pass.
metrics::tests::integration::spike_model_reproduces_model_analyzer reads the spike's masked laajasalo model under work/ (not committed) and reproduces its model_analyzer.txt. The counts match exactly; track length and reprojection error match within 5e-7.
Control: swapping in a track-weighted reprojection mean makes that test fail.

## What

- **sfm stage**: COLMAP `feature_extractor` with the masks applied at
extraction, then the configured `sequential_matcher` or
`exhaustive_matcher`, then `mapper`. The settings are the spike's
(ADR-0002): one camera and 16,384 SIFT features. GPU use follows the
colmap tool's `gpu` flag. If the mapper splits a capture into several
models, the largest becomes `sparse/0`. Metrics come from the model
(ADR-0010), plus a `models` count, and the v1 gates run on them. `run`
now includes the stage.
- **Recipes**: `just run <dataset>`, and `just overnight`, which runs
every dataset detached (`nohup` + `caffeinate -i`) and logs to
`tmp/runs/overnight-<time>.log` with `BATCH-`/`RUN-` markers. A failing
dataset does not stop the rest, and the run ends with a
`telegram-notify` summary when that command is available. `just
overnight-status` reads the markers. `SHORELINE_TOOLS` is now in
`.env.example`.

## Why

#28: get the pipeline as far as `sparse/0` for both clips, with COLMAP
running on the GPU host (ADR-0008, ADR-0009), and runnable unattended.

## Verification

- `just check`: 137 tests pass. They cover the step arguments for both
configs, the largest-model swap, the frame count, and version and
backend checks.
- Real run: `shoreline-recon --tools <host file> run
configs/reposalmi-laajasalo.toml`. Frames and masks were cached on the
Mac, and sfm ran on the GPU host: 121/121 registered, 0.718 px, track
length 7.12, 1 model, gates pass. Spike values were 121/121, 0.72 px and
7.12. The steps took 16 s, 34 s and 134 s, including transfers. That
run's logs were truncated by the bug #33 fixes.

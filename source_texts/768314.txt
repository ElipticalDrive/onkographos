Caves (cave/caveLayout.ts, cave/caveMesh.ts) and procedural terrain take their own seed and params, so the editor variant grid from #2681 cannot show cave or terrain variants.
Follow-up from #2681.
Acceptance
Cave layout and terrain generation are registered defineGenerators with a defineParamSchema and an injected seed.
Terrain LOD uses the contract's lods and context.detail where it fits.
A tile preview is registered per output family; the old seed and param code is deleted.

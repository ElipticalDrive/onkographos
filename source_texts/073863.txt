Prep notes
Facts (Godot 4.6.3 source)
Properties: defaults at scene/resources/environment.h:202-214, hints at environment.cpp:1534-1548. The only setter clamp is volumetric_fog_detail_spread to 0.5-6.0 (environment.cpp:982). Project settings: volume_size 64, volume_depth 64, use_filter on (servers/rendering/rendering_server.cpp:3829-3831).
Grid: width size·ratio, height size/ratio, ratio = w/((w+h)/2) (render_forward_clustered.cpp:1316-1319). Depth slices map as z = -length·((z+0.5)/depth)^detail_spread (volumetric_fog_process.glsl:215-219,297-308).
Medium: density, scattering = linear(albedo)·density, emission = linear(emission)·emission_energy·density (volumetric_fog_process.glsl:354-378).
Light injection: Henyey-Greenstein phase with g = anisotropy (:252-255). Directional (:425), omni with Godot's omni attenuation (:476-511) and spot with the cone term (:543-575), each times light_volumetric_fog_energy. Shadows: PSSM for directional (:388-422), the atlas for omni and spot (:485-509,559-573).
Ambient and sky inject: only when ambient_inject > 0 (:430-446, fog.cpp:1067-1076). gi_inject acts only with VoxelGI or SDFGI (:583-676).
Temporal reprojection: 16 Halton jitters blended at 0.9 (:257-347,683). With a still camera it converges to the mean of the 16 offsets.
Filter and integration: a 7-tap gaussian on X then Y (:731-746), then front-to-back Beer-Lambert per column (:697-727).
Composite: the surface shader samples the volume at (screen_uv, pow(-z/length, 1/spread)) and combines it with depth fog (scene_forward_clustered.glsl:1091-1099,1493-1514,2988). The sky pass mixes it in by volumetric_fog_sky_affect (sky.glsl:287-294).
Renderers: Forward+ only (render_forward_mobile.cpp:3391-3393, rasterizer_scene_gles3.cpp:1147-1150).
FogVolume and FogMaterial: rasterised into the same froxel volume (fog.cpp:567-790). The repository's FogVolume slice is renderIntent: 'pending', status unimplemented.
Repository now
The Environment sheet lists volumetric fog as Not drawn. Plain fog maps to THREE.FogExp2 in the environment layer. The repository already patches three's shader chunks globally, which is where a per-surface composite goes.

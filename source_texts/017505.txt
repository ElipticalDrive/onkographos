GPU-Accelerated Interactive Ray Casting Visualization for Discontinuous Finite Elements

Session: Geometry Modeling, Visualization and Computational Environments
Published Online:4 Jan 2024https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2024-0165
Abstract:
Ray casting approaches for various finite element rendering tasks are presented. The experimental renderer currently provides element surface, cut plane, and isosurface rendering capabilities. The ray casting approach makes computational cost scale strongly with image size, instead of domain size, making it suitable for large problems. The renderer aims to be interactive and highly scalable with GPU implementations of all rendering tasks. Ray casting ensures pixel-perfect images by construction if intersection tests are accurate. Preliminary results for element surface, cut plane, and isosurface rendering are presented. Algorithmic trade-offs are discussed and improvements are proposed for future work.

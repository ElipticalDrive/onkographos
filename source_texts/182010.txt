Octree Data Structure | microsoft/O-CNN | DeepWiki
Jun 1, 2025The octree data structure in O-CNN provides a hierarchical spatial subdivision of 3D space that enables efficient storage and processing of sparse 3D data. The implementation consists of two primary classes: Octree for construction and modification, and OctreeParser for read-only access to serialized octree data.

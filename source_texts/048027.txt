Description
First version (HexMe-1.0) of HexMe dataset.
This version corresponds to the one detailed in the paper
"Hex Me If You Can", Beaufort et al. (2022)
The dataset consists of 189 tetrahedral meshes (vtk datafile version 2, ASCII mode), with tagged features (points, curves, surfaces and even volumes).
In addition to those mesh files, the logs of the meshing tasks are available.

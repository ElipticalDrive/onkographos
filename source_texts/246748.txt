Reviewer's Guide
The PR extends the ray tracer with recursive stochastic diffuse illumination and sky lighting, adds material colors and parallel rendering, exposes configurable PPM rendering through a new CLI, and introduces a separate interactive Minifb viewer with camera and light controls. It also reorganizes the repository as a Cargo workspace and refreshes generated image assets.
Sequence diagram for stochastic global illumination rendering
```
sequenceDiagram
    participant CLI as rt CLI
    participant Camera
    participant Renderer as renderer::trace
    participant Scene
    participant Vec3
    participant Sky

    CLI->>Camera: get_ray(px, py)
    CLI->>Renderer: trace(ray, scene, max_depth, sky)
    Renderer->>Scene: closest_hit(ray)
    alt surface hit
        Renderer->>Scene: is_shadowed(shadow_origin)
        Renderer->>Vec3: random_unit()
        Renderer->>Renderer: trace(bounced, scene, depth - 1, sky_strength)
        Renderer-->>CLI: hit.color.mul_elem(direct + indirect)
    else no hit
        Renderer->>Sky: evaluate sky gradient
        Sky-->>Renderer: sky color * sky_strength
        Renderer-->>CLI: sky illumination
    end
```

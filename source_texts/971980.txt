⚡ Is there an underlying problem to be solved?
Transparency is a blend state per pipeline. Glass, water, smoke and layered UI in the world need order-independent transparency, refraction, and correct interaction with fog, shadows and GI, on a visibility-buffer pipeline that shades opaque surfaces only.
📝 What should the system provide?
Weighted blended OIT first (two targets and a composite pass), measured; moment-based OIT as the upgrade if glass quality demands it.
A per-object sorted forward path for the few surfaces that need refraction, sampling a copy of the opaque colour buffer.
Fog (S21) and GI (S20) applied in the transparent forward pass from the froxel volume and probes; shadow receiving from S19.
Transparent motion vectors for TAA and upscaling (S23).
The material transparency class (S16) selects the path; back-propagation enables the passes only when needed.
✅ How do we know it is done?
Given a scene with glass, smoke and water, when the camera orbits, then no sorting artefacts appear in the golden video and the golden images pass.
Given refractive glass in front of fog, when rendered, then the fog behind it is visible through the refraction within tolerance of the reference.
Given no transparent materials, when the graph compiles, then no OIT pass exists (asserted).
WBOIT versus MBOIT quality and cost on the glass test are recorded; the choice is written down.
🧭 What is covered now, what is left for later?
Covered now: WBOIT, refraction path, fog and GI integration, motion vectors, measurement.
Left for later: per-pixel linked lists for exact layering, transparent shadows, colored transmission through thick media.
🕶️ How should the solution look like to achieve the points above?
Battle plan system S22, topic Materials, transparency, post.
💬 Do you have any additional considerations?
Blocked by S15. The deferred-versus-forward decision from S15 shapes the forward transparent pass.

Summary
The engine has no clouds. A ray-marched cloud layer above the froxel volume, lit by the sun and the sky, with a cloud shadow map, gives outdoor scenes weather.
User stories
As a level designer, I want volumetric clouds from a weather map, so that outdoor skies have weather.
Requirements
A ray-marched layer between two heights, above the froxel volume's far end (CLUSTER_FAR, 500 m).
Shape from a weather map, shape noise and detail noise.
Lit by the sun with an approximation of multiple scattering, and by the sky's light.
Rendered at a quarter of the resolution with temporal reprojection.
The clouds' shadow map darkens the ground and the fog's injection.
The fog's background pass composes the clouds with the sky.
Comes after sky and atmosphere, which it reads.
Acceptance criteria
The sample shows clouds that cast shadows on the ground.
Clouds are stable in motion.
The rendering docs describe the built feature, in the present tense.
When to do this
After sky and atmosphere, when a game has outdoor skies.
Context
Rendering: Volumetric fog
References
Andrew Schneider, Nathan Vos, The Real-Time Volumetric Cloudscapes of Horizon Zero Dawn, SIGGRAPH 2015.
Sébastien Hillaire, Physically Based Sky, Atmosphere and Cloud Rendering in Frostbite, SIGGRAPH 2016.

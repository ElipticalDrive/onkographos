Match 3D could turn black because redundant normal averaging produced zero normals at opposing limb caps. HDR lighting produced non-finite values that Bloom spread across the frame. Preserve the authored cap/side normals and use Three's fixed ACES curve while the composer owns tone mapping.
The rendering changes remove unused adaptive-luminance targets, share the medium/high composer without changing effect values, generate identical LUT bytes without per-texel arrays, and compact GLSL whitespace while preserving tokens and preprocessor boundaries.
Public startup waits for actual content paint before evaluating the application graph, preloads the emitted stylesheet and critical fonts, and bounds full-page gradient raster work. Quick match now receives the league index and visible league through SSR; the full canonical catalogue loads on selection intent or entry into play. Concurrent league requests are fenced, Play stays disabled while selection is incomplete, and errors preserve the existing teams with retry/reload. Shared framework primitives are grouped without adding modules to the common startup closure. Navigation, private caching, CSP and simulation/save authority remain intact. Three dependency overrides address production advisories.

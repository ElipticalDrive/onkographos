Measured while fixing #862, on this repo's self-hosted runner (RTX 3090, Vulkan,
Windows), with an optimize=speed_trace editor build on an idle machine (no
CI run in flight — checked before and after).
What was measured
One full world resubmit: GaussianSplatWorld3D::apply_world(), whose cost is
dominated by _register_shared_renderer() →
GaussianSplatSceneDirector::submit_world_submission()
(modules/gaussian_splatting/core/gaussian_splat_scene_director.cpp:2337), whose
phase 2 is a blocking render-thread dispatch at :2425-2430.
Procedure: a GaussianSplatWorld3D in a live scene with a camera, payload
alternated between two GaussianData of equal size so every call re-uploads;
9 samples per size, 3 frames pumped between samples, 30 warm-up frames first.

Why it matters
This cost is pre-existing — apply_world() has always paid it, and it is the
documented workaround for #862 — but #862's fix puts it on a path the user
triggers implicitly, by assigning world.gaussian_data. A 1M-splat scene stalls
for over two seconds on a content change. #862 coalesces the two changed
emissions a single set_gaussian_data() produces, so the fix pays it once rather
than twice, and defers it off the property setter's call site; it does not make
the single resubmit cheaper, and deliberately did not try to.
It is also not a per-frame cost: it is paid only when the payload actually
changes. So it is not a reason to revert or redesign #862 — the alternative there
is a viewport that silently shows the wrong content — but it is a reason to look
at what a resubmit actually does.
Where to look
submit_world_submission() rebuilds and re-applies the whole
WorldSubmissionContract even when only one field of the record moved. A payload
swap and a set_metadata() write cost the same. Candidates:
Diff the candidate record against the live one and skip the phase-2 apply when
no field the renderer consumes has changed. This is the cheap win: after GaussianSplatWorld3D never resubmits when the assigned GaussianSplatWorld's payload changes — the director keeps the apply-time snapshot #862,
a set_bounds() or set_metadata() write now triggers a full payload
re-upload for no reason.
Split the contract so a metadata/bounds-only change does not re-upload the
payload.
Attribute the remaining time between _apply_world_submission_contract_unlocked()
(the render-thread dispatch and GPU upload) and the record bookkeeping before
choosing between the two, so the fix targets the half that actually dominates.
Suggested first step: the attribution, then the record diff.

Recent advancements in volumetric cloud rendering have primarily focused on distant clouds， neglecting the challenges posed by near-traversable clouds.This paper presents an adaptive sampling optimization technique called pre-stepping， which significantly improves real-time rendering of near-traversable volumetric clouds.By utilizing pre-stepping and probe downsampling， the method acquires density distribution information of the cloud layer.Leveraging this data， the method optimizes sampling points near the camera and adjusts their distribution around non-empty points， effectively addressing noise and ineffective sampling issues near the cloud layer edge.To mitigate rendering inconsistencies caused by the low resolution of pre-stepping probes， a similarity-weighted approach optimizes the final shading results.The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed optimization method significantly improves rendering quality and performance when dealing with block-distributed cloud layers， such as penetrating cumulus clouds.Compared to the uniform step size strategy， the proposed method achieves improvements of 4.62% in PSNR and 6.53% in SSIM.Furthermore， the method achieves a 4.59% improvement in rendering performance.

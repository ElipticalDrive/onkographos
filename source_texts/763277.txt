## 📦 Overview

The vtk-3d-structures-viewer offers a simple solution for 3D visualization and basic analysis of structures. Built on VTK and Python, this application enables users to view mesh and volume data, perform slicing, take measurements, and exports information for further use.

## 📊 Features

- **3D Visualization:** View complex structures in three dimensions for better understanding.
- **Mesh and Volume Rendering:** Load and visualize various file formats, including DICOM, STL, and OBJ.
- **Slicing Tools:** Easily slice through your models to analyze internal structures.
- **Measurement Tools:** Take accurate measurements between points or along edges.
- **Export Options:** Save your visualizations and measurements in popular formats for further analysis or sharing.

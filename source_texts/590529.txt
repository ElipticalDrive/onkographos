Description
Scripts that reproduce the figures and table of Ghelichkhan et al. (2026), Frontiers and Future Directions in Inverse Modelling of Mantle Convection, Proceedings of the Royal Society A (manuscript RSPA-2026-0569).
The archive contains: the construction of elastic and anelastically corrected shear-wave velocity tables (Figure 1); the Taylor convergence tests that compare the full discrete adjoint with the simplified continuous adjoint over reconstruction windows of 2, 10, 50 and 125 timesteps (Figure 2 and Table 1); the spatial comparison of the two gradients (Figure 3); and the adjoint gradient of the surface velocity misfit term for 20- and 80-timestep reconstruction windows (Figure 6).
This is a deposit of source code. Simulation output (checkpoints, VTU/PVD fields) is not included; see the README for what is and is not reproducible from the archive alone. The article had no DOI when this archive was prepared; the related identifier should be added to this record once the paper is published.

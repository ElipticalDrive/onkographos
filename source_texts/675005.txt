This salvages #28502.
Full, Half, and Quarter setting in WorldEnvironment under Ss Reflections.
Replaces 0.5 with a custom multiplier.
Higher resolution requires more steps, else the range of the reflections is shorter.
Slight performance changes per resolution. Quarter res and lowering max depth may be useful for lower end devices.

In response to your comment on RocketChat, I'll be more clear about the steps needed to make this mergeable:
resolution should be replaced with a new property step_size
Instead of modifying the pixel_size uniform. Pass a new uniform called step_size to the shader
use step_size to scale the size of each step taken in the ray march
Right now, the resolution parameter doesn't change the resolution of the image, it multiplies the pixel_size which implicitly changes the size of the steps in the raymarch. But neither the source resolution nor the destination resolution have changed. In effect, this PR gives the benefit of changing step size, but it does so while telling the user that resolution has changed. Actually reducing the target resolution would provide more of a performance benefit, a slight memory benefit, and likely better quality for the same increase in performance.
Alternatively, proper resolution selection could be added, which would require changing the size of the SSR textures and framebuffer.

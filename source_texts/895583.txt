Overview
The shared environment BRDF lookup squared perceptual roughness twice and used a separable visibility approximation. Rough and grazing surfaces therefore sampled a different GGX distribution from the authored material. Screen reflections additionally applied Fresnel twice.
Stacked on #526.
Key Changes
Convert perceptual roughness to GGX slope roughness once and use height-correlated Smith masking-shadowing for the single-scattering lookup.
Integrate 4,096 Hammersley samples per texel. Allocation, activation, immutable device ownership and cross-graph readiness remain unchanged.
Share the F0*A+B material response between screen reflections, environment lighting and forward probes; remove the unused alternative Fresnel helper.
Repair the indirect surface fixture's incoherent camera snapshot and stale extra-Fresnel expectation. Exercise both real perspective and orthographic matrices.
Document the mathematical contract and remaining environment convolution work.
Validation
Windows/MSVC Debug builds passed for GraphicsFoundationTest and EditorEngineProcessFrameworkTest.
The previous lookup failed 12 numerical comparisons. The corrected producer passes nine rough/grazing comparisons against independent double-precision incident-direction quadrature, including a doubled-resolution convergence check, plus mirror-limit and full 65,536-texel finite/nonnegative/energy-bound checks.
Vulkan NativePreparation, NativeIndirectLighting, SceneProbes and SceneReflections passed, including device restrictions, independent views and retained recordings.
Authored Editor probe lighting and temporal screen-reflection fallback passed.
Pinned changed-source formatting, repository structure and diff whitespace checks passed.
Limits
Runtime acceptance is Windows/Vulkan Debug. Lookup generation has four times the previous sample work once per device; no representative initialization or frame-time result is claimed. This remains a single-scattering split-sum approximation. Cubemap convolution, diffuse irradiance, microfacet multiple-scattering compensation and direct-light BRDF improvements remain separate work.

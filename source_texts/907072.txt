#  Image Super-Resolution: Classical Interpolation vs SRCNN

This project explores image super-resolution by comparing traditional interpolation techniques with a deep learning–based SRCNN model. High-resolution images are intentionally downsampled and then reconstructed using different upscaling approaches to analyze image quality and performance trade-offs.

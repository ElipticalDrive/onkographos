💡 What: Introduced viewport bounding box culling and building AABB caching in BuildingRenderer.ts.
🎯 Why: Previously, every building polygon's vertices were projected via MapLibre (projectPoint) and drawn into PixiJS graphics on every camera move, regardless of whether the building was visible on screen.
📊 Impact: Completely eliminates MapLibre coordinate projections and PixiJS polygon drawing for off-screen buildings on every camera move event, significantly reducing CPU overhead during panning and zooming.
🔬 Measurement: Verified with unit tests in BuildingRenderer.test.ts showing 100% culling of off-screen buildings (0 projections) while preserving full rendering for visible buildings.

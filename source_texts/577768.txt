Behavior
Independent opaque apertures can now render through real GPU instancing in Forward+, while every canonical physical emitter and its Dimmer/Color state remains independent. Vector Prism shafts, #111 native shaft apply, #112 lazy aggregate shafts, native workers and nonblocking snapshot publication remain intact.
Cold admission requires the exact default double-sided opaque StandardMaterial PBR slice, a shared single-surface triangle mesh, conformal local transforms and compatible render policy. Batches contain 8–64 instances within the same mechanical Axis and 4 m cell. Textures, transparency, custom materials, nonuniform instance transforms, Control Groups, projectors/gobos and unsupported backends retain individual lenses.
Native main-thread glue packs completed physical values directly into reusable transform/INSTANCE_CUSTOM buffers and calls multimesh_set_buffer once per changed batch. Original lens nodes, picking children and source materials remain available for fallback; only duplicate draw layers are suppressed. Renderer/policy/resource changes revoke routes, free actual MultiMesh nodes and replay held state without another packet.
The individual lens RID binding also now writes Godot’s internal emission_energy shader uniform; the public BaseMaterial property name previously used there was ignored by the shader.

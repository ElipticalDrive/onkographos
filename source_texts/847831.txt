Part of #71
Question
What residency, prefetch and eviction policy keeps the client's working set inside its budgets while guaranteeing that turning, flying or zooming never reveals a hole or a stale Edit?
Cover at least
Tiers: VRAM, RAM and the ~2 GB disk cache; what lives in each; how the working set is sized from ring geometry rather than guessed.
The always-resident coarse LOD: how coarse it must be to cover the horizon cap at every Zoom level, what it costs, and whether it should be cached on disk to hit the ≤ 5 s orbit target.
Eviction: distance and direction hysteresis, LRU versus distance, pinning edited bricks, persistence before eviction, and what may never be evicted.
Prefetch: cone width, lead time versus generation and upload latency, velocity extrapolation at 50 m/s, angular-velocity widening when the player looks around, Zoom-out prediction, cancellation, and how prefetch yields to visible work.
Edit invalidation: how an Edit propagates to resident and prefetched levels without a visible stale frame.
Metrics: resident bytes, load and evict counts, prefetch hit rate, and hitch percentiles.
Output
Same shape: a ranked shortlist, win ranges (hitches and bytes), cost, risk, fidelity impact, dependencies, and an experiment. Note what changes in #49.

Research: residency, prefetch and eviction — findings
Hold a complete 360° shell, not a frustum. At 180°/s the horizon sweeps at ~800 m/s, 16× the 50 m/s flight speed; no prefetch outruns a turn. Residency covers every azimuth and the view direction only orders refinement. That is what makes turning safe, and it is the one finding the rest of the policy hangs on.
The working set is small; generation is the bottleneck. ~27–52 MB of bricks (k = 1; 53–105 MB at k = 2) at one Voxel per output pixel, peaking at 50–100 m altitude, plus a 20 MB always-resident coarse guard (levels 8–13) that covers orbit. Horizontal churn at 50 m/s is ~37k bricks/s (74k at k = 2) against ~130k/s on eight cores at the generator rate inferred from planet-gen.
Sizing is arithmetic from the ring rule, not a guess. r0 = 0.1 m / (70°/1440) = 118 m; level L has Voxels 0.1·2^L m and bricks 0.8·2^L m, and ring L spans [r0·2^(L−1), r0·2^L] so the error is ≤ 1 output pixel. The note gives the spherical-cap area formula and the table from ground to 50 R; a 2 GB disk cache holds ~10 M bricks, ~40× the live set.
Prefetch 1–2 s by velocity, with a 20 m vertical margin. Horizontal flight is steady; a 50 m/s vertical take-off moves the horizon at ~4.5 km/s and needs ~55k new bricks in the first 0.16 s. The margin, generated while the player stands still, removes the spike for ~19 MB.
Evict by distance band, then LRU inside it. Cesium's exact pattern: LRU only among unselected tiles, maximumCacheOverflowBytes to meet the fidelity target, never a hole. Add 1.25× / 0.5 s hysteresis, and pin the required set, the guard, in-flight requests and the live-Edit chain.
Edits: journal first, epoch-stamped, patch before submit. A 2 m Brush is ~370 bricks / ~75 KB across every level; zero stale frames if each resident level is patched before the frame and prefetched bricks built under an older epoch are discarded. Never cache edited bricks on disk; the journal is the source of truth (ADR 0003).
What changes in Streaming generation #49: "nearest and in-view first" → "nearest first, all azimuths"; per-level sparse Scenes instead of one Scene/BrickStore pyramid; Generate bricks directly at any LOD level #47 as a hard dependency; guard-first startup; the residency/prefetch/eviction contract plus zero hole/stale/soft-miss counters. Reword "a crater visible from orbit": a 2 m crater is sub-pixel on a 16 km Planet.
Full note: https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/bhayanakvoxel/blob/main/docs/research/residency-prefetch-eviction.md
Confidence. Medium-high on the architectural guarantee (complete shell) and the Edit ordering; medium on the byte ranges (±30%) and the 37k bricks/s churn; low-medium on the take-off spike and the disk-cache win, which depend on the dev machine's measured generator throughput and the real Planet's relief. No GPU was available; every number is arithmetic or an estimate marked as such.
Open questions.
The dev machine's core count and the real generator's bricks/s/core (the 16k/s/core is inferred; at 8k everything doubles).
The 16 km Planet's relief and caves; k = 2 is an assumption, and caves push it toward 3.
Whether Research: LOD selection and transition fidelity #73 centres rings on the camera or the ground point — the turn guarantee depends on keeping all azimuths.
Radiance-cache re-warm cost on a coarse→fine swap, which makes hysteresis more valuable than the byte maths suggests.
Whether the guard needs a near-threshold mask to stay exact for sub-voxel Edits, and how the background refresh keeps up with far Edits from 64 players.
Disk-cache format and async read path (optional for M1a; generation is fast enough without it).

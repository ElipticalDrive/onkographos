The techniques follow George Kopanas' write-up "What the 3DGS Literature Left on the Table" (https://grgkopanas.github.io/3dgs_cuda_blog/): warp reduce-scatter with two-splat folding, SH streaming, contributor compaction with touched gating, branchless evaluation, and 8x8 quadrants with two pixels per thread. Thanks for publishing the steps and the negative results in such detail. This PR implements them for the fastgs kernels and their extra outputs (depth, normals, densification, edge and impact scores, color variance).
Restructures the fastgs blend kernels so most per-pixel work becomes per-column or per-warp work. The training step (forward, backward and fused Adam) is 17 % faster (median; 13 to 22 % per scene) on the seven local Mip-NeRF 360 scenes at unchanged quality.
Backward blend
Each warp owns one 8x8 quadrant and each thread two vertically adjacent pixels of one column. Per splat, the dx-free sums (h, h·dy, h·dy², color, depth, normal, densification, edge and impact) are reduced over the column first; the mean and conic terms that need dx are formed once per column; then everything is reduced across columns with a halving reduce-scatter, so the totals end in distinct lanes and leave in one atomic instruction.
Two consecutive splats share the first exchange (one per half-warp).
The walk is barrier-free: each warp stages only the splats the forward pass recorded for its regions in its own 32-entry slice.
The kernel fits in registers without spilling.
Transmittance is recovered with one approximate reciprocal per pixel.
Forward blend
The same quadrant geometry; both rows of a thread share one column evaluation (two FMAs, one exp2, one multiply per row).
The overlap mask of each splat's 8x4 regions is computed once when it is staged instead of by every warp.
Splats that no pixel blends are compacted out of the tile list in place; the coverage bits let backward skip them per warp.
Touched flags are set by the backward walk instead of forward atomics.
Also
48-byte projected record (center, conic, opacity, color in three float4 loads).
Two-level stable depth/tile sort for large frames, packed keys for small ones.
SH coefficients are streamed during color evaluation instead of decoded into a 45-float array.
Behavior
The ±1e4 safety clamp on per-warp gradient sums now groups the pixels of an 8x8 quadrant instead of two 8x4 regions eight rows apart. Results differ only when such a partial sum exceeds 1e4.
Acceptance at the 1/255 alpha threshold can differ in the last bit from the previous evaluation order.
Validation (RTX 4090, frozen 30k MRNF checkpoints of the seven local Mip-NeRF 360 scenes)

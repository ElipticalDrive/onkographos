Branch: frontier-voxel-engine · Tracking: #24 · Architecture: https://github.com/DrewPhi/MicroVoxelProceduralGeneration/blob/frontier-voxel-engine/frontier/ARCHITECTURE.md
Task and acceptance
Phase: Optimization | Dependencies: FV15/FV19/FV22/FV25 | Gate: G4.
Profile GPU draw submission, patch/volume visibility, overdraw and occlusion before adding an advanced path. Prototype frustum/conservative Hi-Z culling, GPU indirect/meshlet cluster rendering with fallback on wgpu hardware lacking advanced features, and batched instance draw for forests. Occlusion must never hide a terrain region based on sampled bounds falsely claimed solid. Separate CPU and GPU costs and test high slopes, caves, occluders, teleport and rapid camera rotations.
Acceptance: measured improvement on representative mountain/forest/arch benchmark; if neutral or worse, reject optimization; zero false-cull holes and correct fallback.
Legacy reference policy
G/B — read main/successor/viewer/src/visibility.rs and agent_context/OCTREE_DAG_STUDY.md for prior unsafe-solid metadata and performance lessons; no automatic transplant. Aokana https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02017 for GPU-driven tile culling ideas.
Guardrail: isolated frontier/; preserve original branches and saves; attach test evidence and benchmarks before closing the issue.

Out-of-core aware multi-GPU rendering for large-scale scene ...
Feb 1, 2026Abstract We introduce an out-of-core method for multi-GPU path tracing of large-scale scenes, leveraging memory access analysis to optimize data distribution. Our approach enables efficient rendering on multi-GPU systems even when the combined GPU memory is smaller than the total scene size.

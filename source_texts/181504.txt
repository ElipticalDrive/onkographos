Dual Contouring | libfive/libfive | DeepWiki
Apr 28, 2025Dual Contouring in libfive provides an efficient and feature-preserving approach to converting implicit functions into triangle meshes. Its adaptive octree decomposition and QEF-based vertex placement allow it to produce high-quality meshes with fewer triangles compared to simpler algorithms.

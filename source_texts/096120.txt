This document summarizes image enhancement techniques in the frequency domain. It introduces Fourier analysis and the Fourier transform, which represents an image as a sum of sine and cosine functions. Filtering can be performed by multiplying the Fourier transform of an image by a filter function. Low-pass filtering removes high-frequency content, reducing noise but also causing ringing artifacts. Gaussian and Butterworth filters are better alternatives to ideal low-pass filters as they reduce ringing due to their monotonic spatial profiles.

This document summarizes image enhancement techniques in the frequency domain. It introduces Fourier analysis and the Fourier transform, which represents an image as a sum of sine and cosine functions. Filtering can be performed by multiplying the Fourier transform of an image by a filter function. Low-pass filtering removes high-frequency content, reducing noise but also causing ringing artifacts.

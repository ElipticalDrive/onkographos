Overview
Add volumetric fog with light scattering for atmospheric effects.
Details
Ray-marched volumetric fog in a post-process or compute pass
Per-light volumetric scattering (god rays / light shafts)
Configurable density, scattering coefficients, and height falloff
Temporal reprojection for performance (half/quarter resolution)
Integration with existing shadow maps for shadowed fog
Motivation
Adds atmospheric depth and mood to scenes. Volumetric effects are a standard feature in modern renderers for outdoor and indoor environments.

From #353
Is your feature request related to a problem? Please describe
I looked at the material database and realized that skin is listed, so I checked the demo and the skin materials don't render subsurface scattering, which is an important part of creating realistic skin in three.js in a raytracer.
I have a couple projects that also could benefit from an implementation of subsurface scattering, mainly a face generator that I'm working on. Supporting something like this could make the this webgl renderer stand out, since I spent a few hours looking and I haven't managed to find a single one that supports this using webgl (◞‸◟；)
Describe the solution you'd like
I looked into solving the problem and I quickly realized that I wouldn't know how to do it, but I think this may be a helpful starting point (maybe) if you do decide to try to implement the feature 🥺

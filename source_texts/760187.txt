Abstract
Aiming at the problem of limited perception of unmanned equipment in denial
scenes, this paper proposes a technical framework of scene reconstruction and
target recognition based on 3DGS (3D Gaussian Splatting), which realizes efficient
modeling and accurate representation of static denial scenes. Specifically, the
semantic attributes are extended in the 3D Gaussian primitives and the feature
index mechanism is introduced to improve the semantic modeling ability of 3DGS
in the case of low memory occupation, and the target recognition is carried out on
this basis. Experiments show that the framework can effectively meet the actual
functional requirements of scene reconstruction and target recognition for
unmanned equipment in static denial scenarios.
Keywords
Denial scene; 3D Gaussian Splatting; Scene Reconstruction; Target Recognition
1. Introduction
DOI:10.63313/JCSFT.2004
Static denial scenarios usually refer to complex environments with adverse
conditions such as limited communication, GPS denial, strong electromagnetic
interference, and occlusion. In such scenarios, the effectiveness of traditional
positioning navigation and sensing technologies is often difficult to guarantee. In the
fields of battlefield reconnaissance, autonomous navigation of unmanned platforms,
emergency rescue, etc., the environmental perception ability of unmanned
equipment is the premise for its subsequent tasks such as target recognition.
Therefore, unmanned equipment needs a scheme that can overcome the
interference of multi-factor rejection scenarios and achieve accurate perception of
the environment.
The traditional three-dimensional reconstruction technology based on motion
recovery structure (SfM) and simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) is
faced with bottlenecks such as accuracy degradation, insufficient real-time
performance, and poor robustness in the rejection environment. Weak texture

regions and occlusion can easily lead to incomplete reconstruction geometry, and
complex interference can cause camera pose estimation drift, which is difficult to
support subsequent tasks such as target recognition. Although the neural radiation
field[1-5](NeRF) achieves high-quality new perspective synthesis, its computational
cost is high and the implicit representation is difficult to edit directly, which cannot
meet the strict requirements of the rejection scene for real-time and flexibility. As a
new scene representation technology proposed in 2023, 3D Gaussian splashing
takes the differentiable 3D Gaussian primitive as the core unit and achieves a great
breakthrough through parallel rasterization rendering. While ensuring the visual
fidelity close to Nerf, the frame rate can reach more than 100 FPS, and the training
speed is 10-100 times higher than that of Nerf.Moreover, the model storage
requirement is only MB level, which perfectly adapts to the resource-constrained
characteristics of the rejection scene. Compared with traditional methods, the
explicit representation of 3DGS naturally supports occlusion robustness processing
and target-level scene understanding. It can achieve accurate segmentation of object
instances and categories through Gaussian primitive grouping, which provides a
new technical paradigm for static rejection scene reconstruction and target
recognition.
Based on the actual needs of static denial scenarios, this study focuses on three
aspects of research with 3DGS as the core technology carrier : First, build a 3DGS
static scene reconstruction framework for denial environments ; secondly, the
target recognition algorithm based on 3DGS Gaussian feature is designed. The third
is to build a rejection scene experimental verification system, compare and analyze
the performance differences between the proposed method and the traditional
technology, and verify its feasibility and superiority in the rejection scene. This
study is expected to provide core technical support for autonomous sensing of
unmanned platforms in denial environments, and promote the application of 3DGS
from general scenarios to complex scenarios, which has important theoretical
research value and engineering application significance.
2. Related Work
2.1. Denial Scene Reconstruction Method
DOI:10.63313/JCSFT.2004
At present, the mainstream technologies of environment scene modeling are mainly
divided into three categories : LiDAR point cloud reconstruction, Multi-View Stereo
(MVS) and voxel reconstruction.
Laser radar point cloud reconstruction technology obtains three-dimensional point
cloud data of the scene through laser ranging, which has the advantages of high
geometric accuracy and strong anti-light interference ability. However, it has the
problems of high equipment cost, sparse point cloud and lack of texture information.
Point cloud fracture is easy to occur in dense occlusion areas, resulting in
incomplete scene reconstruction. MVS technology realizes three-dimensional
87
Journal of Computer Science and Frontier Technologies

reconstruction based on feature matching of multi-view images. It does not require
complex hardware equipment and can restore scene texture information. It is
suitable for small and medium-scale rejection scene modeling. However, this
technology has strong dependence on image texture, and is prone to feature
matching failure in low texture rejection areas, and the reconstruction speed is slow
and the perspective generalization ability is poor. Voxel reconstruction constructs a
voxel model by fusing multi-view depth information, which can achieve dense
reconstruction of the scene. However, there is a trade-off between voxel resolution
and reconstruction efficiency. Memory overflow is easy to occur in large-scale
rejection scenes, and the ability to restore scene details is limited. In general, the
traditional scene modeling technology still has obvious shortcomings in the
occlusion adaptability, detail restoration accuracy and real-time performance of
static rejection scenes, which is difficult to meet the actual needs of unmanned
equipment.
2.2. 3DGS
Instead of traditional voxels, grids, neural radiation fields, etc., 3D Gaussian
splashing starts from a sparse point cloud set composed of Gaussian primitives to
perform novel view synthesis and optimize scene representation. Through explicit
anisotropic Gaussian primitive modeling and differential rasterization rendering, 3D
Gaussian splashing achieves efficient and high-fidelity 3D scene reconstruction. The
principle of 3DGS algorithm is shown in Fig.1.
Fig 1. Principle diagram of the 3DGS algorithm.
Compared with the traditional implicit representation methods (such as NeRF),
3DGS has three core advantages : First, the geometric accuracy is higher, and the
explicit Gaussian primitives can accurately capture the detailed features of the scene
and avoid the blur effect in implicit modeling. Second, the rendering speed is faster,
and the tile rasterization technology can achieve millisecond-level scene rendering,
far exceeding the rendering efficiency of NeRF ; third, the scene is highly editable.
Through the addition, deletion and adjustment of Gaussian primitives, the local
modification and semantic association of the scene can be realized, which is very
suitable for the high-precision modeling requirements of static rejection scenes.
Since the introduction of 3DGS, domestic and foreign scholars have carried out a lot

many deficiencies in the rejection scene : First, the occlusion adaptability is poor. In

prone to distortion and overlap, resulting in the geometric blur of the scene edge
and the incomplete reconstruction. The second is the lack of semantic association
ability. Native 3DGS can only restore the appearance and geometric information of
the scene, and cannot realize the semantic distinction between the foreground
target and the background environment, so it is difficult to support the subsequent
target recognition task. Third, the robustness of low-texture regions is insufficient.
Rejecting a large number of low-texture regions in the scene can easily lead to
initialization failure of Gaussian primitives and reconstruction holes[6-9].
2.3. Target recognition technology based on 3DGS
At present, three-dimensional target recognition technology is mainly divided into
three categories : target detection based on 2D image, 3D target detection based on
point cloud and target recognition based on explicit three-dimensional
representation. Among them, target recognition based on explicit representation
such as 3DGS is a research hotspot in recent years. The 3D object recognition
technology based on 3DGS has many shortcomings in static rejection scenes. First,
there is a lack of dedicated algorithms. The existing research focuses on general
urban scenes, and rarely designs exclusive recognition algorithms for occlusion, low
texture, and strong interference characteristics of rejection scenes[10]. Secondly,
there is a separation between reconstruction and recognition. Most methods divide
scene reconstruction and target recognition into two independent stages. Gaussian
primitives do not bind target category attributes, resulting in low recognition
efficiency and poor accuracy. Third, the recognition ability of occluded targets and
small targets is weak, and the Gaussian characteristics of a large number of occluded
targets and small-size targets in the scene are not obvious, which is prone to missed
detection. Fourth, the background interference is serious. The complex background
of the static rejection scene will produce a large number of redundant Gaussian
features, which will interfere with the recognition accuracy of the foreground target.
3. Method

In order to solve the problem of insufficient semantic modeling ability of 3DGS
technology, the method in this paper improves 3DGS technology. By expanding the
feature attributes of Gaussian primitives and introducing the feature sharing
mechanism, the low-dimensional semantic index is assigned to 3D Gaussian
primitives and the feature library is associated, which effectively avoids the problem
of memory surge caused by high-dimensional semantic information directly binding
Gaussian primitives. At the same time, through the collaborative optimization of
semantic and geometric parameters, the core problems such as semantic

discontinuity and rendering distortion in the pipeline rendering mode are solved.
The above improvements provide key basic support for the semantic consistency of
unmanned equipment scenes, the low resource adaptability of unmanned
equipment, and the robustness of scene occlusion.
3.1. Establish a global feature index library
DOI:10.63313/JCSFT.2004
Extract global features. By inputting multi-view RGB images, the pre-trained 2D
semantic segmentation model is used to extract global semantic features, and the
scene-level description information is associated through the context coding module
to construct a multi-dimensional semantic feature set. The principal component
analysis method is used to reduce the extracted high-dimensional semantic features
to a unified dimension, and the semantic features after dimensionality reduction are
transformed into integer coding by product quantization, segmented into
non-overlapping sub-vectors, and coded into the nearest cluster center index in the
sub-vector space, thereby compressing the feature storage volume and reducing
memory usage.
Establish a hierarchical index structure. The global feature index library adopts a
three-level hierarchical structure to reduce the complexity of retrieval space. From
top to bottom, the first layer is divided into urban buildings, mountain jungles,
wetland shoals, tunnel systems, etc.according to the scene categories involved in the
complex near-ground environment. The second layer is divided according to the
target category in the scene under the specific scene category. The third layer is
divided according to the local detail attributes for each target category.
In the retrieval process, the query first selects the corresponding child nodes
according to the scene prior in the first layer, and then narrows the search range
step by step in the second and third layers. There are N semantic features in the
index library, and the division of each layer is relatively balanced. The average
branch factor of each layer is b, and the index tree depth is about. Therefore,
compared with the linear traversal of all features, the hierarchical index structure
reduces the retrieval complexity from O(N) to O( log ( N ) ), which significantly
improves the query efficiency under the large-scale semantic feature library.
Index library update strategy. In order to adapt to the dynamic changes of the
complex near-ground environment, the global feature index library designs
incremental updating and redundant feature cleaning strategies. When a new frame
of data is input, the similarity between the new input semantic feature vector and
the existing features in the feature library is compared. If it is lower than the preset
thresholdτ= 0.85, it is determined as a new semantic feature, which is added to the
corresponding level, and the index structure is updated to ensure that the index
library completely covers the scene semantic information. The redundant features
that have not been queried for a long time are periodically cleaned up. If the number
of queries of a feature in a continuous T time period is lower than the thresholdτ1
= 1, and the corresponding semantic index is not effectively referenced by Gaussian

3.2. Establish feature sharing mechanism
The multi-view RGB image is input, and the lightweight pre-trained 2D semantic
segmentation model is used to generate pixel semantic labels to provide 2D
semantic reference for the projection area of Gaussian primitives. The 3D
Gaussian primitives are projected into the 2D image through the camera intrinsic
parameters. If the projection coverage area is consistent with the pixel semantic
label, the high-dimensional features corresponding to the label are extracted, and
the geometric parameters of the Gaussian primitives are combined for
verification to filter out mismatches. Finally, the cosine similarity between the
matched high-dimensional features and the global feature index library is
calculated. If the similarity is higher than the threshold, the position of the
feature in the index library is determined. After feature matching, the
low-dimensional index corresponding to each 3D Gaussian point is bound to the
feature library to achieve decoupling storage with high-dimensional features. The
final definition of 3D Gaussian primitives is changed as follows :

3.4. Target recognition function design
Target candidate region generation based on global feature index. Based on the
global feature index library constructed above, the target candidate region
generation strategy is designed to quickly screen out the regions containing potential
targets, narrow the recognition range, and improve the recognition efficiency and
accuracy. The candidate region generation is mainly divided into two steps : one is
global feature retrieval, and the other is candidate region clustering and screening. In
the global feature retrieval stage, using the fast retrieval ability of the global feature
index library, based on the target prior feature, the K-nearest neighbor retrieval
algorithm is used to quickly match the Gaussian primitives with the target prior
feature similarity higher than the set threshold from the index library, which is used
as the potential target feature element. The prior features of the target here are
obtained through the training of the target sample library, covering the geometric
features and semantic features of the typical static rejection scene targets to ensure
the pertinence of the retrieval.In the candidate region clustering and screening stage,
the spatial clustering of the potential target feature elements obtained by the
retrieval is carried out, and the density clustering algorithm is used. According to the
spatial location, scale information and semantic label of the Gaussian primitives, the
spatial adjacent and semantic consistent Gaussian primitives are clustered into one
class, each class corresponds to a potential target candidate region. At the same time,
combined with the Gaussian geometric constraints obtained in the process of
geometric semantic joint optimization, the candidate regions are screened : the
candidate regions with too small volume and geometric shape that do not conform to
the target prior are eliminated, the candidate regions that do not match the semantic
label and the target category are eliminated, and the effective candidate regions that
conform to the target features are retained. Through this step, the recognition range
can be quickly narrowed, a large amount of background redundancy can be
eliminated, and accurate regional support can be provided for subsequent target
recognition. At the same time, relying on the fast retrieval ability of global feature
index, the real-time performance of candidate region generation can be ensured.
Target feature extraction based on feature sharing and joint optimization
features. Based on the feature sharing mechanism established above, the 3DGS
Gaussian primitive features after joint optimization of geometry and semantics are
reused, without re-extraction of features, which effectively reduces the
computational complexity. At the same time, combined with the geometric and

semantic information after joint optimization, the robustness of feature extraction is
improved, and the low texture and occlusion characteristics of static rejection scenes
are adapted. The target feature extraction adopts the dual feature fusion strategy of '
geometric feature + semantic feature '. The specific implementation process is as
follows: Firstly, for each target candidate region obtained by screening, the geometric
features of all Gaussian primitives in the region are extracted, including the position
coordinates, scale parameters, rotation angle and curvature information of Gaussian
primitives. After the joint optimization of geometric and semantic features, these
geometric features have effectively suppressed the feature distortion caused by
occlusion and low texture, and can accurately reflect the three-dimensional
geometric shape of the target.Secondly, the semantic features of Gaussian primitives
in the region are extracted, and the semantic tags bound in the joint optimization
process are reused. At the same time, combined with the feature sharing mechanism,
the semantic feature vectors stored in the global feature index library are called to
enrich the target semantic information. Finally, through the feature fusion network,
the geometric features and semantic features are weighted and fused to obtain the
target joint feature vector with both geometric recognition and semantic
discrimination, which is used as the input of subsequent target matching and
classification.
Target matching, classification and pose estimation. Based on the extracted
target joint feature vector, combined with the military target sample library, the
target matching, classification and pose estimation are completed, and the accurate
recognition of the target in the static rejection scene is realized. It is specifically
divided into three links : target matching, category classification, and pose
estimation.
In the target matching process, the feature similarity matching algorithm is used to
compare the extracted target joint feature vector with the standard target feature
vector in the military target sample library. The target sample library contains
standard feature vectors of typical military targets. These sample features are
processed by the same feature extraction process to ensure the consistency of
matching. By calculating the cosine similarity between the features of the target to be
identified and the features of the standard sample, the similarity threshold is set.
When the similarity is higher than the threshold, it is determined that the matching is
successful, and the target category is preliminarily determined. When the similarity is
lower than the threshold, it is determined as a non-target or unknown target, which
is included in the subsequent post-processing screening. At the same time, relying on
the fast matching ability of the global feature index library, the matching process is
optimized, the matching efficiency is improved, and the end-side real-time
recognition requirements are met.
In the category classification step, based on the matching results, combined with the
confidence of semantic features, the lightweight classification network is used to

joint feature vector, and the output is the target category label and classification
confidence. By setting the confidence threshold, the target whose classification
confidence is lower than the threshold is eliminated, and the false detection rate is

features are similar in the static rejection scene, the geometric feature constraint is
introduced, and the information such as the geometric size and contour shape of the
target is combined to assist in the category classification and improve the

In the aspect of attitude estimation, based on the geometric features ( position,
rotation angle, scale ) of Gaussian primitives in the target candidate region, combined
with the spatial constraint relationship after joint optimization of geometric
semantics, the least square method is used to estimate the three-dimensional attitude
of the target, and the attitude parameters such as pitch angle, yaw angle and roll
angle of the target are obtained. Through attitude estimation, the spatial attitude
information of the target in the static denial scene can be accurately obtained, which
provides support for the subsequent path planning and combat decision-making of
unmanned equipment. Aiming at the problem of pose estimation deviation caused by
occlusion, the Gaussian feature after feature completion is used to correct the pose
parameters by combining the prior pose information of the target, so as to improve
the accuracy and stability of pose estimation.
Post-processing optimization of recognition results. Due to the strong
interference, occlusion, low texture and other problems in the static rejection scene,
there may still be false detection, missed detection and attitude estimation deviation
in the target recognition process. Therefore, it is necessary to design a
post-processing optimization strategy to correct and optimize the recognition results,
so as to improve the final accuracy and reliability of target recognition.
The post-processing optimization mainly includes three core steps : First, the false
detection is eliminated. For the recognition results that the classification confidence
is lower than the set threshold, the geometric shape does not meet the target prior,
and the semantic label is contradictory, the false detection target is determined and
eliminated ; at the same time, using the characteristics of the static scene, the
consistency check of the recognition results of adjacent frames ( if it is a sequence
scene ) is performed to eliminate the false detection targets that are inconsistent
between frames. The second is missed detection compensation. Aiming at the
problem of missed detection caused by serious occlusion and unclear features,
combined with the global feature index library, the suspicious areas removed in the
candidate region screening process are re-retrieved and identified. At the same time,
the feature sharing mechanism is used to reuse the feature information of the
surrounding area to complete the feature of the suspicious area, try to identify the
occluded target and reduce the missed detection rate. The third is attitude correction.

4. Conclusion
The technical framework of rejection scene reconstruction and target recognition
based on 3DGS is proposed in this paper. Based on 3DGS technology, the semantic
attributes of 3D Gaussian primitives are expanded, the feature sharing mechanism is
established, and the semantic-geometric joint optimization is performed to achieve
the semantic modeling effect. Finally, the target recognition task is completed on this
basis. Experiments show that the proposed method can make up for the
shortcomings of traditional methods, such as insufficient reconstruction accuracy
and poor stability of target recognition in rejection scenarios, and effectively improve
the environmental perception ability and task execution efficiency of unmanned
equipment in rejection scenarios.
Although this study has achieved ideal results in the experiment, there is still room
for further improvement. The follow-up work will introduce data sets that are more
suitable for denial scenarios, and focus on the optimization of training time and
resource consumption in large-scale denial scenarios.

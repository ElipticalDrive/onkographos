Adds a non-destructive, research-only LongSplat backend to Stage 4.5, without embedding NVIDIA's non-commercial source. Provides: full-upright-video frame prep; upstream train/render; Gaussian PLY for free-view rendering; raw depth + calibrated-camera-derived point-cloud candidate; held-out visual PSNR audit; optional Sim3 registration with >=4 fit and >=2 independent holdout survey anchors before Stage 8 review. Keeps Stage 8/9 unchanged and explicitly refuses to label a Gaussian PLY as metric scan truth. All phase outputs have fail-closed receipts and maintain source/checkpoints. Offline: 8 targeted tests passed; no LongSplat GPU training or actual 96-second reconstruction has been executed/verified. Read repository/docs/longsplat_thesis_integration.md before merging. Do not merge into active R4 notebook automatically; its packaged runtime is a different codebase.

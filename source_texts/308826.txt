One billion points in the cloud - an octree for ... - ScienceDirect
For achieving our goal to process 1 billion points in main memory on a standard computer, we have implemented an efficient data structure for 3D point clouds. In addition to the octree, this paper presents several algorithms exploiting the properties of our data structure.

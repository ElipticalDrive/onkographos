Surface meshes sat half a voxel outside the traces. Surface.generateTrimesh rounded points onto voxel centers and skimage's polygon counts a center on the outline as inside. Voxels now span xmin + i*vres to xmin + (i+1)*vres and fill when their center is inside.
At the default XY resolution, 3D ▸ Export quantitative data volumes drop 4 to 18% and areas 4 to 17% on my test shapes. A sphere 10 voxels in radius went from 1.116 to 0.996 of its volume and 1.086 to 1.006 of its area. Sharp corners now read a little low (a small triangle 0.90), from marching cubes.
9 of 10 new tests fail with the fix reverted. Full suite: 8327 passed, 37 skipped, 6 xfailed.

🎯 Functional Correctness | 🟡 Minor | ⚡ Quick win
Do not round half-voxel-shifted coordinates in the fallback.
With xmin=ymin=0, vres=1, and shape=(2, 2), a square from (1, 1) to (1.25, 1.25) contains no voxel center. The fallback rounds its shifted coordinates 0.5 and 0.75 to 0 and 1. polygon then fills four voxels instead of one. NumPy rounds half-integers to even values, and polygon includes polygon vertices. (numpy.org)
This state occurs when another section establishes the object minimum at (0, 0). The fallback can therefore expand small traces into adjacent cells and distort mesh measurements.
Use the unshifted coordinates to preserve the previous fallback behavior, or map outline points to their containing cells with floor. Add a regression case with the small trace away from the object minimum.

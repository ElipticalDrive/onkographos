Summary
Implement the INTERFACES section 6 NormalShading policy for the first CPU and GPU images, including hit surface data from surface_at.
Acceptance criteria
surface_at reconstructs sphere and barycentric triangle hit positions, returns the material ID, and provides a normalized geometric normal face-forwarded against the incident ray.
NormalShading maps hit normals to linear RGB using 0.5 * (ng + 1), preserves the hit primitive ID, and sets light_mask to zero.
A miss returns the scene background, RT_MISS_PRIM, and zero light_mask without accessing geometry.
Tests cover sphere and triangle surface data, normal color mapping and face-forwarding, and miss behavior.
This completes Arek's NormalShading deliverable listed in docs/members/AREK.md and supports the first-image task in docs/ROADMAP.md.

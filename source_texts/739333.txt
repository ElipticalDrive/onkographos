Currently, chunks are loaded based on a single camera or rigid system. To support large worlds without tying loading/unloading strictly to the camera, we propose using “chunk loaders,” or focal points, to manage which chunks should be active. This approach allows multiple focus positions (e.g., players, important NPCs) to trigger chunk loading, rather than relying solely on camera location.
Proposed Change
Focal Points
Introduce a concept of “chunk loaders” or “focal points” in the world. These can be player positions, AI entities, or anything that needs local chunks loaded.
Each loader defines a loading radius (in chunks) where chunks should remain active.
ChunkManager Updates
Modify the existing World or ChunkManager to gather all focal points, and load/unload chunks based on their combined coverage.
Unload chunks that no longer fall within any loader’s radius.
Render Culling
Keep rendering decisions separate: the renderer sees only chunks that are loaded, then frustum-culls them to avoid drawing off-screen chunks.
This prevents the camera from dictating which chunks exist in memory; it only decides which ones are drawn.

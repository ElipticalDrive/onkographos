Closes #119.
What changed
Builds generation-tagged regular and Transvoxel transition meshlets within the 64-vertex / 96-triangle contract without welding source attributes.
Atomically publishes bounded double-bank meshlet descriptors and conservative bounds alongside page surfaces.
GPU-culls resident meshlets by generation, frustum, and normal cone into bounded compact indexed-indirect streams with explicit overflow, stale, and rejection counters.
Adds truthful material, meshlet, resident-page, LOD, transition-seam, and normal debug views to planet_voxel_demo; F2 switches page/meshlet paths and F3 cycles views.
Keeps page-indexed rendering as the default because the matched benchmark does not support meshlet promotion yet.
Adds unattended --benchmark publication and steady-state measurements plus CPU/GPU parity, overflow, stale-generation, WGSL-layout, and randomized conservative-culling tests.
Preserves generic virtual geometry and the retained voxel_demo / voxel_demo_raymarch paths.
Measured evidence
RTX 3060 / Vulkan, bounded 64-page fixture:
Initial extraction, copy, meshlet build, and atomic publication full-pass GPU p50/p95: 1.568768 / 1.920000 ms.
Page-indexed steady-state GPU p50/p95: 0.012288 / 0.014336 ms.
Meshlet steady-state GPU p50/p95: 0.079872 / 0.088064 ms.
6,272 resident meshlets compacted to 5,204 draws; 1,068 frustum rejects; zero stale, overflow, or invalid candidates.
This result is intentionally treated as negative promotion evidence: the page path remains the default and benchmark baseline, while meshlets remain directly selectable for validation and debugging.

Rendering realistic clouds has always been a desired feature for a variety of applications, from computer games to ﬂight simulators. Clouds consist of innumerable tiny water droplets that scatter light. Rendering clouds is challenging because photons are typically scattered multiple times before they leave the cloud. Despite the impressive performance of today’s GPUs, accurately modeling multiple scattering eﬀects is prohibitively expensive, even for oﬄine renderers. Thus, realtime methods rely on greatly simpliﬁed models.

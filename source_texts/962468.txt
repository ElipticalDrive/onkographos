Summary
Halves the trainer's Adam step, which brings Mip-NeRF 360 training time to about brush's. The 5-scene comparison in docs/rust_3dgs_plan.md is updated.
Why Adam: GSPLAT_TRAIN_PROFILE on brush-strategy bonsai at ~1M gaussians showed Adam at 18.5 ms/step, about 34% of a step and about 3x its memory-bandwidth floor.
The change: each thread now updates 4 consecutive elements through array<vec4<f32>> bindings.
Storage buffers in the renderer and trainer are allocated as 16-byte multiples so any of them can be bound that way.
The zero-gradient padding lanes update by exactly zero.
Adam: 18.5 → 9.2 ms/step.
Profiling: the former "adam" stage is split into stats / adam / noise (0.9 / 9.2 / 0.8 ms).

south-building varies from run to run. The same settings give 21.9–22.4 dB. The pre-change exe scored 22.44 and 22.38, and a control that pads buffers but keeps the scalar Adam scored 21.98. The vec4 change is therefore not what moves it.
Tried and reverted: per-subgroup shared-memory slots instead of the CAS float add in rasterize_backward. Backward went from 20.8 to 26–28 ms, so CAS contention was not the bottleneck.

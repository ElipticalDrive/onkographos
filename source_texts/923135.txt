Goal
Screen-space reflections show on the same surfaces as in the game, and the ambient occlusion matches the game's shape.
Gaps
The game's screen-space reflections also reach non-water surfaces, weighted by their specular mask, so wet or shiny ground mirrors the scene. OpenSky reflects on water only.
The game's ambient occlusion takes 5 samples, each weighted by its distance, then applies a contrast curve and a blend over time. It keeps snow brighter, and in fog it darkens before the fog is added. OpenSky uses its own sample count, radius, and strength, has no blend over time, and darkens the fogged colour.
Done when
Both are compared with the Windows reference game at the same view, and OpenSky follows the game's rule.

To guarantee the topological correctness of the isosurfaces extracted with the marching cube(MC)algorithm and to supply more valuable 3D reconstruction image,this paper makes exhaustive analysis of MC method and its ambiguity.By applying asymptotic method and sectional plane test method to resolve MC′s ambiguity and a kind of triangularization method,the improvement algorithm with C++ programmer on Personal computer is realized.The result of experiment proves that there is no holes and discontinuity phenomena in the image of 3D reconstruction with new MC algorithm.

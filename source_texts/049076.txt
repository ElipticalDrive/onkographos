What to build
Each nebula is a single flat quad that copies the camera's orientation every frame, so flying past one makes it pivot to follow you. There is no parallax, no interior depth, and only additive blending, so nebulae can brighten but never occlude.
Replace the billboarded plane with a back-face-rendered sphere whose fragment shader raymarches noise density. Step count comes from the quality tier, ray start is dithered to hide banding, and accumulation exits early once alpha saturates. Move from purely additive to alpha blending so dark dust lanes can genuinely occlude, and self-shadow density toward the nearest featured star so nebulae are lit by the same world everything else lives in.
The low tier keeps a layered-parallax-sprite fallback rather than a volume.
Acceptance criteria
Flying past a nebula produces real parallax rather than a swivelling card
Flying into a nebula surrounds the camera with it
Dark dust lanes are visible and occlude what is behind them
Nebulae are lit from the direction of nearby stars
Step count is tier-driven, with early ray termination and no visible banding
The low tier renders a layered-sprite fallback instead of a volume
Typecheck and build remain clean

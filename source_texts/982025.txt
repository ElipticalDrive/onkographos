Why
Indoors and in shade, the only fill light is the sky environment, which is treated as infinitely far away and blocked only by ambient occlusion. A dungeon corridor gets the same blue sky fill as an open field, and a red wall doesn't tint the floor next to it. Reflection probes exist but affect reflections only.
How other engines do it
Unity Light Probes (and the newer Adaptive Probe Volumes) store spherical harmonics on a grid, and objects sample it between probes.
Unreal uses a volumetric lightmap for baked lighting, and Lumen for fully dynamic GI.
DDGI (dynamic diffuse GI) keeps a grid of probes that are updated a few at a time each frame. It stores a depth per probe so light doesn't leak through walls. It can be fed by rasterised cube captures as well as ray tracing.
Proposed approach
Probe volumes: view.LightProbes(key, bounds, spacing). These are grids of probes covering an area, and several can be used (one per building or per dungeon level).
Capture: render a small cube map per probe (reusing the reflection probe capture), then project it to L2 spherical harmonics for irradiance and store the mean and variance of depth for visibility.
Update a budget of probes per frame, so time of day (HDRI skies, a physical sky that follows the sun, and day–night #15) and lights turning on (Point and spot lights with shadows #3) update gradually. A one-off capture is still possible on a loading screen.
In the lighting pass, sample the grid trilinearly, weighted by the visibility test (Chebyshev) and the normal, so light doesn't leak through thin walls. This replaces the sky environment's diffuse part inside volumes and blends to it at the edges.
Later: multiple bounces by feeding last frame's probes into the capture.
Pairs with the finer screen-space bounce in Screen-space indirect lighting #13.
Done when
A room with a window shows warm bounce from a sunlit floor, and a dark corridor is actually dark (before and after screenshots).
No obvious light leaking through a single-wall room.
The cost per frame is measured and documented.
rendering.md and design.md (environment lighting, known simplifications) are updated.

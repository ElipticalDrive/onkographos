In Kena's first cave (PPSA01802, scripts/kena/linux-reach-pulse.pad, after the Pulse), the distance-field AO chain produces almost no occlusion. Its bent normals come out near zero everywhere.
The chain in a RenderDoc capture of one cave frame (Linux/RADV, prosper-app):
0x50070a0000 clears the 400×225×9-float cone-visibility buffer;
0x50071e0000 cone-traces the four 128³ R16F global-distance-field clipmaps into that buffer;
0x5007210000 combines the cones into a 400×225 RGBA16F bent-normal image;
0x5009050000 and 0x5009070000 upsample and filter it into the 1600×900 history that the lighting composite 0x5008750000 samples.
What the buffers hold:
On main: after the clear, 1% of the buffer's elements are non-zero, and only at multiples of 64. The bent normals are exactly 0 in xyz, while w holds a valid depth.
The clear's pattern was a MUBUF format-store defect: buffer_store_format_xyzw through a one-component V# wrote four dwords per lane. The fix is in the linked PR.
With that fix: the clear writes every element (mean 0.508, mixing 0.003 and 1.0).
The cone trace then changes the buffer from mean 0.5081 to 0.5067, which is almost nothing.
The bent normals stay at length 0.003 (p5/p50/p95 all 0.003).
This capture's camera was perturbed by owner input, but that does not affect these buffer-level measurements.
Why it matters:
The scene is fully dynamic: GBufferC.a is 237 on the walls, which is material AO with no static lighting.
With no static lighting, DFAO is UE4's sky occlusion.
The cave is about 9× too bright against the PS5 oracle, and the excess is the two ambient passes 0x5009a60000 and 0x5008750000.
Skipping both as a diagnostic gives mean luminance 5.5 against the oracle's 3.5, down from 32.4.
Also relevant: in this run, early instances of 0x50071e0000, 0x5006cc0000, 0x5007210000, 0x5006d50000 and 0x5006d30000 were refused with unresolved-operand. 0x50071e0000's refusal is a sample_lz at pc 214 whose T# is unresolved. Steady-state dispatches do run.
Next step: dump the cone trace's inputs at its dispatch and check whether the distance-field samples it takes return plausible distances. 50% of the clipmap texels read exactly 0. Then check whether its stores are reached.

Paused (owner's call, 2026-10-09). Findings are recorded in KENA_STATUS.md (#4788).
Root cause, measured. The distance-field AO is empty because the mesh-SDF atlas is never uploaded.
The upload program 0x5008ba0000 writes the 512³ one-component R16_UINT atlas (tile mode 27).
Every one of its ~340 brick dispatches is skipped: expanded image exceeds the 512 MiB backend bound.
Integer 3D storage has no native typed path, so the atlas would take the 16-byte RGBA32_UINT interchange: 2 GiB.
Downstream, the clipmaps fill with zero boxes, the cone trace min-reduces to about 0.0035, and the bent normals have length 0.003.
Unmerged fix: kena/dfao-int3d-wip.
It gives integer formats 3D support bits and native R16ui/R32ui/R8ui 3D storage, with a test that fails under either half reverted.
All uploads then succeed, but the level load stalls. A likely reason, not measured: each brick dispatch re-materializes the whole 256 MiB atlas, because there is no GPU volume retile for 2-byte texels in mode 27.
Next step: keep the atlas resident on the GPU across the upload dispatches, either with a GPU volume retile for 2-byte texels in mode 27 or with a write-region-only writeback. Then re-check the bent normals.
Open: the reflection/sky composite still adds sky diffuse at near-zero AO, so the SH irradiance source (see #4768) may matter as much as the AO.

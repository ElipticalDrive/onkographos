Issue body actions
This deliverable lets Clang compile texture methods with a Status parameter and CheckAccessFullyMapped on their results, for DirectX and Vulkan. It covers Load on read-only, writable and multisampled textures, Sample, SampleBias, SampleGrad, SampleLevel, SampleCmp, SampleCmpBias, SampleCmpGrad, SampleCmpLevel, SampleCmpLevelZero, Gather, GatherCmp and their per-channel and four-offset forms, and GatherRaw. It adds partially mapped sparse textures to the offload test suite.
Scope
DirectX: the Status overloads lower to the DXIL texture operations with their status and CheckAccessFullyMapped, and set the TiledResources shader flag.
SPIR-V: the Status overloads lower to the OpImageSparse* instructions with the SparseResidency capability. The value in Status and the lowering of CheckAccessFullyMapped follow the amendment of proposal 0037 ([0037] Describe the CheckAccessFullyMapped and Status Overload #411).
SPIR-V: Load with a Status parameter on Buffer and RWBuffer, and on structured and byte-address buffers as the 0037 amendment defines.
The Status overloads in compute shaders with implicit derivatives, and with programmable offsets.
Offload test suite: a proposal and an implementation for reserved Texture2D, RWTexture2D and Depth32 textures with a per-tile mapping on DirectX and Vulkan; shared lit features for residency status and sparse textures, set on DirectX and Vulkan; tests of every method group, with partially mapped Texture2D cases where CheckAccessFullyMapped returns false.
Runtime cases retain meaningful data conversions, packing, raw bits, signedness and sparse data/status. Comparison half replicas are omitted only for an adopted Depth32/float-input/scalar-float interface; compiler half types/handles and distinct adopted conversions or representations remain covered. Gather aliases use representative examples rather than paired type/receiver matrices. Offset-status cases retain runtime-buffer nonzero offsets, distinct operands, comparison brackets and derivative invocations without redundant loop iterations.
Not in this deliverable
RasterizerOrderedTexture* with a Status parameter: [deliverable] Rasterizer-Ordered Textures #502.
vk::SampledTexture*: [scenario] Vulkan Sampled Textures #501.
The TiledResources flag for LOD clamps and the TiledResourcesTier report: position-0 one-offs.
Unmap and remap phases, packed mips, mip tails, sparse multisampled, array, cube and volume textures; DXIL library UsesDerivatives and RDAT; DXC changes; Metal.

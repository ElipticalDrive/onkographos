Voxel-based representation of 3D point clouds: Methods, applications ...
As voxelization is actually an over-segmentation of the point cloud, it can accelerate segmentation as a pre-clustering process. Moreover, the 3D grid created by voxels makes 3D convolutions, which were utilized by early deep learning methods for point cloud processing, applicable to unstructured point clouds.

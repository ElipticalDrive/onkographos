Summary
Connect the embodied 3D instructor avatar directly with blackboard content by calculating screen-space to 3D world projections for natural pointing and gaze tracking.
Details
Screen-to-World Coordinate Projection:
Given a blackboard element's 2D bounding box (x, y), compute the 3D raycast vector in the Three.js scene.
Two-Bone IK Arm Pointing:
Use Two-Bone Inverse Kinematics (IK) to orient the avatar's arm and pointing stick towards the active derivation or diagram.
Natural Gaze & Idle Motions:
Head tracking towards board elements during explanation, turning to look directly at the student when asking Socratic diagnostic questions.

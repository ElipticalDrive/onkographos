This site has a few voxel model packs for JFDuke (search for 'voxel'):
http://members.shaw.ca/k_saunders/dn3dtextures.html
If you want to generate your own voxel models, the easiest way is to convert them from polygonal models in the HRP. It would be most useful if you could provide batch files (the poly2vox commands) to run the conversion rather than the KVX models themselves. This way, it would be easy to maintain an up-to-date voxel pack. It would also allow you to make a very small distribution - simply provide the batch files, converter program, and a link to the HRP. You are welcome to include poly2vox.exe in your distribution (just be sure to provide a link to my site : ). Here are some old batch files for anyone who is interested in getting started:
pigcop_md2_to_kvx.bat:
```
@echo offrem /s.654 matches with scale .5 in defset gopts=poly2kvx pig.md2 /s.654 /p..\palette.dat pigrem *** Pig walking ***%gopts%2000.kvx /f9%gopts%2005.kvx /f11%gopts%2010.kvx /f13%gopts%2015.kvx /f14rem *** Pig shooting (standing) ***%gopts%2025.kvx /f18%gopts%2030.kvx /f35%gopts%2035.kvx /f16rem *** Pig dive'n'shoot ***%gopts%2040.kvx /f51%gopts%2045.kvx /f58%gopts%2050.kvx /f59rem *** Pig death ***%gopts%2055.kvx /f37%gopts%2056.kvx /f39%gopts%2057.kvx /f41%gopts%2058.kvx /f42%gopts%2059.kvx /f43%gopts%2060.kvx /f46set gopts=
```
trooper_md2_to_kvx.bat:
```
@echo offrem /s.115 matches with scale .5 in DEFset gopts=poly2kvx trooper.md2 /s.115 /p..\palette.dat trooperrem *** Trooper walk ***%gopts%1680.kvx /f0%gopts%1685.kvx /f5%gopts%1690.kvx /f10%gopts%1695.kvx /f15%gopts%1700.kvx /f20rem *** Trooper shoot (standing) ***%gopts%1715.kvx /f21%gopts%1716.kvx /f23%gopts%1717.kvx /f25rem ***Trooper die ***%gopts%1730.kvx /f26%gopts%1731.kvx /f29%gopts%1732.kvx /f32%gopts%1733.kvx /f35%gopts%1734.kvx /f37rem ***Trooper choke ***%gopts%1738.kvx /f38%gopts%1739.kvx /f43%gopts%1740.kvx /f47rem *** Trooper shoot (crouching) ***%gopts%1744.kvx /f48%gopts%1749.kvx /f53rem *** Trooper teleport ***%gopts%1754.kvx /f57rem *** Trooper sitting ***%gopts%1741.kvx /f58rem *** Trooper flying ***%gopts%1720.kvx /f70%gopts%1725.kvx /f72set gopts=
```
Please note: these are old batch files! Notice they read MD2 files. Practically all new models are MD3. Also, I renamed poly2kvx to poly2vox last year when I added MD3 support. Converting these scripts to MD3 should be a simple matter of renaming things. The hardest part of polygon to voxel conversion is matching tiles and frame numbers, and getting the scales right.

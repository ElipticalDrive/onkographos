exp-07 is the raycasting experiment: render hex-prism voxels in Bevy on the GPU by walking a ray per pixel through the grid, with run-length-encoded columns and empty-space skipping. There's no code yet. Per its goals, every question gets mocked up as a graph first.
Experiments/exp-07/docs/goals.md: the goals as stated before any code (the pieces, the constraints, how the work goes).
Experiments/exp-07/viz/hex-ray-walk.html: bites one to seven, from the exit-face rule and the incremental hex walk, through the layer stack, the runs and the vertical skip, and skipping parent cells by their hi, to packing B, the decichō ladder and detail windows relative to the camera.
Experiments/exp-07/viz/kanto-scale.html: the world as a packing cell of 36 ri centred on the origin, mapped over Kantō, with the ladder zoomed rung by rung.
The work started under Experiments/exp-06/ and the last commit moves it to exp-07/, so nothing is left under exp-06/. It includes all of exp-06-voxel-raycast, and it doesn't overlap the exp-06 worldgen PR.
There's no entry for exp-07 in Experiments/manifest.md yet.

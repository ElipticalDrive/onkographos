Implements the expanded volumetric-cloud request on SDL_GPU/Metal and OpenGL, rather than relabeling the old moving cloud cards.
World-space density layer with wind advection, weather-selected coverage and vertical falloff.
48 view-ray integration samples; occupied samples take six sunward density/extinction samples for internal shading.
Opaque scene depth limits cloud composition; race-world perspective single-view only. HUD, menus, reflection and multiple-view paths stay outside the pass.
Dedicated cloud result stores explicit transmission. God rays sample this same transmission along their screen-space taps and at the sun, separate from scene brightness. The mask has independent storage and cannot be overwritten by bloom scratch passes.
Sun direction projects through the actual race view/projection at the world boundary, independent of the legacy sun-icon texture/draw path.
Default-on, reversible through Effect amounts > Volumetric clouds, --effect volumetric_clouds=off or video.volumetric_clouds=0. Separate god-ray switch/amount retained.
Both backends log successful cloud encoding, without claiming completed GPU pixels.
Limits: original textured sky remains as background/fallback. Fixed layer altitude/thickness, fixed wind, simple bounded lighting, no ground cloud shadows/full weather/atmospheric simulation. This is not temporal reconstruction. Mac frame-time optimization is a later pass, per user steering.
Source-inspected, UNTESTED per urgent user instruction. No build/native visual/alignment/performance success claimed. Requires Mac rebuild and visual feedback, especially sun direction, world reconstruction and depth edges. Human-led merge only. No merge/release/workflow rerun/manual dispatch performed.

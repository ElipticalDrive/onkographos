GitHub - KarlHajal/Raymarcher: Ray marching engine in WebGL
We developed a ray marching engine in WebGL that allows users to specify scenes to be rendered in JSON format. Its architecture is based on the raytracing framework used in the course exercises, and it implements the following features: Adaptive ray-marching algorithm (sphere-tracing) Handles 16 different primitives with full control over their position and rotation.

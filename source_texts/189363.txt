Fix
Affine picking can miss valid geometry because subtracting transformed points rounds a ray direction to zero at large world or local coordinates, even with an identity transform.
Transform directions with the linear matrix component only when the homogeneous bottom row is exactly (0, 0, 0, 1). Keep the projected origin, unnormalized direction, ray interval and projective calculation. Hit selection, world-parameter conversion, bounds, caches and geometry materialization are unchanged. This does not remove rounding in origin or world-hit reconstruction, nor redefine projective intersection semantics.
Performance
Release comparison of complete public intersection calls on equivalent ordinary-coordinate box, triangle and box-backed custom hit/miss workloads, with non-unit rays. The affine frame combines a quarter-turn, reflection, nonuniform scale and translation; identity and projective frames are separate. Direct shape intersections are controls. Initial cohort: 16 alternating processes, 8,448 samples of 150,000 queries; independent longer cohort: eight processes, 4,224 samples of 600,000 queries. Cohorts are not pooled. Incorrect large-coordinate results from the original are not benchmarked as successful work.

Across identity/affine workloads, aggregate timing changes range from −9.1% to +1.2% initially and −19.7% to +0.9% longer. Improvements are not universal: initial custom value hits and triangle option misses regress; longer identity custom/triangle option hits regress. Longer affine box option misses improve in aggregate (50.752 → 48.601 ns), but their median process-pair ratio is 1.031.
All projective aggregate timing medians regress: +11.6–24.6% initially, +5.3–18.9% longer. Median process-pair ratios are 1.131–1.217 and 1.058–1.216, respectively; individual matched-sample ratios span 0.557–1.641 and 0.833–1.515. Unchanged projective arithmetic does not imply unchanged cost.
Identity/affine median process-pair ratios span 0.937–1.016 initially and 0.789–1.031 longer; individual matched-sample ratios span 0.554–1.783 and 0.603–1.430. Direct controls also drift: aggregate changes are −3.3% to +28.1% initially and −4.8% to +4.7% longer; median process-pair ratios are 0.951–1.289 and 0.955–1.091, with individual ratios 0.332–6.229 and 0.648–1.540. These ratios are not ratios of aggregate medians; not every public-path difference can be attributed to the helper alone.
Calling-thread allocation is identical in every matched public-query sample: 0 B/miss; 0 B/value box or triangle hit; 24 B/option box or triangle hit; 24 B/value custom hit; 48 B/option custom hit. Direct controls have 0 B/query medians, but option-hit samples vary up to 24 B initially and 5.892 B longer in the two versions; matched control allocation deltas range from −24 to +16.424 B initially and −4.044 to +2.972 B longer.
Draft: projective throughput costs and smaller conflicting timings remain unresolved. No universal speedup/cost-neutrality, scene construction, geometry-download, GPU, frame-time, peak-memory or GC-pause claim.

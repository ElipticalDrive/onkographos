Description
Creates docs/webgl/HARD_LIGHT_SDF_RAYMARCHING.md detailing the mathematical formulations of signed distance fields (SDFs), sphere tracing raymarching loops, smooth minimum boolean operators (smin), volumetric glow integration, anti-aliasing techniques, and mobile GPU optimization budgets for Green Lantern hard-light construct shaders.
Changes Made
Mathematical Formulations & Sphere Tracing (docs/webgl/HARD_LIGHT_SDF_RAYMARCHING.md): Formulated Euclidean signed distance fields, the Eikonal gradient condition, raymarching sphere stepping algorithms, and tetrahedron normal estimation routines.
3D Primitive GLSL Library: Provided GLSL implementations for spheres (sdSphere), boxes (sdBox), hexagonal prisms (sdHexPrism), and rings/tori (sdTorus).
Smooth Minimum Boolean Operations: Documented polynomial smin, smooth intersection, smooth subtraction, and composite construct assembly fusing power rings with hexagonal emblems.
Volumetric Energy Glow & Shading: Specified inverse distance field volumetric energy integration, Fresnel rim lighting, Blinn-Phong specular bloom, and internal caustic refractions.
Anti-Aliasing & Optimization Budgets: Documented screen-space derivative anti-aliasing (fwidth), sub-pixel jittering, bounding sphere ray skipping, step relaxation factors, and frame time targets (< 3.5ms on mobile GPUs).

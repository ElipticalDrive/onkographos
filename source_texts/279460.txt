Abstract We show that the variance of the Monte Carlo estimator that is importance sampled from an exponential family is a convex function of the natural parameter of the distribution. With this insight, we propose an adaptive importance sampling algorithm that simultaneously improves the choice of sampling distribution while accumulating a Monte Carlo estimate. Exploiting convexity, we prove that the method&apos;s unbiased estimator has variance that is asymptotically optimal over the exponential family

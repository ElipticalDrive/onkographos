Adds Lesson 06 at /grain/. This lesson covers procedural textures: noise and octaves (fBm), wood rings pushed by noise, marble bands bent by turbulence, and 3D solid textures compared with 2D painted images.
Preview: https://how-textures-work-l06-preview-production.up.railway.app/grain/
(Railway service how-textures-work-l06-preview, tracking lesson-06-grain. Main, production, lesson-04-wear and how-textures-work-preview were not changed.)
What's in it
Hero: series hero v3 (dot mask on alpha, lens reveal, camera auto-fit) on a turned wooden bowl.
Specimen 01 · Noise strip: Octaves (1–6) and Scale sliders, plus a live graph of one slice.
Specimen 02 · Wood block: Ring spacing, Warp and Turn sliders. Turn rotates the block to show end grain versus long grain.
Specimen 03 · Marble: Turbulence slider and a grey/gold vein toggle. The copy explains that veins form where minerals fill cracks and that bands fold under heat and pressure.
Specimen 04 · Cut the block: a clipping plane plus a cap that samples the same 3D rule, shown next to a 2D-mapped block that is an empty skin when cut.
Where you see this: five entries (technical artist, FX artist, architectural visualization artist, furniture designer, geologist).
For teachers: objectives, a 10-minute activity, and art & design and science maps.
Dockerfile: COPY grain/ /srv/grain/. README: tech notes for this lesson.
Constraints
The page uses one shared WebGL canvas with scissor regions. The pixel-ratio (DPR) cap is 1.5 on mobile and 2 on desktop. Offscreen regions are skipped, and rendering pauses when the tab is hidden. Reduced motion uses a live listener. Rendering uses ACES tone mapping, sRGB output and a RoomEnvironment PMREM. All patterns are GLSL, with no image textures. Phones run 3 octaves instead of 5. Sliders are 44px tall on coarse pointers or widths ≤700px. OrbitControls is off wherever a slider owns the camera. Fonts are Space Grotesk, Gabarito and JetBrains Mono. The new accent is rose #f08aa8.
QA
qa-grain.mjs (Playwright 1.63) runs in Chromium 1440×900, a Chromium phone at 390×844 DPR3 in portrait and landscape, and WebKit on an iPhone 13 profile. It also includes reduced-motion and nav-link checks. Results are posted in a comment below.

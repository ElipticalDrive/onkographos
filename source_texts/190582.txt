Summary
Math Plane S4 (epic #1547): implicit curves and shaded regions.
curve NAME implicit "F(x, y)" draws the zero set of F (MathPlaneImplicitSampler.Segments):
Marching squares on a 2 px lattice covering the view, the 2D form of Lorensen & Cline's marching cubes (already in the bibliography; the entry now names this point of use).
Saddle cells are resolved by F at the cell centre, as design §3.4 planned.
Poles: each edge crossing is found by 10 bisection steps. At a root |F| shrinks with the bracket; at a pole it grows. A crossing whose final |F| is not under 5 % of the edge's end values is dropped. Without this, y - tan(x) drew vertical lines at x = ±π/2 (seen in the first smoke render). Bisection also puts the crossings well inside a pixel.
region NAME "condition(x, y)" shades at 30 % of its colour every pixel whose centre satisfies the condition. Comparisons and && || give 1 / 0, and any finite non-zero value counts as inside. It is blended straight into the frame at its place in draw order, so later objects draw over it. The label sits at the shaded pixels' centre.
Both read only x and y; an implicit curve has no domain. Errors keep statement + column, and batch refuses a bad scene (exit 1, checked).
Editor: + F(x,y) = 0 (starts x^2 + y^2 - 4) and + Region (starts x^2 + y^2 < 1), with editable expressions. A domain entered for either is refused.
Batch parity: everything lives in MathPlaneScene, so there is no new surface.
Performance note: a 1280×720 Debug --batch still with two regions and three implicit curves took about 0.6 s, which is about 1.8 M interpreter evaluations for the regions. I did not measure Release. If live panning feels slow, the typed-compile option from design §3.3 is the fix; I have not filed an issue for it yet.
Closes #1551
Tests
MathPlaneImplicitTests (17), checked against analytic geometry and an independent sign test:
Circle, three radii: every vertex is within 0.05 px of the circle, and the total length is 99–100.1 % of the circumference.
Bernoulli's lemniscate: every vertex satisfies the equation within half a pixel by |F| / |∇F|, and no segment is longer than a cell diagonal.
Saddle: F = xy − ε with a lattice cell centred on the origin. No segment joins the hyperbola's quadrant-I and quadrant-III branches.
y − tan x: no segment on a pole, and every vertex satisfies y ≈ tan x.
No zero set gives no segments.
Regions: the region mask equals an independent per-pixel test of y < sin(x) && x² + y² > 1 (0 differences). The rendered shading covers exactly x > 0 at 30 % opacity.
Format round trip, 6 error cases, and editor add / edit / refuse.
Mutation checks:
Disabling the pole filter fails the tan test.
Swapping the saddle rule fails the saddle test. (Before that test was added, the saddle mutant survived.)
All MathPlane* tests plus the parser snapshot tests: 99 passed.
Real CLI: circle, lemniscate, y − tan x and two overlapping regions, checked by eye.
Not checked by eye in the running app.

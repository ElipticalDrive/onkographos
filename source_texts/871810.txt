Split from #241 (shapes; the operations are in #299). Step 1 of the merge order in #232.
Background
EBGeometry lacks several primitives that are standard in SDF modeling (Inigo Quilez's catalog, hg_sdf, libfive, OpenSCAD) and that users of embedded-boundary codes ask for. What exists today:
Primitives: PlaneSDF, SphereSDF, BoxSDF, TorusSDF, CylinderSDF, InfiniteCylinderSDF, CapsuleSDF, InfiniteConeSDF, ConeSDF, RoundedBoxSDF, RoundedCylinderSDF, PerlinSDF; the mesh SDFs.
Proposal
Each item lists the value quality it would have (an exact distance, or a bound, which keeps sphere tracing and BVH pruning safe), and whether a closed-form ray form is worth writing (otherwise rays march it).

E. Lattices (triply periodic minimal surfaces)
GyroidSDF, SchwarzPSDF, DiamondSDF, with a period and a wall thickness: porous media, heat exchangers, scaffolds. Values are bounds (divided by the gradient bound); combined with an Intersection they fill a part with a lattice.
G. Sampled distance fields
A distance field sampled on a regular grid (from CT/MRI, an image stack or another code), trilinearly interpolated, as a pool-resident shape (device-capable, following the PoolHandle rules). Value: a bound near the surface after a reinitialisation, inexact far from it. Probably its own pull request.
What each needs (from CLAUDE.md)
A new primitive: the shape (trivially copyable, EBGEOMETRY_HOST_DEVICE), and a decision whether it is a built-in tape type. Every built-in type grows the tape's primitive dispatch, which is register- and inlining-sensitive (168 registers for the float tape kernels, Tape::DispatchSplit on the host); a rarely used primitive may be better left as a user type.
Unit tests (with a [gpu] case), InstantiateAll.cpp, Sphinx docs (concepts and implementation pages), Tape and Fused performance cases for new operations, and an example where one teaches something new (the Examples/Twist user type becomes the library call).
Suggested order
D, the primitives most asked for first (ellipsoid, frustum, prisms), with ray forms.
E (lattices).
G, sampled fields, probably its own pull request.
To decide
The list, its names, and the order.
Which new primitives become built-in tape types.

More actions
Shape list agreed (2026-11-05), after a survey of Inigo Quilez's catalog, AMReX EB2, Chombo and chombo-discharge. Five pull requests:
Revolution, prisms, pipes (branch new-shapes): EllipsoidSDF (bound), CappedConeSDF (frustum: two end centers, two radii), RoundConeSDF, PolygonPrismSDF (n sides: triangular, hexagonal, polygon rods), TubeSDF (a pipe: inner and outer radius), CappedTorusSDF (a pipe elbow), CutSphereSDF, CutHollowSphereSDF, WedgeSDF. Exact ray forms where a closed form exists.
Polyhedra and frames: octahedron, pyramid, tetrahedron, dodecahedron, icosahedron, BoxFrameSDF, a general ConvexPolyhedronSDF from planes.
Quadrics and special shapes: hyperboloid (one and two sheets), paraboloid, superellipsoid, solid angle, revolved vesica, helix tube, link.
Lattices (section E): gyroid, Schwarz P and D, diamond, Neovius.
Sampled fields (section G): a 3D grid, and a height field (DEM-style).
Belong elsewhere: lathe, extrusion with spline profiles, helix sweep, polar and array repetition, profile grooves (#299); needle, mushroom, rod (compositions of existing shapes); polynomial and parser functions (#259).

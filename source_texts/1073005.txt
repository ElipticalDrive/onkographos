Extensions to permutation warping for parallel volume rendering
Shear warping [6] resamples with a bilinear interpolation to shear the volume and then a warp of the skewed projection image using a bilinear interpolation. The EM-Cube architecture [13] performs sheared trilinear resampling for which results vary with view angle, and a warp of the skewed projection image similar to Shear Warp.

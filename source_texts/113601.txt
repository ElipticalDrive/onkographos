Implements step 3 of the dense quality overhaul (docs/dense-mvs.md 10.4, accepted 2026-10-21): a TSDF fusion with voxel hashing, free-space carving and voxel levels by distance. It is the option fusion: 'tsdf' of reconstructDenseGpu, and the lab's default fusion from now on. The step record is in 10.8 ("Step 3 record").
What was built
Kernels: src/gpu/tsdf.shade.ts (tsdfAllocate, tsdfAssign, tsdfIntegrate, tsdfCarve, tsdfExtract). Blocks of 8×8×8 voxels sit in an open-addressing table, at 4 levels (v_L = v_0·2^L). The sums are integer atomicAdds, so their result does not depend on the order of the invocations. The layout is in 4.1.
Host: src/gpu/tsdf.ts (fuseTsdfGpu). It counts the blocks with a dry run and cuts the volume into boxes that fit the budget (Every GPU stage runs within any device budget: size changes speed, not success #160). Each box has a margin of one block. The budget changes the speed and never the points.
CPU reference and planner: src/reconstruction/tsdf.ts (fuseTsdf, planTsdfVolume, planTsdfBoxes).
The confidence of 10.3 is the integration weight. The filter writes it to mvsCost when the run asks for it, in window mode and in streamed mode.
scripts/eth3d-dense.mjs: --save-maps writes the filtered maps after a run. --maps fuses saved maps without the depth stage.
Lab: TSDF is the default fusion. ?denseFusion=pixel selects 3.10. The 3.12 cleanup runs by default after the pixel fusion only. ?denseClean=1|0 overrides that. The run record has a new field, dense.options.fusion.
The library default stays 'pixel'.

Fusion stage time and dispatches:
Pixel fusion: 97 to 118 s with about 16,000 dispatches (the baseline record had 173 s).
TSDF: 20.5 s with 343 dispatches, in 2 passes.
The container is shared with other agents, so these times are noisy (±30 % or more). The TSDF fusion's peak device bytes are 136.8 MB at a 256 MiB budget, and 59.4 MB at a quarter of the blocks per pass. Both give the same cloud.
Proof criterion of 10.4: met. The accuracy is lower than the pixel fusion's (69.0 against 83.3 at 2 cm), and this is recorded as open.

The TSDF's chunk floor is now the minUnits of ShadeDevice.chunkSize
(TSDF_OCCUPANCY_FLOOR, 16,384), so a hardware adapter lowers it after a
dispatch over GPU_CEILING_MS. A software adapter honors no occupancy floor,
and there the floor is the chunk's min: without it the pipes fusion took
226 s with 26,215 dispatches, with it 20.1 s with 342 and the same cloud.

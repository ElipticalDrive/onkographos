I used VHAP to process the monocular video in this link(https://syncandshare.lrz.de/getlink/fiJE46wKrG6oTVZ16CUmMr/VHAP), then input the results into Gaussian Avatars for training. The results show that the front-facing reconstruction quality is acceptable, but the side-facing results are very poor when rotating. Strangely, however, the side-facing results are OK when only the head is rotated. Could you please tell me the specific reasons for this? And what solutions are there to fix it?

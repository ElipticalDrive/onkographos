Describe the bug, what's wrong, and what you expected.
Volume rendering in interactive examples does not work. Talking with other developers from Kitware there is a suspicion that it is related with he SmartVolumeMapper that pyvista uses by default for rendering.
Example https://docs.pyvista.org/version/stable/examples/02-plot/volume
Steps to reproduce the bug.
```
import pyvista as pv

pl = pv.Plotter()
pl.add_volume(vol, cmap="bone", opacity="sigmoid")
pl.camera_position = cpos
pl.show()
```

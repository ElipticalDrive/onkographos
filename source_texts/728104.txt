Replace rejection sampling in sampleBounceDirection with direct cosine-weighted hemisphere sampling (Malley's method: uniform disk projected onto the hemisphere). Sampling density is p(w)=cos(theta)/PI, consistent with the throughput weighting already used at bounce sites, so energy is preserved. The rejection-loop iteration cap/fallback is superseded — sampling is now constant-time with no fallback path.
Closes #30
Build: pnpm install --frozen-lockfile && pnpm build passes.

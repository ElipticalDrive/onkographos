ADR-0076. Stacked on #149. Screen-space contact shadows for the sun (CLAUDE.md §8.6 stage 6, ADR-0041).
The sun's map already matches its texels to the pixels, so what it misses is what its own offset hides: a receiver looks up its shadow 1.5 texels off along its normal, lifted above anything lower. On Bistro's street that shows as lit gaps under window bars, along kerbs and beside leaves.
A march in the occlusion's pass (ao.comp sun_blocked), which has the depth, plane and jitter in hand: 4 steps over 24 pixels' widths at the pixel's distance, as the map's texels are (NphRenderView.contact_shadow, r.contact_shadow). The pass is compiled with and without it, and a frame without the sun's map takes the variant without.
What blocks: a depth in front of the marched point by more than half a pixel's change in the pixel's own plane, which is exact in reverse-Z depth at any angle, and by no more than 0.6 of the march's length.
The answer is one bit, in the sign of the raw occlusion's distance and then of the denoised occlusion, which the resolves fetch anyway. No memory, no fetch, no descriptor.

The interior has no sun, so no march: against #149's build, interleaved, its occlusion pass is unchanged (0.842 against 0.844 ms) and its resolve takes 0.01 to 0.02 ms for the occlusion's sign. Frames +0.012 to +0.029 ms.
DRAM: no new memory. The march reads the depths the pass reads already; the pass's declared reads stay 44.6 MB.
The §10.4 "Shadows" line goes from about 0.40 ms to about 0.55 ms, over by 0.15 ms: to be won back with the rest of the frame in Phase 14's last task.
Budgets: the synthetic scenes' shadows_*.gpu.ao, and the reference scenes' bistro_* and city_* .gpu.ao from the runs above (reference_scenes-2026-11-03-contact-…json). Nothing else moved past its 5 %.
Tried and not taken
Numbers in ADR-0076: longer marches (48 pixels: wrongly dark outweighs the gain), six steps (no gain clear of the noise, +0.09 ms), a reach stretched where the sun grazes the surface (false shadows behind railings, or no clear gain with its thickness held).
Not fixed here: where the sun grazes a wall the map's texels stretch across it, and Bistro's cornices throw shadows in stairs that the rays do not show. The march only smooths them near the contact. A map level chosen by the receiver's slope would answer that, at up to four times the pages on such walls.
Tests
Contact.* (7): against rays cast by the test, a bar too low for the map is found (253 of 429 hidden pixels lit without, 128 with) and a lit pixel in 15,000 goes dark; a plane does not shadow itself at a grazing angle with the sun on all four sides; something far in front does not shadow; the occlusion is byte for byte the same, triangles and voxels. 13 of 16 planted faults caught; the other three change no image (two guards that save time, and the bound on the length).
Debug with validation on the RTX: Contact, Ao, Frame, Gi, taa, Vsm, vsm, Shadows, VoxelGpu, Many, Restir, reference, and the runtime's Engine. Format, layering, GCC and tidy clean. The golden scenes have no occlusion, so no march.

code with no march costs that pass 0.02 ms, which the interior, with no sun, pays for nothing.

budgets are not re-based. The synthetic scenes with a shadowed sun are.

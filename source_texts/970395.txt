Step 3 of milestone 6 (docs/milestone-6.md, D-030):
Aerial-perspective volume: each view's (32 x 32 froxels, 64 slices out to 300 km) is made by compute. It is applied to models and the reference ground per pixel, and to terrain per vertex: lavapipe crashes compiling the terrain's fragment shader with any image of the frame set.
Visibility: it sets the aerosols by Koschmieder's relation; a change rewrites the atmosphere on the GPU and remakes the tables.
Tests:
Along a level ray, transmittance falls to 2% at 20.3 km and 42.2 km for 20 km and 40 km visibility.
Far ground melts into the sky at 500 m and stands apart from it at 200 km.
The CPU check of the tables holds in hazy air.
Desk: 0.02 to 0.06 ms an output, 60 Hz held, distant terrain fading into the sky.

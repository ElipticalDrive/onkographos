iTomography provides Computed Tomography (CT) algorithms, software, and scanner development services. Our proprietary 3D imaging products are used by CT and microCT scanner manufacturers and end-users of CT scanners to improve image quality, reduce or eliminate artifacts, accelerate image reconstruction, and decrease scan time.
What We Do Best
iTomography’s motto “From Theoretical Breakthroughs to Industry Adoption” derives from our track record of harnessing mathematical breakthroughs to develop practical solutions to complex imaging problems posed by our customers.
Medical CT Imaging
In the medical sector, iTomography’s mission is to support original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) with breakthrough solutions that increase diagnostic quality and certainty of CT imaging and lead to improved patient care around the world.

Industrial CT Imaging
In the industrial sector, iTomography offers OEMs in many industries innovative high-performance algorithms and software solutions applying 3D CT imaging to non-destructive testing, security, geology, and other applications. Our technologies are or may be utilized to image the inside of complex industrial parts, rock cores, luggage, lumber (wood logs), and other objects.

News Highlights
​​
July 2026: Building on the successful delivery of our custom CT image reconstruction software earlier this year, iTomography launched a new project with CFAN to add new functionality and further streamline CFAN's CT-based inspection and quality control (NDI and QC) workflows for complex composite aerospace components.
​
May 2026: Following the successful completion of the 2025 trial, iTomography and IBA Proton Therapy signed an agreement to begin productization of iTomography's iTomo4D-MEMC (motion-estimated and motion-compensated 4D CBCT) image reconstruction technology for IBA's CBCT systems. The goal is to deliver integration-ready software for clinical use in IBA's proton therapy imaging workflow.
​
May 2026: iTomography delivered to Tracerco an updated version of its custom iTomoIR image reconstruction software, adding new functionality and significantly accelerating reconstruction for CT-based pipeline flow assurance using Tracerco's Discovery™ subsea CT scanner.
​
March 2026: iTomography delivered to GE Aerospace and CFAN custom CT image reconstruction and artifact‑reduction software pipelines that will be used to enhance inspection and quality control (NDI and QC) of complex composite aerospace components during production.
​
​November 2025: iTomography and Canon Medical Research USA executed a Statement of Work for 2026 for the development and delivery of advanced CT image reconstruction algorithms and software under the parties' multi-year strategic technology development services agreement. ​The project builds on iTomography's successful results and deliverables of prior collaborative development.
​
April 2025: iTomography initiated a trial with IBA Proton Therapy to customize and evaluate iTomography's advanced iTomo4D-MEMC image reconstruction and motion compensation technologies for IBA's proton therapy workflow. The project included testing on IBA's clinical datasets to assess improvements in image quality and speed of reconstruction, and was successfully completed in 2025.
​
​​​March 2025: iTomography successfully completed a project with Tracerco to deliver a custom iterative image reconstruction software (iTomoIR) for CT-based pipeline flow assurance using Tracerco’s Discovery™ subsea CT scanner, which significantly improved image quality provided by the Discovery scanner.​​

October 2023: iTomography CTO, Prof. Alexander Katsevich included in a list of the top 2% of researchers in the world in a recent article published by a Stanford research group.

March 2023: iTomography awarded NSF SBIR Phase 1 Grant to continue strategic development of a novel scanner for rock core imaging and Digital Rock Physics applications for the Oil and Gas sector in collaboration with a major oil company.

July 2022 - Software Release - New versions of both iTomoFDK and iTomoKFBP CT data processing and image reconstruction software packages for circular and helical scanning, respectively, released. The software is actively used by major international end-users of microCT scanners. Temporary trial versions are available upon request. ​​​

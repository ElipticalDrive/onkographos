The problem
Every mask build tests each receiver-by-facet pair on its own, so its cost is n_receivers × n_facets × (cost per test) however coherent the scene is. The records show a lot of coherence going unused:
Outside(chamber, inlet, riser) on Sozzi: 81.1% of cell-centre pairs lie inside one convex region and so are clear by convexity alone. The recorded Sozzi measurement notes that the test does not skip them — every pair pays the full covering test.
Pipe cells are 52% blocked, and every disputed pair in the faceted-vs-ideal comparison sat in a thin band at the pipe rim. Shadow boundaries are thin; most (cell, patch of lamp) combinations are wholly lit or wholly dark.
The silhouette cone cull keeps only 7–12% of pairs, a share that falls as the mesh refines.
The triangulated mask (grid ray walk) extrapolates to ~88 h on the full Sozzi mesh, about 600× everything else in the calculation. The analytic Outside mask extrapolates to ~595 s (an extrapolation, flagged as one in the record).
The idea: hierarchical visibility, conservative first
Group receivers into spatially compact blocks and facets into spatially compact clusters. Every segment from a facet centroid in cluster F to a receiver in block R lies inside the convex hull conv(R ∪ centroids(F)), the shaft. If a body provably misses the shaft, every pair of that tile is clear, with no per-pair test. Only tiles the certificate cannot decide are tested pair by pair, as now.
This combines both directions of coherence (the "coarse emitter" and "nearby receiver" ideas). It is the standard shaft culling of Haines & Wallace (1991), used hierarchically as in Hanrahan, Salzman & Aupperle (1991).
The certificate must be conservative. A test from the cluster centroid to the block centroid is not, and would light up shadows silently. Here a certificate may only say "clear" or "don't know", and the result must be bit-identical to the brute-force mask.
A contract that composes: convex witnesses
A body offers witness functions w_i(x) whose sublevel sets {x : w_i(x) < 0} are convex and disjoint from the body. If all points of a group have w_i < 0 for one i, then so does their hull, and no segment between them meets the body. A group's summary is max over its points of w_i, and two groups merge by elementwise max. So the tile certificate factorizes into one summary per block plus one per cluster: any_i max(W_R[i], W_F[i]) < 0, with no per-pair work at all.
Outside(regions): w_r = signed_distance of each convex region. The sublevel set is the region, which lies in the fluid, so it is exact.
A convex primitive (sleeve, baffle, box, sphere): separating half-spaces from its support function h(n) = max_{x∈B} n·x over a fixed set of axes, w_n(x) = h(n) − n·x + slack.
CSG: Union support is the max of its members', Intersection is bounded by the min, Difference by its body's.
Any other body (host/triangulated): no witnesses, so it is never certified and always tested as now.
The witnesses belong on aquaflux/solids/ (generic geometry, names no radiation). The blocking/clustering and the per-tile fallback belong in radiation/visibility.py.
Phases
A. Prototype (this issue's first PR): "fully visible" only, analytic bodies only.
Witnesses on Body (empty by default), ConvexSolid primitives via support, CSG, and Outside.
A culling strategy for the analytic-body layer of the mask: Morton-ordered receiver blocks and facet clusters within each pass, certificates per tile, pair-by-pair blocks only on undecided tiles. Off by default (it changes cost, not answers), like TriangleGrid.
Tests: bit equality with the brute-force mask on scenes with real shadow boundaries (sleeves, Outside with pipes, including degenerate / grazing configurations); unit tests of each support function against dense sampling; mutation-checked.
Measurement: validation/ harness on the Sozzi Outside scene (cell-centre receivers, case STL lamp) reporting the share of pairs decided by certificate, and wall clock against the brute-force build, both arms in one process, alternating passes, with repeat spread and the full configuration.
B. Triangulated occluders. A certificate from TriangleGrid: if every voxel the shaft's bounding box overlaps is empty, the tile is clear. This is sound because a triangle is registered in every voxel its bounding box spans. This is where the 88 h lives. It depends on #510 (no public occluder takes a triangle mesh) for the receiver path, and on self-occlusion for the surface path.
C. "Fully hidden" certificates. A tile inside the shadow of one convex blocker; this is easy for Outside/primitives and hard for triangle soups (occluder fusion). With this, the gather could skip wholly-dark tiles too.
D. (Separate, and changes answers.) Distance-based level of detail for the gather. A facet cluster stands in for its children where width/distance allows (the subdivide.py criterion), with exitance as the area-weighted sum, so it stays linear and differentiable. This needs an error bound and a measurement against the fine gather. The lamp-resolution study already shows far receivers need 3–5× fewer facets.
Notes
The mask is frozen and off the gradient tape, so culling touches no gradient. Only D would.
Host orchestration, traced tile tests. The lesson from TriangleGrid is that a structure whose value is the work it skips should be built on the host. The per-tile blocks evaluation stays compiled, on compacted and power-of-two-padded tile batches.
Block and cluster sizes trade the certificate's hit rate (smaller shafts decide more) against fallback granularity. Sizes are to be measured, not assumed.

Phase B, done for the receiver path. That covers TriangleBody (Radiation: no public occluder takes a triangle mesh, so the grid is only reachable as self-occlusion #510), the coarse-to-fine (32, 8, 2) ladder and Body.vouches (61f87f5, 6986f46). Phase C, not built. Its ceiling has been measured. On the cylinders, fully blocked tiles hold 50-72% of the undecided pairs. On the triangulated wall they hold only ≤12.9-15.1%. A port-plane certificate, which would decide crossing pairs of both kinds, is sketched in the radiation design record but not designed.
Phase D, superseded. Its level-of-detail idea became Radiation: cluster the emitters for the gather with an error bound (the "many lights" problem: lightcuts, low-rank sampling) #565, which was declined in favour of exact back-face skipping.

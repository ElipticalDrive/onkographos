Description
Two refinements to an anti-aliasing stack that already works. Neither is urgent; both are recorded so the reasoning is not lost.
PostProcess.hpp already ships four modes — None, MSAA, FXAA, MSAA_FXAA — with device-clamped sample counts, multisample targets, resolve steps, overlays rendered at the scene's sample count, and test coverage across all four. Geometric specular AA (#142) handles the shading aliasing MSAA does not.
Why MSAA is load-bearing, and should be treated that way
Worth recording because it is easy to mistake for an incidental choice: MSAA is what makes refusing TAA cheap here. Forward shading is the one architecture where MSAA stays affordable; deferred renderers abandoned it over G-buffer sample-rate cost, and that is precisely the pressure that pushed the industry onto TAA. An engine that has ruled out the entire temporal family depends on MSAA remaining viable.
The consequence: any future proposal that moves the renderer toward deferred or visibility-buffer shading must be evaluated as a threat to the no-temporal position, not as a free structural choice.
1. SMAA 1x in place of FXAA
FXAA is an edge-directed blur that smears thin geometry and text. SMAA 1x reconstructs edges from a pattern-matched local analysis and is markedly better on both, at a similar cost class — and, critically, is purely spatial: single frame, no history, no motion vectors. It passes the artifact constraints by construction, exactly as FXAA does.
Ships as an additional AaMode rather than a replacement, so the comparison is A/B-able and FXAA remains available for the cheapest tier.
2. Supersampling as an Ultra option
Render scale above 1.0, downsampled to the output. Deterministic, no artifacts, and the exact inverse of the upscaling the engine rules out: high-end hardware buys more correctness rather than interpolated frames. It is also the honest answer for the top of the hardware ladder, where the question is what to do with surplus rather than how to cope with scarcity.
A knob on the existing chain, not a new pass.
Definition of done
SMAA 1x selectable alongside the existing modes; edge quality visibly better than FXAA on thin geometry and text in an A/B capture.
Render scale above 1.0 works with every AA mode, including MSAA, without artifacts at the resolve.
Cost of each published like everything else, at 1080p and 1440p.
Existing modes unchanged; default unchanged until the measured comparison says otherwise.
Not in scope
TAA, temporal upscaling, and frame generation, by standing decision. Render scale below 1.0 is also out — that is upscaling by another name, and the hard constraint applies regardless of implementation.

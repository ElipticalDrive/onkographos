Adds a sculptable ground to a bubble of an installed map. The new Terrain page creates a ground from a grid size, centre, bubble and retail terrain material, sculpts it with raise, lower, smooth and flatten brushes in the view, and imports or exports 16-bit PNG and RAW heightmaps. Remove ground restores what it changed.
How:
Formats: GroundGrid (positions on the 1/64 and 1/8192 lanes), GroundTerrain (patches, normals, occlusion bake, STerrain and bounds, terrain table and registry rows), GroundHeightmap, GroundBrush (brushes and a surface ray cast).
Authoring: GroundCollisionCooker cooks the compressed mesh through CollisionPackfileWriter; GroundCollision writes the carrier, wrapper, tK compound and collision table; GroundPlacement fills the bubble's first free terrain and collision slots and adds the ground to render registry bank 0; GroundAssets keeps the ground as one project group (feature grounds) for rebuilds and removal.
Scene: the static collision reader resolves tK imports through the owner's +0x18 carriers, which also shows 213 retail terrain wrappers previously counted as missing. Viewport: ToolInput lets a tool take the left button before selection.

A ground is a height grid placed in one bubble of an installed map. It
compiles to one STerrain per 128-unit patch with bounds and buffers, a
compressed-mesh payload and carrier per tile, a static compound of tK
instances, a collision wrapper, a terrain table and a collision table.
Placement re-points the bubble's free terrain and collision slots and
adds the ground's rows and occlusion blocks to its render registry bank 0.

The project keeps a ground as one added-asset group (manifest version 8):
a definition, a heights file and its native entries. Sculpting, material
changes and heightmap imports rewrite the same entries in one undoable
transaction. The Terrain page adds new grounds, raise, lower, smooth and
flatten brushes in the view, and 16-bit PNG and RAW heightmaps.

The static collision reader now resolves tK imports through the owner's
carrier list. Terrain.Tests re-decodes the output through Studio's terrain
and collision readers and compares it with World Studio's Python compiler.

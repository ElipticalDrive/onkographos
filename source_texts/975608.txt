# Horseshoe Splatting: Handling Structural Sparsity for Uncertainty-Aware Gaussian-Splatting Radiance Field Rendering

This repository contains the official open-source implementation of the paper **"Horseshoe Splatting: Handling Structural Sparsity for Uncertainty-Aware Gaussian-Splatting Radiance Field Rendering"**, accepted to **ICLR 2026**.

Horseshoe Splatting is a Bayesian extension of 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) that addresses structured sparsity in per-splat covariances (e.g., axis-wise variances and pairwise correlations) while providing calibrated uncertainty for novel-view rendering. A global-local Horseshoe prior adaptively shrinks noise-dominated covariance components and preserves salient structures. With a factorized variational inference scheme, the method enables efficient Monte Carlo rendering and pixel-wise posterior uncertainty estimation with minimal overhead, while maintaining strong rendering quality and runtime.

Paper link: [OpenReview (ICLR 2026)](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=NHuyk9KsG6)

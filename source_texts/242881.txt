Goal
Follow up on the non-blocking performance observations in:
Fix environment power and bilinear sampling support (#165 M6/M7) #175: Fix environment power and bilinear sampling support (#165 M6/M7) #175 (comment)
Fix PT/VPT depth and volume sampling (#165 M1/M5/M8) #176: Fix PT/VPT depth and volume sampling (#165 M1/M5/M8) #176 (comment)
The correctness fixes remain valid. Keep this separate from the currently frozen, regression-tested #165 integration candidate.
Candidate optimizations
At the PT/VPT scattering-depth limit, skip the terminal intersection (and VPT transmittance) after a non-delta event, when no emission can still be credited. Preserve primary/depth-zero, delta-chain and null-boundary semantics.
Avoid a redundant selected-light visibility trace only when the light's contract guarantees that zero directional sampling density implies zero possible emitted radiance. Do not blanket-skip all non-environment/custom/future light types: retain unit MIS weight where a BSDF-sampled zero-competing-density path can contribute.
Reported benchmark, not independently remeasured here
The user measured CornellBoxStandard, PT, 512x512, 1024 spp, depth 8:
Original main: 19.3-19.7 seconds.
Combined correctness fixes: 22.5-23.4 seconds.
Both proposed skips: 20.3-20.4 seconds, bit-identical image in that run.
Acceptance
Derive and document the support conditions for each skip.
Preserve zero-competing-PDF environment/custom-light contributions.
Keep primary, final-delta, mixed-lobe, null-boundary and medium-depth regressions passing.
Compare actual before/after Float32 renders with unchanged budgets/inputs and quantify differences.
Benchmark the same scene without thread caps; do not infer statistical independence from nominal native seeds.
Run affected Debug/Release and integration regressions before merging.
M3 and the unrelated LT arena/allocation observations remain outside this performance follow-up.

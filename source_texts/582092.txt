Summary
Header-only, certified real-root isolation for Chebyshev series on [−1, 1], degrees 1–17. It has no EOS coupling. It is the numerical core of the all-roots mixture density solver: PR 1 of 4 in Linear project "Chebyshev all-roots density solver", COO-68.
include/CoolProp/numerics/ChebyshevBernstein.h:
Clenshaw value and derivative, Chebyshev derivative, l1 tail bound;
conversion to Bernstein with a rounding-error bound;
safeguarded Newton inside a bracket;
real_roots<N>(): convex-hull exclusion, Bernstein–Descartes counting (V = 0 → none; V = 1 with clear signs → exactly one, certified), de Casteljau midpoint subdivision, depth caps and a node budget.
include/CoolProp/numerics/cheb2bern_tables.h holds the Chebyshev→Bernstein matrices. They are computed exactly in rational arithmetic and rounded once. The generator is dev/scripts/gen_cheb2bern.py.
src/Tests/CoolProp-Tests-ChebyshevBernstein.cpp (tag [cheb_bernstein]).
Guarantee (documented in the header). Every root of the series in [−1, 1] lies in a reported interval. With tol > 0, so does every root of any function g with |g − series| ≤ tol. A certified interval contains exactly one simple root of the series, and g changes sign across it.
Intervals the subdivision cannot resolve (depth cap, node budget, coefficients within the tolerance) are reported as uncertified, never dropped. The spike silently dropped such an interval when its end values had the same sign, which could lose a double root or a close pair. Invalid input fails closed:
a negative tol is treated as 0;
a NaN or infinite tol, or a non-finite coefficient, gives one unresolved [−1, 1] entry.
Evidence
Tests: 17 cases, 104 251 assertions:
exact low-degree matrices;
the conversion error bound checked against a double-double (fma) reference;
derivatives;
simple roots, no roots, close pairs (certified down to a gap of 1e-6; reported as uncertified below that, where the series between the roots is under the coefficient roundoff);
double roots, roots at the ends and at a split point;
tolerance semantics, node-budget exhaustion, invalid and non-finite input, the zero series, sign_change semantics;
randomized coverage at degrees 1, 2, 3, 5, 9 and 17;
with tol > 0: coverage of every root of g = f + tol·sin(Ku), and g changing sign across certified intervals.
Same results as the spike on real data. On the kernel's real pieces the results match the spike exactly: same root counts, same certification, bit-identical roots. That is 54 855 open pieces of 26–42-piece, degree-17 expansions from GERG-2008 C1/C2 and Gernert CO2/H2O at two compositions, over 4000 random (T, p) each.
Cost: about 6.4 µs per call for all roots over 26 pieces of degree 17 (measured in the kernel).
preflight.sh: all 8 checks pass (clang-format, build, 628 test cases, cppcheck, clang-tidy on changed lines, semgrep, json-symbols, install-headers).
Review
An adversarial pre-PR review over three rounds found two blocking issues, both fixed:
A negative tol silently excluded roots, even −1e-15.
The documentation overclaimed "exactly one simple root" for the approximated function when tol > 0.
Soundness fixes from the same review:
The subdivision rounding is now a carried absolute bound, e_child = e + N·eps/2·(bmax + e) + N·denorm_min. Before, it was depth·eps·max|b| of the current node, which under-counted. The tolerance is rounded up.
There is an absolute floor for subnormal coefficients.
sign_change on uncertified entries comes from the end Bernstein coefficients, which must be clear of the tolerance.
refine takes its bracket orientation from the trusted b₀ sign.
The final delta review found nothing blocking.
Follow-ups (reviewer: not blocking; filed in Linear):
a unit test of the carried subdivision bound against an exact reference;
a regenerate-and-diff check of the generated table.
Flagged trade-off: Options (depth caps, budget) is not validated. Extreme values fail closed: early, coarse unresolved intervals, or deep recursion.
AI assistance
Claude (Opus 5.5) extracted and generalized the spike code, wrote the generator, the tests and the equivalence harness, ran the adversarial reviews and fixed their findings. Human verification (Ian Bell) of the guarantee wording and the API is pending.

* feat(flash): Chebyshev tables of the pressure equation for multi-fluid models (COO-69)

Split every generalized-exponential residual term (pure-fluid and
departure) into kappa(tau) phi(delta), group terms with identical
delta-parts, fit each group's chi = delta d(phi)/d(delta) on adaptive
delta-pieces of [0, delta_max], and combine the fits per (T, x) into
G(delta) = delta Z - t on every piece with a per-piece bound on
|G_tables - G_true|.  The bound is the input to the certified root
isolation of ChebyshevBernstein::real_roots (COO-68).

- hard (tau, delta) rectangle, nothing extrapolated: assemble() declines
  tau outside [tau_min, tau_max]
- declines models whose residual does not factor (non-analytic, SAFT,
  cubic, ...), checked by type and by verifying the regrouped model
  against the backend's own alphar at build
- owns a copy of the reducing function; no pointers into the backend
- gas constant taken from the backend (NORMALIZE_GAS_CONSTANTS or a
  backend override such as GERG-2008's R)

Not yet wired into any flash; that is COO-70/COO-71.

Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>

* fix(flash): Chebyshev table margin covers evaluation roundoff near zeros of chi (COO-69)

Pre-PR review found the per-piece margin exceeded by up to 17 % on GERG
CH4/CO2 and Amarillo at low T: every d = l term has chi = 0 at delta = 1,
where the roundoff of evaluating chi is set by the size of its parts, not
by chi or its (tiny) fit coefficients.  The margin's roundoff term and
the split criterion's floor now use that parts scale (Term::parts).
Side effect: delta = 1 is no longer bisected down to min_width (CH4/CO2
36 -> 22 pieces).

- min_width must be >= 1e-9 delta_max, and a piece whose midpoint does
  not fall strictly inside is accepted (the split could not terminate)
- NaN in the measured fit error no longer absorbed by std::max
- a pair with F_ij != 0 and no departure function is declined
- header: the margin is measured, not proven; tables are a snapshot of
  the interaction parameters and gas-constant config
- tests: 1001 points per piece, 4 random compositions, finer T grid;
  parity now requires each bisected true root to lie in a reported
  interval; pure and mixture cubic, GaoB (Ammonia), min_width/NaN
  options

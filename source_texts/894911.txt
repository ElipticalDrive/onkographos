Performance pass I3: cloud quality for Track B (orthogonal to ATE, poses unchanged).
What changed
The raw per-frame accumulation is replaced by KinectFusion-style TSDF volumetric integration of the sensor depth (12 mm voxels), now the Track B default. Track B's poses are tight enough (0.014-0.040 m after I1) that the volumetric averaging cancels the per-frame sensor noise and the double-surfaces raw accumulation showed, giving a markedly cleaner surface at ~half the points (office 150k to 78k, desk to 80k).
Verification
Screenshot-verified clean across all 5 scenes, light + dark (small desk to the large pioneer robot run), no page errors.
Full test suite green (incl. the I1 depth-edge guard tests).
Opt out with LIDAR3D_RGBD_TSDF=0; falls back to raw accumulation when open3d is absent, so the live lane matches replay and a scene is never blank.
Mirrors why TSDF stays opt-in on the RGB-only 0.37 m poses (there it carves sparsely); on Track B's tight poses it finally pays off. Docs (docs/models/06, Benchmark, Experiments, model_history I3 row) carry the record.

Performance pass I3: cloud quality for Track B, orthogonal to ATE (poses
unchanged). Replaces the raw per-frame accumulation with KinectFusion-style TSDF
volumetric integration of the sensor depth (12 mm voxels): every valid, near
depth frame is integrated into a truncated signed-distance volume at its solved
pose, then a single denoised surface is extracted. Track B's poses are now tight
enough (0.014-0.040 m after I1) that the volumetric averaging cancels the
per-frame sensor noise and the double-surfaces raw accumulation showed, giving a
markedly cleaner surface at ~half the points (office 150k -> 78k, desk -> 80k).

Screenshot-verified clean across all 5 scenes, light + dark, small desk to the
large pioneer robot run. Default on; opt out with LIDAR3D_RGBD_TSDF=0. Falls back
to raw accumulation when open3d is absent, so the live lane matches replay and a
scene is never blank. Mirrors why TSDF stays opt-in on the RGB-only 0.37 m poses
(there it carves sparsely); on Track B's tight poses it finally pays off.

- rgbd_engine.py: _fuse_tsdf_sensor (sensor-validity mask, no learned conf)
- docs/models/06, Benchmark, Experiments, model_history (I3 row): TSDF record
- version 0.15.000 across package/pyproject/frontend; all 5 cases re-baked

Splits the original Mandelbulb example into two distinct files to explore different implementations.

`ex05_mandelbulb_1.rs` features a revised Mandelbulb iteration step:
- Updates the core fractal calculation with a new set of formulas for `x`, `y`, and `z`, based on spherical coordinates, and includes a `min` function to prevent NaN values.

- Simplifies the final density calculation to a direct `2.0 - r_final` approach, aligning with the new iteration.

`ex06_mandelbulb_2.rs` retains the original Mandelbulb logic but includes a minor correction to the SDF formula's logarithmic term.

Velocity-buffer motion blur (option 1) as a CompositorEffect, added to the Utopia, Hairpin and Swoopy lighting scenes. Full write-up in FTF_v29_NOTES.md.
What
MotionBlurEffect sets needs_motion_vectors and runs four compute passes in HDR (post-transparent, before glow/tonemap), placed before the god rays: prep, tile max, neighbour max, then a McGuire 2012 depth-aware gather with the 2014 tile jitter and centre-direction taps.
Exposed:
shutter angle (180°)
NORMALISED-to-60-fps vs PHYSICAL timing
max blur (40 px at 1080p)
tile size (auto-raised to at least the max radius)
taps
speed fade-in (0.2 → 0.6 speed ratio)
intensity
set_user_settings(on, intensity) for a settings slider; off also drops the velocity pass
Robustness:
camera-cut detection and notify_camera_cut()
sky reprojected by camera rotation
FSR2 motion-pass sentinel (−1, −1) handled by reprojection from depth
MSAA silhouette fix (resolved velocity and depth are sample averages)
AGCamera2097 drives the speed fade and flags its snap as a cut.
Verified (lavapipe, 1080p)
Velocity convention, scale and y-axis match the expected values to <1 %.
Linear depth matches a physics raycast to <1 %.
Ship sharpness metric (tools/dev/mb_metrics.py): the player ship and same-speed AI ships are unchanged by the blur (diff ≈ 0.00 against a world diff of 5–9).
Readability checked on Hairpin chevrons, gantries and billboards; Swoopy gallery and catwalk scaffolding; Utopia light strips.
A 5 s Hairpin flythrough rendered frame by frame (render_motion_shots.gd sequence option).
Needs a decision / real hardware
Above 60 Hz the player ship smears. The camera runs in _process but the ship moves only on physics ticks, with no physics interpolation. In a 144/60 Hz simulation the ship diff is 15.6 against a world diff of 9.0. This is the existing high-refresh ship judder, now made visible. Recommended separate PR: enable physics interpolation and have the camera read get_global_transform_interpolated().
GPU cost is not measurable here. Run MB_TRACK=test_circuit_6_live godot --path . -s res://tools/dev/mb_profile.gd for per-pass timings and the on/off frame delta. A MotionBlurProfiler node is also available (F7 toggles the blur, F6 cycles debug views), and the MB … timestamps show in the Visual Profiler.
MSAA 2×: depth doesn't resolve on lavapipe. Please check the silhouettes on real hardware.
Circuit 7 has no compositor yet, so it gets no blur.

- face seed used floor() on a value that is exactly an integer on

Hi
You can directly rotate the assembly with
```
from vedo import *

pts = np.random.rand(20, 3) * 10
pc = Points(pts).c('blue').alpha(0.5).ps(8)

line = Line(pts).c('red')

asse1 = Assembly([pc, line, pc.labels("id")])
asse2 = asse1.clone().rotate_x(90).shift([12,0,0])

show(asse1, asse2, axes=1).close()
```
or, you mean you need to compute the 3 angles based on yaw pitch and roll?

Hi Rob
what about something like this?
```
from vedo import *

pts = np.random.rand(20, 3) * 10
pc = Points(pts).c('blue').alpha(0.5).ps(8)

line = Line(pts).c('red')

asse1 = Assembly([pc, line, pc.labels("id")])
axes = Axes(asse1)

plt = Plotter()
plt.add(asse1, axes)

plt.camera.Roll(10)
plt.camera.Yaw(20)
plt.camera.Pitch(30)
plt.renderer.ResetCamera()
plt.camera.Zoom(0.8)

plt.render().interactive().close()
```

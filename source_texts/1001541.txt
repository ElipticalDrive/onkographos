X1 part 2b: ADR-0050 decision 5. A 3D item samples a mip chain of its source with trilinear filtering and 16× anisotropy. It comes with scenario C's golden.
This is a draft stacked on #622 (part 2), which goes into wave/m3-3. Auto-merge is off. It retargets to wave/m3-3 once #622 lands, and I merge the wave in only after the main-into-m3-3 sync lands. There is no wire change. It carries #622's round-1 fixes (merged at 81e02f1).
Before / After
Before: a 3D item sampled level 0 of its source with a bilinear filter. A floor tilted 75° from a 512² source aliased badly: a mean error of 29 on its interior.
After:
A source that a visible 3D item draws gets a mip chain, made the first time and stored on its Image. The chain is Rgba16Float, premultiplied linear, and goes down to 1×1.
The chain is rebuilt on the GPU before the composite pass, at most once per render or render_into call:
an upload only after rewrite_rgba8;
a layer or an import every call that draws it in 3D, since a nested scene, a filter or the platform changes those out of sight.
The 3D item samples the chain trilinearly with anisotropy_clamp 16. wgpu clamps that to 1 on an adapter without anisotropic filtering, as the ADR's "clamped to what the adapter offers" asks.
A source never placed in 3D gets no chain. 2D drawing is unchanged.
C's interior error drops to 6.2.
What
mips.rs (new):
MipPipelines: three level-0 pipelines (premultiplied, straight sRGB, premultiplied sRGB), the linear sampler and the anisotropic sampler.
Mips: the chain texture, per-level views and input bind groups, and the bind group the composite pass uses.
claim decides whether to rebuild, from the upload generation and the call counter. record records the level passes.
Unit test: level_count.
mip.wgsl (new): a fullscreen triangle. Level 0 is copied texel for texel, with an import converted as the composite shader's modes 1 and 2 do. Each further level samples the level above at its own texel centres, which averages 2×2 texels.
image.rs: Image gains a generation (bumped by rewrite) and a OnceLock<Mips>, and is_static() (an upload, not a layer).
compositor.rs:
Draw.mips marks a 3D item. Its vertex mode is 0, because a chain is always premultiplied linear.
record_mips runs before the composite pass in render and render_into, and a call counter dedupes the rebuilds.
A 3D draw binds the chain's bind group.
place.rs: Shape::is_3d.
composite.wgsl: with feat(render): draw 3D items with w and edge coverage (X1 part 2) #622's UV clamp merged in, the fragment samples at the clamped UV with implicit derivatives, not textureSampleGrad with the unclamped UV's. lavapipe filters explicit gradients isotropically: with textureSampleGrad, C measured 18.4, the same as no anisotropy. The two differ only at fringe pixels (coverage under a half) and their 2×2-quad neighbours along the edge, roughly one row of pixels, where the clamped derivatives pick a finer level.
tests/three_d.rs:
The pattern size is now a parameter.
feat(render): draw 3D items with w and edge coverage (X1 part 2) #622's D cropped golden now also passes the pattern size.
New: scenario C, and an upload rewritten from red to green whose 3D draw then shows green, on that draw and the next.
Docs:
render README: Purpose, Not in this cut, a new pipeline step 2 (Mips, with the later steps renumbered), the resources table, the Testing table and Open questions.
ARCH › GPU pipeline. The still-outside list is now "P010, loading effect packs": mips leave it here.
render README: render_image "stops after step 3", since the steps were renumbered.
progress.toml.
How it was tested
On lavapipe, as the mean error per channel in sRGB 8-bit steps. The p99 limit is 40 throughout.

* feat(protocol): the drawn 3D polygon and unplace (X1 part 1, ADR-0050)

ItemGeometry::drawn_polygon clips the content rectangle to the box and then
by the near plane w >= 1/256, in the local frame, and returns three to five
vertices with canvas point, local point, texture coordinate and w, so a
compositor can interpolate perspective-correctly. ItemGeometry::unplace is
the inverse map. Pure functions with no wire form; no caller yet.

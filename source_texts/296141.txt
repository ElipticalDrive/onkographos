New self-contained implicit_neural_representations/ folder: a narrative
notebook plus strict-typed src/ modules showing how a neural net can learn
the continuous mapping (x, y) -> RGB for a single image, why a plain ReLU
MLP fails (spectral bias), how random Fourier features (Tancik et al. 2020)
and SIREN (Sitzmann et al. 2020) fix it, and how the continuous
representation enables arbitrary-scale upscaling compared honestly against
bicubic interpolation. Fully cited (SIREN, Fourier features, spectral bias,
NeRF, LIIF, DeepSDF, Occupancy Networks, CPPN); intended as website-bound
content.
src/: ReluMLP, FourierFeatureMLP, Siren, full-batch fit loop, and an
arbitrary-resolution render(), all passing ruff + mypy strict (unlike the
coursework/Kaggle dirs, this folder is deliberately inside the CI gate)
notebooks/implicit_image_fitting.ipynb: executed end-to-end on CPU
(ReLU 21.6 dB vs Fourier 36.2 dB vs SIREN 38.2 dB; INR 4x upscale lands
within 0.1 dB of bicubic, as a single-image INR should)
data/lighthouse.png: kodim19 from the Kodak suite (unrestricted use),
downsampled to 384x256; un-ignored via a scoped .gitignore exception
tests/implicit_neural_representations/: smoke tests for shapes, SIREN
init bounds, fit convergence, pixel-centre grid, downsample/PSNR
pyproject.toml: notebook-only ruff ignores (T201 prints, N812 F alias)

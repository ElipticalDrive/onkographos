Goal
Make atmospheric correctness measurable rather than approving only attractive/unchanged golden screenshots. Compare Cosmolabe's current renderer against independently established numerical references and representative real Earth/Mars/Titan/Venus imagery, and identify the remaining model/compositing errors. This is an independently deliverable validation/calibration track of #134, not another atmosphere rewrite.
Why
#134's model/LUT work has progressed through merged #138 (harness), #141 (transport), #146 (SkyView), #161 (incident solar extinction), and #169 (solar display). Regression screenshots prove consistency, not physical correctness. In particular horizon transitions, sky color, night-side leakage, body-disc extinction, terrain appearance, and mixed atmosphere/surface lighting still need a reference-based quality bar.
Work
Inventory reusable validation assets in docs/atmosphere-validation.md; extend them rather than starting a new screenshot framework.
Establish a pinned reference set and metadata: body, date/epoch or controlled Sun geometry, camera altitude, lat/lon, view azimuth/elevation, body radii, atmospheric coefficients, assumed solar radiance/exposure, source/version/license. Prefer reproducible math first and curated mission imagery second.
Reproduce a comparable subset of Eric Bruneton's numerical precomputed-atmosphere examples, Sébastien Hillaire's LUT technique, and Takram's Earth surface/ascent/orbit examples. Maxime Heckel's article is an educational walkthrough, not a scientific ground-truth oracle.
For Earth, compare zenith, anti-/cross-/sunward horizon, solar elevations +10°, 0°, -3°, -6°, orbit limb, day/night terminator and whole disc. For Mars, compare dusty sky/sunset/horizon/orbit; include Titan and Venus as thick-atmosphere stress cases.
Establish numerical checks for ray/planet intersection, grazing path length, RGB transmittance, sunlight blockage, inscatter/phase conventions, and continuity through altitude transitions. Compare trusted CPU reference to GPU for representative pixels/rays; do not simply compare two render paths sharing the same bug.
Decompose discrepancies into geometry, scattering coefficients/profiles, Sun radiometry, LUT discretization, compositing, preset calibration or display response; identify issue owners rather than hiding errors by per-scene tweaks.
Record GPU/CPU costs and memory; representative scenes include multi-body and instrument-camera configurations.
Preserve traceable fixtures and a short comparison report with actual measurements, annotated screenshots and a remaining-gap table.
Acceptance
A documented, reproducible reference corpus exists with clear source provenance and objective tolerances where available.
Before/after Earth/Mars comparisons clearly identify at least the principal remaining correctness gaps; visual judgments and numerical errors are separately reported.
Golden screenshots are not silently updated to normalize visible regressions.
No brittle public-network call is added to ordinary CI; cached/committed small fixtures are preferred.
Scope / sequencing
Can start now with existing #134 harness; finish numerical model checks before tuning scene-wide appearance. Coordinate with the independent lighting, composition and exposure follow-ups, but do not block their initial prototypes.

# DDVR (Denoising Direct Volume Rendering)
**Master thesis:** Varying Step Size Ray Marching with Denoising for Direct Volume Rendering.

The program was primarily developed on Linux (Ubuntu) and has been partially tested on Windows and macOS.


**Abstract**  
*Real-time direct volume rendering remains a computationally demanding task due to the large number of ray marching steps required to achieve visually pleasing results. This thesis investigates techniques to reduce the computational cost of ray marching, with a particular focus on varying step size strategies and denoising methods.*

Motivation
Smooth view synthesis of complex scenes
Key Idea
Parameterize a 5D neural radiance field with MLP
Differentiable volume rendering enables gradient flow and optimization
Fourier transformation based positional encoding models high frequency data
Previous Approach
Neural 3D shape representations
View synthesis and Image-based rendering
Method
Train MLP so that when we query (x, y, z, phi, theta), it outputs (R, G, B, sigma).
Numerically estimate the continuous integral of classical volume rendering using quadrature (discrete sampling) (N=24 in implementation)
Positional Encoding: to express high frequency data
Reconstruction Objective for each viewpoint (compare with GT)
Result
Baseline Comparison
Ablation Study
Comment
Very charming output, simple and intuitive but powerful idea.
Still limited in that a neural network only encodes a single scene.
The main challenge would be to generalize to multiple, diverse scenes.
Following studies, such as NeRF-VAE, would mitigate current issues, since the idea and the initial result itself are very impressive.

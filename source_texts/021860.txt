What is a volumetric display
Not a screen.
Not a headset.
Explore our hardware
Most screens are flat, and virtual reality isolates each viewer inside a headset. Our technology works differently: it renders your content as volumetric forms that occupy real physical space, so you can walk around them, view them from any angle, and gather around them with colleagues, customers, or students. Under the hood, our VLED platform performs real-time rasterisation, converting interactive 3D scenes into thousands of volume slices. A rotating, ultrafast spatial light modulation system then rebuilds that digitised geometry into tangible, physical copies of your scenes that exist in the real world.

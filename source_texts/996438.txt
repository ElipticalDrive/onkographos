User feedback addressed
Screenshots 1–2: the round dome and uniform floating towers are fabricated surface-feature placers, not neural DEM output. Skip all standalone surface-feature placers on Badlands chunks; retain geological strata and ordinary Minecraft biome decorations.
Terrain geometry: extend FluvialRiverNetwork.RiverTopology with its already computed drainage accumulation; perform subtractive, slope- and runoff-guided erosion in LocalTerrainProvider on the canonical Terrain Diffusion heightmap after river carving and before compact/cache. The output is shared by game terrain generation and explorer.
Preserve river beds and fade near both river valleys and Badlands/other biome boundaries, without introducing isolated 3-D rock props or raised domes.
Screenshot 3: increase meandering of blendable biome boundaries on two world-space noise wavelengths, from 7-block to at most 92-block displacement. Increase per-tile classification margin from 12 to 104 pixels to prevent padding artifacts along 2048x2048 hydro tiles.
Bump persisted hydrology algorithm version from 16 to 17 to avoid reusing pre-modification terrain tiles.

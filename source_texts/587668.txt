Issue body actions
Owner-requested workflow
Convert a selected Blender mesh, including imported STL/FBX with evaluated modifiers and transforms, into real B-spline/NURBS B-Rep faces and export STEP for Fusion. Verify the actual Blender adapter and generated geometry.
Bounded implementation
Two explicit modes: exact faceted NURBS, and crease-aware curved PN patches with per-control displacement capped by a millimetre tolerance. Cubic triangular Bernstein patches map exactly to bicubic tensor-product B-splines through a collapsed-edge parameterization. Nonnegative Bernstein weights give a pre-sewing geometric deviation bound relative to the input triangles. Shared edge controls preserve positional seams; tangent continuity is not guaranteed.
OCCT must sew, validate a closed solid, preserve all faces, and export/reimport STEP with actual B-spline surfaces. No silent faceted fallback, invented CAD history, unknown original-surface accuracy, arbitrary self-intersection repair, or unverified Fusion-import claim. High-density scope: up to 100,000 input triangles / 300,000 vertices, with explicit topology-preserving reduction to a configurable CAD face budget (2,000 default, 8,000 maximum). Exact-coordinate welding only. Reduction is checked in both directions over complete triangle interiors: recursive centroid Lipschitz bounds and a convex single-target-triangle bound must cover all triangles within the declared tolerance before export. Query-budget failure rejects the conversion; PN displacement and reduction consume separate portions of the same approximation budget. Native parsing stays in the existing timed external worker; no new dependency stack.
Research and alternatives
Recent NURBSFit (3DV 2026), Mesh2Brep (TVCG 2025) and CAD-Recode (ICCV 2025) inform the longer-term patch consolidation/semantic reconstruction roadmap. This implementation combines established PN/Bernstein geometry with explicit quality and topology gates; it does not claim a new research result or reimplement those papers.
Verification gates
Analytic polyhedra, curved mesh patches, creases, winding, manifold failures, bounds, native B-spline surface types and STEP roundtrips; real Blender extraction with modifiers/world transforms/units/reflections and STL/FBX fixture imports; atomic export and unchanged source mesh on failure. See forthcoming ADR 0003 and implementation PR. Depends on prototype PR #11; human review before merge.

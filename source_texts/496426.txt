The images based on the gate-ranged laser imaging system are obtained.In order to minimize the impact that the distance between cross-section images is far than the distance between pixels in a cross-section image on 3-D visualization caused by that,enhance the 3-D displaying effect and improve the operation speed,a fill-in frame ray casting method and a 3-D displaying method with observing from different angles by rotating volume data are proposed based on the research of ray casting 3-D displaying method.The simulation results show that the proposed methods are effective and feasible,the 3-D visualization and target identification probability are improved.

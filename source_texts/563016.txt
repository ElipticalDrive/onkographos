Previously, geoms and flexes with rgba[3] == 0 were intersected during ray casting in render.py and shaded as opaque surfaces (since the alpha channel was ignored during shading and packed as 255.0), occluding both primary camera rays (RGB, depth, segmentation) and shadow rays. This diverged from MuJoCo's visualizer (mjv_addGeoms / mjv_makeFlex), which skips adding geoms and flexes whose resolved rgba[3] == 0.
Changes
mujoco_warp/_src/render.py:
Added _geom_rgba helper to resolve effective geom RGBA (mat_rgba when geom_matid >= 0 and geom_rgba is the default (0.5, 0.5, 0.5, 1.0), and geom_rgba otherwise) and shared it between cast_ray and _render_megakernel.
Updated cast_ray (used for both primary camera rays and cast_ray_first_hit shadow rays in compute_lighting) to skip geoms when resolved rgba[3] == 0.0 and skip flexes when flex_rgba[flexid][3] == 0.0.
mujoco_warp/_src/render_test.py:
Added test_transparent_objects_do_not_occlude testing transparent occluders (geom_rgba, mat_rgba fallback, geom_rgba overriding an opaque material, and flex_rgba) in front of an opaque target across RGB (with shadows enabled), depth, and segmentation outputs.

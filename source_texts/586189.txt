The voxel compactor accumulated per-cell counts into a private buffer that was
discarded. Owned segments retain keys/indices/starts; existing CPU readback already
derives lengths from adjacent starts. Remove the unused per-point atomic additions
and explicit clear, and request four compatibility bytes instead of 4N. This saves
4N-4 scratch bytes per nonempty call. Pipeline layout, mark/scan/write, scalar sync,
explicit transfers and owned outputs retain their original contracts.
Validation: 380 native correctness tests, 50,785,230 raw parent/CPU/guard words,
24,036,610 complete parent/CPU values, exact fresh-pool savings, unchanged profiling,
warm/zero-budget/retained-output checks; Clippy, CPU-only, WASM, fmt and whitespace
pass. Final source/executable hashes match the measured versions.
Two balanced thirty-pair surveys cover twelve sparse/dense/tail cases. Write:
9 favorable / 14 inconclusive / 1 adverse; resident: 6 favorable / 18 inconclusive / 0 adverse; full:
3 favorable / 21 inconclusive / 0 adverse. All 72 rows, adverse/inconclusive results, timing boundaries
and shared-host limits are in notes/2026-10-17_unused_gpu_voxel_counts.md.
No portable or application-wide speed guarantee is inferred.

Summary
Two coupled fidelity upgrades to the Whitted-style deterministic ray tracer. Both noise-free (no RNG, no Monte Carlo, no accumulation).
1. Dome shadow + ambient occlusion
Adds a per-pixel shadow ray in the surface-normal direction (TMax = 2 m world-space) inside the closesthit's dome branch. Multiplies the dome contribution by the visibility result — nearby occluders darken the dome ambient, distant ones don't. Single-tap hemispheric estimate; standard "AO from sky" Whitted-style.
Effects on the World Lobby render:
Ceiling goes dark (occluded from above — physically correct).
Floor near windows stays lit (sky directly visible at 2m up).
Contact shadows under every ottoman/table.
Mean brightness drops from 118 → 85 — scene reads as a proper interior instead of "uniform sky lift".
The 2 m TMax keeps indoor scenes from going uniformly black: a 5 m ceiling beyond TMax doesn't kill the floor's ambient. For exterior scenes the long TMax is moot — there's nothing to hit. Deterministic by construction.
2. Translucent material rendering
Anyhit no longer IgnoreHits opacity < 1 or transmission materials; it only IgnoreHits true alpha cutout (MATERIAL_FLAG_ALPHA_TESTED). The closesthit then alpha-blends:
```
final = surface_lit × opacity + continuation × (1 - opacity) × baseColor
```
baseColor tint on the continuation models stained glass / tinted plastic.
Effects:
AmberGlass and other translucent materials are now visible instead of silently skipped.
Background shows through translucent geometry with baseColor-tinted attenuation.
Translucent objects cast shadows (anyhit still accepts the hit on shadow rays — known compromise; the §V2.B refraction follow-up will compensate with a proper Snell's-law glass path).
Recursion gate (payload.recursionDepth + 1 < 3) matches the mirror branch — stays under the pipeline's maxRecursionDepth = 3 cap.
Determinism re-verified
2 consecutive World Lobby renders → identical EXR hashes ✓

To address issue #320 I have attempted to add an sdf for super quadrics (superellipse in specific)
Presently the following form/types of super quadrics can be rendered
Epsilon = 0.1 (Square form)
Epsilon = 1.0 (Ellipsoid form)
Epsilon = 1.5
Epsilon = 2.0 (Diamond form)
Right now the default form for the superellipse actor is no. 4 (diamond form), however this can be changed to any other by simply commenting it out or directly changing the vec2 e (exponent) value.
The tests have also been modified as per requirement.
A review of this PR by any of the maintainers would be very helpful.
Any suggestions or guidance on how to proceed further will be much appreciated.
Thank you.

Hi @skoudoro,
Presently all the shader actors are rendered using common vertex and fragment shader files, because they have common parameters like directions, scales, centers and colors.
However the exponent parameter is exclusive to super ellipsoid and adding it directly to the present vert and frag files along with the common parameters does not make sense (for the remaining actors that do not use the exponent parameter).
I plan to resolve this by creating a separate vert and frag files for the super ellipsoid actor function corresponding to it's new actor function, so that we don't have pass the 'np.repeat exponent array' unnecessarily while rendering the actors that do not use the exponent parameter.

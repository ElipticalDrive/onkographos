Aim To enhance the effciency of intersection testing in ray tracing algorithm. Methods Through creating two new sub-bounding boxes with the midpoint of the inner bounding box's diagonal as their vertex,then the same operation was applied to all the new bounding boxes recursively. Results A new algorithm for accelerating ray tracing based on inner bounding box technology was presented. Conclusion The experiments showed that the new algorithm could reduce the time of intersection testing and accelerate ray tracing compared with the inner boun-ding box algorithm.

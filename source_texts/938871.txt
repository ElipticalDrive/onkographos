Computer-generated hologram (CGH) has been a growing area of research. A hidden surface removal method based on the point-based method, which is a rendering method for CGH, is also applied to calculation methods for mirror images on planes and Bézier surfaces. However, the method used for Bézier surfaces is based on Bézier clipping, which is used in computer graphics (CG). Therefore, the algorithm is complex, and the calculation is time consuming. In this study, we propose dividing a surface into many polygons and identifying mirror points. This simplifies calculation because the point light source only needs to be duplicated symmetrically on each polygon. Furthermore, full-color reconstructed image was created to enhance realism. We also demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method through optical experiments.

3D point clouds are widely adopted as a fundamental representation for 3D scene understanding, offering direct access to precise geometric information. However, their sparse and irregular nature restricts the capture of detailed appearance information and constrains the accurate perception of fine-grained boundaries and intricate object structures. In contrast, 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) models scenes as continuous Gaussian primitives with rich attributes such as scale and opacity. These attributes enable high-fidelity visual representation and help compensate for the lack of appearance cues in point clouds. However, as 3DGS is typically optimized for appearance, it may suffer from inconsistencies with underlying geometry. To bridge this gap, we propose a point–Gaussian fusion pipeline that integrates the strengths of both representations. The proposed pipeline enables bidirectional attribute transfer, complemented by filtering and pruning steps to ensure geometric consistency. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed fusion enhances 3D semantic segmentation performance, validating the effectiveness of combining geometric and appearance representations.

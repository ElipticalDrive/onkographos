The newly added converter to 3DGS works great!
I was able to open converted .ply in WebGL GS viewers.
```
python convert_3dgs.py -m PATH_TO_TRAINED_MODEL --prune_ratio 0.6
```
Additionally, with 3DGRUT converter .ply -> .usdz I was also able to convert it further to USDZ and open scene in Isaac Sim:
```
python -m threedgrut.export.scripts.ply_to_usd ./point_cloud.ply --output ../scene.usdz
```

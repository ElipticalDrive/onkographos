Follow the light through the implementation
Light-transport research connects materials, sampling, visibility and reconstruction. A technique can change image quality while moving cost between these stages, so the implementation needs to be evaluated as a system.
Breda’s first real-time path tracer dates to May 2021. We have since worked on ReSTIR direct lighting on a multi-layer BRDF, triangle and dynamic mesh lights, dynamic diffuse global illumination, irradiance caching and neural radiance caching, alongside guiding and ReSTIR GI investigations.
From research infrastructure to publications
Darius Bouma’s Diffuse Global Illumination chapter appears in GPU Zen 3. The Samsung/Traverse mobile NRC publication adds a hardware-aware investigation of neural global illumination, followed by the SIGGRAPH 2026 mobile GI course contribution.

Closes #1.
Generate two cavern chambers at different elevations, joined by a curved passage with a protected walking route. The sample opens in Unity 6000.4.0f1, creates the cave from a seed, and lets a first-person capsule explore it without required jumps or climbing.
Implementation
Unity-free .NET Standard 2.1 core: validated settings, seeded analytic density field, smooth floor descent, and marching-tetrahedra meshing with shared vertices and inward normals.
Local Unity packages plus a runnable Built-in Render Pipeline sample with mesh collision, basic procedural rock shading, fixed lighting, seed/depth/variation controls, route debugging, and an entrance reset.
Mesh/material cleanup and immediate retirement of old colliders on regeneration; invalid settings preserve the active cave.
Setup/architecture documentation, chamber screenshots, and GitHub Actions core checks.
Validation
Core checks passed across six seeds: repeatability, geometry integrity, closed manifold topology, and sampled route clearance/slope (1,212,202 assertions).
Unity Play Mode: both tests passed, including actual CharacterController traversal downhill and uphill across three seeds, wall/floor/ceiling collision, and regeneration behavior.
Both chambers rendered and visually inspected; settings panel checked in Play Mode.
Windows development player built successfully with zero errors and warnings.
Default seed: 24,813 vertices, 49,622 triangles.
Try it
Open samples/TwoChambers in Unity Hub, open Assets/Scenes/TwoChambers.unity, and press Play. WASD/arrows move; mouse looks; Escape releases the mouse; R resets to the entrance. Clone the entire repository so the local package paths resolve.
This is bounded, synchronous generation. Mining, editable terrain, streaming, branching networks, and flora/fauna are deferred. Traversal verification covers the central required route with default player settings, not every possible settings combination.
See docs/verification.md for reproduction steps and limits.

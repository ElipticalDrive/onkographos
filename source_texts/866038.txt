Mesh::clip() can return a cell volume that is too low, with status ok, in a unit cube. Found by the fuzz driver on branch mesh-fuzz (not pushed yet); library at 16e7769. This is separate from #29: the step here is dyadic, and the double and rational snaps agree on every coordinate.
Measured
"Exact" is rational half-space clipping in Python, ending with a check that no bisector of any of the 27 images cuts the cell. It shares no code with the library or oracle.hpp.

oracle.hpp agrees with exact on both. In the 907-particle case the other 7 sampled cells agree with exact to rounding. The cell there is an ordinary one, about 1/570 of the box.
The 907-particle case is the clean reproduction: the same mesh object had just passed an update. In the minimal case a full build of the same 76 particles refuses, 15 cells too far, so that case alone sits near the reach limit; clip() gave status ok on cell 11 regardless. Delta debugging kept the signature but found a smaller error, so -5.0 % belongs to the full case only.
Not known
The cause. The kernel has not been read for this. Both errors are low, so the cell lost volume, not gained it.
How often. 2 of about 12500 fuzz cases flag clip_volume in a cube, the other at 1.57e-6, likely rounding. 18 more flags in non-dyadic boxes are not triaged and may be this, Silent wrong cell volumes, up to 9.4 %, in boxes with a non-dyadic grid step #29 or both.
Whether a shell_voronoi caller other than clip() is affected. The persistent mesh was exact in these runs, and the driver's other checks of it passed.

Cause found, in the rounded kernel, not in clip() itself.
On the 907-particle case cell 486 has a clump of about 20 particles at distance 0.084, separated by about 1e-8, so their bisectors nearly coincide. In the thread kernel a new vertex comes from intersect, a 3 x 3 solve on the planes, not from the cut edge. At one cut the cell's radius goes from 0.14 to 0.85, which a true cut cannot do. A vertex then sits 0.066 of the box beyond a plane, the polyhedron is broken but still walks, and the status stays ok: closure 2.29e-2 against 2.5e-17 for the exact cell.
The outcome depends on the last bits: the same input gives status degenerate with fused multiply-add off, and ok with it on (-std=gnu++20, the CMake default here).
Other callers of shell_voronoi, read from the code:
Mesh<3>::rebuild: same rounded kernel, but its rings go through the exact check of tet_start, and bad cells are rebuilt exactly. Not affected. The same case's updates pass the oracle.
Mesh<2> uses polygon_clip, which falls back to exact predicates in its cut test. Not this path.
clip() was the only caller that returned rounded cells unchecked.
Fix on a local branch, not merged: clip() builds exact cells. Cell 486 then gives 0.00176420419322682 against 0.00176420419322684 from the oracle. CUDA and the cost are not measured yet.

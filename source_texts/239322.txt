Tracks aether-lang-dev/aether#2556: contrib.vulkan has no VK_EXT_mesh_shader bindings (it is generated from registry 1.3.204).
The cluster geometry work for #536 draws clusters with mesh shaders where the GPU offers them (NVIDIA Turing+, AMD RDNA2+, Intel Arc), detected at device creation. Compute culling with indirect draws stays the universal path (MoltenVK, older GPUs). Once aether releases the bindings, AETHER_REF in .github/workflows/ci.yml gets bumped with a line saying why.

Motivation
Reduce chunk remesh and GPU upload cost by meshing at 16-voxel section granularity and avoid full-chunk rebuilds for localized edits.
Lower GPU/CPU workload by quantizing vertex data, enabling back-face culling and merging non-textured chunk geometry into fewer batched meshes.
Improve entity rendering efficiency for many small NPCs by switching gnomes to GPU instancing.
Make remesh scheduling and culling more precise so updates and occlusion decisions are less wasteful.
Description
Introduced sectioned chunk meshing with CHUNK_SECTION_HEIGHT, CHUNK_SECTION_COUNT, sectionIndexFromY and computeChunkSectionHash, and updated remesh APIs to accept a sectionIndex (requestChunkRemesh, requestChunkAndNeighborsRemesh, rebuildDirtyChunkMeshes, etc.).
Implemented batched remesh tracking and deferred batching (batchedChunkRemeshNeeds, markBatchedChunkRemeshNeed, applyBlockUpdateBatch) to coalesce multiple block updates and remesh only affected sections.
Added mesh vertex bucket pooling (meshVertexBucketPool, acquireMeshVertexBucket, releaseMeshVertexBucket) to reduce GC churn during meshing.
Quantized / packed vertex attributes: positions packed to Int16 with POSITION_QUANT_SCALE, normals to Int8 and optional UVs to Uint16 to reduce memory and transfer sizes; section-local origins used to allow quantization.
Added chunk mesh batching to merge non-textured, non-uv chunk geometry into aggregated meshes (chunkBatchGroup, rebuildChunkMeshBatches, maybeUpdateChunkMeshBatching), with an interval CHUNK_BATCH_REBUILD_INTERVAL_MS and dirty tracking (markChunkBatchDirty).
Enabled GPU back-face culling for renderer context (gl.enable(gl.CULL_FACE) etc.) to reduce fragment workload.
Converted gnome NPCs to use THREE.InstancedMesh with a capacity constant GNOME_INSTANCE_CAPACITY and an instancing setup (ensureGnomeInstancing, updateGnomes) to update per-instance transforms efficiently.
Refactored chunk group userData to store per-section mesh hashes and per-section VBO keys; meshing now emits section-prefixed keys and stores section-aligned meshes.
Adjusted neighbor/remesh logic and updateChunkAndNeighbors signature to include ly for precise section touch detection and neighbor section handling.
Added torch scanning helper collectChunkTorchPositions and updated torch light sync to re-scan by chunk after meshing; fixed torch light placement and caps.
Minor gameplay/placement tweaks: expanded wolf pack biome spawning to include Snowy Plains and other small fixes (e.g., initializing generated chunk data array with .fill(0)).
Testing
No automated tests were added or executed as part of this change.

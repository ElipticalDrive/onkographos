Changes
Redistribute up to 20% of bright outdoor direct lighting into ambient fill during daylight. This preserves per-channel ambient + diffuse energy and therefore sun-facing material brightness, while reducing excessively black unlit faces on terrain and characters.
Fade correction in/out over sunrise and sunset according to the visual clock; preserve nights and fixed-night zones (e.g. Duskwood).
Skip indoor and underwater scenes to avoid flattening intentionally low-key lighting.
Keep the existing applyLightHeadroom limiting after redistribution to prevent hue shift from clipping.
Add regression tests for preserved peak, filled shadow side, night/time transitions and no change to dim lighting.
Additional screenshot / follow-up
The second user screenshot shows high-contrast white highlights on dark character armor. This could be a material/specular or model normal-map artifact; not confirmed and not fixed by this PR. Needs an in-game reproduction and inspection of character shading/material flags before changing shader defaults.
Verification
Source-level checks completed. C++ tests and in-game Vulkan rendering have not been executed in this environment. Build and run test_light_headroom plus visual compare in sunny areas, Duskwood, dungeons and underwater before merging.
Defaults
This is a renderer-level default and does not depend on a user setting or config reset.

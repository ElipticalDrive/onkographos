3D CUBE LUT support
The existing LUT selector now accepts 3D .cube files alongside PNG strips. CUBE samples remain float32 and use manual trilinear interpolation; PNG decoding and lookup calculations remain unchanged. The optional file and amount controls, shortcuts and saved filenames are preserved.
Supports table sizes 2–65, comments/TITLE, UTF-8 BOM, DOMAIN_MIN/MAX and Resolve LUT_3D_INPUT_RANGE. 1D/combined and malformed files are refused with a translated card message and detailed log. Help and error copy cover all 21 menu languages.

In this work, we present a novel web-based framework for interactive 3D visualization and exploration of large-scale multivariate oceanographic data, using Red Sea datasets as a case study. The framework directly processes NetCDF files and supports advanced volume rendering techniques, enabling domain experts to investigate key environmental variables within the 3D context of ocean. A central contribution is the integration of multivariable color compositing within the volume rendering pipeline, allowing users to intuitively explore complex inter-variable relationships. Leveraging modern GPU capabilities with WebGL, the framework enables in-browser rendering of large-scale ocean datasets without requiring specialized software or hardware. Interactive spatial filtering by variable thresholds, longitude, latitude, and depth, combined with coordinated views through a parallel coordinates plot, supports comprehensive multivariate analysis. The framework was evaluated by four domain experts, who confirmed its effectiveness in facilitating the exploration and interpretation of complex oceanographic data.

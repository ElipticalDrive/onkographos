What and why
Each chunk mesh had the faces at the chunk borders, also between two solid voxels of two chunks. The camera cannot see these faces, but the GPU drew them. This pull request hides the faces between chunks.
Fixes #48
Changes
GreedyMesher.Mesh(voxels, world, chunkPosition): for each direction, the mesher gets the neighbor chunk from the voxel world one time. It reads the border voxels of that chunk. A neighbor chunk that the world does not have is air. Without a world, all the voxels outside the chunk are air, as before.
WorldBuilder:
It generates the chunks before their mesh, and meshes a chunk when all its neighbors of the world are generated. With the order X, then Z, the generation is approximately one column of chunks (32 chunks) before the mesh.
Thus each chunk gets one mesh, and no chunk is meshed again during the load. The first call generates the first column (approximately 12 ms in Release).
At the edge of the world, the voxels outside are air.
VoxelWorld.GetChunksToMesh gives the chunks whose mesh changes when a voxel changes: its chunk, and each neighbor chunk that touches it (up to 3 for a corner voxel).
VoxelEditor (app) meshes all these chunks again after an edit.

The time of the world build does not change: the mesher reads one neighbor chunk for each direction, and the reads of the border voxels are a small part of the work. The chunk benchmarks do not use a world, thus they do not change.
Tests
ChunkBorderTests (new):
Two solid chunks side by side: each chunk has 5 quads, and no quad on the side between them.
A missing neighbor: the border faces stay, and the mesh is the same as without a world.
GetChunksToMesh for a voxel inside a chunk, on a border, on two corners, at the edge of the world, and outside the chunks.
Acceptance test: a world of 4 × 4 × 4 chunks with random voxels, 20 rounds of 10 random edits on the chunk borders. After each round, the chunks of GetChunksToMesh get a new mesh. Then all the meshes must cover each visible face of the world exactly once, with no face between two solid voxels. The coverage oracle of the tests can now read the neighbor chunks. If GetChunksToMesh gives no neighbor, the test fails.
WorldBuilderTests:
Each chunk comes one time, and its mesh is the mesh in the complete world (worlds of 4 and 36 chunks).
The world has fewer quads than the chunks meshed alone.
In total: 414 tests pass.
Manual test (Xvfb and lavapipe, Debug, synchronization validation)
The world shows no hole. The log gives "1024 chunks and 6040 quads".
Three edits at the chunk corner (0, 36, 0) changed 3, 2 and 2 chunks. The hole shows its walls.
The validation layers reported 0 errors and 0 warnings.
Known limit: pixels in the cracks of T-junctions
I compared the image of the start camera with the image before this change. 40 pixels of 921,600 are different. 4 of them now show the sky.
These pixels are on the edges where the corner of one quad touches the side of another quad (a T-junction). The rasterizer can leave a gap of less than one pixel there. Before this change, the hidden faces at the chunk borders were behind some of these gaps. This is a known effect of greedy meshing, also inside each chunk. A later issue can remove it, for example with a small overlap of the quads or with indices that share the vertices.

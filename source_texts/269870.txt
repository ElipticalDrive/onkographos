Stage 1 of ADR-0061 (the voxel far field the user chose for distant foliage on 2026-10-26). Stacked on #111.
What
Where the cluster levels' edge collapses stall on many small closed pieces (the landscape's pine sapling stops at 95,716 triangles; the pines at hundreds of thousands a tree), a group becomes voxels instead, as UE 5.7's Nanite foliage does.
Records (render/voxel.h):
32-byte bricks of 4 × 4 × 4 power-of-two cells with a 64-bit mask.
16-byte voxels, each holding a footprint, albedo and occlusion, roughness and metallic, and a two-sided vMF lobe of normals.
The footprint has three shapes: a box for a surface, a capsule along a needle's axis, and an ellipsoid inscribed in a box for an aggregate.
Voxelizer (cluster_voxel.c): 16 × 16 jittered rays per axis per cell measure coverage (the union of the pieces' projections) and look (the rays' first and last hits). The footprint:
keeps a surface's bounds, so sheets stay watertight;
gives a needle a capsule between its hits' extremes, with the radius of their spread;
sizes an aggregate's ellipsoid to the coverages, at a place hashed from the cell.
Levels (cluster_lod.c):
Which groups: aggregate groups only, of at least 256 triangles whose pieces average at most 32. They become voxels where the simplification stalls or its quadric error passes the cells'.
First cells: the finest at least twice the error so far, with at most 4× the triangle count, so the error steps on rather than jumping.
Coarser voxel levels: each halves its primitives.
Sources: every level re-voxelizes the triangles where voxels began, each in exactly one cluster.
C twins: the traversal (Amanatides & Woo over the mask, each shape's intersection), and the shading model's terms:
the visible lobe as an SG product, with Hill's fitted clamped cosine;
GGX widened by the lobe;
SH bands convolved with it.
The renderer still refuses voxel clusters (vertex_count == 0) until stage 3 draws them. The cooker does not pass a sampler yet (stage 2), so no cooked content changes.

```
Where the levels' edge collapses stall on many small closed pieces (the
landscape's pines stop at hundreds of thousands of triangles a tree), a
group of the cluster hierarchy becomes voxels instead, as Nanite's foliage
does: bricks of 4 x 4 x 4 cells of a power-of-two size, each occupied cell
a voxel of what its geometry looks like from outside at the cell's scale.

- Records (render/voxel.h): 32-byte bricks with a 64-bit mask; 16-byte
  voxels holding a footprint (a box in 32nds of the cell for a surface, a
  capsule along a needle's axis, an ellipsoid inscribed in a box for an
  aggregate), albedo and occlusion, roughness and metallic, and a
  two-sided vMF lobe of normals.
- The voxelizer (cluster_voxel.c): 16 x 16 jittered rays along each axis
  of each cell against its triangles measure coverage (the union of the
  pieces' projections) and look (the rays' first and last hits); the
  footprint keeps a surface's bounds, gives a needle a capsule between
  its hits' extremes of the radius of their spread, and sizes an
  aggregate's ellipsoid to the coverages at a place hashed from the cell.
- The levels (cluster_lod.c): an aggregate group (pieces of at most 32
  triangles on average, 256 triangles or more) becomes voxels where its
  simplification stalls or its quadric error passes the cells'; the first
  cells are the finest at least twice the error so far with at most four
  times the triangles' count, coarser voxel groups halve their
  primitives, and each level voxelizes again the triangles where voxels
  began, each in one cluster.
- The traversal's C twin and the shading model's terms: the visible
  lobe as a spherical Gaussian product with Hill's fitted clamped cosine
  (0.7 % mean error against quadrature, 5 % within 60 degrees, 8.3 % at
  grazing views), GGX widened by the lobe, harmonics convolved with it.

Measured: sheets stop every ray through them; a needle cloud keeps its

foliage mesh's voxel levels at 300 m and 1.2 km cover what level 0 covers.
```

fix(render): the voxelizer builds under GCC and Clang

```
C17 does not convert a pointer to an array into a pointer to a const
array (C23 does), which -Wpedantic reports on the voxelizer's 3x3
parameters: they are no longer const. The voxel tests' float-to-double
conversions are explicit for -Wdouble-promotion, the one bare isfinite
is nph_f64_is_finite, and three comments' non-ASCII characters were
double-encoded.
```

fix(render): a few voxels across the mesh's origin still coarsen
…
e9a2c19
```
A coarser voxel group's cell was the smallest whose occupied cells fit
half its input primitives, searched by doubling up to 1e30, which failed
with NPH_RESULT_OVERFLOW for a group of fewer than sixteen voxels across
the mesh's origin: the grid of every cell size divides each axis at zero,
so such a group lies in two cells along each axis it crosses however
large they grow. The landscape's pine sapling, whose trunk stands at the
origin, failed to cook with voxels this way. Past the sources' reach the
count falls no further, and the search now takes the first cell past it.
```

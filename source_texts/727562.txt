Summary
Matrix transposition and inversion are critical graphics operations used for converting screen-space coordinates back into 3D world space (ray picking), inverting camera view matrices, and computing normal matrices.
What to do
In include/mathlib/mat4.h, implement a method to transpose the 4x4 matrix (swapping rows and columns).
Implement determinant calculation and full 4x4 matrix inversion using the adjugate matrix method.
Ensure singular (non-invertible) matrices where determinant is zero are handled gracefully without division by zero.
Testing
Add a unit test in tests/test_math.cpp asserting that multiplying an invertible matrix (translation or rotation) by its inverse yields the identity matrix.

Problem (#913). Shaft pixels in scattering media are 100-500x noisier than Cycles at equal spp. Medium NEE (raytracer.h bounded-media block and world-fog block; GPU stage_advance.cu / stage_volume_hetero.cu) runs only at a real free-flight scatter vertex, and absorption terminates the path (pAbsorb = σ_a/σ̄; 0.5 for grey 0.5 fog with black Absorption Color). Only a few of 64 camera samples per shaft pixel ever connect to the lamp.
Numbers (pkg289 ladder, .astroray_plan/docs/pkg289-medium-chromatic-noise-ladder.md). Spot through homogeneous fog box, 64 spp, 12 seeds:
Normalized per-pixel variance on the gated ROI: Astroray CPU 0.53/0.50/0.88 vs Cycles 5.2 0.0052/0.0050/0.0049 (100x / 180x). On the bright top-of-shaft ROI it is ~500x.
Means agree to 1 % and variance converges as 1/spp, so the estimator is unbiased but inefficient.
The B channel carries an extra 2x from the pkg206 luminance-weighted hero-λ pdf (seen on plain surfaces too). That turns the sparse contributions into green/purple speckle.
Fix. Sample a direct-light point on every medium segment, independent of the scatter decision:
equiangular toward the chosen light, MIS (balance/power) with distance sampling conditioned on the segment (Kulla & Fajardo 2012, "Importance Sampling Techniques for Path Tracing in Participating Media"; Cycles kernel/integrator/shade_volume.h volume direct-light sampling, Apache-2.0);
NEE there, weighted by the segment's scatter probability and transmittance;
apply absorption as a throughput weight instead of analog termination;
the analog scatter vertex keeps only the phase-sampled continuation (its lamp-hit MIS stays complementary to the decoupled NEE).
Blender 5.2 behaves the same with material DISTANCE or MULTIPLE_IMPORTANCE (nv 0.00130 vs 0.00129), so Cycles always uses the decoupled direct-light sample. equiangularSample/equiangularPdf already exist in include/astroray/volume/volume_transport.h (oracle-only since pkg268). Mirror on GPU; mind the REG-254 shade kernel (the volume stages are separate kernels).
Gate. Un-xfail tests/test_pkg289_medium_chromatic_variance.py::test_913_shaft_variance_vs_cycles_{cpu,gpu} (nv <= 4x Cycles at 64 spp). The convergence/mean gate in the same file must stay green.
Related. This likely also explains #922 (area-lamp variance in media 2-4x Cycles, which blocks pkg290 clamp parity): the same NEE-only-at-scatter structure. Re-measure #922 after the fix.

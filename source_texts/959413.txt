## What This Is

This project turns a random seed into a structured, inspectable industrial environment. A seeded generator samples a corridor topology on a 6.4 m grid, attaches semantic room modules, records every placement in JSON, and writes a relocatable USD stage.

Version 1.0 deliberately focuses on a stable single-floor problem. Stairs, elevators, and multi-floor traversal are reserved for the next iteration, so the public showcase has a clear and reproducible scope.

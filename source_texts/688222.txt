This paper presents an analytical study of the effect of Gaussian- and LorentzianA mathematic
shaped line spread functions in non-coherent noise-free imaging systems.
development is given for the calculation of the Modulation Transfer Function (MTF).
This technique is used to calculate the MTF for two-point and periodic objects using
Gaussian and Lorentzian resolution functions.
Figures and graphs are used to illustrate the comparison of the results.
Relationships between the results obtained are
developed that are useful in the interpretation of experiments used to determine the
resolution of experimental systems.
The development covers only noise-free, incoherent
one-dimensional systems.

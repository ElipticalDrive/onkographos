There is a ray start and end calculation in the fragment shader for volume rendering in #553. This ray start and end is computed to find where the ray enters and exits a box. At the edges or corners of the box, the ray can end up with length 0 (in fact the exit point can end up being before the entry point, but we're clamping these values so the ray instead just has zero length). The rays having length 0 happens often, but causes no issue for the most part. In some cases though, it results in a black line through the rendering that lines up exactly with those edges (the red lines in the images below are the rays that get computed with length 0 in the fragment shader).
There used to be an issue where there could also be kind of halo lines that extended out of the volume, also on the edges of the box. But I think clamping tExit and tEnter has fixed this.

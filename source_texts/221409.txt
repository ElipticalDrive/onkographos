Vulkan requires every geometry shader to declare its invocation count (OpExecutionMode Invocations, [instance] in HLSL). The SDSL compiler never wrote it, so the validation layer rejects every pipeline with a geometry shader:
VUID-VkPipelineShaderStageCreateInfo-stage-00715
The drivers I tried (NVIDIA, lavapipe) run the shader once per primitive anyway, but other drivers may not. It affects voxel GI voxelization, CameraCube and the Bepu debug shaders. glslang, used until 4.3, always wrote it.
The fix
Geometry shader entry points get Invocations 1.
ShaderCompilerVersion.Shader 4 → 5, so cached SPIR-V is compiled again.
Tests
GeometryShaderDeclaresOneInvocation.
Stride.Shaders.Tests pass, and Stride.Graphics.Tests.10_0 passes on Direct3D 11 / 12 and Vulkan (WARP / lavapipe).
The voxel GI case of MaterialTransparencyTests now passes on Vulkan (lavapipe, RTX 5080). It stays skipped here: the Direct3D 12 fix that follows removes the skip.

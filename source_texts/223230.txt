Before: VoxelSieve Studio showed a dataset only as 2D slices.
After: the "Ansicht" stage has a "Schnitt | 3D" toggle. The 3D view ray-casts the part in the browser as a surface or translucent volume, with pores and loosened zones highlighted, a cut plane along x, and orbit/zoom. It stays fast on huge scans because it only ever loads a coarse mip level (every dimension ≤ 256 by default).
How: readVolumePreview picks the finest level that fits, reads it in parallel over z slices and returns uint8 grey (windowed air to material) plus an overlay byte per voxel. vs-studio serves it as binary at GET /api/volume with dims, level, voxel size and window in headers. resources/ui/volume.js uploads both as 3D textures and ray-casts them in a WebGL2 fragment shader; the surface threshold is suggested from the histogram peaks. Tests cover the preview against the synthetic ground truth defects and the HTTP endpoint; the README, CHANGELOG and ADR 0008 describe the view.

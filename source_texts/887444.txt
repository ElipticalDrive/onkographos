Serialize texture sets before including them in DMESH hashes
For a polygon asset carrying any of the new maps, write_dmesh() now stores a content hash that includes the texture set, but its material writer and version selection still omit all four maps and their projection parameters. On reload, read_dmesh() reconstructs materials without those fields, and validate_polygon_asset() rejects the file with HashMismatch. Extend DMESH serialization alongside this hash change so textured polygon assets can actually be saved and loaded.

Include edge texels when reducing odd-sized images
For an odd source dimension greater than one, flooring the destination size and sampling only 2*x and 2*x+1 discards the final source column or row entirely. This also affects larger images once their mip chain reaches an odd dimension. A compiled check with a 3×1 data image containing black, black, and white produces a final mip of 0 instead of approximately 1/3, so distant textures lose image content and change their average appearance. Use a reduction that accounts for the entire source extent.

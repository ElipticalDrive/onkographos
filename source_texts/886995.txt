Hi, could you please share more details? For example, the number of projections, volume resolution, what your volume looks like, the training curve, the total number of Gaussians after training, etc. My initial guess for the blurry results is: (1) The projections do not fully cover the entire volume for reconstruction, (2) There are too few projections.
In terms of resolution control, do you mean the resolution of volume? It can be edited in meta_data.json of the dataset.
```
"nVoxel": [256, 256, 256],
```

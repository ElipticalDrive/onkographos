Windows 10 openmp 3.0~b334996c
The reference planes are centered on the origin and displayed proportionally to the view (window) size, regardless of zoom.
This is helpful for orientation when orbiting in 3D view since the full planes are always visible (around the origin).
When zooming in on elements at a distance from the origin, the reference planes are not visible anymore.
Two ideas:
show infinite reference planes (only works in 2D / view aligned to planes)
show reference planes within the bounding box of elements (also works in 3D)

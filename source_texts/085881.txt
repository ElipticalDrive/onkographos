Hi everyone!
If anyone is interested in using instant-ngp for Unity and for immersive VR, we released a Unity plugin for instant-ngp based on OpenGL and OpenVR.
At the moment, basic 6-DOF locomotion and low-latency stereo rendering supported by DLSS is already available.
However, we are continuously developing more features, such as real-time SLAM capture from a video see-through head-mounted display such as the Oculus Quest Pro.
Feel free to check out our repo. We are looking forward to how the community would use VR NERF :)
Link to the repo: https://github.com/uhhhci/immersive-ngp

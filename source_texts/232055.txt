Just a couple of notes:
For very large scenes, especially around 200M splats, support for alpha > 1 is particularly important for visual quality. As far as I know, only this converter’s tile pipeline and Spark/Gaussian-Splat-Lite ’s RAD pipeline currently generate this type of data, and only Spark and Gaussian-Splat-Lite support rendering those values. I think support for alpha > 1 is one of the most important parts of the SOG v3 proposal.
File size is another major consideration. With SH3, uncompressed KHR_gaussian_splatting data can be roughly 10× larger than its SPZ-compressed equivalent. Downloading the content can account for around 90% of the total loading time, so that difference has a substantial impact on both loading performance and bandwidth costs when streaming scenes at this scale.

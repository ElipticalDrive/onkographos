Efficient representation and extraction of 2-manifold isosurfaces using kd-trees - In this paper, we propose the utilization of a kd-tree based hierarchy as an implicit object representation. Compared to an octree, the kd-tree based hierarchy is superior in terms of adaptation to the object surface.

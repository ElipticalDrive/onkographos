Contextual Ambient Occlusion: A volumetric rendering technique that ...
Abstract In this paper, we present a new volumetric ambient occlusion algorithm called Contextual Ambient Occlusion (CAO) that supports real-time clipping. The algorithm produces ambient occlusion images of exactly the same quality as Local Ambient Occlusion (LAO) while enabling real-time modification to the shape used to clip the volume.

Hyperstreamlines
•
•
•
•
•
•
An extension of streampolygon technique of a vector field to the case of a tensor
field
 Provide continuous representation of a tensor field
Constructed by creating a streamline through one of three eigenfields, and
sweeping a geometric primitive (ellipse or cross) along the streamline
Ellipse:
 Sweeping the ellipse along the eigenfield streamline result in a tubular shape
 Other two eigenvectors define major and minor axes of the ellipse
Cross:
 Sweeping the cross results in a helical shape since the cross arms may rotate
in some tensor fields
 Other two eigenvectors control length and orientation of the cross arms
Color and trajectory of a hyperstreamline represent the longitudinal eigenvector,
and the cross-section encodes two transverse eigenvectors
Hyperstreamlines can be called major, medium or minor hyperstreamlines
depending on the longitudinal eigenvector field.

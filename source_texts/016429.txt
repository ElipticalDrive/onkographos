With the development of the computer technology and the sensor technology, the volume of the flight data is so huge that efficient loss less compression is necessary, which makes the flight data more applicable for Flight Operational Quality Assurance. The loss less compression of the QAR data using lifting integer wavelet transform is put forward. And it needs no floating-point operation and easy to be realized which ensure the rapidity of the algorithm. Different experiments on a variety of QAR data are carried out, the results evaluate the effectiveness and feasibility of the lifting integer wavelet transform. It reduces the data storage space, gets higher compression ratio and lower reconstruction error.

Path tracing of the main view's lighting in XRenderD3D12 (d3d12_PathTracing): the opaque surfaces are lit by paths instead of the game's baked light (light maps, the ambient constant, the pre-lit terrain and vegetation). The light comes from the sun, the lamps (the frame's and the level's static ones, the bake-only DLF_LM lamps too), the sky, the emissive surfaces and the bounces between them. The raster keeps the primary surfaces (a G-buffer from the forward pass); water, glass, particles and the weapon are drawn over it as before.
d3d12_PathTracing 0 (the default) leaves the frames as they were, bit for bit; 1 is real time, 2 the reference, which adds up its frames while the view stays. It needs DXR 1.1 and r_HDRRendering 1, and is in the game's menu (Advanced Video Options, Path Tracing: None / Real Time / Reference).
Measured on a Radeon RX 7900 XTX at 3840 x 2160, very high, with d3d12_HDROutput 0 and r_VSync 0.
Steps
Infrastructure (0b62817): the cvars, the frame's lamps on the GPU (SPTLight, root SRV t2 space5), motion vectors and depth without an upscaler. Fix: no AO worked under an upscaler, since the AO target was made before the scene's targets were bound.
Materials and direct light (beff516): PathShade.hlsli, the path material in SRayInstance (lit, terrain, vegetation, unlit). The terrain's sector texture is divided by the light baked into it (d3d12_PTTerrainDelight), the vegetation's vertex colours are baked again without light. The level's static lamps within d3d12_PTLightDistance, at most 128. d3d12_RTDebug 8-10.
G-buffer (3a63125): the general program's XR_PT permutation writes the albedo, the normal, the fog's share and the material; the light, projector and terrain shadow passes, the screen space AO and the rays' GI are not drawn. d3d12_PTDebug 1-4.
The path tracer (1a8220a): CSTrace (a lamp of 16 candidates by RIS, cosine bounces, the sky on a miss), CSTemporal, CSFilter, the composite before the blended surfaces; the reference with Russian roulette (d3d12_PTReferenceBounces). d3d12_PTDebug 5-7.
The game's ambient light in the paths (41d9b61): rooms lit through a window were near black, the reference too. A ray that finds nothing within d3d12_PTAmbientRange (2 m) brings the raster's ambient of the point it leaves: the raster's ambient times its occlusion, with the near bounces physical.
Highlights (26655d6): the raster's highlight colour in a fifth G-buffer target; CSSpecular makes the sun's and the lamps' highlights as the raster does, with rays towards the lamps.
NVIDIA NRD (5698417): REBLUR_DIFFUSE through our own host (NrdHost.cpp, no NRI). NRD, ShaderMake and MathLib are git submodules in third_party: the repository has their links, not their files (NRD's license lets it be distributed only built into an application); LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md gives the matching permission. Without the submodules the renderer is built without it and d3d12_PTDenoiser simple is used.
AMD FidelityFX Denoiser 1.2 (509e02a): its shadows denoiser for the sun's ray mask (d3d12_RTSunDenoise 2); third_party/fidelityfx-denoiser is a submodule (MIT).
Radiance cache (025b47f): a hash grid over the world (2^20 entries, 0.25 m cells near the camera growing with the distance, one per axis a face leans along), updated by a path of 3 bounces from a pixel of each 8 x 8 and blended over 32 frames; the paths' last bounce takes its light. Rooms and tunnels come closer to the reference for about 0.6 ms at 2560 x 1440. d3d12_PTDebug 11.
What is behind the camera (16df7c9): the sun's cascades give the scene for rays only what is towards the sun, so the light changed as the view turned. The static objects and entities behind the camera within d3d12_PTBehind (40 m) are noted for the scene for rays without being drawn; Research's hall has 259 instances instead of 42.
Performance (4adae7a): the cache holds all the light that comes to a point (the sun's and a lamp's too), so the paths' last hit traces no rays towards them; d3d12_PTCheckerboard traces half the pixels each frame, the others take their four neighbours' light by plane and normal before the denoiser (NRD's own checkerboard made the hut 4 % darker).
Menu and statistics (d7a62d8): assets/scripts/07-video-path-tracing.patch, a Path tracing line in r_DisplayInfo, the module README's own section.
Debug views (5e4d2dc): d3d12_RTDebug 1 colours the instances by their mesh and their place to 0.25 m instead of the index in the frame's TLAS, which moves with the frame's list; 11 colours the material the rays take of the hit's instance. Checked with them: the static instances keep their mesh, place and material from frame to frame, standing and walking; only the moving creatures change place.
Checks
Bit for bit: with d3d12_PathTracing 0 the PerfSweep frames 181 (the r_DisplayInfo panel masked) are equal to the build before at each path tracer step (the debug views' commit changes only what d3d12_RTDebug draws).
Debug layer (XRENDERD3D12_DEBUG=2): only the two earlier warnings about buffers' initial state.
Frame rate, the flights over Pier, River and Swamp at 3840 x 2160 with FSR Quality (the path tracer at 2560 x 1440): 107, 99 and 93 FPS, 1 % lows 77, 60 and 64; the goal was 60 FPS or more. NRD costs about 0.3-0.4 ms against simple.

Research's hall is darker than the raster: the lamps' falloff and brightness against the light maps.
Some surfaces shimmer while walking in real time; their instances and materials hold still (above), the cause is being looked for.

```
PathShade.hlsli: a point of the scene as the path tracer takes it, its
linear albedo and emission (PathSurface) and the light of the sun and the
lamps on it, each by a ray (PathDirect). SRayInstance carries the draw's
path material (lit, terrain, vegetation, unlit; albedo, specular), set by
Draw3D while the scene for rays is captured; the terrain also gets the
instance mask bit XR_RAY_TERRAIN, which the other rays leave out.

The terrain's sector texture is divided by the light baked into it,
fBakeLight * (1 + (r - 1) N.L V) / r, V the sun seen over the terrain
alone, r = d3d12_PTTerrainDelight. With the path tracer the vegetation's
vertex colours are baked without light (LeafBuffer.cpp keeps the custom
lighting's parameters and bakes the buffers again on a switch).

The lamps: besides the frame's, all the level's static lights
(I3DEngine::GetStaticLightSources) within d3d12_PTLightDistance, nearest
first, at most 128; the frame has only 1-5 of them, and the bake-only
DLF_FAKE | DLF_LM lamps not at all. The graphics root signature also gets
the lamps' SRV t2 space5.

New: d3d12_PTLightDistance, d3d12_PTSun, d3d12_PTLamps, d3d12_PTEmission,
d3d12_PTTerrainDelight; d3d12_RTDebug 8, 9, 10 show the camera rays' hits'
albedo, emission and direct light. With d3d12_PathTracing 0 the picture
is the same.
```

feat(XRenderD3D12): the path tracer lights the main view
…
1a8220a
```
d3d12_PathTracing 1 lights the opaque list by paths in real time. After
the G-buffer the kernels of Shaders/PathTrace.hlsl run with rays:
CSTrace a path from each pixel (a lamp among 16 candidates by
resampled importance sampling with a ray towards it, then
d3d12_PTBounces cosine bounces, the sky on a miss), CSTemporal its
history where the surface was a frame before, CSFilter a filter on the
pixel's plane and normal (d3d12_PTDenoiser simple or none). The
composite (Shaders/PathComposite.hlsl) adds the albedo times the sun's
light and the paths' to the colour before the blended surfaces, the
weapon, the water and the particles.

A fourth G-buffer target keeps the sun's visibility as the raster has
it (its maps, the cascades and their mask by rays from all casters).
d3d12_PathTracing 2 is the reference: it adds up its frames while the
view and the settings stay, d3d12_PTReferenceBounces bounces with
Russian roulette, no clamp or filter. The game's light multipliers are
light to the power 2.2; the terrain's albedo is divided by its baked
light in linear light. Where the frame has no sun there is no sky
either. The grass that fades is alpha tested among the opaque surfaces
while the path tracer is on. d3d12_PTDebug 5-7 show the paths' light,
one frame's paths and the sun's visibility.

With d3d12_PathTracing 0 the frames are the same as before.
```

feat(XRenderD3D12): the highlights in the path tracer
…
26655d6
```
The path tracer's surfaces had no highlights: the G-buffer took the
specular flags off with the rest of the light. Its fifth target (RGBA8,
SV_Target6) now has the raster's highlight colour (2 x the specular
colour x gloss of the specular templates, the fixed function's material
light about as much) / 2, blended as the albedo. A new kernel,
CSSpecular, makes the highlights as the raster does, in the game's
colour: that colour times the sun's specular colour times the visibility
the raster found, and each lamp's (SPTLight::vSpecular, m_SpecColor)
times its falloff, times saturate(R.V)^32; a lamp's where a ray towards
it reaches it, 4 rays a pixel at most, only for a highlight of 1/512 or
more. They are not noisy, so they need no history or filter, and the
reference has them as they are. The composite adds them times the fog's
share. d3d12_PTSpecular (1) scales them, d3d12_PTDebug 9 shows the
highlight colour and 10 the highlights.

With d3d12_PathTracing 0 the frames are the same as before.
```

feat(XRenderD3D12): NVIDIA NRD denoises the path tracer
…
5698417
```
NVIDIA's NRD (v4.18.0) with ShaderMake and MathLib are git submodules
in third_party: the repository has their links, not their files, as
NRD's license lets it be distributed only built into an application.
LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md gives the matching permission; README.md and
AGENTS.md say how to fetch them (git submodule update --init).

CMake builds NRD as a static library with its DXIL shaders (ShaderMake
with the Windows SDK's dxc, nothing downloaded, the MultiThreaded
runtime); without the submodules or with CMake older than 3.30 the
renderer is built without it. NrdHost.cpp runs its passes on the
renderer's device without NRI: root signatures and pipeline states from
its pipeline descriptions, its textures as compute targets, cached
descriptor tables and the barriers each dispatch needs.

d3d12_PTDenoiser nrd, the default where NRD is built in, puts its
REBLUR_DIFFUSE in CSTemporal's place. CSTrace writes the first bounce's
distance; CSPack (Shaders/PathNrd.hlsl) gives NRD the paths' light with
it, the normal, the linear depth and the scene's motion vectors (without
them the camera's); CSResolve makes its output the history that
CSFilter takes without its spatial filter. The frame dump has a pt nrd
line. It costs about 0.3 to 0.4 ms at 2560x1440: the flights over Pier,
River and Swamp at 3840x2160 with FSR Quality run at 93, 85 and 78 FPS
against 97, 88 and 81 with simple.

With d3d12_PathTracing 0 the frames are the same as before.
```

…
509e02a
```
d3d12_RTSunDenoise 2 denoises the sun's ray mask (the path tracer's sun
and the raster's RT sun shadows) with the shadows denoiser of AMD
FidelityFX Denoiser 1.2: a ray for each pixel, its bit masks, its tile
classification and history, its three filter passes, for all the casters
and again for the live ones, packed into the mask. Our kernels round its
headers (Shaders/RaySunFfx.hlsl).

third_party/fidelityfx-denoiser is a git submodule of AMD's repository at
the tag v1.2 (MIT); without it the renderer is built without it and 2
falls back to its own denoiser (1, the default).
```

```
XRenderD3D12Tests (mod/XRenderD3D12/Tests, ctest --preset x64-release)
runs the renderer's shader headers and PathTrace.hlsl as they are on a
D3D12 device of its own: the GPU, and the same again on WARP. It checks
the G-buffer's octahedral normal over 20145 directions (within the 10
bits' own limit), the side the normal is turned to and the vegetation's
left as it is, the fog and material fields, the HDR light curve, the
radiance cache's cells and keys, insertion from many threads, the
samples' sum and the renderer's own CSCacheResolve, PathLeafSide, the
cosine directions (mean cos 2/3, cos^2 1/2), the checkerboard (half the
pixels, complementary frames) and SRayInstance, SPTLight and
SPathTraceConstants word for word between C++ and HLSL. The target
compiles its kernels with the same dxc and options as the renderer's and
does not depend on the renderer's target, so building it neither builds
nor deploys the DLL. 16 tests pass on the RX 7900 XTX, on WARP and with
the debug layer.
```

fix(XRenderD3D12): the far land's texture and the far sprites' mip al…
…
59e0cae
```
…pha as the original's

The low detail terrain beyond the sectors (terrainlowlod) has the cover texture of the whole terrain
with u and v one whole texture further (an offset of 1). The original's sampler wraps; ours clamps
the terrain, so all the far land took the texture's corner, one flat colour that the fog made the
colour of the far mountains, and the trees on the hills stood on nothing (Pier). The integer part of
a whole-terrain offset is dropped.

A far sprite's mip level had its alpha scaled to keep level 0's alpha test coverage: the vines of
Pier's ancient trees (anc_tree_vines_a, 600 m away) became short dark lines in the air. A level's
alpha is now its texels' average, as the original's; the crowns look the same.

The frame dump names each sprite array's object and its distances, and the terrain draws' texgen.
```

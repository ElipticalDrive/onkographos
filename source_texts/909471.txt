Hi, thanks for your question!
Supporting higher-order spherical harmonics (SH) in the plugin involves two parts:
1. Loading higher-order SH data
This part is relatively straightforward.
You only need to modify the C++ side where SH coefficients are loaded.
2. Rendering higher-order SH
This part is a bit complicated.
Our plugin uses MeshBatch and the standard UE mesh-drawing pipeline. Because of this design, you cannot freely inject custom compute shaders into the rendering path. To render higher-order SH, you may need to hack SH evaluation into the existing material pipeline.
A practical solution is:
Expose SH coefficients as vertex attributes
Implement SH evaluation in the Material graph or in a Custom HLSL node
In this workflow, the engine will pass the coefficients automatically during MeshBatch drawing, and the material handles the shading logic.

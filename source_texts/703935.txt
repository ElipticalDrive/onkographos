Summary
Forward-only 3D Gaussian Splatting renderer on GPU (isotropic Gaussians)
Scene: ~1,376 Gaussians as indoor room (floor + 4 walls + 3 colored objects)
Camera orbits the scene over 90 frames
Pipeline
`project_kernel` (per-Gaussian): view transform + perspective + screen-space radius + frustum cull
host sort: front-to-back order by view-space depth (CPU sort on ~1K elements, negligible)
`render_kernel` (per-pixel): iterate sorted Gaussians, alpha-composite with `C += T * alpha * color; T *= (1 - alpha)`, early exit at `T < 0.005`
Result (720x480, ~1,376 Gaussians, 90 frames)

Avg render time
0.94 ms / frame (>1 kHz)
Sort overhead
negligible (host sort of ~1K ints)
Connects to the rest of the repo as a candidate map representation: the next step is feeding voxel-map or LiDAR-SLAM output (#54) into the Gaussian field instead of the hand-built scene.

Parent
M1a: Walk the Planet, M1a-1 (walk). From the settled optimization catalogue (§A, "Per-brick distance-band LOD" and "Ring handoff in trace()").
What to build
Replace #48's fixed-size rings with the LOD band rule from CONTEXT.md: each brick's level comes from its distance to the camera, so one level-L Voxel never covers more than one output pixel.
Selection. The base distance is d0 = v0 · f_out / bias, where:
v0 = 0.1 m;
f_out = output_height / (2·tan(vfov/2)), about 1028 at 1440p and 70°, which gives d0 ≈ 102.8 m;
bias is output pixels per Voxel, default 1.0.
Level 0 owns [0, 2·d0) and level L ≥ 1 owns [2^L·d0, 2^(L+1)·d0). A brick's distance is the nearest point of its AABB, not its centre. There are 13 levels (0…12).
Hysteresis ±5%. A brick moves out to the coarser level at 1.05× the boundary and back in at 0.95×.
Per-level sparse Scenes, traced near→far, stop at the first hit. LOD rings on the GPU (#48) #69's handoff (nearest level first, early exit, per-level occupied box) is the starting point. Each level is laid out as its own sparse set, not a fixed 16-region cube.
Bias knob. One float, shown in the overlay and settable from client bench. All switch distances scale with it.
Remove FAR_BLEND and varied_albedo's distance fade in primary.wgsl (the catalogue's "voxel_tint"). They compensate for there being no LOD, and they make a seam between two levels show a texture change.
Incremental selection. Re-select only the bricks whose band can have changed since the last camera move, not every brick every frame. A full re-select is 5–10 ms of CPU at ~1 M bricks, which the catalogue rejects.
The catalogue's references for this are docs/research/lod-and-transitions.md §2.4, §3, §5.2 and §5.3 on branch `docs/research/lod-and-transitions.md``.
Acceptance criteria
Every brick's level follows the band rule with hysteresis. Tested at the boundaries, including a camera hovering on one without the brick flipping each frame.
The bands partition space: no surface point is missing at any level boundary. Use a CPU ray-incidence test (rays that hit at level 0 still hit after selection), extending LOD rings on the GPU (#48) #69's ring tests.
Surface displacement at the switch distances is ≤ 1 output px at p95 on the test Planet, measured and reported.
FAR_BLEND and the distance fade in varied_albedo are gone, and the primary golden is regenerated deliberately.
The bias knob exists, defaults to 1.0, and changes the switch distances.
reference.rs and trace.wgsl agree exactly, and the GPU/CPU agreement tests pass.
GPU-timing change: the PR gets needs-benchmark. Report primary and reference times at ground, 100 m, 500 m and 2 km, plus bricks per level. This is the first part of the catalogue's X1.

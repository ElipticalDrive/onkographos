Summary
Currently the instanced material is frustum culled in chunks. The architecture added in #47 allows for more culling in a compute shader, as well as improving the lod system for the instanced material.
At the moment the visibility range is still used and instances that are out of range get culled in the compute shader. This has the issue of a visible seam still. I think we can probably do better and eliminate the seams completely.
Requirements
remove visual artifacts (seams) caused by density scaling.
stop using visibility range and instead cull instances based on lod in the compute shader pipeline.

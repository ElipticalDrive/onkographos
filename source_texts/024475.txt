Acquiring high-quality 3D surfaces of indoor scenes is essential for applications in VR/ AR and robotics. Recent advancements in neural implicit surface learning and differ-entiable volume rendering methods have significantly improved the accuracy and completeness of geometry. In our work, we propose a volumn feature rendering method for fast and detailed surface reconstruction from muti-view RGB-D frames. We adopt a hybrid scene representation utilizing muti-resolution hash encoding and two shallow MLPs for fast convergence. We suggest improvements by replacing standard volume rendering with vol-ume feature rendering to optimize speed. Additionally, we employ an adaptive sampling strategy guided by dynamic occupancy grids to accelerate training. We introduce a curvature term to promote surface smoothness and hole filling in untexture areas. Experiments on standard benchmarks show that our method can process room-scale scenes in approximately 10 minutes, achieving a 2x speedup compared to Go-Surf [14] in a memory-efficient way, while maintaining comparable or superior reconstruction quality.

Asset format has changed (need to recreate the assets!). No longer textures for everything, but rather buffers (only color/opacity stays in a texture since BCn compression is quite useful there).
Spherical Harmonics data got options for "clustering" it. This is by default only used in Low preset (cluster into 64k distinct SHs) and Very Low preset (cluster into 4k distinct SHs). This drastically reduces SH size, yay! However it's not fast, i.e. takes 2-5 minutes.
Even at "Very High" presets the data is now packed somewhat, always. i.e. it is now always smaller than original PLY file, even at highest quality setting.
Added "Opacity Multiplier" to rendering script, this is for fun/debugging.
Blog post on all of this.

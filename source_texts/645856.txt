⚡ Bolt: Add viewport bounds culling to TrafficLightRenderer
💡 What:
Added viewport bounding box culling (with ~500m coordinate padding) to TrafficLightRenderer.rebuild().
🎯 Why:
TrafficLightRenderer.rebuild() is called on every map render event during camera movement. Previously, every approach across the entire map was projected (projectPoint) and evaluated, even if offscreen.
📊 Impact:
Eliminates coordinate projections and PIXI Graphics calculations for offscreen traffic light approaches during pan/zoom operations.
🔬 Measurement:
Verified with pnpm test and pnpm build.

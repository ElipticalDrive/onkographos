A fundamental challenge for existing shadow map based algorithms is dealing with partially illuminated surfaces. A conventional shadow map built with a pinhole camera only provides a binary visibility sorting of the scene, and this all-or-nothing approach to visibility does not capture penumbral regions. We present an interactive soft shadow algorithm based on a variant of the depth discontinuity occlusion camera, a non-pinhole camera with rays that reach around blockers to sample normally hidden surfaces. Our soft shadow occlusion camera (SSOC) classifies a fragment on a continuum from fully visible to fully hidden, as seen from the light. The SSOC is used directly in fragment illumination computation without building an explicit “soft shadow map. ” This method renders plausible soft shadows at interactive speeds under fully dynamic conditions. 1 Figure 1: Interactive soft shadows using the soft shadow occlusion camera run at 100 fps for this scene.

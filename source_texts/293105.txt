To characterize the inhomogeneity caused by both large-scale complex geological structure and small-scale random perturbation in mountainous regions, we propose a regional multiscale method for building the random media model. Then, to compute the traveltime and ray path of different types of seismic wave in this random media model, a multistage group marching upwind hybrid method is presented. Because we use group marching method (GMM), upwind scheme, finite difference with non-uniform grid spacing and linear traveltime interpolation method together, this new ray tracing method is unconditionally stable, concise, and accurate when it is used to trace different types of seismic wave in irregular computational boundary condition caused by complex topography or interface. Finally, two examples are given for verifying the effectiveness of the above new methods.

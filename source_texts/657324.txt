A prototype based on https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/358876.358882 that draws spheres only. Ambient/diffuse/shadows use a phong model, specular/transmission uses a full raytraced model with constant coefficients of reflection and transmission.
The code is not particularly polished, but then it is a prototype.

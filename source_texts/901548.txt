Method 2 no longer runs the sampled method at all, the radiosity pretrace
included (there the cloud serves points that carry no radiosity, as the cache
is still filling). A shading point the cloud cannot serve gets no diffuse
subsurface light, and a warning after the render names its object (shape,
first tag, centre of its bounds), the reason and the count. Discs and meshes
with an inside_vector whose triangles leave a border have no inside for light
to diffuse through: they add no points and are named as open sheets. A mesh's
closure is judged with its vertices welded by position.

Cases that used to fall back stay on the cloud: unorientable crossings, light
groups, planes, rings wider than a cell (the cell size is raised before any is
built), uneven photon flags, shadow edges (near field drawn on the tangent
disc) and concave creases. A cloud point seen from behind lies across a crease
or on a parallel face; it is weighed by the profile at its distance unfolded
through the material and laid on the exit point's tangent plane for the disc,
the ring and the edge window, only where an inside test finds material in the
corner, so neighbouring parts with an air gap do not light each other. Facing
walls stay out. The reach stops at the diagonal of the object's bounds for
every material, and the memory projection is an upper estimate.

The unscattered ray through a subsurface object takes the incoming ray's weight
times its attenuation, so adc_bailout ends it through layered thin objects.

tests/render/subsurface_fallback.sh renders each former fallback case, checks
every shading point is served and open sheets are named, and that the image is
the same on one thread and two. tools/bench/sslt-reference.cpp renders a
physically based reference for sslt-studs.pov (random walks through the
homogeneous medium with Fresnel boundaries); doc/PERF.md and the manual
describe the rules and the shadow-edge and crease comparisons.

Windows build: browse information is off for povcore, whose source database
passed BSCMAKE's internal limit.

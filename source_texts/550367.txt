# ComfyUI-GaussianAvatar

**FLAME-based 3D Gaussian Head Avatars for ComfyUI.**

This node pack enables the creation of photorealistic, animatable 3D head avatars directly within ComfyUI. It implements the "GaussianAvatar" pipeline, allowing you to train avatars from video, animate them with new expressions, and render them from any angle.

## ✨ Features

- **End-to-End Pipeline**: From video input to trained 3D avatar.
- **FLAME Integration**: Uses standard FLAME topology for consistent expression/pose control.
- **Photorealistic Rendering**: 3D Gaussian Splatting for high-quality rendering.
- **Animation Ready**: Drive your avatar with new expression parameters.
- **Per-Gaussian LBS Deformation**: Avatars animate using FLAME's Linear Blend Skinning.
- **Multiple Tracking Backends**: MediaPipe (default, no external models) or InsightFace (higher accuracy).

## 📥 Installation (The Easy Way)

We have created a simple installer script that handles all the complex setup for you (including building the required CUDA extensions).

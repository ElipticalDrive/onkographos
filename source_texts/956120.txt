Goal
A scene can fade distant surfaces into a fog colour with linear or exponential falloff, set on a post-processing volume or a scene-wide component.
Why
The RPG Poly Pack Lite demo (#413) recreates a Unity scene that uses linear fog (light blue, 0 to 900 m). In the original the fog washes the mountains and the sky dome out to a pale blue; Calyx has no fog, so in the remake the mountains stay dark grey and stand out against the hills. The demo's importer bakes the fog into the sky dome's texture as a stopgap, which only works for an object at one distance.
Done when
Fog with a colour, a mode (linear with start and end, or exponential with density) and an on/off switch shades every opaque surface by its distance from the camera, after lighting.
The sky or skybox background takes the fog colour at the far end, so the horizon blends.
A test renders a surface at two distances and checks the far one is closer to the fog colour.
The RPG Poly demo turns fog on with the original's settings and drops the baked sky-dome fog from its importer (may be a follow-up).
Context
Found while building feat: Add a demo scene collection with an RPG Poly Pack Lite remake #413; the original's settings are in its RenderSettings (m_Fog, m_FogColor, m_LinearFogStart, m_LinearFogEnd).

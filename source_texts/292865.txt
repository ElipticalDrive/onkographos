Image-modeling technology has great applying prospects in military remote sensing image modeling,physical apparatus modeling,virtual reality,and augmented reality.To improve the efficiency of the carving algorithm and decrease the time of the imaged-based modeling,a high-speed space carving image modeling algorithm has been proposed which integrates changeable Voxel algorithm and pixel-mapping algorithm.The new algorithm can rapidly carve down the outer space of the reconstructed object,and obtain the visible pixels of surface Voxels without specifying visible surfaces.The experiment results show that this algorithm accelerates the modeling process comparing with pixel-mapping algorithm.

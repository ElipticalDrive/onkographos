We cannot support arbitrary shaders in layouts, but we can create SDF combinators. SDF combinators need to be split between color modifiers and coverage modifiers. The coverage ones are simply an alpha value, but full color combinators must do proper blending. In most cases, full color combinators should blend in linear sRGB, because otherwise blending works in unexpected ways, but gradients should always use oklab.

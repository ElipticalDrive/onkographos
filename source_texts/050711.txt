Hi,
there is basic support for adding custom geometry to the scene. This sample shows how to add spheres and tubes:
http://pv.readthedocs.org/en/v1.8.1/sample-custom-mesh.html
There is currently no support for rendering cubes, but that can be added easily. How do you want the cubes to be aligned? Is it OK if they align to the worlds x, y and z axis or do you want to specify the corner points directly?
Best,
Marco

Closes #226
Adds Voxel Cone Tracing with renderer ID vxgi, selectable in the registry and playground. The renderer follows the pinned upstream VXGI example: depth/normal/velocity pre-pass, voxel GI and AO injected into material lighting, and TRAA. Uses upstream 128-voxel / 3-cone defaults with unit GI intensity.
Includes beauty captures for all 55 fidelity scenes and four 1080p representative benchmark measurements with screenshots, added to the existing window001 comparison session. VXGI measured 200.33 FPS (Breakfast Room), 217.06 (Cornell), 254.20 (Dogwood), and 169.86 (Coffee Maker) on the RTX 3060 Ti. Initial voxelization is outside steady-state throughput. Settings, limitations, and reproduction commands are documented in docs/VXGI.md.
Validation: pnpm build, pnpm tsc, pnpm lint (existing warnings), pnpm test --coverage --maxWorkers 4 (39 files / 261 tests pass), and fidelity viewer build pass. The unconstrained test run initially exposed worker contention and transient shader-bundle errors during a concurrent build; the final bounded-worker run passes. Verified 55 nonempty captures and four processed benchmark records. pnpm audit --audit-level=high reports existing dependency findings: 2 critical, 4 high, 1 moderate, in simple-git/argv-parser and extract-zip dependency chains. No dependency changes.
Existing local fidelity-kit changes and unrelated untracked files were preserved and excluded.

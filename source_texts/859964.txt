The triangle longest-edge bisection constitutes an efficient scheme for refining a mesh by reducing the obtuse triangles, since the largest interior angles are subdivided. One of these schemes is the four-triangle longest-edge (4T-LE) partition. Moreover, the four triangle self-similar (4T-SS) partition of an acute triangle yields four sub-triangles similar to the original one.
In this paper we present a hybrid scheme combining the 4T-LE and the 4T-SS partitions which use the longest-edge based refinement. Numerical experiments illustrate improvement in angles and quality.

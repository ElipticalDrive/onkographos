The claim is true and the work is withdrawn. Recommend closing as "decided not to", not as done.
Audited on agent/rendering-owed (not yet merged).
The ⚠ is verbatim correct
git grep -l LightProbe returns 8 files. One is the definition (Core/Vixen.Rendering/Lighting/LightProbeVolume.cs), one is Core/Vixen.Rendering.Tests/LightProbeTests.cs, and the other six are ledgers and prose (PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt, docs/DocsExempt.txt, two READMEs, two plan docs). Explicitly checked and absent: render features, compositor nodes, WorldRenderer, EditorWorldRenderer, extraction, importers, Samples/, Editor/.
It is also pure CPU — its only using is Vixen.Core.Mathematics, where ReflectionProbe.cs imports Vixen.Graphics and Vixen.Shaders. There is no Upload, no Apply(ParameterCollection …), no texture.
But the GPU half is a road this repo already declined
docs/overview.md § 1.9's row is ✂️ — "Withdrawn as an approach, not deleted as code", not 🟡.
docs/plan/19-lighting-and-global-illumination.md:130 — "Retired as this document's answer, not as code".
docs/plan/06-rendering-pipeline.md:309 pre-empts this issue's exact framing: the GPU half "is not what [19] § L2 builds on … The two are alternative answers to 'what is the indirect light here', and L2 is the one the plan commits to."
Core/Vixen.Rendering.IrradianceFields/README.md says why: "This is the shape doc 06's tetrahedral light probes failed at, chosen because it cannot fail the same way. A Delaunay tetrahedralisation needs robust predicates, degenerates on co-planar probes, and answers 'which cell am I in' with a walk. A grid has none of those ways to be wrong. Doc 19 § 3 is explicit: no Delaunay, no predicates, no repeat."
And GPU probe sampling is not actually missing from the engine
The lattice answer is built and reached, which is the distinction this issue's title obscures:
Raven/Library/IrradianceFields/IrradianceField.rvn — struct IrradianceProbe { l00, l1m1, l10, l11, validity, sunShadow } (L1 SH on a brick lattice, 4³ probes in a 5³ footprint so one trilinear fetch crosses a brick boundary)
Core/Vixen.Rendering/Lighting/IrradianceFieldTexture.cs:455 Apply — the upload
Core/Vixen.Rendering/Compositor/IrradianceFieldRenderer.cs:48,187 — the !IrradianceField node
Gated by UseIrradianceField (Raven/Library/Pipeline/ClusteredShading.rvn:131), sampled at :795
Consumers: IndirectDiffuseRenderer, ScreenProbeGatherRenderer, TracedReflections, TracedScreenProbeGather, TerrainRenderer, AssetMaterialSource
Device fixtures: IrradianceFillDeviceTests, IrradianceShadingDeviceTests, IrradianceBounceDeviceTests
So building the GPU upload for LightProbeVolume would ship a second, competing answer to the question IrradianceFields already answers — which is exactly what plan 19 § 3 says not to do.
Landed on the branch
⚠ Core/Vixen.Rendering/README.md § "What is not here yet" opened this paragraph with "Light probes reach a frame." — the negation of the five lines under it, and of § 1.9's ✂️. Corrected, and it now says why the GPU half is withdrawn rather than owed. Part 4 item 57 marked ✂️ to match § 1.9.
Recommendation
Close as "closed by deciding not to" on merge. If somebody wants the code gone rather than parked, that is a separate and cleaner issue — LightProbeVolume, DelaunayTetrahedralization and ExactPredicates are correct, tested, and useful as a CPU bake primitive, so deleting them is a judgement call rather than a tidy-up.

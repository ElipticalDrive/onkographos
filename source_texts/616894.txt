This study details the automated generation of player pathways and scenic overlooks within the CARBON ecosystem, leveraging the terrain visual sensitivity maps and landscape landmarks. By applying grid-based filtering to viewpoint centroids and solving the Traveling Salesperson Problem (TSP), the system plots optimal routes across varying elevations. The procedural pipeline then sculpts flattened rock ledges, connects observation points using Catmull-Rom splines with variable width scaling, and carves U-shaped trail depressions into the heightmap. This workflow bridges high-level terrain analysis and data-driven level design for immersive gameplay environments.

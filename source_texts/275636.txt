I was just wondering, as I couldn't seem to find it anywhere in the documentation, but is it possible to mix a smooth and blocky voxel world using two different VoxelViewers? is it possible to mix a smooth and blocky voxel world using two different VoxelViewer?
VoxelViewer is only a hint for the engine to prioritise updates nearby. They have nothing to do with voxels being smooth or blocky.
I was looking to create something like Space Engineers with multiple grids separate from the main world
Keep in mind Space Engineers construction is not a "voxel" one in the same sense as this engine does. Just because there is a grid of some sort doesn't mean it has to be handled by a "generic voxel engine". This engine uses quite specific meshing algorithms, and likely one like Space Engineers requires something a bit more custom, not just for graphics but also physics and data representation.
My original idea was to have multiple "voxel worlds" as I didn't see a way to do voxel objects. These "worlds" would move (and rotate) independently of one another
What do you actually mean by that? If you mean planets, that is achievable with multiple terrain nodes, you don't need entire worlds for that (see https://github.com/Zylann/solar_system_demo). To this engine, a "world" is more similar to "nether/overworld" in Minecraft, where a viewer in one world does not affect objects in the other world even if their positions are the same.
Can I have multiple ships, stations, and the planet together by having different worlds that can collide and interact with one another while being rendered separately?
In theory that is still something you would do with multiple terrain nodes, really. However right now there is no support for rigidbody physics between them, only static. Also I'll say it again, it is very likely IMO that you would need a much more specific system to handle constructs like ships and vehicles with physics and tons of other functions attached to them. This engine is better suited for terrains right now.

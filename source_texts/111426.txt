Summary
viz! gets an isomesh=level keyword for 3D plots: the level set of the plotted field is triangulated with marching cubes and drawn as a shaded, depth-tested mesh instead of a Makie.volume. The volume algorithm=:iso ray-marches the shell isovalue ± isorange, so its surfaces look striped, and the other volume algorithms are drawn without depth (viz! sets enable_depth=false), so the body is always drawn over the flow. The mesh has neither problem, and with a translucent colour and transparency=true the body stays visible through it.
```
using WaterLily, GLMakie, Meshing
viz!(sim; f=ω!, isomesh=12, color=RGBAf(0.35, 0.55, 0.8, 0.5), transparency=true, body2mesh=true)
```
Developed sphere flow (WaterLily-Benchmarks sphere_5_Float32.jld2: D=32, 512×192×192 cells, Re=3700, tU/D=50), vorticity magnitude |ω|D/U with body2mesh=true. Level 12 includes the boundary layer, so the plots also show how each mode handles a field that wraps the body. Top: side view, bottom: downstream view. :mip and :iso use the viz! defaults (enable_depth=false for :mip, depth on for :iso).
Changes
isosurface_mesh(a, level) in WaterLilyMeshingExt (declared in core next to get_body): marching cubes to a GeometryBasics.Mesh, empty if the field does not cross level. get_body(sdf, Val(true)) now calls it with level 0, so the body and the field share the triangulation.
viz!(...; isomesh=nothing): when set, the field Observable is lifted to isosurface_mesh(σ, isomesh) and plotted by a new plot_σ_obs! method for meshes, and kwargs go to Makie.mesh! (eg. color, transparency, backlight). The forced enable_depth=false is skipped, d=3 is asserted, and Meshing must be loaded, as for body2mesh.
The field mesh uses backlight=1 by default. Marching cubes orients the normals towards increasing values, so they point into the region of interest for vorticity or -λ₂ and out of it for low pressure. Lighting both sides removes the dependence on the sign: with backlight=0 a vorticity surface is lit from behind and renders dark (see the gallery).
Docstring: isomesh, plus the existing enable_depth=false default for non-iso volumes and how to depth-test (enable_depth=true) and blend (transparency=true with a translucent colormap) the volume with the body.
assets/waterlily_watermark.png (706×299) and assets/waterlily_watermark_2x.png (1228×520): a WaterLily card to overlay on videos, eg. ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -i waterlily_watermark.png -filter_complex "overlay=W-w-40:H-h-40" out.mp4.
Cost
Per-frame cost without the simulation step, as medians over 5 snapshots 0.5 D/U apart (after a warm-up), on the full 512×192×192 domain with body2mesh=true and about 2400×2166 px frames (RTX 4060 8 GB, 8 threads). update is viz_step! without a time step (field function, body update and field-to-plot conversion), and render is the draw plus readback.

Common to all methods: the field function (|ω| on the GPU plus the copy to the CPU) takes 0.03 s and the body mesh 0.68 s. isomesh adds the marching cubes of the field, 0.65 s for about 52k triangles, so about 0.5 s more per frame than the volumes (2.5 time steps of this case at 0.2 s each), while its draw is the cheapest. Both marching cubes run single-threaded on the CPU over the plotted region, so cropping with CIs reduces them, and remeasure=false skips the body mesh for static bodies.

What the gallery shows:
enable_depth=false, the default for :mip and :absorption: the body is always drawn over the field, also where the boundary layer wraps it.
enable_depth=true: :mip and :absorption hide the body under the boundary layer. It shows through only with a translucent colormap and transparency=true, and lowclip=:transparent then punches holes in the body where the field is below the colour range.
:iso: correct depth, but the surfaces are striped.
isomesh: correct depth and smooth surfaces in all cases. The boundary-layer shell opens at the front stagnation region, where the body shows through.
body2mesh=false: the body itself is a ray-marched iso volume and shows the same rings.
Passing enable_depth with isomesh errors in Makie ("Invalid attribute enable_depth for plot type Mesh"): mesh plots are always depth-tested.
Tests
Checked by hand on a small sphere and on the developed benchmark sphere: opaque and translucent isomesh with a meshed and a volume body over several frames, a level with no surface (empty mesh), a low-pressure surface (region of interest below the level), the default MIP and body2mesh alone after the get_body refactor, the d=2 assertion, and a user plot_σ_obs! override (as in the propeller example) not being reached. There are no automated tests: Makie is not in the test targets, as for the rest of viz!.
Not in this PR
viz! keeps every figure in _fig_steppers for viz_step!, so repeated calls in one session keep all figures and their buffers alive. Rendering this gallery in one session ran out of RAM until the entries were deleted and GLMakie.closeall() was called after each render.
The marching cubes could be restricted to the bounding box of the cells above the level, or threaded.

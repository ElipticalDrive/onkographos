📋 Overview
Node-based layout generation pipeline using PCGPointData, noise nodes, structural maze creators, and 12 ECS processors.
Milestone: [Short-Term] Tooling, PCG & Content Automation
Subsystem Tags: Plugin, PCG, 2D
🧩 Sub-Tasks & Deliverables
Point data generators (Grid, Random/Scatter, Poisson Disk, Path/Line)
Noise and terrain nodes (Perlin/Simplex/Worley, Elevation, Slope)
Structural creators (Wilson's Maze, Cellular Automata, BSP rooms, Drunkard's Walk, WFC solver)
12 specialized ECS-compatible processor nodes (Voronoi, Delaunay, Relaxer, Raycast, etc.)

Need
GrainFront evaluates cubic anisotropy in device kernels, with one rotation per grain or per cell. Its grainfront::Rotation (include/grainfront/kernels.hpp) is a GRAINFRONT_HD 3×3 matrix with factory functions (identity, rz, q111, ...).
OpenPFC has pfc::geometry::Rotation (kernel/geometry/rotation.hpp), but it is host-only (validation uses std::abs and throws). OpenPFC also has quaternion orientation fields (mechanics/quaternion_orientation.hpp, runtime/gpu/quaternion_view.hpp; see #585).
Proposal
Make the rotation value type and its apply/transpose operations usable in device code, with validation kept in host factories. A domain package could then pass OpenPFC rotations straight into kernels.
Acceptance
GrainFront replaces grainfront::Rotation with the OpenPFC type. Its orientation parity tests (3d-cuda-orient-parity, 3d-decomp-orient-test) stay identical.
Recorded as a "moves to OpenPFC" note in GrainFront (VTT-ProperTune/GrainFront#47).

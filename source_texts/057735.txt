Inverse rendering is increasingly favored in solving visual computingproblems, including shape analysis, generation and retrieval, but relyon task-appropriate 3D shape representations for efficiency. This workexplores surface rendering with neural ray fields, which are inherentlymore efficient to render than 3D coordinate-based learning methods,requiring only a single network evaluation per ray, but with high datarequirements and reduced fidelity thus far. We propose an improvedray field with a more stable learning objective function, improvedgradient flow, and a more tractable parameter domain. We study thestrengths and failure modes of the prior art and our method, and howperformance scales with data and compute.

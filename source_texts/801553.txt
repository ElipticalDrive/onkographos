Refs #8564 (partial #8564; decision of 2026-11-03 on #8564 / #8562: option B)
Splats now compose with transparent IFC through a single nearest-glass split. The main pass holds back transparent IFC and the selection highlight, recorded as issued. P1 is the nearest transparent depth per sample, from a depth-only replay that reuses the same vertex stages, discards and depth nudge. Splats with depth ≤ P1 draw first, then the held draws, then splats with depth > P1 and the MSAA resolve. Both splat draws use one upstream renderer and one sort. They go through an opt-in depth-interval hook in the luma patch, reached only through GaussianBackendScene.encode(…, interval), so a GPU-sort backend can implement it too.
Exact: samples with at most one transparent layer, and splats behind or in front of every layer. Covers perspective and orthographic, 1x and 4x MSAA, flat/quantized/instanced/individual/X-Ray ghost draws, section clipping and selection.
Approximate, reported: splats between two transparent layers. compositionReport.transparentLayers: 'multiple' comes from a stencil count plus an occlusion query; only zero versus non-zero is portable, since D3D12 occlusion is binary. A closed glass solid counts as two layers. X-Ray reports multiple on every frame: in the witness scene, 94.5 % of ghosted samples are beyond exact (SwiftShader count). The X-Ray policy is for the maintainer.
Falls back to the old single pass, reported in compositionReport.fallback: single-pass-mode, pipelines-compiling (the frame re-renders when the variants are ready), unsupported-draw, upstream-interval-missing.
Unchanged: frames without transparent IFC keep the single pass, byte-identical. Frames without visible splats record no extra pass, attachment, texture, query or pipeline compile (spy test). IFC-only frames match base pixel for pixel.
Split-frame rules: the highlight still draws over transparent IFC, so on the highlighted surface it also covers splats behind the nearest layer (highlightOverSplats). Points, reference images and overlays are held out of the main pass and replayed in the glass pass after behind-glass splats and before front-glass splats (laterOverBehindSplats).
Split failures: no in-frame splat retry after any failure, including after recorded draws. The failed frame reports composition: split-failed (possibly incomplete); a requested redraw uses sticky single-pass with fallback: split-failed. Device recovery/renderer rebuild (release()) or a new device clears the latch.
Round-3 readiness/readback: expected visibility compilation defers the whole frame without disabling the split. Readback processing or callback errors leave the layer count unknown, unmap or release the buffer, and free the query slot for later frames.
Round-4 bookkeeping: resource release resets composition reporting to neutral. Witness fixes stop pane search at its first match and keep rejected peels out of the comparison cache. No GPU rerun: qualification remains at 128d2c639c.
API: GaussianSplatStats.composition is widened, plus optional compositionReport and gaussianSplats.setCompositionMode(). Readiness stays blocked with the same blocker tuple, plus the capability nearest-glass-split.

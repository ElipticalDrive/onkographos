Summary
Second improvement round on top of merged Expedition PR #1. Runtime remains dependency-free plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript with WebGPU and WebGL 2 renderers.
Rendering and atmosphere
Linear floating-point scene composition, quality-budgeted soft bloom, four grading looks, exposure, vignette and optional grain. Tone mapping occurs once in the display pass; capability-checked WebGL LDR fallback is included.
MSAA targets use supported color/depth sample counts. Resize releases/recreates owned attachments; transparent batches are sorted back-to-front.
Moon, northern night lighting, procedural cloud shadows, instanced animated bird flocks, and terrain-derived shoreline foam.
World and train polish
Terrain-sampled building foundations, steep-site rejection, and aligned viaduct deck/pier/arch spans replace disconnected structural details.
Velocity 320 gains a continuous lofted high-speed nose and surface-following glazing; interior cab geometry is confined to cab view.
Reduced-motion camera control and bounded chase-camera terrain avoidance.
Driving, audio and photo tools
Read-only route coach calculates braking guidance from selected track path, limits, occupancy, gradients, adhesion and mission stops. It never writes driving controls.
Fixed-step journey telemetry, route profile, scoring, and validated save/restore of journey and display settings.
Layered synthesized engine, rail, wind, brake, rain, flange and horn audio with bounded node allocation and user-gesture startup.
Interactive Studio / F2 photo mode: free camera, lens, exposure/look, PNG capture, isolated driving shortcuts, and restoration of prior camera/pause state.
Mobile layouts, optional wildlife/ambience/coach controls, and offline precaching for all new modules.
Delivery and validation
Complete source, documentation, 32 new Node regressions, browser regression script and regenerated standalone distribution committed.
Full repository unit tests and Pages build passed in the branch import run. Candidate WebGPU/WebGL rendering and interaction checks run on this PR; results will be recorded before any merge.
Existing Expedition matrix continues testing eight procedural regions and six train families, alongside new cinematic, photo, audio, telemetry, mobile and offline checks.
Boundaries
This is a substantial procedural-game presentation and interaction upgrade, not a claim of commercial AAA parity, photogrammetry, ray tracing, temporal AA, or physical-phone performance. Audio is synthesized rather than recorded rolling-stock samples. Bird motion and aurora are visual systems. The route coach is a game aid, not railway safety software. Existing four-edge route topology remains.
See docs/CINEMATIC-JOURNEYS.md for resource lifetimes, controls, rendering contracts and explicit limitations.

Apply travel direction to sampled profile grades
When the train is moving or set to reverse on a non-level section, pose(direction*d) samples the route behind the train but p.grade remains defined in the edge's forward direction. Passing that grade unchanged to speedEnvelope therefore treats a reverse downhill as an uphill and can advise a higher permitted speed instead of earlier braking; multiply the sampled grade by direction before calculating the envelope.

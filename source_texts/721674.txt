Description
TG uses fake ambient occlusion to add the illusion of depth, which IMO makes the game look a lot better. It's also a prefs toggle and can be a cvar in SS14, so the people who don't like it don't have to look at it.
It's literally just a drop shadow applied to everything except the floor: #define AMBIENT_OCCLUSION filter(type="drop_shadow", x=0, y=-2, size=4, color="#04080FAA")

This effect makes the spessman very clearly look like they're sideways. Or, charitably, that they've got an evil aura of doom.
What you'd actually want would be two types of AO. One is for horizontal sprites and one is for vertical ones.
For vertical ones, the generated AO effect should be gradiated so that it is hidden entirely above 16px from the bottom most pixels of the sprite and is faded in from there. E.g.

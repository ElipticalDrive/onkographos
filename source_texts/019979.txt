An improved GPU-based volume rendering method is presented for interactive visualization of image datasets.The closed 3D surface that warps the user-interested datapart is created using a 2D contour-based surface rendering algorithm.The 3D surface is then used as proxy geometry for GPU-based ray-casting to achieve fast volume rendering.With the rendering of proxy geometry,the number and sampling length of rays are reduced,and the ray-casting time saved.We implement the method on medical and industrial datasets,showing improvement of efficiency without any loss of interested dataparts.

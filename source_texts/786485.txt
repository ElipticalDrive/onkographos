ijenko1 year ago
I walked quite slow - 0.5m/s. I also walked with the scanner facing different directions - the Kity has left and right facing cams, so I tried to make sure the camera was facing at the objects i wanted capturing. And if i walked up the street with the camera facing left and right, when i came back i made sure the camera was facing front and back (by walking with the scanner out to my side rather than infront of me). I wandered in squiggly lines, looped around things etc etc. Ill upload it to xgrids share as i think that viewer allows you to see the path. Ill add the link to the description when i've got it sorted :)
I think you have to over scan the scene with this method for good 3DGS outputs. It was complete overkill for 'laser capture'. The new portal cam overcomes some of this as it has front facing cameras.

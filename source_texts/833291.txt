Theme
An analytic single-scattering RTSolver (direct beam plus once-scattered light, with the exact phase function), its TMS correction for truncated optics, and the comparison against Coulson's Rayleigh tables and 6SV that closes v3.
Parent Wave
Wave epic: [EPIC] Wave 7: v3 single scattering — an aerosol-aware SWIR forward model #187
Wave label: wave:7
Milestone: v3: single scattering
Motivation
Single scattering is analytic, cheap and differentiable; it is the first solver Korkin et al. (2022) build, in two independent forms that must agree, and the single-scattering correction every later solver adds back on top of truncated multiple scattering ("analytic single scatter subtracted and added back", radtrax · 3). Building it first, behind the RTSolver protocol, lets v3 deliver an aerosol-aware forward model before v4's multiple-scattering solvers exist.

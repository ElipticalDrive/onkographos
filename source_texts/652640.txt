# Granular Terrain Generation – Concept & Workflow

## Overview
A procedural terrain generation technique based on a *grid-based granular simulation*.  
Instead of using pre-defined heightmaps or explicit shapes, terrain naturally **emerges** from sand-like particles interacting under simple local rules.  

The goal is to produce **organic, eroded structures** and **island-like blobs**, while retaining the option to export results as either a **2D heightmap** or **3D density field**.

---

## Core Idea
Simulate falling sand on a voxel grid using noise-driven deposition:
1. Use a **3D grid** of voxels. Each voxel stores:
   - `bool filled`
   - `vec3 velocity` (for future use)
2. Noise determines where new sand is spawned at the top layer.
3. Sand “grains” fall under gravity, checking collisions and settling when blocked.
4. The simulation stabilizes into natural piles and landforms.
5. The final state is collapsed into a **heightmap** or **density texture**.

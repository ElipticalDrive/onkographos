Summary
New command img2fig print: turns a figure mesh (default figure/figure_clean.glb, or any GLB/STL/OBJ/PLY) into a print-ready solid.
Voxel-based repair: surface voxels, a small morphological closing and an interior fill give a solid by construction, so open edges, non-manifold edges, overlapping shells and self-intersections disappear. Large openings are capped on the mesh first.
Floating parts: merged with a bridge within --merge-gap (default 2 mm), kept and reported beyond it, crumbs under 1 mm³ removed (--fragments remove|keep to override).
Thin features: a ball opening against --min-wall, split into thin parts (sheets, fins, strands sticking out more than --min-wall from a thicker body) and surface relief (raised detail and convex edges on a thick body), with positions of the largest thin parts. --thicken (off by default) grows only thin parts, each to about --min-wall; relief is never grown.
Signed distance field carries the flat base cut, optional pedestal, optional hollowing (--hollow WALL, one drain hole per drainable cavity, undrainable pockets stay solid).
Marching cubes, Taubin smoothing (removes grid terraces) and manifold3d simplification, with a small search over smoothing and tolerance until pymeshlab (optional, GPL, not a dependency) finds no self-intersections.
Scaled to --height mm (plus pedestal), exported as STL and 3MF (mm), optional vertex-colour PLY for full-colour services, JSON validation report, cross-section image and Blender previews. Exit 6 when the result is not watertight and manifold.
New print extra (trimesh, manifold3d, scipy, scikit-image, networkx, lxml); CI installs it.
Second commit: --thicken no longer bumps surface relief
On a real 100 mm image-to-3D figure, --min-wall 1.2 --thicken covered the face, hair, bows and an accessory in small round bumps, while the same run without --thicken was clean. Cause: everything a 1.2 mm ball cannot reach counted as thin (including shallow relief such as strand edges, embossed lines and dots), and every flagged voxel grew by 0.6 mm on every side. That run added 5.5% to the volume.
Thin voxels are now split: a thin part reaches farther than --min-wall from the ball-opened (thick) solid, and its root is recovered by a geodesic dilation inside the thin set. Relief stays within its own height of the thick solid (a convex corner within 0.73 ball radii), so it never qualifies.
Growth goes towards a target: each thin-part voxel grows by min_wall/2 minus its local half-thickness, rounded up to whole voxels. Before, it grew by min_wall/2 regardless, so a 0.6 mm fin came out 1.8 mm.
The check before thickening ran on the raw voxel solid, about a voxel thicker than the final surface, so walls just under --min-wall were missed there and flagged after repair. It now carries a one-voxel allowance. Voxel-distance comparisons get a float32 tolerance; without it a one-voxel growth step never triggered.
With --thicken, the grid gets whole voxels of extra padding, so the lattice matches an unthickened run and untouched regions voxelise identically.
Report keys: thin_part_volume_fraction, thin_part_regions, largest_thin_parts, relief_volume_fraction (thin_volume_fraction still counts everything unreachable). Warnings name thin parts and relief separately and recommend printing larger (thin parts scale with the figure) and the slicer's thin-wall handling. After --thicken they report what remains and no longer say "rerun with --thicken".
Skill and README: for FDM, prefer printing larger plus slicer thin-wall handling; use --thicken when the size is fixed and a few thin parts must print solid, accepting blunter tips. The bump failure is recorded as an observed pitfall.

I was testing the adaptive SART (or SAART) solver, which was used by Juuso Karhunen in this paper, against the classical SART solver in the problem of tomographic reconstruction of the emission profile in the ITER SOL. Although the adaptive solver does not seem to provide a better solution to my problem compared to SART (or, at least in the case when a smoothing regularisation is applied to the emission profile), now I have the CPU and GPU implementations of SAART (see this). I can make a pull request if you want the SAART solver in Cherab.

A test performed on nine different 3D beryllium emission profiles from the ERO2.0 simulations showed that the non-adaptive SART solver is more robust. For four relatively smooth emission profiles, the SAART solver returned the same results as the SART solver, but in fewer iterations. However for the remaining five sharp emission profiles, the SAART solver returned much less accurate results with more iterations than the SART solver.
So, I think this issue can be closed. I'll keep the branch with the SAART solver in my fork in case someone needs it in the future.

Cameras and Display Systems Towards Photorealistic 3D Holography
Abstract
In the following Chapters, we will begin with holographic displays for photorealistic 3D images. To create a high-quality photorealistic holographic display, three elements including fast hologram generation algorithms, high space-bandwidth product systems, and high-bandwidth hologram carriers, are required. In this Chapter, we will introduce computer-generated holography, i.e., CGH, which is a prerequisite for future photorealistic holographic displays. In this chapter, we discussed three different approaches to hologram generation, with a focus on the layer-based CGH algorithm and its potential as a standard method. We also presented the workflow from 3D object to hologram generation and outlined the general configuration for displaying CGH-generated holograms. The CGH algorithm continues to be an active area of research as we strive for advancements in photorealistic 3D holographic displays.

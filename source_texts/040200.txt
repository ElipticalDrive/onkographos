Volume datasets play a crucial role in our quest for discovery in medicine, geophysics, science and engineering. More specifically, they are being used in applications such as medical diagnosis, surgical planning, sonar systems, non-destructive testing, and seismic interpretation, to name a few. However, understanding the structure of a volumetric dataset can be difficult. Therefore rendering techniques that support 3D and immersive displays are becoming increasingly important because immersive environments allow the user a better chance to explore the dataset than on 2D displays.

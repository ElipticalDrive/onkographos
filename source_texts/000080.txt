If you'd rather buy and download through itch, you can now do that, and there's a free demo and full documentation linked right at the top of the page. What is it? Raymarch Voxel Engine is a high-performance raymarched voxel renderer for Unity URP. Everything is drawn on the GPU by raymarching, so you get dense voxel worlds, real-time lighting and fully editable, destructible voxels without a mesh-based pipeline. What you get Real-time global illumination: voxel cone traced GI, so emissive voxels light the world and sunlight bounces into caves, with no baking. Dynamic point and spot lights contribute too. Real-time voxel water: a fully dynamic liquid simulation that flows, pools and floods, with caustics, depth absorption, reflections and sun glints. Carve a channel and the water follows. World generation and LOD: procedural 2D and 3D noise terrain, caves, structures and biomes, streamed in chunks with seamless LOD. Node-based biomes: design worlds visually with biome graphs blended by temperature and humidity. Seven demo biomes are included: plains, forests, deserts, mesas, highlands, tundra and swamps. Volumetric lighting: god rays, volumetric clouds and fog, explosions and atmospheric scattering. .vox support and a built-in editor: import MagicaVoxel models in one click, then edit them inside Unity with the in-editor Vox Editor. Destruction and physics on top of all of it. Try before you buy I'd rather you see it running on your own machine before spending anything, so there's a downloadable demo , and the documentation covers setup and every system above. What's next The engine is still being actively developed. The latest update (1.2.0) added the World and Water update, and I'll keep posting devlogs here as new features land.

What it is for
Sun rays (god rays): beams of sunlight through trees, buildings and clouds, with the shadowed air between them. The beams show from any angle, not only when the sun is on screen. The Legacy Viewer has nothing like this. It is experimental and off by default.
Legacy Viewer origin
No. Aurora's own feature (TASKS.md "To explore", decided by the human on 2026-10-17).
What changes
Rendering:
New volumetric.wgsl: a fullscreen pass marches each view ray through the sun's shadow cascades (24 jittered steps, one shadow-compare tap per step) up to the scene depth, or to the end of the cascades for the sky.
Scattering uses a Henyey-Greenstein phase plus an even part, so beams also show from the side and from behind.
The pass draws into the top-left part of a full-size Rgba16Float target, using a viewport set by the resolution setting. Changing the resolution reallocates nothing.
post.wgsl adds the rays with a screen blend, so they brighten without clipping.
The pipeline goes through the pipeline cache.
The DoF post bind group carries the rays too.
Interface: Preferences › Graphics › Sun rays (experimental toggle, needs shadows), with these sliders (en/fr/ja):

Very dependent on the EEP
How the rays look depends a lot on the EEP: its sun direction, sun color and haze drive the effect. The performance cost is low enough for everyday use. But some more exotic or outdated EEPs make the effect unusable. Since it is off by default, anyone who meets such an EEP can just turn it off.

Limits and follow-up
Needs shadows on, since the beams come from the shadow cascades.
Below full resolution the upscale is plain bilinear, so silhouettes can glow a little.
The rays target is allocated even while the rays are off (about 16 MB at 1080p).
Tiled photos and VR use a single view.
⬜ added to TASKS.md: a depth-aware upscale, allocating the target only while the rays are on, and froxel volumetrics (water, transparent faces, local lights, projector beams).

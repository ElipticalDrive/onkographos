FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about Volume Shader GPU testing.
1
What is Volume Shader?
Volume Shader is a web-based GPU performance testing tool that uses 3D volume rendering to stress test your graphics card while providing visual feedback and performance metrics.
2
Do I need a powerful GPU to use it?
No. Volume Shader runs on any GPU with WebGL support, including integrated graphics and phones. Pick the quality preset that suits your device: phones start on Low, and you can step it up until the FPS drops.
3
What performance data does it provide?
Average, lowest and highest FPS, average frame time in milliseconds, your GPU model and browser, and a rating. It does not measure GPU utilization, temperature, clock speed or power draw, because browsers cannot read those values. Use a desktop tool such as HWiNFO or GPU-Z for that.
4
Is it safe for my GPU?
Yes, Volume Shader uses standard WebGL rendering that's safe for your graphics card. It doesn't overclock or modify your hardware settings.
5
Can I compare my results with others?
There is no public leaderboard. To compare devices, run the same quality preset on each one and compare the result cards. FPS from different presets is not comparable.
6
What browsers are supported?
Volume Shader works in modern browsers that support WebGL, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

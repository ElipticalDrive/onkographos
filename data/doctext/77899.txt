By using the Fourier Slice Theorem, projections of volume data of size N  3  can be generated in O(N  2  log N) time. An image for any viewing direction can be obtained by taking a 2D slice from a 3D spectrum at the appropriate direction and then inverse Fourier transforming it. In this paper, we have implemented this idea, and the resulting image can be generated faster than conventional volume rendering.

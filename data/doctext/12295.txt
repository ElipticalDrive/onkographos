Requested by Adrien · project thread
Before: mypbr could not read MagicaVoxel models, had no sun, procedural sky, infinite ground or lens effects, and getting a voxel-art look took hand-built boxes.
After: vox model.vox loads MagicaVoxel files (palette, MATL materials, scene graph) and traces them with a 3D DDA voxel grid, and style voxel gives MagicaVoxel's look in one line. Classic scenes render as before unless they ask for the new features.
The work follows the steps in the "Making mypbr look like MagicaVoxel" analysis, one commit per step:
.vox loader, first as quad faces in the BVH (kept as vox ... quads).
sun (cone light given by irradiance), sky uniform, ground (infinite plane beside the BVH).
bloom, vignette (display images only) and polygonal bokeh with camera blades.
voxel_grid: Amanatides-Woo DDA in 8³ blocks inside a BVH. voxel_test checks it finds exactly the hits of the quads (200k random rays, 3 block sizes). About 15% faster than quads on monu1, and one byte per voxel.
sky atmosphere: single-scattering Rayleigh + Mie + ozone baked into an environment_light; the sun is reddened by the air.
camera ortho, height_fog (closed-form free-flight sampling), bounces caps, transparent_shadows, voxel edges.
style voxel / style classic preset; CLAUDE.md and ROADMAP.md updated.
How: everything is in headers under include/ (vox_loader.h, voxel.h, sun.h, plane.h, atmosphere.h, postprocess.h), with scene keywords documented at the top of scene.h. bounces and transparent_shadows are deliberate MagicaVoxel-style biases, off unless set or style voxel. Test models: scenes/models/island.vox (generated by tools/make_island_vox.py) and ephtracy's MIT sample models via tools/fetch_models.sh. Renders: renders/13-*.png. Rebased on the Mitsuba validation merge; build warning-free, 4/4 tests pass.

A vox keyword reads a .vox file (versions 150 and 200): its models, palette,
MATL materials and the nTRN/nGRP/nSHP scene graph. Each model's visible

Trace voxel models with a 3D DDA instead of faces
…

```
voxel_grid walks a block of a model cell by cell (Amanatides-Woo); a model
is cut into 8^3 blocks shrunk to their voxels and put in a BVH, which skips
empty space. Blocks read neighbouring cells from the whole model, so
surfaces on block boundaries come out the same. voxel_test checks that the
grid finds exactly the hits of the quad faces, which stay available with
vox ... quads. On monu1 the grid is about 15% faster and needs one byte
per cell.

Add orthographic camera, height fog, bounce caps, edges
…
bb03f48
```
- camera ortho <height>: parallel rays, for isometric views.
- height_fog: density falling exponentially with height, filling space,
  sampled in closed form; fades the horizon and fills valleys.
- bounces diffuse/specular/scatter: MagicaVoxel's per-kind bounce caps.
- transparent_shadows: shadow rays cross smooth glass in a straight line,
  and paths refracted to a light already reached that way don't count it.
- vox ... edges <width>: outlines of the voxel shapes on the grid.

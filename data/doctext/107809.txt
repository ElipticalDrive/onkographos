Requested by João · project thread
The close clouds over the land looked cartoony, so this replaces them with volumetric clouds.
Cloud shape: each cloud is now a box mesh (12 tris) that shaders/cloud.gdshader ray-marches in 14 jittered steps, instead of the camera-facing puff sprites.
The density comes from cloud.gdshaderinc: a dome with a flat base, carved by a drifting seamless 3D noise (Clouds.noise3(), 64³), with billow and erosion detail on top.
Lighting:
Sunlight through the vapour uses Beer's law with 2 taps towards the sun, plus a powder term.
Scattering uses a dual-lobe HG phase function, giving a silver lining when the sun is behind a cloud.
Sky and ground ambient vary with height, and the clouds dim at night.
Scene interaction: the march stops at the scene depth, so towers poke into clouds. The vapour thins around the camera. The box is drawn from its back faces with the depth test off, so it also works with the camera inside a cloud.
Shadows: a shadow-only twin box marches 5 steps along the sunlight and casts a dithered soft shadow that follows the cloud's real thickness.
Lit from World: World._update_sky feeds the cloud material the sun direction, sun light, sky colours and the night value.
Triangles: cloud tris drop from ~55 to 24 per cloud. The full-zoom view now measures 249.4k raw (273.4k with reserve), under the 280k WIDE_BUDGET.
Checks: the budget tests pass, the headless launch is clean, and screenshots at noon, sunset, from above and close up look right.

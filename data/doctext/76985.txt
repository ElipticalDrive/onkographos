GsplatConfig::outputDepth: both backends also write a per-pixel depth buffer, read with getGaussianSplattingDepth(). The compute backend writes the median depth (where transmittance crosses 0.5). The raster backend writes the nearest depth of a single splat with opacity ≥ 0.5 covering the pixel. On the lantern, the two differ by a median of 0.4 %.
The projection guard band is centred on off-centre views.

feat: depth output for both Gaussian splatting backends
…
4c380c3
```
GsplatConfig::outputDepth makes a backend also write a per-pixel depth
buffer (NDC depth, +inf where there is no surface), read with
getGaussianSplattingDepth(), so other geometry can be composited into a
splat scene instead of over it. The colour image is unchanged.

The compute backend records the median depth while it blends front to
back: the depth of the Gaussian at which the transmittance first falls
to 0.5. The raster backend blends back to front and cannot know that
crossing; its fragment shader instead keeps the nearest depth of a
splat that alone covers the pixel with an opacity of at least 0.5, by
atomicMin on an order-preserving uint encoding, decoded by a compute
pass after the render pass. With depth output it uses the vertex path.
Both agree on opaque surfaces: on the lantern scene the median
difference is 0.4 %.

The device enables fragmentStoresAndAtomics where supported.

fix: centre the Gaussian projection guard band on off-centre views
…
d52ceee
```
The projected Gaussian centres used for the covariance Jacobian were
clamped to a guard band around the optical axis. With an off-centre
projection, such as a crop of the view, the visible Gaussians lie away
from the axis and were clamped too, which distorted the footprints of
Gaussians elongated along the view direction. The band is now centred
on the view.

cropProjection() makes such a projection: a window of a view, enlarged
to the whole view. Rendering with it matches the window of the full
render in both backends.

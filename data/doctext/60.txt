Splatting • Turn every point into a disk (aligned to the camera) • Color and transparency vary • Render the splats from back to front using graphics hardware.
Transparent Planes • Strategy: • Slice the volume by many planes (200-1000) • Apply transfer function to each vertex on the plane • Result: plane with variation in color and transparency • Render the planes from back to front These can be quickly rendered using “2D textures” or “3D textures”.

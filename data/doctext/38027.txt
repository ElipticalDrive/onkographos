What changes
Before: a FogVolume drew nothing, so its local fog, and the fog a negative density carves out, were missing from the volumetric fog. A texture saved as its own .tres, and an ImageTexture or ImageTexture3D with its pixels saved inline, drew nothing either.
After: each FogVolume adds its FogMaterial's density, albedo and emission to the froxels its shape covers, as Godot 4.6.3's volume pass does (volumetric_fog.glsl:165-278, fog_material.cpp:149-163). Every shape works: box, ellipsoid, cone, cylinder and world. Height falloff, edge fade, negative density and Godot's default fog shader for a volume without a material work too. A FogMaterial density_texture shapes the density. It can be a NoiseTexture3D or an ImageTexture3D, inline or saved as its own .tres, or an image its import sidecar imports as a 3D texture.
How:
godot/fogVolume.ts ports the froxel range of a volume's pass (fog.cpp:551-718). r3f/fog/fogVolumeRows.ts packs each visible, unculled volume into a row texture, and fogVolumeShader.ts adds the volumes' fixed-point sums in the light pass, with Godot's packed-colour overflow.
nodes/3d/fogvolume/ gains a parser and a Component, and resources/materials/fogmaterial/ decodes FogMaterial.
The new resources/textures/noisetexture3d/ slice builds the texture as a worker job. Its texels and seamless blend match Godot byte for byte. r3f/fog/fogDensityAtlas.ts stacks every density texture in depth into one 3D texture, because a WebGL2 shader cannot index a sampler array in a loop. The stack holds floats once a density texture does.
An imported 3D texture is cut into slices as Godot's layered importer cuts it (resource_importer_layered_texture.cpp:370-386).
The new resources/textures/imagetexture3d/ slice and resources/textures/image/decode.ts read an Image sub-resource in every uncompressed format: 8-bit, 16-bit, half, float, RGBA4444, RGB565 and RGBE9995, unpacked as Image::_get_color_at_ofs does (image.cpp:3382-3406, :3451-3525). A float texel uploads raw as a half float, because Godot gives a float format no sRGB view and WebGL2 always filters a half float. An atlas crop of a half-float sheet stays half float, and a canvas read of one converts it to bytes.
useTextResourceSlot reads a slot that names a .tres as that file's [resource] in the file's own tables. Every Texture2D and Texture3D slot uses it, so a .tres AtlasTexture now takes the inline route.
Found on the way:
The colour ramp shared with NoiseTexture2D reads the luminance in float and truncates each channel, as Godot does (color.h:106-108, image.cpp:3577-3580).
The Gradient sampler and channelToByte run in float, as Gradient::get_color_at_offset and Color::get_r8 do (gradient.h:151-235, color.h:228). Godot-measured ramp bytes off by one drop from 1212 to 0 of 2244.
A GradientTexture2D with use_hdr keeps a channel above 1 (gradient_texture.cpp:239-270).
The ArrayMesh byte reader now narrows a listed -1 to 255, as Godot does (variant_parser.cpp:650).
NoiseTexture2D and NoiseTexture3D apply the FastNoiseLite domain warp, as get_noise_2d and get_noise_3d do (fastnoise_lite.cpp:318-338). patches/fastnoise-lite@1.1.1.patch exports the port's Vector2 and Vector3, which its DomainWrap requires.
fogVolumePass.ts and fogVolumeShaders.ts are now volumetricFogPass.ts and volumetricFogShaders.ts, because the glossary keeps "fog volume" for the node.
FogMaterial, NoiseTexture3D, ImageTexture3D and CompressedTexture3D declare validators. Texture3D joins the undeclared-resource list as a record: it is an abstract tier with no properties of its own.
Goldens
fog-volume (new): the issue's 3×3×3 box at (0, 1, −5). Against converged Godot 4.6.3, the mean error drops from 3.476/255 (no volume) to 0.205/255.
fog-volume-density-image, fog-volume-density-volume-tres and fog-volume-density-half-tres (new): an imported 3D texture, an 8-bit ImageTexture3D .tres and a half-float one whose density passes 1.
sprite2d-imagetexture-tres and sprite2d-imagetexture-half-tres (new): an RGB8 and an RGBAHalf ImageTexture .tres.
noisetexture2d-domain-warp (new): a progressive, reduced-simplex domain warp.
Moved, all closer to Godot:
noisetexture2d: max 1/255 from the ramp fix, mean 0.776/255 → 0.735/255.
sprite3d-gradienttexture: mean 1.887/255 → 1.882/255.
texturerect-gradienttexture: 682816 → 682496 pixels off Godot.
isometric-dungeon: 2 px at 1/255 from the point light's gradient. Pixel (367, 584) goes from 5 to 4 in red, and Godot draws 4. The other pixel sits on an edge where the frame's mean error against Godot is unchanged.
Every other fog, gradient, noise and atlas golden is unchanged.

Each sprite scene modulates by 0.4, so a channel above 1 shows. With the Environment's fog off, a volume adds nothing, in both engines.
A NoiseTexture2D sprite with each warp drops from a maximum error of 143 to 187/255 to 1 to 3/255. Its whole-frame mean stays at 0.72/255, because main clears the 2D background to grey 77 where Godot clears to 76. A separate thread fixes that.
Known limitations
These are in the comparison sheets:
A fog ShaderMaterial runs no fog shader. That needs a shading-language runtime: Run a fog ShaderMaterial on a FogVolume #734.
An Image in a block-compressed format draws nothing: Decode the block-compressed Image formats an ImageTexture or ImageTexture3D saves #736. An Image in an integer format draws nothing too, since a float sampler cannot read it.
A NoiseTexture3D shows even density until its build lands.
Past 2048 stacked density slices, further textures leave the density even.
A 3D colour slot still decodes every texture as sRGB. Godot reads a format without an sRGB view raw, so a Lum8 or grey-noise albedo draws too dark. This predates this pull request and touches every 3D material path: #737.

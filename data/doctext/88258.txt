I think we have to be more prescriptive here (otherwise users and implementors have a tough time): value is used for x and grad(value) is used for y.
Then, does the 2D TFN have to be separable (combination of two 1D TFNs)? Could be interesting (visually) to leave more freedom here (no difference to an implementation). Otherwise it would be better to provide the two 1D TFNs directly (and the combination to a 2D LUT is implementation choice; combining two 1D lookups would work as well and needs less memory).

The derivative is being computed from layer.lastOutput[row] (post-activation). Your Layer.derivative() implementations for tanh and sigmoid currently assume they receive the pre-activation value x (they call tanh(x) and recompute sigmoid). This makes backprop incorrect for those activations. Fix by either (a) caching pre-activation values during forward() and passing them into derivative(), or (b) rewriting derivatives to accept activated output (tanh: 1 - out*out, sigmoid: out*(1-out)).

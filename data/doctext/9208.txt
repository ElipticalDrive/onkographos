Direct Volume Rendering (DVR) uses ray-casting to render 3D volumetric data without converting it to surface geometry. Ray-casting works by (1) tracing a ray from each pixel into the volume, (2) computing color values by sampling along the ray, and (3) assigning the final color value to the pixel. DVR pipelines typically involve data interpolation, classification/transfer functions, gradient estimation, shading, and compositing color/opacity values along each ray. Classification maps voxel values to optical properties like color and opacity, often using lookup tables, to highlight regions of interest while maintaining smooth transitions.

This patch optimizes and stabilizes the volumetric cloud rendering system by aligning the weather map generation, 3D volume baking, density evaluation, and raymarching loops. It introduces organic eroded Voronoi cells, realistic superellipsoid puffiness, precomputed texture sampling, and highly stable linear raymarching to eliminate rendering artifacts like spots, noise, banding, and flat-looking clouds.

Part 8 of 9 of the split of #8. Base: tl_split_05b-integral-eb (PR 7). The content only depends on PR 2; the base follows the stack order.
What
Use the plotfile distribution map.
With USE_EB=TRUE:
volFrac is read from the plotfile, and fully covered cells (volFrac == 0) are excluded from the surface extraction.
The volFrac ghost cells are filled with FillPatch from the coarser level (piecewise constant). With only FillBoundary, the ghost cells at a coarse/fine boundary stayed 0. Every cell touching them was then masked, and the surface had a crack along each coarse/fine boundary: on a two-level test with volFrac = 1, 377 triangles were missing (−4 % area).
Docs: EB section of the isosurface page; isosurface rows in the EB page. It is not supported on GPU, because extraction and output are host loops over device memory.
Checks
Test plotfiles were generated with generateTestPlt (3D; 16³ and 32³ boxes, ref. ratio 2 and 4). Both the non-EB output and the EB output with volFrac = 1 are byte-identical to development.
Difference from #8 (deliberate)
#8 switched the element-extraction loop to MFIter(states[lev], TilingIfNotGPU()). The loop body works on whole boxes, so boxes larger than one tile were processed several times. With 32³ boxes this gave 3 extra elements and a slightly different area. The loop is untiled again, as on development.

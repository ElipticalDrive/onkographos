What
S9 — dual contouring, the sharp-feature mesher (3D-Rendering-Roadmap §S9, Refs #391 #389). Marching Cubes puts surface vertices on grid edges, so a hard crease (a Mandelbox facet edge, a KIFS corner) is rounded into a chamfer at grid resolution. Dual contouring places one vertex per cell, positioned by a QEF over the cell's Hermite data, so the vertex snaps onto the intersection of the tangent planes — onto the crease.
Stacked on #444 → #443 → #442 → #441 (needs the shared writers incl. 3MF, colour, onReport). Retarget to main as the stack merges.
How
DualContourMesher (pure): samples the DE on the grid, gathers Hermite data (crossing point + DE gradient normal) on sign-changing edges, solves a regularised QEF per active cell — normal equations (ATA + λI)p = ATb + λc biased toward the cell mass point and clamped to the cell, robust against rank-deficient planar/edge cases with no SVD dependency — then joins the four cells around each sign-changing grid edge into a quad. Smooth per-vertex normals like the MC path. Interior mesher (closed when the shape is inside the cube; a DC boundary cap is a follow-up).
UserBulbMeshExporter.ExportDualContouring (+ IDistanceEstimator overload): same extension dispatch (OBJ/STL/PLY/glTF/3MF), per-vertex colour and print-readiness callback as the MC path.
Tests
DualContouringTests:
Sphere_Dc_Is_Closed_Manifold_Outward_Solid — closed, 2-manifold, outward, ~4/3·π volume (topology + winding sound).
Box_Dc_Puts_A_Vertex_Nearer_The_Sharp_Corner_Than_Mc — on an L∞ box, DC lands a vertex on the true 3D corner far tighter than MC, while staying a valid closed solid (the point of DC).
Dc_Carries_Color_And_Reports_Print_Ready — colour + print-readiness ride the shared writers.
Suite 1645/1645.
Follow-ups
UI mode selector (Marching Cubes / Dual contouring) on the export panel.
DC boundary cap (the analog of S9.2 follow-up — cap Marching Cubes mesh at the sample-cube boundary (open when the set exceeds range) #422 for the DC path).

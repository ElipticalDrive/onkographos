Part of #357. Local lights now scatter in the volumetric fog volume.
Change
Inject shader (fog_scattering.comp.glsl). At each froxel midpoint, the shader runs the same select_lights cluster lookup that surfaces use and loops the selected lights. It skips:
the sun index, which is already added;
lights without LIGHT_VOLUMETRIC;
lights whose sample_light radiance is zero.
Each remaining light adds albedo × HG(dot(ray, light_dir), fog_anisotropy) × radiance × visibility. Visibility is one zero-normal shadow_visibility sample when light_casts_shadow, and 1 otherwise. Directional lights other than the sun are included.
Light.volumetric.
A positive bool. The directional, point and spot constructors set it to true, following the ViewDesc.share_shadows precedent.
A bare Light{} does not scatter, as the docstring, materials.md and sky.md state.
It maps to the new LIGHT_VOLUMETRIC bit (abi/c3d.abi, regenerated). LightGpu keeps its size.
The light inspector gains a checkbox.
Serialization.
Light moves to serial version 2, following the FootIk/LodGroup convention. The old layout is kept as a private LightV1 with the old fingerprint, read by read_light_v1 with volumetric = true, and text_default_versions = {1}.
Checked end to end with throwaway programs. A v1 payload written by main reads back identical with volumetric true, in both binary and text. A v2 round trip keeps volumetric = false.
Example. sky gains:
--local-lights N: lamps along the path ahead of the eye-level presets. The first 8 cast shadows; the atlas grows to 32 layers at 1024 when lamps exist.
--lamp-range and --lamps-in-fog on|off, added for the reproducible cost and opt-out rows below.
A "Lamps in fog" checkbox.
Docs. sky.md describes the rule, the cost lever (froxels × lights per cluster), the opt-out and the cluster-grid fallback share; materials.md, serialization.md and benchmarking.md cover the field, version 2 and the new flags. No measurements.

Verdict: MERGE at ef96ba3, once CI is green
Local lights scatter in the fog volume as approved. A scene without them is byte-identical to main on the 4090, and the cost scales with lights per cell as expected.
Review
Shader.
additional_light_scattering uses the surface's select_lights lookup at the froxel midpoint. It skips the sun (index + 1 == sun_light), lights without LIGHT_VOLUMETRIC, and lights with zero radiance at the midpoint.
The phase dot(direction, L) matches the sun path's -dot(direction, sun_direction), with L pointing toward the light, so positive anisotropy brightens looking toward a lamp.
There's one shadow sample with a zero normal for shadowed lights, and the midpoint and view depth are hoisted so the sun and the lamps share them.
API and serialization.
Light.volumetric is a positive field the three constructors set, and the docs say a bare Light{} doesn't scatter, per the polarity ruling.
Light goes to v2 with a LightV1 reader that adds volumetric = true, registered as the v1 binary and text compatibility path. The policy inventory now expects version 2, and the inspector has the checkbox.
Tests. The two flags == asserts are masked to the bit they check. That's needed, since constructor lights now carry LIGHT_VOLUMETRIC.
Divergences. Accepted: --lamp-range and --lamps-in-fog (they make the cost rows reproducible), and only the first 8 lamps being shadowed (atlas capacity, documented). The run-cap slip is noted.
Docs. The 22.6% / 65.4% flat-fallback shares are geometric, from the grid and field of view, not measurements, so they belong in product docs as the lever's explanation.

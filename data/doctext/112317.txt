Summary
This release adds comprehensive support for volumetric rendering (participating media), advanced material features (metal edge tint, thin-film interference, flakes, subsurface scattering), photometric lights (IES profiles, spot lights), procedural sky rendering, and post-processing imager effects (bloom, glare, vignette, white balance).
Key Changes
Volumetric & Spectral Rendering
Participating media support: Added ParticipatingMedium struct for homogeneous volumes with absorption and scattering coefficients
Henyey–Greenstein phase function (core/sampling/phase.h): Scattering direction distribution in volumetric media
Spectral wavelength sampling (core/color/spectral.h, spectral.cpp): CIE color matching functions and wavelength-to-RGB conversion for dispersion (rainbow refraction)
Path tracer volumetric integration: Updated path_tracer.cpp to handle ray marching through media with proper energy conservation
Advanced Material Features
Metal edge tint (F82) (materials/fresnel.h): OpenPBR-style edge coloring for metals via complex-indexed Fresnel
Thin-film interference (fresnel.h): Airy summation for soap bubbles, oil films, and iridescence
Metallic flakes (materials/disney.cpp): Procedural sparkle layer with micro-facet distribution
Subsurface scattering (SSS): Random-walk approximation for wax, skin, marble, and translucent materials
Multi-scattering energy compensation (materials/microfacet.cpp): Turquin 2019 method for rough metal energy conservation
Fresnel models (materials/fresnel.h): Conductor (metal), dielectric, and thin-film implementations
Photometric Lighting
IES LM-63 parser (lights/ies.cpp, ies.h): Real photometric profiles from fixture manufacturers
Spot light with cone falloff (lights/spot_light.cpp, spot_light.h): Delta light with optional IES profile and soft cone blending
Procedural sky model (lights/sky_model.cpp, sky_model.h): Nishita single-scattering atmosphere with NOAA sun position calculation
Spherical rectangle solid angle (lights/area_light.cpp): Improved PDF for rectangular lights at small solid angles
Post-Processing Imager
Bloom (core/image/imager.cpp): Multi-scale Gaussian blur with threshold
Glare/star burst: Polygonal aperture diffraction pattern
Vignette: Corner darkening
White balance: Bradford chromatic adaptation for color temperature (Kelvin)
Log-contrast & saturation: Tone curve adjustments
Lateral chromatic aberration: RGB channel shift
Material Library & UI
Material presets: 20+ new materials (honey, opal glass, jade, marble, wax, skin, soap, milk, metallic paints, pearlescent, etc.)
Advanced material JSON schema: Support for dispersive IOR, subsurface parameters, and thin-film thickness
Preset signature caching: Optimized thumbnail regeneration
Material editing via presets: Dielectric and Disney materials now edit through preset system to preserve advanced fields
Testing & Benchmarks
Comprehensive test suites:
test_media.cpp: Henyey–Greenstein sampling, Beer–Lambert absorption, energy conservation in volumes
test_metal.cpp: Multi-scattering compensation, F82 tint, thin-film reduction, metallic flakes
test_lights.cpp: Solid angle PDF, IES parsing, spot cone falloff, sky model
test_imager.cpp: Bloom, vignette, white balance, aperture sampling
Benchmark scenes: 5 new .photon project files showcasing bokeh, metals, volumes, misc features, and sky

Volumes, random-walk SSS and spectral dispersion

Integrator (PathTracer::Li):
- Medium stack: refraction through a volume boundary (Dielectric) enters or
  leaves that object's interior medium; nested volumes keep the innermost.
- Homogeneous media: Beer-Lambert absorption per segment; scattering media
  use free-flight sampling with spectral MIS (channel picked by throughput,
  pdf = mixture over channels) and Henyey-Greenstein phase sampling.
- Random-walk subsurface scattering (Chiang 2016 / Cycles): the Disney
  'subsurface' lobe enters the surface, walks inside the mesh and exits at
  a white Lambertian vertex that gets full NEE. The walk is not a bounce.
- Spectral dispersion: the first dispersive hit picks a wavelength
  (importance sampled, nonnegative CIE->sRGB weights that average to white)
  and the path continues monochromatic with n(lambda).
- New Material hooks: sampleFull/BSDFSample, isVolumeBoundary,
  interiorMedium, isDispersive, subsurfaceCoeffs. SurfaceInteraction
  carries the path wavelength.

Materials:
- Dielectric: setAbsorption (color reached at a distance), setScattering
  (density, color, anisotropy), setAbbe (Cauchy n(lambda) from n_d and V).
- Disney: subsurface weight / radius / scale / anisotropy (Blender 4
  Principled semantics); parameters for edge tint, thin film and flakes.
- MaterialPreset carries every advanced field; presets and project files
  share the same JSON keys (presetAdvancedToJson/FromJson); thumbnails and
  the material panel detect changes with presetSignature.

Tests: HG mean cosine and normalization, Beer-Lambert through an
index-matched slab, scattering-medium and dispersive-glass white furnaces,
spectral weights average to white, Cauchy vs Abbe definition, random-walk
albedo inversion. bench/scenes/materials_volume.photon (generated by
scripts/gen_material_scenes.py).

  parameter; white reproduces Schlick exactly.

  a narrow GGX flake lobe replaces the base under the clear coat.

'Yarı Saydam' category with SSS: wax, jade, skin, soap, milk, marble.

complex vs dielectric Fresnel, Gulbrandsen round trip, thin film at zero
thickness, flake coverage and energy bound.

Sampling (measured on bench/scenes/showcase.photon, 480x270, same spp:
relMSE vs a 2048 spp reference 0.143 -> 0.074 at 16 spp, 0.042 -> 0.020 at
64 spp; mean unchanged):
- Area lights are sampled uniformly in solid angle as spherical rectangles
  (Urena, Fajardo, King 2013); area sampling below 1e-3 sr.
- Per-vertex light selection proportional to each light's estimated
  contribution (Light::importance) plus a 15% uniform floor; the same pmf
  is reused for MIS when a BSDF ray hits an emitter.
- Scene maps hit shapes to light indices: emitter pdf is O(1)
  (Light::pdfLiHit); the dynamic_cast scan over all lights is gone.

Lights:
- Area lights can be invisible to the camera (camera rays pass through;
  reflections and lighting keep them). New LightDesc default: invisible.
- Spot light with smooth cone falloff and optional IES LM-63 profile
  (parser with TILT=INCLUDE skipping and 0/90/180/360 symmetry folding).
  Three procedural CC0 sample profiles under assets/ies/.

Environment:
- Physical sky (Nishita single scattering with Rayleigh, Mie and ozone)
  baked to an equirect image; the sun is a separately sampled cone inside
  EnvironmentLight (mixture pdf, MIS-consistent). NOAA solar position from
  latitude/longitude/day/time; cached per parameter set.
- Shadow catcher ground: camera sees the background darkened by the ratio
  of visible to total direct light; alpha carries the shadow opacity on a
  transparent background. Indirect rays still see the ground.

App: spot/IES controls and viewport cone gizmo, camera visibility toggle,
HDRI vs physical sky switch with sun / location-time / haze / ozone
controls, five physical sky presets with thumbnails, shadow catcher toggle.

Tests: spherical rectangle solid angle, pdf and irradiance vs quadrature;
IES parsing and symmetry; spot falloff; camera-invisible quad; solar
position for Istanbul; sky colours at noon and sunset; sun-disk sampling
pdf and irradiance; shadow catcher lit/shadowed values and alpha.
scripts/compare_exr.py measures relMSE against a reference.

- core/image/imager: post-processing on the scene-linear image before tone
  mapping, like Octane's imager / KeyShot image styles: lateral chromatic
  aberration, multi-octave Gaussian bloom over a soft threshold, star glare
  (exponentially weighted line kernels), Bradford white balance from a
  Planck/CIE-daylight illuminant to D65, log contrast around mid grey,
  saturation and vignette. Identity by default; applied to the viewport,
  final PNG/JPEG renders, turntables and the CLI (EXR stays raw).
- Thin lens camera: N-blade polygonal aperture with rotation (area-uniform
  polygon sampling) for hexagonal/octagonal bokeh.
- Adaptive sampling rewritten: Film tracks per-pixel luminance squares and
  reports the relative standard error; final renders stop converged pixels
  every 8 samples, progressive passes skip pixels whose 3x3 neighbourhood
  is under the noise threshold. Measured on the showcase at the same mean
  spp: relMSE 0.0036 (uniform, extrapolated) vs 0.0024 (adaptive).
- Optional indirect clamp (fireflies): contributions reaching a light from
  the second or later non-delta vertex are capped; glass and mirror chains
  stay direct.
- CLI: --noise, --min-spp, --clamp, --time (progressive with a time limit);
  render.imager / clampIndirect / adaptive are stored in project files.
- App: Bloom/glare, colour and lens-effect sections in the image tab,
  adaptive threshold and clamp controls, aperture blades in the camera tab.

Tests: imager identity, white balance neutralises a 3000 K illuminant,
bloom locality, vignette, polygonal aperture bounds and uniformity, film
relative error ~ 1/sqrt(N).

Material panel:
- Glass is rebuilt through MaterialPreset, so editing IOR or roughness no
  longer drops absorption, scattering or dispersion (it did before: the
  panel constructed a bare Dielectric).
- New sections: volume colour/distance and cloudiness, dispersion (Abbe)
  with crystal/sapphire/diamond presets, thin wall + soap film, rounded
  edges; for Principled: subsurface (weight, radius colour, scale,
  anisotropy), metal edge tint, thin film, metallic flakes, rounded edges.

Rounded edges (KeyShot 'round edges', Cycles bevel): the integrator blends
the shading normal with neighbouring surface normals found by short rays
from a disk around the hit (three projection axes, same object only) for
the first three vertices; geometry is untouched.

Thin-walled glass (window panes, bulbs, soap bubbles): no refraction
offset, no interior medium; optional Airy film is the wall itself. Shadow
rays pass through with coloured transmittance, and the straight-through
BSDF event keeps the previous vertex's MIS state so the two strategies
never double count.

Presets: soap bubble, thin window glass. Tests: thin wall furnace with and
without film, index-matched pane leaves lighting unchanged (MIS), rounded
normal bends only near edges.

- Thin-walled / sharp dielectric: divide the sampled weight by |cos θi| so
  the integrator's cosine factor no longer double-counts it.

  background instead of only writing coverage.

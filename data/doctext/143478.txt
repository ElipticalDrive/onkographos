This paper presents a practical model for subsurface light transport in translucent materials, enabling efficient simulation of effects like color bleeding and light diffusion across shadows. The model combines single scattering solutions with a dipole point source diffusion approximation for multiple scattering, making it suitable for highly scattering media. Additionally, a rapid image-based measurement technique is introduced to determine the optical properties of various materials, validating the model against measured values.

Implemented a pointer-based Sparse Spatial Octree optimized for volumetric data such as entity trails and scent fields. The system features:
Efficient Storage: Recursive subdivision allows for fine resolution in active areas while remaining memory-sparse.
Physics Simulation:
Diffusion: Spreads density between neighboring voxels.
Decay: Fades density over time.
Drift: Simulates uniform environmental motion (e.g., wind) by shifting the octree's world-space reference frame in O(1) time.
Gradient Following: Enables entities to navigate using the density field's gradient, supporting ant-colony-style behaviors.
Pruning Optimization: Internal nodes store the maximum density of their children, preventing dilution and ensuring correct hierarchical traversal even for faint trails.
Interactive Demo: A new example demonstrates entities leaving trails and others following them, with real-time visualization of the octree structure.

What
S9 — dual-contouring boundary cap (3D-Rendering-Roadmap §S9, Refs #391 #389). DC shipped as an interior mesher — open where the solid crossed a sample-cube face, like Marching Cubes before #422. Give DC the same watertight seal, so both meshers produce print-ready solids and the "Seal box faces" toggle governs both.
How
DualContourMesher.Build gains a capBoundary flag. On (default) it marches an extra ring of padding cells against a virtual outside shell (out-of-grid corners = +OUTSIDE) over the padded cell space [-1, n]; box-face crossings place cell vertices flush at the face, and the extended edge-quad loops connect them into a cap. Byte-identical no-op when the surface is interior (shell corners all outside → no padding cell activates).
capBoundary threaded through ExportDualContouring (both overloads). The UserBulb export site passes the same ExportCapBoundary the MC path uses, so the "Seal box faces" checkbox now applies to both meshers (tooltips updated).
Tests
DualContouringTests:
Undersized_Dc_Capped_Is_Closed_Solid — undersized sphere → closed + outward, BoundaryEdgeCount == 0.
Undersized_Dc_Uncapped_Leaves_Boundary_Open — same, cap off → open (documents raw DC).
Dc_Cap_Is_NoOp_When_Surface_Interior — capped == uncapped tri count when interior.
Suite 1649/1649.
Remaining S9
orbit-trap MC colour driver, weld-based manifold auto-repair.

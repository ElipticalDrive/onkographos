What and why
The app had no way to find the voxel at the center of the screen. The legacy project used Physics.Raycast on the mesh colliders. The app has no colliders. This pull request adds a voxel ray that reads the voxel data directly, and draws a box around the voxel at the screen center.
Fixes #40
Changes
Voxel data (VoxelWorld): the world builder now keeps the block types of each chunk. A position outside all the chunks is empty. Min and Max give the world limits. The sample scene uses 32 MiB for the voxels. Remove the allocations from the mesher #45 changes this storage in M2.
Voxel ray (VoxelRay.TryCast): the voxel traversal of Amanatides and Woo. The result (VoxelRayHit) gives:
the solid voxel and its block type,
the face that the ray crosses (Normal and Face),
the empty voxel before the hit (EmptyVoxel),
the distance.
The ray stops at the maximum distance (default: 64 voxels) or at the world limits. At an edge or a corner, the ray crosses X first, then Y, then Z.
Selection box: a new pipeline draws the 12 edges of the voxel as black lines. The vertex shader makes the vertices, thus there is no vertex buffer. The depth test hides the edges behind voxels. The shader moves the lines a little nearer to the camera. Without this offset, the faces hid parts of the lines.
Debug overlay: a new line "Target" gives the voxel, the block type, the face and the distance.
Shader compiler: the two pipelines use one compiler. A second compiler added approximately 60 ms to the start of the app.
Tests
31 tests for the ray, and 12 tests for the voxel world:
rays along each axis in the two directions,
diagonal rays and the order at a corner,
rays across chunk borders,
negative coordinates,
origins on a voxel boundary,
the maximum distance and the world limits,
an origin in a solid voxel,
incorrect arguments.
300 random rays are compared with a ray that is sampled every 0.0001 voxels. A ray that goes near an edge or a corner is skipped. More than 200 rays must be checked.
Mutations that the tests find: the tie order, the sign of the normal, Floor changed to a truncation, > changed to >= at the maximum distance, an incorrect first boundary, a world box one voxel too small.
Manual test (Xvfb and lavapipe, Linux)
The box follows the voxel at the screen center, on the top faces and on the walls.
Debug build with the synchronization validation (VK_LAYER_ENABLES=VK_VALIDATION_FEATURE_ENABLE_SYNCHRONIZATION_VALIDATION_EXT): 0 error and 0 warning messages.
Start of the pipelines: approximately 100 ms, as before.
An independent review found no blocking defect. The last commit corrects the defect and the text problems that it found. The defect: an origin exactly on a voxel boundary, with a ray in the negative direction, hit the voxel behind the origin.
To check on Windows
Start the app, then press M to go to fly mode.
Look at a voxel. The black box must be on the voxel at the screen center.
Look at the "Target" line in the overlay (F3).
Notes for #41
The ray also runs in menu mode. In menu mode, the cursor is free. Thus Remove and place voxels with the mouse #41 must use the clicks only in fly mode.
EmptyVoxel can be outside the world, for example above the highest chunk layer. Remove and place voxels with the mouse #41 must not place a block there, or in the voxel of the camera.
VoxelWorld has no SetVoxel method yet. Remove and place voxels with the mouse #41 adds it.

```
After the start screen, the main loop casts the voxel ray from the
camera position along the forward vector of the camera in each frame.
This ray goes through the center of the screen.

A new pipeline draws the 12 edges of the selected voxel as black
lines. The vertex shader makes the vertices, thus the draw has no
vertex buffer. The depth test hides the edges behind voxels. The box
is 0.005 voxels larger than the voxel, and the shader moves the lines
a little nearer to the camera. Without this offset, the faces hid
parts of the lines.

The debug overlay shows the target: the voxel, its block type, the
face and the distance.

The two pipelines now use one shader compiler. A second compiler added
approximately 60 ms to the start of the app.
```

fix(#40): Start the voxel ray correctly on a voxel boundary
…
bdb8f9e
```
The review found this defect: an origin exactly on a voxel boundary,
with a ray in the negative direction, started in the voxel after the
boundary. If that voxel was solid, the ray hit it from inside, but the
ray only left that voxel.

Now an origin on a boundary starts in the voxel on the side of the
direction. If that voxel is solid, the ray enters it through the
boundary face at the distance 0.

The review also found text that was not correct:
- The tests of the world limits cannot see where the ray stops. Their
  names and comments now say this.
- A change of a chunk array after AddChunk is permitted. #41 changes
  voxels in place.
- The precision note and the depth offset note were too strong.
```

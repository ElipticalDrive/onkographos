A texture unit whose SET_TEXTURE_FORMAT dimension is 3 (with a depth in CONTROL3) is a volume. The 3D path added in 1ba3657 takes uncompressed formats only, so a DXT volume still went up as a 2D texture made of its first slice-sized bytes. The fragment program, given the unit in the volume mask, declared it Texture3D all the same.
A retail title draws its explosion smoke from 256x256x16 DXT5 volumes in main memory (SET_TEXTURE_FORMAT 0x0001883A: dimension 3, DXT45; depth 16), the animation running along the third coordinate. Compressed volumes whose sides are powers of two are stored in NVIDIA's VTC order: the slices go in groups of four, and within a group the four slices' blocks at one position follow each other. Read as one 2D image, the first 64 KB are four slices interleaved block by block, and one of the volumes holds a near-empty first group whose every fourth block is opaque black. The smoke drew as dark translucent squares with hard edges.
Change
eng_texture_upload: block-compressed formats take the 3D path too.
A level whose width and height are powers of two is in VTC order. eng_unvtc_3d_slice lays a slice out of it into the staging rows (the depth % 4 slices left over form a smaller last group). It is written from the NV_texture_compression_vtc spec alone, cited in the source. For full groups it is the spec's blockIndex. For the last group the spec's formula stores the slices one after another, which contradicts its prose (4x4x2 and 4x4x3 blocks) when depth % 4 >= 2; the prose is followed.
Any other level is slice after slice, as RPCS3 treats it.
Metal texture_upload_3d: a block-compressed slice spans its block rows. bytesPerImage was row bytes times texel rows, four times the slice. That was harmless while each upload is one slice, but not what the call means.
Tests
test_rsx_draw_engine, a new group with a stub backend that has the 3D calls:
an 8x8x6 DXT5 volume (a group of four and a group of two) goes up as six slices, every block where its slice put it;
a 12x4x2 DXT5 volume (not a power of two) goes up slice after slice;
a 16x16x8 DXT1 volume with three levels (8, 4 and 2 slices) goes up level by level, each out of its own VTC order.
Each check fails without the change. A VTC order without the last group's own stride also fails, and so do levels not a whole level apart.
engine_up became engine_up_with(backend) so a group can bring the engine up on its own backend.
The group passes under ASan, with clang and with gcc.
In the title, a per-draw capture traced the squares to the draws that sample those volumes. Their bytes, taken out of VTC order, are the smoke's slices.
Provenance
The first version of eng_unvtc_3d_slice() was a port of RPCS3's copy_unmodified_block_vtc; the licence review held the PR for those eight lines. 53d9ecd rewrites them (the earlier version is still in this branch's history, so a squash merge would keep it out of master's) from the Khronos spec alone, without the earlier code in view, and drops the RPCS3 link. A standalone check compared the new function with a direct implementation of the spec's blockIndex for every block of eleven volumes (depth % 4 of 0 or 1) and with the prose layout for depths 1-7, 10 and 11, at 8 and 16 bytes per block, under ASan and UBSan. The three volume tests above pass unchanged.
v0.18.0 merged in (acc93bb): the volume path keeps master's !into condition (no 3D path for an upload into an existing texture). On this branch (macOS arm64) ctest passes (22 of 22) and so do the tools/test_*.py scripts (120 of 120).

Licence and provenance review — held on eight lines, and the cheapest hold in this batch to clear
Thank you for declaring the origin in the source. You wrote "Ported from RPCS3's copy_unmodified_block_vtc" with a pinned permalink, which is exactly what CONTRIBUTING.md asks for, and it is the reason this surfaced in review rather than after a release. The problem is the source, not the disclosure.
Why it's held
eng_unvtc_3d_slice() in libs/video/rsx_draw_engine.c is declared a port of RPCS3's copy_unmodified_block_vtc (TextureUtils.cpp#L279-L339). RPCS3 is GPL-2.0, and CONTRIBUTING.md applies the rule regardless of size — "even rewritten line by line". Eight lines is still eight lines from a GPL file.
The way through is already in your own comment
The same comment cites the Khronos NV_texture_compression_vtc extension, and that spec specifies the layout independently and completely: slices in groups of four, the four slices' blocks at one position following each other within a group, and the depth % 4 remainder forming a smaller last group with its own stride. Rewrite the eight lines from that spec alone, cite the spec, drop the RPCS3 link, and this lands.
What is not in question
The rest of the PR is unaffected, and it is a real bug fix I want: block-compressed formats taking the 3D path in eng_texture_upload, the power-of-two VTC condition, the Metal texture_upload_3d bytesPerImage correction, and the three volume tests (8×8×6 DXT5, 12×4×2 non-power-of-two, 16×16×8 DXT1 with three levels). The failure analysis — every fourth block opaque black because the first 64 KB are four interleaved slices — is well traced.
Of the three licence holds in this batch this is the smallest by a wide margin. Re-source the eight lines and it goes in.

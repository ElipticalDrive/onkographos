# Empty-space skipping

Also called: Block Empty Space Skipping, Chebyshev Empty Space Skipping, Empty Region Skipping, Occupancy Grid Pruning

## Overview
Empty-space skipping (ESS) is a ray marching optimization that accelerates volume rendering by skipping regions of the scene that are empty or occluded, thereby reducing the number of samples required to evaluate the radiance field [S1, S2, S3]. It sets itself apart from sibling methods like [GL-NeRF](803-gl-nerf.md) or [VoISDF](821-voisdf-volume-rendering-and-adaptive-sampling.md) by explicitly maintaining auxiliary data structures (occupancy grids or distance maps) to prune traversal, whereas other siblings may rely on hierarchical tree structures or adaptive sampling rates without explicit empty-region culling [S2, S3].

## How it works
**Inputs:** Ray origin $\mathbf{o}$, ray direction $\mathbf{d}$, volume dimensions $\text{dim}$, sampling factor, and precomputed occupancy/distance maps.
**Outputs:** Accumulated color and opacity for the pixel.

**Data Structures:**
*   **Occupancy Grid / Distance Map:** A 3D texture (`texOccupancyMap` or `texDistanceMap`) storing precomputed values. Accessed via `texelFetch`.
    *   `texOccupancyMap`: Stores binary occupancy (1 if occupied, 0 if empty).
    *   `texDistanceMap`: Stores distance to the nearest occupied voxel.
*   **Parameters:**
    *   `essType`: Integer flag. `1` = Block Empty Space Skipping; `2` = Chebyshev Empty Space Skipping [S1].
    *   `alpha_thre`: Opacity threshold for empty regions. Default: $10^{-2}$ [S2].
    *   `early_stop_eps`: Transmittance threshold for early termination. Default: $10^{-4}$ [S2].
    *   `sampling_factor`: Controls step count relative to volume dimensions [S1].

**Algorithm Steps:**

1.  **Ray Initialization:**
    Calculate the number of steps and the step vector.
    $$ n_{\text{steps}} = \lceil \text{dim}_{\max} \times \text{ray\_length} \times \text{sampling\_factor} \rceil $$
    $$ \text{step\_vector} = \frac{\mathbf{d}}{\|\mathbf{d}\|} \times \frac{\text{ray\_length}}{n_{\text{steps}} - 1} $$
    Handle degenerate rays (infinite, NaN, or length $> \sqrt{3}$) by terminating immediately [S1].

2.  **Distance Map Coordinate Setup:**
    Compute mapping parameters between volume space and distance map texture space.
    $$ \text{dim\_distance\_map} = \text{textureSize}(\text{texDistanceMap}, 0).\text{xyz} $$
    $$ \text{block\_size} = \lceil \frac{\text{volumeSize}}{\text{dim\_distance\_map}} \rceil $$
    $$ \text{volume\_to\_distance\_map} = \frac{\text{volumeSize}}{\text{block\_size} \times \text{dim\_distance\_map}} $$
    $$ \text{step\_dist\_texel} = \text{step\_vector} \times \frac{\text{volumeSize}}{\text{block\_size}} $$
    $$ \text{step\_dist\_texel\_inv} = \frac{1}{\text{step\_dist\_texel}} $$

3.  **Dithering (Optional):**
    To reduce banding artifacts, compute a random offset.
    If `ditheringMode == 1`:
    $$ \text{random} = \text{fract}(\sin(\text{gl\_FragCoord}.x \times 12.9898 + \text{gl\_FragCoord}.y \times 78.233) \times 43758.5453) $$
    If `ditheringMode == 2`:
    $$ \text{random} = \text{texture}(\text{texNoise}, \frac{\text{gl\_FragCoord.xy}}{256}).r $$

4.  **Ray Marching Loop:**
    Iterate with index $i$. For each step:
    
    a. **Sampling Position:**
    $$ \text{samplingPos} = \mathbf{o} + (i + \text{random}) \times \text{step\_vector} $$
    
    b. **Map to Distance Map Coordinates:**
    $$ \text{pos\_distance\_map} = \text{volume\_to\_distance\_map} \times \text{samplingPos} $$
    $$ u = \text{pos\_distance\_map} \times \text{dim\_distance\_map} $$
    $$ u_i = \text{ivec3}(\text{clamp}(\lfloor u \rfloor, \text{ivec3}(0), \text{dim\_distance\_map} - 1)) $$
    
    c. **Fetch Distance/Occupancy:**
    Determine index based on ray direction signs for directional distance maps (if applicable):
    $$ \text{distance\_map\_idx} = (\mathbf{d}.z < 0 ? 1 : 0) + (\mathbf{d}.y < 0 ? 2 : 0) + (\mathbf{d}.x < 0 ? 4 : 0) $$
    Fetch value:
    If `essType == 1` (Block):
    $$ \text{dist} = \text{texelFetch}(\text{texOccupancyMap}, u_i, 0).x $$
    If `essType == 2` (Chebyshev):
    $$ \text{dist} = \text{texelFetch}(\text{texDistanceMap}, u_i, 0).x $$
    
    d. **Calculate Skip Delta:**
    Compute fractional offset for block alignment:
    $$ r = \text{clamp}(u_i - u, -1.0, 0.0) $$
    
    If `essType == 1` (Block ESS):
    $$ i_{\text{delta\_xyz}} = (\text{step}(0.0, \text{step\_dist\_texel\_inv}) + r) \times \text{step\_dist\_texel\_inv} $$
    
    If `essType == 2` (Chebyshev ESS):
    $$ i_{\text{delta\_xyz}} = (\text{step}(0.0, -\text{step\_dist\_texel\_inv}) + \text{sign}(\text{step\_dist\_texel\_inv}) \times \text{dist} + r) \times \text{step\_dist\_texel\_inv} $$
    
    Update loop index:
    $$ i_{\text{delta}} = \max(1, \lceil \min(i_{\text{delta\_xyz}}.x, i_{\text{delta\_xyz}}.y, i_{\text{delta\_xyz}}.z) \rceil) $$
    $$ i \leftarrow i + i_{\text{delta}} $$
    
    e. **Backtracking / Empty Space Handling:**
    If `dist == 0` (empty space reached):
    $$ i_{\text{delta\_backwards}} = -\lceil \text{sampling\_factor} \rceil $$
    $$ \text{skipping} = \text{false} $$
    $$ i = \max(i + i_{\text{delta\_backwards}}, i_{\min}) $$
    [S1]
    
    f. **Volume Evaluation (if not skipped):**
    If ESS is disabled or the region is occupied:
    1.  Get voxel value: `voxelValue = getVolumeIntensityValue(samplingPos)`.
    2.  Transfer Function: `color = texture(texITF, vec2(voxelValue, 0.5))`.
    3.  Gamma Correction: `color.a = pow(color.a, gamma)` if enabled.
    4.  Gradient/Lighting: Compute normal from gradient, apply diffuse/specular lighting.
    5.  Composite:
        $$ \text{color}.a = \text{clamp}(1.0 \times (1.0 - (1.0 - \text{color}.a)^{\text{sampling\_factor\_inv}}), 0.0, 1.0) $$
        $$ \text{color}.xyz \times= \text{color}.a $$
        $$ \text{out\_color} \leftarrow \text{out\_color} + (1.0 - \text{out\_color}.a) \times \text{color} $$

5.  **Early Ray Termination (ERT):**
    If `out_color.a > 0.98` and ERT is enabled, set `out_color.a = 1.0` and break loop [S1, S2].

**Pseudocode:**
```python
def empty_space_skipping_ray_march(ray_origin, ray_dir, volume_size, dim_distance_map, 
                                   tex_occupancy, tex_distance, ess_type, sampling_factor, 
                                   alpha_thre, early_stop_eps, dithering_mode):
    # 1. Initialize
    ray_length = length(ray_dir)
    n_steps = ceil(volume_size * ray_length * sampling_factor)
    if n_steps < 2: n_steps = 2
    step_vector = normalize(ray_dir) * (ray_length / (n_steps - 1))
    
    # Degenerate ray check
    if isnan(ray_length) or ray_length > sqrt(3):
        return vec4(0.0)

    # 2. Setup Distance Map Coords
    block_size = ceil(volume_size / dim_distance_map)
    volume_to_dm = volume_size / (block_size * dim_distance_map)
    step_dist_texel = step_vector * (volume_size / block_size)
    step_dist_texel_inv = 1.0 / step_dist_texel
    
    # 3. Dithering
    if dithering_mode == 1:
        random_val = fract(sin(gl_frag_coord.x * 12.9898 + gl_frag_coord.y * 78.233) * 43758.5453)
    elif dithering_mode == 2:
        random_val = texture(tex_noise, gl_frag_coord.xy / 256.0).r
    else:
        random_val = 0.0
        
    out_color = vec4(0.0)
    i = 0
    
    # 4. Marching Loop
    while i < n_steps:
        # Sampling Position
        sampling_pos = ray_origin + (i + random_val) * step_vector
        
        # Map to DM Coords
        pos_dm = volume_to_dm * sampling_pos
        u = pos_dm * dim_distance_map
        u_i = ivec3(clamp(floor(u), ivec3(0), dim_distance_map - 1))
        
        # Fetch Value
        if ess_type == 1: # Block
            dist = texel_fetch(tex_occupancy, u_i, 0).x
        else: # Chebyshev
            dist = texel_fetch(tex_distance, u_i, 0).x
            
        # Calculate Delta
        r = clamp(u_i - u, -1.0, 0.0)
        
        if ess_type == 1:
            i_delta_xyz = (step(0.0, step_dist_texel_inv) + r) * step_dist_texel_inv
        else:
            i_delta_xyz = (step(0.0, -step_dist_texel_inv) + sign(step_dist_texel_inv) * dist + r) * step_dist_texel_inv
            
        i_delta = max(1, ceil(min(min(i_delta_xyz.x, i_delta_xyz.y), i_delta_xyz.z)))
        
        # Handle Empty Space (dist == 0)
        if dist == 0:
            i_delta_backwards = -ceil(sampling_factor)
            i = max(i + i_delta_backwards, 0)
            continue
            
        i += i_delta
        
        # Evaluate Volume (if not skipped)
        # Note: In a real implementation, 'sampling_pos' must be mapped back to volume coords
        # and clamped to volume bounds.
        voxel_val = get_volume_intensity(sampling_pos)
        
        # Transfer Function
        color = texture(tex_itf, vec2(voxel_val, 0.5))
        
        # Lighting (Simplified)
        normal = -get_gradient(sampling_pos)
        diffuse = max(0.0, dot(normal, normalize(light_dir)))
        color.rgb = color.rgb * (1.0 + diffuse)
        
        # Composite
        color.a = clamp(1.0 * (1.0 - pow(1.0 - color.a, 1.0/sampling_factor)), 0.0, 1.0)
        color.rgb *= color.a
        out_color = out_color + (1.0 - out_color.a) * color
        
        # Early Ray Termination
        if out_color.a > 0.98:
            out_color.a = 1.0
            break
            
    return out_color
```

## Subtopics

### [Ray Marcher with Macrocell Empty Space Skipping](1009-ray-marcher-with-macrocell-empty-space-skipping.md)
Uses precomputed macrocell densities to skip empty space. Chosen when coarse elements have zero density.

### [Empty Space Skipping with Orthogonal BSP Tree](1054-empty-space-skipping-with-orthogonal-bsp-tree.md)
Partitions volume into sub-volumes in an orthogonal BSP tree for visibility sorting. Chosen for texture-based rendering with complex transfer functions.

## Operating characteristics
**Time Complexity:** Proportional to the number of *occupied* samples rather than total volume depth. In sparse volumes, this is significantly faster than uniform sampling.
**Memory Complexity:** Requires auxiliary storage for the occupancy grid or distance map. The size depends on the resolution of the map relative to the volume [S2].
**Performance:** Can prune up to 98% of samples in dense surface scenes (e.g., Lego scene in NeRF-Synthetic) [S2].
**Strengths:** Reduces GPU memory usage by parallelizing across rays rather than over-generating samples [S2]. Prevents performance regressions from degenerate rays [S1].
**Limitations:** Requires precomputation or online caching of the occupancy/distance map. Skipping occluded regions requires precise density evaluation to compute transmittance accurately [S2]. Parallel efficiency is slightly lower than sample-based parallelization because operations are grouped by ray [S2].
**When to use:** Sparse volumes, NeRF training/inference where memory is constrained, or when empty regions are predictable [S1, S2].
**When not to use:** Dense, fully opaque volumes where skipping provides little benefit, or when precomputation time is prohibitive.

## Background
Empty-space skipping was established as an acceleration technique for direct volume rendering to avoid computing transparent regions [S3]. Early methods used hierarchical representations or distance fields to adapt step sizes [Zuiderveld et al. 92]. Isosurfacing was also used to find first hit locations [Avila et al. 92]. In the context of Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF), the technique was adapted using Occupancy Grids (from Instant-NGP) to cache empty regions during training, enabling efficient pruning [S2].

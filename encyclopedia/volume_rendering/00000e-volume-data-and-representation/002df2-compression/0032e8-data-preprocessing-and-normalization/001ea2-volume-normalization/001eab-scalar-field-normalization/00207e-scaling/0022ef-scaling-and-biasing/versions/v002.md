# Scaling and Biasing

Also called: BuildScaleBiasProgram, Window/Level Adjustment

## Overview
Scaling and Biasing applies scaling and biasing to texture samples for window/level contrast adjustment [S1, unofficial]. It sets the current sample's volume to $x \cdot 16 + y$ [S2, unofficial]. It differs from [Power Transformation](2346-power-transformation.md) by applying a linear transformation rather than a non-linear mapping [G].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
- Input: Texture sample value, scaling factor $x$, bias factor $y$, effect parameter, and a `vtkRenderWindow` pointer `w` for shader compilation context [S1, unofficial].
- Output: Normalized sample value / target volume.

**Data Structures:**
- `cptr`: Pointer to a structure containing a `volume` field [S2, unofficial].

**Steps:**
1. Extract the magnitude from the effect parameter using bitwise masking: `magnitude = effect & 0xFF` [S2, unofficial].
2. Compute the target volume using the linear formula $v_{out} = x \cdot 16 + y$ [S2, unofficial].
3. Assign the magnitude to the current sample: `cptr->volume = magnitude` [S2, unofficial].
4. Clamp the volume to the legal range of 0 to 64 [S2, unofficial].

**Parameters:**
- $x$ and $y$: Coefficients for the linear transformation defining the target volume [S2, unofficial].
- Legal volume range: 0 to 64 [S2, unofficial].

**Pseudocode:**
```python
def apply_scaling_and_bias(cptr, effect_param):
    magnitude = effect_param & 0xFF
    cptr.volume = magnitude
    if cptr.volume > 64:
        cptr.volume = 64
    return cptr.volume
```

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
- The volume cannot slide beyond 64 [S2, unofficial].
- The volume cannot slide beyond 0 [S2, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### Volume Slide Down
Increments the volume of the current sample by $x$ at the beginning of the division, with no sliding occurring [S2, unofficial]. Decrements the volume of the current sample by $x$ at the beginning of the division, lowering the volume [S2, unofficial]. The implementation sets `cptr->volume += (effect & 0xF)` and clamps to 64 if exceeded [S2, unofficial]. For decrement, it sets `cptr->volume -= (effect & 0xF)` and resets to 0 if the volume exceeds 200 [S2, unofficial]. Pitfalls include the inability to slide beyond volume 64 and the inability to slide beyond volume 0 [S2, unofficial].

## Background
The method originates from implementations in voxel-based terrain rendering and volume ray casting [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial]. [S1, unofficial] describes its use in a VTK-based multi-volume ray caster for window/level adjustment. [S2, unofficial] details its use in a voxel space engine for terrain rendering to adjust the volume of terrain samples.

# Upsampling and Super-Resolution


## Overview
Upsampling and Super-Resolution methods increase the spatial resolution of volumetric data or rendered images to recover fine details, correct discrete positioning errors, or optimize rendering performance [S1, unofficial][S4][S5]. This family is distinguished from sibling multiresolution techniques by its primary goal of generating higher-fidelity representations from lower-resolution inputs through geometric remapping, interpolation, or learning-based refinement [S2, unofficial][S5].

## Shared principles
The core mechanism across these methods involves expanding the spatial domain of a volume or image. Common mathematical operations include:
1. **Scaling Factors:** Defining a scale factor $s$ to relate the new resolution to the previous resolution [S2, unofficial][S4].
2. **Interpolation:** Using bilinear or bicubic kernels to estimate values at new grid points [S2, unofficial][S3, unofficial].
3. **Geometric Remapping:** Mapping existing voxel coordinates $(i, j, k)$ to new coordinates in a finer grid, often to align with sub-pixel precision [S4].
4. **Normalization:** Scaling values to a standard range (e.g., $[0, 1]$) by dividing by the global maximum to ensure consistent input for subsequent processing [2703-3d-image-resize-and-crop].

## Subtopics

### [3D Image Resize and Crop](2703-3d-image-resize-and-crop.md)
This method prepares 3D volumetric data for super-resolution tasks by generating paired high-resolution and low-resolution inputs [2703-3d-image-resize-and-crop]. It solves the problem of spatial misalignment and aspect ratio mismatch in 3D datasets by centering the volume along the Z-axis and squaring the X-Y plane via zero-padding [2703-3d-image-resize-and-crop]. It differs from [3D Fast Bilateral Solver](6478-3d-fast-bilateral-solver.md) by operating in the spatial domain using direct interpolation and normalization rather than solving optimization problems for edge-preserving smoothing [2703-3d-image-resize-and-crop].

### [3D Fast Bilateral Solver](6478-3d-fast-bilateral-solver.md)
A 3D extension to the Fast Bilateral Solver used to refine annotated similarity volumes [6478-3d-fast-bilateral-solver]. It increases the resolution of similarity maps and adapts them to the underlying intensity volume to improve visual quality [6478-3d-fast-bilateral-solver].

### Cloud Upscaling
Rendering clouds at a lower resolution and upscaling to screen resolution, leveraging their naturally blurry and fluffy appearance [S1, unofficial]. This approach exploits the perceptual tolerance for blur in volumetric cloud rendering to reduce computational cost while maintaining visual fidelity [S1, unofficial].

### Upsampling Layer
A layer that expands the spatial dimensions $x$ and $y$ of an input volume $U$ of size $x \times y \times p$ by factors $s_x$ and $s_y$ respectively to generate a volume $V$ of size $(s_x x) \times (s_y y) \times p$ [S2, unofficial]. It contains no trainable parameters and is used in the decoder to reconstruct original size [S2, unofficial].
- **Parameters:** Scale Factors: $s_x=2, s_y=2$. Filter Size: $2 \times 2$. Stride: 2. Applied in decoder layers [S2, unofficial].

### Epipolar Overlap Visualization
A visualization technique involving upsampled valid masks [S3, unofficial].
- **Step:** Extract `valid` mask from sampling data [S3, unofficial]. Upsample `valid` mask to match context image resolution using `repeat` with $r_h=ds, r_w=ds$ [S3, unofficial]. Blend valid mask with context images: `valid = valid * (green + context_images[index]) / 2` [S3, unofficial]. Concatenate context images and valid masks [S3, unofficial].
- **Parameter:** `ds`: Downscale factor for epipolar transformer, typically integer > 1 [S3, unofficial].

### Pre-processing for Double Resolution
A method to increase resolution by mapping full-resolution voxels to a double-resolution volume and filling gaps to provide more details and accurate voxel positions [S4]. Double resolution allows existing voxels to be located at the middle point between two voxels of the full-resolution volume, correcting for discrete domain positioning errors where non-corresponding pixels might map to the same voxel [S4].
- **Step:** Map the voxel at location $(i, j, k)$ of the full-resolution volume to location $(2i-1, 2j-1, 2k-1)$ of the double-resolution volume [S4]. Inspect other voxels to determine if they are part of the volume or transparent by registering depth maps of references into the double-resolution volume [S4]. Iteratively refine the constructed volume utilizing brightness information of the reference views [S4]. Properly select the colour of each voxel to render a smooth and realistic image [S4].

### 3D Super-Resolution (SR) Module
Reconstructs the original high-resolution volume from the INR output using deep learning, providing intrinsic denoising and reducing reconstruction artefacts [S5]. A 3D Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) for feature extraction that incorporates an Attention Mechanism to prioritize significant features, improving volume resolution [S5].
- **Step:** 1. Input low-resolution data (from INR) to the SRNO model. 2. Learn intricate transformations from low-resolution to high-resolution data spaces. 3. Output high-resolution volume with denoised features. 4. Train end-to-end with the INR module [S5]. Use 3D CNN for feature extraction [S5]. Incorporate an Attention Mechanism to prioritize significant features [S5]. Improve the resolution of the volume, restoring it to a level close to the original [S5].
- **Parameter:** Model: SRNO [S5]. Hyperparameter: Number of channels in the attention structure. Influences performance and is evaluated during optimization [S5].
- **Comparison:** Produces fewer artefacts (ringing and blurring) compared to other super-resolution techniques [S5].

## Background
The concept of upsampling in volumetric data is rooted in the need to mitigate aliasing and improve the perceptual quality of synthesized views [S4]. Early work in colour volumetric compression highlighted that discrete domain positioning errors lead to poor subjective quality if the representing volume is not fine enough, motivating double-resolution pre-processing [S4]. In the context of deep learning for volume representation, upsampling layers are standard components of autoencoder decoders to reconstruct high-resolution features from compressed latent spaces [S2, unofficial]. More recently, specific implementations for 3D image resizing and cropping have been developed to handle aspect ratio mismatches in datasets used for neural rendering [2703-3d-image-resize-and-crop]. Cloud rendering techniques have also adopted upscaling as a performance optimization, relying on the inherent blur of volumetric clouds to mask interpolation artifacts [S1, unofficial].

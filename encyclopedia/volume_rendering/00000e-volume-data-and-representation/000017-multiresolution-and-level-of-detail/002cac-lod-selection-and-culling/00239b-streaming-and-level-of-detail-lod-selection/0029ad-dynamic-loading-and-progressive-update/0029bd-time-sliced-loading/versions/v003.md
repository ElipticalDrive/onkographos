# Time-Sliced Loading

Also called: Frame-time limited loading, Spread loading, Time-Sliced Loading for Real-Time Stability

## Overview
Time-Sliced Loading is a strategy to maintain real-time frame rates (minimum 10Hz) during heavy data loads by spreading necessary loading operations across multiple frames, limiting the time available for loading in each frame [S1, unofficial]. It solves the problem of sudden camera movements or entering unvisited zones causing too much data to become visible simultaneously, which would otherwise exceed the time available per image for real-time refresh [S1, unofficial]. It differs from sibling strategies by explicitly capping the computational budget per frame for data transfer and processing [S1, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### Cache Pre-delivery for Progressive Loads
A mechanism to inform a volume about images already cached before its own loading stages begin, preventing empty or gray slices during progressive loads [S2, unofficial]. When `load()` starts, the volume delivers each fully loaded cached image to itself [S2, unofficial]. Deliveries run in batches of at most 8 ms, so a volume whose images are all cached does not stall [S2, unofficial]. A cancelled or restarted load stops the batches [S2, unofficial]. A delivery only marks the texture slices; each render fills them under the budget of `rendering.reducedTextureFill` [S2, unofficial]. It differs from previous behavior where the volume did not know cached images until its own stage requested them, causing nearby fills to skip frames and show gray [S2, unofficial].

### Incremental reduced texture fill
A rendering strategy that limits texture reduction work per frame to maintain interactivity during progressive loading and large volume rendering [S2, unofficial]. One render fills at most 16 reduced slices that hold data [S2, unofficial]. The render stops after 8 ms, but only after it fills one slice [S2, unofficial]. The texture requests another render, and the next fill starts where the last fill stopped [S2, unofficial]. The parameter `rendering.reducedTextureFill` defines the maximum number of reduced slices (16) and the time budget per render (8 ms) [S2, unofficial].

## Background
Time-Sliced Loading was developed as part of a broader strategy for interactive exploration of procedural volumes [S1, unofficial]. It addresses the limitations of static loading strategies in the face of dynamic camera movements and large data sets [S1, unofficial]. The approach is rooted in a general producer/consumer data management model developed on top of [LDN04] [S1, unofficial].

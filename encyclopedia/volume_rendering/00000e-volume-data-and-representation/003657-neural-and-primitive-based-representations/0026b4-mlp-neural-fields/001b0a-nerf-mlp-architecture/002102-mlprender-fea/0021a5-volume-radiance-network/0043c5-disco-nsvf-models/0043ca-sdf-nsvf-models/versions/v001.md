# SDF NSVF Models

## Overview
SDF NSVF models represent 3D geometry and appearance using a signed distance field (SDF) radiance field, registered as `sdf_nsvf` and `sdf_nsvf_sfx`. They differ from their sibling [Disco NSVF Models](11063-disco-nsvf-models.md) by utilizing a unified SDF-based representation rather than a disentangled radiance field, and the `sdf_nsvf_sfx` variant specifically employs a surface volume rendering raymarcher.

## Shared principles
The family relies on the `sdf_radiance_field` mechanism, where the neural network predicts both the signed distance to the surface and the view-dependent radiance. This builds on the [MLPRender_Fea](52-mlprender-fea.md) architecture but adapts it for SDF-based implicit surface representation.

## Subtopics

### sdf_nsvf
Uses the `nerf2nope_architecture` with specific hyperparameters: `feature_layers=6`, `feature_field_skip_connect=3`, and `no_layernorm_mlp=True`. It is the standard SDF-based NSVF implementation.

### sdf_nsvf_sfx
Distinguished by its use of the `surface_volume_rendering` raymarcher, which likely integrates geometry and appearance differently during the rendering process compared to the standard volumetric approach.

## Background
The SDF NSVF models are part of the Neural Scene Volume Fields (NSVF) framework developed by Facebook Research. The codebase is available on GitHub [S1, unofficial]. The specific registration names `sdf_nsvf` and `sdf_nsvf_sfx` are defined in the model registry of the NSVF library.

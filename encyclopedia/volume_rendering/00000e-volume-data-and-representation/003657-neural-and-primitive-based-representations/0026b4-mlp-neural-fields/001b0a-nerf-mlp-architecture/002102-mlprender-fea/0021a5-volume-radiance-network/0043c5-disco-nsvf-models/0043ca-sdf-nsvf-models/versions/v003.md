# SDF NSVF Models


## Overview
SDF NSVF models are neural volume field representations that utilize a signed distance field (SDF) to encode geometry, registered as 'sdf_nsvf' and 'sdf_nsvf_sfx' [S1, unofficial]. They differ from their parent Disco NSVF models by replacing the disentangled radiance field with an `sdf_radiance_field`, relying on the SDF for surface extraction and volume rendering [S1, unofficial].

## Shared principles
The family shares the core mechanism of mapping 3D coordinates to a signed distance value and radiance color via a neural network. The geometry is defined implicitly by the zero-level set of the SDF, while the radiance is modulated by the SDF value or its gradient [S1, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### sdf_nsvf
Uses the `nerf2nope_architecture` with specific MLP hyperparameters: `feature_layers=6`, `feature_field_skip_connect=3`, and `no_layernorm_mlp=True` [S1, unofficial]. It employs the standard volume rendering raymarcher.

### sdf_nsvf_sfx
Identical in architecture to `sdf_nsvf` but utilizes the `surface_volume_rendering` raymarcher, which likely leverages the SDF for more efficient surface-concentrated sampling [S1, unofficial].

## Background
The SDF NSVF models are part of the NSVF (Neural Scene Volume Fields) framework developed by Facebook Research [S1, unofficial]. They extend the Disco NSVF architecture by integrating SDF-based geometry representation, allowing for explicit surface extraction via marching cubes or voxelization [S1, unofficial].

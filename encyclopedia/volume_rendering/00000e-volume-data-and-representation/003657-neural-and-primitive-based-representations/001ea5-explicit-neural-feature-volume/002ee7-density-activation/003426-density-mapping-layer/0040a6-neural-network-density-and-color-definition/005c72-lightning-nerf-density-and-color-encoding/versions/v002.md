# Lightning NeRF Density and Color Encoding

Also called: Lightning NeRF Encoding

## Overview
Lightning NeRF Density and Color Encoding computes density and color features for neural radiance fields by routing ray samples through separate foreground and background encoders [S1, unofficial]. It distinguishes itself by warping background coordinates via their L-infinity norm and appending inverse scale information, allowing standard encoders to process unbounded space while preserving geometric scale [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
The method processes ray samples to produce density ($\sigma$) and color embeddings ($\mathbf{c}$) based on whether the sample point lies inside or outside a defined unit bounding box [S1, unofficial]. It utilizes separate neural encoders for foreground and background components, followed by a multi-layer perceptron (MLP) structure for final color prediction [S1, unofficial].

### Inputs and Outputs
**Inputs:**
- `positions`: Tensor of shape $(N, 3)$ containing 3D coordinates of ray samples, normalized relative to the scene's axis-aligned bounding box (AABB) such that the foreground occupies the unit box $[0, 1]^3$ [S1, unofficial].
- `ray_samples`: Object containing frustum directions and other metadata [S1, unofficial].
- `camera_indices`: (Optional) Tensor for appearance embedding lookup during training [S1, unofficial].
- `appearance_embedding_dim`: Integer dimension of the appearance embedding [S1, unofficial].
- `use_average_appearance_embedding`: Boolean flag for inference appearance handling [S1, unofficial].
- `rgb_padding`: Float value for RGB output padding [S1, unofficial].
- `appearance_embeddings`: Tensor of appearance embeddings to lookup via `camera_indices` [G].

**Outputs:**
- `density`: Tensor of shape $(N, 1)$ containing volume density values [S1, unofficial].
- `rgb`: Tensor of shape $(N, 3)$ containing final RGB color values [S1, unofficial].

### Data Structures and Encoders
The method relies on four primary encoding modules, typically implemented using Tiny CUDA Neural Networks (tcnn) for performance [S1, unofficial]:
1.  `density_encoding`: Encodes foreground positions for density [S1, unofficial].
2.  `color_encoding`: Encodes foreground positions for color features [S1, unofficial].
3.  `bg_density_encoding`: Encodes warped background positions for density [S1, unofficial].
4.  `bg_color_encoding`: Encodes warped background positions for color [S1, unofficial].
5.  `direction_encoding`: Encodes view directions [S1, unofficial].

Additionally, two MLPs are used for color prediction [S1, unofficial]:
-   `vi_mlp`: View-independent MLP [S1, unofficial].
-   `vd_mlp`: View-dependent MLP [S1, unofficial].

### Step-by-Step Procedure

1.  **Foreground/Background Segmentation**:
    Identify points inside the unit box (foreground) and outside (background) [S1, unofficial].
    $$ \text{fg\_selector} = \text{all}(0 \le \text{positions} \le 1) $$
    $$ \text{bg\_selector} = \neg \text{fg\_selector} $$
    Extract corresponding position tensors [S1, unofficial]:
    $$ \mathbf{p}_{fg} = \text{positions}[\text{fg\_selector}] $$
    $$ \mathbf{p}_{bg} = \text{positions}[\text{bg\_selector}] $$

2.  **Foreground Encoding**:
    Pass foreground positions directly through the encoders [S1, unofficial]:
    $$ \mathbf{e}_{fg\_dens} = \text{density\_encoding}(\mathbf{p}_{fg}) $$
    $$ \mathbf{e}_{fg\_col} = \text{color\_encoding}(\mathbf{p}_{fg}) $$

3.  **Background Warping and Encoding**:
    Background points are outside $[0, 1]$ [S1, unofficial]. To map them into a range suitable for standard encoders while preserving scale information [S1, unofficial]:
    
    a. Shift background positions to $[-1, 1]$ range [S1, unofficial]:
    $$ \mathbf{p}_{bg\_shifted} = 2.0 \cdot \mathbf{p}_{bg} - 1.0 $$
    
    b. Compute the L-infinity norm (maximum absolute coordinate) for each point to determine the bounding box scale [S1, unofficial]:
    $$ \mathbf{s} = \max_j |(\mathbf{p}_{bg\_shifted})_j| \quad \text{(shape: } (M, 1) \text{)} $$
    
    c. Normalize positions to $[0, 1]$ by dividing by the infinity norm and shifting [S1, unofficial]:
    $$ \mathbf{p}_{bg\_warp} = \frac{\mathbf{p}_{bg\_shifted}}{\mathbf{s}} + 1.0 $$
    $$ \mathbf{p}_{bg\_warp} = \frac{\mathbf{p}_{bg\_warp}}{2.0} $$
    
    d. Append the inverse scale ($1/s$) as an additional channel to provide explicit scale information to the encoder [S1, unofficial]:
    $$ \mathbf{p}_{bg\_final} = \text{concat}(\mathbf{p}_{bg\_warp}, \frac{1}{\mathbf{s}}) $$
    
    e. Encode the warped positions [S1, unofficial]:
    $$ \mathbf{e}_{bg\_dens} = \text{bg\_density\_encoding}(\mathbf{p}_{bg\_final}) $$
    $$ \mathbf{e}_{bg\_col} = \text{bg\_color\_encoding}(\mathbf{p}_{bg\_final}) $$

4.  **Density and Color Feature Assembly**:
    Initialize zero tensors for density and color embeddings with shapes matching the flattened input positions [S1, unofficial]. Assign the computed foreground and background features to their respective indices using the selectors [S1, unofficial]:
    $$ \mathbf{e}_{dens} = \mathbf{0}_N $$
    $$ \mathbf{e}_{col} = \mathbf{0}_N $$
    $$ \mathbf{e}_{dens}[\text{fg\_selector}] = \mathbf{e}_{fg\_dens} $$
    $$ \mathbf{e}_{dens}[\text{bg\_selector}] = \mathbf{e}_{bg\_dens} $$
    $$ \mathbf{e}_{col}[\text{fg\_selector}] = \mathbf{e}_{fg\_col} $$
    $$ \mathbf{e}_{col}[\text{bg\_selector}] = \mathbf{e}_{bg\_col} $$

5.  **Density Activation**:
    Rectify the density using an exponential activation function for numerical stability, allowing high post-activation float32 density outputs from smaller internal float16 parameters [S1, unofficial]:
    $$ \sigma = \text{trunc\_exp}(\mathbf{e}_{dens}) $$
    Reshape $\sigma$ to $(N, 1)$ [S1, unofficial].

6.  **Color Prediction**:
    a. **Feature Construction**:
       Extract ray directions $\mathbf{d}$ from `ray_samples.frustums.directions`, reshaped to $(N, 3)$ [S1, unofficial].
       
       Initialize feature lists with the density embedding reshaped to the color encoding output dimensions [S1, unofficial]:
       $$ \mathbf{f}_{base} = \text{reshape}(\mathbf{e}_{dens}, (N, \text{dim}_{color\_enc})) $$
       
       $$ \mathbf{F}_{color} = [\mathbf{f}_{base}] $$
       $$ \mathbf{F}_{view} = [\mathbf{f}_{base}] $$

    b. **Appearance Embedding**:
       If `appearance_embedding_dim > 0` [S1, unofficial]:
       - During training: Lookup embeddings using `camera_indices` [S1, unofficial].
       - During inference: Use the mean appearance embedding if `use_average_appearance_embedding` is true; otherwise, use zeros [S1, unofficial].
       Let $\mathbf{a}$ be the appearance embedding tensor of shape $(N, \text{appearance\_dim})$ [S1, unofficial].
       $$ \mathbf{F}_{color}.append(\mathbf{a}) $$

    c. **Direction Encoding**:
       Encode the view directions [S1, unofficial]:
       $$ \mathbf{d}_{enc} = \text{direction\_encoding}(\mathbf{d}) $$
       $$ \mathbf{F}_{view}.append(\mathbf{d}_{enc}) $$

    d. **Concatenation**:
       $$ \mathbf{C}_{feat} = \text{concat}(\mathbf{F}_{color}, \text{dim}=-1) $$
       $$ \mathbf{V}_{feat} = \text{concat}(\mathbf{F}_{view}, \text{dim}=-1) $$

    e. **MLP Prediction**:
       Compute view-independent RGB ($\mathbf{rgb}_{vi}$) and view-dependent RGB ($\mathbf{rgb}_{vd}$) using separate MLPs applied to the concatenated color and view features respectively [S1, unofficial]:
       $$ \mathbf{rgb}_{vi} = \text{vi\_mlp}(\mathbf{C}_{feat}) $$
       $$ \mathbf{rgb}_{vd} = \text{vd\_mlp}(\mathbf{V}_{feat}) $$
       
       Sum the components to get the final RGB [S1, unofficial]:
       $$ \mathbf{rgb} = \mathbf{rgb}_{vi} + \mathbf{rgb}_{vd} $$
       Note that no sigmoid is applied as each component is already in the $(0,1)$ range [S1, unofficial].

    f. **RGB Padding (Optional)**:
       If `rgb_padding` is configured [S1, unofficial]:
       $$ \mathbf{rgb} = \mathbf{rgb} \cdot (1 + 2 \cdot \text{rgb\_padding}) - \text{rgb\_padding} $$

### Pseudocode

```python
import torch

def lightning_nerf_encode(
    positions: torch.Tensor,  # (N, 3), normalized to [0, 1] for foreground
    ray_samples,
    density_encoding,
    color_encoding,
    bg_density_encoding,
    bg_color_encoding,
    direction_encoding,
    vi_mlp,
    vd_mlp,
    appearance_embeddings: torch.Tensor,
    camera_indices: torch.Tensor = None,
    appearance_embedding_dim: int = 0,
    use_average_appearance_embedding: bool = False,
    rgb_padding: float = 0.0,
    is_training: bool = True
) -> tuple:
    """
    Compute density and RGB for Lightning NeRF.
    """
    N = positions.shape[0]
    
    # 1. Foreground/Background Segmentation
    fg_mask = (positions >= 0).all(dim=1) & (positions <= 1).all(dim=1)
    bg_mask = ~fg_mask
    
    p_fg = positions[fg_mask]
    p_bg = positions[bg_mask]
    
    # 2. Foreground Encoding
    e_fg_dens = density_encoding(p_fg)
    e_fg_col = color_encoding(p_fg)
    
    # 3. Background Encoding
    e_bg_dens = torch.zeros(0, device=positions.device)
    e_bg_col = torch.zeros(0, device=positions.device)
    
    if p_bg.numel() > 0:
        # Shift to [-1, 1]
        p_bg_shifted = p_bg * 2.0 - 1.0
        
        # L-infinity norm
        bg_inf_norm = p_bg_shifted.abs().max(dim=1, keepdim=True).values
        
        # Normalize to [0, 1]
        p_bg_warp = (p_bg_shifted / bg_inf_norm + 1.0) / 2.0
        
        # Append inverse scale
        inv_scale = 1.0 / bg_inf_norm
        p_bg_final = torch.cat([p_bg_warp, inv_scale], dim=-1)
        
        e_bg_dens = bg_density_encoding(p_bg_final)
        e_bg_col = bg_color_encoding(p_bg_final)
        
    # 4. Assemble Density and Color Embeddings
    e_dens = torch.zeros(N, e_fg_dens.shape[1], device=positions.device)
    e_col = torch.zeros(N, e_fg_col.shape[1], device=positions.device)
    
    e_dens[fg_mask] = e_fg_dens
    e_dens[bg_mask] = e_bg_dens
    e_col[fg_mask] = e_fg_col
    e_col[bg_mask] = e_bg_col
    
    # 5. Density Activation
    # trunc_exp is an exponential activation function for numerical stability [S1, unofficial]
    def trunc_exp(x):
        return torch.exp(x)
    
    density = trunc_exp(e_dens)
    density = density.view(N, 1)
    
    # 6. Color Prediction
    directions = ray_samples.frustums.directions.reshape(-1, 3)
    
    # Base features from density embedding reshaped to color encoding output dimensions
    base_feat = e_dens.view(N, color_encoding.n_output_dims)
    
    color_features = [base_feat]
    view_features = [base_feat]
    
    # Appearance Embedding
    if appearance_embedding_dim > 0:
        if is_training and camera_indices is not None:
            appearance_emb = appearance_embeddings[camera_indices]
        else:
            if use_average_appearance_embedding:
                appearance_emb = torch.mean(appearance_embeddings, dim=0)
                appearance_emb = appearance_emb.unsqueeze(0).expand(N, -1)
            else:
                appearance_emb = torch.zeros(N, appearance_embedding_dim, device=positions.device)
        color_features.append(appearance_emb)
        
    # Direction Encoding
    d_enc = direction_encoding(directions)
    view_features.append(d_enc)
    
    # Concatenate
    c_feat = torch.cat(color_features, dim=-1)
    v_feat = torch.cat(view_features, dim=-1)
    
    # MLPs
    rgb_vi = vi_mlp(c_feat)
    rgb_vd = vd_mlp(v_feat)
    
    rgb = rgb_vi + rgb_vd
    
    # RGB Padding
    if rgb_padding != 0.0:
        rgb = rgb * (1 + 2 * rgb_padding) - rgb_padding
        
    return density, rgb
```

### Parameters and Typical Values
-   `rgb_padding`: Configurable padding applied to RGB values [S1, unofficial].
-   `appearance_embedding_dim`: Dimension of the appearance embedding; if greater than 0, appearance embeddings are generated based on training mode [S1, unofficial].
-   `use_average_appearance_embedding`: Boolean flag determining whether to use the mean appearance embedding during inference [S1, unofficial].

### Edge Cases and Pitfalls
-   **Zero Infinity Norm**: If a background point is exactly at the origin after shifting, the L-infinity norm is 0, leading to division by zero [S1, unofficial].
-   **Precision**: The exponential activation function is used for numerical stability, allowing high post-activation float32 density outputs from smaller internal float16 parameters [S1, unofficial].

## Background
Lightning NeRF Density and Color Encoding originates from the Lightning-NeRF repository by the Vision-SJTU group [S1, unofficial]. It implements a unified density and color evaluation pipeline that separates foreground and background processing, applying coordinate warping to background points to handle unbounded scenes within a single forward pass [S1, unofficial].

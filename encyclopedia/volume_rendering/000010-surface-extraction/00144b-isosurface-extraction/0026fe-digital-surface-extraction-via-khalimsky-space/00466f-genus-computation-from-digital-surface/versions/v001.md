# Genus Computation from Digital Surface

Also called: Genus from DS

## Overview
Computes the genus $g$ of a digital surface $\partial O_\alpha$, which is a 2-manifold without boundary derived from the Majority Interpolation (MI) reconstruction $O_\alpha$ [S1, unofficial]. It sets itself apart by providing a direct algebraic calculation of the topological genus based solely on local neighbor counts of surfels, avoiding global Euler characteristic integration [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
- Input: A digital surface $\partial O_\alpha$ (a 2-manifold without boundary) extracted from a voxel grid.
- Output: The genus $g$, an integer topological invariant.

**Data Structures:**
- The surface $\partial O_\alpha$ is represented as a set of surfels (surface elements).
- For each surfel $s \in \partial O_\alpha$, the number of neighbors $|N(s)|$ is determined based on the Khalimsky space adjacency rules defined in [Digital Surface Extraction via Khalimsky Space](3937-digital-surface-extraction-via-khalimsky-space.md).

**Steps:**
1. Let $\partial O_\alpha$ be the input 2-manifold without boundary [S1, unofficial].
2. Initialize counters for specific neighbor counts: $|N_3| = 0$, $|N_5| = 0$, $|N_6| = 0$ [S1, unofficial].
3. Iterate through every surfel $s$ in $\partial O_\alpha$:
   a. Calculate the number of neighbors of $s$, denoted as $k = |N(s)|$ [S1, unofficial].
   b. If $k = 3$, increment $|N_3|$ [S1, unofficial].
   c. If $k = 5$, increment $|N_5|$ [S1, unofficial].
   d. If $k = 6$, increment $|N_6|$ [S1, unofficial].
4. Compute the genus $g$ using the formula:
   $$g = 1 + \frac{|N_5| + 2|N_6| - |N_3|}{8}$$
   [S1, unofficial].
5. Return $g$ [S1, unofficial].

**Parameters:**
- $|N_i|$: The number of surface points in the digital surface $\partial O_\alpha$ that have exactly $i$ neighbors [S1, unofficial].
- $g$: The surface genus [S1, unofficial].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
- The method assumes $\partial O_\alpha$ is a valid 2-manifold without boundary [S1, unofficial]. If the surface has boundaries or self-intersections, the neighbor counts may not correspond to the expected topological configurations, leading to incorrect genus values.
- The formula relies on the specific connectivity defined by the Khalimsky space and the Majority Interpolation reconstruction. Using a different surface extraction method may invalidate the neighbor count statistics [S1, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
- **Time Complexity:** Linear with respect to the number of surfels in $\partial O_\alpha$, as it requires a single pass to count neighbors.
- **Memory Complexity:** Constant extra space for counters $|N_3|, |N_5|, |N_6|$ if iterating directly, or linear if storing neighbor counts for all surfels.
- **Strengths:** Direct computation without global topological analysis; efficient for large surfaces.
- **Limitations:** Only applicable to surfaces derived from the specific MI reconstruction and Khalimsky adjacency; does not provide full homology (only genus).

## Background
The method was proposed by Chen and Rong [S1, unofficial]. It leverages the property that the boundary of the union of voxels in the Majority Interpolation reconstruction $O_\alpha$ forms a 2-manifold [S1, unofficial]. This allows the genus to be computed directly from local configurations rather than global Euler characteristic calculations [S1, unofficial].

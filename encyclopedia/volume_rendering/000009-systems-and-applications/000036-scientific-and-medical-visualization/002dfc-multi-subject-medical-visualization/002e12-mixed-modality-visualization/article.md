# Mixed Modality Visualization
## Overview
Mixed Modality Visualization is the visualization of mixed modality data, including imaging, genetics, and blood measurements, acquired from a group of subjects [S1, unofficial]. It solves the problem of analyzing complex medical cohort studies where diverse data types must be integrated to diagnose or predict clinical outcomes [S1, unofficial]. It is distinguished from its sibling [Multi-Subject Medical Visualization](5715-multi-subject-medical-visualization.md) by its specific focus on integrating heterogeneous data types rather than just multiple subjects, though it relies on the same coupled view interface principles.
## Shared principles
The family relies on creating a highly interactive coupled view visualization interface [S1, unofficial]. This interface integrates both information and scientific visualization techniques [S1, unofficial]. The goal is to allow users to extract patterns and hypotheses from the whole data collection [S1, unofficial].

## Background
Builds on [Multi-Subject Medical Visualization](5715-multi-subject-medical-visualization.md) by focusing on heterogeneous data types rather than solely on multiple subjects [S1, unofficial]. Originates from research on interactive visualization of multi-subject and mixed modality datasets in medical cohort studies [S1, unofficial]. Stenwijk demonstrated a highly interactive coupled view interface integrating information and scientific visualization techniques to extract patterns and hypotheses from such collections [S1, unofficial]. The broader medical visualization domain requires collaboration among medical doctors, medical physicists, biomedical engineers, and technicians depending on the context [S1, unofficial].

# Subgrid Surface Reconstruction

## Overview
Subgrid Surface Reconstruction extracts a triangle soup from a tetrahedral grid by processing individual tetrahedra [S1, unofficial]. It distinguishes itself from standard Marching Tetrahedrons by accepting pre-computed boundary curves (open, scoop, normal) and edge occupation flags to guide triangle generation, offering two variants: `subgrid_surface` for general cases and `subgrid_greedy` for optimized performance [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
The method processes a single tetrahedron [S1, unofficial]. Inputs include tetrahedron vertex positions, global vertex indices, pre-computed edge intersection times, boundary curve definitions (open, scoop, normal), non-normal edge occupation flags, and flags for scoop mid-vertices and bulge magnitude [S1, unofficial]. The output is a triangle soup representing the isosurface segment within that tetrahedron [S1, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
*   **Tetrahedron:** 4 vertex positions (3D coordinates), 4 global indices (integers) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Edge Intersections:** 6 intersection times (floats), one per edge [S1, unofficial].
*   **Boundary Curves:** Lists of vertices/edges defining open, scoop, and normal boundaries [S1, unofficial].
*   **Flags:** Boolean flags for scoop mid-vertices and bulge magnitude [S1, unofficial].

**Steps:**
1.  **Filter Unoccupied Edges:** Use `unoccupied_edge_isect_ts` to filter edge intersection times, returning only times for edges that are not occupied [S1, unofficial].
2.  **Reconstruct Surface:** Pass the filtered intersection times, tetrahedron positions, global indices, boundary curves, occupation flags, and magnitude flags to either `subgrid_surface` or `subgrid_greedy` to generate the local triangle soup [S1, unofficial].
3.  **Aggregate:** Combine the triangle soups from all processed tetrahedra into a global mesh [S1, unofficial].

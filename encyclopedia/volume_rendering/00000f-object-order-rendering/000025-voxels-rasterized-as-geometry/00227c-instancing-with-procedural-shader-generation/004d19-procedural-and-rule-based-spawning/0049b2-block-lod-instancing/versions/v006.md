# Block LOD Instancing

Also called: LOD-based Spawning

## Overview
Block LOD Instancing is a method for spawning voxel instances based on the resolution level (LOD) of the underlying terrain chunks [S1]. It solves the problem of efficiently populating large voxel worlds by assigning instance types to specific distance ranges via chunk resolution [S1]. It differs from standard instancing by tying spawn eligibility directly to the terrain's hierarchical LOD structure rather than a fixed global radius, allowing larger items to spawn on distant, low-resolution chunks while smaller items populate high-resolution areas [S1].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Input:** A `VoxelInstanceLibrary` containing items with a `lod_index` property; a voxel terrain with hierarchical LOD chunks [S1].
*   **Output:** A set of transformed instances (position, rotation, scale) placed on the terrain surface [S1].

**Data Structures:**
*   `VoxelInstanceLibraryItem`: Contains `lod_index` (integer) [S1].
    *   `lod_index = 0`: Spawns on LOD0 chunks (highest resolution, closest range) [S1].
    *   `lod_index > 0`: Spawns on progressively lower-resolution, larger chunks [S1].

**Steps:**
1.  **Chunk Eligibility:** Spawn items on chunks matching their configured `lod_index` [S1].
2.  **Candidate Generation:** Evaluate the item's generator conditions (e.g., noise thresholds) at candidate positions within the eligible chunks [Parent](13402-procedural-and-rule-based-spawning.md).
3.  **Surface Snapping (Optional):**
    *   If `snap_to_generator_sdf_enabled` is true, query the `VoxelGenerator` for Signed Distance Field (SDF) values at the candidate position and at least two nearby samples to approximate the surface [S1].
    *   *Pitfall:* This is an approximation, not exact ray-casting [S1].
4.  **Normal Offset:** Displace the instance position along the interpolated surface normal by `offset_along_normal` [S1].
5.  **Alignment:**
    *   If `up_mode` is 'Sphere mode', rotate instances to align with the local origin of the terrain (suitable for planets) [S1].
    *   Otherwise, alignment behavior is not given in the sources.
6.  **Instance Creation:** Not given in the sources.
7.  **Persistence:** Not given in the sources.

**Parameters:**
*   `lod_index`: Determines the chunk resolution for spawning. Value 0 spawns on LOD0 chunks (closest range, fades quickly). Higher indexes spawn on bigger chunks (larger range) [S1].
*   `offset_along_normal`: Parameter in the generator. Depends on the asset; designing assets with part of their bottom sunk into the ground provides margin of error [S1].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
*   **Placement Imprecision:** Larger `lod_index` values are less precise because they work on lower-resolution meshes, potentially causing instances to float above or sink into the ground near sharp terrain changes (ridges, crevices, caves) [S1].
*   **Abrupt Disappearance:** Instances may disappear abruptly at LOD boundaries [S1]. Mitigation involves fading meshes based on distance from the camera using a custom shader or using ground textures of similar colors to blend [S1].

## Subtopics

### Offset Along Normal Placement
Adjusts instance placement by offsetting along the surface normal to correct for floating or sinking caused by low-resolution mesh imprecision [S1]. The `offset_along_normal` parameter depends on the asset; designing assets with part of their bottom sunk into the ground provides a margin of error [S1].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Generation is faster for higher `lod_index` values because they operate on lower-resolution meshes, avoiding full-precision LOD calculations [S1].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Depends on the number of instances spawned per chunk [S1].
*   **Strengths:** Efficiently populates large distances by leveraging coarse terrain geometry; separates instance density by distance via LOD hierarchy [S1].
*   **Limitations:** Placement accuracy degrades significantly with higher `lod_index` values, leading to visual artifacts (floating/sinking) on complex terrain [S1].
*   **When to use:** Best for large-scale vegetation or objects where exact surface contact is less critical than performance and visibility range [S1]. Use higher `lod_index` for big items seen from far away, and lower indexes for smaller items [S1].

## Background
Block LOD Instancing is part of the Voxel Tools ecosystem, specifically designed for voxel-based terrain rendering [S1]. It leverages the inherent hierarchical structure of voxel terrains to optimize instance generation [S1].

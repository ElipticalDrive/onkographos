# Pose-free and Feed-forward Training


## Overview
This family encompasses methods that reconstruct 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) scenes from uncalibrated image collections without requiring external Structure-from-Motion (SfM) pipelines or iterative pose optimization [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, 6137-anysplat.md, 6888-joint-pose-optimization.md, unofficial]. It distinguishes itself from traditional 3DGS by eliminating the dependency on dense pose estimation, and from feed-forward methods like [pixelSplat](6464-pixelsplat.md) by either leveraging foundation model priors for initialization ([InstantSplat](6136-instantsplat.md)) or predicting poses and primitives jointly in a single forward pass ([AnySplat](6137-anysplat.md)).

## Shared principles
All methods in this family rely on differentiable rendering to compute gradients from the rendered image to the Gaussian parameters (position, covariance, opacity, spherical harmonics) and/or network weights [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, 6464-pixelsplat.md, unofficial]. The rendering process remains differentiable with respect to the scene representation [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, 6464-pixelsplat.md, unofficial]. Loss functions typically combine photometric reconstruction error, such as $\mathcal{L}_{L1}$ or $\mathcal{L}_{MSE}$, with perceptual or structural metrics like $\mathcal{L}_{MS-SSIM}$ or $\mathcal{L}_{LPIPS}$ [6136-instantsplat.md][6464-pixelsplat.md].

## Subtopics

### [InstantSplat](6136-instantsplat.md)
InstantSplat is a pose-free, sparse-view 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) method that eliminates the need for Structure-from-Motion (SfM) pipelines by leveraging geometry priors from foundation models like MASt3R or VGGT. It enables training in seconds by directly initializing Gaussians from these priors, distinguishing itself from feed-forward methods like [pixelSplat](6464-pixelsplat.md) which regress primitives directly from images, and from traditional 3DGS which requires dense pose estimation.

### [AnySplat](6137-anysplat.md)
AnySplat is a pose-free, feed-forward network that reconstructs 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) scenes from uncalibrated image collections without requiring camera pose annotations. It differs from sibling methods like [InstantSplat](6136-instantsplat.md) and [pixelSplat](6464-pixelsplat.md) by handling arbitrary multi-view inputs (dense or sparse) in a single forward pass, yielding both scene primitives and per-image intrinsics/extrinsics simultaneously. This enables zero-shot evaluation on casual captures where pose data is unavailable, matching the quality of pose-aware baselines while maintaining real-time rendering latency.

### [pixelSplat](6464-pixelsplat.md)
pixelSplat is a feed-forward model that learns to reconstruct 3D radiance fields parameterized by 3D Gaussian primitives from pairs of images, enabling real-time and memory-efficient rendering and fast 3D reconstruction at inference time. It solves the problem of local minima inherent to sparse and locally supported representations in generalizable novel view synthesis, where non-differentiable pruning/splitting (used in single-scene optimization) cannot be used because gradients must back-propagate through the representation. Unlike light-field transformers which do not reconstruct editable 3D scene representations, pixelSplat generates an explicit 3D representation that is renderable in real time, remains editable, and is cheap to train.

### [Joint Pose Optimization](6888-joint-pose-optimization.md)
Joint Pose Optimization (JPO) eliminates the dependency on external Structure-from-Motion (SfM) pipelines by simultaneously optimizing camera poses and the scene representation (neural or Gaussian) from unposed images. Unlike sibling methods that may rely on incremental depth priors or temporal continuity, JPO directly minimizes photometric reconstruction error to solve for both geometry and pose. It is distinct from feed-forward methods that predict poses via a separate network, as JPO treats poses as learnable parameters optimized via gradient descent.

### [Loss Scheduling for Candidate Head](13095-loss-scheduling-for-candidate-head.md)
Loss scheduling for the candidate head is a training strategy that gradually reduces the influence of an image-dependent 'candidate head' ($\hat{C}_i$) during optimization, transitioning to a shared representation ($\hat{C}$) to stabilize pose estimation for hard-pose images. It prevents the shared representation from being distracted by wrong supervision introduced by hard-pose images in the early stages, allowing the model to assimilate these images into the shared representation once the shared model is accurate enough. This method is distinct from sibling methods like [InstantSplat](6136-instantsplat.md) and [pixelSplat](6464-pixelsplat.md) by explicitly decoupling the training influence of per-image heads via a cosine schedule rather than relying solely on joint pose optimization or fixed head structures.

#### VS-Splat Extension for Noisy Camera Poses
**Definition**
An extension to handle noisy camera poses, following SHARE implementation with modified input module [S1, unofficial].

**Step**
Use three 1x1 2D convolutional layers to reduce channel dimension of feature maps from 768 to 128, matching SHARE input dimension. Each conv layer preceded by normalization and Swish activation [S1, unofficial].

**Parameter**
M (number of fine-grained primitives per voxel): 8 (due to GPU memory constraints) [S1, unofficial].

**Number**
On CO3D dataset, Oursext yields PSNR 21.62, SSIM 0.864, LPIPS 0.211 [S1, unofficial].

## Background
The family originates from the need to bypass the computational bottleneck and fragility of Structure-from-Motion (SfM) in 3D Gaussian Splatting. Traditional 3DGS requires dense, accurate camera poses, which are often unavailable or noisy in casual capture scenarios. Methods like [pixelSplat](6464-pixelsplat.md) introduced feed-forward architectures to predict Gaussian primitives directly from image pairs, addressing local minima issues through differentiable sampling [S1, unofficial]. Subsequent works like [InstantSplat](6136-instantsplat.md) and [AnySplat](6137-anysplat.md) leveraged foundation models (MASt3R, VGGT) for geometry priors or joint pose-primitive prediction to enable pose-free reconstruction from sparse or uncalibrated views [S1, unofficial]. The [VS-Splat](6137-anysplat.md) extension further adapted these feed-forward approaches to handle noisy camera poses by modifying input feature dimensions to match existing pose-aware baselines like SHARE [S1, unofficial].

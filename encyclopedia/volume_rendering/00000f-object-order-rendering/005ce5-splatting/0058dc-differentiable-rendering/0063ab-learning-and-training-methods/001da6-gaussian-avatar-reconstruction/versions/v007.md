# Gaussian Avatar Reconstruction

Also called: Gaussian Avatars, 3DGS Avatars, Animatable Gaussian Avatars

## Overview
Gaussian Avatar Reconstruction is a family of methods that reconstruct photorealistic, animatable 3D human or head avatars from video data by representing them as collections of 3D Gaussians [S1, unofficial][S3]. It solves the challenge of maintaining topological consistency and rigging capabilities in dynamic reconstruction, which is difficult for implicit neural radiance fields (NeRFs) or unstructured Gaussian Splatting [S1, unofficial][S3]. Unlike standard [Gaussian Splatting](946-gaussian-splatting.md) which treats primitives as independent entities, this family anchors Gaussians to parametric mesh priors (such as SMPL for bodies or FLAME for heads) to ensure structural integrity during animation [S1, 8385-smpl-based-rigid-non-rigid-deformation.md, unofficial]. It sets itself apart from siblings like [Pose-free and Feed-forward Training](6500-pose-free-and-feed-forward-training.md) by explicitly binding geometry to a parametric model, and from [Point-Based Neural Renderer Architecture](11612-point-based-neural-renderer-architecture.md) by using explicit Gaussian primitives optimized directly from images .

## Shared principles
The family shares the following mechanisms, terms, and math, building on the splatting principles of primitive representation, transformation, rasterization, and compositing [23-splatting.md].

**Parametric Anchoring:**
Gaussians are initialized on the surface of a parametric mesh (e.g., SMPL, FLAME) in a canonical rest pose [S1, 1477-gaussian-avatars.md, 1482-relightable-gaussian-codec-avatars.md, unofficial]. Each Gaussian $i$ is associated with a specific triangle $t$ or vertex of the mesh [S1, 1477-gaussian-avatars.md, unofficial].

**Deformation Pipeline:**
To animate the avatar, Gaussians are deformed from the canonical pose to a target pose $\theta$ and expression $\beta$ [S1, 8385-smpl-based-rigid-non-rigid-deformation.md, unofficial].
1.  **Rigid Deformation:** The canonical position $\mathbf{p}_i$ is transformed using Linear Blend Skinning (LBS) [S1, 1480-3dgs-avatar.md, 6157-animatable-gaussians.md, unofficial]:
    $$ \mathbf{p}_{rigid} = \sum_{j=1}^{N_{bones}} w_{i,j} \mathbf{T}_j(\theta) \mathbf{p}_i $$
    where $w_{i,j}$ are skinning weights and $\mathbf{T}_j(\theta)$ are bone transformation matrices [S1, 1480-3dgs-avatar.md, unofficial].
2.  **Non-Rigid Deformation:** A residual offset $\Delta \mathbf{p}$ is predicted by a deformation network (e.g., MLP) conditioned on pose and/or canonical position [S1, 1480-3dgs-avatar.md, 6157-animatable-gaussians.md, unofficial]. The final position is:
    $$ \mathbf{p}'_i = \mathbf{p}_{rigid} + \Delta \mathbf{p} $$
    Some methods predict parameters directly via a feedforward network, implicitly handling deformation [S1, 1479-gaussian-avatar.md, unofficial].
3.  **Covariance Update:** The covariance matrix $\Sigma_i$ is transformed using the Jacobian $J$ of the deformation map with respect to the canonical position [G]:
    $$ \Sigma'_i = J \Sigma_i J^T + \sigma^2 I $$
    where $\sigma^2 I$ is a small regularization term to ensure positive definiteness [G]. The Jacobian $J$ includes contributions from both LBS and the non-rigid deformation network [G, 11805-full-body-avatar-modeling.md].

**Differentiable Rendering:**
The deformed Gaussians are rendered using differentiable 3D Gaussian Splatting [S1, 1477-gaussian-avatars.md, 1480-3dgs-avatar.md, 6157-animatable-gaussians.md, 11805-full-body-avatar-modeling.md, unofficial]. Gradients are computed from the rendered image to the Gaussian parameters and network weights [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, 6464-pixelsplat.md, unofficial].

**Loss Function:**
Training minimizes a combination of photometric and perceptual losses [S1, 1477-gaussian-avatars.md, 1482-relightable-gaussian-codec-avatars.md, unofficial]:
$$ \mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{L1} + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{MS-SSIM} $$
where $\mathcal{L}_{L1}$ is the L1 loss between rendered and ground truth images, and $\mathcal{L}_{MS-SSIM}$ is the multi-scale structural similarity loss [S1, 1477-gaussian-avatars.md, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### [Gaussian Splatting](946-gaussian-splatting.md)
The foundational method for 3D scene representation using anisotropic 3D Gaussians. It solves the computational bottleneck of NeRFs by replacing ray marching with tile-based rasterization. Choose this as the base rendering engine for all avatar methods.

### [GaussianShader](1470-gaussianshader.md)
Enriches Gaussians with physical material parameters (diffusiveness, specularity, roughness, normals) to enable specular and reflective rendering. Choose this when photorealistic material appearance is required beyond simple color.

### [SpotlessSplats](1476-spotlesssplats.md)
Improves robustness in casual captures by detecting and ignoring transient distractors in a pre-trained feature space. Choose this when training data contains significant noise or moving occluders.

### [Gaussian Avatars](1477-gaussian-avatars.md)
Reconstructs animatable head avatars by anchoring Gaussians to a FLAME mesh. It solves topological consistency by constraining Gaussians to mesh triangles. Choose this for high-fidelity head avatars with explicit rigging.

### [Relightable Gaussian Codec Avatars](1482-relightable-gaussian-codec-avatars.md)
Supports dynamic relighting under novel illumination by decoupling geometry and appearance into a FLAME-rigged Gaussian representation and a relightable BRDF. Choose this when interactive relighting is required.

### [NoPoSplat](1484-noposplat.md)
Eliminates the requirement for known camera poses by anchoring representation to a canonical system and using intrinsics token embeddings. Choose this when camera poses are unknown or unreliable.

### [SMPL-based rigid/non-rigid deformation](8385-smpl-based-rigid-non-rigid-deformation.md)
Provides the underlying deformation mechanism (rigid LBS + non-rigid offsets) for full-body avatars. Choose this as the deformation backbone for body avatars.

## Background
Gaussian Avatar Reconstruction emerged as a solution to the limitations of NeRF-based avatars, which suffer from slow rendering speeds and lack of explicit rigging [S1, unofficial][S3]. The field leverages the efficiency of 3D Gaussian Splatting [S1, unofficial] and adapts it to parametric human models like SMPL [S1, 8385-smpl-based-rigid-non-rigid-deformation.md, unofficial] and FLAME [S1, 1477-gaussian-avatars.md, unofficial]. Early works like Gaussian Avatars [S1, 1477-gaussian-avatars.md, unofficial] demonstrated the feasibility of anchoring Gaussians to mesh triangles for head avatars. Subsequent developments introduced relighting [S1, 1482-relightable-gaussian-codec-avatars.md, unofficial], robustness to noise [S1, 1476-spotlesssplats.md, unofficial], and full-body deformation [S1, 8385-smpl-based-rigid-non-rigid-deformation.md, unofficial]. The method is used in virtual production, telepresence, and metaverse applications [S1, unofficial][S3].

# Non-Pinhole Camera Training

## Overview
Non-Pinhole Camera Training adapts 3D Gaussian Splatting to scenes captured by non-pinhole cameras by leveraging a differentiable rasterizer [S1]. It addresses the failure of standard 3DGS to reconstruct scenes from non-pinhole camera datasets [S1].

## How it works
The method utilizes the differentiable rasterizer inherent to 3D Gaussian Splatting to accommodate arbitrary camera projection models during optimization [S1].

## Subtopics

### Aperture Randomized Training
Aperture Randomized Training is a technique introduced to disentangle defocus-aware and defocus-independent representations in an unsupervised manner by learning to generate images while randomizing the aperture size and latent codes independently [S2]. During training, the aperture size is changed randomly, and the latent codes are changed randomly [S2]. This approach is similar to techniques commonly used in viewpoint-aware representation learning, which are useful for disentangling the effect of the corresponding factor from latent codes [S2]. It is applied within a GAN framework where an MLP converts point position and view direction into RGB color and volume density [S2].

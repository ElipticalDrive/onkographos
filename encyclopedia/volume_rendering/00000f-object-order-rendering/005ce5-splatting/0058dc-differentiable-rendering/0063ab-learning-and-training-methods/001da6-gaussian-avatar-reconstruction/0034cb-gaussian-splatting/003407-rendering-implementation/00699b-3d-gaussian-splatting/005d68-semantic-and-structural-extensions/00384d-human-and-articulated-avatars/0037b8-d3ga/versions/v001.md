# D3GA

Also called: D3GA, A method that creates animatable human avatars using drivable 3D Gaussians and tetrahedral cages for full-body modeling

## Overview
D3GA is a method for reconstructing animatable full-body human avatars from multi-view videos by combining drivable 3D Gaussians with tetrahedral cage deformation [S1][S2, unofficial]. It addresses the challenge of modeling dynamic human geometry and appearance, serving as a foundational approach in the lineage of Gaussian Avatar Reconstruction [Parent](8209-human-and-articulated-avatars.md). Unlike sibling methods that rely on linear blend skinning [GART](8093-gart.md) or specialized head-only representations [GaussianHead](8051-gaussianhead.md), D3GA utilizes tetrahedral cages to provide explicit geometry and appearance modeling for the full body.

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Inputs:** Multi-view video sequences of a human subject.
*   **Outputs:** An animatable 3D Gaussian avatar representation capable of rendering novel views and poses.

**Data Structures:**
*   **3D Gaussians:** Drivable Gaussian primitives representing the human volume.
*   **Tetrahedral Cage:** A tetrahedral mesh structure used to define the deformation space for geometry and appearance.

**Method Steps:**
1.  Initialize a set of 3D Gaussians to represent the human subject in a canonical space [S1][S2, unofficial].
2.  Construct a tetrahedral cage that encloses the human geometry [S1][S2, unofficial].
3.  Drive the 3D Gaussians using the deformation fields defined by the tetrahedral cage [S1][S2, unofficial].
4.  Optimize the Gaussian parameters (position, scale, rotation, opacity, spherical harmonics) and cage properties to minimize rendering error against input multi-view videos [S1][S2, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### GaussianHead
A method focused on head-only reconstruction using Gaussian Splatting. It differs from D3GA by specializing in facial details and head topology rather than full-body modeling. Choose it when only head/face animation is required. [GaussianHead](8051-gaussianhead.md)

### GART
A method for animatable Gaussian avatars using linear blend skinning. It differs from D3GA by using skeletal skinning weights rather than tetrahedral cages for deformation. Choose it for efficient skeletal-driven animation. [GART](8093-gart.md)

*   **When to Use:** Use D3GA when full-body animatable human avatar reconstruction from multi-view video is required, particularly when tetrahedral cage-based deformation is preferred over skeletal skinning.
*   **When Not to Use:** Avoid if only head/face reconstruction is needed (use [GaussianHead](8051-gaussianhead.md)) or if skeletal-based animation is sufficient and simpler (use [GART](8093-gart.md)).

## Background
D3GA was introduced as one of the early works to push 3D Gaussian Splatting into digital human creation, specifically for full-body modeling [S1][S2, unofficial]. It was cited in the context of recent advances in 3D Gaussian splatting as a method that first created animatable human avatars using drivable 3D Gaussians and tetrahedral cages [S1][S2, unofficial]. It is referenced in the survey "Recent advances in 3D Gaussian splatting" [S1][S2, unofficial].

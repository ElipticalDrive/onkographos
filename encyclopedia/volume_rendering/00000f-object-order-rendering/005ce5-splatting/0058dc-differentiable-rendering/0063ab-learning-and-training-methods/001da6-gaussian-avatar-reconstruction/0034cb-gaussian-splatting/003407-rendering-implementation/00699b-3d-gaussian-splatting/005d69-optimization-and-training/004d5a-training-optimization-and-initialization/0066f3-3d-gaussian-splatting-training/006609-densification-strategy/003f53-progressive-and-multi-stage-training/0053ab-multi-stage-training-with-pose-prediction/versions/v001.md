# Multi-stage Training with Pose Prediction

Also called: FineSplat training phases, RLSplat two-stage training

## Overview
Multi-stage training with pose prediction addresses the instability of jointly optimizing camera poses and 3D Gaussian parameters in feed-forward novel view synthesis. It separates the problem into distinct phases: first learning a robust geometric representation with pose supervision, then refining the Gaussian allocation or rendering pipeline with ground-truth poses, and finally fine-tuning the entire system in a fully pose-free setting [S2, unofficial]. This approach isolates pose errors from geometry errors, preventing the optimizer from getting stuck in local minima where incorrect poses prevent accurate Gaussian placement [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].

## How it works
The method employs a sequential training schedule. While [S1, unofficial] describes a two-stage process for RLSplat and [S2, unofficial] describes a three-stage process for FineSplat, both rely on progressive refinement of the pose-geometry coupling.

### Inputs and Outputs
*   **Inputs:** A set of uncalibrated context views $V_c$ with unknown poses, and target views $V_t$ with known poses (for supervision in early stages).
*   **Outputs:** A set of 3D Gaussians $\mathcal{G}$ and, in the final stage, predicted camera poses for the context views.

### Data Structures
The method operates on the standard 3D Gaussian primitives $\mathcal{G} = \{g_1, \dots, g_N\}$ [Parent].
*   **Pose Parameters:** For each context view, a translation vector $t \in \mathbb{R}^3$, a rotation matrix $R \in SO(3)$ (or quaternion $q \in \mathbb{R}^4$), and field-of-view (FOV) parameters are predicted by a pose encoder [S1, unofficial].
*   **Gaussian Parameters:** Mean $\mu_k$, covariance $\Sigma_k$ (scale $s_k$, rotation $q_k$), opacity $\alpha_k$, and spherical harmonic coefficients $\mathbf{c}_k$ [Parent].
*   **Policy/Allocation (RLSplat):** A policy head outputs probabilities for Gaussian allocation per anchor [S1, unofficial].

### Training Stages

#### Stage 1: Base Geometry and Pose Learning
**Goal:** Learn a coarse geometric backbone and predict initial camera poses.

1.  **Initialization:** Initialize the geometry backbone and Gaussian decoder. For RLSplat, use random Gaussian allocations [S1, unofficial]. For FineSplat, start with a pixel-aligned Gaussian prediction decoder [S2, unofficial].
2.  **Forward Pass:**
    *   Encode context views to predict camera poses $(R_v, t_v, \text{FOV}_v)$ for all context views $v$ [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].
    *   Generate 3D Gaussians $\mathcal{G}$ from the context views using the decoder [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].
    *   Render target views from $\mathcal{G}$ using the predicted poses [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].
3.  **Loss Computation:**
    *   **Reconstruction Loss ($L_{recon}$):** Pixelwise MSE and LPIPS between rendered and ground-truth target views [S1, unofficial].
    *   **Camera Loss ($L_{cam}$):** Supervises the predicted context camera poses against ground-truth poses (translation, rotation, FOV) [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].
    *   **Projection Loss ($L_{proj}$):** (RLSplat) Reprojects anchor Gaussians toward their source image patch with a cheirality penalty to prevent Gaussians behind the camera [S1, unofficial].
    *   **Locality Regularizers:** $L_{vor}$ and $L_{offset}$ to enforce spatial coherence [S1, unofficial].
    *   **Perceptual Loss:** VGG-19 perceptual loss $L_{perc}$ with weight $\lambda = 0.2$ [S2, unofficial].
4.  **Optimization:**
    *   **RLSplat:** Train for 40,000 steps with random allocations [S1, unofficial].
    *   **FineSplat:** Train pixel-aligned decoder for 120K iterations, then replace with patch-aligned decoder and fine-tune for 80K iterations [S2, unofficial].
    *   **Optimizer:** AdamW ($\beta_1=0.9, \beta_2=0.95$), weight decay 0.05 (multi-dim params only), gradient clipping max norm 1.0, bfloat16 mixed precision [S2, unofficial].
    *   **Learning Rate (FineSplat Stage 1):** $2 \times 10^{-5}$, no warm-up, cosine decay to zero [S2, unofficial].

#### Stage 2: Pose-Free Refinement (or Policy Training)
**Goal:** Refine the Gaussian representation or allocation policy, decoupled from pose errors.

*   **Option A: FineSplat (Ground-Truth Pose Supervision)**
    1.  **Freeze Stage 1:** Freeze the pose encoder and initial Gaussian decoder [S2, unofficial].
    2.  **Ground-Truth Poses:** Use ground-truth camera poses for rendering [S2, unofficial].
    3.  **Iterative Refinement:** Train a Stage 2 module (e.g., iterative Gaussian correction) for $T=4$ steps [S2, unofficial].
    4.  **Loss:** Average loss over all $T$ steps:
        $$ L = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{i=1}^{T} \left( L_2(\hat{I}^{(i)}, I) + \lambda L_{perc}(\hat{I}^{(i)}, I) \right) $$
        where $\hat{I}^{(i)}$ is the rendered image after step $i$, and $I$ is the ground truth [S2, unofficial].
    5.  **Token Detachment:** Detach tokens between steps to prevent gradient flow across refinement steps [S2, unofficial].
    6.  **Optimization:** 20K iterations (FineSplat) or 5,000 steps (RLSplat) [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].
    7.  **Learning Rate (FineSplat Stage 2):** $1 \times 10^{-4}$, 200-iteration linear warm-up, constant until 80%, cosine decay to zero [S2, unofficial].
    8.  **RLSplat Specifics:** Add policy head initialized to uniform probabilities. Fine-tune with policy loss $L_{policy} = -J_{policy}$ (GSPO) [S1, unofficial]. Sample budgets skewed toward lower end [S1, unofficial].

*   **Option B: RLSplat (Policy Training)**
    1.  **Add Policy Head:** Initialize to uniform probabilities [S1, unofficial].
    2.  **Fine-tune:** Optimize entire model with $L_{policy}$ [S1, unofficial].
    3.  **No KL Penalty:** Unlike typical RL, no KL penalty is applied as there is no reference policy [S1, unofficial].

#### Stage 3: End-to-End Fine-Tuning (FineSplat)
**Goal:** Jointly optimize poses and Gaussians in a fully pose-free setting.

1.  **Unfreeze:** Unfreeze all parameters, including the camera pose estimator and Gaussian head [S2, unofficial].
2.  **Pose-Free Setting:** Predict both context and target cameras. No camera loss is used [S2, unofficial].
3.  **Supervision:** Supervise Stage-1 Gaussians. Loss is averaged over steps $i=0, \dots, T$ [S2, unofficial].
4.  **Optimization:** 10K iterations on 8 GPUs [S2, unofficial].
5.  **Learning Rate:** $2 \times 10^{-5}$ for all parameters, 200-iteration linear warm-up, cosine decay to zero [S2, unofficial].

### Parameters
*   **Gaussian Budget:** Sampled from $[2048, 16384V]$ where $V$ is the number of input views [S1, unofficial].
*   **Kanc, Kgs:** 16 for Gaussian decoding [S1, unofficial].
*   **Lambda ($\lambda$):** 0.2 for perceptual loss weight [S2, unofficial].

### Edge Cases and Pitfalls
*   **Cheirality:** Without the cheirality penalty in $L_{proj}$, Gaussians may be placed behind the camera, causing rendering artifacts [S1, unofficial].
*   **Pose-Geometry Coupling:** Jointly optimizing poses and Gaussians from scratch often leads to degenerate solutions where poses drift to compensate for poor geometry [S2, unofficial].
*   **Policy Regularization:** In RLSplat, the absence of a KL penalty during policy training requires careful budget sampling to ensure the policy learns meaningful allocations [S1, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Multi-stage training significantly increases total training time compared to single-stage methods. FineSplat requires 120K + 80K + 20K + 10K = 230K iterations [S2, unofficial]. RLSplat requires 40K + 5K = 45K steps [S1, unofficial].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Standard 3D Gaussian memory requirements [Parent]. RLSplat's policy head adds minimal overhead [S1, unofficial].
*   **Performance:** RLSplat achieves superior LPIPS, SSIM, and PSNR at low Gaussian budgets (1K-4K per view) compared to fixed-budget methods [S1, unofficial]. FineSplat's multi-stage approach improves detail recovery in complex scenes [S2, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Robustness to uncalibrated input views; ability to control Gaussian budget efficiently (RLSplat); high-fidelity reconstruction (FineSplat).
*   **Limitations:** High training time; complex implementation due to multiple stages and loss functions; sensitivity to hyperparameters (learning rates, warm-up schedules) [S2, unofficial].

## Background
*   **RLSplat:** Introduced in "RLSplat: Resource-Linear Budget Control for Feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting" (2026) [S1, unofficial]. It addresses the need for variable Gaussian budgets in feed-forward 3DGS.
*   **FineSplat:** Introduced in "FineSplat: Fine Patches, Local Groups, Detailed Splats" (2026) [S2, unofficial]. It builds on iterative refinement techniques for 3DGS, adapting them to a multi-stage pose-geometry training framework.

# LBS Network Variants

Also called: Backward LBS, Deterministic SMPL skinning, Learned forward LBS

## Overview
This family defines neural or deterministic methods for computing skinning weights to deform canonical 3D Gaussians into posed space, serving as the deformation core for [Linear Blend Skinning for Gaussian Splatting](16258-linear-blend-skinning-for-gaussian-splatting.md). It solves the limitation of fixed, pre-computed skinning weights by allowing the deformation to adapt to geometry and pose. The variants differ in how they condition the weight prediction: [Forward LBS Network](19756-forward-lbs-network.md) uses a spatial function, [Backward LBS](19756-forward-lbs-network.md) conditions on local pose, and [Deterministic SMPL skinning](19756-forward-lbs-network.md) uses nearest-neighbor mesh attributes.

## Shared principles
All variants in this family operate within the Linear Blend Skinning (LBS) framework applied to 3D Gaussians. They share the following mathematical structure and constraints:

1.  **Bone Set:** The deformation relies on a set of 24 rigid bone transformation matrices $\{\mathbf{B}_b\}_{b=1}^{24}$, derived from the SMPL model (23 local transformations and one global transformation) [S1].
2.  **Weight Constraints:** For any query point, the predicted skinning weights $\mathbf{w}$ must satisfy:
    $$ \mathbf{w} \in [0, 1]^{24} \quad \text{and} \quad \sum_{b=1}^{24} w_b = 1 $$
    This ensures a convex combination of bone transformations [S1].
3.  **Deformation Formula:** The posed position $\bar{\mathbf{x}}$ of a canonical point $\hat{\mathbf{x}}$ is computed as:
    $$ \bar{\mathbf{x}} = \left(\sum_{b=1}^{24} w_b \mathbf{B}_b\right) \hat{\mathbf{x}} $$
    where $\hat{\mathbf{x}}$ is treated as a homogeneous coordinate [S1].
4.  **Input Domain:** The primary input to the weight prediction mechanism is a point in canonical space. Depending on the variant, additional inputs such as pose vectors or bone matrices may be required [S1].

## Subtopics

### [Forward LBS Network](19756-forward-lbs-network.md)
A learned neural function that predicts bone affinities for any point in canonical space. It differs from siblings by explicitly modeling skinning weights as a continuous function of spatial coordinates, avoiding static mesh attributes or conditional pose inputs. It is chosen when adaptive, geometry-aware deformation is required without pose-conditioning the weight prediction.

### Backward LBS
A variant where the LBS network conditions on local body poses $\theta$. Before querying the network, canonical query points are transformed using the SMPL global translation and rotation. It is distinguished by its dependency on pose vectors for weight prediction. It suffers from difficulties learning weights for points far from the surface, leading to artifacts under specific poses [S1].

### Deterministic SMPL skinning
A method using nearest-neighbor SMPL skinning weights. It is distinguished by its reliance on discrete mesh attributes rather than learned or pose-conditioned functions. It causes discretized artifacts on the cloth surface due to the nearest-neighbor assignment [S1].

## Background
The variants are evaluated in the context of [ARAH: Animatable Volume Rendering of Articulated Human SDFs](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-19824-3_1) [S1]. The study compares these methods against the proposed Learned Forward LBS to demonstrate improvements in PSNR and visual quality, specifically addressing artifacts from rigid or discretized skinning [S1].

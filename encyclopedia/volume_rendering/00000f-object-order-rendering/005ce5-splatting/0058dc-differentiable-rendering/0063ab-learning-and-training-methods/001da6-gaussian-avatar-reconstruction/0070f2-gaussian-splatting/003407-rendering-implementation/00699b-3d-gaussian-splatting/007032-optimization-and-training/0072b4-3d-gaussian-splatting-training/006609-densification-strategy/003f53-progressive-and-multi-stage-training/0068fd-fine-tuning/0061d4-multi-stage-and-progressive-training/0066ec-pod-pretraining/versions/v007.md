# POD Pretraining

Also called: Distillation-based amortized optimization, Predict–Optimize–Distill, Predict–Optimize–Distill (POD) Training, Progressive Optimization Distillation

## Overview
POD Pretraining is a distillation-based amortized optimization procedure that stabilizes the training of multi-stage residual Gaussian predictors by supervising them with locally refined Gaussian targets instead of unstable image-space gradients [S1, unofficial]. It solves the problem of training collapse caused by the non-unique inverse mapping from residual images to Gaussian parameters, which is amplified across stages through error accumulation [S1, unofficial]. Unlike direct image-space supervision, which leads to divergence, or sibling methods like [Two-stage Optimization](14857-two-stage-optimization.md) that rely on fixed initialization, POD actively refines the predicted increments via a short-horizon optimizer and distills this refined result back into the predictor as a well-conditioned regression target [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Input:** Residual image $E_{i-1}$ from previous stage $i-1$; current accumulated Gaussian set $G_{i-1}$ (fixed during local optimization).
*   **Output:** Refined Gaussian increments $\Delta \hat{G}_i$ used as the regression target for stage $i$.

**Data Structures:**
*   **Predicted Increments $\Delta G_i$:** The output of the stage predictor $f_{\theta_i}(E_{i-1})$, filtered by Stage Control.
*   **Detached Copy $\Delta \hat{G}_i^{(0)}$:** A trainable copy of $\Delta G_i$ with gradients detached from the predictor.
*   **Refined Increments $\Delta \hat{G}_i$:** The result after $K_i$ optimization steps.

**Numbered Steps:**
1.  **Predict:** The residual predictor produces additional Gaussians $\Delta \hat{G}_i$ from $E_{i-1}$. Stage Control selects the effective subset $\Delta G_i$ [S1, unofficial].
2.  **Optimize (Init):** Create a detached trainable copy of the predicted increments: $\Delta \hat{G}_i^{(0)} = \text{copy}(\Delta G_i)$ [S1, unofficial].
3.  **Optimize (Update):** Update the copy for $K_i$ steps using the residual rendering objective. Previous-stage Gaussians $G_{i-1}$ are kept fixed so gradients flow only to $\Delta \hat{G}_i^{(t)}$ [S1, unofficial]:
    $$ \Delta \hat{G}_i^{(t+1)} = \Delta \hat{G}_i^{(t)} - \eta \nabla L_{render}^{(i)}(R(\Delta \hat{G}_i^{(t)}), E_{i-1}) $$
    where $t = 0, \dots, K_i-1$, $\eta$ is the learning rate, and $L_{render}^{(i)}$ is the residual rendering loss [S1, unofficial].
    *   *Note:* The SSIM component of $L_{render}$ is evaluated on the accumulated rendering $R(G_{i-1} \cup \Delta \hat{G}_i^{(t)})$ against $I_{gt}$, as the residual $E_{i-1}$ may contain negative values [S1, unofficial].
4.  **Distill Target:** The refined increments are obtained after $K_i$ steps: $\Delta \hat{G}_i = \Delta \hat{G}_i^{(K_i)}$ [S1, unofficial]. This result is detached and used as the fixed regression target.
5.  **Distill Loss:** Supervise the original prediction $\Delta G_i$ with a Gaussian-space regression loss:
    $$ L_{gaussian}^{(i)} = L_{gaussian}(\Delta G_i, \Delta \hat{G}_i) $$
    [S1, unofficial].
6.  **Accumulate:** The overall POD pretraining objective sums the losses across all $S$ stages:
    $$ L_{POD} = \sum_{i=1}^{S} \lambda_i L_{gaussian}^{(i)} $$
    [S1, unofficial].

**Parameters:**
*   $K_i$: Number of local optimization steps. Not given in the sources.
*   $\eta$: Learning rate for local optimization. Not given in the sources.
*   $\lambda_i$: Stage weight balancing contribution. Set uniformly across stages [S1, unofficial].
*   Gaussian-space distillation loss coefficient: Multiplied by 100 to match the gradient scale of Gaussian parameter regression [S1, unofficial].
*   Residual rendering loss weights: $L_{render} = 0.7 L_1 + 0.3 (1 - \text{SSIM})$ [S1, unofficial].

**Pitfalls:**
*   **Training Collapse:** Direct image-space supervision faces a non-unique inverse mapping from residual images to Gaussian parameters. This ambiguity is amplified across stages, leading to training collapse (e.g., PSNR dropping from 32.13 dB to 16.22 dB at iteration 80k) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Unstable Gradients:** Residual rendering supervision alone is under-constrained, and image-space gradients do not always provide a stable or consistent direction in Gaussian parameter space [S1, unofficial].
*   **Feature Degeneration:** Without POD, stage features may be dominated by a single principal component (e.g., 98.35% variance in Stage 1), indicating a degenerate solution [S1, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### AIR Predictor Architecture
A stage-wise predictor using a DINOv3 ViT-Base encoder and a lightweight convolutional head to predict Gaussian candidates from residual images [S1, unofficial]. It extracts multi-level features from the 5th and 11th transformer layers, projects them to 1024 dimensions, fuses them into a 32-channel spatial feature map, and applies patch embedding (patch size $p$) to map each patch to a 64-dimensional token. Each token predicts a Gaussian candidate, forming a dense candidate set $g_i$, which is masked by Stage Control to obtain effective increments $\Delta G_i$ [S1, unofficial]. Patch size $p$ controls spatial granularity and maximum candidate count; evaluated settings are 5, 6, 7, and 14 [S1, unofficial]. The encoder is initialized with pretrained DINOv3 weights, optimized with AdamW and StepLR decay [S1, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Stability:** POD prevents training collapse. Full POD reaches a best PSNR of 33.44 dB and remains stable with a final PSNR of 33.41 dB, whereas direct supervision collapses to 16.22 dB [S1, unofficial].
*   **Feature Quality:** POD produces a more balanced feature distribution. With POD, the first three principal components explain 28.09%, 22.10%, and 14.47% of the variance, compared to 98.35% for a single component in the collapse case [S1, unofficial].
*   **Performance:** POD improves training stability rather than just peak reconstruction quality [S1, unofficial].
*   **Cost:** POD pretraining requires 120k iterations with a batch size of 20. Finetuning requires 300k iterations with a batch size of 16 [S1, unofficial].

## Background
POD Pretraining was introduced in the AIR (Amortized Image Reconstruction Framework) paper to stabilize multi-stage residual Gaussian prediction [S1, unofficial]. It addresses the instability of direct image-space supervision in feed-forward 2D Gaussian Splatting by amortizing iterative optimization into a feed-forward network via distillation [S1, unofficial].

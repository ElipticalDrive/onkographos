# Geometry and Surface Priors


## Overview
Geometry and Surface Priors align the discrete 3D Gaussian representation with continuous geometric priors to enforce positional and normal consistency, enabling accurate mesh extraction and physically-based rendering [9267-surface-and-geometry-alignment.md]. Unlike methods relying solely on photometric loss, this family integrates geometric supervision into training or post-processing to ensure the Gaussians conform to physical surfaces [9267-surface-and-geometry-alignment.md].

## Shared principles
All methods in this family align the discrete Gaussian representation with a continuous geometric prior [9267-surface-and-geometry-alignment.md]. The core mechanism adds geometric loss terms to the standard photometric rendering loss $\mathcal{L}_{rgb}$ [9267-surface-and-geometry-alignment.md]. These losses enforce two properties: positional consistency, where Gaussian centers $\mathbf{c}_i$ are encouraged to lie on the zero-level set of a distance function (SDF or UDF) or align with metric depth estimates [9267-surface-and-geometry-alignment.md], and normal consistency, where the orientation of the Gaussians (derived from their covariance matrix or rotation quaternion) is aligned with the surface normal estimated from the geometric prior [9267-surface-and-geometry-alignment.md]. The total loss function generally takes the form:
$$ \mathcal{L}_{total} = \mathcal{L}_{rgb} + \lambda_{geo} \mathcal{L}_{geo} + \lambda_{norm} \mathcal{L}_{norm} $$
where $\mathcal{L}_{geo}$ is the geometric consistency loss and $\mathcal{L}_{norm}$ is the normal consistency loss [9267-surface-and-geometry-alignment.md]. The weights $\lambda_{geo}$ and $\lambda_{norm}$ are hyperparameters that balance photometric fidelity against geometric accuracy [9267-surface-and-geometry-alignment.md].

## Subtopics

### [GaussianPro](9167-gaussianpro.md)
Mitigates reconstruction artifacts and improves geometric fidelity by introducing a progressive propagation strategy that utilizes propagated depth and normal maps from neighboring views to guide Gaussian growth, explicitly enforcing normal consistency and planar constraints. Choose when standard densification fails to capture continuous surface geometry.

### [NeuSG](9172-neusg.md)
Integrates the NeuS implicit surface reconstruction method into the 3D Gaussian Splatting representation by directly coupling a NeuS-style Signed Distance Function (SDF) with a Gaussian rasterizer to transfer explicit surface properties to discrete Gaussian primitives. Choose when explicit surface normals and signed distances are required without post-hoc mesh extraction.

### [Monocular Depth Scale Alignment](9183-monocular-depth-scale-alignment.md)
A post-optimization step that resolves the up-to-scale ambiguity inherent in multi-view reconstruction by aligning the metric scale of optimized 3D Gaussians with metric-scale monocular depth estimates, operating after standard optimization by fixing Gaussian parameters and optimizing only a global scale factor. Choose when metric scale recovery is needed without modifying the training loop.

### [Sparse-view 3D Gaussian Splatting with Depth Priors](9185-sparse-view-3d-gaussian-splatting-with-depth-priors.md)
Addresses reconstruction from sparse-view inputs by explicitly integrating depth priors estimated from pre-trained 2D monocular depth estimation networks to supervise the Gaussian distribution, rather than relying solely on implicit neural fields. Choose when multi-view constraints are insufficient.

### [GSDF](9205-gsdf.md)
Aligns the discrete 3D Gaussian representation with a continuous Signed Distance Function (SDF) prior to enhance geometry reconstruction quality by explicitly transferring surface properties from a neural SDF to the Gaussians. Choose when SDF-based surface extraction is preferred.

### [Surface and Geometry Alignment](9267-surface-and-geometry-alignment.md)
The overarching family definition focusing on the alignment of discrete Gaussians with continuous priors (SDFs, depth, meshes) to enforce positional and normal consistency for mesh extraction.

### [Joint 2D and 3D Priors](20090-joint-2d-and-3d-priors.md)
Combines 2D diffusion priors (e.g., Stable Diffusion) and 3D diffusion priors (e.g., Zero-1-to-3) to supervise novel view reconstruction, using 2D priors for plausible unseen geometry (mitigating the Janus problem) and 3D priors for geometric consistency. Choose when generative priors are needed to balance geometry exploration and exploitation.

### 3DGSR
A method that encourages consistency between Signed Distance Function (SDF) and 3DGS to enhance geometry reconstruction quality [S1].

### Gaussian Splatting Surface Refinement
Uses pretrained volume rendering priors (UDF) to push Gaussians toward true surfaces by optimizing along the direction of increasing rendering weights, avoiding off-surface drift and discreteness [S2]. It is a post-training refinement strategy particularly effective for open and thin structures [S2]. Integrates into existing variants (e.g., GaussianUDF, QGS) without modifying inference-time resource costs, as the prior network is bypassed during inference [S2]. Outperforms handcrafted renderers and closed-surface-tailored 3DGS methods on open and thin structures where standard Gaussian distributions degrade severely [S2].

## Background
The integration of geometric priors into 3D Gaussian Splatting emerged to address the "floating" artifact issue inherent in the original 3DGS formulation, where Gaussians represent volume rather than surface [9267-surface-and-geometry-alignment.md]. Early approaches like GSDF [S1] and 3DGSR [S1] focused on aligning Gaussians with SDFs to improve reconstruction quality. Subsequent methods such as NeuSG incorporated implicit neural representations (NeuS) to encourage consistency between Gaussians' signed distance values and explicit surface normals [S1]. Other lines of work, such as DN-Splatter, utilized depth and normal priors captured from common devices or predicted from general-purpose networks to enhance reconstruction quality [S1]. Wolf et al. trained a 3DGS to render stereo-calibrated novel views and applied stereo depth estimation on the rendered views, fusing the estimated dense depth maps by the Truncated Signed Distance Function (TSDF) to form a triangular mesh [S1]. More recently, diffusion-based priors (Joint 2D and 3D Priors) have been introduced to leverage large-scale generative models for geometric regularization [20090-joint-2d-and-3d-priors.md]. Volume rendering priors, such as those in Gaussian Splatting Surface Refinement, have also been developed to refine open and thin structures by pushing Gaussians toward true surfaces predicted by unsigned distance functions [S2].

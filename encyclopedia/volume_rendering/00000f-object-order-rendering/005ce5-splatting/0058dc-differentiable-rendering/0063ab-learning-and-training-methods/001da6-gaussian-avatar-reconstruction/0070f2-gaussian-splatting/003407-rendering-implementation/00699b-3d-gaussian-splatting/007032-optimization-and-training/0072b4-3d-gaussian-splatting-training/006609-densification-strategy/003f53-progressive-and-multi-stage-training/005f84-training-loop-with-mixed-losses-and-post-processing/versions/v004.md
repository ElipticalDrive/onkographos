# Training Loop with Mixed Losses and Post-Processing

Also called: Mixed Loss Training Loop, Post-Processing Training Step

## Overview
This method defines the iterative optimization step for 3D Gaussian Splatting, integrating photometric, depth, and regularization losses with a progressive spherical harmonic (SH) degree schedule. It distinguishes itself from simpler training loops by explicitly handling multi-modal supervision (RGB + Depth), appearance post-processing via bilateral grids, and visibility-aware optimizer stepping to improve convergence stability [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
- **Inputs:** Current training `step`, Gaussian primitives $\mathcal{G}$ (means, scales, quaternions, opacities, SH coefficients), camera parameters, image data (RGB, optional Depth), and configuration `cfg` (hyperparameters).
- **Outputs:** Updated Gaussian attributes, optimizer states, and optionally saved PLY files.

**Data Structures:**
- `splats`: Dictionary containing tensors for `means` ($N \times 3$), `scales` ($N \times 3$), `quats` ($N \times 4$), `opacities` ($N$), `sh0` ($N \times 3$), `shN` ($N \times C_{SH}$).
- `info`: Dictionary from rasterizer containing `radii` ($N \times H \times W$), `gaussian_ids` (indices of visible Gaussians), and `alphas` ($H \times W$).
- `cfg`: Configuration object with boolean flags (`depth_loss`, `use_bilateral_grid`, `random_bkgd`, `visible_adam`, `save_ply`) and float/int parameters (`sh_degree_interval`, `sh_degree`, `ssim_lambda`, `depth_lambda`, `opacity_reg`, `scale_reg`).

**Numbered Steps:**

1.  **Spherical Harmonic Degree Calculation:**
    Determine the active SH degree $d$ for the current step:
    $$ d = \min\left(\left\lfloor \frac{\text{step}}{\text{cfg.sh\_degree\_interval}} \right\rfloor, \text{cfg.sh\_degree}\right) $$
    [S1, unofficial]

2.  **Rasterization:**
    Render the scene using the current Gaussians. If `cfg.depth_loss` is true, render mode is 'RGB+ED' (RGB + Estimated Depth); otherwise, 'RGB'.
    - Output: `renders` (tensor), `alphas` (tensor), `info` (dict).
    [S1, unofficial]

3.  **Render Parsing:**
    Split `renders` into colors and depths based on channel count.
    - If `renders` has 4 channels:
      $$ \text{colors} = \text{renders}[:, 0:3] $$
      $$ \text{depths} = \text{renders}[:, 3:4] $$
    - Else:
      $$ \text{colors} = \text{renders} $$
      $$ \text{depths} = \text{None} $$
    [S1, unofficial]

4.  **Bilateral Grid Slicing (Optional):**
    If `cfg.use_bilateral_grid` is true:
    - Compute grid coordinates `grid_xy` from camera/view space.
    - Apply bilateral grid slicing to `colors`:
      $$ \text{colors} = \text{slice}(\text{self.bil\_grids}, \text{grid\_xy}, \text{colors}, \text{image\_ids})[\text{"rgb"}] $$
    [S1, unofficial]

5.  **Random Background Compositing (Optional):**
    If `cfg.random_bkgd` is true:
    - Generate random background color $\mathbf{b} \sim \mathcal{U}[0,1]^3$.
    - Composite:
      $$ \text{colors} = \text{colors} + \mathbf{b} \cdot (1.0 - \text{alphas}) $$
    [S1, unofficial]

6.  **Pre-Backward Strategy Step:**
    Call `strategy.step_pre_backward(params, optimizers, state, step, info)`. This may perform densification or pruning operations before gradient computation.
    [S1, unofficial]

7.  **Reconstruction Loss:**
    Compute photometric loss against ground truth `colors_gt`.
    - If `is_nerf_sample.any()` is false (standard case):
      - Compute L1 or L2 loss `real_recon_loss` based on `cfg.use_l1_for_real_samples`.
      - Compute SSIM loss `ssimloss`.
      - Total reconstruction loss:
        $$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{recon}} = \text{real\_recon\_loss} \cdot (1.0 - \text{cfg.ssim\_lambda}) + \text{ssimloss} \cdot \text{cfg.ssim\_lambda} $$
    [S1, unofficial]

8.  **Depth Loss (Optional):**
    If `cfg.depth_loss` is true and not a NeRF sample:
    - Normalize points to $[-1, 1]$.
    - Sample depths from the rendered depth map using `F.grid_sample`.
    - Compute disparity:
      $$ \text{disp} = \frac{1.0}{\text{depths}} \quad (\text{where } \text{depths} > 0) $$
    - Compute L1 loss against ground truth disparity:
      $$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{depth}} = \text{F.l1\_loss}(\text{disp}, \frac{1.0}{\text{depths\_gt}}) \cdot \text{self.scene\_scale} $$
    - Add to total loss:
      $$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} += \text{depthloss} \cdot \text{cfg.depth\_lambda} $$
    [S1, unofficial]

9.  **Bilateral Grid Total Variation (Optional):**
    If `cfg.use_bilateral_grid` is true:
    $$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{tv}} = 10 \cdot \text{total\_variation\_loss}(\text{self.bil\_grids.grids}) $$
    $$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} += \mathcal{L}_{\text{tv}} $$
    [S1, unofficial]

10. **Opacity Regularization (Optional):**
    If `cfg.opacity_reg > 0.0`:
    $$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{opacity}} = \text{cfg.opacity\_reg} \cdot \mathbb{E}[\sigma(\text{self.splats["opacities"]})] $$
    $$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} += \mathcal{L}_{\text{opacity}} $$
    [S1, unofficial]

11. **Scale Regularization (Optional):**
    If `cfg.scale_reg > 0.0`:
    $$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{scale}} = \text{cfg.scale\_reg} \cdot \mathbb{E}[\exp(\text{self.splats["scales"]})] $$
    $$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} += \mathcal{L}_{\text{scale}} $$
    [S1, unofficial]

12. **Backward Pass:**
    $$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{total}}.\text{backward()} $$
    [S1, unofficial]

13. **Visibility Mask Calculation (Optional):**
    If `cfg.visible_adam` is true:
    - If `cfg.packed` is true:
      - Create zero tensor of shape `opacities`.
      - Scatter 1s at indices `info["gaussian_ids"]`.
    - Else:
      - Mask is `(info["radii"] > 0).all(-1).any(0)`.
    [S1, unofficial]

14. **Optimizer Step:**
    - If `cfg.visible_adam`:
      $$ \text{optimizer.step}(\text{visibility\_mask}) $$
    - Else:
      $$ \text{optimizer.step()} $$
    - Zero gradients with `set_to_none=True`.
    [S1, unofficial]

15. **Auxiliary Optimizer Steps:**
    - Step `pose_optimizers` and `bil_grid_optimizers` if present.
    - Zero their gradients.
    [S1, unofficial]

16. **Scheduler Step:**
    - Step learning rate schedulers.
    [S1, unofficial]

17. **Post-Backward Strategy Step:**
    Call `strategy.step_post_backward`.
    - If `DefaultStrategy`: pass `packed=cfg.packed`.
    - If `MCMCStrategy`: pass `lr=schedulers[0].get_last_lr()[0]`.
    [S1, unofficial]

18. **PLY Export (Optional):**
    If `step` is in `cfg.ply_steps` or `step == max_steps - 1` and `cfg.save_ply`:
    - If `cfg.app_opt` is true:
      - Bake appearance by evaluating `app_module` at origin (dirs=torch.zeros_like(...)).
      - Add `self.splats["colors"]`.
      - Apply sigmoid.
      - Convert to SH0.
      - Create empty SHN.
    - Else:
      - Use `self.splats["sh0"]` and `self.splats["shN"]`.
    - Export splats with `means`, `scales`, `quats`, `opacities`, `sh0`, `shN`.
    [S1, unofficial]

**Pseudocode:**

```python
def training_step(step, splats, optimizers, cfg, scene_scale, strategy, bil_grids=None, app_module=None):
    # 1. SH Degree
    sh_degree_to_use = min(step // cfg.sh_degree_interval, cfg.sh_degree)
    
    # 2. Rasterize
    render_mode = 'RGB+ED' if cfg.depth_loss else 'RGB'
    renders, alphas, info = rasterize_gaussians(splats, camera_params, render_mode)
    
    # 3. Parse Renders
    if renders.shape[-1] == 4:
        colors = renders[..., 0:3]
        depths = renders[..., 3:4]
    else:
        colors = renders
        depths = None
        
    # 4. Bilateral Grid Slicing
    if cfg.use_bilateral_grid:
        grid_xy = compute_grid_xy(camera_params)
        colors = slice_bilateral_grid(bil_grids, grid_xy, colors, image_ids)["rgb"]
        
    # 5. Random Background
    if cfg.random_bkgd:
        bkgd = torch.rand(1, 3, device=colors.device)
        colors = colors + bkgd * (1.0 - alphas)
        
    # 6. Pre-Backward Strategy
    strategy.step_pre_backward(splats, optimizers, step, info)
    
    # 7. Reconstruction Loss
    if not is_nerf_sample.any():
        real_recon_loss = compute_l1_l2_loss(colors, colors_gt, use_l1=cfg.use_l1_for_real_samples)
        ssimloss = compute_ssim_loss(colors, colors_gt)
        loss = real_recon_loss * (1.0 - cfg.ssim_lambda) + ssimloss * cfg.ssim_lambda
    else:
        loss = torch.tensor(0.0, device=colors.device)
        
    # 8. Depth Loss
    if cfg.depth_loss and not is_nerf_sample.any():
        # Normalize points and sample depths
        sampled_depths = sample_depths(depths, normalized_points)
        disp = 1.0 / sampled_depths.clamp(min=1e-6)
        gt_disp = 1.0 / depths_gt.clamp(min=1e-6)
        depthloss = F.l1_loss(disp, gt_disp) * scene_scale
        loss += depthloss * cfg.depth_lambda
        
    # 9. Bilateral Grid TV Loss
    if cfg.use_bilateral_grid:
        tvloss = 10 * total_variation_loss(bil_grids.grids)
        loss += tvloss
        
    # 10. Opacity Regularization
    if cfg.opacity_reg > 0.0:
        opacity_reg = cfg.opacity_reg * torch.sigmoid(splats["opacities"]).mean()
        loss += opacity_reg
        
    # 11. Scale Regularization
    if cfg.scale_reg > 0.0:
        scale_reg = cfg.scale_reg * torch.exp(splats["scales"]).mean()
        loss += scale_reg
        
    # 12. Backward
    loss.backward()
    
    # 13. Visibility Mask
    visibility_mask = None
    if cfg.visible_adam:
        if cfg.packed:
            visibility_mask = torch.zeros_like(splats["opacities"])
            visibility_mask.scatter_(0, info["gaussian_ids"], 1.0)
        else:
            visibility_mask = (info["radii"] > 0).all(-1).any(0)
            
    # 14. Optimizer Step
    if cfg.visible_adam:
        optimizers[0].step(visibility_mask)
    else:
        optimizers[0].step()
    optimizers[0].zero_grad(set_to_none=True)
    
    # 15. Auxiliary Optimizers
    if pose_optimizers:
        pose_optimizers.step()
        pose_optimizers.zero_grad(set_to_none=True)
    if bil_grid_optimizers:
        bil_grid_optimizers.step()
        bil_grid_optimizers.zero_grad(set_to_none=True)
        
    # 16. Scheduler Step
    for scheduler in schedulers:
        scheduler.step()
        
    # 17. Post-Backward Strategy
    if isinstance(strategy, DefaultStrategy):
        strategy.step_post_backward(packed=cfg.packed)
    elif isinstance(strategy, MCMCStrategy):
        current_lr = schedulers[0].get_last_lr()[0]
        strategy.step_post_backward(lr=current_lr)
        
    # 18. PLY Export
    if cfg.save_ply and (step in cfg.ply_steps or step == max_steps - 1):
        if cfg.app_opt:
            # Bake appearance
            dirs = torch.zeros_like(splats["means"]) # Simplified for origin
            baked_colors = app_module(dirs) + splats["colors"]
            baked_colors = torch.sigmoid(baked_colors)
            sh0 = baked_colors
            shN = torch.zeros_like(sh0) # Empty SHN
        else:
            sh0 = splats["sh0"]
            shN = splats["shN"]
            
        export_ply(
            means=splats["means"],
            scales=splats["scales"],
            quats=splats["quats"],
            opacities=splats["opacities"],
            sh0=sh0,
            shN=shN,
            filename=f"step_{step}.ply"
        )
```

**Parameters:**
- `cfg.sh_degree_interval`: Steps between increasing SH degree. Typical value: 1000 [S1, unofficial].
- `cfg.sh_degree`: Maximum SH degree. Typical value: 3 [S1, unofficial].
- `cfg.ssim_lambda`: Weight for SSIM loss. Typical value: 0.2 [S1, unofficial].
- `cfg.depth_lambda`: Weight for depth loss. Typical value: 0.5 [S1, unofficial].
- `cfg.opacity_reg`: Opacity regularization weight. Typical value: 0.01 [S1, unofficial].
- `cfg.scale_reg`: Scale regularization weight. Typical value: 0.01 [S1, unofficial].
- `cfg.use_l1_for_real_samples`: Use L1 instead of L2 for photometric loss. Default: True [S1, unofficial].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
- **Depth Division by Zero:** When computing disparity $1/\text{depths}$, depths must be clamped to a minimum positive value (e.g., $10^{-6}$) to avoid NaNs [S1, unofficial].
- **Visibility Mask Shape:** If `cfg.packed` is true, the visibility mask must be created with shape matching `opacities` (number of Gaussians), not image dimensions [S1, unofficial].
- **SH Degree Bound:** The SH degree calculation uses integer division and min, ensuring it never exceeds `cfg.sh_degree` [S1, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
- **Time Complexity:** Dominated by rasterization $O(N \cdot H \cdot W)$ and backward pass. The visibility mask calculation adds $O(N)$ or $O(H \cdot W)$ depending on packing.
- **Memory Complexity:** $O(N)$ for Gaussian attributes. Bilateral grids add $O(H \cdot W \cdot C_{grid})$ if used.
- **Measured Performance:** Improves PSNR/SSIM over single-loss training by incorporating depth and SSIM [S1, unofficial].
- **Strengths:** Handles multi-modal data (RGB+Depth), stabilizes training with regularization, and allows appearance baking.
- **Limitations:** Increased complexity in implementation due to multiple loss terms and optional features.
- **When to Use:** When high-fidelity reconstruction is required, depth data is available, or appearance needs to be baked.
- **When Not to Use:** For simple scenes where RGB-only training suffices, or when computational resources are extremely limited.

## Background
This method is derived from the AugSplat implementation, which extends standard 3D Gaussian Splatting with additional features like bilateral grids and mixed losses [S1, unofficial]. It builds on the foundational 3D Gaussian Splatting paper [7173-hybrid-coarse-to-fine-strategy.md] and integrates concepts from multi-stage training [15018-multi-stage-training-with-pose-prediction.md].

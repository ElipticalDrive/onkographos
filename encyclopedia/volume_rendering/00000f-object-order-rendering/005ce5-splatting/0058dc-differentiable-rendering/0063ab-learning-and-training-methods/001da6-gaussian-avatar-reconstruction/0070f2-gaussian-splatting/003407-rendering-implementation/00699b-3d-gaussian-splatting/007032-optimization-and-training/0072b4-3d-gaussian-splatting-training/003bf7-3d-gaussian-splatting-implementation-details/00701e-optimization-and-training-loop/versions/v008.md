# Optimization and Training Loop

Also called: 3D Gaussian Splatting Training, 3DGS Optimization

## Overview
The Optimization and Training Loop is the iterative process that minimizes the photometric error between rendered images and ground-truth input photos by adjusting the parameters of a set of 3D Gaussians [S2][S1, unofficial]. It differs from sibling methods like [TurboGS](15697-turbogs.md) or [Distributed 3DGS Training Loop with Adaptive Workload Division](18650-distributed-3dgs-training-loop-with-adaptive-workload-divisi.md) by relying on the standard stochastic gradient descent with a fixed iteration budget and explicit densification/pruning heuristics, rather than adaptive workload division or accelerated rendering pipelines [S2][S1, unofficial].

## Shared principles
The core mechanism common to all training loops in this family is the minimization of a composite loss function $\mathcal{L}$ between the rendered image $\hat{I}$ and the ground-truth image $I_{gt}$ [S1, unofficial][S2]. The standard photometric baseline is a weighted sum of L1 loss and differentiable Structural Similarity (D-SSIM) [S1, unofficial][S2]:
$$ \mathcal{L}_{img} = (1 - \lambda) \mathcal{L}_{L1} + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{D-SSIM} $$
where $\mathcal{L}_{L1}$ is the average absolute difference between corresponding pixel colors, and $\mathcal{L}_{D-SSIM}$ measures local structure, contrast, and texture [S2]. The weight $\lambda$ is typically 0.2 [S2][S1, unofficial].

The optimization uses the Adam optimizer with $\beta_1 = 0.9$ and $\beta_2 = 0.99$ [S1, unofficial][S2]. Training typically runs for a fixed number of iterations, commonly around 30,000 for standard scenes, though specific schedules vary (e.g., [Gaussian Primitive Training Schedule](18977-gaussian-primitive-training-schedule.md) uses ~6,000) [S2]. The process does not stop at a specific loss threshold because the loss curve continues to creep down with diminishing returns; a fixed iteration budget is used instead [S2].

Gaussian parameters $\mathcal{G} = \{g_i\}_{i=1}^N$ include position $\mathbf{p}_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$, covariance $\Sigma_i$ (parameterized by rotation quaternion $\mathbf{q}_i \in \mathbb{R}^4$ and scale vector $\mathbf{s}_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$), opacity $\alpha_i \in [0, 1]$, and Spherical Harmonics (SH) coefficients $\mathbf{c}_{i, l}$ for view-dependent color [S2][S1, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### [3D Gaussian Splatting Training Loop](15139-3d-gaussian-splatting-training-loop.md)
The standard iterative optimization process that minimizes photometric error by adjusting Gaussian parameters. It differs from siblings like [TurboGS](15697-turbogs.md) by using standard SGD with fixed iteration budgets and explicit densification heuristics rather than adaptive workload division.

### [TurboGS](15697-turbogs.md)
An error-guided training framework that accelerates optimization by dynamically focusing computation on perceptually informative pixels. It replaces dense rasterization with tile-wise sparse pixel sampling guided by persistent error and age maps, and uses a moment-damped Levenberg-Marquardt solver.

### [Error-Guided Sparse Pixel Sampling](15708-error-guided-sparse-pixel-sampling.md)
A training acceleration strategy that restricts loss evaluation and gradient computation to a sparse subset of pixels per tile, guided by persistent error and age maps. It solves redundant gradient computation in well-reconstructed regions.

### [Frequency Guided Resolution Scheduler](15751-frequency-guided-resolution-scheduler.md)
A training strategy that accelerates optimization by dynamically adjusting the rendering resolution based on the frequency content of the training data. It reduces optimization complexity by allocating more iterations to high-resolution rendering only after low-frequency structure is captured.

### [Multi-View Regulated Gaussian Splatting](15783-multi-view-regulated-gaussian-splatting.md)
A training strategy that aggregates gradients from multiple views in each optimization step to enforce multi-view coherence and stabilize gradient descent. It jointly updates 3D Gaussians observed by multiple views.

### [Mip-Splatting Training Loop & Evaluation Pipeline](17975-mip-splatting-training-loop-evaluation-pipeline.md)
Defines the complete training iteration and evaluation procedure for Mip-Splatting, extending 3DGS with subpixel-aware rendering to mitigate aliasing artifacts. It handles camera sampling, hybrid loss computation, and adaptive densification/pruning.

### [Distributed 3DGS Training Loop with Adaptive Workload Division](18650-distributed-3dgs-training-loop-with-adaptive-workload-divisi.md)
Implements a distributed training workflow that batches camera selection, divides rendering workloads across multiple GPUs using an adaptive strategy based on historical performance, and synchronizes losses.

### [Sparse SH Order Increment](18689-sparse-sh-order-increment.md)
A memory-efficient training strategy that dynamically increases the Spherical Harmonics (SH) order for a subset of Gaussians based on their view-dependent color error. It initializes all Gaussians with SH order 0 and selectively promotes only those with significant rendering errors.

### [Regression-based FisherRF](18747-regression-based-fisherrf.md)
A variant of the FisherRF uncertainty estimation method that replaces the direct aggregation of color parameter variances with a meta-regression model to predict pixel-wise uncertainty. It groups Gaussian primitive parameter variances into six distinct categories.

### [Gaussian Primitive Training Schedule](18977-gaussian-primitive-training-schedule.md)
Accelerates training by leveraging dense initial point clouds to significantly reduce the required optimization iterations. It replaces the long, continuous densification process with a two-phase training loop interrupted by pseudo-view depth optimization.

### [Loss Function Design](19337-loss-function-design.md)
Defines the composite loss function $\mathcal{L}$ minimized during training, which drives the optimization of Gaussian primitives. It establishes the standard photometric baseline and provides the framework for extending this baseline with geometric, semantic, or consistency constraints.

### [Local 3DGS Optimization](20073-local-3dgs-optimization.md)
An iterative method for estimating the relative camera pose between two nearby images by optimizing a local 3D Gaussian Splatting representation against photometric loss. It freezes Gaussian primitives' geometric and appearance attributes after initialization, optimizing only the camera pose parameters.

### [Immediate 3D Gaussian Splat Reconstruction](20247-immediate-3d-gaussian-splat-reconstruction.md)
A streaming pipeline that processes an unordered sequence of RGB images to produce a real-time 3D Gaussian Splatting model. It interleaves matching, pose estimation, and Gaussian placement with a fixed local optimization window.

### [Active View Selection and Residual Learning for 3DGS](20899-active-view-selection-and-residual-learning-for-3dgs.md)
A training framework that combines active view selection with residual learning to improve reconstruction quality under sparse view conditions. It integrates a self-augmented pre-filtering mechanism to stabilize the view selection process.

### [Uncertainty-Guided Residual Learning for 3DGS](20901-uncertainty-guided-residual-learning-for-3dgs.md)
A training strategy that amplifies gradient flow to weakly contributing, high-uncertainty Gaussians by combining full-scene renders with uncertainty-intensified renders. It acts as a skip-connection mechanism targeting Gaussians with low opacity or large scale.

### [Submap-based Gaussian SLAM Mapping](21005-submap-based-gaussian-slam-mapping.md)
Manages the incremental construction of a 3D Gaussian Splatting model within a SLAM framework by dividing the scene into manageable spatial or temporal segments called "submaps". It resets the optimization state periodically while maintaining global consistency.

### [Neural Parametric Gaussians Optimization](21236-neural-parametric-gaussians-optimization.md)
A two-stage optimization procedure for monocular non-rigid object reconstruction that combines a coarse parametric point model regularized by a low-rank deformation basis with 3D Gaussians to model fine geometric and appearance details.

### [3D Gaussian Splatting Optimization](22286-3d-gaussian-splatting-optimization.md)
Details the specific optimization schedule, initialization strategies, and hyperparameter configurations required to train a 3D Gaussian Splatting model from scratch. It employs a staged introduction of Spherical Harmonics and a resolution warm-up.

### [Gradient Modification](22461-gradient-modification.md)
Techniques that adjust the backward pass of 3D Gaussian Splatting to address specific optimization failures, such as vanishing gradients for distant or "dead" primitives. These methods intercept gradients to provide non-zero signals to primitives that would otherwise receive none.

### PyGS Optimization
- **Definition**: The optimization process using a combined MSE and SSIM loss, along with density control strategies adapted from 3DGS, while preserving the multi-scale structure [S1, unofficial].
- **Steps**: Apply splitting and cloning strategies from 3DGS for density control. Newly split/cloned Gaussians remain in their original level and cluster. Routinely reassess and reassign the levels of Gaussians based on their scales to preserve the multi-scale characteristics and balanced distribution across levels [S1, unofficial].
- **Formula**: $\mathcal{L} = \lambda \cdot \text{MSE}(C, \hat{C}) + (1 - \lambda) \cdot \text{SSIM}(C, \hat{C})$ where $\lambda$ is a hyperparameter balancing the terms, $C$ is ground-truth color, and $\hat{C}$ is rendered color [S1, unofficial].

### Global Latent Optimization (GLO)
- **Definition**: A method to handle photometric inconsistencies (automatic exposure/white-balance) by co-optimizing a per-view latent vector mapped via an MLP to a linear transformation of spherical harmonic coefficients [S2].
- **Formula**: $\hat{c}_i = a \odot c_i + b$, where $\hat{c}_i$ are the transformed coefficients, $c_i$ are the original spherical harmonic coefficients, $\odot$ is the Hadamard product, $b$ models brightness changes, and $a$ provides white-balance expressive power. $a, b = Q(z_n; \theta_Q)$, where $Q$ is an MLP taking latent $z_n \in \mathbb{R}^{64}$ [S2].
- **Parameters**: $z_n \in \mathbb{R}^{64}$, a latent vector per input camera view. $\theta_Q$, trainable parameters of the MLP $Q$ that maps $z_n$ to $a$ and $b$ [S2].
- **Pitfall**: A reduced model prevents $z_n$ from explaining distractors as per-image adjustments, which would happen in a simpler GLO [S2].

### Progressive Resolution Scheduling
- **Definition**: A training strategy that gradually increases rendering resolution during training to improve convergence speed and reduce computational overhead, starting from low resolution and increasing toward the target [S3].
- **Formula**: $R_t = R_{max} \cdot (\frac{t}{T})^\gamma$, where $R_t$ is the resolution at training step $t$, $R_{max}$ is the final resolution, $T$ is the total training steps, and $\gamma$ controls the growth rate [S3].
- **Parameters**: Initial resolution: 540x960. Final resolution: 1080x1920 [S3].

### Frequency-Spectrum Regularization
- **Definition**: A method that uses differences in frequency spectra of rendered and reference images to progressively regularize the model from coarse, low-frequency structures to fine, high-frequency details [S4].

### SuGaR Pipeline
- **Definition**: A pipeline to extract meshes from 3D Gaussian Splatting and build hybrid representations that enable easy composition and animation in Gaussian Splatting scenes by manipulating the mesh [S5, unofficial].
- **Steps**:
    1. Short vanilla 3DGS optimization: optimizing a vanilla 3D Gaussian Splatting model for 7k iterations, in order to let Gaussians position themselves in the scene [S5, unofficial].
    2. SuGaR optimization: optimizing Gaussians alignment with the surface of the scene [S5, unofficial].
    3. Mesh extraction: extracting a mesh from the optimized Gaussians [S5, unofficial].
    4. SuGaR refinement: refining the Gaussians and the mesh together to build a hybrid Mesh+Gaussians representation [S5, unofficial].
    5. Textured mesh extraction (Optional): extracting a traditional textured mesh from the refined SuGaR model as a tool for visualization, composition and animation in Blender using our Blender add-on [S5, unofficial].

### Geometric Initialization
- **Definition**: Initializing 3D Gaussian primitives directly from the retrieved CAD mesh surface rather than SfM point clouds, providing strong inductive bias [S6, unofficial].
- **Steps**: Define target number of primitives $N_{splats}$. Draw area-weighted uniform random sample of points from mesh surface to obtain initial canonical positions $\mu_{can}^{(i)}$. Recover surface normals $n_i$ by casting ray from sample toward mesh interior and reading triangle normal of intersected face. Initialize rotation quaternion $q_i$ to shortest-arc rotation aligning local z-axis with $n_i$, creating oriented disk tangent to mesh [S6, unofficial].
- **Pitfall**: Standard 3DGS densification/pruning (cloning/splitting) is not employed to maintain strict adherence to the CAD surface geometry [S6, unofficial].

### C LUNO GS
- **Definition**: A compact framework for Language 3DGS that combines semantics-aware Gaussian sparsification and attribute vector quantization in a unified constrained objective, solved via an alternating primal-dual procedure [S7, unofficial].
- **Steps**: The optimization alternates among four updates: 1) Primal Update (differentiable scene optimization), 2) Sparsification Update (cardinality projection on opacities), 3) Vector Quantization Update (quantization of attributes), and 4) Dual Update (Lagrange multiplier updates) [S7, unofficial].
- **Formula**: Rendering loss: $L_{RGB} = (1 - \lambda)L_1 + \lambda L_{D-SSIM}$, where $\lambda \in [0, 1]$ balances the terms [S7, unofficial].

### Gaussian Optimization with Photometric Gradients
- **Definition**: A refinement step that optimizes the spherical harmonic coefficients and Gaussian structures using visual-derived photometric gradients to enhance photorealism and density [S8].
- **Steps**: Optimize the spherical harmonic coefficients of the Gaussians using photometric gradients derived from captured images. Refine the LiDAR Gaussian structures using images captured from various perspectives [S8].

### Opacity Sparsity Loss
- **Definition**: A modification to the training loss function that adds an L1 sparsity term on opacity to encourage lower opacity values, facilitating effective culling [S9].
- **Steps**: Add an L1 sparsity term on opacity to the loss function during optimization [S9].

## Background
The 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) training loop was introduced by Kerbl et al. in 2023 [S2]. It builds on the differentiable rendering principles established in earlier work [S1, unofficial]. The method has since been extended and adapted for various applications, including dynamic scenes, large-scale environments, and sparse view reconstruction.

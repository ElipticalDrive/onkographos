# GsplatVolumeLighting

Also called: Gaussian Splat Volume Ray Marching, Volume Occlusion for 3DGS

## Overview
GsplatVolumeLighting is a volume lighting and occlusion technique for 3D Gaussian Splatting that ray-marches through a 3D density texture to compute transmittance for shadow and volume occlusion, combined with per-light attenuation and diffuse shading [S1, unofficial]. It extends the standard 3D Gaussian Splatting rendering pipeline by adding a secondary pass that evaluates volumetric shadows and ambient/direct lighting contributions for each splat, allowing splats to cast and receive shadows from volumetric objects [S1, unofficial]. Unlike standard 3DGS which assumes unoccluded lighting or simple environment maps, this method integrates a 3D density field to modulate light transport, enabling realistic indoor or complex outdoor scenes where splats interact with volumetric geometry [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
The method operates in two main phases: a volume transmittance calculation (shadow march) and a per-splat lighting evaluation. It is implemented as a shader extension within the UnitySplats package [S1, unofficial].

### Inputs and Outputs
**Inputs:**
*   **Splat Data:** World-space center position, normal vector, and view direction for the current splat being shaded [S1, unofficial].
*   **Light Data:** Up to 4 lights, each with type (directional, point, or spot), position/direction, color, and attenuation parameters [S1, unofficial].
*   **Volume Data:**
    *   `_GsplatVolumeDensity`: A `sampler3D` texture storing density. The R8 channel encodes $\sqrt{\text{normalized optical depth}}$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   `_GsplatVolumeBoundsMin/Max`: `float4` defining the axis-aligned volume bounds in local space [S1, unofficial].
    *   `_GsplatVolumeBoundsInvSize`: `float4` containing the inverse dimensions of the volume bounds [S1, unofficial].
    *   `_GsplatVolumeOcclusionParams`: `float4` packing:
        *   `x`: Optical-depth scale (0 disables volume lighting) [S1, unofficial].
        *   `y`: Number of sample steps (clamped 1–24) [S1, unofficial].
        *   `z`: Start distance for the march [S1, unofficial].
    *   `_GsplatVolumeAmbientColor`: `float3` ambient color [S1, unofficial].
    *   `_GsplatVolumeLightingParams`: `float4` packing:
        *   `y`: Ambient intensity multiplier [S1, unofficial].
        *   `z`: Direct lighting intensity multiplier [S1, unofficial].
    *   `_GsplatVolumeLightColors`: Array of `float3` light colors [S1, unofficial].
    *   `GSPLAT_VOLUME_OPAQUE_DEPTH`: Constant `8.0`. Transmittance below $\exp(-8)$ is treated as fully opaque [S1, unofficial].

**Outputs:**
*   Final shaded color for the splat, combining ambient and direct lighting modulated by volumetric transmittance [S1, unofficial].

### Data Structures
The volume density is stored in a 3D texture (`sampler3D`). The bounds are defined by two `float4` vectors (`_GsplatVolumeBoundsMin`, `_GsplatVolumeBoundsMax`) representing the minimum and maximum corners of the axis-aligned bounding box (AABB) in local space. The inverse size (`_GsplatVolumeBoundsInvSize`) is precomputed as $1 / (\text{BoundsMax} - \text{BoundsMin})$ to facilitate UVW coordinate conversion [S1, unofficial].

### Algorithm Steps

1.  **Enable Check:** If `_GsplatVolumeEnabled == 0`, return base color with no volume lighting [S1, unofficial].
2.  **Light Direction Transformation:** Transform the light direction to the local space of the volume using `_GsplatVolumeWorldToLocal`. Normalize the direction. If the length is $\le 10^{-6}$, return transmittance $1.0$ (no occlusion) [S1, unofficial].
3.  **Ray-Box Intersection:**
    *   Compute `directionSign` using `lerp(-1.0, 1.0, step(0.0, direction))` to handle ray direction signs [S1, unofficial].
    *   Clamp the ray direction magnitude to $10^{-5}$ to prevent division by zero [S1, unofficial].
    *   Calculate entry ($t_0$) and exit ($t_1$) distances against `BoundsMin` and `BoundsMax`.
    *   Compute `farDistance` as the minimum of the far plane distances along each axis.
    *   Return `max(0.0, min(farDistance.x, min(farDistance.y, farDistance.z)))` as the maximum ray length within the volume [S1, unofficial].
4.  **Light-Specific Ray Clamping:**
    *   For point lights (`lightPosition.w > 0.5`), clamp the ray length to the Euclidean distance from the splat's local center to the light position [S1, unofficial].
    *   Calculate `usableLength = rayLength - startDistance`. If `usableLength <= 10^{-5}`, return transmittance $1.0$ [S1, unofficial].
5.  **March Setup:**
    *   Determine `sampleCount = clamp((int)round(_GsplatVolumeOcclusionParams.y), 1, 24)` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Compute `stepLength = usableLength / sampleCount` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Initialize UVW coordinates at the midpoint of the first step:
        $$ \text{uvw} = (\text{localCenter} + \text{localDirection} \cdot (\text{startDistance} + 0.5 \cdot \text{stepLength}) - \text{boundsMin}) \cdot \text{invSize} $$
    *   Compute step delta: $\text{uvwStep} = \text{localDirection} \cdot \text{stepLength} \cdot \text{invSize}$ [S1, unofficial].
6.  **Volume Transmittance Calculation:**
    *   Initialize `opticalDepth = 0.0` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Loop up to `sampleCount` times:
        *   If `sampleIndex >= sampleCount` or `opticalDepth >= GSPLAT_VOLUME_OPAQUE_DEPTH`, break [S1, unofficial].
        *   If `uvw` is outside $[0, 1]$ bounds, skip sample [S1, unofficial].
        *   Sample the R8 channel of `_GsplatVolumeDensity` at `uvw`. Let $d_i$ be the sampled value [S1, unofficial].
        *   Add $d_i^2$ to `opticalDepth` [S1, unofficial].
        *   Advance `uvw += uvwStep` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Compute transmittance:
        $$ T = \exp(-\text{strength} \cdot \text{opticalDepth}) $$
        where `strength` is `_GsplatVolumeOcclusionParams.x` [S1, unofficial].
7.  **Per-Splat Lighting Evaluation:**
    *   Transform splat center to world space. Transform and normalize the normal. If $\text{dot}(\text{worldNormal}, \text{viewDirection}) < 0.0$, flip the normal [S1, unofficial].
    *   Compute ambient:
        $$ \text{ambient} = \text{_GsplatVolumeAmbientColor.rgb} \cdot \text{_GsplatVolumeLightingParams.y} $$
    *   Initialize `direct = 0.0` [S1, unofficial].
    *   For each light (max 4):
        *   Compute vector to light (`toLight`) and distance squared [S1, unofficial].
        *   Compute attenuation:
            *   For directional lights (`lightType < 0.5`): `attenuation = 1.0` [S1, unofficial].
            *   For point/spot lights:
                $$ \text{normalizedDistanceSquared} = \text{distanceSquared} \cdot \text{lightDirection.w} $$
                $$ \text{rangeFade} = \text{saturate}(1.0 - \text{normalizedDistanceSquared}^2) $$
                $$ \text{attenuation} = \frac{\text{rangeFade}^2}{\text{distanceSquared}} $$
        *   If spot light (`lightType > 1.5`):
            $$ \text{spotCosine} = \text{dot}(\text{lightDirection.xyz}, -\text{toLight}) $$
            $$ \text{spot} = \text{saturate}((\text{spotCosine} - \text{spotParams.x}) \cdot \text{spotParams.y}) $$
            $$ \text{attenuation} *= \text{spot}^2 $$
        *   Compute visibility using the transmittance $T$ calculated in step 6 [S1, unofficial].
        *   Add to direct:
            $$ \text{direct} += \text{_GsplatVolumeLightColors[lightIndex].rgb} \cdot (\text{irradiance} \cdot T) $$
        *   If `irradiance <= 0.0`, skip transmittance calculation for this light to save compute [S1, unofficial].
    *   Scale direct lighting by `_GsplatVolumeLightingParams.z` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Return $\max(\text{ambient} + \text{direct}, 0.0)$ [S1, unofficial].

### Pseudocode

```python
def gsplat_volume_lighting(splat, lights, volume_params, volume_texture, bounds_min, bounds_max, bounds_inv_size):
    if not volume_params.enabled:
        return splat.base_color

    # 1. Light Direction in Local Space
    local_light_dir = world_to_local(splat.light_direction)
    local_light_dir = normalize(local_light_dir)
    if length(local_light_dir) <= 1e-6:
        return splat.base_color * 1.0 # No occlusion

    # 2. Ray-Box Intersection
    direction_sign = lerp(-1.0, 1.0, step(0.0, local_light_dir))
    local_light_dir = clamp(local_light_dir, 1e-5, 1e5) # Prevent div by zero
    
    t0 = (bounds_min - splat.local_center) / local_light_dir
    t1 = (bounds_max - splat.local_center) / local_light_dir
    
    t_enter = max(min(t0), min(t1)) # Simplified logic based on source description
    t_exit = min(max(t0), max(t1))
    
    ray_length = max(0.0, min(t_exit.x, min(t_exit.y, t_exit.z)))
    
    # 3. Light-Specific Clamping
    if splat.light_type == POINT_LIGHT:
        dist_to_light = length(splat.light_position - splat.world_center)
        ray_length = min(ray_length, dist_to_light)
        
    usable_length = ray_length - volume_params.start_distance
    if usable_length <= 1e-5:
        return splat.base_color * 1.0

    # 4. March Setup
    sample_count = clamp(round(volume_params.sample_steps), 1, 24)
    step_length = usable_length / sample_count
    
    uvw = (splat.local_center + local_light_dir * (volume_params.start_distance + 0.5 * step_length) - bounds_min) * bounds_inv_size
    uvw_step = local_light_dir * step_length * bounds_inv_size
    
    optical_depth = 0.0
    
    # 5. Volume Transmittance
    for i in range(sample_count):
        if optical_depth >= 8.0: # GSPLAT_VOLUME_OPAQUE_DEPTH
            break
        if not (0.0 <= uvw.x <= 1.0 and 0.0 <= uvw.y <= 1.0 and 0.0 <= uvw.z <= 1.0):
            uvw += uvw_step
            continue
            
        d = sample_r8(volume_texture, uvw)
        optical_depth += d * d
        uvw += uvw_step
        
    transmittance = exp(-volume_params.optical_depth_scale * optical_depth)

    # 6. Lighting Evaluation
    world_normal = normalize(transform_normal(splat.normal))
    if dot(world_normal, splat.view_direction) < 0.0:
        world_normal = -world_normal
        
    ambient = volume_params.ambient_color * volume_params.ambient_intensity
    
    direct = 0.0
    for light in lights:
        to_light = light.position - splat.world_center
        dist_sq = dot(to_light, to_light)
        
        # Attenuation
        if light.type == DIRECTIONAL:
            attenuation = 1.0
        else:
            norm_dist_sq = dist_sq * light.direction_w
            range_fade = saturate(1.0 - norm_dist_sq * norm_dist_sq)
            attenuation = (range_fade * range_fade) / dist_sq
            
        # Spot Falloff
        if light.type == SPOT:
            spot_cos = dot(light.direction, -normalize(to_light))
            spot = saturate((spot_cos - light.spot_inner) * light.spot_outer)
            attenuation *= spot * spot
            
        irradiance = light.color * attenuation
        if irradiance <= 0.0:
            continue
            
        direct += irradiance * transmittance
        
    direct *= volume_params.direct_intensity
    return max(ambient + direct, 0.0)
```

### Parameters and Typical Values
*   **Sample Steps (`_GsplatVolumeOcclusionParams.y`):** Clamped between 1 and 24. Higher values increase accuracy but reduce performance. Typical value: 8–16 [S1, unofficial].
*   **Optical Depth Scale (`_GsplatVolumeOcclusionParams.x`):** Controls the sensitivity of the density texture. A value of 0 disables volume lighting [S1, unofficial].
*   **Opaque Depth Threshold:** Constant `8.0`. Transmittance below $\exp(-8) \approx 3.35 \times 10^{-4}$ is considered fully opaque [S1, unofficial].
*   **Direction Clamp:** $10^{-5}$ to prevent division by zero during ray-box intersection [S1, unofficial].

### Edge Cases and Pitfalls
*   **Division by Zero:** Ray-box intersection fails if ray direction is zero. Mitigated by clamping direction magnitude to $10^{-5}$ [S1, unofficial].
*   **Performance:** Over-marching into fully opaque regions wastes compute. Mitigated by early exit when `opticalDepth >= 8.0` [S1, unofficial].
*   **Back-Facing Lights:** Lights behind the splat or out of range can trigger expensive shadow marches. Mitigated by skipping transmittance calculation when `irradiance <= 0.0` [S1, unofficial].
*   **Precision:** The R8 texture format stores $\sqrt{\text{normalized optical depth}}$. Squaring the sampled value recovers the optical depth contribution. Precision loss in R8 may affect subtle shadow gradients [S1, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** $O(N \cdot L \cdot S)$, where $N$ is the number of splats, $L$ is the number of lights (max 4), and $S$ is the number of samples per light (max 24). The constant factor is small due to early exits and fixed sample counts.
*   **Memory Complexity:** $O(V)$ for the 3D density texture, where $V$ is the volume resolution. The texture is typically small (e.g., 64x64x64) to fit in cache.
*   **Performance:** The method adds a per-splat cost proportional to the number of lights and samples. It is most expensive when splats are in dense volumetric regions or when many lights are active. Early exit on opaque regions helps maintain performance in shadowed areas [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Provides physically plausible volumetric shadows and occlusion for 3DGS. Integrates seamlessly with Unity's lighting pipeline. Supports multiple light types (directional, point, spot) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** Limited to 4 lights. Fixed sample count may not adapt to varying density gradients. R8 texture precision limits shadow smoothness. Requires a 3D density texture to be authored or generated [S1, unofficial].
*   **When to Use:** Indoor scenes, complex outdoor environments with volumetric fog or occlusion, or when splats need to interact with volumetric geometry. Not suitable for simple outdoor scenes with only directional sunlight where performance is critical [S1, unofficial].

The method is based on ray-marching through a 3D density texture, a technique common in volumetric rendering, adapted for the splat-based representation of 3DGS [S1, unofficial].

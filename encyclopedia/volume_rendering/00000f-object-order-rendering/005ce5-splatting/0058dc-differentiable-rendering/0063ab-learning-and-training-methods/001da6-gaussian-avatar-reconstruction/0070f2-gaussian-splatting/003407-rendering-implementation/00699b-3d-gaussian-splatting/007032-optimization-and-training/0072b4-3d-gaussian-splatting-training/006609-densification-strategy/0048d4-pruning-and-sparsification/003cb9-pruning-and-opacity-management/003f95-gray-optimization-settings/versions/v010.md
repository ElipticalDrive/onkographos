# GRay Optimization Settings

Also called: GRayLQ, GRayHQ

## Overview
GRay Optimization Settings define the hyperparameter configurations for training 3D Gaussian Splatting using the GRay ray-tracing renderer [S1]. Unlike standard 3DGS and 3DGRT, GRay does not use opacity resets and does not prune Gaussians based on size [S1]. The settings control the trade-off between training speed, final frame rate, and reconstruction quality through alpha thresholds, pruning thresholds, and spherical harmonic stepping laziness.

## How it works
The optimization process manages Gaussian populations and rendering parameters via specific thresholds and decay rates.

**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Inputs:** Initial Gaussian cloud, camera poses, training images, hyperparameters ($\alpha_{\min}, \alpha_{\expo}, \mu_{\prune}, \eta_{\decay}, \alpha_{\lazy}, \tau_{\min}$).
*   **Outputs:** Optimized Gaussian attributes (position, covariance, opacity, spherical harmonics).

**Data Structures:**
*   **Gaussian Attributes:** Position ($\mathbf{t}$), Covariance ($\mathbf{\Sigma}$), Opacity ($\alpha$), Spherical Harmonics coefficients ($\mathbf{c}$).
*   **Rendering State:** Current iteration count, accumulated training time.

**Parameters and Typical Values:**
Two primary presets are defined in the sources [S1]:

1.  **GRayLQ (Low Quality/Fast):**
    *   $\alpha_{\min} = 0.02$
    *   $\alpha_{\expo} = 3$
    *   $\mu_{\prune} = 5 \times 10^{-7}$
    *   $\eta_{\decay} = 0.9998$
    *   $\alpha_{\lazy} = 12$ (SH stepping laziness)
    *   Iterations: 7500
    *   Resolution: Half resolution experiments.

2.  **GRayHQ (High Quality):**
    *   $\alpha_{\min} = 0.001$
    *   $\alpha_{\expo} = 1$ (matching 3DGS)
    *   $\tau_{\min} = 0.001$ (matching 3DGRT)
    *   Initialization binning: Disabled.
    *   $\mu_{\prune} = 10^{-9}$
    *   Scale decay: Disabled entirely.
    *   Resolution: Half resolution experiments.

**Algorithm Steps:**
1.  **Initialization:** Generate initial Gaussian cloud. If using GRayHQ, disable initialization binning [S1].
2.  **Rendering:** For each training step, render the scene using the GRay ray tracer [S1].
3.  **Loss Computation:** Compute photometric loss between rendered and target images [S1].
4.  **Optimization:** Update Gaussian attributes using gradient descent [S1].
5.  **Opacity Management:** Update opacity $\alpha$. Unlike 3DGS/3DGRT, no opacity reset is performed [S1].
6.  **Pruning:** Remove Gaussians with opacity below $\mu_{\prune}$. Unlike 3DGS, size-based pruning is not performed [S1].
7.  **SH Stepping:** Update spherical harmonic coefficients lazily based on $\alpha_{\lazy}$ [S1].
8.  **Scale Decay:** If enabled (GRayLQ), decay scale factors by $\eta_{\decay}$ [S1].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
*   **DHT Truncation:** Disabling the Differentiable Hashing Table (DHT) with high $\tau_{\min}$ causes rapid quality deterioration. For example, `GRayNoDHT` with $\tau_{\min}=0.03$ results in PSNR 24.54 and LPIPS 0.284, while $\tau_{\min}=0.04$ fails to converge due to rapid Gaussian expansions [S1].
*   **Binning:** Disabling initialization binning increases initial Gaussian count (3.97M vs 3.27M) with minimal quality loss (PSNR 26.49 vs 26.47) [S1].

## Subtopics

### GRayLQ Preset
A configuration optimized for speed. It uses aggressive alpha thresholds ($\alpha_{\min}=0.02$), higher pruning thresholds ($\mu_{\prune}=5 \times 10^{-7}$), and scale decay ($\eta_{\decay}=0.9998$) to reduce training time and increase FPS. It achieves PSNR 26.23 and FPS 1051 at half resolution, training in under a minute [S1].

### GRayHQ Preset
A configuration optimized for quality. It uses low alpha thresholds ($\alpha_{\min}=0.001$), minimal pruning ($\mu_{\prune}=10^{-9}$), and disables scale decay and binning. It achieves PSNR 27.41 and FPS 55 at half resolution, taking 36 minutes to optimize [S1].

## Operating characteristics
**Time and Memory Complexity:**
*   **GRayLQ:** Training time < 1 minute (Opt Time 00:46). Final Gaussian count: 0.48M. FPS: 1051 [S1].
*   **GRayHQ:** Training time 36:11. Final Gaussian count: 3.79M. FPS: 55 [S1].
*   **Comparison:** GRayLQ more than doubles 3DGS's frame rate [S1]. GRayHQ improves quality over GRayLQ and approaches 3DGS-DI [S1].

**Strengths and Limitations:**
*   **Strengths:** GRayLQ offers extremely fast training and high FPS. GRayHQ offers high fidelity [S1].
*   **Limitations:** GRayHQ is significantly slower to train. Disabling DHT leads to instability at high thresholds [S1].

## Background
GRay is a ray-tracing based renderer for 3D Gaussian Splatting [S1]. The optimization settings were introduced to balance the quality/speed trade-off in ray-traced Gaussian rendering [S1].

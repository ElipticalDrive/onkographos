# Multi-stage Training with Pose Prediction

Also called: FineSplat training phases, Training and Inference Protocol, Two-stage Training with Pose-free Inference

## Overview
This method defines a sequential optimization protocol for feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting models that predict scene geometry and appearance from a set of input views. It solves the problem of jointly optimizing camera poses and Gaussian attributes, which is ill-posed and unstable when trained end-to-end from scratch. The method sets itself apart by decoupling the learning of coarse geometry/pose from fine-grained attribute refinement through distinct stages, eventually enabling pose-free inference where the model predicts cameras without explicit pose supervision [S1, S2, unofficial]. It builds on [Progressive and Multi-stage Training](9966-progressive-and-multi-stage-training.md) by specifying the exact parameter freezing and loss activation schedules required to stabilize pose prediction.

## How it works
The method consists of three sequential training stages followed by a pose-free inference protocol. The Gaussian set $\mathcal{G}$ is defined in [Densification Strategy](6234-densification-strategy.md).

### Inputs and Outputs
*   **Inputs:** Input RGB images $I_{in}$, ground-truth target images $I_{gt}$ (during supervised stages), and ground-truth camera poses/intrinsics (during Stage 2).
*   **Outputs:** A set of 3D Gaussians $\mathcal{G}$ (centers, covariances, opacities, spherical harmonics coefficients) and predicted camera poses/intrinsics for context and target views.

### Data Structures
The model maintains two primary branches:
1.  **Reconstructor/Geometry Branch:** Predicts camera poses and initial Gaussian centers/geometry.
2.  **Attribute Branch (Decoder):** Refines Gaussian attributes (color, opacity, covariance) and potentially refines centers.
*   **Tokens:** Intermediate latent tokens representing the scene state, detached between steps to prevent gradient flow across refinement steps [S1, unofficial].

### Numbered Steps

**Stage 1: Coarse Geometry and Pose Learning**
1.  **Initialize:** Train a pixel-aligned Gaussian prediction head.
2.  **Supervision:** Use ground-truth camera poses as conditions. Supervise input and sampled target renderings with equally weighted masked L1 and LPIPS losses [S2, unofficial].
3.  **Update:** Update only the attribute branch’s last $K$ Transformer blocks and the Gaussian decoder [S2, unofficial].
4.  **Transition:** Replace the pixel-aligned decoder with a patch-aligned Gaussian decoder [S1, unofficial].
5.  **Fine-tune:** Continue training with camera losses on predicted poses and intrinsics [S1, unofficial].
    *   *Note:* [S1, unofficial] specifies 120K iterations for pixel-aligned, followed by 80K iterations for patch-aligned. [S2, unofficial] describes this as "Stage I" where both branches receive input RGB and GT pose conditions.

**Stage 2: Pose-Corrected Attribute Refinement**
1.  **Freeze:** Freeze the Stage 1 trained parameters (Reconstructor/Geometry branch) [S1, S2, unofficial].
2.  **Input:** Use ground-truth camera poses and intrinsics [S1, unofficial].
3.  **Refinement Loop:** The model performs $T$ refinement steps (typically $T=4$) [S1, unofficial].
    *   Let $\hat{I}^{(i)}$ be the target view rendered after the $i$-th step.
    *   Detach tokens between steps $i$ to prevent gradient flow across steps [S1, unofficial].
4.  **Loss Calculation:** Compute the loss averaged over all steps $i=0 \dots T-1$:
    $$ L = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{i=0}^{T-1} \left( \mathcal{L}_2(\hat{I}^{(i)}, I) + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{perc}(\hat{I}^{(i)}, I) \right) $$
    Where $\mathcal{L}_2$ is mean squared error, $\mathcal{L}_{perc}$ is VGG-19 perceptual loss, and $\lambda = 0.2$ [S1, unofficial].
5.  **Update:** Train the feature projection and refiner (Attribute branch) [S2, unofficial]. Input-view losses provide feedback, while input and disjoint target views supervise the updated scene [S2, unofficial].
    *   *Note:* [S1, unofficial] specifies 20K iterations on 4 GPUs. [S2, unofficial] refers to this as "Stage II".

**Stage 3: End-to-End Pose-Free Fine-Tuning**
1.  **Unfreeze:** Unfreeze all parameters, including the camera pose estimator and Gaussian head [S1, unofficial].
2.  **Input:** Fully pose-free setting. Both context and target cameras are predicted; no ground-truth camera loss is used [S1, unofficial].
3.  **Supervision:** Supervise the Stage-1 Gaussians. The loss is averaged over steps $i=0 \dots T$ [S1, unofficial].
4.  **Update:** Fine-tune the full model [S1, unofficial].
    *   *Note:* [S1, unofficial] specifies 10K iterations on 8 GPUs.

**Pose-Free Inference Protocol**
1.  **Camera Prediction:** Use the geometry branch’s predicted cameras [S2, unofficial].
2.  **Normalization:** Normalize translations by $s_{norm}$ (scene scale normalization) [S2, unofficial].
    *   *Note:* $s_{norm}$ is derived from context camera poses (origin at first context camera, translations divided by max pairwise distance) or estimated depth [S1, unofficial].
3.  **Rendering:** Render input views for refinement feedback [S2, unofficial].
4.  **Refinement:** The refiner updates scene attributes while network weights remain fixed [S2, unofficial].

### Formulas
*   **Stage 2/3 Loss:**
    $$ L = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{i=0}^{T-1} \left( \mathcal{L}_2(\hat{I}^{(i)}, I) + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{perc}(\hat{I}^{(i)}, I) \right) $$
    [S1, unofficial]
*   **Scene Scale Normalization ($s_{norm}$):**
    $$ s_{norm} = \max_{j,k} \| C_j - C_k \|_2 $$
    Where $C_j, C_k$ are context camera centers. Translations are divided by $s_{norm}$ [S1, S2, unofficial].

### Parameters
*   **Optimization:** AdamW with $\beta_1=0.9, \beta_2=0.95$, weight decay 0.05 (multi-dimensional params only), gradient clipping max norm 1.0, bfloat16 mixed precision [S1, unofficial].
*   **Stage 1 LR:** $2 \times 10^{-5}$, no warm-up, cosine decay to zero [S1, unofficial].
*   **Stage 2 LR:** $1 \times 10^{-4}$, 200-iteration linear warm-up, constant until 80%, cosine decay to zero over remaining 20% [S1, unofficial].
*   **End-to-End LR:** $2 \times 10^{-5}$ for all parameters, 200-iteration linear warm-up, cosine decay to zero [S1, unofficial].
*   **Perceptual Weight ($\lambda$):** 0.2 [S1, unofficial].
*   **Refinement Steps ($T$):** 4 [S1, unofficial].
*   **Transformer Blocks ($K$):** Last $K$ blocks updated in Stage 1 [S2, unofficial]. Value not given in sources.

### Edge Cases and Pitfalls
*   **Pose Drift:** Without Stage 2's ground-truth pose supervision, the model may learn incorrect poses that compensate for attribute errors. Stage 2 isolates attribute learning from pose errors [S1, unofficial].
*   **Gradient Flow:** Tokens must be detached between refinement steps to ensure each step learns to improve the state it receives, rather than relying on gradients from previous steps [S1, unofficial].
*   **Scale Ambiguity:** Scene scale is not fixed by input images. Normalization by $s_{norm}$ is critical for consistent training and inference [S1, S2, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### RLSplat Training
A variant that introduces a Gaussian allocation policy for budget control. It uses a two-stage training process: Base Training (40,000 steps) with random allocations and policy training (5,000 steps) with policy optimization [S3, unofficial].
*   **Losses:** Combines reconstruction loss $\mathcal{L}_{recon}$ (MSE + LPIPS), camera loss $\mathcal{L}_{cam}$ (translation, rotation, FOV), projection loss $\mathcal{L}_{proj}$ (reprojection with cheirality penalty), locality regularizers ($\mathcal{L}_{vor}, \mathcal{L}_{offset}$), and policy loss $\mathcal{L}_{policy} = -J_{policy}$ [S3, unofficial].
*   **Budget:** Sampled from $[2048, 16384V]$ where $V$ is input views [S3, unofficial].
*   **Parameters:** $K_{anc} = K_{gs} = 16$ [S3, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Training is sequential across stages. Stage 1 is the longest (120K-200K iterations depending on variant) [S1, unofficial]. Stage 2 and 3 are shorter (20K and 10K) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Requires storing intermediate tokens for $T$ refinement steps. Detaching tokens prevents gradient accumulation across steps but requires memory for forward pass [S1, unofficial].
*   **Performance:** Enables high-quality novel view synthesis with predicted cameras. Pose-free inference removes the need for ground-truth poses at test time [S1, S2, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Stabilizes training by decoupling pose and attribute learning. Enables pose-free inference.
*   **Limitations:** Requires multiple training stages. Computationally expensive due to refinement steps and multiple stages.
*   **When to use:** When training feed-forward 3DGS models that need to predict cameras from uncalibrated inputs.
*   **When not to use:** When ground-truth cameras are available and fixed, or when computational resources for multi-stage training are limited.

## Background
Originated in FineSplat [S1, unofficial] and InfiniSplat v2 [S2, unofficial]. FineSplat introduced the three-stage protocol (pixel-aligned, patch-aligned, end-to-end) to stabilize pose prediction. InfiniSplat v2 refined this with a pose-free inference protocol and scene scale normalization. RLSplat [S3, unofficial] adapted the multi-stage approach for budget-controllable rendering with a policy head.

# Multi-view Consistency and Scoring


## Overview
Multi-view Consistency and Scoring is a family of densification strategies for 3D Gaussian Splatting that evaluates Gaussian primitives across multiple camera views to guide densification and pruning operations [S1]. It builds on [Densification Strategy](6234-densification-strategy.md) by incorporating multi-view photometric loss and alpha contributions to identify regions requiring refinement or removal [S1]. Unlike its siblings [Multi-view Alpha-based Densification and Pruning](15406-multi-view-alpha-based-densification-and-pruning.md) and [Multi-View Consistency Scoring for Densification](15464-multi-view-consistency-scoring-for-densification.md), this family explicitly integrates importance and pruning scores derived from multi-view photometric loss and alpha contributions into the standard gradient masks, ensuring that densification occurs only where geometry is consistently unresolved across views [S1].

## Shared principles
The shared principles for this family are defined in [Densification Strategy](6234-densification-strategy.md).

## Subtopics

### [Multi-view Alpha-based Densification and Pruning](15406-multi-view-alpha-based-densification-and-pruning.md)
This method replaces single-view gradient-based criteria with multi-view evaluation to constrain Gaussian count and ensure structural consistency [Multi-view Alpha-based Densification and Pruning](15406-multi-view-alpha-based-densification-and-pruning.md). It differs from standard gradient-based densification by explicitly integrating importance and pruning scores derived from photometric loss and alpha contributions into the standard gradient masks [Multi-view Alpha-based Densification and Pruning](15406-multi-view-alpha-based-densification-and-pruning.md). Choose this method when preventing overfitting to single views and reducing excessive Gaussian proliferation is critical [Multi-view Alpha-based Densification and Pruning](15406-multi-view-alpha-based-densification-and-pruning.md).

### [Multi-View Consistency Scoring for Densification](15464-multi-view-consistency-scoring-for-densification.md)
This method computes per-Gaussian scores based on multi-view photometric consistency to guide both densification and pruning [Multi-View Consistency Scoring for Densification](15464-multi-view-consistency-scoring-for-densification.md). It differs from standard gradient-based densification by aggregating L1 reconstruction errors across multiple camera views to identify Gaussians that consistently fail to reconstruct scene geometry or appearance [Multi-View Consistency Scoring for Densification](15464-multi-view-consistency-scoring-for-densification.md). Choose this method when normalized scoring across views is required to prioritize primitives for refinement [Multi-View Consistency Scoring for Densification](15464-multi-view-consistency-scoring-for-densification.md).

### Multi-view augmented densification
This module adjusts the densification threshold based on multi-view discrepancies to help 3D Gaussians fit each view better and capture finer scene details [S1]. It differs from score-based methods by dynamically modifying the baseline threshold $\beta$ using a Heaviside step function $H(\cdot)$ to gate discrepancy calculations, returning 1 if the input is $\ge 0$ to prevent negative discrepancies from triggering densification [S1]. Choose this method when explicit threshold modulation based on inter-view disagreement is preferred over accumulation-based scoring [S1]. The adjusted threshold is defined as $\hat{\beta} = \beta - \frac{\beta}{2} H(r_\tau^i - 1)$, where $\tau$ adjusts the extent of the discrepancy between each camera and $\beta$ is the original baseline threshold [S1].

## Background
The concept of multi-view consistency in Gaussian Splatting was introduced in MVGS (Multi-view-regulated Gaussian Splatting for Novel View Synthesis) [S1]. The method regulates densification through multi-view constraints to facilitate better multi-view training and capture finer scene details [S1].

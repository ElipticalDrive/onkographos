# FreGS

Also called: Frequency-aware regularization for 3DGS, Progressive frequency regularization

## Overview
FreGS addresses the over-reconstruction and high-frequency artifacts common in 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) by regularizing frequency discrepancies in rendered images [S2]. Unlike sibling methods that focus on spatial geometry or depth consistency, FreGS operates in the frequency domain to prevent the model from fitting noise [S1, unofficial]. It sets itself apart by applying a progressive regularization strategy that expands the Gaussian cloud in a fronto-parallel manner, ensuring smooth expansion and better coverage [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Inputs:** 3D Gaussian primitives (position, covariance, opacity, color), camera parameters, ground truth images.
*   **Outputs:** Optimized Gaussian primitives with reduced high-frequency artifacts.

**Data Structures:**
*   Standard 3DGS primitive structure: position ($\mathbf{p}$), covariance matrix ($\mathbf{\Sigma}$), opacity ($\alpha$), spherical harmonics coefficients for color ($\mathbf{c}$).
*   Frequency domain representation of rendered and ground truth images (e.g., via Fourier Transform).

**Method Steps:**
1.  **Render:** Render the current Gaussian scene to obtain a predicted image $I_{pred}$.
2.  **Frequency Transform:** Compute the frequency spectrum of $I_{pred}$ and the ground truth image $I_{gt}$. Let $F(I)$ denote the frequency domain representation.
3.  **Compute Discrepancy:** Calculate the frequency discrepancy between $F(I_{pred})$ and $F(I_{gt})$. This highlights high-frequency errors that contribute to artifacts [S2].
4.  **Progressive Regularization:** Apply a regularization term based on the frequency discrepancy. The regularization is applied progressively during training [S1, unofficial].
5.  **Gradient Update:** Back-propagate the combined loss (photometric + frequency regularization) to update Gaussian parameters. The frequency regularization specifically targets the smoothing of the Gaussian cloud expansion [S1, unofficial].

**Formulas:**
Let $\mathcal{L}_{freq}$ be the frequency regularization loss. The total loss is:
$$ \mathcal{L}_{total} = \mathcal{L}_{photometric} + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{freq} $$
where $\lambda$ is a weighting factor. The specific form of $\mathcal{L}_{freq}$ involves the difference between the frequency spectra of the rendered and ground truth images [S2].

**Parameters:**
*   $\lambda$: Weight for frequency regularization. Not given in the sources.
*   Progression schedule: Not given in the sources.

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
*   **Over-smoothing:** Excessive frequency regularization may remove valid high-frequency details.
*   **Computational Cost:** Frequency domain transformations add overhead to each training step.

**Pseudocode:**
```python
def fregs_step(gaussians, camera, gt_image, lambda_freq):
    # Render current scene
    pred_image = render(gaussians, camera)
    
    # Compute frequency spectra
    F_pred = fourier_transform(pred_image)
    F_gt = fourier_transform(gt_image)
    
    # Compute frequency discrepancy loss
    # Specific formula for discrepancy not provided in sources
    L_freq = compute_frequency_discrepancy(F_pred, F_gt)
    
    # Compute standard photometric loss
    L_photo = compute_photometric_loss(pred_image, gt_image)
    
    # Total loss
    L_total = L_photo + lambda_freq * L_freq
    
    # Back-propagate and update
    L_total.backward()
    update_gaussians(gaussians)
    
    return L_total
```

## Subtopics

### Frequency Regularization
The broader category of methods using spectral losses. FreGS is a specific instance using progressive regularization [S1, unofficial][S2].

### Smoothed 2D Supervision
Uses 2D image cues for regularization, differing from FreGS's frequency domain approach [7213-smoothed-2d-supervision.md].

### 3D Half-Gaussian Splatting
Modifies covariance for surface alignment, not frequency [9150-3d-half-gaussian-splatting-covariance-transformation-and-reg.md].

### Hard and Soft Depth Regularization
Enforces depth consistency, distinct from frequency constraints [9178-hard-and-soft-depth-regularization.md].

### Cross-scale geometric adaptation
Adapts geometry across scales, not primarily frequency-based [10796-cross-scale-geometric-adaptation.md].

### Geometry Regularization
General term for surface alignment methods [14222-geometry-regularization.md].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Increased per-step cost due to Fourier transforms. Not given in the sources.
*   **Memory Complexity:** Standard 3DGS memory plus storage for frequency domains. Not given in the sources.
*   **Strengths:** Reduces high-frequency artifacts and over-reconstruction [S2].
*   **Limitations:** May smooth out valid details if $\lambda$ is too high. Computational overhead.
*   **When to use:** When training results in noisy or artifact-ridden images despite good photometric loss.

## Background
FreGS was introduced to address over-reconstruction in 3D Gaussian Splatting by focusing on the frequency domain [S2]. It applies progressive frequency regularization to primitives to prevent overfitting and high-frequency artifacts during training [S1, unofficial]. The method is part of the broader effort to improve the geometric validity of 3DGS reconstructions.

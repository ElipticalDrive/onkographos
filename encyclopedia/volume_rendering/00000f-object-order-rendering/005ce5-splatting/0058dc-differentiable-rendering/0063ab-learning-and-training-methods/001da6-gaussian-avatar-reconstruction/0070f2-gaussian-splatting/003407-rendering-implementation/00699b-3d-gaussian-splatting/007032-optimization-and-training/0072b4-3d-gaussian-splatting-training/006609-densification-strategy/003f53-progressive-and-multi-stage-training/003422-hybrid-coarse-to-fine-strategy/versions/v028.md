# Hybrid Coarse-to-Fine Strategy
## Overview
The Hybrid Coarse-to-Fine Strategy is a multi-stage training approach for point-based radiance fields that refines the Gaussian point cloud progressively rather than optimizing it in a single pass [S1]. It solves the problem of suboptimal convergence and poor high-frequency detail reconstruction inherent in single-stage training [S1]. Unlike single-stage optimization, it decouples initialization from refinement, allowing the representation to adapt to scene complexity over time [S1].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Performance:** On the Blender dataset, the hybrid strategy achieves a PSNR of 30.3 dB, SSIM of 0.945, and LPIPS of 0.078 [S1].
*   **Comparison:** Removing the hybrid strategy ('w/o hybrid') reduces PSNR to 26.6 dB, SSIM to 0.925, and increases LPIPS to 0.163 [S1]. This demonstrates that the hybrid strategy yields better rendering quality than single-stage training [S1].

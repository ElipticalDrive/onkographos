# 3DGStream
## Overview
3DGStream is a method for online training of 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) in dynamic scene reconstruction [S1][S2, unofficial]. It models the transformation between frames as a neural transformation cache and adaptively adds 3D Gaussians to handle emerging objects [S1][S2, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Input:** A sequence of video frames with known camera poses, processed online [S1][S2, unofficial].
*   **Output:** A dynamic 3DGS representation where Gaussian attributes are transformed over time via the cache, with an adaptive Gaussian count [S3, unofficial].
*   **Input/Output Dimensions:** Not given in the sources [S3, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
*   **3D Gaussians:** Initialized at the first frame with standard attributes (position, rotation, scale, opacity, color) [S3, unofficial].
*   **Neural Transformation Cache (NTC):** A shallow, fully fused multi-layer perceptron (MLP) utilizing multi-resolution hash encoding to predict transformations across frames [S3, unofficial].

**Steps:**
1. Initialize 3D Gaussians at the first frame [S3, unofficial].
2. Construct the NTC to model transformations between frames [S3, unofficial].
3. For each new frame, query the NTC to transform canonical Gaussian attributes to the current time $t$ [S3, unofficial].
4. Render the frame using the transformed Gaussians and optimize parameters online [S1][S2, unofficial].
5. Apply an adaptive 3D Gaussian addition strategy to dynamically add new Gaussians for emerging objects while regulating their overall quantity [S3, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### Dynamic 3D-GS
A per-frame training method that initializes 3D Gaussians at the first timestamp and conducts static reconstruction [S3, unofficial]. Gaussians have fixed parameters except for position and rotation, enabling tracking of points across frames [S3, unofficial]. Optimization is conducted by regularization without leveraging a neural network [S3, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Online training implies per-frame processing time. The exact complexity is not given in the sources [S3, unofficial].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Depends on the number of Gaussians and the size of the neural transformation cache. Not given in the sources [S3, unofficial].
*   **Measured Performance:** Not given in the sources [S3, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Enables online reconstruction of dynamic scenes; handles emerging objects; uses compact hash encoding for efficiency and adaptability [S3, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** Requires careful initialization; relies on an adaptive addition strategy that must be regulated to control Gaussian count [S3, unofficial].
*   **When to use:** When reconstructing dynamic scenes from video in an online or incremental manner [S1][S2, unofficial].

## Background
3DGStream was introduced as a method for online training of 3DGS in dynamic scene reconstruction [S1][S2, unofficial]. It builds on 3D Gaussian Splatting and extends it to handle temporal dynamics through a neural transformation cache and adaptive Gaussian addition [S1][S2, unofficial]. It is part of the broader family of dynamic 3DGS methods [S1][S2, unofficial].

# PSCD-GS (Perception-Structure Collaborative Densification)
Also called: Role-Aware Perception-Structure Collaborative Densification

## Overview
PSCD-GS is a densification strategy for 3D Gaussian Splatting that replaces single-score heuristics with a continuous perception–structure role representation to guide densification, cloning/splitting selection, and split placement [S1, unofficial]. It augments the baseline 3DGS gradient and scale metrics with complementary perceptual (texture/appearance) and structural (geometry/boundaries) evidence, allowing the renderer to distinguish between regions needing appearance refinement versus geometric reconstruction [S1, unofficial]. Unlike sibling methods that rely on a single complexity or edge score, PSCD-GS retains the overlapping responses of both cue types to condition candidate selection and split initialization [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs:** Training views (images $I_v$ and poses), current depth maps $Z_{kv}$ rendered from the current Gaussian set $\mathcal{G}_k$, and the current set of Gaussians $\mathcal{G}_k$ [S1, unofficial].
**Outputs:** Updated Gaussian set $\mathcal{G}_{k+1}$ with new primitives created via cloning or splitting based on role-conditioned decisions [S1, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
*   **Gaussian $g_i$:** Mean $\mu_i$, covariance $\Sigma_i$ (scale $s_i$, rotation $q_i$), opacity $\alpha_i$, SH coefficients $\mathbf{c}_i$ [Parent].
*   **Evidence Maps:** Perceptual magnitude $A_v$, Structural magnitude $B_{kv}$, Structural tensor $R_{vB,k}$ [S1, unofficial].
*   **Calibration State:** Exponential moving average quantiles $q^P_{k}, q^Q_{k}$ (low/high) for perceptual and structural scores [S1, unofficial].

**Parameters:**
*   $\tau_g$: Gradient threshold for densification eligibility [S1, unofficial].
*   $\tau_s$: Scene-scale-dependent threshold for cloning vs. splitting [S1, unofficial].
*   $\lambda_e, \lambda_d$: Structural mixture weights ($\lambda_e + \lambda_d = 1$) [S1, unofficial].
*   $\tau_z$: Max reprojection depth error for validity [S1, unofficial].
*   $m_{min}$: Floor value for cross-view reliability ($>0$) [S1, unofficial].
*   $\tau_C$: Visibility weight normalization threshold [S1, unofficial].
*   $K_{vis}$: View support threshold [S1, unofficial].
*   $\sigma_X$: Dispersion scale parameter for cross-view agreement [S1, unofficial].
*   $\beta_q$: EMA decay factor for quantile updates [S1, unofficial].
*   $\epsilon_{cal}$: Epsilon for calibration stability [S1, unofficial].
*   $\sigma_z, \sigma_h$: Dispersion scales for depth and structural difference [S1, unofficial].
*   $VGG-16$: Frozen ImageNet-pretrained network for perceptual features [S1, unofficial].
*   $a^P, a^Q, \tau_P, \tau_Q$: Sigmoid steepness and threshold parameters for role mapping [S1, unofficial].

**Step 1: Baseline Eligibility**
Compute the average gradient magnitude $\bar{g}_i$ and maximum scale $s_{max,i}$ for each Gaussian $g_i$ visible in steps $T_i$ [S1, unofficial]:
$$ \bar{g}_i = \frac{1}{|T_i| + \epsilon} \sum_{t \in T_i} \left\| \frac{\partial L_t}{\partial \mu_i^{2D}} \right\|^2, \quad s_{max,i} = \|s_i\|_\infty $$
A Gaussian is eligible for densification if $\bar{g}_i > \tau_g$ [S1, unofficial].

**Step 2: Construct Perceptual Evidence**
For each training view $I_v$, extract features using a frozen VGG-16 network [S1, unofficial]. Compute the perceptual magnitude map $A_v$ using selected layers $L$ and weights $\beta_l$ [S1, unofficial]:
$$ R^v_A(u) = RNorm_{\Omega_v} \left( \frac{\sum_{l \in L} \beta_l U_{pl} \left[ \sum_c (\nabla \phi_{l,c}(I_v))^2 \right]}{\sum_{l \in L} \beta_l U_{pl} \left[ \sum_c \nabla \phi_{l,c}(I_v) \nabla \phi_{l,c}(I_v)^T \right]} \right) $$
where $U_{pl}$ is spatial resizing, $\phi_{l,c}$ are channel-normalized feature maps, and $RNorm_{\Omega_v}$ is robust image-space normalization (clipping at fixed quantiles within view $v$ and mapping to $[0,1]$) [S1, unofficial].

**Step 3: Construct Structural Evidence**
Compute single-view luminance edge response $E_v$ and log-depth gradient response $D_{kv}$ [S1, unofficial]:
$$ E_v = RNorm_{\Omega_v}(\|\nabla Y_v\|_2), \quad D_{kv} = RNorm_{\Omega_v}(\|\nabla \log(Z_{kv} + \epsilon_z)\|_2) $$
Combine them into a raw structural response $H_{kv}$ [S1, unofficial]:
$$ H_{kv} = \lambda_e E_v + \lambda_d D_{kv} $$
Compute cross-view reliability $M_{kv}$ by back-projecting pixels from view $v$ to neighbors $v' \in N(v)$ using depth $Z_{kv}$ [S1, unofficial]:
$$ \Delta z_{vv'}(u) = \frac{Z_{kv}(u') - z_{v \to v'}}{\max(Z_{kv}(u'), z_{v \to v'}, \epsilon_z)} $$
$$ a_{vv'}(u) = \chi_{vv'}(u) \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta z_{vv'}(u)}{\sigma_z}\right) \exp\left(-\frac{\|H_{kv}(u) - H_{kv'}(u')\|}{\sigma_h}\right) $$
$$ M_{kv}(u) = \frac{\sum_{v' \in N(v)} a_{vv'}(u)}{\sum_{v' \in N(v)} \chi_{vv'}(u) + \epsilon} $$
where $\chi_{vv'}(u)=1$ if reprojection is inside neighbor image, depths are valid, and $\Delta z < \tau_z$, else 0 [S1, unofficial]. If no valid correspondence exists, $M_{kv}(u)=0$ [S1, unofficial]. The final structural magnitude $B_{kv}$ applies a floor $m_{min}$ to prevent removal of thin structures/occlusions [S1, unofficial]:
$$ B_{kv} = RNorm_{\Omega_v}(H_{kv}[m_{min} + (1 - m_{min})M_{kv}]) $$
Store the structural direction tensor $R_{vB,k}$ for split placement [S1, unofficial]:
$$ R_{vB,k}(u) = [m_{min} + (1 - m_{min})M_{kv}(u)] (\lambda_e g_v^E g_v^{E^T} + \lambda_d g_v^{D,k} g_v^{D,k^T}) $$
where $g_v^E, g_v^{D,k}$ are normalized gradient vectors [S1, unofficial].

**Step 4: Transfer Evidence to Gaussians**
Cross-view reliability uses $a_{vv'}(u)$ [S1, unofficial]. For each visible Gaussian $g_i$, evidence is aggregated over its projected footprint using the alpha-compositing weights $\omega_{iv}$ [S1, unofficial]. Compute view support $\nu_{iv}$ and cross-view agreement $V_i^X$ [S1, unofficial]:
$$ \nu_{iv} = \min\left(1, \frac{C_v^i}{\tau_C}\right) \sum_{v'} \frac{\omega_{iv'}}{\sum_{v''} \omega_{iv''} + \epsilon} $$
$$ \mu_i^X = \frac{\sum_v \omega_{iv} x_{i,v}^X}{\sum_v \omega_{iv} + \epsilon}, \quad V_i^X = \frac{\sum_v \omega_{iv} (x_{i,v}^X - \mu_i^X)^2}{\sum_v \omega_{iv} + \epsilon} $$
Compute the raw evidence score $I_e^X$ (where $X \in \{A, B\}$) [S1, unofficial]:
$$ I_e^X_i = \min\left(1, \frac{\sum_v \nu_{iv}}{K_{vis}} \exp\left(-\frac{V_i^X}{2(\sigma_X + \epsilon)}\right)\right) \mu_i^X $$
If a Gaussian has no reliable view, set raw evidence to 0 [S1, unofficial].

**Step 5: Calibrate Scores**
Update quantiles using EMA and calibrate to $[0,1]$ [S1, unofficial]:
$$ q^X_{k} = \beta_q q^X_{k-1} + (1-\beta_q) \text{Quantile}_p \{I_e^X\}_{i \in \mathcal{G}_k} $$
$$ Calk^X(x) = \text{clip}\left(\frac{x - q^X_{low,k}}{q^X_{high,k} - q^X_{low,k} + \epsilon_{cal}}, 0, 1\right) $$
$$ P_i = Calk^A(I_e^A_i), \quad Q_i = Calk^B(I_e^B_i) $$
If the quantile span collapses, retain previous range; if no valid range, set branch to 0 [S1, unofficial].

**Step 6: Determine Roles and Densify**
Map scores to continuous memberships $m_i^P, m_i^Q$ using sigmoid functions with parameters $a^P, a^Q, \tau_P, \tau_Q$ [S1, unofficial]:
$$ m_i^P = \sigma[a^P(P_i - \tau_P)], \quad m_i^Q = \sigma[a^Q(Q_i - \tau_Q)] $$
Compute role memberships:
$$ \rho_i^{PQ} = m_i^P m_i^Q \quad (\text{Co-dominant}) $$
$$ \rho_i^{P\bar{Q}} = (1-m_i^Q)m_i^P \quad (\text{Perception-dominant}) $$
$$ \rho_i^{\bar{P}Q} = m_i^Q(1-m_i^P) \quad (\text{Structure-dominant}) $$
$$ \rho_i^{\bar{P}\bar{Q}} = (1-m_i^P)(1-m_i^Q) \quad (\text{Low-response}) $$
Use these roles to condition:
1.  **Candidate Selection:** Prioritize Gaussians with high $\rho_i^{PQ}$ or specific dominant roles based on loss [S1, unofficial].
2.  **Operation Selection:** Use $\tau_s$ and role to choose cloning (small scale, same center) or splitting (large scale, sampled from covariance) [S1, unofficial].
3.  **Split Placement:** Use the directional evidence from $R_{vB,k}$ or perceptual gradients to initialize child positions, rather than random sampling [S1, unofficial].

import numpy as np

def sigmoid(x):
    return 1.0 / (1.0 + np.exp(-x))

def clip(x, a, b):
    return np.clip(x, a, b)

def robust_normalize(response, q_low=0.01, q_high=0.99):
    flat = response.flatten()
    low_q = np.quantile(flat, q_low)
    high_q = np.quantile(flat, q_high)
    denom = high_q - low_q + 1e-6
    return clip((response - low_q) / denom, 0, 1)

def compute_perceptual_map(image, vgg_features, layers, beta_l):
    total_num = np.zeros_like(image[:, :, 0])
    total_den = np.zeros_like(image[:, :, 0])
    for l in layers:
        feat = vgg_features[l]
        dH, dW = np.gradient(feat, axis=(0, 1))
        grad_sq = dH**2 + dW**2
        num = np.sum(grad_sq, axis=2)
        den = np.sum(grad_sq, axis=2)
        total_num += beta_l[l] * num
        total_den += beta_l[l] * den
    ratio = total_num / (total_den + 1e-6)
    return robust_normalize(ratio)

def compute_structural_evidence(Y_v, Z_kv, neighbors, tau_z, sigma_z, sigma_h, lambda_e, lambda_d, m_min):
    grad_Y = np.gradient(Y_v, axis=(0, 1))
    E_v = robust_normalize(np.sqrt(grad_Y[0]**2 + grad_Y[1]**2))
    grad_Z = np.gradient(np.log(Z_kv + 1e-6), axis=(0, 1))
    D_kv = robust_normalize(np.sqrt(grad_Z[0]**2 + grad_Z[1]**2))
    H_kv = lambda_e * E_v + lambda_d * D_kv

    M_kv = np.zeros_like(H_kv)
    chi_sum = np.zeros_like(H_kv)
    for v_prime in neighbors:
        delta_z, chi, H_prime = reprojection_step(Z_kv, v_prime)
        exp_z = np.exp(-delta_z / sigma_z)
        exp_h = np.exp(-np.abs(H_kv - H_prime) / sigma_h)
        a_vv = chi * exp_z * exp_h
        M_kv += a_vv
        chi_sum += chi
    M_kv = M_kv / (chi_sum + 1e-6)
    B_kv = robust_normalize(H_kv * (m_min + (1 - m_min) * M_kv))

    g_E = np.stack([grad_Y[0], grad_Y[1]], axis=2)
    g_D = np.stack([grad_Z[0], grad_Z[1]], axis=2)
    norm_E = np.sqrt(np.sum(g_E**2, axis=2, keepdims=True)) + 1e-6
    norm_D = np.sqrt(np.sum(g_D**2, axis=2, keepdims=True)) + 1e-6
    g_E_norm = g_E / norm_E
    g_D_norm = g_D / norm_D
    outer_E = g_E_norm[:, :, :, None] * g_E_norm[:, :, None, :]
    outer_D = g_D_norm[:, :, :, None] * g_D_norm[:, :, None, :]
    R_vB = (m_min + (1 - m_min) * M_kv[:, :, None, None]) * (lambda_e * outer_E + lambda_d * outer_D)
    return B_kv, R_vB

def aggregate_evidence(gaussians, perceptual_maps, structural_maps, neighbors, tau_C, K_vis, sigma_X, eps):
    raw_A = {}
    raw_B = {}
    for g in gaussians:
        omega = g.omega_per_view
        nu_iv = np.zeros(len(neighbors))
        for idx, v in enumerate(neighbors):
            C_v = np.sum(omega[v])
            nu_iv[idx] = min(1.0, C_v / tau_C) * np.sum(omega[v]) / (np.sum(list(omega.values())) + eps)
        x_vals_A = []
        x_vals_B = []
        for v in neighbors:
            if v in perceptual_maps:
                x_vals_A.append(perceptual_maps[v][g.proj_u, g.proj_v])
            if v in structural_maps:
                x_vals_B.append(structural_maps[v][g.proj_u, g.proj_v])
        if not x_vals_A:
            raw_A[g.id] = 0.0
            raw_B[g.id] = 0.0
            continue
        mu_A = np.mean(x_vals_A)
        V_A = np.mean((np.array(x_vals_A) - mu_A)**2)
        mu_B = np.mean(x_vals_B) if x_vals_B else 0.0
        V_B = np.mean((np.array(x_vals_B) - mu_B)**2) if x_vals_B else 0.0
        support_factor = min(1.0, np.sum(nu_iv) / K_vis)
        agreement_factor_A = np.exp(-V_A / (2 * (sigma_X + eps)))
        agreement_factor_B = np.exp(-V_B / (2 * (sigma_X + eps)))
        raw_A[g.id] = support_factor * agreement_factor_A * mu_A
        raw_B[g.id] = support_factor * agreement_factor_B * mu_B
    return raw_A, raw_B

def update_quantiles(raw_scores, prev_low, prev_high, beta_q, p=0.5):
    vals = list(raw_scores.values())
    if not vals:
        return prev_low, prev_high
    curr_low = np.quantile(vals, p)
    curr_high = np.quantile(vals, 1 - p)
    new_low = beta_q * prev_low + (1 - beta_q) * curr_low
    new_high = beta_q * prev_high + (1 - beta_q) * curr_high
    return new_low, new_high

def pscd_densification(G, training_views, depth_maps, iteration, tau_g, tau_s, lambda_e, lambda_d, tau_z, m_min, tau_C, K_vis, sigma_X, beta_q, eps_cal, sigma_z, sigma_h, a_P, tau_P, a_Q, tau_Q):
    eligible = []
    for g in G:
        if hasattr(g, 'visible_steps') and len(g.visible_steps) > 0:
            grad_sq_sum = sum(np.linalg.norm(g.grad_2D[t])**2 for t in g.visible_steps)
            g.avg_grad = grad_sq_sum / (len(g.visible_steps) + 1e-6)
            g.max_scale = np.max(g.scale)
            if g.avg_grad > tau_g:
                eligible.append(g)
    if not eligible:
        return G

    perceptual_maps = {}
    structural_maps = {}
    structural_tensors = {}

    for v in training_views:
        feats = extract_vgg16_features(v.image)
        perceptual_maps[v.id] = compute_perceptual_map(v.image, feats, layers=L, beta_l=beta_l)
        Y_v = 0.299*v.image[:,:,0] + 0.587*v.image[:,:,1] + 0.114*v.image[:,:,2]
        Z_kv = depth_maps[v.id]
        B_kv, R_vB = compute_structural_evidence(Y_v, Z_kv, v.neighbors, tau_z, sigma_z, sigma_h, lambda_e, lambda_d, m_min)
        structural_maps[v.id] = B_kv
        structural_tensors[v.id] = R_vB

    raw_A, raw_B = aggregate_evidence(eligible, perceptual_maps, structural_maps, training_views, tau_C, K_vis, sigma_X, 1e-6)

    q_A_low, q_A_high = update_quantiles(raw_A, q_A_prev_low, q_A_prev_high, beta_q)
    q_B_low, q_B_high = update_quantiles(raw_B, q_B_prev_low, q_B_prev_high, beta_q)

    scores_P = {}
    scores_Q = {}
    for g in eligible:
        denom_A = q_A_high - q_A_low + eps_cal
        scores_P[g.id] = clip((raw_A[g.id] - q_A_low) / denom_A, 0, 1) if denom_A > 1e-6 else 0.0
        denom_B = q_B_high - q_B_low + eps_cal
        scores_Q[g.id] = clip((raw_B[g.id] - q_B_low) / denom_B, 0, 1) if denom_B > 1e-6 else 0.0

    new_gaussians = []
    for g in eligible:
        m_P = sigmoid(a_P * (scores_P[g.id] - tau_P))
        m_Q = sigmoid(a_Q * (scores_Q[g.id] - tau_Q))

            new_gaussians.append(child)
        else:

            children = split_gaussian(g, direction=direction)
            new_gaussians.extend(children)

## Operating characteristics
*   **Complexity:** Adds overhead of VGG-16 feature extraction and cross-view reprojection for structural reliability at each densification event [S1, unofficial]. VGG-16 is frozen and efficient [S1, unofficial]. Cross-view pooling is $O(N \cdot |N(v)|)$ [S1, unofficial].
*   **Memory:** Requires storing evidence maps and tensors per view during densification events [S1, unofficial]. Quantile states are minimal [S1, unofficial].
*   **Performance:** Improves reconstruction quality in texture-rich and geometrically complex regions by providing more targeted densification signals than gradient magnitude alone [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Handles regions with high texture but low geometry (perception-dominant) and vice versa (structure-dominant) differently [S1, unofficial]. Prevents artificial evidence from constant fields via calibration guards [S1, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** Requires depth maps for structural cues [S1, unofficial]. Sensitive to depth accuracy [S1, unofficial]. More hyperparameters than baseline 3DGS ($\lambda_e, \lambda_d, m_{min}, \sigma_X$, etc.) [S1, unofficial].

## Background
PSCD-GS was introduced to address the limitations of single-score densification in 3D Gaussian Splatting, where gradients and scales alone cannot distinguish between appearance and geometry needs [S1, unofficial]. It builds on the baseline adaptive density control of 3DGS [Parent] and integrates concepts from perceptual loss (LPIPS/VGG) and multi-view geometry [S1, unofficial].

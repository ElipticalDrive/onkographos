# Scaffold-GS

Also called: Scaffold-Gaussian Splatting, Structured 3D Gaussians, Structured 3D Gaussians for View-Adaptive Rendering

## Overview
Scaffold-GS is a Gaussian-based scene representation that utilizes anchor points to establish a hierarchical and region-aware 3D scene structure, predicting neural Gaussian attributes on-the-fly based on viewing direction and distance [S2]. Unlike vanilla 3D-GS, which allows Gaussians to freely drift and split, Scaffold-GS exploits scene structure to guide and constrain Gaussian distribution while allowing local adaptation to varying viewing angles [S2]. It achieves comparable or superior reconstruction quality to 3D-GS with significantly reduced storage requirements (4.4x–10.2x reduction) and real-time rendering speeds (~100 FPS at 1K resolution) [S2].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Input:** Sparse Structure-from-Motion (SfM) point cloud $P \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times 3}$ and a set of camera poses.
*   **Output:** A set of anchor points with learnable features, and a rendering pipeline that dynamically spawns and rasterizes "neural Gaussians" conditioned on the camera view.

**Data Structures:**
1.  **Voxel Grid:** A sparse grid of voxel centers $V \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times 3}$ derived from $P$.
2.  **Anchor Point $v$:** Each voxel center $v$ is an anchor point equipped with:
    *   Local context feature $f_v \in \mathbb{R}^{32}$.
    *   Scaling factor $l_v \in \mathbb{R}^3$.
    *   $k$ learnable offsets $O_v \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times 3}$.
3.  **Neural Gaussian:** A 3D Gaussian primitive spawned from an anchor, defined by position $\mu$, opacity $\alpha$, covariance (via quaternion $q$ and scale $s$), and color $c$.

**Parameters:**
*   $k$: Number of neural Gaussians spawned per visible anchor. Default: 10 [S2].
*   $\epsilon$: Voxel size for initializing anchors from SfM points [S2].
*   $\epsilon_g$: Base voxel size for gradient-based growing. Default: $64\epsilon$ (or $16\epsilon$ for intricate/texture-less scenes) [S2].
*   $\tau_g$: Gradient threshold for anchor growing. Default: $64\epsilon$ [S2].
*   $N$: Number of training iterations over which gradients/opacities are averaged for growing/pruning. Default: 100 [S2].
*   $\tau_\alpha$: Opacity threshold for rasterization. Only Gaussians with $\alpha \ge \tau_\alpha$ are rasterized [S2].
*   $\lambda_{SSIM}$: Weight for SSIM loss. Value: 0.2 [S2].
*   $\lambda_{vol}$: Weight for volume regularization loss. Value: 0.001 [S2].
*   MLP Architecture: All MLPs ($F_\alpha, F_c, F_q, F_s, F_w$) are 2-layer MLPs with ReLU activation and hidden dimension 32 [S2].

**Method Steps:**

1.  **Anchor Initialization:**
    Voxelize the input SfM point cloud $P$ to create voxel centers $V$:
    $$ V = \{ \lfloor P / \epsilon \rfloor \cdot \epsilon \} $$
    Remove duplicate entries [S2]. For each voxel $v \in V$, initialize an anchor point with $f_v, l_v, O_v$ [S2].

2.  **Neural Gaussian Derivation (On-the-Fly Prediction):**
    For each visible anchor $v$ within the viewing frustum of camera $x_c$:
    a.  **Feature Bank Creation:** Create a multi-resolution feature bank $\{f_v, f_{v\downarrow1}, f_{v\downarrow2}\}$, where $\downarrow n$ denotes $f_v$ down-sampled by $2^n$ factors [S2].
    b.  **View-Dependent Blending:** Calculate relative distance $\delta_{vc} = \|x_v - x_c\|_2$ and viewing direction $d_{vc} = (x_v - x_c) / \|x_v - x_c\|_2$. Predict blending weights using MLP $F_w$:
        $$ \{w, w_1, w_2\} = \text{Softmax}(F_w(\delta_{vc}, d_{vc})) $$
        Compute integrated feature:
        $$ \hat{f}_v = w \cdot f_v + w_1 \cdot f_{v\downarrow1} + w_2 \cdot f_{v\downarrow2} $$
    c.  **Spawn Gaussians:** Spawn $k$ neural Gaussians from anchor $x_v$. Positions are calculated as:
        $$ \{\mu_0, \dots, \mu_{k-1}\} = x_v + \{O_0, \dots, O_{k-1}\} \cdot l_v $$
    d.  **Attribute Decoding:** Decode attributes in one-pass from $\hat{f}_v, \delta_{vc}, d_{vc}$ via MLPs $F_\alpha, F_c, F_q, F_s$. For example, opacity:
        $$ \{\alpha_0, \dots, \alpha_{k-1}\} = F_\alpha(\hat{f}_v, \delta_{vc}, d_{vc}) $$
        Similarly derive colors $\{c_i\}$, quaternions $\{q_i\}$, and scales $\{s_i\}$ [S2].
    e.  **Filtering:** Discard neural Gaussians with $\alpha < \tau_\alpha$ [S2].

3.  **Rasterization:**
    Project the surviving neural Gaussians to 2D ellipses. Sort by depth. Accumulate color via alpha blending:
    $$ C(x') = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{N}} c_i \sigma_i \prod_{j=1}^{i-1} (1 - \sigma_j) $$
    where $\sigma_i = \alpha_i G'_i(x')$ and $G'_i(x')$ is the 2D Gaussian projection [S2].

4.  **Anchor Refinement (Growing and Pruning):**
    Performed every $N$ iterations [S2].
    a.  **Growing:** Quantize neural Gaussians into multi-resolution voxels of size $\epsilon_g^{(m)} = \epsilon_g / 4^{m-1}$ for levels $m \in \{1, 2, 3\}$. Compute averaged gradients $\nabla_g$ over $N$ iterations. If $\nabla_g > \tau_g^{(m)} = \tau_g \cdot 2^{m-1}$, deploy a new anchor at the voxel center if none exists. Apply random elimination to candidates to curb rapid expansion [S2].
    b.  **Pruning:** Accumulate opacity values of neural Gaussians associated with an anchor over $N$ iterations. If accumulated opacity $< 0.5$, remove the anchor [S2].

5.  **Optimization:**
    Minimize total loss:
    $$ L = L_1 + \lambda_{SSIM} L_{SSIM} + \lambda_{vol} L_{vol} $$
    Optimize learnable parameters ($f_v, l_v, O_v$) and MLP weights [S2].

## Subtopics

### [Scaffold-GS Integration](12513-scaffold-gs-integration.md)
Applies DART and AGE modules to the Scaffold-GS backbone. It augments the anchor-seeding SfM cloud before optimization and gates primitive growth during it, without modifying the rendering path or the optimizer. This approach is distinct from representation-level changes (like 3D-HGS) by operating as a backbone-agnostic add-on that specifically targets sparse initialization regions.

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Rendering speed is around 100 FPS at 1K resolution, comparable to original 3D-GS with little computational overhead [S2]. On-the-fly prediction is limited to anchors within the view frustum [S2].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Storage requirements are significantly reduced. Only anchor points and MLP predictors are stored per scene. Reduction is 4.4x–10.2x compared to 3D-GS [S2].
*   **Performance:** Achieves comparable PSNR/SSIM to 3D-GS on Mip-NeRF360 but surpasses SOTA on Tanks&Temples and DeepBlending [S2].
*   **Strengths:** Robust to view changes due to view-dependent attribute prediction; compact storage; real-time rendering; handles noisy initialization via growing/pruning [S2].
*   **Limitations:** Relies on initial SfM points for anchor placement; MLP inference adds some overhead compared to direct attribute lookup, though mitigated by frustum culling and opacity filtering [S2].

## Background
Scaffold-GS was introduced by Tao Lu, Mulin Yu, Linning Xu, Yuanbo Xiangli, Limin Wang, Dahua Lin, and Bo Dai from Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Nanjing University, and Cornell University [S2]. It was presented at CVPR 2024 (arXiv:2312.00109v1, Nov 2023) [S2]. The method builds on the differentiable rendering framework of 3D Gaussian Splatting [Parent](3761-3d-gaussian-splatting-training.md) and addresses limitations of vanilla 3D-GS regarding free drifting Gaussians and lack of structural guidance [S2].

# Stage II: Align 3DGS with Radiance Field

Also called: Field-wise Feature Error Minimization, Radial Feature Optimization

## Overview
This method is the second stage of the iVR-GS training pipeline, designed to minimize the discrepancy between the rendering of deformed 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) and the rendering of the deformed radiance field defined by radial features [S1]. While Stage I establishes the initial deformation, Stage II finetunes the Gaussian configuration (position, rotation, scaling, spherical harmonics, and opacity) to ensure the deformed Gaussians faithfully capture the field-wise deformation [S1]. It distinguishes itself from standard 3DGS optimization by explicitly aligning the Gaussian splats with a ground-truth radiance field derived from an embedded deformation graph, rather than just matching pixel colors [S1].

## How it works

### Inputs and Outputs
**Inputs:**
*   A set of $n$ Gaussians $\{G_i\}$ with parameters $\{p_i, r_i, s_i, H_i, \alpha_i\}$ at rest shape [S1].
*   A deformation graph defined by control points (selected via Furthest Point Sampling from Gaussian centers) and KNN edge connections [S1].
*   A rest-shape radiance field representation [S1].
*   Hyperparameters: $\lambda_{lpf}$ (low-pass filter bandwidth), $n_{dim}$ (sample points per axis), $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$ (error weights) [S1].

**Outputs:**
*   Optimized Gaussian parameters $\{p_i, r_i, s_i, H_i, \alpha_i\}$ that minimize the total error $E_{total}$ [S1].

### Data Structures
*   **Gaussian $i$**:
    *   $p_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$: Position [S1].
    *   $r_i \in \mathbb{R}^4$: Rotation (quaternion) [S1].
    *   $s_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$: Scaling [S1].
    *   $H_i$: Spherical Harmonics coefficients [S1].
    *   $\alpha_i$: Opacity (raw scalar) [S1].
    *   $\Sigma_i \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$: Covariance matrix, computed from $r_i, s_i$ [S1].
*   **Sample Points**:
    *   Set $\{x_j\}$ uniformly distributed within the Axis-Aligned Bounding Boxes (AABB) of Gaussians at rest shape [S1].
    *   Adjacency list mapping each sample point to its nearest control points on the deformation graph (computed via KNN) [S1].
*   **Spatial Hash**:
    *   Used to map Gaussians to grid cells for fast updates of spatial relations between Gaussians and sample points during optimization [S1].

### Formulas
The radiance field is characterized by two radial features: Feature $F$ (encoding rotated harmonics color) and Opacity $O$ (indicating transparency) [S1]:

$$
F(p) = \sum_{i=1}^n \alpha_i G_i(p - p_i) \cdot H_i \quad (7)
$$

$$
O(p) = \sum_{i=1}^n \alpha_i G_i(p - p_i) \quad (8)
$$

where $G_i(x)$ is the Gaussian kernel [S1]:

$$
G_i(x) = \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2} x^\top \Sigma_i^{-1} x\right) \quad (8)
$$

The ground truth radial features $F^*$ and $O^*$ are computed at sample points $x_j$ after applying embedded deformation [S1]. The deformation of a point $v_i$ to $\tilde{v}_i$ is given by [S1]:

$$
\tilde{v}_i = \sum_j w_j(v_i) \left( A_j (v_i - g_j) + g_j \right) + t_j \quad (5)
$$

where $w_j(v_i)$ is the weight from the $j$-th control node, $g_j$ is the control point position, $A_j$ is the affine transformation, and $t_j$ is translation [S1]. The deformation gradient tensor is used to compute $F^*$ and $O^*$ at the new positions [S1].

The total error to minimize is [S1]:

$$
E_{total} = E_{feature} + \lambda_1 E_{opacity} + \lambda_2 E_{boundary} \quad (14)
$$

Where:
*   $E_{feature} \approx \sum_{j=1}^m |F^*(x_j) - F(x_j)|$ (9, 11) [S1]
*   $E_{opacity} \approx \sum_{j=1}^m |O^*(x_j) - O(x_j)|$ (10, 12) [S1]
*   $E_{boundary} = \sum_{x_j \in \mathcal{O}} |O^*(x_j) - O(x_j)|$ (13) [S1]
*   $\mathcal{O} = \{ x_j \mid O^*(x_j) = 0 \}$ denotes sample points in unoccupied space [S1].

The adaptive anisotropic low-pass filter adjusts the covariance matrix $\Sigma_i$ to handle aliasing during deformation [S1]:

$$
\Sigma_i = R_i S_i S_i^\top R_i^\top + \frac{\lambda_{lpf}}{n_{dim}^2} A_k A_k^\top \quad (15)
$$

where $A_k$ is a $3 \times 3$ matrix estimating the first-order approximation of the deformation field near the grid cell center $k$, and $n_{dim}$ is the number of sample points per axis [S1].

### Parameters
*   $\lambda_{lpf}$: Controls the passing bandwidth of the low-pass filter. Typical value: 0.2 [S1].
*   $n_{dim}$: Number of sample points per axis in each grid cell [S1].
*   $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$: Weights for opacity and boundary errors. Not given in the sources.
*   $w_{rot}, w_{reg}, w_{con}$: Weights for the deformation graph optimization (Stage I). Values: 1, 10, 100 respectively [S1].

### Pseudocode

```python
def stage_2_align_3dgs_with_radiance_field(gaussians, deformation_graph, hyperparams):
    """
    Finetunes Gaussian configuration to align with deformed radiance field.
    
    Args:
        gaussians: List of Gaussian objects with attributes p, r, s, H, alpha_raw.
        deformation_graph: Object containing control_points (list of 3D vectors) and edge connections.
        hyperparams: Dict with keys lambda_lpf, n_dim, lambda_1, lambda_2, max_iterations.
    
    Returns:
        Optimized gaussians.
    """
    # 1. Initialization and Sampling
    aabbs = [compute_aabb(g.p, g.s) for g in gaussians]
    sample_points = []
    for aabb in aabbs:
        sample_points.extend(uniform_grid_sample(aabb, hyperparams['n_dim']))
        
    # KNN adjacency to control points
    control_points = deformation_graph.control_points
    sample_point_adjacency = compute_knn(sample_points, control_points, k=4)
    
    # Spatial hash for fast updates
    spatial_hash = build_spatial_hash(gaussians, cell_size=hyperparams['n_dim'])
    
    # Pre-compute rest-shape features (baseline)
    F_rest = compute_radial_features(gaussians, sample_points)
    O_rest = compute_opacity_features(gaussians, sample_points)
    
    optimizer = AdamOptimizer(gaussians.parameters(), lr=0.001)
    
    for iteration in range(hyperparams['max_iterations']):
        optimizer.zero_grad()
        
        # A. Ground Truth Features (F*, O*)
        deformed_points = []
        for x_j in sample_points:
            weights = compute_deformation_weights(x_j, sample_point_adjacency)
            deformed_pos = compute_embedded_deformation(x_j, weights, deformation_graph)
            deformed_points.append(deformed_pos)
            
        # Compute F* and O* at deformed positions using deformation gradient tensor
        F_star, O_star = compute_ground_truth_features(deformed_points, deformation_graph, hyperparams['n_dim'])
        
        # B. Deformed Gaussian Features (F, O)
        F_gauss = compute_radial_features(gaussians, deformed_points)
        O_gauss = compute_opacity_features(gaussians, deformed_points)
        
        # C. Errors
        E_feature = sum(abs(F_star[j] - F_gauss[j]) for j in range(len(sample_points)))
        E_opacity = sum(abs(O_star[j] - O_gauss[j]) for j in range(len(sample_points)))
        
        unoccupied_indices = [j for j in range(len(sample_points)) if O_star[j] == 0]
        E_boundary = sum(abs(O_star[j] - O_gauss[j]) for j in unoccupied_indices)
        
        E_total = E_feature + hyperparams['lambda_1'] * E_opacity + hyperparams['lambda_2'] * E_boundary
        
        # D. Adaptive Anisotropic Low-Pass Filter
        for g in gaussians:
            grid_cell = spatial_hash.get_cell(g.p)
            A_k = compute_deformation_gradient_matrix(grid_cell, deformation_graph)
            R = quaternion_to_rotation_matrix(g.r)
            S = scale_to_matrix(g.s)
            Sigma_base = R @ S @ S.T @ R.T
            Sigma_filtered = Sigma_base + (hyperparams['lambda_lpf'] / (hyperparams['n_dim'] ** 2)) * (A_k @ A_k.T)
            g.Sigma = Sigma_filtered
        
        # E. Backpropagation and Update
        E_total.backward()
        for g in gaussians:
            g.alpha = sigmoid(g.alpha_raw)
            g.s = exp(g.s_raw)
        optimizer.step()
        
    return gaussians

# Helper functions (self-contained)
def compute_aabb(center, scale):
    return (center - scale, center + scale)

def uniform_grid_sample(aabb, n_dim):
    points = []
    for i in range(n_dim):
        for j in range(n_dim):
            for k in range(n_dim):
                x = aabb[0][0] + (aabb[1][0] - aabb[0][0]) * (i + 0.5) / n_dim
                y = aabb[0][1] + (aabb[1][1] - aabb[0][1]) * (j + 0.5) / n_dim
                z = aabb[0][2] + (aabb[1][2] - aabb[0][2]) * (k + 0.5) / n_dim
                points.append([x, y, z])
    return points

def compute_knn(query_points, reference_points, k):
    adj = []
    for q in query_points:
        dists = [sum((q[i] - r[i])**2 for i in range(3)) for r in reference_points]
        indices = sorted(range(len(dists)), key=lambda i: dists[i])[:k]
        adj.append(indices)
    return adj

def build_spatial_hash(gaussians, cell_size):
    grid = {}
    for i, g in enumerate(gaussians):
        cx, cy, cz = g.p
        gx, gy, gz = int(cx / cell_size), int(cy / cell_size), int(cz / cell_size)
        grid[(gx, gy, gz)] = grid.get((gx, gy, gz), []) + [i]
    return grid

def compute_deformation_weights(x_j, adjacency):
    weights = []
    for idx in adjacency:
        dist_sq = sum((x_j[i] - deformation_graph.control_points[idx][i])**2 for i in range(3))
        w = 1.0 / (dist_sq + 1e-8)
        weights.append(w)
    total = sum(weights)
    return [w / total for w in weights]

def compute_embedded_deformation(x_j, weights, graph):
    pos = [0.0, 0.0, 0.0]
    for idx, w in enumerate(weights):
        cp = graph.control_points[idx]
        Aj = graph.affine_matrices[idx]
        tj = graph.translations[idx]
        diff = [x_j[i] - cp[i] for i in range(3)]
        transformed = [sum(Aj[i][k] * diff[k] for k in range(3)) + cp[i] + tj[i] for i in range(3)]
        pos = [pos[i] + w * transformed[i] for i in range(3)]
    return pos

def compute_ground_truth_features(deformed_points, graph, n_dim):
    F_star = []
    O_star = []
    for x in deformed_points:
        cell = (int(x[0]/n_dim), int(x[1]/n_dim), int(x[2]/n_dim))
        A_k = compute_deformation_gradient_matrix(cell, graph)
        # Transform rest-shape features using deformation gradient [G]
        F_star.append(A_k @ [1.0, 0.0, 0.0]) # Simplified harmonic transform
        O_star.append(0.0) # Placeholder for opacity ground truth computation
    return F_star, O_star

def compute_radial_features(gaussians, points):
    features = []
    for p in points:
        val = 0.0
        for g in gaussians:
            diff = [p[i] - g.p[i] for i in range(3)]
            inv_cov = g.Sigma_inv
            exponent = -0.5 * sum(diff[i] * sum(inv_cov[i][k] * diff[k] for k in range(3)) for i in range(3))
            val += g.alpha * exp(exponent) * sum(g.H)
        features.append(val)
    return features

def compute_opacity_features(gaussians, points):
    opacities = []
    for p in points:
        val = 0.0
        for g in gaussians:
            diff = [p[i] - g.p[i] for i in range(3)]
            inv_cov = g.Sigma_inv
            exponent = -0.5 * sum(diff[i] * sum(inv_cov[i][k] * diff[k] for k in range(3)) for i in range(3))
            val += g.alpha * exp(exponent)
        opacities.append(val)
    return opacities

def compute_deformation_gradient_matrix(cell, graph):
    # Finite difference approximation of deformation field over grid cell [G]
    h = 1.0 / graph.n_dim
    J = [[0.0]*3 for _ in range(3)]
    base = [cell[0]*h, cell[1]*h, cell[2]*h]
    for d in range(3):
        offset = base[:]
        offset[d] += h
        deformed_offset = compute_embedded_deformation(offset, compute_knn([offset], graph.control_points, 4), graph)
        J[d] = [(deformed_offset[i] - base[i]) / h for i in range(3)]
    return J

def quaternion_to_rotation_matrix(q):
    x, y, z, w = q
    return [[1-2*(y**2+z**2), 2*(x*y-z*w), 2*(x*z+y*w)],
            [2*(x*y+z*w), 1-2*(x**2+z**2), 2*(y*z-x*w)],
            [2*(x*z-y*w), 2*(y*z+x*w), 1-2*(x**2+y**2)]]

def scale_to_matrix(s):
    return [[s[0], 0, 0], [0, s[1], 0], [0, 0, s[2]]]

class AdamOptimizer:
    def __init__(self, params, lr=0.001):
        self.params = params
        self.lr = lr
        self.m = [0.0] * len(params)
        self.v = [0.0] * len(params)
        self.t = 0
    def zero_grad(self):
        for p in self.params: p.grad = 0.0
    def step(self):
        self.t += 1
        beta1, beta2, eps = 0.9, 0.999, 1e-8
        for i, p in enumerate(self.params):
            self.m[i] = beta1 * self.m[i] + (1 - beta1) * p.grad
            self.v[i] = beta2 * self.v[i] + (1 - beta2) * p.grad**2
            m_hat = self.m[i] / (1 - beta1**self.t)
            v_hat = self.v[i] / (1 - beta2**self.t)
            p.raw -= self.lr * m_hat / (v_hat**0.5 + eps)
```

### Edge Cases and Pitfalls
*   **Boundary Deformation**: The deformation graph is agnostic to model boundaries, potentially deforming unoccupied space [S1]. This is handled by the $E_{boundary}$ term, which restricts optimization influence to sample points where $O^*(x_j) = 0$ [S1].
*   **Aliasing**: Undersampling high-frequency Gaussians during deformation causes aliasing [S1]. This is handled by an adaptive anisotropic low-pass filter applied to the covariance matrix $\Sigma_i$ to adjust Gaussian size based on the deformation gradient [S1].
*   **Filter Sensitivity**: An over-conservative $\lambda_{lpf}$ (e.g., 0.02) leads to under-sampling, while an over-aggressive value (e.g., 2.0) causes blurriness [S1]. A value of 0.2 is empirically found to be robust [S1].

## Operating characteristics

### Time and Memory Complexity
*   **Time**: The method requires computing KNN for sample points and updating spatial hashes at each optimization iteration [S1]. The computation of radial features $F$ and $O$ scales with the number of Gaussians $n$ and sample points $m$ [S1]. The use of spatial hashing accelerates the update of spatial relations [S1].
*   **Memory**: Requires storing sample points, their adjacency to control points, and the spatial hash structure [S1]. The number of sample points is proportional to the number of Gaussians [S1].

### Measured Performance
*   Implemented on a single NVIDIA RTX 4090 24GB GPU using C++ and CUDA [S1].
*   The method is validated on synthetic and real-world data (e.g., Stripes, Glove, Pinocchio, Bunny, Shoe, Cup) [S1].
*   The results are not sensitive to $\lambda_{lpf}$ within a reasonable range [S1].

### Strengths and Limitations
*   **Strengths**: Faithfully captures field-wise deformation; handles aliasing adaptively; prevents deformation of unoccupied space [S1].
*   **Limitations**: Requires pre-computation of features and KNN adjacency; adds computational overhead to the training process [S1].

## Background

### Origin
The method originates from the paper "As-Rigid-As-Possible Deformation of Gaussian Radiance Fields" [S1]. It was developed to address the issue where rasterizing deformed 3DGS does not always align with the rendering obtained from the deformed radiance field [S1].

### History and Development
The method builds upon previous work in 3DGS [9], EWA-Splatting [42], and Mip-Splatting [20], adapting their filtering strategies to the dynamic deformation context [S1]. It utilizes embedded deformation techniques similar to those in [16] and [36] [S1].

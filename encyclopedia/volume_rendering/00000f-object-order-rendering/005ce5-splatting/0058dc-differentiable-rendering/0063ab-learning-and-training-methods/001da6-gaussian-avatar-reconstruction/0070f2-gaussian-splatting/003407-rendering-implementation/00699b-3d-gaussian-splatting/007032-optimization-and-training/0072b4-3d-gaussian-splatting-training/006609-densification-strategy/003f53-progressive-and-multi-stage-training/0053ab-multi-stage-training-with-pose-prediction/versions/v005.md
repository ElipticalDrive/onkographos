# Multi-stage Training with Pose Prediction

Also called: FineSplat training phases, Training and Inference Protocol, Two-stage Training with Pose-free Inference

## Overview
Multi-stage training with pose prediction addresses the instability of jointly optimizing 3D Gaussian geometry and camera poses in feed-forward neural rendering. By decoupling the learning of scene representation from pose estimation, it prevents error propagation between the two domains. It differs from sibling methods like [Hybrid Coarse-to-Fine Strategy](7173-hybrid-coarse-to-fine-strategy.md) by explicitly freezing pose-sensitive components during intermediate stages, and from [Free-View Training (FVT)](15162-free-view-training-fvt.md) by using a structured, iterative refinement of Gaussian attributes rather than a single end-to-end pass.

## How it works
The method implements a progressive training protocol, primarily detailed in FineSplat [S1, unofficial] and InfiniSplat v2 [S2, unofficial], which divides optimization into distinct phases to stabilize convergence.

### Inputs and Outputs
*   **Inputs:** A set of input views $\{I_v\}$ with known or predicted intrinsics and poses. Ground truth target views $\{I_{target}\}$ are used for supervision.
*   **Outputs:** A set of 3D Gaussians $\mathcal{G}$ and, in later stages, predicted camera poses for target views.

### Data Structures
The method operates on the standard 3D Gaussian primitive set $\mathcal{G} = \{g_1, \dots, g_N\}$ defined in [Densification Strategy](6234-densification-strategy.md).
*   **Gaussian Attributes:** Position $\mathbf{p}$, covariance $\mathbf{\Sigma}$ (or scale/rotation), opacity $\alpha$, and spherical harmonics coefficients $\mathbf{c}$.
*   **Camera Parameters:** Intrinsics $\mathbf{K}$ and extrinsics $\mathbf{T}$ (rotation $\mathbf{R}$, translation $\mathbf{t}$).
*   **Scene Normalization:** Translations are normalized by a scale factor $s_{norm}$. In FineSplat, $s_{norm}$ is derived from the maximum pairwise distance between context camera centers [S1, unofficial]. In InfiniSplat, it is derived from predicted depths [S2, unofficial].

### Training Stages (FineSplat Protocol)
The FineSplat protocol [S1, unofficial] consists of three stages:

1.  **Stage 1 (Pose-Aware Geometry Initialization):**
    *   Train a pixel-aligned Gaussian prediction network for 120,000 iterations.
    *   Replace the pixel-aligned decoder with a patch-aligned Gaussian decoder.
    *   Fine-tune for 80,000 iterations on 8 GPUs.
    *   **Loss:** Supervise predicted poses and intrinsics using camera losses [S1, unofficial].
    *   **Learning Rate:** $2 \times 10^{-5}$, no warm-up, cosine decay to zero.

2.  **Stage 2 (Pose-Free Attribute Refinement):**
    *   Freeze Stage 1 parameters.
    *   Train Stage 2 using **ground-truth** camera poses and intrinsics. This isolates the learning of Gaussian attributes from pose errors.
    *   The model processes $T=4$ steps. Let $\hat{I}^{(i)}$ be the rendered target view after step $i$.
    *   **Loss:** Averaged over all steps $i=1 \dots T$:
        $$ \mathcal{L} = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{i=1}^{T} \left( \mathcal{L}_2(\hat{I}^{(i)}, I) + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{perc}(\hat{I}^{(i)}, I) \right) $$
        where $\mathcal{L}_2$ is mean squared error, $\mathcal{L}_{perc}$ is VGG-19 perceptual loss, and $\lambda = 0.2$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   Tokens are detached between steps to prevent gradient flow across refinement steps.
    *   **Learning Rate:** $1 \times 10^{-4}$, 200-iteration linear warm-up, constant until 80% of training, cosine decay to zero over the remaining 20%.

3.  **Stage 3 (End-to-End Pose-Free Fine-Tuning):**
    *   Unfreeze all parameters, including the camera pose estimator.
    *   Train in a fully pose-free setting: both context and target cameras are predicted, and no camera loss is applied.
    *   Supervise Stage-1 Gaussians.
    *   **Loss:** Averaged over steps $i=0 \dots T$ (including the initial state):
        $$ \mathcal{L} = \frac{1}{T+1} \sum_{i=0}^{T} \left( \mathcal{L}_2(\hat{I}^{(i)}, I) + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{perc}(\hat{I}^{(i)}, I) \right) $$
    *   **Learning Rate:** $2 \times 10^{-5}$ for all parameters, 200-iteration linear warm-up, cosine decay to zero.

### Training Stages (InfiniSplat v2 Protocol)
The InfiniSplat v2 protocol [S2, unofficial] uses a two-branch architecture (geometry and attribute) with two stages:

1.  **Stage I (Attribute Learning):**
    *   Both branches receive input RGB and **Ground Truth** pose conditions.
    *   Supervise input and sampled target renderings with equally weighted masked L1 and LPIPS losses.
    *   Update only the attribute branch’s last $K$ Transformer blocks and the Gaussian decoder.
    *   The geometry branch (reconstructor) is frozen.

2.  **Stage II (Feature Refinement):**
    *   Freeze the reconstructor (geometry branch).
    *   Train the feature projection and refiner.
    *   Input-view losses provide feedback, while input and disjoint target views supervise the updated scene.

### Inference
*   **Pose-Conditioned:** Use supplied intrinsics and poses. Rescale translations to match normalized predicted depths [S2, unofficial].
*   **Pose-Free:** Use the geometry branch’s predicted cameras. Normalize translations by $s_{norm}$. Render input views for refinement feedback. The refiner updates scene attributes while network weights remain fixed [S2, unofficial].

### Optimization Parameters
*   **Optimizer:** AdamW with $\beta_1 = 0.9$, $\beta_2 = 0.95$, weight decay 0.05 (multi-dimensional parameters only) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Gradient Clipping:** Max norm 1.0 [S1, unofficial].
*   **Precision:** bfloat16 mixed precision [S1, unofficial].
*   **Batching:** Scenes sampled from input view counts $\{6, 12, 24, 32\}$ with corresponding target views and per-GPU batch sizes $\{4, 2, 1, 1\}$ [S1, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### RLSplat Training
RLSplat [S3, unofficial] uses a two-stage training process to introduce a Gaussian allocation policy.
*   **Stage 1 (Base Training):** Train the geometry backbone and decoder with random allocations for 40,000 steps. Sample budgets from $[2048, 16384V]$, where $V$ is the number of input views. This exposes the decoder to diverse allocations.
*   **Stage 2 (Policy Training):** Add a policy head initialized to uniform probabilities. Fine-tune the entire model for 5,000 steps with the policy loss $\mathcal{L}_{policy} = -J_{policy}$. Sample budgets skewed toward the lower end of the range.
*   **Losses:**
    *   $\mathcal{L}_{recon}$: Pixelwise MSE and LPIPS between rendered and ground-truth views.
    *   $\mathcal{L}_{cam}$: Supervises translation, rotation, and FOV of predicted context cameras.
    *   $\mathcal{L}_{proj}$: Reprojects anchor Gaussians toward source image patches, incorporating a cheirality penalty to prevent Gaussians behind the camera.
    *   Total Loss: $\mathcal{L}_{recon} + \mathcal{L}_{cam} + \mathcal{L}_{proj} + \mathcal{L}_{vor} + \mathcal{L}_{offset} + \mathcal{L}_{policy}$ [S3, unofficial].
*   **Parameters:** $K_{anc} = K_{gs} = 16$ [S3, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Training is significantly longer than single-stage methods due to multiple phases. FineSplat requires 210K iterations total (120K + 80K + 10K) [S1, unofficial]. RLSplat requires 45K steps [S3, unofficial].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Requires storing intermediate states for multi-step refinement (e.g., tokens detached between steps in FineSplat) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Stabilizes training by preventing pose errors from corrupting geometry learning. Allows for high-quality pose-free inference by gradually introducing pose prediction [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** Increased training time and complexity. Requires careful tuning of stage transitions and learning rates.
*   **When to Use:** When joint optimization of pose and geometry is unstable or fails to converge. When high-fidelity novel view synthesis is required with predicted poses.

## Background
Multi-stage training with pose prediction originated in feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting methods to address the difficulty of learning camera poses and scene geometry simultaneously. FineSplat [S1, unofficial] introduced the three-stage protocol to decouple pose estimation from Gaussian refinement. InfiniSplat v2 [S2, unofficial] adapted this into a two-branch architecture with pose-conditioned and pose-free inference modes. RLSplat [S3, unofficial] extended the concept to include resource-linear budget control via a policy network trained in two stages.

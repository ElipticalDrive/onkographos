# GART

Also called: GART, A method that extends the human to more articulated models (e.g, animals) based on the 3DGS representation

## Overview
GART is a method that extends the human to more articulated models (e.g., animals) based on the 3DGS representation [S1][S2, unofficial]. It builds on [Human and Articulated Avatars](8209-human-and-articulated-avatars.md) by generalizing the 3D Gaussian Splatting framework beyond human subjects to other articulated entities.

## Background
GART is cited in recent surveys of 3D Gaussian Splatting as an extension of human modeling techniques to other articulated models [S1][S2, unofficial]. It is referenced alongside other methods such as GPS-Gaussian, Animatable Gaussians, Gaussian Shell Maps, and ASH, which also aim to enhance 3DGS for dynamic or detailed subjects [S1][S2, unofficial].

# Gaussian Splat Rasterization (Vertex Shader)

Also called: 2D Gaussian Splatting Vertex Shader, Fast Gaussian Rasterization Vertex Shader, GSplat Vert, Splat Vertex Shader, dsplat.vert

## Overview
This method implements the vertex shader stage of a GPU-accelerated 3D Gaussian Splatting rasterizer, specifically designed to compute 2D screen-space projections and basis vectors for instanced quad rendering [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial]. It solves the problem of efficiently transforming 3D Gaussian parameters (mean, covariance, opacity) into screen-space ellipses while performing early culling to reduce geometry shader load. It differs from CUDA-based variants by executing these calculations in the vertex shader stage, relying on subsequent geometry or fragment shaders for final pixel accumulation [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].

## How it works
### Inputs and Outputs
**Inputs:**
*   **Attributes:**
    *   `aPos` (vec3): 3D position of the Gaussian center [S1, unofficial].
    *   `aCov0_3` (vec3): First three components of the 6-component symmetric 3D covariance matrix [S1, unofficial].
    *   `aCov3_6` (vec3): Last three components of the 6-component symmetric 3D covariance matrix [S1, unofficial].
    *   `aColor` (vec4): RGBA color/feature vector [S1, unofficial].
    *   `position` (vec2): Instance attribute defining the local quad basis coordinates (typically corners of a unit square or similar) [S2, unofficial].
    *   `index` (uint): Instance attribute identifying the Gaussian splat ID [S2, unofficial].
*   **Uniforms:**
    *   `P` (mat4x4): Projection matrix [S1, unofficial].
    *   `VM` (mat4x4): View-Model matrix [S1, unofficial].
    *   `focal` (vec2): Focal lengths $(f_x, f_y)$ [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].
    *   `principal` (vec2): Principal point $(c_x, c_y)$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   `basisViewport` (vec2): Viewport dimensions $(width, height)$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   `discardAlpha` (float): Alpha threshold for culling. Default: $0.0001$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   `sqrt8` (float): Constant $\sqrt{8}$ for scaling eigenvalues [S1, unofficial].
    *   `u_texture` (usampler2D): Texture storing packed Gaussian data (used in [S2, unofficial] variant) [S2, unofficial].
    *   `projection` (mat4): Projection matrix [S2, unofficial].
    *   `view` (mat4): View matrix [S2, unofficial].
    *   `viewport` (vec2): Viewport dimensions [S2, unofficial].

**Outputs:**
*   `gl_Position` (vec4): Clip-space position of the quad vertices [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].
*   `basisVector0` (vec2): First basis vector for the geometry shader [S1, unofficial].
*   `basisVector1` (vec2): Second basis vector for the geometry shader [S1, unofficial].
*   `gColor` (vec4): Output color/opacity [S1, unofficial].
*   `gDepth` (float): Output depth [S1, unofficial].

### Data Structures
*   **Covariance Storage ([S1, unofficial]):** The 3D covariance matrix $\mathbf{V}_{rk}$ is stored as two `vec3` attributes. The symmetric matrix is reconstructed as:
    $$
    \mathbf{V}_{rk} = \begin{bmatrix}
    aCov0\_3.x & aCov0\_3.y & aCov0\_3.z \\
    aCov0\_3.y & aCov3\_6.x & aCov3\_6.y \\
    aCov0\_3.z & aCov3\_6.y & aCov3\_6.z
    \end{bmatrix}
    $$
*   **Packed Texture Storage ([S2, unofficial]):** Gaussian data is stored in a `usampler2D` texture. Each splat index $i$ occupies two texels:
    *   Center/Covariance Texel: `texelFetch(u_texture, ivec2((uint(index) & 0x3ffu) << 1, uint(index) >> 10), 0)`
    *   Color Texel: `texelFetch(u_texture, ivec2(((uint(index) & 0x3ffu) << 1) | 1u, uint(index) >> 10), 0)`
    *   The low 10 bits of the index determine the column, and the high bits determine the row.

### Steps
1.  **Alpha Culling ([S1, unofficial]):** If `aColor.a < discardAlpha`, set `gColor.a = 0.0` and return immediately to prevent geometry shader emission.
2.  **Data Fetching:**
    *   ([S1, unofficial]) Use attributes directly.
    *   ([S2, unofficial]) Fetch center data from `u_texture` using the instance index. Unpack half-precision covariance components from `cov.x`, `cov.y`, `cov.z` into `vec2` pairs using `unpackHalf2x16`. Reconstruct $\mathbf{V}_{rk}$:
        $$
        \mathbf{V}_{rk} = \begin{bmatrix}
        u1.x & u1.y & u2.x \\
        u1.y & u2.y & u3.x \\
        u2.x & u3.x & u3.y
        \end{bmatrix}
        $$
3.  **Position Transformation:**
    *   ([S1, unofficial]) Compute view space center: `viewCenter = VM * vec4(aPos, 1.0)`. Compute clip space center: `clipCenter = P * vec4(aPos, 1.0)`. Perform perspective division: `clipCenter = clipCenter / clipCenter.w`.
    *   ([S2, unofficial]) Transform center to camera space: `cam = view * vec4(uintBitsToFloat(cen.xyz), 1.0)`. Project to clip space: `pos2d = projection * cam`.
4.  **Frustum Culling:**
    *   ([S1, unofficial]) Compute FOV limits:
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{tan\_fovx} &= 0.5 \times \text{width} / f_x \\
        \text{tan\_fovy} &= 0.5 \times \text{height} / f_y \\
        \text{lim\_x\_pos} &= (\text{width} - c_x) / f_x + 0.3 \times \text{tan\_fov}x \\
        \text{lim\_x\_neg} &= c_x / f_x + 0.3 \times \text{tan\_fov}x \\
        \text{lim\_y\_pos} &= (\text{height} - c_y) / f_y + 0.3 \times \text{tan\_fov}y \\
        \text{lim\_y\_neg} &= c_y / f_y + 0.3 \times \text{tan\_fov}y
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Compute Jacobian parameters: $r_z = 1.0/z$, $r_{z2} = r_z^2$.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        t_x &= z \times \min(\text{lim\_x\_pos}, \max(-\text{lim\_x\_neg}, x \times r_z)) \\
        t_y &= z \times \min(\text{lim\_y\_pos}, \max(-\text{lim\_y\_neg}, y \times r_z))
        \end{aligned}
        $$
    *   ([S2, unofficial]) Perform frustum culling with a 1.2x margin. If `pos2d.z < -pos2d.w || pos2d.z > pos2d.w || pos2d.x < -clip || pos2d.x > clip || pos2d.y < -clip || pos2d.y > clip` (where `clip = 1.2 * pos2d.w`), discard the splat by setting `gl_Position = vec4(0.0, 0.0, 2.0, 1.0)`.
5.  **Jacobian Construction:**
    *   ([S1, unofficial]) Construct Jacobian matrix $\mathbf{J}$:
        $$
        \mathbf{J} = \begin{bmatrix}
        f_x r_z & 0 & -f_x t_x r_{z2} \\
        0 & f_y r_z & -f_y t_y r_{z2} \\
        0 & 0 & 0
        \end{bmatrix}
        $$
    *   ([S2, unofficial]) Construct Jacobian matrix $\mathbf{J}$:
        $$
        \mathbf{J} = \begin{bmatrix}
        f_x / z & 0 & -f_x x / z^2 \\
        0 & f_y / z & -f_y y / z^2 \\
        0 & 0 & 0
        \end{bmatrix}
        $$
6.  **2D Covariance Transformation:**
    *   ([S1, unofficial]) Compute $\mathbf{W} = \text{transpose}(\text{mat3}(\mathbf{VM}))$. Compute $\mathbf{T} = \mathbf{W} \times \mathbf{J}$. Compute 2D covariance $\mathbf{cov}_{2Dm} = \text{transpose}(\mathbf{T}) \times \mathbf{V}_{rk} \times \mathbf{T}$. Add blur term: $\mathbf{cov}_{2Dm}[0][0] += 0.3$, $\mathbf{cov}_{2Dm}[1][1] += 0.3$.
    *   ([S2, unofficial]) Compute $\mathbf{T} = \text{transpose}(\text{mat3}(\text{view})) \times \mathbf{J}$. Compute $\mathbf{cov}_{2d} = \text{transpose}(\mathbf{T}) \times \mathbf{V}_{rk} \times \mathbf{T}$. Add low-pass filter: $\mathbf{cov}_{2d}[0][0] += 0.1$, $\mathbf{cov}_{2d}[1][1] += 0.1$.
7.  **Eigenvalue Decomposition:**
    *   ([S1, unofficial]) Extract upper-left 2x2 components into `cov2Dv`: `cov2Dv.x = cov2Dm[0][0]`, `cov2Dv.y = cov2Dm[0][1]`, `cov2Dv.z = cov2Dm[1][1]`. Let $a=\text{cov2Dv}.x$, $d=\text{cov2Dv}.z$, $b=\text{cov2Dv}.y$.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        D &= a \times d - b^2 \\
        \text{trace} &= a + d \\
        \text{traceOver2} &= 0.5 \times \text{trace} \\
        \text{term2} &= \sqrt{\max(0.1, \text{traceOver2}^2 - D)} \\
        \text{eigenValue0} &= \text{traceOver2} + \text{term2} \\
        \text{eigenValue1} &= \text{traceOver2} - \text{term2}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
    *   ([S2, unofficial]) Compute eigenvalues of the 2x2 upper-left submatrix of $\mathbf{cov}_{2d}$:
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{mid} &= (\mathbf{cov}_{2d}[0][0] + \mathbf{cov}_{2d}[1][1]) / 2.0 \\
        \text{radius} &= \text{length}(\text{vec2}((\mathbf{cov}_{2d}[0][0] - \mathbf{cov}_{2d}[1][1]) / 2.0, \mathbf{cov}_{2d}[0][1])) \\
        \lambda_1 &= \text{mid} + \text{radius} \\
        \lambda_2 &= \text{mid} - \text{radius}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
8.  **Basis Vector Calculation:**
    *   ([S1, unofficial]) Scale eigenvalues by $\sqrt{8}$ to determine the rendering basis.
    *   ([S2, unofficial]) Compute major axis direction: `diagonalVector = normalize(vec2(cov2d[0][1], lambda1 - cov2d[0][0]))`. Compute major axis vector: `majorAxis = scale * min(sqrt(2.0 * lambda1), 1024.0) * diagonalVector` where `scale = 2.5`. Compute minor axis vector: `minorAxis = scale * min(sqrt(2.0 * lambda2), 1024.0) * vec2(diagonalVector.y, -diagonalVector.x)`.
9.  **Final Position and Output:**
    *   ([S1, unofficial]) Pass through color and depth.
    *   ([S2, unofficial]) Unpack color from `cov.w`. Compute final screen position: `vCenter = vec2(pos2d) / pos2d.w`. `gl_Position = vec4(vCenter + position.x * majorAxis / viewport + position.y * minorAxis / viewport, pos2d.z / pos2d.w, 1.0)`.

### Pseudocode
```python
def vertex_shader(aPos, aCov0_3, aCov3_6, aColor, position, index, 
                  P, VM, focal, principal, basisViewport, discardAlpha, sqrt8):
    # 1. Alpha Culling
    if aColor.a < discardAlpha:
        return (vec4(0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0), vec2(0.0, 0.0), vec2(0.0, 0.0), aColor, 0.0)

    # 2. Reconstruct 3D Covariance
    Vrk = mat3(
        aCov0_3.x, aCov0_3.y, aCov0_3.z,
        aCov0_3.y, aCov3_6.x, aCov3_6.y,
        aCov0_3.z, aCov3_6.y, aCov3_6.z
    )

    # 3. Position Transformation
    viewCenter = VM * vec4(aPos, 1.0)
    clipCenter = P * vec4(aPos, 1.0)
    clipCenter = clipCenter / clipCenter.w
    
    # 4. Jacobian Parameters
    width = 1.0 / basisViewport.x
    height = 1.0 / basisViewport.y
    fx = focal.x
    fy = focal.y
    cx = principal.x
    cy = principal.y
    x = viewCenter.x
    y = viewCenter.y
    z = viewCenter.z
    
    tan_fovx = 0.5 * width / fx
    tan_fovy = 0.5 * height / fy
    
    lim_x_pos = (width - cx) / fx + 0.3 * tan_fovx
    lim_x_neg = cx / fx + 0.3 * tan_fovx
    lim_y_pos = (height - cy) / fy + 0.3 * tan_fovy
    lim_y_neg = cy / fy + 0.3 * tan_fovy
    
    rz = 1.0 / z
    rz2 = rz * rz
    tx = z * min(lim_x_pos, max(-lim_x_neg, x * rz))
    ty = z * min(lim_y_pos, max(-lim_y_neg, y * rz))
    
    # 5. Jacobian Construction
    J = mat3(
        fx * rz, 0.0, -fx * tx * rz2,
        0.0, fy * rz, -fy * ty * rz2,
        0.0, 0.0, 0.0
    )
    
    # 6. 2D Covariance Transformation
    W = transpose(mat3(VM))
    T = W * J
    cov2Dm = transpose(T) * Vrk * T
    
    # Add blur term
    cov2Dm[0][0] += 0.3
    cov2Dm[1][1] += 0.3
    
    # 7. Eigenvalue Decomposition
    a = cov2Dm[0][0]
    d = cov2Dm[1][1]
    b = cov2Dm[0][1]
    
    D = a * d - b * b
    trace = a + d
    traceOver2 = 0.5 * trace
    term2 = sqrt(max(0.1, traceOver2 * traceOver2 - D))
    
    eigenValue0 = traceOver2 + term2
    eigenValue1 = traceOver2 - term2
    
    # 8. Basis Vector Calculation
    # Scale by sqrt(8) standard deviations
    basisVector0 = vec2(eigenValue0, 0.0) * sqrt8 # Simplified representation
    basisVector1 = vec2(0.0, eigenValue1) * sqrt8 # Simplified representation
    
    # Note: The actual basis vectors passed to geometry shader are derived from 
    # eigenvalues and eigenvectors. In this simplified pseudocode, we assume 
    # the geometry shader handles the rotation if not explicitly computed here.
    # For explicit computation (as in [S2, unofficial]):
    # diagonalVector = normalize(vec2(b, eigenValue0 - a))
    # scale_factor = 2.5
    # majorAxis = scale_factor * min(sqrt(2.0 * eigenValue0), 1024.0) * diagonalVector
    # minorAxis = scale_factor * min(sqrt(2.0 * eigenValue1), 1024.0) * vec2(diagonalVector.y, -diagonalVector.x)
    
    # 9. Output
    gColor = aColor
    gDepth = clipCenter.z / clipCenter.w
    
    return (clipCenter, basisVector0, basisVector1, gColor, gDepth)
```

### Parameters
*   `discardAlpha`: Default $0.0001$ [S1, unofficial].
*   `maxScreenSpaceSplatSize`: $2048.0$ [S1, unofficial].
*   `sqrt8`: $\sqrt{8}$ [S1, unofficial].
*   `scale`: $2.5$ [S2, unofficial].

### Pitfalls
*   **Invalid Covariance:** If $D \le 0.0$, or $\text{cov2Dv}.x \le 0.0$, or $\text{cov2Dv}.z \le 0.0$, the covariance matrix is illegal. Set `gColor.a = 0.0` and return [S1, unofficial].
*   **Negative Eigenvalue:** If $\lambda_2 < 0.0$, the covariance is invalid. Discard the splat [S2, unofficial].
*   **Degenerate Eigenvalues:** If $\text{eigenValue0} \le 0.01$, or $\text{eigenValue1} \le 0.01$, or $\text{eigenValue0} < \text{eigenValue1}$, or ratio $\text{eigenValue0}/\text{eigenValue1} > 10000.0$, the point is degenerate [S1, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** $O(1)$ per Gaussian splat in the vertex shader. The dominant cost is the matrix multiplications and square root operations for eigenvalue decomposition.
*   **Memory Complexity:** $O(1)$ per splat for vertex shader registers. Texture storage is $O(N)$ for $N$ splats.
*   **Performance:** Offloads projection and culling from the CPU to the GPU vertex shader, reducing CPU-GPU transfer overhead. However, it may be less efficient than CUDA-based variants for very large scenes due to vertex shader instruction limits and lack of specialized tensor cores [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Simple integration into existing OpenGL/Vulkan pipelines; leverages hardware vertex processing.
*   **Limitations:** Limited precision compared to CUDA; harder to implement complex culling or sorting logic; may require geometry shader for quad generation, adding overhead.

## Background
This method is part of the 3D Gaussian Splatting rendering pipeline, specifically the vertex shader variant. It was developed to provide a GPU-accelerated alternative to CPU-based rasterization, enabling real-time rendering of Gaussian splats in graphics APIs like OpenGL and Vulkan [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial]. The approach builds on the foundational 3D Gaussian Splatting paper by Kerbl et al. [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].

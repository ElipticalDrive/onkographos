# FreGS

Also called: Frequency-aware regularization for 3DGS, Progressive frequency regularization

## Overview
FreGS is a regularization method for 3D Gaussian Splatting that prevents overfitting and high-frequency artifacts by penalizing frequency discrepancies between rendered and ground-truth images [S1, unofficial][S2]. Unlike spatial or depth-based constraints, it operates in the frequency domain to guide the optimization of Gaussian primitives [S2]. It addresses the problem of over-reconstruction, where the model captures noise or fine details that do not generalize, by focusing on spectral consistency [S2].

## How it works
Inputs: Rendered image, ground-truth image, Gaussian primitives.
Outputs: Regularized loss term.

The method applies progressive frequency regularization to the primitives [S1, unofficial]. It compares the Fourier spectra of the rendered image against the ground-truth image [S2]. The regularization term penalizes high-frequency discrepancies [S2]. This process ensures smooth expansion of the Gaussian cloud in a fronto-parallel manner, ensuring better coverage [S1, unofficial].

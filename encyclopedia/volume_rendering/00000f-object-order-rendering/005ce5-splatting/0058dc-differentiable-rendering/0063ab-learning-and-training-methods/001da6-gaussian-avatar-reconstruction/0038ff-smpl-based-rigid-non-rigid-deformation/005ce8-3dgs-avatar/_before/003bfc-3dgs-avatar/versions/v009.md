# 3DGS-Avatar
## Overview
3DGS-Avatar reconstructs full-body human avatars by combining 3D Gaussians with rigid and non-rigid transformations of an underlying SMPL model [S3, unofficial]. It distinguishes itself from sibling methods by replacing spherical harmonics with a shallow MLP to model per-Gaussian color, enabling pose-dependent appearance modeling [S1].

## How it works
**Inputs:** Not given in the sources.
**Outputs:** Not given in the sources.
**Data Structures:** Not given in the sources.
**Steps:**
1. **Initialization:** Gaussians are initialized on the surface of the canonical SMPL mesh [G]. Initial skinning weights $w_{i,j}$ are assigned based on the SMPL vertex weights [G].
2. **Deformation:** For a target pose $\theta$, each Gaussian $i$ is transformed:
   a. Rigid transformation via Linear Blend Skinning (LBS): $\mathbf{p}_{\text{rigid}} = \sum_{j=1}^{N_{\text{bones}}} w_{i,j} \mathbf{T}_j(\theta) \mathbf{p}_i$, where $N_{\text{bones}}$ is the number of bones, $\mathbf{T}_j(\theta)$ are bone transformation matrices, and $\mathbf{p}_i$ is the canonical position [S1].
   b. Non-rigid residual $\Delta \mathbf{p}$ is predicted by a deformation network conditioned on pose and canonical position [G].
   c. Final position: $\mathbf{p}'_i = \mathbf{p}_{\text{rigid}} + \Delta \mathbf{p}$ [G].
3. **Covariance Update:** The covariance matrix is transformed using the Jacobian $J$ of the deformation map: $\Sigma'_i = J \Sigma_i J^T + \sigma^2 I$, where $\Sigma_i$ is the original covariance and $\sigma^2 I$ is a small regularization term [G].
4. **Color Prediction:** A shallow MLP replaces spherical harmonics to model per-Gaussian color [S1].
5. **Rendering:** Deformed Gaussians are projected and composited using differentiable 3D Gaussian splatting [S1].
6. **Optimization:** Training minimizes a combination of photometric and perceptual losses: $\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{L1}} + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{\text{MS-SSIM}}$, where $\mathcal{L}_{\text{L1}}$ is the L1 loss, $\lambda$ is a weighting factor, and $\mathcal{L}_{\text{MS-SSIM}}$ is the multi-scale structural similarity loss, with deformation regularized by geometry priors [S1].
**Parameters:**
*   MLP depth/width: Described as "shallow" [S1].
**Edge Cases:**
*   **Pose Extrapolation:** Generalization to novel poses relies on the MLP's capacity and deformation regularization [S1].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Rendering scales linearly with the number of Gaussians $O(N)$ [G]. Training requires MLP inference per Gaussian, increasing compute time [S1].
*   **Strengths:** Captures pose-dependent cloth deformation and generalizes effectively to novel poses [S1].
*   **Limitations:** Requires accurate SMPL fitting; replacing spherical harmonics with an MLP increases per-Gaussian computational overhead during training and rendering [S1].

## Background
3DGS-Avatar was introduced to overcome the limitations of spherical harmonics in capturing complex, pose-dependent appearance changes such as cloth wrinkles [S1]. It builds on the SMPL-based rigid/non-rigid deformation framework [S3, unofficial].

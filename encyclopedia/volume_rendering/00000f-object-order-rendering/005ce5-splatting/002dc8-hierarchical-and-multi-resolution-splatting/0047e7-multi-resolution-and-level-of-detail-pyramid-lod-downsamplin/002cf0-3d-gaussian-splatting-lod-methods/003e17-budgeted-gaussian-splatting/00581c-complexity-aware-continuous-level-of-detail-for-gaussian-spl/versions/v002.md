# Complexity-Aware Continuous Level of Detail for Gaussian Splatting

Also called: KoalaGS

## Overview
Complexity-Aware Continuous Level of Detail (LoD) for Gaussian Splatting, also known as KoalaGS, is a global, view-independent LoD formulation that provides a single primitive ordering whose prefixes define successive approximations of a scene [S1, unofficial]. It solves the problem of variable-quality rendering in 3D Gaussian Splatting by enabling progressive streaming and continuous budget adjustment without retraining, distinguishing itself from camera-dependent hierarchical selectors like FlexGS and discrete hierarchy methods like Octree-GS [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs**
*   **Input:** A set of 3D Gaussians (means, covariances, opacities, spherical harmonic coefficients) initialized from a standard 3DGS or 3DGS-MCMC training run; input images for complexity estimation [S1, unofficial].
*   **Output:** A sorted list of primitives based on a computed "complexity" metric, allowing any prefix of the list to be rendered to achieve a specific budget (primitive count) [S1, unofficial].

**Data Structures**
*   **Complexity Map:** A precomputed field associated with each Gaussian [S1, unofficial]. For a newly added or relocated Gaussian, the complexity is initialized to the minimum possible value until observed by a training view [S1, unofficial].
*   **Primitive Ordering:** A static array of indices sorted by descending complexity [S1, unofficial]. This ordering is view-independent and fixed after training [S1, unofficial].

**Parameters**
*   **Receptive Field:** 32 pixels (fixed) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Stride:** 4 to 8 pixels, depending on image resolution [S1, unofficial].
*   **Training Steps:** 30,000 steps [S1, unofficial].
*   **Initial Full-Capacity Steps:** First 500 steps render only at full capacity [S1, unofficial].
*   **Budget Sampling:** $\tau \sim U(0, 1)$, sampled uniformly at each training step [S1, unofficial].
*   **Complexity Dropout Rate ($\delta$):** Default 0.2 [S1, unofficial].
*   **Spatial-Variance Loss Weight ($\lambda_{var}$):** 2.0 [S1, unofficial].
*   **Importance-Score Weight ($\lambda$):** 2.0 [S1, unofficial].
*   **Complexity EMA Rate ($\eta$):** 0.8 [S1, unofficial].

**Steps**

1.  **Complexity Map Computation (Pre-training):**
    Compute complexity maps once per scene before training [S1, unofficial]. This is implemented in PyTorch on the GPU [S1, unofficial].
    *   Use a fixed receptive field of 32 pixels [S1, unofficial].
    *   Use a stride of 4 to 8 pixels depending on image resolution [S1, unofficial].
    *   Cache the results to disk [S1, unofficial].
    *   Cost: 20–60 seconds per scene [S1, unofficial].

2.  **Initialization:**
    Initialize Gaussians from a standard 3DGS or 3DGS-MCMC checkpoint [S1, unofficial]. Set the complexity of any newly added or relocated Gaussian to the minimum value [S1, unofficial].

3.  **Training Loop (30,000 steps):**
    For each step $t$:
    *   If $t < 500$: Render the training view at full capacity (all primitives) [S1, unofficial].
    *   If $t \ge 500$:
        *   Sample a budget $\tau \sim U(0, 1)$ [S1, unofficial].
        *   Render the training view twice:
            1.  At full capacity ($\hat{I}_i^{full}$) [S1, unofficial].
            2.  At the sampled budget $\tau$ ($\hat{I}_i^{\tau}$), using the prefix of the sorted primitive list corresponding to $\tau$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   Compute the loss $L$ using the formula below [S1, unofficial].
    *   Perform gradient descent [S1, unofficial].

4.  **Relocation and Complexity Update:**
    During training, when a Gaussian is relocated or added:
    *   If it has no complexity estimate (newly added/relocated), rank it by opacity alone until a training view observes it and updates its complexity via the EMA [S1, unofficial].
    *   Apply complexity dropout during relocation selection with rate $\delta=0.2$ [S1, unofficial]. With probability $\delta$, the complexity term is set to zero, causing the Gaussian to compete based only on opacity [S1, unofficial].

**Formula**
The total objective $L$ is defined as [S1, unofficial]:

$$
L = \frac{1}{2} L_{rec} (\hat{I}_i^{full}, I_i) + L_{rec} (\hat{I}_i^{\tau}, I_i) + \lambda_{var} L_{var} + \lambda_{\alpha} \bar{\alpha} + \lambda_{\sigma} \bar{\sigma}
$$

Where:
*   $L_{rec}$ is the L1+D-SSIM loss of 3DGS [S1, unofficial].
*   $\hat{I}_i^{full}$ is the rendered image at full capacity [S1, unofficial].
*   $\hat{I}_i^{\tau}$ is the rendered image at budget $\tau$ [S1, unofficial].
*   $I_i$ is the ground truth image [S1, unofficial].
*   $L_{var}$ is the spatial-variance loss (Eq. 6 in [S1, unofficial]) [S1, unofficial].
*   $\bar{\alpha}$ is the mean opacity of the primitives [S1, unofficial].
*   $\bar{\sigma}$ is the mean scale of the primitives [S1, unofficial].
*   $\lambda_{var}, \lambda_{\alpha}, \lambda_{\sigma}$ are weighting hyperparameters [S1, unofficial].

**Pitfalls and Edge Cases**
*   **New/Relocated Gaussians:** A newly added or relocated Gaussian has no complexity estimate until a training view observes it [S1, unofficial]. It is handled by setting its complexity to the minimum, so relocation ranks it by opacity alone until the update assigns it a value [S1, unofficial].
*   **Complexity Dropout:** The dropout rate $\delta$ interpolates between applying complexity always ($\delta=0$) and dropping it always ($\delta=1$, reducing to original opacity sampling) [S1, unofficial]. A default $\delta=0.2$ prevents relocation from repeatedly targeting the same high-complexity regions [S1, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Training takes approximately 15 minutes on a single NVIDIA H100 for a scene with ~2M primitives [S1, unofficial]. Pre-processing (complexity map computation) takes 20–60 seconds per scene [S1, unofficial].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Requires storing the complexity map for each Gaussian. No additional hierarchical data structures are needed compared to standard 3DGS.
*   **Performance:** On Tanks and Temples, the default dropout rate $\delta=0.2$ improves AUC PSNR by 0.14 dB (24.02 vs. 23.88) compared to original opacity sampling ($\delta=1$) [S1, unofficial]. On Deep Blending, it improves AUC PSNR by 0.23 dB (29.51 vs. 29.28) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Continuous LoD allows any budget within the range; progressive streaming is enabled by the fixed ordering; view-independent selection simplifies deployment.
*   **Limitations:** Requires a one-time pre-processing step; the ordering is fixed, so it may not adapt to specific camera viewpoints as well as camera-dependent methods.

## Background
KoalaGS was introduced in the paper "KoalaGS: Complexity-Aware Continuous Level of Detail for Gaussian Splatting" [S1, unofficial]. It builds on the 3DGS-MCMC framework [S1, unofficial] and addresses the limitations of discrete LoD methods (like Octree-GS) and camera-dependent selectors (like FlexGS) by providing a global, view-independent ordering.

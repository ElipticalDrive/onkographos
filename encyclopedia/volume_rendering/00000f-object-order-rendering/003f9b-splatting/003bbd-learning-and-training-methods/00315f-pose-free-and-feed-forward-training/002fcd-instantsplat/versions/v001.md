# InstantSplat

Also called: MASt3R/VGGT-guided 3DGS, Pose-free 3D Gaussian Splatting

## Overview
InstantSplat is a pose-free, sparse-view 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) training method that eliminates the need for Structure-from-Motion (SfM) pose estimation by leveraging geometric priors from MASt3R or VGGT [S1, unofficial]. It solves the initialization bottleneck of traditional 3DGS, enabling training from sparse or uncalibrated inputs in seconds [S1, unofficial]. Unlike feed-forward methods like pixelSplat which generate Gaussians via a neural network forward pass, InstantSplat performs rapid differentiable optimization initialized by prior geometry [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs:** A set of $N$ uncalibrated images (typically sparse, $N \ll 100$) and pre-computed geometric priors (depth maps and/or point clouds) generated by MASt3R or VGGT.
**Outputs:** A set of 3D Gaussian primitives $\{G_i\}$ defined by mean $\mu_i$, covariance $\Sigma_i$, opacity $\alpha_i$, and spherical harmonics coefficients $c_{i,lm}$ for color.

**Data Structures:**
The Gaussian cloud is stored as a list of structures. Each Gaussian $i$ contains:
- $\mu_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$: 3D position.
- $\Sigma_i \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$: Covariance matrix, typically parameterized by a rotation quaternion $q_i \in \mathbb{R}^4$ and scale vector $s_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$ such that $\Sigma_i = R(q_i) S(s_i) S(s_i)^T R(q_i)^T$.
- $\alpha_i \in [0, 1]$: Opacity.
- $c_{i,lm}$: SH coefficients for view-dependent color.

**Steps:**
1. **Prior Extraction:** Run MASt3R or VGGT on the input image set to obtain dense depth maps and a sparse point cloud. [S1, unofficial]
2. **Initialization:** Initialize the Gaussian means $\mu_i$ using the points from the prior point cloud. [S1, unofficial]
3. **Covariance Initialization:** Set initial scales $s_i$ based on the local point density or a fixed small value. Set initial rotations $q_i$ to identity. [G]
4. **Optimization:** Perform differentiable rasterization-based optimization. Minimize the loss:
   $$ \mathcal{L} = \lambda_{\text{rec}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{rec}} + \lambda_{\text{reg}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{reg}} $$
   where $\mathcal{L}_{\text{rec}}$ is the photometric reconstruction loss (L1 + D-SSIM) between rendered and input images, and $\mathcal{L}_{\text{reg}}$ is a regularization term (e.g., opacity penalty). [G]
5. **Densification:** Apply standard 3DGS densification (clone/split) based on gradient magnitude of camera positions and opacity thresholds to fill gaps, guided by the prior geometry to prevent drift. [G]
6. **Pruning:** Remove Gaussians with low opacity or those deviating significantly from the prior depth constraints. [G]

**Parameters:**
- Learning rate: Standard 3DGS values (e.g., $1.6 \times 10^{-4}$ for positions). [G]
- Optimization steps: Significantly fewer than standard 3DGS (seconds to minutes). [S1, unofficial]

**Edge Cases:**
- **Degenerate Input:** If priors are noisy, initialization may fail. [G]
- **Precision:** Floating-point precision in rasterization must be sufficient for small Gaussian scales. [G]

## Subtopics

### MoSca
A prior-aided monocular dynamic Gaussian Splatting method using CoTracker, VGG-T, DUSt3R, or metric-depth priors [S1, unofficial]. It extends InstantSplat's pose-free philosophy to dynamic scenes by tracking motion via CoTracker and using VGG-T/DUSt3R for geometry [S1, unofficial]. Choose when dealing with monocular video where pose is unavailable.

### Shape-of-Motion
A prior-aided monocular dynamic Gaussian Splatting method using CoTracker, VGG-T, DUSt3R, or metric-depth priors [S1, unofficial]. Similar to MoSca, it leverages geometric and tracking priors to reconstruct dynamic scenes from a single camera without pose estimation [S1, unofficial]. Choose for dynamic content from casual handheld video.

### Feature-3DGS
A method storing per-Gaussian language features (CLIP, LSeg) for open-vocabulary 3D segmentation [S1, unofficial]. It integrates semantic features into the Gaussian representation, allowing for query-based segmentation. Choose when semantic understanding is required alongside rendering.

## Operating characteristics
**Time Complexity:** Training time is measured in seconds, significantly faster than standard 3DGS which takes minutes to hours [S1, unofficial].
**Memory Complexity:** Depends on the number of Gaussians, which is determined by the input sparsity and densification steps.
**Performance:** Matches pose-aware baselines in zero-shot evaluations when priors are accurate [S1, unofficial].
**Strengths:** No SfM required; works with sparse/casual captures; extremely fast training. [S1, unofficial]
**Limitations:** Quality depends heavily on the accuracy of MASt3R/VGGT priors; may struggle with textureless regions where priors are weak. [G]
**When to use:** Sparse-view inputs, uncalibrated image collections, or when SfM fails. [S1, unofficial]
**When not to use:** Dense, calibrated multi-view setups where standard 3DGS is sufficient and more robust.

## Background
InstantSplat was introduced as a solution to the dependency on SfM in 3D Gaussian Splatting. It builds on the success of MASt3R and VGGT in providing robust geometric priors from uncalibrated images [S1, unofficial]. It is part of the broader trend of pose-free and feed-forward training methods in the 3DGS ecosystem [S1, unofficial].

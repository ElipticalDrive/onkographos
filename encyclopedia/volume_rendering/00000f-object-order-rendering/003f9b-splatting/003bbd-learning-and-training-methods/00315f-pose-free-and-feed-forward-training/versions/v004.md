# Pose-free and Feed-forward Training

Also called: Pose-free 3DGS, Feed-forward Gaussian Splatting, Generalizable 3DGS

## Overview
This family addresses the dependency on Structure-from-Motion (SfM) pipelines and iterative per-scene optimization in standard 3D Gaussian Splatting. It enables reconstruction from uncalibrated images either by bypassing pose estimation via external geometric priors (InstantSplat) or by predicting Gaussian parameters directly in a single forward pass (pixelSplat, AnySplat). Unlike iterative methods, these approaches offer real-time inference or significantly reduced training times by leveraging learned priors or dense correspondence networks.

## Shared principles
All methods in this family output a set of 3D Gaussian primitives $\{G_i\}$ defined by mean $\mu_i$, covariance $\Sigma_i$, opacity $\alpha_i$, and spherical harmonics coefficients $c_{i,lm}$ [6136-instantsplat.md][6464-pixelsplat.md]. The rendering process remains differentiable with respect to the scene representation [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, 6464-pixelsplat.md, unofficial]. In feed-forward variants, gradients flow through the network weights during training; in prior-based variants, gradients update Gaussian attributes while poses remain fixed or are ignored [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, 6464-pixelsplat.md, unofficial]. Loss functions typically combine photometric reconstruction error, such as $\mathcal{L}_{L1}$ or $\mathcal{L}_{MSE}$, with perceptual or structural metrics like $\mathcal{L}_{MS-SSIM}$ or $\mathcal{L}_{LPIPS}$ [6136-instantsplat.md][6464-pixelsplat.md].

## Subtopics

### [InstantSplat](6136-instantsplat.md)
InstantSplat is a pose-free, sparse-view 3D Gaussian Splatting method that reconstructs scenes from uncalibrated image collections by leveraging external geometry priors [6136-instantsplat.md]. It differs from iterative optimization approaches by using MASt3R or VGGT to provide initial geometric constraints, enabling training in seconds rather than minutes [6136-instantsplat.md]. Unlike feed-forward methods like pixelSplat, it retains the optimization-based refinement of Gaussian attributes while bypassing the need for explicit camera pose estimation [6136-instantsplat.md]. Choose this method when high-quality reconstruction from sparse, uncalibrated views is required without the latency of iterative optimization.

### [pixelSplat](6464-pixelsplat.md)
pixelSplat is a feed-forward model that reconstructs a 3D radiance field parameterized by 3D Gaussian primitives from a pair of input images [6464-pixelsplat.md]. It solves the problem of generalizable novel view synthesis by predicting an explicit, editable 3D representation in a single forward pass, avoiding the iterative optimization loops of single-scene methods [6464-pixelsplat.md]. Unlike light-field transformers which output black-box ray colors, pixelSplat generates a primitive-based representation that enables real-time rasterization-based rendering [6464-pixelsplat.md]. It distinguishes itself from siblings like InstantSplat by using a probabilistic sampling scheme to overcome local minima in primitive regression and an epipolar transformer to resolve scale ambiguity inherent in SfM poses [6464-pixelsplat.md]. Choose this method for real-time synthesis from calibrated stereo pairs where generalization across scenes is prioritized.

### AnySplat
AnySplat is a pose-free feed-forward network where a single forward pass over uncalibrated image collections yields 3D Gaussian primitives plus per-image intrinsics and extrinsics [S1, unofficial]. It scales to casual dense or sparse multi-view captures with no pose annotations, matching pose-aware baselines and surpassing prior pose-free methods (NoPoSplat-class) in zero-shot evaluations at real-time rendering latency [S1, unofficial]. Choose this method for generalizable reconstruction from uncalibrated multi-view captures without pose annotations.

## Background
Standard 3D Gaussian Splatting requires accurate camera poses from SfM and iterative per-scene optimization [3761-3d-gaussian-splatting-training.md]. Pose-free and feed-forward methods emerged to remove these bottlenecks. InstantSplat utilizes external geometry priors from models like MASt3R or VGGT to initialize Gaussians, enabling rapid reconstruction [6136-instantsplat.md]. pixelSplat introduces a feed-forward architecture using epipolar transformers and probabilistic sampling to predict Gaussians directly from image pairs, addressing scale ambiguity and local minima [6464-pixelsplat.md]. AnySplat extends this to uncalibrated multi-view collections, predicting poses and Gaussians simultaneously [S1, unofficial]. These methods are used in applications requiring real-time rendering from casual captures or generalizable scene representations.

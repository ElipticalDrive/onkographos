# Gaussian Avatars

Also called: FLAME Gaussian Splatting, PSAvatar, PSAvatar / Relightable Avatar, Relightable Avatar

## Overview
Gaussian Avatars is a method for reconstructing photorealistic, animatable head avatars by associating 3D Gaussians with the triangles of a parametric FLAME mesh [S1][S3, unofficial]. It solves the challenge of dynamic avatar rendering by constraining Gaussians to remain within their corresponding mesh triangles while allowing their parameters to deform based on pose and expression [S1][S3, unofficial]. It is distinguished from sibling methods like Gaussian Head Avatar (GHA) by its explicit triangle-to-Gaussian mapping rather than MLP-based position prediction [S1][S3, unofficial], and from full-body methods like 3DGS-Avatar by its focus on the bust/head region using the FLAME rig [S1][S2, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Inputs:** Multi-view video of a subject; initial FLAME mesh fit (pose $\beta$, shape $\theta$, expression $\psi$) [S2, unofficial].
*   **Outputs:** A set of deformable 3D Gaussians representing the avatar, capable of rendering novel views and expressions [S1][S3, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
*   **Gaussian Set:** Each Gaussian $i$ is associated with a specific triangle $t$ of the FLAME mesh.
*   **Attributes per Gaussian:**
    *   Position $\mathbf{p}_i$: Constrained to lie on triangle $t$.
    *   Covariance $\Sigma_i$: Defines orientation and scale.
    *   Opacity $\alpha_i$: Visibility weight.
    *   Spherical Harmonics (SH) coefficients $\mathbf{c}_i$: View-dependent color.
    *   *Note:* The exact bit-packing or tensor layout for SH coefficients is not specified in the sources [S1][S3, unofficial].

**Initialization:**
1.  Fit a FLAME model to the input video using tools like MICA or DECA [S2, unofficial].
2.  Initialize one Gaussian per triangle of the FLAME mesh [S2, unofficial].
3.  Place the Gaussian position $\mathbf{p}_i$ in the tangent space of its corresponding triangle [S2, unofficial].

**Deformation and Optimization:**
The method uses a spatiotemporal structure encoder to capture deformations of positions and colors over time [S1][S3, unofficial]. The core mechanism involves optimizing Gaussian parameters such that they remain constrained to their respective triangles while adapting to the FLAME pose and expression parameters.

1.  **Pose/Expression Input:** Given current pose $\beta$ and expression $\psi$, compute the deformed FLAME mesh vertices.
2.  **Gaussian Transformation:** Transform the initial Gaussian parameters (position, covariance) according to the deformation of the underlying triangle. The position $\mathbf{p}_i$ is updated to stay on the deformed triangle surface [S1][S3, unofficial].
3.  **Color Prediction:** The color (SH coefficients) is optimized or predicted based on the current view and expression [S1][S3, unofficial].
4.  **Rendering:** Render the Gaussians using standard differentiable splatting [S1][S3, unofficial].
5.  **Loss Calculation:** Compute photometric loss (e.g., $\mathcal{L}_{L1}$) and perceptual loss (e.g., $\mathcal{L}_{MS-SSIM}$) against ground truth images [S1][S3, unofficial].
6.  **Backpropagation:** Update Gaussian parameters and any network weights (if a color/position network is used) to minimize the loss [S1][S3, unofficial].

**Pseudocode:**
```python
# Initialization
flame_mesh = fit_flame(video_frames) # Using MICA/DECA [S2, unofficial]
gaussians = []
for triangle in flame_mesh.triangles:
    g = Gaussian()
    g.triangle_id = triangle.id
    g.position = init_in_tangent_space(triangle) # [S2, unofficial]
    g.covariance = identity_matrix()
    g.opacity = 1.0
    g.sh_coefficients = random_init()
    gaussians.append(g)

# Training Loop
for batch in training_data:
    pose, expression, gt_images = batch
    
    # Deform mesh
    deformed_mesh = deform_flame(fixed_flame_template, pose, expression)
    
    # Update Gaussians based on mesh deformation
    for g in gaussians:
        triangle = deformed_mesh.triangles[g.triangle_id]
        # Constrain position to deformed triangle [S1][S3, unofficial]
        g.position = project_to_triangle(g.position, triangle)
        # Update covariance/orientation to match triangle normal/shape [G]
        g.covariance = compute_covariance_from_triangle(triangle)
        
    # Render
    rendered_images = render_gaussians(gaussians, camera_params)
    
    # Compute Loss
    loss = photometric_loss(rendered_images, gt_images) + perceptual_loss(rendered_images, gt_images)
    
    # Optimize
    optimize(gaussians, loss)
```

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
*   **Triangle Degeneracy:** If a FLAME triangle collapses during extreme expressions, Gaussian initialization or deformation may fail. [G] Guard against zero-area triangles.
*   **Precision:** Maintaining strict constraint to the triangle surface requires careful numerical handling during deformation [S1][S3, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### [Head Avatar Modeling](11813-head-avatar-modeling.md)
A method that combines parametric morphable face models (e.g., FLAME) with deformable 3D Gaussians, employing diffusion strategies and expression-aware deformation fields to disentangle rigid head poses from non-rigid facial movements.

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Not given in the sources.
*   **Memory Complexity:** Not given in the sources.
*   **Measured Performance:** Not given in the sources.
*   **Strengths:** Riggable avatar; photorealistic head rendering; explicit constraint to parametric mesh ensures structural integrity [S1][S2, S3, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** Limited to head/bust region (FLAME); requires initial FLAME fit [S2, unofficial].
*   **When to use:** When animatable, photorealistic head avatars are needed from multi-view video [S2, unofficial].
*   **When not to use:** For full-body avatars (use 3DGS-Avatar) or when no parametric head model is desired [S1].

## Background
Gaussian Avatars was introduced as a solution to the challenges of reconstructing human avatars using 3D Gaussian Splatting, specifically addressing dynamic Gaussians and relighting [S1][S3, unofficial]. It trains a FLAME model of the bust, associating each triangle with a Gaussian optimized for the scene [S1][S3, unofficial]. It is part of the broader family of learning and training methods for Gaussian Avatar Reconstruction [S1][S2, unofficial].

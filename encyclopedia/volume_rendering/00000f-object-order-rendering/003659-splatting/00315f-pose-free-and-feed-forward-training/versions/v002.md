# Pose-free and Feed-forward Training

Also called: Pose-free training, Feed-forward 3DGS, Generalizable 3DGS

## Overview
This family addresses the dependency on external Structure-from-Motion (SfM) pipelines and iterative per-scene optimization in standard 3D Gaussian Splatting. It enables reconstruction from uncalibrated images either by eliminating iterative camera optimization in favor of geometric priors (pose-free) or by replacing the optimization loop entirely with a direct neural mapping from images to Gaussians (feed-forward). It distinguishes itself from standard iterative methods by removing the need for known camera poses and reducing training time from minutes to seconds or milliseconds.

## Shared principles
The core mechanism replaces the fixed-camera assumption of standard differentiable rendering with either learnable pose parameters or a direct regression network.

1.  **Input Flexibility:** Inputs are uncalibrated images $\{I_i\}$ without guaranteed known intrinsics or extrinsics.
2.  **Output:** A set of 3D Gaussian primitives $\{G_i\}$ defined by mean $\mu_i$, covariance $\Sigma_i$, opacity $\alpha_i$, and spherical harmonics coefficients $c_{i,lm}$ [6136-instantsplat.md][6464-pixelsplat.md].
3.  **Differentiability:** The mapping from input images to Gaussian parameters is differentiable, allowing gradients to flow from the rendering loss to the network weights or pose parameters [6136-instantsplat.md][6464-pixelsplat.md].
4.  **Loss Function:** The optimization minimizes photometric reconstruction error, typically combining L1 loss and perceptual metrics:
    $$ \mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{L1} + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{MS-SSIM} $$
    or
    $$ \mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{MSE} + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{LPIPS} $$
    [6136-instantsplat.md][6464-pixelsplat.md].

## Subtopics

### [InstantSplat](6136-instantsplat.md)
InstantSplat is a pose-free, sparse-view method that reconstructs scenes from uncalibrated images in seconds by leveraging geometric priors [6136-instantsplat.md]. It distinguishes itself by using MASt3R or VGGT to provide direct geometry priors, eliminating the need for SfM initialization and iterative camera optimization [6136-instantsplat.md]. Choose this when rapid reconstruction from sparse, uncalibrated views is required. [6136-instantsplat.md]

### [pixelSplat](6464-pixelsplat.md)
pixelSplat is a feed-forward model that reconstructs a 3D radiance field from a pair of input images [6464-pixelsplat.md]. It solves the problem of generalizable novel view synthesis by replacing iterative optimization with a direct neural mapping, avoiding local minima inherent to sparse primitive representations [6464-pixelsplat.md]. Choose this for real-time, generalizable synthesis from stereo pairs. [6464-pixelsplat.md]

### [Joint Pose Optimization](6888-joint-pose-optimization.md)
Joint Pose Optimization (JPO) is a family of methods that simultaneously optimize the 3D scene representation and camera poses from unposed input images [6888-joint-pose-optimization.md]. Unlike feed-forward methods, JPO treats camera extrinsics as learnable parameters updated via gradient descent alongside the scene representation [6888-joint-pose-optimization.md]. Choose this when a dense, optimized reconstruction is needed without external pose estimation. [6888-joint-pose-optimization.md]

#### AnySplat
AnySplat is a pose-free feed-forward network where a single forward pass over uncalibrated image collections yields 3D Gaussian primitives plus per-image intrinsics and extrinsics [S1, unofficial]. It scales to casual dense or sparse multi-view captures with no pose annotations, matching pose-aware baselines and surpassing prior pose-free methods (NoPoSplat-class) in zero-shot evaluations at real-time rendering latency [S1, unofficial].

## Background
Originating from the need to decouple 3D Gaussian Splatting from the brittle SfM pipeline, this family emerged as a response to the computational cost and failure modes of iterative optimization. InstantSplat introduced the use of monocular depth estimators (MASt3R/VGGT) for initialization [6136-instantsplat.md]. pixelSplat and related methods (MVSplat, latentSplat, Splatter Image) pioneered the feed-forward approach for generalizable synthesis [S1, unofficial]. Joint Pose Optimization extends the differentiable rendering framework to include camera parameters as learnable variables [6888-joint-pose-optimization.md]. AnySplat represents a recent SOTA update in pose-free feed-forward networks [S1, unofficial].

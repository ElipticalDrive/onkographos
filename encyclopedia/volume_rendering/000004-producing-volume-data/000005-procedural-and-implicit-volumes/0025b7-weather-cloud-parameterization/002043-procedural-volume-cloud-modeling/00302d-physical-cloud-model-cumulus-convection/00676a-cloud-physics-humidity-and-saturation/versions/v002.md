# Cloud Physics: Humidity and Saturation

Also called: Absolute Humidity, Humidity and Saturation, Relative Humidity, Saturation

## Overview
This method computes the saturation state of water vapor in an air parcel to determine condensation and evaporation thresholds [S1]. It solves the problem of identifying when water vapor transitions into liquid water, which is a prerequisite for cloud formation and precipitation in physical cloud models [S1]. It differs from [Cloud Physics: Temperature and Buoyancy](19845-cloud-physics-temperature-and-buoyancy.md) by focusing on mass concentration ratios rather than thermal energy and vertical forces, and from [Hybrid Physics-Driven Procedural Model](19848-hybrid-physics-driven-procedural-model.md) by providing the fundamental physical constraints rather than procedural noise generation [G].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Inputs:** Atmospheric pressure ($P$), saturated vapor pressure ($e_s$), current absolute humidity (mass of water vapor per mass of dry air).
*   **Outputs:** Saturated mixing ratio ($\omega_s$), relative humidity status (saturated/unsaturated), and excess vapor mass.

**Formulas and Definitions:**
1.  **Absolute Humidity:** Defined as the concentration of water vapor, measured by the mass of water vapor divided by the mass of dry air in a volume at a given temperature [S1].
2.  **Relative Humidity:** Defined as the ratio of the current absolute humidity to the highest possible maximum humidity [S1].
3.  **Saturation Threshold:** At 100% relative humidity, the air is saturated [S1]. The threshold for saturation is the saturated mixing ratio ($\omega_s$) [S1].
4.  **Saturated Mixing Ratio Formula:**
    $$ \omega_s = 621.97 \left( \frac{e_s}{P - e_s} \right) $$
    Where:
    *   $\omega_s$ is the saturated mixing ratio.
    *   $e_s$ is the saturated vapor pressure.
    *   $P$ is the atmospheric pressure [S1].

**Algorithm:**
1.  Calculate the saturated vapor pressure ($e_s$) based on the current temperature of the air parcel [G].
2.  Retrieve the local atmospheric pressure ($P$) [S1].
3.  Compute the saturated mixing ratio $\omega_s$ using the formula above [S1].
4.  Compare the current absolute humidity ($\omega$) against $\omega_s$ [S1].
5.  If $\omega > \omega_s$, the air is supersaturated and the excess vapor condenses into liquid water [S1].
6.  If $\omega \le \omega_s$, the air is unsaturated or saturated, and no condensation occurs [S1].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
Not given in the sources.

**Pseudocode:**
```python
def calculate_saturation_state(atmospheric_pressure, saturated_vapor_pressure, current_absolute_humidity):
    """
    Determines if air is saturated and calculates the saturation threshold.
    """
    if atmospheric_pressure <= saturated_vapor_pressure:
        return {
            "saturated_mixing_ratio": float('inf'),
            "is_saturated": True,
            "excess_vapor": 0.0
        }
    saturated_mixing_ratio = 621.97 * (saturated_vapor_pressure / (atmospheric_pressure - saturated_vapor_pressure))
    is_saturated = current_absolute_humidity >= saturated_mixing_ratio
    excess_vapor = max(0.0, current_absolute_humidity - saturated_mixing_ratio)
    return {
        "saturated_mixing_ratio": saturated_mixing_ratio,
        "is_saturated": is_saturated,
        "excess_vapor": excess_vapor
    }
```

## Subtopics

### Cloud Physics: Thermodynamics
This variant extends the humidity model by accounting for the energy changes during phase transitions [S1]. Water vapor releases latent heat of condensation ($L_c$) upon condensing to liquid water and absorbs latent heat of evaporation ($L_e$) during evaporation [S1]. The specific heat of air ($C_p$) is defined as the energy required to raise the temperature of air by one degree Celsius [S1]. These parameters are required to compute the evaporation/condensation of water content inside the parcel and the change in its temperature [S1].

## Operating characteristics
**Time and Memory Complexity:**
The calculation is $O(1)$ time and $O(1)$ memory per air parcel, as it involves only basic arithmetic operations on scalar values [G].

**Strengths and Limitations:**
The method provides a physically accurate threshold for condensation [S1]. It does not account for dynamic temperature changes caused by latent heat release without the companion Thermodynamics model [S1].

**When to Use:**
Use this method when implementing the mass conservation aspect of a physical cloud simulation to determine when water vapor should be converted to liquid water content [S1].

## Background
The concepts of absolute humidity, relative humidity, and saturated mixing ratio are standard meteorological definitions adopted into computer graphics for physically based cloud simulation [S1]. The specific formula for saturated mixing ratio using the constant $621.97$ is cited from general atmospheric physics literature referenced in the survey [S1]. The integration of these concepts into GPU-based cloud simulation allows for the generation of realistic cumulus clouds by modeling the condensation process [S1].

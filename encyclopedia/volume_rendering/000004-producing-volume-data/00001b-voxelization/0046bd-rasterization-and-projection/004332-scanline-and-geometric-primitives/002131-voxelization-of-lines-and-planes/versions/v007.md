# Voxelization of Lines and Planes
Also called: Slab-based Voxelization, N-separating Voxelization, 6-separating voxelization, 26-separating voxelization, 26-Separating Plane Voxelization

## Overview
This method generates discrete volumetric representations of 2D lines and 3D planes that are topologically **N-separating** (preventing N-connected ray paths from penetrating the surface) and **N-minimal** (containing no redundant voxels) [S2]. It achieves this by defining a slab of thickness $t$ around the geometric primitive and including all voxel centers falling within this slab [S2]. Unlike general 3D voxel covers which are 26-separating but dense, this method uses 1D intersection targets (slabs) sufficient for effectively 2D inputs, offering a balance between topological correctness and sparsity [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs:**
- Geometric primitive: A 2D line ($Ax + By + D = 0$) or 3D plane ($A_p X + B_p Y + C_p Z + D_p = 0$) [S2].
- Grid parameters: Voxel width $L$, grid resolution, and desired separability $N \in \{4, 6, 8, 26\}$ [S2].
- Normal vector: $(A_p, B_p, C_p)$ normalized such that $\sqrt{A_p^2 + B_p^2 + C_p^2} = 1$ [S2].

**Outputs:**
- A set of voxel coordinates $\tilde{S}$ representing the discretized primitive [S2].

**Data Structures:**
- A 3D boolean array or sparse voxel grid where each voxel $(x,y,z)$ is marked as "black" (1, included) or "white" (0, excluded) [S2].

**Parameters:**
- $t$: The half-thickness of the slab.
  - For 26-separability: $t_{26} = \frac{L}{2 \sqrt{3} \cos \alpha}$, where $\alpha$ is the angle between the voxel diagonal $K$ and the plane normal $N$ [S2].
  - For 6-separability: $t_{6} = \frac{L}{2 \cos \beta}$, where $\beta$ is the angle between the normal to the voxel face and the plane normal [S2].
  - For 8-separability (2D): $t_{8} = \frac{L}{2 \cos \alpha}$, where $\alpha$ is the angle between the pixel diagonal and the line normal [S2].
  - For 4-separability (2D): $t_{4} = \frac{L}{2 \cos \beta}$, where $\beta$ is the angle between the normal to the pixel edge and the line normal [S2].

**Steps:**
1. **Normalization:** Ensure the normal vector $(A_p, B_p, C_p)$ is unit length [S2].
2. **Thickness Calculation:** Compute $t$ based on the desired separability $N$ and the angle between the primitive's normal and the grid's diagonal/face normal [S2].
3. **Slab Definition:** Define two parallel planes (or lines in 2D) offset by $t$ from the original primitive:
   $$ A_p X + B_p Y + C_p Z + D_p \pm t = 0 $$
   [S2]
4. **Voxel Classification:** Iterate through all voxels $(x, y, z)$ in the domain.
   - Calculate the signed distance from the voxel center to the primitive: $val = A_p x + B_p y + C_p z + D_p$ [S2].
   - If $-t \leq val \leq t$, mark the voxel as included (black) [S2].
   - Otherwise, mark as excluded (white) [S2].
5. **Degeneracy Handling:** Use infinitesimal perturbation to ensure that if the surface passes exactly through a corner, edge, or face, the intersection is treated as occurring inside a voxel, never exactly on the boundary [S1, unofficial].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
- **Degeneracies:** If the surface passes exactly through a corner, edge, or face, infinitesimal perturbation is required to ensure the intersection is treated as occurring inside a voxel, preventing ambiguous boundary cases [S1, unofficial].
- **26-Holes:** If a voxel is intersected by the plane but excluded, it creates a 26-hole. The proof demonstrates that if such a voxel $P$ exists with neighbors $Q$ and $R$ on opposite sides not included, the distance from the center $C$ to the plane must be less than or equal to $t_{26}$, meaning $P$ should have been included [S2].
- **Performance:** The method is computationally intensive if implemented naively. Timing results are based on non-optimized implementations; an efficient incremental algorithm is needed for faster voxelization [S2].

## Subtopics

### [4-Separating Line Voxelization](2502-4-separating-line-voxelization.md)
A method to generate a discrete representation of a 2D line that is 4-separating (no 4-holes) and 4-minimal (no redundant voxels). It uses a similar slab-based approach with thickness $t_4 = \frac{L}{2 \cos \beta}$, where $\beta$ is the angle between the line normal and the pixel edge normal. Choose this when 4-connectivity is sufficient for ray casting in 2D slices.

## Operating characteristics
- **Time Complexity:** Linear with respect to the number of voxels in the bounding box of the primitive. The source notes that VPM (Voxels per millisecond) decreases as the number of voxels increases due to linked list maintenance costs in their implementation [S2].
- **Memory Complexity:** Proportional to the number of included voxels. 26-separating voxelization produces significantly more Vertex Voxels and Edge Voxels than 6-separating voxelization, while the difference in Body Voxels is smaller [S2].
- **Measured Performance:** Tested on a 256x256x256 grid. Visual comparison of 6- and 26-separating models is virtually indistinguishable, but 6-separating takes less time [S2].
- **Strengths:** Guarantees topological correctness (separability and minimality) for lines and planes. Eliminates common voxelization artifacts at edges and vertices [S2].
- **Limitations:** Requires calculation of angles and trigonometric functions. Non-optimized implementations can be slow. The method is primarily designed for planar/polygonal inputs; extension to other objects requires additional steps [S2].

## Background
The method originates from research into topological voxelization, specifically addressing the need for N-separating and N-minimal representations of surfaces [S1, unofficial][S2]. Early work focused on 2D polygons or 2D projections, but lacked mechanisms to guarantee both separability and minimality for 3D polygon meshes, especially at edges and vertices [S2]. The slab-based approach using distance criteria derived from normal vectors was introduced to provide numerical accuracy and topological correctness [S2]. Cohen-Or and Kaufman [COK97] provided in-depth analysis of separability, noting that 26-connected ray paths require 26-separating voxelizations to prevent penetration [S1, unofficial].

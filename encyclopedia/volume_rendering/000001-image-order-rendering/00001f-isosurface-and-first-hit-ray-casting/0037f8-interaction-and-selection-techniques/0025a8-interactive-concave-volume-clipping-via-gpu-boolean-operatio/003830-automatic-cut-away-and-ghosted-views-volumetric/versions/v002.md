# Automatic Cut-Away and Ghosted Views (Volumetric)
Also called: Importance-Driven Feature Enhancement, Viola et al. Cut-away Views, Viola et al

## Overview
A method for automatically generating cut-away and ghosted views of volumetric datasets by employing an importance-based scheme to select which features remain visible or suppressed [S1][S2, unofficial]. It generalizes traditional cut-away views by allowing inspection of the internals of opaque objects based on feature significance rather than fixed geometric planes [S1]. This approach is distinct from sibling methods like [GPU-based Volume Sculpting System](13711-gpu-based-volume-sculpting-system.md) by focusing on semantic importance rather than manual sculpting, and builds on [Interactive Concave Volume Clipping via GPU Boolean Operations](3592-interactive-concave-volume-clipping-via-gpu-boolean-operatio.md) by mapping clipping operations to commodity graphics hardware for interactive performance [S2, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs:**
1. Volumetric dataset.
2. Importance information for particular features [S2, unofficial].
3. Clipping geometry defining the cut-away region [S2, unofficial].

**Outputs:**
1. Rendered image where features are visible, ghosted, or suppressed based on their importance and position relative to the clipping geometry [S1][S2, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
- Volumetric data representation (e.g., 3D grid or texture) [S2, unofficial].
- Importance information associated with features [S2, unofficial].
- Clipping geometry defining visibility regions [S2, unofficial].

**Steps:**
1. Map clipping operations to commodity graphics hardware to achieve interactive framerates [S2, unofficial].
2. Perform all rendering computations on the graphics hardware [S2, unofficial].
3. Use per-fragment operations to estimate on-the-fly visibility according to the clipping geometry [S2, unofficial].
4. Apply the importance scheme to select which parts of the object remain visible or are suppressed [S1].
5. Adjust shading in areas where clipping occurs to enhance illustrative quality [S2, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### Importance-Driven Feature Enhancement
A specific application of this method where the importance field is derived from feature significance, allowing for automatic enhancement of relevant structures [S2, unofficial].

### Viola et al. Cut-away Views
The original method proposed by Viola et al. [VKG04], which introduced the importance-based scheme for cut-away views [S1].

## Background
The method was introduced by Viola et al. [VKG04] as a generalization of cut-away views, introducing an importance-based scheme to select which parts of an object remain visible or suppressed [S1]. It builds on earlier work in cut-away views for polygonal data by Feiner and Seligmann [FS92] and techniques for increasing performance and visual quality in volumetric clipping by Weiskopf et al. [WEE03] [S2, unofficial]. The approach is part of the broader field of Non-Photorealistic Rendering (NPR) techniques, which seek to enhance the comprehensibility of images [S1].

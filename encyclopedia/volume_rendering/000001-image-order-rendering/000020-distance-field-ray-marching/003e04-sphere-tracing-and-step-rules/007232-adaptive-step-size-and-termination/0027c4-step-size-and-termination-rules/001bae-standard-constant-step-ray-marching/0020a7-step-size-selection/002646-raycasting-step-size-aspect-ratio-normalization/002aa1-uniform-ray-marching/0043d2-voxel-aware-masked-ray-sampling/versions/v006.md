# Voxel-Aware Masked Ray Sampling

Also called: Hierarchical Sampling for Sparse Feature Volume, Inside-Voxel Sampling, NSVF Hierarchical Ray Sampling with Voxel-Aware Masking, NSVF parallel ray sampling, NSVF voxel intersection sampling, Ray-Voxel Intersection Sampling

## Overview
Voxel-Aware Masked Ray Sampling is a ray sampling strategy for sparse voxel fields that ensures all sampling points lie strictly within intersected voxels, preventing invalid "air" samples common in unconstrained hierarchical sampling [S2, unofficial]. It handles variable numbers of voxel intersections per ray by padding and masking tensors, supporting hierarchical sampling ratios where coarse and fine passes are constrained to valid sparse regions [S1, unofficial]. Unlike standard NeRF which uses uniform or stratified sampling along the entire ray, this method restricts integration to occupied sparse voxels, offering significant memory and compute savings for sparse scenes [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs:**
- Ray origins and directions for a batch of rays [S1, unofficial].
- Sparse voxel grid structure (defining voxel bounds and IDs) [S1, unofficial].
- Parameters: `chunk_size` (default 4096), `max_hits` (default 60), `raymarching_stepsize` (default 0.01), `fine_num_sample_ratio` (default 0), `inverse_distance_coarse_sampling` (default 'none'), `background_depth` (default 5.0), `fixed_num_samples` (default 0) [S1, unofficial].

**Outputs:**
- `sampled_dists`: Tensor of shape `(num_rays, max_steps)` containing distances from camera origin for each sample point [S1, unofficial].
- `sampled_idx`: Tensor of shape `(num_rays, max_steps)` containing voxel IDs for each sample point [S1, unofficial].
- `sampled_masks`: Boolean tensor indicating valid samples [S1, unofficial].
- Rendered colors, depths, and other attributes after compositing [S1, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
1.  **Intersection Outputs Dictionary:**
    -   `min_depth`: Tensor of shape `(num_rays,)`, distance to voxel entry [S1, unofficial].
    -   `max_depth`: Tensor of shape `(num_rays,)`, distance to voxel exit [S1, unofficial].
    -   `intersected_voxel_idx`: Tensor of shape `(num_rays, max_hits)`, voxel IDs [S1, unofficial].
    -   `probs`: Tensor of shape `(num_rays, max_hits)`, sampling probabilities [S1, unofficial].
    -   `steps`: Tensor of shape `(num_rays,)`, number of samples per ray [S1, unofficial].
2.  **All Results Dictionary (Post-processing):**
    -   `missed`: Tensor of shape `(fullsize,)`, boolean mask for rays hitting no voxels [S1, unofficial].
    -   `colors`: Tensor of shape `(fullsize, 3)` [S1, unofficial].
    -   `depths`: Tensor of shape `(fullsize,)` [S1, unofficial].
    -   `normal`: Tensor of shape `(fullsize, 3)` (optional) [S1, unofficial].
    -   `voxel_depth`: Tensor of shape `(fullsize,)` (optional) [S1, unofficial].
    -   `voxel_edges`: Tensor of shape `(fullsize, 3)` (optional) [S1, unofficial].
    -   `feat_n2`: Tensor of shape `(fullsize,)` (optional) [S1, unofficial].

**Steps:**

1.  **Ray-Voxel Intersection Sampling:**
    Identify pairs $P = \{(r, v)\}$ where ray $r$ intersects voxel $v$ [S2, unofficial]. For each ray, determine the entry (`min_depth`) and exit (`max_depth`) distances in world space, and the corresponding voxel indices [S1, unofficial]. Calculate sampling probabilities `probs` proportional to the distance within each voxel:
    $$ \text{dists}_i = \max(0, \text{max\_depth}_i - \text{min\_depth}_i) $$
    Mask out invalid voxels (where `pts_idx == -1`) by setting their dists to 0 [S1, unofficial]:
    $$ \text{probs}_i = \frac{\text{dists}_i}{\sum_j \text{dists}_j} $$
    Determine the number of steps `steps` for each ray [S1, unofficial]. If `fixed_num_samples > 0`, `steps` is constant [S1, unofficial]. Otherwise:
    $$ \text{steps} = \frac{\sum_i \text{dists}_i}{\text{raymarching\_stepsize}} $$
    If `fine_num_sample_ratio > 0` (hierarchical fine pass), update steps:
    $$ \text{steps} = (\text{intersected\_voxel\_idx} \neq -1).\text{sum}(-1).\text{float}() \times \text{fine\_num\_sample\_ratio} $$

2.  **Inside-Voxel Sampling:**
    For each intersected voxel $v$ and ray $r$, sample points [S2, unofficial]. Convert world-space distance $d_w$ to sparse-space distance $d_s$ using the ray-voxel exit positions:
    $$ d_s = d_w - (d_w^{(r,v)} - d_s^{(r,v)}) $$
    where $d_w^{(r,v)}$ is the world-space distance of the ray-voxel exit, and $d_s^{(r,v)}$ is the sparse-space distance of the same exit point [S2, unofficial]. Perform uniform coarse sampling and importance sampling in the sparse space to generate $N_c$ coarse and $N_i$ importance samples [S2, unofficial].

3.  **Parallel Processing and Padding:**
    If the total number of rays `full_size` exceeds `chunk_size` (4096), split rays into chunks [S1, unofficial]. Process each chunk independently.
    -   Find `maxlen`, the maximum number of steps across all chunks [S1, unofficial].
    -   Pad outputs:
        -   `sampled_idx`: Fill masked/invalid positions with -1 [S1, unofficial].
        -   `sampled_depth`: Fill masked/invalid positions with `MAX_DEPTH` [S1, unofficial].
        -   `sampled_dists`: Fill masked/invalid positions with 0.0 [S1, unofficial].
    -   Concatenate chunks and truncate to `maxlen` [S1, unofficial].

4.  **Masking and Truncation:**
    Identify rays that hit the maximum depth boundary:
    $$ \text{sampled\_masks} = (\text{sampled\_depth} == \text{MAX\_DEPTH}) $$
    Calculate the maximum valid steps across all rays:
    $$ \text{num\_max\_steps} = \max_i (\sim \text{sampled\_masks}_i).\text{sum}() $$
    Truncate tensors to `num_max_steps`:
    $$ \text{sampled\_dists} = \text{sampled\_dists}[:, : \text{num\_max\_steps}] $$
    $$ \text{sampled\_idx} = \text{sampled\_idx}[:, : \text{num\_max\_steps}] $$

5.  **Compositing and Background Handling:**
    Render colors and depths. Identify missed rays (those with no valid voxel intersections) [S1, unofficial].
    $$ \text{missed} = \text{fill\_in}((\text{fullsize},), \text{hits}, \text{all\_results}['\text{missed}'], 1.0) $$
    Fill missed rays with background values:
    $$ \text{colors} = \text{fill\_in}((\text{fullsize}, 3), \text{hits}, \text{all\_results}['\text{colors}'], 0.0) $$
    $$ \text{depths} = \text{fill\_in}((\text{fullsize},), \text{hits}, \text{all\_results}['\text{depths}'], 0.0) $$
    Add background contribution:
    $$ \text{colors} += \text{missed}.\text{unsqueeze}(-1) \times \text{bg\_color} $$
    $$ \text{depths} += \text{missed} \times \text{BG\_DEPTH} $$
    where `bg_color` is derived from `all_results['colors']` and `BG_DEPTH` is `self.field.bg_color.depth` [S1, unofficial].

**Pseudocode:**

```python
import torch

def voxel_aware_masked_ray_sampling(rays, voxel_grid, args):
    """
    rays: (num_rays, 3) origins, (num_rays, 3) directions
    voxel_grid: Sparse voxel structure
    args: Configuration object
    """
    full_size = rays.shape[0]
    chunk_size = args.chunk_size if hasattr(args, 'chunk_size') else 4096
    max_hits = args.max_hits if hasattr(args, 'max_hits') else 60
    raymarching_stepsize = args.raymarching_stepsize if hasattr(args, 'raymarching_stepsize') else 0.01
    fine_num_sample_ratio = args.fine_num_sample_ratio if hasattr(args, 'fine_num_sample_ratio') else 0.0
    fixed_num_samples = args.fixed_num_samples if hasattr(args, 'fixed_num_samples') else 0
    bg_depth = args.background_depth if hasattr(args, 'background_depth') else 5.0
    MAX_DEPTH = 1e9

    # [G] Ray-box intersection logic to compute min_depth, max_depth, intersected_voxel_idx, probs, steps.
    min_depth, max_depth, intersected_voxel_idx, probs, steps = ray_voxel_intersection(rays, voxel_grid, max_hits)

    # Mask invalid voxels (idx == -1)
    valid_mask = intersected_voxel_idx != -1
    dists = (max_depth - min_depth).masked_fill(~valid_mask, 0.0)

    # Normalize probabilities
    dists_sum = dists.sum(dim=-1, keepdim=True)
    probs = dists / dists_sum.masked_fill(dists_sum == 0, 1.0)

    # Determine number of samples per ray
    if fixed_num_samples > 0:
        steps = torch.full((full_size,), fixed_num_samples, dtype=torch.float32, device=rays.device)
    else:
        steps = dists.sum(dim=-1) / raymarching_stepsize

    # Hierarchical fine pass adjustment
    if fine_num_sample_ratio > 0:
        valid_count = valid_mask.sum(dim=-1).float()
        steps = valid_count * fine_num_sample_ratio

    # Inside-voxel sampling
    sampled_dists, sampled_idx, sampled_depth = inside_voxel_sampling(
        rays, min_depth, max_depth, intersected_voxel_idx, probs, steps, args
    )

    # Parallel processing with chunking
    if full_size > chunk_size:
        chunks_dists, chunks_idx, chunks_depth = [], [], []
        for i in range(0, full_size, chunk_size):
            c_dists, c_idx, c_depth = inside_voxel_sampling(
                rays[i:i+chunk_size], min_depth[i:i+chunk_size], max_depth[i:i+chunk_size],
                intersected_voxel_idx[i:i+chunk_size], probs[i:i+chunk_size],
                steps[i:i+chunk_size], args
            )
            chunks_dists.append(c_dists)
            chunks_idx.append(c_idx)
            chunks_depth.append(c_depth)
        
        max_len = max(c.shape[1] for c in chunks_dists)
        padded_dists = [pad_tensor(c, max_len, 0.0) for c in chunks_dists]
        padded_idx = [pad_tensor(c, max_len, -1) for c in chunks_idx]
        padded_depth = [pad_tensor(c, max_len, MAX_DEPTH) for c in chunks_depth]
        
        sampled_dists = torch.cat(padded_dists, dim=0)
        sampled_idx = torch.cat(padded_idx, dim=0)
        sampled_depth = torch.cat(padded_depth, dim=0)
    else:
        max_steps = sampled_dists.shape[1]
        sampled_dists = pad_tensor(sampled_dists, max_steps, 0.0)
        sampled_idx = pad_tensor(sampled_idx, max_steps, -1)
        sampled_depth = pad_tensor(sampled_depth, max_steps, MAX_DEPTH)

    # Masking and Truncation
    sampled_masks = sampled_depth.eq(MAX_DEPTH)
    num_max_steps = (~sampled_masks).sum(dim=-1).max().item()
    
    # Generate ordered_index for gathering (standard sequential indices per ray)
    ordered_index = torch.arange(num_max_steps, device=rays.device).unsqueeze(0).expand(full_size, -1)
    
    sampled_dists = sampled_dists.gather(1, ordered_index).masked_fill_(sampled_masks, 0.0)[:, :num_max_steps]
    sampled_idx = sampled_idx.gather(1, ordered_index).masked_fill_(sampled_masks, -1)[:, :num_max_steps]
    sampled_depth = sampled_depth.gather(1, ordered_index).masked_fill_(sampled_masks, MAX_DEPTH)[:, :num_max_steps]

    # Compositing
    # [G] Volume rendering/compositing step aggregating sampled SDF and color values.
    colors, depths = render_volume(sampled_dists, sampled_idx, rays, voxel_grid)

    # Handle missed rays
    missed = (sampled_idx[:, 0] == -1)
    bg_color = torch.tensor([0.0, 0.0, 0.0], device=rays.device) # [G] Derived from field

    colors = fill_in(colors, ~missed, 0.0)
    depths = fill_in(depths, ~missed, 0.0)
    colors += missed.unsqueeze(-1) * bg_color
    depths += missed * bg_depth

    return colors, depths, sampled_dists, sampled_idx

def inside_voxel_sampling(rays, min_depth, max_depth, intersected_voxel_idx, probs, steps, args):
    """
    Samples points inside voxels using distance conversion.
    """
    num_rays = rays.shape[0]
    max_steps = steps.max().int().item()
    sampled_dists = torch.zeros((num_rays, max_steps), device=rays.device)
    sampled_idx = torch.full((num_rays, max_steps), -1, dtype=torch.long, device=rays.device)
    sampled_depth = torch.full((num_rays, max_steps), 1e9, dtype=torch.float32, device=rays.device)
    
    for i in range(num_rays):
        n_samples = int(steps[i].item())
        if n_samples == 0:
            continue
            
        voxel_indices = intersected_voxel_idx[i]
        valid_voxels = voxel_indices != -1
        valid_voxel_indices = voxel_indices[valid_voxels]
        
        d_min = min_depth[i][valid_voxels]
        d_max = max_depth[i][valid_voxels]
        
        # Generate sample offsets within each intersected voxel
        # Uniform coarse sampling + importance sampling in sparse space
        offsets = torch.linspace(0.0, 1.0, n_samples, device=rays.device)
        voxel_lengths = d_max - d_min
        sample_offsets = offsets * voxel_lengths.unsqueeze(0)
        
        # World-space distances
        d_w = d_min.unsqueeze(0) + sample_offsets
        
        # Distance conversion: d_s = d_w - (d_w_out - d_s_out)
        # d_w_out is d_max, d_s_out is the sparse-space exit distance (assumed equal to d_max for uniform sparse grid)
        d_s = d_w - (d_max.unsqueeze(0) - d_max.unsqueeze(0))
        
        # Fill tensors
        sampled_dists[i, :n_samples] = d_s
        sampled_idx[i, :n_samples] = valid_voxel_indices
        sampled_depth[i, :n_samples] = d_w
        
    return sampled_dists, sampled_idx, sampled_depth

def pad_tensor(tensor, max_len, pad_value):
    current_len = tensor.shape[1]
    if current_len < max_len:
        padding = torch.full((tensor.shape[0], max_len - current_len), pad_value, device=tensor.device)
        if tensor.dtype == torch.bool:
            padding = padding.bool()
        tensor = torch.cat([tensor, padding], dim=1)
    return tensor

def fill_in(tensor, mask, fill_value):
    tensor = tensor.clone()
    tensor[~mask] = fill_value
    return tensor
```

## Subtopics

### Sparse Voxel-based Sampling and Rendering
A rendering pipeline that dynamically allocates voxels only where objects and surfaces exist, restricting ray marching and volume integration to occupied regions [S3]. It performs ray-voxel intersection tests to identify sparse voxels traversed by camera rays, samples points within them, and applies volume rendering [S3]. Pitfalls include reduced throughput in high-complexity or noisy scenarios [S3]. Operating frequencies are typically 5 Hz for tracking and 2 Hz for optimization on the synthetic Replica dataset [S3].

## Operating characteristics
- **Time Complexity:** Depends on the number of intersected voxels per ray and the number of samples per voxel [S1, unofficial]. Parallel processing with chunking (default 4096 rays) helps manage memory and latency [S1, unofficial].
- **Memory Complexity:** Proportional to the number of rays and the maximum number of voxel intersections (`max_hits`, default 60) plus samples per intersection [S1, unofficial]. Padding ensures uniform tensor shapes [S1, unofficial].
- **Performance:** On a single NVIDIA RTX 3090, per-frame tracking takes ~150-200 ms and joint frame/map optimization takes 450-550 ms [S3]. Typical operating frequencies are 5 Hz for tracking and 2 Hz for optimization on the synthetic Replica dataset [S3].
- **Strengths:** Efficient for sparse scenes by ignoring empty space. Prevents invalid sampling outside voxels, which is crucial for sparse feature volumes [S2, unofficial].
- **Limitations:** Performance degrades in highly complex or noisy scenes [S3]. Requires careful handling of variable intersection counts via padding and masking [S1, unofficial].

## Background
Originating from the NSVF (Neural Sparse Voxel Fields) framework [S1, unofficial], this method was developed to address the inefficiency of dense volume rendering in sparse environments. It builds on the concept of sparse voxel grids, where only occupied regions are stored and processed [S3]. The hierarchical sampling strategy ensures that sampling points remain within valid voxels, avoiding the "air" sampling issues of unconstrained methods [S2, unofficial]. It is used in neural implicit reconstruction and rendering pipelines, particularly for dynamic scenes and SLAM systems [S3].

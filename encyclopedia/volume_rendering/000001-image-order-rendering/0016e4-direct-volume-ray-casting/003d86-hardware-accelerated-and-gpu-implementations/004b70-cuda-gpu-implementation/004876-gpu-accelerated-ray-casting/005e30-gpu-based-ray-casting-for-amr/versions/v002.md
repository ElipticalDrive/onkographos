## Background
Kähler et al. (2006) implemented this GPU-based ray-casting approach for AMR data [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial]. It builds on the re-accelerated volume rendering approach by Kähler & Hege (2002) [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial]. To enhance depth perception, rendered images are augmented with a background that is obtained by rendering a particle simulation of the formation of the early universe [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].

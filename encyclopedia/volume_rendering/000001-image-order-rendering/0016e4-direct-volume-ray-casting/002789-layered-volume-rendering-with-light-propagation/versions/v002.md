# Layered Volume Rendering with Light Propagation

Also called: Layer-based volumetric ray marching, Plane-sweep volume rendering

## Overview
Layered Volume Rendering with Light Propagation is a compute-shader-based technique that processes a stack of parallel planes to render volumetric data [S1, unofficial]. It decouples light transport into a separate propagation pass across discrete Z-layers, applying irradiance scaling and refraction gradients before accumulating view rays, which distinguishes it from standard front-to-back ray casting [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
The method operates on a stack of $N$ parallel planes [S1, unofficial].
*   **Input Buffers:**
    *   `ldb_in`: Light direction buffer (sampled at UV) [S1, unofficial].
    *   `lb_in`: Light color buffer (sampled at previous light hit point) [S1, unofficial].
    *   `vpb_in`: View position buffer [S1, unofficial].
    *   `vdb_in`: View direction buffer [S1, unofficial].
    *   `cb_in`: Color buffer (accumulated color and alpha) [S1, unofficial].
    *   `mb_in`: Material buffer [S1, unofficial].
    *   `volume_transfer`: Texture mapping `vec2(density_in, density_prev)` to `vec4(alpha_out)` [S1, unofficial].
    *   `medium_transfer`: Texture mapping `vec2(density_in, density_prev)` to `vec4(tint_out)` [S1, unofficial].
*   **Output Buffers:**
    *   `lb_out`: Updated light color [S1, unofficial].
    *   `ldb_out`: Updated light direction [S1, unofficial].
    *   `cb_out`: Final color and alpha [S1, unofficial].
    *   `mb_out`: Updated material [S1, unofficial].
    *   `vpb_out`: Next view position [S1, unofficial].
    *   `vdb_out`: Next view direction [S1, unofficial].
*   **Uniforms (UBO):**
    *   `plane_pixel_size`: Pixel size of rendering planes in world units [S1, unofficial].
    *   `distance_between_planes`: Constant Z-distance between consecutive layers [S1, unofficial].
    *   `lightVSPos`: View-space position of the light source [S1, unofficial].
    *   `light_color`: View-space color/intensity of the light source [S1, unofficial].
    *   `plane_count`: Total number of layers [S1, unofficial].
    *   `middle_of_front_plane_vs.z`: Z-coordinate of the front plane in view space [S1, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
All buffers are accessed via `imageLoad`/`imageStore` using `ivec2` pixel coordinates derived from `gl_GlobalInvocationID` [S1, unofficial].

**Steps:**

1.  **Coordinate Calculation:**
    Compute UV and pixel coordinates from `gl_GlobalInvocationID` [S1, unofficial]. Calculate the current layer's Z-coordinate in view space:
    $$ z_{current} = z_{front} - \text{layer\_index} \times d_{plane} $$
    where $z_{front}$ is `ubo.middle_of_front_plane_vs.z`, $\text{layer\_index}$ is the current layer index, and $d_{plane}$ is `ubo.distance_between_planes` [S1, unofficial].

2.  **Light Propagation Intersection:**
    Sample the current light direction $L_{dir}$ from `ldb_in` at the current UV [S1, unofficial]. Intersect a ray starting at $(0.5, 0.5, z_{current} + d_{plane})$ with direction $-L_{dir}$ to find the hit point $P_{lp\_prev}$ on the current plane [S1, unofficial].

3.  **Light Propagation Sampling & Averaging:**
    Sample the light color $L_{prev}$ from `lb_in` at $P_{lp\_prev}.xy$ [S1, unofficial]. Sample `ldb_in` at $P_{lp\_prev}.xy$ and apply 4-tap averaging:
    $$ L_{dir\_avg} = 0.5 \times L_{center} + 0.125 \times (L_{n} + L_{s} + L_{e} + L_{w}) $$
    Normalize $L_{dir\_avg}$ to get $L_{prev}$ [S1, unofficial]. If $\|L_{prev}\| < 10^{-6}$, set $L_{prev} = (0, 0, 1)$ to prevent NaN propagation [S1, unofficial].

4.  **Irradiance Scaling:**
    Compute screen-space area factors:
    $$ S_{current} = \left| \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} \right| \times \left| \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} \right| $$
    $$ S_{prev} = \left| \frac{\partial u_{prev}}{\partial x} \right| \times \left| \frac{\partial v_{prev}}{\partial y} \right| $$
    where derivatives are computed via `dFdx`/`dFdy` [G]. Compute irradiance scaling factor:
    $$ I = \begin{cases} S_{prev} / S_{current} & \text{if } S_{current} > 10^{-8} \\ 1.0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} $$
    This prevents division by zero [S1, unofficial].

5.  **Transfer Function Application:**
    Convert UV and $P_{lp\_prev}.xy$ to world positions at $z_{current}$ and $z_{current} + d_{plane}$ [S1, unofficial]. Sample `volume_density` and `volume_density_prev` [S1, unofficial]. Sample `volume_transfer` with `vec2(volume_density, volume_density_prev)` to get alpha $\alpha$ [S1, unofficial]. Sample `medium_transfer` to get tint $T$ via:
    $$ T = \text{mix}((1,1,1), M_{data}.rgb, M_{data}.a) $$
    Compute light color for output:
    $$ L_{out} = L_{prev} \times I \times (1.0 - \alpha) \times T $$
    Clamp $L_{out}$ to `vec3(20.0)` [S1, unofficial]. Store to `lb_out` [S1, unofficial].

6.  **Light Refraction Update:**
    Compute midpoint in world space: $P_{mid} = (P_{lp\_ws} + P_{prev\_lp\_ws}) \times 0.5$ [S1, unofficial]. Get refraction gradient $\nabla_{ref}$ [S1, unofficial]. Update light direction:
    $$ L_{next} = L_{prev} + \nabla_{ref} \times d_{plane} $$
    Normalize $L_{next}$ and store to `ldb_out` [S1, unofficial].

7.  **Ray Propagation Setup:**
    Sample view position $V_{pos}$ from `vpb_in` and direction $V_{dir}$ from `vdb_in` [S1, unofficial]. Convert $V_{pos}$ to world space [S1, unofficial]. Sample color buffer `cb_in` for $C_{prev}$ and $A_{prev}$ [S1, unofficial]. Sample material buffer `mb_in` for $M_{prev}$ [S1, unofficial]. Sample light buffer `lb_in` for light color/intensity $I_{light}$ [S1, unofficial].

8.  **Ray Propagation Intersection:**
    Intersect a ray from $V_{pos}$ with direction $-V_{dir}$ to the plane at $z_{current} + d_{plane}$ to get previous view point $V_{prev}$ [S1, unofficial]. Convert to world space [S1, unofficial].

9.  **Ray Propagation Shading:**
    Compute volume gradient $N$ at $V_{ws} + 0.5$ [S1, unofficial]. Let $V = \text{normalize}(-V_{dir})$ [S1, unofficial]. Compute light direction $L_{dir} = \text{normalize}(\text{ubo.lightVSPos} - V_{pos})$ [S1, unofficial]. Compute half-vector $H = \text{normalize}(L_{dir} + V)$ [S1, unofficial]. Compute specular term:
    $$ S_{pec} = \max(\text{dot}(N, H), 0.0)^{128.0} $$
    Compute specular intensity:
    $$ I_{spec} = \text{ubo.light\_color} \times S_{pec} \times 0.05 $$
    Compute material mask:
    $$ M_{ask} = \text{smoothstep}(0.0, 0.05, \text{volume\_density}) $$
    [S1, unofficial].

10. **Ray Propagation Accumulation:**
    Update color, alpha, and material:
    $$ C_{current} = C_{prev} + (1.0 - A_{prev}) \times M_{prev} \times (\alpha \times C_{light} \times I_{light} + I_{spec} \times M_{ask}) $$
    $$ A_{current} = A_{prev} + (1.0 - A_{prev}) \times \alpha $$
    $$ M_{current} = M_{prev} \times (1.0 - M_{data}.w) + M_{prev} \times M_{data}.xyz \times M_{data}.w $$
    [S1, unofficial].

11. **Ray Propagation Refraction & Next Step:**
    Compute midpoint: $P_{mid\_vp} = (V_{ws} + V_{prev\_ws}) \times 0.5 + 0.5$ [S1, unofficial]. Get refraction gradient $\nabla_{vd}$ [S1, unofficial]. Update view direction:
    $$ V_{next} = V_{dir} + \nabla_{vd} \times d_{plane} $$
    Normalize $V_{next}$ [S1, unofficial]. Intersect ray from $V_{pos}$ with $V_{next}$ to plane at $z_{current} - d_{plane}$ to get $V_{next\_pos}$ [S1, unofficial].

12. **Background Replacement:**
    If $\text{layer\_index} == \text{ubo.plane\_count} - 1$:
    Draw checkerboard background:
    $$ S_{cale} = 16 $$
    $$ UV_{check} = V_{next}.xy \times S_{cale} $$
    $$ F = \text{mod}(\lfloor UV_{check}.x \rfloor + \lfloor UV_{check}.y \rfloor, 2.0) $$
    $$ C_{bg} = \text{mix}((0.2, 0.2, 0.2), (0.5, 0.5, 0.5), F) $$
    Blend $C_{bg}$ with $C_{current}$ using $(1.0 - A_{current})$ [S1, unofficial]. Set $A_{current} = 1.0$ [S1, unofficial].

13. **Output Storage:**
    Store $C_{current}$ and $A_{current}$ to `cb_out`, $M_{current}$ to `mb_out`, $V_{next\_pos}$ to `vpb_out`, and $V_{next}$ to `vdb_out` [S1, unofficial].

**Pseudocode:**

```python
# Layered Volume Rendering with Light Propagation
# Self-contained Python-like pseudocode for a single compute shader invocation.
# Assumes global state: gl_GlobalInvocationID, ubo, textures, buffers.

def main():
    # 1. Coordinate Calculation
    uv = vec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy) / vec2(ubo.plane_pixel_size)
    layer_index = gl_GlobalInvocationID.z
    z_current = ubo.middle_of_front_plane_vs.z - float(layer_index) * ubo.distance_between_planes
    
    # 2. Light Propagation Intersection
    ld_current = imageLoad(ldb_in, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy)).xyz
    # Ray origin: (0.5, 0.5, z_current + distance), direction: -ld_current
    # Intersect with plane at z = z_current + distance
    lp_prev_xy = intersect_plane(vec2(0.5, 0.5), z_current + ubo.distance_between_planes, -ld_current).xy
    
    # 3. Light Propagation Sampling & Averaging
    l_prev = imageLoad(lb_in, ivec2(lp_prev_xy)).rgb
    ld_sample = imageLoad(ldb_in, ivec2(lp_prev_xy)).xyz
    
    # 4-tap averaging: center * 0.5, four neighbors * 0.125
    ld_avg = ld_sample * 0.5
    ld_avg += imageLoad(ldb_in, ivec2(lp_prev_xy + ivec2(1, 0))).xyz * 0.125
    ld_avg += imageLoad(ldb_in, ivec2(lp_prev_xy + ivec2(-1, 0))).xyz * 0.125
    ld_avg += imageLoad(ldb_in, ivec2(lp_prev_xy + ivec2(0, 1))).xyz * 0.125
    ld_avg += imageLoad(ldb_in, ivec2(lp_prev_xy + ivec2(0, -1))).xyz * 0.125
    
    ld_prev = normalize(ld_avg)
    if length(ld_prev) < 1e-6:
        ld_prev = vec3(0, 0, 1)
        
    # 4. Irradiance Scaling
    s_current = abs(dFdx(uv.x)) * abs(dFdy(uv.y))
    s_prev = abs(dFdx(lp_prev_xy.x)) * abs(dFdy(lp_prev_xy.y))
    
    if s_current > 1e-8:
        I = s_prev / s_current
    else:
        I = 1.0
        
    # 5. Transfer Function Application
    pos_curr_ws = uv_to_world(uv, z_current)
    pos_prev_ws = uv_to_world(lp_prev_xy, z_current + ubo.distance_between_planes)
    
    vol_density = sample_volume_density(pos_curr_ws)
    vol_density_prev = sample_volume_density(pos_prev_ws)
    
    alpha = sample_volume_transfer(vec2(vol_density, vol_density_prev)).a
    m_data = sample_medium_transfer(vec2(vol_density, vol_density_prev))
    tint = mix(vec3(1.0), m_data.rgb, m_data.a)
    
    l_out = l_prev * I * (1.0 - alpha) * tint
    l_out = clamp(l_out, vec3(0.0), vec3(20.0))
    
    imageStore(lb_out, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy), vec4(l_out, 1.0))
    
    # 6. Light Refraction Update
    mid_ws = (pos_curr_ws + pos_prev_ws) * 0.5
    ref_grad = compute_refraction_gradient(mid_ws) # [G] Gradient computation not specified in sources
    ld_next = ld_prev + ref_grad * ubo.distance_between_planes
    ld_next = normalize(ld_next)
    
    imageStore(ldb_out, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy), vec4(ld_next, 1.0))
    
    # 7. Ray Propagation Setup
    vp_current = imageLoad(vpb_in, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy)).xyz
    vd_current = imageLoad(vdb_in, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy)).xyz
    vp_ws = world_pos_from_view(vp_current)
    
    c_prev = imageLoad(cb_in, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy)).rgb
    a_prev = imageLoad(cb_in, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy)).a
    m_prev = imageLoad(mb_in, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy)).xyzw
    id_light = imageLoad(lb_in, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy)).r
    
    # 8. Ray Propagation Intersection
    vp_prev_ws = intersect_view_ray_to_plane(vp_current, -vd_current, z_current + ubo.distance_between_planes)
    
    # 9. Ray Propagation Shading
    n = compute_volume_gradient(vp_ws + 0.5) # [G] Gradient computation not specified in sources
    v = normalize(-vd_current)
    l_dir = normalize(ubo.lightVSPos - vp_current)
    h = normalize(l_dir + v)
    spec = pow(max(dot(n, h), 0.0), 128.0)
    is_val = ubo.light_color * spec * 0.05
    material_mask = smoothstep(0.0, 0.05, vol_density)
    
    # 10. Ray Propagation Accumulation
    c_light = l_out
    c_current = c_prev + (1.0 - a_prev) * m_prev * (alpha * c_light * id_light + is_val * material_mask)
    a_current = a_prev + (1.0 - a_prev) * alpha
    m_current = m_prev * (1.0 - m_data.w) + m_prev * m_data.xyz * m_data.w
    
    # 11. Ray Propagation Refraction & Next Step
    mid_vp_ws = (vp_ws + vp_prev_ws) * 0.5 + 0.5
    vd_grad = compute_view_refraction_gradient(mid_vp_ws) # [G] Gradient computation not specified in sources
    vd_next = vd_current + vd_grad * ubo.distance_between_planes
    vd_next = normalize(vd_next)
    
    vp_next_ws = intersect_view_ray_to_plane(vp_current, vd_next, z_current - ubo.distance_between_planes)
    
    # 12. Background Replacement
    if layer_index == ubo.plane_count - 1:
        scale = 16.0
        check_uv = vp_next_ws.xy * scale
        f = mod(floor(check_uv.x) + floor(check_uv.y), 2.0)
        bg = mix(vec3(0.2), vec3(0.5), f)
        c_current = mix(c_current, bg, 1.0 - a_current)
        a_current = 1.0
        
    # 13. Output Storage
    imageStore(cb_out, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy), vec4(c_current, a_current))
    imageStore(mb_out, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy), m_current)
    imageStore(vpb_out, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy), vec4(vp_next_ws, 1.0))
    imageStore(vdb_out, ivec2(gl_GlobalInvocationID.xy), vec4(vd_next, 1.0))

# Helper functions (standard operations or [G] marked)
def dFdx(v): return abs(v - v_left_neighbor) # [G] Built-in derivative approximation
def dFdy(v): return abs(v - v_top_neighbor)  # [G] Built-in derivative approximation
def uv_to_world(uv, z): return vec3(uv * ubo.plane_pixel_size, z) # [G] Simple affine mapping
def world_pos_from_view(vp): return vp # [G] Identity if already in world space, or apply inverse view matrix
def intersect_plane(origin_xy, target_z, dir):
    t = (target_z - origin_xy.z) / dir.z
    return vec3(origin_xy.xy + dir.xy * t, target_z)
def intersect_view_ray_to_plane(origin, dir, target_z):
    t = (target_z - origin.z) / dir.z
    return origin + dir * t
def compute_refraction_gradient(pos): return vec3(0.0) # [G] Placeholder for gradient lookup
def compute_volume_gradient(pos): return vec3(0.0) # [G] Placeholder for gradient lookup
def compute_view_refraction_gradient(pos): return vec3(0.0) # [G] Placeholder for gradient lookup
```

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** $O(N \times W \times H)$ per frame, where $N$ is the number of layers, and $W \times H$ is the resolution [S1, unofficial]. Each layer requires a constant number of texture samples and arithmetic operations per pixel [S1, unofficial].
*   **Memory Complexity:** $O(W \times H)$ for the input and output buffers [S1, unofficial]. The method requires ping-ponging buffers for light and view directions/colors across layers [S1, unofficial].
*   **Performance:** Performance is heavily dependent on the number of layers and the cost of texture sampling [S1, unofficial]. The use of compute shaders allows for parallel processing of pixels [S1, unofficial]. The 4-tap averaging and irradiance scaling add computational overhead but improve visual quality by smoothing light propagation [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Efficient handling of light transport in volumetric scenes by decoupling light and view rays [S1, unofficial]. The layer-based approach allows for easy integration of refraction and transfer functions [S1, unofficial]. The use of compute shaders provides flexibility in shader logic [S1, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** The method assumes a regular stack of parallel planes, which may not fit all volumetric data structures [S1, unofficial]. The refraction gradient calculation is a simplification and may not accurately model complex light bending [S1, unofficial]. The background replacement is a hack for the last layer and may not be suitable for all scenes [S1, unofficial].
*   **When to use:** Suitable for real-time rendering of volumetric data where light transport is important, such as clouds, smoke, or medical imaging [S1, unofficial].
*   **When not to use:** Not suitable for scenes with complex geometry or non-planar layer structures [S1, unofficial]. Not ideal for offline rendering where higher accuracy is required [S1, unofficial].

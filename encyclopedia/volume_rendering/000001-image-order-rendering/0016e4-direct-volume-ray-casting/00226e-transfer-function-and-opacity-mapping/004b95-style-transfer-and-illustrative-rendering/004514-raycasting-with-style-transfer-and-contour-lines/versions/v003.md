# Raycasting with Style Transfer and Contour Lines

Also called: FGC_T3 Raycasting, Style-based Volume Raycasting

## Overview
This method performs direct volume ray casting to render 3D scalar fields with artistic style transfer and illustrative contour lines. It solves the problem of making volumetric data visually expressive by mapping surface normals and material indices to 2D style textures (such as lit spheres or matcaps) and overlaying curvature-based contour lines. It differs from standard ray casting by replacing the final color accumulation with a style lookup, and differs from pure illustrative rendering by integrating the style transfer directly into the ray marching loop rather than as a post-process.

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Inputs:** A 3D volume texture (`VolumeTex`), an `ExitPoints` texture storing back-face coordinates, a 1D transfer function texture, a 1D index function texture, and a 2D array style transfer texture.
*   **Outputs:** A final color fragment (`FragColor`).

**Data Structures and Layout:**
*   `VolumeTex`: `sampler3D`. Stores density in the red channel (`x`).
*   `ExitPoints`: `sampler2D`. Stores the 3D exit point of the ray in the fragment's screen-space coordinate.
*   `transferFunctionTexture`: `sampler1D`. Maps density to opacity (green channel) and material index (red channel).
*   `indexFunctionTexture`: `sampler1D`. Maps the material index to a discrete style index.
*   `styleTransferTexture`: `sampler2DArray`. Contains $N$ 2D style images, where $N$ is `AVAILABLE_STYLE_COUNT`.
*   **Uniforms:**
    *   `NormalMatrix`: `mat4`. Transforms normals to view space.
    *   `StepSize`: `float`. Distance between samples. Default: `0.01f`.
    *   `Threshold`: `float`. Minimum density to process. Default: `0.15f`.
    *   `ScreenSize`: `vec2`. Resolution of the output buffer.
    *   `AVAILABLE_STYLE_COUNT`: `int`. Number of styles in the array texture. Value: `34`.
*   **Attributes/Inputs:**
    *   `EntryPoint`: `vec3`. The front-face entry point of the ray.
    *   `gl_FragCoord`: Built-in fragment coordinate.

**Algorithm Steps:**

1.  **Ray Initialization:**
    Retrieve the exit point from the `ExitPoints` texture using normalized fragment coordinates:
    $$ \text{exitPoint} = \text{texture}(\text{ExitPoints}, \frac{\text{gl\_FragCoord.st}}{\text{ScreenSize}}).\text{xyz} $$
    If the entry point equals the exit point, discard the fragment (background).
    Calculate the ray direction and length:
    $$ \text{rayDirection} = \text{exitPoint} - \text{EntryPoint} $$
    $$ \text{rayLength} = \|\text{rayDirection}\| $$
    Calculate the step vector:
    $$ \text{stepVector} = \text{StepSize} \times \frac{\text{rayDirection}}{\text{rayLength}} $$
    Initialize the ray start position. If `USE_NOISE_JITTER` is defined, add jitter:
    $$ \text{rayStart} += \text{stepVector} \times \text{snoise}\left(\frac{\text{gl\_FragCoord.xy}}{2.5}\right) $$
    Initialize accumulators:
    $$ \text{pos} = \text{rayStart} $$
    $$ \text{dst} = (0, 0, 0, 0) $$
    $$ \text{normal} = (1, 0, 0) $$
    $$ \text{baseColor} = (0, 0, 0, 0) $$
    $$ \text{src} = (0, 0, 0, 0) $$

2.  **Ray Marching Loop:**
    While accumulated opacity $\text{dst}.a < 1.0$ and $\text{rayLength} > 0$:
    
    a. **Sample Volume:**
    $$ \text{density} = \text{texture}(\text{VolumeTex}, \text{pos}).x $$
    If `USE_THRESHOLD` is defined and $\text{density} \le \text{Threshold}$, skip to step 2g.

    b. **Transfer Function Lookup:**
    $$ \text{opacity} = \text{texture}(\text{transferFunctionTexture}, \text{density}).g $$
    $$ \text{index} = \text{texture}(\text{transferFunctionTexture}, \text{density}).r $$
    
    c. **Style Index Determination:**
    $$ \text{styleIndex} = \lfloor \text{texture}(\text{indexFunctionTexture}, \text{index}).x \times \text{AVAILABLE\_STYLE\_COUNT} \rfloor $$

    d. **Normal Estimation:**
    Compute the gradient at `pos` with lookup value `density`:
    $$ \nabla = \begin{bmatrix} \text{texture}(\text{VolumeTex}, \text{pos} + (L, 0, 0)).x - \text{density} \\ \text{texture}(\text{VolumeTex}, \text{pos} + (0, L, 0)).x - \text{density} \\ \text{texture}(\text{VolumeTex}, \text{pos} + (0, 0, L)).x - \text{density} \end{bmatrix} $$
    where $L = \text{StepSize}$.
    Transform the gradient to view space:
    $$ \text{previousNormal} = \text{normal} $$
    $$ \text{normal} = \text{mat4}(\text{NormalMatrix}) \times \begin{bmatrix} \nabla \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}.\text{xyz} $$

    e. **Style Transfer:**
    Map the normal to 2D coordinates for the "litsphere" style:
    $$ \text{litCoords} = \begin{bmatrix} \text{normal}.x \\ -\text{normal}.y \end{bmatrix} \times 0.5 + 0.5 $$
    Sample the style texture:
    $$ \text{baseColor} = \text{texture}(\text{styleTransferTexture}, \begin{bmatrix} \text{litCoords} \\ \text{styleIndex} \end{bmatrix}) $$

    f. **Contour Lines (Optional):**
    If `USE_CONTOUR` is defined:
    Calculate curvature approximation:
    $$ \text{magnitudes} = \|\text{normal}\| \times \|\text{previousNormal}\| $$
    $$ \text{curvature} = \arccos\left(\frac{\text{normal} \cdot \text{previousNormal}}{\text{magnitudes}}\right) \times \text{StepSize} $$
    Calculate contour condition:
    $$ \text{thickness} = 1.0 $$
    $$ T_{kv} = \text{thickness} \times \text{curvature} $$
    $$ \text{cond} = \sqrt{T_{kv} \times (2.0 - T_{kv})} $$
    Calculate view dot product:
    $$ nDotV = |\text{normal} \cdot \text{normalize}(-\text{pos})| $$
    If $nDotV \le \text{cond}$, apply matcap style:
    $$ \text{litDelta} = 1.0 - \min(1.0, \frac{\text{cond} - nDotV}{\text{cond}}) $$
    $$ \text{adjustedLength} = \min(1.0, \frac{\|\text{normal}\|}{\text{litDelta}}) $$
    $$ \text{baseColor} = \text{texture}(\text{styleTransferTexture}, \begin{bmatrix} \text{matcap}(\text{pos}.xyz, \text{normal}).xy \\ \text{styleIndex} \end{bmatrix}) $$
    *(Note: The `matcap` function is not defined in the source code snippets provided; it is assumed to map position and normal to matcap coordinates.)*

    g. **Compositing:**
    $$ \text{src} = \begin{bmatrix} \text{baseColor}.rgb \\ \text{opacity} \end{bmatrix} $$
    Pre-multiply alpha:
    $$ \text{src}.rgb \times= \text{src}.a $$
    Composite into destination:
    $$ \text{dst} = (1.0 - \text{dst}.a) \times \text{src} + \text{dst} $$
    Advance ray:
    $$ \text{pos} += \text{stepVector} $$
    $$ \text{rayLength} -= \text{StepSize} $$

3.  **Output:**
    $$ \text{FragColor} = \text{dst} $$

**Pseudocode:**

```python
def raycast_with_style_transfer_and_contour_lines(
    volume_tex, exit_points_tex, transfer_func_tex, 
    index_func_tex, style_tex, normal_matrix,
    entry_point, screen_size, step_size=0.01, 
    threshold=0.15, available_style_count=34,
    use_noise_jitter=False, use_threshold=True, use_contour=False
):
    """
    Renders a single fragment using style transfer and optional contour lines.
    
    Args:
        volume_tex: 3D texture sampler for density.
        exit_points_tex: 2D texture sampler for exit points.
        transfer_func_tex: 1D texture sampler for opacity (g) and index (r).
        index_func_tex: 1D texture sampler for style index mapping.
        style_tex: 2D array texture for style images.
        normal_matrix: 4x4 matrix for normal transformation.
        entry_point: vec3, front face entry point.
        screen_size: vec2, output resolution.
        step_size: float, distance between samples.
        threshold: float, minimum density to process.
        available_style_count: int, number of styles in style_tex.
        use_noise_jitter: bool, enable jittered sampling.
        use_threshold: bool, skip low density.
        use_contour: bool, enable contour lines.
        
    Returns:
        vec4: Final color.
    """
    # 1. Ray Initialization
    # Retrieve exit point from texture using normalized fragment coordinates
    # Note: gl_FragCoord.st is assumed to be the x,y of the fragment
    exit_point = exit_points_tex.sample(screen_size.x, screen_size.y) # Pseudo-code for texture lookup
    
    # Discard if background
    if entry_point == exit_point:
        return vec4(0, 0, 0, 0)
    
    ray_direction = exit_point - entry_point
    ray_length = length(ray_direction)
    step_vector = step_size * normalize(ray_direction)
    
    ray_start = entry_point
    if use_noise_jitter:
        # snoise is a 2D simplex noise function
        ray_start += step_vector * snoise(screen_size.x / 2.5, screen_size.y / 2.5)
        
    pos = ray_start
    dst = vec4(0, 0, 0, 0)
    normal = vec3(1, 0, 0)
    base_color = vec4(0, 0, 0, 0)
    src = vec4(0, 0, 0, 0)
    
    # 2. Ray Marching Loop
    while dst.a < 1.0 and ray_length > 0:
        # Sample density
        density = volume_tex.sample(pos).x
        
        # Threshold check
        if use_threshold and density <= threshold:
            pos += step_vector
            ray_length -= step_size
            continue
            
        # Transfer function lookup
        tf_sample = transfer_func_tex.sample(density)
        opacity = tf_sample.g
        index = tf_sample.r
        
        # Style index determination
        idx_sample = index_func_tex.sample(index)
        style_index = int(idx_sample.x * available_style_count)
        
        # Normal estimation
        L = step_size
        look_up = density
        grad_e = volume_tex.sample(pos + vec3(L, 0, 0)).x - look_up
        grad_n = volume_tex.sample(pos + vec3(0, L, 0)).x - look_up
        grad_u = volume_tex.sample(pos + vec3(0, 0, L)).x - look_up
        gradient = vec3(grad_e, grad_n, grad_u)
        
        previous_normal = normal
        normal = (normal_matrix * vec4(gradient, 0)).xyz
        
        # Style transfer (Litsphere)
        lit_coords = vec2(normal.x, -normal.y) * 0.5 + 0.5
        base_color = style_tex.sample(vec3(lit_coords, style_index))
        
        # Contour lines
        if use_contour:
            magnitudes = length(normal) * length(previous_normal)
            if magnitudes > 0:
                curvature = acos(dot(normal, previous_normal) / magnitudes) * step_size
            else:
                curvature = 0.0
                
            thickness = 1.0
            t_kv = thickness * curvature
            cond = sqrt(t_kv * (2.0 - t_kv))
            
            n_dot_v = abs(dot(normal, normalize(-pos)))
            
            if n_dot_v <= cond:
                lit_delta = 1.0 - min(1.0, (cond - n_dot_v) / cond)
                # matcap function is not defined in source, assuming it returns vec2
                # matcap_coords = matcap(pos.xyz, normal) 
                # Placeholder for undefined matcap logic
                # base_color = style_tex.sample(vec3(matcap_coords, style_index))
                pass 
        
        # Compositing
        src = vec4(base_color.rgb, opacity)
        src.rgb *= src.a
        dst = (1.0 - dst.a) * src + dst
        
        pos += step_vector
        ray_length -= step_size
        
    return dst
```

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
*   **Precision:** The curvature calculation involves `acos` and division by magnitudes. If the normal or previous normal is zero or near-zero, this results in NaN or infinity. Guard against `magnitudes == 0`.
*   **Boundaries:** The gradient calculation samples `pos + (L,0,0)`, etc. If `pos` is near the volume boundary, these samples may be out of bounds. The source code mentions `isInBounds` and `sampleGradients` functions, implying boundary checks are required in the implementation, though the specific logic is not detailed in the shader snippets.
*   **Matcap Function:** The `matcap` function is called in the contour logic but is not defined in the provided source code. Its implementation is required for the contour feature to work.

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Linear with respect to the number of samples along the ray. Each sample involves texture lookups (volume, transfer function, index function, style texture) and gradient computation.
*   **Memory Complexity:** Depends on the size of the volume texture, exit points texture, and style transfer textures.
*   **Performance:** The method is computationally intensive due to multiple texture lookups per sample and the gradient calculation. The use of a 2D array texture for style transfer may have performance implications depending on the GPU driver and texture size.
*   **Strengths:** Produces visually appealing, artistic results. Integrates style and contours directly into the rendering pipeline.
*   **Limitations:** Requires pre-computed exit points. The `matcap` function is undefined in the source, making the contour feature incomplete. Gradient calculation is sensitive to noise and boundary conditions.

## Background
This method is based on the "FGC_T3" implementation found in the GitHub repository `jose-villegas/IllustrativeVolumeRendering` [S1, unofficial]. It represents a specific approach to illustrative volume rendering that combines direct ray casting with style transfer and contour lines.

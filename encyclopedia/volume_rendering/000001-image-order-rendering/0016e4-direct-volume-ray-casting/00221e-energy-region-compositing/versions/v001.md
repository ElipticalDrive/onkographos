# Energy Region Compositing

Also called: Energy Compositing

## Overview
Energy Region Compositing is a volume rendering technique that composites multiple energy volumes within a specific region of interest defined by a magnitude volume, applying a threshold to energy intensity before compositing [S1, unofficial]. It renders a volume using multiple energy volumes, blending colors based on normalized intensities above a threshold [S1, unofficial]. Unlike standard direct volume ray casting which uses a single transfer function for opacity and color, this method uses a magnitude volume to gate visibility and blends distinct energy contributions based on their relative intensity ratios [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Inputs:**
    *   `view_matrix`: A $4 \times 4$ matrix containing view vectors. Indices 0:3 are the U vector, 4:7 are the V vector, and 8:11 are the View vector [S1, unofficial].
    *   `volume`: The primary magnitude volume (scalar field) [S1, unofficial].
    *   `magnitudeVolume`: A separate volume defining the region of interest (magnitude) [S1, unofficial].
    *   `energy_volumes`: A dictionary mapping integer energy IDs to Volume objects [S1, unofficial].
    *   `image_size`: The width and height of the output image (assumed square) [S1, unofficial].
    *   `interactive_mode`: A boolean flag determining the step size [S1, unofficial].
*   **Outputs:**
    *   `image`: A flat array of RGBA values (8-bit integers) of size `image_size * image_size * 4` [S1, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
*   **Color Dictionary:** Maps energy IDs to RGB colors. Colors are generated using permutations of the list `[1, 0, 0.5, 1]` taken 3 at a time [S1, unofficial].
    *   `colorDictionary[0] = [0, 0, 0]` (Background/Default)
    *   `colorDictionary[id] = perm[i]` for other IDs [S1, unofficial].
*   **TFColor:** A temporary structure holding RGBA components (floats) used during accumulation [S1, unofficial].

**Parameters:**
*   `step`: Loop step size for pixel iteration. Set to 20 if `interactive_mode` is true, otherwise 1 [S1, unofficial].
*   `threshold`: Energy intensity ratio threshold. Set to 0.3 (30%) [S1, unofficial].

**Algorithm Steps:**

1.  **Initialization:**
    *   Clear the image array to zeros [S1, unofficial].
    *   Extract `u_vector`, `v_vector`, and `view_vector` from `view_matrix` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Calculate `image_center` as `image_size / 2` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Calculate `volume_center` as `[volume.dim_x / 2, volume.dim_y / 2, volume.dim_z / 2]` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Calculate `diagonal` extent:
        $$ \text{diagonal} = \lfloor \sqrt{3} \times \max(\text{dim}_x, \text{dim}_y, \text{dim}_z) / 2 \rfloor + 1 $$
        where $\text{dim}_x, \text{dim}_y, \text{dim}_z$ are the dimensions of the volume [S1, unofficial].

2.  **Pixel Loop:**
    *   Iterate over pixel coordinates `i` from 0 to `image_size` with step `step` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Iterate over pixel coordinates `j` from 0 to `image_size` with step `step` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Initialize accumulated color components `initial_r`, `initial_g`, `initial_b` to 0 [S1, unofficial].

3.  **Ray Marching:**
    *   Iterate along the ray depth `k` from `diagonal` down to `-diagonal` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Compute voxel coordinates using vector dot products:
        $$ \text{voxel\_cords} = \mathbf{u} \cdot (i - \text{image\_center}) + \mathbf{v} \cdot (j - \text{image\_center}) + \mathbf{view} \cdot k + \text{volume\_center} $$
        where $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}, \mathbf{view}$ are the extracted view vectors [S1, unofficial].
    *   Sample the `magnitudeVolume` at `voxel_cords` to get value $g$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   If $g \neq 0$:
        *   Sample the primary `volume` at `voxel_cords` to get the scalar value [S1, unofficial].
        *   Apply a transfer function `tfunc.get_color(value)` to get an initial `color_voxel` (RGBA) [S1, unofficial].
        *   Initialize `color_voxel` to `[0, 0, 0]` if the value is not valid or zero [S1, unofficial].
        *   For each `key` in `energy_volumes`:
            *   Get `intensity_max` for the energy volume (precomputed maximum intensity) [S1, unofficial].
            *   Sample the `energy_volumes[key]` at `voxel_cords` to get `energy_intensity` [S1, unofficial].
            *   Calculate ratio:
                $$ \text{ratio} = \frac{\text{energy\_intensity}}{\text{intensity\_max}} $$
            *   If `ratio` > 0.3:
                *   Update `color_voxel` by blending the current voxel color with the energy's dictionary color:
                    $$ \text{color\_voxel} = \text{color\_voxel} \times (1 - \text{ratio}) + \text{colorDictionary}[key] \times \text{ratio} $$
    *   Calculate `color_a` as the maximum component of `color_voxel` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Update the accumulated color using front-to-back compositing logic:
        $$ \text{current\_r} = \text{color\_a} \times \text{color\_voxel}[0] + (1 - \text{color\_a}) \times \text{initial\_r} $$
        $$ \text{current\_g} = \text{color\_a} \times \text{color\_voxel}[1] + (1 - \text{color\_a}) \times \text{initial\_g} $$
        $$ \text{current\_b} = \text{color\_a} \times \text{color\_voxel}[2] + (1 - \text{color\_a}) \times \text{initial\_b} $$
        $$ \text{initial\_r}, \text{initial\_g}, \text{initial\_b} = \text{current\_r}, \text{current\_g}, \text{current\_b} $$

4.  **Finalization:**
    *   After the ray loop, clamp and convert final colors to 8-bit integers:
        $$ \text{red} = \lfloor \text{current\_r} \times 255 \rfloor \text{ if } < 255 \text{ else } 255 $$
        (Same for green and blue) [S1, unofficial].
    *   Set alpha to 255 [S1, unofficial].
    *   Assign RGBA values to the image array at index `(j * image_size + i) * 4` [S1, unofficial].

def energy_region_compositing(view_matrix, volume, magnitude_volume, energy_volumes, image_size, interactive_mode):

    Renders a volume using Energy Region Compositing.
    """
    # 1. Initialization
    image = np.zeros((image_size * image_size * 4), dtype=np.uint8)
    
    # Extract view vectors
    u_vector = view_matrix[0:3].reshape(-1, 1)
    v_vector = view_matrix[4:7].reshape(-1, 1)
    view_vector = view_matrix[8:11].reshape(-1, 1)
    
    image_center = image_size / 2
    volume_center = np.asarray([volume.dim_x / 2, volume.dim_y / 2, volume.dim_z / 2]).reshape(-1, 1)
    
    # Calculate diagonal extent
    max_dim = max(volume.dim_x, volume.dim_y, volume.dim_z)
    diagonal = int(math.floor(np.sqrt(3) * max_dim / 2)) + 1
    
    # Define color dictionary
    perm = np.asarray(list(permutations([1, 0, 0.5, 1], 3)))
    ids = list(energy_volumes.keys())
    color_dictionary = {0: [0, 0, 0]}
    for i in range(len(ids)):
        color_dictionary[ids[i]] = perm[i]
        
    step = 20 if interactive_mode else 1
    
    # 2. Pixel Loop

            initial_r, initial_g, initial_b = 0.0, 0.0, 0.0
            
            # 3. Ray Marching
            for k in range(diagonal, -diagonal, -1):
                # Compute voxel coordinates
                voxel_cords = (np.dot(u_vector, i - image_center) + 
                               np.dot(v_vector, j - image_center) + 
                               np.dot(view_vector, k) + 
                               volume_center)

                    color_voxel = base_color[:3]
                    
                    # Blend energy volumes

                        energy_intensity = get_voxel_interpolated(e_vol, voxel_cords[0], voxel_cords[1], voxel_cords[2])

            # 4. Finalization

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** $O(W \cdot H \cdot D / \text{step})$, where $W, H$ are image dimensions and $D$ is the diagonal extent of the volume. The inner loop iterates over all energy volumes for each sample, adding a factor of $N_{\text{energies}}$.
*   **Memory Complexity:** $O(V + \sum E_i)$, where $V$ is the magnitude volume size and $E_i$ are the energy volume sizes. The image buffer is $O(W \cdot H)$.
*   **Performance:** The method is computationally expensive due to multiple volume samples per ray (one for magnitude, one for primary volume, and one for each energy volume). The `interactive_mode` parameter allows for faster, lower-resolution previews by increasing the step size [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Allows for the visualization of multiple distinct energy fields within a single volume, highlighting regions where specific energies are dominant relative to their maximums [S1, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** The reliance on a magnitude volume to gate visibility means regions with zero magnitude are completely invisible, regardless of energy content [S1, unofficial]. The fixed threshold of 0.3 may hide subtle energy contributions [S1, unofficial].

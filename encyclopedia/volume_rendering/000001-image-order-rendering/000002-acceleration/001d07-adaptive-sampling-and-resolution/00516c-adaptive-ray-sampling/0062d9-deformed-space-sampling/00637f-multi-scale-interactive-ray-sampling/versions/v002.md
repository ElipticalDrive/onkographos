# Multi-scale Interactive Ray Sampling

Also called: Compact multiscale interaction feature storage, Hyperplanar factorization

## Overview
Multi-scale Interactive Ray Sampling is a technique for projecting high-dimensional interaction features into a compact 4D space ($\mathbb{R}^4$) using ray sampling, allowing the retrieval of deformable features from local 4D deformable fields by maximizing sampling probability [S1, unofficial]. It builds on [Deformed Space Sampling](18731-deformed-space-sampling.md) by factorizing high-dimensional interaction variables into a 4D representation compressed via K-Planes, enabling efficient iterative sampling from coarse to fine scales [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
* **Input:** A ray $r(t)$ defined by origin $[o_x | o_\kappa]$ and direction $[d_x | d_\kappa]$, where $o_x, d_x \in \mathbb{R}^3$ and $o_\kappa, d_\kappa \in \mathbb{R}^\alpha$ (interaction variables) [S1, unofficial]. A probability decoder $\Theta(\kappa, \theta)$ and multi-scale feature planes [S1, unofficial].
* **Output:** The optimal 4D sampling point $p_u = [x | \kappa(u)]$ and the corresponding deformable features [S1, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
* **Feature Planes:** The multiple 4D local deformable spaces are compressed into $C^{24} = 6$ feature planes using K-Planes [S1, unofficial].
* **Probability Decoder:** $\Theta(\kappa, \theta)$ maps interaction variables $\kappa$ to probability $P$ [S1, unofficial].

**Steps:**
1. **Ray Definition:** Define the ray in the joint 3D and interaction space:
   $$ r(t) = [o_x | o_\kappa] + t [d_x | d_\kappa] $$
   where $t$ is the ray parameter [S1, unofficial].
2. **Intersection:** Determine the intersection point $p = [x | \kappa]$ with the interaction region, where $x \in \mathbb{R}^3$ is the 3D position and $\kappa \in \mathbb{R}^\alpha$ are the interaction variables [S1, unofficial].
3. **Probability Maximization:** Retrieve deformable features by finding the point $p_u$ that maximizes the sampling probability $P$ [S1, unofficial]. The interaction variables $\kappa$ are mapped to the most probable cluster region in the 4D space via:
   $$ u = \arg \max_i \{ P_i \} $$
   $$ P = \Theta(\kappa, \theta) $$
   where $p_u = [x | \kappa(u)]$ is the projected sample [S1, unofficial].
4. **Iterative Multi-scale Sampling:** Leverage the compressed K-Planes representation [S1, unofficial]. Iteratively sample from coarse to fine within the multi-scale feature plane [S1, unofficial]. At each scale, retrieve the maximum probability interaction variables $\kappa_u$ and indices $u$ [S1, unofficial].
5. **Feature Retrieval:** Use the retrieved $\kappa_u$ to fetch deformable features and render the local deformable scene color at the sampling point [S1, unofficial].

**Training Optimization:**
The probability decoder $\Theta$ is optimized by minimizing the focal loss $L_{focus}$ across training camera views to minimize mask errors [S1, unofficial]:
$$ L_{focus} = \beta \cdot \left( 1 - e^{\sum_{i=1}^\alpha (P_i^\gamma / M_i)} \right) \cdot \left( -\sum_{i=1}^M \frac{1}{M_i} \log(\hat{P}_i) \right) $$
where:
* $M$ is the ground truth mask label [S1, unofficial].
* $\hat{P}$ is the probability map rendered from the interactive probability field [S1, unofficial].
* $\beta$ is a balancing factor [S1, unofficial].
* $\gamma$ is a focusing parameter [S1, unofficial].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
* **Boundary Blurring:** Optimizing the interaction probability decoder with varying masks can lead to blurred boundaries in the local deformable field, causing ray sampling and feature storage conflicts between adjacent regions [S1, unofficial].
* **Feature Repulsion:** When a point moves from the cluster center of one region $R_i$ towards another $R_j$, the probability of belonging to $R_i$ decreases [S1, unofficial]. Special handling (Feature Repulsion and Probability Rejection) is required to manage these transitions [S1, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
* **Complexity:** Not given in the sources.
* **Performance:** Compresses multiple 4D local deformable spaces into only 6 feature planes using K-Planes, reducing storage requirements [S1, unofficial].
* **Strengths:** Projects high-dimensional interaction features into a compact 4D space, enabling efficient retrieval via ray sampling [S1, unofficial].
* **Limitations:** Prone to blurred boundaries if not properly regularized; requires careful handling of adjacent region transitions [S1, unofficial].

## Background
Multi-scale Interactive Ray Sampling is introduced in the context of "LiveScene: Language Embedding Interactive Radiance Fields for Physical Scene Rendering and Control" [S1, unofficial]. It addresses the challenge of storing and retrieving high-dimensional interaction features for deformable scenes by factorizing them into a 4D space and compressing them using K-Planes [S1, unofficial].

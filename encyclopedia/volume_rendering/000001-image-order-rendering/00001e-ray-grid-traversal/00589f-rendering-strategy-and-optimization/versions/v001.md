# Rendering Strategy and Optimization


## Overview
Rendering Strategy and Optimization defines the high-level policies for executing ray-grid traversal, including the order of cell processing, the grouping of rays for coherence, and the cost models used to select data structures or resolutions. It solves the problem of translating geometric traversal sequences into efficient, correct image buffers by addressing computational bottlenecks, memory bandwidth limits, and optical correctness requirements. It differs from sibling methods by focusing on *how* and *when* to traverse (strategy/coherence) and *what* to traverse (cost/resolution), rather than the specific geometric algorithm for finding the next cell, which is handled by the parent [Ray-grid traversal](17-ray-grid-traversal.md).

## Shared principles
All methods in this family build on [Ray-grid traversal](17-ray-grid-traversal.md), which advances analytically from one cell boundary to the next . The algorithm determines the next cell by comparing the distances to the upcoming boundaries on the x, y, and z axes, selecting the minimum distance to advance .

Beyond the geometric traversal logic, the shared principles involve:
1.  **Optical Correctness:** Ensuring that the processing order respects the viewing direction to correctly accumulate opacity and color, typically requiring front-to-back compositing for absorption-based rendering .
2.  **Computational Efficiency:** Minimizing the total rendering time by reducing redundant evaluations of empty space or coarse sampling, often through hardware-aware optimizations or algorithmic shortcuts .
3.  **Coherence Exploitation:** Leveraging spatial or temporal coherence among ray bundles to process multiple rays together, reducing divergence and overhead .

## Subtopics

### [Coherent and Connectivity Traversal](14777-coherent-and-connectivity-traversal.md)
Accelerates traversal by exploiting spatial coherence among ray bundles or enforcing topological connectivity rules. It solves the problem of packet divergence in coherent ray tracing and normal inaccuracies in uniform grid traversal, distinguishing itself from standard cell-by-cell methods like [Digital Differential Analyzer (DDA) Traversal](3061-digital-differential-analyzer-dda-traversal.md).

### [Rendering Order and Strategy](14814-rendering-order-and-strategy.md)
Defines the sequence in which volumetric cells are processed to ensure correct optical compositing and efficient occlusion culling. It decouples geometric traversal logic from semantic processing order, solving the problem of determining which cells contribute to the final pixel color and in what order.

### [Optimization and Cost Models](15795-optimization-and-cost-models.md)
Encompasses techniques to accelerate traversal via hardware awareness or algorithmic shortcuts and determines optimal data structures/resolutions. It addresses the computational bottleneck of evaluating every voxel, focusing on *how* to execute traversal more efficiently and *what* resolution to use to minimize total rendering time.

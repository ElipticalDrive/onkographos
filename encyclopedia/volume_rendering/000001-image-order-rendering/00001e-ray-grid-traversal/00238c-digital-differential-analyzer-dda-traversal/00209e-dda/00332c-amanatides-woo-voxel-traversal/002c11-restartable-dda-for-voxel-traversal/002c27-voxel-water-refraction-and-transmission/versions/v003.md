# Voxel Water Refraction and Transmission

Also called: Phase 1-3 Water March, See-through water marcher

## Overview
A three-phase rendering technique for simulating light interaction with a voxel-based water surface and volume [S1, unofficial]. It solves the problem of rendering transparent, refractive water over a complex terrain by marching a ray from the camera to the surface, calculating surface reflections and refraction, and then transmitting light through the water volume to the background [S1, unofficial]. It is distinct from full volumetric path tracing by using a deterministic ray-marching approach optimized for voxel grids, relying on [Restartable DDA for Voxel Traversal](5187-restartable-dda-for-voxel-traversal.md) to handle the change in ray direction at the surface [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs:**
- Camera position ($org$) and direction ($dir$) for the current pixel [S1, unofficial].
- Inverse view-projection matrix ($U.inv\_view\_proj$) for ray origin reconstruction [S1, unofficial].
- Voxel grid description ($vg$) including extent, cell sizes, and bounds [S1, unofficial].
- Sky cube map ($sky\_cube$) for environment reflection [S1, unofficial].
- Material data structure ($vg$) for reading voxel contents [S1, unofficial].
- Parameters: $U.water\_ior$ (Index of Refraction), $U.depth\_fog\_density$, $U.extinction\_rgb$, $U.deep\_water\_color$, $U.max\_steps$ [S1, unofficial].

**Outputs:**
- Final pixel color ($color$) after tonemapping [S1, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
- `DdaState W`: State structure for the DDA traversal, containing current voxel index ($W.idx$), current distance along ray ($W.t\_cur$), ray direction ($W.d$), and the axis of the current voxel face ($W.axis$) [S1, unofficial]. This structure is re-initialized in Phase 2 [S1, unofficial].

**Steps:**

1.  **Ray Initialization (Camera to Surface):** Construct the ray origin and direction [S1, unofficial]. For perspective projection, the origin is the camera position [S1, unofficial]. For orthographic projection, the origin is backed up beyond the grid AABB [S1, unofficial].
    $$
    p_0 = U.inv\_view\_proj \cdot [ndc_x, ndc_y, 0.0, 1.0]^T
    $$
    $$
    p_1 = U.inv\_view\_proj \cdot [ndc_x, ndc_y, 1.0, 1.0]^T
    $$
    $$
    near\_pt = p_0.xyz / p_0.w
    $$
    $$
    dir = normalize(p_1.xyz / p_1.w - near\_pt)
    $$
    $$
    org = \begin{cases} U.camera\_pos & \text{if } U.ortho\_backup == 0 \\ near\_pt - dir \cdot U.ortho\_backup & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}
    $$

2.  **Phase 1: Surface Entry:** March the ray from $org$ along $dir$ using the DDA until the first non-air/water cell is found [S1, unofficial]. Let the entry point be $entry\_p$ and the entry axis be $entry\_axis$ [S1, unofficial].

3.  **Phase 2: Surface Interaction:** Calculate the surface normal $n_{entry}$ based on the entry axis [S1, unofficial]. Calculate the view vector $V$ from the entry point to the camera [S1, unofficial].
    $$
    V = normalize(org - entry\_p)
    $$
    Calculate Fresnel reflectance $F$ using the Schlick approximation:
    $$
    nv = \max(dot(n_{entry}, V), 0.0)
    $$
    $$
    F = 0.02 + 0.98 \cdot (1.0 - nv)^5
    $$
    Calculate the reflection ray $R$:
    $$
    R = reflect(-V, n_{entry})
    $$
    Sample the sky cube for the reflection color, adding sun specular highlights:
    $$
    reflection = sky\_cube.sample(R).rgb + U.sun\_color \cdot \max(dot(R, sun), 0.0)^{U.sun\_shininess}
    $$
    Calculate the refracted ray direction $rdir$:
    $$
    rdir = refract(dir, n_{entry}, 1.0 / U.water\_ior)
    $$
    Guard against degenerate refraction (total internal reflection or grazing angles):
    $$
    \text{if } dot(rdir, rdir) < 10^{-6} \text{ then } rdir = dir
    $$
    $$
    rdir = normalize(rdir)
    $$
    Re-initialize the DDA state $W$ with the entry point (offset slightly along $rdir$ to avoid self-intersection) and the refracted direction:
    $$
    walking = dda\_init(W, entry\_p + rdir \cdot 10^{-4}, rdir, G)
    $$

4.  **Phase 3: Volume Transmission:** Initialize $water\_dist = 0.0$, $end\_mat = MAT\_AIR$, $end\_axis = 1$, and $exited\_up = false$ [S1, unofficial]. Loop while $walking$ and $steps < U.max\_steps$:
    1.  Increment $steps$.
    2.  Read material $m$ at $W.idx$.
    3.  If $m$ is not $MAT\_AIR$, $MAT\_WATER$, or $MAT\_BUBBLE$:
        - Set $end\_mat = m$, $end\_axis = W.axis$ [S1, unofficial].
        - Break loop.
    4.  Record $t\_enter = W.t\_cur$.
    5.  Step the DDA: $alive = dda\_step(W, G)$.
    6.  If $m == MAT\_WATER$:
        - $water\_dist += W.t\_cur - t\_enter$.
    7.  If not $alive$:
        - Set $exited\_up = (rdir.y > 0.0)$.
        - Break loop.

5.  **Special Material Handling:** If $end\_mat$ is $MAT\_FISH$, $MAT\_FIRE$, or $MAT\_LAVA$, render the material opaque and unattenuated by the water path or surface reflection [S1, unofficial].
    - Fish: Return tonemapped terrain color for fish.
    - Fire: Return tonemapped palette color multiplied by 3.0.
    - Lava: Return tonemapped palette color multiplied by 1.25.

6.  **Background and Attenuation:** Determine the background color $bg$ behind the water:
    - If $end\_mat \neq MAT\_AIR$: $bg = terrain\_color(end\_mat, face\_normal(end\_axis, W.d), sun, U)$ [S1, unofficial].
    - Else if $exited\_up$: $bg = sky\_cube.sample(cube\_smp, rdir).rgb$ [S1, unofficial].
    - Else: $bg = U.deep\_water\_color$.

    Apply Beer-Lambert transmission:
    $$
    T = \exp(-U.depth\_fog\_density \cdot water\_dist \cdot U.extinction\_rgb)
    $$
    $$
    refr = bg \cdot T + U.deep\_water\_color \cdot (1.0 - T)
    $$

7.  **Final Composition:** Mix the refracted and reflected colors based on Fresnel reflectance:
    $$
    color = mix(refr, reflection, F)
    $$
    Apply ACES tonemapping and return [S1, unofficial].

**Pseudocode:**

```python
# Constants
MAT_AIR = 0
MAT_WATER = 1
MAT_BUBBLE = 2
MAT_FISH = 3
MAT_FIRE = 4
MAT_LAVA = 5

def dda_init(start_pos, direction, grid):
    # Initialize Amanatides-Woo DDA state [G]
    W = {}
    W['idx'] = [int((start_pos[i] - grid['bmin'][i]) / grid['cell'][i]) for i in range(3)]
    W['t_cur'] = 0.0
    W['d'] = direction
    W['step'] = [1 if direction[i] > 0 else -1 for i in range(3)]
    W['t_max'] = [0.0, 0.0, 0.0]
    W['t_step'] = [0.0, 0.0, 0.0]
    for i in range(3):
        if direction[i] != 0:
            # Distance to next grid boundary
            boundary = grid['bmin'][i] + (W['idx'][i] + 1) * grid['cell'][i] if direction[i] > 0 else grid['bmin'][i] + W['idx'][i] * grid['cell'][i]
            W['t_max'][i] = (boundary - start_pos[i]) / direction[i]
            W['t_step'][i] = grid['cell'][i] / abs(direction[i])
        else:
            W['t_max'][i] = float('inf')
            W['t_step'][i] = float('inf')
    W['axis'] = -1
    return W

def dda_step(W, grid):
    # Advance DDA to next voxel [G]
    if W['t_max'][0] < W['t_max'][1] and W['t_max'][0] < W['t_max'][2]:
        axis = 0
    elif W['t_max'][1] < W['t_max'][2]:
        axis = 1
    else:
        axis = 2
    W['idx'][axis] += W['step'][axis]
    W['t_cur'] = W['t_max'][axis]
    W['t_max'][axis] += W['t_step'][axis]
    W['axis'] = axis
    # Check bounds against grid dimensions
    if (W['idx'][0] < grid['bmin_idx'][0] or W['idx'][0] >= grid['bmax_idx'][0] or
        W['idx'][1] < grid['bmin_idx'][1] or W['idx'][1] >= grid['bmax_idx'][1] or
        W['idx'][2] < grid['bmin_idx'][2] or W['idx'][2] >= grid['bmax_idx'][2]):
        return False
    return True

def read_material(vg, idx):
    # Read material ID from voxel grid at index [G]
    return vg.materials[idx[0], idx[1], idx[2]]

def march_to_surface(org, dir, vg, G, max_steps):
    # Phase 1: March from camera to first non-air/water cell [S1, unofficial]
    W = dda_init(org, dir, G)
    steps = 0
    while steps < max_steps:
        m = read_material(vg, W['idx'])
        if m != MAT_AIR and m != MAT_WATER:
            return [org[i] + dir[i] * W['t_cur'] for i in range(3)], W['axis']
        alive = dda_step(W, G)
        if not alive: break
        steps += 1
    return org, 1

def get_surface_normal(axis):
    # Returns axis-aligned normal [G]
    n = [0.0, 0.0, 0.0]
    n[axis] = 1.0
    return n

def voxel_water_refraction(ndc, inv_view_proj, camera_pos, ortho_backup,
                           sky_cube, vg, G, U, sun):
    # 1. Ray Initialization
    p0 = inv_view_proj * [ndc.x, ndc.y, 0.0, 1.0]
    p1 = inv_view_proj * [ndc.x, ndc.y, 1.0, 1.0]
    near_pt = [p0[0]/p0[3], p0[1]/p0[3], p0[2]/p0[3]]
    dir = normalize([p1[0]/p1[3] - near_pt[0], p1[1]/p1[3] - near_pt[1], p1[2]/p1[3] - near_pt[2]])

    if ortho_backup == 0:
        org = camera_pos
    else:
        org = [near_pt[i] - dir[i] * ortho_backup for i in range(3)]

    # Phase 1: March to surface
    entry_p, entry_axis = march_to_surface(org, dir, vg, G, U.max_steps)

    # Phase 2: Surface Interaction
    n_entry = get_surface_normal(entry_axis)
    V = normalize([org[i] - entry_p[i] for i in range(3)])
    nv = max(dot(n_entry, V), 0.0)
    F = 0.02 + 0.98 * pow(1.0 - nv, 5.0)

    R = reflect([-V[i] for i in range(3)], n_entry)
    reflection = sky_cube.sample(R).rgb + \
                 U.sun_color * pow(max(dot(R, sun), 0.0), U.sun_shininess)

    rdir = refract(dir, n_entry, 1.0 / U.water_ior)
    if dot(rdir, rdir) < 1e-6:
        rdir = dir
    rdir = normalize(rdir)

    # Re-initialize DDA for refraction
    W = dda_init([entry_p[i] + rdir[i] * 1e-4 for i in range(3)], rdir, G)
    walking = True
    steps = 0
    water_dist = 0.0
    end_mat = MAT_AIR
    end_axis = 1
    exited_up = False

    # Phase 3: Volume Transmission
    while walking and steps < U.max_steps:
        steps += 1
        m = read_material(vg, W['idx'])

        if m != MAT_AIR and m != MAT_WATER and m != MAT_BUBBLE:
            end_mat = m
            end_axis = W['axis']
            break

        t_enter = W['t_cur']
        alive = dda_step(W, G)

        if m == MAT_WATER:
            water_dist += W['t_cur'] - t_enter

        if not alive:
            exited_up = rdir[1] > 0.0
            break

    # Special Materials
    if end_mat == MAT_FISH:
        return aces_tonemap(terrain_color(MAT_FISH, face_normal(end_axis, W['d']), sun, U))
    if end_mat == MAT_FIRE:
        return aces_tonemap(U.palette[MAT_FIRE] * 3.0)
    if end_mat == MAT_LAVA:
        return aces_tonemap(U.palette[MAT_LAVA] * 1.25)

    # Background
    if end_mat != MAT_AIR:
        bg = terrain_color(end_mat, face_normal(end_axis, W['d']), sun, U)
    elif exited_up:
        bg = sky_cube.sample(rdir).rgb
    else:
        bg = U.deep_water_color

    # Beer-Lambert
    T = [exp(-U.depth_fog_density * water_dist * U.extinction_rgb[i]) for i in range(3)]
    refr = [bg[i] * T[i] + U.deep_water_color[i] * (1.0 - T[i]) for i in range(3)]

    color = [refr[i] * (1.0 - F) + reflection[i] * F for i in range(3)]
    return aces_tonemap(color)
```

## Operating characteristics
- **Time Complexity:** Linear with respect to the number of voxels traversed in the water volume and the distance to the surface [S1, unofficial]. Bounded by $U.max\_steps$ [S1, unofficial].
- **Memory Complexity:** Constant, $O(1)$, aside from the voxel grid storage [S1, unofficial].
- **Strengths:** Efficiently handles refraction and reflection in a single pass without recursive ray tracing. Correctly handles complex terrain occlusion behind the water [S1, unofficial].
- **Limitations:** Does not simulate subsurface scattering or multiple bounces within the water. Accuracy depends on voxel resolution [S1, unofficial].
- **When to use:** Real-time rendering of voxel-based water bodies where performance is critical and full volumetric path tracing is too expensive [S1, unofficial].

## Background
Originating from the "Vox-Ocean" project by wlim33, this method implements a specialized water rendering pipeline for voxel dioramas [S1, unofficial]. It combines standard ray-marching techniques with specific optimizations for voxel grids, such as the restartable DDA for handling refraction [S1, unofficial]. The technique is documented in the project's source code and shader implementations [S1, unofficial].

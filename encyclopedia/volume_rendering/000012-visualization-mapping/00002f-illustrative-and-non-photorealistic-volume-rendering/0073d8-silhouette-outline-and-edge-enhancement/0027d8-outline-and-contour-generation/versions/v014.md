# Outline and Contour Generation


## Overview
Outline and Contour Generation is a family of non-photorealistic volume rendering techniques that enhance perceptual clarity by explicitly rendering edges, silhouettes, and topological boundaries as line primitives or stylized strokes [G]. Unlike standard photorealistic volume rendering which relies on shading and opacity to imply form, this family solves the problem of ambiguous object boundaries and occlusion in complex volumetric data by providing high-contrast geometric cues [S1, unofficial]. It sets itself apart from sibling methods like [Line Illustration Detection](772-line-illustration-detection.md) by focusing on global object boundaries and topological features rather than local surface texture or hatching, and from [Contour Rendering](8471-contour-rendering.md) by often operating in object or image space to define structural limits rather than just scalar iso-surfaces.

## Shared principles
The family shares the fundamental goal of enhancing perceptual clarity through non-photorealistic edge representation [G]. Common mechanisms include:
1. **Edge Detection:** Identifying boundaries based on local volumetric properties (gradients, Laplacian of Gaussian) or image-space properties (depth, normal discontinuities) .
2. **Line Rendering:** Converting detected edges into line primitives (strips, segments) or stylized strokes .

## Subtopics

### [Object Contour NPR](787-object-contour-npr.md)
Generates view-dependent line primitives demarcating where an object's surface turns away from the viewer, relying on gradient magnitude and viewing angle for real-time tracing.

### [Image Space Outline Generation](788-image-space-outline-generation.md)
A post-processing technique detecting discontinuities in screen-space depth or normal buffers to define boundaries without analytical geometry processing.

### [Lasso Contour Rendering](1855-lasso-contour-rendering.md)
An interactive technique allowing users to manually define regions of interest to generate contour overlays, isolating complex structures lacking sharp boundaries [G].

### [Curvature-Controlled Style Contours](5350-curvature-controlled-style-contours.md)
Regulates contour thickness based on normal curvature to prevent uncontrolled variation in standard dot-product silhouette methods, integrating curvature-aware detection with style transfer.

### [Silhouette Rendering](7959-silhouette-rendering.md)
An object-space technique extracting significant edges (silhouettes and feature lines) from geometry to render "interesting" features with stylized strokes while leaving others unmarked.

### [Topological Landscape](8481-topological-landscape.md)
Maps the connectivity of a scalar field’s level sets onto a 3D terrain model using the contour tree, using peaks and valleys to encode topological events like merges and splits.

### [Boundary Enhancement Volume Style](10863-boundary-enhancement-volume-style.md)
Highlights feature boundaries within volumetric data while reducing visual noise by detecting local intensity gradients, distinct from standard photorealistic shading.

### [Suggestive Contour Extraction](13101-suggestive-contour-extraction.md)
Identifies surface locations where radial curvature is zero but increasing towards the viewer, revealing hidden or occluded geometry such as indentation rims.

### [Bounding Wall Contour Overlay](16354-bounding-wall-contour-overlay.md)
Displays line contours of a reference scalar field on the vertical bounding walls of a 3D volume to provide spatial context without occluding primary data.

#### Ghosted Views with Contours
Ghosted Views with Contours is a specific application of outline generation used to clarify internal structures obscured by context objects [S1, unofficial]. The method involves pre-segmenting the most interesting internal structures (e.g., small internal organs) and identifying context information (e.g., the body) that obscures them [S1, unofficial]. In areas of occlusion, the visual representation of the context object is reduced to contours, providing a clear view of the interesting internal organ while maintaining spatial context [S1, unofficial]. This technique is particularly useful in medical visualization, such as CT-angiography, where vessels must be viewed clearly against surrounding tissue and bone [S1, unofficial].

## Background
The concept of using outlines and contours to enhance volume rendering stems from the broader field of Illustrative Visualization, which applies artistic techniques to scientific data. The specific application of ghosted views with contours to clarify internal structures in medical datasets, such as the Leopard gecko dataset, was documented in the context of expressive visualizations for medicine and science [S1, unofficial]. This approach addresses the limitations of standard cut-away techniques by providing continuous boundary cues rather than just removing occluding geometry.

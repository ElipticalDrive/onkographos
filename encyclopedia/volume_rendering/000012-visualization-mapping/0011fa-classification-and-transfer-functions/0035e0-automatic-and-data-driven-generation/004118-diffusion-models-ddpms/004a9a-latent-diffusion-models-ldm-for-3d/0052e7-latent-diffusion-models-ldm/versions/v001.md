# Latent Diffusion Models (LDM)

Also called: Hierarchical Latent Diffusion Models, NF-LDM, Stable Diffusion for 3D

## Overview
Latent Diffusion Models (LDM) for 3D scene generation are generative models that learn a hierarchical prior distribution over latent variables representing voxel-based neural fields, enabling the synthesis of complex 3D scenes from text or other conditional inputs [S2]. Unlike global latent resampling which regenerates the entire scene, this method allows for localized modifications by treating the 3D coarse latent as an image and applying diffusion-based inpainting to specific spatial regions [S2]. It builds on [Latent Diffusion Models (LDM) for 3D](12787-latent-diffusion-models-ldm-for-3d.md) by explicitly defining a hierarchical generative process $p(V, g, c, f)$ using Denoising Diffusion Models (DDMs) to model the dependencies between global geometry, coarse structure, and fine details [S2].

## How it works
The method defines a generative model over a voxel-based scene representation $V$ using three latent variables: $g$ (global geometry/camera trajectory), $c$ (coarse structure), and $f$ (fine details), such that $p(V, g, c, f) = p(V |g, c, f)p(f |g, c)p(c|g)p(g)$ [S2]. The model is trained using Denoising Diffusion Models (DDMs) [24] to learn the reverse process of a fixed Markovian forward process that adds noise to data $x_0$ until it approaches a standard normal distribution [S2].

### Inputs and Outputs
*   **Inputs:** Conditioning variables (e.g., text prompts, camera trajectories, or other conditional inputs) and noise samples. For scene editing, the input includes a mask or region specification for the coarse latent $c$ to be edited [S2].
*   **Outputs:** Sampled latent variables $g, c, f$ which are decoded into a voxel-based neural field $V$ representing the 3D scene [S2].

### Data Structures
*   **Latent Variables:**
    *   $g$: Global geometry and camera trajectory information. Camera trajectory information is concatenated to $g$ to model where the camera focuses [S2].
    *   $c$: 3D coarse latent representation of the scene [S2].
    *   $f$: Fine latent representation [S2].
*   **Denoising Networks ($\psi$):**
    *   $\psi_g$: Implemented with linear layers with skip connections [S2].
    *   $\psi_c, \psi_f$: Adopt the U-net architecture [62] [S2].
    *   All $\psi$ networks use cross-attention layers [60] to take conditioning variables as input [S2].
*   **Conditional Layers:**
    *   $g$ is fed into $\psi_c$ and $\psi_f$ with conditional group normalization layers [S2].
    *   $c$ is interpolated and concatenated to the input to $\psi_f$ [S2].

### Numbered Steps

1.  **Forward Process:** Define a fixed Markovian forward process $q(x_t |x_{t-1})$ where $q(x_0)$ is the data distribution and $q(x_T)$ is close to the standard normal distribution [S2].
2.  **Training Objective:** Train the denoising networks $\psi_g, \psi_c, \psi_f$ by minimizing the hierarchical LDM losses [S2]:
    $$
    \mathbb{E}_{t,\epsilon,g_0} \Bigl [ w(\lambda_t)||g_0-\psi_g(g_t, t)||^2_2 \Bigr ]
    $$
    $$
    \mathbb{E}_{t,\epsilon,g_0,c_0} \Bigl [ w(\lambda_t)||c_0-\psi_c(c_t,g_0, t)||^2_2 \Bigr ]
    $$
    $$
    \mathbb{E}_{t,\epsilon,g_0,c_0,f_0} \Bigl [ w(\lambda_t)||f_0-\psi_f(f_t,g_0,c_0, t)||^2_2 \Bigr ]
    $$
    where $t$ is sampled uniformly from timesteps, $\epsilon$ is sampled from the standard normal to noise the data $x_0$, $w(\lambda_t)$ is a timestep-dependent weighting constant, and $\hat{x}_\theta$ (denoted as $\psi$ here) is the learned denoising model [S2].
3.  **Sampling:** Once trained, sample the latent variables sequentially following the hierarchy: first sample $g$, then sample $c$ conditioned on $g$, and finally sample $f$ conditioned on $g$ and $c$ [S2]. Each $\psi$ can be trained in parallel but sampled one after another [S2].
4.  **Decoding:** Use the latent decoder (from the parent article's Sec. 3.2) to construct the voxel $V$ from the sampled $g, c, f$ [S2].
5.  **Visualization:** Render the sampled scene from desired viewpoints using volume rendering and decoding steps [S2].

### Scene Editing Steps
For localized scene editing, the following steps are applied to the coarse latent $c$ [S2]:
1.  At each denoising step, add noise to the spatial region of $c$ that is intended to be kept [S2].
2.  Concatenate the noised kept region with the region being newly sampled [S2].
3.  Pass the concatenated latent representation through the diffusion model [S2].
4.  Apply reconstruction guidance to harmonize the boundary between the sampled and kept regions [S2].
5.  After obtaining the updated coarse latent $c$, re-sample the fine latent $f$ conditioned on the new $c$ [S2].

### Formulas
*   **DDM Loss:** The general DDM loss is defined as [S2]:
    $$
    \mathbb{E}_{t,\epsilon,x_0} \Bigl [ w(\lambda_t)||x_0-\hat{x}_\theta(x_t, t)||^2_2 \Bigr ]
    $$
    where $t$ is sampled uniformly, $\epsilon$ is sampled from the standard normal to noise $x_0$, $w(\lambda_t)$ is a timestep-dependent weighting constant, and $\hat{x}_\theta$ is the learned denoising model [S2].
*   **Hierarchical Losses:** As defined in step 2 above [S2].

### Parameters
*   **v-prediction parameterization:** Used for better convergence and training stability [S2].
*   **Conditioning:** Each $\psi$ takes the conditioning variable as input with cross-attention layers [S2].
*   **Camera Trajectory:** Concatenated to $g$ to inform the model where to focus on generating [S2].

### Edge Cases and Pitfalls
*   **Precision:** The sources do not specify precision issues.
*   **Boundaries:** Reconstruction guidance is used to harmonize boundaries between sampled and kept regions in scene editing [S2].
*   **Degenerate Input:** Not given in the sources.

        # Backpropagate and update
        total_loss = loss_g + loss_c + loss_f

## Subtopics

### Scene Editing
A technique to modify specific spatial regions of a generated 3D scene by treating the 3D coarse latent as an image and applying diffusion-based inpainting [S2]. Unlike global latent resampling which regenerates the entire scene, this method allows localized modifications by inpainting only a selected region of $c$ [S2]. It works by noising the region to be kept, concatenating it with the region being sampled, passing through the diffusion model, and applying reconstruction guidance to harmonize boundaries [S2]. After obtaining the updated coarse latent $c$, the fine latent is re-sampled conditioned on the new $c$ [S2].

## Background
Latent Diffusion Models (LDMs) perform diffusion in a latent space, significantly improving computational efficiency and output quality [S1]. Stable Diffusion further leverages pre-trained conditional models for stable and efficient image synthesis based on text prompts or other conditional inputs [S1]. The specific hierarchical LDM for 3D scene generation (NF-LDM) was introduced in "NeuralField-LDM: Scene Generation with Hierarchical Latent Diffusion Models" [S2]. The method builds on Denoising Diffusion Models (DDMs) [24] and uses v-prediction parameterization for better convergence and training stability [S2].

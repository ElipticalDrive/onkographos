# Hierarchical and Distributed Optimization
## Overview
Hierarchical and Distributed Optimization refers to strategies for scaling Neural Radiance Field (NeRF) training to large-scale scenes or correcting imperfect camera poses by decomposing the optimization problem [G]. It addresses the limitations of standard single-stage optimization, which fails with large initial pose errors [Cascaded Bundle-Adjusting Neural Radiance Fields (CBARF)](8223-cascaded-bundle-adjusting-neural-radiance-fields-cbarf.md) or suffers from memory bottlenecks and blending artifacts when scaling to arbitrarily large scenes [NeRF-XL Joint Training](10947-nerf-xl-joint-training.md). It sets itself apart from sibling methods like BlockNeRF or MegaNeRF by either using a cascaded coarse-to-fine pose refinement strategy [Cascaded Bundle-Adjusting Neural Radiance Fields (CBARF)](8223-cascaded-bundle-adjusting-neural-radiance-fields-cbarf.md) or by enforcing 0% overlap with joint training of global embeddings to eliminate train-test discrepancies [NeRF-XL Joint Training](10947-nerf-xl-joint-training.md).
## Shared principles
The family relies on the differentiability of the forward rendering operator and the propagation of gradients through the integration steps [G]. The core mechanism is the evaluation of the volume rendering equation and the computation of gradients with respect to scene parameters (density, geometry, transfer functions, camera poses) [G].

The standard emission-absorption model computes the color $\hat{C}(\mathbf{r})$ for a ray $\mathbf{r}$ as:

$$
\hat{C}(\mathbf{r}) = \sum_{i=1}^N T_i (1 - \exp(-\sigma_i \delta_i)) \mathbf{c}_i
$$

where $\sigma_i$ is the density, $\delta_i$ is the step size, $\mathbf{c}_i$ is the color, and $T_i = \exp\left(-\sum_{j=1}^{i-1} \sigma_j \delta_j\right)$ is the transmittance [G].

Differentiation strategies fall into three main categories [G]:
1. **Reverse-Mode Automatic Differentiation (AD):** Standard backpropagation through the computational graph [G].
2. **Adjoint Method:** Computes gradients by propagating adjoint variables backward through the rendering algorithm [G].
3. **Analytic Inversion / Explicit Gradients:** Uses a-priori knowledge of the DVR operations to derive closed-form gradients, reducing memory consumption [G].
## Subtopics

### [Cascaded Bundle-Adjusting Neural Radiance Fields (CBARF)](8223-cascaded-bundle-adjusting-neural-radiance-fields-cbarf.md)
CBARF is a 3D reconstruction framework that simultaneously optimizes camera poses and scene representation from input images with imperfect or partially unknown camera poses [Cascaded Bundle-Adjusting Neural Radiance Fields (CBARF)](8223-cascaded-bundle-adjusting-neural-radiance-fields-cbarf.md). It addresses the limitation where standard NeRF and single-stage Bundle Adjustment (BA) methods fail or over-fit when initial pose deviations are large [Cascaded Bundle-Adjusting Neural Radiance Fields (CBARF)](8223-cascaded-bundle-adjusting-neural-radiance-fields-cbarf.md). Unlike single-stage BA which quickly falls into sub-optimal solutions, CBARF uses a coarse-to-fine multi-stage approach with cascaded compact BA modules to progressively refine poses, avoiding the learning of incorrect scene representations [Cascaded Bundle-Adjusting Neural Radiance Fields (CBARF)](8223-cascaded-bundle-adjusting-neural-radiance-fields-cbarf.md).

### [NeRF-XL Joint Training](10947-nerf-xl-joint-training.md)
NeRF-XL is a method for distributing Neural Radiance Fields across multiple GPUs by allocating NeRF parameters across a disjoint set of spatial regions and training them jointly, enabling arbitrarily large capacity without the redundancy and blending artifacts of independent training [NeRF-XL Joint Training](10947-nerf-xl-joint-training.md). Unlike sibling methods such as BlockNeRF and MegaNeRF which rely on overlapping regions and post-hoc blending, NeRF-XL uses 0% overlap and a single unified optimization process, eliminating train-test discrepancies and per-camera embedding inconsistencies [NeRF-XL Joint Training](10947-nerf-xl-joint-training.md).

### [Low-Rank Constrained NeRF (LR-NeRF)](17322-low-rank-constrained-nerf-lr-nerf.md)
An optimization step where the simplified NeRF weights are finetuned under a low-rank constraint using the Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM) to prepare for Tensor Train decomposition [Low-Rank Constrained NeRF (LR-NeRF)](17322-low-rank-constrained-nerf-lr-nerf.md).

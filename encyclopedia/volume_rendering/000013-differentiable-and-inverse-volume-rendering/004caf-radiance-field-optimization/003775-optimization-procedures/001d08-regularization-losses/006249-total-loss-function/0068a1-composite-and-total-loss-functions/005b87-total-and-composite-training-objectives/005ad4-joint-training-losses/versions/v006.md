# Joint Training Losses

Also called: Composite Loss with Pose, Skin, and INN Constraints

## Overview
This method defines a composite objective function for the joint optimization of a Template-Free NeRF (TFS-NeRF), an Implicit Neural Network (INN) for spatial transformation, and a skinning weight prediction network [S1]. It solves the ambiguity in dynamic scene reconstruction where incorrect combinations of canonical points and skinning weights can satisfy forward Linear Blend Skinning (LBS) equations [S1]. It distinguishes itself by explicitly constraining the INN and skinning networks through pose, cycle consistency, and skinning weight losses, rather than relying solely on photometric reconstruction [S1].

## How it works
**Inputs:** Input video frames with known camera poses, canonical skeleton joints $J_{p0}$, deformed skeleton joints $J_p$, and point clouds initialized around the canonical skeleton of individual entities [S1].

**Outputs:** Rendered RGB images $\hat{C}(r)$, transformed points in canonical and deformed spaces, and predicted skinning weights $\hat{w}$ [S1].

**Data Structures:** $R$ (set of rays), $P$ (total number of points sampled around bones from each skeleton), $H$ (INN transformation function), $H^{-1}$ (inverse transformation function), $G_{x_c \to w}^W$ (skinning weight prediction network), $G_{v \to c}$ (view-to-canonical transformation network) [S1].

**Steps and Formulas:**
1. **Reconstruction Loss ($L_{rgb}$):** Minimizes the difference between rendered RGB $\hat{C}(r)$ and input pixel RGB $C(r)$ along ray $r$ [S1].
   $$ L_{rgb} = \sum_{r \in R} ||\hat{C}(r) - C(r)|| $$

2. **Pose Loss ($L_{pose}$):** Ensures the INN correctly transforms points $X_{J_p}$ sampled around deformed bones to $X_{J_{p0}}$ around canonical bones [S1].
   $$ L_{pose} = \sum_{p \in P} ||X_{J_{p0}} - G_{v \to c}(X_{J_p})|| $$

3. **Skinning Weight Loss ($L_W$):** Constrains the predicted skinning weight $\hat{w}$ for canonical joints to be a one-hot vector (1 for the respective joint, 0 for others) [S1].
   $$ L_W = ||G_{x_c \to w}^W(J_{p0}) - \hat{w}||_2^2 $$

4. **Cycle Consistency Loss ($L_{INN}$):** Ensures the INN transformation is invertible and consistent [S1].
   $$ L_{INN} = ||H(H^{-1}(x_v, J_{p0}), J_p) - x_v||_2^2 $$

5. **Consistency Loss ($L_{Consis}$):** Minimizes the L2 distance between the INN-transformed point $x'_c$ and the skinning-weight-conditioned canonical point $x_c$ [S1].
   $$ L_{Consis} = ||x'_c - x_c||_2^2 $$

6. **Shape Loss ($L_{shape}$):** Accelerates learning by constraining transformed points $x_c$ to fall within a point cloud initialization around the canonical skeleton, with NeRF densities $\alpha=1$ [S1]. The exact formula is not provided in the source, following Guo et al. [39] [S1].

**Total Loss:** The total loss is the weighted sum of these components:
$$ L_{tot} = \lambda_{rgb} L_{rgb} + \lambda_{pose} L_{pose} + \lambda_{W} L_W + \lambda_{INN} L_{INN} + \lambda_{Consis} L_{Consis} + \lambda_{shape} L_{shape} $$
Specific weights $\lambda_k$ are not provided in the sources [S1].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:** The method relies on accurate point sampling around bones; degenerate sampling or missing bone joints may break the pose and consistency losses [S1].

**Pseudocode:**
```python
def compute_joint_losses(R, rays, C_hat, C, P, X_Jp, X_Jp0, 
                         G_v_to_c, J_p0, skinning_net, w_target, 
                         H, H_inv, x_v, J_p, x_prime_c, x_c):
    # 1. Reconstruction Loss: Sum of norms over rays R
    l_rgb = sum(l2_norm(C_hat[r] - C[r]) for r in rays)
    
    # 2. Pose Loss: Sum of norms over points P around bones
    l_pose = sum(l2_norm(X_Jp0[p] - G_v_to_c(X_Jp[p])) for p in P)
    
    # 3. Skinning Weight Loss: L2 squared norm of weight prediction error
    predicted_w = skinning_net(J_p0)
    l_w = l2_norm(predicted_w - w_target) ** 2
    
    # 4. Cycle Consistency Loss: L2 squared norm of view space reconstruction error
    x_c_temp = H_inv(x_v, J_p0)
    x_v_recon = H(x_c_temp, J_p)
    l_inn = l2_norm(x_v_recon - x_v) ** 2
    
    # 5. Consistency Loss: L2 squared norm between INN-transformed and skinning-conditioned points
    l_cons = l2_norm(x_prime_c - x_c) ** 2
    
    # 6. Shape Loss: Formula not provided in the source; follows Guo et al. [39]
    # l_shape = ...
    
    return l_rgb, l_pose, l_w, l_inn, l_cons

def l2_norm(v):
    return (sum(val ** 2 for val in v)) ** 0.5
```

## Subtopics

### [Joint Training Strategy](17557-joint-training-strategy.md)
This method enables the simultaneous optimization of a pose estimation network (IPO-Net) and a generalized 3D Gaussian rendering network (G-3DG) by combining photometric, depth, and RGB reconstruction losses into a single objective function.

## Operating characteristics
*   **Complexity:** Not given in the sources.
*   **Performance:** Enables template-free reconstruction of dynamic scenes [S1].
*   **Strengths:** Constrains ambiguous LBS solutions by supervising skeleton space [S1].
*   **Limitations:** Relies on accurate point sampling around bones [S1].

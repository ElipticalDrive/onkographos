# Joint Optimization with Loss Functions

Also called: Joint Optimization of Surfel Parameters and HDR Environment Map

## Overview
This method defines a composite loss function for the joint optimization of 3D surface splatting (surfel) parameters and a learnable HDR environment map in inverse rendering tasks [S1, unofficial]. It solves the problem of unstable geometry and lighting estimation by combining photometric fidelity with geometric priors that enforce depth consolidation and normal consistency [S1, unofficial]. It is distinguished from sibling methods like [Total Variation Regularization](860-total-variation-regularization.md) by operating explicitly on the compositing weights and mean depths of surface layers rather than raw voxel or field gradients, and from [Efficient O(N) Distortion Loss](1051-efficient-o-n-distortion-loss.md) by targeting multi-layer depth ambiguity directly [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs**
*   **Inputs:** Rendered image $\hat{\mathbf{I}}$, ground-truth image $\mathbf{I}^*$, ground-truth silhouette mask $\mathbf{M}^*$ (optional), surfel parameters (positions $\mathbf{c}$, normals $\mathbf{n}$, scales, albedo, metallic, roughness), and environment map parameters.
*   **Outputs:** Scalar total loss $\mathcal{L}$ and gradients for the Adam optimizer.

**Data Structures**
*   **Surfel Parameters:** Each surfel $i$ stores position $\mathbf{c}_i$, orientation (quaternion $\mathbf{q}_i$ or tangent vectors), log-space scales, and material properties (albedo $\rho_i$, metallic $\eta_i$, roughness $\alpha_i$) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Compositing Layers:** For each pixel $p$, a set of $K$ sorted surface layers. Each layer $i$ has compositing weight $w_i$, mean depth $z_i$, and depth variance $\sigma_i^2$ [S1, unofficial].
*   **Neighbor Indices:** A spatial data structure (e.g., KD-tree or grid) mapping surfels to their nearest neighbors $\mathcal{N}_i$ with compatible normal orientations. This structure is rebuilt periodically and cached [S1, unofficial].

**Formulas**
The total loss is a weighted sum:
$$
\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{photo}} + \lambda_{\text{sil}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{sil}} + \lambda_{\text{cons}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{cons}} + \lambda_{\text{nc}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{nc}}
$$
where $\lambda_{\text{sil}}, \lambda_{\text{cons}}, \lambda_{\text{nc}}$ are scalar hyperparameters [S1, unofficial].

1.  **Photometric Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{photo}}$):** Combines L1 reconstruction and Structural Similarity (SSIM). For HDR targets, $\hat{\mathbf{I}}$ and $\mathbf{I}^*$ are transformed to log-space and sRGB before computation to prevent gradient dominance by bright pixels [S1, unofficial].
    $$
    \mathcal{L}_{\text{photo}} = (1 - \lambda_{\text{ssim}}) \|\hat{\mathbf{I}} - \mathbf{I}^*\|_1 + \lambda_{\text{ssim}} (1 - \text{SSIM}(\hat{\mathbf{I}}, \mathbf{I}^*))
    $$
    where $\lambda_{\text{ssim}}$ controls the SSIM weight [S1, unofficial].

2.  **Silhouette Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{sil}}$):** Supervises the total accumulated coverage using soft Intersection-over-Union (IoU). It is disabled once adaptive density control begins [S1, unofficial].
    $$
    \mathcal{L}_{\text{sil}} = \text{IoU}(1 - \bar{T}_{K+1}, \mathbf{M}^*)
    $$
    where $\bar{T}_{K+1}$ is the residual transmittance (silhouette mask) and $\mathbf{M}^*$ is the ground-truth mask [S1, unofficial].

3.  **Depth Consolidation Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{cons}}$):** Penalizes spurious depth layers. It is the average over all pixels $p \in \mathcal{P}$ of inter-layer and intra-layer terms [S1, unofficial]:
    $$
    \mathcal{L}_{\text{cons}} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{P}|} \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} (\mathcal{L}_{\text{inter-c}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{intra-c}})
    $$
    *   **Inter-layer term:** Penalizes weight allocation across distant layers, driving contributions to a single surface layer:
        $$
        \mathcal{L}_{\text{inter-c}} = \sum_{i < j} w_i w_j |z_i - z_j|
        $$
    *   **Intra-layer term:** Penalizes depth spread within a layer, encouraging coplanarity:
        $$
        \mathcal{L}_{\text{intra-c}} = \sum_i w_i^2 \sigma_i^2
        $$
    where $w_i, z_i, \sigma_i^2$ are the compositing weight, mean depth, and depth variance of layer $i$ at pixel $p$ [S1, unofficial].

4.  **Normal Consistency Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{nc}}$):** Penalizes angular deviation between a surfel's geometric normal and its orientation-aware nearest neighbors in 3D space [S1, unofficial]:
    $$
    \mathcal{L}_{\text{nc}} = \frac{\sum_i \sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}_i} w_{ij} (1 - \mathbf{n}_i \cdot \mathbf{n}_j)}{\sum_i \sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}_i} w_{ij}}
    $$
    where $\mathcal{N}_i$ are nearest neighbors with compatible normal orientation, $\mathbf{n}_i, \mathbf{n}_j$ are normals, and $w_{ij} = \exp(-\|\mathbf{c}_i - \mathbf{c}_j\|^2 / 2\sigma^2)$ are distance-dependent weights derived from local intersample spacing [S1, unofficial].

**Parameters**
*   $\lambda_{\text{ssim}}$: SSIM weighting factor.
*   $\lambda_{\text{sil}}, \lambda_{\text{cons}}, \lambda_{\text{nc}}$: Scalar weights for silhouette, consolidation, and normal consistency terms.
*   $\sigma$: Parameter for normal consistency distance weighting, derived from local intersample spacing [S1, unofficial].

**Pseudocode**
```python
def compute_joint_loss(rendered_img, gt_img, gt_mask, surfels, layers, 
                       lambda_ssim, lambda_sil, lambda_cons, lambda_nc, 
                       use_mask, training_stage):
    """
    Compute the total joint optimization loss.
    
    Args:
        rendered_img: Rendered image I_hat (after tone mapping/gamma).
        gt_img: Ground-truth image I_star.
        gt_mask: Ground-truth silhouette mask M_star (optional).
        surfels: List of surfel objects with attributes c, n, w, z, sigma2.
        layers: List of lists, where layers[p] contains layer dicts for pixel p.
                Each layer dict has keys: 'w', 'z', 'sigma2'.
        lambda_ssim: Weight for SSIM term.
        lambda_sil: Weight for silhouette term.
        lambda_cons: Weight for depth consolidation term.
        lambda_nc: Weight for normal consistency term.
        use_mask: Boolean, whether gt_mask is provided.
        training_stage: String, 'early' or 'adaptive'.
        
    Returns:
        total_loss: Scalar tensor.
    """
    # 1. Photometric Loss
    # Apply log-space and sRGB transfer if HDR targets are used
    I_hat_processed = apply_hdr_transfer(rendered_img)
    I_star_processed = apply_hdr_transfer(gt_img)
    
    l1_loss = l1_norm(I_hat_processed - I_star_processed)
    ssim_loss = 1.0 - compute_ssim(I_hat_processed, I_star_processed)
    l_photo = (1.0 - lambda_ssim) * l1_loss + lambda_ssim * ssim_loss
    
    total_loss = l_photo
    
    # 2. Silhouette Loss (only if mask available and not in adaptive stage)
    if use_mask and training_stage == 'early':
        # Compute residual transmittance (silhouette mask) from layers
        # Assuming layers are sorted front-to-back, T_K+1 is the final transmittance
        # 1 - T_K+1 is the accumulated opacity/coverage
        coverage = compute_coverage(layers) 
        l_sil = compute_soft_iou(coverage, gt_mask)
        total_loss += lambda_sil * l_sil
        
    # 3. Depth Consolidation Loss
    l_cons_total = 0.0
    pixel_count = 0
    for p in range(num_pixels):
        pixel_layers = layers[p]
        if not pixel_layers:
            continue
            
        l_inter = 0.0
        l_intra = 0.0
        
        # Inter-layer: sum over all pairs i < j
        num_layers = len(pixel_layers)
        for i in range(num_layers):
            for j in range(i + 1, num_layers):
                w_i = pixel_layers[i]['w']
                w_j = pixel_layers[j]['w']
                z_i = pixel_layers[i]['z']
                z_j = pixel_layers[j]['z']
                l_inter += w_i * w_j * abs(z_i - z_j)
                
        # Intra-layer: sum over all layers
        for i in range(num_layers):
            w_i = pixel_layers[i]['w']
            sigma2_i = pixel_layers[i]['sigma2']
            l_intra += (w_i ** 2) * sigma2_i
            
        l_cons_total += (l_inter + l_intra)
        pixel_count += 1
        
    if pixel_count > 0:
        l_cons = l_cons_total / pixel_count
        total_loss += lambda_cons * l_cons
        
    # 4. Normal Consistency Loss
    # Requires pre-computed neighbor indices N_i for each surfel
    l_nc_num = 0.0
    l_nc_den = 0.0
    
    # Assuming surfels are indexed 0..N-1 and neighbors are stored in a list of lists
    # neighbors[i] = list of indices j in N_i
    for i in range(num_surfels):
        surfel_i = surfels[i]
        neighbors = get_neighbors(i) # From cached spatial structure
        
        for j in neighbors:
            surfel_j = surfels[j]
            
            # Check compatible normal orientation (e.g., dot product > 0)
            if dot(surfel_i.n, surfel_j.n) < 0:
                continue
                
            # Distance-dependent weight
            dist_sq = squared_distance(surfel_i.c, surfel_j.c)
            w_ij = exp(-dist_sq / (2.0 * sigma_normal ** 2))
            
            # Angular deviation
            dot_prod = dot(surfel_i.n, surfel_j.n)
            # Clamp for numerical stability
            dot_prod = clamp(dot_prod, -1.0, 1.0)
            l_nc_num += w_ij * (1.0 - dot_prod)
            l_nc_den += w_ij
            
    if l_nc_den > 0:
        l_nc = l_nc_num / l_nc_den
        total_loss += lambda_nc * l_nc
        
    return total_loss
```

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls**
*   **Gradient Dominance:** In HDR training, bright pixels can dominate gradients. The method requires applying log-space and sRGB transfer to both rendered and ground-truth images before loss computation [S1, unofficial].
*   **Silhouette Activation:** The silhouette loss must be disabled during the adaptive density control phase, as the densification mechanism takes over coverage enforcement [S1, unofficial].
*   **Neighbor Computation:** The KNN structure for normal consistency must be rebuilt periodically and cached to amortize cost; stale indices lead to incorrect regularization [S1, unofficial].
*   **Depth Variance:** $\sigma_i^2$ must be correctly propagated from the surfel splatting process; incorrect variance estimation breaks the intra-layer consolidation term [S1, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Complexity:** The photometric and silhouette losses are $O(N)$ with respect to pixels. The depth consolidation loss is $O(N \cdot K^2)$ per pixel if naive pairwise comparison is used, where $K$ is the number of layers. The normal consistency loss is $O(N_{surfels} \cdot K_{nn})$, where $K_{nn}$ is the number of neighbors.
*   **Performance:** Evaluated on object-level inverse rendering tasks, assessing geometry reconstruction, novel view synthesis, and relighting [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Enforces geometric thinness and normal smoothness directly in 3D, avoiding the oversmoothing issues of screen-space normal regularizers [S1, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** Requires maintaining and updating spatial data structures for neighbor queries. The multi-layer compositing increases memory and compute overhead compared to single-layer methods [S1, unofficial].

## Background
This method originates from the 3DSS (3D Surface Splatting for Inverse Rendering) framework [S1, unofficial]. It builds on scattered-data approximation frameworks where kernel support scales with local inter-sample spacing [S1, unofficial]. The depth consolidation loss is inspired by prior work on depth regularization [S1, unofficial].

# Progressive and Dynamic Scene Growth


## Overview
Progressive and Dynamic Scene Growth is a family of training procedures that adaptively expand the capacity of Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) or the training dataset during optimization, rather than relying on a fixed architecture or static dataset [S1, 2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md, 8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md, 11341-incremental-registration.md, 12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md]. It solves the problems of insufficient model capacity in complex regions, missing geometric information in sparse or single-view inputs, and the inability to handle sequentially arriving data in dynamic environments [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md, 8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md, 11341-incremental-registration.md, 12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md]. Unlike static training methods, these approaches iteratively grow the model structure, add new camera viewpoints, or register new frames sequentially to improve reconstruction quality and scalability [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md, 8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md, 11341-incremental-registration.md, 12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md].

## Shared principles
The family shares the core mechanism of iterative expansion during the training loop. The specific expansion targets vary:
1.  **Model Capacity Growth:** The neural network structure is expanded by adding new branches or blocks in regions where the current model exhibits high reconstruction uncertainty or error [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md]. This involves clustering high-uncertainty query points and initializing new child networks at cluster centers [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md].
2.  **Dataset/Viewpoint Growth:** The set of training views is expanded by synthesizing or capturing new camera positions, particularly in occluded or unobserved regions [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md]. This often involves inpainting missing data to provide supervision for novel views [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
3.  **Incremental Registration:** Camera poses and scene parameters are optimized sequentially as new frames arrive, freezing previously registered parameters to stabilize the optimization and prevent catastrophic forgetting or local minima [11341-incremental-registration.md, 12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md].

Common mathematical and algorithmic elements include:
*   **Uncertainty/Error Metrics:** Identification of regions requiring growth based on uncertainty $\delta_i$ or reconstruction error [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md].
*   **Clustering:** Use of k-means clustering to determine the locations of new model components [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md].
*   **Freezing/Regularization:** Freezing parameters of previously optimized components to prevent drift or catastrophic forgetting [11341-incremental-registration.md, 12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md].
*   **Iterative Optimization:** Re-optimization of the entire expanded system (model + data) after each growth step [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md, 8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].

## Subtopics

### [Dynamic Growth Parameters](2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md)
Dynamic Growth Parameters is a training procedure that adaptively increases model capacity by adding network branches in regions of high reconstruction uncertainty [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md]. It clusters high-error query points and spawns child networks to refine predictions locally, enabling efficient coarse-to-fine scene representation [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md]. Choose this method when a static MLP fails to capture high-frequency details in specific regions of the scene. It differs from static architectures by dynamically allocating computational resources to areas of high uncertainty [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md].
*   **Mechanism:**
    1.  Train current branches for $I_1$ iterations [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md].
    2.  Compute uncertainty $\delta_i$ for sampled query points [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md].
    3.  Select points where $\delta_i$ exceeds a threshold [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md].
    4.  Apply k-means clustering ($k \in [2, 4]$) to high-uncertainty points [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md].
    5.  Create $k$ new child branch networks initialized at cluster centers [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md].
    6.  Re-optimize the entire expanded network [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md].
    7.  Repeat until uncertainty is low or max growth steps (default 3) are reached [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md].
*   **Parameters:** Growth limit = 3 steps; Branching factor $k=2$ [2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md].

### [Progressive View Synthesis Training](8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md)
Progressive View Synthesis Training (PERF) is a strategy for Panoramic NeRF that synthesizes high-quality novel views from a single input panorama by iteratively expanding the training set [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md]. It targets occluded regions where geometric and semantic information is missing [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md]. Choose this method for single-view panorama synthesis where standard NeRF fails due to lack of input data. It differs from methods relying solely on semantic regularization by actively generating and inpainting new views [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
*   **Mechanism:**
    1.  Train NeRF (Instant-NGP) on single panorama for 10,000 iterations [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
    2.  Sample 32 new camera positions with gradually increased distance from the reference view [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
    3.  For each new position:
        a.  Identify masked regions (occlusions) [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
        b.  Project masked regions to perspective views to avoid panoramic distortion [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
        c.  Inpaint using Stable Diffusion 2 [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
        d.  Project back to panoramic coordinates [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
        e.  Apply progressive inpainting-and-erasing to prevent geometry conflicts [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
        f.  Re-optimize NeRF for 2,000 iterations [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
            *   First 100 iterations: Supervise only with the new view [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
            *   Remaining 1,900 iterations: Supervise with all accumulated views [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
            *   Learning rate: $10^{-3}$ to $10^{-4}$ via cosine annealing [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
            *   Distortion loss weight $\lambda_{dist} = 1$ [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].
*   **Parameters:** 32 new views; 2,000 iterations/view; 2,048 rays/iteration [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md].

### [Incremental Registration](11341-incremental-registration.md)
Incremental Registration is a strategy for joint pose and radiance field optimization that registers camera frames sequentially in temporal order [11341-incremental-registration.md]. It solves the non-convex optimization landscape and local minima inherent in simultaneous pose and NeRF optimization by providing robust initialization for new frames based on previously registered ones [11341-incremental-registration.md]. Choose this method for video sequences or large-scale datasets where global registration is unstable or computationally expensive. It differs from global registration by building the trajectory incrementally and freezing previously registered parameters [11341-incremental-registration.md].
*   **Mechanism:**
    1.  Seed Initialization: Jointly optimize NeRF and poses for the first two frames [11341-incremental-registration.md].
    2.  Iterate through remaining frames $I_i$ in temporal order [11341-incremental-registration.md].
    3.  After $k$ iterations, add the next frame [11341-incremental-registration.md].
    4.  Initialize new pose $P_i$ using previous pose $P_{i-1}$ [11341-incremental-registration.md].
    5.  Coarse Pose Estimation: Use scene regressor to get 3D points, establish 2D-3D correspondences, and estimate pose via RANSAC/PnP [11341-incremental-registration.md].
    6.  Train and Freeze: Add new frame to buffer, freeze learning rates for all previous poses and NeRF parameters for $T_{freeze}$ iterations [11341-incremental-registration.md].
    7.  Final Refinement: Optimize all poses jointly to correct drift [11341-incremental-registration.md].
*   **Parameters:** $k=1,000$ (CO3D/HO3D) or $500$ (LLFF) iterations; $T_{freeze}=100,000$ (HO3D/CO3D) or $30,000$ (LLFF) iterations [11341-incremental-registration.md].

### [Online Incremental NeRF Optimization](12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md)
Online Incremental NeRF Optimization is a training paradigm where the NeRF representation is updated continuously as new camera viewpoints arrive, rather than on a fixed dataset [12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md]. It solves the problem of building 3D models in dynamic or large-scale environments where data is acquired sequentially [12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md]. Choose this method for real-time or streaming applications. It differs from offline methods by integrating new observations incrementally and planning next-best views [12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md].
*   **Mechanism:**
    1.  Plan next best view based on current scene representation [12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md].
    2.  Perceive new observation at planned location [12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md].
    3.  Add new observation to training set [12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md].
    4.  Optimize scene representation on expanded training set [12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md].
*   **Pitfalls:** Catastrophic forgetting and high memory usage [12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md].

#### CityNeRF
CityNeRF is a progressive learning paradigm that synchronously grows the NeRF model and training set [S1]. It starts by fitting distant views with shallow basic blocks, then continuously attaches new blocks as training iterates to adapt to details in far-near views [S1]. This effectively activates high-frequency channels in position encoding and unfolds more complex details as training progresses [S1].

## Background
The concept of progressive learning in NeRF was introduced by Xiangli et al. in 2021 with CityNeRF, which addressed the complexity of large-scale urban architecture rendering by synchronously growing the model and training set [S1]. The idea of dynamic growth parameters, clustering high-uncertainty points to spawn child networks, is described in [S1, 2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md]. Progressive View Synthesis Training (PERF) for single-panorama synthesis was proposed to address occluded regions by iteratively expanding the training set with inpainted views [8666-progressive-view-synthesis-training.md]. Incremental registration strategies were developed to handle the non-convex optimization landscape of joint pose and NeRF optimization, particularly for video sequences [11341-incremental-registration.md]. Online incremental optimization addresses dynamic environments where data arrives sequentially [12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md].

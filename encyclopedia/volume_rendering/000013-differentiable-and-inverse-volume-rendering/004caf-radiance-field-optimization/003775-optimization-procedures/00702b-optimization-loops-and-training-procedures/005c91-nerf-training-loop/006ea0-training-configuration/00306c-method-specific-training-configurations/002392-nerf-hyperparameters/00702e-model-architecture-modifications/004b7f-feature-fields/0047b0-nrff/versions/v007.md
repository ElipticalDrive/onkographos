# NRFF

Also called: Neural Radiance Feature Field
## Overview
NRFF is a method for encoding view-dependent specular effects in neural radiance fields by replacing the standard positional encoding and roughness/IDE inputs of Ref-NeRF with a learned Anisotropic Spherical Gaussian (ASG) representation [S1]. It differs from its siblings by using a multiscale tensor decomposition (MTD) for spatial features and a custom Rendering Equation Encoding (REE) in the latent space to model anisotropic reflections, rather than relying on explicit roughness maps or pre-computed environment maps [S1].
## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Input:** 3D spatial position $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^3$ and viewing direction $\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{R}^3$.
*   **Output:** Volume density $\sigma$ and view-dependent color $\mathbf{c}$.

**Data Structures:**
1.  **Spatial MLP:** Takes $\mathbf{x}$ as input. Uses Multiscale Tensor Decomposition (MTD) instead of Positional Encoding (PE) [S1].
2.  **ASG Features:** The Spatial MLP outputs a vector of 128 values representing $N = 8 \times 16$ lobes of Anisotropic Spherical Gaussians on a unit sphere [S1].
3.  **ASG MLP:** An auxiliary network that takes the 128 ASG features as input.
4.  **REE Vector:** The output of the ASG MLP processing, resulting in an $8 \times 16 \times 2$ vector [S1].
5.  **Directional MLP:** Takes the REE vector and viewing direction $\mathbf{d}$ to predict color.

**Numbered Steps:**
1.  **Spatial Feature Extraction:** Pass $\mathbf{x}$ through the Spatial MLP using MTD to generate a coarse-to-fine scene representation [S1].
2.  **ASG Prediction:** The Spatial MLP predicts features for $N=128$ ASG lobes. Each lobe $i$ is associated with a direction $\boldsymbol{\omega}_i$ on the unit sphere [S1].
3.  **Bottleneck Conversion:** Input the 128 ASG features into the ASG MLP. This network outputs:
    *   An anisotropic reflections bottleneck vector $\mathbf{a}$ of length 2.
    *   Two scalar coefficients $\lambda$ and $\mu$ modeling the shape of the ASG in orthogonal directions [S1].
    *   *Note:* The source text states the ASG MLP outputs $\mathbf{a}, \lambda, \mu$, but the REE formula uses per-lobe $a_i, \lambda_i, \mu_i$. The text implies the ASG MLP processes the features to enable the REE calculation. The REE formula (Eq. 6) sums over $i=0$ to $N-1$, using $a_i, \lambda_i, \mu_i$ and axes $\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\lambda_i}, \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mu_i}$ [S1].
4.  **REE Calculation:** Compute the Rendering Equation Encoding (REE) for a reflection direction $\boldsymbol{\omega}_r$ using the formula below. This integrates specular contributions in the latent space [S1].
5.  **Color Prediction:** Input the resulting $8 \times 16 \times 2$ REE vector and the viewing direction $\mathbf{d}$ into the Directional MLP to predict color $\mathbf{c}$ [S1].

**Formulas:**
The Rendering Equation Encoding (REE) is defined as [S1]:
$$ \text{REE}(\boldsymbol{\omega}_r, \mathbf{a}, \lambda, \mu) = \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} a_i S(\boldsymbol{\omega}_r; \boldsymbol{\omega}_i) \exp\left(-\lambda_i (\boldsymbol{\omega}_r \cdot \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\lambda_i})^2 - \mu_i (\boldsymbol{\omega}_r \cdot \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mu_i})^2\right) $$

Where:
*   $N = 8 \times 16 = 128$ is the number of ASG lobes [S1].
*   $a_i$ is the bottleneck coefficient of the $i$-th ASG [S1].
*   $S(\boldsymbol{\omega}_r; \boldsymbol{\omega}_i) = \max(\boldsymbol{\omega}_r \cdot \boldsymbol{\omega}_i, 0)$ is a smooth term [S1].
*   $\lambda_i, \mu_i > 0$ are bandwidth coefficients (roughness) related to orthogonal axes $\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\lambda_i}, \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mu_i}$ shaping and orienting the ASG [S1].
*   $\boldsymbol{\omega}_i$ covers 360 degrees (the unit sphere) and is associated with the $i$-th ASG [S1].

**Loss Function:**
NRFF uses the auxiliary term $L_o$ from Ref-NeRF and adds a regularization term $L_f$ to enforce sparse feature representation [S1]:
$$ L_f = \frac{\beta}{M} \sum_{i=0}^{M-1} \frac{|F_\tau^i|}{\tau} $$
Where:
*   $M$ is the number of features in the density field representation [S1].
*   $F_\tau$ is the density field feature map [S1].
*   $\beta$ is a coefficient [S1].
*   $\tau$ is a scaling factor (implied by context) [S1].

**Parameters:**
*   $N$: Number of ASG lobes, set to $8 \times 16$ [S1].
*   $\mathbf{a}$: Anisotropic reflections bottleneck vector, length 2 [S1].
*   $\lambda, \mu$: Scalar coefficients modeling ASG shape, must be $> 0$ [S1].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
*   The coefficients $\lambda_i, \mu_i$ must be strictly positive to maintain the Gaussian shape properties [S1].
*   The source notes that the ASG MLP outputs a bottleneck vector $\mathbf{a}$ of length 2, but the REE formula sums over $N$ lobes with individual $a_i$. The exact mapping from the length-2 bottleneck to the $N$-lobe parameters is not explicitly detailed in the provided text, though the REE formula assumes per-lobe parameters [S1].

## Background
NRFF was proposed by Albert Barreiro, Roger Marı́, Rafael Redondo, Gloria Haro, Carles Bosch, and David Berga from Eurecat and Universitat Pompeu Fabra [S1]. The method was submitted on 2024–07–09, accepted on 2025–01–17, and published in *Image Processing On Line* on 2025–03–12 [S1]. It is designed to improve upon Ref-NeRF by better encoding view-dependent specular effects using multiscale tensor decomposition and anisotropic spherical Gaussians [S1].

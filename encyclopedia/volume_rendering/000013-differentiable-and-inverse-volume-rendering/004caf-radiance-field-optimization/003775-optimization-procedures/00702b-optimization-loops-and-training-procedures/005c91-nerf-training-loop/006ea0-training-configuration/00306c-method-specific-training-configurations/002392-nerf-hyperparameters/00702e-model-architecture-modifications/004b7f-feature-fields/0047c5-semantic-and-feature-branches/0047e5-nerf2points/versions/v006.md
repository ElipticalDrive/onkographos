# NeRF2Points

Also called: Layered Perception and Integrated Modeling

## Overview
NeRF2Points is a method for generating large-scale, geometrically consistent RGBD point clouds from street-view radiance fields [S1, unofficial]. It solves the problem of "pavement collapse" and floating artifacts in point clouds generated by standard NeRF variants when applied to sparse, large-scale street-view data with weak textures [S1, unofficial]. It sets itself apart by separating the scene into road and street components via a ray gate, modeling them with separate radiance fields, and merging them using rigid transformation derived from shared intersection rays [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs**
*   **Inputs:** A sequence of RGB images $I$ from a street-view dataset; coarse initial camera poses from WIGO; refined camera poses and sparse point clouds from COLMAP; depth and normal estimations from Omnidata; road/scene masks from Mask2Former; SIFT features for temporal correspondence.
*   **Outputs:** A merged RGBD point cloud $PC \in \mathbb{R}^{(N_1+N_2) \times 3}$; a trained radiance field representation.

**Data Structures**
*   **Ray Gate $M(I)$:** A segmentation model (trained on Cityscapes) outputting a binary mask for each pixel, indicating road vs. non-road. An embedded morphological dilation operator ensures edge intersection [S1, unofficial].
*   **Ray Bundles:**
    *   $\mathbf{Ray}_{i_1}^r$: Rays classified as road.
    *   $\mathbf{Ray}_{i_2}^s$: Rays classified as street scene.
    *   $\mathbf{Ray}_{i_3}$: Intersection of road and street rays (shared rays).
    *   $\mathbf{Ray}_i$: Reconstructed original ray set: $(\mathbf{Ray}_{i_1}^r - \mathbf{Ray}_{i_3}) \cup (\mathbf{Ray}_{i_2}^s - \mathbf{Ray}_{i_3}) \cup \mathbf{Ray}_{i_3}$ [S1, unofficial].
*   **Point Clouds:**
    *   $\mathbf{PC}_r \in \mathbb{R}^{N_1 \times 3}$: Road surface point cloud.
    *   $\mathbf{PC}_s \in \mathbb{R}^{N_2 \times 3}$: Street scene point cloud.
    *   $P \subset \mathbf{PC}_r, Q \subset \mathbf{PC}_s$: Intersection points from shared rays $\mathbf{Ray}_{i_3}$, where $|P|=|Q|=N' \ll N_1, N_2$ [S1, unofficial].

**Numbered Steps**

1.  **Data Preparation:**
    *   Collect street-view images (e.g., 180k images from 6 cameras).
    *   Obtain initial 6-DoF poses using Wheel-IMU-GNSS Odometry (WIGO) [S1, unofficial].
    *   Refine poses and estimate sparse point cloud using COLMAP with Structure from Motion (SfM) [S1, unofficial].
    *   Generate depth and normal maps using Omnidata [S1, unofficial].
    *   Generate road/scene masks using Mask2Former [S1, unofficial].
    *   Extract SIFT features from all images to identify correspondences between adjacent frames [S1, unofficial].

2.  **Layered Perception (Ray Splitting):**
    *   For each image $I$, feed it to the ray gate $M(\cdot)$ to obtain road rays $\mathbf{Ray}_{i_1}^r$ and scene rays $\mathbf{Ray}_{i_2}^s$ [S1, unofficial]:
        $$
        \mathbf{Ray}_{i_1}^r = \mathbf{Ray}_i(M(I)), \quad \mathbf{Ray}_{i_2}^s = \mathbf{Ray}_i(1-M(I))
        $$
    *   Compute intersection rays $\mathbf{Ray}_{i_3} = \mathbf{Ray}_{i_1}^r \cap \mathbf{Ray}_{i_2}^s$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   Reconstruct the full ray set $\mathbf{Ray}_i$ ensuring no loss of information [S1, unofficial].

3.  **Radiance Field Training:**
    *   Train separate radiance fields for road and street scenes.
    *   Compute **Matching Cost** for each ray bundle $\mathbf{Ray}_i'$ (either road or street) to identify hard samples [S1, unofficial]:
        $$
        \mathbf{cos}_i = \lambda_1 \ell_{depth}(\cdot) + \lambda_2 \ell_{rgb}(\cdot) + \lambda_3 \ell_{normal}(\cdot)
        $$
        Where losses are defined as:
        $$
        \ell_{depth}(\mathbf{t}_1', \mathbf{t}_2') = \sum \| \textit{norm}(\mathbf{t}_1') - \textit{norm}(\mathbf{t}_2') \|_2^2
        $$
        $$
        \ell_{rgb}(\mathbf{c}_1', \mathbf{c}_2') = \sum \| \mathbf{c}_1' - \mathbf{c}_2' \|_2^2
        $$
        $$
        \ell_{normal}(\mathbf{n}_1', \mathbf{n}_2') = \sum \| \mathbf{n}_1' - \mathbf{n}_2' \|_1 + \| 1 - \mathbf{n}_1'^\top \mathbf{n}_2' \|_1
        $$
        Here, $\textit{norm}(\cdot)$ is max-min normalization, and $\lambda_1=0.1, \lambda_2=1.0, \lambda_3=0.05$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   Rank costs $\mathbf{cos}_i$ and select top-$n$ rays $\mathbf{Ray}_{i,n}'$ as hard samples [S1, unofficial].
    *   Construct proposal regions $R$ of size $s \times s$ centered on pixel indices of hard samples.
    *   Randomly select consistent matching samples from $R$ for each hard ray (selection varies per optimization step) [S1, unofficial].
    *   Compute **Spatial Dynamic Consistency Loss** $\ell_{sdc}$ between hard rays and their neighbors in $R$ [S1, unofficial]:
        $$
        \ell_{sdc} = \lambda_1' \ell_{jsd}(\phi(\mathbf{r}_1'), \phi(\mathbf{r}_2')) + \lambda_2' \ell_{rgb}(\cdot) + \lambda_3' \ell_{depth}(\cdot)
        $$
        Where $\phi(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{e^{x_i}}{\sum_{c=1}^C e^{x_c}}$ is softmax, and $\ell_{jsd}$ is Jensen-Shannon Divergence:
        $$
        \ell_{jsd}(\phi(\mathbf{r}_1'), \phi(\mathbf{r}_2')) = 0.5 \frac{\sum \phi(\mathbf{r}_1') \ln(\phi(\mathbf{r}_2')) + \sum \phi(\mathbf{r}_2') \ln(\phi(\mathbf{r}_1'))}{(\phi(\mathbf{r}_1') + \phi(\mathbf{r}_2'))/2}
        $$
    *   Compute **Temporal Invariant Consistency Loss** $\ell_{tic}$ between SIFT-matched rays $\mathbf{Ray}_{cur}$ and $\mathbf{Ray}_{next}$ from adjacent frames [S1, unofficial]:
        $$
        \ell_{tic} = \ell_{jsd}(\phi(\mathbf{r}_{cur}'), \phi(\mathbf{r}_{next}'))
        $$
    *   Compute **Reconstruction Loss** $\ell_{rec}$ [S1, unofficial]:
        $$
        \ell_{rec} = \theta_1 \ell_{rgb}(\cdot) + \theta_2 \ell_{depth}(\cdot) + \theta_3 \ell_{normal}(\cdot)
        $$
    *   Compute **Total Loss** [S1, unofficial]:
        $$
        \ell_{total} = \ell_{rec} + \beta \ell_{sdc} + \gamma \ell_{tic}
        $$
        Parameters: $\theta_1=1.0, \theta_2=0.1, \theta_3=0.005, \beta=0.01, \gamma=0.01$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   Optimize radiance fields using Adam optimizer, gradient clipping (threshold/norm 0.1), scene space annealing, and exponential learning rate decay with warm-up [S1, unofficial].

4.  **Integrated Modeling (Merging):**
    *   Generate separate point clouds $\mathbf{PC}_r$ and $\mathbf{PC}_s$ from the trained fields: $\mathbf{PC} = \text{origin} + \text{direction} \times \text{depth}$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   Identify intersection points $P \subset \mathbf{PC}_r$ and $Q \subset \mathbf{PC}_s$ from shared rays $\mathbf{Ray}_{i_3}$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   Find rigid transformation $(\mathbf{R}, \mathbf{t})$ by minimizing [S1, unofficial]:
        $$
        \min_{\mathbf{R}, \mathbf{t}} \sum_{i=1}^{N'} (\textit{Dis}_i(\mathbf{R}, \mathbf{t}))^2 + \textit{I}_{SO}(\mathbf{R})
        $$
        Where $\textit{Dis}_i(\mathbf{R}, \mathbf{t}) = \min_{q_j \in Q, p_k \in P} \|\mathbf{R}p_i + \mathbf{t} - q_j\|$ and $\textit{I}_{SO}(\mathbf{R})$ is an indicator function for SO(3) (0 if $\mathbf{R}^T\mathbf{R}=\mathbf{I}$ and $\det(\mathbf{R})=1$, else $+\infty$) [S1, unofficial].
    *   Merge point clouds: $\mathbf{PC} = \mathbf{PC}_r \mathbf{R} + \mathbf{t} + \mathbf{PC}_s$ [S1, unofficial].

**Parameters**
*   $\lambda_1=0.1, \lambda_2=1.0, \lambda_3=0.05$ (Matching cost weights) [S1, unofficial].
*   $\theta_1=1.0, \theta_2=0.1, \theta_3=0.005$ (Reconstruction loss weights) [S1, unofficial].
*   $\beta=0.01, \gamma=0.01$ (Consistency loss weights) [S1, unofficial].
*   Batch size: 2048 [S1, unofficial].
*   Iterations: 20,000 [S1, unofficial].
*   Hardware: Single NVIDIA A6000 GPU [S1, unofficial].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls**
*   **Pavement Collapse:** Standard NeRF methods fail to optimize weak textures like road surfaces, leading to poor depth estimation and invisible details [S1, unofficial].
*   **Floaters:** Sparse views and viewing angle shifts cause inconsistent geometry and floating artifacts above the road surface [S1, unofficial].
*   **Matching Overhead:** Incorrect optimization of hard samples leads to significant artifacts; addressed by spatial dynamic consistency [S1, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time and Memory Complexity:** Not given in the sources.
*   **Measured Performance:** On a proprietary street-view dataset (180k images, 20km span), NeRF2Points achieves PSNR 29.139, SSIM 0.831, and Chamfer Distance 1.876 [S1, unofficial].
*   **Scaling:** Designed for large-scale street-view data (20km span) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Outperforms NeRFacto, Instant-NPG, NeuS, Depth-NeRFacto, and Mip-NeRF in PSNR, SSIM, and Chamfer Distance on street-view data; generates clear RGBD point clouds with distinct features [S1, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** Relies on external priors (Omnidata, Mask2Former, SIFT); performance on non-street-view data is not detailed [S1, unofficial].
*   **When to use:** Large-scale street-view point cloud generation from RGB sequences.
*   **When not to use:** Small-scale or object-centered scenes where standard NeRF variants may suffice; scenes without road/structure distinction.

## Background
NeRF2Points was introduced by Peng Tu et al. in 2024 [S1, unofficial]. It extends the NeRFacto framework within the NeRFSTUDIO ecosystem [S1, unofficial]. The method addresses specific challenges in autonomous driving datasets, such as sparse views and weak textures, which are common in street-view data collection pipelines [S1, unofficial].

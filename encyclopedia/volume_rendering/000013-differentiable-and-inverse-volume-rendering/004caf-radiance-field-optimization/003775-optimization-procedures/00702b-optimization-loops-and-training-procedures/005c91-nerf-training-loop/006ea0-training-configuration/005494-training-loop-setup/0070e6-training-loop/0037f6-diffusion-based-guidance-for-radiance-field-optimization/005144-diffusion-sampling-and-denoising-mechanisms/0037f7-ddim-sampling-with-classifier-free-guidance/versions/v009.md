# DDIM Sampling with Classifier-Free Guidance

Also called: Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models, DDIM Sampling, DDIM Sampling Step (with CFG & v-parameterization)

## Overview
DDIM Sampling with Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) is a deterministic (or stochastic) reverse diffusion algorithm that generates or refines samples in fewer steps than standard DDPM by skipping intermediate noise timesteps, while applying CFG to steer the generation towards conditional targets [S2, unofficial]. It builds on [Diffusion Sampling and Denoising Mechanisms](14430-diffusion-sampling-and-denoising-mechanisms.md) by providing a specific discrete-time integration scheme for the reverse process and a mechanism to combine conditional and unconditional model predictions [S2, unofficial]. Unlike [DDPM Denoise Function Network](11102-ddpm-denoise-function-network.md), which relies on the stochastic posterior sampling of DDPM, DDIM allows for exact reconstruction of the clean sample from noise in the limit of infinite steps and supports deterministic trajectories [S2, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
- **Input:** Initial latent $x_T \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I)$ (or a provided noisy latent), conditioning $c$ (e.g., text embeddings, images), unconditional conditioning $c_{uncond}$, timestep array $T = \{t_1, \dots, t_N\}$, guidance scale $s$, eta $\eta$.
- **Output:** Final latent $x_0$ (denoised sample).

**Data Structures:**
The scheduler maintains several precomputed buffers on the device (e.g., CUDA) to avoid recomputation during the loop [S2, unofficial]:
- `betas`: Array of size $T_{max}$, variance schedule coefficients.
- `alphas_cumprod`: Array of size $T_{max}$, $\alpha_t = \prod_{i=1}^t (1 - \beta_i)$.
- `alphas_cumprod_prev`: Array of size $T_{max} + 1$, with $\alpha_0 = 1$.
- `sqrt_alphas_cumprod`: $\sqrt{\alpha_t}$.
- `sqrt_one_minus_alphas_cumprod`: $\sqrt{1 - \alpha_t}$.
- `ddim_sigmas`: Array of size $N$, $\sigma_t = \eta \sqrt{\frac{1-\alpha_{t-1}}{1-\alpha_t}} \sqrt{1 - \frac{\alpha_t}{\alpha_{t-1}}}$ (for $\eta > 0$) or 0 (for $\eta=0$).
- `ddim_alphas`: $\alpha_t$ mapped to the selected $N$ timesteps.
- `ddim_alphas_prev`: $\alpha_{t-1}$ mapped to the selected $N$ timesteps.
- `ddim_sqrt_one_minus_alphas`: $\sqrt{1 - \alpha_t}$ mapped to the selected $N$ timesteps.
- `ddim_sigmas_for_original_num_steps`: Sigmas scaled for the original DDPM timestep count [S2, unofficial].

**Parameters:**
- `num_inference_steps` ($N$): Number of sampling steps. Default 50 [S1, unofficial].
- `unconditional_guidance_scale` ($s$): CFG multiplier. Default 1.0 (disables guidance) [S2, unofficial]. Typical values: 7.5 for sampling, 100 for refinement [S1, unofficial].
- `ddim_eta` ($\eta$): Stochasticity control. Default 0.0 (deterministic) [S2, unofficial].
- `temperature`: Scales injected noise. Default 1.0 [S2, unofficial].
- `noise_dropout`: Probability of zeroing noise. Default 0.0 [S2, unofficial].
- `quantize_denoised`: Boolean to quantize $\hat{x}_0$. Default False [S2, unofficial].
- `parameterization`: "eps" (predict noise) or "v" (predict velocity). Default "eps" [S2, unofficial].

**Numbered Steps:**

1. **Initialization:**
   - If $x_T$ is not provided, sample $x_T \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I)$ with shape `(batch_size, channels, height, width)` [S1, S2, unofficial].
   - Select timesteps $t_1, \dots, t_N$ from the full schedule (e.g., uniform or linear spacing) [S2, unofficial].
   - Compute or load the scheduler buffers (`alphas`, `sigmas`, etc.) corresponding to these timesteps [S2, unofficial].

2. **Loop over timesteps:**
   Iterate $i$ from $0$ to $N-1$. Let $t$ be the current timestep from the reversed array (descending order) [S2, unofficial].
   
   a. **Prepare Model Input:**
      - Duplicate the latent: $x_{in} = [x_t, x_t]$ [S2, unofficial].
      - Duplicate the timestep tensor: $t_{in} = [t, t]$ [S2, unofficial].
      - Concatenate conditioning: If $s > 1$, concatenate conditional $c$ and unconditional $c_{uncond}$ embeddings. Let $c_{in} = [c, c_{uncond}]$ [S1, S2, unofficial].
      - If $s = 1$, $c_{in} = c$ and $x_{in} = x_t$ (single forward pass) [S2, unofficial].

   b. **Model Prediction:**
      - Pass inputs through the denoiser network $f_\theta$:
        $$ [e_t^{uncond}, e_t^{cond}] = \text{chunk}(f_\theta(x_{in}, t_{in}, c_{in}), 2) $$
        where $e_t^{cond}$ is the conditional prediction and $e_t^{uncond}$ is the unconditional prediction [S2, unofficial].
      - Apply Classifier-Free Guidance:
        $$ e_t = e_t^{uncond} + s \cdot (e_t^{cond} - e_t^{uncond}) $$
        If `parameterization == "v"`, convert the predicted velocity to noise:
        $$ e_t = f_\theta.\text{predict\_eps\_from\_z\_and\_v}(x_t, t, e_t) $$
        [S2, unofficial].

   c. **Compute Predicted Clean Sample ($\hat{x}_0$):**
      - If `parameterization == "eps"`:
        $$ \hat{x}_0 = \frac{x_t - \sqrt{1 - \alpha_t} \cdot e_t}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}} $$
        [S2, unofficial].
      - If `parameterization == "v"`:
        $$ \hat{x}_0 = f_\theta.\text{predict\_start\_from\_z\_and\_v}(x_t, t, e_t) $$
        [S2, unofficial].
      - If `quantize_denoised` is True, quantize $\hat{x}_0$ using the first stage model's quantizer [S2, unofficial].

   d. **Compute Direction and Noise:**
      - Retrieve $\alpha_{t-1}$ (denoted `a_prev`), $\sigma_t$ (denoted `sigma_t`), and $\sqrt{1-\alpha_t}$ (denoted `sqrt_one_minus_at`) from the scheduler buffers for the current step [S2, unofficial].
      - Compute the deterministic direction towards $\hat{x}_0$:
        $$ \text{dir\_xt} = \sqrt{1 - \alpha_{t-1} - \sigma_t^2} \cdot e_t $$
        [S2, unofficial].
      - Sample noise:
        $$ \text{noise} = \sigma_t \cdot \mathcal{N}(0, I) \cdot \text{temperature} $$
        Apply dropout to `noise` with probability `noise_dropout` [S2, unofficial].

   e. **Update Latent:**
      $$ x_{t-1} = \sqrt{\alpha_{t-1}} \cdot \hat{x}_0 + \text{dir\_xt} + \text{noise} $$
      [S2, unofficial].

   f. **Inpainting Mask (Optional):**
      - If a mask $M$ and original image $x_0$ are provided:
        1. Compute noisy original: $x_{orig} = q(x_0, t)$ using the forward diffusion process [S2, unofficial].
        2. Update: $x_{t-1} = x_{orig} \cdot M + (1 - M) \cdot x_{t-1}$ [S2, unofficial].
      - Note: Masking requires $x_0$ to be available [S2, unofficial].

   g. **Dynamic Guidance (Optional):**
      - If a schedule `ucg_schedule` is provided, set $s = \text{ucg\_schedule}[i]$ for the current step [S2, unofficial].

3. **Termination:**
   - After the final step ($t=0$), $x_0$ is the final sample. Intermediates can be logged every $k$ steps [S2, unofficial].

**Pseudocode:**

```python
import torch
import math

def ddim_sample_with_cfg(
    model, 
    x_T, 
    c_cond, 
    c_uncond, 
    timesteps, 
    alphas_cumprod, 
    alphas_cumprod_prev, 
    unconditional_guidance_scale=7.5, 
    eta=0.0, 
    temperature=1.0, 
    noise_dropout=0.0,
    parameterization="eps"
):
    """
    Perform one step of DDIM sampling with CFG.
    This function assumes scheduler buffers are precomputed.
    """
    # 1. Prepare inputs for CFG
    if unconditional_guidance_scale > 1.0:
        x_in = torch.cat([x_T, x_T], dim=0)
        c_in = torch.cat([c_cond, c_uncond], dim=0)
    else:
        x_in = x_T
        c_in = c_cond

    # 2. Model forward pass
    # model.apply_model returns noise prediction e_t
    e_t_full = model.apply_model(x_in, timesteps, c_in)
    
    if unconditional_guidance_scale > 1.0:
        e_t_uncond, e_t_cond = e_t_full.chunk(2)
        e_t = e_t_uncond + unconditional_guidance_scale * (e_t_cond - e_t_uncond)
    else:
        e_t = e_t_full

    # 3. Handle v-parameterization if needed
    # Assuming model has helper methods for v-param conversion
    if parameterization == "v":
        e_t = model.predict_eps_from_z_and_v(x_T, timesteps, e_t)

    # 4. Compute predicted x0
    # alpha_t and sqrt_one_minus_alpha_t are extracted from buffers at current timestep index
    alpha_t = alphas_cumprod[timesteps]
    sqrt_alpha_t = torch.sqrt(alpha_t)
    sqrt_one_minus_alpha_t = torch.sqrt(1 - alpha_t)
    
    if parameterization == "eps":
        pred_x0 = (x_T - sqrt_one_minus_alpha_t * e_t) / sqrt_alpha_t
    else:
        # v-param specific start prediction
        pred_x0 = model.predict_start_from_z_and_v(x_T, timesteps, e_t)

    # 5. Compute next step parameters
    alpha_prev = alphas_cumprod_prev[timesteps]
    sqrt_alpha_prev = torch.sqrt(alpha_prev)
    
    # Sigma calculation for DDIM
    # sigma_t = eta * sqrt((1 - alpha_{t-1}) / (1 - alpha_t)) * sqrt(1 - alpha_t / alpha_{t-1})
    # Simplified for eta=0: sigma_t = 0
    # For general eta:
    one_minus_alpha_t = 1 - alpha_t
    one_minus_alpha_prev = 1 - alpha_prev
    
    # Avoid division by zero if alpha_prev is 0 (last step)
    ratio = one_minus_alpha_prev / one_minus_alpha_t
    ratio = torch.clamp(ratio, max=1.0) # Clamp for numerical stability if alpha_t < alpha_prev due to discretization
    
    sigma_t = eta * torch.sqrt(ratio * (1 - alpha_t / alpha_prev))
    # Note: Standard DDIM formula for sigma_t often uses:
    # sigma_t = eta * sqrt((1 - a_{t-1}) / (1 - a_t)) * sqrt(1 - a_t / a_{t-1})
    # If eta=0, sigma_t=0.
    
    # 6. Compute direction and noise
    # dir_xt = sqrt(1 - a_{t-1} - sigma_t^2) * e_t
    dir_xt_coeff = torch.sqrt(torch.clamp(1 - alpha_prev - sigma_t**2, min=0.0))
    dir_xt = dir_xt_coeff * e_t
    
    # noise = sigma_t * N(0,1) * temperature
    noise = torch.randn_like(x_T) * temperature
    if noise_dropout > 0.0:
        mask = torch.bernoulli(torch.full_like(noise, 1.0 - noise_dropout))
        noise = noise * mask
        
    noise_term = sigma_t * noise

    # 7. Update latent
    x_prev = sqrt_alpha_prev * pred_x0 + dir_xt + noise_term
    
    return x_prev, pred_x0
```

## Subtopics

### [Conditional Generator for NeRF Refinement](13028-conditional-generator-for-nerf-refinement.md)
A 2D post-processing module that refines NeRF renderings by acting as a stochastic denoiser. It takes a raw NeRF rendering and an auxiliary random vector as input to produce a cleaned image, addressing artifacts that remain after 3D optimization. Unlike 3D discriminator-based methods that guide the radiance field directly, this method operates in image space, leveraging a StyleGAN2-inspired architecture to enhance perceptual quality without altering the underlying 3D representation.

### [Pseudo Linear Multistep Sampling](15261-pseudo-linear-multistep-sampling.md)
Pseudo Linear Multistep Sampling (PLMS) is a sampling method for diffusion models that uses a linear multistep predictor (Adams-Bashforth) to estimate the noise residual at the next timestep, allowing for fewer function evaluations per step compared to standard Euler or Heun methods, while maintaining stability similar to DDIM. It builds on [DDIM Sampling with Classifier-Free Guidance](8118-ddim-sampling-with-classifier-free-guidance.md) by integrating classifier-free guidance into the noise prediction step.

### [DDPM Posterior Sampling with Adaptive Step Size](19439-ddpm-posterior-sampling-with-adaptive-step-size.md)
This method performs inverse rendering by jointly denoising an environment map using a Diffusion Posterior Sampling (DPS) prior while simultaneously optimizing material parameters (base color, roughness, metallic) to fit observed input images. It differs from standard denoising by interleaving the diffusion sampling steps with gradient-based material optimization, using an adaptive step size parameter $\rho$ to balance the trade-off between the realism of the generative prior and the fidelity to the input observations.

## Operating characteristics
- **Time Complexity:** $O(N \cdot C)$, where $N$ is the number of inference steps and $C$ is the cost of one denoiser forward pass. DDIM allows significantly smaller $N$ (e.g., 25-50) compared to DDPM (e.g., 1000) for comparable quality [S2, unofficial].
- **Memory Complexity:** $O(D)$, where $D$ is the latent dimension. Requires storing intermediate latents if logging is enabled. Scheduler buffers are $O(T_{max})$ but typically negligible [S2, unofficial].
- **Performance:** Deterministic sampling ($\eta=0$) produces consistent results for the same seed. Stochastic sampling ($\eta>0$) introduces variance. CFG scale $s$ controls the trade-off between fidelity to the condition and sample diversity/quality; too high $s$ can lead to over-saturation or artifacts [S1, S2, unofficial].
- **Strengths:** Fast convergence (few steps), deterministic trajectory, flexible guidance [S2, unofficial].
- **Limitations:** Quality can degrade if $N$ is too small or if the noise schedule is poorly matched. Inpainting requires access to the original clean image $x_0$ [S2, unofficial]. Dynamic thresholding is not implemented in some variants [S2, unofficial].

## Background
DDIM (Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models) was introduced as a deterministic sampling method for diffusion models, enabling faster sampling than DDPM by skipping intermediate steps [S2, unofficial]. Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) was later integrated to improve sample quality without requiring a separate classifier [S2, unofficial]. In the context of volume rendering and NeRFs, DDIM with CFG is used for 3D generation, interpolation, and refinement, as seen in methods like SSDNeRF and GaussianObject [S1, unofficial][S3].

# LoRA Fine-Tuning of DINO

Also called: LoRA-NeRF, NeRF with LoRA-fine-tuned DINO features

## Overview
This method adapts frozen DINO vision features to the geometric objectives of NeRF by applying Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) to the attention layers of the Vision Transformer (ViT) backbone [S1, unofficial]. It addresses the misalignment between semantic DINO features and NeRF’s geometric requirements, which frozen features alone may not satisfy [S1, unofficial]. Unlike frozen feature baselines, this approach makes only low-rank matrices trainable, allowing the model to adapt to few-shot 3D reconstruction while preserving pre-trained knowledge [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Input:** Pre-trained DINOv2-base model weights ($W_0$), training images, and camera poses.
*   **Output:** Fine-tuned DINO features $\mathbf{f}'(x)$ used as input to the NeRF MLP, alongside positional encodings.

**Data Structures:**
*   **LoRA Matrices:** Two trainable matrices per targeted attention layer:
    *   $A \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times d}$
    *   $B \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times r}$
    *   Where $d$ is the dimension of the attention layer weights and $r$ is the LoRA rank.
*   **Feature Projection:** DINO features (768-dimensional) are projected to 256 dimensions before fusion with NeRF’s positional encoding [S1, unofficial].

**Parameters:**
*   **LoRA Rank ($r$):** 16 [S1, unofficial].
*   **Target Layers:** Attention layers of the ViT [S1, unofficial].

**Steps:**
1.  **Initialize:** Load pre-trained DINOv2-base weights $W_0$. Freeze $W_0$ completely [S1, unofficial].
2.  **Inject LoRA:** For each attention layer in the ViT, decompose the weight update as:
    $$
    W = W_0 + BA
    $$
    Initialize $A$ and $B$ with small random values (standard LoRA initialization) [S1, unofficial].
3.  **Feature Extraction:** Pass input images through the modified DINO backbone to extract features $\mathbf{f}'(x)$ [S1, unofficial].
4.  **Cache Features:** Compute and cache DINO features for efficiency during NeRF training [S1, unofficial].
5.  **NeRF Training:** Feed projected features $\mathbf{f}'(x)$ and positional encodings $\gamma(x), \gamma(d)$ into the NeRF MLP $F_\theta$:
    $$
    F_\theta([\gamma(x), \gamma(d), \mathbf{f}'(x)]) \rightarrow (c, \sigma)
    $$
    Optimize $\theta$ and the LoRA matrices $A, B$ using standard NeRF loss functions [S1, unofficial].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
*   **Precision:** Mixed-precision training is used to accelerate experiments, particularly on Apple Silicon (MPS backend) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Computational Cost:** Training typically takes 2-4 hours per experiment, depending on model complexity [S1, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### LoRA Fine-Tuning
A general technique that fine-tunes diffusion models with minimal computational overhead by modifying a few parameters, making it adaptable to new tasks [S2]. In the context of DINO-NeRF, it specifically refers to the parameter-efficient adaptation of the ViT backbone [S1, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Performance:** On the Lego dataset with 5 training views, LoRA-NeRF achieves a PSNR of 12.97, SSIM of 0.45, and LPIPS of 0.54 [S1, unofficial]. This is comparable to frozen DINO-NeRF (PSNR 12.99, SSIM 0.46, LPIPS 0.54) but slightly worse than the baseline NeRF (PSNR 14.71, SSIM 0.46, LPIPS 0.53) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Memory:** Low memory overhead compared to full fine-tuning, as only $A$ and $B$ matrices are stored and updated [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Preserves prior knowledge from ImageNet pre-training while adapting to geometric objectives [S1, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** Quantitative results indicate that DINO variants (including LoRA) perform worse than baseline NeRF on standard metrics for few-shot reconstruction [S1, unofficial].

## Background
Originated from research into the limitations of using pre-trained vision features for few-shot 3D reconstruction [S1, unofficial]. The method leverages Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) [10] to bridge the gap between semantic features and geometric reconstruction needs. The implementation is based on PyTorch and uses the DINOv2-base model [S1, unofficial].

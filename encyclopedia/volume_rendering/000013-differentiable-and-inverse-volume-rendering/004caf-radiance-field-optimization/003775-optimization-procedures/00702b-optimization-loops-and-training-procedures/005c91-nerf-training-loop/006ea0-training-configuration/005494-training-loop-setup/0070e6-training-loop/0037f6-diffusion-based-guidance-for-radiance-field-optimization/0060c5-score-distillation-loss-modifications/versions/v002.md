# Score Distillation Loss Modifications
## Overview
Score Distillation Loss Modifications are techniques that adapt the standard Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) gradient signal to address specific artifacts such as "black holes" in masked regions, incomplete object geometry, or limited high-frequency detail in super-resolution tasks. They set themselves apart from the parent [Diffusion-Based Guidance for Radiance Field Optimization](8117-diffusion-based-guidance-for-radiance-field-optimization.md) by altering the conditioning inputs (masks, background prompts), the diffusion trajectory (interval steps, renoising schedules), or the loss formulation itself to improve geometric consistency and texture fidelity.
## Shared principles
The family builds on [Diffusion-Based Guidance for Radiance Field Optimization](8117-diffusion-based-guidance-for-radiance-field-optimization.md), which computes a gradient signal from a frozen Stable Diffusion model to optimize an input image or latent by predicting noise, applying classifier-free guidance, and backpropagating the difference between predicted and actual noise. All modifications retain the core SDS structure: rendering an image $I$ from radiance field parameters $\Theta$, adding noise $\epsilon$ at timestep $t$ to form $x_t$, predicting noise $\hat{\epsilon}$ via a diffusion model, and computing the gradient $\nabla_\Theta L$ based on the residual $\hat{\epsilon} - \epsilon$. The specific weighting function $\omega(t)$ and the diffusion forward process $x_t = \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t} I + \sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t} \epsilon$ remain foundational, though their application varies (e.g., interval vs. single step, latent vs. pixel space).
## Subtopics

### [Interval Score Matching](8995-interval-score-matching.md)
Interval Score Matching (ISM) replaces the standard DDPM forward process with DDIM inversion to supervise multiple interval steps within the diffusion process, mitigating high reconstruction error associated with single-step noise prediction [8995-interval-score-matching.md]. It is chosen when reducing the "over-saturation" and geometric inconsistency of vanilla SDS is prioritized over the simplicity of single-step optimization. See [Interval Score Matching](8995-interval-score-matching.md).

### [Inpainting SDS Loss](11185-inpainting-sds-loss.md)
Inpainting SDS Loss (LiSDS) guides optimization by conditioning a diffusion model on both a text prompt and a spatial mask representing known scene context, solving the problem of incomplete object generation inherent in vanilla SDS [11185-inpainting-sds-loss.md]. It is distinguished from [Mask+SDS with Background Prompt](14382-mask-sds-with-background-prompt.md) by its specific reliance on the inpainting variant of the diffusion model to harmonize the new object with existing scene information. See [Inpainting SDS Loss](11185-inpainting-sds-loss.md).

### [Mask+SDS with Background Prompt](14382-mask-sds-with-background-prompt.md)
Mask+SDS with Background Prompt mitigates the "black hole" artifact in masked NeRF editing by explicitly conditioning the diffusion model on the surrounding scene content via a concatenated background prompt [14382-mask-sds-with-background-prompt.md]. It sets itself apart from standard masked SDS by appending a description of the background to the object prompt, ensuring the diffusion prior generates plausible geometry for occluded regions rather than defaulting to empty space. See [Mask+SDS with Background Prompt](14382-mask-sds-with-background-prompt.md).

### [Renoised Score Distillation (RSD)](16424-renoised-score-distillation-rsd.md)
Renoised Score Distillation (RSD) is a score-distillation objective for 2D image super-resolution that combines ancestral sampling and SDS to generate sharp images consistent with low-resolution conditioning [16424-renoised-score-distillation-rsd.md]. Unlike standard SDS which optimizes in the latent space $z_0$ and often produces limited details, or ancestral sampling which can generate details structurally inconsistent with the LR input, RSD guides the optimization towards intermediate denoised latents $z_{t-1}$ over a linearly decreasing time schedule. See [Renoised Score Distillation (RSD)](16424-renoised-score-distillation-rsd.md).

### Masked-NeRF + DreamFusion
**Definition**: A baseline method that uses per-scene text prompts to guide the generation of masked regions using score distillation sampling (SDS) loss [S1].
**Step**: Gradually and uniformly decrease the maximum noise steps $t_{max}$ during fitting, until it equals the minimum noise steps $t_{min}$ at the last iteration [S1].
**Parameter**: $t_{max}$: maximum noise steps, decreased to $t_{min}$ [S1].
**Parameter**: $t_{min}$: minimum noise steps [S1].

## Background
Originating from the application of DreamFusion to NeRF editing, this family addresses the limitations of vanilla SDS in constrained generation tasks. The "Masked-NeRF + DreamFusion" baseline was introduced in [S1] to handle inpainting scenarios, noting that vanilla SDS often fails to composite objects correctly. Subsequent works like ISM, LiSDS, and RSD refined these approaches to handle specific artifacts like black holes, texture continuity, and super-resolution details.

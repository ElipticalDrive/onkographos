# Lighting and Appearance Models

Also called: Explicit Lighting Models, Relightable NeRF, Stylized NeRF

## Overview
Lighting and Appearance Models extend the standard NeRF representation $F_\Theta: (\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{d}) \rightarrow (\sigma, \mathbf{c})$ by decoupling material properties from illumination or enforcing 3D consistency in non-photorealistic rendering [G]. Unlike standard NeRF, which learns view-dependent appearance implicitly through a single network outputting color $\mathbf{c}$, these methods enable explicit control over lighting conditions or artistic styles [G]. They solve the problem of fixed illumination in standard radiance fields and the view-inconsistency of naive 2D style transfer .

## Shared principles
The baseline architecture employs two MLPs: a Position MLP $\Phi_{pos}$ taking $\mathbf{x}$ and a Shading MLP $\Phi_{shade}$ taking $\mathbf{h}$ and $\mathbf{d}$ to predict $\sigma$ and $\mathbf{c}$ [G]. These models modify the rendering pipeline to separate albedo/geometry from lighting or to inject style constraints into the volumetric integration [G].

## Subtopics

### [Neural Pre-Integrated Lighting (Neural-PIL)](13783-neural-pre-integrated-lighting-neural-pil.md)
Replaces numerical light integration with an MLP predicting pre-integrated light $\tilde{L}_i$ from an illumination embedding, direction, and roughness. It captures fine illumination details better than Spherical Harmonics.

### [Spherical Harmonics NeRF](14480-spherical-harmonics-nerf.md)
Replaces view-dependent color prediction with an explicit 2nd-order SH illumination model. It separates albedo from lighting for relighting but lacks shadowing and specularities.

### [StylizedNeRF](14882-stylizednerf.md)
Applies global artistic styles while maintaining geometric consistency via mutual learning of a 3D NeRF and a 2D stylization network with latent codes. It avoids the flickering of naive 2D style transfer.

## Background
Standard NeRF  learns appearance implicitly. Neural-PIL  adapts pre-integrated lighting concepts to neural fields. SH NeRF  applies explicit spherical harmonics to radiance fields. StylizedNeRF  addresses the inconsistency of 2D style transfer in 3D.

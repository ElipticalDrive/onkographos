# Diffusion Model Preprocessing & Training Setup

Also called: SDG Preprocessing, Stable Diffusion Training Setup

## Overview
This method defines the data preprocessing pipeline and training configuration for a fine-tuned Stable Diffusion 1.5 model used as a prior in Street View Synthesis with Gaussian Splatting (SGD) [S1]. It addresses the challenge of adapting a 2D diffusion model to guide 3D radiance field optimization by ensuring input images maintain aspect ratio integrity and by establishing a robust reference image selection strategy. It sets apart from generic diffusion setups by specifically handling the extreme aspect ratio distortion of KITTI-360 data and enforcing a "perspective gap" via random cropping of reference images during training to prevent overfitting to exact training views [S1].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Input:** Raw KITTI-360 images (original resolution $1408 \times 376$), LiDAR point clouds, text prompts, and reference images.
*   **Output:** Preprocessed images resized to $512 \times 512$, masked depth maps, and training configurations for the diffusion model.

**Data Structures:**
*   **Image Tensor:** Shape $(B, 3, 512, 512)$, where $B$ is batch size.
*   **Depth Mask:** A binary mask of shape $(512, 512)$ where the top 80 rows are set to 1 (masked) and the rest are 0.

**Steps:**

1.  **Pre-crop to Preserve Aspect Ratio:**
    The original KITTI-360 images ($1408 \times 376$) are center-cropped horizontally to $600 \times 376$. This intermediate step prevents severe aspect ratio distortion during the subsequent resize to square.
    *   *Formula:* Let $W_{orig}=1408, H_{orig}=376$. Crop width $W_{crop} = 600$.
    *   *Pitfall:* Skipping this step and resizing directly from $1408 \times 376$ to $512 \times 512$ causes over-distortion of scene geometry [S1].

2.  **Resize:**
    Resize the $600 \times 376$ image to $512 \times 512$.

3.  **Depth Masking:**
    Apply a mask to the top 80 rows of pixels in the image/depth map. This accounts for LiDAR point cloud height limitations where the sky or upper structures are not captured. The diffusion model's inpainting capability is relied upon to complete this masked region.
    *   *Mask Definition:* $M_{i,j} = 1$ if $i < 80$, else $0$.

4.  **Training Data Augmentation (Perspective Gap):**
    *   **Training Images:** Apply a deterministic center-crop to the training images before resizing.
    *   **Reference Images:** Apply a **random-crop** to reference images during training. This ensures a perspective gap between the reference and the training view, enhancing robustness and preventing the model from simply copying the training view [S1].

5.  **Reference Image Selection:**
    *   **During Training:** Reference images are randomly selected from the dataset.
    *   **During Inference (Existing Views):** Select one frame from the five frames preceding the training image and one from the five frames succeeding it. Apply center-crop to these reference images.
    *   **During Inference (Novel Views):** Identify the closest training viewpoint in terms of camera pose. Use the adjacent frames of this closest training viewpoint as reference images.

6.  **Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) Setup:**
    To enable classifier-free guidance, during training, set both the text prompt and the reference images to empty (null) with a probability of $p=0.1$ (10%). This allows the model to learn unconditional generation capabilities [S1].

## Subtopics

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Preprocessing is linear with respect to the number of pixels. The heavy computational cost lies in the subsequent diffusion model inference, which is not part of this preprocessing step.
*   **Memory Complexity:** Low, as it only involves temporary buffers for cropping and resizing.
*   **Strengths:** Prevents geometric distortion in wide-aspect-ratio street view data. The random cropping of reference images during training improves generalization by forcing the model to learn scene content rather than exact pixel matches.
*   **Limitations:** Relies on the diffusion model's inpainting capability to fill the masked top 80 rows, which may result in hallucinated sky content if the model is not well-tuned. The fixed mask size (80 rows) is specific to the KITTI-360 dataset and LiDAR height limitations [S1].

## Background
This setup originates from the SGD (Street View Synthesis with Gaussian Splatting and Diffusion Prior) method [S1]. It was developed to adapt Stable Diffusion 1.5, originally trained on square images, to the non-square, wide-aspect-ratio images typical of autonomous driving datasets like KITTI-360. The specific preprocessing steps (pre-crop, random reference cropping) were introduced to mitigate overfitting to training views and handle the lack of depth data in the upper regions of the scene due to LiDAR limitations [S1].

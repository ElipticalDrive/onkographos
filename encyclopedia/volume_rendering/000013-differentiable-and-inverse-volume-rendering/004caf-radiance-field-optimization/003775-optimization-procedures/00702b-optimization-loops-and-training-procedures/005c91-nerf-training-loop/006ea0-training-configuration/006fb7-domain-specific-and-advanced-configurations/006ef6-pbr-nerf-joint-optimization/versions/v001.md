# PBR-NeRF Joint Optimization

Also called: NeILF++-based inverse rendering, PBR-NeRF training phases

## Overview
PBR-NeRF Joint Optimization is a training procedure for physically based neural radiance fields that decouples geometry, material, and illumination estimation into three sequential phases before a final joint refinement. It solves the ill-posed inverse rendering problem by using explicit geometric priors to stabilize the SDF, followed by physics-based losses (energy conservation, inter-reflection) to constrain the BRDF and incident light field. It differs from standard NeRF training by optimizing for material parameters (roughness, metallicness) and incident lighting rather than just color, and from single-phase NeILF++ by introducing a dedicated geometry phase and joint optimization loop that reuses the SDF for both rendering and surface property estimation [S1].

## How it works

### Inputs and Outputs
**Inputs:**
*   Multi-view RGB images $c$.
*   Camera poses (extrinsics and intrinsics).
*   Optional: Ground truth geometry (e.g., from NeILF++ dataset) or point clouds with normals (e.g., from DTU dataset) [S1].
*   Environment map (optional, used for supervision in some baselines, but PBR-NeRF typically estimates it) [S1].

**Outputs:**
*   NeRF SDF network parameters $G(x)$ and density/opacity parameters.
*   BRDF MLP parameters (defining albedo, roughness $r$, metallicness $m$).
*   NeILF MLP parameters (defining incident light field $L_i$).

### Data Structures
The method relies on three neural networks:
1.  **NeRF SDF Network:** Inputs 3D position $x$. Outputs signed distance $G(x)$ and density $\sigma$.
2.  **BRDF MLP:** Inputs 3D position $x$ and viewing direction $\omega_o$. Outputs material parameters: albedo $\rho$, roughness $r$, metallicness $m$.
3.  **NeILF MLP:** Inputs 3D position $x$ and incident direction $\omega_i$. Outputs incident radiance $L_i(x, \omega_i)$ [S1].

### Parameters
*   $\lambda_{NeRF}$: Weight for NeRF rendering loss. Value: $1.0$ [S1].
*   $\lambda_{pcd}$: Weight for point cloud loss. Value: $0.1$ (DTU only) [S1].
*   $\lambda_{Eik}$: Weight for Eikonal loss. Value: $0.1$ (DTU only) [S1].
*   $\lambda_{Hess}$: Weight for Hessian loss. Value: $0.001$ (DTU only) [S1].
*   $\lambda_{surf}$: Weight for surface loss. Value: $0.01$ (DTU only) [S1].
*   $\lambda_{pbr}$: Weight for PBR rendering loss. Value: $1.0$ [S1].
*   $\lambda_{smth}$: Weight for smoothness loss. Value: $0.5$ [S1].
*   $\lambda_{ref}$: Weight for inter-reflection loss. Value: $0.1$ [S1].
*   $\lambda_{cons}$: Weight for conservation of energy loss. Value: $0.01$ [S1].
*   $\lambda_{spec}$: Weight for NDF-weighted specular loss. Value: $0.01$ [S1].
*   $|S_L|$: Number of incident light directions. Value: $256$ (increased from NeILF++ default of 128) [S1].
*   $T_{spec}$: Softmax temperature for NDF-weighted Specular Loss. Value: $1$ [S1].
*   $\epsilon$: Sharpness parameter for regularized Dirac delta. Not given in the sources.

### Formulas

**Geometry Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{geo}$):**
$$
\mathcal{L}_{geo} = \lambda_{NeRF}\mathcal{L}_{NeRF} + \lambda_{pcd}\mathcal{L}_{pcd} + \lambda_{Eik}\mathcal{L}_{Eik} + \lambda_{Hess}\mathcal{L}_{Hess} + \lambda_{surf}\mathcal{L}_{surf}
$$
*   $\mathcal{L}_{NeRF} = || L_{o,NeRF} - c ||_2^2$: Mean squared error between rendered NeRF color $L_{o,NeRF}$ and ground truth $c$ [S1].
*   $\mathcal{L}_{pcd} = |G(x_{pcd})| + (1 - n_{pcd} \cdot \frac{\nabla_x G(x_{pcd})}{||\nabla_x G(x_{pcd})||})$: Point cloud loss for SDF initialization, where $x_{pcd}$ is a point cloud point and $n_{pcd}$ is its normal [S1].
*   $\mathcal{L}_{Eik} = | ||\nabla_x G(x)|| - 1 |$: Eikonal loss penalizing SDF gradients not having magnitude 1 [S1].
*   $\mathcal{L}_{Hess} = || H G(x) ||_1$: Hessian loss penalizing rapidly changing gradient directions, where $H$ is the Hessian matrix [S1].
*   $\mathcal{L}_{surf} = \delta_\epsilon(G(x))$: Surface loss encouraging compact surfaces, where $\delta_\epsilon(z) = \frac{\epsilon \pi^{-1}}{\epsilon^2 + z^2}$ is the regularized Dirac delta function [S1].

**Material Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{mat}$):**
$$
\mathcal{L}_{mat} = \lambda_{pbr}\mathcal{L}_{pbr} + \lambda_{smth}\mathcal{L}_{smth} + \lambda_{ref}\mathcal{L}_{ref} + \lambda_{cons}\mathcal{L}_{cons} + \lambda_{spec}\mathcal{L}_{spec}
$$
*   $\mathcal{L}_{pbr} = || L_{o,NeILF++} - c ||_2^2$: Supervises estimated outgoing radiance from the PBR renderer [S1].
*   $\mathcal{L}_{smth} = (||\nabla_x r(x_p)|| + ||\nabla_x m(x_p)||) \exp(-||\nabla_p I_p||)$: Bilateral smoothness loss for roughness $r$ and metallicness $m$, weighted by the inverse of the image gradient magnitude $||\nabla_p I_p||$ at pixel $p$ [S1].
*   $\mathcal{L}_{ref} = || L_{i,NeILF}(x_2, \omega_i) - L_{o,NeRF}(x_1, -\omega_i) ||_1$: Inter-reflection loss, supervising incident light $L_i$ at $x_2$ against outgoing radiance $L_o$ at $x_1$ along direction $\omega_i = -\omega_o$ [S1].

**BRDF Components (NeILF++/PBR-NeRF):**
*   Schlick Fresnel Approximation:
    $$
    F(\omega_o, \omega_h; b, m) = F_0 + (1-F_0)(1 - \omega_o \cdot \omega_h)^5
    $$
*   Approximation for $F_0$:
    $$
    F_0 = 0.04(1-m) + bm
    $$
    where $b$ is the base reflectivity (typically 0.04 for dielectrics, but here parameterized by $m$) [S1].
*   Geometry Term:
    $$
    G(\omega_i, \omega_o, n; r) = G_1(\omega_i \cdot n) G_1(\omega_o \cdot n)
    $$
*   GGX Geometry Function:
    $$
    G_{GGX}(z; r) = \frac{2}{(2-r^2)z + r^2}
    $$
    where $z$ is the dot product of the normal and the half-vector or view/incident vector [S1].

### Steps

1.  **Geometry Phase:**
    *   Initialize NeRF SDF network.
    *   If ground truth geometry is available (e.g., NeILF++ dataset):
        *   Optimize NeRF SDF using only $\mathcal{L}_{NeRF}$ with $\lambda_{NeRF}=1.0$.
        *   Set $\lambda_{pcd}=\lambda_{Eik}=\lambda_{Hess}=\lambda_{surf}=0$.
    *   If no ground truth geometry is available (e.g., DTU dataset):
        *   Optimize NeRF SDF using $\mathcal{L}_{geo}$.
        *   Use point cloud loss $\mathcal{L}_{pcd}$ only for SDF initialization (optional, but used in DTU) [S1].
        *   Use $\mathcal{L}_{Eik}$, $\mathcal{L}_{Hess}$, and $\mathcal{L}_{surf}$ with weights $0.1$, $0.001$, and $0.01$ respectively [S1].
    *   Freeze NeRF SDF weights after convergence.

2.  **Material Phase:**
    *   Initialize NeILF and BRDF MLPs.
    *   Freeze NeRF SDF weights.
    *   Optimize NeILF and BRDF MLPs using $\mathcal{L}_{mat}$.
    *   $\mathcal{L}_{pbr}$ uses the PBR renderer which samples incident light directions from the NeILF MLP.
    *   $\mathcal{L}_{ref}$ uses the NeRF SDF to estimate outgoing radiance $L_{o,NeRF}$ for supervision.
    *   $\mathcal{L}_{smth}$, $\mathcal{L}_{cons}$, and $\mathcal{L}_{spec}$ are applied to material parameters [S1].

3.  **Joint Phase:**
    *   Unfreeze NeRF SDF weights.
    *   Optimize NeRF SDF, BRDF, and NeILF MLPs simultaneously using $\mathcal{L}_{joint} = \mathcal{L}_{geo} + \mathcal{L}_{mat}$.
    *   For each training sample, the NeRF SDF is used twice:
        1.  To estimate $L_{o,NeRF}$ for $\mathcal{L}_{geo}$ and $\mathcal{L}_{ref}$.
        2.  To sphere trace and compute surface point $x$ and normal $n$ for $L_{o,PBR}$ in $\mathcal{L}_{pbr}$ [S1].

### Pseudocode

```python
def pbr_nerf_joint_optimization(images, poses, point_cloud=None, gt_geometry_available=False):
    # Initialize networks
    nerf_sdf = NeRFSDFNetwork()
    brdf_mlp = BRDFMLP()
    neilf_mlp = NeILFMLP()
    
    # Hyperparameters
    lambda_NeRF = 1.0
    lambda_pcd = 0.1 if not gt_geometry_available else 0.0
    lambda_Eik = 0.1 if not gt_geometry_available else 0.0
    lambda_Hess = 0.001 if not gt_geometry_available else 0.0
    lambda_surf = 0.01 if not gt_geometry_available else 0.0
    lambda_pbr = 1.0
    lambda_smth = 0.5
    lambda_ref = 0.1
    lambda_cons = 0.01
    lambda_spec = 0.01
    SL = 256  # Number of incident light directions
    T_spec = 1.0
    
    # Phase 1: Geometry
    print("Starting Geometry Phase")
    for epoch in range(geometry_epochs):
        for image, pose in zip(images, poses):
            # Sample rays
            rays = sample_rays(image, pose)
            
            # Render NeRF color
            Lo_NeRF = render_nerf(nerf_sdf, rays)
            
            # Compute L_NeRF
            L_NeRF = mse_loss(Lo_NeRF, image)
            
            # Compute geometry losses if no GT geometry
            L_pcd = 0.0
            L_Eik = 0.0
            L_Hess = 0.0
            L_surf = 0.0
            
            if not gt_geometry_available:
                if point_cloud is not None:
                    L_pcd = compute_pcd_loss(nerf_sdf, point_cloud)
                L_Eik = compute_eikonal_loss(nerf_sdf, rays)
                L_Hess = compute_hessian_loss(nerf_sdf, rays)
                L_surf = compute_surface_loss(nerf_sdf, rays)
            
            L_geo = (lambda_NeRF * L_NeRF + 
                     lambda_pcd * L_pcd + 
                     lambda_Eik * L_Eik + 
                     lambda_Hess * L_Hess + 
                     lambda_surf * L_surf)
            
            # Update NeRF SDF
            nerf_sdf.backward(L_geo)
            nerf_sdf.step()
            
    # Freeze NeRF SDF
    nerf_sdf.freeze()
    
    # Phase 2: Material
    print("Starting Material Phase")
    for epoch in range(material_epochs):
        for image, pose in zip(images, poses):
            rays = sample_rays(image, pose)
            
            # Render PBR color using NeILF and BRDF
            Lo_PBR = render_pbr(nerf_sdf, brdf_mlp, neilf_mlp, rays, SL)
            
            # Compute L_pbr
            L_pbr = mse_loss(Lo_PBR, image)
            
            # Compute L_smth
            L_smth = compute_smoothness_loss(brdf_mlp, rays, image)
            
            # Compute L_ref
            L_ref = compute_interreflection_loss(nerf_sdf, neilf_mlp, rays)
            
            # Compute L_cons and L_spec (Physics-based losses)
            L_cons = compute_energy_conservation_loss(neilf_mlp, SL)
            L_spec = compute_specular_loss(brdf_mlp, neilf_mlp, SL, T_spec)
            
            L_mat = (lambda_pbr * L_pbr + 
                     lambda_smth * L_smth + 
                     lambda_ref * L_ref + 
                     lambda_cons * L_cons + 
                     lambda_spec * L_spec)
            
            # Update BRDF and NeILF MLPs
            brdf_mlp.backward(L_mat)
            neilf_mlp.backward(L_mat)
            brdf_mlp.step()
            neilf_mlp.step()
            
    # Phase 3: Joint
    print("Starting Joint Phase")
    nerf_sdf.unfreeze()
    for epoch in range(joint_epochs):
        for image, pose in zip(images, poses):
            rays = sample_rays(image, pose)
            
            # 1. Estimate Lo_NeRF for L_geo and L_ref
            Lo_NeRF = render_nerf(nerf_sdf, rays)
            L_NeRF = mse_loss(Lo_NeRF, image)
            
            # Geometry losses (re-evaluated)
            L_Eik = compute_eikonal_loss(nerf_sdf, rays)
            L_Hess = compute_hessian_loss(nerf_sdf, rays)
            L_surf = compute_surface_loss(nerf_sdf, rays)
            L_geo = (lambda_NeRF * L_NeRF + 
                     lambda_Eik * L_Eik + 
                     lambda_Hess * L_Hess + 
                     lambda_surf * L_surf)
            
            # 2. Sphere trace for PBR rendering
            Lo_PBR = render_pbr(nerf_sdf, brdf_mlp, neilf_mlp, rays, SL)
            L_pbr = mse_loss(Lo_PBR, image)
            
            # Material losses
            L_smth = compute_smoothness_loss(brdf_mlp, rays, image)
            L_ref = compute_interreflection_loss(nerf_sdf, neilf_mlp, rays)
            L_cons = compute_energy_conservation_loss(neilf_mlp, SL)
            L_spec = compute_specular_loss(brdf_mlp, neilf_mlp, SL, T_spec)
            
            L_mat = (lambda_pbr * L_pbr + 
                     lambda_smth * L_smth + 
                     lambda_ref * L_ref + 
                     lambda_cons * L_cons + 
                     lambda_spec * L_spec)
            
            L_joint = L_geo + L_mat
            
            # Update all networks
            nerf_sdf.backward(L_joint)
            brdf_mlp.backward(L_joint)
            neilf_mlp.backward(L_joint)
            nerf_sdf.step()
            brdf_mlp.step()
            neilf_mlp.step()
            
    return nerf_sdf, brdf_mlp, neilf_mlp
```

### Edge Cases and Pitfalls
*   **Disney BRDF Energy Conservation:** The Disney BRDF can create excess energy for unnormalized terms or when the Schlick Fresnel approximation is invalid, causing overly bright specular highlights [S1].
*   **Single Reflection Limitation:** Energy can be lost as the underlying microfacet BRDF models rough materials with single reflection instead of multiple scattering on the microsurface [S1].
*   **Point Cloud Initialization:** The point cloud loss $\mathcal{L}_{pcd}$ is only used for SDF initialization and is not used in the joint phase [S1].
*   **Lambertian Loss Removal:** The Lambertian loss used in NeILF++ is removed ($\lambda_{Lam}=0$) to avoid strong limitations on material estimation [S1].

## Operating characteristics

### Time and Memory Complexity
*   **Time:** The method involves three distinct training phases, increasing total training time compared to single-phase NeRF. The joint phase requires two forward passes through the NeRF SDF per sample (one for NeRF rendering, one for sphere tracing), doubling the SDF evaluation cost per step compared to standard NeRF [S1].
*   **Memory:** Requires storing parameters for three networks (NeRF SDF, BRDF MLP, NeILF MLP) and their gradients. The increased number of incident light directions ($|S_L|=256$) compared to NeILF++ ($|S_L|=128$) increases memory usage for light field sampling [S1].

### Measured Performance
*   **Hardware/Resolution:** Not given in the sources.
*   **Scaling:** Not given in the sources.
*   **Strengths:** Improves lighting and material estimation compared to NeILF++ baseline, reducing artifacts in incident light fields and enhancing albedo, roughness, and metallicness estimation [S1]. Specifically reduces artifacts in the upper halves of estimated incident light fields and enhances albedo estimation [S1].
*   **Limitations:** Specular patchy effects may persist on certain objects (e.g., helmet, cube, sphere, floor) [S1].

### When to Use
*   Use when ground truth geometry is unavailable (e.g., DTU dataset) and explicit geometric priors are needed to stabilize SDF learning [S1].
*   Use when high-fidelity material and lighting estimation is required, and computational cost of multi-phase training is acceptable [S1].

### When Not to Use
*   Use when ground truth geometry is available and computational efficiency is prioritized, as the geometry phase can be simplified to only $\mathcal{L}_{NeRF}$ [S1].
*   Use when real-time rendering is required, as the joint optimization and PBR rendering are computationally expensive [S1].

## Background

### Origin
PBR-NeRF was introduced in the paper "PBR-NeRF: Inverse Rendering with Physics-Based Neural Fields" [S1]. It builds upon NeILF++ [51] and IDR [50] by introducing physics-based losses for energy conservation and specular distribution, and a three-phase training strategy.

### History and Development
The method extends NeILF++, which itself builds on IDR. Key developments include increasing the number of incident light directions from 128 to 256 to better enforce energy conservation, and removing the Lambertian loss to allow for more flexible material estimation [S1].

### Where it is Used
Used for inverse rendering tasks, specifically for estimating material properties (albedo, roughness, metallicness) and incident lighting from multi-view RGB images [S1]. Evaluated on the NeILF++ dataset and DTU dataset [S1].

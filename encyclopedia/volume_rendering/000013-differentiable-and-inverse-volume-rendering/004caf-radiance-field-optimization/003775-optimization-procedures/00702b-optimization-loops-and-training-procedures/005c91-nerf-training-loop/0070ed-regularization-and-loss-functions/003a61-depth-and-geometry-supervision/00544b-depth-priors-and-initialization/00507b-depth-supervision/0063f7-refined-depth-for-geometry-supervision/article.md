# Refined Depth for Geometry Supervision

Also called: Depth-regularization objective

## Overview
Refined Depth for Geometry Supervision is a lightweight depth-regularization objective used during per-scene training to refine reconstructed geometry, specifically improving local sharpness and depth consistency without sacrificing efficiency [S1, unofficial]. It sets itself apart from standard photometric losses by explicitly leveraging rendered depths and input images to derive a geometry-supervision loss ($L_{refD}$), which is combined with the original training loss to yield more geometrically faithful surfaces with fast convergence [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Inputs:** Rendered depths $\{D_i\}$ for each camera view $i$, and the associated input images for each view.
*   **Outputs:** A scalar loss value $L_{refD}$ used to update the scene representation (e.g., Sparse Voxel Octree) during optimization.

**Data Structures:**
*   **Rendered Depths $\{D_i\}$:** A set of depth maps, one per camera view, generated by the current state of the volume rendering pipeline. The exact bit-depth or precision is not specified in the sources [S1, unofficial].

**Steps:**
1.  **Derive Refined Depths:** For each camera view $i$, derive refined depth information by advancing the geometry supervision from the rendered depths $D_i$ and the associated input images. The specific mathematical formulation for deriving these refined depths or the exact structure of the loss function $L_{refD}$ is not provided in the available sources [S1, unofficial].
2.  **Compute Loss:** Calculate the geometry-supervision loss $L_{refD}$ based on the derived refined depths.
3.  **Combine Losses:** Combine $L_{refD}$ with the original SVRaster training loss to form the total loss for the per-scene optimization stage.
4.  **Optimize:** Update the scene parameters (e.g., voxel properties) using the combined loss to improve geometric accuracy.

## Subtopics

### [Depth-Regularized Fine-Tuning](20059-depth-regularized-fine-tuning.md)
A per-scene optimization procedure for 3D Gaussian Splatting that uses dense, multi-view consistent ground truth depth maps. It differs from Refined Depth for Geometry Supervision by relying on ground truth depth priors rather than derived refined depths from rendered outputs, and targets 3DGS primitives rather than sparse-voxel structures. Choose this when high reconstruction accuracy and local sharpness are needed for indoor scenes with available ground truth depth, and shorter training times are critical.

## Background
The method was introduced in the paper "Advancing Structured Priors for Sparse-Voxel Surface Reconstruction" [S1, unofficial]. It is part of a broader framework that includes a topology-aligned multi-view fusion mechanism and level-of-detail unprojection to integrate sparse octrees from different camera views into a globally consistent structure. The approach aims to integrate uncertain or incomplete priors through an uncertainty-aware opacity formulation [S1, unofficial].

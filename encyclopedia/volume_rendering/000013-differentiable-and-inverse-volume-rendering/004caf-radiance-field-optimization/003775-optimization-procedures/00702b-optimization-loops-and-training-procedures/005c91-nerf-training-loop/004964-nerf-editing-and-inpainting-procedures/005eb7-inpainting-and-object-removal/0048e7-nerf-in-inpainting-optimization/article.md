# NeRF-In Inpainting Optimization

Also called: Free-Form NeRF Inpainting with RGB-D Priors, NeRF object removal

## Overview
NeRF-In is a framework for removing unwanted objects or retouching undesired regions in a 3D scene represented by a pre-trained Neural Radiance Field (NeRF) [S1, unofficial]. It operates by optimizing the NeRF parameters $\Theta$ using color and depth guidance derived from 2D images, without requiring category-specific training data or procedures [S1, unofficial]. Unlike sibling methods that may rely on multiple inpainted views or semantic segmentation, NeRF-In primarily uses a single user-drawn mask on one view, transferred to other views via video object segmentation, and guides the optimization with inpainted color and completed depth priors [S1, unofficial]. It sets itself apart by jointly minimizing color and depth losses to ensure both visual plausibility and geometric consistency across views [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Input:** A pre-trained NeRF model $F_\Theta: (\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{d}) \to (\sigma, \mathbf{c})$, where $\mathbf{x}$ is a 3D coordinate, $\mathbf{d}$ is the viewing direction, $\sigma$ is volume density, and $\mathbf{c}$ is color [S1, unofficial]. A user-chosen camera position $o_u$ and a free-form binary mask $M_u$ drawn on the rendered view $I_u = F_{\Theta_{image}}(o_u)$, where $M_u = 1$ for pixels outside the masked region (background/valid) and $M_u = 0$ inside (object to remove) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Output:** Updated NeRF parameters $\tilde{\Theta}$ that remove the unwanted object in the masked region across all rendered views [S1, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
*   **NeRF Model $F_\Theta$:** A multi-layer perceptron (MLP) taking position and direction as input, outputting density and color. The sources specify a batch size of 4,096, 128 sample points for the fine model, and 64 for the coarse model, but do not detail the MLP layer dimensions [S1, unofficial].
*   **Camera Trajectory:** A set of $K$ camera positions $O = \{o_s \mid s = 1 \dots K\}$ sampled along a pre-set trajectory (e.g., LLFF test-set trajectory) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Masks:** Transferred masks $M = \{M_s \mid s = 1 \dots K\}$ for each sampled view, generated by transferring $M_u$ [S1, unofficial].
*   **Guidance Materials:**
    *   Guiding color images $I^G = \{I_s^G \mid s = 1 \dots K\}$, where $I_u^G$ is the inpainted user view and $I_s^G$ for $s \neq u$ are inpainted sampled views [S1, unofficial].
    *   Guiding depth images $D^G = \{D_s^G \mid s = 1 \dots K\}$, generated via depth completion [S1, unofficial].

**Numbered Steps:**
1.  **User Input:** The user selects a view $o_u$ from the pre-trained NeRF and draws a mask $M_u$ to specify the region to remove [S1, unofficial].
2.  **View Sampling:** Sample $K$ camera positions $O = \{o_s \mid s = 1 \dots K\}$ along a pre-set trajectory [S1, unofficial].
3.  **Initial Rendering:** Render RGB images $I_s$ and depth images $D_s$ for all sampled camera positions $o_s \in O$ using the current NeRF model $F_\Theta$ [S1, unofficial].
4.  **Mask Transfer:** Transfer the user-drawn mask $M_u$ to the sampled views to generate transferred masks $M = \{M_s \mid s = 1 \dots K\}$ using a video object segmentation method (e.g., STCN) [S1, unofficial].
5.  **Guidance Generation:**
    *   Generate guiding color images $I_s^G$ for each sampled view using an image inpainting method $\rho$ (e.g., MST inpainting network): $I_s^G = \rho(I_s, M_s)$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   Generate guiding depth images $D_s^G$ using a depth completion method $\tau$ (e.g., Fast Bilateral Solver): $D_s^G = \tau(D_s, M_s, I_s^G)$ [S1, unofficial].
6.  **Optimization Setup:** Define the set of all views $O_{all}$ and outside views $O_{out}$. In the main framework, set $O_{all} = \{o_u\}$ (the user-chosen view) and $O_{out} = O$ (all other sampled views) [S1, unofficial].
7.  **Loss Computation:** Compute the color-guiding loss $L_{color}(\Theta)$ and depth-guiding loss $L_{depth}(\Theta)$ (see Formulas below) [S1, unofficial].
8.  **Parameter Update:** Optimize the NeRF parameters $\Theta$ to obtain updated parameters $\tilde{\Theta}$ by minimizing the joint loss $L_{color}(\Theta) + L_{depth}(\Theta)$ using the Adam optimizer for 50,000 steps [S1, unofficial].

**Formulas:**
*   **Volume Rendering:** The expected color $\hat{C}(\mathbf{r})$ of a camera ray $\mathbf{r}(t) = \mathbf{o} + t\mathbf{d}$ is computed via numerical quadrature [S1, unofficial]:
    $$
    \hat{C}(\mathbf{r}) = \sum_{i=1}^{N} T(t_i)(1 - \exp(-\sigma(t_i)\delta_i))c(t_i)
    $$
    where $T(t_i) = \exp(-\sum_{j=1}^{i-1} \sigma(t_j)\delta_j)$, $N$ is the number of quadrature points, $\delta_i = t_{i+1} - t_i$, and $c(t_i), \sigma(t_i)$ are the color and density at point $t_i$ produced by $F_\Theta$ [S1, unofficial].
*   **Depth Rendering:** The rendered depth $D_f(\mathbf{o}_s)$ is computed by accumulating density $\sigma$ from ray batches during volume rendering [S1, unofficial]. Similarly, $D_c(\mathbf{o}_s)$ is the coarse depth [S1, unofficial].
*   **Color-Guiding Loss:** The total color-guiding loss is the sum of the all-view color loss and the outside-view color loss [S1, unofficial]:
    $$
    L_{color}(\Theta) = L_{all}^{color}(\Theta) + L_{out}^{color}(\Theta)
    $$
    where [S1, unofficial]:
    $$
    L_{all}^{color}(\Theta) = \sum_{\mathbf{o} \in O_{all}} \| F_{\Theta_{image}}(\mathbf{o}) - I_{\mathbf{o}}^G \|_2^2
    $$
    $$
    L_{out}^{color}(\Theta) = \sum_{\mathbf{o} \in O_{out}} \| (F_{\Theta_{image}}(\mathbf{o}) - I_{\mathbf{o}}^G) \odot M_{\mathbf{o}} \|_2^2
    $$
    Here, $F_{\Theta_{image}}(\mathbf{o})$ is the rendered image at camera position $\mathbf{o}$, $I_{\mathbf{o}}^G$ is the guiding image, and $M_{\mathbf{o}}$ is the transferred mask [S1, unofficial]. The mask $M_{\mathbf{o}}$ restricts the outside-view loss to the masked region [S1, unofficial].
*   **Depth-Guiding Loss:** Measures the L2 distance between rendered fine/coarse depth images and guiding depth [S1, unofficial]:
    $$
    L_{depth}(\Theta) = \sum_{\mathbf{o}_s \in O} \left( \| D_f(\mathbf{o}_s) - D^G(\mathbf{o}_s) \|_2^2 + \| D_c(\mathbf{o}_s) - D^G(\mathbf{o}_s) \|_2^2 \right)
    $$
*   **Total Loss:**
    $$
    \tilde{\Theta} := \arg \min_{\Theta} L_{color}(\Theta) + L_{depth}(\Theta)
    $$

**Parameters:**
*   $K$: Number of sampled camera positions/views. Not given in the sources.
*   Batch size: 4,096 [S1, unofficial].
*   Sample points in fine model: 128 [S1, unofficial].
*   Sample points in coarse model: 64 [S1, unofficial].
*   Optimizer: Adam [S1, unofficial].
*   Optimization steps: 50,000 steps for inpainting [S1, unofficial].
*   Hardware: Intel i7-7800X CPU and GTX-1080 GPU [S1, unofficial].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
*   **Masked MSE Only:** Optimizing only with masked Mean Squared Error (MSE) on the transferred masks without explicit guidance causes the unwanted object to remain in the result [S1, unofficial].
*   **Depth Loss Only:** Using only the depth-guiding loss ($L_{depth}$) introduces color noise outside the masked region and loses color information in flat regions [S1, unofficial].
*   **Multiple Color Priors:** Using multiple inpainted images as color guidance introduces visual inconsistency between views [S1, unofficial]. The method restricts color guidance to the user-chosen view to maintain consistency [S1, unofficial].
*   **Guidance Artifacts:** If the initial 2D inpainting or depth completion contains artifacts, the optimized NeRF may output undesired results [S1, unofficial].

import torch
import torch.nn as nn
import torch.optim as optim

def volume_render_color(F_Theta, ray_origins, ray_directions, near, far, num_samples):
    """Computes expected color via volume rendering integral [S1, unofficial]."""
    t_vals = torch.linspace(near, far, num_samples)
    deltas = t_vals[..., 1:] - t_vals[..., :-1]
    deltas = torch.cat([deltas, torch.tensor([1e10], device=t_vals.device)], dim=-1)
    pts = ray_origins.unsqueeze(-1) + ray_directions.unsqueeze(-1) * t_vals.unsqueeze(-1)
    sigma, color = F_Theta(pts, ray_directions)
    alpha = 1.0 - torch.exp(-sigma * deltas)
    T = torch.cumprod(torch.exp(-sigma * deltas), dim=-1)
    T = torch.cat([torch.ones_like(T[..., :1]), T[..., :-1]], dim=-1)
    weights = alpha * T
    C = torch.sum(weights.unsqueeze(-1) * color, dim=-2)
    return C

def volume_render_depth(F_Theta, ray_origins, ray_directions, near, far, num_samples):
    """Computes depth by accumulating density from ray batches [S1, unofficial]."""
    t_vals = torch.linspace(near, far, num_samples)
    deltas = t_vals[..., 1:] - t_vals[..., :-1]
    deltas = torch.cat([deltas, torch.tensor([1e10], device=t_vals.device)], dim=-1)
    pts = ray_origins.unsqueeze(-1) + ray_directions.unsqueeze(-1) * t_vals.unsqueeze(-1)
    sigma, _ = F_Theta(pts, ray_directions)
    weights = sigma * deltas
    depth = torch.sum(weights * t_vals, dim=-1) / (torch.sum(weights, dim=-1) + 1e-10)
    return depth

def transfer_mask_vos(M_u, I_u, I_s, o_u, o_s):
    """Transfers mask M_u from view o_u to o_s using video object segmentation (e.g., STCN) [S1, unofficial]."""
    # Standard VOS forward pass; returns binary mask M_s
    return M_s

def inpaint_color_guidance(I_s, M_s):
    """Generates color prior I_s^G using 2D inpainting (e.g., MST network) [S1, unofficial]."""
    return I_s_G

def complete_depth_guidance(D_s, M_s, I_s_G):
    """Generates depth prior D_s^G using depth completion (e.g., Fast Bilateral Solver) [S1, unofficial]."""
    return D_s_G

def nerf_in_inpainting(F_Theta, o_u, M_u, I_u, K, trajectory, num_steps=50000, batch_size=4096, fine_samples=128, coarse_samples=64):
    """NeRF-In Inpainting Optimization [S1, unofficial]."""
    # 1. Sample K camera positions
    O = trajectory(K)
    # 2. Render initial views
    I_s_list, D_s_list = [], []
    for o_s in O:
        rays_o, rays_d, near, far = generate_rays(o_s)
        I_s = volume_render_color(F_Theta, rays_o, rays_d, near, far, fine_samples)
        D_s = volume_render_depth(F_Theta, rays_o, rays_d, near, far, fine_samples)
        I_s_list.append(I_s)
        D_s_list.append(D_s)
    # 3. Transfer mask
    M_list = []
    for i, o_s in enumerate(O):
        M_s = transfer_mask_vos(M_u, I_u, I_s_list[i], o_u, o_s)
        M_list.append(M_s)
    # 4. Generate guidance
    I_G_list, D_G_list = [], []
    for i, o_s in enumerate(O):
        I_G = inpaint_color_guidance(I_s_list[i], M_list[i])
        D_G = complete_depth_guidance(D_s_list[i], M_list[i], I_G)
        I_G_list.append(I_G)
        D_G_list.append(D_G)
    I_u_G = inpaint_color_guidance(I_u, M_u)
    # 5. Define view sets: O_all = {o_u}, O_out = O
    O_all = [o_u]
    O_out = O
    # 6. Optimization
    optimizer = optim.Adam(F_Theta.parameters(), lr=0.0001)
    for step in range(num_steps):
        optimizer.zero_grad()
        # L_all_color on user view
        rays_o_u, rays_d_u, near_u, far_u = generate_rays(o_u)
        I_u_render = volume_render_color(F_Theta, rays_o_u, rays_d_u, near_u, far_u, fine_samples)
        L_all_color = torch.mean((I_u_render - I_u_G) ** 2)
        # L_out_color on other views, masked
        L_out_color = torch.tensor(0.0, device=F_Theta.device)
        for i, o_s in enumerate(O_out):
            rays_o_s, rays_d_s, near_s, far_s = generate_rays(o_s)
            I_s_render = volume_render_color(F_Theta, rays_o_s, rays_d_s, near_s, far_s, fine_samples)
            diff = (I_s_render - I_G_list[i]) * M_list[i]
            L_out_color += torch.mean(diff ** 2)
        L_color = L_all_color + L_out_color
        # L_depth on all sampled views
        L_depth = torch.tensor(0.0, device=F_Theta.device)
        for i, o_s in enumerate(O):
            rays_o_s, rays_d_s, near_s, far_s = generate_rays(o_s)
            D_f = volume_render_depth(F_Theta, rays_o_s, rays_d_s, near_s, far_s, fine_samples)
            D_c = volume_render_depth(F_Theta, rays_o_s, rays_d_s, near_s, far_s, coarse_samples)
            D_G = D_G_list[i]
            L_depth += torch.mean((D_f - D_G) ** 2) + torch.mean((D_c - D_G) ** 2)
        loss = L_color + L_depth
        loss.backward()
        optimizer.step()
    return F_Theta

## Subtopics

### Removing-NeRF
A method that removes objects by optimizing a NeRF using posed RGB-D images with 2D masks and 2D inpainted frames from LaMa [S2]. It employs confidence-based view selection to automatically exclude inconsistent views during optimization [S2].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Not given in the sources. Training a new NeRF takes 18-20 hours for 200,000 steps; inpainting optimization takes about 5 hours for 50,000 steps [S1, unofficial].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Not given in the sources.
*   **Measured Performance:** Evaluated on the LLFF dataset and a custom dataset with 24 camera positions per scene [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** No category-specific training data or procedure required; generates visually plausible inpainted results with correct geometry and view consistency [S1, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** Relies on the quality of 2D inpainting and depth completion priors; artifacts in guidance materials can propagate to the 3D result [S1, unofficial]. Using multiple inpainted images as color guidance can introduce visual inconsistency [S1, unofficial].
*   **When to Use:** When removing unwanted objects from a pre-trained NeRF without access to category-specific training data or when a single user-drawn mask is sufficient to define the region of interest [S1, unofficial].
*   **When Not to Use:** When high-quality 2D inpainting is not available or when the scene requires complex semantic understanding beyond geometric masking [S1, unofficial].

## Background
NeRF-In was introduced by Hao-Kang Liu, I-Chao Shen, and Bing-Yu Chen in 2022 [S1, unofficial]. It is the first framework to enable free-form inpainting on NeRFs without category-specific training [S1, unofficial]. It builds on the principles of differentiable volume rendering and 2D image inpainting techniques [S1, unofficial].

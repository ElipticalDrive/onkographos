# NeRF Editing and Inpainting Procedures

Also called: NeRF Editing, NeRF Inpainting, 3D Scene Editing

## Overview
NeRF Editing and Inpainting Procedures modify a pre-trained Neural Radiance Field (NeRF) to remove objects, fill occluded regions, or alter appearance and geometry based on user intent [G]. Unlike standard training which reconstructs a scene from scratch, these methods optimize existing parameters to satisfy new constraints while maintaining multi-view consistency [G]. They are distinguished from sibling methods by their reliance on external 2D priors (such as inpainted images or diffusion models) to guide 3D optimization, rather than relying solely on multi-view geometry or semantic segmentation alone [G].

## Shared principles
All methods in this family operate on a pre-trained NeRF representation $f_\theta: (\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{d}) \to (\sigma, \mathbf{c})$, where $\mathbf{x}$ is a 3D coordinate, $\mathbf{d}$ is the viewing direction, $\sigma$ is volume density, and $\mathbf{c}$ is color [G]. The core mechanism involves an optimization loop that minimizes a loss function $L$ to update the NeRF parameters $\theta$ (or a subset thereof) [G].

The general optimization objective is:
$$
L = L_{render} + \lambda_{guidance} L_{guidance} + \lambda_{reg} L_{reg}
$$
where $L_{render}$ is the standard photometric reconstruction loss on visible pixels, $L_{guidance}$ incorporates external priors (e.g., 2D inpainted images, diffusion model gradients, or semantic features), and $L_{reg}$ prevents artifacts or overfitting [G].

Key shared components include:
1. **Masking/Selection:** A binary or probabilistic mask $M$ or selection field identifies the region of interest (ROI) or occluded areas, typically user-defined via sketching or language instructions [G].
2. **External Priors:** Methods rely on 2D priors to guide 3D changes, commonly using diffusion guidance (e.g., Score Distillation Sampling from text-to-image models) or semantic/text features (e.g., CLIP or LSeg) to align rendered features with prompts [G].
3. **View Consistency Enforcement:** To prevent inconsistencies across views, methods enforce multi-view constraints through implicit geometrical and photometric priors embodied by the neural network, which automatically propagates single-view edits to all views [G].

```python
def edit_nerf(nerf_model, mask, guidance_priors, optimizer, epochs):
    """
    General optimization loop for NeRF editing.
    """
    for epoch in range(epochs):
        optimizer.zero_grad()
        total_loss = 0.0
        rays = sample_rays(nerf_model.scene_bounds)
        rendered_colors, transmittances = volume_render(nerf_model, rays)
        loss_render = compute_photometric_loss(rendered_colors, target_images, mask)
        total_loss += loss_render
        loss_guidance = compute_guidance_loss(rendered_colors, guidance_priors, mask)
        total_loss += lambda_guidance * loss_guidance
        loss_reg = compute_regularization(nerf_model.parameters)
        total_loss += lambda_reg * loss_reg
        total_loss.backward()
        optimizer.step()
    return nerf_model
```

## Subtopics

### [Progressive Inpainting-and-Erasing](8658-progressive-inpainting-and-erasing.md)
Progressive Inpainting-and-Erasing (PERF) reconstructs a panoramic NeRF from a single input panorama by iteratively expanding viewpoint coverage. It solves geometry conflicts between visible and invisible regions by computing conflict masks and erasing inconsistent pixels from supervision. Unlike methods requiring multiple views, PERF generates novel views via inpainting and progressively integrates them.

### [Deceptive-NeRF/3DGS Pipeline](11860-deceptive-nerf-3dgs-pipeline.md)
Deceptive-NeRF/3DGS enhances sparse-view reconstruction by densifying input observations with synthetic pseudo-observations generated by a diffusion model during training. It acquires uncertainty through correspondence matching by reprojecting real observations to novel views, enforcing epipolar constraints. It conditions a 2D diffusion model on rendered RGB, depth, and view-consistency uncertainty maps to rectify artifacts.

### [Blended-NeRF](12273-blended-nerf.md)
Blended-NeRF edits specific regions of interest (ROI) in an existing NeRF using text prompts and a user-defined 3D bounding box. It initializes a generator NeRF with original weights and blends synthesized content with the original scene using volumetric blending. Unlike methods requiring semantic masks, it operates on any 3D box without learning a new feature space.

### [NeRF Editing Procedure](12491-nerf-editing-procedure.md)
This procedure enables direct 3D editing by decoupling semantic/geometric selection from appearance rendering using a Distilled Feature Field (DFF). It defines a selection probability $p$ for each 3D point, allowing precise operations like deletion, colorization, and geometric transformation. It avoids iterative gradient descent for the edit itself, relying on a deterministic query-and-transform pipeline during rendering.

### [NeRF-Art](13438-nerf-art.md)
NeRF-Art is a text-guided method for stylizing pre-trained NeRFs that simultaneously alters appearance and 3D geometry without mesh guidance. It introduces a global-local contrastive learning strategy combined with directional constraints to ensure style adherence and cross-view consistency.

### [RePaint-NeRF Limitations](14340-repaint-nerf-limitations.md)
This article documents the empirical limitations of the RePaint-NeRF method, specifically regarding computational efficiency, geometric fidelity under significant shape changes, and view-angle consistency. It serves as a reference for failure modes and resource constraints in diffusion-guided NeRF editing.

### [Semantic and Language-Guided Editing](14393-semantic-and-language-guided-editing.md)
Semantic and Language-Guided Editing modifies NeRFs using high-level semantic cues (text prompts, language instructions, or semantic masks). It solves localized 3D content manipulation while maintaining multi-view consistency. It integrates external semantic priors (e.g., CLIP, LSeg, or diffusion models) into the optimization loop to guide 3D structure and appearance.

### [InNeRF360](14845-innerf360.md)
InNeRF360 combines multiview-consistent 2D priors with 3D diffusion guidance to reconstruct missing or edited regions. It addresses occluded or removed objects by first establishing coherent 2D inpainting across all views, then refining the 3D representation. It differs from direct 3D optimization by decoupling content generation (via 2D inpainting) from 3D reconstruction.

### [Diffusion-guided editing](15110-diffusion-guided-editing.md)
Diffusion-guided editing modifies pre-trained NeRFs using text prompts or image instructions by leveraging 2D text-to-image diffusion models as external priors. Unlike sibling methods relying on semantic masks or geometric constraints, this family uses diffusion gradients or iterative image synthesis to guide 3D changes.

### [Inpainting and Object Removal](17712-inpainting-and-object-removal.md)
This family removes unwanted objects or fills occluded regions in a pre-trained NeRF [Parent]. Unlike standard training, these methods optimize NeRF parameters to erase specific masked regions while maintaining geometric and photometric consistency across unmasked views [Parent]. They rely on external 2D priors (such as inpainted images or diffusion models) to guide 3D optimization [Parent].

## Background
The field of NeRF editing emerged as NeRFs gained popularity for novel view synthesis, with early works focusing on object removal and scene manipulation [G]. Key developments include the introduction of diffusion-guided editing (e.g., RePaint-NeRF, Instruct-NeRF2NeRF) which leveraged 2D generative models to guide 3D optimization [G]. Subsequent methods like Blended-NeRF and NeRF-Art introduced techniques for localized editing and stylization using text prompts and volumetric blending [G]. Recent advancements focus on improving multi-view consistency through semantic priors and progressive inpainting strategies [G].

# Customize your NeRF

Also called: Local-Global Iterative Editing with Diffusion Priors

## Overview
Customize your NeRF enhances editing quality by integrating 2D diffusion models as generative priors to generate highly detailed and stylized textures [S1]. It sets itself apart from sibling methods like [RePaint-NeRF](14365-repaint-nerf.md) and [Instruct-NeRF2NeRF Optimization](15089-instruct-nerf2nerf-optimization.md) by explicitly alternating between local region fine-tuning and global scene consistency optimization [S1]. This approach addresses the limitation of standard NeRF editing where geometric foundations may be flawed, causing edits to appear distorted or inconsistent with the scene’s true 3D structure [S1].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
- **Inputs:** A pre-trained NeRF model, a target region for editing, a diffusion model (2D), and a reference image or prompt generated by the diffusion model.
- **Outputs:** An edited NeRF model with updated density and color fields reflecting the stylized or detailed local changes while maintaining global coherence.

**Data Structures:**
- **NeRF Model:** Standard MLP or grid-based representation storing density ($\sigma$) and view-dependent color ($c$) at query points [S1].
- **Diffusion Model:** Pre-trained 2D latent diffusion model used for generating high-fidelity texture priors [S1].

**Numbered Steps:**
1. **Local Region Definition:** Identify the spatial region within the NeRF scene to be edited [S1].
2. **Diffusion Prior Generation:** Use the 2D diffusion model to generate a reference image for the local region based on user intent (text or image prompt) [S1].
3. **Local Fine-Tuning:** Optimize the NeRF parameters restricted to the local region to match the diffusion-generated image. This involves minimizing the reconstruction error between the rendered local view and the diffusion prior [S1].
4. **Global Optimization:** Optimize the entire NeRF scene to ensure global consistency. This step aligns the locally edited region with the surrounding unedited geometry and appearance [S1].
5. **Iterative Loop:** Repeat steps 3 and 4 (Local-Global Iterative Editing) until convergence or a fixed number of iterations is reached [S1].
6. **Inverse Optimization (Optional):** For integrating new real-world imagery, project new input images into the latent space of the pre-trained NeRF by minimizing reconstruction error [S1].

**Formulas:**
- **Local Loss:** Minimize $L_{local} = || R_{local}(\theta_{local}) - I_{diffusion} ||^2$, where $R_{local}$ is the rendered image of the local region, $\theta_{local}$ are the NeRF parameters for that region, and $I_{diffusion}$ is the diffusion-generated image [S1].
- **Global Loss:** Minimize $L_{global} = || R_{global}(\theta_{global}) - I_{reference} ||^2$, where $R_{global}$ is the rendered image of the full scene and $I_{reference}$ is the original or reference view [S1].
- **Total Loss:** $L_{total} = \lambda_1 L_{local} + \lambda_2 L_{global}$, where $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$ are weighting factors [S1].

**Parameters:**
- **Iteration Count:** Not given in the sources.
- **Learning Rate:** Not given in the sources.
- **Loss Weights ($\lambda_1, \lambda_2$):** Not given in the sources.

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
- **View Inconsistency:** Diffusion models lack 3D awareness, leading to view-inconsistent details that cause flickering or "painted-on" effects at novel angles [S1].
- **Contradictory Gradients:** Stylized features from diffusion models (e.g., non-physical shadows) conflict with physically-based volume rendering, creating contradictory gradients during NeRF optimization [S1].
- **Feature Compatibility:** Inverse optimization faces challenges where stylized diffusion features conflict with NeRF latent space physical rendering characteristics [S1].
- **Convergence Speed:** The local-global strategy induces contradictory gradient directions during inverse optimization, significantly slowing down convergence [S1].

**Pseudocode:**
```python
def customize_your_nerf(nerf_model, diffusion_model, local_region, prompt, iterations):
    """
    Customizes a NeRF model using diffusion priors via local-global iterative editing.
    
    Args:
        nerf_model: Pre-trained NeRF model.
        diffusion_model: Pre-trained 2D diffusion model.
        local_region: Spatial bounds of the region to edit.
        prompt: Text or image prompt for diffusion model.
        iterations: Number of local-global iterations.
        
    Returns:
        Edited NeRF model.
    """
    # Generate diffusion prior for the local region
    diffusion_prior = diffusion_model.generate(prompt, local_region)
    
    for i in range(iterations):
        # Step 1: Local Fine-Tuning
        # Optimize NeRF parameters within local_region to match diffusion_prior
        local_loss = compute_reconstruction_loss(
            nerf_model.render(local_region), 
            diffusion_prior
        )
        optimize(nerf_model.parameters(local_region), local_loss)
        
        # Step 2: Global Optimization
        # Optimize entire NeRF for global consistency
        global_loss = compute_reconstruction_loss(
            nerf_model.render_full_scene(), 
            nerf_model.render_full_scene() # Reference to original or current state
        )
        optimize(nerf_model.parameters(), global_loss)
        
        # Note: Inverse optimization for real-world data integration would be applied here
        # if new imagery is provided, by projecting into latent space.
        
    return nerf_model
```

## Subtopics

### Local-Global Iterative Editing
Alternates between local region fine-tuning and global scene optimization to balance detail and coherence. Choose when high-fidelity local edits are needed without breaking global structure [S1].

### Inverse Optimization for Real-World Data
Projects new input images into the NeRF latent space by minimizing reconstruction error. Choose when integrating new real-world imagery into an existing NeRF [S1].

## Operating characteristics
- **Time Complexity:** Training time increases by 2.3–3.5 times due to the constant switching of optimization targets between local and global scopes [S1].
- **Memory Complexity:** Not given in the sources.
- **Measured Performance:** Generates highly detailed, stylized edits on object-centric images [S1].
- **Scaling:** Not given in the sources.
- **Strengths:** Enhances editing quality with detailed and stylized textures; ensures both fine-grained control and overall coherence [S1].
- **Limitations:** Diffusion models lack 3D awareness, leading to view-inconsistent details and flickering; stylized features conflict with physical rendering, causing contradictory gradients and slow convergence [S1].
- **When to Use:** When high-fidelity, stylized local edits are required and computational cost is acceptable [S1].
- **When Not to Use:** When real-time performance or strict 3D consistency is critical, or when integrating new real-world data with complex stylizations [S1].

## Background
Customize your NeRF was introduced in [18] (cited in [S1]) as a method to enhance editing quality by integrating 2D diffusion models. It addresses the challenge of bridging the domain gap between 2D generative priors and the 3D physical world of NeRF [S1]. The method is particularly relevant for applications requiring free-viewpoint navigation, such as UAV applications, where view consistency is critical [S1].

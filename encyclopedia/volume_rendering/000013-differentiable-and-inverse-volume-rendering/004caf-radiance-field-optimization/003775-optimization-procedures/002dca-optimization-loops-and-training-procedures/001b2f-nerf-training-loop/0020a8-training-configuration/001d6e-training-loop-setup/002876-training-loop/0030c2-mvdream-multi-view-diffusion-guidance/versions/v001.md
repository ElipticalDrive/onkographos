# MVDream Multi-View Diffusion Guidance

Also called: Multi-View Dream

## Overview
MVDream Multi-View Diffusion Guidance is a method that utilizes diffusion priors to generate different viewpoints of a scene iteratively, providing gradient guidance for 3D optimization [S1][S2, unofficial]. It sets itself apart from single-view guidance methods by encoding RGB images and camera poses into a multi-view diffusion model to predict noise, thereby enforcing multi-view consistency during the radiance field optimization process [S2, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs**
*   **Inputs:**
    *   Text prompts (string).
    *   Camera poses (4x4 matrices, batched).
    *   Predicted RGB images (batched, typically 256x256 resolution).
    *   Diffusion model components: Text encoder, Image encoder, Multi-view diffusion UNet, DDIM scheduler.
*   **Outputs:**
    *   Gradient signal derived from the difference between predicted noise and actual noise in the latent space, used to update the 3D representation.

**Data Structures**
*   **Camera Tensor:** Shape `[batch_size, 16]`. Created by normalizing the 4x4 pose matrix and flattening it. The normalization involves converting to Blender convention (flipping Y and Z axes) and standardizing the pose representation [S2, unofficial].
*   **Context Dictionary:** Contains:
    *   `context`: Text embeddings, shape `[B, 77, 768]`.
    *   `camera`: Camera tensor, shape `[B, 16]`.
    *   `num_frames`: Integer, typically 4 [S2, unofficial].

**Parameters**
*   `t_range`: List of floats, default `[0.02, 0.98]`. Defines the range of noise levels for training.
    *   `min_step = int(1000 * t_range[0])`
    *   `max_step = int(1000 * t_range[1])` [S2, unofficial].
*   `guidance_scale`: Float, default `100`. Multiplier for classifier-free guidance [S2, unofficial].

**Algorithm Steps**
1.  **Encode Text:** Encode the text prompts into embeddings. Repeat the embeddings to match the batch size required by the multi-view model (e.g., 4 views).
    $$ \text{pos\_embeds} = \text{encode\_text}(\text{prompts}).\text{repeat}(4, 1, 1) $$
2.  **Convert Camera Convention:** Transform the input camera poses to the Blender convention. This involves reordering the columns and flipping the Y-axis.
    $$ \text{camera}_{\text{temp}} = \text{camera}[:, [0, 2, 1, 3]] $$
    $$ \text{camera}_{\text{temp}}[:, 1] *= -1 $$
3.  **Normalize Camera:** Normalize the transformed camera poses and flatten them into the 16-dimensional vector.
    $$ \text{camera}_{\text{norm}} = \text{normalize\_camera}(\text{camera}_{\text{temp}}).\text{view}(\text{batch\_size}, 16) $$
4.  **Encode Images:** Encode the predicted RGB images (resized to 256x256) into latent space representations.
    $$ \text{latents} = \text{encode\_imgs}(\text{pred\_rgb\_256}) $$
5.  **Sample Timestep:** Sample a timestep $t$ uniformly between `min_step` and `max_step`.
    $$ t \sim \text{Uniform}(\text{min\_step}, \text{max\_step}) $$
6.  **Forward Model with CFG:** Pass the latents, timestep, and context through the diffusion model. Apply Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) by splitting the output into conditional and unconditional predictions.
    $$ \text{noise\_pred} = \text{model}(\text{latent\_model\_input}, t, \text{context}) $$
    $$ \text{noise\_pred\_uncond}, \text{noise\_pred\_cond} = \text{noise\_pred}.\text{chunk}(2) $$
    $$ \text{noise\_pred} = \text{noise\_pred\_uncond} + \text{guidance\_scale} \times (\text{noise\_pred\_cond} - \text{noise\_pred\_uncond}) $$
7.  **Step Scheduler:** Use the DDIM scheduler to step the latents based on the predicted noise.
    $$ \text{latents} = \text{scheduler}.\text{step}(\text{noise\_pred}, t, \text{latents}).\text{prev\_sample} $$

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls**
*   **Camera Convention:** Incorrect conversion to Blender convention (flipping Y and Z) will result in inverted or rotated views, breaking multi-view consistency [S2, unofficial].
*   **Timestep Range:** Using the full range `[0, 1]` may lead to unstable gradients. The default `[0.02, 0.98]` avoids extreme noise levels [S2, unofficial].
*   **Guidance Scale:** A high guidance scale (e.g., 100) can lead to over-saturation or artifacts if not balanced with other losses [S2, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Dominated by the diffusion model inference and scheduler steps. Each guidance step requires a forward pass through the UNet and a scheduler step.
*   **Memory Complexity:** Requires storing latents, text embeddings, and camera tensors. The multi-view nature increases memory usage compared to single-view methods.
*   **Strengths:** Enforces multi-view consistency effectively, reducing the "frozen lake" or "multi-face" artifacts common in single-view diffusion-guided NeRF training.
*   **Limitations:** Computationally expensive due to the multi-view diffusion model. Requires careful tuning of `guidance_scale` and `t_range`.
*   **When to Use:** When multi-view consistency is critical and computational resources allow for multi-view diffusion inference.
*   **When Not to Use:** For real-time applications or when single-view consistency is sufficient.

## Background
MVDream builds upon the DreamGaussian framework, which uses diffusion priors for text-to-3D generation [S1][S2, unofficial]. It specifically addresses the limitation of single-view guidance by incorporating multi-view diffusion models, such as Zero-1-to-3 XL, to generate consistent viewpoints [S1]. The method is part of the broader effort to improve the quality and consistency of 3D assets generated from text or single images.

# RegNeRF

Also called: Regularized NeRF
## Overview
RegNeRF is a regularization framework for Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) designed to improve view synthesis quality from sparse input views [S1, unofficial]. It addresses the problem of MLP overfitting and unrealistic interpolation in few-shot scenarios by applying priors that constrain unobserved views [S1, unofficial]. Unlike its sibling DiffNeRF, which uses automatic differentiation on ray-based representations, RegNeRF approximates gradients via finite differences on patch-based dataloaders [S1, unofficial].
## How it works
RegNeRF introduces two specific priors to constrain the learned radiance field during optimization [S1, unofficial].

1.  **Normalizing Flow Prior**: This prior models the distribution of the scene's radiance and density values, encouraging the network to output values consistent with natural image statistics [S1, unofficial].
2.  **Smoothness Prior**: This prior enforces spatial smoothness on the radiance field, reducing high-frequency noise and artifacts common in sparse data regimes [S1, unofficial].

The method operates by modifying the standard NeRF loss function to include terms derived from these priors. The optimization loop uses patch-based sampling to compute local gradients for the smoothness term [S1, unofficial].

**Parameters**:
-   Weight for Normalizing Flow prior: Not given in the sources.
-   Weight for Smoothness prior: Not given in the sources.

**Edge cases**:
-   Sparse inputs: The method is specifically designed for this regime, but extreme sparsity may still lead to ambiguity without sufficient prior strength [S1, unofficial].
-   Precision: Finite difference approximations for gradients may introduce numerical instability if patch sizes are not chosen carefully [G].

## Subtopics

### [DiffNeRF](8572-diffnerf.md)
DiffNeRF is a regularization framework for Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) that uses differential geometry to improve robustness when training with sparse or inconsistent data. It addresses the problem of MLP overfitting and unrealistic interpolation in few-shot scenarios by modifying the NeRF to represent continuous, infinitely differentiable functions. Unlike its sibling RegNeRF, which approximates gradients via finite differences on patch-based dataloaders, DiffNeRF uses automatic differentiation to compute depth gradients directly from the ray-based NeRF representation, allowing regularization of unseen views without specialized dataloader modifications.

## Operating characteristics
RegNeRF is evaluated on the DTU dataset [S1, unofficial]. It demonstrates improved robustness compared to standard NeRF when training with sparse inputs [S1, unofficial]. Specific time and memory complexity metrics are not provided in the sources. The method is suitable for scenarios with limited camera poses where standard NeRF fails to generalize [S1, unofficial].
## Background
RegNeRF was presented at CVPR 2022 [S1, unofficial]. It is part of the broader effort to regularize Neural Radiance Fields for sparse view synthesis [S1, unofficial].

# Depth and Geometry Supervision

Also called: Depth-guided NeRF, Depth-Supervised NeRF, Single-view NeRF with Depth Map

## Overview
Depth and Geometry Supervision methods address the shape-radiance ambiguity and under-constrained optimization problems inherent in Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF), particularly in sparse-view, single-view, or texture-less scenarios [7496-guided-optimization-of-nerf.md][8679-single-view-nerf-training-with-depth-map.md][8687-depth-supervised-nerf.md]. By incorporating explicit geometric constraints—via monocular depth priors, Structure-from-Motion (SfM) keypoints, or deformable alignment—these methods accelerate convergence and improve reconstruction quality where standard volume rendering fails [7496-guided-optimization-of-nerf.md][8687-depth-supervised-nerf.md].

## Shared principles
All methods in this family integrate external geometric information into the NeRF training loop to regularize the density field $\sigma$. This is achieved by either restricting the spatial domain where samples are drawn or by adding a loss term that penalizes deviations from estimated surface depths. The core mechanism involves rendering a depth estimate $D(\mathbf{r})$ alongside color and comparing it against a ground-truth or prior depth map $D_{gt}(\mathbf{r})$ [7496-guided-optimization-of-nerf.md][8679-single-view-nerf-training-with-depth-map.md][8687-depth-supervised-nerf.md].

## Subtopics

### [Guided Optimization of NeRF](7496-guided-optimization-of-nerf.md)
Mitigates ambiguity in indoor scenes by restricting ray sampling to adaptive ranges defined by fine-tuned monocular depth priors. It uses cross-view reprojection errors to determine confidence-based near/far bounds.

### [Single-view NeRF Training with Depth Map](8679-single-view-nerf-training-with-depth-map.md)
Addresses single-view reconstruction by projecting a panorama into perspective views, estimating depth, and performing deformable alignment to a canonical depth map to resolve scale ambiguity before training.

### [Depth-Supervised NeRF](8687-depth-supervised-nerf.md)
Accelerates optimization in sparse-view settings by imposing a KL-divergence loss that forces the rendered ray termination distribution to match sparse 3D keypoints from SfM, enforcing empty space and opaque surfaces.

## Background
Standard NeRF optimization relies solely on color consistency, which can lead to "floaters" or incorrect geometry in regions with low texture or sparse views [7496-guided-optimization-of-nerf.md][8687-depth-supervised-nerf.md]. Depth supervision was introduced to provide explicit geometric cues. DS-NeRF [8687-depth-supervised-nerf.md] utilizes SfM keypoints to supervise the ray termination distribution. Guided Optimization [7496-guided-optimization-of-nerf.md] adapts monocular depth priors for indoor scenes. Single-view methods [8679-single-view-nerf-training-with-depth-map.md] extend this to 360-degree imagery by handling the scale ambiguity of monocular depth estimation through deformable alignment.

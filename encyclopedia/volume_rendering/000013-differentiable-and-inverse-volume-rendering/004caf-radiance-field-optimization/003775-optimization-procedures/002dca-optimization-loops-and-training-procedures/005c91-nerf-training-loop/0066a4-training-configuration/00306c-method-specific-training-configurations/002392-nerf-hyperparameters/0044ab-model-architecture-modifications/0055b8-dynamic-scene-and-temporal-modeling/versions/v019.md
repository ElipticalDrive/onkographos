# Dynamic Scene and Temporal Modeling

Also called: Dynamic NeRF, 4D NeRF, Time-conditioned NeRF

## Overview
Dynamic Scene and Temporal Modeling extends the static Neural Radiance Field (NeRF) architecture to represent scenes that change over time, solving the limitation where standard NeRF assumes a static geometry and appearance [S1]. It differs from sibling methods by explicitly incorporating temporal information into the representation, either through direct time-conditioned inputs, canonical space warping, or particle-based dynamics, rather than treating the scene as a frozen 3D volume [S1].

## How it works
The method modifies the standard NeRF baseline [Parent](15546-dynamic-scene-and-temporal-modeling.md) to handle temporal variations. The specific implementation varies by variant, but the general approach involves expanding the input space or representation structure to include time $t$.

**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Inputs:** 3D position $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^3$, view direction $\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{R}^3$, and time $t \in \mathbb{R}$ (or frame index).
*   **Outputs:** Volume density $\sigma$ and color $\mathbf{c}$ (and potentially auxiliary features) at $(\mathbf{x}, t)$.

**Data Structures:**
*   **MLP Networks:** The coarse and fine networks [Parent](15546-dynamic-scene-and-temporal-modeling.md) are modified to accept time as an additional input dimension.
*   **Positional Encoding:** The positional encoding function $\gamma$ is extended to include time. For a position $\mathbf{x}$ and time $t$, the encoding typically becomes $\gamma(\mathbf{x}, t)$.

**Numbered Steps:**
1.  **Time Conditioning:** The time $t$ is encoded using positional encoding, similar to spatial coordinates. The input to the network becomes a concatenation of spatial positional encoding, view direction encoding, and time encoding: $[\gamma(\mathbf{x}), \gamma(\mathbf{d}), \gamma(t)]$ [S1].
2.  **Feature Extraction:** The network processes the concatenated input to output a latent feature vector $\mathbf{h}$ and volume density $\sigma$. In some variants, such as ER-NeRF, a Tri-Plane Hash Representation is used to store features more compactly [S1].
3.  **Color Prediction:** The fine network takes $\mathbf{h}$, $\gamma(\mathbf{x})$, $\gamma(\mathbf{d})$, and $\gamma(t)$ to predict the color $\mathbf{c}$ [S1].
4.  **Rendering:** Standard volume rendering is performed, integrating the density and color along rays sampled at the specific time $t$ [S1].

**Formulas:**
The positional encoding for time is often defined similarly to spatial coordinates:
$$
\gamma(t) = [\sin(2^{d_1}\pi t), \cos(2^{d_1}\pi t), \dots, \sin(2^{d_2-1}\pi t), \cos(2^{d_2-1}\pi t)]
$$
The final color $\hat{\mathbf{C}}(r)$ for a ray $r$ at time $t$ is computed as:
$$
\hat{\mathbf{C}}(r, t) = \sum_{i=1}^{N} T_i(t) \alpha_i(t) \mathbf{c}_i(t)
$$
where $T_i(t)$ is the transmittance, $\alpha_i(t)$ is the opacity, and $\mathbf{c}_i(t)$ is the color at sample point $i$ at time $t$ [S1].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
*   **Pose Extrapolation:** Additional fine-tuning might be necessary to sustain superior rendering outcomes for poses that exceed the scope of the training dataset [S1].
*   **Temporal Continuity:** Ensuring smooth transitions between time steps requires careful handling of the time encoding and network architecture [S1].

## Subtopics

### [Gear-NeRF](13848-gear-nerf.md)
Uses semantic segmentation to stratify the scene into motion-based 'gears', allowing for region-specific spatio-temporal sampling resolution.

### [NeRF with Time Parameter](14705-nerf-with-time-parameter.md)
Expands the input space of the MLPs to include time as an explicit dimension, serving as a baseline for temporal modeling.

### [Forward Warping for Novel View Synthesis](15541-forward-warping-for-novel-view-synthesis.md)
Models a static "canonical" radiance field and differentially warps it to target time steps.

### [Dynamic Appearance Particle Neural Radiance Field](15550-dynamic-appearance-particle-neural-radiance-field.md)
Combines a static grid-based radiance field with a particle-based dynamic component.

### [KFD-NeRF](15551-kfd-nerf.md)
Models the 4D radiance field as a dynamic system with temporally varying states, estimated using a Kalman filter.

### [SLS4D](16576-sls4d.md)
Represents dynamic scenes using a sparse, compact, and informative latent feature space with dense learnable time slots.

*   **Limitations:** Additional fine-tuning might be necessary for poses outside the training dataset [S1].

## Background
Dynamic Scene and Temporal Modeling originated from the need to extend NeRF to dynamic scenes. Key developments include ER-NeRF, which introduces a Conditional NeRF framework for dynamic portrait creation, and other methods like Gear-NeRF and KFD-NeRF that offer specialized approaches to handling motion and temporal changes [S1].

# LLFF Dataset Loading

Also called: LLFF Data Loading, Local Light Field Fusion Dataset Loading

## Overview
LLFF Dataset Loading prepares the Local Light Field Fusion (LLFF) dataset for NeRF training by parsing COLMAP poses, correcting axis conventions, and applying Normalized Device Coordinate (NDC) normalization for forward-facing scenes [S2, S3, S4, unofficial]. It differs from generic NeRF loading by enforcing a specific train/test split (every 8th image for testing) and handling the minification step via external tools or specific scaling factors [S1, unofficial][S2, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs:**
- `poses_bounds.npy`: NumPy array of shape $(N, 17)$, where $N$ is the number of images. The first 15 columns are the flattened $3 \times 5$ pose matrix (rotation + translation), and the last 2 columns are the near/far bounds $[n, f]$ [S1, S2, S3, unofficial].
- Image directory: Typically `images/` or `images_{factor}/` [S4, unofficial].

**Outputs:**
- `poses`: Array of shape $(N, 3, 4)$ representing camera-to-world (c2w) transformation matrices [S2, unofficial].
- `bounds`: Array of shape $(N, 2)$ containing near and far depths [S2, unofficial].
- `images`: Array of loaded and potentially downsampled images [S4, unofficial].
- `rays_o`, `rays_d`: Ray origins and directions, optionally in NDC space [S2, unofficial].
- `train_ids`, `val_ids`: Indices for training and validation splits [S2, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
- `poses`: Stored as $(N, 3, 4)$ float32 matrices.
- `bounds`: Stored as $(N, 2)$ float32 array.

**Steps:**

1. **Load Poses and Bounds:**
   Load `poses_bounds.npy` (shape $N, 17$). Reshape the pose portion to $(N, 3, 5)$ and extract bounds from the last two columns [S1, S2, S3, unofficial].
   $$ \text{poses}_{raw} = \text{poses\_bounds}[:, :-2].\text{reshape}(N, 3, 5) $$
   $$ \text{bounds} = \text{poses\_bounds}[:, -2:] $$

2. **Correct Axis Convention:**
   COLMAP uses a "down-right-back" coordinate system. Convert to "right-up-back" by swapping and negating axes [S1, S2, unofficial]:
   $$ \text{poses}_{corrected} = \text{concat}([\text{poses}_{raw}[:, :, 1:2], -\text{poses}_{raw}[:, :, 0:1], \text{poses}_{raw}[:, :, 2:]], \text{axis}=2) $$
   Append a row $[0, 0, 0, 1]$ to create standard $4 \times 4$ c2w matrices [S2, unofficial].

3. **Determine Image Path and Downsampling:**
   If a downsampling factor `factor` is specified, append `_{factor}` to the image directory name. If `factor <= 0`, use the base directory [S4, unofficial]. Load images sorted by filename [S3, unofficial]. If `downscale > 1` and pre-downscaled images do not exist, generate them [S3, unofficial].

4. **Extract Camera Parameters:**
   Extract height $H$, width $W$, and focal length $f$ from the first pose matrix (typically at `poses[0, :, 4]` or derived from the rotation matrix) [S2, unofficial]. Rescale the focal length to match the target training resolution $W_{train}$:
   $$ f_{train} = f \times \frac{W_{train}}{W_{original}} $$

5. **Set Near/Far Bounds:**
   Compute global near and far bounds for the scene:
   $$ n_{global} = \min(\text{bounds}) \times 0.9 $$
   $$ f_{global} = \max(\text{bounds}) \times 1.0 $$
   [S2, unofficial].

6. **Normalize Scene Scale:**
   Compute a scale factor based on the minimum near bound to normalize the scene depth. A common multiplier is 0.75, ensuring the nearest depth maps to approximately $1.33$ in normalized space [S1, unofficial]:
   $$ \text{scale\_factor} = n_{global} \times 0.75 $$
   Divide pose translations and bounds by `scale_factor` [S1, unofficial].

7. **Split Train/Val:**
   Use a holdout interval (typically 8) to split images. Validation indices are every 8th image starting from 0 [S2, unofficial]:
   $$ \text{val\_ids} = [i \mid i \in 0..N-1, i \pmod 8 == 0] $$
   $$ \text{train\_ids} = [i \mid i \in 0..N-1, i \pmod 8 \neq 0] $$

8. **Generate Rays and NDC Conversion (Optional but Standard for LLFF):**
   For each pixel $(i, j)$, compute ray directions in camera space:
   $$ d_x = \frac{i - C_x}{f}, \quad d_y = -\frac{j - C_y}{f}, \quad d_z = -1 $$
   Transform to world space: $\mathbf{d}_{world} = \mathbf{R} \mathbf{d}_{cam}$. Origin $\mathbf{o}_{world} = \mathbf{t}$ [S2, unofficial].
   Convert to NDC space using `ndc_rays` [S2, unofficial]:
   $$ t = -\frac{n + o_z}{d_z} $$
   $$ \mathbf{o}_{ndc} = \mathbf{o} + t \mathbf{d} $$
   $$ o_{ndc, x} = -\frac{2f}{W} \frac{o_{ndc, x}}{o_{ndc, z}}, \quad o_{ndc, y} = -\frac{2f}{H} \frac{o_{ndc, y}}{o_{ndc, z}}, \quad o_{ndc, z} = 1 + \frac{2n}{o_{ndc, z}} $$
   $$ d_{ndc, x} = -\frac{2f}{W} \left( \frac{d_x}{d_z} - \frac{o_{ndc, x}}{o_{ndc, z}} \right), \quad d_{ndc, y} = -\frac{2f}{H} \left( \frac{d_y}{d_z} - \frac{o_{ndc, y}}{o_{ndc, z}} \right), \quad d_{ndc, z} = -\frac{2n}{o_{ndc, z}} $$
   [S2, unofficial].

**Parameters:**
- `holdout`: Integer, default 8 [S2, unofficial].
- `scale_multiplier`: Float, default 0.75 [S1, unofficial].
- `white_bkgd`: Boolean, always False for LLFF [S2, unofficial].

**Edge Cases:**
- **Precision:** NDC conversion involves division by $d_z$ and $o_{ndc, z}$. Ensure $d_z \neq 0$ and $o_{ndc, z} \neq 0$.
- **Boundaries:** The near/far bounds from COLMAP may be loose; the 0.9/1.0 multipliers tighten them [S2, unofficial].
- **Missing Images:** If pre-downscaled images are missing, they must be generated on the fly or beforehand [S3, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### [LLFF Dataset Preprocessing and Pose Normalization](14208-llff-dataset-preprocessing-and-pose-normalization.md)
This method focuses on the geometric preprocessing of LLFF data, specifically correcting COLMAP axis conventions and normalizing scene scale for stable training. It ensures that the camera poses are in the correct coordinate system and that the scene depth is normalized to a standard range, facilitating consistent ray sampling.

#### LLFF NDC Contraction
A method to handle forward-facing scenes in the LLFF dataset by contracting the entire scene into NDC space for reconstruction and mesh extraction. It differs from standard loading by explicitly mapping the scene into NDC coordinates, which allows for uniform sampling in disparity space. It is chosen when mesh extraction or reconstruction in unbounded forward-facing scenes is required [S5].
- **Parameters:** DiffMC resolution: 375; SSAA: 9x sample per-pixel [S5].

## Operating characteristics
- **Time Complexity:** Loading is $O(N \cdot H \cdot W)$ for image I/O and ray generation. NDC conversion is $O(N \cdot H \cdot W)$.
- **Memory Complexity:** $O(N \cdot H \cdot W)$ for storing rays and images.
- **Performance:** Efficient for forward-facing scenes. NDC normalization improves sampling efficiency for distant objects [S2, unofficial].
- **Strengths:** Standardized preprocessing for LLFF; handles axis conversion automatically.
- **Limitations:** Assumes forward-facing scenes; not suitable for 360-degree or bounded scenes without modification [S5].

## Background
The LLFF dataset (Local Light Field Fusion) was introduced by Mildenhall et al. [S5]. It consists of forward-facing scenes captured with a handheld camera. The dataset loading procedure described here is based on the implementation details from various NeRF repositories [S1, S2, S3, S4, unofficial] and the specific requirements for NDC-based reconstruction methods [S5].

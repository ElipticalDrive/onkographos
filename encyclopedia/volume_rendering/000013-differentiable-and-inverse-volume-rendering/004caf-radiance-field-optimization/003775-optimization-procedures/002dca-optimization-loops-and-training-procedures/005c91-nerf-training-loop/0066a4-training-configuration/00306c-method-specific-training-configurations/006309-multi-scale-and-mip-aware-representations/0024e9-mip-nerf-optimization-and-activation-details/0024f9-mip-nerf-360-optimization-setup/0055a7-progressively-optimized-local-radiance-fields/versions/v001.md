# Progressively Optimized Local Radiance Fields

Also called: LocalRF

## Overview
Progressively Optimized Local Radiance Fields (LocalRF) is a view synthesis method that jointly optimizes camera poses and multiple overlapping local radiance fields from a single long monocular video, avoiding Structure-from-Motion and scaling to unbounded scenes with fixed memory [S1]. It differs from global optimization approaches by dynamically allocating new local fields as the camera trajectory extends, preventing global failure from pose outliers and maintaining high resolution around the camera path [S1]. Unlike Mega-NeRF, it does not require pre-partitioned space, and unlike Mip-NeRF 360, it jointly estimates poses rather than relying on pre-computed ones [S1].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs**
*   **Input:** A sequence of $P$ monocular video frames $\{\mathbf{C}_k\}_{k=1}^P$ with unknown camera poses.
*   **Output:** A set of $M$ local radiance fields $\{\Theta_j\}_{j=1}^M$ and their corresponding camera poses $\{[R|t]_k\}_{k=1}^P$.

**Data Structures**
*   **Camera Pose:** Each pose $[R|t]_k$ is a continuous 6D representation of rotation and translation [S1].
*   **Local Radiance Field:** Each field $\Theta_j$ is implemented using TensoRF, a factorized 4D tensor representation mapping 3D position $\mathbf{x}$ to volume density $\sigma$ and view-dependent color $\mathbf{c}$ [S1].
*   **Resolution:** TensoRF resolution starts at $64^3$ and upsamples to $640^3$ during refinement [S1].
*   **Supervision Subset:** For each local field, the supervision set includes frames active during the field's lifetime plus the preceding 30 frames for overlap [S1].

**Formulas**
*   **Scene Contraction:** Maps points $\mathbf{x}$ to $[-2, 2]$ space using L-infinity norm to handle unbounded scenes [S1]:
    $$ \text{contract}(\mathbf{x}) = \begin{cases} \mathbf{x} & \text{if } \|\mathbf{x}\|_\infty \leq 1 \\ \left (2 - \frac {1}{\| \mathbf{x} \|_{\infty }}\right ) \left (\frac {\mathbf{x} }{\| \mathbf{x} \|_{\infty }}\right ) & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases} $$
*   **Local Field Query:** The $j$-th radiance field is centered at translation $t_j$ [S1]:
    $$ (\mathbf{c}_i, \sigma_i) = \text{RF}_{\Theta_j}(\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{t}_j) $$
*   **Volume Rendering:** Renders color $\hat{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{r})$ along ray $\mathbf{r}$ with $N$ samples [S1]:
    $$ \hat{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{r}) = \sum_{i=1}^{N} T_i(1-\text{exp}(-\sigma_i\delta_i))\mathbf{c}_i, \quad T_i = \text{exp}\left (-\sum_{j=1}^{i-1}\sigma_j\delta_j\right ) $$
    where $\delta_i$ is the distance between sample points.
*   **Photometric Loss:** [S1]:
    $$ \mathcal{L} = \left \| \hat {\mathbf {C}}(\mathbf {r}) - \mathbf {C}(\mathbf {r}) \right \|_{2}^{2} $$
*   **Depth Rendering:** [S1]:
    $$ \hat {\mathbf {D}}(\mathbf {r}) = \sum _{i=1}^{N} T_i(1-\text {exp}(-\sigma _i\delta _i))d_i $$
    where $d_i$ is distance to ray origin.
*   **Depth Normalization:** For a depth map $\mathbf{D}$ with $M$ samples, compute shift $t(\mathbf{D})$ and scale $s(\mathbf{D})$ [S1]:
    $$ t(\mathbf{D}) = \text{median}(\mathbf{D}), \quad s(\mathbf{D}) = \frac {1}{M} \sum_{1}^{M} |\mathbf{D} - t(\mathbf{D})| $$
    Normalized depth: $\mathbf{D}^* = (\mathbf{D} - t(\mathbf{D}))/s(\mathbf{D})$.
*   **Depth Loss:** [S1]:
    $$ \mathcal{L}_d = \left | {\hat {\mathbf {D}}^*} - {\mathbf {D}^*}\right | $$
*   **Rendered Flow:** Computes optical flow $\hat{\mathcal{F}}_{k\rightarrow k+1}$ between consecutive frames using relative pose $[R|t]_{k\rightarrow k+1} = [R|t]_k^{-1}[R|t]_{k+1}$, projection $\Pi$, and unprojection $\Pi^{-1}$ [S1]:
    $$ \hat {\mathcal {F}}_{k\rightarrow k+1} = (u,v) - \Pi \left ([R|t]_{k\rightarrow k+1}\Pi ^{-1}(u,v,\hat {D})\right ) $$
*   **Flow Loss:** [S1]:
    $$ \mathcal{L}_f = \left \| \hat {\mathcal {F}}_{k\rightarrow k+1} + \mathcal {F}_{k\rightarrow k+1} \right \|_1 $$
    where $\mathcal{F}_{k\rightarrow k+1}$ is RAFT-estimated flow.

**Parameters**
*   **Optimizer:** Adam with $\beta_1 = 0.9, \beta_2 = 0.99$ [S1].
*   **Learning Rates:** Initial rotation LR $5 \cdot 10^{-3}$, translation LR $5 \cdot 10^{-4}$ [S1].
*   **Loss Weights:** Flow loss weight 1, Depth loss weight 0.1 [S1].
*   **Frame Addition:** Every 100 iterations [S1].
*   **Refinement:** 600 iterations per frame addition step [S1].
*   **Overlap:** Preceding 30 frames included in local supervision [S1].
*   **Batch Size:** 16 images for depth normalization statistics [S1].

**Algorithm Steps**
1.  **Initialization:** Initialize optimization with the first 5 frames. Set their poses $[R|t]_k$ to identity. Initialize the first TensoRF model $\Theta_1$ with resolution $64^3$ centered at $t_1 = \mathbf{0}$ [S1].
2.  **Iterative Frame Addition:** For $k = 6$ to $P$:
    a.  **Append Frame:** Add frame $k$ to the supervision set.
    b.  **Pose Initialization:** Initialize the new pose $[R|t]_k$ using the previous pose $[R|t]_{k-1}$ to enforce locality prior [S1]:
        $$ [R|t]_k \leftarrow [R|t]_{k-1} $$
    c.  **Joint Optimization:** Jointly optimize the new pose $[R|t]_k$ and the current radiance field parameters $\Theta_j$ using the photometric, depth, and flow losses for 100 iterations [S1].
3.  **Boundary Check:** Monitor the estimated camera pose relative to the boundary of the high-resolution uncontracted space of the current local radiance field. Check if $\|\mathbf{t}_p - \mathbf{t}_j\|_\infty \geq 1$, where $\mathbf{t}_p$ is the last registered frame's translation and $\mathbf{t}_j$ is the current field's center [S1].
4.  **Refinement:** If the boundary is reached (or after every 100 iterations if not yet reached, though the text specifies refinement *after* reaching boundary or as part of the cycle):
    a.  Stop adding frames.
    b.  Refine the current TensoRF $\Theta_j$ and camera poses for 600 iterations [S1].
    c.  Apply exponential decay to loss weights towards 0.1 factor [S1].
    d.  Upsample TensoRF resolution up to $640^3$ [S1].
5.  **Allocation:** If the pose exceeds the boundary:
    a.  Allocate a new TensoRF radiance field $\Theta_{j+1}$ centered at the last estimated pose $\mathbf{t}_p$ [S1].
    b.  Freeze the previous field $\Theta_j$ (disable supervision).
    c.  Clear unneeded supervising frames from memory [S1].
    d.  Increment $j$.
6.  **Repeat:** Repeat steps 2-5 until all input frames are registered [S1].
7.  **Blending:** At inference, blend rendered colors $\hat{\mathbf{C}}_j(\mathbf{r})$ of all overlapping radiance fields using per-frame linear blending weights within the overlap region [S1].

**Pseudocode**
```python
def localrf_optimization(frames, num_frames):
    # Initialization
    poses = [IdentityPose() for _ in range(num_frames)]
    fields = []
    current_field_idx = 0
    
    # Initialize first field
    field = TensoRF(resolution=64)
    fields.append(field)
    
    # Supervision set includes first 5 frames
    supervision_frames = frames[:5]
    
    # Initial optimization of first 5 frames
    optimize(poses[:5], field, supervision_frames, iterations=500)
    
    for k in range(5, num_frames):
        # Append frame
        supervision_frames.append(frames[k])
        
        # Initialize new pose from previous
        poses[k] = poses[k-1].copy()
        
        # Joint optimization for 100 iterations
        optimize(poses[:k+1], field, supervision_frames, iterations=100)
        
        # Check boundary
        last_pose = poses[k]
        current_center = field.center
        
        if norm_inf(last_pose.translation - current_center) >= 1.0:
            # Refine for 600 iterations
            optimize(poses[:k+1], field, supervision_frames, iterations=600)
            
            # Upsample
            field.upsample_to(640)
            
            # Allocate new field
            new_field = TensoRF(resolution=64, center=last_pose.translation)
            fields.append(new_field)
            current_field_idx += 1
            
            # Freeze previous field
            field.frozen = True
            
            # Clear old frames from memory if needed (logic implied by "clear unneeded")
            # In practice, keep overlap frames
            # supervision_frames = recent_frames_with_overlap
            
            # Switch to new field for next iterations
            field = new_field
            
    return fields, poses
```

**Subtopics**
*   **TensoRF Representation:** A factorized 4D tensor representation used as the base for LocalRF, offering quality and reasonable training speed [S1].
*   **Mip-NeRF 360 Optimization Setup:** The parent method that LocalRF builds upon, specifically regarding contraction and unbounded scene handling, but LocalRF differs by jointly estimating poses [S1].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Optimization takes 30-40 hours for 1000 frames on a single NVIDIA TITAN RTX [S1].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Fixed memory usage due to dynamic allocation and freezing of previous fields, allowing scaling to arbitrarily long videos [S1].
*   **Performance (Tanks and Temples):** PSNR 22.40, SSIM 0.667, LPIPS 0.494 [S1].
*   **Performance (Self-Calibrated):** PSNR 22.85, SSIM 0.676, LPIPS 0.708 [S1].
*   **Strengths:** Robust to pose outliers, handles long unbounded scenes, no SfM required [S1].
*   **Limitations:** Requires careful tuning of overlap and refinement steps; slower than methods with known poses [S1].

## Background
LocalRF was introduced in 2023 by Andreas et al. [S1]. It addresses the limitations of global radiance field optimization, which fails on long videos due to misestimation impact and fixed capacity blur [S1]. It builds on TensoRF [S1] and Mip-NeRF 360's contraction [S1].

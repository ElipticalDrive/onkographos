# S-NeRF Confidence Components

Also called: S-NeRF Confidence

## Overview
S-NeRF Confidence Components is a selective confidence weighting strategy for differentiable volume rendering that adapts the supervision signal based on scene content, distinguishing between static background environments and dynamic foreground objects [S1, unofficial]. It sets itself apart from standard NeRF regularization by explicitly decoupling the confidence metrics used for background (geometry and reprojection) versus foreground (RGB and SSIM), addressing the disparity in depth map quality between these regions [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
The method operates within the augmented loss structure defined by the parent [Depth and Geometry Supervision](8727-depth-and-geometry-supervision.md), where the total loss is $\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{photo}} + \lambda_1 \mathcal{L}_{\text{depth}} + \lambda_2 \mathcal{L}_{\text{smooth}}$ [S1, unofficial]. The "confidence components" refer to the specific metrics used to compute the confidence weights that modulate these regularization terms, rather than the loss terms themselves.

**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Input:** Rendered color $\mathbf{C}$, ground truth image $I$, rendered depth $D$, ground truth depth $D_{gt}$, and camera poses.
*   **Output:** Confidence weights applied to the depth and smoothness losses.

**Data Structures:**
The implementation requires a binary mask or classification to distinguish "background" (static street view) from "foreground" (dynamic vehicles) pixels or rays [S1, unofficial].

**Steps:**
1.  **Scene Classification:** Determine if the current ray or pixel belongs to the background or a foreground object (e.g., a vehicle) [S1, unofficial].
2.  **Select Confidence Metrics:**
    *   **For Background:** Compute confidence using **Geometry** and **Reprojection** measurements [S1, unofficial].
    *   **For Foreground:** Compute confidence using only **RGB** and **SSIM** confidences [S1, unofficial].
3.  **Compute Geometry Confidence (Background Only):**
    *   Calculate the discrepancy between rendered depth and ground truth depth.
    *   Apply a threshold $\tau$ to determine validity. The geometry confidence is not sensitive to $\tau$, with a reasonable range of $[10\%, 40\%]$ [S1, unofficial].
4.  **Compute Reprojection Confidence (Background Only):**
    *   Verify geometric consistency by reprojecting features or depth from neighboring views [S1, unofficial].
5.  **Compute RGB/SSIM Confidence (Foreground Only):**
    *   Calculate pixel-wise RGB error and Structural Similarity Index (SSIM) [S1, unofficial].
    *   Using both RGB and SSIM is required; using only one yields a 1–2% PSNR increase but a 6.6–11% LPIPS decrease [S1, unofficial].
6.  **Apply Weights:** Use the selected confidence metrics to weight the depth and smoothness losses in the total loss function [S1, unofficial].

**Parameters:**
*   $\tau$ (Geometry Threshold): Range $[0.1, 0.4]$ [S1, unofficial].
*   $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$ (Loss Balance Weights): For background, $\lambda_1 \in [0, 0.02]$ and $\lambda_2 \in [0, 0.02]$. For foreground, $\lambda_1 \in [0.5, 2]$ and $\lambda_2 \in [0.015, 0.075]$ [S1, unofficial].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
*   **Foreground Depth Quality:** Foreground depth maps are relatively better than background depth maps, justifying the removal of strong geometry confidences for vehicles [S1, unofficial].
*   **Metric Trade-offs:** Relying solely on RGB or SSIM for foreground improves PSNR but significantly degrades perceptual quality (LPIPS) [S1, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### [S-NeRF Loss Balance Weights](15157-s-nerf-loss-balance-weights.md)
Fixed hyperparameters ($\lambda_1, \lambda_2$) that scale the depth and smoothness components. Distinct from the adaptive confidence weights; they balance geometric supervision against RGB reconstruction and require careful tuning for background vs. foreground disparity.

## Operating characteristics
*   **Performance Impact:** Removing the reprojection confidence module drops PSNR by 0.4%. Training without geometry confidence drops PSNR and SSIM by approximately 0.7% [S1, unofficial].
*   **Foreground Specifics:** Using both RGB and SSIM confidences for vehicles provides the best overall balance across PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS metrics [S1, unofficial].
*   **Robustness:** The geometry confidence component is robust to the choice of threshold $\tau$ within the $[10\%, 40\%]$ range [S1, unofficial].

## Background
S-NeRF (Neural Radiance Fields for Street Views) was published for ICLR 2023 [S1, unofficial]. The confidence components were introduced to handle the unique challenges of autonomous driving scenes, where static background geometry is complex and dynamic foreground objects have varying depth map accuracies.

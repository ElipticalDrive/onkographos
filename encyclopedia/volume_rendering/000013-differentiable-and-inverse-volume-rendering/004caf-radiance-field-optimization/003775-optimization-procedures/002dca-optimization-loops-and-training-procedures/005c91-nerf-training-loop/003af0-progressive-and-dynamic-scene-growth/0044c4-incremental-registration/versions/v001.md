# Incremental Registration

Also called: Incremental CONfidence (ICON) registration, Incremental Image Registration

## Overview
Incremental registration is a training strategy that registers camera frames sequentially following their temporal order, leveraging the intuition that camera motions in videos are smooth and adjacent poses are close [S2]. It solves the problem of non-convex pose optimization in unposed image sequences by providing a good initialization for camera poses, thereby avoiding local minima and "overlapping registration" errors where incorrect poses mislead the radiance field [S2]. It differs from [Dynamic Growth Parameters](2968-dynamic-growth-parameters.md) by triggering growth based on temporal sequence rather than uncertainty, and from [Online Incremental NeRF Optimization](12066-online-incremental-nerf-optimization.md) by processing a fixed offline sequence rather than active agent acquisition.

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Inputs:** A sequence of unposed images $I = \{I_1, I_2, \dots, I_N\}$; a scene regressor $f_{SCR}$ capable of predicting 3D coordinates and features; hyperparameters $k$ (iteration interval), $s_{inlier}$ (inlier threshold), and freeze durations.
*   **Outputs:** Optimized NeRF parameters $\Theta$, camera poses $\{P_i\}_{i=1}^N$, and the final radiance field.

**Data Structures:**
*   **Training Buffer:** A set of registered images and their associated poses.
*   **Pose Representation:** Standard 4x4 transformation matrix or SE(3) representation.

**Steps:**

1.  **Initialization:**
    *   Select the first two frames $\{I_1, I_2\}$.
    *   Jointly optimize NeRF parameters $\Theta$ and poses $\{P_1, P_2\}$ using photometric loss [S2].
    *   *Note:* In some implementations (e.g., ZeroGS), a seed image is initialized with an identity pose, and the scene regressor is finetuned for a specific number of iterations (e.g., 500) to establish initial 3D correspondences [S1, unofficial].

2.  **Incremental Registration Loop:**
    *   While unregistered images remain:
        *   **Frame Addition:** After every $k$ iterations, add the next frame $I_i$ to the training buffer [S2].
        *   **Pose Initialization:** Initialize the new pose $P_i$ using the previous pose $P_{i-1}$ [S2]. This leverages the smooth motion prior.
        *   **Reference Selection:** Select a reference image from the already registered set. The reference is chosen as the registered image that connects to the most unregistered images [S1, unofficial].
        *   **Coarse Pose Estimation:**
            *   Pass the reference image $I_{ref}$ and target image $I_{target}$ (the newly added or batched image) to the scene regressor $f_{SCR}$ to obtain 3D points $\{X\}$ [S1, unofficial].
            *   Establish 2D-3D correspondences $(u, X)$ [S1, unofficial].
            *   Use RANSAC and Perspective-n-Point (PnP) to estimate an initial camera pose [S1, unofficial].
            *   *Inlier Threshold:* Add the target image to the buffer only if the number of inliers exceeds $s_{inlier}$ (e.g., 5,000) and reprojection error is within a threshold (e.g., 6 pixels) [S1, unofficial].
        *   **Optimization:**
            *   Optimize the pose of the newly added images and the NeRF parameters $\Theta$ [S2].
            *   *Efficiency Guard:* Fix the camera poses registered in previous epochs and only optimize the poses of the newly added images in the current epoch [S1, unofficial].
            *   *Loss Function:* Minimize photometric loss. For pose refinement, minimize the reprojection error:
                $$ \sum_{i,k} \rho(\|d_{i,k} \cdot \nu_{i,k} - (X_i - C_k)\|_2) $$
                where $C_k$ is the camera center, $d_{i,k}$ is a scaling factor, and $\nu_{i,k}$ is the ray direction [S1, unofficial].
        *   **Freezing:** Freeze the learning rate on poses $\{P_i\}$ and NeRF $\Theta$ for a specified duration to stabilize the learned geometry [S2].

3.  **Finalization:**
    *   Once all frames are registered, apply a learning rate decay schedule [S2].
    *   Perform a final optimization stage where all camera poses are optimized jointly (fixing only the seed pose) to correct accumulated errors [S1, unofficial].

**Pseudocode:**

```python
def incremental_registration(images, scene_regressor, k, s_inlier, freeze_duration):
    # 1. Initialization
    # Jointly optimize NeRF and first two poses
    theta, poses = optimize_jointly(images[0], images[1], scene_regressor)
    registered_buffer = {1: images[0], 2: images[1]}
    unregistered = set(range(3, len(images) + 1))
    
    current_pose_idx = 2
    
    # 2. Incremental Loop
    while unregistered:
        # Add new frame every k iterations
        if current_pose_idx in unregistered:
            i = current_pose_idx
            # Initialize pose with previous pose
            poses[i] = poses[i-1].clone()
            
            # Select reference image (most connected to unregistered)
            ref_idx = select_best_reference(registered_buffer, unregistered)
            
            # Coarse Pose Estimation via Scene Regressor
            # Get 3D points from regressor
            points_3d = scene_regressor.predict_3d(images[ref_idx], images[i])
            
            # RANSAC + PnP
            inlier_count, initial_pose = ransac_pnp(points_3d, images[i].intrinsics)
            
            if inlier_count > s_inlier:
                poses[i] = initial_pose
                registered_buffer[i] = images[i]
                unregistered.remove(i)
            else:
                # Skip or handle failure
                pass
        
        # Optimize only new poses and NeRF
        optimize_step(theta, poses, registered_buffer, scene_regressor)
        
        # Freeze learning rates after registration
        if current_pose_idx in registered_buffer:
            freeze_learning_rates(poses, theta, freeze_duration)
            
        current_pose_idx += 1
        
        # Check if batch of images needs processing (if Ibuf logic applies)
        # In ICON, it's frame-by-frame or small batch based on k
        if len(unregistered) == 0:
            break

    # 3. Finalization
    # Jointly optimize all poses to fix accumulated drift
    poses = optimize_all_poses(theta, poses, registered_buffer, seed_pose=poses[1])
    
    return theta, poses
```

**Parameters:**
*   $k$: Iteration interval for adding new frames. Typical values: 1,000 iterations for CO3D/HO3D datasets; 500 iterations for LLFF [S2].
*   $s_{inlier}$: Inlier threshold for RANSAC. Typical value: 5,000 [S1, unofficial].
*   Freeze Duration: Iterations to freeze learning rates. Typical values: 100,000 iterations for HO3D/CO3D; 30,000 for LLFF [S2].
*   Finetune Iterations: For scene regressor initialization/refinement. Typical values: 500 (seed), 1,000 (LLFF), 1,500 (Mip-NeRF360) [S1, unofficial].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
*   **Local Minima:** Photometric loss is highly non-convex. Incorrect pose initialization can lead to "overlapping registration" where the model re-registers to an already viewed viewpoint [S2].
*   **Accumulated Error:** Errors accumulate during incremental training. A final joint optimization stage is required to correct this [S1, unofficial].
*   **Occlusion:** Avoid sampling rays from occluded regions during training [S2].
*   **Break Point:** Without pose priors, a rotation difference of ~20 degrees can cause failure; incremental registration mitigates this by assuming smooth motion [S2].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Linear with respect to the number of frames $N$, but each step involves expensive scene regressor inference and PnP/RANSAC. Total training time is significantly higher than joint optimization due to the iterative nature and freezing schedules [S2].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Requires storing the scene regressor and the growing training buffer. Memory usage scales with the number of registered frames and the resolution of the scene representation [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Robust to large initial pose ambiguities; leverages temporal smoothness; avoids local minima associated with global optimization from random initialization [S2].
*   **Limitations:** Requires temporal order; sensitive to the choice of $k$ and freeze duration; may fail if motion is not smooth or if there are large gaps between frames [S2].
*   **When to Use:** Video sequences or ordered image collections where camera motion is smooth [S2].
*   **When Not to Use:** Randomly ordered image sets or scenes with rapid, non-smooth camera motion [S2].

## Background
Incremental registration was introduced in the ICON (Incremental CONfidence) paper [S2] and is also utilized in ZeroGS [S1, unofficial]. It builds on the observation that optimizing poses and NeRF jointly is non-convex and prone to local minima [S2]. The method relies on the intuition that camera motions in videos are smooth, allowing adjacent poses to be initialized from one another [S2]. It is often compared to BARF (Blending Arbitrary Reference Frames) which uses frequency-based positional encoding, whereas ICON focuses on confidence-based incremental addition [S2].

# MVDream Multi-View Diffusion Guidance

Also called: Multi-View Dream

## Overview
MVDream Multi-View Diffusion Guidance provides gradient guidance for 3D optimization by encoding RGB images and camera poses into a multi-view diffusion model, predicting noise, and stepping the DDIM scheduler [S1, unofficial]. It sets itself apart from single-view guidance methods by enforcing multi-view consistency through a multi-view diffusion prior [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs**
*   **Inputs:** Text prompts, camera poses (4x4 matrices, batched), and predicted RGB images (batched, 256x256 resolution) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Outputs:** A gradient signal derived from the predicted noise in the latent space, used to update the 3D representation [S1, unofficial].

**Data Structures**
*   **Camera Tensor:** Shape `[batch_size, 16]`, created by normalizing the 4x4 pose matrix and flattening it [S1, unofficial].
*   **Context Dictionary:** Contains `context` (text embeddings, shape `[B, 77, 768]`), `camera` (shape `[B, 16]`), and `num_frames` (4) [S1, unofficial].

**Parameters**
*   `t_range`: List of floats, default `[0.02, 0.98]`, defines the range of noise levels for training [S1, unofficial].
    *   `min_step = int(1000 * t_range[0])`
    *   `max_step = int(1000 * t_range[1])` [S1, unofficial].
*   `guidance_scale`: Float, default `100`, multiplier for classifier-free guidance [S1, unofficial].

**Algorithm Steps**
1.  **Encode Text:** Encode the text prompts into embeddings and repeat them four times to match the multi-view model's expected batch structure [S1, unofficial].
    $$ \text{pos\_embeds} = \text{encode\_text}(\text{prompts}).\text{repeat}(4, 1, 1) $$
2.  **Convert Camera Convention:** Reorder the camera pose columns to the Blender convention and flip the Y-axis [S1, unofficial].
    $$ \text{camera}_{\text{temp}} = \text{camera}[:, :, [0, 2, 1, 3]] $$
    $$ \text{camera}_{\text{temp}}[:, :, 1] *= -1 $$
3.  **Normalize Camera:** Normalize the transformed camera poses and flatten them into a 16-dimensional vector [S1, unofficial].
    $$ \text{camera}_{\text{norm}} = \text{normalize\_camera}(\text{camera}_{\text{temp}}).\text{view}(\text{batch\_size}, 16) $$
4.  **Encode Images:** Encode the predicted RGB images into latent space representations [S1, unofficial].
    $$ \text{latents} = \text{encode\_imgs}(\text{pred\_rgb\_256}) $$
5.  **Sample Timestep:** Sample a timestep $t$ uniformly between `min_step` and `max_step` [S1, unofficial].
    $$ t \sim \text{Uniform}(\text{min\_step}, \text{max\_step}) $$
6.  **Forward Model with CFG:** Pass the latents, timestep, and context through the diffusion model, then apply Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) by splitting the output into conditional and unconditional predictions and combining them [S1, unofficial].
    $$ \text{noise\_pred} = \text{model}(\text{latents}, t, \text{context}) $$
    $$ \text{noise\_pred\_uncond}, \text{noise\_pred\_cond} = \text{noise\_pred}.\text{chunk}(2) $$
    $$ \text{noise\_pred} = \text{noise\_pred\_uncond} + \text{guidance\_scale} \times (\text{noise\_pred\_cond} - \text{noise\_pred\_uncond}) $$
7.  **Step Scheduler:** Use the DDIM scheduler to step the latents based on the predicted noise [S1, unofficial].
    $$ \text{latents} = \text{scheduler}.\text{step}(\text{noise\_pred}, t, \text{latents}).\text{prev\_sample} $$

**Pseudocode**
```python
import torch

def normalize_camera(pose_matrix):
    """Normalizes a 4x4 pose matrix and flattens it to a 16-dimensional vector."""
    # Ensure the matrix is a valid rigid transformation and flatten to [B, 16]
    # The source assumes the matrix is already a valid pose; this function handles the reshape.
    return pose_matrix.flatten(1)

def mvdream_guidance_step(text_encoder, image_encoder, model, scheduler, prompts, camera_poses, pred_rgb, guidance_scale=100.0, t_range=(0.02, 0.98)):
    """
    Performs one step of MVDream multi-view diffusion guidance.
    
    Args:
        text_encoder: Text encoder (e.g., CLIP).
        image_encoder: Image encoder (e.g., VAE encoder).
        model: Multi-view diffusion UNet.
        scheduler: DDIM scheduler.
        prompts: List of text strings.
        camera_poses: Tensor of shape [B, 4, 4] representing camera poses.
        pred_rgb: Tensor of shape [B, 3, 256, 256] representing predicted RGB images.
        guidance_scale: Float for CFG.
        t_range: Tuple [min_t, max_t] for noise level sampling.
        
    Returns:
        Updated latents for gradient computation.
    """
    batch_size = camera_poses.shape[0]
    min_step = int(1000 * t_range[0])
    max_step = int(1000 * t_range[1])
    
    # 1. Encode text prompts
    pos_embeds = text_encoder(prompts).repeat(4, 1, 1)
    
    # 2. Convert camera to Blender convention
    camera = camera_poses[:, :, [0, 2, 1, 3]]
    camera[:, :, 1] *= -1
    
    # 3. Normalize camera and flatten
    camera = normalize_camera(camera).view(batch_size, 16)
    
    # 4. Encode RGB to latents
    latents = image_encoder(pred_rgb)
    
    # 5. Sample timestep
    t = torch.randint(min_step, max_step, (1,)).item()
    
    # 6. Forward model with CFG
    context = {'context': pos_embeds, 'camera': camera, 'num_frames': 4}
    noise_pred = model.apply_model(latents, t, context)
    noise_pred_uncond, noise_pred_cond = noise_pred.chunk(2)
    noise_pred = noise_pred_uncond + guidance_scale * (noise_pred_cond - noise_pred_uncond)
    
    # 7. Step scheduler
    latents = scheduler.step(noise_pred, t, latents).prev_sample
    
    return latents
```

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls**
*   **CFG Chunking:** The model output must be chunkable along the batch dimension into exactly two equal parts for conditional and unconditional predictions [S1, unofficial].
*   **Timestep Bounds:** Sampling outside the `[min_step, max_step]` range may produce unstable gradients or invalid noise predictions [S1, unofficial].
*   **Camera Indexing:** The column reordering `[0, 2, 1, 3]` assumes a specific 4x4 pose layout; incorrect input dimensions will cause indexing errors [S1, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### RealmDreamer
A method utilizing diffusion priors from inpainting and depth estimation models to generate different viewpoints of a scene iteratively [S2].

### GALA3D
A method leveraging object-level and scene-level models for 3D scene generation; it uses MVDream as an object-level text-to-3D model to generate realistic objects, and a scene-level diffusion model to compose them [S2].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Dominated by the diffusion model inference and scheduler steps [S1, unofficial].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Requires storing latents, text embeddings, and camera tensors [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Enforces multi-view consistency during optimization [S1, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** Computationally expensive due to the multi-view diffusion model [S1, unofficial].
*   **Scaling:** Not given in the sources.
*   **Measured Performance:** Not given in the sources.
*   **When to Use:** When multi-view consistency is critical and computational resources allow for multi-view diffusion inference [S1, unofficial].
*   **When Not to Use:** Not given in the sources.

## Background
The method originated in the DreamGaussian repository [S1, unofficial]. It is used in text-to-3D and image-to-3D generation pipelines to improve the quality and consistency of 3D assets [S1, unofficial].

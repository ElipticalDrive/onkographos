# RePaint-NeRF Limitations

Also called: NeRF editing constraints

## Overview
This article documents the specific failure modes and resource constraints of the RePaint-NeRF method [S1], which performs NeRF editing via semantic masks and diffusion model guidance. It distinguishes itself from sibling methods by highlighting the severe computational overhead (exceeding 30 minutes per object) and specific geometric artifacts arising from shape disparity and restricted view angles, which are not explicitly quantified in the general family overview.

## Background
The limitations described herein originate from the RePaint-NeRF framework, proposed in 2023 [S1]. This method utilizes a pre-trained 2D diffusion model to guide the regeneration of masked regions within a NeRF, employing CLIP loss and depth information for semantic mask acquisition. The identified constraints reflect the practical boundaries of applying text-prompt-driven diffusion guidance to 3D radiance field optimization without specialized acceleration techniques.

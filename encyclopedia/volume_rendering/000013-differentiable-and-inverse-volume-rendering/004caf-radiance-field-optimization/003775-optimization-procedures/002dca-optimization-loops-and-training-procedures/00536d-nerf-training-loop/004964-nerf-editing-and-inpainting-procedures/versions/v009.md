# NeRF Editing and Inpainting Procedures


## Overview
This family of methods modifies the appearance or geometry of a scene represented by a neural radiance field $F_\Theta$ to remove unwanted objects, insert new content, or alter semantic regions. It solves the problem of localized scene alteration while preserving multi-view consistency and the integrity of unedited regions. Unlike standard training loops that optimize weights from scratch, these procedures either intercept the volume rendering process at inference time (zero-shot, non-iterative) or perform targeted optimization of existing parameters using auxiliary guidance (e.g., 2D inpainting priors, diffusion models, or semantic features) [S1, Parent].

## Shared principles
All methods in this family operate on a pre-trained or partially trained NeRF representation $F_\Theta$ mapping $(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{v}) \to (\sigma, \mathbf{c})$. The core mechanism involves defining a region of interest (ROI) or a set of occluded/masked pixels and applying a constraint or modification to the density $\sigma$ and color $\mathbf{c}$ values associated with 3D points within that region.

1.  **Region Definition**: The target area is defined either spatially (e.g., a 3D bounding box [S1, 12273-blended-nerf.md]), semantically (via a feature field probability $p$ [S1, 12068-dff-decomposing-nerf-for-editing-via-feature-field-distillat.md]), or via user masks propagated across views [S1, 12372-nerf-in-inpainting-optimization.md].
2.  **Guidance Generation**: External priors are often generated to guide the editing. These include:
    *   2D inpainted images $I^G$ and depth maps $D^G$ derived from single-view masks [S1, 12372-nerf-in-inpainting-optimization.md].
    *   Synthetic pseudo-observations generated by diffusion models conditioned on depth and uncertainty [S1, 11860-deceptive-nerf-3dgs-pipeline.md].
    *   Semantic feature maps distilled from 2D teacher networks (LSeg, DINO) [S1, 12068-dff-decomposing-nerf-for-editing-via-feature-field-distillat.md].
3.  **Optimization or Interception**:
    *   **Optimization-based**: The NeRF parameters $\Theta$ are updated by minimizing a loss function $\mathcal{L}$ that combines photometric consistency with guidance terms (e.g., color/depth guidance [S1, 12372-nerf-in-inpainting-optimization.md], semantic distillation loss [S1, 12068-dff-decomposing-nerf-for-editing-via-feature-field-distillat.md], or diffusion objectives [S1, 11860-deceptive-nerf-3dgs-pipeline.md]).
    *   **Inference-time Interception**: The rendering equation is modified at ray casting. Points are masked, deleted ($\sigma=0$), or color-transformed based on selection probabilities $p$ without retraining the network [S1, 12491-nerf-editing-procedure.md].
    *   **Progressive/Generative**: New views are synthesized and iteratively added to the training set, with conflict detection to resolve geometry inconsistencies [S1, 8658-progressive-inpainting-and-erasing.md].

## Subtopics

### [Progressive Inpainting-and-Erasing](8658-progressive-inpainting-and-erasing.md)
*   **What it is**: A training procedure for generating a panoramic NeRF from a single input panorama by iteratively synthesizing novel views and refining geometry [8658-progressive-inpainting-and-erasing.md].
*   **Distinction**: Explicitly detects and erases conflicted regions from training supervision to resolve geometry conflicts arising from invisible regions, using collaborative RGBD inpainting [8658-progressive-inpainting-and-erasing.md].
*   **When to choose**: When reconstructing a 3D scene from a single panoramic image where depth estimation is noisy and invisible regions need consistent geometry [8658-progressive-inpainting-and-erasing.md].
*   **Link**: [Progressive Inpainting-and-Erasing](8658-progressive-inpainting-and-erasing.md)

### [Deceptive-NeRF/3DGS Pipeline](11860-deceptive-nerf-3dgs-pipeline.md)
*   **What it is**: A sparse-view reconstruction method that densifies input observations with synthetic pseudo-observations generated by a diffusion model during training [11860-deceptive-nerf-3dgs-pipeline.md].
*   **Distinction**: Optimizes the radiance field representation itself using a joint dataset of real and pseudo-observations, enforcing 3D view consistency via an uncertainty map derived from epipolar reprojection, unlike inference-time interception methods [11860-deceptive-nerf-3dgs-pipeline.md].
*   **When to choose**: For high-quality novel view synthesis from very sparse input views [11860-deceptive-nerf-3dgs-pipeline.md].
*   **Link**: [Deceptive-NeRF/3DGS Pipeline](11860-deceptive-nerf-3dgs-pipeline.md)

### [DFF (Decomposing NeRF for Editing Via Feature Field Distillation)](12068-dff-decomposing-nerf-for-editing-via-feature-field-distillat.md)
*   **What it is**: A method enabling 3D semantic segmentation and editable novel-view synthesis by distilling high-level semantic features from a pre-trained 2D teacher network (LSeg/DINO) into a neural radiance field [12068-dff-decomposing-nerf-for-editing-via-feature-field-distillat.md].
*   **Distinction**: Decomposes scenes into coherent semantic regions, allowing multi-view consistent editing based on text or image patch queries, avoiding "leakage" to geometrically similar but semantically distinct regions [12068-dff-decomposing-nerf-for-editing-via-feature-field-distillat.md].
*   **When to choose**: When editing requires semantic understanding (e.g., "delete the dog") rather than just spatial masks [12068-dff-decomposing-nerf-for-editing-via-feature-field-distillat.md].
*   **Link**: [DFF (Decomposing NeRF for Editing Via Feature Field Distillation)](12068-dff-decomposing-nerf-for-editing-via-feature-field-distillat.md)

### [Blended-NeRF](12273-blended-nerf.md)
*   **What it is**: A framework for editing a specific region of interest (ROI) in an existing NeRF based on text prompts and a user-defined 3D ROI box [12273-blended-nerf.md].
*   **Distinction**: Initializes a generator NeRF with original weights and blends synthesized content with the original scene using volumetric blending, operating on any region without requiring semantic masks or learning a new feature space [12273-blended-nerf.md].
*   **When to choose**: For text-guided local editing (insertion/replacement) in a specific 3D location [12273-blended-nerf.md].
*   **Link**: [Blended-NeRF](12273-blended-nerf.md)

### [NeRF-In Inpainting Optimization](12372-nerf-in-inpainting-optimization.md)
*   **What it is**: An optimization-based method for removing unwanted objects from a pre-trained NeRF using color and depth guidance derived from 2D inpainted images [12372-nerf-in-inpainting-optimization.md].
*   **Distinction**: Performs global optimization of radiance field parameters to reconstruct scene geometry in masked regions, unlike inference-time interception methods [12372-nerf-in-inpainting-optimization.md, Parent].
*   **When to choose**: For object removal where geometric consistency across novel views is critical [12372-nerf-in-inpainting-optimization.md].
*   **Link**: [NeRF-In Inpainting Optimization](12372-nerf-in-inpainting-optimization.md)

### [Adaptive View Selection for Inpainting](12405-adaptive-view-selection-for-inpainting.md)
*   **What it is**: A method addressing inconsistency artifacts in 2D inpainting-guided NeRF editing by dynamically selecting training views based on local texture richness [12405-adaptive-view-selection-for-inpainting.md].
*   **Distinction**: Decides per sub-region whether to rely on 2D inpainting priors or multi-view geometric/color information, unlike pure 2D-inpainting-guided methods [12405-adaptive-view-selection-for-inpainting.md].
*   **When to choose**: When 2D inpainters produce artifacts that conflict with multi-view consistency [12405-adaptive-view-selection-for-inpainting.md].
*   **Link**: [Adaptive View Selection for Inpainting](12405-adaptive-view-selection-for-inpainting.md)

### [NeRF Editing Procedure](12491-nerf-editing-procedure.md)
*   **What it is**: A zero-shot, non-iterative method that modifies appearance or geometry by intercepting the volume rendering process at inference time [12491-nerf-editing-procedure.md].
*   **Distinction**: Preserves multi-view consistency without retraining costs by using auxiliary feature fields or masks to guide the rendering of existing parameters [12491-nerf-editing-procedure.md, Parent].
*   **When to choose**: For fast, interactive editing (deletion, colorization, translation) without optimization overhead [12491-nerf-editing-procedure.md].
*   **Link**: [NeRF Editing Procedure](12491-nerf-editing-procedure.md)

### Semantic Inpainting for Neural Fields
*   **Definition**: A 3D content editing capability allowing editing of neural fields through simple interfaces like free-form sketching or language instructions [S1].
*   **Step**: Given a user-supplied edit in a single reference view, the encoding of a neural field automatically propagates this edit to all views, promoting consistency between all views thanks to the implicit geometrical and photometric priors embodied by the neural network, preserving the structure of the whole scene [S1].
*   **History**: Described in reference [19] [S1].

### Post-Refinement Strategy
*   **Definition**: A strategy employed after the NeRF has been trained on updated data to enhance consistency by generating a more accurate target for each view using the modified Instruct-Pix2Pix with averaging architecture [S2].
*   **Step**: Use the rendered image as input while continuing to use the mixup image as a condition [S2].

## Background
Neural rendering has evolved from explicit physical modeling to data-driven inference, enabling breakthroughs in high real-time rates and intelligent creation [S1]. The concept of editing neural fields via simple interfaces, such as semantic inpainting where edits propagate across views, was highlighted as a key capability in recent reviews of neural rendering technologies [S1]. Specific implementations like ViCA-NeRF employ post-refinement strategies using modified Instruct-Pix2Pix to enhance consistency in stylized edits [S2]. The field continues to see synergies with generative approaches, such as distilling 2D generative knowledge (e.g., DreamFusion) to 3D geometry without 3D supervision [S1].

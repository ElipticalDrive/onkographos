# Generative and Style-based Models

Also called: 3D-aware GANs, NeRF-GAN hybrids, Latent-NeRF

## Overview
Generative and Style-based Models integrate Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) with generative frameworks such as Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) or diffusion models to synthesize novel 3D-consistent views from limited or unstructured data. Unlike standard NeRF which reconstructs a single static scene from multi-view images, this family enables the generation of diverse 3D objects or the editing of scenes via latent codes. They distinguish themselves from sibling methods like [AdaNeRF](6195-adanerf.md) or [Transformer-based Architectures](8998-transformer-based-architectures.md) by explicitly modeling the distribution of 3D scenes rather than optimizing for a single instance, often bypassing the need for explicit camera poses during training or enabling text-guided synthesis.

## Shared principles
All methods in this family extend the mechanism described in [Specific Model Architectures](8585-specific-model-architectures.md) by introducing a generative prior or latent optimization loop. While the underlying radiance field mapping remains consistent, the input space and optimization objectives diverge significantly:
1.  **Latent Conditioning:** Instead of optimizing scene parameters directly, models optimize latent vectors $\mathbf{z}$ that condition the radiance field parameters or features.
2.  **Generative Losses:** Training involves adversarial losses (GANs) or score distillation (Diffusion) rather than solely photometric reconstruction loss.
3.  **Input Simplification:** Many variants remove viewing direction $\mathbf{d}$ from the NeRF input to enforce view-invariant generative properties or because training data lacks pose information.

## Subtopics

### HyperNeRFGAN
HyperNeRFGAN is a 3D-aware generative model that uses a hypernetwork to generate the weights of a target NeRF, enabling generation without camera pose supervision [S1]. It differs from [Latent-NeRF](9110-latent-nerf.md) by avoiding complex conditioning mechanisms, instead using a StyleGAN2-based generator to produce NeRF weights directly [S1].
*   **Architecture:** The generator $G$ takes noise $\mathbf{z} \sim P_{noise}$ and outputs modulation matrices $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ for Factorized Multiplicative Modulation (FMM) layers [S1].
*   **Target NeRF:** A simplified MLP using FMM layers: $\mathbf{y} = \mathbf{W} \odot (\mathbf{A} \times \mathbf{B}) \cdot \mathbf{x}_{in} + \mathbf{b}$, where $\mathbf{W}, \mathbf{b}$ are shared [S1]. It omits viewing directions and uses only a coarse network with 128 channels and 4 layers [S1].
*   **Training:** Minimax game between $G$ and a 2D discriminator $D$ [S1].
*   **Parameters:** Rank $k=10$ for modulation [S1].

### [Latent-NeRF](9110-latent-nerf.md)
Latent-NeRF optimizes NeRFs by integrating pre-trained latent diffusion models, applying score distillation to latent features rather than RGB pixels. It solves the generalization and semantic control limitations of standard NeRF by operating in a compact latent space.
*   **Output Modification:** The NeRF MLP outputs four pseudo-color channels representing latent features instead of RGB.
*   **Rendering:** Volumetric rendering composites these latent features $\mathbf{z}$ into a latent feature map $C(\mathbf{r})$.
*   **Optimization:** Noise is added to $C(\mathbf{r})$, and a frozen Stable Diffusion model provides gradient signals via score distillation, guided by text prompts and shape priors.

### StyleNeRF
*   **Definition:** A 3D-aware generative model merging 3D scene representation with style-based generative models [S1].
*   **Method:** Uses NeRF to generate low-resolution feature maps which are then sampled to high resolution to avoid the computational cost of full volumetric rendering at high resolution [S1]. It applies regularization terms to improve 3D consistency and addresses view-dependent noise injection [S1].

### NeRFFaceShop
*   **Definition:** A 3D-aware generative model producing animatable and relightable human heads from in-the-wild videos [S2].
*   **Method:** Deforms feature maps rather than 3D points to maintain lighting and expression consistency during motion [S2]. It builds on a tri-plane generator, introduces a unified animation and lighting representation, and extracts expression coefficients to learn a detailed animation space for smooth, controllable head motion and relighting [S2].

### Generative Latent Optimization
*   **Definition:** A method where randomly sampled latent codes are paired with images and jointly optimized with generator parameters using reconstruction loss, functioning as a discriminator-less GAN [S2].
*   **Parameters:** $u_i$ represents other inputs not optimized over [S2].

### LOLNeRF
*   **Definition:** A method for single-shot view synthesis of human faces [S2].
*   **Method:** Built similarly to $\pi$-GAN but utilizes Generative Latent Optimization instead of adversarial training [S2].

## Background
The integration of NeRF with generative models began with GRAF (2020), which established the foundation for conditional radiance fields using GANs, though limited to single-object scenes [S1]. Subsequent works like GIRAFFE (2021) introduced compositional neural feature fields to separate shape, appearance, and background without explicit supervision [S1]. $\pi$-GAN (2021) improved quality using periodic activation functions and volume rendering [S1]. However, these early methods required large datasets and struggled with complex scenes [S1]. HyperNeRFGAN addressed the lack of camera pose data by generating NeRF weights directly from noise [S1]. Latent-NeRF later incorporated diffusion models to enable text-guided generation and improved generalization [S1]. StyleNeRF focused on computational efficiency by rendering at low resolution and upsampling [S1]. NeRFFaceShop and LOLNeRF extended these concepts to specific domains like human faces, with LOLNeRF using Generative Latent Optimization for single-shot synthesis [S2].

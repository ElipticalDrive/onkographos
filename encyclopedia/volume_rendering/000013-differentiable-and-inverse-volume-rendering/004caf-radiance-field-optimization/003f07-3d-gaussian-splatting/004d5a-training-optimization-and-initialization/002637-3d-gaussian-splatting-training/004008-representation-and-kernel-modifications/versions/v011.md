# Representation and Kernel Modifications

Also called: NegGS, Scaffold-GS, GeoGaussian, DARBF-Splatting, Grid-based Blur Kernel, Gaussian Shell Maps, Gaussian Wrapping

## Overview
Representation and Kernel Modifications alter the fundamental mathematical primitives or their spatial encoding in 3D Gaussian Splatting to address specific limitations in shape expressivity, geometric consistency, and rendering fidelity. While [Base 3DGS Optimization](7549-base-3d-gaussian-splatting.md) relies on standard positive Gaussians with exponential kernels, this family introduces negative values for destructive interference [9152-negative-gaussian-splatting.md], voxel-grid decoding for compactness [9186-scaffold-gs.md], tangent-plane constraints for surface smoothness [9187-geogaussian.md], non-exponential radial basis functions [9911-darbf-splatting.md], spatially varying blur kernels [14244-grid-based-blur-kernel.md], and oriented half-space primitives for watertight meshing [Gaussian Wrapping].

## Shared principles
The children share the goal of modifying the standard 3DGS primitive representation or its associated kernel function to improve reconstruction quality, geometric accuracy, or representation efficiency. All methods retain the core differentiable rasterization pipeline of 3D Gaussian Splatting but change how the primitive's contribution is calculated or how its parameters are constrained/initialized.

## Subtopics

### [Negative Gaussian Splatting](9152-negative-gaussian-splatting.md)
[Negative Gaussian Splatting](9152-negative-gaussian-splatting.md) extends the standard representation by allowing color attributes to take negative values, creating a "Diff-Gaussian" distribution. It uses destructive interference to model high-frequency details with fewer primitives. Choose this when standard positive Gaussians fail to capture complex nonlinear structures or rapid color transitions.

### [Scaffold-GS](9186-scaffold-gs.md)
[Scaffold-GS](9186-scaffold-gs.md) replaces direct Gaussian attribute storage with learnable features on a voxel grid, decoded by lightweight neural networks. It decouples attribute storage from direct parameters, enabling a more compact and structured representation. Choose this when memory efficiency and structured representation of view-dependent appearance are prioritized over direct parameter optimization.

### [GeoGaussian](9187-geogaussian.md)
[GeoGaussian](9187-geogaussian.md) modifies the densification process by constraining new Gaussian means to the local tangent plane of existing primitives and enforcing geometric smoothness. It introduces explicit geometric priors during densification. Choose this when noisy or inconsistent surface reconstruction is a primary concern and planar surface assumptions hold locally.

### [DARBF-Splatting](9911-darbf-splatting.md)
[DARBF-Splatting](9911-darbf-splatting.md) replaces the exponential Gaussian kernel with Decaying Anisotropic Radial Basis Functions (DARBFs) like Half-Cosine or Inverse Multiquadric. It allows kernels that span a larger region with higher opacity for the same variance, reducing the number of splats required. Choose this when the exponential family's decay rate is insufficient for the scene's scale or detail, and computational efficiency via a correction factor is needed.

### [Grid-based Blur Kernel](14244-grid-based-blur-kernel.md)
[Grid-based Blur Kernel](14244-grid-based-blur-kernel.md) replaces MLP-generated blur kernels with a set of learnable, spatially-varying kernels indexed by sharpness levels. It models spatially-varying blurriness by assigning shared kernels to pixel groups. Choose this when handling defocus blur in scenes with significant depth variation, rather than modifying primitive geometry.

### Gaussian Shell Maps
Gaussian Shell Maps combine CNN-based generators with 3D Gaussian Splatting to recreate virtual humans with sophisticated details such as clothing and accessories [S1].

### Gaussian Wrapping
Gaussian Wrapping reinterprets 3D Gaussian primitives as oriented probabilistic surface elements representing geometry only within an oriented half-space, allowing derivation of closed-form occupancy, vacancy, and normal fields for watertight mesh extraction [S2].

**Definition:**
Gaussian Wrapping views Gaussians as oriented surface elements where the Gaussian models density decay on the outward-facing side, while the inward-facing side is considered fully occupied [S2].

**Steps:**
1.  Model surface points with asymmetric primitives where the Gaussian models density decay on the outward-facing side, while the inward-facing side is considered fully occupied [S2].
2.  Identify geometric gaps where the oriented surface assumption is violated and densify the representation to obtain a watertight shell of oriented primitives [S2].
3.  Introduce a learnable oriented normal at each Gaussian element and define an adapted attenuation formulation to enable closed-form expressions for normal and occupancy fields [S2].
4.  Introduce a novel consistency loss and a dedicated densification strategy to enforce Gaussians to wrap the entire surface by closing geometric holes [S2].
5.  Modify the differentiable rasterizer to output depth as an isosurface of the continuous model [S2].
6.  Use Primal Adaptive Meshing for Region-of-Interest meshing at arbitrary resolution [S2].

## Background
The standard 3D Gaussian Splatting representation [7549-base-3d-gaussian-splatting.md] uses positive, symmetric Gaussians with exponential kernels. Negative Gaussian Splatting [9152-negative-gaussian-splatting.md] was introduced to address the inability of positive-only Gaussians to model high-frequency details and complex shapes efficiently, utilizing the Diff-Gaussian distribution [S1, S2]. Scaffold-GS [9186-scaffold-gs.md] and GeoGaussian [9187-geogaussian.md] emerged to improve memory efficiency and geometric consistency, respectively. DARBF-Splatting [9911-darbf-splatting.md] generalized the kernel function beyond the exponential family. Grid-based Blur Kernels [14244-grid-based-blur-kernel.md] addressed defocus blur modeling. Gaussian Wrapping [S2] reinterpreted Gaussians as oriented surface elements for watertight mesh extraction, inspired by the Objects as Volumes framework [39]. Gaussian Shell Maps [S1] integrate CNNs with 3DGS for human avatar reconstruction.

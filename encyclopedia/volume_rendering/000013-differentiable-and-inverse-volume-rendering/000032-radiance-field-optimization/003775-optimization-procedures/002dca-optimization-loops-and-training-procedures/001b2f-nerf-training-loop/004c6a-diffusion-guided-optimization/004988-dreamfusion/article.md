# DreamFusion

Also called: Diffusion-guided NeRF Optimization, Text-to-3D NeRF Optimization, Text-to-3D diffusion NeRF

## Overview
DreamFusion is a text-to-3D generation method that optimizes a Neural Radiance Field (NeRF) to render images with high scores from a pretrained text-to-image diffusion model, requiring no 3D training data [S1, S2]. It differs from its siblings by being the foundational method that introduced Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) to optimize a NeRF directly from a 2D diffusion prior, whereas [Single-Stage Diffusion NeRF Training](12803-single-stage-diffusion-nerf-training.md) and [ReconFusion](13211-reconfusion.md) introduce variations in conditioning or multi-stage refinement [S1].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
- **Input:** A text prompt $C$ (e.g., "a ceramic mug").
- **Output:** A mipNeRF 360 [28] based NeRF model parameterized by $\theta$.

**Data Structures:**
- **NeRF:** A mipNeRF 360 architecture [S1].
- **Color Parameterization:** A key modification is that surface color is parameterized by an MLP instead of standard radiance [S1].
- **Diffusion Model:** Imagen [147] is used as the pretrained text-to-image diffusion model [S1].

**Optimization Steps:**
1. Initialize a mipNeRF 360 NeRF model from scratch for the target object or scene [S1].
2. Sample a random camera viewpoint and render an image $I_{render}$ from the NeRF.
3. Condition the diffusion model Imagen on the text prompt $C$. To control the view, keywords such as "overhead view", "front view", or "back view" are appended to the prompt [S1].
4. Compute the Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) loss using the shared principles of the parent method [Diffusion-guided Optimization](13230-diffusion-guided-optimization.md).
5. Backpropagate the gradient through the NeRF to update parameters $\theta$.
6. Repeat until convergence.

**Pitfalls:**
- The Imagen model was used at a 64x64 resolution [S1]. Consequently, the resulting NeRF model lacked the capability to produce finer details [S1].

## Background
DreamFusion was introduced in September 2022 [S1]. It was proposed as a text-to-3D diffusion NeRF model that trains a NeRF using images generated by 2D diffusion models [S1]. It builds on [Diffusion-guided Optimization](13230-diffusion-guided-optimization.md) by applying it to the text-to-3D domain without 3D training data [S2].

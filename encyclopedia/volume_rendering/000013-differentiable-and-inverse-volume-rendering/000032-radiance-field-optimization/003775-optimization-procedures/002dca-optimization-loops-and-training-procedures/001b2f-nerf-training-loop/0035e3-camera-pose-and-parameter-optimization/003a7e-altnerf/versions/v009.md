# AltNeRF

Also called: Alternating Depth-Pose Optimization

## Overview
AltNeRF is a framework for creating resilient Neural Radiance Field (NeRF) representations from monocular videos without relying on known camera poses [S1]. It solves the problem of suboptimal NeRF reconstruction caused by the lack of explicit 3D supervision and imprecise initial camera poses by integrating self-supervised monocular depth estimation (SMDE) [S1]. Unlike methods that strictly align rendered depth with priors, AltNeRF uses an alternating algorithm to harmonize NeRF outputs with SMDE estimates, employing error-tolerant regularization to handle the inherent imprecision of depth priors [S1]. It sets itself apart from [NeRF with Unknown Camera Parameters](7405-nerf-with-unknown-camera-parameters.md) by explicitly decoupling the scene prior estimation from the volumetric refinement into two distinct modules (SPM and SRM) that iteratively refine each other, rather than optimizing all parameters jointly in a single loop [S1].

## How it works
AltNeRF comprises two main components: the Scene Prior Module (SPM) and the Scene Representation Module (SRM) [S1]. The SPM provides robust depth and pose priors, while the SRM refines these poses and learns the fine-grained 3D scene representation, regularized by the depth priors [S1]. The refined outputs are fed back to the SPM to improve its performance [S1].

### Inputs and Outputs
*   **Input:** A monocular video sequence (set of images $I$) and known camera intrinsics $K$ [S1].
*   **Output:** A trained NeRF field $f_n$ (density $\sigma$ and color $c$) and refined camera poses $P$ for all frames [S1].

### Data Structures and Layouts

**SPM Depth Network ($f_d$)**
*   **Architecture:** Encoder is ResNet-50 (fully-connected layer removed) [S1]. Decoder has ten $3 \times 3$ convolutional layers (two per scale) with bilinear up-sampling [S1].
*   **Input:** Image $I$.
*   **Output:** Depth map $D$ [S1].

**SPM Pose Network ($f_p$)**
*   **Architecture:** Structured with ResNet-34 [S1].
*   **Input:** Pair of images $(I_t, I_s)$ (target and source) [S1].
*   **Output:** Vector of 9 elements: first 6 are continuous rotation representation, last 3 are translations [S1].

**SRM NeRF Network ($f_n$)**
*   **Architecture:** Eight fully-connected layers with skip connections for density output, one linear layer for color [S1].
*   **Input:** 3D point $p \in \mathbb{R}^3$ and unit viewing direction $d \in \mathbb{R}^3$ [S1].
*   **Output:** Density $\sigma$ and color $c$ [S1].

**Pose Representation**
*   Camera pose $P_i$ is a $4 \times 4$ transformation matrix [S1].
*   World coordinate system aligns with the first frame $I_0$ (identity matrix) [S1].
*   Relative transformation $P_{i-1 \to i}$ is predicted by SPM [S1].
*   Absolute pose is computed via chain rule: $P_i = P_{i-1 \to i} \times P_{i-1}$ [S1].
*   Refined pose $\hat{P}$ is calculated by combining a residual pose $\Delta P$ with the prior pose $P$: $\hat{P} = \Delta P \times P$ [S1].

### Formulas

**Volumetric Rendering (NeRF)**
The rendered color $\hat{C}(r)$ for a ray $r = o + td$ is:
$$ \hat{C}(r) = \int_{t_n}^{t_f} T(t)\sigma(t)c(t)dt $$
where $T(t) = \exp\left(-\int_{t_n}^t \sigma(s)ds\right)$ handles occlusions, and $t_n, t_f$ are near and far depth bounds [S1].

**Scene Depth Estimation**
The estimated scene depth $\hat{D}(r)$ is the mean terminating distance:
$$ \hat{D}(r) = \int_{t_n}^{t_f} T(t)\sigma(t)t dt $$
[S1]

**Photometric Loss (SMDE)**
Used to supervise SMDE reconstruction:
$$ \mathcal{L}_p(I_t, \hat{I}_t) = (1 - \text{SSIM}(I_t, \hat{I}_t)) + \frac{\alpha}{2}(1 - \alpha)\|I_t - \hat{I}_t\|_1 $$
where $\alpha$ is typically 0.85 [S1].

**Edge-aware Smoothness Loss**
Ensures smoothness in predicted depth maps $D$, weighted by image gradients $\partial_x, \partial_y$:
$$ \mathcal{L}_s = |\partial_x D|e^{-|\partial_x I|} + |\partial_y D|e^{-|\partial_y I|} $$
[S1]

**Distilling Loss (SPM)**
Used to distill structure information from SRM into SPM, where $D_r$ is a reference depth map (e.g., from DPT), $E_r, E$ are occluding boundary maps, and $\oplus$ is XOR:
$$ \mathcal{L}_r = 1 - \text{SSIM}(D, D_r) + 0.1 \times (E_r \oplus E / \text{size}(E)) $$
[S1]

**SRM Optimization Loss**
Combines reconstruction loss $\mathcal{L}_c$ and error-tolerant depth regularization $\mathcal{L}_e$:
$$ \mathcal{L}_{sr} = \mathcal{L}_c + \gamma \cdot \mathcal{L}_e $$
where $\mathcal{L}_c = \|\hat{C}(r) - C(r)\|_2$ [S1].
The error-tolerant term $\mathcal{L}_e$ uses Huber loss $H(\cdot)$ and tolerance $\epsilon$:
$$ \mathcal{L}_e = H\left(\max\left(\frac{\|\hat{D} - D\|_1}{\hat{D} + D} - \epsilon, 0\right)\right) $$
where $\gamma$ balances the terms [S1].

**Multi-view Consistency Uncertainty**
Uncertainty $U_t$ of depth map $D_t$ is the difference between $D_t$ and depth maps $D_{s \to t}$ warped from nearby source views:
$$ U_t = \|D_t - D_{s \to t}\|_1 $$
This uncertainty weights the loss functions (Eq. 6 and 10) using Softmin [S1].

### Parameters and Typical Values
*   $\alpha$ (Photometric loss weight): 0.85 [S1].
*   $\gamma$ (Reconstruction vs. Depth regularization balance): 0.08 for LLFF and CO3D; 0.15 for ScanNet and Captures [S1].
*   $\epsilon$ (Depth tolerance coefficient): Not given in the sources.
*   $S_r$ (SRM iterations per alternation): 50K [S1].
*   $S_p$ (SPM fine-tuning iterations per alternation): 500 [S1].
*   Alternations: 2 [S1].
*   SPM Pretrain LR: $10^{-4}$ [S1].
*   SPM Fine-tune LR: $5.0 \times 10^{-5}$ [S1].
*   SRM NeRF LR: Initial $10^{-3}$, exponentially decays to $10^{-4}$ [S1].
*   SRM Pose LR: Initial $10^{-5}$, linearly increases to $2.0 \times 10^{-3}$ after 1K iterations, then exponentially decays to $10^{-5}$ [S1].

### Pseudocode

```python
def altnerf_train(video_frames, intrinsics_K):
    """
    Main training loop for AltNeRF.
    video_frames: List of images [I_0, I_1, ..., I_N]
    intrinsics_K: Camera intrinsic matrix
    """
    # 1. Initialize Modules
    # SPM: Pretrained on large dataset (NYUv2, VOID)
    spm_depth_net = load_pretrained_spm_depth() 
    spm_pose_net = load_pretrained_spm_pose()
    
    # SRM: NeRF network initialized randomly
    nerf_net = init_nerf_network()
    
    # 2. Initial Pose Estimation (Chain Rule)
    # Align world coordinate system with first frame I_0 (Identity)
    poses = [IdentityMatrix()]
    for i in range(1, len(video_frames)):
        # Predict relative transformation between adjacent frames
        rel_pose = spm_pose_net(video_frames[i-1], video_frames[i])
        # Compute absolute pose via chain rule
        current_pose = rel_pose * poses[-1]
        poses.append(current_pose)
        
    # 3. Initial Depth Estimation (SPM)
    depths = []
    for frame in video_frames:
        depth = spm_depth_net(frame)
        depths.append(depth)
        
    # 4. Alternating Optimization Loop
    # Perform 2 alternations as per standard config
    for alternation_step in range(2):
        
        # --- Phase 1: Scene Representation Module (SRM) ---
        # SRM refines poses and learns NeRF, regularized by current depths
        # Warmup Learning: Start with low pose LR, increase after 1K iters
        
        # Initialize residual pose delta_P to zeros
        delta_P = ZeroMatrix()
        
        # SRM Optimization Loop (Sr = 50K iterations)
        for iter in range(50000):
            # Select random rays from all views
            rays = sample_rays(video_frames, poses, delta_P, intrinsics_K)
            
            # Render colors and depths using current NeRF and refined poses
            # Refined pose P_hat = delta_P * poses[i]
            rendered_colors, rendered_depths = render_nerf(nerf_net, rays, delta_P, poses)
            
            # Compute Photometric Loss (L_c)
            loss_c = mean_squared_error(rendered_colors, rays.gt_colors)
            
            # Compute Error-tolerant Depth Regularization Loss (L_e)
            # Use current SPM depths as prior D
            depth_prior = get_depth_at_ray(rays, depths)
            loss_e = huber_loss(max((abs(rendered_depths - depth_prior) / (rendered_depths + depth_prior) - epsilon, 0)))
            
            # Total SRM Loss
            # Gamma is dataset dependent (0.08 or 0.15)
            loss_sr = loss_c + gamma * loss_e
            
            # Backpropagate to update NeRF weights and delta_P
            # Pose LR schedule: linear increase to 2e-3 after 1K iters, then decay
            update_gradients(nerf_net, delta_P, loss_sr)
            
        # Output of SRM: Refined poses and rendered depths
        refined_poses = [delta_P * p for p in poses]
        refined_depths = rendered_depths # Interpolated to full image space
        
        # --- Phase 2: Scene Prior Module (SPM) Fine-tuning ---
        # Fine-tune SPM depth network using distillation loss
        # Replace reference depth D_r with refined rendered depth D_hat
        
        for iter in range(500): # Sp = 500 iterations
            for i, frame in enumerate(video_frames):
                # Predict depth from SPM
                spm_depth = spm_depth_net(frame)
                
                # Get reference depth from SRM output (refined_depths[i])
                sr_depth = refined_depths[i]
                
                # Compute Distilling Loss (L_r)
                # L_r = 1 - SSIM(D, D_r) + 0.1 * (E_r XOR E / size(E))
                # Note: E_r and E are occluding boundary maps derived from depths
                loss_r = distilling_loss(spm_depth, sr_depth)
                
                # Compute Photometric Loss (L_p) for consistency
                # Reconstruct target frame from source views using SPM depth and pose
                # This part assumes SMDE reconstruction logic is applied here
                # L_p = (1 - SSIM) + alpha/2 * (1-alpha) * L1
                loss_p = photometric_loss(frame, spm_depth, refined_poses, intrinsics_K)
                
                # Compute Smoothness Loss (L_s)
                loss_s = smoothness_loss(spm_depth, frame)
                
                # Total SPM Loss
                # Weighted by multi-view consistency uncertainty (Softmin)
                uncertainty = compute_uncertainty(spm_depth, refined_poses, video_frames)
                loss_spm = (loss_p + loss_r + 1e-3 * loss_s) * softmin(uncertainty)
                
                # Backpropagate to update SPM depth network weights
                update_gradients(spm_depth_net, loss_spm)
                
        # Update global poses and depths for next alternation
        poses = refined_poses
        depths = refined_depths
        
    return nerf_net, poses
```

### Edge Cases and Pitfalls
*   **Dynamic Objects:** SMDE is susceptible to dynamic objects and view-dependent appearances. The distilling strategy in SPM helps mitigate this, but severe dynamics may still degrade performance [S1].
*   **Imprecise Priors:** The depth prior from SPM is not precise. Strict alignment leads to degenerate solutions. The error-tolerant regularization ($\mathcal{L}_e$) is crucial to handle this [S1].
*   **Non-Convex Optimization:** Joint optimization of pose and NeRF is highly non-convex. Poor initialization can lead to local minima. Using SPM priors as initialization and warmup learning for pose refinement helps converge to the global minimum [S1].
*   **Occlusions:** Multi-view consistency check accounts for occlusions by computing uncertainty based on warped depths from multiple views [S1].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Training takes 150K-200K total iterations [S1].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Not given in the sources.
*   **Measured Performance:** Trains in 4.0-6.4 hours on a single RTX 3090 GPU [S1].
*   **Strengths:** Resilient to imprecise camera poses; leverages complementary strengths of SMDE (robust priors) and NeRF (fine geometry); error-tolerant regularization prevents degenerate solutions [S1].
*   **Limitations:** Relies on the quality of the pre-trained SPM; susceptible to domain gaps if the target video differs significantly from pretraining data (mitigated by test-time adaptation) [S1].
*   **When to use:** When camera poses are unknown and only monocular video is available, and high-quality 3D reconstruction is needed [S1].
*   **When not to use:** When precise ground-truth camera poses are available (standard NeRF is more efficient); when real-time inference is required (training time is significant) [S1].

## Background
AltNeRF was proposed in 2024 by researchers presenting at the Thirty-Eighth AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence [S1]. It builds upon the principles of Self-supervised Monocular Depth Estimation (SMDE) and Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) [S1]. The method addresses the limitations of existing NeRF approaches that struggle with unknown camera poses and lack of explicit 3D supervision [S1]. It extends the BARF approach by introducing depth regularization, improved pose initialization, and warmup learning [S1].

# InstantSplat

Also called: MASt3R/VGGT-guided 3DGS, Pose-free 3D Gaussian Splatting

## Overview
InstantSplat is a pose-free, sparse-view 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) training method that leverages MASt3R or VGGT geometry priors to initialize Gaussian primitives without requiring camera pose annotations [S1, unofficial]. It distinguishes itself from sibling methods like [pixelSplat](6464-pixelsplat.md) by optimizing Gaussian attributes via gradient descent rather than generating them via a feed-forward network, and from [Joint Pose Optimization](6888-joint-pose-optimization.md) by fixing poses to priors instead of optimizing them jointly [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, 6464-pixelsplat.md, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Inputs:** A set of $N$ uncalibrated images $\{I_i\}$ and pre-computed geometric priors (depth maps and camera poses) generated by MASt3R or VGGT [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, unofficial].
*   **Outputs:** A set of 3D Gaussian primitives $\{G_i\}$ defined by mean $\mu_i$, covariance $\Sigma_i$, opacity $\alpha_i$, and spherical harmonics coefficients $c_{i,lm}$ [6136-instantsplat.md, 6464-pixelsplat.md].

**Data Structures:**
The scene is represented as a list of Gaussian primitives. Each primitive $i$ stores:
*   $\mu_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$: 3D position.
*   $\Sigma_i \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$: Covariance matrix (often stored as rotation quaternion $q_i \in \mathbb{R}^4$ and scale vector $s_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$ for efficient parameterization).
*   $\alpha_i \in [0, 1]$: Opacity.
*   $c_{i,lm}$: Spherical harmonics coefficients for view-dependent color.

**Steps:**
1.  **Prior Generation:** Run MASt3R or VGGT on the input image set to estimate per-image depth maps and relative camera poses [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, unofficial].
2.  **Initialization:** Initialize the Gaussian cloud $\{G_i\}$ using the estimated depth and poses. Points are sampled from the depth maps and projected into 3D space using the estimated camera intrinsics and extrinsics [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, unofficial].
3.  **Differentiable Rendering:** Render the Gaussian primitives to image space using differentiable volume rendering [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, 6464-pixelsplat.md, unofficial].
4.  **Loss Computation:** Compute the loss between rendered images and ground truth images. The loss typically combines photometric reconstruction error ($\mathcal{L}_{L1}$ or $\mathcal{L}_{MSE}$) with perceptual metrics ($\mathcal{L}_{MS-SSIM}$ or $\mathcal{L}_{LPIPS}$) [6136-instantsplat.md, 6464-pixelsplat.md].
5.  **Optimization:** Update Gaussian attributes ($\mu_i, \Sigma_i, \alpha_i, c_{i,lm}$) via gradient descent. Poses remain fixed to the priors [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, 6464-pixelsplat.md, unofficial].

**Parameters:**
*   Training duration: Seconds [S1, unofficial].
*   View count: Sparse view [S1, unofficial].

**Edge Cases:**
*   Precision: Relies on the accuracy of MASt3R/VGGT priors; poor priors lead to initialization errors [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, unofficial].
*   Boundaries: Not given in the sources.

## Subtopics

### MoSca
A prior-aided monocular dynamic Gaussian Splatting method using CoTracker, VGG-T, DUSt3R, or metric-depth priors [S1, unofficial]. Choose this for dynamic scenes from monocular video where temporal consistency is required [S1, unofficial].

### Shape-of-Motion
A prior-aided monocular dynamic Gaussian Splatting method using CoTracker, VGG-T, DUSt3R, or metric-depth priors [S1, unofficial]. Choose this for dynamic scenes from monocular video where temporal consistency is required [S1, unofficial].

### Feature-3DGS
A method storing per-Gaussian language features (CLIP, LSeg) for open-vocabulary 3D segmentation [S1, unofficial]. Choose this when semantic segmentation or language-based querying of the 3D scene is needed [S1, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Training takes seconds [S1, unofficial].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Not given in the sources.
*   **Measured Performance:** Matches pose-aware baselines in zero-shot evaluations [S1, unofficial].
*   **Scaling:** Scales to sparse view inputs [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** No pose annotations required; fast training [S1, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** Dependent on prior quality; sparse view input [S1, unofficial].
*   **When to use:** Sparse-view, uncalibrated image collections where pose estimation is difficult or unavailable [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, unofficial].
*   **When not:** Dense view captures with known poses (use standard 3DGS) or real-time feed-forward inference (use [pixelSplat](6464-pixelsplat.md)) [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, 6464-pixelsplat.md, unofficial].

## Background
InstantSplat was introduced as a pose-free, sparse-view 3DGS method leveraging MASt3R/VGGT geometry priors [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, unofficial]. It is part of the evolution of 3D Gaussian Splatting towards handling casual, uncalured captures [S1, 6136-instantsplat.md, unofficial].

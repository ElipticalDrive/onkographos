# InstantSplat

Also called: MASt3R/VGGT-guided 3DGS, Pose-free 3D Gaussian Splatting

## Overview
InstantSplat is a pose-free, sparse-view 3D Gaussian Splatting training method that reconstructs scenes from uncalibrated image collections in seconds by leveraging geometric priors [S1, unofficial]. It distinguishes itself from standard 3DGS by eliminating the need for Structure-from-Motion (SfM) pose initialization and iterative camera optimization, instead using MASt3R or VGGT to provide direct geometry priors for rapid convergence [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
*   **Input:** A set of $N$ uncalibrated images $\{I_i\}$ with unknown intrinsics and extrinsics.
*   **Output:** A set of 3D Gaussian primitives $\{G_i\}$ defined by mean $\mu_i$, covariance $\Sigma_i$, opacity $\alpha_i$, and spherical harmonics coefficients $c_{i,lm}$, along with estimated camera poses and intrinsics [6136-instantsplat.md][6464-pixelsplat.md].

**Data Structures:**
The method initializes a point cloud using geometric priors. The exact layout of the prior data structure is not specified in the sources, but it typically consists of 3D points and associated feature descriptors [S1, unofficial].

**Steps:**
1.  **Geometric Prior Extraction:** Use a monocular depth estimation or stereo matching network (MASt3R or VGGT) to generate dense depth maps and camera poses for the input images [S1, unofficial].
2.  **Point Cloud Initialization:** Triangulate or back-project the depth maps to initialize the 3D Gaussian means $\mu_i$ and covariances $\Sigma_i$. This step replaces the SfM pipeline [S1, unofficial].
3.  **Differentiable Optimization:** Perform a rapid differentiable optimization loop. The loss function combines photometric reconstruction errors (L1 and MS-SSIM) between rendered and ground-truth images [6136-instantsplat.md].
4.  **Pose and Intrinsics Refinement:** Simultaneously optimize camera poses and intrinsics alongside the Gaussian parameters to correct errors in the initial geometric priors [S1, unofficial].

**Formulas:**
The rendering equation follows standard differentiable volume rendering [6136-instantsplat.md][6464-pixelsplat.md]. The loss function is:
$$ \mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{L1} + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{MS-SSIM} $$
where $\mathcal{L}_{L1}$ is the L1 loss and $\mathcal{L}_{MS-SSIM}$ is the Multi-Scale Structural Similarity index loss [6136-instantsplat.md].

**Parameters:**
*   Training time: Seconds [S1, unofficial].
*   View count: Sparse views [S1, unofficial].

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
*   **Prior Accuracy:** The quality of the reconstruction is heavily dependent on the accuracy of the MASt3R/VGGT priors. Inaccurate priors may lead to local minima or geometric distortions [S1, unofficial].
*   **Precision:** Standard floating-point precision is used; no specific bit-packing is mentioned [S1, unofficial].

## Subtopics

### MoSca
A prior-aided monocular dynamic Gaussian Splatting method using CoTracker, VGG-T, DUSt3R, or metric-depth priors [S1, unofficial]. It extends InstantSplat's pose-free approach to dynamic scenes by incorporating temporal tracking and depth priors.

### Shape-of-Motion
A prior-aided monocular dynamic Gaussian Splatting method using CoTracker, VGG-T, DUSt3R, or metric-depth priors [S1, unofficial]. Similar to MoSca, it focuses on reconstructing dynamic scenes from monocular video using geometric and motion priors.

### Feature-3DGS
A method storing per-Gaussian language features (CLIP, LSeg) for open-vocabulary 3D segmentation [S1, unofficial]. It enhances the Gaussian representation with semantic information, allowing for language-based queries and segmentation.

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Training takes seconds, significantly faster than standard 3DGS which takes minutes [S1, unofficial].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Not given in the sources.
*   **Measured Performance:** Matches pose-aware baselines in zero-shot evaluations [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** No need for SfM; rapid training; works with sparse views.
*   **Limitations:** Dependent on the quality of geometric priors; may struggle with highly ambiguous scenes where priors are inaccurate.
*   **When to use:** Sparse-view reconstruction, casual capture, or when SfM fails.
*   **When not to use:** Dense-view scenarios where standard 3DGS is sufficient, or when high-precision SfM is available.

## Background
InstantSplat was introduced as a solution for sparse-view and pose-free 3D Gaussian Splatting, leveraging recent advances in monocular depth estimation and stereo matching (MASt3R/VGGT) to bypass the need for Structure-from-Motion [S1, unofficial]. It is part of the broader trend of making 3DGS more accessible by reducing dependency on complex preprocessing pipelines.

# NeILF Rendering and Loss Calculation

Also called: inverse rendering with Blinn-Phong

## Overview
NeILF Rendering and Loss Calculation is a differentiable rendering pipeline that decomposes Gaussian Splatting features into diffuse, specular, and ambient components using a Blinn-Phong shading model [S1, unofficial]. It enables inverse rendering by optimizing scene parameters against ground-truth images, depth, and normals through a composite loss function [S1, unofficial]. Unlike standard Gaussian Splatting which renders a single composite color, this method explicitly separates lighting terms to support material editing and physically-based constraints [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs:**
- Camera parameters: pose (view matrix), intrinsics (focal length, principal point), resolution ($H, W$) [S1, unofficial].
- Gaussian primitives: positions, covariances, opacities, and feature vectors including `diffuse_factor`, `specular_factor`, `ambient_factor`, `shininess`, and `offset_color` [S1, unofficial].
- Light parameters: `light_transform` (optional transformation for diffuse, shininess, ambient, and specular factors) [S1, unofficial].
- Optimization weights: `lambda_render`, `lambda_phong`, `lambda_depth`, `lambda_opacity`, `lambda_normal_render_depth`, `lambda_normal_mvs_depth`, `lambda_offset_color_sparsity`, `lambda_diffuse_factor_smooth`, `lambda_ambient_factor_smooth`, `lambda_normal_smooth`, `lambda_dssim` [S1, unofficial].
- Ground truth: `gt_image`, `gt_depth`, `image_mask`, `depth_mask` [S1, unofficial].
- Optional MVS normal map [S1, unofficial].

**Outputs:**
- `render`: Final rendered color image [S1, unofficial].
- `phong`: Blinn-Phong rendered color image [S1, unofficial].
- `normal`: Rendered surface normals [S1, unofficial].
- `pseudo_normal`: Normals derived from depth [S1, unofficial].
- `opacity`: Rendered opacity mask [S1, unofficial].
- `depth`: Rendered depth map [S1, unofficial].
- `diffuse_term`, `specular_term`, `ambient_term`: Individual lighting components [S1, unofficial].
- `loss`: Total optimization loss value [S1, unofficial].
- Auxiliary data: `surface_xyz`, `viewspace_points`, `visibility_filter`, `radii`, `num_rendered`, `num_contrib`, `hdr`, `val_gamma`, `tb_dict` [S1, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
The rendering results are stored in a dictionary `results` with the following keys:
- `'render'`: Tensor $(3, H, W)$, final color.
- `'phong'`: Tensor $(3, H, W)$, Blinn-Phong color.
- `'normal'`: Tensor $(3, H, W)$, normal vectors.
- `'pseudo_normal'`: Tensor $(3, H, W)$, depth-derived normals.
- `'surface_xyz'`: Tensor $(3, H, W)$, 3D surface positions.
- `'opacity'`: Tensor $(1, H, W)$, opacity mask.
- `'depth'`: Tensor $(1, H, W)$, depth values.
- `'viewspace_points'`: Tensor for view-space coordinates.
- `'visibility_filter'`: Boolean mask for active Gaussians.
- `'radii'`: Integer mask for visible Gaussians.
- `'num_rendered'`: Count of rendered Gaussians.
- `'num_contrib'`: Count of contributing Gaussians per pixel.
- `'diffuse_factor'`: Tensor, diffuse material factor.
- `'offset_color'`: Tensor, color offset for blending.
- `'shininess'`: Tensor, specular exponent.
- `'ambient_factor'`: Tensor, ambient intensity.
- `'specular_factor'`: Tensor, specular intensity.
- `'diffuse_term'`: Tensor, computed diffuse color.
- `'specular_term'`: Tensor, computed specular color.
- `'ambient_term'`: Tensor, computed ambient color.
- `'hdr'`: Boolean, HDR flag.
- `'val_gamma'`: Float, gamma value.
- `'tb_dict'`: Dictionary for TensorBoard logging.
- `'loss'`: Float, total loss.

**Algorithm Steps:**

1. **Base Rendering:** Call `render_view` to obtain base Gaussian splatting features, including `diffuse_factor`, `specular_factor`, `ambient_factor`, `shininess`, `offset_color`, `normals`, and `opacity` [S1, unofficial].

2. **Light Transform:** If `light_transform` is not None, apply it to the material factors:
   $$ \text{factor}_{new} = \text{factor}_{old} \times \text{light\_transform} + \text{offset} $$
   for `diffuse_factor`, `shininess`, `ambient_factor`, and `specular_factor` [S1, unofficial].

3. **Offset Color Norm:** Compute the squared norm of `offset_color` along the color dimensions:
   $$ \text{offset\_color\_norm} = \sum_{c} (\text{offset\_color}_c)^2 $$
   keeping dimensions for broadcasting [S1, unofficial].

4. **Multi-Gaussian Grouping:** If multiple Gaussian groups exist (`len(num_GSs_TFs) > 1`):
   - For each group $i$:
     - Compute `diffuse_color` for the group's Gaussians:
       $$ \text{diffuse\_color} = (\text{offset\_color} + \text{palette\_color}) \cdot \text{clamp}(0, 1) \cdot \text{detach}() $$
     - If `opacity_factors[i]` exists, multiply the group's opacity by `opacity_factors[i]` [S1, unofficial].

5. **Diffuse Term Calculation:**
   - Compute diffuse intensity:
     $$ I_{diffuse} = \text{diffuse\_factor} \cdot |\mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{inc}| $$
     where $\mathbf{n}$ is the surface normal and $\mathbf{d}_{inc}$ is the incident light direction [S1, unofficial].
   - Compute diffuse color:
     $$ C_{diffuse} = (\text{ambient\_factor} + I_{diffuse}) \cdot \text{diffuse\_color} $$
     [S1, unofficial].

6. **Specular Term Calculation:**
   - Compute half-vector:
     $$ \mathbf{h} = \frac{\mathbf{d}_{inc} + \mathbf{d}_{view}}{\|\mathbf{d}_{inc} + \mathbf{d}_{view}\|} $$
     where $\mathbf{d}_{view}$ is the view direction [S1, unofficial].
   - Compute cosine of angle between normal and half-vector:
     $$ \cos_h = \mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{h} $$
   - Compute cosine of angle between normal and incident light:
     $$ \cos_l = \mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{inc} $$
   - Compute specular intensity:
     $$ I_{specular} = \text{specular\_factor} \cdot \begin{cases} |\cos_h|^{\text{shininess}} & \text{if } \cos_l \neq 0 \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} $$
     [S1, unofficial].
   - Compute specular color:
     $$ C_{specular} = I_{specular} \cdot \text{diffuse\_color} $$
     [S1, unofficial].

7. **PBR Combination:**
   $$ C_{PBR} = \text{clamp}(C_{diffuse} + C_{specular}, 0, 1) $$
   [S1, unofficial].

8. **Loss Calculation (if training):**
   - **Render Loss:** If `lambda_render > 0`:
     $$ L_{render} = (1 - \lambda_{dssim}) \cdot L1(C_{PBR}, \text{gt\_image}) + \lambda_{dssim} \cdot (1 - SSIM(C_{PBR}, \text{gt\_image})) $$
     [S1, unofficial].
   - **Phong Loss:** If `lambda_phong > 0`:
     $$ L_{phong} = \lambda_{phong} \cdot \left[ (1 - \lambda_{dssim}) \cdot L1(C_{PBR}, \text{gt\_image}) + \lambda_{dssim} \cdot (1 - SSIM(C_{PBR}, \text{gt\_image})) \right] $$
     [S1, unofficial].
   - **Depth Loss:** If `lambda_depth > 0`:
     $$ L_{depth} = \lambda_{depth} \cdot L1(C_{depth}, \text{gt\_depth}) \cdot \text{mask}(\text{image\_mask} \oplus \text{depth\_mask} == \text{False}) $$
     where $\oplus$ is XOR [S1, unofficial].
   - **Opacity Loss:** If `lambda_opacity > 0`:
     - Clamp `opacity` to $[10^{-6}, 1 - 10^{-6}]$ [G].
     $$ L_{opacity} = \lambda_{opacity} \cdot \left[ (1 - \lambda_{dssim}) \cdot L1(\text{opacity}, \text{image\_mask}) + \lambda_{dssim} \cdot (1 - SSIM(\text{opacity}, \text{image\_mask})) \right] $$
     [S1, unofficial].
   - **Normal Loss (Render Depth):** If `lambda_normal_render_depth > 0`:
     $$ L_{normal\_render} = \lambda_{normal\_render\_depth} \cdot MSE(C_{normal}, \text{pseudo\_normal}) \cdot \text{mask}(\text{image\_mask}) $$
     [S1, unofficial].
   - **Normal Loss (MVS Depth):** If `lambda_normal_mvs_depth > 0` and MVS normal is zero:
     - Compute `pseudo_normal` from `gt_depth` using `kornia.depth_to_normals` [S1, unofficial].
     - Transform `pseudo_normal` to world space using camera intrinsics and `world_view_transform` [S1, unofficial].
     $$ L_{normal\_mvs} = \lambda_{normal\_mvs\_depth} \cdot MSE(C_{normal}, \text{pseudo\_normal}) \cdot \text{mask}(\text{depth\_mask}) $$
     [S1, unofficial].
   - **Sparsity Loss:** If `lambda_offset_color_sparsity > 0`:
     $$ L_{sparsity} = \lambda_{offset\_color\_sparsity} \cdot \text{sparsity}(\text{offset\_color\_norm}) \cdot \text{mask}(\text{image\_mask}) $$
     [S1, unofficial].
   - **Smoothness Losses:**
     - If `lambda_diffuse_factor_smooth > 0`:
       $$ L_{smooth\_diffuse} = \lambda_{diffuse\_factor\_smooth} \cdot \text{bilateral\_smooth}(C_{diffuse\_factor}, \text{gt\_image}, \text{image\_mask}) $$
       [S1, unofficial].
     - If `lambda_ambient_factor_smooth > 0`:
       $$ L_{smooth\_ambient} = \lambda_{ambient\_factor\_smooth} \cdot \left[ \text{bilateral\_smooth}(C_{specular\_intensity}, \dots) + \text{bilateral\_smooth}(C_{ambient\_intensity}, \dots) \right] $$
       [S1, unofficial].
     - If `lambda_normal_smooth > 0`:
       $$ L_{smooth\_normal} = \lambda_{normal\_smooth} \cdot \text{bilateral\_smooth}(C_{normal}, \text{gt\_image}, \text{image\_mask}) $$
       [S1, unofficial].
   - **Total Loss:**
     $$ L_{total} = L_{render} + L_{phong} + L_{depth} + L_{opacity} + L_{normal\_render} + L_{normal\_mvs} + L_{sparsity} + L_{smooth\_diffuse} + L_{smooth\_ambient} + L_{smooth\_normal} $$
     [S1, unofficial].

**Pseudocode:**

```python
import torch
import torch.nn.functional as F

def render_neilf(camera, gaussians, light_transform=None, gt_image=None, gt_depth=None, 
                 image_mask=None, depth_mask=None, mvs_normal=None, 
                 lambda_render=1.0, lambda_phong=0.0, lambda_depth=0.0, lambda_opacity=0.0,
                 lambda_normal_render_depth=0.0, lambda_normal_mvs_depth=0.0, 
                 lambda_offset_color_sparsity=0.0, lambda_diffuse_factor_smooth=0.0,
                 lambda_ambient_factor_smooth=0.0, lambda_normal_smooth=0.0, lambda_dssim=0.2):
    """
    Renders Gaussian Splatting with Blinn-Phong lighting and calculates loss.
    """
    # 1. Base Rendering
    # Assume render_view returns a dict with base features
    base_features = render_view(camera, gaussians)
    
    diffuse_factor = base_features['diffuse_factor']
    specular_factor = base_features['specular_factor']
    ambient_factor = base_features['ambient_factor']
    shininess = base_features['shininess']
    offset_color = base_features['offset_color']
    normals = base_features['normal']
    opacity = base_features['opacity']
    depth = base_features['depth']
    
    # 2. Light Transform
    if light_transform is not None:
        diffuse_factor = diffuse_factor * light_transform + light_transform.offset
        shininess = shininess * light_transform + light_transform.offset
        ambient_factor = ambient_factor * light_transform + light_transform.offset
        specular_factor = specular_factor * light_transform + light_transform.offset

    # 3. Offset Color Norm
    # Sum squared offset_color along last two dimensions (H, W), keeping dims
    offset_color_norm = torch.sum(offset_color ** 2, dim=-3, keepdim=True) # Assuming C, H, W
    
    # 4. Multi-Gaussian Grouping (Simplified for single group if not specified)
    # If multiple groups, iterate and apply opacity_factors
    # For this implementation, assume single group or pre-aggregated features
    
    # 5. Diffuse Term
    # incident_dirs and viewdirs assumed to be computed from camera and surface_xyz
    # For simplicity, assume incident_dirs is provided or computed from light position
    # Assuming point light at camera or directional light
    incident_dirs = base_features.get('incident_dirs', torch.zeros_like(normals)) # Placeholder for direction
    viewdirs = base_features.get('viewdirs', torch.zeros_like(normals))
    
    # Normalize directions if not already
    incident_dirs = F.normalize(incident_dirs, dim=-3)
    viewdirs = F.normalize(viewdirs, dim=-3)
    
    # Diffuse intensity: diffuse_factor * abs(dot(normals, incident_dirs))
    cos_incident = torch.abs(torch.sum(normals * incident_dirs, dim=-3, keepdim=True))
    diffuse_intensity = diffuse_factor * cos_incident
    
    # Diffuse color: (ambient_factor + diffuse_intensity) * diffuse_color
    # diffuse_color is derived from offset_color + palette_color
    palette_color = base_features.get('palette_color', torch.ones_like(offset_color))
    diffuse_color = (offset_color + palette_color).clamp(0, 1).detach()
    diffuse_term = (ambient_factor + diffuse_intensity) * diffuse_color
    
    # 6. Specular Term
    # Half vector h = normalize(incident_dirs + viewdirs)
    h = F.normalize(incident_dirs + viewdirs, dim=-3)
    
    # cos_h = dot(normals, h)
    cos_h = torch.sum(normals * h, dim=-3, keepdim=True)
    
    # cos_l = dot(normals, incident_dirs)
    cos_l = torch.sum(normals * incident_dirs, dim=-3, keepdim=True)
    
    # Specular intensity: specular_factor * where(cos_l != 0, abs(cos_h)^shininess, 0)
    specular_intensity = specular_factor * torch.where(
        cos_l != 0, 
        torch.abs(cos_h).pow(shininess), 
        torch.zeros_like(cos_h)
    )
    
    specular_term = specular_intensity * diffuse_color
    
    # 7. PBR Combination
    phong_color = (diffuse_term + specular_term).clamp(0, 1)
    
    # 8. Loss Calculation
    loss = torch.tensor(0.0, device=diffuse_term.device)
    
    if lambda_render > 0 and gt_image is not None:
        l1_render = F.l1_loss(phong_color, gt_image)
        ssim_render = 1 - ssim_loss(phong_color, gt_image) # Assuming ssim_loss function
        loss += (1 - lambda_dssim) * l1_render + lambda_dssim * ssim_render
        
    if lambda_phong > 0 and gt_image is not None:
        l1_phong = F.l1_loss(phong_color, gt_image)
        ssim_phong = 1 - ssim_loss(phong_color, gt_image)
        loss += lambda_phong * ((1 - lambda_dssim) * l1_phong + lambda_dssim * ssim_phong)
        
    if lambda_depth > 0 and gt_depth is not None and image_mask is not None and depth_mask is not None:
        # Exclude pixels where image_mask XOR depth_mask is true
        mask = ~(image_mask ^ depth_mask)
        l1_depth = F.l1_loss(depth[mask], gt_depth[mask])
        loss += lambda_depth * l1_depth
        
    if lambda_opacity > 0 and image_mask is not None:
        opacity_clamped = opacity.clamp(1e-6, 1 - 1e-6)
        l1_opacity = F.l1_loss(opacity_clamped, image_mask)
        ssim_opacity = 1 - ssim_loss(opacity_clamped, image_mask)
        loss += lambda_opacity * ((1 - lambda_dssim) * l1_opacity + lambda_dssim * ssim_opacity)
        
    if lambda_normal_render_depth > 0 and base_features.get('pseudo_normal') is not None and image_mask is not None:
        pseudo_normal = base_features['pseudo_normal']
        mse_normal = F.mse_loss(normals[image_mask], pseudo_normal[image_mask])
        loss += lambda_normal_render_depth * mse_normal
        
    if lambda_normal_mvs_depth > 0 and mvs_normal is not None and torch.all(mvs_normal == 0) and depth_mask is not None:
        # Compute pseudo normal from gt_depth
        pseudo_normal_mvs = depth_to_normals(gt_depth) # Assuming kornia.depth_to_normals
        # Transform to world space
        pseudo_normal_mvs = transform_to_world(pseudo_normal_mvs, camera.intrinsics, camera.world_view_transform)
        mse_normal_mvs = F.mse_loss(normals[depth_mask], pseudo_normal_mvs[depth_mask])
        loss += lambda_normal_mvs_depth * mse_normal_mvs
        
    if lambda_offset_color_sparsity > 0 and image_mask is not None:
        sparsity_loss = compute_sparsity(offset_color_norm[image_mask])
        loss += lambda_offset_color_sparsity * sparsity_loss
        
    if lambda_diffuse_factor_smooth > 0 and gt_image is not None and image_mask is not None:
        smooth_diffuse = bilateral_smooth_loss(diffuse_factor, gt_image, image_mask)
        loss += lambda_diffuse_factor_smooth * smooth_diffuse
        
    if lambda_ambient_factor_smooth > 0:
        smooth_ambient_spec = bilateral_smooth_loss(specular_intensity, gt_image, image_mask)
        smooth_ambient_amb = bilateral_smooth_loss(ambient_factor, gt_image, image_mask)
        loss += lambda_ambient_factor_smooth * (smooth_ambient_spec + smooth_ambient_amb)
        
    if lambda_normal_smooth > 0 and gt_image is not None and image_mask is not None:
        smooth_normal = bilateral_smooth_loss(normals, gt_image, image_mask)
        loss += lambda_normal_smooth * smooth_normal
        
    results = {
        'render': phong_color,
        'phong': phong_color,
        'normal': normals,
        'pseudo_normal': base_features.get('pseudo_normal'),
        'surface_xyz': base_features.get('surface_xyz'),
        'opacity': opacity,
        'depth': depth,
        'viewspace_points': base_features.get('viewspace_points'),
        'visibility_filter': base_features.get('visibility_filter'),
        'radii': base_features.get('radii'),
        'num_rendered': base_features.get('num_rendered'),
        'num_contrib': base_features.get('num_contrib'),
        'diffuse_factor': diffuse_factor,
        'offset_color': offset_color,
        'shininess': shininess,
        'ambient_factor': ambient_factor,
        'specular_factor': specular_factor,
        'diffuse_term': diffuse_term,
        'specular_term': specular_term,
        'ambient_term': ambient_factor * diffuse_color, # Simplified
        'hdr': base_features.get('hdr'),
        'val_gamma': base_features.get('val_gamma'),
        'tb_dict': {},
        'loss': loss
    }
    
    return results
```

**Edge Cases and Pitfalls:**
- Gaussians with radius 0 or frustum culled are excluded from value updates in splitting criteria via `visibility_filter` (radii > 0) [S1, unofficial].
- MVS normal map may be all zeros; in this case, pseudo normal is computed from `gt_depth` using `kornia.depth_to_normals` and transformed to world space [S1, unofficial].
- Precision issues may arise in `shininess` exponentiation for very small `cos_h` values [G].
- Boundary conditions for `clamp` operations on color values [G].

## Operating characteristics
- **Time Complexity:** Dominated by Gaussian rasterization and shading per pixel. Linear in the number of Gaussians and pixels [S1, unofficial].
- **Memory Complexity:** Proportional to the number of Gaussians and image resolution for storing features and intermediate buffers [S1, unofficial].
- **Measured Performance:** Not given in the sources.
- **Scaling:** Scales with the number of Gaussian primitives and image resolution [S1, unofficial].
- **Strengths:** Explicit separation of lighting components allows for inverse rendering and material editing [S1, unofficial].
- **Limitations:** Relies on ground-truth data for loss calculation; may require careful tuning of loss weights [S1, unofficial].
- **When to use:** When inverse rendering or material decomposition is required [S1, unofficial].
- **When not to use:** For standard forward rendering without optimization [S1, unofficial].

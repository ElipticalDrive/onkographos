# GSPull
Also called: GSDF / GSPull
## Overview
GSPull is a technique that leverages neural signed distance functions (SDFs) for surface reconstruction to recover more detailed mesh structures from 3D Gaussian Splatting representations [S1][S2, unofficial].

## Background
Origin: GSPull was introduced as a technique that leverages neural signed distance functions (SDFs) for surface reconstruction, drawing inspiration from NeRF-based surface reconstruction methods [S1][S2, unofficial].
History and development: It follows the trend of adapting 3D Gaussian Splatting for surface reconstruction, alongside related methods such as GSDF [S1][S2, unofficial].

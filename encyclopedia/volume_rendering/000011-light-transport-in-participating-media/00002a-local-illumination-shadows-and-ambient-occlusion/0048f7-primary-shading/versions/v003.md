# Primary Shading
## Overview
Primary Shading evaluates the final radiance of a traced voxel material at a specific hit point by combining direct lighting, indirect lighting (Global Illumination or Ambient), environment lighting, and subsurface translucency [S1, unofficial]. It integrates visibility results into a comprehensive appearance model that supports multiple material shading models, including unlit, emissive, clear coat, two-sided foliage, and subsurface scattering [S1, unofficial].

## How it works
**Inputs and Outputs:**
The method operates on a per-pixel basis using structured buffers [S1, unofficial].
*   **Inputs:**
    *   `gPrimaryResults`: `StructuredBuffer<TraceResult>` containing hit information (material ID, normal, voxel position) [S1, unofficial].
    *   `gPrimaryRays`: `StructuredBuffer<TraceRay>` containing ray origins and directions [S1, unofficial].
    *   `gAmbientOcclusion`: `StructuredBuffer<float>` containing precomputed AO values [S1, unofficial].
    *   `gShadowVisibility`: `StructuredBuffer<float>` containing shadow visibility factors [S1, unofficial].
    *   `gIndirectDiffuse`: `StructuredBuffer<float4>` containing precomputed indirect diffuse lighting [S1, unofficial].
    *   `gDiffuseIrradiance`: `TextureCube<float4>` for environment irradiance [S1, unofficial].
    *   `gSpecularPrefilter`: `TextureCube<float4>` for environment specular prefiltering [S1, unofficial].
    *   `gEnvironmentBrdfLut`: `Texture2D<float2>` for environment BRDF lookup [S1, unofficial].
    *   `gEnvironmentSampler`: `SamplerState` for texture sampling [S1, unofficial].
    *   Constants: `gEnvironmentLightingEnabled`, `gEnvironmentMaximumSpecularLod`, `gEnvironmentIntensity`, `gEnvironmentRotationRadians` [S1, unofficial].
*   **Output:**
    *   `gRadiance`: `RWStructuredBuffer<float4>` storing the final radiance and alpha [S1, unofficial].

**Data Structures:**
The implementation relies on specific material records and shading models [S1, unofficial].
*   `MaterialRecord`: Contains `baseColorRGB`, `baseColorA`, `roughness`, `metallic`, `specular`, `emissiveRGB`, `shadingModel` (enum), and subsurface parameters (`subsurfaceScatterDistanceMeters`, `subsurfaceColorRGB`) [S1, unofficial].
*   `TraceResult`: Contains `hit` (boolean), `material` (ID), `normal`, `voxel` (position), and `hitPosition` [S1, unofficial].
*   `TraceRay`: Contains `direction` [S1, unofficial].

**Parameters:**
*   `kMpcTimeOfDayTint`: Material global vector for time-of-day color tint [S1, unofficial].
*   `kMpcWetness`: Material global scalar for wetness factor (0.0 to 1.0) [S1, unofficial].
*   `gSunColor`, `gSunIntensity`, `gSunDirection`: Global light source properties [S1, unofficial].
*   `gShadowMode`: Enum (`kShadowOff`, etc.) [S1, unofficial].
*   `gGlobalIlluminationMode`: Enum (`kGlobalIlluminationAmbientHemisphere`, `kGlobalIlluminationVoxelOneBounce`) [S1, unofficial].
*   `gSkyColor`, `gGroundColor`: Hemisphere lighting colors [S1, unofficial].
*   `gSubsurfaceMaxDistanceMeters`: Maximum distance for subsurface scattering calculation [S1, unofficial].
*   `gShadowBiasMeters`, `gShadowStrength`, `gShadowMaxDistanceMeters`: Shadow tuning parameters [S1, unofficial].

**Steps:**

1.  **Miss Handling:** If the ray misses (`hit.hit == 0`), compute the background color based on the ray's upwardness [S1, unofficial].
    $$ \text{upness} = 0.5 + 0.5 \cdot \text{normalize}(\mathbf{rayDirection}).y $$
    $$ \text{radiance} = \text{lerp}(\text{gGroundColor}, \text{gSkyColor}, \text{upness}) $$
    Store `float4(radiance, 0.0)` in `gRadiance` and return [S1, unofficial].

2.  **Material Loading:** Load the material record using `hit.material` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Compute `timeOfDayTint` from global constants [S1, unofficial].
    *   Compute `wetness` from global constants, clamped to [0, 1] [S1, unofficial].
    *   Compute `baseColor`:
        $$ \text{baseColor} = \text{MaterialBaseColorRGB} \cdot \text{timeOfDayTint} \cdot \text{lerp}(1.0, 0.76, \text{wetness}) $$
    *   Compute `roughness`:
        $$ \text{roughness} = \text{lerp}(\text{material.roughness}, 0.08, \text{wetness}) $$
    *   Get `emissive` from material [S1, unofficial].

3.  **Simple Shading Models:**
    *   If `shadingModel == kShadingModelUnlit`:
        $$ \text{radiance} = \text{baseColor} $$
        Store `float4(baseColor, material.baseColorA)` and return [S1, unofficial].
    *   If `shadingModel == kShadingModelEmissive`:
        $$ \text{radiance} = \text{emissive} $$
        Store `float4(emissive, material.baseColorA)` and return [S1, unofficial].

4.  **Normal Computation:**
    *   Compute `geometricNormal` from `hit.normal` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Compute `viewDirection` as `-normalize(rayDirection)` [S1, unofficial].
    *   If `shadingModel == kShadingModelTwoSidedFoliage`, apply wind deformation to `geometricNormal` to get `normal` [S1, unofficial]. Otherwise, `normal = geometricNormal` [S1, unofficial].

5.  **Lighting Visibility:**
    *   Load `ao` from `gAmbientOcclusion` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Compute `shadowVisibility`:
        $$ \text{shadowVisibility} = \begin{cases} 1.0 & \text{if } gShadowMode == kShadowOff \\ \text{saturate}(gShadowVisibility) & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} $$
    *   Compute `sunRadiance`:
        $$ \text{sunRadiance} = \text{gSunColor} \cdot \text{gSunIntensity} \cdot \text{shadowVisibility} $$

6.  **Direct Lighting:**
    *   Call the appropriate direct lighting function based on `shadingModel`:
        *   `kShadingModelClearCoat`: `ShadeClearCoatDirect`
        *   `kShadingModelTwoSidedFoliage`: `ShadeTwoSidedFoliageDirect`
        *   Others: `ShadeDirectLight`
    *   Inputs to these functions include `normal`, `viewDirection`, `gSunDirection`, `sunRadiance`, `baseColor`, `material.metallic`, `roughness`, and `material.specular` [S1, unofficial].
    *   Let the result be `direct`.

7.  **Indirect Lighting:**
    *   Initialize `indirect = 0`.
    *   If `gGlobalIlluminationMode == kGlobalIlluminationAmbientHemisphere` and `gEnvironmentLightingEnabled == 0`:
        $$ \text{indirect} = \text{ShadeAmbient}(\text{ambientNormal}, \text{gSkyColor}, \text{gGroundColor}, \text{ao}) \cdot \text{baseColor} \cdot (1.0 - \text{material.metallic}) \cdot \text{gGlobalIlluminationIntensity} $$
    *   If `gGlobalIlluminationMode == kGlobalIlluminationVoxelOneBounce`:
        $$ \text{indirect} = \text{gIndirectDiffuse} \cdot \text{baseColor} \cdot (1.0 - \text{material.metallic}) $$
    *   If `gEnvironmentLightingEnabled != 0`:
        Add image-based lighting contribution using `EvaluateImageBasedLighting` with inputs: irradiance (`gDiffuseIrradiance`), prefilter (`gSpecularPrefilter`), BRDF LUT (`gEnvironmentBrdfLut`), sampler, `ambientNormal`, `viewDirection`, `baseColor`, `material.metallic`, `roughness`, `material.specular`, `ao`, `gEnvironmentMaximumSpecularLod`, `gEnvironmentRotationRadians` [S1, unofficial].
        $$ \text{indirect} += \text{EvaluateImageBasedLighting}(\dots) \cdot \max(\text{gEnvironmentIntensity}, 0.0) $$

8.  **Subsurface Scattering (Conditional):**
    *   Condition: `shadingModel == kShadingModelSubsurface` AND `material.subsurfaceScatterDistanceMeters > 0` AND `gSubsurfaceMaxDistanceMeters > 0` AND $\text{dot}(\text{geometricNormal}, \text{gSunDirection}) < 0$ [S1, unofficial].
    *   Compute `interiorBiasVoxels`:
        $$ \text{interiorBiasVoxels} = \max(1.0 \times 10^{-3}, \text{MetersToVoxelUnits}(0.001)) $$
    *   Compute `hitPoint`:
        $$ \text{hitPoint} = \text{TraceHitPosition}(\text{ray}, \text{hit}) - \text{geometricNormal} \cdot \text{interiorBiasVoxels} $$
    *   March subsurface thickness:
        $$ \text{march} = \text{MarchSubsurfaceThicknessVoxels}(\text{hitPoint}, \text{gSunDirection}, \text{hit.material}, \text{MetersToVoxelUnits}(\text{gSubsurfaceMaxDistanceMeters}), 64) $$
    *   If `march.exited`:
        *   Compute `farVisibility`:
            *   If `gShadowMode != kShadowOff`:
                *   Trace a far ray from `march.exitPoint + march.exitNormal * \max(1.0 \times 10^{-3}, \text{MetersToVoxelUnits}(gShadowBiasMeters))` in direction `gSunDirection` with max distance `MetersToVoxelUnits(gShadowMaxDistanceMeters)` [S1, unofficial].
                *   If hit: `farVisibility = \text{saturate}(1.0 - gShadowStrength)` [S1, unofficial].
                *   Else: `farVisibility = 1.0`.
            *   Else: `farVisibility = 1.0`.
        *   Add subsurface transmission:
            $$ \text{radiance} += \text{ShadeSubsurfaceTransmission}(\text{thickness}, \text{scatterDistance}, \text{subsurfaceColor}, \text{exitNormal}, \text{sunDirection}, \text{sunRadiance} \cdot \text{farVisibility}) $$

9.  **Final Computation:**
    $$ \text{radiance} = \text{direct} + \text{indirect} + \text{emissive} $$
    Store `float4(radiance, material.baseColorA)` in `gRadiance` [S1, unofficial].

                march.thickness_meters, material.subsurface_scatter_distance_meters,
                MaterialSubsurfaceColorRGB(material), march.exit_normal, gSunDirection,
                sun_radiance * far_visibility
            )

## Subtopics

### [Ray Casting and Fragment Shading](12415-ray-casting-and-fragment-shading.md)
This method evaluates shading for traced voxel materials by casting primary rays per fragment to determine surface interaction, then computing direct lighting, ambient occlusion (AO), and indirect lighting contributions. It differs from [Basic Gas Rendering Algorithm](12423-basic-gas-rendering-algorithm.md) by focusing on discrete surface hits and fragment-based shading rather than volumetric integration along the ray, and builds on [Primary Shading](12386-primary-shading.md) by handling the specific ray-casting-to-fragment pipeline for voxel environments.

### [Basic Gas Rendering Algorithm](12423-basic-gas-rendering-algorithm.md)
The Basic Gas Rendering Algorithm is a volume rendering method designed for visualizing Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) data, specifically gaseous flows and atmospheric phenomena. It solves the problem of revealing internal flow details, shock waves, and pressure changes that are often obscured by traditional surface-based rendering techniques. Unlike methods that rely on gradient-based surface implication and Phong illumination, this approach uses self-shadowing and volume transparency to provide a deeper understanding of three-dimensional flow structures.

## Operating characteristics
*   **Time Complexity:** Dominated by the cost of the shading model evaluation (e.g., `ShadeClearCoatDirect`) and the subsurface scattering march [S1, unofficial]. Subsurface scattering adds a linear cost proportional to the scattering distance and sample count (64 samples in the reference) [S1, unofficial]. Environment lighting involves cube map sampling [S1, unofficial].
*   **Memory Complexity:** Requires storage for multiple structured buffers (`gPrimaryResults`, `gPrimaryRays`, `gAmbientOcclusion`, `gShadowVisibility`, `gIndirectDiffuse`) and environment textures (`gDiffuseIrradiance`, `gSpecularPrefilter`, `gEnvironmentBrdfLut`) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Performance:** The reference implementation reports metrics for solid and textured wall scenes, including cook, write, and read times in milliseconds, and voxel byte sizes [S1, unofficial]. Performance scales with the number of unique materials and the complexity of the shading model [S1, unofficial].
*   **Strengths:** Supports a wide range of material types (unlit, emissive, foliage, clear coat, subsurface) and lighting conditions (direct, indirect, environment) [S1, unofficial].
*   **Limitations:** Computationally expensive due to multiple lighting passes and potential subsurface marching [S1, unofficial]. Requires precomputed lighting buffers (AO, Indirect Diffuse) which add to the pipeline complexity [S1, unofficial].
*   **When to Use:** When high-quality, physically-based shading for voxel materials is required, including complex effects like subsurface scattering and environment reflections [S1, unofficial].
*   **When Not to Use:** For simple volumetric effects where performance is critical and complex material properties are not needed [S1, unofficial].

## Background
Primary Shading is implemented in the `destructible-voxel-engine` repository [S1, unofficial]. It is designed to handle shading for traced voxel materials, integrating various lighting components to achieve realistic appearances [S1, unofficial]. The method reflects modern real-time rendering techniques adapted for voxel-based geometry, including image-based lighting and subsurface scattering [S1, unofficial].

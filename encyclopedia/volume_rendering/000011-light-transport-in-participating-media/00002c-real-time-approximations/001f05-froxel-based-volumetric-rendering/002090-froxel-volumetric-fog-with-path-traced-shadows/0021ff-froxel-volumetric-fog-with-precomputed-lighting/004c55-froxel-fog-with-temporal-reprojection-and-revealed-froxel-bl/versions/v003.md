# Froxel Fog with Temporal Reprojection and Revealed Froxel Blur

Also called: Breda volumetric fog, Shadowing newly revealed froxels, Volumetric Fog (Froxel-based)

## Overview
This method improves the visual quality of froxel-based volumetric fog by reducing noise in shadow factors when froxels are "revealed" (i.e., when temporal reprojection fails and history is discarded) [S2, unofficial]. It combines increased sampling for revealed froxels with a targeted blur pass that only processes those specific cells, avoiding the cost of blurring converged history [S2, unofficial]. It builds on [Froxel Volumetric Fog with Precomputed Lighting](2647-froxel-volumetric-fog-with-precomputed-lighting.md) by adding temporal reprojection and a targeted blur pass for revealed cells [S2, unofficial].

## How it works
### Inputs and Outputs
**Inputs:**
- Current frame's camera projection and view matrices [G].
- Previous frame's camera projection and view matrices (for reprojection) [G].
- Current frame's shadow map(s) [S1, unofficial].
- Previous frame's froxel density volume (R16Sfloat) [S1, unofficial].
- Previous frame's froxel shadow factor volume (R8Unorm) [S1, unofficial].
- Opaque surface depth buffer (tiled) [S1, unofficial].

**Outputs:**
- Updated froxel density volume (R16Sfloat) [S1, unofficial].
- Updated froxel shadow factor volume (R8Unorm) [S1, unofficial].

### Data Structures
1.  **Froxel Grid:**
    -   Dimensions: 8x8 pixel tiles across the screen, 64 slices in depth [S2, unofficial].
    -   Density Storage: `R16Sfloat` volume [S2, unofficial].
    -   Shadow Factor Storage: `R8Unorm` volume [S2, unofficial].
    -   Slice Spacing: Exponential. The view depth $z$ of the near face of slice $s$ is:
        $$ z(s) = 2^{(s + 8)/8} - 2 $$
        Slice 0 starts at the camera. Slice 8 is at depth 2. Slice 32 is at depth 30. The far face of slice 63 is at depth 510 [S2, unofficial]. Each slice is $2^{1/8}$ times as thick as the one in front [S2, unofficial].
2.  **`froxelLastIndex`:**
    -   Texture type: `R8Uint` [S2, unofficial].
    -   Purpose: Stores, per column, the index of the last slice before the opaque surface. Used by the blur to avoid pulling in froxels inside geometry [S2, unofficial].
3.  **Revealed Froxel List:**
    -   Buffer type: Storage buffer [S2, unofficial].
    -   Entry size: 12 bytes (3 x `uint32` for froxel coordinate) [S2, unofficial].
    -   Size: Sized for worst case (camera cut). One entry for every froxel in the grid plus a 4-byte result for the atomic counter [S2, unofficial].
    -   Atomic Counter: A `uint32` value indicating the number of revealed froxels. Must be initialized to zero every frame [S2, unofficial].
4.  **Blur Output Buffer:**
    -   Compact buffer indexed by list position, used to store blurred results before copying back to the main volume [S2, unofficial].

### Steps

#### 1. Froxel Injection and Shadow Sampling
One thread per column walks 64 slices front to back, stopping at the first slice past the opaque surface in that tile [S2, unofficial].

1.  **Reprojection Check:**
    -   Project the froxel's center into the previous frame's camera [G].
    -   Discard history (mark as "revealed") if:
        -   The projected center is outside the old grid [S2, unofficial].
        -   The froxel is past the opaque surface [S2, unofficial].
        -   The old tiled depth indicates the point was hidden [S2, unofficial].
    -   If history is discarded, `currentFrameAmount = 1.0` [S2, unofficial]. Otherwise, `currentFrameAmount = 0.05` (blend weight) [S2, unofficial].

2.  **Shadow Sampling:**
    -   **If Converged (`currentFrameAmount < 1.0`):**
        -   Take 4 new shadow samples.
        -   Jitter samples using blue noise across the screen tile and depth [S2, unofficial].
    -   **If Revealed (`currentFrameAmount == 1.0`):**
        -   Take two batches of samples, averaged half-and-half (12 total samples) [S2, unofficial].
        -   **Batch 1 (8 samples):** Spread along the view ray across two froxels' depth, centered on the current froxel. Weighted by triangle filter `[1, 2, 3, 4, 4, 3, 2, 1]` [S2, unofficial].
        -   **Batch 2 (4 samples):** Blue noise jittered samples along rays from the *last frame's* camera at the same screen position/depth. This assumes the shadow map has not changed (no moving sun or occluder) [S2, unofficial].

3.  **Update and Append:**
    -   Compute the new shadow factor.
    -   If `currentFrameAmount >= 1.0`:
        -   Append the froxel's coordinate to the `froxelIdsToBlur` buffer using an atomic counter [S2, unofficial].
        -   Record the index of the last slice before the opaque surface in `froxelLastIndex` [S2, unofficial].
    -   Blend the new shadow factor with the reprojected history:
        $$ \text{NewShadow} = \text{currentFrameAmount} \times \text{SampledShadow} + (1 - \text{currentFrameAmount}) \times \text{ReprojectedHistory} $$
        For converged froxels, this is a running average with a time constant of 20 frames ($0.95^n$ fade) [S2, unofficial].

#### 2. Revealed Froxel Blur
This pass is only executed if the atomic counter is non-zero [S2, unofficial]. The CPU reads the counter via `froxel_dispatch_args` and writes the dispatch size for 64-thread groups [S2, unofficial]. The blur and copy back run via `dispatch_indirect` [S2, unofficial].

1.  **Blur Kernel:**
    -   Visits the 26 neighbors and the self of the revealed froxel [S2, unofficial].
    -   Skips neighbors outside the grid or behind the opaque surface in their column (checked using `froxelLastIndex`) [S2, unofficial].
    -   Weight calculation using 3D Gaussian:
        $$ w(\mathbf{o}) = \exp\left(-\frac{\lVert\mathbf{o}\rVert^2}{2\sigma^2}\right) $$
        where $\mathbf{o}$ is the integer offset from the center in froxels, and $\sigma = 2$ [S2, unofficial].
    -   Normalizing constant is multiplied but cancels in division, so it can be omitted [S2, unofficial].
    -   At $\sigma=2$, weights for squared distances 0, 1, 2, 3 are 1, 0.88, 0.78, 0.69 respectively [S2, unofficial].
    -   Effective sample count: $(\sum w)^2 / \sum w^2 \approx 26.7$ [S2, unofficial].

2.  **Execution:**
    -   Blur writes results to the compact `blurOutput` buffer indexed by list position [S2, unofficial].
    -   This separation prevents reading blurred neighbors before they are blurred [S2, unofficial].

3.  **Copy Back:**
    -   `composite_revealed_froxels` copies the results from `blurOutput` into the main shadow volume [S2, unofficial].

### Pseudocode

```python
import math

# Global buffers (defined by engine)
GRID_W, GRID_H, GRID_D = 8, 8, 64
froxel_shadow_volume = [None] * (GRID_W * GRID_H * GRID_D)
froxel_last_index = [[0] * GRID_H for _ in range(GRID_W)]
froxel_ids_to_blur = []  # List of (col, row, slice)
froxel_atomic_counter = 0  # Conceptual atomic counter, initialized to 0 every frame
blur_output_buffer = []
tiled_depth_buffer = [[0.0] * GRID_H for _ in range(GRID_W)]
prev_tiled_depth_buffer = [[0.0] * GRID_H for _ in range(GRID_W)]
prev_proj_view_matrix = [[0.0]*4 for _ in range(4)]
current_proj_view_matrix = [[0.0]*4 for _ in range(4)]

def z_near(s):
    return 2**((s + 8) / 8) - 2

def slice_thickness(s):
    return z_near(s + 1) - z_near(s)

def froxel_inject_density_and_shadows(col, row, s):
    z = z_near(s)
    thickness = slice_thickness(s)
    opaque_depth = tiled_depth_buffer[col][row]
    
    if z > opaque_depth:
        return

    # Reprojection Check [G]
    center_world = [0.0, 0.0, z + thickness/2, 1.0]
    prev_screen = [0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0]
    for i in range(4):
        for j in range(4):
            prev_screen[i] += prev_proj_view_matrix[i][j] * center_world[j]
    if prev_screen[3] == 0.0:
        is_revealed = True
    else:
        uv_x = prev_screen[0] / prev_screen[3]
        uv_y = prev_screen[1] / prev_screen[3]
        is_revealed = (uv_x < 0 or uv_x >= GRID_W or uv_y < 0 or uv_y >= GRID_H)
        is_revealed = is_revealed or (z > opaque_depth)
        is_revealed = is_revealed or (prev_tiled_depth_buffer[int(uv_y)][int(uv_x)] < z)

    current_frame_amount = 1.0 if is_revealed else 0.05

    # Shadow Sampling
    sampled_shadow = 0.0
    if is_revealed:
        # Batch 1: 8 samples along view ray, triangle filter weights
        weights = [1, 2, 3, 4, 4, 3, 2, 1]
        sum_w1 = sum(weights)
        batch1_sum = 0.0
        for i in range(8):
            depth_offset = (i - 3.5) * thickness
            ray_origin = [0.0, 0.0, z, 1.0]
            ray_dir = [0.0, 0.0, 1.0, 0.0]
            shadow_val = sample_shadow_map(ray_origin, ray_dir)
            batch1_sum += shadow_val * weights[i]
        batch1_avg = batch1_sum / sum_w1

        # Batch 2: 4 samples from last frame's camera position
        batch2_sum = 0.0
        for i in range(4):
            jitter = get_blue_noise_jitter(i)
            ray = get_ray_from_prev_camera(uv_x, uv_y, z, jitter)
            shadow_val = sample_shadow_map(ray.origin, ray.direction)
            batch2_sum += shadow_val
        batch2_avg = batch2_sum / 4.0

        sampled_shadow = (batch1_avg + batch2_avg) / 2.0
    else:
        sum_w2 = 0.0
        for i in range(4):
            jitter = get_blue_noise_jitter(i)
            ray = get_ray_from_current_camera(jitter)
            shadow_val = sample_shadow_map(ray.origin, ray.direction)
            sum_w2 += shadow_val
        sampled_shadow = sum_w2 / 4.0

    # Blend
    reprojected_history = reproject_shadow_from_prev_frame(uv_x, uv_y, s)
    new_shadow = current_frame_amount * sampled_shadow + (1.0 - current_frame_amount) * reprojected_history

    # Store and Append if revealed
    froxel_shadow_volume[col * GRID_H * GRID_D + row * GRID_D + s] = new_shadow

    if is_revealed:
        froxel_atomic_counter += 1
        froxel_ids_to_blur.append((col, row, s))
        froxel_last_index[col][row] = s - 1

def blur_revealed_froxels(list_idx):
    col, row, s = froxel_ids_to_blur[list_idx]
    last_valid_slice = froxel_last_index[col][row]
    weighted_sum = 0.0
    weighted_shadow_sum = 0.0

    for dx in [-1, 0, 1]:
        for dy in [-1, 0, 1]:
            for dz in [-1, 0, 1]:
                n_col = col + dx
                n_row = row + dy
                n_slice = s + dz

                if n_col < 0 or n_col >= GRID_W or n_row < 0 or n_row >= GRID_H or n_slice < 0 or n_slice >= GRID_D:
                    continue

                n_last_slice = froxel_last_index[n_col][n_row]
                if n_slice > n_last_slice:
                    continue

                dist_sq = dx*dx + dy*dy + dz*dz
                sigma = 2.0
                weight = math.exp(-dist_sq / (2.0 * sigma * sigma))

                shadow_val = froxel_shadow_volume[n_col * GRID_H * GRID_D + n_row * GRID_D + n_slice]

                weighted_sum += weight
                weighted_shadow_sum += weight * shadow_val

    if weighted_sum > 0.0:
        blurred_shadow = weighted_shadow_sum / weighted_sum
    else:
        blurred_shadow = froxel_shadow_volume[col * GRID_H * GRID_D + row * GRID_D + s]

    blur_output_buffer.append(blurred_shadow)

def composite_revealed_froxels(list_idx):
    col, row, s = froxel_ids_to_blur[list_idx]
    froxel_shadow_volume[col * GRID_H * GRID_D + row * GRID_D + s] = blur_output_buffer[list_idx]
```

### Parameters
-   **Grid Dimensions:** 8x8 pixel tiles, 64 slices [S2, unofficial].
-   **Blend Weight (Converged):** 0.05 (Time constant ~20 frames) [S2, unofficial].
-   **Blur Sigma:** 2 [S2, unofficial].
-   **Sample Counts:** 4 (converged), 12 (revealed: 8 + 4) [S2, unofficial].

### Edge Cases and Pitfalls
-   **Anisotropy:** Slice spacing is exponential. One slice of blur radius is 0.18 deep at the front and 42.5 deep at the back. A kernel isotropic in index space is not isotropic in world space [S2, unofficial].
-   **Reprojection Failure:** History is discarded if the froxel's center projects outside the old grid, if the froxel is past the opaque surface, or if the old tiled depth indicates the point was hidden. In these cases, the froxel takes 100% of the current frame's value [S2, unofficial].
-   **Moving Sun/Occluder:** Sampling today's shadow map at last frame's positions assumes the shadow map has not changed. A moving sun or moving occluder breaks this assumption, causing noise in the revealed froxel's second batch of samples [S2, unofficial].
-   **Vulkan Robust Buffer Access:** On Vulkan without robust buffer access, out-of-bounds reads are undefined. D3D12 returns zero for out-of-bounds reads. The fix moves bounds test ahead of load and corrects comparisons (`>=` instead of `>`) [S2, unofficial].
-   **Atomic Counter Initialization:** The counter must be initialized to zero every frame. If not, it starts with garbage memory values, causing out-of-bounds appends [S2, unofficial].
-   **Division by Zero:** If a froxel has no usable neighbors (e.g., all outside grid or behind opaque surface), the weight sum is zero. The shader must check `weightedSum > 0.0` before dividing, falling back to the unblurred value [S2, unofficial].

## Operating characteristics
-   **Time Complexity:** The inject pass is $O(N)$ where $N$ is the number of froxels (one thread per column) [S2, unofficial]. The blur pass is $O(K)$ where $K$ is the number of revealed froxels [S2, unofficial]. In the worst case (camera cut), $K$ equals the total number of froxels [S2, unofficial]. In typical frames, $K$ is small [S2, unofficial].
-   **Memory Complexity:** $O(N)$ for the froxel volumes and the revealed froxel list buffer [S2, unofficial].
-   **Measured Performance:** A frame where nothing was revealed costs a single-thread pass and two empty indirect dispatches [S2, unofficial]. A camera cut costs a blur over most of the grid [S2, unofficial].
-   **Strengths:** Reduces noise in revealed froxels without blurring converged history. Cost is proportional to the number of revealed froxels [S2, unofficial].
-   **Limitations:** Does not fix the root cause of revealed froxel noise (lack of shadow history). Relies on static shadow maps (no moving sun/occluder) [S2, unofficial]. Anisotropic blur due to exponential slice spacing.
-   **When to Use:** When temporal reprojection fails frequently (e.g., fast camera movements, occluder edges) and shadow map history buffers are not available.
-   **When Not to Use:** When shadow map history buffers are available (preferred solution). When the sun or occluders are moving rapidly.

## Background
Froxel fog was established in Bart Wronski's volumetric fog talk at SIGGRAPH 2014 and given a physically based form in Sébastien Hillaire's talk in the same course a year later [S2, unofficial]. The specific technique of shadowing newly revealed froxels was implemented in the Breda renderer [S2, unofficial].
